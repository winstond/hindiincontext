असल में (asal men)

adverb A common discourse adverb meaning 'actually, in reality' -- used to correct a misconception or to introduce the real state of affairs. Built from असल (asal, 'reality/essence') plus में (men, 'in').
Sense 1 : actually, in fact, in reality
adverb
Synonyms: वास्तव में (vaastav men, formal), दरअसल (daraasal, one-word variant), सच तो यह है कि (sach to yah hai ki, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase people reach for when gently correcting someone: someone assumes one thing, and you step in with असल में (asal men, 'actually...') to reveal what's really going on. Its close cousin दरअसल (daraasal) does the same job in a single word and is extremely common in spoken Delhi Hindi -- you'll hear the two used interchangeably by the same speaker in one conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often place असल में (asal men) only at the end of a sentence, as English speakers do with 'actually'; native speakers most often put it at the very start of the sentence, right where the correction or clarification begins. 2) Don't confuse it with सच में (sach men, 'really/truly'), which expresses sincerity or surprise rather than correcting a wrong impression.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे लगा वो नाराज़ है, पर असल में वो बस थका हुआ था। I thought he was angry, but actually he was just tired.
(mujhe lagaa vo naaraaz hai, par asal men vo bas thakaa huaa thaa।)
