व्यापारी (vyaapaarii)

noun A single core meaning: someone who does trade or business, i.e. 'merchant/trader/businessman.' Related to व्यापार (vyaapaar, 'business/trade').
Sense 1 : merchant, trader, businessman
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: दुकानदार (dukaandaar, shopkeeper specifically), कारोबारी (kaarobaarii), सौदागर (saudaagar, literary/old-fashioned flavor), बिज़नेसमैन (biznesmain, colloquial English loan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: व्यापारी (vyaapaarii) is the word you'll hear for the local cloth merchant, grain wholesaler, or the uncle who runs three shops in the market -- it carries a slightly more 'traditional business family' flavor than the newer loanword बिज़नेसमैन (biznesmain), which younger, English-educated speakers use for a more corporate or modern entrepreneur. Many व्यापारी (vyaapaarii) families belong to trading communities like Marwaris or Baniyas, and the word often shows up in news about व्यापारी संघ (vyaapaarii sangh, 'traders' association') protests over taxes or market regulations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use व्यापारी (vyaapaarii) for any employee of a company, but it specifically means someone who owns or runs the trade themselves, not a salaried worker. 2) Don't confuse with व्यापार (vyaapaar), which is the abstract noun 'business/trade' itself, not the person doing it.
