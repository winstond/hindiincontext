भाषा (bhaashaa)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'language' -- the words and grammar a community speaks, or a named language like Hindi or English.
Sense 1 : language (a system of speech/writing, or a named tongue)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: ज़बान (zabaan, everyday, especially in speech), बोली (bolii, usually 'dialect/spoken tongue' rather than a full standardized language)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see भाषा (bhaashaa) everywhere official -- राजभाषा (raajbhaashaa, 'official language') and मातृभाषा (maatribhaashaa, 'mother tongue') are both built on this root, and India's own multilingual identity makes phrases like कौन सी भाषा बोलते हैं? (kaun sii bhaashaa bolte hain?, 'which language do you speak?') a very natural icebreaker between strangers on a train.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बोली (bolii) as a plain synonym, but it usually implies a spoken dialect or mother tongue rather than an official/written language -- हिंदी भाषा (hindii bhaashaa, 'the Hindi language') sounds more standard than हिंदी बोली (hindii bolii) in a formal context. 2) भाषा (bhaashaa) is feminine, so adjectives and verbs agreeing with it take feminine forms: यह भाषा कठिन है (yah bhaashaa kathin hai, 'this language is difficult'), not the masculine form.

(aap kaun sii bhaashaa bolte hain?)
