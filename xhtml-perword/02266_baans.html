बाँस (baans)

noun This word means 'bamboo' -- the plant and its wood/cane. It has one primary sense, though it appears in a few common idioms about trouble and conflict.
Sense 1 : bamboo (plant or cane/pole)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Bamboo is everywhere in North Indian rural and small-town life -- scaffolding at construction sites, ladders, fishing poles, and the poles used to pitch shamiana (shaamiyaanaa) tents for weddings all use बाँस (baans). It also shows up in the common idiom बाँस बजना (baans bajnaa, 'for it to go badly for someone') and the saying ना रहेगा बाँस, ना बजेगी बांसुरी (naa rahegaa baans, naa bajegii baansurii, 'no bamboo, no flute' -- meaning remove the root cause and the problem disappears), which many Delhi-NCR speakers know as a political and everyday proverb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget the chandrabindu and write बास (baas) instead of बाँस (baans) -- बास is a different word meaning 'smell/stench' (or 'residence' in compounds), so dropping the nasalization changes the meaning entirely.
