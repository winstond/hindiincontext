कमाई (kamaaii)

noun A noun meaning 'earnings' or 'income', derived from the verb कमाना (kamaanaa, 'to earn'); it also carries an extended sense of 'gain, reward' for effort more broadly.
Sense 1 : earnings, income
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आमदनी (aamadnii), आय (aay, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in everyday talk about jobs and money -- अच्छी कमाई है (achchhii kamaaii hai, 'there's good earning [in this]') is a classic way Delhi shopkeepers and auto drivers size up a line of work. It carries a slightly more colloquial, everyday feel than the more formal आय (aay) used in tax forms and news reports.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse कमाई (kamaaii) with तनख्वाह (tanakhvaah, 'salary'); कमाई (kamaaii) is broader and covers any money earned, not just a fixed monthly wage. 2) The verb often paired with this noun is होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) -- e.g. कमाई होना (kamaaii honaa, 'to earn/generate income') vs. कमाई करना (kamaaii karnaa, 'to do earning'), and learners sometimes force the wrong one into a sentence.

Sense 2 : gain, reward, fruit of effort (figurative)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फ़ायदा (faaydaa), हासिल (haasil, gain/achievement)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Beyond literal money, कमाई (kamaaii) extends to any hard-won reward -- as in पुण्य की कमाई (puny kii kamaaii, 'the earnings/merit of virtue'), a common phrase in religious and moral talk that treats good deeds like a kind of spiritual income.
