मसजिद (masjid)

noun A variant spelling of मस्जिद (masjid, 'mosque') without the conjunct halant; same single meaning.
Sense 1 : mosque
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मस्जिद (masjid, dominant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear मस्जिद (masjid) constantly in everyday Delhi-NCR speech around Old Delhi landmarks like जामा मसजिद (jaamaa masjid, 'Jama Masjid') -- interestingly, that very landmark's name is very commonly seen written exactly in this simplified spelling on signs and maps, which is probably the most familiar place learners encounter this particular variant.
Common Mistakes: 1) This spelling drops the conjunct in मस्जिद (masjid), writing सज (saj) as a plain sequence instead of स्ज (sj) -- both are read the same way, but मस्जिद (masjid) is what you will see far more often in print and signage, so recognize this form but lean on the standard one when writing yourself. 2) Learners sometimes try to guess the gender from the ending -- it is feminine, so it takes वाली (vaalii), बड़ी (badii), etc., not बड़ा (badaa).
