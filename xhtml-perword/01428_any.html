अन्य (any)

adjective/pronoun A formal, written-register word meaning 'other/another', used mainly in writing and careful speech where everyday conversation would use दूसरा (doosraa).
Sense 1 : other, another (of a different kind)
adjective
Synonyms: दूसरा (doosraa), और (aur)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see अन्य (any) constantly on forms, in news reports, and in official notices -- 'अन्य जानकारी' (any jaankaarii, 'other information') is a classic bureaucratic phrase. In everyday chat among friends, दूसरा (doosraa) is what people actually say, so think of अन्य (any) as the 'other' you read, not the 'other' you speak.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अन्य (any) in speech where दूसरा (doosraa) is the natural choice; अन्य (any) sounds bookish or official in casual conversation, more like the English 'other' in a report than in a chat. 2) Unlike दूसरा (doosraa), अन्य (any) is invariant and does not change for gender or number, so learners sometimes wrongly add endings like अन्या or अन्ये by analogy with other adjectives.

फॉर्म में अन्य जानकारी भरना अनिवार्य है। Filling in other information on the form is mandatory.
(phorm men any jaankaarii bharnaa anivaary hai।)

Sense 2 : another one, others (used standalone, without a following noun)
pronoun Used without a following noun, standing in for 'other people' or 'other things,' often in lists or formal enumerations: 'कुछ अन्य' (kuchh any, 'some others').
Synonyms: दूसरे (doosre), और लोग (aur log, others, people)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This use shows up a lot in written lists and announcements, like 'अन्य उपस्थित थे' (any upasthit the, 'others were present') in a meeting report -- again, a register you read more than you speak.
