पृष्ठभूमि (prishthbhoomi)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'background' -- used both literally (visual background) and figuratively (a person's background or the backdrop/context of events).
Sense 1 : background (context, backdrop, or a person's background)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बैकग्राउंड (baikagraaund, colloquial, English loanword), संदर्भ (sandarbh, context/setting)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly on Hindi news channels, as in इस फैसले की पृष्ठभूमि में (is phaisle kii prishthbhoomi men, 'against the background of this decision') -- it is a favorite of political and journalistic commentary. It literally breaks down as पृष्ठ (prishth, 'page/back/surface') plus भूमि (bhoomi, 'ground/land'), so the image is quite literally 'the ground behind' -- a nice parallel to the English word 'background' itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बैकग्राउंड (baikagraaund) in casual speech, which is fine informally, but पृष्ठभूमि (prishthbhoomi) is the word expected in writing, news, and formal contexts -- know both registers. 2) When describing a person's background (family, education, social origin), पृष्ठभूमि (prishthbhoomi) is used with की (kii): जैसे किसान परिवार की पृष्ठभूमि (kisaan parivaar kii prishthbhoomi, 'a background from a farming family') -- learners sometimes try to use से (se) here by analogy with English 'from,' which sounds off.
Sample Sentences:
इस फैसले की पृष्ठभूमि में कई राजनीतिक कारण हैं। There are many political reasons behind the background of this decision.
(is phaisle kii prishthbhoomi men kaii raajniitik kaaran hain।)
