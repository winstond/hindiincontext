सादा (saadaa)

adjective Mainly describes something plain or simple -- unadorned food, clothes, or lifestyle -- and by extension a person's simple, unpretentious character.
Sense 1 : plain, unadorned (food, clothes, style)
adjective
Synonyms: सामान्य (saamaany), सरल (saral), साधारण (saadhaaran)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around food: सादा खाना (saadaa khaanaa, 'plain/simple food') means something light and unspiced, like plain dal-chawal, often what people eat when they're unwell or trying to eat healthy. It carries a gentle positive connotation of purity and moderation rather than blandness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सादा (saadaa, 'plain') with साफ़ (saaf, 'clean') -- a सादा (saadaa) khaanaa is plain/simple food, not necessarily 'clean' food. 2) Do not confuse with सादी (saadii), a feminine-inflected form of this same word, and शादी (shaadii, 'wedding') which sounds similar but is unrelated.

Sense 2 : simple, unpretentious (of a person, lifestyle, manner)
adjective
Synonyms: सीधा (siidhaa), निश्छल (nishchhal, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind the well-known phrase सादा जीवन, उच्च विचार (saadaa jiivan, uchch vichaar, 'simple living, high thinking') associated with Gandhian values -- describing someone as सादा (saadaa) is a compliment, suggesting humility and lack of showiness rather than dullness.
