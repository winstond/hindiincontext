हासिल करना (haasil karnaa)

verb A compound built from the Persian-origin noun हासिल (haasil, 'obtained/acquired') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); together they mean 'to obtain, achieve, attain.'
Sense 1 : to obtain, achieve, attain (a goal, degree, victory, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: प्राप्त करना (praapt karnaa), पाना (paanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in the context of exams and careers: मंज़िल हासिल करना (manzil haasil karnaa, 'to achieve one's goal') is a very common motivational phrase in Hindi speeches and songs. It carries a slightly more formal, aspirational flavor than पाना (paanaa), which is why it shows up so often in parents' pep talks about students' futures.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पाना (paanaa) in every context, but हासिल करना (haasil karnaa) specifically implies effortful achievement -- a degree, a target, a victory -- and sounds off if used for something simply received, like a gift. 2) The object of हासिल करना (haasil karnaa) is marked with ने (ne) in the past perfective, just like other transitive verbs with करना (karnaa) -- learners sometimes forget this because हासिल (haasil) itself feels like an adjective, not a verb.
Sample Sentences:
अगर तुम मेहनत करो तो अपनी मंज़िल हासिल कर सकते हो। If you work hard, you can achieve your goal.
(agar tum mehnat karo to apnii manzil haasil kar sakte ho।)
