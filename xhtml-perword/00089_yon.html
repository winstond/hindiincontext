यों (yon)

adverb A small adverb/particle covering 'like this, in this way' and the filler sense 'just like that, for no particular reason' (as in यों ही, yon hii).
Sense 1 : just like that, without particular reason or effort
adverb Most often appears as the fixed phrase यों ही (yon hii), which functions almost like a single adverbial unit meaning 'idly, casually, for no reason.'
Synonyms: बस ऐसे ही (bas aise hii), ऐसे ही (aise hii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: यों ही (yon hii) is the phrase you'll hear when someone brushes off a question, like कुछ नहीं, यों ही पूछ रहा था (kuchh nahiin, yon hii poochh rahaa thaa, 'nothing, I was just asking casually') -- a very Delhi way of deflecting without being rude.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often skip the ही (hii) and just say यों (yon) alone when they mean 'just like that' -- native speakers almost always reach for यों ही (yon hii) in this casual sense, so यों (yon) by itself can sound oddly literary or clipped. 2) Don't confuse this with वैसे (vaise, 'by the way/anyway'), which is far more common in casual conversation for a similar dismissive shrug.

Sense 2 : in this way, thus
adverb Used to refer forward or backward to a manner already described or about to be described, similar to इस प्रकार (is prakaar) but shorter and more literary in feel.
Synonyms: इस तरह (is tarah), ऐसे (aise)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense leans a bit more formal/written than spoken Hindi -- in everyday conversation people reach for ऐसे (aise) or इस तरह (is tarah) instead, so यों (yon) in this meaning shows up more in speeches, essays, or careful narration.
