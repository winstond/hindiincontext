होश सँभालना (hosh sambhaalnaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to reach the age of awareness/consciousness' -- used almost always to talk about the point in childhood when a person's memory and understanding of the world begins.
Sense 1 : to become aware/conscious (of one's surroundings), usually as a child growing up
verb, transitive Almost always used in the perfective with a possessive/genitive subject construction: X ने होश सँभाला (X ne hosh sambhaalaa) or जब से होश सँभाला है (jab se hosh sambhaalaa hai, 'since I've been aware/since I can remember'). होश (hosh, 'senses/consciousness') is the object, सँभालना (sambhaalnaa, 'to hold/manage') is the verb -- literally 'to take hold of one's senses'.
Synonyms: समझदार होना (samajhdaar honaa, to become sensible/aware), सूझ-बूझ आना (soojh-boojh aanaa, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase is how Hindi speakers talk about 'as far back as I can remember' -- a grandmother might say जब मैंने होश सँभाला, तब से यही घर देखा है (jab mainne hosh sambhaalaa, tab se yahii ghar dekhaa hai, 'since I became aware, I've only known this house'), anchoring a whole life story to the moment memory begins. It pairs naturally with बचपन (bachpan, 'childhood') in reminiscing conversations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use this only in the simple present, but it is overwhelmingly used in the perfective (होश सँभाला (hosh sambhaalaa)) or with जब से (jab se, 'since') to mean 'for as long as I can remember.' 2) Do not confuse with होश में आना (hosh men aanaa), which means regaining consciousness after fainting or being knocked out -- a physical, momentary sense, not this developmental one.

(jab mainne hosh sambhaalaa, tab se yahii ghar dekhaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to regain composure or self-control (after a shock, fear, or strong emotion)
verb, transitive Used when someone recovers their composure after a jolt, e.g. डर के मारे बदहवास हो गया, फिर होश सँभाला (dar ke maare badahvaas ho gayaa, phir hosh sambhaalaa, 'he panicked from fear, then pulled himself together').
Synonyms: संभलना (sambhalnaa, to steady oneself), खुद को काबू करना (khud ko kaaboo karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense overlaps in flavor with होश में आना (hosh men aanaa) but leans more toward regaining emotional/mental composure rather than physical consciousness -- think of steadying your nerves rather than waking up from a faint.
