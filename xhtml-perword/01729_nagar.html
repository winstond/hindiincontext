नगर (nagar)

noun A formal/literary word for 'city' or 'town', most often met today in place names, civic institution names, and news/formal registers rather than casual chat.
Sense 1 : city, town
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शहर (shahar), महानगर (mahaangar, big city)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet नगर (nagar) far more on signboards and in names than in conversation -- Delhi itself is full of colonies ending in -नगर (nagar) like करोल बाग़ या लक्ष्मी नगर (lakshmii nagar), and municipal bodies call themselves नगर निगम (nagar nigam). For everyday talk about 'the city,' शहर (shahar) is what a Delhi-ite actually says.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नगर (nagar) in everyday speech where शहर (shahar) is what people actually say; नगर (nagar) sounds bookish or official in casual conversation. 2) It shows up constantly as a suffix in place and institution names (नगर निगम (nagar nigam, 'municipal corporation'), लक्ष्मी नगर (lakshmii nagar)) even though it is rare as a standalone word in speech.
