व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'personal/individual' -- used for personal belongings, personal matters, and personal opinions alike.
Sense 1 : personal, individual, private
adjective
Synonyms: निजी (nijii), शख्सी (shakhsii, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear व्यक्तिगत रूप से (vyaktigat roop se, 'personally speaking') a lot in Indian offices and news interviews when someone wants to separate their own view from their official one -- it is the go-to hedge before giving an opinion that might ruffle feathers. It is built from व्यक्ति (vyakti, 'person/individual') plus the adjectival suffix -गत (gat, roughly 'pertaining to'), the same pattern you see in मानसिक (maansik) or सामाजिक (saamaajik)-type formations from abstract nouns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use यह मेरा व्यक्तिगत है (yah meraa vyaktigat hai) to mean 'this is mine personally,' but व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat) modifies a noun (व्यक्तिगत राय (vyaktigat raay, 'personal opinion')) -- it does not stand alone as a predicate meaning 'belongs to me personally'; use यह मेरी निजी चीज़ है (yah merii nijii chiiz hai, 'this is my personal thing') instead. 2) व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat) and निजी (nijii) overlap heavily, but व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat) leans toward 'pertaining to a specific individual' (व्यक्तिगत राय (vyaktigat raay, 'personal opinion'), व्यक्तिगत जीवन (vyaktigat jiivan, 'personal life')) while निजी (nijii) leans toward 'private/not public' (निजी कंपनी (nijii kampnii, 'private company')) -- they are often interchangeable but not always.

यह मेरा व्यक्तिगत मामला है, इसमें दखल मत दो। This is my personal matter, don't interfere in it.
(yah meraa vyaktigat maamlaa hai, ismen dakhal mat do।)
