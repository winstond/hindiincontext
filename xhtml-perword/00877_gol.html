गोल (gol)

adjective/noun Two main threads: an adjective meaning 'round/circular', and a noun meaning 'goal' (as in sports) or 'group/party' -- the sporting sense is a borrowing that rides on the same sound.
Sense 1 : round, circular
adjective
Synonyms: वृत्ताकार (vrittaakaar, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in the kitchen -- रोटी को गोल बेलो (rotii ko gol belo, 'roll the roti round') is a classic line a mother or grandmother says while teaching someone to cook. It's also the base of the common phrase गोल-गोल बातें करना (gol-gol baaten karnaa, 'to talk in circles, to be evasive'), which every Hindi speaker recognizes instantly.
Common Mistakes: 1) गोल (gol) itself never changes form for gender or number -- learners sometimes try to inflect it like कड़ा (kadaa) and add endings that don't belong.
Sample Sentences:
यह गेंद पूरी तरह गोल है। This ball is completely round.
(yah gend poorii tarah gol hai।)

Sense 2 : goal (in sports, e.g. football/hockey)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a borrowing from English 'goal', used exactly as in cricket-obsessed India's other sporting passions -- football and hockey commentary is full of गोल! गोल! (gol! gol!, 'goal! goal!'), shouted with real excitement.
Common Mistakes: 1) This गोल (gol) is unrelated in meaning to the adjective 'round' even though they're spelled identically -- context (sports commentary) makes it clear.
Sample Sentences:
उसने आखिरी मिनट में गोल कर दिया। He scored a goal in the last minute.

Sense 3 : group, circle, ring (of people)
noun, masculine Often appears in the phrase गोल बनाना (gol banaanaa, 'to form a circle/huddle') or बच्चों का गोल (bachchon kaa gol, 'a group of children').
Synonyms: घेरा (gheraa), टोली (tolii)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Think of kids playing कबड्डी (kabaddii) or a group of aunties settling into a गोल (gol) of plastic chairs at a neighborhood gathering -- the word captures that sense of people naturally clustering into a ring.
Sample Sentences:
