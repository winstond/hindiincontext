मोम (mom)

noun/adjective A concrete noun meaning wax, and by extension used figuratively for someone who is soft-hearted or easily melted emotionally.
Sense 1 : wax (the substance)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the wax used for candles (मोमबत्ती, mombattii, 'wax-candle') or for sealing letters -- मोम (mom) is the base word tucked inside that compound. If you've heard मोमबत्ती (mombattii) for 'candle,' you already know this word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to make मोम (mom) agree like a countable noun (giving it a plural form); it is treated as a mass noun, much like पानी (paanii) or दूध (doodh), so it normally stays in the singular even when referring to a general quantity.

Sense 2 : soft-hearted, easily melted (emotionally)
adjective Used mainly in the fixed comparison मोम हो जाना (mom ho jaanaa, 'to turn to wax') or दिल मोम का होना (dil mom kaa honaa, 'to have a heart of wax') describing someone whose resolve or hard exterior melts, typically under emotion or pleading.
Synonyms: नरम दिल (naram dil, softhearted), भावुक (bhaavuk, emotional/sentimental)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This image works the same way in Hindi as in English -- a heart 'melting' with compassion -- so it's an easy metaphor to adopt. You'll often hear it paired with दिल (dil, 'heart'), as in दिल मोम का हो गया (dil mom kaa ho gayaa, 'the heart turned to wax/melted').
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense almost always appears inside the fixed expressions मोम हो जाना (mom ho jaanaa) or दिल मोम का (dil mom kaa) rather than मोम (mom) modifying a noun directly like a typical adjective (e.g., you won't usually hear मोम इंसान, mom insaan); learners should treat it as tied to these set phrases rather than a free-standing descriptive word.
