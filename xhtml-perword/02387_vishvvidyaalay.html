विश्वविद्यालय (vishvvidyaalay)

noun A single, straightforward sense: 'university.' A formal Sanskritic compound built from विश्व (vishv, 'world/universe') and विद्यालय (vidyaalay, 'place of learning') -- literally 'world-place-of-learning.'
Sense 1 : university
noun, masculine
Synonyms: यूनिवर्सिटी (yoonivarsitii, common in casual speech)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word on the gates and letterheads of institutions like दिल्ली विश्वविद्यालय (dillii vishvvidyaalay, 'Delhi University') and जवाहरलाल नेहरू विश्वविद्यालय (javaaharlaal nehroo vishvvidyaalay, 'Jawaharlal Nehru University', often called JNU). In everyday chat, though, most Delhi students just say यूनिवर्सिटी (yoonivarsitii) or its short form 'DU' or 'JNU' -- विश्वविद्यालय (vishvvidyaalay) feels a notch more formal, like something you'd write on a form rather than say to a friend on the phone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to यूनिवर्सिटी (yoonivarsitii) in speech since it is shorter and very common in casual conversation; विश्वविद्यालय (vishvvidyaalay) is the word you will see on signboards, official documents, and news, and is expected in formal writing. 2) The word is long but perfectly regular -- learners sometimes try to shorten or contract it in speech, but native speakers pronounce it in full even when speaking quickly.

(dillii vishvvidyaalay bhaarat ke sabse puraane vishvvidyaalyon men se ek hai।)
