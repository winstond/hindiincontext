कोमल (komal)

adjective An adjective meaning 'soft, tender, delicate' -- used both for physical texture and for gentle emotions/nature; also survives as a common personal name.
Sense 1 : gentle, tender (emotions, nature, temperament)
adjective
Synonyms: सौम्य (saumy), नाज़ुक (naazuk, can overlap with 'fragile')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense you meet in phrases like कोमल भावनाएँ (komal bhaavnaaen, 'tender feelings') or कोमल स्वभाव (komal svabhaav, 'gentle nature') -- Hindi often uses this same word to bridge the physical and emotional, so a 'soft heart' and 'soft cloth' share one adjective where English would split them.

Sense 2 : soft, delicate (physical texture)
adjective
Synonyms: मुलायम (mulaayam), नरम (naram)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of कोमल (komal) as the word you'd use for a baby's skin, a flower petal, or a soft voice -- it carries a gentleness that goes beyond just 'not hard.' It's a common girl's name in India for exactly this reason, evoking tenderness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नरम (naram) or मुलायम (mulaayam) by default; कोमल (komal) is not wrong but leans slightly more literary/refined than these two everyday words, so it sounds a touch elevated in casual speech about, say, bread or cloth.
