पन्ना (pannaa)

noun Two unrelated meanings share this spelling: 'page' (of a book) and the gemstone 'emerald.'
Sense 1 : page (of a book, notebook, etc.)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पृष्ठ (prishth, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a schoolkid flipping through a notebook: 'is pannaa mode with kitaab' ke panne palatna (kitaab ke panne palatnaa, 'to flip through the pages of a book') is the everyday image behind this word. पृष्ठ (prishth) shows up on official forms and in printed citations, but panna is what you'd hear at home or in school.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पृष्ठ (prishth) by default because it looks like the 'dictionary' word; in everyday speech about a book or notebook, पन्ना (pannaa) is what people actually say. 2) पन्ना (pannaa) refers to a leaf/page of a bound object; for a loose sheet of paper, कागज़ (kaagaz) is more natural.

Sense 2 : emerald (the gemstone)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मरकत (markat, literary)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In Indian astrology (jyotish), पन्ना (pannaa) is the gemstone linked to the planet Budh (budh, Mercury), and you will hear jewelers and astrologers alike recommend wearing one set in a ring for luck or to calm a difficult planetary placement.
