खुलकर (khulkar)

adverb An adverb built from the verb खुलना (khulnaa, 'to open') plus the conjunctive participle suffix -कर (kar); it describes doing something 'openly, freely, without holding back.'
Sense 1 : openly, freely, without inhibition
adverb Commonly pairs with verbs like बोलना (bolnaa), हँसना (hansnaa), बताना (bataanaa), and मिलना (milnaa) to mean speaking, laughing, or meeting without holding back.
Synonyms: बेझिझक (bejhijhak, without hesitation), साफ़ साफ़ (saaf saaf, plainly/frankly), बिना रोक-टोक (binaa rok-tok, without restriction)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word captures a very Hindi idea: opening up like a closed thing (खुलना (khulnaa)) becoming unlocked. You will hear it a lot in advice like खुलकर बात करो (khulkar baat karo, 'talk openly') from a friend or counselor encouraging someone who is holding feelings in, much like English 'open up.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat खुलकर (khulkar) as an adjective and try to inflect it -- it is an invariant adverb and never changes form. 2) It is often confused with सीधे (siidhe, 'directly'); खुलकर (khulkar) is about emotional openness or lack of restraint, not about directness of route or manner.

(tum mujhse khulkar baat kar sakte ho।)
