सड़सठ (sadsath)

numeral The cardinal number 67 (sixty-seven).
Sense 1 : the number 67
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers between 21 and 99, सड़सठ (sadsath) has to be learned as its own memorized item rather than built from साठ (saath, '60') plus सात (saat, '7') the way English combines 'sixty' and 'seven' -- this irregularity is a well-known hurdle for learners and even many Hindi-speaking schoolchildren rely on rote tables (paharas) to master this range.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mix up सड़सठ (sadsath, '67') with similar-sounding numbers in the sixties and seventies, like सड़सठ (sadsath) vs. सत्तर (sattar, '70') or सैंसठ (sainsath, '67' is correct but sometimes confused with सतसठ-type mishearings); the safest approach is to simply memorize each number in this range individually rather than trying to derive it from a pattern, since Hindi numerals 21-99 are largely irregular and not compositional the way English tens-and-units are.
