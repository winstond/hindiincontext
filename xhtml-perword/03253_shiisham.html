शीशम (shiisham)

noun A tree name -- refers to the sheesham or Indian rosewood tree, and by extension the wood/timber from it, prized for furniture.
Sense 1 : the sheesham tree (Indian rosewood)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: टाहली (taahlii, regional/Punjabi term), शीशम का पेड़ (shiisham kaa ped, explicit 'tree' phrasing)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: शीशम (shiisham) is a common shade tree seen lining roads and village fields across North India, especially in Punjab, Haryana, and the Delhi-NCR belt. If you grew up here, you have probably sat under one -- it is as familiar a roadside sight as the नीम (niim) tree.

Sense 2 : sheesham wood/timber
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शीशम की लकड़ी (shiisham kii lakdii, explicit 'wood' phrasing)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Ask any furniture-maker in Delhi's Kirti Nagar market about शीशम (shiisham) and they will light up -- it is one of the most respected woods for tables, beds, and doors because of its dark grain and durability, often positioned as a sturdier, cheaper alternative to teak.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse शीशम (shiisham) with सागौन (saagaun, teak) -- both are prized furniture woods but are different trees and have different grain/color; a furniture shopkeeper will assume you know the difference if you ask for either by name.
