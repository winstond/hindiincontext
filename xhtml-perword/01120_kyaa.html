क्या (kyaa)

pronoun/particle/adverb The core interrogative word for 'what', which also does double duty as a yes/no question marker and as an intensifier in exclamations; the senses split along those three lines.
Sense 1 : what (interrogative pronoun)
pronoun Used to ask about the identity or content of a thing, as the object or subject of a verb: क्या हुआ (kyaa huaa, 'what happened'), क्या चाहिए (kyaa chaahie, 'what do you need').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the first words every learner picks up, right alongside नमस्ते (namaste) -- you will hear क्या हुआ (kyaa huaa, 'what happened') and क्या बात है (kyaa baat hai, 'what's up/what's the matter') constantly in daily banter among friends and family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse क्या (kyaa, 'what') with कौन (kaun, 'who') when asking about people versus things -- क्या (kyaa) is strictly for things, ideas, and actions, never for people. 2) In a sentence like आपका नाम क्या है (aapkaa naam kyaa hai, 'what is your name'), learners sometimes try to front क्या (kyaa) the way English fronts 'what', but Hindi word order keeps it in its natural slot before है (hai).

Sense 2 : yes/no question marker
particle Placed at the start of a sentence to turn a statement into a yes/no question without changing word order: तुम आ रहे हो (tum aa rahe ho, 'you are coming') becomes क्या तुम आ रहे हो (kyaa tum aa rahe ho, 'are you coming?').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a wonderfully economical trick in Hindi: no need to invert subject and verb like English 'are you...' -- just drop क्या (kyaa) on the front of any statement and it becomes a question. Very handy once it clicks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often skip this क्या (kyaa) entirely and rely only on rising intonation, which is fine in casual speech but sounds incomplete in careful or written Hindi. 2) It's easy to confuse this particle with the interrogative pronoun sense (question word 'what') -- here it carries no meaning of its own and simply flags that a yes/no answer is expected.

Sense 3 : what a...! / how...! (exclamatory intensifier)
adverb Introduces an exclamation about a quality or scene: क्या मज़ा है (kyaa mazaa hai, 'what fun!'), क्या बात है (kyaa baat hai, 'what a thing! / how wonderful!').
Synonyms: कितना (kitnaa, overlaps in exclamatory 'how much/how' use)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: क्या बात है (kyaa baat hai) is a classic all-purpose exclamation -- it can mean 'how wonderful!' with a big smile, or 'what's going on?' with a raised eyebrow, entirely depending on tone. Delhi friends toss this around constantly, so tuning your ear to the emotional pitch matters more than the words themselves.
Common Mistakes: 1) This exclamatory क्या (kyaa) is easy to mistake for the question marker if you only listen for the word itself -- tone and context (an exuberant, admiring delivery) are what signal the exclamation here, not word order.
