स्थल (sthal)

noun A formal/written word for a 'place' or 'site' -- most often met in compounds like जन्मस्थल (janmasthal, birthplace) or दुर्घटनास्थल (durghatnaasthal, accident site) rather than standing alone in casual speech.
Sense 1 : place, site, spot
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जगह (jagah), स्थान (sthaan), जगह-जगह (jagah-jagah, not equivalent, only for repetition sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see स्थल (sthal) everywhere on official signage in India -- ऐतिहासिक स्थल (aitihaasik sthal, 'historical site') outside a monument, or पूजा स्थल (poojaa sthal, 'place of worship') on a map -- it has that formal, signboard/news-register feel rather than kitchen-table Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use स्थल (sthal) in everyday speech where जगह (jagah) is the natural choice; स्थल (sthal) sounds bookish or official in casual conversation, more like something you'd read on a sign or in a news report. 2) It shows up constantly as the second half of compound nouns (जन्मस्थल janmasthal, 'birthplace'; समाधिस्थल samaadhisthal, 'memorial/tomb site'; पर्यटन स्थल parytan sthal, 'tourist site') -- learning it as a compound-building piece is often more useful than as a standalone word.
Sample Sentences:
यह मंदिर एक ऐतिहासिक स्थल है। This temple is a historical site.
(yah mandir ek aitihaasik sthal hai।)
