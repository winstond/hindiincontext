उपयोग (upyog)

noun A formal, Sanskritic word for 'use/usage,' functioning both as a noun and, with करना (karnaa), as a compound-verb phrase meaning 'to use.'
Sense 1 : use, usage, application
noun, masculine
Synonyms: इस्तेमाल (istemaal), प्रयोग (prayog)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly on medicine strips, product packaging, and government forms -- 'उपयोग विधि' (upyog vidhi, 'method of use') is a standard label heading. In casual conversation, though, most Delhi speakers will say इस्तेमाल (istemaal) instead, so उपयोग (upyog) has a slightly bookish, official flavor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word by default because it looks close to English 'use,' but in everyday speech इस्तेमाल (istemaal) is far more common; उपयोग (upyog) tends to surface in writing, instructions, or formal contexts (e.g. product labels, official notices). 2) The verb phrase is उपयोग करना (upyog karnaa, to use), not a standalone verb -- do not try to conjugate उपयोग (upyog) itself.
Sample Sentences:
इस दवा का उपयोग कैसे करें? How should this medicine be used?
(is davaa kaa upyog kaise karen?)

Sense 2 : usefulness, purpose (as in 'being of use')
noun, masculine Common in the frame X के उपयोग का (X ke upyog kaa) or उपयोग में आना (upyog men aanaa, to come into use / prove useful), where उपयोग (upyog) shifts from the act of using to the quality of being useful.
Synonyms: फ़ायदा (faaydaa), लाभ (laabh, more formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense pairs with आना (aanaa) to build a mild impersonal construction -- यह चीज़ उपयोग में आती है (yah chiiz upyog men aatii hai, 'this thing comes in useful') -- which sidesteps naming who is doing the using, a pattern Hindi favors more than English does.
Common Mistakes: 1) उपयोग में आना (upyog men aanaa) is a fixed phrase meaning 'to come in handy / be put to use' -- learners sometimes translate it too literally as 'to come into usage,' missing that it is the natural way to say something 'gets used' or 'proves useful' without naming an agent.

(yah puraanii almaarii abhii bhii upyog men aatii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
