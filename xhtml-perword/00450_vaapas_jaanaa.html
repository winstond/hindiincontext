वापस जाना (vaapas jaanaa)

verb A compound expression combining वापस (vaapas, 'back') with the verb जाना (jaanaa, 'to go'), meaning 'to go back, to return.' Functions as an ordinary intransitive verb phrase.
Sense 1 : to go back, to return (to a place)
verb, intransitive वापस (vaapas) here functions adverbially, modifying जाना (jaanaa); the destination, if stated, takes को (ko) or में (men), e.g. घर वापस जाना (ghar vaapas jaanaa, 'to go back home').
Synonyms: लौटना (lautnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will hear constantly at the end of a visit -- a guest announcing मुझे वापस जाना है (mujhe vaapas jaanaa hai, 'I need to go back') to signal it is time to leave. It pairs naturally with लौटना (lautnaa), which is a touch more formal or literary and often used in writing or when describing journeys.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes put वापस (vaapas) after जाना (jaanaa) instead of before it -- the natural word order is वापस जाना (vaapas jaanaa), not जाना वापस (jaanaa vaapas). 2) Some learners confuse this with वापस आना (vaapas aanaa, 'to come back') -- वापस जाना (vaapas jaanaa) is used when the movement is away from the speaker's current location, while वापस आना (vaapas aanaa) is used when returning toward the speaker.
