बरदाश्त (bardaasht)

noun A borrowed noun meaning 'tolerance/endurance,' almost always used with a light verb like करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) rather than alone. Also spelled बर्दाश्त (bardaasht); both spellings are common.
Sense 1 : tolerance, capacity to endure something unpleasant
noun, feminine Almost always appears as बरदाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa, 'to tolerate/put up with') or in the negative बरदाश्त नहीं होना (bardaasht nahiin honaa, 'to be unbearable to someone'), the latter with a को-marked experiencer.
Synonyms: सहनशक्ति (sahanshakti), सहनशीलता (sahanshiiltaa), धैर्य (dhairy, patience, meaning scope narrower)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a slightly dramatic, exasperated flavor in everyday speech -- यह बरदाश्त नहीं होता! (yah bardaasht nahiin hotaa, 'this is unbearable!') is the kind of thing you say about noisy neighbors, bad traffic, or a relative's nagging. It comes from Persian and sits comfortably in both Hindi and Urdu registers, so you will hear it just as often in Bollywood dialogue as in daily conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) In the 'unbearable to someone' sense, the person who can't stand it is marked with को (ko), not ने (ne) -- मुझसे बरदाश्त नहीं होता (mujhse bardaasht nahiin hotaa) or मुझे बरदाश्त नहीं होता (mujhe bardaasht nahiin hotaa) are both heard, but learners often wrongly reach for a plain subject-verb structure. 2) Learners sometimes treat बरदाश्त (bardaasht) as a verb itself; it is a noun and needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to function as a predicate.

(mujhse yah shor bardaasht nahiin hotaa।)
