कान (kaan)

noun A single-sense body-part noun meaning 'ear', but rich in idiomatic use -- it shows up constantly in fixed expressions about attention, warning, and gossip.
Sense 1 : ear (body part)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: श्रवण (shravan, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: कान (kaan) anchors a whole family of idioms about hearing, heeding, and rumor: कान में कहना (kaan men kahnaa, 'to whisper in someone's ear'), कान का कच्चा (kaan kaa kachchaa, 'gullible', literally 'raw/unbaked in the ear', someone whose ear is easily swayed by whatever they hear last), and कानों में उंगली डालना (kaanon men unglii daalnaa, 'to plug one's ears', i.e. refuse to listen). A Delhi elder scolding a child for not listening might well say कान खोलकर सुनो (kaan kholkar suno, 'listen with your ears open', i.e. listen carefully) -- the ear is treated almost as a gate that can be opened or shut to attention itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to translate English idioms like 'lend an ear' literally; Hindi has its own set of कान (kaan) idioms -- कान देना (kaan denaa, 'to pay attention'), कान भरना (kaan bharnaa, 'to poison someone's mind with gossip'), कान खड़े होना (kaan khade honaa, 'to prick up one's ears in alertness') -- that don't map word-for-word onto English. 2) कान (kaan) is masculine, so possessives and adjectives agreeing with it take masculine forms, e.g. मेरा कान (meraa kaan) not मेरी कान.
