खोट (khot)

noun A noun meaning a flaw, defect, or dishonest streak in something or someone -- used for a fault in an object, a character defect, or adulteration in metal/material.
Sense 1 : flaw, defect, fault
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कमी (kamii), दोष (dosh), ऐब (aib, colloquial), खराबी (kharaabii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear this in the phrase नीयत में खोट (niiyat men khot, 'a flaw in one's intentions') -- Hindi loves locating moral failing inside the नीयत (niiyat, 'intention') rather than in the person directly, which softens direct accusation while still landing the point.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कमी (kamii) in every context, but खोट (khot) carries a stronger sense of something being wrong or tainted at the core, not just insufficient in quantity. 2) खोट (khot) is typically used with होना (honaa) or निकालना (nikaalnaa) -- e.g. खोट निकालना (khot nikaalnaa, 'to find fault') -- rather than being used as a standalone descriptive adjective.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी नीयत में खोट है। There is a flaw in his intentions.
(uskii niiyat men khot hai।)

Sense 2 : adulteration, impurity (in metal or material)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मिलावट (milaavat), अशुद्धि (ashuddhi, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense comes from goldsmiths' shops -- सुनार (sunaar, 'goldsmith') -- where checking gold or silver for खोट (khot) means testing for base metal mixed in. From there it extended naturally to the moral sense of a hidden impurity in a person's character.
