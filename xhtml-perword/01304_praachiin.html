प्राचीन (praachiin)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'ancient, old, of a bygone era' -- used for historical, archaeological, or civilizational old age rather than the old age of a person or everyday object.
Sense 1 : ancient, age-old (historical/civilizational)
adjective
Synonyms: पुराना (puraanaa, everyday word for old things), प्राचीनतम (praachiintam, superlative form, rare), पुरातन (puraatan, literary), प्राचीन काल का (praachiin kaal kaa, of ancient times)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see प्राचीन (praachiin) all over museum plaques and tourist signboards -- प्राचीन मंदिर (praachiin mandir, 'ancient temple'), प्राचीन सभ्यता (praachiin sabhytaa, 'ancient civilization') -- it carries a certain reverence, almost pulling the listener back into इतिहास (itihaas, history) rather than just marking the passage of time. Contrast this with पुराना (puraanaa), which is what you'd say about your grandfather's old scooter, no reverence required.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for प्राचीन (praachiin) when they mean an old chair or an old friend -- for everyday oldness of objects, age, or relationships, पुराना (puraanaa) is the natural word; प्राचीन (praachiin) is reserved for temples, civilizations, texts, cities, and historical eras. 2) It does not describe an elderly person -- for that, learners want बूढ़ा (boodhaa) or वृद्ध (vriddh), not प्राचीन (praachiin).
