शिष्ट (shisht)

adjective A formal, somewhat literary adjective meaning 'polite, well-mannered, cultured' -- describes refined behavior or speech.
Sense 1 : polite, well-mannered, cultured
adjective
Synonyms: विनीत (viniit, formal), सभ्य (sabhy), भद्र (bhadr, formal), नफ़ीस (nafiis, refers more to refined taste/manner)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet शिष्ट (shisht) more in written Hindi -- essays, formal letters, character descriptions in stories -- than in everyday chatter, where सभ्य (sabhy) or विनम्र (vinamr) do the job. It carries a slightly old-world, refined flavor, the kind of word a teacher might use to praise a student's conduct: शिष्ट व्यवहार (shisht vyavahaar, 'well-mannered behavior') is a common set phrase.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शिष्ट (shisht) where सभ्य (sabhy) or विनम्र (vinamr) would sound more natural in casual speech -- शिष्ट (shisht) leans formal/written and describes overall cultured demeanor rather than a single polite act. 2) Do not confuse with शिस्त (shist), a colloquial Punjabi-influenced word for 'aim' (as in aiming a gun) -- unrelated in meaning despite the similar sound.
