चूर्ण (choorn)

noun A largely medical/culinary term for a fine powder, especially an Ayurvedic digestive powder; also used more generally in chemistry/technical contexts for 'powder.'
Sense 1 : Ayurvedic digestive powder
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पाउडर (paaudar, general/English loan, less specific to Ayurveda)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Ask almost any Indian household about चूर्ण (choorn) and someone will mention a bottle kept in the kitchen shelf for after a heavy meal -- it is deeply tied to Ayurvedic home remedies, distinct from cooking powders like मसाला (masaalaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पाउडर (paaudar) as a catch-all, but चूर्ण (choorn) specifically evokes an Ayurvedic remedy, like हाजमोला (haajmolaa)-style digestive mixes, not a generic powder such as face powder or spice powder.

Sense 2 : powder (general/technical)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पाउडर (paaudar)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In chemistry or pharmacy contexts, चूर्ण (choorn) can simply mean any fine powder, but in everyday conversation the Ayurvedic-remedy sense (above) is far more common, so this general sense is mostly met in textbooks or formal writing.
