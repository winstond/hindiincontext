अनुनासिक (anunaasik)

adjective/noun A technical grammar/phonetics term meaning 'nasal' -- most learners will meet it as an adjective describing a nasalized sound or vowel, and occasionally as the noun naming that sound itself.
Sense 1 : nasal (describing a sound)
adjective
Synonyms: नासिक्य (naasiky, rare)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word belongs to the world of Hindi grammar class rather than daily chat -- you'll meet it in school textbooks explaining why words like हाँ (haan, 'yes') or चाँद (chaand, 'moon') carry that little moon-shaped mark. Native speakers rarely say the word itself in conversation, but every Hindi-medium student remembers being taught it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with अनुस्वार (anusvaar), which is the specific nasal mark/sound written as a dot above a letter (like in हंस, hans, 'swan'); अनुनासिक (anunaasik) is the broader grammatical term for nasalized sounds in general, including the chandrabindu (chandrbindu) nasalization seen in words like हँस (hans, 'laugh').

Sense 2 : a nasal sound/letter
noun, masculine Used as a noun to refer to the nasalized sound or its written symbol itself, typically in grammar discussions.
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the adjective sense, just used to name the sound/symbol rather than describe it -- grammar textbooks in Hindi often let one word do double duty this way, much like 'nasal' can be both adjective and noun in English.
