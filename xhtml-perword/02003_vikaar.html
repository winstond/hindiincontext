विकार (vikaar)

noun A somewhat formal/literary noun covering distortion, disorder, or corruption -- used for physical/mental ailments, moral corruption, and grammatical/phonetic change. All three senses share the root idea of 'a departure from the normal or pure state.'
Sense 1 : disorder, ailment (physical or mental)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रोग (rog), बीमारी (biimaarii), गड़बड़ी (gadabdii, informal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word most often on medicine labels, in doctors' explanations, or in newspaper health columns -- it has a slightly clinical, Sanskritic flavor compared to the everyday बीमारी (biimaarii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बीमारी (biimaarii) as the default word for 'illness' -- विकार (vikaar) is more formal/clinical and typically appears in compounds like पेट का विकार (pet kaa vikaar, 'stomach disorder') or मानसिक विकार (maansik vikaar, 'mental disorder') rather than in casual chat about being sick.
Sample Sentences:
त्वचा विकार के लिए यह दवा बहुत असरदार है। This medicine is very effective for skin disorders.
(tvachaa vikaar ke lie yah davaa bahut asardaar hai।)

Sense 2 : moral corruption, depravity, vice
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बुराई (buraaii), भ्रष्टता (bhrashttaa, formal), दोष (dosh)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in moral or religious speech -- a preacher or elder might warn against विकार (vikaar) creeping into the mind or society, echoing the same 'departure from a pure state' idea as the physical-ailment sense but applied to character.
Sample Sentences:
मन में विकार आने लगे तो सतर्क हो जाओ। If corruption starts entering the mind, become alert.
(man men vikaar aane lage to satark ho jaao।)

(guru jii ne kahaa ki vikaar manushy ke charitr ko dhiire-dhiire nasht kar detaa hai।)
Register: Devotional -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
समाज में फैलते विकार को रोकना हर किसी का कर्तव्य है। Stopping the depravity spreading in society is everyone's duty.
(samaaj men phailte vikaar ko roknaa har kisii kaa kartavy hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगर संस्कार अच्छे होते तो विकार इतनी आसानी से न पनपते। If one's upbringing were good, vice would not flourish so easily.

Sense 3 : alteration, distortion (of sound, form, or structure)
noun, masculine Common in linguistic and grammatical contexts, e.g. describing sound changes between related words (ध्वनि विकार, dhvani vikaar).
Synonyms: परिवर्तन (parivartan), बदलाव (badlaav)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the technical sense you will meet in Hindi grammar classes or etymology discussions, where it describes how a word's sound or form shifts over time -- a linguist's cousin of the 'disorder' sense above.
Sample Sentences:
संस्कृत के शब्दों में समय के साथ ध्वनि-विकार होता है। Sound-modification occurs in Sanskrit words over time.
(sanskrit ke shabdon men samay ke saath dhvani-vikaar hotaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
यह शब्द तद्भव है, यानी संस्कृत से विकार के बाद बना है। This word is 'tadbhav', that is, it was formed after modification from Sanskrit.
(yah shabd tadbhav hai, yaanii sanskrit se vikaar ke baad banaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive
प्राकृत भाषाओं में मूल संस्कृत रूपों का काफी विकार देखा जाता है। Considerable distortion of the original Sanskrit forms is seen in the Prakrit languages.
(praakrit bhaashaaon men mool sanskrit roopon kaa kaaphii vikaar dekhaa jaataa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
