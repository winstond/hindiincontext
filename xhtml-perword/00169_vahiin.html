वहीं (vahiin)

adverb An emphatic form of वहाँ (vahaan, 'there'), built with the emphatic ही (hii); mainly used as a place adverb meaning 'right there / that very place,' with a related discourse use meaning 'at that very point.'
Sense 1 : right there, that very place
adverb
Synonyms: उसी जगह (usii jagah), उसी स्थान पर (usii sthaan par, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If someone asks मेरा फ़ोन कहाँ है? (meraa fon kahaan hai?, 'where is my phone?') and you saw it on the kitchen counter, you'd say वहीं है (vahiin hai, 'it's right there') pointing at the exact spot -- the ही (hii) does the pointing work that English does with 'right' or 'exactly'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use वहाँ (vahaan) when they actually mean to add emphasis ('that exact spot'), missing the extra force that वहीं (vahiin) carries. 2) Don't confuse with यहीं (yahiin, 'right here') -- the initial vowel carries the near/far distinction just like वहाँ (vahaan) vs. यहाँ (yahaan).
Sample Sentences:
मेरा फ़ोन वहीं है, मेज़ पर। My phone is right there, on the table.
(meraa fon vahiin hai, mez par।)

Sense 2 : at that very point/moment (temporal/discourse use)
adverb Used in narrative to anchor an action to a moment or point already established in the discourse, often continuing a story rather than marking physical location.
Synonyms: उसी वक्त (usii vakt), तभी (tabhii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In storytelling, वहीं (vahiin) often glues one clause to the next: वह रुका, और वहीं उसे आवाज़ सुनाई दी (vah rukaa, aur vahiin use aavaaz sunaaii dii, 'he stopped, and right then/there he heard a voice') -- the word blurs place and moment together, which is very natural in Hindi narration but can feel ambiguous to an English speaker used to separating 'there' from 'then'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for तभी (tabhii) exclusively for 'right then,' not realizing वहीं (vahiin) can do the same job when the sentence is also carrying a spatial flavor tied to what was just described.

(vah bol hii rahaa thaa ki vahiin use ek ajiib sii aavaaz sunaaii dii।)
