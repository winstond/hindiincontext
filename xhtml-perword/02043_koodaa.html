कूड़ा (koodaa)

noun A single core noun meaning garbage/trash, with a natural figurative extension to 'junk' for anything shoddy or worthless. Also appears in compounds like कूड़ेदान (koodedaan, 'trash can').
Sense 1 : garbage, trash, rubbish
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कचरा (kachraa), गंदगी (gandgii, filth/mess, broader sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi household has a running battle about कूड़ा (koodaa) -- who takes it down, when the कूड़ेवाला (koodevaalaa, 'garbage collector') comes by, and the daily ritual of separating गीला कूड़ा (giilaa koodaa, 'wet waste') from सूखा कूड़ा (sookhaa koodaa, 'dry waste') that municipal campaigns have pushed hard in recent years.
Common Mistakes: 1) कूड़ा (koodaa) is a mass noun like 'garbage' in English -- learners often reach for the plural (कूड़े koode) to mean 'pieces of trash,' but native speakers just use कूड़ा (koodaa) for the whole pile. 2) The natural verb pairing is कूड़ा फेंकना (koodaa phenknaa, 'to throw out the garbage') or कूड़ा डालना (koodaa daalnaa) -- learners sometimes reach for निकालना (nikaalnaa) instead, which sounds off here.

Sense 2 : junk (useless or shoddy stuff, in scorn)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बेकार सामान (bekaar saamaan, useless stuff)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just like in English, कूड़ा (koodaa) slides easily from literal trash to a dismissive label for anything of poor quality -- a bad film, a shoddy gadget, junk food. You will hear ये सब कूड़ा है (ye sab koodaa hai, 'all this is junk') about a disappointing purchase just as naturally as about actual garbage.
Sample Sentences:
