सेब (seb)

noun A simple, everyday noun meaning 'apple' -- one sense, no complications.
Sense 1 : apple (the fruit)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Apples in India are strongly associated with Himachal Pradesh and Kashmir, and सेब (seb) shows up constantly in the classic health saying सेब खाओ, डॉक्टर को दूर रखो (seb khaao, doktar ko door rakho, 'eat an apple, keep the doctor away') -- a direct echo of the English proverb that most Indian schoolkids grow up hearing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes guess the gender as feminine by analogy with other fruit names, but सेब (seb) is masculine -- मुझे एक सेब चाहिए (mujhe ek seb chaahie, 'I want an apple') takes masculine agreement, not feminine.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe seb bahut pasand hai।)
