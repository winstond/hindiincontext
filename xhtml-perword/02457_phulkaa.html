फुलका (phulkaa)

noun A thin, soft, puffed-up whole-wheat flatbread -- basically a lighter, more delicately puffed cousin of the everyday roti (rotii).
Sense 1 : a soft, puffed whole-wheat flatbread
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रोटी (rotii, broader everyday term), चपाती (chapaatii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In many North Indian kitchens, watching the फुलका (phulkaa) balloon up on the open flame (tave then straight onto the gas) is a small daily kitchen ritual -- a good cook is often judged by how evenly and fully their फुलके (phulke) puff. It is considered a lighter, slightly more refined version of a regular roti, often served in restaurants or on special-diet plates.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use फुलका (phulkaa) and रोटी (rotii) as if they were strictly interchangeable -- फुलका (phulkaa) specifically implies a thin bread that has puffed up with air over a direct flame, while रोटी (rotii) is the general word covering any flatbread, puffed or not.
