स्थिर (sthir)

adjective स्थिर (sthir) is primarily an adjective meaning 'stable, steady, fixed, calm' -- applying to physical objects, prices/markets, minds, and situations alike.
Sense 1 : stable, fixed, steady (of objects, prices, situations)
adjective
Synonyms: अचल (achal, literary), दृढ़ (dridh, firm/resolute), अटल (atal, unshakeable)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly on the evening business news -- शेयर बाज़ार स्थिर रहा (sheyar baazaar sthir rahaa, 'the stock market remained stable') -- and equally often about a person's temperament, दिमाग़ स्थिर रखो (dimaagh sthir rakho, 'keep your mind steady'), which reflects the common Hindi habit of describing emotional composure in physical, almost engineering-like terms.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse स्थिर (sthir) with रुका हुआ (rukaa huaa, 'stopped') -- स्थिर (sthir) means 'steady/unchanging,' not necessarily 'not moving at all,' so a car idling could be रुका हुआ (rukaa huaa) while a steady exchange rate is स्थिर (sthir). 2) In market/news contexts, बाज़ार स्थिर है (baazaar sthir hai, 'the market is stable') is a set phrase learners should recognize even before they can produce it.

Sense 2 : calm, composed (of mind or emotional state)
adjective
Synonyms: शांत (shaant, calm), संयत (sanyat, composed, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is often paired with मन (man, 'mind/heart') or दिमाग़ (dimaagh, 'brain/mind') -- मन स्थिर करो (man sthir karo, 'steady your mind') is common advice, echoing a broader Hindi habit of talking about emotional control as if settling something physical, much like calming still water.
