बुनियादी (buniyaadii)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'basic, fundamental' -- used constantly for basic needs, rights, education, and infrastructure. Related to बुनियाद (buniyaad, 'foundation'), the noun it derives from.
Sense 1 : basic, fundamental
adjective
Synonyms: मूलभूत (moolbhoot), प्राथमिक (praathmik), मौलिक (maulik)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear बुनियादी (buniyaadii) everywhere in civic and political speech -- बुनियादी ढांचा (buniyaadii dhaanchaa, 'infrastructure'), बुनियादी अधिकार (buniyaadii adhikaar, 'fundamental rights'), बुनियादी शिक्षा (buniyaadii shikshaa, 'basic education') -- it carries a slightly formal, official-document flavor compared to a plainer word like ज़रूरी (zaroorii). It literally grows from बुनियाद (buniyaad, 'foundation, base'), so picture a building's foundation whenever you use it: 'basic' in Hindi is 'foundational.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat बुनियादी (buniyaadii) as a variable adjective and try to change its ending for gender/number agreement (e.g. बुनियादा, बुनियादे) -- it never changes; it stays बुनियादी (buniyaadii) before masculine or feminine, singular or plural nouns. 2) It is often confused with ज़रूरी (zaroorii, 'necessary') -- बुनियादी (buniyaadii) means 'foundational/basic in nature,' not 'urgently needed,' though the two overlap in phrases like बुनियादी ज़रूरतें (buniyaadii zaroorten, 'basic needs').
