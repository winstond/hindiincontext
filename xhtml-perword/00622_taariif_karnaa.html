तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the noun तारीफ़ (taariif, 'praise', a Persian-origin loanword) plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means 'to praise, compliment.' One straightforward sense covers both admiring remarks about people and appreciative remarks about things.
Sense 1 : to praise, compliment
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सराहना (saraahnaa), प्रशंसा करना (prashansaa karnaa, formal), बड़ाई करना (badaaii karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi hosts are famously generous with तारीफ़ (taariif) -- praising a host's खाना (khaanaa, food) lavishly, sometimes beyond what's literally meant, is a social ritual of politeness. Note also वाह वाह करना (vaah vaah karnaa), a related expression of loud, enthusiastic appreciation, often used interchangeably in casual praise of performances or food.
Common Mistakes: 1) The person or thing being praised takes की (kii): तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa) is used as तारीफ़ करना (kisii kii taariif karnaa, 'to praise someone') -- learners often drop की (kii) and say the object directly, which sounds off. 2) In the perfective, the subject takes ने (ne) since करना (karnaa) is transitive: उसने तारीफ़ की (usne taariif kii, 'he/she praised'), and तारीफ़ (taariif) being feminine triggers की (kii) agreement, not किया (kiyaa) -- a common slip.

(sabne uske kaam kii taariif kii।)
