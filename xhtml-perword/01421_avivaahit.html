अविवाहित (avivaahit)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'unmarried, single'; built from the negative prefix अ- (a) plus विवाहित (vivaahit, 'married'). One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : unmarried, single
adjective
Synonyms: कुँवारा (kunvaaraa, everyday), अनब्याहा (anabyaahaa, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly on official paperwork -- matrimonial ads, ration cards, court affidavits -- where it sits alongside विवाहित (vivaahit, 'married') and तलाकशुदा (talaakshudaa, 'divorced') as one of the standard marital-status labels. In a normal conversation about a friend's love life, though, people reach for कुँवारा (kunvaaraa) instead, so treat अविवाहित (avivaahit) as the bureaucratic register and कुँवारा (kunvaaraa) as the kitchen-table one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech, but native speakers usually say कुँवारा (kunvaaraa) or use the phrase शादी नहीं हुई (shaadii nahiin huii, 'hasn't gotten married yet') in everyday conversation; अविवाहित (avivaahit) sounds like it belongs on a government form. 2) Like most adjectives ending in -इत (it), it does not change form for gender or number -- अविवाहित लड़का (avivaahit ladkaa, 'unmarried boy') and अविवाहित लड़की (avivaahit ladkii, 'unmarried girl') use the same word.
