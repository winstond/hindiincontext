बक्सा (baksaa)

noun A concrete noun meaning a box or trunk -- one straightforward sense, common in everyday talk about luggage and storage.
Sense 1 : box, trunk, suitcase
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संदूक (sandook), डिब्बा (dibbaa, smaller box or tin), ट्रंक (trank, large travel trunk)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture the big tin or wooden बक्सा (baksaa) under a bed in an Indian household, packed with winter quilts or old saris -- it's a staple of household storage before built-in cupboards became common. In Delhi-NCR speech you'll also hear it for a steel trunk that goes to hostel or boarding school with a student.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use डिब्बा (dibbaa) and बक्सा (baksaa) interchangeably, but बक्सा (baksaa) leans toward a bigger, sturdier container (like a wooden or metal trunk for storing clothes), while डिब्बा (dibbaa) suggests something smaller, like a tiffin box or a cardboard carton. 2) For a train compartment or a TV/cinema screen, speakers reach for डिब्बा (dibbaa) or स्क्रीन (skriin) rather than बक्सा (baksaa), which stays literal.
