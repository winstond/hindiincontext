इस्तेमाल (istemaal)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'use' or 'usage', almost always paired with करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to form a compound verb meaning 'to use' / 'to be used'.
Sense 1 : use, usage, application
noun, masculine Almost always appears with करना (karnaa) ('to use') or होना (honaa) ('to be used') as a light verb; the noun itself is invariant.
Synonyms: उपयोग (upyog), प्रयोग (prayog), काम (kaam, informal, as in 'काम में लाना' meaning 'to put to use')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes from Persian/Arabic and is completely everyday in spoken Hindi -- you'll hear इस्तेमाल करना (istemaal karnaa) far more often than its more Sanskritic cousin उपयोग करना (upyog karnaa), which feels slightly more formal or written. Shopkeepers, doctors, and instruction manuals alike reach for इस्तेमाल (istemaal) as the default word for 'use'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use इस्तेमाल (istemaal) alone as a verb; it needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) attached to actually mean 'to use'. 2) The thing being used takes का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) before इस्तेमाल (istemaal), not को (ko) -- e.g. 'फोन का इस्तेमाल' (phon kaa istemaal, 'use of the phone'), not 'फोन को इस्तेमाल'.
