बंद करना (band karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the adjective बंद (band, 'closed/shut/off') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'), used to mean 'to close, shut, turn off, or stop' something. Covers physical closing (a door, a shop), switching off (a light, a phone), and stopping an activity (a habit, a noise).
Sense 1 : to close or shut something
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday word you will hear a shopkeeper use when locking up for the night, or a mother saying दरवाज़ा बंद करो (darvaazaa band karo, 'close the door') to a child letting in mosquitoes. Note the close pairing with बंद होना (band honaa), the intransitive partner meaning 'to become closed/to close (on its own)' -- shops, roads, and eyes all 'बंद होना' rather than 'बंद करना' when no one is actively doing the closing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use बंद होना (band honaa) when they mean बंद करना (band karnaa) -- होना (honaa) is for something becoming closed on its own (intransitive, 'the shop closed'), while करना (karnaa) is for someone actively closing it (transitive, 'I closed the shop'). 2) Since this is transitive, remember it takes ने (ne) in the perfective: मैंने दरवाज़ा बंद किया (mainne darvaazaa band kiyaa, 'I closed the door').

Sense 2 : to switch off or turn off (a device, light, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बुझाना (bujhaanaa, used specifically for lights/flames)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around the house: लाइट बंद करो (laait band karo, 'turn off the light'), पंखा बंद कर दो (pankhaa band kar do, 'turn off the fan'), or फ़ोन बंद कर दो (fon band kar do, 'turn off the phone'). The compound often appears with दे (de) inserted (बंद कर देना, band kar denaa) to add a sense of finality or completeness to the action.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ऑफ करना (oph karnaa, a common Hinglish calque) but बंद करना (band karnaa) is the native and far more natural choice for phones, lights, fans, and TVs in ordinary conversation.

Sense 3 : to stop or discontinue an activity
verb, transitive Frequently used with a verb stem or gerund as object, e.g. शोर करना बंद करो (shor karnaa band karo, 'stop making noise') or पीना बंद कर दिया (piinaa band kar diyaa, 'stopped drinking').
Synonyms: रोकना (roknaa, to stop/halt), छोड़ना (chhodnaa, to give up/leave)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic scolding phrase in any Hindi household -- रोना बंद करो (ronaa band karo, 'stop crying') or बहाने बनाना बंद करो (bahaane banaanaa band karo, 'stop making excuses') are things almost every Indian kid has heard from a parent. It carries a slightly sharper, more exasperated tone than the neutral रोकना (roknaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) When telling someone to stop doing something, the pattern is [verb-ना] + बंद करो (band karo), not just बंद करो (band karo) alone with the object -- रोना बंद करो (ronaa band karo, 'stop crying'), not रो बंद करो.
Sample Sentences:
