वास्तव में (vaastav men)

adverb A common formal-leaning adverb meaning 'actually, in reality' -- used to assert the true state of things, often contrasting with appearance or assumption.
Sense 1 : in reality, actually
adverb
Synonyms: सच में (sach men), असल में (asal men), दरअसल (daraasal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in news broadcasts, essays, and slightly formal speech -- like a teacher or news anchor saying 'वास्तव में यह सच है' (vaastav men yah sach hai, 'in reality, this is true'). In everyday chai-shop chatter, people often reach for असल में (asal men) or सच में (sach men) instead, which feel warmer and more conversational.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often overuse this where सच में (sach men) or असल में (asal men) would sound more natural in casual speech; वास्तव में (vaastav men) leans slightly more formal/written. 2) It is placed flexibly -- sentence-initial for emphasis on the whole statement, or right before the verb/adjective to emphasize just that part.
Sample Sentences:
वास्तव में यह सच है। In reality, this is true.
(vaastav men yah sach hai।)
