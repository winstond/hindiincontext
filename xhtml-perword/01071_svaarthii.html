स्वार्थी (svaarthii)

adjective An adjective (and by extension noun-like usage) meaning 'selfish' -- built from स्वार्थ (svaarth, 'self-interest') plus the possessive suffix -ई (ii).
Sense 1 : selfish, self-centered
adjective
Synonyms: मतलबी (matalbii), खुदगर्ज़ (khudgarz)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes up constantly in moral commentary -- a mother scolding a child for not sharing, a TV serial villain's defining trait, or a friend complaining about someone who only calls when they need a favor. Hindi builds it from स्व (sv, 'self') and अर्थ (arth, 'purpose/interest'), so literally it means 'one whose purpose is the self' -- a nice window into how Hindi often builds character-adjectives from abstract nouns plus -ई (ii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse स्वार्थी (svaarthii) with स्वतंत्र (svatantr, 'independent') because both start with स्व (sv) -- but स्वार्थी (svaarthii) is about self-interest, not independence. 2) मतलबी (matalbii) is a common colloquial synonym learners hear in daily speech, while स्वार्थी (svaarthii) is slightly more formal/written but still very usable in conversation.
