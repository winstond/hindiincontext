दुआ (duaa)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'prayer' in the sense of a blessing or well-wishing for someone, distinct from formal ritual worship.
Sense 1 : prayer, blessing, good wishes
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आशीर्वाद (aashiirvaad, typically from an elder to a younger person), प्रार्थना (praarthnaa, more formal/ritual worship)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries real warmth in everyday Hindi-Urdu speech -- elders send off travelers with meri dua hai tumhaare saath (merii duaa hai tumhaare saath, 'my prayers are with you'), and asking for someone's दुआ (duaa) before an exam or journey is a common, affectionate ritual. It is a Persian-origin word shared with Urdu, and feels a notch softer and more personal than the Sanskritic प्रार्थना (praarthnaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) दुआ (duaa) is not the word for formal ritual prayer or worship (that is प्रार्थना praarthnaa or पूजा poojaa) -- it means a heartfelt wish for someone's wellbeing, often requested or given between people. 2) The common verb pairing is दुआ करना (duaa karnaa, 'to pray/wish well') or दुआ माँगना (duaa maangnaa, 'to ask for someone's blessing'), not पढ़ना (padhnaa) as with reciting scripture.
