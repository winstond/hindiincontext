चौथाई (chauthaaii)

numeral A fractional-numeral word meaning 'one-fourth, a quarter,' used both as a noun (a quarter of something) and as an adjective-like modifier before a noun (चौथाई हिस्सा (chauthaaii hissaa, 'a quarter part')).
Sense 1 : one-fourth, a quarter (fraction)
numeral Used both as a standalone noun ('a quarter of it') and attributively before a noun, e.g. चौथाई किलो (chauthaaii kilo, 'a quarter kilo'), without changing form.
Synonyms: एक चौथाई (ek chauthaaii, explicit form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday word you'll hear in Delhi kitchens and markets: 'चौथाई किलो प्याज़ दे दो' (chauthaaii kilo pyaaz de do, 'give me a quarter kilo of onions') is completely natural grocery-shopping Hindi. It pairs conceptually with आधा (aadhaa, 'half') and तिहाई (tihaaii, 'one-third') as the common everyday fraction words, all built the same way from the ordinal plus -आई (-aaii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect चौथाई (chauthaaii) like an adjective agreeing with the noun's gender/number, but it stays the same form regardless -- चौथाई रोटी (chauthaaii rotii, 'a quarter of a roti') and चौथाई सेब (chauthaaii seb, 'a quarter of an apple') both use the identical word. 2) Don't confuse it with चौथा (chauthaa, 'fourth' as in ordinal position, e.g. चौथा नंबर (chauthaa nambar, 'fourth number')) -- चौथाई (chauthaaii) is specifically the fraction one-fourth, not the ordinal.
