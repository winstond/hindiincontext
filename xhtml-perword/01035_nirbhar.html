निर्भर (nirbhar)

adjective An adjective meaning 'dependent' or 'reliant', almost always used with the postposition पर (par) and the verb करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to form the fixed pattern 'X पर निर्भर होना/करना' -- 'to depend on X'.
Sense 1 : dependent, reliant (on something/someone)
adjective Almost always occurs in the frame '[X] पर निर्भर (par nirbhar)' -- 'dependent on [X]' -- paired with होना (honaa) for a state ('is dependent') or करना (karnaa) for an action ('to depend/rely on').
Synonyms: आश्रित (aashrit, slightly more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word is a workhorse in everyday reasoning and decision-talk in Hindi -- you'll hear यह निर्भर करता है (yah nirbhar kartaa hai, 'it depends') constantly in conversation, much like English speakers say 'it depends' as a hedge before giving a nuanced answer.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the postposition पर (par) and say something like *उस पैसे निर्भर* instead of उस पैसे पर निर्भर (us paise par nirbhar, 'dependent on that money'); पर (par) is obligatory before निर्भर (nirbhar). 2) With करना (karnaa), the subject that 'depends' is still marked by the postposition पर (par) on the thing depended upon, not on the person -- e.g. यह तुम पर निर्भर करता है (yah tum par nirbhar kartaa hai, 'it depends on you'), where यह (yah, 'it/this') is the grammatical subject, not तुम (tum, 'you').
Sample Sentences:
यह इस बात पर निर्भर करता है कि तुम क्या चाहते हो। It depends on what you want.
(yah is baat par nirbhar kartaa hai ki tum kyaa chaahte ho।)
