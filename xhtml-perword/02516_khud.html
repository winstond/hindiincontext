ख़ुद (khud)

pronoun/adverb A single-sense reflexive/emphatic word meaning 'self' -- used both as a reflexive pronoun (myself, yourself, himself, etc.) and as an emphatic adverb meaning 'personally, on one's own.' Closely related in function to अपने आप (apne aap) and स्वयं (svayan).
Sense 1 : oneself (reflexive, referring back to the subject)
pronoun Invariant -- does not change form for gender, number, or person; it simply agrees in reference with whichever subject is in play. Often paired with the subject for emphasis: मैं ख़ुद (main khud, 'I myself').
Synonyms: अपने आप (apne aap), स्वयं (svayan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi speakers use ख़ुद (khud) constantly to add a little emphasis or exasperation -- उसने ख़ुद बताया (usne khud bataayaa, 'he told me himself') carries a flavor of 'you'd think I was making this up, but he said it with his own mouth.' It is the everyday word; स्वयं (svayan) is its more formal, slightly bookish twin.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect ख़ुद (khud) like an adjective or add case endings to it -- it stays the same regardless of who or what it refers to. 2) Don't confuse it with अपना (apnaa); ख़ुद (khud) points back to the person, while अपना (apnaa) is the possessive 'one's own' used before a noun.

उसने ख़ुद बताया कि उसे नौकरी मिल गई है। He told me himself that he got the job.
(usne khud bataayaa ki use naukrii mil gaii hai।)

Sense 2 : personally, on one's own, without help
adverb Functions adverbially to stress that an action was done without assistance or without being asked: उसने ख़ुद खाना बनाया (usne khud khaanaa banaayaa, 'she cooked the food herself/on her own').
Synonyms: अपने आप (apne aap), स्वयं (svayan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear when someone wants credit (or blame) for doing something without help -- मैंने ख़ुद किया (mainne khud kiyaa, 'I did it myself') is a classic bit of Indian pride, whether it's fixing a scooter or acing an exam without tuition classes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes add से (se) after ख़ुद (khud) thinking it needs a postposition like 'by myself' in English -- ख़ुद (khud) already carries that sense on its own and normally stands unmarked.
