लटकना (lataknaa)

verb A physical intransitive verb meaning 'to hang, dangle' -- and by extension, to hang around idly or (of a matter) to remain unresolved/pending. Related transitive/causative form is लटकाना (latkaanaa, 'to hang something up').
Sense 1 : to hang, dangle (physically)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: झूलना (jhoolnaa, to swing/dangle)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a calendar or a बंदर (bandar, 'monkey') on a branch -- लटकना (lataknaa) is the everyday word Delhi folks use for anything dangling in the air, from earrings to a kid hanging off a bus door.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners reach for लटकाना (latkaanaa) when they mean the thing itself is hanging -- use लटकना (lataknaa) for the intransitive sense ('the picture hangs on the wall') and reserve लटकाना (latkaanaa) for 'to hang something up' (someone doing the hanging).

Sense 2 : to loiter, hang around aimlessly
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: घूमना (ghoomnaa, to wander/roam), मंडराना (mandraanaa, to hover/loiter)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A mother scolding her son with बाहर मत लटको (baahar mat latko, 'don't hang around outside') is a classic scene -- the image is of someone dangling without purpose, the same visual root as physical hanging.

Sense 3 : to remain pending, unresolved (of a matter, decision, work)
verb, intransitive Often used with काम (kaam, 'work') or मामला (maamlaa, 'matter') as subject -- the issue itself is described as 'hanging' rather than someone hanging it.
Synonyms: अटकना (ataknaa, to get stuck), लंबित रहना (lambit rahnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly about sarkaari kaam (sarkaarii kaam, 'government work') or file-work stuck in an office: फ़ाइल लटकी हुई है (faail latkii huii hai, 'the file is hanging/pending') -- a very Indian-bureaucracy image of paperwork dangling in limbo.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense overlaps with अटकना (ataknaa) but लटकना (lataknaa) specifically evokes something left dangling/unfinished rather than jammed or blocked -- native speakers pick between them by feel, not by strict rule.
Sample Sentences:
यह फ़ाइल पिछले तीन महीनों से लटकी हुई है। This file has been hanging pending for the last three months.
(yah faail pichhle tiin mahiinon se latkii huii hai।)
