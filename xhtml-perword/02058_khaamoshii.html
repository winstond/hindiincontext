ख़ामोशी (khaamoshii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning silence or quietness, used both literally (absence of sound) and figuratively (an emotional or reproachful silence between people).
Sense 1 : silence, quietness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: शांति (shaanti, also 'peace/calm'), सन्नाटा (sannaataa, eerie or total silence), चुप्पी (chuppii, everyday word for staying silent)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a certain poetic weight in Hindi/Urdu -- you'll hear it in ghazals and film dialogues describing the silence between lovers or the tense quiet after an argument, in a way that plain चुप्पी (chuppii) doesn't quite capture. It comes from Persian ख़ामोश (khaamosh, 'silent'), which is itself a common adjective you'll hear as a sharp command -- ख़ामोश! (khaamosh, 'Silence!') -- in courtroom or classroom scenes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चुप्पी (chuppii) and ख़ामोशी (khaamoshii) as if fully interchangeable; ख़ामोशी (khaamoshii) leans slightly more literary/emotional (a heavy, meaningful silence), while चुप्पी (chuppii) is the plain, everyday 'keeping quiet.' 2) Note the nuqta under ख़ (kh) -- dropping it (writing खामोशी) is common in casual writing and still understood, but the dotted form is the standard spelling.
