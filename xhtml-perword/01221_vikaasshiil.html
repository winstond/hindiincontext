विकासशील (vikaasshiil)

adjective A compound adjective built from विकास (vikaas, 'development') plus the suffix -शील (shiil, denoting a tendency or state), giving the single meaning 'developing.'
Sense 1 : developing (as in a nation or economy)
adjective
Synonyms: उन्नतिशील (unntishiil, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is classic newspaper and NCERT-textbook vocabulary -- you will meet विकासशील देश (vikaasshiil desh, 'developing country') constantly in civics and economics contexts, right alongside its counterpart विकसित देश (viksit desh, 'developed country'). It rarely comes up in casual chai-time conversation, so treat it as reading/formal vocabulary rather than something you need to produce spontaneously in speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with विकसित (viksit, 'developed'): विकासशील (vikaasshiil) describes a country still on the path of development, while विकसित (viksit) describes one that has already arrived there. 2) It is almost always used to modify देश (desh, 'country') or अर्थव्यवस्था (arthavyvasthaa, 'economy') and sounds odd applied to a person.
Sample Sentences:

(bhaarat ek vikaasshiil desh hai।)
