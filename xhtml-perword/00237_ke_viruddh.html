के विरुद्ध (ke viruddh)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'against' -- used for opposition, contrast, or resistance, as in going against someone's wishes or a case filed against a person. Formal register cousin of के खिलाफ़ (ke khilaaf).
Sense 1 : against (in opposition to)
postposition Takes a genitive-marked noun before it, following the के pattern (e.g. उसके विरुद्ध (uske viruddh, 'against him')); functions like other compound postpositions built on के.
Synonyms: के खिलाफ़ (ke khilaaf), के प्रतिकूल (ke pratikool, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this constantly in news reports and legal language -- FIR दर्ज की गई उसके विरुद्ध (uske viruddh, 'against him') is standard courtroom and journalistic phrasing, whereas your friend complaining about their boss would more naturally say के खिलाफ़ (ke khilaaf). Sensing this formal/informal split between the two near-synonyms is a genuinely useful ear to develop.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for के खिलाफ़ (ke khilaaf) by default since it is more common in spoken Hindi -- के विरुद्ध (ke viruddh) is the choice in formal, legal, or written contexts, such as a court case or an official complaint. 2) It is not used for simple physical facing-direction contrast (like 'against the wall') -- that calls for a different postposition such as से सटा (se sataa) or टेककर (tekkar), not के विरुद्ध (ke viruddh).
