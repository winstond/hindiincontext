कड़ाही (kadaahii)

noun The wide, deep pan (like a wok) used for Indian frying and cooking -- almost always literal kitchenware; also spelled कड़ाई (kadaaii), a common variant.
Sense 1 : wok-like cooking vessel
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कड़ाई (kadaaii, variant form), तसला (taslaa, larger/wider metal pan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian kitchen has at least one कड़ाही (kadaahii) -- often a heavy iron one seasoned over years, kept just for पूरी (poorii, 'fried bread']) or पकौड़े (pakaude, 'fritters']). Street-food stalls making जलेबी (jalebii) or समोसे (samose) are instantly recognizable by the giant कड़ाही (kadaahii) of bubbling oil out front.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate this as 'pot' or 'saucepan,' but कड़ाही (kadaahii) specifically means the wide, shallow-sided round pan used for frying and deep-frying (like a wok), not a straight-sided pot -- that would be भगोना (bhagonaa) or पतीला (patiilaa).
