आश्चर्य (aashchary)

noun/interjection A somewhat formal/literary word for surprise or astonishment, used as a noun and in the common exclamation आश्चर्य! (aashchary) 'How astonishing!'. In everyday speech, हैरानी (hairaanii) is more common; this word leans a bit bookish but is still widely understood and used.
Sense 1 : surprise, astonishment, wonder
noun, masculine Commonly used with होना (honaa) in the dative-subject frame: मुझे आश्चर्य हुआ (mujhe aashchary huaa, 'I was surprised'), literally 'surprise happened to me.'
Synonyms: हैरानी (hairaanii), अचरज (achraj, everyday, slightly colloquial), विस्मय (vismay, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll often hear आश्चर्य की बात है (aashchary kii baat hai, 'it's a matter of surprise/it's surprising') as a slightly formal way to comment on something unexpected -- a notch more bookish than the everyday हैरानी की बात है (hairaanii kii baat hai).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैं आश्चर्य हुआ (main aashchary huaa) by analogy with English 'I was surprised,' but Hindi requires the dative-subject frame: मुझे आश्चर्य हुआ (mujhe aashchary huaa). 2) आश्चर्य (aashchary) is grammatically masculine even though it names an emotion/state -- learners sometimes wrongly feminize agreement with it.

Sense 2 : expression of astonishment
interjection
Synonyms: अरे वाह (are vaah, everyday), हैरानी की बात है (hairaanii kii baat hai, everyday)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Said alone as आश्चर्य! (aashchary) it has a slightly theatrical, almost storybook or old-fashioned flavor -- more at home in narration or a teacher's dramatic aside than in casual chat among friends, where अरे वाह (are vaah) or हैरानी की बात है (hairaanii kii baat hai) would feel more natural.
