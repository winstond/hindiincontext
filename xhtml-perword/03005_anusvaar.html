अनुस्वार (anusvaar)

noun A grammar/script term: the nasal mark (the dot placed above a letter, as in हिंदी (hindii)) used in Devanagari to show nasalization of a vowel.
Sense 1 : the nasalization mark in Devanagari script (the dot, as in हिंदी)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बिंदी (bindii, the mark itself, colloquial), चंद्रबिंदु (chandrbindu, related but distinct mark)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word mostly in school grammar lessons or when someone is explaining Devanagari spelling rules -- for instance, explaining why हिंदी (hindii) has a dot over the 'hi' but गांधी (gaandhii) also does. It's a technical, literary-register term rather than something used in daily talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse अनुस्वार (anusvaar) (the dot, as in हंस hans) with चंद्रबिंदु (chandrbindu) (the crescent-plus-dot mark, as in हूँ hoon) -- they are visually and functionally different marks, not interchangeable. 2) This word is a grammar/script term used when talking about Hindi, not a word you'll hear in casual conversation about anything else.
