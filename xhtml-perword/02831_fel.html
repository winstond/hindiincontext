फ़ेल (fel)

adjective/verb An English loanword ('fail') used in everyday Hindi for failing an exam or a plan/thing not working; almost always paired with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa).
Sense 1 : having failed (an exam, test, attempt)
adjective Used predicatively with होना (honaa): फ़ेल होना (fel honaa, 'to fail/be failed').
Synonyms: असफल (asphal, formal), नाकाम (naakaam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian student dreads hearing about their result card: बोर्ड में फ़ेल हो गया (bord men fel ho gayaa, 'failed in the board exams') is a stock phrase in family conversations and even news headlines. It sits comfortably alongside its Hindi cousin असफल (asphal), but फ़ेल (fel) is the word actually used at home and in school corridors.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to conjugate फ़ेल (fel) like a native adjective (फ़ेला, फ़ेली) -- it never changes form; only the accompanying होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) inflects. 2) The subject who fails takes no ने (ne) marking with फ़ेल होना (fel honaa) since होना (honaa) is intransitive, e.g. वह फ़ेल हो गया (vah fel ho gayaa, 'he failed'), not वह ने.
Sample Sentences:
वो इस बार गणित में फ़ेल हो गया। He failed in math this time.
(vo is baar ganit men fel ho gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to fail, flunk (an exam)
verb, intransitive Functions as a compound verb: फ़ेल (fel) + होना (honaa) (intransitive, 'to become failed') or + करना (karnaa) (transitive, 'to fail someone/something,' e.g. a teacher failing a student).
Synonyms: फ़ेल होना (fel honaa), असफल होना (asphal honaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear both usages constantly around exam season: students say मैं फ़ेल हो गया (main fel ho gayaa, 'I failed'), while a strict teacher might threaten मैं तुम्हें फ़ेल कर दूँगा (main tumhen fel kar doongaa, 'I'll fail you'). This pairing with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) is the standard pattern for absorbing English verbs into Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) फ़ेल करना (fel karnaa) and फ़ेल होना (fel honaa) are easy to mix up: करना (karnaa) means someone else causes the failure (टीचर ने उसे फ़ेल कर दिया (tiichar ne use fel kar diyaa, 'the teacher failed him')), while होना (honaa) means the subject itself failed.
