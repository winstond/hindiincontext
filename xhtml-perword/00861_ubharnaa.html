उभरना (ubharnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to emerge, surface, or rise into prominence' -- used both literally (a shape rising into view) and figuratively (a trend, feeling, or person coming to prominence).
Sense 1 : to emerge into prominence, come to the fore (figurative)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: उदय होना (uday honaa, literary), सामने आना (saamne aanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on Hindi news channels: a new political face उभर कर आया (ubhar kar aayaa, emerged/came to the fore) or a city उभरता हुआ शहर (ubhartaa huaa shahar, an up-and-coming city) is being talked about. It is the standard verb for describing rising stars, trends, and emerging economies.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is the sense used constantly in news and analysis -- a new leader उभरता है (ubhartaa hai), a trend उभर रहा है (ubhar rahaa hai) -- learners who only know the literal 'surface' meaning miss how naturally this word extends to careers, movements, and ideas.
Sample Sentences:

(pichhle kuchh saalon men yah ilaakaa ek ubharte hue baazaar ke roop men jaanaa jaane lagaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to emerge physically / become visible
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: दिखना (dikhnaa), नज़र आना (nazar aanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of ice cream bumps under a warm blanket, or a bruise slowly appearing on skin -- उभरना (ubharnaa) is the verb for something pushing itself up into visibility, whether it's a vein, a hill on the horizon, or letters embossed on a card.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for दिखना (dikhnaa) when the sense is really 'rising up from beneath/within,' like a shape emerging from water or veins showing on skin -- उभरना (ubharnaa) carries that sense of coming up from a surface or depth, which दिखना (dikhnaa) does not capture.
