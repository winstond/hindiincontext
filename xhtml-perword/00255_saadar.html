सादर (saadar)

adverb/adjective A formal, respect-marking word used mainly as an adverb ('respectfully') and in polite letter-closings; also occasionally seen as an adjective. Common on invitation cards and in official correspondence.
Sense 1 : respectfully, with respect
adverb
Synonyms: आदरपूर्वक (aadarpoorvak), विनम्रतापूर्वक (vinamrtaapoorvak, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word most often at the bottom of a formal letter or on a wedding card: सादर निमंत्रण (saadar nimantran, 'respectful invitation') is the standard phrase printed at the top of Indian wedding invitations. It carries the flavor of the old Hindi/Sanskrit-inflected register used in formal writing, much like 'respectfully' or 'sincerely' does in an English letter.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use सादर (saadar) in casual spoken sentences where a native speaker would just use कृपया (kripyaa) or nothing at all -- सादर (saadar) belongs to formal/written registers like invitations, letters, and speeches, not everyday chat. 2) It is not synonymous with साथ (saath, 'with') despite the similar look -- there is no root connection, and confusing the two is a common beginner error.
Sample Sentences:
सादर निमंत्रण Respectful invitation.
(saadar nimantran)

Sense 2 : respectful (describing something offered with respect)
adjective Used attributively before a noun in fixed formal phrases, e.g. सादर निवेदन (saadar nivedan, 'respectful request'); it does not inflect for gender or number.
Synonyms: आदरपूर्ण (aadarpoorn)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This adjectival use overlaps heavily with the adverb sense above -- both trace to the same root (स + आदर, 'with respect') -- and you will see them in near-identical formal phrases like सादर निवेदन (saadar nivedan) versus सादर निवेदन करता हूँ (saadar nivedan kartaa hoon, 'I respectfully request').
