छिपाना (chhipaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to hide/conceal' something -- an object, a fact, an emotion. Causative partner of the intransitive छिपना (chhipnaa, 'to hide oneself/become hidden').
Sense 1 : to hide or conceal (an object)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: छुपाना (chhupaanaa, common variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of छिपाना (chhipaanaa) alongside its intransitive twin छिपना (chhipnaa): the आ (aa) ending is doing causative work here, turning 'to become hidden' into 'to make hidden.' You'll hear this verb in every context from hiding Eid-ii sweets from siblings to a politician accused of छिपाना (chhipaanaa) the truth.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use the intransitive छिपना (chhipnaa) when they mean to hide something -- चाबी छिप गई (chaabii chhip gaii, 'the key got hidden') describes the key's own state, while चाबी छिपाई (chaabii chhipaaii, 'hid the key') is the deliberate act; keep the pair straight, since mixing them is the classic error with this verb. 2) Also commonly spelled छुपाना (chhupaanaa) -- both are heard constantly in speech and are not a mistake.
Sample Sentences:
माँ ने मिठाई अलमारी में छिपा दी। Mom hid the sweets in the cupboard.
(maan ne mithaaii almaarii men chhipaa dii।)

Sense 2 : to conceal, keep secret (a fact, feeling, or information)
verb, transitive Commonly takes an abstract object like बात (baat, 'matter'), राज़ (raaz, 'secret'), or दर्द (dard, 'pain'), and often appears with से (se) marking the person being kept in the dark: मुझसे कुछ छिपाना (mujhse kuchh chhipaanaa, 'to hide something from me').
Synonyms: छुपाना (chhupaanaa, common variant spelling), दबाना (dabaanaa, to suppress/keep down, narrower)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind common life-advice lines like दिल की बात मत छिपाओ (dil kii baat mat chhipaao, 'don't hide what's in your heart') -- Hindi treats concealing a feeling and concealing an object with the exact same verb, which says something about how directly emotions are talked about in the language.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छिपाना (chhipaanaa) with को (ko) for the person kept in the dark; native speakers use से (se) instead -- उससे सच छिपाना (usse sach chhipaanaa, 'to hide the truth from him/her'), not उसे। 2) This sense extends naturally from the physical-hiding sense above; the same verb covers both a hidden object and a hidden fact, so don't expect a separate word for 'lie by omission.'
