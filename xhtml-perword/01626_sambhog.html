संभोग (sambhog)

noun A formal/clinical word for sexual intercourse (संभोग (sambhog)); it is the term you'd meet in a biology textbook or a doctor's office, not in casual chat.
Sense 1 : sexual intercourse
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सेक्स (seks, colloquial, English loanword), मैथुन (maithun, clinical/literary), सहवास (sahvaas, literary/formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Hindi keeps a clear register ladder here: संभोग (sambhog) is the clinical/legal term, सेक्स (seks) is what people actually say informally (borrowed straight from English), and there's a whole further layer of slang below that. A teacher or doctor uses this word; friends joking around reach for the English loanword instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) This word belongs in medical, legal, or academic registers (sambhog appears in textbooks and law text about consent) -- using it in ordinary conversation sounds stilted or overly clinical, the way 'coitus' would in English. 2) Learners sometimes reach for this word to mean romance or physical affection generally; it specifically denotes the act itself, not kissing, dating, or intimacy in the broader sense.
