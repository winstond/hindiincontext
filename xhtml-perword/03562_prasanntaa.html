प्रसन्नता (prasanntaa)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'happiness' or 'joy' -- a more formal, literary-leaning counterpart to everyday खुशी (khushii).
Sense 1 : happiness, joy, pleasure
noun, feminine
Synonyms: खुशी (khushii), आनंद (aanand, slightly more literary), हर्ष (harsh, formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear प्रसन्नता (prasanntaa) in the mouths of announcers, teachers, and officials -- it has a slightly ceremonial ring, the kind of word used when someone wants to sound a bit more dignified than a casual मैं बहुत खुश हूँ (main bahut khush hoon, 'I am very happy') would. Government notices and school prize functions love this word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to खुशी (khushii) in all contexts, but प्रसन्नता (prasanntaa) is the word you will meet in speeches, written announcements, and polite formal speech, e.g. मुझे प्रसन्नता है (mujhe prasanntaa hai, 'it gives me pleasure/I am pleased') -- swapping in खुशी there is fine but sounds less formal, not wrong. 2) Like खुशी (khushii), the feeler is marked with को (ko), not ने (ne) -- it patterns like a dative-subject construction, not a transitive verb.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे यह बताते हुए प्रसन्नता हो रही है कि हमारी टीम जीत गई है। I am pleased to inform you that our team has won.
(mujhe yah bataate hue prasanntaa ho rahii hai ki hamaarii tiim jiit gaii hai।)
