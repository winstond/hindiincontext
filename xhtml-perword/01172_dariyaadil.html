दरियादिल (dariyaadil)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning generous or open-hearted, built from दरिया (dariyaa, 'river') plus दिल (dil, 'heart') -- literally 'river-hearted', evoking a heart as wide and flowing as a river.
Sense 1 : generous, open-hearted
adjective
Synonyms: उदार (udaar), दयालु (dayaalu, leans toward 'kind/compassionate' rather than 'generous with money'), खुले दिल का (khule dil kaa, descriptive phrase, same image of an 'open heart')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a lovely example of how Hindi builds character words from vivid images -- someone दरियादिल (dariyaadil) has a heart as generous and unstoppable as a flowing river, especially with money, food, or hospitality. You will hear it as a compliment for a host who refuses to let your plate go empty, or for someone who lends money without a second thought.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect it like a regular -आ (aa) adjective (दरियादिली, दरियादिले) -- it does not change form for gender or number. 2) It describes a person's nature or a specific generous act, not literally a river; do not use it to describe actual water or geography.
