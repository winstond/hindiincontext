टहलना (tahalnaa)

verb A single, simple meaning: to go for a leisurely stroll or walk, purely for relaxation or fresh air, not to get somewhere.
Sense 1 : to stroll, to take a leisurely walk
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: घूमना (ghoomnaa, broader, also covers roaming/visiting), सैर करना (sair karnaa, compound-verb phrase for the same activity)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic after-dinner verb in Indian households -- खाना खाकर टहलना (khaanaa khaakar tahalnaa, 'to stroll after eating') is considered good for digestion, and you'll hear grandparents insist on it every evening in a park or even just up and down the colony street.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for घूमना (ghoomnaa) for every kind of walking, but टहलना (tahalnaa) specifically means an unhurried, purposeless stroll (e.g. after dinner) -- it sounds odd if used for a purposeful walk to a destination. 2) It is intransitive with no direct object; you don't 'टहलना' a place, you टहलना (in/at) a park -- पार्क में टहलना (paark men tahalnaa, 'to stroll in the park'), using में (men), not a bare object.
Sample Sentences:
खाना खाकर थोड़ी देर टहलना चाहिए। One should stroll for a while after eating.
(khaanaa khaakar thodii der tahalnaa chaahie।)
