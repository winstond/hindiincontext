ख़तरा (khatraa)

noun A common noun meaning 'danger' or 'risk'; also frequently used inside set phrases like ख़तरे में (khatre men) and ख़तरा होना (khatraa honaa).
Sense 1 : danger, risk, threat
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जोखिम (jokhim), संकट (sankat, more formal/serious), भय (bhay, fear, meaning shifted toward the source of fear)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly in news broadcasts and safety announcements -- जान को ख़तरा (jaan ko khatraa, 'danger to life') is a stock phrase. In everyday speech, Delhi parents love warning kids with ख़तरे से खेलना मत (khatre se khelnaa mat, 'don't play with danger') when they're doing something reckless, even metaphorically, like skipping study for exams.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say ख़तरा है (khatraa hai) when they mean someone is 'in danger' -- the correct frame is X को ख़तरा है (X ko khatraa hai) or X ख़तरे में है (X khatre men hai), using a dative-subject or locative construction rather than making the person the plain subject. 2) The nuqta on ख़ is often dropped in casual typing (खतरा), which is common and still understood, but the dotted form is the standard spelling.
