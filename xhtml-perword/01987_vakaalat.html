वकालत (vakaalat)

noun A noun with a literal sense (the legal profession, practicing as a lawyer) and a common figurative sense (arguing/advocating in favor of someone or something).
Sense 1 : the practice of law, the legal profession
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वकालत करना (vakaalat karnaa, to practice law)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: वकालत (vakaalat) comes from the same root as वकील (vakiil, 'lawyer'), from Arabic/Persian via Urdu into everyday Hindi legal vocabulary. Saying कोई वकालत करता है (koii vakaalat kartaa hai, 'someone practices law') is the standard way to describe a person's profession, much like डॉक्टरी करना (doktrii karnaa) for medicine.
Common Mistakes: 1) वकालत (vakaalat) itself means the profession/practice of law, not a single 'case' -- a case is मुकदमा (mukadmaa) or केस (kes). 2) Learners sometimes say वकालत करना (vakaalat karnaa) when they mean 'to hire a lawyer' -- that would be वकील करना (vakiil karnaa) instead.
Sample Sentences:
मेरे पिताजी दिल्ली में वकालत करते हैं। My father practices law in Delhi.
(mere pitaajii dillii men vakaalat karte hain।)

Sense 2 : advocacy, pleading/arguing in favor of someone or something
noun, feminine Commonly used in the pattern किसी की/का वकालत करना (kisii kii vakaalat karnaa, 'to advocate for someone/something').
Synonyms: समर्थन (samarthan, support, broader/less personal), पैरवी (pairvii, championing/backing someone)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the extended, metaphorical use of a lawyer 'pleading a case' -- in everyday speech, वकालत करना (vakaalat karnaa) is how you say someone is 'making the case for' or 'championing' an idea, a person, or a policy, heard constantly in news debates and family arguments alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is very common in news and everyday debate-talk, e.g. वह इस बदलाव की वकालत करता है (vah is badlaav kii vakaalat kartaa hai, 'he advocates for this change') -- learners often only know the 'lawyer' sense and miss this extended usage. 2) The thing being advocated for takes की (kii), matching वकालत's feminine gender, not का (kaa) or के (ke).
