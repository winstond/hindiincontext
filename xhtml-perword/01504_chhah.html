छह (chhah)

numeral The number six (chhah); a basic counting numeral used constantly in dates, time, prices, and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number six
numeral
Synonyms: छे (chhe, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear छह (chhah) constantly around clock times (छह बजे chhah baje, 'at six o'clock') and dates (छह तारीख़ chhah taariikh, 'the sixth of the month'), so it is worth learning early alongside the other single-digit numerals.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write or say छह (chhah) as if it were spelled like सात (saat) with a similar vowel pattern, but the internal ह (h) is easy to drop in fast speech, so it can come out sounding like छे (chhe) -- both are heard, but छह (chhah) is the standard written form. 2) When counting objects, छह (chhah) itself does not change form regardless of the noun's gender, unlike some other numerals in related contexts -- there is no separate feminine form.
