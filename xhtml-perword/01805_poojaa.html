पूजा (poojaa)

noun Mainly a noun meaning worship/religious ritual, but also a common Hindu girls' name -- context makes the difference obvious to native speakers.
Sense 1 : worship, ritual prayer
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आराधना (aaraadhnaa, formal), अर्चना (archnaa, literary), उपासना (upaasnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पूजा (poojaa) refers to the whole ritual -- lighting a diya, offering flowers and prasad, ringing a small bell -- not just silent prayer; in most Hindu homes there's a पूजा घर (poojaa ghar, household shrine room) or at least a shelf-corner where the morning पूजा (poojaa) happens before the day starts. During Durga Puja season in Delhi-NCR you'll hear the word everywhere, from pandal announcements to office greetings.
Common Mistakes: 1) पूजा (poojaa) pairs with the verb करना (karnaa) -- पूजा करना (poojaa karnaa, to worship/perform puja) -- learners sometimes reach for होना (honaa) by analogy with English 'worship happens,' but the doer must be the subject with करना (karnaa). 2) Don't confuse with प्रार्थना (praarthnaa), which leans toward a spoken prayer or petition rather than the full ritual act.

Sense 2 : a female given name
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: पूजा (poojaa) is one of the most common Hindu girls' names in North India, drawn directly from the word for worship -- much like 'Grace' or 'Faith' as English names. Context (a person being addressed or referred to) makes clear this isn't the ritual sense.
