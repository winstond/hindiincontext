भूलकर भी (bhoolkar bhii)

adverb A fixed emphatic phrase built from भूलना (bhoolnaa, 'to forget') plus भी (bhii, 'even'), used to mean 'even by mistake' or 'under any circumstances' -- almost always inside a warning or prohibition telling someone what never to do.
Sense 1 : not even by mistake, under no circumstances (in warnings/prohibitions)
adverb Almost always paired with a negative imperative or न/मत clause: भूलकर भी ऐसा मत करना (bhoolkar bhii aisaa mat karnaa, 'do not do this, even by mistake'). It front-loads the warning for emphasis and rarely appears outside a prohibition frame.
Synonyms: कभी भी (kabhii bhii, broader: 'ever/at any time'), किसी भी सूरत में (kisii bhii soorat men, 'under no circumstances')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the phrase a Delhi mother or a strict boss reaches for when the stakes feel high: भूलकर भी उससे पैसे मत माँगना (bhoolkar bhii usse paise mat maangnaa, 'do not, even by mistake, ask him for money'). It carries real weight and urgency, more forceful than a plain कभी मत करना (kabhii mat karnaa), so save it for warnings that genuinely matter.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use भूलकर भी (bhoolkar bhii) as a neutral way to say 'even if I forget,' but it is not descriptive -- it is only used to intensify a warning or command, never a plain statement about forgetting. 2) It needs a negative word (मत/न) later in the sentence to complete the sense; dropping that negative breaks the idiom. 3) Learners may place it mid-sentence out of habit from English word order, but native speakers strongly prefer it at the very start of the clause for maximum emphasis.

भूलकर भी उससे पैसे मत माँगना। Do not, even by mistake, ask him for money.
(bhoolkar bhii usse paise mat maangnaa।)
