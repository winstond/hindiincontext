घेरना (ghernaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to surround or encircle, physically or figuratively; also used for laying siege to a place.
Sense 1 : to surround, encircle (physically)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: घेर लेना (gher lenaa, compound verb variant, completed sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in news reports about police or crowds: पुलिस ने इलाके को घेर लिया (pulis ne ilaake ko gher liyaa, 'the police surrounded the area'). It gives a strong visual of people or things closing in from all sides, like a circle tightening.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the compound form घेर लेना (gher lenaa) is far more common in narration than the bare verb, since it emphasizes the completed encirclement rather than the ongoing act.
Sample Sentences:
पुलिस ने इलाके को घेर लिया। The police surrounded the area.
(pulis ne ilaake ko gher liyaa।)

Sense 2 : to besiege, blockade (a place, in a military or political sense)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: घेराव करना (gheraav karnaa, noun+verb phrase, common in protest contexts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up a lot in Indian political news: किसानों ने संसद को घेर लिया (kisaanon ne sansad ko gher liyaa, 'the farmers surrounded/blockaded the parliament'). The related noun घेराव (gheraav) is the standard word for a protest-blockade you'll see in headlines.

Sense 3 : to corner, trap someone (figuratively, e.g. with questions or arguments)
verb, transitive Often used with words like सवालों (savaalon, 'with questions') or बहस में (bahas men, 'in an argument') to show what someone is being cornered by.
Synonyms: फँसाना (phansaanaa, to trap, slightly different shade of trickery)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly on Hindi news channels during a heated debate -- नेता को सवालों में घेर लिया (netaa ko savaalon men gher liyaa, 'they cornered the leader with questions'). It captures the feeling of being boxed in verbally, the same circle-closing image as the physical sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes miss this figurative sense entirely and only know the physical 'surround' meaning, but it is very common in political and debate contexts: विपक्ष ने मंत्री को घेर लिया (vipaksh ne mantrii ko gher liyaa, 'the opposition cornered the minister').
Sample Sentences:
पत्रकारों ने मंत्री को सवालों में घेर लिया। The journalists cornered the minister with questions.
(patrkaaron ne mantrii ko savaalon men gher liyaa।)
