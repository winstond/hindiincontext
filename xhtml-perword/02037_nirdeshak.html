निर्देशक (nirdeshak)

noun/adjective Mainly the person who directs a film, play, or organization (a director); also used as an adjective meaning 'guiding' or 'instructive' in more formal/written contexts.
Sense 1 : director (of a film, play, organization, department)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: डायरेक्टर (daayrektar, common in film/media contexts)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday conversation about movies, most Delhi-NCR speakers actually say डायरेक्टर (daayrektar) rather than निर्देशक (nirdeshak), which feels a notch more formal -- you will see निर्देशक (nirdeshak) printed in film credits and news articles, but hear डायरेक्टर (daayrektar) in casual chat about a movie.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use निर्देशक (nirdeshak) for any 'boss' or 'manager' -- it specifically implies someone who directs creative or organizational work (film director, project director), whereas a general workplace boss is more often मैनेजर (mainejar) or अफ़सर (afsar).

Sense 2 : guiding, directive, instructive
adjective Used before a noun in formal or written registers, e.g. निर्देशक सिद्धांत (nirdeshak siddhaant, 'guiding principles').
Synonyms: मार्गदर्शक (maargdarshak)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This adjectival use shows up in formal or bureaucratic language -- constitutional or policy documents refer to निर्देशक तत्व (nirdeshak tatv, 'directive principles') -- but it is rare in casual speech, where a learner is far more likely to meet the noun sense (director) than this one.
