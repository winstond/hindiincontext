उन्यासी (unyaasii)

numeral The numeral for '79' (seventy-nine), used both for counting and as an ordinal-like quantity marker before a noun.
Sense 1 : the number seventy-nine (79)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 are famously irregular and not built from simple patterns the way English tens-and-units are, so native speakers themselves usually just recite them as memorized chunks (often learned in school as a sung or rhythmic table). Don't worry if उन्यासी (unyaasii) feels arbitrary next to अठहत्तर (athahattar, seventy-eight) or अस्सी (assii, eighty) -- that discontinuity is normal and every Hindi speaker went through the same rote-learning process as a child.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from सत्तर (sattar, seventy) + नौ (nau, nine), but like most Hindi numbers 71-99 it is a fused, irregular form that must simply be memorized rather than derived. 2) It is easy to confuse with similar-sounding numbers in the seventies and eighties (e.g. उन्नासी-adjacent forms); careful listening practice with the sequence 76-79-89 helps distinguish them.
