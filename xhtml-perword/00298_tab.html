तब (tab)

adverb/conjunction A basic time adverb meaning 'then/at that time', usually paired with जब (jab, 'when') in correlative constructions; it also has a discourse use meaning 'in that case, so'.
Sense 1 : at that time, then
adverb Forms a correlative pair with जब (jab, 'when'): जब...तब... (jab...tab..., 'when... then...') is the standard frame, parallel to English 'when... then...' but far more obligatory in Hindi.
Synonyms: उस समय (us samay, 'at that time', two-word phrase), उस वक़्त (us vaqt, 'at that time', two-word phrase)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of जब...तब... (jab...tab..., 'when... then...') as a matched set, like 'if...then...' in English -- native speakers reach for both halves together almost reflexively. तब (tab) pairs naturally with इससे पहले (isse pahle) or उसके बाद (uske baad) type time markers in longer narratives too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop जब (jab) and use तब (tab) alone where English would just say 'then', but in Hindi तब (tab) usually wants its jab-clause partner nearby for the sentence to sound complete. 2) Confusing तब (tab) (time: 'then/at that time') with तो (to) (logical: 'so/in that case') -- they overlap in translation but not in flavor.

Sense 2 : then, in that case (logical consequence)
conjunction Used at the head of a clause to mark a consequence or conclusion drawn from what was just said, close to फिर (phir) in this use.
Synonyms: तो (to, 'so, then'), फिर (phir, 'then, in that case')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in everyday problem-solving talk -- someone lays out a situation, and the reply starts with तब (tab) or फिर (phir) to mean 'well then...', much like an English speaker pausing before drawing a conclusion.
