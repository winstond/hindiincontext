फ़िकर (fikar)

noun A common everyday word for 'worry' or 'concern' -- a variant spelling of फ़िक्र (fikr), used interchangeably in speech.
Sense 1 : worry, concern, anxiety
noun, feminine Commonly used in the construction फ़िकर होना (fikar honaa) with a को-marked person -- 'mujhe fikar hai' style dative-subject sentences, paralleling how चिंता (chintaa) is used.
Synonyms: चिंता (chintaa), फ़िक्र (fikr, variant form), परेशानी (pareshaanii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly in Delhi households -- a mother calling out फ़िकर मत करो, सब ठीक हो जाएगा (fikar mat karo, sab thiik ho jaaegaa, 'don't worry, everything will be fine') captures its everyday warmth. It is spelled with a nuqta under the फ़ (f) reflecting its Persian origin, but you will also see it written without the nuqta as फिकर (phikar) in casual writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make this the grammatical subject with ने (ne), but the person who worries is usually marked with को (ko) while फ़िकर (fikar) itself is the subject that 'happens' to them (dative-subject pattern, like लगना (lagnaa)). 2) फ़िकर मत करो (fikar mat karo, 'don't worry') is the everyday phrase learners reach for -- don't overthink it with a more literary alternative.
