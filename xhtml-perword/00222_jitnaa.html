जितना (jitnaa)

adjective/pronoun The correlative 'as much/many as' word, forming a pair with उतना (utnaa, 'that much'); it also stands alone as 'as much as/whatever amount' and inflects like an adjective (जितना/जितने/जितनी) to agree with the noun it quantifies.
Sense 1 : as much/as many (correlative with उतना)
adjective Works as the जितना...उतना (jitnaa...utnaa) correlative pair: जितना introduces the relative quantity, उतना (utnaa) picks it up in the main clause, e.g. जितना पैसा है, उतना खर्च करो (jitnaa paisaa hai, utnaa kharch karo, 'spend as much money as you have').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This जितना...उतना (jitnaa...utnaa) pattern is one of the first correlative structures learners meet, and it's everywhere in daily bargaining and advice: a shopkeeper might say जितना चाहिए उतना ले लो (jitnaa chaahie utnaa le lo, 'take as much as you need').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop the उतना (utnaa) half and just use जितना alone, which sounds incomplete in formal correlative sentences -- native speakers usually keep both sides. 2) जितना agrees in gender/number with the noun it quantifies (जितनी किताबें, jitnii kitaaben, 'as many books'), so learners sometimes leave it stuck in the default masculine form.
Sample Sentences:
जितना पैसा तुम्हारे पास है, उतना ही मेरे पास है। I have exactly as much money as you have.
(jitnaa paisaa tumhaare paas hai, utnaa hii mere paas hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : whatever amount, however much (standalone quantity)
pronoun Used without a following उतना (utnaa) when the sentence just names a capped or open amount: जितना हो सके (jitnaa ho sake, 'as much as possible').
Synonyms: जो कुछ (jo kuchh, whatever there is)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear जितना हो सके (jitnaa ho sake, 'as much as possible') constantly in polite requests and instructions, from recipes to office emails, as a softer way of setting an open-ended limit.
Sample Sentences:
