अनुवाद (anuvaad)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'translation' -- both the act of translating and the resulting translated text.
Sense 1 : translation (act or result)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तर्जुमा (tarjumaa, colloquial/Urdu-influenced, common in speech), भाषांतर (bhaashaantar, literary, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word everywhere on book covers as अनुवादक (anuvaadak, 'translator') and अनुवादित (anuvaadit, 'translated'), both built on the same root. In everyday Delhi speech, especially among older or more Persianized speakers, तर्जुमा (tarjumaa) is just as common as अनुवाद (anuvaad) for casual conversation, while अनुवाद itself is the neutral, standard-register choice used in schools, offices, and media.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to verbify it directly as अनुवादना (anuvaadnaa), which is not idiomatic; the standard verb phrase is अनुवाद करना (anuvaad karnaa, 'to do/make a translation') or अनुवाद होना (anuvaad honaa) for the passive/impersonal 'to be translated.' 2) When naming the source or target language, use में (men): हिंदी में अनुवाद (hindii men anuvaad, 'translation into Hindi'), not से (se) for the target.
Sample Sentences:
क्या आप इस पत्र का अनुवाद कर सकते हैं? Can you translate this letter?
(kyaa aap is patr kaa anuvaad kar sakte hain?)
