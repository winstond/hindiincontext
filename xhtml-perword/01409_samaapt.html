समाप्त (samaapt)

adjective A formal/written-register adjective meaning 'finished' or 'ended', almost always paired with the verb होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) to form a compound expression -- it does not conjugate as a verb on its own.
Sense 1 : finished, over, ended
adjective Used predicatively with होना (honaa): काम समाप्त हो गया (kaam samaapt ho gayaa, 'the work got finished'). Not typically used attributively before a noun in casual speech.
Synonyms: खत्म (khatm), पूरा (pooraa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear समाप्त (samaapt) constantly in train station announcements, news broadcasts, and school/office contexts -- 'यह गाड़ी अपनी यात्रा समाप्त करती है' (yah gaadii apnii yaatraa samaapt kartii hai, 'this train ends its journey here') is classic Indian Railways language. In daily home or bazaar talk, though, most Delhi-NCR speakers would just reach for खत्म (khatm) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use समाप्त (samaapt) in casual conversation where खत्म (khatm) would sound more natural -- समाप्त (samaapt) leans formal/written and is common in news, announcements, and official writing rather than everyday chat. 2) Since it's an adjective, not a verb, learners sometimes try to conjugate it directly (e.g. adding verb endings) instead of pairing it with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa).

(baithak samaapt ho gaii hai।)
