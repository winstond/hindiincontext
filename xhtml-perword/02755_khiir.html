खीर (khiir)

noun A sweet rice pudding, one of the most beloved festive desserts across North India; single sense, single POS.
Sense 1 : sweet rice pudding made with milk
noun, feminine
Synonyms: चावल की खीर (chaaval kii khiir, full/specific name)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: खीर (khiir) is the dessert of festivals and satisfying endings -- served at Diwali, on birthdays, after a puja, or as the finishing course at a family dinner when someone says मीठा हो जाए (miithaa ho jaae, 'let's have something sweet'). Every household has its own ratio of milk, sugar, and cardamom, and a grandmother's खीर (khiir) recipe is often a small point of family pride.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume खीर (khiir) is always rice-based; it can also be made with vermicelli (सेवई, sevaii) or sago (साबूदाना, saaboodaanaa), though the rice version is the default reference. 2) Do not confuse with खिचड़ी (khichdii), a savory rice-lentil dish -- the two sound similar to new learners but are opposites in flavor (sweet vs. savory).
