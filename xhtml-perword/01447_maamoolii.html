मामूली (maamoolii)

adjective An adjective meaning 'ordinary, common' or, depending on context, 'minor, trivial' -- both senses come from the same idea of something not being special or serious.
Sense 1 : ordinary, common, unremarkable
adjective
Synonyms: साधारण (saadhaaran), सामान्य (saamaany), आम (aam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday put-downs and modest disclaimers alike: a host might call an elaborate meal मामूली खाना (maamoolii khaanaa, 'ordinary food') out of politeness, while the same word aimed at a person -- मामूली आदमी (maamoolii aadmii, 'an ordinary/insignificant man') -- can sting a little.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for साधारण (saadhaaran) in every context, but मामूली (maamoolii) often carries a slightly more dismissive or belittling shade -- 'nothing special,' sometimes verging on 'not worth your attention' -- while साधारण (saadhaaran) is more neutral.
Sample Sentences:
यह तो बिल्कुल मामूली खाना है, आप बैठिए। This is just ordinary food, please sit.
(yah to bilkul maamoolii khaanaa hai, aap baithie।)

Sense 2 : minor, slight, trivial (of a problem, injury, amount, etc.)
adjective
Synonyms: छोटा (chhotaa, covers only physical/quantity smallness), हल्का (halkaa, covers only severity of illness/injury)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear from a doctor or a mechanic reassuring you: मामूली सी बात है (maamoolii sii baat hai, 'it's a trivial/minor matter'), a very common way to downplay a problem and put someone at ease.
Common Mistakes: 1) मामूली चोट (maamoolii chot, 'a minor injury') and मामूली बात (maamoolii baat, 'a trivial matter') are extremely common collocations; learners often default to छोटा (chhotaa) here, which sounds odd since छोटा (chhotaa) is about physical size, not severity or importance.
Sample Sentences:
चिंता मत करो, यह मामूली सी बात है। Don't worry, it's a trivial matter.
(chintaa mat karo, yah maamoolii sii baat hai।)
