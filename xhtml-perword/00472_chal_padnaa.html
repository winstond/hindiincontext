चल पड़ना (chal padnaa)

verb A compound verb built from चलना (chalnaa, 'to move/walk') plus पड़ना (padnaa) as a vector verb, meaning 'to set off, start moving' -- it captures the sudden onset of movement or a journey.
Sense 1 : to set off, start walking/moving (literal onset of motion)
verb, intransitive The पड़ना (padnaa) vector adds a sense of suddenness or decisiveness to the onset of motion, more than plain चलना (chalnaa) alone would convey.
Synonyms: रवाना होना (ravaanaa honaa), निकल पड़ना (nikal padnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in stories and travel narration a lot: बच्चे स्कूल के लिए चल पड़े (bachche skool ke lie chal pade, 'the kids set off for school') has a brisk, narrative feel, like a scene starting to move.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use plain चलना (chalnaa) when they mean the sudden 'off we go' moment; चल पड़ना (chal padnaa) specifically marks the instant the movement begins, not the ongoing act of walking. 2) As an intransitive compound, the subject takes no ने (ne) in the perfective: वह चल पड़ा (vah chal padaa, 'he set off'), not वह ने चल पड़ा।
Sample Sentences:

(bachche skool ke lie chal pade।)

Sense 2 : to embark on, take up (a course of action, figuratively)
verb, intransitive Used with an abstract path or direction, e.g. एक नए रास्ते पर चल पड़ना (ek nae raaste par chal padnaa, 'to set out on a new path'), extending the physical sense to a metaphorical journey or decision.
Synonyms: शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa, for a plan or task), अग्रसर होना (agrsar honaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative use is common in motivational speech and writing -- someone 'चल पड़ता है' (chal padtaa hai) on a mission or a new phase of life, echoing the same forward-momentum feel as the literal sense.
