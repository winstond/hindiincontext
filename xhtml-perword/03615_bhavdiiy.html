भवदीय (bhavdiiy)

adjective A highly formal letter-closing word meaning 'yours' (as in 'yours sincerely'), used mainly in written correspondence.
Sense 1 : yours (formal letter closing)
adjective Used only as a self-standing closing formula before the signature in formal or official letters, not in spoken sentences.
Synonyms: आपका (aapkaa, everyday equivalent)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the word you find at the bottom of an old-style Hindi formal letter, right before the writer's name -- school Hindi textbooks still teach 'पत्र-लेखन' (patr-lekhan, letter-writing) format with भवदीय (bhavdiiy) as the closing for a male writer and भवदीया (bhavdiiyaa) for a female writer. In everyday emails and messages today, most people have shifted to आपका (aapkaa) or just a plain sign-off, so भवदीय (bhavdiiy) has a slightly old-fashioned, official flavor, like 'yours faithfully' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use भवदीय (bhavdiiy) in speech or casual writing where आपका (aapkaa) is the natural choice; भवदीय (bhavdiiy) belongs strictly to formal letter-writing register. 2) It is invariant in this closing-formula use even though it looks like it should inflect like a normal -आ adjective -- it is not modified for the gender of the writer or recipient in this fixed function.
Sample Sentences:
पत्र के अंत में उसने लिखा, भवदीय, रमेश कुमार। At the end of the letter he wrote, yours sincerely, Ramesh Kumar.
(patr ke ant men usne likhaa, bhavdiiy, ramesh kumaar।)
