प्राकृतिक (praakritik)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'natural' -- relating to nature or occurring without human intervention.
Sense 1 : natural, of/relating to nature
adjective
Synonyms: स्वाभाविक (svaabhaavik, often means 'natural' in the sense of 'expected/normal behavior' rather than 'of nature')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in the phrases प्राकृतिक आपदा (praakritik aapdaa, 'natural disaster') on the news and प्राकृतिक सुंदरता (praakritik sundartaa, 'natural beauty') when Indians describe hill stations like Shimla or Manali. It sits comfortably in both spoken and written registers, unlike some of its more Sanskrit-heavy cousins.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for प्राकृतिक (praakritik) when they mean 'normal/to be expected,' which is स्वाभाविक (svaabhaavik) -- प्राकृतिक (praakritik) is reserved for things connected to nature itself, like आपदा (aapdaa, 'disaster') or सुंदरता (sundartaa, 'beauty'). 2) It does not inflect for gender/number the way a word like कड़ा (kadaa) does -- प्राकृतिक (praakritik) stays the same before masculine and feminine nouns.

उत्तराखंड में हर साल कोई न कोई प्राकृतिक आपदा आती है। Some natural disaster or the other occurs in Uttarakhand every year.
(uttraakhand men har saal koii n koii praakritik aapdaa aatii hai।)
