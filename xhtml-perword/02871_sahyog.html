सहयोग (sahyog)

noun A single core meaning of cooperation/collaboration/support, used constantly in both everyday requests for help and formal/institutional contexts. The verb form सहयोग करना (sahyog karnaa) is how you actually use it in a sentence.
Sense 1 : cooperation, collaboration, support (mutual help toward a shared goal)
noun, masculine Almost always appears with करना (karnaa) 'to give/extend cooperation' or चाहिए/माँगना (chaahie/maangnaa) 'to request cooperation.' The person cooperated with is marked with का/की/के ...सहयोग, or the phrase [X] के साथ सहयोग करना (ke saath sahyog karnaa) is used.
Synonyms: मदद (madad), सहायता (sahaaytaa), साथ (saath, informal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you hear on posters, in office memos, from teachers, and in public announcements: सबका सहयोग ज़रूरी है (sabkaa sahyog zaroorii hai, 'everyone's cooperation is necessary'). It carries a slightly more formal, civic-minded flavor than मदद (madad), which is why NGOs, schools, and government notices lean on it heavily -- it frames the listener as a partner in a joint effort rather than someone doing a favor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मदद (madad) in every context, but सहयोग (sahyog) implies a two-way, joint effort toward a common goal, not one person simply helping another -- so a boss asking for सहयोग (sahyog) from a team sounds more natural than मदद (madad) in that formal setting. 2) The set phrase आपका सहयोग चाहिए (aapkaa sahyog chaahie, 'your cooperation is needed') is very common in notices, announcements, and requests -- learners often construct this more awkwardly with कृपया मदद करें (kripyaa madad karen) instead, which sounds more like a plea than a polite institutional request.
Sample Sentences:
इस काम के लिए आप सबका सहयोग ज़रूरी है। Everyone's cooperation is necessary for this work.
(is kaam ke lie aap sabkaa sahyog zaroorii hai।)
