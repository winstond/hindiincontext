योगदान (yogdaan)

noun A formal, single-sense noun meaning 'contribution' -- something you give toward a shared goal, project, or cause.
Sense 1 : contribution
noun, masculine Almost always paired with the verb देना (denaa, 'to give') as योगदान देना (yogdaan denaa, 'to contribute'); the thing contributed to takes में (men).
Synonyms: अंशदान (anshdaan, formal, mainly financial), भागीदारी (bhaagiidaarii, leans toward 'participation/share')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic office-and-speech word in Delhi-NCR -- you hear it constantly in NGO reports, school annual functions, and political speeches: समाज में योगदान (samaaj men yogdaan, 'contribution to society'). It carries a slightly formal, civic-minded tone, so in casual chat about, say, splitting a restaurant bill, people reach for हिस्सा (hissaa) or contribution-in-English instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use योगदान (yogdaan) as a verb by itself; it needs देना (denaa) or करना (karnaa) attached -- you cannot say 'मैं योगदान' (main yogdaan) and stop there. 2) The field or project being contributed to is marked with में (men), not को (ko): देश के विकास में योगदान (desh ke vikaas men yogdaan, 'contribution to the country's development'), not देश के विकास को (desh ke vikaas ko).
Sample Sentences:
समाज के विकास में हर नागरिक का योगदान ज़रूरी है। Every citizen's contribution to the development of society is necessary.
(samaaj ke vikaas men har naagrik kaa yogdaan zaroorii hai।)
