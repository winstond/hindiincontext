सुस्ताना (sustaanaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning to rest or take a short break, usually after exertion -- to catch one's breath.
Sense 1 : to rest, take a breather
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: आराम करना (aaraam karnaa), दम लेना (dam lenaa, breathe/pause briefly), विश्राम करना (vishraam karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word used about travelers stopping under a tree, farmers pausing mid-field, or someone catching their breath on a staircase -- it carries the flavor of a brief, practical pause rather than leisurely relaxation. It pairs naturally with छाया (chhaayaa, shade) or थोड़ी देर (thodii der, a little while): थोड़ी देर सुस्ता लें (thodii der sustaa len, 'let's rest for a little while').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आराम करना (aaraam karnaa) in every context, but सुस्ताना (sustaanaa) specifically suggests a short pause during or after physical exertion (a walk, work in the fields, climbing stairs) rather than general relaxing or resting at home. 2) It is intransitive -- there is no object one 'सुस्ताता' है; you just pause and rest yourself.
Sample Sentences:
चलो, थोड़ी देर पेड़ की छाया में सुस्ता लें। Come on, let's rest for a little while in the shade of the tree.
(chalo, thodii der ped kii chhaayaa men sustaa len।)
