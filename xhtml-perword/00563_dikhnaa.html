दिखना (dikhnaa)

verb Means 'to be visible, to appear (to the eye)' -- an intransitive verb often used with a को (ko) marked experiencer, as in 'X मुझे अच्छा दिखता है' (X mujhe achchhaa dikhtaa hai, 'X looks good to me'). Closely related to the transitive दिखाना (dikhaanaa, 'to show'), which is its causative partner.
Sense 1 : to be visible, to be seen
verb, intransitive Commonly used with a को (ko) marked experiencer: पहाड़ मुझे दिखा (pahaad mujhe dikhaa, 'I could see the mountain', literally 'the mountain became visible to me').
Synonyms: नज़र आना (nazar aanaa), दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This verb reflects a very Hindi way of framing perception: things 'become visible' to you rather than you actively perceiving them, similar to लगना (lagnaa, 'to seem/feel') and सुनना (sunnaa) vs सुनाई देना (sunaaii denaa). It pairs neatly with दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa), which means almost the same thing and is used interchangeably in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for देखना (dekhnaa, 'to see/look at') here, but देखना (dekhnaa) is what the viewer actively does, while दिखना (dikhnaa) is about the thing itself being visible -- the grammatical subject is the object seen, not the person seeing. 2) The experiencer takes को (ko), not ने (ne), since दिखना (dikhnaa) is intransitive: मुझे वह दिखा (mujhe vah dikhaa, 'I saw him/it' / 'he was visible to me'), not मैंने (mainne).

Sense 2 : to appear, to look (a certain way)
verb, intransitive Takes an adjective complement describing how something/someone appears: वह थका हुआ दिखता है (vah thakaa huaa dikhtaa hai, 'he looks tired').
Synonyms: लगना (lagnaa), नज़र आना (nazar aanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the go-to verb for commenting on how someone looks -- compliments like तुम बहुत अच्छी दिखती हो (tum bahut achchhii dikhtii ho, 'you look very nice') are everyday currency at weddings and family gatherings across Delhi-NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with लगना (lagnaa) in sentences like तुम अच्छी दिखती हो (tum achchhii dikhtii ho, 'you look nice') vs तुम्हें अच्छा लगता है (tumhen achchhaa lagtaa hai, 'you feel good/like it') -- दिखना (dikhnaa) describes outward appearance, लगना (lagnaa) describes an impression or feeling, and mixing them up changes the meaning.
