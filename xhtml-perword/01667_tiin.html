तीन (tiin)

numeral The number three (tiin) -- a basic cardinal numeral used constantly in counting, quantities, time, and prices.
Sense 1 : the number three
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: तीन (tiin) shows up everywhere in daily Delhi life -- from तीन मूर्ति (tiin moorti, a well-known Delhi landmark) to the common phrase तीन-चार (tiin-chaar, 'three or four') used when giving an approximate quantity, much like English speakers say 'a few.' It also appears in the idiom तीन-तेरह होना (tiin-terah honaa, 'to scatter/disperse in confusion'), a nice example of how numbers get pressed into idiomatic service in Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) तीन (tiin) does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- unlike एक (ek) or दो (do) in some contexts, it stays invariant: तीन लड़के (tiin ladke, 'three boys'), तीन लड़कियाँ (tiin ladkiyaan, 'three girls'). 2) Learners sometimes confuse तीन (tiin, 'three') with तीस (tiis, 'thirty') because of the similar spelling and sound -- listen carefully for the long ी versus the स ending.
