पचास (pachaas)

numeral The number fifty (pachaas) -- a single-sense numeral used for counting and quantity.
Sense 1 : fifty
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear पचास (pachaas) constantly in markets and while bargaining -- prices, ages, and quantities in daily Delhi-NCR life are full of round numbers like this one. It also shows up in the common phrase पचास बार (pachaas baar, 'fifty times'), used loosely to mean 'so many times, over and over,' much like English 'a hundred times' -- a small window into how Hindi speakers use round numbers hyperbolically rather than literally.
Common Mistakes: 1) पचास (pachaas) is invariant -- it does not change form regardless of the gender or number of the noun it counts, unlike ordinal or fractional number words. 2) Learners sometimes try to decline it like an adjective (adding -ई or -े); it stays fixed: पचास रुपये (pachaas rupye, 'fifty rupees'), पचास किताबें (pachaas kitaaben, 'fifty books').
