फ़्रांसीसी (fraansiisii)

adjective/noun A loanword-derived adjective/noun referring to France: 'French' (the language, the nationality/people, or a French person). Related to फ़्रांस (fraans, 'France').
Sense 1 : French (relating to France)
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word is built the same way as many other nationality adjectives in Hindi: take the country name (फ़्रांस, fraans) and add the -ई/-ईसी pattern, similar to अंग्रेज़ी (angrezii) from अंग्रेज़ (angrez). In everyday Delhi conversation you will hear it most often attached to food, fashion, or language -- फ़्रांसीसी खाना (fraansiisii khaanaa, 'French food') or फ़्रांसीसी भाषा (fraansiisii bhaashaa, 'French language').
Common Mistakes: 1) फ़्रांसीसी (fraansiisii) does not inflect for gender or number like a typical -आ adjective (e.g. कड़ा kadaa) -- it stays the same before masculine and feminine nouns: फ़्रांसीसी लड़का (fraansiisii ladkaa, 'French boy') and फ़्रांसीसी लड़की (fraansiisii ladkii, 'French girl') both use the identical form. 2) Learners sometimes drop the nuqta and write फ्रांसीसी (phraansiisii) -- both are seen, but the dotted स (z-style nuqta on the initial sound) reflects the more careful pronunciation closer to the French original.

(mujhe fraansiisii khaanaa bahut pasand hai।)

Sense 2 : a French person / the French language
noun, masculine/feminine As a noun this word can refer either to a person of French nationality or, especially when unmarked by a following noun, to the French language itself -- context decides which.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As with many nationality words in Hindi, the same form covers 'a French person' and 'the French language' -- you'll see this pattern repeat with जापानी (jaapaanii, 'Japanese person/language') and चीनी (chiinii, 'Chinese person/language', though चीनी also separately means 'sugar').
Common Mistakes: 1) When meaning 'the French language,' फ़्रांसीसी (fraansiisii) is used on its own, without needing भाषा (bhaashaa, 'language') attached, much like हिंदी (hindii) or अंग्रेज़ी (angrezii) -- learners sometimes over-add भाषा unnecessarily, though it is not wrong to include it.
