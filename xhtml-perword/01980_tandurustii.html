तंदुरुस्ती (tandurustii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning physical health or fitness, most often heard in everyday well-wishing and casual health talk.
Sense 1 : physical health, good health, fitness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सेहत (sehat), स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy, formal), निरोगता (nirogtaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries a homely, affectionate warmth -- it is what a grandmother asks about, not what a doctor writes on a chart (that would be स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy)). It comes from तंदुरुस्त (tandurust, 'healthy, fit'), a Persian-derived adjective still very much alive in everyday Hindi-Urdu.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy) in every context because it is the 'textbook' word, but तंदुरुस्ती (tandurustii) is what actually comes out in warm, casual speech, like asking after an elder's well-being. 2) It pairs naturally with होना (honaa) or रहना (rahnaa) -- अच्छी तंदुरुस्ती है (achchhii tandurustii hai, '[his/her] health is good') -- rather than verbs of possession.
Sample Sentences:
तंदुरुस्ती ही सबसे बड़ी दौलत है। Health is the biggest wealth.
(tandurustii hii sabse badii daulat hai।)
