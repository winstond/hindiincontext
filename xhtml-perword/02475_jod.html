जोड़ (jod)

noun Primarily a noun meaning 'joint/junction' (as in a physical joining point) or 'sum/addition' in math; also used figuratively for a 'pair' or 'match' between two people or things. Related to the verb जोड़ना (jodnaa, 'to join, add').
Sense 1 : the physical point where two things join; a joint
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जोड़ाई (jodaaii, less common), संधि (sandhi, formal/technical, also used for grammatical joins)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word at the doctor's when someone complains of घुटनों के जोड़ों में दर्द (ghutnon ke jodon men dard, 'pain in the knee joints') -- a very common complaint among elders in Delhi winters. It is also used for the joint in a pipe, a piece of furniture, or any two parts fitted together.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with हड्डी का जोड़ (haddii kaa jod, 'bone joint') as if जोड़ (jod) always means a body joint -- it is the general word for any junction point, not limited to anatomy.

Sense 2 : the sum or total in addition
noun, masculine Often appears in the phrase जोड़ लगाना (jod lagaanaa, 'to do the addition/tally up').
Synonyms: योग (yog, formal/mathematical), कुल (kul, total, broader use)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian schoolkid learns in primary math class alongside घटाव (ghataav, subtraction), गुणा (gunaa, multiplication), and भाग (bhaag, division) -- जोड़, घटाव, गुणा, भाग (jod, ghataav, gunaa, bhaag, 'addition, subtraction, multiplication, division') is a set phrase drilled into every student.
Common Mistakes: 1) जोड़ (jod) specifically means 'addition/sum,' while घटाव (ghataav) is 'subtraction' -- learners sometimes mix these up when talking about basic math operations.

Sense 3 : a matching pair or well-suited match, especially between two people
noun, masculine Common in the set phrase जोड़ी जमना (jodii jamnaa, 'to make a good pair') -- though that specific idiom uses जोड़ी (jodii) rather than जोड़ (jod) itself; जोड़ appears in phrases like क्या जोड़ है (kyaa jod hai, 'what a pair/match').
Synonyms: जोड़ी (jodii, more common for 'pair'), युगल (yugal, literary/formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You might hear an elder remark क्या जोड़ है (kyaa jod hai, 'what a pair/match [they make]') admiringly about a newly married couple -- it carries a sense of destined or fitting compatibility, much like 'made for each other' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) जोड़ (jod) and जोड़ी (jodii) overlap in meaning ('pair, match') but जोड़ी (jodii) is far more frequent in everyday speech for describing a couple; using जोड़ (jod) alone in this sense can sound slightly literary or old-fashioned to some ears.
