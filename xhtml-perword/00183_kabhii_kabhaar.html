कभी कभार (kabhii kabhaar)

adverb An adverb meaning 'once in a while, occasionally' -- a folksy-sounding echo pair built on कभी (kabhii, 'sometimes').
Sense 1 : occasionally, once in a while
adverb
Synonyms: कभी कभी (kabhii kabhii), इधर उधर (idhar udhar, rare in this sense), यदा कदा (yadaa kadaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the kind of phrase you hear when someone is being a little self-deprecating: मैं कभी कभार सिगरेट पी लेता हूँ (main kabhii kabhaar sigret pii letaa hoon, 'I smoke every once in a while') sounds gentler and more confessional than a flat statement of habit. The repetition-with-variation pattern (कभी...कभार) is a common flavor in Hindi adverbs, similar in spirit to इधर उधर (idhar udhar) or आगे पीछे (aage piichhe).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कभी कभी (kabhii kabhii) by default -- both are fine and mean almost the same thing, but कभी कभार (kabhii kabhaar) has a slightly more colloquial, chatty feel, like something you'd say about a habit you're mildly confessing to. 2) It describes frequency over time (how often something happens), not a single occasion -- it can't replace कभी (kabhii) meaning 'ever' or 'at some point.'
Sample Sentences:
मैं कभी कभार सिगरेट पी लेता हूँ। I smoke every once in a while.
(main kabhii kabhaar sigret pii letaa hoon।)
