आतंकवाद (aatankvaad)

noun A modern political/news-register noun meaning 'terrorism' -- built from आतंक (aatank, 'terror, dread') plus the suffix -वाद (-vaad, 'ism, doctrine'). One sense, used mainly in news and formal discussion.
Sense 1 : terrorism
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word lives almost entirely in newspapers, TV news, and political speeches -- you will rarely hear it in casual home conversation the way you'd hear आतंक (aatank) itself, which can describe everyday fear or dread ('घर में आतंक छा गया' ghar men aatank chhaa gayaa, 'panic spread through the house'). Recognizing the -वाद (-vaad) suffix pattern is useful beyond this word: it turns concrete or abstract nouns into 'isms' across Hindi's political vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse आतंकवाद (aatankvaad, 'terrorism' -- the ideology/phenomenon) with आतंकवादी (aatankvaadii, 'terrorist' -- the person); the -वादी (-vaadii) ending marks the practitioner/adherent, a pattern shared by many other -वाद (-vaad) nouns like साम्यवाद (saamyvaad, 'communism') / साम्यवादी (saamyvaadii, 'communist'). 2) It is always masculine and essentially never used in the plural, since it names an abstract phenomenon rather than a countable thing.
