उपेक्षा (upekshaa)

noun A formal, Sanskrit-derived noun meaning neglect or disregard -- the act of treating someone or something as unworthy of attention.
Sense 1 : neglect, disregard
noun, feminine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) as उपेक्षा करना (upekshaa karnaa, 'to neglect/disregard') -- the thing neglected is marked with की (kii), as in उसकी उपेक्षा करना (uskii upekshaa karnaa, 'to neglect him/her').
Synonyms: अनदेखी (andekhii), लापरवाही (laaparvaahii, focuses on carelessness rather than deliberate disregard), तिरस्कार (tiraskaar, stronger, closer to scorn)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up often in serious contexts -- a parent's उपेक्षा (upekshaa) of a child, a government's उपेक्षा (upekshaa) of a region or issue, or a partner feeling emotionally neglected. It carries a note of quiet hurt or injustice, more editorial-page than everyday chit-chat, so you will hear it more in news, essays, and heartfelt conversation than in casual banter.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लापरवाही (laaparvaahii) when they mean उपेक्षा (upekshaa) -- लापरवाही (laaparvaahii) is about carelessness or negligence in doing a task, while उपेक्षा (upekshaa) is about willfully ignoring or looking down on a person or issue. 2) The verb pairing is करना (karnaa), not होना (honaa) -- you 'do' neglect of something, you don't 'become' neglect.
