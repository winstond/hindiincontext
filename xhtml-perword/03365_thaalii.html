थाली (thaalii)

noun Primarily the metal or steel plate used for eating (Meaning 1), and by extension the whole meal served on it, as in a fixed-price restaurant thali (Meaning 2).
Sense 1 : eating plate
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्लेट (plet)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian homes the थाली (thaalii) is stainless steel, sometimes brass in older or ceremonial settings, and is used alongside small बटलोई (batloii)-style bowls (कटोरी, katorii) for curries and dal. Washing and putting away the थाली (thaalii) after a meal is such a routine household chore that it comes up constantly in daily conversation about chores.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for प्लेट (plet) by default; थाली (thaalii) specifically means the large round metal/steel plate traditional in Indian homes, not just any plate -- a small side plate or saucer is still called प्लेट (plet).

Sense 2 : full-meal set/platter (as served in restaurants)
noun, feminine Used as a countable menu item, e.g. एक थाली (ek thaalii, 'one thali/meal set') or शाकाहारी थाली (shaakaahaarii thaalii, 'vegetarian thali').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Restaurants across Delhi-NCR advertise a 'थाली' (thaalii) as a fixed-price meal with dal, sabzi, roti, rice, and a sweet, all served together -- the word has traveled into English-language Indian restaurant menus worldwide with this exact meaning.
