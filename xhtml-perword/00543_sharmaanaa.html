शरमाना (sharmaanaa)

verb One core meaning here: to feel or show shyness/embarrassment, often with a blush. Closely related to the noun शर्म (sharm, 'shame/shyness').
Sense 1 : to feel shy, to blush, to be bashful
verb, intransitive Intransitive; often used with से (se) to mark the cause or person one is shy in front of, e.g. लोगों से शरमाना (logon se sharmaanaa, 'to be shy in front of people').
Synonyms: शर्माना (sharmaanaa, variant spelling), झिझकना (jhijhaknaa, to hesitate out of shyness), सकुचाना (sakuchaanaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word for that classic blush-and-look-away moment -- a new bahu (bahoo, daughter-in-law) being teased by relatives, or a kid shrinking behind a parent's leg when a guest asks their name, is described as शरमा रही है (sharmaa rahii hai, 'is being shy'). You will also spell it शर्माना (sharmaanaa) with the halant-र् -- both are common and completely interchangeable in speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for डरना (darnaa, 'to fear') when they mean shy embarrassment -- शरमाना (sharmaanaa) is about bashfulness or blushing, not fear. 2) The cause of shyness is marked with से (se), not पर (par) or को (ko) -- सबके सामने शरमाना (sabke saamne sharmaanaa, 'to feel shy in front of everyone') is more natural than trying to mark the crowd with को (ko).

(naii bahoo sabko dekhkar sharmaa rahii hai।)
