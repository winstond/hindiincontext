कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin)

pronoun/interjection A combination of कुछ (kuchh, 'something/anything') and नहीं (nahiin, 'not') that means 'nothing'; also doubles as a dismissive reply meaning 'it's nothing' or 'never mind.'
Sense 1 : nothing (negative quantity/thing)
pronoun Functions as the negative counterpart of कुछ (kuchh) used alone -- कुछ (kuchh) means 'something/anything' and needs a separate नहीं (nahiin) in the sentence to become negative; कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin) is that pairing fixed together as one answer or object.
Synonyms: शून्य (shoony, literary/formal, 'zero, nil')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly as a short, complete answer: कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin, 'nothing') on its own, said with a shrug, closes off a question like क्या हुआ? (kyaa huaa?, 'what happened?') -- it is one of the most common two-word answers in everyday Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मुझे कुछ नहीं चाहिए (mujhe kuchh nahiin chaahie, 'I don't want anything') but forget that कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin) already carries the negation, so adding a second नहीं (nahiin) later in the sentence is wrong and creates a double negative. 2) As an object, it behaves like कुछ (kuchh) and does not trigger ने-agreement changes on its own -- the verb still agrees per the usual transitive/intransitive rules of the main verb, not because of this word.
Sample Sentences:

(mere paas kuchh nahiin hai।)

Sense 2 : dismissive reply: 'it's nothing', 'never mind', 'don't worry about it'
interjection
Synonyms: कोई बात नहीं (koii baat nahiin, 'no problem'), छोड़ो (chhodo, colloquial, 'forget it')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic non-answer in Hindi conversation -- said with a certain tone, कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin) politely shuts a topic down, much like English 'oh, nothing' when someone catches you sighing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate this too literally as 'nothing' when a native speaker uses it purely as a social deflection -- e.g. when asked तुम उदास क्यों हो? (tum udaas kyon ho?, 'why are you sad?'), the reply कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin) often really means 'I don't want to talk about it' rather than a literal denial.

(kyaa huaa? -- kuchh nahiin, bas thakaan hai।)
