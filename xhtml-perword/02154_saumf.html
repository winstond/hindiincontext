सौंफ़ (saumf)

noun A single-sense word: सौंफ़ (saumf) is fennel seeds, the small greenish seeds used both as a spice and as a post-meal digestive/mouth-freshener.
Sense 1 : fennel seeds
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will find little bowls of सौंफ़ (saumf) -- often mixed with मिश्री (mishrii, sugar crystals) or sugar-coated as मुखवास (mukhvaas) -- kept by the till at almost every Delhi dhaba and restaurant, meant to be chewed after a meal to freshen the breath and aid digestion. It also goes into the spice mix for chai and into dishes like पंजाबी छोले (panjaabii chhole) for its mild, sweet aroma.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सौंफ़ (saumf) with सोंठ (sonth, dry ginger) or जीरा (jiiraa, cumin) since all three are common spice-box items -- सौंफ़ (saumf) is specifically the sweetish, greenish fennel seed, easily recognized by its aniseed-like smell. 2) The nuqta on the फ़ (f) is often dropped in casual writing as सौंफ (saumph), but the pronunciation stays the same.
