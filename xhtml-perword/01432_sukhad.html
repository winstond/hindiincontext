सुखद (sukhad)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'pleasant' or 'pleasing' -- used for experiences, feelings, and occasions rather than for people's personalities.
Sense 1 : pleasant, agreeable (of an experience, feeling, or occasion)
adjective
Synonyms: सुहावना (suhaavnaa), आनंददायक (aananddaayak, slightly more formal), मनभावन (manbhaavan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in slightly formal or written contexts -- weather reports (सुखद मौसम (sukhad mausam, 'pleasant weather')), travel talk (सुखद यात्रा (sukhad yaatraa, 'pleasant journey')), or news anchors signing off with शुभकामनाएँ (shubhkaamnaaen). It sits a notch above everyday अच्छा (achchhaa) in register -- the word a host uses to wish you a nice stay, not what you'd casually say about your lunch.
Common Mistakes: 1) सुखद (sukhad) describes experiences, weather, news, journeys, surprises -- not people's personalities; for a pleasant/nice person, learners should reach for अच्छा (achchhaa) or सुहाना (suhaanaa) type words instead. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with सुखी (sukhii, 'happy, content') -- सुखद (sukhad) describes what causes pleasure (an outside quality), while सुखी (sukhii) describes the state of the person feeling it.
Sample Sentences:
आपकी यात्रा सुखद रहे। May your journey be pleasant.
(aapkii yaatraa sukhad rahe।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
आज का मौसम बहुत सुखद है। Today's weather is very pleasant.
(aaj kaa mausam bahut sukhad hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
पहाड़ों पर बिताया गया वह हफ्ता बहुत सुखद था। That week spent in the mountains was very pleasant.
(pahaadon par bitaayaa gayaa vah haphtaa bahut sukhad thaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive
उम्मीद है आपका प्रवास सुखद रहेगा। I hope your stay will be pleasant.
(ummiid hai aapkaa pravaas sukhad rahegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
अगर मौसम इतना सुखद न होता, तो हम पिकनिक नहीं मनाते। If the weather hadn't been so pleasant, we wouldn't have celebrated with a picnic.
(agar mausam itnaa sukhad n hotaa, to ham piknik nahiin manaate।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
नए साल के इस सुखद अवसर पर हमारी शुभकामनाएँ स्वीकार करें। On this pleasant occasion of the new year, please accept our best wishes.
(nae saal ke is sukhad avsar par hamaarii shubhkaamnaaen sviikaar karen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
समाचार वाचिका ने सुखद यात्रा की शुभकामनाओं के साथ बुलेटिन समाप्त किया। The news anchor ended the bulletin with wishes for a pleasant journey.
(samaachaar vaachikaa ne sukhad yaatraa kii shubhkaamnaaon ke saath buletin samaapt kiyaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
शायद यह अनुभव इतना सुखद हो कि आप दोबारा आना चाहें। Perhaps this experience will be pleasant enough that you'd want to come again.
(shaayad yah anubhav itnaa sukhad ho ki aap dobaaraa aanaa chaahen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
जीवन के वे सुखद पल स्मृति में सदा बने रहते हैं। Those pleasant moments of life always remain in memory.
(jiivan ke ve sukhad pal smriti men sadaa bane rahte hain।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
सम्मेलन में सभी अतिथियों को सुखद अनुभव प्रदान करने का प्रयास किया जा रहा है। Efforts are being made to provide all the guests at the conference with a pleasant experience.
(sammelan men sabhii atithiyon ko sukhad anubhav pradaan karne kaa prayaas kiyaa jaa rahaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
