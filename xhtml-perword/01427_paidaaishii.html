पैदाइशी (paidaaishii)

adjective An adjective meaning 'born with, congenital, by birth' -- describes a trait, defect, or status someone has had since birth. Related to पैदा होना (paidaa honaa, 'to be born') and पैदाइश (paidaaish, 'birth').
Sense 1 : congenital, innate, by birth
adjective
Synonyms: जन्मजात (janmjaat, more formal/literary), जन्म से (janm se, phrase variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word most often in slightly informal or spoken contexts describing a person's character flaw or physical trait as something 'baked in' since birth -- पैदाइशी झूठा (paidaaishii jhoothaa, 'a born liar') is a classic example, often said half-jokingly about a friend or relative who just can't help exaggerating.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पैदाइशी (paidaaishii) with पैदा (paidaa) alone -- पैदा (paidaa) only works in the fixed phrase पैदा होना (paidaa honaa, 'to be born'), while पैदाइशी (paidaaishii) is the standalone descriptive adjective meaning 'congenital' and is used to modify a noun directly, e.g. पैदाइशी अंधा (paidaaishii andhaa, 'blind from birth').

वह पैदाइशी झूठा है, उसकी किसी बात पर यकीन मत करो। He's a born liar, don't believe a single thing he says.
(vah paidaaishii jhoothaa hai, uskii kisii baat par yakiin mat karo।)
