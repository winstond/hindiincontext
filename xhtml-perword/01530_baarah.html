बारह (baarah)

numeral The number twelve (baarah) -- a simple cardinal numeral with one core meaning.
Sense 1 : the number twelve
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numerals from eleven to ninety-nine, बारह (baarah) simply has to be memorized as its own word rather than built compositionally the way English does with 'twelve' from 'ten' and 'two'. You will meet it constantly in dates, ages, prices, and clock time (बारह बजे, baarah baje, 'twelve o'clock'), so it is well worth learning solidly early on.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बारह (baarah, 'twelve') with बीस (biis, 'twenty') or बारहवाँ (baarahvaan, 'twelfth') -- the ordinal is a separate word, not this one with a suffix tacked on casually. 2) In clock time, बारह बजे (baarah baje, 'at twelve o'clock') is the natural phrase -- learners sometimes forget the बजे (baje) and just say बारह (baarah) alone, which sounds incomplete.
Sample Sentences:

(abhii baarah baj rahe hain।)
