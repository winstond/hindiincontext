तना (tanaa)

noun/adjective Mainly a noun meaning the stem/trunk of a plant; also functions as a past-participle-derived adjective meaning 'stretched, taut' from the verb तनना (tannaa).
Sense 1 : stem, stalk, trunk (of a plant or tree)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तनु (tanu, literary), डंठल (danthal, used more for thinner stalks)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday Hindi, तना (tanaa) comes up in school biology lessons and gardening talk -- think of a teacher pointing at a plant diagram and saying जड़, तना, पत्ती (jad, tanaa, pattii, 'root, stem, leaf').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तना (tanaa, 'stem/trunk') with टहनी (tahnii, 'branch/twig') -- तना (tanaa) is the main upright stem, not a side branch.

Sense 2 : stretched, taut, tense (of a rope, muscle, or figuratively a face/mood)
adjective This is the perfective participle of तनना (tannaa, 'to stretch, become taut'), used adjectivally -- it inflects like a regular -आ adjective.
Synonyms: खिंचा हुआ (khinchaa huaa), कसा हुआ (kasaa huaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense often pairs with हुआ (huaa) as तना हुआ (tanaa huaa) -- you'll hear it for a tight rope (रस्सी तनी हुई है, rassii tanii huii hai, 'the rope is taut') or a tense atmosphere between people who are not on speaking terms.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often only learn तना (tanaa) as 'stem' and miss this adjectival use, e.g. उसका चेहरा तना हुआ था (uskaa chehraa tanaa huaa thaa, 'his face was tense/strained') -- here it describes mood, not a plant part.
Sample Sentences:
रस्सी तनी हुई है, इसे मत छेड़ो। The rope is taut, don't touch it.
(rassii tanii huii hai, ise mat chhedo।)
