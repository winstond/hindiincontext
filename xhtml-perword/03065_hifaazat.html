हिफ़ाज़त (hifaazat)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'protection, safekeeping, care' -- the state of being kept safe from harm, often used in the fixed phrase हिफ़ाज़त से (hifaazat se, 'safely, carefully').
Sense 1 : protection, safekeeping, care
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सुरक्षा (surakshaa), रक्षा (rakshaa), निगरानी (nigraanii, more like 'watch over, supervision')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes from Persian/Urdu and has a caring, almost parental warmth to it -- a mother telling a child अपनी हिफ़ाज़त करना (apnii hifaazat karnaa, 'take care of yourself') before they leave the house is a very common, tender scene in Delhi households. It sits alongside सुरक्षा (surakshaa) as a near-synonym, but सुरक्षा (surakshaa) leans more formal/institutional (police, security guards) while हिफ़ाज़त (hifaazat) leans personal and everyday.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सुरक्षा (surakshaa) by default; हिफ़ाज़त (hifaazat) is just as common in spoken Hindi and carries a slightly warmer, more personal tone, as if someone is looking after you rather than a system protecting you. 2) The common adverbial phrase हिफ़ाज़त से (hifaazat se, 'carefully, safely') is often used with रखना (rakhnaa) or चलना (chalnaa) -- e.g. sामान हिफ़ाज़त से रखो (saamaan hifaazat se rakho, 'keep the luggage safely') -- and learners sometimes forget the से (se) here, treating it like an adjective instead of an adverbial phrase.
