कट्टरपंथी (kattarpanthii)

noun/adjective A word from political and religious commentary meaning 'fundamentalist/extremist' -- it works both as an adjective describing a person, group, or belief, and as a noun for the person themselves.
Sense 1 : a fundamentalist or extremist person
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: कट्टरवादी (kattarvaadii), चरमपंथी (charampanthii, leans toward political/violent extremism), मौलवादी (maulvaadii, less common)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Hindi news and political debate -- newspapers routinely talk about धार्मिक कट्टरपंथी (dhaarmik kattarpanthii, 'religious fundamentalists') or कट्टरपंथी सोच (kattarpanthii soch, 'fundamentalist thinking'). It's built from कट्टर (kattar, 'staunch, hardcore') plus पंथ (panth, 'path, sect'), so it literally evokes someone rigidly walking one ideological path.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this loosely for anyone 'strict' or 'traditional' -- but कट्टरपंथी (kattarpanthii) carries a real edge of rigidity or extremism, usually in religious or ideological contexts, and is not a compliment. 2) Don't confuse with कट्टर (kattar) alone, which just means 'staunch/hardcore' and can be used more neutrally or even admiringly (कट्टर समर्थक (kattar samarthak, 'die-hard supporter')); कट्टरपंथी (kattarpanthii) specifically implies a 'path' (पंथ (panth)) of rigid belief.

Sense 2 : fundamentalist, extremist (describing beliefs, groups, ideology)
adjective
Synonyms: कट्टरवादी (kattarvaadii), चरमपंथी (charampanthii, leans toward political/violent extremism)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this paired with संगठन (sangthan, 'organization'), विचारधारा (vichaardhaaraa, 'ideology'), or गुट (gut, 'faction') in news coverage -- कट्टरपंथी संगठन (kattarpanthii sangthan, 'fundamentalist organization') is a very standard collocation.
