ख़ुशक़िस्मती (khushqismtii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'good luck, good fortune' -- built from ख़ुश (khush, 'happy') plus क़िस्मत (qismat, 'fate') plus the abstract-noun suffix ई (ii).
Sense 1 : good luck, good fortune
noun, feminine Very commonly used in the adverbial phrase ख़ुशक़िस्मती से (khushqismtii se, 'fortunately') to open a sentence.
Synonyms: क़िस्मत (qismat), भाग्य (bhaagy), सौभाग्य (saubhaagy, formal), नसीब (nasiib)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word is a nice example of Hindi-Urdu blending: ख़ुश (khush) and क़िस्मत (qismat) are both Persian-derived, glued together with the native Hindi abstract suffix ई (ii) -- you'll see the same pattern in बदक़िस्मती (badqismtii, 'bad luck'), its natural opposite.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the से (se) when trying to say 'fortunately' -- ख़ुशक़िस्मती से (khushqismtii se) is the fixed sentence-opener, while ख़ुशक़िस्मती (khushqismtii) alone is just the noun 'good luck', not the adverb. 2) The nuqta on both ख़ and क़ is often dropped in casual writing (खुशकिस्मती), which is common and acceptable in informal contexts but not the standard spelling.

यह मेरी ख़ुशक़िस्मती है कि मुझे ऐसे दोस्त मिले। It's my good fortune that I got such friends.
(yah merii khushqismtii hai ki mujhe aise dost mile।)
