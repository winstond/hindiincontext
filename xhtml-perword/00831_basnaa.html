बसना (basnaa)

verb Basic meaning is 'to settle, take up residence' -- of people, towns, or communities coming to inhabit a place. A figurative sense extends this to feelings or images 'settling' in the heart or eyes. Related to the causative बसाना (basaanaa, 'to settle/found', processed separately).
Sense 1 : to settle, take up residence (of people/places)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: रहना (rahnaa, to live/reside), आबाद होना (aabaad honaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word in descriptions of migration and city life, like दिल्ली में बसना (dillii men basnaa, 'to settle in Delhi') -- a very common thing to say about people who move to the capital for work. It also appears often in the passive-sounding but grammatically active phrase शहर बसा (shahar basaa, 'the city was founded/came to be settled'), used when talking about how old towns came into being.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बसना (basnaa) with रहना (rahnaa): बसना (basnaa) implies making a place one's home or a place becoming inhabited/founded, while रहना (rahnaa) is the neutral verb for simply living or staying somewhere. 2) बसना (basnaa) is intransitive; the transitive counterpart 'to settle someone/something' is बसाना (basaanaa), a separate word -- do not use बसना (basnaa) with an object.

(bahut log kaam ke lie dillii men basnaa chaahte hain।)

Sense 2 : to dwell, be lodged (of feelings, images, memories -- figurative)
verb, intransitive Common frame: [thing] दिल/आँखों में बसना (dil/aankhon men basnaa) -- 'to dwell in the heart/eyes,' used for a person or image someone can't stop thinking about.
Synonyms: समाना (samaanaa, to be contained/fit within)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the Bollywood-song sense: तू दिल में बसा है (too dil men basaa hai, 'you are settled in my heart') or किसी की सूरत आँखों में बसना (kisii kii soorat aankhon men basnaa, 'someone's face to be lodged in one's eyes') are classic romantic images. Hindi treats the heart or eyes as a place where a memory or beloved comes to live, much like a person settling in a city -- the same verb links both ideas.
