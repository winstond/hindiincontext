घबराना (ghabraanaa)

verb One core meaning cluster: to feel nervous, flustered, or panicked -- covers everything from mild jitters before an exam to genuine panic in a crisis.
Sense 1 : to become nervous, flustered, or panicked
verb, intransitive Almost always used intransitively with the person who feels the emotion as subject; commonly heard in the negative imperative घबराओ मत (ghabraao mat, 'don't panic') as a reassuring phrase.
Synonyms: डरना (darnaa, to be scared -- narrower, fear-specific), बेचैन होना (bechain honaa, to become restless/uneasy), परेशान होना (pareshaan honaa, to become troubled/worried)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word an elder or doctor uses to calm you down -- घबराइए नहीं, सब ठीक हो जायेगा (ghabraaie nahiin, sab thiik ho jaayegaa, 'don't worry, everything will be fine') has that classic reassuring, almost parental tone you hear constantly in Indian households and clinics alike. It captures a very physical, whole-body sense of unease -- racing heart, sweaty palms -- more than abstract worry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for डरना (darnaa) when they mean घबराना (ghabraanaa) -- डरना is about fear of something specific, while घबराना is more about losing composure or feeling flustered, even without a clear threat. 2) The comforting phrase घबराओ मत (ghabraao mat, 'don't worry, don't panic') or घबराइए नहीं (ghabraaie nahiin) is a fixed, very common way to reassure someone -- learners often construct clunkier equivalents instead of using this natural set phrase.
Sample Sentences:
घबराइए नहीं, सब ठीक हो जायेगा। Don't panic, everything will be fine.
(ghabraaie nahiin, sab thiik ho jaayegaa।)
