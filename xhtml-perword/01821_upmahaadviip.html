उपमहाद्वीप (upmahaadviip)

noun A single-sense geographic/political noun meaning 'subcontinent', most often heard as भारतीय उपमहाद्वीप (bhaartiiy upmahaadviip, the Indian subcontinent). Built from the prefix उप- (up-, 'sub-, near') plus महाद्वीप (mahaadviip, 'continent').
Sense 1 : subcontinent
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in the phrase भारतीय उपमहाद्वीप (bhaartiiy upmahaadviip, 'Indian subcontinent'), which refers to India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, and Sri Lanka together -- it is the standard term in news broadcasts and school geography books when discussing the whole region's shared history, climate, or cricket rivalries.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this word for any large region; in practice it is reserved for the specific geopolitical sense of 'subcontinent' -- almost always South Asia -- not a generic 'big area.' 2) It is a formal/textbook-register word: in everyday conversation people usually just say भारत (bhaarat, India) or say which countries they mean, and reach for उपमहाद्वीप (upmahaadviip) mainly in news, history, or geography contexts.
