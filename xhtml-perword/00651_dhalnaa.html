ढलना (dhalnaa)

verb Primarily a physical-process verb: to be cast/molded (metal, idols), and by extension to slope, decline, or gradually turn into something -- covering the sun setting, the evening falling, and one's age or mood shifting. Causative is ढालना (dhaalnaa).
Sense 1 : for the sun/day/light to decline, for evening to fall
verb, intransitive Common in the fixed phrase दिन ढलना (din dhalnaa, 'for the day to draw to a close') and सूरज ढलना (sooraj dhalnaa, 'for the sun to set').
Synonyms: डूबना (doobnaa, to sink, used for sun setting)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ढलती शाम (dhaltii shaam, 'the fading evening') is a favorite image in Hindi film lyrics and shayari (shaayrii) for that golden, slightly melancholic hour -- it evokes the same mood as 'dusk' or 'twilight' in English romantic poetry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to डूबना (doobnaa) for any sunset image, but ढलना (dhalnaa) is the word for time itself gently declining -- ढलती शाम (dhaltii shaam, 'the fading evening') carries a soft, literary feel that डूबना (doobnaa) doesn't.

Sense 2 : to gradually turn into, to age, to pass (of time/life/mood)
verb, intransitive Often used with ढलती उम्र (dhaltii umr, 'advancing/declining age') and ढलना में (dhalnaa men) constructions describing one state slowly becoming another.
Synonyms: बदलना (badalnaa, to change), गुजरना (gujarnaa, to pass, of time)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ढलती उम्र में (dhaltii umr men, 'in one's declining years') is a gentle, respectful way Hindi speakers talk about old age -- similar in warmth to English 'in his twilight years,' and often paired with respect for a parent or grandparent's wisdom rather than any negative judgment.
Common Mistakes: 1) ढलती उम्र (dhaltii umr) means someone is getting older/past their prime -- it's not neutral like उम्र बढ़ना (umr badhnaa, 'age increasing'); it carries a wistful sense of decline that learners should not use carelessly about someone's age to their face.

Sense 3 : to be cast or molded (metal, statues)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बनना (bannaa, general 'to be made')
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Think of the murtikar (moortikaar, 'idol-maker') pouring molten metal into a mold before a big Ganesh Chaturthi or Durga Puja season -- मूर्ति ढल रही है (moorti dhal rahii hai, 'the idol is being cast') is the kind of line you'd hear near a workshop in Old Delhi or in Moradabad's brass-work lanes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बनना (bannaa) for anything 'made,' but ढलना (dhalnaa) is specific to shaping via a mold or cast (metal statues, bells, bangles) -- it carries the image of molten material taking a fixed form. 2) The causative ढालना (dhaalnaa, 'to cast/mold something') is the transitive partner; ढलना (dhalnaa) itself is intransitive and describes the material undergoing the process, not the person doing it.

Sense 4 : to slope or slant downward
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: झुकना (jhuknaa, to bend/tilt)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Used for a roof, a hillside, or a piece of land that slants down -- छत ढलवाँ है (chhat dhalvaan hai, 'the roof is sloped') uses the related adjective ढलवाँ (dhalvaan), showing how this physical-shape sense of ढलना (dhalnaa) branches into descriptive words too.
