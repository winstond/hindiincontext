ठप्प (thapp)

adjective An informal, expressive word describing something that has come to a complete standstill or stopped working -- used for machines, businesses, transport, or activities grinding to a halt.
Sense 1 : shut down, at a standstill, defunct
adjective Almost always used predicatively with होना (honaa) or पड़ना (padnaa), e.g. काम ठप्प है (kaam thapp hai, 'the work has come to a standstill'), rather than attributively before a noun.
Synonyms: बंद (band), निष्क्रिय (nishkriy, formal), खराब (kharaab, broken, narrower sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in news reports and everyday complaints -- ट्रैफिक ठप्प है (traiphik thapp hai, 'traffic is at a standstill') during a Delhi jam, or बिजली ठप्प है (bijlii thapp hai, 'the power is out') during a cut. It has a punchy, almost onomatopoeic feel -- the doubled प्प gives it a sense of something stopping dead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बंद (band) in every context, but ठप्प (thapp) specifically conveys a sudden or complete halt (a factory stops running, traffic grinds to a stop) rather than simply 'closed' (a shop being closed for the day). 2) It is invariant in this adjectival use -- it does not change form for gender or number of the noun it describes.

(aaj subah se traiphik thapp hai।)
