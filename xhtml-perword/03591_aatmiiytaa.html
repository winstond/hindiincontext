आत्मीयता (aatmiiytaa)

noun A warm, somewhat literary noun meaning the sense of closeness or intimacy between people -- a feeling of belonging to each other, as with family or dear friends.
Sense 1 : closeness, intimacy, warmth of relationship
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अपनापन (apnaapan), नज़दीकी (nazdiikii), स्नेह (sneh, warmth/affection, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries a slightly formal, writerly warmth -- you're more likely to hear it in a heartfelt speech, a eulogy, or a thoughtful essay than in casual chat, where अपनापन (apnaapan) is the everyday equivalent. It's built from आत्मा (aatmaa, 'soul/self') plus the abstract-noun suffix -ता (-taa), literally something like 'soul-connectedness' -- which explains why it feels deeper than ordinary friendliness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for 'friendship' as the translation, but आत्मीयता (aatmiiytaa) is the felt quality of closeness itself, not the relationship or bond as a social fact -- दोस्ती (dostii) is the friendship, आत्मीयता (aatmiiytaa) is how warmly connected you feel. 2) It is used with से (se) to describe how someone treats you ('उससे आत्मीयता से मिला', usse aatmiiytaa se milaa, 'met him warmly/affectionately') rather than as a direct object of common verbs like करना (karnaa).
