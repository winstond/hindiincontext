शुभ (shubh)

adjective/noun An auspiciousness word: an adjective meaning 'auspicious, good, favorable', used constantly as the first half of greeting compounds like शुभ प्रभात (shubh prabhaat, 'good morning').
Sense 1 : auspicious, favorable, good (of an omen, occasion, or quality)
adjective Almost always appears attributively, fused to the front of another noun as in शुभ यात्रा (shubh yaatraa, 'safe/auspicious journey') or शुभ अवसर (shubh avsar, 'auspicious occasion'), rather than predicated alone.
Synonyms: मंगल (mangal), कल्याणकारी (kalyaankaarii, formal), पुण्य (puny, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word every single day stitched onto greetings: शुभ प्रभात (shubh prabhaat, 'good morning'), शुभ रात्रि (shubh raatri, 'good night'), and शुभकामनाएं (shubhkaamnaaen, 'best wishes') on birthday cards and WhatsApp forwards. It carries a slightly more formal, written flavor than the everyday spoken greetings like गुड मॉर्निंग (gud morning), so families often use the English loan casually but switch to शुभ (shubh) compounds for greeting cards, invitations, and festival messages.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use शुभ (shubh) as a standalone greeting the way English speakers say 'good' -- but it almost never stands alone; it needs a following noun (प्रभात (prabhaat), रात्रि (raatri), दिन (din)) to form the actual greeting. 2) Since it is invariant, learners sometimes wrongly add gender endings like शुभा (shubhaa) by analogy with words like अच्छा (achchhaa) -- शुभ (shubh) itself never changes form.

Sense 2 : auspiciousness, that which is good/favorable (as a thing in itself)
noun, masculine Used in fixed phrases contrasting good and bad outcomes, e.g. शुभ-अशुभ (shubh-ashubh, 'auspicious and inauspicious [things]'), where it functions as a noun rather than modifying another word.
Synonyms: मंगल (mangal), कल्याण (kalyaan, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun use shows up in astrology and ritual contexts -- people consult a pandit (pandit) for a शुभ मुहूर्त (shubh muhoort, 'auspicious moment') before weddings, house-warmings, or launching a new business, treating शुभ (shubh) almost like a measurable quality of time itself.
