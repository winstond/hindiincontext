वैवाहिक (vaivaahik)

adjective A single-sense formal adjective meaning 'marital' or 'matrimonial' -- the adjective form built from विवाह (vivaah, marriage).
Sense 1 : relating to marriage
adjective
Synonyms: शादी-संबंधी (shaadii-sambandhii, everyday compound)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly on official forms and matrimonial ads -- वैवाहिक स्थिति (vaivaahik sthiti, 'marital status') is a standard phrase on Indian government and bank forms, and वैवाहिक जीवन (vaivaahik jiivan, 'married life') is the go-to phrase in advice columns and family conversations about a couple's life together. It sits at a noticeably more formal register than just saying शादी (shaadii) casually, similar to how English 'matrimonial' feels stiffer than 'married'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शादी (shaadii) to modify a noun directly (e.g. trying 'शादी जीवन' for 'marital life'), but the adjective वैवाहिक (vaivaahik) is what actually combines with nouns like जीवन (jiivan) or स्थिति (sthiti) in formal or written registers -- शादी (shaadii) itself stays a noun and needs a postposition or compound to modify anything. 2) वैवाहिक (vaivaahik) sounds bureaucratic/formal; in casual speech, describing married life or status, people often just use शादी (shaadii) plus context rather than this adjective.
