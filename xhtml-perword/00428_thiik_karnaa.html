ठीक करना (thiik karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the adjective/adverb ठीक (thiik, 'okay/correct') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); covers fixing/repairing things and correcting/settling matters -- a very common everyday verb.
Sense 1 : to fix, repair (a physical object)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: मरम्मत करना (marammat karnaa, for machines/structures), सुधारना (sudhaarnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi uncle whose cooler has stopped working in June and he'll say मिस्त्री को बुलाओ, कूलर ठीक करेगा (mistrii ko bulaao, koolar thiik karegaa, 'call the repairman, he'll fix the cooler') -- ठीक करना (thiik karnaa) is the go-to verb for calling in any repairman, from an electrician to a plumber.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मरम्मत करना (marammat karnaa) by default, but ठीक करना (thiik karnaa) is the everyday, all-purpose choice -- used for anything from a fan to a phone to a car -- while मरम्मत करना (marammat karnaa) sounds a bit more formal/technical. 2) The thing being fixed takes को (ko) only when it needs emphasis or is a pronoun (उसे ठीक करो, use thiik karo, 'fix it'); with a plain noun object it's often left unmarked (गाड़ी ठीक करो, gaadii thiik karo, 'fix the car').

Sense 2 : to correct, fix (a mistake, situation, or non-physical problem)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सुधारना (sudhaarnaa), सही करना (sahii karnaa, close synonym, often interchangeable)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A teacher marking homework says गलतियाँ ठीक करो (galtiyaan thiik karo, 'correct the mistakes'), and the same verb stretches easily to abstract messes: रिश्ता ठीक करना (rishtaa thiik karnaa, 'to mend a relationship') or हालात ठीक करना (haalaat thiik karnaa, 'to sort out the situation') -- Hindi treats fixing a broken fan and fixing a broken friendship with the same verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) सही करना (sahii karnaa) and ठीक करना (thiik karnaa) overlap heavily for correcting mistakes (spelling, homework, behavior); learners can use either, but ठीक करना (thiik karnaa) leans slightly more toward 'setting right/sorting out' a situation while सही करना (sahii karnaa) leans toward 'making correct/accurate'. 2) Don't confuse this with ठीक होना (thiik honaa) -- होना (honaa) is used when something becomes fine on its own (intransitive), while करना (karnaa) means someone actively fixes it.

Sense 3 : to fix, arrange, finalize (a time, price, deal, plan)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: तय करना (tay karnaa, very common alternative), निश्चित करना (nishchit karnaa, more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: At a Delhi bazaar you'll hear a shopkeeper and customer going back and forth until one says ठीक है, दाम ठीक कर लो (thiik hai, daam thiik kar lo, 'okay, let's settle the price') -- the same root ठीक (thiik) that means 'okay' shows up again, tying the phrase 'let's make it okay/settled' neatly together.
Common Mistakes: 1) For fixing a price, date, or appointment, तय करना (tay karnaa) is actually the more common everyday choice; ठीक करना (thiik karnaa) is used too but a learner will hear तय करना (tay karnaa) at least as often in this sense. 2) In bargaining contexts (haggling at a market), दाम ठीक करना (daam thiik karnaa, 'to settle the price') is a natural fixed phrase.
