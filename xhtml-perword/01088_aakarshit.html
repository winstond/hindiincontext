आकर्षित (aakarshit)

adjective/verb A borrowed-feeling but fully Hindi adjective meaning 'attracted/drawn,' almost always used with करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) as a compound expression rather than standing alone as a verb.
Sense 1 : attracted, drawn (toward something)
adjective Used with होना (honaa) to describe the state of being drawn to something: X, Y की ओर आकर्षित हुआ (X, Y kii or aakarshit huaa, 'X was attracted toward Y').
Synonyms: मोहित (mohit, literary), आकृष्ट (aakrisht, formal/literary variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word has a slightly bookish, considered feel -- in casual chat about fancying someone, people are just as likely to say पसंद आना (pasand aanaa) or दिल खिंचना (dil khinchnaa, 'the heart gets pulled'), which are more colloquial and body-centered ways of expressing the same pull.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use आकर्षित (aakarshit) as a standalone verb; it is an adjective and needs होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) to function in a sentence. 2) The target of attraction is usually marked with की ओर (kii or) or से (se), not को (ko) -- saying मैं उसे आकर्षित हुआ (main use aakarshit huaa) sounds off; better is मैं उसकी ओर आकर्षित हुआ (main uskii or aakarshit huaa).

(mujhe uskii baaton kii taraph aakarshit honaa achchhaa lagtaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to attract, draw in (someone/something)
verb, transitive Functions as a compound verb with करना (karnaa): X ने Y को आकर्षित किया (X ne Y ko aakarshit kiyaa, 'X attracted Y'). The object takes को (ko).
Synonyms: लुभाना (lubhaanaa), खींचना (khiinchnaa, informal, 'to pull/draw')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this construction constantly in advertising and tourism language -- पर्यटकों को आकर्षित करना (paryatkon ko aakarshit karnaa, 'to attract tourists') is a stock phrase on posters and in news reports about festivals, fairs, and monuments.
Common Mistakes: 1) Because आकर्षित (aakarshit) looks like a verb root, learners sometimes try to conjugate it directly (e.g., आकर्षा (aakarshaa)) -- this is wrong; only करना (karnaa) conjugates, आकर्षित (aakarshit) stays fixed.
