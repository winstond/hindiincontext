कब्ज़ (kabz)

noun A medical/everyday term meaning constipation. It has essentially one core meaning, used constantly in daily talk about health and digestion.
Sense 1 : constipation
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कोष्ठबद्धता (koshthbaddhtaa, formal/medical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of those words every Indian household discusses with surprising openness -- over chai, in the kitchen, with the family doctor -- because digestion and constipation are treated as a normal, even central, topic of daily wellbeing talk. You will constantly hear मुझे कब्ज़ है (mujhe kabz hai, 'I have constipation') or पेट में कब्ज़ है (pet men kabz hai, 'there is constipation in the stomach'), both using the dative/locative-subject pattern rather than an active 'I have' construction.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say कब्ज़ होना (kabz honaa) with a subject-agent construction like in English ('I have constipation'), but Hindi uses the dative-subject frame: मुझे कब्ज़ है (mujhe kabz hai, 'I have constipation'), literally 'to me constipation is.' 2) Note the nuqta: कब्ज़ (kabz) with the dot under ज is the correct everyday spelling; कब्ज (kabj) without it is also seen but less standard.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे कब्ज़ है। I have constipation.
(mujhe kabz hai।)
