उद्देश्य (uddeshy)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'purpose, aim, objective' -- the thing you are trying to achieve. It is a slightly formal/bookish word, common in writing, speeches, and planning-type conversation.
Sense 1 : purpose, aim, objective, goal
noun, masculine Common frame: X का उद्देश्य (kaa uddeshy) hai... 'the purpose of X is...' Also used with prepositional phrase के उद्देश्य से (ke uddeshy se, 'with the purpose/aim of').
Synonyms: लक्ष्य (lakshy, goal, more concrete/measurable), मकसद (maksad), इरादा (iraadaa, intention, closer to personal intent than an official aim)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in official and academic Hindi -- mission statements, essay questions ('इस निबंध का उद्देश्य...', 'this essay's purpose...'), and school civics classes talking about the उद्देश्य (uddeshy) of the Constitution's Preamble. It carries a slightly serious, institutional flavor compared to the everyday मकसद (maksad).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मतलब (matlab) to mean 'purpose', but मतलब (matlab) usually means 'meaning' or 'self-interest' in casual speech -- उद्देश्य (uddeshy) is the right word for a stated goal or objective. 2) In writing, उद्देश्य (uddeshy) sounds more formal than मकसद (maksad); using it in very casual chat can sound a bit stiff, like saying 'objective' instead of 'goal' in English.
