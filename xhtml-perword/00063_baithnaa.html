बैठना (baithnaa)

verb बैठना (baithnaa) is the basic verb 'to sit,' extending to boarding a vehicle, sitting for an exam, and settling/subsiding (voice, structures, business); as a compound-verb vector it also marks sudden, rash, or regrettable actions (V-stem + बैठना).
Sense 1 : to sit, to sit down
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: विराजना (viraajnaa, literary/honorific)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: When a guest arrives, the standard hospitality phrase is आइए, बैठिए (aaie, baithie, 'please come in, have a seat') -- said almost automatically in any Delhi home before offering tea.
Common Mistakes: 1) Use पर (par) with the surface one sits on -- कुर्सी पर बैठना (kursii par baithnaa, 'to sit on the chair') -- not में (men), which learners sometimes reach for by analogy with English 'in a chair.'

(aaie, baithie।)

Sense 2 : to board, to get into/onto a vehicle
verb, intransitive Takes में (men) with enclosed vehicles (गाड़ी में बैठना, gaadii men baithnaa, 'to get into the car') and पर (par) with vehicles one straddles or mounts (स्कूटर पर बैठना, skootar par baithnaa, 'to get on the scooter').
Synonyms: सवार होना (savaar honaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear auto and e-rickshaw drivers in Delhi call out ऑटो में बैठिए! (oto men baithie!, 'hop into the auto!') to hail passengers off the street.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to में (men) for every vehicle; बाइक/स्कूटर/साइकिल (baaik/skootar/saaikil) take पर (par) instead, since you sit astride rather than inside them.

Sense 5 : vector verb marking a sudden, rash, or regrettable action (compound verb: V-stem + बैठना)
verb Attaches to the stem of another verb to add a flavor of suddenness, rashness, or regret, with reduced agency on the subject's part: मार बैठना (maar baithnaa, 'to suddenly hit/attack'), कह बैठना (kah baithnaa, 'to blurt out'), कर बैठना (kar baithnaa, 'to end up doing (something regrettable)'), लड़ बैठना (lad baithnaa, 'to end up fighting'), उठ बैठना (uth baithnaa, 'to suddenly sit/get up' -- here बैठना is purely grammatical, not literal sitting).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This vector use is one of the workhorses of Hindi compound verbs, coloring an action with impulsiveness or regret; लड़ बैठना (lad baithnaa) is often used for a recurring trait, as in वह सबसे लड़ बैठता है (vah sabse lad baithtaa hai, 'he ends up fighting with everybody').
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't read बैठना literally in these compounds -- मार बैठना (maar baithnaa) means 'to suddenly hit/attack,' not anything about sitting. 2) The vector carries a sense of the subject not fully controlling the action -- कह बैठना (kah baithnaa) implies something slipped out, not a deliberate statement. 3) कर बैठना (kar baithnaa) shows up in the classic regret exclamation अरे! यह मैंने क्या कर बैठा! (are! yah mainne kyaa kar baithaa!, 'oh no, what have I done!').
Sample Sentences:
वह सबसे लड़ बैठता है। He ends up fighting with everybody.
