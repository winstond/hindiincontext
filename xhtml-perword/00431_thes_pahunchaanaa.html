ठेस पहुँचाना (thes pahunchaanaa)

verb A compound expression built from the noun ठेस (thes, 'hurt/injury') plus पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa, 'to cause to reach'), used almost entirely in the figurative sense of hurting someone's feelings.
Sense 1 : to hurt someone's feelings, to wound emotionally
verb, transitive The person affected is marked with को (ko): दिल को ठेस पहुँचाना (dil ko thes pahunchaanaa, 'to hurt the heart') or सीधे किसी को ठेस पहुँचाना (kisii ko thes pahunchaanaa, 'to hurt someone'). ठेस (thes) itself stays uninflected here as the object of पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa).
Synonyms: दिल दुखाना (dil dukhaanaa), दुख देना (dukh denaa), आहत करना (aahat karnaa, more formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase captures a very Hindi way of talking about emotional pain as something physical that 'reaches' a person, almost like a small blow landing on the heart or mind (दिल (dil) or मन (man)). You will hear it often when someone describes a harsh comment or a betrayal by family or friends -- ऐसी बात कहकर उसने मेरे दिल को ठेस पहुँचाई (usne mere dil ko thes pahunchaaii, 'by saying such a thing, he hurt my heart') is the classic register for this kind of complaint.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try दुख पहुँचाना (dukh pahunchaanaa) instead, which is understandable but less idiomatic than the fixed collocation ठेस पहुँचाना (thes pahunchaanaa) -- native speakers reach for this exact phrase by default. 2) The verb here is पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa), not पहुँचना (pahunchnaa) -- the causative form is essential to this idiom; पहुँचना (pahunchnaa) alone would mean 'to arrive,' not 'to cause.'

उसकी बात ने मेरे दिल को ठेस पहुँचाई। What he said hurt my heart.
(uskii baat ne mere dil ko thes pahunchaaii।)
