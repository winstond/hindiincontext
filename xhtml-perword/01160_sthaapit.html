स्थापित (sthaapit)

adjective A learned/formal adjective (and past-participle-like form) meaning 'established, set up, installed'; regularly paired with the verb करना (karnaa) as स्थापित करना (sthaapit karnaa, 'to establish') or with होना (honaa) as स्थापित होना (sthaapit honaa, 'to be/get established').
Sense 1 : established, set up (institutions, facts, positions)
adjective Almost always used with करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to form the action verb phrases 'to establish' / 'to become established'; on its own it modifies a noun like a normal adjective, e.g. स्थापित परंपरा (sthaapit parampraa, 'an established tradition').
Synonyms: स्थापित (kaayam, one-word variant sense overlap), कायम (kaayam), स्थिर (sthir, meaning scope: more 'stable' than 'founded')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a formal, slightly bookish word -- you will hear it in news, speeches, and official contexts (कंपनी की स्थापना 1990 में हुई, kampnii kii sthaapnaa 1990 men huii, 'the company was established in 1990' uses the related noun स्थापना, sthaapnaa) more than in casual chat, where लोग बनाना (banaanaa, 'to make/build') or शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa, 'to start') often do the everyday job instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use स्थापित (sthaapit) alone where they mean 'to establish' as an action -- remember it needs करना (karnaa) attached (स्थापित करना, sthaapit karnaa) to work as a verb phrase; the bare adjective just describes a noun. 2) Don't confuse with स्थिर (sthir, 'stable/steady') -- स्थापित is about founding or setting something up, not about it staying still.

Sense 2 : installed, fitted (of a physical object, statue, machine)
adjective Common in the context of murtis/idols and monuments: मंदिर में मूर्ति स्थापित की गई (mandir men moorti sthaapit kii gaii, 'the idol was installed in the temple').
Synonyms: लगाया गया (lagaayaa gayaa, meaning scope: more casual for machines/fixtures)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense often around festivals -- during Durga Puja or Ganesh Chaturthi, news and invitations describe मूर्ति की स्थापना (moorti kii sthaapnaa, 'installation of the idol'), so स्थापित carries a slightly ceremonial, respectful weight beyond plain 'installed.'
