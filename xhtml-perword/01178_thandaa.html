ठंडा (thandaa)

adjective/noun Basic adjective meaning 'cold/cool', with a common noun use for a cold drink and a figurative sense for calming down or losing intensity.
Sense 1 : cold, cool (temperature)
adjective
Synonyms: शीतल (shiital, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: At a Delhi dhaba you will constantly hear ठंडा पानी (thandaa paanii, 'cold water') offered alongside गरम चाय (garam chaay, 'hot tea') -- the ठंडा/गरम (thandaa/garam) contrast is one of the first survival pairs learners pick up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ठंडा (thandaa) for weather and forget it must agree with the noun it describes -- पानी ठंडा है (paanii thandaa hai, 'the water is cold') but हवा ठंडी है (havaa thandii hai, 'the wind is cold').

Sense 2 : a cold drink, something cold to drink
noun, masculine Used as a noun on its own, typically in casual requests like कुछ ठंडा लाओ (kuchh thandaa laao, 'bring something cold').
Synonyms: कोल्ड ड्रिंक (kold drink, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: At weddings and parties in Delhi-NCR, a host will often ask गरम या ठंडा? (garam yaa thandaa, 'something hot or something cold?') -- ठंडा (thandaa) here just means a cold beverage, no noun needed after it.

Sense 3 : calm, subdued, lacking intensity (figurative)
adjective Applies to mood, atmosphere, or a situation that has lost its heat, e.g. माहौल ठंडा हो गया (maahaul thandaa ho gayaa, 'the atmosphere cooled down/fizzled out').
Synonyms: शांत (shaant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Elders often tell an angry youngster दिमाग ठंडा करो (dimaag thandaa karo, 'cool your head') -- the same hot/cold metaphor for temper that English uses, which makes this sense easy for learners to grab onto.
Common Mistakes: 1) दिमाग ठंडा रखो (dimaag thandaa rakho, 'keep your head cool') is a set expression for staying calm -- learners sometimes translate 'cool-headed' with शांत (shaant) alone and miss this very common idiom.
Sample Sentences:
दिमाग ठंडा करो, गुस्सा करने से कुछ नहीं होगा। Cool your head, getting angry won't achieve anything.
(dimaag thandaa karo, gussaa karne se kuchh nahiin hogaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
वह बहुत ठंडे दिमाग का इंसान है, कभी जल्दी गुस्सा नहीं करता। He is a very cool-headed person, he never gets angry quickly.
(vah bahut thande dimaag kaa insaan hai, kabhii jaldii gussaa nahiin kartaa।)
