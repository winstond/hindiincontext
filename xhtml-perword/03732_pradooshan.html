प्रदूषण (pradooshan)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'pollution' -- used for environmental pollution (air, water, noise) in everyday and news Hindi.
Sense 1 : pollution, contamination of the environment
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गंदगी (gandgii, meaning scope: general dirtiness/filth, not specifically environmental pollution), दूषण (dooshan, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR this word is inescapable every winter -- you will hear it constantly in the context of वायु प्रदूषण (vaayu pradooshan, 'air pollution') and स्मॉग (smog, 'smog') on the news, in taxi conversations, and in school essays. It is a Sanskrit-derived formal-register word but has become completely ordinary and current through daily use, much like 'pollution' itself feels totally normal in English despite its Latin roots.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use प्रदूषण (pradooshan) as a verb by adding endings directly; it only works as a noun, paired with verbs like होना (honaa, 'to occur/be') or फैलाना (phailaanaa, 'to spread'), as in प्रदूषण फैलाना (pradooshan phailaanaa, 'to spread/cause pollution'). 2) The word is masculine, so learners often mismatch adjective/verb agreement, saying प्रदूषण बढ़ रही है (pradooshan badh rahii hai) instead of the correct प्रदूषण बढ़ रहा है (pradooshan badh rahaa hai, 'pollution is increasing').
Sample Sentences:
दिल्ली में सर्दियों में प्रदूषण बहुत बढ़ जाता है। Pollution increases a lot in Delhi during winter.
(dillii men sardiyon men pradooshan bahut badh jaataa hai।)
