तीसरा (tiisraa)

adjective/pronoun The ordinal number 'third'; it works as an inflecting adjective agreeing with the noun it modifies, and also stands alone as 'the third one'.
Sense 1 : third (ordinal number)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ordinals like तीसरा (tiisraa) show up constantly in daily life -- floors of a building, kids' class rankings in school, or someone's turn in a queue ('तीसरा नंबर' tiisraa nambar, 'third in line'). It pairs naturally with पहला (pahlaa, 'first') and दूसरा (doosraa, 'second') as the only three ordinals that are irregular in form; from चौथा (chauthaa, 'fourth') onward the pattern becomes more predictable.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that तीसरा (tiisraa) must agree in gender and number with the noun it describes, giving तीसरी (tiisrii) before a feminine noun like तीसरी मंज़िल (tiisrii manzil, 'third floor') rather than leaving it invariant. 2) Unlike English 'three' to 'third', there is no shared root visible on the surface -- तीन (tiin, 'three') and तीसरा (tiisraa) look quite different, so learners sometimes try to build the ordinal by adding a suffix to तीन (tiin) directly.
Sample Sentences:

(yah is imaarat kii tiisrii manzil hai।)

Sense 2 : the third one (standing in for a noun)
pronoun, masculine Used without an accompanying noun when the referent is clear from context, e.g. यह तीसरा है (yah tiisraa hai, 'this is the third one').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the adjective sense simply used on its own -- much like English speakers say 'I'll take the third one' without repeating the noun. Hindi does this very naturally with ordinals.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe tiisraa pasand hai।)
