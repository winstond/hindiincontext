मरुस्थल (marusthal)

noun A single-sense literary/geographical noun meaning 'desert'; the everyday word रेगिस्तान (registaan) is far more common in speech.
Sense 1 : desert (arid, sandy region)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रेगिस्तान (registaan), थार (thaar, specific desert name, not a true synonym)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word is the kind you meet in a school geography chapter about the थार मरुस्थल (thaar marusthal, 'the Thar Desert') rather than in a market conversation; for everyday talk about heat and sand, Delhi speakers reach for रेगिस्तान (registaan) instead. Knowing मरुस्थल (marusthal) still helps because it appears constantly in written Hindi, news, and formal descriptions of Rajasthan's landscape.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech, but रेगिस्तान (registaan) is what most people actually say at home or in conversation; मरुस्थल (marusthal) shows up mainly in textbooks, geography lessons, and formal writing. 2) The word is a Sanskrit compound (मरु (maru, 'desert/wasteland') + स्थल (sthal, 'place/land')), so its meaning is transparent once broken down, but learners sometimes mistake स्थल (sthal) here for its more common standalone sense of 'site' or 'spot'.
