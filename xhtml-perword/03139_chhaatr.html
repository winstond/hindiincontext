छात्र (chhaatr)

noun A formal, everyday word for a student, most often heard in school and academic contexts; the feminine counterpart छात्रा (chhaatraa) is a distinct word, not an inflection of this one.
Sense 1 : student (male, or male-included group)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विद्यार्थी (vidyaarthii), स्टूडेंट (stoodent, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see this word everywhere around Indian campuses -- छात्र संघ (chhaatr sangh, 'student union') and छात्रावास (chhaatraavaas, 'hostel') are the words plastered on hostel gates and college notice boards across Delhi University and beyond. In spoken conversation, especially among younger people, स्टूडेंट (stoodent) is just as common, but छात्र (chhaatr) is the word you'll find in news, official forms, and school contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use छात्र (chhaatr) for a young child in primary school; it's more natural for school-age and older students, especially in secondary school, college, and university contexts -- for a small child, बच्चा (bachchaa) or बच्चे (bachche) is more common. 2) छात्र (chhaatr) refers only to a male student or a mixed-gender group; for a specifically female student, use छात्रा (chhaatraa) instead -- it is not the same word with a feminine ending.
