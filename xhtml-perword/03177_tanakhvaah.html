तनख़्वाह (tanakhvaah)

noun A single-sense everyday word meaning 'salary' or 'wages' -- the money you get paid regularly for a job.
Sense 1 : salary, wages
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वेतन (vetan, formal), पगार (pagaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word everyone actually uses at the office or in the market when talking about pay day -- 'तनख़्वाह कब मिलेगी? (tanakhvaah kab milegii?, when will the salary come?)' is a classic end-of-month question in any Indian household. वेतन (vetan) is what you will see on an official payslip or in a government form, but तनख़्वाह (tanakhvaah) is what you will hear spoken out loud.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the nuqta and write/say तनख्वाह (tanakhvaah) -- this is a common casual spelling and is widely understood, but the ख़ (kh) with nuqta reflects the word's Persian origin and is the more careful form. 2) The verb usually paired with this noun is मिलना (milnaa) -- 'तनख़्वाह मिलती है (tanakhvaah miltii hai, the salary is received)' -- rather than पाना (paanaa), which sounds stiffer here.

(is mahiine tanakhvaah abhii tak nahiin aaii।)
