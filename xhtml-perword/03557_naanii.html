नानी (naanii)

noun Means 'maternal grandmother' (mother's mother); also lives inside the very common idiom नानी याद आना (naanii yaad aanaa, 'to remember one's grandmother' -- i.e. to be scared out of one's wits).
Sense 1 : maternal grandmother
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मातामह (maataamah, literary/rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most Indian families, a child's nानी (naanii) is a huge presence, especially during summer holidays when kids are packed off to nानी के घर (naanii ke ghar, 'grandmother's house') for weeks. This side of the family (mother's side, or nानी-side) tends to feel a bit more relaxed and indulgent compared to the father's side.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use नानी (naanii) and दादी (daadii) interchangeably as 'grandmother,' but Hindi always distinguishes the side: नानी (naanii) is specifically the mother's mother, दादी (daadii) the father's mother -- there is no single unmarked word for 'grandmother.'
Sample Sentences:

(merii naanii dillii men rahtii hain।)

Sense 2 : used only inside the idiom 'to be terrified'
noun, feminine Almost always appears as नानी याद आना (naanii yaad aanaa, 'to have one's grandmother come to mind' -- i.e. to be scared witless), with an experiencer marked by को: उसे नानी याद आ गई (use naanii yaad aa gaii, 'he got the fright of his life').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: The logic is almost comic: when someone is truly terrified, they supposedly 'remember their grandmother' -- the person who represents ultimate safety and comfort -- because that is who you'd cry out for in real danger. You will hear this idiom constantly in casual boasting or teasing, like ऐसा डराऊंगा कि नानी याद आ जाएगी (aisaa daraaoongaa ki naanii yaad aa jaaegii, 'I'll scare you so bad you'll remember your grandmother').
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense only exists inside the fixed idiom नानी याद आना (naanii yaad aanaa) -- you cannot use नानी (naanii) alone to mean 'fright,' and swapping in दादी (daadii) instead sounds wrong to native ears; the idiom is fixed to नानी (naanii).

(is philm kaa horar siin itnaa daraavnaa hai ki naanii yaad aa jaaegii।)
