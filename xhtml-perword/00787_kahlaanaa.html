कहलाना (kahlaanaa)

verb A causative-flavored verb built on कहना (kahnaa, 'to say'), meaning 'to be called/known as' -- it describes how something or someone is named or reputed, not the act of naming itself.
Sense 1 : to be called/known as (name, title, identity)
verb, intransitive Takes a complement noun/adjective directly, without a postposition: X कहलाता है (X kahlaataa hai) = 'X is called...'.
Synonyms: कहा जाना (kahaa jaanaa, passive-construction alternative)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries a nice cultural flavor of reputation and standing: कहलाना (kahlaanaa) is what you use when something has earned or acquired a name over time, like शेर को जंगल का राजा कहलाया जाता है (sher ko jangal kaa raajaa kahlaayaa jaataa hai, 'the lion is called the king of the forest'). It pairs naturally with words about titles, fame, and social identity -- मशहूर (mashahoor, 'famous'), प्रसिद्ध (prasiddh, 'renowned') -- and often shows up in proverbs and general statements about how the world labels people and things.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for the passive कहा जाना (kahaa jaanaa) by default, but कहलाना (kahlaanaa) is the more natural, idiomatic choice when talking about names, titles, or reputations -- e.g. दिल्ली भारत की राजधानी कहलाती है (dillii bhaarat kii raajdhaanii kahlaatii hai, 'Delhi is called the capital of India'). 2) Because it looks causative in form, learners sometimes expect an agent marked with से (se) doing the naming, but the sentence usually has no explicit namer at all -- the focus is on the label sticking to the subject, not on who assigned it.
Sample Sentences:
दिल्ली भारत की राजधानी कहलाती है। Delhi is called the capital of India.
(dillii bhaarat kii raajdhaanii kahlaatii hai।)
