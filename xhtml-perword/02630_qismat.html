क़िस्मत (qismat)

noun A single-sense noun meaning fate or fortune -- the force believed to determine what happens in a person's life, good or bad.
Sense 1 : fate, destiny, luck
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भाग्य (bhaagy), नसीब (nasiib), तक़दीर (taqdiir)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a resigned, philosophical weight in everyday Hindi -- when something good or bad happens beyond anyone's control, people shrug and say यह क़िस्मत है (yah qismat hai, 'this is fate'). It is a loanword from Arabic via Urdu/Persian, and its close cousin नसीब (nasiib) is used almost interchangeably, especially in Bollywood dialogue and everyday grumbling about bad luck.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for भाग्य (bhaagy) in every context, but क़िस्मत (qismat) is the word people actually reach for in daily speech -- 'क़िस्मत अच्छी है' (qismat achchhii hai, 'the luck is good') sounds natural, while भाग्य (bhaagy) can feel a touch bookish in casual talk. 2) The nuqta under the क़ (qismat) is often dropped in casual writing as किस्मत (kismat); both are heard and accepted in speech, so do not treat the version without the dot as wrong.
