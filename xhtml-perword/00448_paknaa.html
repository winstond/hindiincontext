पकना (paknaa)

verb The everyday intransitive verb for food or fruit 'cooking/ripening' on its own; the causative पकाना (pakaanaa, 'to cook') is a separate lemma for when someone actively cooks something.
Sense 1 : to cook (of food, intransitively)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb you hear in the kitchen when someone checks on the दाल (daal, 'lentils') or चावल (chaaval, 'rice') -- खाना पक रहा है (khaanaa pak rahaa hai, 'the food is cooking') describes the food as doing the cooking itself, with no cook mentioned, which is a very Hindi way of framing a process happening on its own.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पकाना (pakaanaa) even when there is no agent actively cooking -- if the sentence is about rice or dal cooking on the stove by itself, पकना (paknaa) is the natural intransitive choice, not its causative pair. 2) Because it is intransitive, this verb never takes ने (ne) marking on the subject in the past tense, unlike पकाना (pakaanaa).
Sample Sentences:
खाना पक रहा है, बस दस मिनट और लगेंगे। The food is cooking, it'll take just ten more minutes.
(khaanaa pak rahaa hai, bas das minat aur lagenge।)

Sense 2 : to ripen (of fruit)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Used constantly for fruit like आम (aam, 'mango') and केला (kelaa, 'banana') -- आम पक गया है (aam pak gayaa hai, 'the mango has ripened') is the kind of line you hear at a फल वाला (phal vaalaa, 'fruit seller')'s cart in season.

Sense 3 : to fester, become infected (of a wound/boil)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The same verb used for a घाव (ghaav, 'wound') or फोड़ा (phodaa, 'boil') that has festered -- Hindi treats an infected wound as 'ripening' just like fruit, which feels vivid once you notice the shared image of something swelling and coming to a head.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 4 : to become thoroughly experienced/hardened (figurative, of a person)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: पक्का होना (pakkaa honaa, related idiom)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this in phrases like यह खिलाड़ी अब पक चुका है (yah khilaadii ab pak chukaa hai, 'this player has now become seasoned') -- the same 'ripening' image is extended to a person who has been through enough experience to be fully matured or hardened, often with a slightly worldly or streetwise flavor.
Sample Sentences:
यह खिलाड़ी अब पक चुका है। This player has now become seasoned.
(yah khilaadii ab pak chukaa hai।)
