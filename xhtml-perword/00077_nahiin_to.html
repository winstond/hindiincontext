नहीं तो (nahiin to)

conjunction/particle A fixed connective meaning 'otherwise' or 'or else' -- literally 'not then' -- used to warn of a consequence if a condition isn't met. Related to नहीं (nahiin, 'no/not') and तो (to, 'then'), but functions as its own frozen unit.
Sense 1 : otherwise / or else (consequence warning)
conjunction Joins two clauses where the second states what happens if the first (often an implicit command or requirement) isn't followed: जल्दी करो, नहीं तो बस छूट जाएगी (jaldii karo, nahiin to bas chhoot jaaegii, 'hurry up, otherwise the bus will be missed').
Synonyms: वरना (varnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly from parents, teachers, and shopkeepers alike -- it's the classic shape of a Hindi warning: do X, नहीं तो (nahiin to) Y (bad thing) happens. वरना (varnaa) is a near-perfect synonym and the two are used interchangeably in casual speech; नहीं तो (nahiin to) feels a shade more conversational to my ear.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to translate this word-for-word as 'no then', missing that as a fixed unit it means 'otherwise' -- the individual pieces नहीं (nahiin) and तो (to) don't need to be parsed separately once you know the phrase. 2) It comes second, after the instruction or condition, never first: डरो नहीं, नहीं तो गिर जाओगे (daro nahiin, nahiin to gir jaaoge, 'don't be afraid, otherwise you'll fall') -- not placed before the warning.

Sense 2 : or, alternatively (offering a choice)
particle Used at the end of a suggestion to offer an alternative, softer than a strict warning: चाय पी लो, नहीं तो कॉफ़ी बना दूँ? (chaay pii lo, nahiin to kofii banaa doon?, 'have some tea, or else should I make coffee?').
Synonyms: या (yaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same phrase softened into a hostess's or friend's offer rather than a threat -- context and tone of voice do the work of telling you which flavor of नहीं तो (nahiin to) you're hearing.
Sample Sentences:
