पड़ोसी (padosii)

noun/adjective A single-sense everyday word meaning 'neighbor'; also functions as an adjective meaning 'neighboring' (as in पड़ोसी देश (padosii desh, 'neighboring country')).
Sense 1 : neighbor (person living nearby)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR life, the पड़ोसी (padosii) is a whole social institution -- lending sugar, keeping spare keys, and knowing everyone's business, for better or worse. There is even a common saying, पड़ोसी का दुख-सुख अपना होता है (padosii kaa dukh-sukh apnaa hotaa hai, 'a neighbor's joys and sorrows become your own'), which captures how central this relationship is seen to be in everyday neighborhood culture.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat पड़ोसी (padosii) as invariant like दोस्त (dost), but it actually inflects like other -ई (ii)-ending masculine nouns for the oblique/plural; the feminine form पड़ोसन (padosan) is a distinct word, not a gender variant of this one, so this entry itself stays masculine only.

Sense 2 : neighboring, adjacent
adjective
Synonyms: समीपवर्ती (samiipvartii, literary), सटा हुआ (sataa huaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in news and geopolitics talk, as in पड़ोसी देश (padosii desh, 'neighboring country') or पड़ोसी राज्य (padosii raajy, 'neighboring state') -- the same word for a friendly next-door neighbor scales up naturally to describe entire countries sharing a border.
