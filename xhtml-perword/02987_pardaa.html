परदा (pardaa)

noun Basic sense is a physical curtain or screen; from there it extends to the social/cultural practice of veiling or seclusion, and figuratively to secrecy or concealment. Also commonly spelled पर्दा (pardaa) with a half-र.
Sense 1 : curtain, screen
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चिलमन (chilman, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian home has परदे (parde) on doors and windows rather than solid doors in many cases, so the word comes up constantly in daily domestic conversation, e.g. asking someone to close it against sun, dust, or prying eyes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Also refers to a cinema/projection screen, so फ़िल्म का परदा (film kaa pardaa, 'the film screen') and परदे पर आना (parde par aanaa, 'to appear on screen', of actors) are common extensions learners meet early, not separate slang.
Sample Sentences:
खिड़की का परदा हटाओ, बाहर बारिश देखनी है। Move the window curtain aside, I want to see the rain outside.
(khidkii kaa pardaa hataao, baahar baarish dekhnii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
धूप बहुत तेज़ है, परदा खींच दो। The sunlight is very strong, pull the curtain shut.

Sense 2 : veiling, seclusion of women (social practice)
noun, masculine Common in the phrase परदा करना (pardaa karnaa, 'to observe purdah/veiling') and परदे में रहना (parde men rahnaa, 'to stay in seclusion/veiled').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense reflects a real historical and still-practiced social custom in parts of North India, where women veil their faces or stay behind screens/curtains before elders, in-laws, or strangers; the English loanword 'purdah' actually comes from this very word.
Common Mistakes: 1) परदा करना (pardaa karnaa) does not mean 'to make a curtain' -- it specifically means to observe the custom of veiling or staying secluded, so learners should not read करना (karnaa) literally here.

गाँव में आज भी कुछ घरों में परदा प्रथा चलती है। Even today, in some households in the village, the custom of purdah is practiced.
(gaanv men aaj bhii kuchh gharon men pardaa prathaa chaltii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
ससुर के सामने आने पर बहू को परदा करना पड़ता था। The daughter-in-law had to veil herself when coming in front of her father-in-law.
(sasur ke saamne aane par bahoo ko pardaa karnaa padtaa thaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : secrecy, concealment (figurative)
noun, masculine Appears mainly in fixed idioms: परदा डालना (pardaa daalnaa, 'to cover up/hush up'), परदा उठाना/परदा फ़ाश करना (pardaa uthaanaa/pardaa faash karnaa, 'to lift the veil/expose a secret'), परदा रखना (pardaa rakhnaa, 'to keep something hidden').
Synonyms: छिपाव (chhipaav), गोपनीयता (gopniiytaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense is everywhere in news headlines and gossip -- राज़ पर परदा डालना (raaz par pardaa daalnaa, 'to cover up a secret') is the kind of phrase you'll hear about political scandals or family secrets alike, treating hidden truths as something literally covered by a curtain.
Common Mistakes: 1) परदा फ़ाश करना (pardaa faash karnaa) means 'to expose a secret/scandal' -- learners sometimes miss that फ़ाश (faash) here means 'exposed', not a verb of its own, so the whole phrase must be learned as a unit.
Sample Sentences:
मंत्री ने घोटाले पर परदा डालने की कोशिश की। The minister tried to cover up the scandal.
(mantrii ne ghotaale par pardaa daalne kii koshish kii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
