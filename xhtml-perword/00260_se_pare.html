से परे (se pare)

postposition A postpositional phrase meaning 'beyond' or 'past' something, whether physically or in a more abstract/figurative sense. Built from परे (pare, 'beyond, further on') governed by the postposition से (se).
Sense 1 : beyond, past (physical or abstract)
postposition Takes a noun in the oblique case followed by से परे (se pare): e.g., शहर से परे (shahar se pare, 'beyond the city'). Commonly used in abstract senses too, like समझ से परे (samajh se pare, 'beyond understanding').
Synonyms: के आगे (ke aage), के पार (ke paar), के बाहर (ke baahar, scope: often for limits/boundaries rather than physical distance)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear से परे (se pare) a lot in slightly reflective or philosophical speech -- फ़िल्में (filmen) and gaanay often use lines like समझ से परे (samajh se pare, 'beyond comprehension') or शब्दों से परे (shabdon se pare, 'beyond words'), giving it a slightly elevated, thoughtful register compared to the more mundane के पार (ke paar).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use के परे (ke pare) by analogy with के आगे (ke aage) or के पार (ke paar) -- but परे (pare) specifically pairs with से (se), not के (ke). 2) In abstract contexts (like 'beyond doubt' or 'beyond comprehension'), learners sometimes reach for a literal spatial word like पार (paar) instead of परे (pare), which carries the more figurative, almost philosophical tone.

(yah baat merii samajh se pare hai।)
