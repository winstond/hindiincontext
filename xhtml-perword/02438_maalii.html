माली (maalii)

noun A single-sense everyday noun: the person who tends a garden -- a gardener.
Sense 1 : gardener
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In many North Indian households and colonies, the माली (maalii) is a familiar figure who comes on fixed days to water plants and tend the garden, much like a milkman or cook has a fixed schedule -- part of the informal household staff network common in Delhi-NCR homes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat माली (maalii) as invariant because it ends in ी, but it inflects like a standard masculine -ई (ii) noun: the plural/oblique form is माली (maalii) staying the same in casual speech but taking मालियों (maaliyon) in the oblique plural -- check the morphology table rather than assuming from the singular shape. 2) Do not confuse with माला (maalaa, 'garland/necklace') -- similar sound, unrelated meaning.
