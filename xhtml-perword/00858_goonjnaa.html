गूँजना (goonjnaa)

verb A single core sense: to resound or echo, said of sounds filling a space -- often used figuratively for words, names, or memories that 'echo' in a place or in someone's mind.
Sense 1 : to resound, echo, reverberate
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: गुंजायमान होना (gunjaaymaan honaa, formal), बजना (bajnaa, for bells/instruments)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Delhi speakers reach for this word well beyond literal acoustics: a slogan can गूँजना (goonjnaa) through a protest march, a poet's name can गूँजना (goonjnaa) across generations, and a childhood song can still गूँजना (goonjnaa) in your ears years later. It pairs naturally with कान (kaan, ears) and दिल/दिमाग़ (dil/dimaagh, heart/mind) when the echo is emotional rather than physical.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to give this verb a direct object as if it meant 'to echo something' -- it is intransitive; the thing that echoes is the subject, and the space it fills in takes में (men): कमरे में उसकी आवाज़ गूँजी (kamre men uskii aavaaz goonjii, 'his/her voice echoed in the room'). 2) The nasalized ँ is easy to drop in writing; without it the word looks like a typo rather than गूँजना (goonjnaa).
Sample Sentences:
मंदिर में घंटियों की आवाज़ गूँज रही थी। The sound of bells was echoing in the temple.
(mandir men ghantiyon kii aavaaz goonj rahii thii।)
