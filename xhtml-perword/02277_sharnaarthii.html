शरणार्थी (sharnaarthii)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'refugee' -- someone who has fled to seek shelter or asylum. Built from शरण (sharan, 'shelter/refuge') plus अर्थी (arthii, 'one who seeks'), the same pattern seen in words like स्वार्थी (svaarthii, 'selfish').
Sense 1 : refugee
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: निर्वासित (nirvaasit, exile/deportee, narrower sense), प्रवासी (pravaasii, migrant, broader/less precise)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly on Indian news channels (samaachaar) when discussing refugee crises -- from Partition-era (vibhaajan) refugees to more recent situations involving Rohingya or Afghan refugees (sharnaarthii). It carries real historical weight in India, since Partition created one of the largest refugee movements in modern history, and the word still evokes that collective memory for many older Hindi speakers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use प्रवासी (pravaasii) as a direct substitute, but that word covers migrants and expatriates generally, not specifically people fleeing danger or persecution -- शरणार्थी (sharnaarthii) is the precise term used in news and legal contexts for 'refugee.' 2) The word is grammatically masculine in form but is used for people of any gender (sharnaarthii does not change for a female refugee) -- this can surprise learners expecting a feminine counterpart like the -ी ending might suggest.

विभाजन के समय लाखों शरणार्थी सीमा पार करके भारत आए थे। At the time of Partition, millions of refugees crossed the border and came to India.
