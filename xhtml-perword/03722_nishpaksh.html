निष्पक्ष (nishpaksh)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning impartial or unbiased, used mainly of people, judgments, and processes.
Sense 1 : impartial, unbiased, neutral
adjective
Synonyms: तटस्थ (tatasth), निरपेक्ष (nirpeksh, formal), न्यायपूर्ण (nyaaypoorn, focuses on fairness of judgment rather than neutrality)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around Indian elections and court cases -- निष्पक्ष चुनाव (nishpaksh chunaav, 'fair/impartial elections') and निष्पक्ष जाँच (nishpaksh jaanch, 'impartial investigation') are practically fixed collocations in news Hindi. It pairs naturally with पक्ष (paksh, 'side, party, faction'), the same root you'll meet in words like पक्षपात (pakshpaat, 'bias, favoritism') -- so once you know पक्ष, निष्पक्ष clicks as its logical opposite.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use निष्पक्ष (nishpaksh) where they mean simply 'honest' or 'good' -- it specifically means not favoring any side, and is most natural with nouns like जज (jaj, 'judge'), मीडिया (miidiyaa, 'media'), चुनाव (chunaav, 'election'), or जाँच (jaanch, 'investigation'). 2) The negative prefix निष्- (nish-) plus पक्ष (paksh, 'side, party') is easy to miss -- learners occasionally write सक्ष (saksh) or drop the nasalization, so it helps to remember it literally as 'without-a-side.'

(chunaav aayog ko nishpaksh chunaav karvaane chaahie।)
