नौजवान (naujvaan)

noun/adjective A single-sense word meaning 'young person/youth,' used both as a noun and as an adjective describing someone young and vigorous.
Sense 1 : a young person, youth
noun, masculine
Synonyms: युवा (yuvaa), जवान (javaan), युवक (yuvak, young man specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear नौजवान (naujvaan) constantly in political speeches and news headlines talking about देश के नौजवान (desh ke naujvaan, 'the youth of the country') -- it carries a slightly more formal, public-address flavor than the everyday जवान (javaan) or युवा (yuvaa). It's built from नौ (nau, 'new') plus जवान (javaan, 'young/vigorous'), literally something like 'newly young.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat नौजवान (naujvaan) as gender-fixed masculine, but it is used for both men and women in modern speech, especially in plural/collective contexts like नौजवान पीढ़ी (naujvaan piidhii, 'the young generation'). 2) Don't confuse with जवान (javaan), which can also mean 'soldier' in certain contexts -- नौजवान (naujvaan) does not carry that military sense.
Sample Sentences:
देश के नौजवानों को आगे आना चाहिए। The youth of the country should come forward.
(desh ke naujvaanon ko aage aanaa chaahie।)

Sense 2 : young, youthful (describing a person)
adjective Used attributively before a noun, e.g. नौजवान लड़का (naujvaan ladkaa, 'young boy'), or predicatively to describe someone as youthful and energetic.
Synonyms: जवान (javaan), युवा (yuvaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As an adjective it often implies not just age but energy and vigor -- calling someone नौजवान (naujvaan) rather than just young in years suggests they're full of youthful spirit, the kind of word used to rally or praise energetic young people.
