अदा (adaa)

noun Two distinct words sharing a spelling: a feminine noun meaning 'grace, charming mannerism' (often plural, अदाएँ (adaaen)), and a separate use meaning 'payment/discharge (of a debt or duty)' found mainly in the fixed verb pairing अदा करना (adaa karnaa).
Sense 1 : charming gesture, coquettish manner, style
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नाज़ (naaz, often paired as नाज़-अदा), नज़ाकत (nazaakat), अंदाज़ (andaaz, style/manner, broader sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a classic word from Urdu-Hindi film and ghazal vocabulary -- think of an actress's नखरे (nakhre) and अदाएँ (adaaen) being praised together, a coy glance or a graceful hand gesture. It has a slightly old-world, romantic flavor, evoking the language of Bollywood love songs and shayari more than everyday small talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this only in the singular, but it appears very frequently in the plural अदाएँ (adaaen) when describing someone's overall charm or mannerisms -- e.g. उसकी अदाएँ (uskii adaaen, 'her mannerisms/charms'). 2) This word carries a flavor of flirtatious or theatrical charm; it is not a neutral word for 'style' in general (for that, अंदाज़ (andaaz) is more common).

Sense 2 : payment, discharge (of debt, duty, dues)
noun, feminine Lives almost entirely inside the compound अदा करना (adaa karnaa, 'to pay/discharge/fulfill') -- used for bills, debts, taxes, and abstract duties alike, e.g. किराया अदा करना (kiraayaa adaa karnaa, 'to pay the rent') or फ़र्ज़ अदा करना (farz adaa karnaa, 'to fulfill a duty').
Synonyms: भुगतान (bhugtaan, more common for money specifically), चुकौती (chukautii, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this in formal or semi-formal contexts: bank notices, rent conversations, or someone solemnly saying अपना फ़र्ज़ अदा करना (apnaa farz adaa karnaa, 'to fulfill one's duty'), which carries a slightly noble, dutiful tone rather than a casual one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat अदा (adaa) here as a standalone verb, but it functions as a noun that needs करना (karnaa) to become an action -- you cannot say अदाया (adaayaa) for 'paid'; it must be अदा किया (adaa kiyaa). 2) This sense is unrelated in feeling to the 'charm' sense, despite sharing a spelling -- context (bills, debts, duties vs. glances, gestures) makes clear which is meant.
