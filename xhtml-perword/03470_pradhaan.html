प्रधान (pradhaan)

noun/adjective प्रधान (pradhaan) works as both a noun for 'head/chief' (of a village, institution, or organization) and an adjective meaning 'main/principal'.
Sense 1 : head, chief, leader (esp. of a village or panchayat)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मुखिया (mukhiyaa), सरपंच (sarpanch, village-council head specifically), नेता (netaa, leader in general)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In rural north India, ग्राम प्रधान (graam pradhaan) is a familiar term for the elected village head, a figure of real local authority in panchayat politics. You will also hear it in compounds like प्रधानमंत्री (pradhaanmantrii, 'prime minister') and प्रधानाचार्य (pradhaanaachaary, 'school principal'), where प्रधान (pradhaan) supplies the sense of 'chief' or 'head'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume प्रधान (pradhaan) always means 'principal' as in a school head -- for that role, प्रिंसिपल (prinsipal) is the common everyday word, while प्रधान (pradhaan) is more associated with village or organizational heads (e.g. ग्राम प्रधान, graam pradhaan, 'village head').

Sense 2 : main, principal, chief (of importance)
adjective
Synonyms: मुख्य (mukhy), मुख्तार (mukhtaar, rare), असल (asal, meaning scope: 'real/main')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this adjectival sense often in news and formal writing -- प्रधान कारण (pradhaan kaaran, 'the main reason'), प्रधान उद्देश्य (pradhaan uddeshy, 'the main objective') -- giving text a slightly more formal register than मुख्य (mukhy) would.
Common Mistakes: 1) As an adjective, प्रधान (pradhaan) is used mostly in somewhat formal or written contexts (e.g. प्रधान कारण, pradhaan kaaran, 'main reason'); in casual speech मुख्य (mukhy) is far more common for 'main.'
