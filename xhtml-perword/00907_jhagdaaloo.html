झगड़ालू (jhagdaaloo)

adjective An adjective (occasionally used as a noun for a person) describing someone quarrelsome or prone to fighting; derived from झगड़ा (jhagdaa, 'quarrel/fight').
Sense 1 : quarrelsome, prone to fighting
adjective
Synonyms: लड़ाकू (ladaakoo), फ़सादी (fasaadii, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word for that one relative, neighbor, or colleague who seems to pick a fight over everything -- from the price of tomatoes to whose turn it is to park where. It carries a tone of exasperated affection or mild complaint rather than outright hostility, the kind of thing a mother might sigh while describing her son's नज़दीकी दोस्त (nazdiikii dost, close friend) or a neighbor everyone tolerates but gossips about.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as a neutral descriptor, but it is mildly critical -- it labels someone as habitually difficult, not just someone in a one-off argument. 2) It describes a person's general temperament (a quarrelsome nature), not a single incident -- for that, Hindi would just describe the event with झगड़ा करना (jhagdaa karnaa), not call the person झगड़ालू (jhagdaaloo).
