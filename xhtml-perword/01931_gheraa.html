घेरा (gheraa)

noun Primarily a noun meaning a circle, ring, or enclosed boundary of people/things; also used for the flare/spread of a skirt, and as the base of the common verb घेरना (ghernaa, 'to surround'), which is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : a ring/circle of people or things; a surrounding boundary
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दायरा (daayraa), घेरा-बंदी (gheraa-bandii, siege/cordon, narrower scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear घेरा (gheraa) in everyday contexts like a crowd forming a circle around a street performer or an accident scene -- लोगों ने घेरा बना लिया (logon ne gheraa banaa liyaa, 'people formed a circle'). It also shows up in news language as पुलिस का घेरा (pulis kaa gheraa, 'police cordon').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse घेरा (gheraa) the noun with घेरना (ghernaa) the verb 'to surround' -- they are related but घेरा (gheraa) itself does not carry verbal force on its own; it needs a verb like डालना (daalnaa) or करना (karnaa) to form a phrase like घेरा डालना (gheraa daalnaa, 'to lay siege/cordon off').

Sense 2 : the flare or spread of a skirt/garment
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is common in the context of clothes shopping or tailoring -- घेरेवाला लहंगा (gherevaalaa lahangaa, 'a flared lehenga') describes a skirt with a wide, circular spread, popular for festive and wedding wear.

Sense 3 : a figurative sphere, circle, or scope of influence/activity
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दायरा (daayraa), क्षेत्र (kshetr)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Used abstractly, as in अपने घेरे में रखना (apne ghere men rakhnaa, 'to keep within one's own circle/orbit') -- similar to how English speakers talk about someone's 'circle of influence.'
Sample Sentences:
यह मुद्दा हमारे विषय के घेरे में नहीं आता। This issue does not fall within the scope of our subject.
(yah muddaa hamaare vishay ke ghere men nahiin aataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उसने पूरे इलाके को अपने घेरे में रखा हुआ है। He has kept the whole area within his sphere of influence.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
यह फैसला सरकार के घेरे से बाहर की बात है। This decision is a matter outside the government's sphere.
