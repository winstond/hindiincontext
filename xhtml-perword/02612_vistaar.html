विस्तार (vistaar)

noun A Sanskritic noun built on the root that means 'to spread out'; its core sense is physical or topical extension (expanse, elaboration), with a secondary adverbial-phrase use meaning 'in detail'.
Sense 1 : expansion, extent, spread
noun, masculine
Synonyms: फैलाव (phailaav), प्रसार (prasaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in news and business Hindi: शहर का विस्तार (shahar kaa vistaar, 'expansion of the city'), व्यापार का विस्तार (vyaapaar kaa vistaar, 'business expansion'). It has a solid, formal-but-common feel -- not literary, just a step up from casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बड़ा (badaa) or बढ़ना (badhnaa) when they mean 'expansion' as a noun -- विस्तार (vistaar) is the noun form, paired with the verb बढ़ना (badhnaa) or होना (honaa), not used as a verb itself. 2) कारोबार का विस्तार (kaarobaar kaa vistaar, 'expansion of the business') takes का (kaa) to link to what is expanding, which learners sometimes replace with में (men).
Sample Sentences:
शहर का विस्तार बहुत तेज़ी से हो रहा है। The city's expansion is happening very quickly.
(shahar kaa vistaar bahut tezii se ho rahaa hai।)

Sense 2 : elaboration, detailed account
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ब्यौरा (byauraa), विवरण (vivran)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in the fixed phrase विस्तार से (vistaar se, 'in detail'), which is the form learners actually use most -- as in कृपया विस्तार से बताइए (kripyaa vistaar se bataaie, 'please explain in detail'). Think of विस्तार (vistaar) here as the noun 'expanse' applied to an explanation rather than to space.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया इस विषय पर विस्तार से बताइए। Please explain this topic in detail.
(kripyaa is vishay par vistaar se bataaie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
