सीटी बजाना (siitii bajaanaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to whistle' -- literally 'to make a whistle sound,' pairing the noun सीटी (siitii, 'whistle') with बजाना (bajaanaa, 'to play/sound an instrument'). Covers both whistling with one's mouth and blowing a physical whistle.
Sense 1 : to whistle (with mouth, or blow a whistle device)
verb, transitive सीटी (siitii) takes को-marking only when specifying direction ('to whistle at someone'): किसी को सीटी बजाना (kisii ko siitii bajaanaa, 'to whistle at someone').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR, सीटी बजाना (siitii bajaanaa) is what a रेफरी (rephrii, 'referee') or ट्रैफिक पुलिस (traiphik pulis, 'traffic police') does constantly on the street -- the sharp पीं-पीं (piin-piin) sound is part of the city's everyday soundscape. It is also what boys do to catch attention or tease, so सीटी बजाना (siitii bajaanaa) at someone in public can carry a flirtatious or catcalling connotation depending on context and tone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try सीटी करना (siitii karnaa) by analogy with other noun+करना constructions, but the fixed collocation always uses बजाना (bajaanaa), never करना (karnaa). 2) The subject is the person or device doing the whistling, and सीटी (siitii) is the object -- learners occasionally reverse this when translating from English 'the whistle blew,' where in Hindi it would be सीटी बजी (siitii bajii, 'the whistle sounded'), an intransitive use of बजना (bajnaa) rather than बजाना (bajaanaa).

Sense 2 : to cheer/applaud loudly by whistling (at a performance, film, match)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: शोर मचाना (shor machaanaa, broader: to make noise)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: At a single-screen सिनेमा हॉल (sinemaa hol) or during a cricket match, दर्शक (darshak, 'audience/spectators') सीटी बजाना (siitii bajaanaa) to celebrate a hero's dramatic entry or a sixer -- it is a mark of enthusiastic approval, quite unlike the sometimes-negative 'booing' connotation whistling can have in English contexts.
