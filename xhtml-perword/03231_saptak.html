सप्तक (saptak)

noun A music-theory term meaning 'octave' (the set of seven notes, सप्त (sapt) meaning 'seven'); mostly heard in the context of classical singing, harmonium, or music lessons rather than everyday conversation.
Sense 1 : octave (in Indian classical music)
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Indian classical music divides the voice or instrument's range into three सप्तक (saptak): मंद्र सप्तक (mandr saptak, lower octave), मध्य सप्तक (madhy saptak, middle octave), and तार सप्तक (taar saptak, upper octave). If you have ever taken harmonium or singing lessons in India, this is one of the first technical words a teacher (गुरु guru or उस्ताद ustaad) will use.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume this is a general music word like 'song' or 'tune' -- it specifically refers to the octave/scale register (मंद्र (mandr, lower), मध्य (madhy, middle), तार (taar, upper) सप्तक (saptak)), not a melody or composition itself.
