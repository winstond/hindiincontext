इनसान (insaan)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'human being, person' -- often used with a warm, humanistic tone that goes beyond just biological classification. Also commonly spelled इंसान (insaan) with an anusvara/nasalization mark instead of the full न (n); both spellings are equally standard.
Sense 1 : human being, person
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आदमी (aadmii, can also mean 'man' specifically), मानव (maanav, formal/scientific), व्यक्ति (vyakti, formal, individual)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a word full of heart in Hindi -- when someone says वह बहुत अच्छा इनसान है (vah bahut achchhaa insaan hai, 'he is a very good human being'), they are praising someone's basic humanity and decency, not just being polite. You'll hear it in the classic complaint इनसान तो इनसान है, गलती हो जाती है (insaan to insaan hai, galtii ho jaatii hai, 'a human is only human, mistakes happen'), a very Delhi way of forgiving a slip-up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आदमी (aadmii) as the default word for 'person,' but इनसान (insaan) carries a broader, more philosophical sense of 'human being' as opposed to animals or god, and is the natural choice in moral or emotional statements like इनसान गलतियाँ करता है (insaan galtiyaan kartaa hai, 'a human makes mistakes'). 2) Unlike आदमी (aadmii), इनसान (insaan) is not gender-specific and does not mean 'man' -- it applies equally to any person regardless of gender.
