बानवे (baanve)

numeral This is simply the Hindi numeral for 92 (English 'ninety-two').
Sense 1 : the number 92
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like all Hindi numbers in the 90s, बानवे (baanve) is built on a historical contraction of नब्बे (nabbe, ninety) and दो (do, two), though the modern word doesn't sound like a simple sum of the two anymore. Learners usually find it easiest to just drill the 91-99 block as a set, the way you'd memorize irregular verbs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are each an individual memorized word rather than a predictable combination of tens and units, so बानवे (baanve) has to be learned on its own like the others nearby (इक्यानवे ikyaanve for 91, तिरानवे tiraanve for 93) rather than derived from नब्बे (nabbe, ninety) plus दो (do, two).
Sample Sentences:
मेरे दादाजी की उम्र बानवे साल है। My grandfather is ninety-two years old.
(mere daadaajii kii umr baanve saal hai।)
