लेकिन (lekin)

conjunction A very common word for 'but' -- one straightforward sense, used constantly to contrast two ideas.
Sense 1 : but, however
conjunction
Synonyms: मगर (magar), पर (par), किन्तु (kintu, literary), परन्तु (parantu, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: लेकिन (lekin) is one of those words you'll hear in every single conversation, from a market haggle to a heartfelt apology -- 'मैं आना चाहता था, लेकिन...' (main aanaa chaahtaa thaa, lekin..., 'I wanted to come, but...') is basically the national excuse-starter. It sits comfortably in both casual chat and more careful speech, unlike its more literary cousins किन्तु (kintu) and परन्तु (parantu), which you'll mostly meet in writing or formal speeches.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat पर (par) and लेकिन (lekin) as interchangeable in every context, which is mostly true, but लेकिन (lekin) feels slightly more emphatic when the contrast is sharper or more surprising. 2) लेकिन (lekin) is placed at the start of the second clause, not mid-sentence like English 'however' sometimes is.

मैं आना चाहता था, लेकिन बारिश बहुत तेज़ थी। I wanted to come, but it was raining very hard.
(main aanaa chaahtaa thaa, lekin baarish bahut tez thii।)
