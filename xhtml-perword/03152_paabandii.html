पाबंदी (paabandii)

noun A single core meaning: a restriction, ban, or curb imposed on some activity, used across everyday, legal, and news contexts.
Sense 1 : restriction, ban, curb
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रतिबंध (pratibandh, formal), रोक (rok), मनाही (manaahii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Indian news broadcasts -- सरकार ने पाबंदी लगा दी (sarkaar ne paabandii lagaa dii, 'the government imposed a ban') is a stock headline phrase, whether about firecrackers, traffic, or imports. At home, parents also use it more casually, like देर रात बाहर जाने पर पाबंदी है (der raat baahar jaane par paabandii hai, 'there's a restriction on going out late at night'), so it spans from bureaucratic to domestic registers easily.
Common Mistakes: 1) The verb pairing is पाबंदी लगाना (paabandii lagaanaa, 'to impose a restriction') for the person/authority imposing it, and पाबंदी होना (paabandii honaa) or पाबंदी लगना (paabandii lagnaa) for describing the state of being restricted -- learners sometimes force लगाना (lagaanaa) into contexts where होना (honaa) is more natural. 2) The thing being restricted is marked with पर (par), not को (ko): प्लास्टिक पर पाबंदी (plaastik par paabandii, 'a ban on plastic'), not प्लास्टिक को (plaastik ko).
