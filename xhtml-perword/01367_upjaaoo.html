उपजाऊ (upjaaoo)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'fertile' -- used for land, soil, and by extension for anything productive or generative, like a fertile mind or region.
Sense 1 : fertile, productive (of land/soil)
adjective
Synonyms: उर्वर (urvar, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word comes up constantly in conversations about Indian geography and agriculture -- the Gangetic plain (गंगा का मैदान (gangaa kaa maidaan)) is the textbook example of उपजाऊ (upjaaoo) land that Indian schoolchildren learn about early on. It also extends nicely into metaphor: an उपजाऊ (upjaaoo) दिमाग़ (dimaagh) is a 'fertile mind,' full of ideas, just as in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for this word to describe a fertile person or animal (as in biological fertility) -- उपजाऊ (upjaaoo) is used for land, soil, and figuratively for productive minds/regions, not for reproductive fertility, which uses separate vocabulary. 2) It is invariant in form, so learners familiar with the -आ adjective pattern sometimes wrongly try to inflect it for feminine or plural nouns (e.g. उपजाऊ ज़मीन (upjaaoo zamiin, fertile land) stays उपजाऊ (upjaaoo), not उपजाई (upjaaii)).

गंगा के मैदान की मिट्टी बहुत उपजाऊ है। The soil of the Gangetic plain is very fertile.
(gangaa ke maidaan kii mittii bahut upjaaoo hai।)
