राजा (raajaa)

noun राजा (raajaa) is the everyday word for 'king' -- used literally for a monarch, for the king piece/card in games, and affectionately as a term of endearment for a son.
Sense 1 : king, monarch
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सम्राट (samraat, emperor), नरेश (naresh, literary), महाराजा (mahaaraajaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi folk tales almost always open with राजा और रानी (raajaa aur raanii, 'the king and queen'), the Hindi equivalent of 'once upon a time.' The idiom अंधों में काना राजा (andhon men kaanaa raajaa, 'among the blind, the one-eyed man is king') is a very common way to say someone stands out only because everyone else is worse.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use राजा (raajaa) for any powerful or rich person; in ordinary speech it is reserved for actual kings, royalty, or figurative/affectionate use -- for a rich person, use अमीर (amiir) or धनवान (dhanvaan) instead.

(ek samay kii baat hai, ek raajaa thaa jo apne raajy men bahut nyaay se shaasan kartaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : king (in chess or playing cards)
noun, masculine Used in शतरंज (shatranj, chess) for the king piece, and in card games for the King card, parallel to रानी (raanii, queen) and इक्का (ikkaa, ace).
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In a Delhi tea-stall game of ताश (taash, cards), you will hear players call out राजा (raajaa) the same casual way English speakers say 'king' -- no royal solemnity attached, it is just game vocabulary.

Sense 3 : term of affection for a boy/son ('little prince')
noun, masculine Functions almost like an adjective of endearment before बेटा (betaa) or a child's name, rather than a claim about actual royalty.
Synonyms: बेटा (betaa, son -- often paired as मेरा राजा बेटा (meraa raajaa betaa, 'my darling son'))
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Any Delhi mother coaxing a fussy child to eat might say मेरा राजा बेटा है, खाना खा लो (meraa raajaa betaa hai, khaanaa khaa lo, 'be my good prince and eat your food') -- राजा (raajaa) here carries no throne, just pure affection.
Common Mistakes: 1) This affectionate sense is used for boys/sons; for a girl the parallel endearment is रानी (raanii), not राजा (raajaa).
Sample Sentences:
मेरा राजा बेटा है, खाना खा लो। Be my good prince and eat your food.
(meraa raajaa betaa hai, khaanaa khaa lo।)
