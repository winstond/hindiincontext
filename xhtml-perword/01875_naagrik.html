नागरिक (naagrik)

noun/adjective Primarily a noun meaning 'citizen,' with a related adjective sense meaning 'civic/civil.'
Sense 1 : citizen
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in civics class, news, and government forms -- भारतीय नागरिक (bhaartiiy naagrik, 'Indian citizen') is the standard phrase you'll see on passports and official documents. It carries a formal, institutional flavor rather than a warm, everyday one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use नागरिक (naagrik) when वासी (vaasii, 'resident/inhabitant') or निवासी (nivaasii) is meant -- नागरिक (naagrik) specifically implies legal/political membership in a state (citizenship), not just living somewhere.

(main bhaarat kaa naagrik hoon।)

Sense 2 : civic, civil
adjective
Synonyms: शहरी (shahrii, urban/civic, partial overlap)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used in compounds like नागरिक अधिकार (naagrik adhikaar, 'civic/civil rights') or नागरिक सुविधाएँ (naagrik suvidhaaen, 'civic amenities') -- you'll meet this adjectival use mostly in news and formal writing rather than casual chat.
Sample Sentences:
हर नागरिक को अपने नागरिक अधिकारों की जानकारी होनी चाहिए। Every citizen should have knowledge of their civic rights.
(har naagrik ko apne naagrik adhikaaron kii jaankaarii honii chaahie।)
