जो कोई (jo koii)

pronoun A compound relative pronoun meaning 'whoever, whosoever' -- combines जो (jo, 'who/which') with कोई (koii, 'someone/anyone') to intensify the indefinite-relative sense. Used mainly in its correlative frame with वह/उसको/उसे in the following clause.
Sense 1 : whoever, whosoever (indefinite relative pronoun for people)
pronoun Works in the standard relative-correlative frame: जो कोई (jo koii) ... वह (vah) ..., 'whoever ... that person ...'. The correlative pronoun in the second clause is often वह (vah) or उसे (use) depending on case.
Synonyms: जो भी (jo bhii, more common everyday variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear जो भी (jo bhii) far more often in daily conversation -- जो कोई (jo koii) has a slightly more formal, almost proverbial ring, the kind of phrasing you find in moral sayings or announcements, like जो कोई सच बोलेगा, वही जीतेगा (jo koii sach bolegaa, vahii jiitegaa, 'whoever speaks the truth will win').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop the correlative pronoun in the second clause, saying only जो कोई करेगा, अच्छा होगा (jo koii karegaa, achchhaa hogaa) instead of including वह (vah) or a suitable stand-in -- Hindi usually wants the second clause explicitly picked back up. 2) जो कोई (jo koii) is slightly more formal/emphatic than जो भी (jo bhii); learners defaulting to जो कोई (jo koii) everywhere can sound a touch bookish in casual speech where जो भी (jo bhii) is more natural.
Sample Sentences:
जो कोई सच बोलेगा, वही जीतेगा। Whoever speaks the truth will win.
(jo koii sach bolegaa, vahii jiitegaa।)
