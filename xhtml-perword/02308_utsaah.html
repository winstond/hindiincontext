उत्साह (utsaah)

noun A single-sense noun meaning enthusiasm or zeal -- the energy and eagerness someone brings to an activity.
Sense 1 : enthusiasm, zeal, eagerness
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जोश (josh), उमंग (umang), जज़्बा (jazbaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around exams, sports events, and festivals -- a teacher might say बच्चों में बहुत उत्साह है (bachchon men bahut utsaah hai, 'the children are very enthusiastic') to describe a classroom buzzing with excitement. It pairs naturally with बढ़ाना (badhaanaa, 'to increase/boost') and भरना (bharnaa, 'to fill') -- both common collocations you'll meet in motivational speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try 'मुझे उत्साह है' when they mean 'I am enthusiastic' -- more natural phrasings use it with से (se) as an adverbial: उत्साह से करना (utsaah se karnaa, 'to do with enthusiasm'), or with बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) meaning to boost someone's enthusiasm. 2) Don't confuse with जोश (josh), which leans more toward passionate/fiery energy (used for pep talks, sports, patriotism), while उत्साह (utsaah) is the more general, everyday word for positive eagerness or motivation.
Sample Sentences:
बच्चों में बहुत उत्साह है। The children are very enthusiastic.
(bachchon men bahut utsaah hai।)
