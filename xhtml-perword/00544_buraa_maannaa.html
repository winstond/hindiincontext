बुरा मानना (buraa maannaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to feel offended/hurt' or 'to take something to heart' -- built from बुरा (buraa, 'bad') plus मानना (maannaa, 'to consider/regard'). One core sense, almost always seen in negative or conditional frames asking someone not to be offended.
Sense 1 : to feel offended, to take offense, to mind (something said or done)
verb, transitive The person offended is marked with को: उसे बुरा लगा (use buraa lagaa) is the related लगना construction, but with मानना the offended person is usually the subject: मैंने बुरा नहीं माना (mainne buraa nahiin maanaa, 'I did not take offense'). Frequently used in the polite request frame बुरा न मानिए (buraa n maanie, 'please don't be offended'), often preceding a frank remark.
Synonyms: नाराज़ होना (naaraaz honaa), खफ़ा होना (khafaa honaa, colloquial), अपमानित महसूस करना (apmaanit mahsoos karnaa, more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase is a staple of Delhi-style bluntness: before criticizing someone's cooking, choice of spouse, or driving, people often soften the blow with बुरा न मानिए तो (buraa n maanie to, 'if you don't mind my saying so'), then say exactly what they think anyway. It pairs naturally with the involuntary-feeling verb बुरा लगना (buraa lagnaa) -- मानना puts the emphasis on the person's own reaction/choice to be hurt, while लगना puts it on the feeling arriving unbidden.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बुरा लगना (buraa lagnaa) in every context, but बुरा मानना (buraa maannaa) specifically frames the person as actively 'holding' the offense (with ने-marking in the past, since मानना is transitive), whereas बुरा लगना (buraa lagnaa) frames the bad feeling as something that happened to them (को-construction, no ने). 2) The polite disclaimer बुरा न मानिए (buraa n maanie, 'don't take this the wrong way') is a fixed social formula said *before* a blunt comment -- learners sometimes only learn the literal meaning and miss this everyday pragmatic use.
Sample Sentences:
बुरा न मानिए तो, ये कमीज़ आप पर अच्छी नहीं लग रही। If you don't mind my saying so, this shirt doesn't look good on you.
(buraa n maanie to, ye kamiiz aap par achchhii nahiin lag rahii।)
