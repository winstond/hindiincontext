तैरना (tairnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to swim' or 'to float.'
Sense 1 : to swim, to float
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi kids mostly learn तैरना (tairnaa) at a सरकारी स्विमिंग पूल (sarkaarii sviming pool, 'government swimming pool') in the summer holidays -- ask any local and they'll have a story about learning in a river or canal in their native village instead. The same verb extends nicely to floating objects, like पत्ता पानी पर तैर रहा है (pattaa paanii par tair rahaa hai, 'the leaf is floating on the water'), so don't be surprised to see it used for things that drift rather than actively swim.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to add an object directly, e.g. attempting 'to swim the river,' but तैरना (tairnaa) is intransitive -- the river or pool is marked with में (men), as in नदी में तैरना (nadii men tairnaa, 'to swim in the river'), not taken as a direct object. 2) Since it's intransitive, the past tense takes the ordinary subject agreement pattern (वह तैरा vah tairaa) rather than ने-marking -- learners who over-apply ने from other verbs sometimes reach for it here by mistake.
