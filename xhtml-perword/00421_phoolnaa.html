फूलना (phoolnaa)

verb Basic meaning is 'to swell up' or 'to bloom/blossom' -- covers literal swelling (dough, a bruised limb) and the figurative sense of puffing up with pride. Closely related to फूल (phool, 'flower').
Sense 1 : to swell, puff up (physically)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: सूजना (soojnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this about bread or पूरी (poorii) puffing up on the tava, or about feet swelling (pair phoolnaa) after a long day of standing -- it is a very physical, visual verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with सूजना (soojnaa); फूलना (phoolnaa) is broader and can describe dough rising or a balloon inflating, not just an injury swelling up.
Sample Sentences:
गर्म तवे पर पूरी फूल जाती है। The puri puffs up on the hot tava.
(garm tave par poorii phool jaatii hai।)

Sense 2 : to swell with pride, be delighted
verb, intransitive Very commonly used in the compound expression फूले नहीं समाना (phoole nahiin samaanaa, 'to be so happy one can't contain oneself').
Synonyms: फूला न समाना (phoolaa n samaanaa, idiom: to be overjoyed)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A mother watching her son get top marks might be described as फूली नहीं समा रही (phoolii nahiin samaa rahii, 'she's beside herself with joy') -- the body-as-container image is very Hindi: joy literally doesn't 'fit' inside the person.
Common Mistakes: 1) फूलना (phoolnaa) alone in this sense usually implies a slightly negative or ironic edge -- 'puffed up with pride/vanity' -- whereas the idiom फूला न समाना (phoolaa n samaanaa) is purely positive, meaning overjoyed; learners sometimes mix up the tone of the two.
Sample Sentences:
बेटे के नंबर सुनकर माँ फूली नहीं समा रही थी। Hearing her son's marks, the mother was beside herself with joy.
(bete ke nambar sunkar maan phoolii nahiin samaa rahii thii।)

Sense 3 : to bloom, blossom
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: खिलना (khilnaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: खिलना (khilnaa) is the far more common word for flowers blooming in everyday speech; फूलना (phoolnaa) in this sense leans slightly more literary or descriptive, often paired with खिलना (phoolnaa-khilnaa) as a set phrase for a garden in full bloom.
Sample Sentences:
बगीचे में फूल फूल-खिल रहे हैं। The flowers in the garden are blossoming.
(bagiiche men phool phool-khil rahe hain।)

वसंत में यह पेड़ फूलता है। This tree blossoms in spring.

(pooraa bagiichaa phool-khilkar kisii svarg jaisaa lag rahaa thaa।)
