तत्त्व (tattv)

noun A somewhat learned/philosophical word covering 'element/essence' (the underlying substance or principle of something) and, by extension, 'factor/component' in analytical speech. Also commonly spelled तत्व (tatv) with a single त.
Sense 1 : essence, underlying principle or truth
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सार (saar), मूलतत्त्व (mooltattv, compound form, less common in speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you meet in discussions of philosophy or spirituality, as in ज्ञान का तत्त्व (gyaan kaa tattv, 'the essence of knowledge'). It carries a slightly weighty, thoughtful tone -- not a word you'd casually drop at the dinner table, but one that surfaces in serious conversation or writing about life and truth.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सार (saar) when they mean the 'gist' of a story, but तत्त्व (tattv) in this sense points to a deeper philosophical or spiritual essence rather than a summary -- it belongs to more reflective or academic speech, not casual conversation.

Sense 2 : element (constituent substance, as in chemistry or the classical elements)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in both traditional and modern contexts: the five classical elements are called पंचतत्त्व (panchtattv, 'the five elements' -- earth, water, fire, air, space), and the same word is used in school-level chemistry for a chemical element. It's a nice example of an old philosophical term being repurposed for modern science.

Sense 3 : factor, component (in analytical or formal contexts)
noun, masculine Common in phrases like महत्त्वपूर्ण तत्त्व (mahattvpoorn tattv, 'important factor') in essays, news analysis, or formal discussion.
Synonyms: कारक (kaarak), पहलू (pahloo, aspect, meaning scope narrower -- emphasizes 'side' or 'angle' rather than 'component')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this sense in news commentary or academic writing when someone lists the 'factors' behind an event -- it has a formal, analytical flavor rather than everyday chit-chat register.
