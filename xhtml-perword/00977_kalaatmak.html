कलात्मक (kalaatmak)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'artistic' -- built from कला (kalaa, 'art') plus the suffix -आत्मक (aatmak), which turns a noun into a quality-adjective roughly like English '-istic' or '-esque'.
Sense 1 : artistic, aesthetically skillful
adjective
Synonyms: सुंदर (sundar, meaning scope: 'beautiful' rather than specifically 'artistic'), सौंदर्यपूर्ण (saundarypoorn, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot around Delhi's craft markets and heritage sites -- a shopkeeper in Dilli Haat might describe a hand-carved box as बहुत कलात्मक (bahut kalaatmak, 'very artistic') to justify its price. It pairs naturally with words like डिज़ाइन (dizaain, 'design') and शिल्प (shilp, 'craft'), reflecting a broader family of -आत्मक (aatmak) words (भावात्मक bhaavaatmak 'emotional', रचनात्मक rachnaatmak 'creative') that Hindi uses to build abstract-quality adjectives from nouns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use कलात्मक (kalaatmak) as a general compliment like 'nice' or 'pretty' -- it specifically praises craftsmanship, design, or artistic quality (a painting, a building, handwriting), not just physical beauty in a person. 2) Because it is invariant, learners often mistakenly add gender/number endings as if it were a regular -आ (aa) adjective like अच्छा (achchhaa).
