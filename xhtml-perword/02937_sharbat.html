शरबत (sharbat)

noun A sweet, diluted drink -- usually fruit- or flower-syrup-based -- served cold, especially in summer. One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : sweet syrup-based drink
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जूस (joos, usually fruit juice specifically), शीतल पेय (shiital pey, formal, generic 'cold beverage')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi summers, शरबत (sharbat) is what a guest gets offered the moment they sit down -- rooh afza-based rose sherbet is the classic version, poured from a tall glass jug at any family gathering. It is also strongly linked with Ramzan iftaars and hot-weather roadside stalls selling nimbu (lemon) or bel (wood-apple) sharbat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not confuse with the English 'sherbet' (frozen dessert) -- शरबत (sharbat) is always a liquid you drink, made by diluting a syrup (like rose or lemon syrup) with water. 2) घर पर शरबत बनाना (ghar par sharbat banaanaa, 'to make sharbat at home') is the everyday phrase; learners sometimes reach for पीना (piinaa, 'to drink') alone without बनाना (banaanaa) when talking about preparing it, missing the two-step framing of making a syrup and then serving it diluted.
