कितनी बार (kitnii baar)

adverb A fixed question phrase meaning 'how many times/how often', combining the interrogative कितनी (kitnii, feminine agreement with बार (baar), 'time/occasion').
Sense 1 : how many times / how often (asking frequency)
adverb
Synonyms: कितने दफ़ा (kitne dafaa, colloquial variant), कितनी दफ़ा (kitnii dafaa, colloquial variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday way to ask 'how often' in casual conversation -- a mother might ask her kid कितनी बार बोला है? (kitnii baar bolaa hai?, 'how many times have I told you?') when exasperated, and that exasperated-repetition use is extremely common in Hindi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say कितना बार (kitnaa baar) using the masculine कितना (kitnaa), but बार (baar) 'time/occasion' is grammatically feminine here, so it must agree as कितनी (kitnii). 2) Don't confuse this बार (baar, 'time/occasion') with the identical-looking word बार (baar, 'door/turn' in some fixed expressions) or with वार (vaar, 'day of the week' or 'attack') -- they are unrelated words that just sound alike.
