फलना (phalnaa)

verb Mainly used in the fixed pair फलना-फूलना (phalnaa-phoolnaa) meaning 'to flourish, thrive'; the standalone literal sense 'to bear fruit' also exists but is much less common in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : to prosper, thrive, flourish
verb, intransitive Almost always used with फूलना (phoolnaa) as the compound pair फलना-फूलना (phalnaa-phoolnaa), applied to people, families, businesses, or relationships rather than plants.
Synonyms: फलना-फूलना (phalnaa-phoolnaa), तरक्की करना (tarakkii karnaa), उन्नति करना (unnti karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear elders bless a young couple or a new shop with फलो-फूलो (phalo-phoolo, 'may you flourish') -- it carries the warmth of a blessing for growth and prosperity, much like wishing someone a bountiful harvest of life.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use फलना (phalnaa) alone when talking about a business or family doing well; native speakers almost always say फल-फूल रहा है (phal-phool rahaa hai, 'is thriving') rather than just फल रहा है (phal rahaa hai). 2) This figurative sense takes an intransitive frame with होना (honaa) or रहना (rahnaa) auxiliaries, e.g. उसका कारोबार फल-फूल रहा है (uskaa kaarobaar phal-phool rahaa hai, 'his business is flourishing') -- not a direct object.

Sense 2 : to bear fruit (literal, of a plant)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: फल देना (phal denaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In villages you might hear a farmer say यह पेड़ अच्छा फलता है (yah ped achchhaa phaltaa hai, 'this tree bears fruit well') -- a straightforward, literal use that is the etymological root behind the more common figurative sense above.
