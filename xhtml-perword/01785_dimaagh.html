दिमाग़ (dimaagh)

noun Means 'brain' or 'mind' in the physical/mental sense, and by extension 'head' as the seat of thought, temper, or attitude -- most of the word's idiomatic life is in this extended sense.
Sense 1 : brain / mind (as the organ of thought)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मस्तिष्क (mastishk, formal/anatomical), बुद्धि (buddhi, intellect rather than the organ)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: दिमाग़ (dimaagh) is a Persian loanword and is extremely common in everyday speech; you will hear it constantly in phrases about thinking hard, being smart, or losing your cool, far more often than in a strict anatomical sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मन (man) when they mean दिमाग़ (dimaagh): मन (man) is the heart/mind of feeling and desire, while दिमाग़ (dimaagh) is the mind of reasoning, memory, and calculation -- 'use your brain' is दिमाग़ लगाओ (dimaagh lagaao, 'use your brain'), not मन लगाओ (man lagaao).

Sense 2 : head/temper as seat of attitude, arrogance, or irritation (idiomatic)
noun, masculine Appears mainly inside fixed expressions such as दिमाग़ ख़राब होना (dimaagh kharaab honaa, 'to lose one's cool/go crazy'), दिमाग़ चलना (dimaagh chalnaa, 'to rack one's brains'), and दिमाग़ लगाना (dimaagh lagaanaa, 'to apply one's mind'); the referent is still 'brain/mind' but the meaning is about attitude, temper, or mental effort rather than the physical organ.
Synonyms: अकड़ (akad, arrogance/stiffness, narrower), मूड (mood, mood, narrower)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is where दिमाग़ (dimaagh) really comes alive: a mother scolding a child might say दिमाग़ ख़राब हो गया है क्या? (dimaagh kharaab ho gayaa hai kyaa?, 'have you lost your mind?'), and calling someone दिमाग़वाला (dimaaghvaalaa) or saying उसका दिमाग़ सातवें आसमान पर है (uskaa dimaagh saatven aasmaan par hai, 'his head is in the seventh sky') pokes fun at someone acting too big for their boots.
Common Mistakes: 1) दिमाग़ ख़राब है (dimaagh kharaab hai) does not mean someone is literally sick -- it means they're being unreasonable, worked up, or acting spoiled, similar to 'have you lost your mind?' 2) दिमाग़ है क्या? (dimaagh hai kyaa?) is a common rhetorical jab meaning 'are you out of your mind?' or 'do you even think?' -- it's an insult about judgment, not a real question about brain function.
