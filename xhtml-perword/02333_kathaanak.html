कथानक (kathaanak)

noun A single-sense literary/formal noun meaning the plot or storyline of a narrative work.
Sense 1 : plot, storyline
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कहानी (kahaanii, everyday word for story, broader than plot), प्लॉट (plot, colloquial English loanword), वृत्तांत (vrittaant, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet कथानक (kathaanak) most often in book reviews, literature classes, or when someone is discussing the संरचना (sanrachnaa, 'structure') of a novel or film -- it has a slightly academic or critical flavor compared to the everyday कहानी (kahaanii). If a friend asks 'कहानी क्या है?' (kahaanii kyaa hai?, 'what's the story?') they want the gist; a film critic asking about the कथानक (kathaanak) wants the plot's construction and arc.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use कहानी (kahaanii) where कथानक (kathaanak) would be more precise; कहानी (kahaanii) means 'story' in general, while कथानक (kathaanak) specifically refers to the structured plot or storyline of a novel, film, or play. 2) This word is rarely used in casual conversation about everyday events -- it belongs to discussions of literature, film, and drama, so using it to describe, say, a friend's gossip would sound overly formal or odd.
