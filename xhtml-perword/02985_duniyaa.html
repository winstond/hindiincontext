दुनिया (duniyaa)

noun A very common feminine noun meaning 'world' -- used both literally (the physical/geographic world) and in frequent figurative/idiomatic senses (a social sphere, 'everyone', or a personal outlook).
Sense 1 : the world (physical/geographic or global sense)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जगत (jagat, literary), विश्व (vishv, formal), संसार (sansaar, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: दुनिया (duniyaa) is the word you will hear constantly in everyday Hindi -- in news, songs, and casual talk -- while विश्व (vishv) and संसार (sansaar) stay mostly in formal writing or official titles like विश्व बैंक (vishv baink, 'World Bank'). It comes from Arabic/Persian via Urdu, reflecting Delhi's long Indo-Persian linguistic heritage.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for विश्व (vishv) or संसार (sansaar) in everyday speech where a native speaker would simply say दुनिया (duniyaa) -- those alternatives sound bookish or formal by comparison. 2) दुनिया (duniyaa) is feminine, so adjectives and verbs agreeing with it take feminine forms, e.g. पूरी दुनिया (poorii duniyaa, 'the whole world'), not पूरा (pooraa).

(poorii duniyaa men koronaa phail gayaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : a social sphere or community of people ('everyone', 'people at large')
noun, feminine Often used with सारी (saarii) or पूरी (poorii) to mean 'everybody' -- सारी दुनिया जानती है (saarii duniyaa jaantii hai, 'everyone knows') -- functioning almost like an emphatic collective subject rather than a literal planet.
Synonyms: लोग (log)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the दुनिया (duniyaa) of gossip and reputation -- दुनिया क्या कहेगी (duniyaa kyaa kahegii, 'what will people/the world say') is a classic anxious refrain in Indian families about social judgment, capturing how strongly collective opinion is felt to weigh on personal choices.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners translate सारी दुनिया (saarii duniyaa) too literally as 'the whole planet' when it usually just means 'everybody' in context, similar to English 'the whole world is talking about it.'

Sense 3 : a particular domain, sphere, or way of life (figurative 'world of...')
noun, feminine Used in phrases like अपनी दुनिया में खोया रहना (apnii duniyaa men khoyaa rahnaa, 'to stay lost in one's own world'), where दुनिया (duniyaa) means a personal mental/emotional space rather than a literal place.
Synonyms: जगत (jagat, used in compounds like फ़िल्म जगत (film jagat, 'film world'))
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense shows up in everyday emotional talk: someone described as अपनी दुनिया में रहना (apnii duniyaa men rahnaa, 'to live in one's own world') is seen as absorbed, distracted, or a bit of a daydreamer -- a gently affectionate or mildly critical remark depending on tone.
