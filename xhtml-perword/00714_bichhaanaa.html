बिछाना (bichhaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to spread out or lay down something flat, like bedding, a mat, or a sheet.
Sense 1 : to spread out, lay out (bedding, a sheet, a mat)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: फैलाना (phailaanaa, for spreading flat things generally)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear every night in an Indian household when someone says बिस्तर बिछाना (bistar bichhaanaa, 'to make/lay out the bed') -- since many homes still use a mattress or mat (गद्दा gaddaa or चटाई chataaii) that is spread on the floor at night and rolled up in the morning, this verb carries a very concrete, physical image of unrolling and flattening something out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse बिछाना (bichhaanaa) with बैठना (baithnaa, 'to sit') because of the similar sound -- they are unrelated. 2) बिछाना (bichhaanaa) is used for the act of laying something flat (a bed, mat, cloth), while ओढ़ना (odhnaa) is for covering yourself with something like a blanket -- the two are often mixed up by beginners talking about bedtime routines.

Sense 2 : to lay out, arrange (a table, tray, spread of items)
verb, transitive Used when arranging multiple items across a surface, such as food on a thaali (thaalii) or wares in a shop display.
Synonyms: सजाना (sajaanaa, for decorative arranging)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in the context of a meal being laid out -- खाना बिछाना (khaanaa bichhaanaa) or थाली बिछाना (thaalii bichhaanaa) -- evoking the image of dishes spread across a cloth or tray on the floor, a very traditional Indian dining setup.
