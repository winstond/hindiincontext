रोकना (roknaa)

verb One core meaning that branches into several everyday uses: to physically stop/halt something, to block or obstruct a path, to prevent or restrain someone from doing something, and to withhold or hold back (money, words, feelings).
Sense 1 : to stop (something/someone in motion)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: थामना (thaamnaa, to hold/steady), ठहराना (thahraanaa, to bring to a halt)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture flagging down an auto-rickshaw on a Delhi road: you shout ऑटो रोको! (oto roko, 'stop the auto!') and that is रोकना (roknaa) in its most physical, everyday sense -- halting motion.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बंद करना (band karnaa) when they mean 'stop a moving thing' -- रोकना (roknaa) is the natural verb for halting a car, a person walking, a bus, etc., while बंद करना (band karnaa) suits switching something off or closing it. 2) रोकना (roknaa) is transitive and takes the thing/person stopped as its direct object: गाड़ी रोको (gaadii roko, 'stop the car') -- learners sometimes insert a postposition where none is needed.
Sample Sentences:
ऑटो रोको! Stop the auto!
(oto roko!)

Sense 3 : to stop/prevent someone from doing something
verb, transitive Commonly built with a following infinitive plus से (se): उसे जाने से रोका (use jaane se rokaa, 'stopped him from going') -- the person restrained is को-marked and the action is oblique-infinitive + से (se).
Synonyms: मनाना (manaanaa, to dissuade), रोक लगाना (rok lagaanaa, to impose a restriction)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A very familiar family scene: माँ ने मुझे जाने से रोका (maan ne mujhe jaane se rokaa, 'Mother stopped me from going') -- the protective, sometimes overprotective, parental रोकना (roknaa) is practically a genre of its own in Hindi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop से (se) after the oblique infinitive: the correct frame is X को [infinitive-ने] से रोकना (roknaa), not just X को [infinitive] रोकना. 2) This sense is where रोकना (roknaa) overlaps with मनाना (manaanaa) -- रोकना (roknaa) implies stopping an action already in motion or about to happen, while मनाना (manaanaa) implies persuading someone out of an intention.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 4 : to withhold or hold back (money, words, emotion)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: दबाना (dabaanaa, to suppress, of emotion), रोक रखना (rok rakhnaa, to keep held back)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind अपने आँसू रोक न सका (apne aansoo rok n sakaa, 'couldn't hold back his tears') -- a very common emotional register in Hindi film dialogue and everyday storytelling, where feelings are described as things you physically dam up.
Common Mistakes: 1) With emotions, आँसू रोकना (aansoo roknaa, 'to hold back tears') and गुस्सा रोकना (gussaa roknaa, 'to hold back anger') are set collocations learners should absorb as chunks rather than building word by word.
