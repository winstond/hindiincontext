पंद्रह (pandrah)

numeral The number fifteen (15), used for counting, dates, ages, and quantities. Also seen spelled पन्द्रह (pandrah) with the nasal written as a half-न instead of the anusvaar; both are common.
Sense 1 : the number fifteen
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear पंद्रह (pandrah) constantly around 15 अगस्त (15 agast, 'August 15th'), भारत का स्वतंत्रता दिवस (bhaarat kaa svatantrtaa divas, 'India's Independence Day') -- it is one of those numbers every Indian schoolchild has emotionally memorized because of the date, much the way July 4 sticks in an American's head. It is also common in prices, ages, and phone-recharge amounts (पंद्रह रुपये, pandrah rupye, 'fifteen rupees').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build compound forms like "pandrah-first" for dates instead of using the correct ordinal पंद्रहवाँ (pandrahvaan, 'fifteenth'); पंद्रह (pandrah) itself is only the cardinal number, used for counting and quantities, not for ranking or naming positions in a sequence.
