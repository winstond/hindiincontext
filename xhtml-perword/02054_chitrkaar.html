चित्रकार (chitrkaar)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'painter/artist' -- someone who makes चित्र (chitr, 'pictures'), the -कार (kaar) suffix meaning 'maker/doer.'
Sense 1 : painter, artist (visual)
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: कलाकार (kalaakaar, broader: any artist), पेंटर (pentar, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: चित्रकार (chitrkaar) is the word you will see under a painting in a museum in India, right before the artist's name -- it carries the same respectful, slightly formal weight as 'artist' does in an English gallery caption. In everyday chat about a kid who loves drawing, people often say यह तो चित्रकार बन जाएगा (yah to chitrkaar ban jaaegaa, 'this one will become a painter'), so the word slips easily from formal to affectionate use.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use चित्रकार (chitrkaar) for any kind of artist, but it specifically means one who paints or draws; a musician or actor is कलाकार (kalaakaar), not चित्रकार (chitrkaar). 2) The feminine form चित्रकार (chitrkaar) itself does not change; only the verb agreement and sometimes चित्रकारा (chitrkaaraa, rare, feminine variant) mark gender in speech, so learners should rely on the sentence's verb to know the painter's gender.
