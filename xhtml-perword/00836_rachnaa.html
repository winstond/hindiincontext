रचना (rachnaa)

verb/noun Has two related lives: as a verb it means to create or compose (rachnaa), and as a noun it means a creation or composition (rachnaa as noun) -- a piece of writing, art, or the act/structure of creation itself. Also a very common girl's name in North India.
Sense 1 : to create, compose (a work of art, literature, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बनाना (banaanaa), सृजन करना (srijan karnaa, formal), गढ़ना (gadhnaa, to fashion/craft)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb used for divine or grand creation -- ब्रह्मा ने सृष्टि रची (brahmaa ne srishti rachii, 'Brahma created the universe') -- so it carries a certain weight and is reached for when the act of creating feels significant, artistic, or foundational, rather than everyday assembly or building.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बनाना (banaanaa) for everything, but रचना (rachnaa) carries a more literary, creative-artistic register -- it's what a poet does to a poem (kavitaa rachnaa, 'to compose a poem'), not what a carpenter does to a table. 2) As a transitive verb in the perfective, it takes ने (ne) marking like any other transitive verb -- e.g. कवि ने कविता रची (kavi ne kavitaa rachii, 'the poet composed a poem') -- learners sometimes forget this because the verb feels abstract/literary rather than physical.

Sense 2 : a creation, composition, or literary/artistic work
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कृति (kriti, formal/literary), सृष्टि (srishti, creation in the cosmic sense), लेखन (lekhan, writing, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word on the cover or byline of poems and short stories in Hindi textbooks and magazines -- एक रचना (ek rachnaa, 'a composition') is the standard label for a literary piece being presented to readers, much like 'a work' in English literary criticism.
Common Mistakes: 1) रचना (rachnaa) as a noun leans toward artistic/literary output -- a poem, story, or piece of music -- rather than a physical object someone built; learners sometimes overextend it to mean any 'made thing.'
