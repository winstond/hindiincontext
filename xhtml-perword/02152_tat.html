तट (tat)

noun A literary/formal word for a riverbank, lakeshore, or seacoast; in everyday speech learners will hear किनारा (kinaaraa) far more often.
Sense 1 : bank, shore, coast
noun, masculine
Synonyms: किनारा (kinaaraa), साहिल (saahil)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word constantly in the names and descriptions of India's sacred rivers -- गंगा तट (gangaa tat, 'the bank of the Ganga') is classic pilgrimage-guidebook language, evoking ghats, temples, and evening आरती (aartii) rather than a casual riverside stroll. It carries a slightly formal, almost reverential color that किनारा (kinaaraa) doesn't have.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तट (tat) in casual conversation about a riverside picnic or a beach trip, where a native speaker would almost always say किनारा (kinaaraa) instead; तट (tat) shows up more in writing, geography lessons, news reports, and poetic descriptions of rivers like गंगा (gangaa).
Sample Sentences:
गंगा तट पर हर शाम आरती होती है। An aarti is held on the bank of the Ganga every evening.
(gangaa tat par har shaam aartii hotii hai।)
