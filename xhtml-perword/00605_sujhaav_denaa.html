सुझाव देना (sujhaav denaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to suggest' or 'to give a suggestion' -- built from सुझाव (sujhaav, 'suggestion') plus the light verb देना (denaa, 'to give'). One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : to suggest, to offer a suggestion
verb, transitive The person receiving the suggestion is marked with को (ko): उसने मुझे सुझाव दिया (usne mujhe sujhaav diyaa, 'he gave me a suggestion').
Synonyms: सलाह देना (salaah denaa, to advise), राय देना (raay denaa, to give an opinion/suggest)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will hear constantly in Indian offices, classrooms, and family discussions when someone politely offers an idea rather than issuing an order -- कृपया अपना सुझाव दें (kripyaa apnaa sujhaav den, 'please give your suggestion') is a staple of formal requests, feedback forms, and meetings across Delhi-NCR workplaces.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use सुझाना (sujhaanaa) and सुझाव देना (sujhaav denaa) interchangeably without realizing सुझाना (sujhaanaa) alone already means 'to suggest' as a single verb -- सुझाव देना (sujhaav denaa) is the more common, everyday way to say it, built with the noun plus देना (denaa). 2) Since देना (denaa) is transitive, the subject takes ने (ne) in the perfective: मैंने सुझाव दिया (mainne sujhaav diyaa, 'I suggested'), not मैं सुझाव दिया (main sujhaav diyaa).

(kyaa aap mujhe koii sujhaav de sakte hain?)
