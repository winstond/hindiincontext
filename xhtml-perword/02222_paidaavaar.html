पैदावार (paidaavaar)

noun A single-sense noun meaning agricultural produce or yield -- what a field, crop, or region produces.
Sense 1 : yield, produce (agricultural)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: उपज (upaj), फ़सल (fasal, meaning scope: specifically 'crop' rather than 'output')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly on the news and in farmer conversations around Delhi-NCR's mandis (grain markets) -- गेहूँ की पैदावार (gehoon kii paidaavaar, 'wheat yield') is a staple phrase in discussions of monsoon, irrigation, and government support prices. It also gets used loosely for any region's or era's output, even non-agricultural, as a mild extension.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use फ़सल (fasal) and पैदावार (paidaavaar) interchangeably, but फ़सल (fasal) names the crop itself (wheat, rice, etc.) while पैदावार (paidaavaar) names the quantity or output produced -- you talk about अच्छी पैदावार (achchhii paidaavaar, 'good yield') even when discussing the same फ़सल (fasal).

इस साल गेहूँ की पैदावार बहुत अच्छी हुई है। This year the wheat yield has been very good.
(is saal gehoon kii paidaavaar bahut achchhii huii hai।)
