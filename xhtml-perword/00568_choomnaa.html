चूमना (choomnaa)

verb A simple transitive verb meaning 'to kiss'; only one core sense in everyday use.
Sense 1 : to kiss
verb, transitive
Synonyms: किस करना (kis karnaa, colloquial, English loan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Physical affection like kissing is generally kept private in North Indian family life, so चूमना (choomnaa) shows up more in songs, poetry, and romantic contexts than in casual descriptions of daily life -- unlike, say, hugging (गले लगाना, gale lagaanaa), which is freely mentioned between friends and family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for the English loan किस करना (kis karnaa) in casual speech; चूमना (choomnaa) is the native verb and is completely normal in both spoken and written Hindi, not a formal-only choice. 2) The person or thing kissed takes the direct object marker को (ko) when definite/specific, e.g. बच्चे को चूमा (bachche ko choomaa, 'kissed the child') -- easy to drop by analogy with English.
