बाज़ार (baazaar)

noun A single core sense: the physical marketplace or bazaar, extended naturally to mean 'the market' in an economic sense.
Sense 1 : marketplace, market area
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मंडी (mandii, wholesale/produce market), हाट (haat, rural periodic market)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every neighborhood in Delhi has its own छोटा बाज़ार (chhotaa baazaar, 'little market') for daily vegetables, milk, and household bits -- it is as much a social space as a commercial one, where you run into neighbors and haggle a little out of habit even when the price is fixed. Also spelled बाजार (baajaar) without the nuqta, especially in older or more formal printed Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैं बाज़ार में जा रहा हूँ (main baazaar men jaa rahaa hoon, 'I am going to the market') when they mean simply going out shopping -- native speakers frequently drop में (men) and just say बाज़ार जा रहा हूँ (baazaar jaa rahaa hoon), treating बाज़ार (baazaar) almost like a destination adverb rather than a location requiring a postposition. 2) Don't confuse बाज़ार (baazaar) with दुकान (dukaan) -- दुकान (dukaan) is a single shop, बाज़ार (baazaar) is the whole market area or street of shops.
Sample Sentences:

(main shaam ko baazaar jaataa hoon।)

Sense 2 : the market (economic sense: demand, trade, prices)
noun, masculine Used in abstract economic expressions such as बाज़ार में तेज़ी (baazaar men tezii, 'a boom in the market') or शेयर बाज़ार (sheyar baazaar, 'stock market').
Synonyms: मार्केट (maarket, colloquial English loan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly on the evening news -- शेयर बाज़ार गिर गया (sheyar baazaar gir gayaa, 'the stock market fell') -- where बाज़ार (baazaar) stands in for the whole abstract system of trade and prices, much like English 'the market' does.
