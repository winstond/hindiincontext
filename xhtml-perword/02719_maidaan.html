मैदान (maidaan)

noun A concrete open space -- a field, ground, or plain -- that extends into a common figurative sense of 'arena' or 'field of action' (as in a contest or battle). One word covers both the physical park down the street and the metaphorical battlefield.
Sense 1 : open ground, field, park
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खेल का मैदान (khel kaa maidaan, playground/sports field), प्रांगण (praangan, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi-NCR neighborhood has 'the मैदान (maidaan)' -- the local ground where kids play cricket in the evening, morning walkers do their rounds, and election rallies or Ramlila stages get set up. It is a genuinely communal space in a way 'field' in English doesn't quite capture.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पार्क (paark) for any green space, but मैदान (maidaan) is the more native word for a large open patch used for sports, walking, or gathering -- not necessarily landscaped like a formal park. 2) Note the fixed phrase मैदान में जाना (maidaan men jaanaa, to go out to the field/grounds), which locals use for heading out to play or exercise.

Sense 2 : arena, field of action/battle (figurative)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रणभूमि (ranbhoomi, literary, specifically battlefield), क्षेत्र (kshetr, field/domain)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense turns any contest -- a cricket match, an election, a business rivalry -- into a 'field' the way English does with 'battleground,' but Hindi leans on it more heavily in everyday commentary: cricket commentators and political reporters both use मैदान (maidaan) constantly for 'the contest is on.'
Common Mistakes: 1) मैदान मारना (maidaan maarnaa, 'to win the field') and मैदान छोड़ना (maidaan chhodnaa, 'to abandon the field, give up/flee') are set idioms -- don't translate them literally as being about physical ground when the context is a contest, election, or argument.

Sense 3 : plain (geographic)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: समतल भूमि (samtal bhoomi, formal/geographic)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense in geography class or the news: गंगा का मैदान (gangaa kaa maidaan, 'the Gangetic plain') is the standard term for the great northern plains of India.
