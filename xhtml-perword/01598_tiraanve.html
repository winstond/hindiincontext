तिरानवे (tiraanve)

numeral The cardinal number 93 (ninety-three) in Hindi.
Sense 1 : the number 93
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers above twenty, तिरानवे (tiraanve) just has to be learned as a standalone item -- native speakers rattle these off from rote memorization drilled in school, not from any tens-plus-units formula the way English speakers build 'ninety-three' from 'ninety' and 'three'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with similar-sounding numbers in the 90s range like तिरासी (tiraasii, 83) or तिरानबे (tiraanbe) as a spelling variant of this same number -- Hindi numerals 21-99 must each be memorized individually since there is no fully predictable pattern the way there is in English.
