संतुलन (santulan)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'balance' or 'equilibrium', used both literally (physical balance) and figuratively (balance in life, mind, budgets, relationships).
Sense 1 : balance, equilibrium
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तवाज़ुन (tavaazun, literary), बैलेंस (bailens, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi speakers love this word for talking about life-balance -- काम और ज़िंदगी में संतुलन (kaam aur zindgii men santulan, 'balance between work and life') is a very common phrase in everyday advice-giving, much like in English. You'll also hear it constantly in the context of cycling, walking on a narrow ledge, or a tightrope walker (नट, nat) at a village fair keeping his संतुलन (santulan) -- a vivid, physical image Delhi kids grow up with at melas.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say संतुलन करना (santulan karnaa) when they mean 'to maintain balance' -- the natural verb pairing is संतुलन बनाना (santulan banaanaa, 'to create/maintain balance') or संतुलन रखना (santulan rakhnaa, 'to keep balance'), not करना (karnaa). 2) 'To lose balance' is संतुलन बिगड़ना (santulan bigadnaa, balance becomes disturbed) or संतुलन खोना (santulan khonaa, to lose balance) -- बिगड़ना (bigadnaa) treats the loss of balance as something that happens to you, an involuntary event, rather than something you actively do.

साइकिल चलाते समय अपना संतुलन बनाए रखो। Keep your balance while riding the bicycle.
(saaikil chalaate samay apnaa santulan banaae rakho।)
