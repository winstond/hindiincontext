तिहत्तर (tihattar)

numeral The cardinal number 73 (English: 73). A single-sense numeral like all Hindi numbers 1-99, each of which is memorized individually rather than built from a regular pattern.
Sense 1 : the number seventy-three
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Numbers like तिहत्तर (tihattar) show up constantly in everyday life -- ages, prices, cricket scores, bus numbers -- so it is worth drilling the 70s decade as a block rather than translating from English on the fly, since native speakers retrieve these as single memorized words, not sums.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to derive तिहत्तर (tihattar) logically from तीन (tiin, 'three') and सत्तर (sattar, 'seventy') the way English builds 'seventy-three' -- Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 are each a fused, irregular form that must be learned individually rather than composed on the fly. 2) It is easy to mix up with तिरासी (tiraasii, '83') or सत्तर (sattar, '70') since the numbers in this range share overlapping sounds; careful listening practice with the 70s block helps.
