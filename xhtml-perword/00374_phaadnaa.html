फाड़ना (phaadnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning 'to tear/rip' something; it also shows up in a few vivid colloquial extensions built on the same image of tearing or ripping something apart.
Sense 1 : to tear, rip (paper, cloth, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: चीरना (chiirnaa, often for cutting/slicing lengthwise), फटना (phatnaa, intransitive counterpart)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a kid tearing a page out of a notebook, कागज़ फाड़ना (kaagaz phaadnaa, 'to tear paper') -- this is the most basic, everyday use of the verb, the mental image every Hindi speaker has for it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for फटना (phatnaa) when they mean फाड़ना (phaadnaa) -- फटना (phatnaa) is intransitive ('the paper tore by itself'), while फाड़ना (phaadnaa) needs an agent who does the tearing ('he tore the paper').

बच्चे ने किताब का पन्ना फाड़ दिया। The child tore a page out of the book.
(bachche ne kitaab kaa pannaa phaad diyaa।)

Sense 2 : to shout loudly, bawl (colloquial, of a voice)
verb, transitive Almost always appears as the set phrase गला फाड़कर (galaa phaadkar, 'shouting at the top of one's voice') or गला फाड़ना (galaa phaadnaa) rather than फाड़ना (phaadnaa) alone in this sense.
Synonyms: चिल्लाना (chillaanaa), गला फाड़ना (galaa phaadnaa, idiomatic phrase, 'to tear one's throat shouting')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: गला फाड़कर चिल्लाना (galaa phaadkar chillaanaa, 'to scream till your throat tears') is the classic combination -- Hindi imagines the throat itself being ripped apart by the effort of shouting, a nice example of how the language likes to locate effort in the body.

Sense 3 : to run very fast, speed (colloquial, of vehicles/running)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उड़ना (udnaa, figurative, 'to fly'), भगाना (bhagaanaa, colloquial)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this among friends about a motorbike or a car: गाड़ी फाड़ रहा है (gaadii phaad rahaa hai, 'he's tearing along in the car') -- it is casual, slangy, and common among younger Delhi speakers describing reckless or thrilling speed.
