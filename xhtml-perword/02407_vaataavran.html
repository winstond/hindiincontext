वातावरण (vaataavran)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'atmosphere' or 'environment' -- used both literally (the physical atmosphere, climate, surroundings) and figuratively (the mood or ambience of a place or situation).
Sense 1 : environment, atmosphere, ambience
noun, masculine
Synonyms: परिवेश (parivesh), माहौल (maahaul), वायुमंडल (vaayumandal, the physical atmosphere specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word does a lot of work in Hindi -- a teacher might talk about वातावरण (vaataavran) प्रदूषण (vaataavran pradooshan, 'environmental pollution') in a civics class, and in the very next breath a friend might describe a wedding's वातावरण (vaataavran) as खुशनुमा (khushnumaa, 'pleasant'). Think of it as covering both 'the air around us' and 'the feeling in the air' -- English needs two different words (environment, atmosphere) where Hindi comfortably uses one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for माहौल (maahaul) in casual speech and वातावरण (vaataavran) in writing or formal talk -- both are correct, but वातावरण (vaataavran) sounds a touch more considered, like something you'd say in a school essay or a news report rather than while chatting with friends. 2) Don't confuse it with जलवायु (jalvaayu), which specifically means 'climate' in the meteorological sense (rainfall, temperature patterns over time) -- वातावरण (vaataavran) is broader and can describe a single day's air or a room's mood, not just long-term climate.

प्रदूषण की वजह से शहर का वातावरण खराब होता जा रहा है। Due to pollution, the city's environment is getting worse.
(pradooshan kii vajah se shahar kaa vaataavran kharaab hotaa jaa rahaa hai।)
