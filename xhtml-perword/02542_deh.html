देह (deh)

noun A literary/formal word for the physical body, used mostly in writing, spiritual contexts, and set expressions rather than everyday chit-chat about one's body.
Sense 1 : the physical body
noun, feminine
Synonyms: शरीर (shariir), तन (tan, literary), बदन (badan)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet देह (deh) far more in spiritual and philosophical talk -- discourses on the soul (आत्मा, aatmaa) versus the body -- than in a doctor's office, where शरीर (shariir) is the word people actually reach for. It also carries a slightly formal, almost reverent tone, which is why देहांत (dehaant) is the respectful word for death rather than something blunter.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners reach for देह (deh) in casual conversation where शरीर (shariir) is the natural everyday choice; देह (deh) sounds bookish or poetic in ordinary speech. 2) It shows up often in compound/set phrases like देहदान (dehdaan, 'body donation') and देहांत (dehaant, 'passing away, lit. departure of the body') -- learners sometimes miss that देहांत (dehaant) is the polite way to say someone died, not a literal 'body-related' statement.

(yah deh naashvaan hai, aatmaa amar hai।)
