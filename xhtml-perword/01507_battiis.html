बत्तीस (battiis)

numeral The number 32 (thirty-two), used for counting and as a numeral adjective.
Sense 1 : the number thirty-two
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 1 to 100 are each their own memorized word rather than built compositionally like English 'thirty-two,' so learners typically just have to drill बत्तीस (battiis) as a standalone item rather than deriving it from तीस (tiis, 'thirty') and दो (do, 'two'). A common colloquial idiom worth knowing separately is बत्तीसी (battiisii), literally 'the set of thirty-two' referring to the full set of teeth, used in phrases like बत्तीसी दिखाना (battiisii dikhaanaa, 'to show one's teeth,' i.e. to grin broadly).
Common Mistakes: 1) Like other Hindi numerals, बत्तीस (battiis) precedes the noun it counts and does not change form for gender or case: बत्तीस किताबें (battiis kitaaben, 'thirty-two books') and बत्तीस लड़के (battiis ladke, 'thirty-two boys') both use the same form.
