नियमित रूप से (niymit roop se)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'regularly, on a regular basis' -- built from नियमित (niymit, 'regular') plus रूप से (roop se, 'in a manner/way').
Sense 1 : regularly, on a regular basis
adverb Functions as a manner adverb built on the pattern [adjective] + रूप से (roop se); this pattern productively turns many Sanskritic adjectives into adverbs, e.g. स्वाभाविक रूप से (svaabhaavik roop se, 'naturally').
Synonyms: रोज़ (roz, daily), हमेशा (hameshaa, meaning scope: emphasizes constancy more than schedule), समय पर (samay par, meaning scope: emphasizes punctuality rather than repetition)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see नियमित रूप से (niymit roop se) constantly in written contexts -- health advice, school notices, government circulars -- telling people to exercise, take medicine, or attend regularly. In spoken Delhi-NCR Hindi, though, people usually reach for something simpler like रोज़ (roz) or हर रोज़ (har roz) to say the same thing, so this phrase has a slightly formal, 'notice board' flavor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop रूप से (roop se) and just say नियमित (niymit) as if it were the adverb itself, but नियमित (niymit) alone is an adjective ('regular') and needs a noun to modify -- to say 'regularly' as an adverb, रूप से (roop se) is needed. 2) This phrase is noticeably more formal/written-sounding than रोज़ (roz) or हर रोज़ (har roz), which are what people actually say in casual daily conversation.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया अपनी दवाई नियमित रूप से लें। Please take your medicine regularly.
(kripyaa apnii davaaii niymit roop se len।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
डॉक्टर ने कहा कि उन्हें नियमित रूप से व्यायाम करना चाहिए। The doctor said that he should exercise regularly.
(doktar ne kahaa ki unhen niymit roop se vyaayaam karnaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
सभी छात्रों को नियमित रूप से कक्षा में उपस्थित रहना अनिवार्य है। It is mandatory for all students to attend class regularly.
(sabhii chhaatron ko niymit roop se kakshaa men upasthit rahnaa anivaary hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
इस मशीन की जाँच नियमित रूप से की जाती है। This machine's inspection is carried out regularly.
(is mashiin kii jaanch niymit roop se kii jaatii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
अगर वह नियमित रूप से पढ़ता तो शायद पास हो जाता। If he had studied regularly, he might have passed.
(agar vah niymit roop se padhtaa to shaayad paas ho jaataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
कर्मचारियों को समय पर और नियमित रूप से वेतन मिलना चाहिए। Employees should receive their salary on time and on a regular basis.
(karmchaariyon ko samay par aur niymit roop se vetan milnaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
कंपनी का सुझाव है कि उपकरणों की सफ़ाई नियमित रूप से होनी चाहिए। The company's suggestion is that the equipment's cleaning should happen regularly.
(kampnii kaa sujhaav hai ki upkarnon kii safaaii niymit roop se honii chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
स्कूल के नोटिस में लिखा था कि अभिभावक नियमित रूप से बैठकों में शामिल हों। The school notice stated that parents should attend meetings regularly.
(skool ke notis men likhaa thaa ki abhibhaavak niymit roop se baithkon men shaamil hon।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive
सरकारी परिपत्र में कहा गया है कि रिपोर्ट नियमित रूप से भेजी जाए। The government circular states that the report should be sent regularly.
(sarkaarii paripatr men kahaa gayaa hai ki riport niymit roop se bhejii jaae।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
