बैलगाड़ी (bailgaadii)

noun A compound noun combining बैल (bail, 'ox/bullock') and गाड़ी (gaadii, 'cart/vehicle'); it has a single, concrete meaning.
Sense 1 : bullock cart
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बैलगाड़ा (bailgaadaa, less common variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बैलगाड़ी (bailgaadii) is a classic image of rural India, still seen in villages carrying grain, sugarcane, or wedding parties, and it shows up often in Hindi textbooks, folk songs, and paintings as a symbol of simple village life. In everyday city conversation it is now mostly a nostalgic or literary reference rather than something people use daily.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use गाड़ी (gaadii) alone expecting it to be understood as a bullock cart, but गाड़ी (gaadii) by itself defaults to a car or generic vehicle in modern speech; the बैल- (bail) prefix is necessary to specify the animal-drawn cart.
