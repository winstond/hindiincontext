आवश्यक (aavashyak)

adjective A single-sense formal adjective meaning 'necessary/essential', commonly paired with or contrasted against the everyday word ज़रूरी (zaroorii).
Sense 1 : necessary, essential
adjective
Synonyms: ज़रूरी (zaroorii), अनिवार्य (anivaary, stronger: mandatory/compulsory)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet आवश्यक (aavashyak) constantly in official notices, exam instructions, and news broadaasts -- 'सूचना: उपस्थिति आवश्यक है' (soochnaa: upasthiti aavashyak hai, 'Notice: attendance is necessary') is the kind of register it belongs to. In daily conversation among friends or family, most Delhi speakers reach for ज़रूरी (zaroorii) instead, so think of आवश्यक (aavashyak) as the 'office memo and textbook' twin of a very common everyday word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use आवश्यक (aavashyak) and ज़रूरी (zaroorii) as if they were in different registers only in theory; in practice आवश्यक (aavashyak) sounds noticeably more formal or written, so using it in casual chat about, say, buying milk can come across as stiff -- ज़रूरी (zaroorii) is the natural spoken choice there. 2) With चाहिए (chaahie), learners sometimes double up the sense of obligation, producing overly heavy phrasing like यह आवश्यक होना चाहिए (yah aavashyak honaa chaahie, 'this should be necessary') when a simpler construction is more natural.
Sample Sentences:
सभी छात्रों के लिए यह आवश्यक है कि वे समय पर पहुँचें। It is necessary for all students to arrive on time.
(sabhii chhaatron ke lie yah aavashyak hai ki ve samay par pahunchen।)
