निपटना (nipatnaa)

verb One main verb of 'getting done with' something -- finishing a task, settling a matter, or (colloquially and darkly) disposing of someone/something for good.
Sense 1 : to finish, get done with (a task, work)
verb, intransitive Often used with से: काम से निपटना (kaam se nipatnaa, 'to get done with the work') -- the task takes से, not को.
Synonyms: खत्म करना (khatm karnaa), निबटना (nibatnaa, variant form), पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb every busy Delhi office-goer uses at the end of the day: 'aaj ka kaam nipata diya' -- said with real relief. It pairs constantly with the compound-verb helper देना (denaa) -- निपटा देना (niptaa denaa) -- to add a sense of finality, 'get it over and done with.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the task with को (ko) by analogy with English 'finish the work' -- but निपटना (nipatnaa) wants से (se): काम से निपटना (kaam se nipatnaa), not काम को निपटना. 2) This sense is intransitive despite feeling like it takes an object in English -- the 'object' is actually marked with से (se) as an oblique, so there is no ने (ne) in the perfective: मैंने निपटा नहीं, मैं निपटा (main niptaa).
Sample Sentences:
मैंने आज का सारा काम निपटा दिया। I finished off all of today's work.
(mainne aaj kaa saaraa kaam niptaa diyaa।)

Sense 2 : to settle, resolve (a dispute, matter, account)
verb, intransitive Common in the frame मामला निपटना (maamlaa nipatnaa, 'the matter to get settled') or झगड़ा निपटना (jhagdaa nipatnaa, 'the quarrel to get settled') -- the dispute itself is the grammatical subject.
Synonyms: सुलझाना (suljhaanaa), सुलटाना (sultaanaa, variant/regional), हल करना (hal karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around family property disputes and business accounts -- हिसाब निपट गया (hisaab nipat gayaa, 'the accounts are settled') has that satisfying sense of a ledger finally closed, no loose ends.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is the intransitive counterpart of निपटाना (niptaanaa) -- learners reach for निपटना (nipatnaa) when they actually mean 'to settle something' (a transitive act), which needs निपटाना (niptaanaa) instead: झगड़ा निपटाया (jhagdaa niptaayaa, 'settled the quarrel') vs. झगड़ा निपट गया (jhagdaa nipat gayaa, 'the quarrel got settled').

Sense 3 : to deal with, take care of (a person), sometimes euphemistically 'do away with'
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the person: उससे निपट लूँगा (usse nipat loongaa, 'I'll deal with him') -- register ranges from a boss handling a difficult client to gangster-movie threats.
Synonyms: ठिकाने लगाना (thikaane lagaanaa, euphemistic, colloquial), निबटना (nibatnaa, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb of Bollywood villain threats -- 'main usse nipat lunga' -- but it is equally at home said by an annoyed parent about a misbehaving relative at a wedding. The ambiguity is the fun of it.
Common Mistakes: 1) The menacing 'finish him off' reading is entirely tone-dependent -- the same sentence उससे निपट लूँगा (usse nipat loongaa) can mean 'I'll sort things out with him' in an office context or something far more sinister in a thriller, so learners should gauge context, not assume danger by default.

पहले उस लड़के से निपट लो, फिर बात करते हैं। Deal with that guy first, then we'll talk.
