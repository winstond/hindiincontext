टक्कर (takkar)

noun Primarily a physical or figurative 'collision/clash' word; also lives in a very common idiom about being someone's 'match' or equal.
Sense 1 : collision, crash
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भिड़ंत (bhidant), दुर्घटना (durghatnaa, broader: accident in general)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in traffic-news contexts: दो गाड़ियों में टक्कर हो गई (do gaadiyon men takkar ho gaii, 'there was a collision between two cars') is the standard way Delhi radio/TV reports a crash. The related verb टकराना (takraanaa) is worth learning alongside this noun.
Common Mistakes: 1) टक्कर (takkar) itself is a noun, not a verb -- learners often try to conjugate it directly; the action is expressed with a light verb, as in टक्कर मारना (takkar maarnaa, 'to ram into') or टक्कर होना (takkar honaa, 'a collision to occur'). 2) With टक्कर होना (takkar honaa) the vehicles/people involved take से (se), not को (ko) or पर (par): गाड़ी ट्रक से टकराई (gaadii trak se takraaii, 'the car collided with the truck').
Sample Sentences:
आज सुबह दो गाड़ियों में टक्कर हो गई। This morning there was a collision between two cars.
(aaj subah do gaadiyon men takkar ho gaii।)

Sense 2 : rivalry, competing match (figurative clash)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मुकाबला (mukaablaa), प्रतिस्पर्धा (pratispardhaa, more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you hear in sports commentary and everyday boasting: वो मेरी टक्कर का नहीं है (vo merii takkar kaa nahiin hai, 'he's not in my league') is a very common put-down or compliment depending on tone. It extends naturally from the physical collision sense -- two forces meeting head-on becomes two rivals meeting as equals.
Common Mistakes: 1) The set phrase टक्कर देना (takkar denaa) means 'to rival/match someone,' and टक्कर का (takkar kaa) means 'a match for, equal in strength to' -- learners sometimes translate this too literally as 'to give a collision' instead of recognizing the idiom. 2) The person being rivaled is marked with को (ko): उसे टक्कर देना (use takkar denaa, 'to rival him/her').
