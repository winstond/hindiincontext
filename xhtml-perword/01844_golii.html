गोली (golii)

noun Covers three everyday senses: a bullet, a round pill/tablet, and a small round ball used in the children's marble game.
Sense 1 : bullet
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear गोली मारना (golii maarnaa, 'to shoot someone') constantly in news reports and Bollywood dialogue about crime and violence; it is the standard, unmarked way to say 'shot,' not a dramatic flourish.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse गोली (golii, 'bullet') with बंदूक (bandook, 'gun') -- the gun fires the गोली (golii), it is not the gun itself. 2) The common phrase गोली मारना (golii maarnaa, 'to shoot') uses मारना (maarnaa, 'to hit/kill') rather than चलाना (chalaanaa) for the everyday sense of shooting someone.
Sample Sentences:

(pulis ne chor ko golii maar dii।)

Sense 2 : pill, tablet (medicine)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: टैबलेट (taiblet, borrowed from English, common for factory-made tablets), दवा (davaa, meaning scope: medicine generally, not just pills)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every household in Delhi-NCR has a मम्मी (mammii, 'mom') or दादी (daadii, 'grandmother') who asks सिर दर्द है? गोली ले लो (sir dard hai? golii le lo, 'You have a headache? Take a pill') -- गोली (golii) is the default word for any small tablet, whether it's a painkiller or a vitamin.
Common Mistakes: 1) गोली (golii) is used for solid round tablets and pills; syrups or liquid medicine are called दवा (davaa) or सिरप (sirap), never गोली (golii).

Sense 3 : marble (toy)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कंचा (kanchaa, the more common word for the toy marble in North India)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: गोली-कंचे खेलना (golii-kanche khelnaa, 'to play marbles') is a classic gully (street) game of Indian childhood, played by flicking small glass or clay balls along the ground -- mentioning it to an older Delhi resident often triggers a nostalgic story.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense गोली (golii) is somewhat regional/dated next to the more widespread कंचा (kanchaa); if a learner hears गोली-कंचा (golii-kanchaa) together, both refer to the same game/object.
