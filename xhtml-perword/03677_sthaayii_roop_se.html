स्थायी रूप से (sthaayii roop se)

adverb A compound adverbial phrase meaning 'permanently' or 'on a permanent basis' -- built from स्थायी (sthaayii, 'permanent') plus रूप से (roop se, 'in the manner/form of'), the same से-frame you see in अस्थायी रूप से (asthaayii roop se, 'temporarily').
Sense 1 : permanently, on a permanent basis
adverb Modifies the verb of the clause, typically placed just before it, e.g. यहाँ स्थायी रूप से रहना (yahaan sthaayii roop se rahnaa, 'to live here permanently').
Synonyms: हमेशा के लिए (hameshaa ke lie, 'for good'), सदा के लिए (sadaa ke lie, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in bureaucratic and everyday contexts alike -- job offers, visa forms, and residence permits all ask whether your stay is स्थायी (sthaayii) or अस्थायी (asthaayii, 'temporary'). It pairs naturally with its opposite, अस्थायी रूप से (asthaayii roop se), and Hindi speakers reach for this ...रूप से (roop se) frame to turn many adjectives into adverbs of manner.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use स्थायी (sthaayii) alone as if it were the adverb; but without रूप से (roop se) it reads as a bare adjective ('permanent [thing]') rather than 'permanently.' 2) Confusing this with हमेशा (hameshaa, 'always'): स्थायी रूप से (sthaayii roop se) describes a lasting state or change (a job, a move, a settlement), while हमेशा (hameshaa) describes frequency or habitual recurrence.
