वेश्या (veshyaa)

noun A formal/clinical noun meaning 'prostitute, sex worker' -- one sense, used mainly in written, legal, or journalistic registers rather than casual speech.
Sense 1 : prostitute, sex worker
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रंडी (randii, vulgar/slang), तवायफ़ (tavaayaf, historical, refers to courtesans/performers), सेक्स वर्कर (seks varkar, modern/formal English loan used in NGO and legal contexts)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word sits firmly in formal registers -- news reports, legal documents, sociological writing -- and carries a clinical distance rather than warmth or slang. In everyday conversation, Hindi speakers tend to avoid naming the profession directly at all, using vague or euphemistic phrasing instead, which tells you something about how sensitive the topic remains in mainstream discourse.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for this word in casual conversation, but it belongs to formal, legal, or journalistic registers (veshyaa) -- native speakers rarely use it in everyday speech, preferring euphemism or avoiding the topic directly. 2) Do not confuse with रंडी (randii), which carries the same core meaning but is a crude insult in casual speech, not a neutral descriptor.
