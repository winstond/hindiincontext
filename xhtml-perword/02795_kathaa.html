कथा (kathaa)

noun A noun centered on 'story/narrative,' ranging from an everyday tale to a formal religious discourse or recitation (kathaa).
Sense 1 : story, tale, narrative
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कहानी (kahaanii), किस्सा (kissaa), दास्तान (daastaan, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of कथा (kathaa) as the word you'd use for a story with a lesson or a traditional flavor -- panchatantra-style animal fables, moral tales grandparents tell, or stories with a spiritual undertone. For a bedtime story or a Bollywood plot, कहानी (kahaanii) is what you'll actually hear.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat कथा (kathaa) and कहानी (kahaanii) as fully interchangeable; कथा (kathaa) leans more formal or moral/instructive, while कहानी (kahaanii) is the default word for a story told casually, in books, or to children.

Sense 2 : religious discourse, ritual recitation/narration (e.g. Satyanarayan katha)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रवचन (pravchan, formal)
Frequency: devotional
Cultural Note: This is the कथा (kathaa) you hear about at home when someone says 'आज घर में कथा है' (aaj ghar men kathaa hai, 'there's a katha at home today') -- a priest or elder narrates stories of a deity's deeds, often tied to a vrat (vrat, fast) like सत्यनारायण कथा (satynaaraayan kathaa). It's a social and devotional event, not just a reading.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense usually appears with verbs like सुनना (sunnaa) and कहना/करना (kahnaa/karnaa) -- लोग कथा सुनते हैं (log kathaa sunte hain, 'people listen to the katha') rather than पढ़ना (padhnaa), since it is traditionally recited aloud, not silently read.
