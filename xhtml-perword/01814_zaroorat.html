ज़रूरत (zaroorat)

noun A single core sense: 'need, necessity.' Also spelled जरूरत (jaroorat) without the nuqta -- both spellings are common; this entry stores the submitted form with the nuqta.
Sense 1 : need, necessity, requirement
noun, feminine Most common in the construction X को Y की ज़रूरत है (X ko Y kii zaroorat hai, 'X needs Y'), a dative-subject frame parallel to चाहिए (chaahie) and पसंद (pasand) constructions.
Synonyms: आवश्यकता (aavashyaktaa, formal), ज़रूरत (zaroorat, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word is everywhere in daily Delhi life, from a shopkeeper asking क्या ज़रूरत है? (kyaa zaroorat hai?, 'what do you need?') to a mother telling her kid ज़रूरत से ज़्यादा मत खाओ (zaroorat se zyaadaa mat khaao, 'don't eat more than you need'). Note the related adjective ज़रूरी (zaroorii, 'necessary/important'), which shares the root but functions quite differently in a sentence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मुझे ज़रूरत है पानी (mujhe zaroorat hai paanii) instead of the correct word order मुझे पानी की ज़रूरत है (mujhe paanii kii zaroorat hai, 'I need water') -- the needed thing takes की (kii) and comes before ज़रूरत (zaroorat). 2) Confusing ज़रूरत होना (zaroorat honaa, 'to need') with ज़रूरी होना (zaroorii honaa, 'to be necessary') -- the noun takes है/होना (hai/honaa) with की (kii), while the adjective ज़रूरी (zaroorii) directly describes the thing itself, as in यह ज़रूरी है (yah zaroorii hai, 'this is necessary').
Sample Sentences:
मुझे पैसों की ज़रूरत है। I need money.
(mujhe paison kii zaroorat hai।)
