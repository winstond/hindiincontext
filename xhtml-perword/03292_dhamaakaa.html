धमाका (dhamaakaa)

noun Primarily means a literal 'explosion' or 'bang' (a bomb blast, a loud noise), and by extension a figurative 'sensation' or 'blockbuster' impact -- something that creates a stir.
Sense 1 : explosion, loud bang
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विस्फोट (visphot, formal), धड़ाका (dhadaakaa, variant form/onomatopoeic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around Diwali -- पटाकों का धमाका (pataakon kaa dhamaakaa, 'the bang of firecrackers') -- and just as often in grim news reports about bomb blasts, so context does a lot of work in telling you which flavor is meant.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for विस्फोट (visphot) by default because it looks more 'dictionary-official,' but धमाका (dhamaakaa) is what people actually say in the news and on the street for bomb blasts, firecrackers, or any loud bang.
Sample Sentences:
कल रात बाजार में एक जोरदार धमाका हुआ। Last night there was a loud explosion in the market.
(kal raat baajaar men ek jordaar dhamaakaa huaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
दिवाली पर पटाकों का धमाका पूरी रात सुनाई देता है। On Diwali, the bang of firecrackers can be heard all night.
(divaalii par pataakon kaa dhamaakaa poorii raat sunaaii detaa hai।)

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(agar gais silendar phattaa to kitnaa badaa dhamaakaa hotaa!)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : sensation, blockbuster impact
noun, masculine Common in the phrase धमाका करना (dhamaakaa karnaa, 'to create a sensation'), used for a hit film, a shocking announcement, or a big splashy success.
Synonyms: सनसनी (sanasnii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Bollywood posters and TV promos love this word -- a film's first-day collection or a surprise plot twist gets called a धमाका (dhamaakaa), the same word used for a literal blast, so the metaphor of 'explosive impact' carries over neatly into English too.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is almost always paired with करना (karnaa) as a light-verb frame -- learners sometimes try to use धमाका (dhamaakaa) alone as if it were an adjective meaning 'sensational,' but it stays a noun and needs the verb.
Sample Sentences:
इस फिल्म ने बॉक्स ऑफिस पर धमाका कर दिया। This film created a sensation at the box office.
(is philm ne boks ophis par dhamaakaa kar diyaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
उसकी नई एल्बम ने संगीत जगत में धमाका मचा दिया है। Her new album has created a sensation in the music world.
(uskii naii elbam ne sangiit jagat men dhamaakaa machaa diyaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
देखना, अगली रिलीज़ जरूर धमाका करेगी। You'll see, the next release will definitely be a blockbuster hit.
(dekhnaa, aglii riliiz jaroor dhamaakaa karegii।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
अगर वह डेब्यू फिल्म फ्लॉप न होती, तो निर्देशक अपना पहला धमाका कर चुका होता। If that debut film hadn't flopped, the director would have already scored his first sensational hit.
(agar vah debyoo philm phlop n hotii, to nirdeshak apnaa pahlaa dhamaakaa kar chukaa hotaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
पहले दिन की कमाई ने तो पूरे इंडस्ट्री में धमाका मचा दिया। The first-day collection created an absolute sensation across the whole industry.
(pahle din kii kamaaii ne to poore indastrii men dhamaakaa machaa diyaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
क्या यह ट्विस्ट सच में इतना बड़ा धमाका है जितना प्रोमो में दिखाया गया? Is this twist really as big a bombshell as it was shown to be in the promo?
(kyaa yah tvist sach men itnaa badaa dhamaakaa hai jitnaa promo men dikhaayaa gayaa?)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
निर्माता चाहते हैं कि सीक्वल पहले वाले से भी बड़ा धमाका करे। The producers want the sequel to create an even bigger sensation than the first one.
(nirmaataa chaahte hain ki siikval pahle vaale se bhii badaa dhamaakaa kare।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
समीक्षकों के मुताबिक इस सीज़न का सबसे बड़ा धमाका यही शो है। According to critics, this show is the biggest sensation of the season.
(samiikshkon ke mutaabik is siizan kaa sabse badaa dhamaakaa yahii sho hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
पत्रकार सम्मेलन में निर्देशक ने कहा, "अगली फिल्म से धमाका मचाना ही हमारा लक्ष्य है।" At the press conference, the director said, "Creating a sensation with our next film is our only goal."
(patrkaar sammelan men nirdeshak ne kahaa, "aglii philm se dhamaakaa machaanaa hii hamaaraa lakshy hai।")
