के बाद (ke baad)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'after' -- used to sequence events or mark a point in time following something. Governs the oblique case on whatever precedes it.
Sense 1 : after (in time or sequence)
postposition Attaches to a noun, pronoun, or verb stem + के (ke) construction (e.g. खाने के बाद (khaane ke baad, 'after eating')); the word before के बाद (ke baad) goes into the oblique case.
Synonyms: उपरांत (upraant, formal/literary), पश्चात (pashchaat, formal/literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear के बाद (ke baad) constantly in daily narration -- खाना खाने के बाद (khaanaa khaane ke baad, 'after eating food'), स्कूल के बाद (skool ke baad, 'after school') -- it is the default way Hindi speakers sequence their day. Its opposite number, से पहले (se pahle, 'before'), is worth learning alongside it since the two form a natural pair in storytelling and instructions.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use बाद में (baad men, 'afterward') interchangeably with के बाद (ke baad), but बाद में (baad men) stands alone as 'later/afterward' and does not take an object the way के बाद (ke baad) does -- you cannot say 'खाने बाद में' when you mean 'after eating.' 2) With verbs, learners forget the infinitive must be marked with के (ke) before बाद (baad), e.g. आने के बाद (aane ke baad, 'after coming'), not just आना बाद (aanaa baad).
