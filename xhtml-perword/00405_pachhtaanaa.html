पछताना (pachhtaanaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning to regret or repent -- the everyday word for feeling sorry about something you did or failed to do.
Sense 1 : to regret, to repent
verb, intransitive Commonly used with बाद में (baad men, 'later') to set up the classic warning shape: do X now, or you'll regret it later.
Synonyms: अफ़सोस करना (afsos karnaa), पश्चाताप करना (pashchaataap karnaa, formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word behind every elder's favorite warning: अभी नहीं समझोगे तो बाद में पछताओगे (abhii nahiin samjhoge to baad men pachhtaaoge, 'if you don't understand now, you'll regret it later') -- a classic scolding rhythm you'll hear from parents, teachers, and worried aunties alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अफ़सोस करना (afsos karnaa) by default since it is taught early, but पछताना (pachhtaanaa) is the more natural, punchier verb in warnings and scoldings like बाद में पछताओगे (baad men pachhtaaoge, 'you'll regret it later'). 2) It is intransitive and does not take ने (ne) in the past tense -- मैंने पछताया (mainne pachhtaayaa) is wrong; the correct form is मैं पछताया (main pachhtaayaa).
