भाग्यवान (bhaagyvaan)

adjective An adjective meaning 'fortunate, lucky' -- built from भाग्य (bhaagy, 'fate/fortune') plus the possessive suffix -वान (vaan, 'possessing'), so it literally means 'one who possesses good fortune.'
Sense 1 : fortunate, lucky (blessed by fate)
adjective
Synonyms: खुशनसीब (khushansiib), भाग्यशाली (bhaagyshaalii), किस्मत वाला (kismat vaalaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear this word in blessings from elders, like भाग्यवान बनो (bhaagyvaan bano, 'may you be fortunate'), especially directed at children or newlyweds -- it carries a warmer, more devotional flavor than the everyday भाग्यशाली (bhaagyshaalii). It shares its root भाग्य (bhaagy) with common expressions like भाग्य में लिखा है (bhaagy men likhaa hai, 'it is written in one's fate'), reflecting the deep cultural belief that fortune is something pre-written rather than earned.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for भाग्यशाली (bhaagyshaalii) by default since it is more common in daily speech; भाग्यवान (bhaagyvaan) is used just as correctly but carries a slightly more old-fashioned, blessing-like ring, often heard from elders. 2) Some learners try to use it as a noun ('a lucky person'); it functions strictly as a descriptive adjective, so it needs a noun to modify or a copula like है (hai) to complete the sentence.
Sample Sentences:
तुम बहुत भाग्यवान हो कि तुम्हें ऐसे माता-पिता मिले। You are very fortunate that you got such parents.
(tum bahut bhaagyvaan ho ki tumhen aise maataa-pitaa mile।)
