निगम (nigam)

noun Mainly a civic/administrative noun meaning 'corporation' or 'corporate body' (as in a municipal or public-sector corporation); it also has a rarer literary/religious sense referring to the Vedas or scriptural texts.
Sense 1 : corporation, civic body
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संगठन (sangthan, organization, broader meaning), परिषद (parishad, council, related but distinct)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: If you have lived in or visited Delhi, you have seen this word on the sides of garbage trucks and water tankers stamped MCD -- दिल्ली नगर निगम (dillii nagar nigam, 'Municipal Corporation of Delhi'). Public-sector companies also carry this word in their names, like भारतीय खाद्य निगम (bhaartiiy khaady nigam, 'Food Corporation of India'), so for most learners निगम (nigam) is really the word you meet on signboards and news headlines about civic bodies and state enterprises.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse नगर निगम (nagar nigam, 'municipal corporation') with नगर पालिका (nagar paalikaa, 'municipal council') -- the two are different tiers of urban local government, not interchangeable synonyms. 2) निगम (nigam) alone usually implies a formal, often government-linked body (municipal, public-sector) -- it is not used loosely for any company or business.

Sense 2 : the Vedas, scriptural text
noun, masculine
Synonyms: वेद (ved, literary), आगम (aagam, literary, rare)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This older sense, where निगम (nigam) refers to the Vedic texts, survives mainly in classical and religious writing -- a learner focused on everyday conversation will almost never need it, since modern speech has fully claimed the word for corporations and civic bodies.
