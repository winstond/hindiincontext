कोई (koii)

pronoun/adjective/adverb कोई (koii) covers 'someone/anyone' as a pronoun and 'some/any' as an adjective before a noun; it also has a fixed idiomatic use meaning 'not particularly/not really' in casual replies.
Sense 1 : someone, anyone
pronoun
Synonyms: कोई भी (koii bhii, anyone at all)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you shout into an empty house or office when you want to know if anyone's around: कोई है? (koii hai?, 'anyone there?'). It's also the polite, vague way to refer to an unnamed person, similar to how English uses 'someone' to avoid naming names.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make कोई (koii) agree in gender or number when it stands alone as a pronoun -- it doesn't change form here regardless of who is meant. 2) In questions like कोई है? (koii hai?, 'is anyone there?') learners sometimes add a plural verb, but the verb stays singular even though the person referred to is unspecified.
Sample Sentences:
कोई है? Is anyone there?
(koii hai?)

Sense 2 : some, any (before a noun)
adjective Placed directly before a singular countable noun: कोई किताब (koii kitaab, 'some book/any book'), कोई आदमी (koii aadmii, 'some man').
Synonyms: कुछ (kuchh, used with non-count/plural nouns)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of कोई (koii) as Hindi's 'a certain' or 'some' for one thing at a time -- कोई बात नहीं (koii baat nahiin, 'no problem/never mind') literally uses it this way, treating 'the matter' as an unspecified single thing not worth worrying about.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners reach for कुछ (kuchh) with singular countable nouns out of habit from English 'some', but कोई (koii) is the correct choice there -- कुछ (kuchh) belongs with uncountables or plurals instead.
Sample Sentences:

(kyaa koii kitaab mez par rakhii hai?)

Sense 3 : not particularly, not really (in dismissive/downplaying replies)
adverb Occurs before an adjective in a negative frame: कोई खास बात नहीं (koii khaas baat nahiin, 'nothing special'), कोई ज़रूरी नहीं (koii zaroorii nahiin, 'not really necessary').
Synonyms: ज़्यादा नहीं (zyaadaa nahiin, not much, longer phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in casual downplaying: कोई खास बात नहीं (koii khaas baat nahiin, 'nothing much/no big deal'). It's the verbal shrug Hindi speakers give when brushing something off as unimportant.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners translate this construction word-for-word and miss that कोई (koii) here isn't asking 'is there someone' -- it's softening or downplaying the adjective that follows, closer to 'not all that.'
Sample Sentences:
कोई खास बात नहीं है, बस थोड़ा थक गया हूँ। It's nothing much, I'm just a bit tired.
(koii khaas baat nahiin hai, bas thodaa thak gayaa hoon।)
