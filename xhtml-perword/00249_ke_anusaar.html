के अनुसार (ke anusaar)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'according to' or 'as per'; governs the oblique/genitive form of the noun it follows via के (ke).
Sense 1 : according to, as per
postposition Follows a noun in the genitive-like construction: [noun] + के अनुसार (ke anusaar). Used to attribute a claim, rule, or source: नियम के अनुसार (niyam ke anusaar, 'according to the rule').
Synonyms: मुताबिक (mutaabik), हिसाब से (hisaab se)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will hear on the news constantly: सूत्रों के अनुसार (sootron ke anusaar, 'according to sources') is practically a stock opener for Hindi news reports, much like English 'sources say.' In daily life it shows up whenever someone cites a rule, a doctor's advice, or an elder's instruction, e.g. डॉक्टर के अनुसार (doktar ke anusaar, 'according to the doctor'). Its close cousin मुताबिक (mutaabik) is used identically and interchangeably in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop के (ke) and just say नियम अनुसार (niyam anusaar) -- this shortened form does exist in more formal/written registers, but in everyday speech के अनुसार (ke anusaar) is standard and safer to default to. 2) Confusing this with की तरह (kii tarah, 'like/similar to') -- के अनुसार (ke anusaar) is about conformity to a source or standard, not resemblance.
Sample Sentences:
सूत्रों के अनुसार, नई सरकार अगले महीने बनेगी। According to sources, the new government will be formed next month.
(sootron ke anusaar, naii sarkaar agle mahiine banegii।)
