यहाँ (yahaan)

adverb A basic locative adverb meaning 'here'; also used idiomatically to mean 'at this point/stage' in a narrative or argument.
Sense 1 : in this place
adverb
Synonyms: इस जगह (is jagah, literally 'in this place')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Note the pair यहाँ (yahaan) 'here' and वहाँ (vahaan) 'there' -- Hindi builds a whole family of place, time, and manner words (यहाँ, वहाँ, कहाँ, जहाँ; यह, वह, कौन, जो) on this same correlative pattern, so learning one member helps you recognize the rest.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse यहाँ (yahaan) 'here' with वहाँ (vahaan) 'there' -- the initial sound is the only difference, so listen carefully in fast speech. 2) यहाँ (yahaan) points to the speaker's own location; to indicate motion toward here, it still combines with आना (aanaa) rather than needing a separate 'to here' word, unlike English's directional prepositions.

Sense 2 : at this point (in a narrative, argument, or situation)
adverb Used metaphorically to mark a point in discourse or a situation rather than a physical place, e.g. यहाँ मुझे लगता है कि... (yahaan mujhe lagtaa hai ki..., 'here I feel that...').
Synonyms: इस मामले में (is maamle men, 'in this matter')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same spatial word यहाँ (yahaan) stretched to mark a moment in an unfolding story or argument -- much like English speakers say 'here' when they mean 'at this juncture,' not a physical spot.
