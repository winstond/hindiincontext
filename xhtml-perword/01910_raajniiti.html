राजनीति (raajniiti)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'politics' or 'political affairs', built from राज (raaj, rule/kingship) and नीति (niiti, policy/conduct).
Sense 1 : politics; political affairs/strategy
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सियासत (siyaasat, commonly heard in news/spoken Hindi)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday conversation, राजनीति (raajniiti) carries a mildly negative shade when people complain about it -- यह सब राजनीति है (yah sab raajniiti hai, 'this is all just politics') is a common way to dismiss office drama, family favoritism, or shady dealings, much like the English idiom. It is also the standard word for the academic subject 'Political Science', usually as राजनीति शास्त्र (raajniiti shaastr).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use it narrowly for 'government policy' when it more broadly covers politics as an activity, profession, or field of study -- राजनीति में जाना (raajniiti men jaanaa, 'to go into politics') is a very common phrase. 2) राजनीति (raajniiti) can also mean 'scheming/office politics' in a workplace or family context, not just national politics -- घर की राजनीति (ghar kii raajniiti, 'household politics') is a natural extension a learner might not expect.
