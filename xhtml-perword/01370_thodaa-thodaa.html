थोड़ा-थोड़ा (thodaa-thodaa)

adjective/adverb A reduplicated form of थोड़ा (thodaa, 'a little'), used as an adjective/adverb meaning 'little by little' or 'a bit of each/gradually'.
Sense 1 : a little of each; small amounts distributed
adjective Agrees in gender/number with the noun it modifies, just like थोड़ा (thodaa) itself, since reduplication doesn't change its adjectival behavior.
Synonyms: कुछ-कुछ (kuchh-kuchh, overlaps in 'a bit of each' sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a mother at the dinner table doling out थोड़ा-थोड़ा (thodaa-thodaa) चावल (chaaval, 'rice') to everyone from a shared pot -- a little to this plate, a little to that one. The reduplication captures that 'bit by bit, to each' feel that a single थोड़ा (thodaa) can't convey alone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat थोड़ा-थोड़ा (thodaa-thodaa) as simply an emphatic 'very little' -- it's not intensifying smallness, it's distributing it across items or portions, e.g. sharing a little of several things rather than saying one thing is very small. 2) Confusing this with ज़रा-सा (zaraa-saa), which emphasizes smallness of a single quantity rather than distribution.

Sense 2 : gradually, little by little (over time)
adverb Used with verbs to describe an action happening in small increments over time, often paired with बढ़ना (badhnaa, 'to increase') or सीखना (siikhnaa, 'to learn').
Synonyms: धीरे-धीरे (dhiire-dhiire, focuses on slowness/pace rather than incremental amount), क्रमशः (kramshah, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear from a Hindi teacher encouraging a nervous beginner: थोड़ा-थोड़ा सीखो (thodaa-thodaa siikho, 'learn a little at a time') -- it carries a gentle, encouraging tone about incremental progress rather than urgency.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for धीरे-धीरे (dhiire-dhiire) when they mean 'gradually,' but that word emphasizes slow pace, while थोड़ा-थोड़ा (thodaa-thodaa) emphasizes small increments -- the two overlap but aren't interchangeable in every context, e.g. 'walking slowly' wants धीरे-धीरे (dhiire-dhiire), not थोड़ा-थोड़ा (thodaa-thodaa).
