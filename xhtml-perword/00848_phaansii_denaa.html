फाँसी देना (phaansii denaa)

verb A fixed verb-noun collocation meaning 'to hang someone' (as capital punishment). फाँसी (phaansii) itself means 'hanging/noose,' and देना (denaa) here works as the light verb that pairs with it, similar to how सजा देना (sajaa denaa, 'to give punishment') works.
Sense 1 : to execute by hanging
verb, transitive The person being hanged is marked with को (ko): उसे फाँसी दी गई (use phaansii dii gaii, 'he was hanged'). This construction is very often seen in the passive (फाँसी दी जाना, phaansii dii jaanaa) since news and legal contexts usually focus on the person receiving the punishment rather than the state/court doing it.
Synonyms: फाँसी पर लटकाना (phaansii par latkaanaa, more descriptive/graphic phrasing)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase shows up constantly in Hindi news reports on court verdicts -- फाँसी की सज़ा (phaansii kii sazaa, 'death sentence by hanging') and उसे फाँसी दी गई (use phaansii dii gaii, 'he was hanged') are standard headline language. It is worth knowing that फाँसी (phaansii) refers specifically to death by hanging, so this phrase is narrower than English 'execute,' which can cover other methods too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to say 'hang' using लटकाना (latkaanaa) alone for this meaning, but लटकाना (latkaanaa) alone just means 'to hang/suspend' an object, not to execute someone; फाँसी देना (phaansii denaa) is the fixed idiom for capital punishment. 2) Do not drop फाँसी (phaansii) and use देना (denaa) alone -- देना (denaa) by itself just means 'to give' and carries none of this meaning without the noun.
Sample Sentences:
अदालत ने दोषी को फाँसी की सज़ा दी। The court gave the convict the death sentence by hanging.
(adaalat ne doshii ko phaansii kii sazaa dii।)
