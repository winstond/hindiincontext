पौधा (paudhaa)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'plant' or 'sapling' -- the small, living, growing kind you'd find in a nursery or a garden pot, as opposed to a full-grown tree (ped).
Sense 1 : plant, sapling
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पादप (paadap, literary/botanical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi balcony seems to have at least one गमला (gamlaa, 'flowerpot') with a तुलसी का पौधा (tulsii kaa paudhaa, 'basil plant') in it -- tulsi is watered daily and often treated with real reverence, not just as decoration. The verb pairing पौधा लगाना (paudhaa lagaanaa, 'to plant a sapling') is also the standard phrase used in tree-planting drives and environmental campaigns you'll see on posters and news reports.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use पौधा (paudhaa) and पेड़ (ped) interchangeably, but Hindi speakers keep a fairly firm size distinction: पौधा (paudhaa) is a young or small plant (something you'd grow in a pot or nursery), while पेड़ (ped) is a proper tree with a trunk. Calling a mango tree a पौधा (paudhaa) would sound odd unless it is genuinely still a sapling.
