जाना-पहचाना (jaanaa-pahchaanaa)

adjective A compound adjective meaning 'familiar' or 'well-known,' built from the perfective participles of जाना (jaanaa, 'to go/be known') and पहचानना (pahchaannaa, 'to recognize'); the two halves together intensify the sense of familiarity.
Sense 1 : familiar, well-known (person, place, face, voice, etc.)
adjective
Synonyms: परिचित (parichit), जाना-माना (jaanaa-maanaa, often used for well-known public figures rather than personal familiarity)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word captures a very Hindi habit of doubling near-synonyms for emphasis -- 'gone and recognized' becomes 'thoroughly familiar.' You will often hear it describe a face in a crowd or a street corner someone has passed a thousand times: जाना-पहचाना चेहरा (jaanaa-pahchaanaa chehraa, 'a familiar face').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as a simple synonym of परिचित (parichit), but जाना-पहचाना (jaanaa-pahchaanaa) leans toward describing something sensorily or experientially familiar (a face, a voice, a street), while परिचित (parichit) works more broadly, including for people one has been formally introduced to. 2) Because it looks like two verb forms glued together, learners sometimes try to conjugate the pieces separately; the whole compound simply agrees as one adjective with the noun it modifies (जानी-पहचानी (jaanii-pahchaanii) आवाज़ (aavaaz), 'familiar voice').
