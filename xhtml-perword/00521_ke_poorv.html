के पूर्व (ke poorv)

postposition A formal, literary postposition meaning 'before' (in time), used as a more Sanskritic alternative to the everyday से पहले (se pahle).
Sense 1 : before (in time)
postposition Follows a noun or verbal noun in the oblique case, exactly parallel to से पहले (se pahle): X के पूर्व (X ke poorv) = 'before X'.
Synonyms: से पहले (se pahle), पूर्व (poorv, one-word variant used after oblique noun without के)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this phrase constantly in newspapers, official announcements, and TV news anchors' scripts -- चुनाव के पूर्व (chunaav ke poorv, 'before the election') is a classic headline pattern. In daily conversation among friends or family, though, Delhi speakers almost always reach for से पहले (se pahle) instead; के पूर्व (ke poorv) signals a slightly Sanskritized, official register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this in casual speech where से पहले (se pahle) would sound far more natural; के पूर्व (ke poorv) belongs in writing, news, or formal speeches. 2) Some drop के and just say पूर्व (poorv) directly after the noun, e.g. युद्ध पूर्व (yuddh poorv, 'pre-war') -- this compound-like usage is also correct but is a distinct construction from के पूर्व (ke poorv).

(chunaav ke poorv sabhii paartiyaan apne ghoshnaapatr jaarii kartii hain।)
