याददाश्त (yaaddaasht)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'memory' in the sense of the mental faculty of remembering (not a specific remembered moment). Borrowed from Persian, and very common in everyday speech about remembering, forgetting, and memory loss.
Sense 1 : memory (mental faculty)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: स्मृति (smriti, formal/literary), याद (yaad, meaning scope: often 'a memory/recollection' or 'remembrance' rather than the faculty itself)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly around health and aging -- doctors, worried children, and elderly relatives all use याददाश्त (yaaddaasht) when discussing memory loss, e.g. concerns about dadi's or nani's memory getting weaker. It pairs naturally with adjectives like तेज़ (tez, sharp) and कमज़ोर (kamzor, weak) to describe someone's memory as strong or fading.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for याद (yaad) when they mean the faculty itself, e.g. 'my memory is weak' -- here याददाश्त (yaaddaasht) is the natural choice: मेरी याददाश्त कमज़ोर है (merii yaaddaasht kamzor hai, 'my memory is weak'), whereas याद (yaad) fits phrases like मुझे याद है (mujhe yaad hai, 'I remember') about a specific thing. 2) It is grammatically feminine, so adjectives and verbs agreeing with it must take feminine forms, which learners sometimes miss since the word doesn't end in the typical ी.
