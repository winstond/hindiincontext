ऊबना (oobnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to feel bored, to get weary' -- describes the internal state of boredom rather than the act of boring someone else.
Sense 1 : to feel bored, to grow weary/tired of something
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) to mark the source of boredom: किताब से ऊबना (kitaab se oobnaa, 'to get bored of the book').
Synonyms: उकताना (uktaanaa, slightly more literary), तंग आना (tang aanaa, 'to get fed up', idiomatic phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ऊबना (oobnaa) captures a listless, restless kind of boredom -- the feeling you get stuck in traffic or sitting through a long, dull meeting. In everyday Delhi speech, you'll hear it just as often replaced by the Hinglish बोर होना (bor honaa, 'to be bored'), which has become extremely common, especially among younger speakers -- ऊबना (oobnaa) retains a slightly more literate, deliberate feel.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बोर होना (bor honaa) -- a common English-loan alternative -- when ऊबना (oobnaa) is the more native-feeling verb, especially in writing or careful speech; both are heard in casual conversation, though. 2) The person who feels bored is the subject with normal agreement (no ने-marking, since this is intransitive), which can trip up learners used to ने-constructions for expressing feelings via को (as with लगना).
Sample Sentences:
मैं इस काम से ऊब गया हूँ। I have gotten bored of this work.
(main is kaam se oob gayaa hoon।)
