घूँट (ghoont)

noun A single sip or gulp of a liquid; also used figuratively for 'swallowing' something unpleasant, like an insult or emotion.
Sense 1 : a sip or gulp (of liquid)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चुस्की (chuskii, a smaller, lighter sip), जुर्राब (jurraab, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around chai: थोड़ा घूँट लो (thodaa ghoont lo, 'take a little sip') is what an aunty says when she's testing if the tea is too hot for you. It's also the word behind the common phrase घूँट भरना (ghoont bharnaa, 'to take a sip/gulp'), which pairs naturally with पानी (paanii) or चाय (chaay).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for चुस्की (chuskii) for any 'sip,' but घूँट (ghoont) is the more common everyday word and covers bigger gulps too, not just delicate sips of tea. 2) Common in the frame एक घूँट पानी पीना (ek ghoont paanii piinaa, 'to drink a sip of water') -- घूँट (ghoont) itself is the countable unit, so you say एक घूँट (ek ghoont) not एक घूँट का (ek ghoont kaa).
Sample Sentences:
थोड़ा घूँट लो, देखो चाय ज़्यादा गरम तो नहीं है। Take a little sip, see if the tea is too hot.
(thodaa ghoont lo, dekho chaay zyaadaa garam to nahiin hai।)

Sense 2 : figurative: something unpleasant that must be swallowed/endured
noun, masculine Almost always appears with पीना (piinaa) or गटकना (gataknaa) in the idiom घूँट पीना (ghoont piinaa) or कड़वा घूँट पीना (kadvaa ghoont piinaa, 'to drink a bitter gulp'), meaning to silently endure an insult, humiliation, or painful truth.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: कड़वा घूँट पीना (kadvaa ghoont piinaa) is the classic Hindi image for swallowing pride or pain in silence -- think of a mother-in-law's sharp remark that a daughter-in-law absorbs without answering back. It reflects a broader pattern in Hindi where emotional endurance is described through the body: swallowing, not just 'feeling.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense only works with verbs of drinking/swallowing (पीना, गटकना) -- you can't use it with 'to feel' or 'to bear' the way English might say 'swallow a hurt.' 2) Almost always modified by कड़वा (kadvaa, 'bitter') -- कड़वा घूँट (kadvaa ghoont) is the set phrase, said without कड़वा (kadvaa) it can just sound literal.
Sample Sentences:
उसे अपने पति की बेइज़्ज़ती का घूँट चुपचाप पीना पड़ा। She had to silently swallow the bitter pill of her husband's insult.
