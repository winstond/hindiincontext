ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa)

verb A compound verb built from ध्यान (dhyaan, 'attention, mind') plus रखना (rakhnaa, 'to keep'); it covers 'to take care of / look after' and 'to keep in mind / bear in mind, watch out for.'
Sense 1 : to take care of, look after (someone or something's wellbeing)
verb, transitive Takes the object of care marked with का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) -- अपनी सेहत का ध्यान रखना (apnii sehat kaa dhyaan rakhnaa, 'to take care of one's own health').
Synonyms: देखभाल करना (dekhbhaal karnaa), संभालना (sambhaalnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase behind the everyday parting warmth ख़याल रखना (khayaal rakhnaa) / ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa, 'take care'), both used interchangeably when family or friends part ways -- a mother telling a child अपना ध्यान रखना (apnaa dhyaan rakhnaa, 'take care of yourself') is a very Delhi-household sound.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the cared-for person or thing with को (ko) by analogy with English 'take care of him,' but Hindi wants the genitive: बच्चे का ध्यान रखना (bachche kaa dhyaan rakhnaa), not बच्चे को ध्यान रखना. 2) This sense takes ने (ne) in the perfective since रखना (rakhnaa) is transitive: उसने मेरा ध्यान रखा (usne meraa dhyaan rakhaa, 'he took care of me').

माँ हमेशा कहती है, अपना ध्यान रखना। Mom always says, take care of yourself.
(maan hameshaa kahtii hai, apnaa dhyaan rakhnaa।)

Sense 2 : to keep in mind, bear in mind, be mindful/watchful of
verb, transitive Often followed by a कि (ki) clause or a genitive-marked noun: ध्यान रखना कि लेट न हों (dhyaan rakhnaa ki let n hon, 'keep in mind not to be late'); समय का ध्यान रखना (samay kaa dhyaan rakhnaa, 'be mindful of the time').
Synonyms: याद रखना (yaad rakhnaa, overlaps for 'remember'), सावधान रहना (saavdhaan rahnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Said as a standalone warning -- ध्यान रखना! (dhyaan rakhnaa) -- it works like 'watch out!' or 'be careful!', commonly heard from parents, shopkeepers pointing out a step, or friends warning about traffic.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse this with याद रखना (yaad rakhnaa): याद रखना is about not forgetting a fact, while ध्यान रखना here leans toward ongoing vigilance or caution about a situation. 2) In imperative warnings like ध्यान रखना, गिर मत जाना (dhyaan rakhnaa, gir mat jaanaa, 'watch out, don't fall'), it functions almost like 'be careful,' which surprises learners expecting a literal 'keep attention.'
