कमाल (kamaal)

noun/adjective Primarily a noun meaning 'wonder, marvel, feat of skill,' but very commonly used as an exclamatory adjective ('amazing!') and in fixed idiomatic phrases praising or mocking someone's skill or audacity.
Sense 1 : a marvel, feat, or extraordinary thing/skill
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अजूबा (ajoobaa), करिश्मा (karishmaa), चमत्कार (chamatkaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear कमाल है! (kamaal hai, 'that's amazing/incredible') constantly in Delhi conversation, said with a shake of the head, whether someone just pulled off a brilliant trick or just did something utterly ridiculous. The word carries this double edge, genuine wonder and mock exasperation, in almost equal measure.
Common Mistakes: 1) कमाल (kamaal) is masculine, so possessives and adjectives agreeing with it take the masculine form, e.g. यह कमाल है (yah kamaal hai, 'this is a marvel'), not the feminine agreement a learner might default to. 2) In the common phrase कमाल करना (kamaal karnaa, 'to do something amazing/outrageous'), the tone can be either genuinely admiring or sarcastic depending on context -- learners often miss the sarcastic reading, e.g. when someone has done something foolish.
Sample Sentences:
यार, ये तो कमाल है! Dude, that's amazing!
(yaar, ye to kamaal hai!)

Sense 2 : amazing, wonderful (used predicatively/exclamatorily)
adjective Used invariantly, most often as an exclamation or predicate (कमाल है! kamaal hai) rather than directly before a noun; when used attributively it does not inflect for gender or number, e.g. कमाल आदमी (kamaal aadmii, 'an amazing man') and कमाल औरत (kamaal aurat, 'an amazing woman') both keep the same form.
Synonyms: अद्भुत (adbhut), शानदार (shaandaar), लाजवाब (laajvaab)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This exclamatory use overlaps closely with the noun sense above, both trace to the same root idea of 'a marvel,' but functioning as a standalone reaction word (कमाल! kamaal) is so common in spoken Hindi that it deserves its own mental slot as an adjective-like exclamation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect it like a regular -आ adjective (कमाला/कमाली); it never changes form regardless of the gender or number of what it describes.
