आत्महत्या (aatmahatyaa)

noun A compound noun meaning 'suicide' -- built from आत्म (aatm, 'self') plus हत्या (hatyaa, 'killing/murder'). It has essentially one core meaning; the entry below also covers its standard verb pairing.
Sense 1 : suicide, self-killing
noun, feminine Almost always paired with करना (karnaa) as आत्महत्या करना (aatmahatyaa karnaa, 'to commit suicide') -- the noun itself is the object in this light-verb construction.
Synonyms: खुदकुशी (khudkushii), स्वहत्या (svahatyaa, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a serious, formal-register word you will see in news headlines and official contexts -- आत्महत्या की दर (aatmahatyaa kii dar, 'suicide rate') is common in reporting on farmer distress, for instance. In casual or sensitive conversation, people often soften with indirect phrasing like खुद को खत्म कर लिया (khud ko khatm kar liyaa, 'ended himself/herself') rather than using this word bluntly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build a verb directly from आत्महत्या (aatmahatyaa) itself; in practice it is always paired with a separate verb like करना (karnaa) or कर लेना (kar lenaa), never conjugated on its own. 2) करने की कोशिश की (karne kii koshish kii, 'attempted [suicide]') is the standard way to say 'attempted suicide' -- learners sometimes reach for a single word instead of this phrase.

(pichhle saal us gaanv men kisaanon kii aatmahatyaa kii dar bahut badh gaii।)
