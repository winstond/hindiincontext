समानता (samaantaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'equality' or 'similarity' -- the state of being समान (samaan, 'similar/equal').
Sense 1 : equality, sameness, similarity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बराबरी (baraabrii), साम्य (saamy, literary), एकरूपता (ekrooptaa, meaning scope: sameness of form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear समानता (samaantaa) constantly in the context of भारतीय संविधान (bhaartiiy sanvidhaan, 'the Indian Constitution'), which guarantees समता का अधिकार (samtaa kaa adhikaar, 'the right to equality') -- it is the go-to word in civics class, news debates, and political speeches, giving it a slightly formal, public-discourse flavor compared to the everyday बराबरी (baraabrii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बराबरी (baraabrii) in casual speech and समानता (samaantaa) in formal or written contexts (e.g. constitutional or social discussions like स्त्री-पुरुष समानता (strii-purush samaantaa, 'gender equality')) -- both are correct, but mixing up the register can sound off. 2) The comparison word is के बराबर (ke baraabar) or के समान (ke samaan) when saying 'equal to' something, not समानता के साथ (samaantaa ke saath) -- learners sometimes try to force समानता itself into that slot instead of using समान (samaan) or बराबर (baraabar).
Sample Sentences:
भारतीय संविधान सभी नागरिकों को समानता का अधिकार देता है। The Indian Constitution gives all citizens the right to equality.
(bhaartiiy sanvidhaan sabhii naagrikon ko samaantaa kaa adhikaar detaa hai।)
