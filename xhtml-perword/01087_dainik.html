दैनिक (dainik)

adjective A formal/written-register adjective meaning 'daily,' used mainly for schedules, publications, and routines in slightly formal contexts; the everyday spoken alternative is रोज़ाना (rozaanaa) or रोज़ (roz).
Sense 1 : daily, occurring every day
adjective
Synonyms: रोज़ाना (rozaanaa), रोज़ (roz), प्रतिदिन (pratidin, literary/formal, adverbial use)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly in the mastheads of newspapers -- names like दैनिक भास्कर (dainik bhaaskar) and दैनिक जागरण (dainik jaagran) literally mean 'Daily Bhaskar' and 'Daily Jagran.' That association makes दैनिक (dainik) feel print-and-schedule flavored to native ears, while रोज़ (roz) and रोज़ाना (rozaanaa) are what people actually say about their own daily habits.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use दैनिक (dainik) in casual speech where रोज़ाना (rozaanaa) or रोज़ (roz) would sound far more natural -- दैनिक (dainik) is common in writing (newspapers, timetables, reports) but can feel stiff in a spoken sentence like 'main roz school jaata hoon.' 2) Because it ends in -इक (-ik) rather than -आ (-aa), learners sometimes try to inflect it for gender/number like a regular adjective; it stays invariant across masculine, feminine, and plural nouns.
