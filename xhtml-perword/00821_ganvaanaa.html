गँवाना (ganvaanaa)

verb One core meaning: to lose or waste something you had -- time, money, opportunity, health, or reputation -- usually through carelessness, neglect, or foolishness.
Sense 1 : to waste or lose (something valuable) through carelessness
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बर्बाद करना (barbaad karnaa), खोना (khonaa, often for losing an object or feeling; गँवाना (ganvaanaa) stresses one's own fault or carelessness), नष्ट करना (nasht karnaa, more formal/literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Hindi-speaking parent or teacher reaches for when scolding someone about squandered potential: वक़्त गँवाना (vaqt ganvaanaa, 'to waste time') and मौका गँवाना (maukaa ganvaanaa, 'to lose/blow a chance') are extremely common phrases, carrying a faint tone of regret or reproach that plain 'lose' doesn't capture in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खोना (khonaa) in every 'lose' context, but गँवाना (ganvaanaa) specifically implies the loss was one's own fault -- squandered through carelessness, laziness, or bad judgment, not simply misplaced or lost by chance. 2) The object lost with गँवाना (ganvaanaa) is usually abstract or valuable -- समय (samay, time), मौका (maukaa, chance), इज़्ज़त (izzat, honor), पैसा (paisaa, money) -- rather than a physical object you simply can't find, like keys or a pen.
Sample Sentences:
समय मत गँवाओ, काम शुरू करो। Don't waste time, start the work.
(samay mat ganvaao, kaam shuroo karo।)
