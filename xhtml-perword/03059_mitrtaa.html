मित्रता (mitrtaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'friendship,' built from मित्र (mitr, 'friend') plus the abstract-noun suffix -ता (taa).
Sense 1 : friendship, amity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दोस्ती (dostii), याराना (yaaraanaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: मित्रता (mitrtaa) has a slightly bookish, formal ring compared to the everyday दोस्ती (dostii) -- you will meet it in a school essay titled 'मित्रता का महत्व' (mitrtaa kaa mahatv, 'the importance of friendship') more often than in casual chat, where दोस्ती (dostii) rules. Both share the root मित्र (mitr, 'friend'), a word itself with a formal, almost literary flavor next to यार (yaar) or दोस्त (dost).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for दोस्ती (dostii) by default since it is more common in casual speech; मित्रता (mitrtaa) is the word you will meet in writing, school essays, or slightly formal speech, e.g. talking about friendship between countries -- दोनों देशों में मित्रता (donon deshon men mitrtaa, 'friendship between the two countries'). 2) It takes से (se) to name the other party, not के साथ (ke saath) alone: उससे मित्रता है (usse mitrtaa hai, 'I have a friendship with him'), though के साथ (ke saath) is also heard.
