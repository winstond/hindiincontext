जुआ खेलना (juaa khelnaa)

verb A verb phrase meaning 'to gamble' -- literally 'to play gambling,' combining जुआ (juaa, 'gambling/gamble') with खेलना (khelnaa, 'to play').
Sense 1 : to gamble
verb, transitive जुआ (juaa) acts as the object here; the phrase behaves as a normal transitive verb-plus-object combination, so जुआ (juaa) can be dropped in context once established, leaving just खेलना (khelnaa) understood as 'to gamble.'
Synonyms: सट्टा खेलना (sattaa khelnaa, betting specifically), जुआ बजाना (juaa bajaanaa, less common variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: जुआ (juaa) carries a strong moral shadow in everyday Hindi -- it shows up in family warnings and moral stories (much like in the Mahabharat, where Yudhishthir's game of dice is the cultural reference point every Indian grows up hearing about). Calling someone जुआरी (juaarii, 'gambler') is a real insult, not a neutral description of a hobby.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate 'gamble' word for word using जोखिम लेना (jokhim lenaa, 'to take a risk') -- that phrase means taking a risk generally, not betting money on cards, dice, or games. 2) In the past tense, remember ने-marking applies since खेलना (khelnaa) is transitive here: उसने जुआ खेला (usne juaa khelaa, 'he gambled'), not उसने जुआ खेली (usne juaa khelii) -- agreement is with जुआ (juaa), which is masculine.

Sense 2 : to take a big risk, gamble figuratively
verb, transitive Used metaphorically for high-stakes decisions outside literal gambling, often with पर (par) marking what is being risked: अपनी जान पर जुआ खेलना (apnii jaan par juaa khelnaa, 'to gamble with one's life').
Synonyms: दाँव लगाना (daanv lagaanaa, to place a stake, also figurative), जोखिम उठाना (jokhim uthaanaa, to take a risk)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative extension is common in news commentary and everyday advice-giving -- a parent might say मत करो, यह जुआ है (mat karo, yah juaa hai, 'don't do it, it's a gamble') about a risky investment or a sudden career jump, tying back to the same disapproving cultural undertone as the literal sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may not realize this figurative sense exists and only think of card tables or casinos -- native speakers use it freely for business, relationships, or career decisions. 2) The risked thing is marked with पर (par), not से (se) -- अपनी नौकरी पर जुआ खेलना (apnii naukrii par juaa khelnaa, 'to gamble with one's job'), a pattern learners often get wrong by defaulting to से (se).
