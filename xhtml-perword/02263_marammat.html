मरम्मत (marammat)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'repair' -- used constantly for anything from a punctured tire to a leaky roof. It is almost always paired with the verb करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa).
Sense 1 : repair, fixing, mending
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सुधार (sudhaar, covers 'improvement/correction' more broadly), मरम्मती (marammtii, rare adjectival form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear मरम्मत (marammat) everywhere from the mistri (mistrii) fixing a scooter to a government notice about सड़क की मरम्मत (sadak kii marammat, 'road repair'). It is a Persian-derived loanword so thoroughly naturalized into Hindi that it feels entirely native to most speakers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use मरम्मत (marammat) as a verb directly -- it needs a light verb, as in मरम्मत करना (marammat karnaa, 'to repair') or मरम्मत होना (marammat honaa, 'to get repaired/be repaired'). 2) Since मरम्मत (marammat) is feminine, past-tense agreement with मरम्मत करना (marammat karnaa) goes with मरम्मत (marammat) itself when ने is used, e.g. मैंने गाड़ी की मरम्मत की (mainne gaadii kii marammat kii, 'I repaired the car') -- the verb की (kii) agrees with the feminine object मरम्मत (marammat), not गाड़ी (gaadii) directly, since गाड़ी is linked via की (kii) as a possessive.
