तंदुरुस्त (tandurust)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning healthy or fit, describing physical wellbeing. Of Persian origin, common in everyday speech about health and body condition.
Sense 1 : healthy, physically fit
adjective
Synonyms: स्वस्थ (svasth), निरोग (nirog, literary), चंगा (changaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in the common blessing तंदुरुस्त रहो (tandurust raho, 'stay healthy'), said to elders and children alike, and in the popular saying जान है तो जहान है (jaan hai to jahaan hai) which carries a similar spirit -- health as the foundation of everything else. It often pairs with मोटा-ताज़ा (motaa-taazaa) in casual talk about someone looking well-fed and robust, especially from doting relatives commenting on a child's or guest's appearance.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use तंदुरुस्त (tandurust) for someone who has just recovered from a specific illness -- native speakers more naturally say ठीक हो गया (thiik ho gayaa, 'got well/recovered') for that moment, reserving तंदुरुस्त (tandurust) for general robust health or build over time. 2) Also spelled तन्दुरुस्त (tandurust) with a full न instead of the anusvaar -- both are seen in print, so don't be thrown by the variant.
