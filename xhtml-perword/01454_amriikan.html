अमरीकन (amriikan)

adjective/noun A common colloquial adjective (and, by extension, noun) meaning 'American,' used interchangeably with the more formal अमेरिकी (amerikii) in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : American (relating to the USA)
adjective
Synonyms: अमेरिकी (amerikii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In casual Delhi conversation you will hear अमरीकन (amriikan) and अमेरिकी (amerikii) used almost interchangeably, though अमेरिकी (amerikii) tends to sound slightly more polished or written, the kind of word you would see in a newspaper headline, while अमरीकन (amriikan) feels more like everyday chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat अमरीकन (amriikan) as inflecting like a typical -आ (aa) adjective, but it is invariant -- it never changes for gender or number: अमरीकन लड़की (amriikan ladkii, American girl) and अमरीकन लड़के (amriikan ladke, American boys) both keep the same form.

Sense 2 : an American person
noun, masculine/feminine Used as a noun by simply dropping any following noun, e.g. वह अमरीकन है (vah amriikan hai, 'he/she is an American'); the same invariant form covers both genders.
Synonyms: अमेरिकी (amerikii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just like 'Indian' or 'Japanese' in English can describe both the nationality and the person, अमरीकन (amriikan) slides easily between adjective and noun without changing form -- a very common pattern for nationality words borrowed into Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to mark this noun for gender with an ending change; instead, gender is shown only by the verb or other agreement in the sentence, not by the word itself, e.g. वह अमरीकन है (vah amriikan hai) works for both a man and a woman.
