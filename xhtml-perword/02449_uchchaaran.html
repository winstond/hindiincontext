उच्चारण (uchchaaran)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'pronunciation' or 'articulation' of sounds/words -- the standard word Hindi teachers and speakers use when discussing how a word should be spoken.
Sense 1 : pronunciation, articulation of speech sounds
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in any Hindi classroom or language-learning context in Delhi -- teachers correcting a student's उच्चारण (uchchaaran) is a very normal, almost affectionate ritual, especially around tricky retroflex versus dental sounds (ड़ vs द, ट vs त). It pairs naturally with शुद्ध (shuddh, 'pure/correct'), as in शुद्ध उच्चारण (shuddh uchchaaran, 'correct pronunciation'), a phrase you will see on language-learning materials everywhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use उच्चारण (uchchaaran) when they mean 'accent' in the sense of a regional or foreign way of speaking -- Hindi speakers would more naturally say लहजा (lahjaa) for that nuance, reserving उच्चारण (uchchaaran) for the correctness or clarity of individual sounds. 2) The verb used with it is usually करना (karnaa) -- शब्द का उच्चारण करना (shabd kaa uchchaaran karnaa, 'to pronounce a word') -- not बोलना (bolnaa), which learners default to from English 'to speak/say.'

आपका उच्चारण बहुत अच्छा है। Your pronunciation is very good.
(aapkaa uchchaaran bahut achchhaa hai।)
