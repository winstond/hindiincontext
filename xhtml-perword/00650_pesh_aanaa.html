पेश आना (pesh aanaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to behave/treat (in a certain way)' or 'to happen/occur, come to pass' -- both senses hinge on the borrowed Persian element पेश (pesh, 'forward/before').
Sense 1 : to behave, treat someone in a certain manner
verb, intransitive Takes the manner adverb plus से (se) marking the person: X ने Y से अच्छा/बुरा पेश आना (pesh aanaa) -- 'X treated Y well/badly.'
Synonyms: व्यवहार करना (vyavahaar karnaa), बरतना (baratnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase is common in advice about manners and workplace or family conduct -- a parent might say किसी से भी बुरा पेश मत आओ (kisii se bhii buraa pesh mat aao, 'don't behave badly with anyone'). It has a slightly more formal, considered flavor than the everyday व्यवहार करना (vyavahaar karnaa), often used when discussing how one *should* treat others.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the person with को (ko) instead of से (se); the person being treated must take से (se), e.g. उससे अच्छा पेश आओ (usse achchhaa pesh aao, 'behave well with him'), not उसको (usko). 2) The manner word (अच्छा/बुरा/नरमी से, etc.) comes before पेश आना (pesh aanaa) and often looks like a separate adjective rather than part of the verb phrase, which can confuse learners into treating it as a description rather than the manner of the action.
Sample Sentences:
हमें सबसे अच्छे से पेश आना चाहिए। We should treat everyone well.
(hamen sabse achchhe se pesh aanaa chaahie।)

Sense 2 : to happen, occur, come to pass (of a situation, event, difficulty)
verb, intransitive The subject is typically an abstract situation (मुश्किल, समस्या, हालात), often with a को (ko)-marked experiencer: जो मुश्किलें उसे पेश आईं (jo mushkilen use pesh aaiin, 'the difficulties that came his way').
Synonyms: होना (honaa), घटित होना (ghatit honaa, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this in slightly more literary or formal spoken registers, such as news reports or serious conversation: जो समस्याएँ पेश आई हैं (jo samasyaaen pesh aaii hain, 'the problems that have arisen'). It pairs naturally with words like मुश्किल (mushkil) and परिस्थिति (paristhiti).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may reach for होना (honaa) in every context, missing that पेश आना (pesh aanaa) specifically frames a difficulty or circumstance as something that 'presents itself' to a person, giving a more vivid, almost personified sense of the event confronting someone.
