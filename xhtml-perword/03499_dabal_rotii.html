डबल रोटी (dabal rotii)

noun A compound noun meaning 'bread' (the loaf kind), literally 'double roti (rotii)' -- borrowed 'double' plus the native word for flatbread, used to distinguish sliced/baked loaf bread from everyday Indian flatbreads.
Sense 1 : bread (loaf, sliced bakery bread)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: ब्रेड (bred, common alternative, English loanword)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The name literally means 'double bread' -- the story goes that it was called this because a loaf, when sliced, looks like doubled-up or layered roti (rotii), or because it puffs up 'double' the size compared to flatbread. Older generations and small-town shopkeepers still say डबल रोटी (dabal rotii) while younger urban speakers increasingly just say ब्रेड (bred).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use रोटी (rotii) alone to mean 'bread' as in a bakery loaf -- but रोटी (rotii) alone means the flatbread Indians eat with meals, so डबल रोटी (dabal rotii) or ब्रेड (bred) is needed for the Western-style loaf. 2) The word is grammatically singular in form but refers to the whole loaf or to slices collectively depending on context, much like English 'bread.'
