चादर (chaadar)

noun Mainly a household noun for a large sheet of cloth -- a bedsheet or a shawl-like wrap -- with a well-known idiom about living within one's means and a devotional sense tied to shrines.
Sense 1 : bedsheet
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बिछावन (bichhaavan, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear a mother say when telling a child to spread the चादर (chaadar) on the bed before guests arrive -- it is a staple of household vocabulary, right up there with तकिया (takiyaa, pillow) and रजाई (rajaaii, quilt).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for शीट (shiit) from English, but चादर (chaadar) is the natural word Hindi speakers use for a bedsheet in daily conversation.

Sense 2 : shawl or large wrap
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दुपट्टा (dupattaa, smaller, worn differently)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In colder months you will see people wrap a चादर (chaadar) around their shoulders like a light shawl while sitting outside in the winter sun -- it is bigger and plainer than a दुपट्टा (dupattaa) and used more for warmth than for style.

Sense 3 : ceremonial cloth offered at a shrine
noun, feminine
Frequency: devotional
Cultural Note: At a dargah (dargaah) or Sufi shrine, devotees offer a green or embroidered चादर (chaadar) over the saint's grave as an act of reverence -- you will often hear the phrase चादर चढ़ाना (chaadar chadhaanaa, 'to offer a ceremonial cloth') in this context.

Sense 4 : idiom: living within one's means
noun, feminine Lives almost entirely in the fixed idiom जितनी चादर हो उतने पैर फैलाना (jitnii chaadar ho utne pair phailaanaa, 'stretch your legs only as far as the sheet allows') -- meaning to live within one's means.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This proverb is something elders say often, especially about money -- if a young person overspends, someone might gently remind them: जितनी चादर हो उतने पैर फैलाओ (jitnii chaadar ho utne pair phailaao, 'stretch your legs only as far as your sheet allows'), meaning don't live beyond your means.
Common Mistakes: 1) This chadar is figurative, not a literal bedsheet -- learners should recognize the whole idiom rather than translating word by word.
Sample Sentences:
जितनी चादर हो उतने पैर फैलाने चाहिए। One should stretch one's legs only as far as the sheet allows.
