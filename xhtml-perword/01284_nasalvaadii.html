नसलवादी (nasalvaadii)

adjective/noun A single-sense word meaning 'racist' -- both as a description of a person and as an adjective for attitudes, policies, or remarks. Built from नसल (nasal, 'race/breed') plus the -वादी (vaadii) suffix that turns a noun into 'one who follows/believes in X' or 'pertaining to X'.
Sense 1 : racist (describing views, policies, remarks)
adjective
Synonyms: जातिवादी (jaativaadii, often used for caste/ethnic prejudice rather than race)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear नसलवादी (nasalvaadii) most often in news broadcasts and political commentary -- discussions of racism abroad (the US, South Africa, Europe) or of racial discrimination against Indians overseas -- rather than in casual household talk, where caste-based prejudice is more commonly labeled जातिवादी (jaativaadii) or छुआछूत (chhuaachhoot, 'untouchability') instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse नसलवादी (nasalvaadii) with जातिवादी (jaativaadii); the former targets race/ethnicity (often in an international or historical context, e.g. apartheid, colonial attitudes), while जातिवादी (jaativaadii) is the word reached for in everyday Indian conversation about caste-based prejudice. 2) As an invariant adjective, it does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- learners familiar with कड़ा-type adjectives may wrongly expect an ending change.

Sense 2 : a racist (person)
noun, masculine/feminine Functions as the same invariant word used substantively -- 'वह एक नसलवादी है (vah ek nasalvaadii hai, "he is a racist")' -- rather than as a separate derived noun form.
Synonyms: जातिवादी (jaativaadii, often used for caste/ethnic prejudice rather than race)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like many -वादी (vaadii) words in Hindi (राष्ट्रवादी raashtrvaadii 'nationalist', साम्प्रदायिकतावादी saamprdaayiktaavaadii 'communalist'), नसलवादी (nasalvaadii) slides freely between describing a person and describing their belief without changing form -- a pattern worth noticing across this whole word family.
