धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad)

interjection/noun A formal way to say 'thank you' -- one core meaning, used as both an interjection and a noun.
Sense 1 : thank you
interjection
Synonyms: शुक्रिया (shukriyaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) as the word you'd hear at the end of a formal speech, a wedding stage announcement, or from a news anchor signing off -- it carries a slightly official, written-register weight compared to the everyday शुक्रिया (shukriyaa). Many Hindi speakers actually default to English 'thank you' in casual talk, reserving धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) for when they want to sound respectful or ceremonious.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) in every casual situation because it is the word taught first, but in daily chat among friends or family, शुक्रिया (shukriyaa) or just a warm smile/nod is far more natural; धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) can feel stiff, like reading off a certificate. 2) It is invariant here -- it does not change form or take verb agreement when used as this stand-alone exclamation.
Sample Sentences:
धन्यवाद, आप सबका आना सफल रहा। Thank you, all of your coming here made this a success.
(dhanyvaad, aap sabkaa aanaa saphal rahaa।)

Sense 2 : thanks, gratitude (as a noun)
noun, masculine Common in the frame धन्यवाद देना (dhanyvaad denaa, 'to give thanks') or धन्यवाद करना (dhanyvaad karnaa), where it functions as an object rather than a standalone exclamation.
Synonyms: शुक्रिया (shukriyaa), आभार (aabhaar, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun use shows up in slightly more reflective or written contexts, like a speaker saying मैं आपका धन्यवाद करना चाहता हूँ (main aapkaa dhanyvaad karnaa chaahtaa hoon, 'I want to express my thanks to you') -- a step more formal than simply exclaiming धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) on its own.
Common Mistakes: 1) As a noun, it is masculine and invariant, so learners shouldn't try to feminize or pluralize it when it takes verbs like देना (denaa) -- you'll see सबका धन्यवाद (sabkaa dhanyvaad, 'thanks to everyone') without any ending change.
