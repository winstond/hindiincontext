पैंतालीस (paintaaliis)

numeral The number forty-five (paintaaliis), used for counting and quantities. A single-sense numeral like most Hindi numbers.
Sense 1 : the number 45
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 each have their own unique, non-transparent form (unlike English's neat tens-plus-units pattern), and पैंतालीस (paintaaliis) is a good example -- native speakers just know it as a single word, the way you know 'eleven' isn't built from 'ten' and 'one.' You will hear it constantly in prices, ages, and phone numbers, so it is worth drilling early despite the irregularity.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from चालीस (chaaliis, forty) plus पांच (paanch, five), but like most Hindi numbers 21-99, पैंतालीस (paintaaliis) is a fused, irregular form that must simply be memorized rather than derived. 2) It is easy to confuse with पचास (pachaas, fifty) or चौंतीस (chauntiis, thirty-four) due to the similar shapes and sounds of Hindi numerals in this range -- careful listening practice helps.
