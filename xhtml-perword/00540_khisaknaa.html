खिसकना (khisaknaa)

verb A physical verb of slipping or shifting position, ranging from a chair sliding on the floor to a person slyly slipping away from a place -- and colloquially, a body part 'slipping' out of joint.
Sense 1 : to move over, shift, slide (of objects or people making room/space)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: सरकना (saraknaa), हटना (hatnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on crowded buses and at family gatherings on a shared sofa: ज़रा खिसकिए (zaraa khiskie, 'please shift over a bit') is the everyday polite request to make space for one more person.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हटना (hatnaa) here, but खिसकना (khisaknaa) carries a gentler, incremental 'shifting a bit' sense -- like sliding a chair over to make room -- rather than हटना (hatnaa)'s more general 'move away/remove.'
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा खिसकिए, मुझे भी बैठना है। Please move over a bit, I need to sit too.
(zaraa khiskie, mujhe bhii baithnaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
थोड़ा खिसक जाओ, यार, जगह नहीं है। Move over a little, man, there's no room.
(thodaa khisak jaao, yaar, jagah nahiin hai।)

(vah apnii kursii thodii aur khiskaakar mere paas aa gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to slip away, sneak off (from a place or gathering, often unnoticed)
verb, intransitive Often used self-referentially about quietly leaving a boring or awkward situation, sometimes with a slightly comic or sheepish tone.
Synonyms: निकल जाना (nikal jaanaa), भाग जाना (bhaag jaanaa, stronger: to flee/run off)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic word for ducking out of a dull family function or a long meeting without saying formal goodbyes -- वह चुपचाप खिसक गया (vah chupchaap khisak gayaa, 'he quietly slipped away') paints a very familiar Delhi drawing-room scene.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense implies discretion or a bit of guilt/awkwardness in leaving -- it is not a neutral 'to leave' like जाना (jaanaa); using it for a plain, announced departure sounds odd.
Sample Sentences:
वह पार्टी से चुपचाप खिसक गया। He quietly slipped away from the party.
(vah paartii se chupchaap khisak gayaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
मीटिंग बोरिंग हो रही थी तो मैं धीरे से खिसक गई। The meeting was getting boring, so I quietly slipped away.
(miiting boring ho rahii thii to main dhiire se khisak gaii।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
जब भी घर में लंबा भाषण शुरू होता है, वह खिसक जाता है। Whenever a long lecture starts at home, he sneaks off.

Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Register: Casual -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
शादी की रस्में शुरू होने से पहले ही कई मेहमान खिसक चुके थे। Many guests had already slipped away before the wedding rituals even began.
(shaadii kii rasmen shuroo hone se pahle hii kaii mehmaan khisak chuke the।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
लंबी बैठक से बीच में खिसकना अच्छा नहीं लगता, फिर भी लोग खिसक ही जाते हैं। It doesn't look good to sneak out of a long meeting midway, yet people go ahead and do it.

Sense 3 : to slip out of place, become dislocated (of a joint, bone, or fixed part)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: उतरना (utarnaa, of a joint)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this at home when someone says उसका कंधा खिसक गया (uskaa kandhaa khisak gayaa, 'his shoulder slipped out of joint') -- a common way to describe a minor dislocation before rushing to the doctor or the local हड्डी वाला (haddii vaalaa, 'bone-setter').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with टूटना (tootnaa, 'to break') -- खिसकना (khisaknaa) here means the joint has shifted/slipped out of its socket, not that anything has broken or snapped.
Sample Sentences:
खेलते वक़्त उसका कंधा खिसक गया। His shoulder got dislocated while playing.
(khelte vaqt uskaa kandhaa khisak gayaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
गिरने से उसकी कमर की हड्डी खिसक गई। His back bone slipped out of place from the fall.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
