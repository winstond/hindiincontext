धब्बा (dhabbaa)

noun Mainly a concrete noun for a stain or blot on cloth/surface, with a common figurative extension to a stain on one's reputation or character.
Sense 1 : stain, spot, blot (physical)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दाग (daag), निशान (nishaan, mark, broader scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any mother about a शर्ट पर धब्बा (shart par dhabbaa, 'a stain on the shirt') and you'll hear about ठंडे पानी से धोना (thande paanii se dhonaa, 'washing with cold water') as the go-to fix -- a very domestic, kitchen-and-laundry word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for दाग (daag) automatically; धब्बा (dhabbaa) is equally common in everyday speech for a visible splotch on clothes, walls, etc., and the two are largely interchangeable in this literal sense.

Sense 2 : blot on reputation, disgrace
noun, masculine Commonly used in the pattern X पर धब्बा लगना/लगाना (X par dhabbaa lagnaa/lagaanaa, 'a stain to fall on/be put on X'), where X is honor, name, or family.
Synonyms: दाग (daag, also used figuratively), कलंक (kalank, stronger, more literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in family dramas and news reports alike: खानदान के नाम पर धब्बा (khaandaan ke naam par dhabbaa, 'a stain on the family's name') captures how seriously reputation is guarded in everyday Hindi-speaking households.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense almost always pairs with लगना (lagnaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa) rather than standing alone -- साख पर धब्बा लगा (saakh par dhabbaa lagaa, 'a stain fell on the reputation') is the natural phrasing, not just धब्बा (dhabbaa) by itself.
