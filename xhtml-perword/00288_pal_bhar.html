पल भर (pal bhar)

adverb A compact time expression meaning 'a moment', 'the blink of an eye' -- पल (pal, 'moment') plus भर (bhar, 'full/whole'), literally 'a full moment', used to stress extreme brevity.
Sense 1 : for just a moment, for the briefest instant
adverb Functions adverbially without a postposition: पल भर रुको (pal bhar ruko, 'wait a moment') -- no के लिए (ke lie) is needed, though it can be added for emphasis.
Synonyms: एक पल (ek pal), ज़रा सी देर (zaraa sii der), लम्हा भर (lamhaa bhar, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in everyday requests like पल भर ठहरो (pal bhar thahro, 'hold on a second') -- it has a slightly more literary or emotive color than the plain एक मिनट (ek minat), so it shows up often in songs, poetry, and heartfelt speech about fleeting time.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often insert के लिए (ke lie) after पल भर (pal bhar) thinking it is required like in English 'for a moment' -- it is optional here, since भर (bhar) itself already carries the sense of 'for the duration of'. 2) Do not confuse with पल पल (pal pal, 'every moment, constantly') -- the reduplication changes the meaning to continuousness rather than brevity.
Sample Sentences:
पल भर ठहरो, मैं अभी आता हूँ। Wait for a moment, I'll be right there.
(pal bhar thahro, main abhii aataa hoon।)
