आंदोलन (aandolan)

noun A single core meaning: an organized movement or agitation, usually political or social. Also commonly spelled with the nasalization dot as आन्दोलन (aandolan).
Sense 1 : movement (political/social)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आन्दोलन (aandolan, variant spelling with chandrabindu), प्रदर्शन (pradarshan, protest/demonstration), विरोध (virodh, opposition/protest), अभियान (abhiyaan, campaign)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear constantly around Indian civics and history: असहयोग आंदोलन (asahyog aandolan, the Non-Cooperation Movement), किसान आंदोलन (kisaan aandolan, the farmers' movement). It carries real weight in the news and in political conversation, and Delhi in particular -- with its protest sites and marches near India Gate or Jantar Mantar -- has made this a very live, everyday word rather than a dusty textbook term.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use आंदोलन (aandolan) for any personal 'movement' (like moving house or moving an object) -- it's reserved for organized collective action, not physical motion. 2) Confusable with प्रदर्शन (pradarshan): a प्रदर्शन (pradarshan) is a single protest/demonstration event, while आंदोलन (aandolan) is the broader sustained campaign it might be part of.
