भद्दा (bhaddaa)

adjective One adjective with two closely linked senses: physically ugly/unattractive, and (by extension) crude, tasteless, or unseemly in manner, speech, or humor.
Sense 1 : ugly, unattractive in appearance
adjective
Synonyms: बदसूरत (badsoorat), कुरूप (kuroop, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly about clothes, colors, haircuts, or furniture -- यह रंग भद्दा है (yah rang bhaddaa hai, 'this color is ugly') is a very ordinary complaint in a Delhi market or living room. It agrees in gender and number with the noun it describes, so you will meet भद्दा (bhaddaa), भद्दे (bhadde), and भद्दी (bhaddii) depending on context.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बुरा (buraa, 'bad') when they mean 'ugly' -- भद्दा (bhaddaa) is the word for visual/aesthetic ugliness, while बुरा (buraa) covers general badness of quality or behavior.
Sample Sentences:

(yah rang bahut bhaddaa hai।)

Sense 2 : crude, tasteless, or unseemly (of behavior, jokes, remarks)
adjective Commonly pairs with मज़ाक (mazaak, 'joke'), बात (baat, 'remark'), or हरकत (harkat, 'act/gesture') to mean a joke or comment that crosses a line of taste or propriety.
Synonyms: फूहड़ (phoohad), अश्लील (ashliil, leans toward vulgar/obscene)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the extended, figurative cousin of the 'ugly' sense: something that looks fine on the surface but feels distasteful or improper once you notice it, much like an object that is visually भद्दा (bhaddaa). Elders often use it to scold -- ऐसी भद्दी बात मत करो (aisii bhaddii baat mat karo, 'don't say such a crude thing') is a classic mild reprimand.
Common Mistakes: 1) भद्दा मज़ाक (bhaddaa mazaak, 'crude joke') is stronger than just 'a bad joke' -- it implies the joke was embarrassing, in poor taste, or crossed a social line, not merely unfunny.
