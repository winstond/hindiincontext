तनना (tannaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to stretch, become taut/tense' -- used both literally (a rope, a wire, a muscle going tight) and figuratively (a person's mood or relations turning tense/strained). Related to the transitive तानना (taannaa, 'to stretch/pull taut'), which is its causative-like counterpart.
Sense 1 : to become taut, stretch tight (of a rope, wire, muscle, etc.)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: खिंचना (khinchnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a clothesline or a tent rope: रस्सी तन गई (rassii tan gaii, 'the rope became taut') is the everyday image Hindi speakers reach for. The same verb extends naturally to a body part going stiff, like a neck or a nerve, before it extends further into the emotional sense below.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for तानना (taannaa) when they mean तनना (tannaa); तानना (taannaa) is transitive ('to pull/stretch something taut'), while तनना (tannaa) is what the rope or wire itself does on its own.

धूप में सूखने से रस्सी तन गई। The rope became taut from drying in the sun.
(dhoop men sookhne se rassii tan gaii।)

Sense 2 : to become tense, strained (of mood, relations, or atmosphere)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बिगड़ना (bigadnaa, of relations turning sour), तनातनी होना (tanaatnii honaa, to have tension/friction)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same physical image as the literal sense, just applied to people: a strained relationship or a stiff, unfriendly face is described as if it were a rope pulled taut, तना हुआ (tanaa huaa). The related noun तनाव (tanaav, 'tension, stress') comes directly from this root and is one of the most common words for stress in modern Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is often used with रिश्ते (rishte, 'relations') or माहौल (maahaul, 'atmosphere') as the subject, not with a person directly -- learners tend to say 'वह तन गया' (vah tan gayaa) to mean 'he got tense,' when a native speaker is more likely to say उसका चेहरा तन गया (uskaa chehraa tan gayaa, 'his face went taut/tense') or describe the relationship or mood itself as तना हुआ (tanaa huaa, 'strained').

(donon deshon ke biich rishte tan gae hain।)
