धारावाहिक (dhaaraavaahik)

noun/adjective Primarily used as a noun for a TV serial/soap opera, with a related adjective sense meaning 'serial, continuous, in installments' (dhaaraavaahik).
Sense 1 : TV serial, soap opera
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सीरियल (siiriyal, very common, from English)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Indian households, especially in the evening, are famous for their loyalty to a favorite धारावाहिक (dhaaraavaahik) -- mothers and grandmothers planning dinner around the serial's timing is a very real, very common scene in Delhi-NCR homes. The word itself comes from धारा (dhaaraa, 'stream/flow'), so a धारावाहिक (dhaaraavaahik) is literally something that 'flows on' episode after episode.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for सीरियल (siiriyal) since it is so common in speech; धारावाहिक (dhaaraavaahik) is the word you will see in newspapers, TV guides, and formal announcements about a show, so recognizing it in writing matters even if you say सीरियल (siiriyal) out loud. 2) Do not confuse with फ़िल्म (film, 'film/movie') -- धारावाहिक (dhaaraavaahik) specifically implies an ongoing, episodic show, not a one-off movie.

Sense 2 : serial, continuous, in installments
adjective Used before a noun to describe something released or happening in a continuous series, e.g. a story published in installments in a magazine.
Synonyms: क्रमिक (kramik, more formal/technical, emphasizes sequence)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This adjective sense is less common in casual speech than the noun sense (TV serial), but you will meet it in phrases like धारावाहिक प्रकाशन (dhaaraavaahik prakaashan, 'serialized publication') describing novels or stories published chapter by chapter in old-school magazines and newspapers.
