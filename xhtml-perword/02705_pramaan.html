प्रमाण (pramaan)

noun A formal, somewhat learned word for 'proof' or 'evidence'; also extends to 'certificate/document of proof' in compounds like प्रमाण पत्र (pramaan patr).
Sense 1 : proof, evidence
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सबूत (saboot), गवाही (gavaahii, testimony/evidence given by a witness)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word stamped on every official document in India -- जन्म प्रमाण पत्र (janm pramaan patr, birth certificate), जाति प्रमाण पत्र (jaati pramaan patr, caste certificate) -- so even learners who never use प्रमाण in daily talk will see it constantly on forms and government offices. Think of सबूत as what you say to a friend, and प्रमाण as what a lawyer or news anchor says.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सबूत (saboot) by default since it is more common in spoken Hindi; प्रमाण (pramaan) is the word you will meet in writing, news, courtroom drama, and academic or official speech rather than casual conversation. 2) It combines heavily in compounds -- प्रमाण पत्र (pramaan patr, certificate) is extremely common even though प्रमाण alone stays formal.

इस दावे का कोई प्रमाण नहीं है। There is no proof of this claim.
(is daave kaa koii pramaan nahiin hai।)
