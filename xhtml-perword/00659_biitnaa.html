बीतना (biitnaa)

verb An intransitive verb used mainly for time passing or elapsing, and by extension for going through/undergoing a period of life or an experience.
Sense 1 : for time to pass/elapse
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: गुज़रना (guzarnaa), गुजरना (gujarnaa, variant spelling without nuqta)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi treats time as something that flows past on its own, not something a person actively moves through -- साल बीत गया (saal biit gayaa, 'the year passed') has no human agent at all. This fits the broader pattern of involuntary/experiential verbs in Hindi where time, feelings, and states happen *to* you rather than being controlled by you.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make a person the grammatical subject with ने (ne), but बीतना (biitnaa) is intransitive and takes only time-expressions (साल (saal), वक़्त (vaqt), घंटा (ghantaa)) as its subject -- the person appears with पर (par) or का/की (kaa/kii), never as the one who 'does' the passing. 2) Do not confuse with बिताना (bitaanaa), the transitive causative meaning 'to spend/pass (time)' -- वक़्त बीता (vaqt biitaa, 'time passed') versus मैंने वक़्त बिताया (mainne vaqt bitaayaa, 'I spent time').
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : for a period/experience to be undergone or lived through
verb, intransitive Used with पर (par) to mark the person affected: उस पर बहुत मुश्किल वक़्त बीता (us par bahut mushkil vaqt biitaa, 'she went through a very hard time') -- the difficulty is framed as passing over the person, not being caused by them.
Synonyms: गुज़रना (guzarnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense extends the time-passing image to life experiences: बचपन गाँव में बीता (bachpan gaanv men biitaa, 'childhood was spent in the village') describes a whole phase of life flowing past, echoing how Hindi speakers often narrate their life story as things that happened to them rather than things they did.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to साथ (saath) instead of पर (par) when marking who underwent the experience -- the correct construction is दिन/वक़्त किसी पर बीतना (kisii par biitnaa), literally 'a day/time passes upon someone.'
