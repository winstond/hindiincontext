ख़त (khat)

noun A single everyday sense: a written letter (post). Also commonly spelled without the nuqta as खत (khat).
Sense 1 : letter (written correspondence)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पत्र (patr, formal), चिट्ठी (chitthii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: ख़त (khat) carries a slightly romantic, old-world flavor in Hindi film and song culture -- think of the entire genre of prem-patra (love letter) songs -- even though actual letter-writing has faded with email and texting. In everyday speech today it often shows up nostalgically, as in ख़त लिखा करते थे (khat likhaa karte the, 'we used to write letters'), evoking a slower, pre-digital era.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पत्र (patr) in casual speech; among friends and family, ख़त (khat) or चिट्ठी (chitthii) sounds far more natural, with पत्र (patr) reserved for formal or official contexts. 2) The verb pairing is ख़त लिखना (khat likhnaa, 'to write a letter') or ख़त भेजना (khat bhejnaa, 'to send a letter') -- learners sometimes default to देना (denaa) as if handing something over, which sounds off for postal letters.
