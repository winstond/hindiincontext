संदर्भ (sandarbh)

noun A formal/written-register noun meaning 'context' or 'reference' -- the two senses are closely related and often overlap in usage.
Sense 1 : context, background situation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रसंग (prasang), परिप्रेक्ष्य (pariprekshy, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in newspapers and news broadcasts -- phrases like इस संदर्भ में (is sandarbh men, 'in this context') are a staple of Hindi journalistic and bureaucratic style. It has a slightly bookish flavor, so a Delhi shopkeeper is unlikely to use it, but a news anchor uses it every day.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for यह (yah) or स्थिति (sthiti) when they mean 'context,' but संदर्भ (sandarbh) is the standard word in written and formal spoken Hindi for the surrounding circumstances that give meaning to a statement or event. 2) It rarely appears in casual chit-chat -- expect it in news, essays, and academic or official speech rather than everyday bazaar conversation.
Sample Sentences:
इस संदर्भ में सरकार ने अभी कोई बयान नहीं दिया है। In this context, the government has not yet given any statement.
(is sandarbh men sarkaar ne abhii koii bayaan nahiin diyaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(is mudde ko sahii sandarbh men samajhnaa zaroorii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(binaa sandarbh jaane us bayaan kaa matlab nikaalnaa mushkil hai।)

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(agar sandarbh badal jaae, to is vaaky kaa arth bhii badal jaaegaa।)

(is sandarbh men dekhaa jaae to unkaa faislaa sahii lagtaa hai।)

(riport men ghatnaa kaa sandarbh spasht roop se nahiin diyaa gayaa।)

(aap kripyaa is bayaan kaa sandarbh spasht karen।)

(us zamaane ke saamaajik sandarbh men yah faislaa kraantikaarii thaa।)

Sense 2 : reference, citation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हवाला (havaalaa), उल्लेख (ullekh)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up heavily in academic writing -- संदर्भ ग्रंथ (sandarbh granth, 'reference book') and संदर्भ सूची (sandarbh soochii, 'bibliography/list of references') are standard terms any Hindi-medium student learns for essays and theses.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense संदर्भ (sandarbh) pairs naturally with देना (denaa) or लेना (lenaa) -- संदर्भ देना (sandarbh denaa, 'to give a reference') -- much as 'reference' does in academic English; learners sometimes instead force in हवाला देना (havaalaa denaa) even when the more bookish संदर्भ (sandarbh) is what a written source calls for.
Sample Sentences:
इस निबंध के अंत में संदर्भ सूची दी गई है। A bibliography is given at the end of this essay.
(is nibandh ke ant men sandarbh soochii dii gaii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
कृपया हर तथ्य के साथ उसका संदर्भ भी लिखिए। Please also write the reference along with every fact.
(kripyaa har tathy ke saath uskaa sandarbh bhii likhie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
उसने अपने शोधपत्र में दस संदर्भों का इस्तेमाल किया। He used ten references in his research paper.
(usne apne shodhpatr men das sandarbhon kaa istemaal kiyaa।)

अगर तुम यह पंक्ति उद्धृत कर रहे हो, तो संदर्भ भी देना ज़रूरी है। If you are quoting this line, giving the reference is also necessary.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
