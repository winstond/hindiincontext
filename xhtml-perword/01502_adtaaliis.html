अड़तालीस (adtaaliis)

numeral The cardinal number 'forty-eight' (adtaaliis). A single-sense number word used for counting and quantity, like all other Hindi numerals from 1 to 100.
Sense 1 : the number 48
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, अड़तालीस (adtaaliis) is irregular and just needs rote learning -- Hindi numerals don't follow the clean base-ten logic English speakers expect, which is why Delhi kids spend a good chunk of primary school chanting number tables from 1 to 100.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals 1-99 are not built from predictable patterns the way English tens-and-units are; अड़तालीस (adtaaliis) must simply be memorized as a whole word rather than assembled from अड़ (ad) + तालीस (taaliis) pieces. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with अड़तीस (adtiis, 'thirty-eight') or अड़सठ (adsath, 'sixty-eight') since several numbers share the अड़- prefix pattern across different decades.
