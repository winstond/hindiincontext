महत्त्व (mahattv)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'importance/significance.' Also commonly spelled महत्व (mahatv) with a single त -- both spellings are widely used and accepted.
Sense 1 : importance, significance
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अहमियत (ahmiyat), प्रधानता (pradhaantaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in slightly formal contexts -- school essays, news anchors, office meetings -- with the common frame X का महत्त्व (X kaa mahattv, 'the importance of X'), as in समय का महत्त्व (samay kaa mahattv, 'the importance of time'). In everyday chat, people often reach for the more casual अहमियत (ahmiyat) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use this as an adjective ('important'); for that meaning Hindi switches to महत्त्वपूर्ण (mahattvpoorn) or ज़रूरी (zaroorii), not महत्त्व (mahattv) itself. 2) The double त in महत्त्व (mahattv) is frequently simplified to महत्व (mahatv) in casual writing; both are correct, so don't be alarmed seeing either.
Sample Sentences:
समय का महत्त्व समझना बहुत ज़रूरी है। It is very important to understand the importance of time.
(samay kaa mahattv samajhnaa bahut zaroorii hai।)
