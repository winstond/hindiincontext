निगरानी (nigraanii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'supervision, surveillance, monitoring, or watch/oversight' -- covers everything from a guard keeping watch to a manager overseeing work.
Sense 1 : supervision, surveillance, watch/oversight
noun, feminine Commonly used with रखना (rakhnaa) as in निगरानी रखना (nigraanii rakhnaa, 'to keep watch/surveillance') or the verb करना (karnaa) as निगरानी करना (nigraanii karnaa, 'to supervise/monitor'). The object being watched is marked with की (kii): किसी की निगरानी करना (kisii kii nigraanii karnaa, 'to keep watch over someone').
Synonyms: देखरेख (dekhrekh), निरीक्षण (niriikshan, formal), पहरा (pahraa, guarding specifically), देखभाल (dekhbhaal, care/looking after)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in news broadcasts about police या (yaa) CCTV निगरानी (nigraanii) ('surveillance'), but it is just as at home in a family context -- a mother keeping निगरानी (nigraanii) on a child doing homework, or a landlord keeping निगरानी (nigraanii) on tenants. It carries a slightly more formal, watchful-authority tone than the softer देखभाल (dekhbhaal), which leans toward caring attention rather than guarding against something going wrong.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say निगरानी देना (nigraanii denaa) by analogy with English 'give supervision', but the natural verbs are निगरानी करना (nigraanii karnaa) or निगरानी रखना (nigraanii rakhnaa). 2) The person or thing being monitored takes की (kii), not पर (par) or को (ko) -- घर की निगरानी (ghar kii nigraanii, 'watch over the house'), not घर पर निगरानी (ghar par nigraanii) in the sense of watching the house itself (पर (par) works only when talking about location, e.g. सड़क पर निगरानी (sadak par nigraanii, 'surveillance on the street')).
