सहायता (sahaaytaa)

noun A single-sense formal noun meaning 'help/assistance'; often paired with the verb करना (karnaa) or contrasted with the more everyday मदद (madad).
Sense 1 : help, assistance, aid
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मदद (madad), इमदाद (imdaad, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet सहायता (sahaaytaa) constantly in official and written contexts -- bank forms, disaster relief bulletins, NGO names -- while मदद (madad) is what you'd actually say to a friend on the street. Think of सहायता (sahaaytaa) as the Hindi register you reach for when writing an email or reading a newspaper headline.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मदद (madad) in every context out of habit; सहायता (sahaaytaa) is the word you'll see on government forms, NGO signage, and news reports ('राहत और सहायता', raahat aur sahaaytaa, 'relief and aid') and sounds noticeably more formal in speech. 2) The common verb pairing is सहायता करना (sahaaytaa karnaa, 'to help') or सहायता देना (sahaaytaa denaa, 'to give aid') -- not सहायता होना (sahaaytaa honaa) for the act of helping someone.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार ने बाढ़ पीड़ितों को हर संभव सहायता देने का आश्वासन दिया। The government assured that it would give all possible help to the flood victims.
(sarkaar ne baadh piiditon ko har sambhav sahaaytaa dene kaa aashvaasan diyaa।)
