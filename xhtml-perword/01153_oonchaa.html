ऊँचा (oonchaa)

adjective A common variable adjective covering physical height/elevation and its figurative extensions into loudness, social rank, and moral/emotional stature.
Sense 1 : physically tall or high
adjective
Synonyms: लंबा (lambaa), बुलंद (buland, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a building, a wall, a mountain, or a stack of rotis on a plate -- ऊँचा (oonchaa) is the go-to word for vertical extent, while लंबा (lambaa) covers a person's height or a road's length.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लंबा (lambaa) for both 'tall' and 'long', which is fine for people and roads, but for a wall, mountain, or building ऊँचा (oonchaa) is the natural choice -- लंबा (lambaa) there would sound odd, describing length rather than height.
Sample Sentences:
यह इमारत बहुत ऊँची है। This building is very tall.
(yah imaarat bahut oonchii hai।)

Sense 2 : loud (of sound/voice)
adjective
Synonyms: तेज़ (tez)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Just as English says 'raise your voice', Hindi literally makes the voice tall: ऊँची आवाज़ (oonchii aavaaz) is what a mother uses when calling kids in from the street for dinner.
Common Mistakes: 1) With आवाज़ (aavaaz, 'voice'), ऊँचा (oonchaa) means 'loud/raised', not 'high-pitched' -- Hindi maps volume onto the height metaphor, so ऊँची आवाज़ में बोलना (oonchii aavaaz men bolnaa, 'to speak loudly') is about volume, not pitch.
Sample Sentences:
माँ ने ऊँची आवाज़ में बच्चों को बुलाया। The mother called the children in a loud voice.
(maan ne oonchii aavaaz men bachchon ko bulaayaa।)

Sense 3 : socially elevated, high-status
adjective
Synonyms: उच्च (uchch, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ऊँचा खानदान (oonchaa khaandaan, 'a high/respected family') or ऊँची पहुँच (oonchii pahunch, 'high-level connections') show how vertical height doubles as social height in Hindi -- much like English 'upper class', but the metaphor runs even more pervasively through everyday speech about family, caste, and connections.
Sample Sentences:
उसका खानदान बहुत ऊँचा माना जाता है। His family is considered very high-status.
(uskaa khaandaan bahut oonchaa maanaa jaataa hai।)

Sense 4 : morally or emotionally elevated; noble/lofty
adjective
Synonyms: महान (mahaan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ऊँचे विचार (oonche vichaar, 'lofty thoughts/ideals') or ऊँची सोच (oonchii soch, 'elevated thinking') extend the height metaphor to character -- a person who thinks big or acts nobly is described as reaching upward, just as in English we say someone has 'high ideals'.
