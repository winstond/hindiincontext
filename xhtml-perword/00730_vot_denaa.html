वोट देना (vot denaa)

verb A borrowed-plus-native compound: the English loanword वोट (vot, 'vote') combined with देना (denaa, 'to give'), meaning 'to cast a vote/to vote.'
Sense 1 : to vote (for someone/something)
verb, transitive The person or party voted for is marked with को (ko): उसे वोट देना (use vot denaa, 'to vote for him/her').
Synonyms: मतदान करना (matdaan karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around elections in India -- news anchors, posters, and family debates around election season all use वोट देना (vot denaa) rather than the more formal मतदान करना (matdaan karnaa), which shows up mainly in official announcements and civic education campaigns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say वोट करना (vot karnaa) by analogy with other English-loanword verbs, but the natural collocation is वोट देना (vot denaa) -- you 'give' a vote in Hindi, you don't 'do' one. 2) The candidate/party voted for takes को (ko), not के लिए (ke lie): मैंने उसे वोट दिया (mainne use vot diyaa, 'I voted for him'), not मैंने उसके लिए वोट दिया.
Sample Sentences:
मैं हर बार चुनाव में वोट देता हूँ। I vote in every election.
(main har baar chunaav men vot detaa hoon।)
