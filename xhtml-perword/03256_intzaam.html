इंतज़ाम (intzaam)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'arrangement' or 'organization' of something practical -- a party, a trip, a system of doing things.
Sense 1 : arrangement, organization, management (of a practical matter)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: व्यवस्था (vyavasthaa), बंदोबस्त (bandobast)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly around weddings, parties, and travel -- 'शादी का इंतज़ाम' (shaadii kaa intzaam, 'wedding arrangements') is a phrase you will hear from every Delhi family in the months before a wedding. It is a Persian-origin loanword fully at home in everyday Hindi, so there's no formality baggage attached to using it in casual conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often pair it with होना (honaa) alone when the natural verb of action is करना (karnaa): इंतज़ाम करना (intzaam karnaa, 'to make arrangements') is the go-to phrase for actively organizing something, while इंतज़ाम होना (intzaam honaa) describes the arrangement existing or being in place. 2) The compound verb इंतज़ाम कर देना (intzaam kar denaa, 'to go ahead and arrange it') is very common in spoken requests and offers, and learners tend to drop the देना (denaa) even when the sense is 'take care of it for you.'

(shaadii kaa intzaam bahut badaa kaam hotaa hai।)
