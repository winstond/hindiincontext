पैदाइश (paidaaish)

noun A noun meaning 'birth' or 'origin/production' -- most often met in the fixed phrase for stating where someone was born, and in formal/administrative talk of birth or manufacture.
Sense 1 : birth (of a person); native origin
noun, feminine Common in the pattern X की पैदाइश होना (X kii paidaaish honaa) meaning 'to be a native of X,' e.g. दिल्ली की पैदाइश (dillii kii paidaaish, 'a native of Delhi').
Synonyms: जन्म (janm), उत्पत्ति (utpatti, formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in casual conversation when people are placing each other: कहाँ की पैदाइश हो? (kahaan kii paidaaish ho?, 'where are you originally from?'). It has a slightly earthier, more spoken-Hindustani feel than जन्म (janm), which leans more formal or written.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for जन्म (janm) by default; पैदाइश (paidaaish) is the more colloquial, everyday-spoken equivalent, especially when describing someone's hometown of origin rather than the formal fact of birth. 2) In the 'native of' sense, it is used as a noun describing the person (वह दिल्ली की पैदाइश है, vah dillii kii paidaaish hai, 'he is a native of Delhi'), not as a verb -- learners sometimes try to verb-ify it directly.

Sense 2 : production, manufacture, or generation (of goods/things)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: उत्पादन (utpaadan, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This usage shows up mostly in older or formal administrative Hindi-Urdu, describing where something is 'produced' or 'made' -- less alive in daily speech than the birth/origin sense above.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense overlaps with उत्पादन (utpaadan) but पैदाइश (paidaaish) is less common in this meaning today and can sound slightly dated or bureaucratic when applied to goods rather than living things.
Sample Sentences:
इस इलाके की पैदाइश गेहूं और गन्ना है। The produce of this region is wheat and sugarcane.
(is ilaake kii paidaaish gehoon aur gannaa hai।)
