गुब्बारा (gubbaaraa)

noun A concrete everyday noun meaning 'balloon' -- both the toy/party object and, by extension, a hot-air balloon.
Sense 1 : balloon (toy/party balloon)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian birthday party or Independence Day/Republic Day celebration is decked out with strings of गुब्बारे (gubbaare, 'balloons'), and vendors selling them outside parks and metro stations are a familiar sight in Delhi-NCR. Kids also use the word playfully to mean someone or something 'puffed up' -- see the figurative sense below.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget गुब्बारा (gubbaaraa) is masculine, so its adjectives and verb agreement must follow the masculine pattern, e.g. लाल गुब्बारा (laal gubbaaraa, 'red balloon'), not the feminine form.

Sense 2 : hot-air balloon
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: The same word covers the large hot-air balloon used in fairs, festivals like Pushkar, or news reports about ballooning -- Hindi doesn't distinguish it from the party balloon with a separate word, so context alone tells you the size.

Sense 3 : something inflated/exaggerated, empty bluster (figurative)
noun, masculine Used mainly in fixed expressions like गुब्बारा फूटना (gubbaaraa phootnaa, 'the balloon bursts,' i.e. pretensions collapse) or गुब्बारा फुलाना (gubbaaraa phulaanaa, 'to inflate the balloon,' i.e. to talk something up).
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as English speakers say someone's 'balloon got burst,' Hindi speakers use गुब्बारा फूटना (gubbaaraa phootnaa) for a boast or grand plan collapsing suddenly -- a nice case where the two languages share almost the exact same image.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense only surfaces inside set phrases about inflating or bursting -- learners sometimes try to use गुब्बारा (gubbaaraa) alone to mean 'bragging,' which doesn't work without the verb (फूटना (phootnaa) or फुलाना (phulaanaa)) that carries the metaphor.
Sample Sentences:
उसके सारे दावे सिर्फ गुब्बारा थे। All his claims were just hot air.
(uske saare daave sirph gubbaaraa the।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
