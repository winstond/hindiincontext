तसल्ली (tasallii)

noun A warm, everyday word centered on emotional reassurance and peace of mind; mainly one core sense used in a few very common fixed phrases.
Sense 1 : reassurance, peace of mind, satisfaction
noun, feminine Frequently appears with दिलाना (dilaanaa, 'to give reassurance to someone') and होना (honaa), as in तसल्ली होना (tasallii honaa, 'to feel reassured') and तसल्ली देना (tasallii denaa, 'to reassure someone').
Synonyms: संतोष (santosh), इत्मीनान (itmiinaan, variant register, often more common in Urdu-influenced speech), सुकून (sukoon, leans toward inner peace)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic word for the comfort a mother, doctor, or friend gives you when you're worried -- तसल्ली रखो (tasallii rakho, 'stay assured/calm') is something you'll hear constantly in Delhi households when someone is anxious about exam results, a sick relative, or a delayed train. It has a distinctly Urdu-Persian warmth to it, sitting comfortably in both casual and slightly formal speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for संतुष्टि (santushti) or संतोष (santosh) when तसल्ली (tasallii) is the more natural, warmer choice in spoken reassurance -- it carries a comforting, personal tone that the more formal Sanskritic words lack. 2) तसल्ली (tasallii) pairs with होना (honaa) or मिलना (milnaa) for 'feeling reassured' (मुझे तसल्ली हुई, mujhe tasallii huii, 'I felt reassured') -- learners sometimes wrongly use करना (karnaa) here instead of देना (denaa) when the meaning is 'to reassure someone.'
Sample Sentences:
तसल्ली रखो, सब ठीक हो जाएगा। Stay assured, everything will be fine.
(tasallii rakho, sab thiik ho jaaegaa।)
