पोती (potii)

noun Refers to a son's daughter, i.e. one's paternal granddaughter (English has no single word for this specific relation).
Sense 1 : granddaughter (through a son)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi family vocabulary is very precise about lineage: पोती (potii) is specifically your son's daughter, while your daughter's daughter is नातिन (naatin). In many North Indian families, this distinction still carries real social weight, since a पोती (potii) is traditionally seen as carrying forward the father's family line, whereas a नातिन (naatin) belongs to her father's family. The masculine pair is पोता (potaa) (son's son) and नाती (naatii) (daughter's son).
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not confuse with नातिन (naatin), which is a daughter's daughter -- Hindi kinship terms distinguish the paternal versus maternal line, unlike English 'granddaughter,' which covers both. 2) Do not confuse with पोता (potaa), the masculine counterpart meaning grandson through a son.
Sample Sentences:
यह मेरी पोती है। This is my granddaughter (son's daughter).
(yah merii potii hai।)
