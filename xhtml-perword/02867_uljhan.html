उलझन (uljhan)

noun A feminine noun meaning a state of being tangled or confused -- used both literally (a knot, a tangle) and, far more commonly, figuratively for mental confusion or a perplexing problem. Related to the verb उलझना (ulajhnaa, 'to get entangled').
Sense 1 : mental confusion, perplexity, dilemma
noun, feminine
Synonyms: उलझाव (uljhaav), असमंजस (asmanjas), दुविधा (duvidhaa), भ्रम (bhram)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi speakers reach for उलझन (uljhan) constantly in everyday complaint-mode -- about traffic, relationships, paperwork, exam choices -- anywhere your head feels tangled rather than simply sad or angry. It pairs naturally with the phrase उलझन में पड़ना (uljhan men padnaa, 'to fall into confusion'), which frames confusion as a state you fall into, not one you actively create.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for भ्रम (bhram) when they mean a nagging, unresolved worry rather than a simple factual mistake or illusion -- उलझन (uljhan) is the better word when someone is stuck weighing options or tangled in a problem, not merely mistaken about a fact. 2) The verb form उलझना (ulajhnaa) is intransitive ('to become entangled'); learners sometimes try to use उलझन (uljhan) itself as if it were a verb -- it is only the noun, so you need a separate verb like होना (honaa) or पड़ना (padnaa) to build a sentence around it.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : a physical tangle or knot (of thread, hair, wires)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गुत्थी (gutthii), गांठ (gaanth, mainly a tied knot rather than a loose tangle)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the literal root sense -- think of tangled thread or hair, बालों की उलझन (baalon kii uljhan, 'the tangle in one's hair') -- from which the far more frequent 'mental confusion' sense grows by natural metaphor, the same way English uses 'tangled up' for a messy problem.
