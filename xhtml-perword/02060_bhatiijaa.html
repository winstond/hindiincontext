भतीजा (bhatiijaa)

noun A kinship noun meaning 'nephew' (specifically brother's son) -- one sense only.
Sense 1 : nephew (brother's son)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most Indian families, the distinction between a brother's child and a sister's child matters a lot more than it does in English, because these relationships often carry different social and ritual expectations (a भतीजा (bhatiijaa) usually stays closer to the paternal family line, especially in traditional or joint-family setups). Compare the parallel pair: भतीजी (bhatiijii) is 'niece' on the brother's side, while भांजा/भांजी (bhaanjaa/bhaanjii) cover the sister's side.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi distinguishes nephew by side: भतीजा (bhatiijaa) is specifically the son of one's brother, while भांजा (bhaanjaa) is the son of one's sister -- English 'nephew' collapses this distinction, so learners often use the wrong one. 2) Learners sometimes forget this noun takes the masculine oblique/plural pattern like लड़का (ladkaa) -- e.g. भतीजे को (bhatiije ko, 'to the nephew') -- not an invariant form.
