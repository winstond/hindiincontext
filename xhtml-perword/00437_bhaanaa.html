भाना (bhaanaa)

verb A verb meaning 'to seem pleasing/agreeable to' -- it almost always appears with a dative (को-marked) experiencer, like लगना (lagnaa) and पसंद आना (pasand aanaa), and is most common in its negative form.
Sense 1 : to be pleasing, to appeal (to someone)
verb, intransitive Takes a को-marked experiencer: X को Y भाता है (X ko Y bhaataa hai, 'X finds Y pleasing') -- the thing that pleases is the grammatical subject, and the verb agrees with it, not with the person it pleases.
Synonyms: पसंद आना (pasand aanaa), अच्छा लगना (achchhaa lagnaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this most in slightly reflective or old-fashioned speech -- someone saying किसी को कुछ नहीं भाता (kisii ko kuchh nahiin bhaataa, 'nothing appeals to someone') to describe a sulky mood or a picky eater, with a gentle, almost storytelling tone rather than everyday chit-chat register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पसंद आना (pasand aanaa) or अच्छा लगना (achchhaa lagnaa) instead, since भाना (bhaanaa) is less common in casual speech and can sound slightly literary or old-fashioned in the affirmative. 2) भाना (bhaanaa) is heard far more often in the negative -- न भाना (n bhaanaa) or नहीं भाना (nahiin bhaanaa) -- describing something that fails to sit right with someone, than in plain positive statements.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे यह जगह बहुत भाती है। This place appeals to me a lot.
(mujhe yah jagah bahut bhaatii hai।)
