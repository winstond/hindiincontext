तबीयत (tabiiyat)

noun Mainly means physical/mental health or state of well-being, as in asking how someone is feeling; also extends to a person's temperament or natural disposition.
Sense 1 : health, physical/mental state
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सेहत (sehat), हालत (haalat, can also mean 'condition' more broadly)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Asking तबीयत (tabiiyat) कैसी है? (tabiiyat kaisii hai, 'how is your health?') is a warm, slightly more caring version of हाल (haal) चाल (chaal) पूछना (poochhnaa) -- you'll hear it especially when someone has been sick or is elderly, said with real concern in the voice.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सेहत (sehat) exclusively and miss that तबीयत (tabiiyat) is the more natural choice when asking how someone is feeling right now, especially if they were unwell. 2) तबीयत (tabiiyat) पड़ना (padnaa) is not a real combination -- the common phrase for 'to fall ill' is तबीयत (tabiiyat) ख़राब (kharaab) होना (honaa), not a verb built directly on तबीयत (tabiiyat).
Sample Sentences:
आज आपकी तबीयत कैसी है? How is your health today?
(aaj aapkii tabiiyat kaisii hai?)

Sense 2 : temperament, natural disposition
noun, feminine Used in fixed phrases like तबीयत (tabiiyat) का (kaa) (+ adjective) to describe someone's inherent nature, e.g. 'a person of a generous nature.'
Synonyms: मिज़ाज (mizaaj), स्वभाव (svabhaav)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in phrases like तबीयत (tabiiyat) से (se) काम (kaam) करना (karnaa, 'to work wholeheartedly/with relish') -- here तबीयत (tabiiyat) captures a sense of doing something with your whole heart and personal flair, not just health.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense only appears in set phrases like तबीयत (tabiiyat) का (kaa) शायर (shaayar, 'a poet by temperament') -- learners sometimes try to use it as freely as स्वभाव (svabhaav) in any sentence about personality, but it sounds natural mainly in these fixed constructions.

(uskii tabiiyat hii kuchh aisii hai ki vah har kaam men jaan daal detaa hai।)
