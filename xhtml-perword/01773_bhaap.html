भाप (bhaap)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'steam/vapor', with a natural extension into kitchen-related compound usage (steaming food).
Sense 1 : steam, vapor
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वाष्प (vaashp, literary/technical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of the rising cloud off a hot cup of chai (chaay) on a winter morning in Delhi, or the whistle of a pressure cooker -- that's भाप (bhaap) in everyday life. It's also the word behind भाप में पकाना (bhaap men pakaanaa, 'to steam-cook'), used for idlis, momos, and other steamed foods that are very much part of the modern Indian kitchen.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for वाष्प (vaashp) as the default word for 'steam', but in daily kitchen or bathroom contexts भाप (bhaap) is what people actually say -- वाष्प (vaashp) sounds bookish/scientific. 2) The common cooking phrase is भाप में पकाना (bhaap men pakaanaa, 'to cook in steam / to steam something') -- learners sometimes say भाप से पकाना (bhaap se pakaanaa) by analogy with other instrumental uses of से (se), but में (men) is the natural choice here since the food sits inside the steam.
