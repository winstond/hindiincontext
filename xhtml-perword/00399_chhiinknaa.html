छींकना (chhiinknaa)

verb A single-sense verb: 'to sneeze' -- a straightforward everyday bodily-action verb.
Sense 1 : to sneeze
verb, intransitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As with sneezing superstitions in many cultures, older generations in Delhi households sometimes pause for a second if someone sneezes right as they're about to leave the house, treating it as a small omen to wait a moment before stepping out. Related to the noun छींक (chhiink, 'sneeze'), which is more often the word you'll hear in the involuntary construction छींक आना (chhiink aanaa) than the verb छींकना (chhiinknaa) itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for a noun+करना (karnaa) construction (like छींक करना (chhiink karnaa)) by analogy with other bodily actions, but Hindi uses the simple verb छींकना (chhiinknaa) directly -- there is a related noun छींक (chhiink, 'a sneeze') but it pairs with आना (aanaa), as in छींक आना (chhiink aanaa, 'to feel a sneeze coming'), not करना (karnaa).
