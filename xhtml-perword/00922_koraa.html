कोरा (koraa)

adjective Primarily 'blank/unused' as with paper or cloth (कोरा कागज़ (koraa kaagaz)); extends figuratively to 'empty/hollow' (talk, promises) and 'mere/sheer' as an intensifying adjective before an abstract noun.
Sense 1 : blank, unused (paper, cloth, page)
adjective
Synonyms: खाली (khaalii, empty/vacant, broader sense), अलिखित (alikhit, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask for कोरा कागज़ (koraa kaagaz) at a shop and everyone knows you mean a plain, unruled, untouched sheet -- this is the anchor sense from which the 'empty talk' meaning grows.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खाली (khaalii) here, but कोरा (koraa) specifically means 'never written/used on' -- a खाली पेज (khaalii pej) could just mean an empty page in a stack, while कोरा पेज (koraa pej) emphasizes it is untouched, fresh from the packet.

Sense 2 : empty, hollow, insincere (of words, promises, talk)
adjective Commonly pairs with बातें (baaten) or वादा (vaadaa) -- कोरी बातें (korii baaten, 'mere/empty talk') and कोरा वादा (koraa vaadaa, 'empty promise') are frequent set phrases.
Synonyms: झूठा (jhoothaa, false, stronger claim), बेबुनियाद (bebuniyaad, baseless)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear elders scold with कोरी बातों से कुछ नहीं होता (korii baaton se kuchh nahiin hotaa, 'nothing comes of empty talk') -- the image is of a page with fine-sounding words on it but no real substance behind them, echoing the literal 'blank' sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a figurative extension of the 'blank' sense -- a learner who only knows कोरा (koraa) as 'unused paper' may miss it entirely when someone says कोरी बातें मत करो (korii baaten mat karo, 'don't just talk empty words'), where no paper is involved at all.

Sense 3 : mere, sheer, nothing but (intensifier before an abstract noun)
adjective Used attributively before an abstract noun to dismiss it as insufficient on its own, e.g. कोरा ज्ञान किसी काम का नहीं (koraa gyaan kisii kaam kaa nahiin, 'mere/bookish knowledge is of no use').
Synonyms: सिर्फ (sirph, only, adverb rather than adjective), महज़ (mahaz, merely)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Teachers and elders love this construction to puncture pure theory: कोरा सिद्धांत काम नहीं आता (koraa siddhaant kaam nahiin aataa, 'bare theory is no use') -- it carries a practical, no-nonsense Indian sensibility that values tangible experience over abstraction.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense overlaps with the 'hollow talk' sense but is broader -- it can attach to almost any abstract noun (ज्ञान (gyaan), आदर्शवाद (aadarshvaad)) to mean 'that and nothing more,' not just speech.
Sample Sentences:
