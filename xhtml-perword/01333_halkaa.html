हलका (halkaa)

adjective/noun Primarily an adjective meaning 'light' (in weight, intensity, or seriousness); also used as a noun meaning an administrative 'zone' or 'jurisdiction area'. Also commonly spelled हल्का (halkaa) with a halant on the ल.
Sense 1 : light in weight
adjective
Synonyms: वज़नहीन (vaznahiin, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly at the market when picking out a bag or suitcase: यह बैग हलका है (yah baig halkaa hai, 'this bag is light') is the kind of line a shopkeeper uses to sell you something. Also commonly spelled हल्का (halkaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for छोटा (chhotaa, 'small') when they mean 'light' -- हलका (halkaa) is about weight, not size.

(yah baig bahut halkaa hai।)

Sense 2 : light/mild in intensity, quantity, or strength
adjective
Synonyms: मामूली (maamoolii, 'slight, ordinary'), थोड़ा (thodaa, 'a little')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up everywhere in daily talk about weather, food, and health: हलकी बारिश (halkii baarish, 'light rain'), हलका बुखार (halkaa bukhaar, 'mild fever'), हलका खाना (halkaa khaanaa, 'light food'). A mother telling you to eat हलका खाना (halkaa khaanaa) when you're sick is a very Indian moment.

Sense 3 : light-hearted, relaxed, not serious (mood, mind, or tone)
adjective
Synonyms: सहज (sahaj, 'relaxed, natural')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the figurative cousin of the weight sense -- Hindi treats a light mood the same way it treats a light bag. दिल हलका होना (dil halkaa honaa, 'for the heart to feel light/relieved') is a lovely idiom for the relief you feel after getting something off your chest, much like sharing a worry with a friend over chai.
Sample Sentences:
उससे बात करके मेरा दिल हलका हो गया। Talking to her made my heart feel light.
(usse baat karke meraa dil halkaa ho gayaa।)

Sense 4 : of low social standing or disreputable character
adjective
Synonyms: घटिया (ghatiyaa, 'low-quality, cheap'), नीच (niich, 'lowly, base')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear elders warn young people away from हलकी हरकतें (halkii harakten, 'cheap/petty acts') or हलके लोग (halke log, 'lowly/disreputable people') -- it carries real social disapproval, the kind a strict uncle or teacher might voice.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is judgmental and can sound harsh -- हलकी हरकत (halkii harkat, 'a cheap/petty act') criticizes behavior, not weight, so don't confuse it with the neutral weight sense.
Sample Sentences:
वह बहुत हलका आदमी है, उससे दूर ही रहो। He is a very lowly/disreputable man, just stay away from him.
(vah bahut halkaa aadmii hai, usse door hii raho।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
मेरे पापा हमेशा कहते हैं कि हलकी हरकतें करने वालों से दोस्ती मत करो। My father always says, don't befriend those who do cheap/petty things.
(mere paapaa hameshaa kahte hain ki halkii harakten karne vaalon se dostii mat karo।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उसने पैसों के लिए इतनी हलकी हरकत की कि सब शर्मिंदा हो गए। He did such a cheap/petty thing for money that everyone was embarrassed.

Sense 5 : administrative zone, jurisdiction, or precinct
noun, masculine Common in official and police/administrative contexts, e.g. थाना हलका (thaanaa halkaa, 'police station jurisdiction').
Synonyms: क्षेत्र (kshetr, 'area, region'), ज़ोन (zon, colloquial)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll see on government notices or hear from a पुलिस थाना (pulis thaanaa, 'police station') official describing which हलका (halkaa) an area falls under -- a very bureaucratic, everyday-India usage distinct from the adjective senses above.
