बेकार (bekaar)

adjective/adverb A common Persian-origin compound (be- 'without' + kaar 'work') covering three related senses: 'useless/pointless' (adjective, the core everyday use), 'unemployed' (adjective, literal 'without work'), and 'uselessly/for nothing' (adverb).
Sense 1 : useless, pointless, worthless
adjective
Synonyms: फ़िज़ूल (fizool), व्यर्थ (vyarth, formal), बेकाम (bekaam, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear a Delhi shopkeeper or auto driver use constantly to dismiss a bad product, a bad idea, or a wasted trip -- 'बेकार का सामान' (bekaar kaa saamaan, 'useless stuff'), 'बेकार की बात' (bekaar kii baat, 'pointless talk'). It carries a slightly exasperated, dismissive tone that is very natural in everyday griping.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for व्यर्थ (vyarth) in casual speech, but बेकार (bekaar) is the everyday choice -- व्यर्थ (vyarth) sounds bookish in conversation. 2) बेकार (bekaar) can describe both things ('यह चीज़ बेकार है', yah chiiz bekaar hai, 'this thing is useless') and situations/plans, not just objects -- learners sometimes restrict it to physical items only.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : unemployed, without work
adjective
Synonyms: बेरोज़गार (berozgaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Literally 'without work' (be- + kaar), this sense overlaps with the 'useless' meaning in a way that can feel pointed in Hindi -- calling someone बेकार (bekaar) instead of बेरोज़गार (berozgaar) can sound like a dig at their worth, not just their job status, so context and affection matter.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense बेकार (bekaar) is largely interchangeable with बेरोज़गार (berozgaar), but बेरोज़गार (berozgaar) is the more precise and common word specifically for 'unemployed'; बेकार (bekaar) can sound like it's also implying the person is generally 'useless,' so tone matters.
Sample Sentences:
वह पिछले छह महीने से बेकार है। He has been unemployed for the last six months.
(vah pichhle chhah mahiine se bekaar hai।)

Sense 3 : uselessly, for nothing, in vain
adverb Modifies a verb phrase, typically describing wasted effort, time, or money: 'बेकार समय बर्बाद मत करो' (bekaar samay barbaad mat karo, 'don't waste time for nothing').
Synonyms: फ़िज़ूल में (fizool men), व्यर्थ में (vyarth men, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in complaints about wasted effort -- 'बेकार गया' (bekaar gayaa, 'it went to waste') is a classic phrase for a trip, a plan, or money that came to nothing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this adverbial use with the adjective sense; here बेकार (bekaar) sits before a verb or verb phrase rather than describing a noun with है (hai) or था (thaa).
Sample Sentences:
