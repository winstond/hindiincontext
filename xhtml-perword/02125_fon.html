फ़ोन (fon)

noun/verb A borrowed English word meaning 'phone' -- covers both the device (noun) and the act of calling (verb).
Sense 1 : telephone (the device)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: टेलीफ़ोन (teliifon)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like most tech vocabulary in modern Hindi, फ़ोन (fon) is simply the English word absorbed wholesale -- you'll hear it constantly in Delhi homes and markets, far more than any Sanskritic alternative like दूरभाष (doorbhaash), which sounds stiff and bureaucratic.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes add an extra Hindi word for 'mobile' when फ़ोन (fon) alone already covers mobile phones in casual speech -- मेरा फ़ोन (meraa fon, 'my phone') is enough; मोबाइल (mobaail) is only needed if you want to specifically contrast it with a landline.

(meraa fon kahaan hai?)

Sense 2 : to phone, to call (someone)
verb, transitive Used as फ़ोन करना (fon karnaa, 'to make a phone call') -- फ़ोन (fon) here acts as the noun object of करना (karnaa), forming a compound expression rather than फ़ोन (fon) itself conjugating as a verb.
Synonyms: कॉल करना (kol karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: फ़ोन करना (fon karnaa) is the everyday way to say 'call' -- you'll hear it constantly, as in मुझे फ़ोन करना (mujhe fon karnaa, 'call me') at the end of almost any conversation in Delhi.
Common Mistakes: 1) The person being called takes को (ko): उसे फ़ोन करना (use fon karnaa, 'to call him/her') -- learners sometimes try से (se) here by analogy with 'talk to someone,' but से (se) is wrong for this frame.
