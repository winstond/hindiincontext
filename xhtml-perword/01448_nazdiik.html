नज़दीक (nazdiik)

postposition/adjective A single core meaning of physical or figurative closeness, used as a postposition ('near') and as an adjective ('nearby').
Sense 1 : near, close to
postposition Takes के (ke) before it, as in के नज़दीक (ke nazdiik, 'near to'), the same frame used by पास (paas) and करीब (kariib).
Synonyms: पास (paas), करीब (kariib), समीप (samiip, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi you will hear मेट्रो स्टेशन के नज़दीक (metro steshan ke nazdiik, 'near the metro station')) constantly when people give directions or describe where they live -- नज़दीक (nazdiik) and पास (paas) are near-total overlaps in daily speech, with नज़दीक (nazdiik) feeling very slightly more formal/written, as on a real estate listing or a written address.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use नज़दीक (nazdiik) alone without के (ke), producing घर नज़दीक है (ghar nazdiik hai) when the location-relative sense needs मेरे घर के नज़दीक (mere ghar ke nazdiik, 'near my house'); as a plain predicate ('is nearby') it does work without के (ke), so the mistake is specifically dropping के (ke) when comparing two places. 2) Confusing करीब (kariib) in its 'approximately' sense (करीब पाँच बजे (kariib paanch baje, 'around five o'clock')) with नज़दीक (nazdiik), which never means 'approximately'.

Sense 2 : nearby, close (in relationship or time)
adjective
Synonyms: करीबी (kariibii), पास वाला (paas vaalaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As a plain adjective, नज़दीक (nazdiik) shows up describing something close in time too, like नज़दीक भविष्य (nazdiik bhavishy, 'near future')), a phrase you will meet often in news Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners use नज़दीक (nazdiik) for close personal relationships (a close friend) where करीबी (kariibii) is the natural adjective, as in करीबी दोस्त (kariibii dost, 'close friend') -- नज़दीक (nazdiik) here would sound odd to a native speaker.
