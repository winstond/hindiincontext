मोड़ना (modnaa)

verb A physical-action verb meaning 'to turn/bend something' (transitive); it also extends to turning a vehicle or a route, and to bending an object like paper or a stick.
Sense 1 : to turn (a vehicle, direction, path)
verb, transitive Commonly used with गाड़ी (gaadii), मोटरसाइकिल (motarsaaikil), or रास्ता (raastaa) as object -- 'turn the car/turn onto the road.'
Synonyms: घुमाना (ghumaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi auto and cab drivers use this constantly when giving or getting directions -- यहाँ से बायें मोड़ लो (yahaan se baayen mod lo, 'turn left from here') is a classic backseat instruction on any Delhi road trip.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for घूमना (ghoomnaa, to roam/turn intransitively) when they mean मोड़ना (modnaa) -- घूमना (ghoomnaa) describes the vehicle or person turning on its own, while मोड़ना (modnaa) is what the driver does to it. 2) With directions, the object is often the road or the vehicle, not the destination -- गाड़ी को दायें मोड़ो (gaadii ko daayen modo, 'turn the car to the right'), not the destination itself.
Sample Sentences:

(yahaan se gaadii baayen mod lo।)

Sense 2 : to bend, fold (an object)
verb, transitive Takes a physical object as the direct object -- कागज़ (kaagaz, paper), तार (taar, wire), पन्ना (pannaa, page).
Synonyms: झुकाना (jhukaanaa), मोडना (modnaa, variant spelling without nukta)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common in everyday instructions like पन्ना मोड़ना (pannaa modnaa, 'to fold a page') -- folding down a book page as a bookmark is a habit most Hindi speakers will recognize (and many teachers will scold you for!).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse this with तोड़ना (todnaa, to break) because of the similar sound and shared vowel pattern -- मोड़ना (modnaa) leaves the object intact but bent, तोड़ना (todnaa) breaks it.

Sense 3 : to turn away, divert (attention, topic, someone from a path)
verb, transitive Used figuratively with abstract objects like ध्यान (dhyaan, attention) or बात (baat, topic/matter) -- बात मोड़ना (baat modnaa) means to steer a conversation away from something.
Synonyms: फेरना (phernaa, less common in this sense), बदलना (badalnaa, change, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बात मोड़ना (baat modnaa) is the polite Indian way of dodging an awkward question at a family gathering -- changing the subject smoothly rather than refusing to answer outright.
Sample Sentences:
उसने चतुराई से बात मोड़ दी। He cleverly diverted the topic.
(usne chaturaaii se baat mod dii।)
