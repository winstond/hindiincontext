कल्पना (kalpnaa)

noun/verb Mainly a feminine noun meaning 'imagination' or 'a fictional/imagined idea'; it also has a common verb-phrase use with करना (karnaa) meaning 'to imagine.'
Sense 1 : imagination, the mental faculty
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फ़ैंटेसी (faintesii, loanword, less common), ख़याल (khayaal, meaning shades toward 'thought/idea')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in school contexts -- कल्पना शक्ति (kalpnaa shakti, 'power of imagination') is a phrase teachers use to praise creative kids. It also shows up in everyday reflection, like जीवन की कल्पना (jiivan kii kalpnaa, 'imagining life/what life would be like').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सोच (soch) when they mean कल्पना (kalpnaa) -- सोच (soch) is closer to 'thinking/opinion,' while कल्पना (kalpnaa) specifically implies imagining something not present or not real.

(bachchon men kalpnaa shakti bahut zyaadaa hotii hai।)

Sense 2 : a specific imagined idea, notion, or fictional concept
noun, feminine
Synonyms: धारणा (dhaarnaa, closer to 'assumption/notion'), विचार (vichaar, meaning shades toward 'thought')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is what you hear in काल्पनिक (kaalpnik, 'imaginary/fictional') contexts -- a कल्पना (kalpnaa) here is a specific 'what if' idea rather than the general faculty of imagining.

Sense 3 : to imagine, to picture mentally
verb, transitive Functions as a compound with करना (karnaa): कल्पना करना (kalpnaa karnaa, 'to imagine'), often followed by कि (ki, 'that') to introduce the imagined scenario, e.g. कल्पना करो कि... (kalpnaa karo ki, 'imagine that...').
Synonyms: सोचना (sochnaa, meaning shades toward 'to think'), तसव्वुर करना (tasavvur karnaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: कल्पना करो (kalpnaa karo, 'imagine') is a classic way Hindi teachers or storytellers set a scene, much like English 'picture this' -- it invites the listener into a mental scenario before the sentence continues.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a noun-plus-करना (karnaa) construction, not a simple verb root -- learners sometimes try to conjugate कल्पना (kalpnaa) itself as though it were a verb.
