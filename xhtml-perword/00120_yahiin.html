यहीं (yahiin)

adverb An emphatic form of यहाँ (yahaan, 'here') meaning 'right here' or 'this very place' -- the ही (hii) emphatic particle fused into the location word.
Sense 1 : right here, this very place
adverb
Synonyms: इसी जगह (isii jagah)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of यहीं (yahiin) as pointing with your finger at the exact spot: अपनी चाबी यहीं रख दो (apnii chaabii yahiin rakh do, 'put your key right here') pins down the location in a way यहाँ (yahaan) alone doesn't. You'll hear it constantly when people give directions or settle an argument about where something happened -- यहीं तो हुआ था (yahiin to huaa thaa, 'it happened right here') is a classic Delhi-street-argument line.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use यहाँ (yahaan) when they actually mean to add emphasis -- if you want to stress 'right here' (not somewhere else nearby), यहीं (yahiin) is the natural choice, not यहाँ (yahaan) plus a separate emphasis word. 2) यहीं पर (yahiin par, 'right here') is extremely common in speech -- पर (par) doesn't cancel the emphasis, it just adds a locative flavor, so don't assume the ही (hii) is redundant once पर (par) shows up.
Sample Sentences:
अपनी चाबी यहीं रख दो। Put your key right here.
(apnii chaabii yahiin rakh do।)

Sense 2 : right now, immediately (temporal, paired with अभी)
adverb Used almost always in the fixed pair अभी यहीं (abhii yahiin, 'right here and now') rather than standing alone with a temporal meaning.
Synonyms: अभी (abhii), इसी वक़्त (isii vaqt)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: अभी यहीं (abhii yahiin, 'right here, right now') is a common urgent phrase -- a parent might snap अभी यहीं आओ (abhii yahiin aao, 'come right here, right now') to a child, stacking both immediacy and location for full effect.
Common Mistakes: 1) यहीं (yahiin) by itself is spatial, not temporal -- learners sometimes try to use it alone to mean 'right now,' but that job belongs to अभी (abhii); यहीं (yahiin) only picks up a time sense when paired with अभी (abhii) as in अभी यहीं (abhii yahiin).
Sample Sentences:
अभी यहीं आओ, बहस मत करो। Come right now, don't argue.
(abhii yahiin aao, bahas mat karo।)
