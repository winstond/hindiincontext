ध्यान से (dhyaan se)

adverb An adverbial phrase built from ध्यान (dhyaan, 'attention') plus the postposition से (se, 'with'), meaning 'carefully' or 'attentively'. Used constantly as a caution or instruction before an action.
Sense 1 : carefully, attentively
adverb
Synonyms: सावधानी से (saavdhaanii se), होशियारी से (hoshiyaarii se)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the first phrases Indian parents, teachers, and bus conductors drill into you -- ध्यान से चलो (dhyaan se chalo, 'walk carefully') or ध्यान से देखो (dhyaan se dekho, 'look carefully') are everyday warnings you hear on streets, in classrooms, and at home. It literally means 'with attention/focus,' tying back to ध्यान (dhyaan), the same word used for meditation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say सावधान से (saavdhaan se) by analogy with सावधान (saavdhaan, 'careful/alert'), but the natural everyday phrase for 'carefully' is ध्यान से (dhyaan se), not a से-form of सावधान (saavdhaan). 2) Placed right before the verb it modifies, e.g. ध्यान से सुनो (dhyaan se suno, 'listen carefully') -- putting it too far from the verb can sound off.
Sample Sentences:
सड़क पार करते वक्त ध्यान से चलो। Walk carefully while crossing the road.
(sadak paar karte vakt dhyaan se chalo।)
