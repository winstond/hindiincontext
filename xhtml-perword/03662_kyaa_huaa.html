क्या हुआ (kyaa huaa)

phrase A common conversational question meaning 'what happened' or 'what's wrong' -- used both as a literal request for information and as a caring or impatient check-in on someone's state.
Sense 1 : asking what happened / what's the matter
phrase
Synonyms: क्या बात है (kyaa baat hai, asks specifically what's going on/what's the issue), हुआ क्या (huaa kyaa, word-order variant, same meaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the first things a mother, friend, or shopkeeper says when your face gives something away -- Hindi speakers read faces closely, and क्या हुआ (kyaa huaa) is the standard opener for 'you look upset, tell me.' You'll also hear it used sarcastically or impatiently, as in क्या हुआ, इतना गुस्सा क्यों? (kyaa huaa, itnaa gussaa kyon, 'what's the matter, why so angry?').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this only to ask about a past event, but native speakers use it constantly as a present-tense check-in on someone's mood or condition -- said to a friend who looks upset, it means 'what's wrong?' not 'what happened in the past?' 2) The tone carries the meaning: said sharply it can mean 'what's your problem?', said softly it means 'are you okay?'.
Sample Sentences:
क्या हुआ? तुम इतने परेशान क्यों लग रहे हो? What happened? Why do you look so worried?
(kyaa huaa? tum itne pareshaan kyon lag rahe ho?)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(kyaa huaa, itnaa gussaa kyon?)

(kyaa huaa thaa kal raat, tum phon kyon nahiin uthaa rahe the?)
