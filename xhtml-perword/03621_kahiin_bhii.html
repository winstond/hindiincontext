कहीं भी (kahiin bhii)

adverb A compound of कहीं (kahiin, 'somewhere/anywhere') and भी (bhii, 'also/even'), meaning 'anywhere at all' -- used to express an unrestricted or emphatic sense of location, and by extension, indifference or randomness.
Sense 1 : at any place whatsoever (unrestricted location)
adverb Often paired with negative or conditional clauses to mean 'nowhere in particular' or to license free choice of location, similar to English 'anywhere at all.'
Synonyms: किसी भी जगह (kisii bhii jagah, longer phrase)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in casual Delhi speech -- कहीं भी मत जाना (kahiin bhii mat jaanaa, 'don't go anywhere') is a classic parental warning line. The भी (bhii) here does the same emphatic work it does elsewhere (जब भी jab bhii 'whenever', कोई भी koii bhii 'anyone at all'), so once you know this pattern with कहीं (kahiin), it transfers easily to other question-word + भी combinations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop भी (bhii) and just say कहीं (kahiin), which means 'somewhere' -- a much weaker, non-emphatic claim; कहीं भी (kahiin bhii) specifically stresses 'no matter where.' 2) In negative sentences like मुझे कहीं भी नहीं जाना है (mujhe kahiin bhii nahiin jaanaa hai, 'I don't want to go anywhere at all'), learners sometimes mistranslate this as 'somewhere' rather than catching the emphatic 'nowhere at all' force that भी (bhii) adds.

Sense 2 : haphazardly, carelessly, just anywhere (implying disorder)
adverb Used critically to describe something done without care or order -- objects left lying around, or an action performed without proper thought.
Synonyms: जहाँ तहाँ (jahaan tahaan, colloquial, implies scattering)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the phrase a Delhi mother uses when she walks into a room and sees shoes, bags, and books scattered around -- अपने जूते कहीं भी मत फेंको (apne joote kahiin bhii mat phenko, 'don't just throw your shoes anywhere'). The tone is exasperated rather than purely descriptive.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners miss the disapproving tone here -- सामान कहीं भी रख दिया (saamaan kahiin bhii rakh diyaa, 'you put the stuff down just anywhere') carries a note of mild complaint about carelessness, not a neutral statement of location.
