विरोध (virodh)

noun A single core meaning of opposition/resistance, used both for concrete protest and for abstract contradiction or objection.
Sense 1 : opposition, protest, resistance
noun, masculine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) 'to oppose/protest' and का सामना करना (kaa saamnaa karnaa) 'to face opposition'; the person or thing opposed is marked with का/की/के (kaa).
Synonyms: विरोधाभास (virodhaabhaas, contradiction, narrower sense), प्रतिरोध (pratirodh, resistance, more formal), आपत्ति (aapatti, objection)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly in the news -- विरोध प्रदर्शन (virodh pradarshan, 'protest demonstration') is the standard phrase for any march, dharna, or rally covered on Indian television. In everyday family life, it also covers milder friction, like a parent's विरोध (virodh) to a child's choice of career or marriage partner.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use विरोध (virodh) with से (se) when they mean 'opposition to X' -- the correct pattern is X का विरोध (kaa virodh), not X से विरोध. 2) विरोध करना (virodh karnaa) 'to oppose/protest' takes ने (ne) in the past tense since करना (karnaa) is transitive, which learners sometimes forget when विरोध itself feels like the 'subject' of the protest.

किसानों ने नए कानून के विरोध में प्रदर्शन किया। The farmers held a protest in opposition to the new law.
(kisaanon ne nae kaanoon ke virodh men pradarshan kiyaa।)
