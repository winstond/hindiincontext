राष्ट्रीयता (raashtriiytaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'nationality' -- the legal/civic status of belonging to a nation, and by extension the sense of national identity or feeling.
Sense 1 : nationality; national identity
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly on visa forms and in passport-office lines in Delhi -- राष्ट्रीयता: भारतीय (raashtriiytaa: bhaartiiy, 'Nationality: Indian') is a standard printed line. It is built from राष्ट्र (raashtr, 'nation') plus the abstract-noun suffix ता (taa, '-ness/-ity'), the same pattern you see in words like मानवता (maanavtaa, 'humanity').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse राष्ट्रीयता (raashtriiytaa, nationality/citizenship-identity) with राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad, nationalism as a political ideology) -- the two are related but not interchangeable; राष्ट्रीयता is the identity itself, राष्ट्रवाद is the '-ism' built on it. 2) On official forms the word used is usually राष्ट्रीयता (raashtriiytaa) rather than नागरिकता (naagriktaa, citizenship), though in casual speech people sometimes blur the two -- नागरिकता is the more precise legal term for citizenship status.
