बहाव (bahaav)

noun A noun meaning 'flow' or 'current' -- the physical movement of a liquid or, figuratively, of a trend, mood, or momentum. Related to the verb बहना (bahnaa, 'to flow').
Sense 1 : flow, current (of water or a liquid)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रवाह (pravaah, formal), धारा (dhaaraa, stream/current)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word near any river or during monsoon flooding -- नदी का बहाव तेज़ है (nadii kaa bahaav tez hai, 'the river's current is strong') is the kind of line you hear on the news every July when the Yamuna rises.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बहाव (bahaav) with धारा (dhaaraa) -- धारा (dhaaraa) leans toward a distinct 'stream' or 'channel' as a thing, while बहाव (bahaav) is the quality or act of flowing, the current's force or direction.

Sense 2 : flow, trend, drift (figurative, of events, mood, or momentum)
noun, masculine Often appears in the phrase बहाव में आना (bahaav men aanaa, to get swept up/carried along by a mood or momentum) or बहाव में बहना (bahaav men bahnaa, to go with the flow).
Synonyms: रुझान (rujhaan, trend/inclination), रवैया (ravaiyaa, attitude/drift, meaning scope narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This captures a very Hindi way of talking about peer pressure or crowd psychology -- बहाव में बह जाना (bahaav men bah jaanaa, 'to get carried away by the flow') describes someone swept along by what everyone else is doing or feeling, without quite deciding to be.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is used for social or emotional momentum -- like getting carried away in a crowd's mood -- not for a business or market 'trend' in the statistical sense, where रुझान (rujhaan) is more natural.
