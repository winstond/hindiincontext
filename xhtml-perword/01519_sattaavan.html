सत्तावन (sattaavan)

numeral The cardinal number '57' (fifty-seven), used for counting and quantities like any other Hindi numeral.
Sense 1 : the number fifty-seven
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like all two-digit Hindi numbers, सत्तावन (sattaavan) has its own irregular shape rather than a predictable tens-plus-units pattern -- native speakers just memorize the 1 to 100 sequence as children, and adult learners are best served doing the same in small chunks rather than looking for logic in the endings.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build this compositionally from सात (saat, 'seven') and पचास (pachaas, 'fifty') the way English does with 'fifty-seven' -- Hindi numerals 21-99 are memorized as single unpredictable words, and सत्तावन (sattaavan) must simply be learned as its own item rather than derived.

(mere paapaa kii umr sattaavan saal hai।)
