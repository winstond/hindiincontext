नौ (nau)

numeral/noun This word has two unrelated meanings: the number 'nine' and the word for 'boat' (the latter mostly seen in compounds like नौका (naukaa).
Sense 1 : the number nine
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: नौ (nau) is used exactly like other cardinal numbers in Hindi -- for counting, prices, ages, and time (नौ बजे (nau baje, 'at nine o'clock')). You will hear it constantly in daily contexts like bus numbers, addresses, and market haggling.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse नौ (nau, 'nine') with नो (no), which is not a Hindi word in this sense -- the nasalized vowel matters, so listen for the light nasal sound at the end. 2) Do not confuse with नई (naii, 'new, feminine'), which looks unrelated in Devanagari but learners sometimes mix up in fast speech.

Sense 2 : boat, vessel
noun, feminine This standalone sense is rare in everyday speech; the root is far more commonly seen inside compounds such as नौका (naukaa, 'boat') and नौसेना (nausenaa, 'navy').
Synonyms: नाव (naav), नौका (naukaa, more common in this exact form), किश्ती (kishtii, literary)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: As a standalone word for 'boat', नौ (nau) is largely literary today -- in daily conversation people say नाव (naav) instead. You are much more likely to meet this root inside words like नौसेना (nausenaa, 'navy', literally 'boat-army') than on its own.
