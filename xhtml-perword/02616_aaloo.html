आलू (aaloo)

noun A single-sense everyday noun: the potato. Shows up constantly in food vocabulary and common compounds like आलू-गोभी (aaloo-gobhii).
Sense 1 : potato
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: आलू (aaloo) is the backbone of North Indian home cooking -- from breakfast आलू पराठा (aaloo paraathaa) to street-food staples like आलू टिक्की (aaloo tikkii) and समोसा (samosaa) filling. It is so versatile in Delhi-NCR kitchens that there is a common joke about आलू (aaloo) going into every single vegetable dish when nothing else is in the fridge.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat आलू (aaloo) as a mass/uncountable noun the way 'potato' can be in English recipes, but in Hindi it takes normal countable plural agreement when you mean individual potatoes, e.g. आलू उबल गए (aaloo ubal gae, 'the potatoes boiled') -- the noun form itself does not change, but verb agreement should be plural. 2) The vegetable dish name आलू (aaloo) is often the first element in compounds (आलू पराठा aaloo paraathaa, आलू भुजिया aaloo bhujiyaa) and stays unmarked/uninflected in that position, which can look odd to learners expecting a genitive link.
