के अलावा (ke alaavaa)

postposition A postposition phrase meaning 'besides, apart from, in addition to' -- it attaches to the oblique form of the preceding noun/pronoun, just like other compound postpositions such as के बारे में (ke baare men).
Sense 1 : besides, apart from (exclusive sense)
postposition Takes the oblique case on the preceding noun/pronoun, e.g. मुझ (mujh) + के अलावा (ke alaavaa).
Synonyms: के सिवा (ke sivaa), के सिवाय (ke sivaay)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Context decides whether के अलावा (ke alaavaa) means 'in addition to' or 'except for' -- Hindi doesn't force you to choose grammatically, so listen for whether the sentence is piling things on or narrowing things down. This dual sense trips up learners more than any single grammar rule about the word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget the oblique case is required before के अलावा (ke alaavaa), saying मैं (main) के अलावा instead of मुझ (mujh) के अलावा. 2) In this exclusive sense, के अलावा (ke alaavaa) can mean 'no one/nothing other than,' which reads like 'only' -- e.g. उसके अलावा कोई नहीं आया (uske alaavaa koii nahiin aayaa, 'no one came except him') -- learners sometimes miss this exclusive-only shade and read it as purely additive.
Sample Sentences:
उसके अलावा यहाँ कोई नहीं आया। No one came here except for him.
(uske alaavaa yahaan koii nahiin aayaa।)

Sense 2 : in addition to, besides (additive sense)
postposition
Synonyms: के साथ साथ (ke saath saath), के अतिरिक्त (ke atirikt, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common in everyday listing -- e.g. चाय के अलावा कॉफ़ी भी है (chaay ke alaavaa kofii bhii hai, 'besides tea, there is coffee too']) -- the भी (bhii) later in the sentence usually confirms you're in the 'also' sense rather than the exclusive one.
