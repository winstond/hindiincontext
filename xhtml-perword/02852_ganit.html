गणित (ganit)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'mathematics', the school subject and the field of study.
Sense 1 : mathematics
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: गणित (ganit) is one of the core subjects drilled into every Indian schoolkid, and phrases like गणित में कमज़ोर (ganit men kamzor, 'weak in math') or गणित में तेज़ (ganit men tez, 'sharp at math') come up constantly when parents and teachers compare students. India's strong cultural emphasis on math and engineering (think IIT entrance exams) makes this word feel loaded with a bit more social weight than its English equivalent.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes add an article or plural sense as in English ('the maths'); गणित (ganit) is always treated as a singular mass noun, like 'mathematics' in English, never गणितें (ganiten).
