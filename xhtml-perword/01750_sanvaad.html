संवाद (sanvaad)

noun A somewhat formal/literary word covering 'dialogue' or 'conversation' (as between two parties, or in a play/film) and, by extension, 'communication' in a broader sense. Related to the everyday verb बोलना (bolnaa) and noun बात (baat), but pitched at a more structured or formal register.
Sense 1 : dialogue / conversation (structured exchange between parties)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बातचीत (baatchiit), वार्ता (vaartaa, formal), बहस (bahas, debate, meaning scope narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly in the news: भारत-पाक संवाद (bhaarat-paak sanvaad, India-Pakistan dialogue) or संवाद की ज़रूरत है (sanvaad kii zaroorat hai, there is a need for dialogue). It carries a slightly hopeful, diplomatic tone, as if talking itself is the solution to a conflict.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for संवाद (sanvaad) in casual chat where बातचीत (baatchiit) or बात (baat) would sound far more natural; संवाद (sanvaad) suggests a more formal, deliberate exchange, like between officials, communities, or characters in a story. 2) In news and political contexts, संवाद (sanvaad) often implies constructive, ongoing communication (e.g., between two countries or groups), not just any conversation.

Sense 2 : dialogue lines (in a play, film, or script)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: डायलॉग (daaylog, colloquial, film context)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Watch the opening or closing credits of a Hindi film and you will often see संवाद (sanvaad) listed as its own credit, separate from कहानी (kahaanii, story) and पटकथा (patakthaa, screenplay) -- a nod to how much weight punchy dialogue carries in Hindi cinema.
Common Mistakes: 1) In everyday film-talk, most people actually say डायलॉग (daaylog) rather than संवाद (sanvaad) -- संवाद (sanvaad) shows up more in formal writing about theater or cinema, like a film review or a script credit ('संवाद: ...').

इस फ़िल्म के संवाद बहुत दिलचस्प हैं। This film's dialogue is very interesting.
