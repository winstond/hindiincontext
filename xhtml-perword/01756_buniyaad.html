बुनियाद (buniyaad)

noun Means 'foundation' -- both the literal base of a building and the figurative base of an idea, relationship, or system.
Sense 1 : foundation (figurative -- basis of something abstract)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आधार (aadhaar), जड़ (jad, figurative root/basis)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in everyday advice-giving -- parents and teachers love saying things like मेहनत ही सफलता की बुनियाद है (mehnat hii saphaltaa kii buniyaad hai, 'hard work is the foundation of success'), treating abstract qualities as literal structures you build on.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आधार (aadhaar) by default; बुनियाद (buniyaad) is just as common in speech, especially in phrases like बुनियादी बात (buniyaadii baat, 'basic/fundamental point') and किसी रिश्ते की बुनियाद (kisii rishte kii buniyaad, 'the foundation of a relationship'). 2) Don't confuse with बुनियादी (buniyaadii), the adjective 'basic/fundamental' derived from this noun -- बुनियाद (buniyaad) itself is only the noun.
Sample Sentences:
मेहनत ही सफलता की बुनियाद है। Hard work is the very foundation of success.
(mehnat hii saphaltaa kii buniyaad hai।)

(aapsii sammaan ke binaa koii bhii rishtaa apnii buniyaad kho detaa hai।)

Sense 2 : foundation (literal -- base of a building or structure)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आधार (aadhaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In construction talk you'll hear बुनियाद डालना (buniyaad daalnaa, 'to lay the foundation') or बुनियाद रखना (buniyaad rakhnaa, 'to set the foundation') -- the same physical image Hindi then borrows wholesale for the figurative sense above.
Sample Sentences:
मजदूरों ने घर की बुनियाद डाली। The laborers laid the foundation of the house.
(majdooron ne ghar kii buniyaad daalii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
इस इमारत की बुनियाद बहुत गहरी है। The foundation of this building is very deep.
(is imaarat kii buniyaad bahut gahrii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
बारिश की वजह से बुनियाद में दरारें पड़ रही हैं। Because of the rain, cracks are appearing in the foundation.
(baarish kii vajah se buniyaad men daraaren pad rahii hain।)

पंडित जी ने मुहूर्त देखकर बुनियाद रखी। The priest checked the auspicious time and then set the foundation.

मंदिर की बुनियाद खोदते समय पुराने सिक्के मिले। While digging the temple's foundation, old coins were found.
