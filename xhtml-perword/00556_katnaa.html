कटना (katnaa)

verb The intransitive partner of काटना (kaatnaa, 'to cut'): कटना (katnaa) describes something undergoing cutting, being cut off, or getting slashed/deducted, plus extended senses like time passing or a call getting disconnected.
Sense 1 : to be cut / to get cut
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday partner of काटना (kaatnaa): whenever a subject undergoes the action of cutting rather than performing it, कटना (katnaa) is the natural verb, no passive needed.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for the passive of काटना (kaatnaa) (e.g. काटा जाता है, kaataa jaataa hai) when कटना (katnaa) already does this job as a plain intransitive verb -- 'रोटी कट गई' (rotii kat gaii, 'the bread got cut') is far more natural than a passive construction.
Sample Sentences:
मेरी उंगली चाकू से कट गई। My finger got cut by the knife.
(merii unglii chaakoo se kat gaii।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : to be deducted / docked (money, marks, salary)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around payday or exam season -- तनख्वाह कट गई (tanakhvaah kat gaii, 'the salary got docked') or नंबर कट गए (nambar kat gae, 'marks got deducted') are common office and school complaints in Delhi-NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) With money or marks, कटना (katnaa) takes से (se) for the source: 'तनख्वाह से पैसे कट गए' (tanakhvaah se paise kat gae, 'money got deducted from the salary') -- learners sometimes try का (kaa) here instead.

Sense 3 : (of time) to pass, to be spent
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: गुज़रना (guzarnaa), बीतना (biitnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: दिन कट गया (din kat gayaa, 'the day got through') carries a slight sense of endurance or getting by, unlike the more neutral बीतना (biitnaa) -- Hindi often frames a hard day as something 'cut through' rather than simply 'passing.'

Sense 4 : (of a call/connection) to get cut off, disconnected
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: डिस्कनेक्ट होना (disknekt honaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the most common uses you will hear on the phone in India -- फ़ोन कट गया (fon kat gayaa) is the standard way to say a call dropped or someone hung up, often followed by एक मिनट रुको, फिर से लगाता हूँ (ek minat ruko, phir se lagaataa hoon, 'wait a minute, I will call again').
Common Mistakes: 1) फ़ोन कट गया (fon kat gayaa, 'the call got cut off') describes the call ending abruptly (dropped or hung up), not the phone breaking -- a common point of confusion for learners taking फ़ोन (fon) to mean the device itself is damaged.
Sample Sentences:
अरे, फ़ोन कट गया! Oh, the call got disconnected!
(are, fon kat gayaa!)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
एक मिनट रुको, फिर से लगाता हूँ, कॉल कट गई थी। Wait a minute, I'll call again, the call had gotten disconnected.

Sense 5 : to be canceled or omitted (e.g. from a list, plan, invitation)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: हटना (hatnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in casual planning talk -- 'उसका नाम लिस्ट से कट गया' (uskaa naam list se kat gayaa, 'his name got struck off the list') -- framing removal as something that happens to the name, echoing Hindi's broader habit of putting the affected thing in subject position.
