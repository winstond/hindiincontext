चीख़ना (chiikhnaa)

verb A vivid verb meaning 'to scream, shriek, yell out' -- used for a sudden loud cry of pain, fear, or shock. Related to the noun चीख़ (chiikh, 'a scream').
Sense 1 : to scream, shriek
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चिल्लाना (chillaanaa), चीत्कारना (chiitkaarnaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in descriptions of horror movies or accidents -- वह डर के मारे चीख़ी (vah dar ke maare chiikhii, 'she screamed out of fear') is the kind of line that shows up constantly in storytelling. It pairs naturally with मारे (maare, 'out of, because of') to explain the emotion behind the scream.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mix this up with चिल्लाना (chillaanaa) -- चीख़ना (chiikhnaa) leans toward a sharp, involuntary cry of pain, fear, or shock, while चिल्लाना (chillaanaa) is more general shouting, including in anger or to get attention. 2) The nuqta under ख़ (kh) softens the sound to a more guttural 'kh' -- dropping it to चीखना (chiikhnaa) is common in casual writing and still understood, but the nuqta form is considered more correct.
Sample Sentences:
वह डर के मारे चीख़ी। She screamed out of fear.
(vah dar ke maare chiikhii।)
