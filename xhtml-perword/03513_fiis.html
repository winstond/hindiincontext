फ़ीस (fiis)

noun A single-sense loanword meaning 'fee' -- the money paid for schooling, a doctor's visit, a membership, or similar services.
Sense 1 : fee, charge for a service
noun, feminine
Synonyms: शुल्क (shulk, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around admission season -- स्कूल की फ़ीस (skool kii fiis, 'school fee'), कॉलेज की फ़ीस (kolej kii fiis, 'college fee'), डॉक्टर की फ़ीस (doktar kii fiis, 'doctor's fee') are everyday phrases in Delhi-NCR households, especially when parents discuss budgets. It is a Persian-derived loanword absorbed into everyday Hindi so thoroughly that most speakers do not think of it as foreign at all.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often pair it with the wrong verb -- फ़ीस is typically भरना (bharnaa, 'to fill/pay') or जमा करना (jamaa karnaa, 'to deposit') rather than देना (denaa) alone, e.g. फ़ीस भरना (fiis bharnaa, 'to pay the fee') is the natural collocation for school/college fees. 2) The nuqta is often dropped in casual writing (फीस), which is common and acceptable, but फ़ीस with the nuqta is the standard form reflecting its foreign-origin sound.
