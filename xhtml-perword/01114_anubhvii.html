अनुभवी (anubhvii)

adjective This word means 'experienced' -- someone who has gathered practical knowledge or skill through lived experience. It is built from अनुभव (anubhav, 'experience') plus the possessive suffix -ई (ii).
Sense 1 : experienced, seasoned
adjective
Synonyms: माहिर (maahir), दक्ष (daksh, formal), कुशल (kushal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in job interviews and matrimonial-style descriptions -- an अनुभवी डॉक्टर (anubhvii doktar, 'an experienced doctor') or अनुभवी शिक्षक (anubhvii shikshak, 'an experienced teacher') carries real social weight in India, where seniority and years-on-the-job are respected markers of trustworthiness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect this like a regular -आ (aa) adjective; it is invariant, so the same form अनुभवी (anubhvii) is used for masculine, feminine, singular, and plural nouns. 2) Do not confuse with अनुभव (anubhav), the noun 'experience' itself -- अनुभवी (anubhvii) is the adjective describing a person who has it.
