सिद्ध करना (siddh karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the adjective सिद्ध (siddh, 'proven/accomplished') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); mainly used to mean 'to prove' in logical, academic, or legal contexts.
Sense 1 : to prove (an argument, claim, or theorem)
verb, transitive Takes a clause or noun phrase as the thing proven, often with a correlative कि (ki) clause: यह सिद्ध करना कि (yah siddh karnaa ki, 'to prove that...').
Synonyms: साबित करना (saabit karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear साबित करना (saabit karnaa) far more in daily conversation, while सिद्ध करना (siddh karnaa) has a slightly bookish, formal ring -- it's the word you meet in a geometry textbook (यह सिद्ध करें कि (yah siddh karen ki, 'prove that...')) or in courtroom Hindi news reports about a person being proven guilty. The adjective सिद्ध (siddh) itself carries a spiritual undertone in other contexts (a सिद्ध (siddh) is an accomplished yogi/adept), which gives सिद्ध करना (siddh karnaa) a certain gravitas compared to its plainer cousin.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for साबित करना (saabit karnaa) and don't realize सिद्ध करना (siddh karnaa) is the more formal/academic register version, common in math, logic, and legal writing. 2) The object of proof is often marked with को (ko) when it's a person or specific claim being disproven/proven against, e.g. उसे दोषी सिद्ध करना (use doshii siddh karnaa, 'to prove him guilty') -- learners sometimes drop the को (ko) here.
Sample Sentences:
इस प्रमेय को सिद्ध करना आसान नहीं है। It is not easy to prove this theorem.
(is pramey ko siddh karnaa aasaan nahiin hai।)
