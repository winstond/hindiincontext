बाद में (baad men)

adverb A common adverbial phrase meaning 'later' or 'afterward'; built from बाद (baad, 'after') plus the postposition में (men, 'in'), functioning as a fixed unit.
Sense 1 : later, afterward
adverb
Synonyms: बाद (baad, one-word variant, less common alone in this sense), आगे (aage, 'later on', context-dependent)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear बाद में (baad men) constantly in daily life -- it's the classic Delhi way of politely postponing something, as in बाद में बताता हूँ (baad men bataataa hoon, 'I'll tell you later'). It pairs naturally with पहले (pahle, 'before/first') to sequence events: पहले खाना, बाद में सोना (pahle khaanaa, baad men sonaa, 'eat first, sleep later').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop में (men) and say just बाद (baad) to mean 'later', but बाद (baad) alone usually needs to attach to something (बाद में (baad men) is the free-standing adverb for 'later'). 2) बाद में (baad men) is not used for 'behind' in a spatial sense (that's पीछे (piichhe)); it is strictly about time.
