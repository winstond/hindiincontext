जब भी (jab bhii)

conjunction A correlative conjunction meaning 'whenever' -- it sets up a time clause that pairs with तब (tab, 'then') or is left implied. One core sense; the grammar is the point of this word.
Sense 1 : whenever
conjunction Works as the जब-half of a जब भी...तब correlative pair; तब (tab) is often dropped in casual speech when the main clause follows naturally, e.g. जब भी वह आता है, खाना बना होता है (jab bhii vah aataa hai, khaanaa banaa hotaa hai, 'whenever he comes, the food is ready').
Synonyms: जिस समय (jis samay, less common), जब कभी (jab kabhii, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the Hindi equivalent of English 'whenever' and behaves exactly like other जो...वह (jo...vah) style correlatives you have likely met -- the जब भी (jab bhii) clause almost always comes first, with the result clause following, mirroring the natural order of 'whenever X, Y' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that the तब (tab) clause can be silently dropped, and try to force it into every sentence -- native speakers frequently just let the जब भी (jab bhii) clause stand alone with the main clause following. 2) Confusing जब भी (jab bhii, 'whenever') with कभी भी (kabhii bhii, 'anytime, at all'); the former always introduces a clause with a verb, the latter is a standalone adverb of time.

(jab bhii main dillii aataa hoon, main usse miltaa hoon।)
