संगी (sangii)

noun A companion or partner, most often heard in the fixed pair साथी-संगी (saathii-sangii) or in romantic/poetic contexts meaning a life-partner or lover; also used in music for an accompanying performer.
Sense 1 : companion, partner, fellow-traveler
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: साथी (saathii), हमराही (hamraahii, literary), साथी-संगी (saathii-sangii, fixed pair)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in songs and poetry describing a life's journey shared with someone -- a spouse, a fellow traveler on a spiritual path, or comrades in a struggle. It carries a warmer, more soulful weight than the everyday साथी (saathii), which is why it shows up so often in film lyrics and bhajans.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for दोस्त (dost) as the default word for 'companion' -- संगी (sangii) is a touch more literary/emotive and fits journeys, struggles, or shared life-paths better than casual friendship. 2) It is frequently paired with साथी (saathii) as साथी-संगी (saathii-sangii), a set phrase meaning 'companions/comrades' collectively -- learners sometimes try to use संगी (sangii) alone where the pair would sound more natural.

Sense 2 : lover, romantic partner
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: प्रियतम (priytam, literary), साजन (saajan, literary), महबूब (mahboob, colloquial)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: In romantic songs and ghazals, संगी (sangii) slides easily from 'companion' into 'beloved' -- the idea being that a true life-partner is, at heart, a companion for the journey. Context (a love song versus a talk about friendship) usually tells you which sense is meant.
