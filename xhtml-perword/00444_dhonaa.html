ढोना (dhonaa)

verb One core sense: to carry or haul a load, physically or with effort. Extends naturally into a figurative use for bearing a burden (responsibility, blame, someone else's work).
Sense 1 : to carry/haul a physical load
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उठाना (uthaanaa, to lift/carry), लादना (laadnaa, to load onto something)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a laborer hauling bricks at a construction site, or someone carrying pots of water from a hand pump -- ढोना (dhonaa) is the verb for that kind of physical, grinding labor. It is a word steeped in the texture of manual work in India, not a neutral 'to carry.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ले जाना (le jaanaa) for any 'carrying,' but ढोना (dhonaa) specifically implies a heavy, tiring, or repetitive load -- water, bricks, sacks -- not just moving something from A to B. 2) It carries a mild flavor of drudgery, so using it for a light object (like carrying a phone) sounds odd or comic.
Sample Sentences:

(mazdoor sir par iinten dhotaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to bear a burden (figurative)
verb, transitive Commonly used with abstract nouns like बोझ (bojh, 'burden'), जिम्मेदारी (jimmedaarii, 'responsibility'), or इल्ज़ाम (ilzaam, 'blame') as the object.
Synonyms: सहना (sahnaa, to endure), उठाना (uthaanaa, to bear/take on)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in phrases like दूसरों का बोझ ढोना (doosron kaa bojh dhonaa, 'to carry others' burden') -- said of someone stuck doing extra work or shouldering blame that is not really theirs. It has the same weary, put-upon feeling as its physical sense, just moved into the emotional register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes think this figurative sense is a separate word, but it is the same ढोना (dhonaa) -- the image of hauling a physical weight is deliberately carried over to emotional or social burdens. 2) It has a resigned, weary tone; it is not neutral like 'to have a responsibility,' so overusing it can sound overly dramatic for small duties.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे क्यों हमेशा दूसरों का बोझ ढोना पड़ता है? Why do I always have to bear the burden of others?
(mujhe kyon hameshaa doosron kaa bojh dhonaa padtaa hai?)
