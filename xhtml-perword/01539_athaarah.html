अठारह (athaarah)

numeral The number eighteen (18); a single-sense numeral used for counting, quantities, and dates.
Sense 1 : the number eighteen (18)
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers from 1 to 99, अठारह (athaarah) is a fused, unpredictable form rather than a compound of दस (das, 'ten') and आठ (aath, 'eight') -- Hindi numerals were memorized as a block by generations of schoolchildren, and eighteen is simply one you have to learn by rote like the rest.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse अठारह (athaarah, 'eighteen') with अट्ठारह-sounding neighbors like अठाईस (athaaiis, 'twenty-eight') due to similar-feeling shapes -- there is no real overlap, but the numbers eighteen through thirty are all irregular and worth memorizing as a block rather than deriving from tens plus units.
