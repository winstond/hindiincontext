साधन (saadhan)

noun A word for the tools, means, or resources by which something is accomplished; it also extends to 'means of transport' and, in a plural/collective sense, to 'resources' or 'wealth.'
Sense 1 : means, method, instrument for achieving something
noun, masculine Often used with के (ke) + साधन (saadhan) to mean 'means of ___,' e.g. संचार के साधन (sanchaar ke saadhan, 'means of communication').
Synonyms: उपाय (upaay), तरीका (tariikaa), ज़रिया (zariyaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word everywhere in formal and journalistic Hindi: यातायात के साधन (yaataayaat ke saadhan, 'means of transportation'), मनोरंजन के साधन (manoranjan ke saadhan, 'means of entertainment'), संचार के साधन (sanchaar ke saadhan, 'means of communication'). It has a slightly bureaucratic/formal flavor compared to everyday words like तरीका (tariikaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तरीका (tariikaa) when साधन (saadhan) is meant -- तरीका (tariikaa) is more about the 'method/way' of doing something, while साधन (saadhan) is the actual instrument or resource that makes it possible. 2) साधन (saadhan) is often used in the plural (साधन (saadhan) is invariant in its plural form) to mean 'means/resources' collectively, which can confuse learners expecting a distinct plural marker.

Sense 2 : resources, wealth, means (financial capacity)
noun, masculine Typically appears in the plural साधन (saadhan) to mean the financial or material resources a person or family has, e.g. सीमित साधन (siimit saadhan, 'limited resources').
Synonyms: संसाधन (sansaadhan), पूँजी (poonjii, scope: financial resources specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear parents and elders in Delhi-NCR say things like हमारे पास ज़्यादा साधन नहीं हैं (hamaare paas zyaadaa saadhan nahiin hain, 'we don't have many resources') -- it's a common, slightly formal way to talk about limited means without directly saying 'poor' or 'no money,' which softens the statement.
