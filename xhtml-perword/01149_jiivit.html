जीवित (jiivit)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'alive, living' -- used for people, animals, and sometimes traditions or memories that are kept 'alive'.
Sense 1 : alive, living
adjective
Synonyms: ज़िंदा (zindaa), जिंदा (jindaa, variant spelling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet जीवित (jiivit) most often in news reports, official statements, and medical contexts -- "घायल व्यक्ति जीवित है" (ghaayal vyakti jiivit hai, 'the injured person is alive') -- whereas in daily conversation about family and friends, people almost always reach for ज़िंदा (zindaa) instead, e.g. asking "क्या वो अभी भी ज़िंदा है?" (kyaa vo abhii bhii zindaa hai, 'is he still alive?'). The two are near-perfect synonyms, but the register gap is real and worth internalizing.
Common Mistakes: 1) जीवित (jiivit) is the formal/written register word; in everyday speech ज़िंदा (zindaa) is far more common -- saying "वह जीवित है" (vah jiivit hai, 'he is alive') is correct but sounds bookish or medical/legal compared to "वह ज़िंदा है" (vah zindaa hai). 2) Learners sometimes try to use जीवित (jiivit) as a verb; it is only an adjective and needs होना (honaa) or रहना (rahnaa) alongside it to form a sentence.

घायल व्यक्ति जीवित है। The injured person is alive.
(ghaayal vyakti jiivit hai।)
