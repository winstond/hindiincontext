ज़बरदस्ती से (zabardastii se)

adverb An adverbial phrase meaning 'forcibly, by force' -- ज़बरदस्ती (zabardastii) 'force/coercion' plus the postposition से (se, 'by/with'). Closely related to the standalone noun/adverb ज़बरदस्ती (zabardastii), which can express the same meaning without से (se).
Sense 1 : forcibly, against someone's will
adverb Modifies a verb to show the action was imposed on someone rather than chosen by them; often pairs with verbs like करना (karnaa), खिलाना (khilaanaa), or भेजना (bhejnaa).
Synonyms: बलपूर्वक (balpoorvak, formal), जबरन (jabran, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in family contexts -- a mother saying बच्चे को ज़बरदस्ती से दवाई पिलाई (bachche ko zabardastii se davaaii pilaaii, 'forced the medicine on the child') captures a very familiar Indian household scene. It also shows up in discussions of social pressure, like arranged situations where someone is made to agree ज़बरदस्ती से (zabardastii se) rather than out of free will.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop से (se) and just say ज़बरदस्ती (zabardastii) alone -- both are heard in speech, but ज़बरदस्ती से (zabardastii se) more explicitly frames it as 'by force' as a manner adverbial, while bare ज़बरदस्ती (zabardastii) can also function as a noun in the sentence. 2) Do not confuse with ज़रूरत से (zaroorat se, 'more than necessary'), which uses a similar से (se) pattern but has nothing to do with force.
Sample Sentences:
माँ ने बच्चे को ज़बरदस्ती से दवाई पिलाई। The mother forced the medicine on the child.
(maan ne bachche ko zabardastii se davaaii pilaaii।)
