गहराई (gahraaii)

noun A noun meaning 'depth' -- used both literally (of water, wells, wounds) and figuratively (of thought, relationships, or feeling). Related to the adjective गहरा (gahraa, 'deep').
Sense 1 : physical depth
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गहराव (gahraav, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in contexts about wells (kuaan), rivers, and swimming pools -- India's relationship with water bodies (village ponds, the Ganga's depth near ghats) makes गहराई (gahraaii) a very concrete, physically grounded word before it ever becomes abstract.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use the adjective गहरा (gahraa) where the noun गहराई (gahraaii) is needed, e.g. asking 'how deep' requires the noun: कुएँ की गहराई कितनी है? (kuen kii gahraaii kitnii hai?, 'how deep is the well?') rather than trying to inflect गहरा (gahraa) into a noun form.

Sense 2 : figurative depth (of thought, emotion, relationship)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गंभीरता (gambhiirtaa, seriousness/gravity, close but not identical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word Hindi speakers reach for when praising a poem, a film's dialogue, or a person's character as having substance -- उसकी बातों में गहराई है (uskii baaton men gahraaii hai, 'there is depth in what he says') is a common compliment in everyday conversation, not just literary criticism.
