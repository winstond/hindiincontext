दक्षिणा (dakshinaa)

noun A ritual gift or offering, usually money, given to a priest, teacher, or holy person after a religious ceremony or as a mark of respect for guidance received.
Sense 1 : ritual/priestly offering
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भेंट (bhent, gift given respectfully), नज़राना (nazraanaa, offering to a superior/deity)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around pooja (poojaa) and havan (havan) ceremonies -- after the pandit (pandit) finishes the rituals, the host offers दक्षिणा (dakshinaa), often just a few coins or notes wrapped with a coconut or fruit, more a gesture of respect and tradition than payment in the commercial sense. It also survives in the phrase गुरु दक्षिणा (guru dakshinaa, teacher's fee/tribute), the traditional offering a student gives a guru upon completing their education, an idea familiar to anyone who knows the Mahabharata story of Dronacharya and Eklavya.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use दान (daan) interchangeably, but दान is charitable giving to the needy or a temple in general, while दक्षिणा (dakshinaa) is specifically the payment or offering made to a priest, guru, or officiant for performing a ritual or imparting teaching. 2) It is usually a fixed or symbolic amount handed over at the end of a pooja (poojaa) or yagya (yagy), not an open-ended donation.
