निंदा (nindaa)

noun A formal/written-register noun meaning criticism or condemnation, most often met in the phrase निंदा करना (nindaa karnaa) 'to condemn/criticize.' Also spelled निन्दा (nindaa) with the conjunct nasal instead of the anusvaar.
Sense 1 : criticism, condemnation, denunciation
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आलोचना (aalochnaa), बुराई (buraaii, colloquial, esp. in 'बुराई करना'), भर्त्सना (bhartsnaa, formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear निंदा (nindaa) constantly in news broadcasts: politicians and officials 'निंदा करते हैं' (nindaa karte hain, 'condemn') violence, attacks, or statements -- it is the standard word for official condemnation. In everyday family or neighborhood contexts, though, people are more likely to say कोई किसी की बुराई कर रहा है (koii kisii kii buraaii kar rahaa hai, 'someone is bad-mouthing someone'), which feels warmer and more colloquial than the somewhat stern निंदा (nindaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) निंदा (nindaa) itself is a noun and needs करना (karnaa) to become an action -- learners sometimes try to use it directly as a verb. 2) It leans more formal than बुराई (buraaii); in casual gossip about a person's faults, native speakers often reach for बुराई करना (buraaii karnaa) instead, reserving निंदा (nindaa) for public or serious condemnation (of an act, policy, or statement).

(sarkaar ne hamle kii kadii nindaa kii।)
