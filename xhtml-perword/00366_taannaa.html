तानना (taannaa)

verb A physical verb meaning to stretch, pull taut, or aim/point (a weapon), with an extended figurative sense of taunting or sarcastic speech (taanaa maarnaa) that shares the same root idea of 'pulling' at someone.
Sense 1 : to stretch/pull taut (a rope, cloth, bow, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: खींचना (khiinchnaa), कसना (kasnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of putting up a तंबू (tamboo, 'tent') at a wedding or pulling a चारपाई (chaarpaaii, 'cot') rope tight -- that stretching-to-make-firm action is the heart of तानना (taannaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खींचना (khiinchnaa) in every 'pull' context, but तानना (taannaa) specifically implies pulling something to make it taut or fully extended (a rope, a bowstring, a tent), not just dragging it toward oneself.
Sample Sentences:
मजदूर तंबू की रस्सी तान रहे हैं। The laborers are pulling the tent rope tight.
(majdoor tamboo kii rassii taan rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : to aim or point (a weapon) at someone
verb, transitive Commonly used with बंदूक (bandook, 'gun') or पिस्तौल (pistaul, 'pistol') as object, with पर (par) marking the target: किसी पर बंदूक तानना (kisii par bandook taannaa, 'to aim a gun at someone').
Synonyms: निशाना लगाना (nishaanaa lagaanaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense extends naturally from the 'stretch taut' meaning -- drawing back a bow or leveling a gun involves the same taut, ready-to-release posture.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to taunt, mock, or make a sarcastic remark
verb, transitive Almost always heard as the compound ताना मारना (taanaa maarnaa, 'to hurl a taunt') or ताना कसना (taanaa kasnaa); the bare verb तानना (taannaa) alone in this sense is less common in casual speech.
Synonyms: ताना मारना (taanaa maarnaa, the more common phrasal form), चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the classic mother-in-law-to-daughter-in-law verb of Hindi soap operas -- ताने मारना (taane maarnaa, 'to keep taunting') captures that drip-drip of pointed remarks so common in domestic drama.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use तानना (taannaa) alone when they mean 'to taunt', but native speakers usually say ताना मारना (taanaa maarnaa) or ताने देना (taane denaa) instead of the bare verb.
Sample Sentences:
सास हर बात पर बहू को ताना मारती है। The mother-in-law taunts the daughter-in-law over every little thing.
(saas har baat par bahoo ko taanaa maartii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
