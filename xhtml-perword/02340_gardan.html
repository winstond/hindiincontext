गरदन (gardan)

noun A single-sense body-part noun meaning 'neck'; also commonly spelled गर्दन (gardan) with the conjunct र्द -- both spellings are heard and read equally often in Delhi-NCR.
Sense 1 : neck (body part)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गर्दन (gardan, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in everyday physical complaints -- गरदन में दर्द है (gardan men dard hai, 'there's pain in the neck'), a very common line after a bad night's sleep or too much phone-scrolling. It also shows up in idiomatic expressions about being under pressure or obligation, similar to how English uses 'neck' in phrases like 'breathing down someone's neck.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes guess the gender as masculine because many body-part words are (हाथ (haath), पैर (pair)) -- गरदन (gardan) is feminine, so adjectives and verbs agreeing with it take feminine forms, e.g. लंबी गरदन (lambii gardan, 'a long neck').
