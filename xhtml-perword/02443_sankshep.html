संक्षेप (sankshep)

adverb/noun A somewhat formal noun meaning 'summary/brevity', mostly encountered in the fixed adverbial phrase संक्षेप में (sankshep men, 'in short/briefly').
Sense 1 : in short, briefly (as part of the fixed phrase)
adverb Functions adverbially only inside the postpositional phrase संक्षेप में (sankshep men, 'in short'), which is how this sense is almost always realized in a sentence.
Synonyms: थोड़े में (thode men), संक्षेप में (sankshep men, the phrase itself, listed for cross-reference)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: संक्षेप में (sankshep men) is the go-to phrase for wrapping up a long explanation, much like English 'to sum up' or 'in a nutshell' -- you will hear it a lot from teachers and news presenters right before they give the bottom line.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the में (men) and say just संक्षेप (sankshep) to mean 'in short' -- native speakers expect the full phrase संक्षेप में (sankshep men).
Sample Sentences:
संक्षेप में, हमें कल तक यह काम पूरा करना है। In short, we have to finish this work by tomorrow.
(sankshep men, hamen kal tak yah kaam pooraa karnaa hai।)

Sense 2 : summary, abridgement
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सार (saar), सारांश (saaraansh, slightly more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word belongs to written and semi-formal registers -- news anchors, teachers, and office memos use it, but in casual chat people are more likely to just say छोटी सी बात (chhotii sii baat, 'the short version') or थोड़े में (thode men, 'in a few words').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use संक्षेप (sankshep) as a verb meaning 'to summarize' -- it is only a noun; the action is expressed with a phrase like संक्षेप में कहना (sankshep men kahnaa, 'to say in brief') or संक्षिप्त करना (sankshipt karnaa, 'to shorten/abridge').
