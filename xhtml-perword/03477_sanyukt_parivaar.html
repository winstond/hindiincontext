संयुक्त परिवार (sanyukt parivaar)

noun A compound noun meaning 'joint family' -- the extended family household living together, a single core sense with strong cultural weight in Indian life.
Sense 1 : joint family (extended family living/functioning as one household)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बड़ा परिवार (badaa parivaar, less formal way of describing it)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This term names a structure most Indian learners of Hindi will hear discussed constantly -- grandparents, parents, married sons and their wives and children all sharing one household, one kitchen, and often one budget. It's frequently invoked with pride (as a marker of togetherness and support) but also increasingly discussed as something under strain as young couples move to cities and set up their own एकल परिवार (ekal parivaar, 'nuclear family'). You will hear it in everything from matrimonial ads to sociology lectures to grandmothers' complaints.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate 'joint family' too literally with a word like साझा (saajhaa, 'shared') instead of संयुक्त (sanyukt) -- साझा (saajhaa) is used for shared property or business, not for describing this family structure, so it sounds odd here. 2) The opposite concept, a nuclear family, is एकल परिवार (ekal parivaar) or न्यूक्लियर परिवार (nyookliyar parivaar) -- learners sometimes just say छोटा परिवार (chhotaa parivaar, 'small family'), which describes size, not structure, and isn't quite the same contrast.
