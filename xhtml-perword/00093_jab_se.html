जब से (jab se)

conjunction A correlative conjunction meaning 'ever since / from the time that,' typically paired with तब से (tab se, 'since then') in the other clause. One core sense; the pairing pattern is the main thing to internalize.
Sense 1 : ever since, from the time that
conjunction Works as part of a correlative pair: जब से ... तब से (jab se ... tab se), though तब से is often dropped in casual speech when the main clause follows naturally.
Synonyms: जिस समय से (jis samay se, more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the natural way Hindi speakers narrate a change that started at some point and continues -- जब से शादी हुई है (jab se shaadii huii hai, 'ever since the wedding happened') is the kind of line you'll hear constantly in family conversation, gossip, and complaints alike. It pairs naturally with the perfect tense in the जब से clause, since you're marking a completed starting event whose effects continue.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that the तब से (tab se) half can be silently dropped -- सुनकर, जब से वह गया है, घर सुना-सुना लगता है (jab se vah gayaa hai, ghar sunaa-sunaa lagtaa hai, 'ever since he left, the house feels empty') is complete without a following तब से. 2) Don't confuse with जब तक (jab tak, 'until/as long as') -- जब से marks a starting point, जब तक marks a boundary or duration.
Sample Sentences:
जब से उसकी शादी हुई है, वह बहुत बदल गया है। Ever since he got married, he has changed a lot.
(jab se uskii shaadii huii hai, vah bahut badal gayaa hai।)
