जैसे ही (jaise hii)

conjunction A correlative conjunction meaning 'as soon as' -- it sets up a time-sequence clause, usually paired with वैसे ही (vaise hii) or तभी (tabhii) in the following clause. Built from जैसे (jaise, 'like/as') plus the emphatic ही (hii).
Sense 1 : as soon as
conjunction Introduces the first clause of a two-clause sequence: जैसे ही + clause 1, (वैसे ही/तभी) + clause 2. The second clause often starts with वैसे ही (vaise hii) or तभी (tabhii), though these are frequently dropped in casual speech.
Synonyms: जब भी (jab bhii, whenever, meaning scope differs slightly), ज्योंही (jyonhii, literary variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Delhi households and offices for describing chain-reaction events: जैसे ही बारिश शुरू हुई, बिजली चली गई (jaise hii baarish shuroo huii, bijlii chalii gaii, 'as soon as the rain started, the power went out') -- a very familiar monsoon-season sentence shape. Note the family resemblance to जैसे (jaise, 'like/as') used elsewhere for comparisons; ही (hii) here just sharpens the immediacy, the same way it does in अभी ही (abhii hii, 'just now').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that the verb in the जैसे ही (jaise hii) clause is usually in the perfective/past form even when describing a general or future habitual sequence, e.g. जैसे ही वह आया (jaise hii vah aayaa, 'as soon as he arrived') -- not a present or future form. 2) Some learners try to translate 'as soon as' word-for-word using ज्योंही (jyonhii) as the default, when जैसे ही (jaise hii) is the far more common everyday choice.
