ख़तरे (khatre)

noun This is the oblique/plural inflected form of ख़तरा (khatraa, 'danger, risk'); it appears before postpositions like में (men) or as the plural subject/object form.
Sense 1 : danger, risk (oblique/plural form)
noun, masculine This is not a separate lemma-level sense but the inflected form of ख़तरा (khatraa) used either as the plural (ख़तरे, khatre, 'dangers') or as the oblique singular before a postposition, e.g. ख़तरे में (khatre men, 'in danger').
Synonyms: जोखिम (jokhim), संकट (sankat, more formal, often 'crisis')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this form constantly in the fixed phrase ख़तरे में (khatre men, 'in danger'), used everywhere from news headlines about wildlife to a friend joking that their diet is 'ख़तरे में' after a big meal. It is essentially always encountered as this inflected form or as the plural, rarely as bare ख़तरा (khatraa) itself in running speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat ख़तरे (khatre) as a distinct word rather than recognizing it as ख़तरा (khatraa) changing form before postpositions like में (men) or से (se) -- compare जान ख़तरे में है (jaan khatre men hai, 'life is in danger'). 2) The nuqta on ख़ (kh) is often dropped in casual writing as खतरे (khatre); both are read the same in speech by most Delhi-NCR speakers.
