शौक़ (shauq)

noun A single core sense: a personal hobby, interest, or fondness for doing something. Often spelled without the nuqta as शौक (shauk) -- both are common.
Sense 1 : hobby, interest, keen liking
noun, masculine Commonly used with रखना (rakhnaa, 'to keep/have') as in किताबें पढ़ने का शौक़ रखना (kitaaben padhne kaa shauq rakhnaa, 'to have a fondness for reading books'), or with होना (honaa) in dative-subject constructions like मुझे शौक़ है (mujhe shauq hai, 'I have a fondness/hobby').
Synonyms: रुचि (ruchi, formal), दिलचस्पी (dilchaspii), चाव (chaav)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word came from Persian/Urdu and is completely at home in everyday Hindi -- you'll hear it constantly when people talk about hobbies, from music and cooking to cricket. A classic getting-to-know-you question in Delhi is आपको क्या शौक़ है? (aapko kyaa shauq hai?, 'What are your hobbies/interests?'), and the answer usually follows the ...का शौक़ है (...kaa shauq hai) pattern.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैं शौक़ करता हूँ (main shauq kartaa hoon) to mean 'I have a hobby,' but native speakers prefer the dative construction मुझे...का शौक़ है (mujhe...kaa shauq hai) -- the interest 'occurs to' you rather than being something you actively 'do.' 2) The object of the interest is marked with का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) plus a verbal noun (e.g., घूमने का शौक़ (ghoomne kaa shauq, 'a fondness for traveling')), not with a bare infinitive.
