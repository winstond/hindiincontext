परीक्षा (pariikshaa)

noun A single-sense everyday word: 'exam/test', extending naturally to the figurative sense of a 'trial' or 'test' of character/patience.
Sense 1 : examination, test (academic)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इम्तिहान (imtihaan), टेस्ट (test, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: परीक्षा (pariikshaa) looms large in Indian school life -- board exams (बोर्ड परीक्षा, bord pariikshaa) are a huge family event, often discussed with a mix of dread and prayer. Note the curious logic: the student 'gives' (देना, denaa) the exam while the examiner 'takes' (लेना, lenaa) it, the reverse of English's 'take an exam'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use करना (karnaa) with परीक्षा (pariikshaa) by analogy with English 'do an exam', but the natural collocations are परीक्षा देना (pariikshaa denaa, to take/give an exam) and परीक्षा लेना (pariikshaa lenaa, for a teacher to administer/test someone). 2) 'To pass' is परीक्षा में उत्तीर्ण होना (pariikshaa men uttiirn honaa) or पास होना (paas honaa), not a literal translation of 'pass the exam' with a direct object verb.

Sense 2 : trial, ordeal, test (of character, patience, faith)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आज़माइश (aazmaaish), कसौटी (kasautii, figurative, 'touchstone')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense is everywhere in Hindi speech and storytelling -- अग्नि परीक्षा (agni pariikshaa, 'trial by fire') recalls Sita's ordeal in the Ramayana, and people still say यह मेरे धैर्य की परीक्षा है (yah mere dhairy kii pariikshaa hai, 'this is a test of my patience') in ordinary frustration.
