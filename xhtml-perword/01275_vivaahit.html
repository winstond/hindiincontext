विवाहित (vivaahit)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'married'; related to the noun विवाह (vivaah, 'marriage') and the verb विवाह करना (vivaah karnaa, 'to marry'). More formal/official-sounding than the everyday शादीशुदा (shaadiishudaa).
Sense 1 : married
adjective
Synonyms: शादीशुदा (shaadiishudaa, everyday), ब्याहता (byaahtaa, literary, used mainly for a married woman)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet विवाहित (vivaahit) constantly on government forms, matrimonial ads, and in news reports asking someone's वैवाहिक स्थिति (vaivaahik sthiti, 'marital status'), right alongside अविवाहित (avivaahit, 'unmarried'). In everyday chat among friends and family, though, people almost always reach for the warmer, more colloquial शादीशुदा (shaadiishudaa) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to विवाहित (vivaahit) in casual speech where a native speaker would say शादीशुदा (shaadiishudaa); विवाहित (vivaahit) sounds more like it belongs on a form or in a news report. 2) As an invariant-looking form ending in -इत, it is tempting to add gender endings; it does not change for masculine/feminine subjects.
