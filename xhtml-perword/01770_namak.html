नमक (namak)

noun A common kitchen and table noun meaning 'salt', with a well-known figurative extension (loyalty/gratitude owed for sustenance) that lives mainly in idioms.
Sense 1 : salt (the mineral/seasoning)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: लवण (lavan, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In an Indian kitchen, नमक (namak) is the taste-test word above all others -- 'नमक ठीक है?' (namak thiik hai?, 'Is the salt right?') is practically a ritual line while cooking is being tasted. It is grammatically masculine, so you will hear 'नमक कम है' (namak kam hai, 'the salt is less/not enough'), never a feminine agreement.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try को-marking with चाहिए-type constructions (e.g. 'मुझे नमक चाहिए' (mujhe namak chaahie, 'I need salt')) but forget that नमक (namak) itself stays unmarked as the thing needed, not the needer. 2) थोड़ा सा नमक (thodaa saa namak, 'a little salt') is the natural way to say 'a bit of salt' -- learners often over-translate 'some' as कुछ (kuchh), which sounds off with नमक (namak).
Sample Sentences:
सब्ज़ी में नमक कम है। There isn't enough salt in the vegetable dish.
(sabzii men namak kam hai।)

Sense 2 : loyalty/debt owed for sustenance received (figurative)
noun, masculine Occurs almost exclusively inside fixed idiomatic phrases rather than as a freestanding noun with this meaning.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense reflects an old feudal-era idea: eating someone's नमक (namak) -- i.e., being fed or employed by them -- created a moral debt of loyalty. You still hear नमक हराम (namak haraam) hurled as a serious insult at someone seen as a traitor to their boss, family, or benefactor, and नमक हलाल (namak halaal) praising a devoted employee or servant.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense cannot be used outside its set idioms -- you cannot say a plain sentence like 'उसका नमक अच्छा है' to mean 'he is loyal'; the meaning only surfaces in fixed phrases like नमक हराम (namak haraam, 'ungrateful/disloyal [to one who fed/employed you]') and नमक हलाल (namak halaal, 'loyal/faithful [to one's employer/benefactor]').
