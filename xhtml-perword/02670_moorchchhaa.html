मूर्च्छा (moorchchhaa)

noun A formal/medical word for fainting or unconsciousness; you will meet it in health contexts, in classical/mythological narration, and inside the compound verb मूर्च्छित होना (moorchchhit honaa) far more often than as a bare noun in casual speech.
Sense 1 : fainting spell, unconsciousness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बेहोशी (behoshii), अचेतावस्था (achetaavasthaa, rare)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word sits a notch above बेहोशी (behoshii) on the formality ladder -- you will see मूर्च्छा (moorchchhaa) in a hospital discharge slip or in a description of a warrior collapsing on a battlefield in the Mahabharat, but a friend telling you they almost fainted from the heat will say बेहोशी (behoshii) or चक्कर आना (chakkar aanaa) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to बेहोशी (behoshii) for 'fainting' in everyday speech and rarely need मूर्च्छा (moorchchhaa) at all -- it belongs to doctors' reports, textbooks, and older narrative prose rather than casual conversation. 2) It is used with आना (aanaa) (मूर्च्छा आना, moorchchhaa aanaa, 'a fainting spell comes over someone') in the same dative-subject pattern as नींद आना (niind aanaa) -- the person who faints takes को (ko), not ने (ne).
