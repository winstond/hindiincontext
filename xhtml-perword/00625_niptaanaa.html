निपटाना (niptaanaa)

verb A practical, everyday verb meaning to finish off or settle a task, job, or matter -- the causative-flavored partner of निपटना (nipatnaa, 'to get finished/settled'). Mostly one core sense that stretches across chores, business, and disputes.
Sense 1 : to finish off, get through, settle (a task, chore, or piece of work)
verb, transitive Commonly used with काम (kaam) as object: काम निपटाना (kaam niptaanaa, 'to finish off the work') -- carries a sense of clearing something off your plate rather than just completing it.
Synonyms: पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa), खत्म करना (khatm karnaa), निबटाना (nibtaanaa, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb every busy Delhi parent or office-goer uses about the to-do list -- घर का काम निपटाना (ghar kaa kaam niptaanaa, 'to get the housework out of the way') or बिल निपटाना (bil niptaanaa, 'to settle the bills'). It pairs naturally with the compound verb construction निपटा देना (niptaa denaa), giving a brisk, 'let me just get this done' energy.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खत्म करना (khatm karnaa) in every context, but निपटाना (niptaanaa) specifically suggests clearing a backlog or getting a pending job out of the way -- it has a 'dealt with, off my list' flavor that खत्म करना (khatm karnaa) lacks. 2) This is a transitive verb and takes ने (ne) in the perfective, e.g. मैंने काम निपटा दिया (mainne kaam niptaa diyaa, 'I finished off the work') -- easy to forget since it's often used casually about chores.

(main subah hii saaraa kaam niptaa detii hoon।)

Sense 2 : to settle, resolve, sort out (a dispute, matter, or disagreement)
verb, transitive Common object nouns: मामला (maamlaa, 'matter'), झगड़ा (jhagdaa, 'quarrel'), विवाद (vivaad, 'dispute') -- मामला निपटाना (maamlaa niptaanaa, 'to settle the matter').
Synonyms: सुलझाना (suljhaanaa), सुलटाना (sultaanaa, rare), हल करना (hal karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in the context of family property disputes or office politics -- रिश्तेदारों में मामला निपटाना (rishtedaaron men maamlaa niptaanaa, 'to settle the matter among relatives') is a very Indian scene, often meaning informal mediation rather than going to court.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with सुलझाना (suljhaanaa); सुलझाना (suljhaanaa) leans toward untangling something confusing or complicated, while निपटाना (niptaanaa) leans toward closing something out decisively, sometimes with an air of finality or even force -- it can carry a faint undertone of 'dealing with' someone in a firm or dismissive way, depending on context.
