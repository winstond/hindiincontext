इस्तरी (istrii)

noun Means a clothes iron, the household appliance; also used as a verb-phrase base meaning 'to iron' clothes. Related spelling इस्त्री (istrii) is common too.
Sense 1 : the household appliance for pressing clothes
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रेस (pres)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most Delhi-NCR homes and colonies you'll spot the neighborhood इस्तरी वाला (istrii vaalaa, the ironing-man) with his little handcart and coal-heated iron, pressing clothes for the whole block for a few rupees a piece. Many people also just say प्रेस (pres) for the appliance, borrowed straight from English 'press'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not confuse with इस्त्री (istrii), a common alternate spelling of the same word, or with स्त्री (strii, woman), which looks and sounds similar but is unrelated. 2) The action of ironing clothes is usually expressed as इस्तरी करना (istrii karnaa, to do ironing) rather than a single dedicated verb.

Sense 2 : the act/service of ironing clothes
noun, feminine Used with करना (karnaa) as a light-verb construction: इस्तरी करना (istrii karnaa, to iron/do the ironing), and with होना (honaa) for the passive sense, इस्तरी होना (istrii honaa, to be ironed/get ironed).
Synonyms: प्रेस (pres)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in everyday household talk like कपड़ों की इस्तरी करा दो (kapdon kii istrii karaa do, 'get the clothes ironed'), often handed off to domestic help or the local इस्तरी वाला (istrii vaalaa) rather than done at home.
