हस्ताक्षर (hastaakshar)

noun A formal, Sanskritic word for 'signature'; used as a noun and in the common compound verb phrase हस्ताक्षर करना (hastaakshar karnaa) meaning 'to sign'.
Sense 1 : signature
noun, masculine Almost always used with करना (karnaa) as a light verb ('to do a signature' = 'to sign'), rather than as the subject of its own action verb.
Synonyms: दस्तख़त (dastkhat, more common in everyday speech), सिग्नेचर (signechar, colloquial, English loanword)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see हस्ताक्षर (hastaakshar) stamped on official forms, bank documents, and government letters -- it has a formal, bureaucratic flavor. In casual conversation, most Delhi-NCR speakers reach for दस्तख़त (dastkhat) instead, so हस्ताक्षर (hastaakshar) is the one you read on paper more than the one you say out loud.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use हस्ताक्षर (hastaakshar) as a verb by itself; it needs करना (karnaa) (हस्ताक्षर करना, hastaakshar karnaa, 'to sign') or होना (honaa) for the passive sense ('to be signed'). 2) Gender is masculine despite ending in a consonant cluster that some learners mistake for feminine; it also does not change form in the plural in most everyday usage, staying हस्ताक्षर (hastaakshar) for one or many signatures.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया इस फ़ॉर्म पर अपने हस्ताक्षर करें। Please sign your signature on this form.
(kripyaa is form par apne hastaakshar karen।)

(prabandhak is samay antim riport par hastaakshar kar rahe hain।)

(agar aapke hastaakshar mel nahiin khaate, to baink chek asviikaar kar degaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active

(yadi us samay unke hastaakshar hote, to yah samjhautaa vaidh maanaa jaataa।)

समिति चाहती है कि सचिव के हस्ताक्षर से रिपोर्ट प्रमाणित हो। The committee wants the report to be certified with the secretary's signature.
(samiti chaahtii hai ki sachiv ke hastaakshar se riport pramaanit ho।)
