बिताना (bitaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to spend or pass time; also used for living through or getting through a period of one's life. Related to the intransitive बीतना (biitnaa, 'to pass, elapse') -- बिताना is its causative counterpart.
Sense 1 : to spend or pass (time)
verb, transitive Takes a time expression as its direct object, e.g. समय बिताना (samay bitaanaa, 'to spend time'), छुट्टियाँ बिताना (chhuttiyaan bitaanaa, 'to spend the holidays').
Synonyms: गुज़ारना (guzaarnaa), काटना (kaatnaa, informal, esp. for hard/boring time)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday word for describing how a day, evening, or vacation went -- कैसा वक़्त बिताया? (kaisaa vaqt bitaayaa?, 'how did you spend your time?') is a very natural way to ask a friend about their trip or weekend. Because it is causative in form (from बीतना, 'to elapse'), it subtly frames time as something you actively push along or get through, which fits its second sense of enduring a hard patch.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for करना (karnaa) with time words out of habit -- but Hindi treats time as something you 'pass' or 'spend' via बिताना (bitaanaa) or गुज़ारना (guzaarnaa), not 'do'. 2) Despite being transitive, do not confuse it with its intransitive root बीतना (biitnaa) -- 'साल बीत गया' (saal biit gayaa, 'the year passed') has no subject doing the passing, while 'मैंने साल बिताया' (mainne saal bitaayaa, 'I spent the year') does.
Sample Sentences:
हमने छुट्टियाँ गोवा में बिताईं। We spent our vacation in Goa.
(hamne chhuttiyaan govaa men bitaaiin।)

Sense 2 : to live through, get through (a difficult period)
verb, transitive Used with expressions of hardship or difficult circumstance, e.g. मुश्किल से दिन बिताना (mushkil se din bitaanaa, 'to get through the day with difficulty'), अकेले ज़िंदगी बिताना (akele zindgii bitaanaa, 'to live out one's life alone').
Synonyms: गुज़ारना (guzaarnaa), काटना (kaatnaa, common for hardship, e.g. 'रात काटना')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense carries a note of endurance -- ज़िंदगी बिताना (zindgii bitaanaa, 'to live out one's life') often appears in conversations about someone's struggles or a modest, hard-won existence, giving it a slightly wistful or resigned color compared to the more neutral 'spend time' sense.
