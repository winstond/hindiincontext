ज़बरदस्त (zabardast)

adjective An enthusiastic, all-purpose intensifying adjective meaning 'amazing, tremendous, powerful' -- used constantly in everyday praise of food, movies, performances, and events.
Sense 1 : excellent, amazing, terrific
adjective
Synonyms: बेहतरीन (behatriin), शानदार (shaandaar), कमाल (kamaal, colloquial), लाजवाब (laajvaab)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you shout after a great cricket six, a spicy plate of chaat, or a friend's new job news -- it is the default 'wow, that's great' of Delhi conversation. It pairs naturally with गजब (gajab) and कमाल (kamaal) in a string of enthusiastic praise, which is very much the Delhi style of complimenting things.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बहुत अच्छा (bahut achchhaa) when praising something, but ज़बरदस्त (zabardast) is the word Hindi speakers actually reach for to sound genuinely impressed -- it carries more punch than a plain 'very good.' 2) It is invariant in casual speech for many speakers despite ending in अ (a) -- see the morphology notes for its formal inflection pattern.

Sense 2 : powerful, forceful, intense (of impact, strength, or effect)
adjective
Synonyms: ज़ोरदार (zordaar), प्रचंड (prachand, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used for a ज़बरदस्त बारिश (zabardast baarish, 'intense rain') or a ज़बरदस्त झटका (zabardast jhatkaa, 'powerful shock/jolt') -- this sense keeps the root idea of 'forceful' that connects it to ज़बरदस्ती (zabardastii, 'by force'), even though the praise-sense (above) has drifted away from that literal force.
