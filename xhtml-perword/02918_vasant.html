वसंत (vasant)

noun Means 'spring', the season; also lives on as a common personal name and appears in the name of the festival वसंत पंचमी (vasant panchmii).
Sense 1 : the spring season
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बहार (bahaar, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In North India, वसंत (vasant) is closely tied to the festival वसंत पंचमी (vasant panchmii), which marks the start of spring and is celebrated by flying kites and wearing yellow, the color of mustard fields in bloom. Hindi traditionally recognizes six seasons (ऋतुएँ, rituen) rather than four, and वसंत (vasant) is considered the most beautiful and beloved of them, often called ऋतुराज (rituraaj, 'king of seasons') in literature.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बहार (bahaar) as the everyday word for the season, but बहार (bahaar) is mainly used in poetic or figurative contexts (like बहार आ गई (bahaar aa gaii, 'spring/good times have arrived') as a metaphor); वसंत (vasant) is the plain, neutral word for the calendar season itself.

Sense 2 : a common Hindu male given name
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: वसंत (vasant) is a widely used personal name for boys across North India, chosen for its association with renewal, beauty, and the arrival of spring; you'll meet plenty of men named वसंत (vasant) in everyday life, much like 'April' or 'Spring' might evoke freshness in English, though used far more commonly as a first name here.
