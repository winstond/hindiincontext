गिराना (giraanaa)

verb The causative of गिरना (girnaa, 'to fall'): the core sense is 'to cause to fall, drop, or knock down,' with a common extended sense for lowering values like prices or rates.
Sense 1 : to drop, knock down, cause to fall
verb, transitive
Synonyms: फेंकना (phenknaa, 'to throw/toss down'), ढहाना (dhahaanaa, 'to demolish, cause to collapse')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of गिराना (giraanaa) as the answer to 'who made it fall?' -- English often just says 'the glass fell' passively, but Hindi's pair गिरना (girnaa)/गिराना (giraanaa) forces you to be clear about whether there was a hand behind it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use गिरना (girnaa) when they mean someone deliberately dropped or knocked something over -- if there's an agent doing the dropping, गिराना (giraanaa) is needed, not the intransitive गिरना (girnaa). 2) The thing that falls takes को (ko) only in specific contexts (like targeting a person/object); in most simple sentences it's just the direct object with no postposition, e.g. गिलास गिरा दिया (gilaas giraa diyaa, 'dropped the glass').
Sample Sentences:
उसने गलती से गिलास गिरा दिया। He accidentally dropped the glass.
(usne galtii se gilaas giraa diyaa।)

Sense 2 : to lower, bring down (price, level, value, status)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: कम करना (kam karnaa, 'to reduce')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this a lot in bazaar haggling -- भाव मत गिराओ (bhaav mat giraao, 'don't lower the price') is something a shopkeeper might say back to you, half-joking, half-serious, when you try to bargain too hard.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कम करना (kam karnaa) by default for 'lower/reduce'; गिराना (giraanaa) is the more vivid, natural choice specifically for prices, rates, or someone's standing/reputation (जैसे भाव गिराना (bhaav giraanaa, 'to bring down prices'), किसी की इज़्ज़त गिराना (kisii kii izzat giraanaa, 'to lower someone's respect/standing')).
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to shoot down, bring down (a plane, bird, etc.) or overthrow (a government)
verb, transitive Often appears as the compound verb मार गिराना (maar giraanaa) in news contexts about planes, drones, or targets; used alone, गिराना (giraanaa) can also mean toppling a government or bringing down a regime.
Synonyms: मार गिराना (maar giraanaa, 'to shoot down, compound-verb form'), उखाड़ना (ukhaadnaa, 'to uproot, oust')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in Hindi news headlines -- सरकार गिरा दी (sarkaar giraa dii, 'toppled the government') is standard political vocabulary, extending the physical 'knock down' sense into the world of politics.
