सुनहरा (sunahraa)

adjective An adjective meaning 'golden', used both for the literal color/sheen of gold and figuratively for something wonderful or precious, as in सुनहरा भविष्य (sunahraa bhavishy, 'golden future').
Sense 1 : golden in color
adjective
Synonyms: स्वर्णिम (svarnim, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly on packaging and in ads -- सुनहरा रंग (sunahraa rang, 'golden color') is a favorite descriptor for festive sweets boxes, saree borders, and diyas at Diwali, since gold-color evokes prosperity and auspiciousness in everyday Indian aesthetics.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सुनहरा (sunahraa) with सोने का (sone kaa, 'made of gold') -- सुनहरा (sunahraa) describes color/appearance, not material; a plastic trophy can be सुनहरा (sunahraa) without containing any gold.

Sense 2 : figuratively wonderful, glorious, or prime (of a period)
adjective
Synonyms: स्वर्णिम (svarnim, literary), शानदार (shaandaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word behind phrases like सुनहरा दौर (sunahraa daur, 'golden era') and सुनहरा मौका (sunahraa maukaa, 'golden opportunity') -- much like English, Hindi reaches for gold to mean a peak or precious moment, so this sense will feel intuitive once you know the literal one.
Sample Sentences:
यह दौर भारतीय सिनेमा का सुनहरा दौर कहा जाता है। This period is called the golden era of Indian cinema.
(yah daur bhaartiiy sinemaa kaa sunahraa daur kahaa jaataa hai।)
