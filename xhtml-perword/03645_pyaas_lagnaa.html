प्यास लगना (pyaas lagnaa)

verb A single-sense compound verb meaning 'to feel thirsty', built from the noun प्यास (pyaas, 'thirst') plus लगना (lagnaa); grammatically parallel to भूख लगना (bhookh lagnaa) and नींद आना (niind aanaa).
Sense 1 : to feel thirsty
verb, intransitive The experiencer takes को (ko): मुझे प्यास लगी है (mujhe pyaas lagii hai, 'I am/have become thirsty'). प्यास (pyaas) is the grammatical subject and लगना (lagnaa) agrees with it (feminine).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like भूख लगना (bhookh lagnaa) for hunger, Hindi treats thirst as something that 'attaches itself to' you rather than a state you actively are in -- the body is the site where the sensation lands, not the agent producing it. This dative-subject frame is one of the most useful patterns for talking naturally about physical sensations in Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to say 'I feel thirsty' with मैं (main) as subject and a form of होना (honaa), producing something unnatural like मैं प्यासा हूँ (main pyaasaa hoon) when they mean the momentary sensation -- native speakers overwhelmingly reach for मुझे प्यास लगी है (mujhe pyaas lagii hai) instead. 2) Since प्यास (pyaas) is feminine, the verb must agree with it, not with the को-marked experiencer -- learners sometimes wrongly default to masculine agreement.
