शांत (shaant)

adjective An adjective describing calm or quiet -- covering both a peaceful state of mind or personality and a physically quiet/still place or thing.
Sense 1 : calm, peaceful (person, mind, temperament)
adjective
Synonyms: स्थिर (sthir, steady/composed), संयत (sanyat, restrained), सौम्य (saumy, gentle)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word behind the peace mantra ओम् शांति शांति शांति (om shaanti shaanti shaanti) chanted at the end of pujas and yoga sessions -- शांत (shaant) and शांति (shaanti, peace/the noun form) share this root, and a mother telling a fussy child शांत हो जाओ (shaant ho jaao, 'settle down') is drawing on the same idea of restoring calm.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चुप (chup) when they mean शांत (shaant) -- चुप (chup) is specifically 'silent, not talking,' while शांत (shaant) describes an inner state of calm that may or may not involve silence. 2) होना (honaa) is used with शांत (shaant) for a state ('वह शांत है', vah shaant hai, 'he is calm'), while हो जाना (ho jaanaa) marks the change into that state ('शांत हो जाओ', shaant ho jaao, 'calm down') -- learners sometimes drop जाना (jaanaa) when they actually mean the transition.

Sense 2 : quiet, still (place, environment, sound)
adjective
Synonyms: सुनसान (sunsaan, deserted/still), नीरव (niirav, literary), खामोश (khaamosh, hushed)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this used for a शांत गाँव (shaant gaanv, 'a quiet village') or a शांत झील (shaant jhiil, 'a still lake') -- it extends the calm-mind sense outward to describe an environment that has no disturbance, whether of noise, movement, or activity.
