जोड़ा (jodaa)

noun/verb Primarily a noun meaning 'pair/couple' or a matched set of clothes, and also the masculine past-tense form of the verb जोड़ना (jodnaa, 'to add/join') -- context tells you which.
Sense 1 : a pair or couple (two people, often a bride and groom, or two matching things)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: युगल (yugal, formal), जोड़ी (jodii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: At weddings you will hear दुल्हा-दुल्हन का जोड़ा (dulhaa-dulhan kaa jodaa, 'the bride-and-groom pair') -- the word carries a slightly formal, almost ceremonial weight compared to its cousin जोड़ी (jodii), which is what you'd use for two friends or a comic duo.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse जोड़ा (jodaa) with जोड़ी (jodii) -- जोड़ी (jodii) is feminine and far more common for 'a couple/pair' in casual speech (like a dance pair or a couple at a party), while जोड़ा (jodaa) leans toward formal or ceremonial pairings (a wedding couple) or literal matched sets of objects.

Sense 2 : a matching set of clothes (e.g. a suit or an outfit sold/worn as one unit)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सूट (soot)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: When a shopkeeper in a Delhi market says एक जोड़ा दिखाइए (ek jodaa dikhaaie, 'show me one outfit'), they mean a full matching set -- like a kurta-pajama or a salwar-suit sold together, not just a single piece.

Sense 3 : added, joined, connected (past tense, masculine singular)
verb, transitive This is simply the masculine singular perfective form of जोड़ना (jodnaa); it agrees with a masculine singular subject or object (in ने (ne) constructions) and is not a separate lexical item.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this constantly in arithmetic contexts, e.g. उस ने दो और तीन जोड़ा (us ne do aur tiin jodaa, 'he/she added two and three') -- it's the same verb root as जोड़ी (jodii) and जोड़ा (jodaa) the noun, since 'joining' two things is the conceptual thread linking pairs, matched outfits, and addition.
Sample Sentences:
उस ने दो और तीन जोड़ा। He added two and three.
(us ne do aur tiin jodaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
