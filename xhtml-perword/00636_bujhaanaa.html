बुझाना (bujhaanaa)

verb The causative counterpart of बुझना (bujhnaa, 'to be extinguished') -- means 'to put out/extinguish' (fire, light) and, by extension, 'to quench' (thirst).
Sense 1 : to extinguish, put out (fire, lamp, light)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the causative of बुझना (bujhnaa) -- if a diya या (diiyaa) 'goes out' on its own, it बुझ जाता है (bujh jaataa hai); if you actively blow it out or douse it, you बुझाते हैं (bujhaate hain) it. This active/passive pairing is a classic Hindi causative pattern worth noticing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बुझाना (bujhaanaa) with बंद करना (band karnaa, 'to turn off'); बुझाना (bujhaanaa) is specifically for flame/fire/light, while बंद करना (band karnaa) is used for electrical switches and appliances, e.g. पंखा बंद करो (pankhaa band karo, 'turn off the fan') rather than पंखा बुझाओ (pankhaa bujhaao).

Sense 2 : to quench (thirst)
verb, transitive Used almost exclusively in the fixed collocation प्यास बुझाना (pyaas bujhaanaa, 'to quench thirst').
Synonyms: शांत करना (shaant karnaa, used with प्यास)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi extends the 'putting out a fire' image to thirst: प्यास बुझाना (pyaas bujhaanaa, 'to quench thirst') treats thirst like a flame that needs dousing -- a nice window into how the language reuses concrete verbs for bodily sensations.
