पश्चिमी (pashchimii)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'western' -- used for directions, cultures, and geopolitical regions alike, e.g. पश्चिमी देश (pashchimii desh, 'western countries').
Sense 1 : western
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पश्चिमी (pashchimii) comes from पश्चिम (pashchim, 'west') and shows up constantly in everyday talk about culture and lifestyle -- पश्चिमी सभ्यता (pashchimii sabhytaa, 'western civilization') or पश्चिमी कपड़े (pashchimii kapde, 'western clothes') are common phrases when comparing Indian and non-Indian ways of doing things. It pairs naturally with its counterparts पूर्वी (poorvii, 'eastern'), उत्तरी (uttrii, 'northern'), and दक्षिणी (dakshinii, 'southern').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect it like a variable adjective (पश्चिमा, पश्चिमे) -- it never changes form regardless of the noun's gender or number, e.g. पश्चिमी हवा (pashchimii havaa, 'western wind') and पश्चिमी रीति-रिवाज (pashchimii riiti-rivaaj, 'western customs') both keep पश्चिमी (pashchimii) unchanged.
Sample Sentences:
उसने पश्चिमी कपड़े पहने हैं। She is wearing western clothes.
(usne pashchimii kapde pahne hain।)
