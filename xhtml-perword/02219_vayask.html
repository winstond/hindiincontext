वयस्क (vayask)

adjective/noun A formal/administrative word meaning 'adult' -- used as both an adjective (adult, of-age) and a noun (an adult person). Common in legal, medical, and official contexts rather than everyday chit-chat.
Sense 1 : adult, of legal age
adjective
Synonyms: बालिग (baalig)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll spot this word on election posters ('वयस्क मतदाता' (vayask matdaataa, 'adult voters')) and on film certificates and hospital forms -- it's the bureaucratic register's word for 'adult,' the same way English uses 'adult' on a form but 'grown-up' at the dinner table.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for वयस्क (vayask) in casual speech where बड़ा (badaa) or बड़े लोग (bade log, 'grown-ups') would sound far more natural -- वयस्क (vayask) belongs mainly to forms, signs, and news reports (e.g. voting age, film certification).

Sense 2 : an adult (person)
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: बालिग (baalig)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As a noun this pairs naturally with नाबालिग (naabaalig, 'minor') in legal and medical contexts -- the two words are commonly taught as a pair, much like 'adult' and 'minor' in English legal language.
