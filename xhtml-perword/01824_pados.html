पड़ोस (pados)

noun A single, straightforward sense: the physical neighborhood or vicinity around a place. Closely related to the more frequently used adjective/noun पड़ोसी (padosii, 'neighbor').
Sense 1 : neighborhood, vicinity
noun, masculine Usually appears with के (ke) as पड़ोस के (pados ke) meaning 'from/of the neighborhood', or with में (men) as पड़ोस में (pados men) meaning 'in the neighborhood'.
Synonyms: आस-पास (aas-paas, nearby area), नज़दीक (nazdiik, vicinity)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR life, पड़ोस (pados) carries a warmth beyond the English 'neighborhood' -- it evokes the aunty next door who sends over food during festivals, or the group of neighbors who gather for evening chai. Talking about 'हमारे पड़ोस में' (hamaare pados men, 'in our neighborhood') often implies a small community you know personally, not just a geographic zone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पड़ोस (pados, the place/area) with पड़ोसी (padosii, the person who lives there) -- पड़ोस is where you live, पड़ोसी is who lives next to you. 2) पड़ोस में रहना (pados men rahnaa, 'to live in the neighborhood') is the natural phrasing; saying पड़ोस पर (pados par) sounds off since पड़ोस takes में or के, not पर.
