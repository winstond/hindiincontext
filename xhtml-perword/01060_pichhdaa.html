पिछड़ा (pichhdaa)

adjective/verb Primarily an adjective meaning 'backward' (in development, progress, or social/economic standing); also functions as the past participle of the verb पिछड़ना (pichhadnaa, 'to fall behind, lag behind').
Sense 1 : backward (undeveloped, lagging in progress)
adjective
Synonyms: अविकसित (aviksit, underdeveloped, formal), पीछे (piichhe, behind)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Indian public life, पिछड़ा वर्ग (pichhdaa varg, 'backward class') is a specific administrative and constitutional category -- OBC (Other Backward Classes) -- so this word carries real political weight beyond its plain dictionary sense, much like 'underprivileged' or 'disadvantaged' does in English policy talk. In everyday speech, though, it is used quite freely for anything lagging, from a पिछड़ा गाँव (pichhdaa gaanv, 'backward village') to a पिछड़ा हुआ सिस्टम (pichhdaa huaa sistam, 'an outdated/backward system').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this only for people, but it is applied broadly to regions, castes, communities, economies, and even individual subjects at school -- पिछड़ा इलाका (pichhdaa ilaakaa, 'backward area') is just as natural as पिछड़ा बच्चा (pichhdaa bachchaa, 'a child who is behind/lagging'). 2) In social and political contexts it is a loaded, semi-technical term (see cultural note) -- using it casually about a person's caste or community can come across as insensitive rather than merely descriptive.

Sense 2 : fell behind, lagged (past participle used as adjective/predicate)
verb, intransitive This is the perfective participle of पिछड़ना (pichhadnaa), an intransitive verb; used predicatively as in वह दौड़ में पिछड़ गया (vah daud men pichhad gayaa, 'he fell behind in the race').
Synonyms: पीछे रह गया (piichhe rah gayaa, fell behind, phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This verbal sense is the root from which the adjective sense grew -- someone or something that 'has fallen behind' (पिछड़ा, pichhdaa) becomes, by extension, simply 'backward.' The two senses are best learned together: one is an action, the other its lasting label.
Common Mistakes: 1) Because पिछड़ना (pichhadnaa) is intransitive, it never takes ने (ne) -- learners sometimes add it by habit since the word looks like a past-tense verb form.
