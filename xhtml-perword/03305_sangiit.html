संगीत (sangiit)

noun A single, straightforward noun meaning 'music' -- no significant secondary senses to track.
Sense 1 : music
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: संगीत (sangiit) covers everything from classical रागों (raagon) to Bollywood songs to background film scores -- Hindi doesn't usually split 'music' into narrower everyday words the way English sometimes does with 'song' vs 'tune' vs 'score'. You'll hear it constantly in phrases like संगीत सुनना (sangiit sunnaa, 'to listen to music') and शास्त्रीय संगीत (shaastriiy sangiit, 'classical music'), the latter pointing to the deep prestige of Hindustani and Carnatic traditions in Indian cultural life.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use संगीत (sangiit) as a verb or adjective ('musical'); it is only a noun -- for 'musical' use संगीत से जुड़ा (sangiit se judaa) or संगीतमय (sangiitmay). 2) संगीत (sangiit) is uncountable in the everyday sense -- you don't pluralize it to mean 'songs' or 'pieces of music'; use गाने (gaane) or धुनें (dhunen) for that.
