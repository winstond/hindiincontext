चुंबन (chumban)

noun A formal/literary word meaning 'a kiss' -- the noun for the act itself. Everyday speech tends to use the verb phrase किस करना (kis karnaa) or बोसा (bosaa) in more colloquial/Urdu-influenced registers instead.
Sense 1 : a kiss
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बोसा (bosaa, literary/Urdu-influenced), किस (kis, colloquial English loan)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word carries a slightly formal, almost literary flavor -- you will meet it in old film songs, poetry, or news headlines about a 'kiss of peace' or protest, but rarely in casual chat among friends, where किस (kis) or किस करना (kis karnaa) is far more natural. Public displays of affection are still a sensitive topic in much of Indian society, so even the vocabulary around kissing tends to lean toward euphemism or English borrowing in daily speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चुंबन (chumban) in casual speech, but native speakers overwhelmingly say किस करना (kis karnaa) or किस लेना (kis lenaa) in everyday conversation; चुंबन (chumban) shows up more in writing, poetry, or slightly formal/old-fashioned contexts. 2) The common verb pairing is चुंबन करना (chumban karnaa, to kiss) or चुंबन लेना (chumban lenaa, to take a kiss) -- चुंबन itself is just the noun and does not conjugate as a verb.
