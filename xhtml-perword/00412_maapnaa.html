मापना (maapnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to measure' -- taking dimensions, quantity, or extent of something with an instrument or standard.
Sense 1 : to measure
verb, transitive
Synonyms: नापना (naapnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this most in practical, hands-on contexts -- a tailor measuring cloth, a shopkeeper measuring grain, an engineer measuring a plot of land. It carries a slightly more technical or deliberate feel than its close twin नापना (naapnaa), which is the word you will hear far more often in casual daily speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with नापना (naapnaa) -- both are correct and used interchangeably for physical measurement (length, weight, distance), so no real distinction to worry about. 2) For measuring abstract things like worth, ability, or importance, native speakers often prefer the compound आँकना (aanknaa) or the phrase अंदाज़ा लगाना (andaazaa lagaanaa) rather than मापना (maapnaa), even though मापना is not wrong.
