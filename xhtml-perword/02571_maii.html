मई (maii)

noun This word simply names the month of May; it has one straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : the month of May
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like all the Hindi month names borrowed from the English/Gregorian calendar (जनवरी, फ़रवरी, मई, जून...), मई (maii) is used everywhere in daily life, on calendars, in offices, and in casual conversation, alongside (and largely replacing) the older Hindu calendar months like वैशाख (vaishaakh) or जेठ (jeth) which now survive mainly in festival and almanac contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect मई (maii) like a regular feminine noun in oblique constructions, but as a month name it stays invariant; only the postposition changes, e.g. मई में (maii men, 'in May').

(maii men bahut garmii padtii hai।)
