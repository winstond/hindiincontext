सारणी (saarnii)

noun A formal, written-register word meaning 'table' or 'chart' -- the kind of grid of rows and columns you'd find in a textbook, report, or spreadsheet. It has essentially one core meaning.
Sense 1 : table/chart (rows and columns of data)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: टेबल (tebal, borrowed word, refers to furniture not data-tables), तालिका (taalikaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word most often in compounds like समय-सारणी (samay-saarnii, 'timetable/schedule') pinned up in schools and railway stations, or in textbooks introducing a गुणा सारणी (gunaa saarnii, 'multiplication table'). It belongs to the written, official register -- the kind of Hindi you see printed on a form or a syllabus, not the kind you'd use chatting with a friend.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for टेबल (tebal) to mean a data table, but in everyday Hindi टेबल (tebal) almost always means the physical piece of furniture; सारणी (saarnii) or तालिका (taalikaa) is what you want for rows-and-columns data. 2) This word is bookish -- in casual speech about, say, a train timetable, people are just as likely to say टाइम टेबल (taaim tebal) or समय-सारणी (samay-saarnii) rather than सारणी (saarnii) alone.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया नीचे दी गई सारणी को ध्यान से देखें। Please look carefully at the table given below.
(kripyaa niiche dii gaii saarnii ko dhyaan se dekhen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
स्कूल के बाहर समय-सारणी लगी हुई है। The timetable is put up outside the school.
(skool ke baahar samay-saarnii lagii huii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
बच्चे कक्षा में गुणा सारणी याद कर रहे थे। The children were memorizing the multiplication table in class.
(bachche kakshaa men gunaa saarnii yaad kar rahe the।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
रेलवे स्टेशन पर गाड़ियों की समय-सारणी हर महीने बदली जाती है। The trains' timetable at the railway station is changed every month.
(relve steshan par gaadiyon kii samay-saarnii har mahiine badlii jaatii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
इस सारणी में पिछले पाँच वर्षों के आँकड़े दर्शाए गए हैं। This table shows the data of the last five years.
(is saarnii men pichhle paanch varshon ke aankde darshaae gae hain।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
अगर तुमने सारणी ठीक से बनाई होती, तो जवाब आसानी से समझ आ जाता। If you had made the table properly, the answer would have been easy to understand.
(agar tumne saarnii thiik se banaaii hotii, to javaab aasaanii se samajh aa jaataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
शिक्षक ने सुझाव दिया कि हम अपना दैनिक कार्यक्रम एक सारणी के रूप में बनाएँ। The teacher suggested that we make our daily schedule in the form of a table.
(shikshak ne sujhaav diyaa ki ham apnaa dainik kaaryakram ek saarnii ke roop men banaaen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
परीक्षा की समय-सारणी अगले सप्ताह जारी की जाएगी। The exam timetable will be released next week.
(pariikshaa kii samay-saarnii agle saptaah jaarii kii jaaegii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive
