मसलन (maslan)

adverb A discourse adverb meaning 'for example' or 'for instance' -- used to introduce an illustration in speech or writing.
Sense 1 : for example, for instance
adverb Typically opens a clause or follows a comma, setting up an example for what was just said.
Synonyms: जैसे (jaise), उदाहरण के लिए (udaahran ke lie, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: मसलन (maslan) has a slightly more educated, articulate flavor than जैसे (jaise) -- you will hear it a lot from someone explaining or arguing a point, like a teacher or a journalist thinking aloud. It comes from Urdu/Persian मसल (masal, 'example, proverb'), and that heritage still gives it a faint literary polish even in everyday use.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for उदाहरण के लिए (udaahran ke lie) by default since it is taught first, but मसलन (maslan) is what most people actually say in casual conversation. 2) It is placed at the start of the illustrating clause, not attached to the noun being exemplified.
