ग़लती (ghaltii)

noun A single core sense: a mistake or error, used constantly in everyday speech about slip-ups big and small. Also commonly spelled without the nuqta as गलती (galtii).
Sense 1 : mistake, error, fault
noun, feminine Common frame: ग़लती करना (ghaltii karnaa, 'to make a mistake') is transitive; ग़लती होना (ghaltii honaa, 'a mistake to happen/occur') uses a से-marked or को-marked experiencer, as in मुझसे ग़लती हुई (mujhse ghaltii huii, 'I made a mistake' -- literally 'a mistake happened from me').
Synonyms: भूल (bhool), त्रुटि (truti, formal), चूक (chook, a slip/oversight), कमी (kamii, shortcoming)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Notice how ग़लती से (ghaltii se, 'by mistake') is one of the most useful adverbial phrases in daily Hindi, covering everything from bumping into someone on the metro to sending a text to the wrong person. The से-marked 'mistake happened from me' pattern (मुझसे ग़लती हुई, mujhse ghaltii huii) reflects a broader Hindi habit of framing unintentional acts as things that happen to or through a person rather than things a person actively does -- softer and less accusatory than English 'I made a mistake.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैंने ग़लती हुई (mainne ghaltii huii) by analogy with ने-marked verbs, but होना (honaa) here takes से (se), not ने (ne): the correct form is मुझसे ग़लती हुई (mujhse ghaltii huii). 2) ग़लती करना (ghaltii karnaa, 'to make a mistake') and ग़लती होना (ghaltii honaa, 'a mistake to happen') shade the blame differently -- करना (karnaa) frames it as your deliberate act, होना (honaa) frames it as something that just occurred, softening responsibility -- and learners often pick the wrong one when apologizing.

माफ़ कीजिए, मुझसे बड़ी ग़लती हुई। I'm sorry, I made a big mistake.
(maaf kiijie, mujhse badii ghaltii huii।)
