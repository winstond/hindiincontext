विचार-विमर्श करना (vichaar-vimarsh karnaa)

verb A compound noun-plus-verb phrase meaning 'to deliberate' or 'to discuss/consult' -- built from विचार (vichaar, 'thought') and विमर्श (vimarsh, 'discussion') paired together, plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'). It has just one core sense used in formal or serious discussion contexts.
Sense 1 : to deliberate, discuss, or consult (formally, weighing options)
verb, intransitive Takes पर (par) or के बारे में (ke baare men) to mark the topic being discussed, e.g. इस मुद्दे पर विचार-विमर्श करना (is mudde par vichaar-vimarsh karnaa, 'to deliberate on this issue').
Synonyms: चर्चा करना (charchaa karnaa), बातचीत करना (baatchiit karnaa, more casual), सलाह-मशविरा करना (salaah-mashviraa karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase shows up constantly in news reports and official language -- 'सरकार ने विचार-विमर्श किया' (sarkaar ne vichaar-vimarsh kiyaa, 'the government deliberated') is a classic register you will hear on the evening news. In a family context, elders might say 'हमें आपस में विचार-विमर्श करना चाहिए' (hamen aapas men vichaar-vimarsh karnaa chaahie, 'we should discuss this among ourselves') before a big decision like a wedding or property matter, giving it a weighty, consultative feel rather than a light conversational one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this in casual settings where बातचीत करना (baatchiit karnaa) or बात करना (baat karnaa) would sound more natural -- विचार-विमर्श करना (vichaar-vimarsh karnaa) carries a formal, deliberative tone suited to meetings, committees, or serious family decisions, not everyday chit-chat. 2) The two halves विचार (vichaar) and विमर्श (vimarsh) are near-synonyms paired for emphasis (a common Hindi rhetorical device); learners sometimes try to use विमर्श (vimarsh) alone in casual speech, but it barely appears outside this fixed pairing or academic writing.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार ने नई नीति पर विचार-विमर्श किया। The government deliberated on the new policy.
(sarkaar ne naii niiti par vichaar-vimarsh kiyaa।)

हमें इस मामले में आपस में विचार-विमर्श करना चाहिए। We should deliberate among ourselves on this matter.
(hamen is maamle men aapas men vichaar-vimarsh karnaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
अधिकारी हर हफ्ते बैठक में विचार-विमर्श करते हैं। The officials deliberate in the meeting every week.
(adhikaarii har haphte baithak men vichaar-vimarsh karte hain।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
समिति इस प्रस्ताव पर विचार-विमर्श कर रही है। The committee is deliberating on this proposal.
(samiti is prastaav par vichaar-vimarsh kar rahii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
कृपया शादी की तारीख तय करने से पहले सबसे विचार-विमर्श कर लीजिए। Please consult with everyone before fixing the wedding date.
(kripyaa shaadii kii taariikh tay karne se pahle sabse vichaar-vimarsh kar liijie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
अगर उन्होंने पहले विचार-विमर्श किया होता, तो यह गलती न होती। If they had deliberated earlier, this mistake would not have happened.
(agar unhonne pahle vichaar-vimarsh kiyaa hotaa, to yah galtii n hotii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
इस विषय पर विशेषज्ञों के साथ विचार-विमर्श किया जाएगा। On this subject, deliberation will be done with experts.
(is vishay par visheshgyon ke saath vichaar-vimarsh kiyaa jaaegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive
क्या हम इस पर थोड़ा विचार-विमर्श कर सकते हैं? Could we discuss this a little?
(kyaa ham is par thodaa vichaar-vimarsh kar sakte hain?)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
बोर्ड की बैठक में सभी सदस्यों ने मिलकर इस योजना पर विचार-विमर्श किया। In the board meeting, all the members deliberated together on this plan.
(bord kii baithak men sabhii sadasyon ne milkar is yojnaa par vichaar-vimarsh kiyaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
परिवार में संपत्ति के बंटवारे को लेकर लंबा विचार-विमर्श हुआ। There was a long deliberation in the family over the division of property.
(parivaar men sampatti ke bantvaare ko lekar lambaa vichaar-vimarsh huaa।)
