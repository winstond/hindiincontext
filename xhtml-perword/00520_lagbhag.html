लगभग (lagbhag)

adverb A single-sense word meaning 'approximately, about' -- used before numbers, quantities, and time expressions to soften an exact figure.
Sense 1 : approximately, about
adverb Placed directly before the number or quantity it modifies: लगभग दस लोग (lagbhag das log, 'approximately ten people').
Synonyms: करीब (kariib), तकरीबन (takriiban), अंदाज़न (andaazan, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday estimates -- prices, distances, timings -- much like English 'about' or 'roughly.' In casual speech करीब (kariib) is at least as common as लगभग (lagbhag), and the two are freely interchangeable in this sense; लगभग (lagbhag) leans very slightly toward written or careful speech, but you will hear both from any Delhi auto-wallah giving you an estimated fare.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes place लगभग (lagbhag) after the number instead of before it, e.g. saying दस लगभग लोग (das lagbhag log) instead of लगभग दस लोग (lagbhag das log). 2) लगभग (lagbhag) is often confused with करीब (kariib) -- both mean 'about,' but करीब (kariib) also independently means 'near' as a postposition/adjective, while लगभग (lagbhag) only ever means 'approximately' and has no spatial sense.
