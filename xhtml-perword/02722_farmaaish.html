फ़रमाइश (farmaaish)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'request' or 'demand', usually for something specific like a song, a dish, or a favor -- it carries a slightly indulgent, affectionate flavor compared to a plain request.
Sense 1 : a request, especially for a favorite song, dish, or item
noun, feminine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) as फ़रमाइश करना (farmaaish karnaa, 'to make a request') and takes an object marked with की (kii), as in गाने की फ़रमाइश (gaane kii farmaaish, 'a request for a song').
Synonyms: गुज़ारिश (guzaarish), माँग (maang, more neutral, everyday 'demand/request'), विनती (vintii, more formal, closer to 'plea')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word has a warm, old-world Bollywood-radio flavor to it -- think of a listener writing in to a radio show with a फ़रमाइश (farmaaish) for their favorite song, or a guest at a dinner table making a फ़रमाइश (farmaaish) for a particular dish. It comes from Persian/Urdu and still carries that slightly courtly, affectionate politeness rather than sounding like a bald demand.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for माँग (maang) or request-loanword रिक्वेस्ट (rikvest) in contexts where फ़रमाइश (farmaaish) is the more natural, idiomatic choice -- especially for songs, food, or small indulgent favors. 2) The verb pairing is करना (karnaa), not देना (denaa) -- you 'make' a फ़रमाइश, you don't 'give' one.
