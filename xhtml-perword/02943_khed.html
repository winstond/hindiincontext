खेद (khed)

noun A formal noun meaning regret or sorrow, most often met in the fixed phrase मुझे खेद है (mujhe khed hai, 'I regret/I am sorry') used to formally apologize or express regret.
Sense 1 : regret, sorrow, remorse
noun, masculine Typically appears in the dative-subject frame X को खेद है (X ko khed hai, 'X regrets') rather than as a free-standing object of an action verb.
Synonyms: अफ़सोस (afsos), पश्चाताप (pashchaataap, deeper remorse), दुख (dukh, sorrow more broadly)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see खेद (khed) on official notices and announcements -- खेद है, ट्रेन रद्द कर दी गई है (khed hai, tren radd kar dii gaii hai, 'Regrettably, the train has been cancelled') -- where it lends a formal, almost bureaucratic tone that अफ़सोस (afsos) would not carry. In daily conversation among friends, अफ़सोस (afsos) is far more natural; save खेद (khed) for letters, apologies in writing, or public announcements.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अफ़सोस (afsos) in casual speech and खेद (khed) in writing or formal announcements -- using खेद (khed) in an everyday spoken apology can sound stiff, almost like a press release. 2) The verb here is है (hai), not किया (kiyaa) -- खेद is treated as a state one 'has,' not an action one 'does,' so learners sometimes wrongly say खेद किया (khed kiyaa) instead of खेद है (khed hai).
Sample Sentences:
खेद है, ट्रेन रद्द कर दी गई है। Regrettably, the train has been cancelled.
(khed hai, tren radd kar dii gaii hai।)
