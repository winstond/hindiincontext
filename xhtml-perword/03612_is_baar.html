इस बार (is baar)

adverb A common two-word phrase meaning 'this time' -- built from इस (is, the oblique form of यह, 'this') plus बार (baar, 'time/occasion'). One core sense, used constantly to mark a contrast with previous occasions.
Sense 1 : this time (on this occasion, as opposed to other occasions)
adverb Functions adverbially in a sentence to set up a contrast, often implicit, with a previous or future occasion: इस बार मैं नहीं जाऊँगा (is baar main nahiin jaaoongaa, 'this time I won't go') implies that other times the speaker did go.
Synonyms: इस दफ़ा (is dafaa, variant form), इस मर्तबा (is martbaa, less common variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in everyday chatter -- from a shopkeeper promising इस बार सही दाम दूँगा (is baar sahii daam doongaa, 'this time I'll give you the right price') to a parent sighing इस बार पास हो जाओ (is baar paas ho jaao, 'pass this time around'). It carries a built-in sense of history -- there's always an unspoken 'unlike before' hanging behind it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write यह बार (yah baar) instead of इस बार (is baar) -- because यह (yah) must switch to its oblique form इस (is) before बार (baar), just as it does before any noun it directly modifies. 2) बार (baar) here means 'occasion/instance,' not 'time' in the sense of duration or clock-time (समय, samay) -- learners occasionally reach for वक़्त (vaqt) or समय (samay) when they mean इस बार (is baar).

(is baar main jaroor jiitoongaa।)
