अतिशयोक्ति (atishyokti)

noun A formal/literary noun meaning 'exaggeration, hyperbole' -- used both as a rhetorical/literary term and in everyday speech to call out someone overstating something.
Sense 1 : exaggeration, hyperbole
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बढ़ा-चढ़ाकर कहना (badhaa-chadhaakar kahnaa, everyday phrase), अतिरंजना (atiranjnaa, rare, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word has a slightly bookish, essay-writing feel -- you will meet it in school Hindi classes discussing अलंकार (alankaar, 'figures of speech'), where अतिशयोक्ति (atishyokti) is named as one of the classic poetic devices (like saying a person's anger 'burned down mountains'). In casual daily conversation, people are just as likely to say बढ़ा-चढ़ाकर बोलना (badhaa-chadhaakar bolnaa) or ज़्यादा बोलना (zyaadaa bolnaa) instead, saving अतिशयोक्ति (atishyokti) for slightly more formal or written contexts, like calling out a politician's or ad's exaggerated claim.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) alone to mean 'exaggerate', but अतिशयोक्ति (atishyokti) itself is a noun ('an exaggeration/the act of exaggerating'), not a verb -- to use it in a sentence you typically need it with करना (karnaa), e.g. अतिशयोक्ति करना (atishyokti karnaa, 'to exaggerate'), or as a standalone label for someone's statement.
Sample Sentences:
यह तो अतिशयोक्ति है, इतना बड़ा दावा सच नहीं हो सकता। This is an exaggeration, such a big claim can't be true.
(yah to atishyokti hai, itnaa badaa daavaa sach nahiin ho saktaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(vigyaapan men kampnii ne apne utpaad ke baare men saaf atishyokti kii hai।)

(alankaar vaale paath men hamen atishyokti kii paribhaashaa aur udaahran yaad karne padte hain।)

(samiikshak ne likhaa ki lekhak kii bhaashaa men atishyokti kaa anaavashyak prayog huaa hai।)
