सीधा (siidhaa)

adjective/adverb A variable adjective covering 'straight,' 'direct,' and 'simple/plain,' with an important extended sense meaning 'honest/naive/guileless' when describing a person; also used as an adverb meaning 'directly/straight away.'
Sense 1 : straight (physically, not curved or crooked)
adjective
Synonyms: सीधा-सादा (siidhaa-saadaa, emphatic doubled form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a tailor telling you सीधी लाइन (siidhii laain, 'straight line') or a mother correcting a child's posture: सीधे बैठो (siidhe baitho, 'sit up straight'). This is the most physical, literal sense of the word.
Sample Sentences:
यह लाइन सीधी नहीं है, थोड़ी टेढ़ी है। This line isn't straight, it's a bit crooked.
(yah laain siidhii nahiin hai, thodii tedhii hai।)

Sense 2 : direct (route, path, or process, without detour)
adjective
Synonyms: प्रत्यक्ष (pratyaksh, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask for directions in Delhi and you will hear सीधे चले जाओ (siidhe chale jaao, 'go straight ahead') constantly -- this sense bridges naturally into the adverb sense of 'directly.'

Sense 4 : honest, naive, guileless (of a person's character)
adjective
Synonyms: भोला (bholaa, emphasizes naivety), मासूम (maasoom, innocent)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is where सीधा (siidhaa) gets its real color: the common phrase सीधा-सादा इंसान (siidhaa-saadaa insaan, 'a plain, guileless person') describes someone too trusting for their own good -- there's a whole cultural type here, the good-hearted person who gets taken advantage of at the sabzi mandi or in office politics.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense can carry a mildly pitying or patronizing edge -- सीधा आदमी (siidhaa aadmii, 'a simple/naive man') often implies someone easily fooled, not just an admirable honesty, which surprises learners expecting a purely positive compliment.

वह बहुत सीधा इंसान है, कभी किसी का बुरा नहीं सोचता। He is a very simple-hearted person, he never thinks ill of anyone.
(vah bahut siidhaa insaan hai, kabhii kisii kaa buraa nahiin sochtaa।)

Sense 5 : directly, straight (to a place), right away
adverb Functions adverbially in constructions like सीधा घर जाओ (siidhaa ghar jaao, 'go straight home'), where सीधा modifies the verb of motion rather than a noun.
Synonyms: तुरंत (turant, means 'immediately,' narrower time sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in travel contexts: सीधा दिल्ली जाना है (siidhaa dillii jaanaa hai, 'have to go straight to Delhi, no stops') -- it's the go-to word for 'no detours, no stopping.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes inflect this as सीधे (siidhe) unnecessarily or inconsistently; both सीधा (siidhaa) and सीधे (siidhe) are heard adverbially in casual speech, with सीधे being the more standard adverbial form.
Sample Sentences:
हमें सीधा दिल्ली जाना है, रास्ते में कहीं नहीं रुकना। We have to go straight to Delhi, no stopping anywhere on the way.
