आसन (aasan)

noun Primarily known through yoga as a seated posture or pose (aasan), with an older, less common sense of a seat or stool one sits on.
Sense 1 : yoga posture/pose
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense every learner meets first, usually attached to a specific pose name like पद्मासन (padmaasan, 'lotus pose') or शवासन (shavaasan, 'corpse pose') -- आसन (aasan) itself just means the posture, and most pose names are built by adding it as a suffix to another word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat आसन (aasan) as a verb meaning 'to do yoga' -- it is only the noun for the pose itself; the verb needed is करना (karnaa), as in आसन करना (aasan karnaa, 'to do a yoga pose').

(yog kakshaa men ham roz nae aasan siikhte hain।)

Sense 2 : seat, stool, small mat to sit on
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गद्दी (gaddii, cushioned seat), कुर्सी (kursii, chair)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This older sense survives in everyday religious and ritual settings -- the small mat or cloth a priest or guest of honor sits on is called an आसन (aasan), and you will still hear आसन बिछाना (aasan bichhaanaa, 'to lay out a seat/mat') before a puja or a formal sitting.
