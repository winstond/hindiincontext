बातूनी (baatoonii)

adjective A single-sense adjective (occasionally used as a noun for the person themselves) describing someone who talks a lot; related to बात (baat, talk/matter).
Sense 1 : talkative, chatty
adjective Can be used attributively before a noun (बातूनी आदमी (baatoonii aadmii, 'talkative man')) or predicatively, and can also stand alone as a noun meaning 'a talkative person' -- same word, same sense.
Synonyms: बकबक करने वाला (bakbak karne vaalaa, colloquial, descriptive phrase), मुँहफट (munhphat, blunt-spoken, not identical in meaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in Delhi households about that one rिश्तेदार (rishtedaar, relative) who calls and talks for an hour, or a child who won't stop chattering: बड़ी बातूनी है (badii baatoonii hai, 'she's such a chatterbox'). It pairs naturally with बात (baat, talk), बातचीत (baatchiit, conversation), and बकबक (bakbak, chatter/nonsense talk), all sharing the same root idea of 'talk.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat बातूनी (baatoonii) as purely negative, but it is often used affectionately or teasingly about a chatty friend or relative, not just as a criticism. 2) Because it ends in ई (ii), learners may expect it to inflect like a feminine noun ending or like a variable adjective (बातूना/बातूनी) -- it does not change for gender; the same form is used for men and women.
