भाजी (bhaajii)

noun A general word for cooked vegetables/greens, used both for the everyday dish sense and, in the compound पाव भाजी (paav bhaajii), as half of a beloved Mumbai-origin street food name.
Sense 1 : the mashed-vegetable dish served with bread in पाव भाजी
noun, feminine Almost always appears as part of the fixed compound पाव भाजी (paav bhaajii, 'bread rolls with spiced mashed vegetables'), rather than standing alone with this specific dish meaning.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पाव भाजी (paav bhaajii) started as Mumbai mill-worker street food and is now a pan-India favorite; order it at any Delhi food stall or dhaba and you will get a buttery mash of vegetables with soft pav (paav) rolls on the side.

Sense 2 : cooked vegetable dish, especially leafy greens
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सब्ज़ी (sabzii), साग (saag, leafy greens specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR kitchens you will hear सब्ज़ी (sabzii) far more often for 'the vegetable dish for dinner'; भाजी (bhaajii) has a more western-Indian ring to a north Indian ear, though everyone recognizes it, especially through the dish below.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat भाजी (bhaajii) and सब्ज़ी (sabzii) as regionally interchangeable everywhere, but भाजी (bhaajii) is heard far more in Maharashtra/western India, while Delhi-NCR speakers default to सब्ज़ी (sabzii) for the same everyday meaning.
