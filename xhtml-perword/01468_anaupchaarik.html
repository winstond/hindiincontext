अनौपचारिक (anaupchaarik)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'informal, casual' -- built from the negative prefix अ- (a) plus औपचारिक (aupchaarik, 'formal, ceremonial').
Sense 1 : informal, casual (not following formal rules or ceremony)
adjective
Synonyms: अनियमित (aniymit, irregular -- narrower, not a direct synonym), साधारण (saadhaaran, ordinary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in office and invitation contexts in Delhi-NCR -- an अनौपचारिक निमंत्रण (anaupchaarik nimantran, 'informal invitation') signals no need for a suit or gift-wrapped formality, just show up and relax. Its opposite, औपचारिक (aupchaarik, 'formal'), is worth learning alongside it since the two are constantly paired in real speech, as in official notices distinguishing formal versus informal settings.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this for people ('an informal person') when Hindi speakers more naturally reserve it for settings, meetings, language, or dress -- e.g. अनौपचारिक बातचीत (anaupchaarik baatchiit, 'informal conversation') rather than describing someone's personality. 2) Don't confuse with अनियमित (aniymit, 'irregular'), which sounds similar but means something different -- irregularity, not lack of formality.
Sample Sentences:
यह एक अनौपचारिक निमंत्रण है, तुम जींस में भी आ सकते हो। This is an informal invitation, you can even come in jeans.
(yah ek anaupchaarik nimantran hai, tum jiins men bhii aa sakte ho।)
