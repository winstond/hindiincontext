ध्यान (dhyaan)

noun Mainly means 'attention' in everyday speech, but also carries its classical sense of 'meditation/contemplation' -- the two are related (focused mind), and the word switches between them depending on context.
Sense 1 : attention, notice, care
noun, masculine Frequently used with रखना (rakhnaa, 'to keep/pay') and देना (denaa, 'to give') as light-verb pairings: ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa) and ध्यान देना (dhyaan denaa).
Synonyms: तवज्जो (tavajjo), खयाल (khayaal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear ध्यान से (dhyaan se, 'carefully') constantly in daily life -- from a mother telling a child ध्यान से चलो (dhyaan se chalo, 'walk carefully') to a driver being told ध्यान से चलाओ (dhyaan se chalaao, 'drive carefully'). It's one of the most useful adverbial phrases you'll pick up in India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use पर (par) or में (men) inconsistently -- the standard frame is X पर ध्यान देना (X par dhyaan denaa, 'to pay attention to X'), not X को ध्यान देना. 2) ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa) often means 'to take care of / look after' (e.g. बच्चों का ध्यान रखना, bachchon kaa dhyaan rakhnaa, 'to look after the children'), not just 'to pay attention' -- learners sometimes miss this caretaking sense.
Sample Sentences:
ध्यान से चलो, सड़क गीली है। Walk carefully, the road is wet.
(dhyaan se chalo, sadak giilii hai।)

Sense 2 : meditation, contemplation
noun, masculine Common in the fixed collocation ध्यान लगाना (dhyaan lagaanaa, 'to meditate, to focus the mind') and ध्यान करना (dhyaan karnaa, 'to meditate').
Synonyms: साधना (saadhnaa, literary/spiritual), मनन (manan, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same ध्यान (dhyaan) you'll see on yoga studio signs and hear in spiritual contexts -- it's the root of the Japanese word 'Zen,' having traveled through Chinese Chan from Sanskrit dhyaana. In Delhi you'll hear it used seriously in yoga/meditation classes and jokingly when someone tells a distracted friend, ध्यान लगाओ! (dhyaan lagaao, 'focus!').
Sample Sentences:
हर सुबह वह बीस मिनट ध्यान करता है। Every morning he meditates for twenty minutes.
(har subah vah biis minat dhyaan kartaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
गुरुजी अभी ध्यान में बैठे हैं, उन्हें मत जगाओ। The guru is sitting in meditation right now, don't wake him.
(gurujii abhii dhyaan men baithe hain, unhen mat jagaao।)
Register: Devotional -- Mood: Imperative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
आँखें बंद करो और ध्यान लगाओ। Close your eyes and meditate.
