लगाना (lagaanaa)

verb The transitive causative partner of लगना (lagnaa); core physical sense is 'to affix/apply/plant/set,' extending to 'to invest/apply (effort, money)' and a few fixed idiomatic frames like अंदाज़ा लगाना (andaazaa lagaanaa, 'to guess') and चिल्लाना-type shouting expressions.
Sense 1 : to apply, stick, affix (something to a surface)
verb, transitive Takes the pattern X ko Y par lagaanaa (X ko Y par lagaanaa) -- 'to put/apply X onto Y' -- e.g. cream on skin, a poster on a wall.
Synonyms: चिपकाना (chipkaanaa, for sticking/pasting), जमाना (jamaanaa, for setting in place)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common in everyday grooming and household talk: क्रीम लगाना (kriim lagaanaa, 'to apply cream'), मेहंदी लगाना (mehandii lagaanaa, 'to apply henna'), पोस्टर लगाना (postar lagaanaa, 'to put up a poster'). Picture a mother telling a child तेल लगाना (tel lagaanaa) before a head massage.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for रखना (rakhnaa) when they mean 'apply/stick,' but रखना (rakhnaa) is just 'to place' -- लगाना (lagaanaa) implies the thing now adheres to or is fixed onto the surface, not just resting on it.

Sense 3 : to invest or apply (effort, money, time) as an agent
verb, transitive The agent is the grammatical subject actively spending the resource: मेहनत लगाना (mehnat lagaanaa, 'to put in effort'), पैसा लगाना (paisaa lagaanaa, 'to invest money'). Contrast with लगना (lagnaa), where the resource itself is the subject and is described as being required, e.g. समय लगना (samay lagnaa, 'time is required') -- that construction belongs to लगना, not this word.
Synonyms: निवेश करना (nivesh karnaa, formal, for money specifically), खर्च करना (kharch karnaa, for spending money)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Common in business and effort-related talk: पैसा लगाना (paisaa lagaanaa) is what people say about investing in a shop or a stock, and पूरी मेहनत लगाना (poorii mehnat lagaanaa) is the classic encouragement before an exam.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to say 'it took me two hours' with लगाना (lagaanaa) and an agent subject; that meaning uses लगना (lagnaa) instead (मुझे दो घंटे लगे, mujhe do ghante lage, 'it took me two hours'), not this transitive verb.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 4 : to guess or estimate (in the fixed idiom अंदाज़ा लगाना)
verb, transitive Occurs almost exclusively in the fixed collocations अंदाज़ा लगाना (andaazaa lagaanaa) and अनुमान लगाना (anumaan lagaanaa), both meaning 'to make a guess/estimate.'
Synonyms: अनुमान लगाना (anumaan lagaanaa, more formal variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: ज़रा अंदाज़ा लगाओ (zaraa andaazaa lagaao, 'just take a guess') is a very natural conversational opener before sharing surprising news -- a staple of casual storytelling among friends.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा अंदाज़ा लगाओ, आज कौन आया था! Just take a guess, who came today!

Sense 5 : to let out, raise (a shout, cry, or similar vocal act)
verb, transitive Appears in fixed frames like चीख़ लगाना (chiikh lagaanaa, 'to let out a scream') and नारा लगाना (naaraa lagaanaa, 'to raise a slogan').
Synonyms: चिल्लाना (chillaanaa, plain verb 'to shout,' not a collocation with लगाना)

Cultural Note: नारा लगाना (naaraa lagaanaa) is the verb for chanting slogans at protests and rallies, a fixture of Indian political and campus life.
