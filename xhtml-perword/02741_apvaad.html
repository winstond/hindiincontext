अपवाद (apvaad)

noun A single-sense noun: 'exception' (to a rule or pattern). Used constantly in everyday explanatory speech, especially the phrase अपवाद है (apvaad hai).
Sense 1 : exception (to a rule)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear अपवाद (apvaad) constantly in classroom-style explanations: a teacher lays down a grammar rule and then says कुछ अपवाद भी हैं (kuchh apvaad bhii hain, 'there are also some exceptions'). It pairs naturally with नियम (niyam, 'rule') -- Hindi speakers often state the two together, नियम और अपवाद (niyam aur apvaad, 'rule and exception'), much like in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for यह ठीक नहीं है (yah thiik nahiin hai) to mean 'this doesn't count/apply' instead of the natural यह अपवाद है (yah apvaad hai, 'this is an exception'). 2) The common frame is X का अपवाद (X kaa apvaad, 'an exception to X') -- learners sometimes try to use में (men) instead of का (kaa) here, on the model of English 'exception in'.
Sample Sentences:
हर नियम के कुछ अपवाद होते हैं। Every rule has some exceptions.
(har niyam ke kuchh apvaad hote hain।)
