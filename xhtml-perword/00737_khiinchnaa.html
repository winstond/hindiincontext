खींचना (khiinchnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning 'to pull/drag' that extends naturally into related senses: drawing a line or picture, taking a photograph, and figuratively pulling attention or teasing someone.
Sense 1 : to pull, drag, tug
verb, transitive
Synonyms: घसीटना (ghasiitnaa, to drag along, often forcefully)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear from an auto-rickshaw driver telling you to pull the door shut, or a mother telling a child to stop pulling the cat's tail -- it is the default physical 'pull' verb in daily life, paired naturally with धकेलना (dhakelnaa, 'to push').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for घसीटना (ghasiitnaa) when खींचना (khiinchnaa) is more natural -- खींचना (khiinchnaa) is the everyday default for pulling a rope, door, or someone's sleeve, while घसीटना (ghasiitnaa) implies dragging something heavy or unwilling across a surface. 2) रस्सी खींचो (rassii khiincho, 'pull the rope') uses खींचना (khiinchnaa) even for a light tug; do not assume it always implies great effort.
Sample Sentences:
दरवाज़ा ठीक से खींचना, वरना खुल जाएगा। Pull the door properly, otherwise it will open.
(darvaazaa thiik se khiinchnaa, varnaa khul jaaegaa।)

Sense 2 : to take a photograph
verb, transitive Used in the fixed collocation फोटो खींचना (photo khiinchnaa, 'to take a photo') alongside the equally common फोटो लेना (photo lenaa).
Synonyms: फोटो लेना (photo lenaa, more literal, 'to take a photo')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense comes from old film cameras where you literally had to pull or wind a lever to advance the film -- so photographers still say कैमरा खींचना (kaimraa khiinchnaa) or फोटो खींचना (photo khiinchnaa) even though nobody winds film anymore.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes only know फोटो लेना (photo lenaa) and are surprised to hear फोटो खींचना (photo khiinchnaa) used just as often, especially by older speakers -- both are correct and interchangeable in this collocation.

Sense 3 : to draw (a line, picture, or diagram)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बनाना (banaanaa, to make/draw, broader sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a schoolteacher telling a student to draw a straight line in their notebook -- लाइन खींचो (laain khiincho, 'draw a line') -- the verb captures the physical motion of the hand dragging the pen or chalk across the page.
Common Mistakes: 1) Used specifically for lines, diagrams, or maps -- लाइन खींचना (laain khiinchnaa, 'to draw a line') and नक्शा खींचना (nakshaa khiinchnaa, 'to draw a map/plan') are the classic collocations; for a full artistic painting, बनाना (banaanaa) or चित्र बनाना (chitr banaanaa) is more common.
Sample Sentences:
अध्यापक ने बच्चों से कॉपी में एक सीधी लाइन खींचने को कहा। The teacher told the children to draw a straight line in their notebook.

Sense 4 : to attract or draw (attention, a crowd)
verb, transitive Common in the collocation ध्यान खींचना (dhyaan khiinchnaa, 'to draw attention').
Synonyms: आकर्षित करना (aakarshit karnaa, more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in advertising and everyday commentary alike -- इस विज्ञापन ने सबका ध्यान खींचा (is vigyaapan ne sabkaa dhyaan khiinchaa, 'this ad grabbed everyone's attention') -- treating attention as something physically pulled toward the source, much like English 'drew a crowd.'
Sample Sentences:
इस विज्ञापन ने सबका ध्यान खींचा। This ad grabbed everyone's attention.

Sense 5 : to tease, provoke, or wind someone up (figurative)
verb, transitive Often appears as किसी को खींचना (kisii ko khiinchnaa) in colloquial banter, close in feel to छेड़ना (chhednaa) but with a slight edge of provoking a reaction.
Synonyms: छेड़ना (chhednaa, more common for teasing)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Among friends you might hear यार, उसे मत खींच (yaar, use mat khiinch, 'dude, don't provoke him') -- the imagery is of pulling someone's strings to get a rise out of them.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative use is far less common than छेड़ना (chhednaa) for playful teasing -- learners should recognize it when they hear it but default to छेड़ना (chhednaa) in their own speech.
Sample Sentences:
यार, उसे मत खींच, वो सच में गुस्सा हो जाएगा। Dude, don't tease him, he'll actually get angry.
