पैदा (paidaa)

adjective An invariant word used almost exclusively inside compound expressions -- most importantly पैदा होना (paidaa honaa, 'to be born/to arise') and पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa, 'to produce/create'). It does not function as a free-standing adjective with independent meaning outside these frames.
Sense 1 : born, arisen (used with होना)
adjective Occurs almost only in the fixed pairing पैदा होना (paidaa honaa); the subject takes normal intransitive agreement, and पैदा itself never inflects.
Synonyms: जन्मा (janmaa, used mainly in formal or written contexts)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पैदा होना (paidaa honaa) is the everyday way Hindi speakers talk about birth -- you'll hear it in casual family conversation ('मैं दिल्ली में पैदा हुआ' (main dillii men paidaa huaa, 'I was born in Delhi')) far more often than the more formal जन्म लेना (janm lenaa). Note the perfective agreement pattern: होना (honaa) is intransitive, so the subject (not an object) controls verb agreement here.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect पैदा (paidaa) like a regular -आ adjective (e.g. पैदी, पैदे) to agree with gender/number -- it never changes form. 2) Confusing पैदा होना (paidaa honaa, 'to be born') with जन्म लेना (janm lenaa, 'to take birth') -- both are correct and common, but पैदा होना (paidaa honaa) is the more everyday, spoken choice, while जन्म लेना (janm lenaa) leans slightly more formal/written.

Sense 2 : produced, generated, brought about (used with करना)
adjective, transitive Occurs in the fixed transitive frame पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa, 'to produce/create/give rise to'), applied to crops, problems, feelings, children, and more.
Synonyms: उत्पन्न (utpann, more formal, used in technical or written contexts)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) is wonderfully versatile in everyday Hindi -- it's used for a farmer growing crops (फ़सल पैदा करना (fasal paidaa karnaa)), a person causing trouble (झगड़ा पैदा करना (jhagdaa paidaa karnaa)), or a situation generating interest (दिलचस्पी पैदा करना (dilchaspii paidaa karnaa)). Think of it as Hindi's general-purpose verb for 'bringing something into existence,' much broader than English 'to produce.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to बनाना (banaanaa, 'to make') where पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) is more natural -- e.g. मुश्किलें पैदा करना (mushkilen paidaa karnaa, 'to create difficulties') sounds native, while मुश्किलें बनाना (mushkilen banaanaa) sounds off. 2) Because करना (karnaa) is transitive, the ने-construction applies in the past tense: उसने समस्या पैदा की (usne samasyaa paidaa kii, 'he/she created a problem') -- learners sometimes forget agreement shifts to the object (समस्या, feminine) rather than the subject.
