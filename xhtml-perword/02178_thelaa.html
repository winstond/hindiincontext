ठेला (thelaa)

noun A handcart or pushcart, the wheeled cart used by street vendors (thelaa); it also names the vendor's stall/setup built on that cart.
Sense 1 : handcart / pushcart used by street vendors
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गाड़ी (gaadii, broader term), रेहड़ी (rehdii, regional variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Walk through any Delhi market or gali (galii) and you will see ठेले (thele) loaded with everything from फल (phal, 'fruit') to गोलगप्पे (golgappe, 'golgappas') -- the ठेला is the backbone of India's informal street food and produce economy. A ठेलेवाला (thelevaalaa) is the person who pushes and runs one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse ठेला (thelaa) with गाड़ी (gaadii) -- गाड़ी (gaadii) covers any vehicle from car to cart, while ठेला (thelaa) specifically means the hand-pushed or cycle-pulled vendor's cart, never a car or motor vehicle.

Sense 2 : the vendor's stall/setup as a small business (metonymic extension)
noun, masculine Used when referring to the cart as a place of business rather than the physical vehicle, e.g. 'ठेले पर मिलता है' (thele par miltaa hai, 'you get it at the cart-stall') focuses on location and goods sold, not on the wheeled object itself.
Synonyms: दुकान (dukaan, shop, broader term)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: When someone says खोमचे या ठेले वाला (khomchevaalaa / thelevaalaa), they are talking about a whole micro-business identity -- a chaat (chaat) ठेला or फल (phal) ठेला is a known fixture in the neighborhood, often parked at the same spot every day and locally famous.
