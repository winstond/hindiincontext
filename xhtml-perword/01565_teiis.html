तेईस (teiis)

numeral The number 23 (twenty-three), used for counting and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number twenty-three
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, तेईस (teiis) does not break down neatly for a learner the way English 'twenty-three' does -- native speakers just know it as a single memorized unit, so drilling the 21-99 block by rote (often via a printed numeral chart on a schoolroom wall) is still the standard approach in Indian classrooms.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build तेईस (teiis) compositionally from तीन (tiin, 'three') and बीस (biis, 'twenty') the way English says 'twenty-three' -- but Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are mostly historically fused and irregular, so each one (including तेईस (teiis)) is best learned as its own memorized word rather than derived on the fly.
