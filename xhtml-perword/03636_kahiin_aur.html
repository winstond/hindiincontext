कहीं और (kahiin aur)

adverb A compound adverb combining कहीं (kahiin, 'somewhere') with और (aur, 'else/more') to mean 'somewhere else, elsewhere.' It has one core meaning used across contexts.
Sense 1 : somewhere else, elsewhere
adverb
Synonyms: अन्यत्र (anyatr, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday requests and complaints, like चलो, कहीं और चलते हैं (chalo, kahiin aur chalte hain, 'come on, let's go somewhere else') when a place is too crowded or not to someone's liking. It pairs naturally with other कहीं-compounds like कहीं भी (kahiin bhii, 'anywhere at all') and कहीं नहीं (kahiin nahiin, 'nowhere'), all built on the same कहीं base.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say कोई और जगह (koii aur jagah) as a literal calque, which is understandable but wordier -- कहीं और (kahiin aur) is the natural, compact way to say 'somewhere else.' 2) Word order stays fixed as कहीं और (kahiin aur); reversing to और कहीं (aur kahiin) shifts the emphasis to 'anywhere else at all' (often in negative or questioning contexts) rather than a neutral 'somewhere else.'
Sample Sentences:
चलो, कहीं और चलते हैं, यहाँ बहुत भीड़ है। Come on, let's go somewhere else, it's very crowded here.
(chalo, kahiin aur chalte hain, yahaan bahut bhiid hai।)
