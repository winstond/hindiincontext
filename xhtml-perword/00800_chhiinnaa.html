छीनना (chhiinnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to snatch, grab, or seize' something from someone, usually by force; it also extends figuratively to taking away opportunities, rights, or abstract things like happiness or freedom.
Sense 1 : to snatch/grab something physically from someone
verb, transitive The person deprived is marked with से (se): किसी से कुछ छीनना (kisii se kuchh chhiinnaa, 'to snatch something from someone').
Synonyms: झपटना (jhapatnaa, quicker/more sudden), लूटना (lootnaa, to loot/rob)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a child's toy being grabbed by another kid at a park -- that sudden, forceful pull is exactly the image छीनना (chhiinnaa) evokes. It is a very physical, visceral verb, often used in news reports about theft or snatching (mobile-छीनना mobaail chhiinnaa cases are common tabloid fare in Delhi).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the person from whom something is taken with को (ko) instead of से (se) -- छीनना (chhiinnaa) takes से (se) for the source, unlike verbs like देना (denaa) which take को (ko) for the recipient. 2) Do not confuse with लेना (lenaa, 'to take'), which is neutral -- छीनना (chhiinnaa) always implies force or unwillingness on the other side.

Sense 2 : to take away something abstract (rights, freedom, happiness, opportunity) by force or unfairly
verb, transitive Common with abstract nouns: हक़ छीनना (haq chhiinnaa, 'to snatch away rights'), खुशी छीनना (khushii chhiinnaa, 'to take away happiness'), मौका छीनना (maukaa chhiinnaa, 'to snatch an opportunity').
Synonyms: वंचित करना (vanchit karnaa, more formal, 'to deprive')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This figurative extension shows up a lot in emotional or political speech -- a mother might say किसी ने मेरे बच्चे की मुस्कान छीन ली (kisii ne mere bachche kii muskaan chhiin lii, 'someone snatched away my child's smile'), and activists talk about हक़ छीनना (haq chhiinnaa) when demanding rights that were denied rather than given.
