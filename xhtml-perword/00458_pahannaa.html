पहनना (pahannaa)

verb One main sense: to put on or wear clothing, footwear, and accessories.
Sense 1 : to wear / put on (clothes, shoes, jewelry, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: धारण करना (dhaaran karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a normal transitive verb, so in the past tense it takes ने (ne) marking on the subject, e.g. उसने साड़ी पहनी (usne saadii pahnii, 'she wore a sari') -- a good one to drill early since clothing talk comes up constantly (getting ready for a wedding, deciding what to wear to college, etc.).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for धारण करना (dhaaran karnaa) in everyday speech because it looks like a direct 'wear' equivalent from dictionaries, but it sounds stiff and formal (used for things like धारण करना (dhaaran karnaa) a religious symbol or title) -- पहनना (pahannaa) is what people actually say for putting on clothes, shoes, or jewelry. 2) पहनना (pahannaa) covers clothes, shoes, glasses, jewelry, and even perfume-adjacent items like a garland, unlike English which sometimes splits 'wear' from 'put on' -- Hindi just uses one verb throughout.
