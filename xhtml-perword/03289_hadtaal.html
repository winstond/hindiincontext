हड़ताल (hadtaal)

noun A single-sense noun: a 'strike' -- workers, employees, or students collectively refusing to work in protest. Common in news and civic life.
Sense 1 : strike (labor/protest action)
noun, feminine Commonly used with verbs like करना (karnaa, 'to do/hold') for the act of striking, and पर जाना (par jaanaa, 'to go on') as in हड़ताल पर जाना (hadtaal par jaanaa, 'to go on strike').
Synonyms: आंदोलन (aandolan, broader: movement/agitation, not always a work-stoppage)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: हड़ताल (hadtaal) is everyday vocabulary in Indian civic life -- you'll see it in headlines about auto-rickshaw unions, government employees, or students, and it often overlaps with बंद (band), which refers more to a total shutdown of a city or market called by political groups, sometimes as a show of solidarity with a हड़ताल (hadtaal) elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say हड़ताल लेना (hadtaal lenaa) by analogy with English 'take a strike' -- the natural verbs are करना (karnaa) or पर जाना (par jaanaa), not लेना (lenaa). 2) हड़ताल is a strike specifically as a protest action; for a lightning strike or hitting something, unrelated words like बिजली (bijlii) or मार (maar) are used instead.
