गूँज (goonj)

noun A noun meaning 'echo' or 'resonance' (literal and figurative), with a closely related verb गूँजना (goonjnaa, 'to echo/resound') built from the same root.
Sense 1 : echo, reverberating sound
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a shout bouncing back off a hill or the walls of an empty stairwell -- that returning sound is गूँज (goonj). It pairs naturally with the verb गूँजना (goonjnaa), as in किसी चीज़ की गूँज सुनाई देना (kisii chiiz kii goonj sunaaii denaa, 'for the echo of something to be heard').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni) as the default word for 'echo', but in everyday speech गूँज (goonj) is what people actually say -- प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni) sounds bookish by comparison.

Sense 2 : figurative resonance, lingering impact or influence
noun, feminine Used for the lasting impression an event, speech, or piece of news leaves behind, e.g. उस भाषण की गूँज अब भी सुनाई देती है (us bhaashan kii goonj ab bhii sunaaii detii hai, 'the resonance of that speech is still felt').
Synonyms: असर (asar, effect), प्रभाव (prabhaav, impact)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: News anchors and writers love this word: a political statement, a controversy, or a piece of music can 'echo' (गूँज (goonj)) through public conversation for days. It carries a slightly dramatic, media-friendly flavor -- much like English 'reverberations' in a news headline.
