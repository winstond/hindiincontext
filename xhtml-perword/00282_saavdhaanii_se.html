सावधानी से (saavdhaanii se)

adverb An adverbial phrase meaning 'carefully' or 'cautiously', built from the noun सावधानी (saavdhaanii, 'caution, care') plus the instrumental postposition से (se).
Sense 1 : carefully, cautiously
adverb
Synonyms: ध्यान से (dhyaan se), होशियारी से (hoshiyaarii se), सतर्कता से (satarktaa se, slightly more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly from parents, bus conductors, and signboards alike: सावधानी से चलाएँ (saavdhaanii se chalaaen, 'drive carefully') is a classic road-safety phrase painted on the backs of trucks all over Delhi-NCR. It has that slightly official, cautionary ring, like a parent or a public notice reminding you to be careful, which is a bit more formal in tone than the everyday ध्यान से (dhyaan se).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop से (se) and just say सावधानी (saavdhaanii) as if it were the adverb itself; the से (se) is what turns the noun 'caution' into the adverbial 'carefully'. 2) It is easy to confuse with ध्यान से (dhyaan se) -- both translate as 'carefully,' but सावधानी से (saavdhaanii se) leans toward avoiding danger or mistakes (driving, handling something fragile), while ध्यान से (dhyaan se) leans toward attentiveness (listening, watching).
Sample Sentences:
सावधानी से चलाएँ। Drive carefully.
(saavdhaanii se chalaaen।)
