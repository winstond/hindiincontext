प्रदर्शन (pradarshan)

noun A Sanskrit-derived word with two main strands: a public protest/demonstration, and a performance/exhibition/display of something (an artist's performance, a product's performance, or a show of goods).
Sense 1 : protest, demonstration
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विरोध (virodh, often paired as 'विरोध-प्रदर्शन'), धरना (dharnaa, sit-in protest)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly on Indian news channels -- किसान प्रदर्शन (kisaan pradarshan, 'farmers' protest') or छात्र प्रदर्शन (chhaatr pradarshan, 'student protest') are everyday headlines. It's the standard formal/media word for a protest, more neutral and journalistic than धरना (dharnaa), which specifically evokes people sitting down at one spot.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume प्रदर्शन (pradarshan) always means a peaceful/organized protest like a march; in news Hindi it covers everything from a small sit-in to a large rally. 2) The common collocation is प्रदर्शन करना (pradarshan karnaa, 'to hold/stage a protest') -- not प्रदर्शन होना used for the organizers' action; होना is used when describing that a protest 'took place.'

Sense 2 : performance (of a person, in a role or activity)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अभिनय (abhinay, specifically acting performance), खेल (khel, in a sports context, informal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in cricket commentary -- टीम का प्रदर्शन (tiim kaa pradarshan, 'the team's performance') is a staple phrase during match analysis, and it extends naturally to students' exam results or an employee's work review.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for परफॉर्मेंस as a loanword, but प्रदर्शन (pradarshan) is the standard native word used in formal writing, sports commentary, and reviews for how well someone did. 2) Note the construction अच्छा प्रदर्शन करना (achchhaa pradarshan karnaa, 'to perform well') -- the adjective modifies प्रदर्शन, not a separate adverb form.

Sense 3 : exhibition, display, showing (of an object, product, or show)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रदर्शनी (pradarshnii, the noun for a formal exhibition/expo, related but distinct), दिखावा (dikhaavaa, often with a negative 'showing off' connotation)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in phrases like फिल्म का प्रदर्शन (philm kaa pradarshan, 'the screening/release of a film') or वस्तुओं का प्रदर्शन (vastuon kaa pradarshan, 'display of goods'), linking back to the root idea of दर्शन (darshan, 'sight, viewing') -- all three senses of this word trace back to that core idea of something being shown or seen.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse प्रदर्शन (pradarshan) with प्रदर्शनी (pradarshnii) -- the latter specifically means 'an exhibition' (the event/venue), while प्रदर्शन is the act or instance of displaying/showing something.
