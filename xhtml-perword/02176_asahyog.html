असहयोग (asahyog)

noun A formal noun meaning 'non-cooperation' -- built from अ- (a-, 'not') + सहयोग (sahyog, 'cooperation'). Best known to learners through its history-textbook fame as the Non-Cooperation Movement, but it is also used in plain modern speech about refusing to cooperate.
Sense 1 : non-cooperation, refusal to cooperate
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विरोध (virodh, opposition/resistance, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Every Indian student meets this word first in history class as part of असहयोग आंदोलन (asahyog aandolan, 'Non-Cooperation Movement'), Gandhi's 1920-22 campaign of civil resistance. Because of that association, the word carries a slightly formal, almost civic-institutional flavor even outside the historical context -- you will hear it in news reports about unions or officials 'not cooperating,' but rarely in casual chat about friends or family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use असहयोग (asahyog) loosely for any 'disagreement,' but it specifically means withholding cooperation or refusing to participate/help, not just disagreeing in opinion. 2) It is a fairly formal/written-register noun -- in casual speech, Hindi speakers are more likely to say साथ नहीं देना (saath nahiin denaa, 'to not support/cooperate') than to reach for असहयोग (asahyog).
