कुशलपूर्वक (kushalpoorvak)

adverb An adverb meaning 'skillfully' or 'competently' -- describes the manner in which an action is done, with the sense of proficiency or expertise.
Sense 1 : skillfully, competently
adverb
Synonyms: निपुणता से (nipuntaa se), दक्षता से (dakshtaa se), होशियारी से (hoshiyaarii se, everyday)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word is built from कुशल (kushal, 'skilled/capable') plus the formal adverb-forming suffix पूर्वक (poorvak, 'in a manner of'), a pattern shared by words like सावधानीपूर्वक (saavdhaaniipoorvak, 'carefully'). You will hear it in a boss's performance review or a news report about a rescue operation, but rarely at the dinner table -- there, अच्छे से (achchhe se) or ठीक से (thiik se) does the same job more naturally.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech where होशियारी से (hoshiyaarii se) or अच्छे से (achchhe se) would sound far more natural -- कुशलपूर्वक (kushalpoorvak) belongs mainly to formal, written, or professional register (reports, speeches, official praise) rather than everyday conversation.

उसने अपना काम कुशलपूर्वक पूरा किया। He completed his work skillfully.
(usne apnaa kaam kushalpoorvak pooraa kiyaa।)
