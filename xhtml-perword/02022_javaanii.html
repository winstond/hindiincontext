जवानी (javaanii)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'youth' or 'the prime of life' -- the vigorous, physically strong phase of adulthood, distinct from बचपन (bachpan, childhood) and बुढ़ापा (budhaapaa, old age).
Sense 1 : youth (the life-stage of young adulthood)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: यौवन (yauvan, literary), जवानी का दौर (javaanii kaa daur, the phase of youth)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: जवानी (javaanii) carries a strong charge of physical vigor and passion in everyday speech -- elders often warn the young with phrases like जवानी का जोश (javaanii kaa josh, 'the heat/impulsiveness of youth'), implying energy that can outrun good sense. It is closely tied to जवान (javaan, 'young person, soldier'), from which it derives.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बचपन (bachpan) when they mean the young-adult years -- बचपन (bachpan) is specifically childhood, while जवानी (javaanii) covers the teens through the thirties, the peak of physical strength and vitality. 2) जवानी (javaanii) is not really used for 'youthfulness' as an abstract quality of appearance in the way English uses 'youth' -- for that sense, speakers often lean on जवां (javaan) or ताज़गी (taazgii) instead.

Sense 2 : prime, peak vigor (figurative extension)
noun, feminine Used figuratively for the peak/prime of something beyond human life, e.g. a season or a movement, though this extension is less common than the literal sense.
Synonyms: उमंग (umang, enthusiasm, partial overlap), जोश (josh, zeal/energy, partial overlap)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll sometimes hear जवानी (javaanii) used poetically for the 'prime' of a river in spate or a storm at its height -- an extension of the human sense of vitality onto nature, common in Hindi film lyrics and shayari.
