विदा (vidaa)

verb/noun One core idea, 'farewell/departure,' expressed mainly through two set phrases: विदा होना (vidaa honaa, to leave/depart) and विदा करना (vidaa karnaa, to see someone off), plus its use as a standalone noun meaning 'farewell.'
Sense 1 : to see someone off, bid farewell to
verb, transitive Compound with करना (karnaa); takes a direct object for the person being sent off, e.g. उसे विदा करना (use vidaa karnaa, 'to see him off').
Synonyms: छोड़ने जाना (chhodne jaanaa, 'to go to drop off', narrower -- physical send-off only)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb used at railway platforms and airports across North India for that lingering goodbye -- and centrally, for the moment a bride's family विदा करते हैं (vidaa karte hain, 'see off') the bride to her new home, one of the most emotional scenes in a Hindu wedding.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for छोड़ना (chhodnaa) alone to mean 'see off,' but विदा करना (vidaa karnaa) is the natural set phrase for a farewell send-off at a station, airport, or wedding.

Sense 2 : farewell, leave-taking, departure
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अलविदा (alvidaa, used as an interjection more than a noun), प्रस्थान (prasthaan, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: विदा (vidaa) carries real emotional weight in Hindi -- it's the word used for the tearful send-off of a bride leaving her parental home after the wedding (vidaaii, vidaaii, the noun for that specific ceremony is closely related). Even outside weddings, विदा होना (vidaa honaa) has a slightly wistful, formal flavor compared to a casual 'goodbye.'
Common Mistakes: 1) विदा (vidaa) itself is not used as a parting greeting like 'goodbye' -- for that, learners want अलविदा (alvidaa) or फिर मिलेंगे (phir milenge, 'we'll meet again'). 2) It almost always appears bound to होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) rather than standing alone as a bare object of another verb.

Sense 3 : to depart, take one's leave (of someone)
verb, intransitive Functions as a compound with होना (honaa): subject + से (se) marking the person taken leave of, e.g. विदा होना (vidaa honaa).
Synonyms: रवाना होना (ravaanaa honaa), चला जाना (chalaa jaanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense pairs with विदा करना (vidaa karnaa) below the way a door has two sides: one person is विदा होना (vidaa honaa, the one leaving), the other is विदा करना (vidaa karnaa, the one seeing them off).
Common Mistakes: 1) The person being left is marked with से (se), not को (ko) -- मैं उससे विदा हुआ (main usse vidaa huaa, 'I took leave of him'), not उसे (use).
