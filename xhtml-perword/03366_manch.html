मंच (manch)

noun Primarily a physical or figurative 'stage/platform' word: from a raised stage for performances to an organizational 'platform' or 'forum' for a cause, and by extension a digital platform. Inline Hindi as Devanagari (placeholder).
Sense 1 : stage, dais
noun, masculine
Synonyms: स्टेज (stej, common in spoken Hindi), व्यासपीठ (vyaaspiith, formal/literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of any Indian school's annual function or a neta's (leader's) rally: the raised platform where speeches happen and prizes are given out is मंच (manch). The person who introduces speakers and keeps the event running is called the मंच संचालक (manch sanchaalak, 'stage host/anchor'), a role you'll hear announced constantly at Indian public events.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use स्टेज (stej) and मंच (manch) interchangeably without noticing that मंच (manch) sounds slightly more formal or literary, fitting for a school function, political rally, or awards ceremony, while स्टेज (stej) is the casual everyday choice.

Sense 2 : platform, forum (organizational or ideological)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: फ़ोरम (foram), संगठन (sangthan, organization, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this in the names of civic and political groups, like किसान मंच (kisaan manch, 'Farmers' Forum') -- मंच (manch) here means a shared platform where people with a common cause come together, much like 'forum' or 'front' in English political vocabulary.

Sense 3 : platform (digital/media, extended modern usage)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्लेटफ़ॉर्म (pletform, more common in casual speech)

Cultural Note: In recent years मंच (manch) has picked up a tech-flavored sense, used in phrases like डिजिटल मंच (dijital manch, 'digital platform') in news broadcasts and government messaging, though everyday speakers often just borrow English platform directly.
