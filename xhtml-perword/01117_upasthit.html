उपस्थित (upasthit)

adjective A formal/administrative word meaning 'present' (physically there, as at a roll call or meeting), and by extension 'occurring, arising' in a more literary sense.
Sense 1 : present (physically, at a place/event)
adjective
Synonyms: मौजूद (maujood)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word stamped on every Indian school and office attendance register -- teachers call रोल नंबर (rol nambar, roll number) and students answer उपस्थित (upasthit) instead of a plain 'yes' or 'here,' much like 'present, sir/ma'am' in English classrooms.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मौजूद (maujood) in casual speech and उपस्थित (upasthit) in formal/written contexts (attendance registers, official reports, school circulars) -- both mean 'present,' but उपस्थित (upasthit) sounds stiffer if used in everyday conversation, like saying 'in attendance' instead of 'here.' 2) It pairs with होना (honaa) -- उपस्थित होना (upasthit honaa, to be present) -- not with रहना (rahnaa) or आना (aanaa) in the way learners might expect from English 'to attend.'

Sense 2 : arisen, occurring (of a situation, problem, opportunity)
adjective Common in the phrase समस्या उपस्थित होना (samasyaa upasthit honaa, for a problem to arise), where उपस्थित (upasthit) extends the 'presence' idea from people to abstract situations.
Synonyms: पैदा (paidaa), उत्पन्न (utpann, more literary)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense most often in news reports or government documents -- 'ऐसी स्थिति उपस्थित हो गई' (aisii sthiti upasthit ho gaii, 'such a situation has arisen') is classic officialese, the kind of line that shows up in a memo rather than a conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is confined to formal, written, or bureaucratic Hindi (reports, news, official notices) -- using it in casual talk about a problem 'coming up' sounds bookish; स्थिति आ गई (sthiti aa gaii, the situation has come about) is what most people would actually say out loud.

(aisii sthiti upasthit ho gaii ki adhikaariyon ko turant baithak bulaanii padii।)
