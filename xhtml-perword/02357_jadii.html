जड़ी (jadii)

noun/verb Mainly a noun meaning 'herb/medicinal root,' most often met in the fixed pair जड़ी-बूटी (jadii-bootii, 'herbs and roots, medicinal plants'); also occurs as the feminine perfective participle of जड़ना (jadnaa, 'to embed/set, e.g. jewels').
Sense 1 : medicinal herb or root
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बूटी (bootii), औषधि (aushdhi, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the wandering baba or hakim in old Hindi films who cures ailments with जड़ी-बूटी (jadii-bootii) from the forest -- this compound carries a strong flavor of traditional, folk, and Ayurvedic medicine. Related to जड़ (jad, 'root'), since these plants are valued for their roots.
Common Mistakes: 1) Almost always heard in the compound जड़ी-बूटी (jadii-bootii, 'herbs and roots'); using जड़ी (jadii) alone sounds oddly bare to native ears outside of specific botanical or medicinal contexts. 2) Don't confuse with जड़ (jad, 'root' in the plain botanical/foundational sense) -- जड़ी (jadii) specifically implies a root or plant used for its medicinal properties.

Sense 2 : set, embedded, studded (perfective participle)
verb, transitive This is the feminine singular perfective participle of जड़ना (jadnaa, 'to embed, set, stud'), used adjectivally, as in हीरे जड़ी अंगूठी (hiire jadii angoothii, 'a ring studded with diamonds').
Synonyms: लगी (lagii, in the sense of 'fitted/attached')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Very common in describing jewelry and ornamentation -- सोना जड़ी (sonaa jadii, 'set with gold') or मोती जड़ी (motii jadii, 'studded with pearls') are phrases you will hear at any jeweler's shop in Delhi's Chandni Chowk describing wedding jewelry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat जड़ी (jadii) here as the noun 'herb' rather than recognizing it as a participle of जड़ना (jadnaa); context (usually following a material or gem, and preceding a feminine noun) disambiguates. 2) Agreement is with the noun being described, not with any implied subject -- सोने जड़ी चूड़ियाँ (sone jadii choodiyaan, 'bangles set with gold') shows plural agreement pulling it to जड़ी (jadii) or जड़ीं (jadiin) depending on register.
