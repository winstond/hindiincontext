इलाज करना (ilaaj karnaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to treat (medically)' or 'to cure' -- combines the noun इलाज (ilaaj, 'treatment/cure', from Arabic-Persian) with करना (karnaa, 'to do').
Sense 1 : to treat medically, to give treatment to
verb, transitive Takes का (kaa) before the thing being treated: बीमारी का इलाज करना (biimaarii kaa ilaaj karnaa, 'to treat the illness'); the person is often marked with का (kaa) as well, e.g. मरीज़ का इलाज करना (mariiz kaa ilaaj karnaa, 'to treat the patient').
Synonyms: उपचार करना (upchaar karnaa, formal), चिकित्सा करना (chikitsaa karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday phrase you'll hear at any clinic or in family conversations about health -- डॉक्टर साहब, ज़रा इलाज कर दीजिए (doktar saahab, zaraa ilaaj kar diijie, 'Doctor sahib, please treat this') is a common request. Note the related but distinct इलाज होना (ilaaj honaa), used when talking about treatment being possible or undergone (इसका कोई इलाज नहीं है, iskaa koii ilaaj nahiin hai, 'there's no cure for this').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to mark the patient with को (ko) as a direct object, but इलाज करना (ilaaj karnaa) treats इलाज (ilaaj) itself as the object, with the patient or ailment linked via का/की/के (kaa); so it's डॉक्टर मरीज़ का इलाज करता है (doktar mariiz kaa ilaaj kartaa hai, 'the doctor treats the patient'), not डॉक्टर मरीज़ को इलाज करता है. 2) Confusing इलाज करना (ilaaj karnaa, to administer treatment) with इलाज होना (ilaaj honaa, for treatment to happen/be available), which shifts the grammatical subject to इलाज (ilaaj) itself.
Sample Sentences:
डॉक्टर साहब, ज़रा इलाज कर दीजिए। Doctor sahib, please treat this.
(doktar saahab, zaraa ilaaj kar diijie।)
