ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedaar)

adjective/noun One core meaning: 'responsible' -- covering both the personality trait (a responsible person) and the accountable-for-something relationship, plus its common noun use for 'the person responsible/in charge'.
Sense 1 : responsible (character trait)
adjective
Synonyms: समझदार (samajhdaar, sensible/mature), भरोसेमंद (bharosemand, trustworthy)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in family contexts: a parent praising a son or daughter as ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedaar) is high praise in Indian households, often tied to taking care of siblings, finances, or aging parents -- it carries a slightly heavier moral weight than the breezier English 'responsible.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for समझदार (samajhdaar) when they mean 'responsible' in this sense -- समझदार (samajhdaar) leans more toward 'sensible/mature in judgment' rather than 'dutiful and accountable.' 2) To say 'responsible for X,' use की ज़िम्मेदारी (kii zimmedaarii) as a noun construction (X की ज़िम्मेदारी है, 'X is the responsibility') rather than trying to force ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedaar) directly onto the object with a postposition.

मेरी बड़ी बेटी बहुत ज़िम्मेदार है। My eldest daughter is very responsible.
(merii badii betii bahut zimmedaar hai।)

Sense 2 : responsible for / accountable (in a specific situation)
adjective Used with के लिए (ke lie) to mark what someone is responsible for: इस गलती के लिए वह ज़िम्मेदार है (is galtii ke lie vah zimmedaar hai, 'he is responsible for this mistake').
Synonyms: दोषी (doshii, guilty/at fault), कारण (kaaran, cause of)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you will hear on the news constantly -- कौन ज़िम्मेदार है? (kaun zimmedaar hai?, 'who is responsible?') is the go-to phrase after any accident, scam, or civic failure, often followed by a demand for जवाबदेही (javaabdehii, 'accountability').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use को (ko) instead of के लिए (ke lie) to mark the thing one is responsible for -- के लिए (ke lie) is the natural choice here, not को (ko). 2) In blame contexts, दोषी (doshii) is often more natural than ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedaar) when the tone is accusatory rather than neutral-factual.
Sample Sentences:
इस हादसे के लिए कौन ज़िम्मेदार है? Who is responsible for this accident?
(is haadse ke lie kaun zimmedaar hai?)

Sense 3 : the responsible person / person in charge
noun, masculine Functions as a noun when used with an article-like determiner or standing alone as 'the responsible one,' e.g. यहाँ का ज़िम्मेदार कौन है (yahaan kaa zimmedaar kaun hai, 'who is in charge here').
Synonyms: प्रभारी (prabhaarii, formal, 'in-charge'), मुखिया (mukhiyaa, head/chief)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In offices and shops, asking यहाँ का ज़िम्मेदार कौन है? (yahaan kaa zimmedaar kaun hai?, 'who's in charge here?') is a common, slightly formal way to ask for the person who can actually make a decision -- the manager or owner, not just any employee.
