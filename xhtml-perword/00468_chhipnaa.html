छिपना (chhipnaa)

verb A single core sense: to hide or be hidden, used both literally (people, objects) and figuratively (feelings, truths). Closely related to its causative छिपाना (chhipaanaa, 'to hide something'), which is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to hide oneself, be concealed
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: छिप जाना (chhip jaanaa, compound form, common), दुबकना (dubaknaa, to cower/hide by crouching), गुम होना (gum honaa, to disappear from sight)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of children playing छुपन-छुपाई (chhupan-chhupaaii, 'hide and seek') -- छिपना (chhipnaa) is exactly the 'hide' half of that game, the act of making yourself unseen. It extends naturally to emotions and truths: बात छिप नहीं सकती (baat chhip nahiin saktii, 'the matter cannot stay hidden') is a very common way to say the truth always comes out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छिपाना (chhipaanaa) when they mean छिपना (chhipnaa) -- छिपना (chhipnaa) is what the subject itself does (intransitive, no object), while छिपाना (chhipaanaa) is what you do to something else (transitive, needs an object). 2) In the perfective, since छिपना (chhipnaa) is intransitive, it agrees with the subject and takes no ने (ne): वह छिपा (vah chhipaa, 'he hid'), not वह ने छिपा।

Sense 2 : to be kept secret, remain undisclosed (of facts, feelings, truths)
verb, intransitive This sense governs abstract subjects like बात (baat, 'matter'), राज़ (raaz, 'secret'), or दर्द (dard, 'pain') rather than physical people or objects.
Synonyms: गुप्त रहना (gupt rahnaa, more formal/written), ढका रहना (dhakaa rahnaa, to stay covered up)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the figurative cousin of the literal 'hiding' sense -- just as a person tucks themselves out of sight, a secret or feeling is imagined as tucking itself away from others' eyes. Elders often warn कुछ छिपा नहीं रहता (kuchh chhipaa nahiin rahtaa, 'nothing stays hidden') as a moral about honesty.
