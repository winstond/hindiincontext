पलटाना (paltaanaa)

verb The causative of पलटना (palatnaa, 'to turn/flip over, to return'), meaning 'to make something turn over/flip' or 'to have something turned/returned'.
Sense 1 : to cause to turn over, flip, or reverse (something)
verb, transitive Causative of पलटना (palatnaa); takes a direct object that undergoes the flipping/turning, e.g. पेज़ पलटाना (pez paltaanaa, 'to have the page turned').
Synonyms: उलटना (ulatnaa), मोड़ना (modnaa, to bend/turn, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is one of a family of causatives built from पलटना (palatnaa); think of it alongside verbs like बनना/बनाना (bannaa/banaanaa) or कटना/कटाना (katnaa/kataanaa) -- the -आना (-aanaa) suffix regularly signals 'get someone else to do X' or 'cause X to happen indirectly.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use पलटना (palatnaa) when they mean to make someone else flip or turn something -- पलटना (palatnaa) is what the thing itself does or what you do directly, while पलटाना (paltaanaa) adds the sense of getting/causing another person or agent to do the turning. 2) Because it is a causative, the person doing the actual flipping is often marked with से (se), not को (ko), e.g. उससे किताब का पन्ना पलटाना (usse kitaab kaa pannaa paltaanaa, 'to have him turn the page of the book').

Sense 2 : to get (money, a debt, favor) returned or repaid
verb, transitive Used especially with money or favors: पैसे पलटाना (paise paltaanaa, 'to get the money returned/repaid') implies causing or arranging for repayment, often through pressure or reminder.
Synonyms: वापस दिलाना (vaapas dilaanaa, more explicit phrase), लौटाना (lautaanaa, to give back, focuses on the returner's action)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in everyday money talk -- a shopkeeper or friend saying उधार पलटाना है (udhaar paltaanaa hai, 'the loan needs to be gotten back') captures the mild insistence Hindi speakers often build into talk about debts among friends and family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with लौटाना (lautaanaa): लौटाना (lautaanaa) is used from the giver's perspective ('I am returning it'), while पलटाना (paltaanaa) more often implies getting someone else to hand something back to you.
