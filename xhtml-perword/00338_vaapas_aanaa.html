वापस आना (vaapas aanaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to come back, return' -- वापस (vaapas) is an adverb meaning 'back' and आना (aanaa) is the ordinary verb 'to come'; together they form the everyday phrase for returning somewhere.
Sense 1 : to return, come back (physically)
verb, intransitive वापस (vaapas) functions as an adverbial modifier before आना (aanaa); the two words are written separately but learned as one functional unit.
Synonyms: लौटना (lautnaa), लौटकर आना (lautkar aanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the most common ways to say 'I'll be right back' in daily life -- मैं अभी वापस आता हूँ (main abhii vaapas aataa hoon, 'I'll come back right now') is heard constantly when someone steps out of a shop, office, or gathering for a moment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to conjugate वापस (vaapas) itself -- it never changes form; only आना (aanaa) inflects for tense, gender, and number. 2) Do not confuse with वापस देना (vaapas denaa, 'to give back'), which uses the same वापस (vaapas) but a different main verb for a completely different meaning (returning an object, not oneself).
Sample Sentences:

(main abhii vaapas aataa hoon।)

Sense 2 : to come back to normal, recover, return to a previous state (figurative)
verb, intransitive Used with abstract subjects like तबीयत (tabiiyat, 'health'), हालात (haalaat, 'situation'), or होश (hosh, 'senses/consciousness') to describe returning to a normal or prior condition.
Synonyms: पहले जैसा हो जाना (pahle jaisaa ho jaanaa, descriptive phrase, not a single-word synonym)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this when someone recovers from illness or shock -- होश में वापस आना (hosh men vaapas aanaa, 'to come back to one's senses') is a common way to describe someone regaining consciousness or composure after a jolt.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often only learn the physical 'come back' sense and miss that यह अपनी हालत पर वापस आ गया (yah apnii haalat par vaapas aa gayaa, 'it came back to its normal state') uses the same verb figuratively for recovery or restoration.
