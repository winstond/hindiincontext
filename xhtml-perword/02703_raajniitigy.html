राजनीतिज्ञ (raajniitigy)

noun A formal, single-sense word meaning 'politician' or 'statesman' -- a compound of राज (raaj, rule) + नीति (niiti, policy) + ज्ञ (gy, one who knows).
Sense 1 : politician, statesman
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नेता (netaa, more common in everyday speech), सियासतदान (siyaasatdaan, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up in newspapers, textbooks, and formal speeches, but around the dinner table or in casual chat, people almost always say नेता (netaa) instead -- if you say a friend's uncle is a राजनीतिज्ञ (raajniitigy), it sounds oddly formal, like calling him 'a statesman' rather than just 'a politician.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word by default because it looks like a direct match for 'politician,' but in everyday speech नेता (netaa) is far more common; राजनीतिज्ञ (raajniitigy) sounds bookish or like something from a news report. 2) It carries a more respectful, statesman-like tone than नेता (netaa), which can be neutral or even mildly negative depending on context.
