एक एक करके (ek ek karke)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'one by one, one at a time' -- built from एक (ek, 'one') repeated and करके (karke, the conjunctive-participle form of करना (karnaa, 'to do')).
Sense 1 : one by one, sequentially
adverb Functions as an adverbial phrase modifying the main verb, describing the manner in which an action is carried out across multiple items or people in sequence.
Synonyms: एक-एक करके (ek-ek karke, variant form with hyphen), बारी-बारी से (baarii-baarii se, taking turns)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday instructions -- a mother telling kids एक एक करके अंदर आओ (ek ek karke andar aao, 'come inside one by one') at a crowded doorway, or a teacher calling students up one at a time. The repetition of एक (ek) itself does the work of expressing 'each individually,' a common Hindi pattern where doubling a word adds a distributive sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect or conjugate करके (karke) as if it were a full verb form -- it stays frozen in this participial shape regardless of the subject or tense of the main verb. 2) Do not confuse this with एक बार (ek baar, 'once'), which is about frequency, not sequence.

(bachchon, ek ek karke andar aao।)
