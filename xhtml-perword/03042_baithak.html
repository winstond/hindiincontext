बैठक (baithak)

noun Primarily a noun meaning either a formal meeting/session or a sitting room/living room in a house; both senses come from the verb बैठना (baithnaa, 'to sit').
Sense 1 : meeting, session
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सभा (sabhaa, formal), मीटिंग (miiting)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly in Indian news reports -- संसद की बैठक (sansad kii baithak, 'parliament session'), आपात बैठक (aapaat baithak, 'emergency meeting') -- it's the standard bureaucratic and political term for a meeting, more common in that register than मीटिंग (miiting).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मीटिंग (miiting) by default; बैठक (baithak) is the word you'll actually hear in government, office, and society contexts in India, e.g. कैबिनेट की बैठक (kaibinet kii baithak, 'cabinet meeting'). 2) The verb pairing is होना (honaa) or बुलाना (bulaanaa) -- बैठक होती है (baithak hotii hai, 'a meeting takes place'), बैठक बुलाना (baithak bulaanaa, 'to call a meeting') -- not करना (karnaa).
Sample Sentences:
आज शाम को मंत्रियों की बैठक है। There is a ministers' meeting this evening.
(aaj shaam ko mantriyon kii baithak hai।)

Sense 2 : sitting room, drawing room, living room
noun, feminine
Synonyms: ड्राइंग रूम (draaing room), दीवान-खाना (diivaan-khaanaa, somewhat dated)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In many North Indian homes, especially older houses, the बैठक (baithak) is the front room reserved for guests -- separate from the family's more private inner rooms -- reflecting a traditional layout where hospitality and privacy occupy distinct spaces.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse with बेडरूम (bedroom) or रसोई (rasoii, 'kitchen') -- बैठक (baithak) specifically means the room where guests are received and the family sits together, closer to a living/drawing room than a bedroom.
