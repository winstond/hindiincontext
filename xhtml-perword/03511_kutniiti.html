कुटनीति (kutniiti)

noun The standard spelling is कूटनीति (kootniiti) with a long ऊ; कुटनीति (kutniiti) is a common short-vowel variant/misspelling you will see written but should recognize as the same word, meaning 'diplomacy' or 'strategic/crafty dealing.'
Sense 1 : diplomacy, statecraft
noun, feminine
Synonyms: राजनय (raajnay, formal), विदेश नीति (videsh niiti, foreign policy, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word constantly in newspaper headlines about India's relations with Pakistan, China, or the US -- कूटनीति (kootniiti) is the standard term for 'diplomacy' in that register. It's built from कूट (koot, crafty/hidden) plus नीति (niiti, policy/principle), so at its root it carries a flavor of 'shrewd strategy,' not just polite negotiation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often write it exactly as spoken, with a short उ, but the standard written form is कूटनीति (kootniiti); when reading or writing formally, use the long ऊ. 2) Don't confuse with सामान्य नीति (saamaany niiti, general policy) -- this word specifically implies the political/international arena, not just any policy.

Sense 2 : cunning, crafty scheming (in interpersonal or office politics)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: चालबाज़ी (chaalbaazii, colloquial), छल-कपट (chhal-kapat, deceit)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Outside of politics, you'll hear someone accuse a colleague or relative of doing कूटनीति (kootniiti) when they're playing people against each other for personal gain -- it shifts from the neutral 'diplomacy' of international relations to a distinctly negative 'scheming' when applied to everyday human relationships.
