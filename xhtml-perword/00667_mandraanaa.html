मंडराना (mandraanaa)

verb A vivid verb meaning to hover or circle in the air, extended figuratively to a threat or danger looming over someone or something.
Sense 1 : to hover, circle, or loiter around
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चक्कर लगाना (chakkar lagaanaa, to circle around), घूमना (ghoomnaa, to wander/circle)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a bird of prey or a kite (चील, chiil) circling slowly over a market -- that image is the heart of this word. It is also commonly used for a person 'hanging around' somewhere, often with a slightly suspicious or watchful implication, like someone मंडरा रहा है (mandraa rahaa hai, 'is loitering/hovering about') near a house.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for घूमना (ghoomnaa) for any circling motion, but मंडराना (mandraanaa) specifically evokes something lingering or circling with intent -- a bird over prey, a person loitering nearby with a purpose -- rather than neutral wandering.

Sense 2 : (of danger, death, threat) to loom over
verb, intransitive Takes पर (par) to mark the person or thing being threatened: खतरा उस पर मंडरा रहा है (khatraa us par mandraa rahaa hai, 'danger is looming over him').
Synonyms: छाना (chhaanaa, to spread/loom, of clouds or gloom), घेरना (ghernaa, to surround, transitive counterpart)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same circling-bird image turned metaphorical: danger or death 'circles overhead' the way a vulture might circle something dying. You will hear this often in news reports -- मंडराता खतरा (mandraataa khatraa, 'looming danger') is a stock phrase in Hindi journalism.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use ऊपर (oopar) where पर (par) alone is the natural marker for what the danger looms 'over'; 2) This sense pairs almost exclusively with abstract threats like खतरा (khatraa, danger), मौत (maut, death), or संकट (sankat, crisis) -- using it for a mundane worry can sound overly dramatic.
