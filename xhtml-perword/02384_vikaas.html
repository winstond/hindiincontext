विकास (vikaas)

noun A single core meaning of growth or development, used both for concrete/biological growth and for abstract progress (personal, economic, national).
Sense 1 : development, progress (economic, national, personal)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रगति (pragti), उन्नति (unnti), तरक्की (tarakkii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: विकास (vikaas) is everywhere in Indian political and civic life -- from election slogans promising विकास (vikaas) to school essays on 'भारत का विकास' (bhaarat kaa vikaas, 'India's development'). It carries a hopeful, forward-looking tone, almost always used positively.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तरक्की (tarakkii) in casual talk about a person's career or life, while विकास (vikaas) is the word news, government, and formal contexts prefer for national or economic development -- both are correct, but mixing up the register can sound slightly off. 2) विकास (vikaas) is used with होना (honaa) ('development happens/occurs') or करना (karnaa) ('to develop something'), not with a verb like बढ़ना (badhnaa) which describes simple increase in size or amount.
Sample Sentences:
इस देश का विकास बहुत तेज़ी से हो रहा है। This country's development is happening very fast.
(is desh kaa vikaas bahut tezii se ho rahaa hai।)

Sense 2 : growth (biological or physical)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बढ़ोतरी (badhotrii), वृद्धि (vriddhi)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense in a doctor's office or in a school biology lesson -- बच्चे का विकास (bachche kaa vikaas, 'the child's growth') is a common phrase parents use when discussing a child's physical or mental development with a pediatrician.

(doktar ne kahaa ki bachche kaa vikaas bilkul thiik chal rahaa hai।)

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
