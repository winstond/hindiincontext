संतोष (santosh)

noun A noun meaning contentment or satisfaction, and also used as a common male given name; both uses are covered below.
Sense 1 : contentment, satisfaction
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संतुष्टि (santushti), तृप्ति (tripti, more about satisfaction from fulfillment, e.g. after eating or achieving something), सुकून (sukoon, peace of mind)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a quietly moral flavor in Hindi -- a parent or elder might tell a child संतोष रखो (santosh rakho, 'keep contentment/be satisfied') as a piece of life advice, almost like a mini-lesson in gratitude rather than just describing a mood. It pairs naturally with words like धैर्य (dhairy, patience) in advice-giving speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खुशी (khushii, happiness) when संतोष (santosh) is meant -- संतोष (santosh) is calmer and more inward, closer to 'being at peace with what one has' than to active joy. 2) The common phrase is संतोष होना (santosh honaa, to feel contentment) or संतोष करना (santosh karnaa, to make do / be content with something), often with से (se) marking what one is content with, e.g. इससे संतोष करो (isse santosh karo, 'be content with this').

Sense 2 : male given name
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: संतोष (santosh) is a very common North Indian male first name, directly carrying the same 'contentment' meaning -- parents naming a child this are literally wishing him a peaceful, satisfied disposition in life.
