अट्ठानवे (atthaanve)

numeral The number ninety-eight (atthaanve, '98'); used both to count items and to name the number itself.
Sense 1 : the number 98 (ninety-eight)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like all Hindi numbers from 21 to 99, this one is its own fixed word rather than a predictable combination -- Hindi speakers simply memorize the whole set as children, the same way English speakers memorize eleven and twelve rather than deriving them.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from अस्सी (assii, 'eighty') plus अठारह (athaarah, 'eighteen'), but Hindi numerals 21-99 are memorized as individual words, not calculated on the fly -- native speakers just know अट्ठानवे (atthaanve) as its own word.
