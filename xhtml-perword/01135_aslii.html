असली (aslii)

adjective A single core adjective meaning 'real, genuine, authentic' -- used both literally (real vs. fake objects) and figuratively (true vs. superficial qualities).
Sense 1 : genuine, authentic (not fake or duplicate)
adjective
Synonyms: वास्तविक (vaastvik, formal), खरा (kharaa, of metals/goods; also figurative for honest)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any shopkeeper in Chandni Chowk about gold or leather and you'll hear this word constantly: असली सोना (aslii sonaa, 'real gold') versus नकली (naklii, 'fake'). The असली-नकली (aslii-naklii) pairing is one of the most reflexive contrasts in everyday Hindi, used for everything from jewelry to friendships.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use वास्तविक (vaastvik) in casual speech where असली (aslii) sounds far more natural -- in the market or at home, असली (aslii) is the everyday word, वास्तविक (vaastvik) feels bookish. 2) Its opposite pair नकली (naklii, fake) is learned together with this word almost as a fixed unit -- असली या नकली (aslii yaa naklii, 'real or fake') is a very common phrase when checking goods.

Sense 2 : true, real (essential nature, as opposed to apparent or superficial)
adjective
Synonyms: सच्चा (sachchaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the असली (aslii) of असली मुद्दा (aslii muddaa, 'the real issue') or असली दोस्त (aslii dost, 'a true friend') -- it points at what's essential beneath the surface, the same instinct as English 'the real deal.' It pairs naturally with सच्चा (sachchaa) but is used more broadly, including for objects and situations, not just people's sincerity.
