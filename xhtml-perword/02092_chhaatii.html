छाती (chhaatii)

noun Primarily the body part 'chest,' extended into common idioms about courage, emotional weight, and pride. A feminine noun.
Sense 1 : chest (body part)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सीना (siinaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word at the doctor's clinic all the time: छाती में दर्द (chhaatii men dard, 'chest pain') is a very ordinary complaint to describe. It is a plain, physical-body word, not a poetic one -- for that, Hindi reaches for सीना (siinaa) or हृदय (hriday) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for सीना (siinaa) only, not realizing छाती (chhaatii) is just as common in everyday speech, especially when talking about physical sensations like pain or tightness in the chest.

Sense 2 : courage, heart (figurative, as seat of emotional strength)
noun, feminine Appears mainly inside fixed idiomatic phrases rather than as a free-standing word for 'courage.'
Synonyms: हिम्मत (himmat), दिल (dil)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is where the body-as-emotion logic of Hindi really shows itself: instead of saying 'I have the courage,' people say उसकी छाती में हिम्मत है (uskii chhaatii men himmat hai, 'he has courage in his chest'), locating bravery physically in the chest rather than as an abstract quality one 'has.'
Common Mistakes: 1) छाती ठोकना (chhaatii thoknaa, 'to thump one's chest') means to boast or assert something with confidence, not literally to hit oneself -- learners sometimes take these idioms too literally. 2) छाती पर पत्थर रखना (chhaatii par patthar rakhnaa, 'to put a stone on one's chest') means to steel oneself against grief, not a literal action.

उसकी छाती में इतनी हिम्मत नहीं थी कि सच बोल सके। He didn't have the nerve in his heart to speak the truth.

Sense 3 : pride, satisfaction (figurative, chest swelling with pride)
noun, feminine Occurs almost exclusively in the set phrase छाती चौड़ी होना (chhaatii chaudii honaa, 'for one's chest to broaden/swell with pride').
Synonyms: गर्व (garv)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A father watching his child succeed will often be described with उसकी छाती चौड़ी हो गई (uskii chhaatii chaudii ho gaii, 'his chest swelled with pride') -- a very familiar, almost cinematic image of parental pride in Hindi storytelling and daily speech alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) छाती चौड़ी होना (chhaatii chaudii honaa) is used for pride, often about someone else's achievement (a parent's pride in a child, for instance), and learners may not realize it is not literally about physique.
Sample Sentences:
