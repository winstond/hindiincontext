भी (bhii)

particle A small but essential particle meaning 'also/too/even', placed right after the word it emphasizes rather than at the sentence edge as in English. It also shows up in fixed combinations like कोई भी (koii bhii, anyone at all) and कभी भी (kabhii bhii, anytime at all), which add a sense of 'no matter which/when.'
Sense 1 : also, too
particle Placed directly after the word being included/added, not necessarily at the end of the sentence: वह भी आया (vah bhii aayaa, 'he also came') focuses on 'he' as an additional person who came.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Where you put भी (bhii) is basically where the emphasis lands: राम भी गया (raam bhii gayaa, 'Ram also went') implies others went and Ram is an addition, while राम गया भी (raam gayaa bhii) shifts to 'Ram even went' -- a subtle but real difference native speakers feel immediately.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often place भी (bhii) at the very end of the sentence out of habit from English 'too', e.g. saying मैं जा रहा हूँ भी (main jaa rahaa hoon bhii) instead of मैं भी जा रहा हूँ (main bhii jaa rahaa hoon, 'I am also going') -- the position of भी (bhii) changes exactly what is being added, so it must sit right after that word.
Sample Sentences:
राम भी पार्टी में गया। Ram also went to the party.
(raam bhii paartii men gayaa।)

Sense 2 : even
particle Here भी (bhii) intensifies rather than adds, often following a verb or extreme case: उसने देखा भी नहीं (usne dekhaa bhii nahiin, 'he did not even look').
Synonyms: तक (tak, can overlap in 'even' sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This 'even' sense often rides on a negative, as in खाया भी नहीं (khaayaa bhii nahiin, 'didn't even eat') -- a classic pattern for expressing mild shock or disappointment in everyday complaints, like a mother describing a fussy child.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : used in 'no matter which/any' combinations (कोई भी, कभी भी, कहीं भी, etc.)
particle Attaches to interrogative-derived words (कोई, कब, कहाँ, कैसे) to turn them into 'any-' or 'no matter' expressions: कोई भी (koii bhii, 'anyone at all'), कभी भी (kabhii bhii, 'anytime at all'), कहीं भी (kahiin bhii, 'anywhere at all').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the same particle as the 'also/even' senses, just doing extra work once it's fused onto question words -- worth noticing that it's one flexible little word carrying three related jobs rather than three unrelated words.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop भी (bhii) from these set combinations, saying just कोई (koii) when they mean 'anyone at all' -- without भी (bhii) the sense of 'no matter who/where/when' weakens to a plain 'someone/somewhere'.
Sample Sentences:

(tum koii bhii kitaab padh lo, mujhe farq nahiin padtaa।)
