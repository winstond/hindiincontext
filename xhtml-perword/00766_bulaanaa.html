बुलाना (bulaanaa)

verb One core sense: to call or summon someone, in the physical sense of getting them to come (by voice, invitation, or message). Related to बोलना (bolnaa, 'to speak') but not interchangeable with it -- बुलाना (bulaanaa) is causative in feel: you cause someone to come.
Sense 1 : to call/summon someone (to come)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: आमंत्रित करना (aamantrit karnaa, formal, for invitations), डाकना (daaknaa, colloquial/regional)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a mother standing at the door shouting खाना खाओ, अंदर आ जाओ (khaanaa khaao, andar aa jaao, 'eat food, come inside') -- that summoning action is बुलाना (bulaanaa) in its purest everyday form. It is also the word used when you invite someone to a wedding or function: उसे शादी में बुलाया था (use shaadii men bulaayaa thaa, 'he/she was invited to the wedding'), which is why it does double duty for both 'call over' and 'invite.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse बुलाना (bulaanaa) with बोलना (bolnaa, 'to speak') because they look and sound similar -- बुलाना (bulaanaa) is about summoning someone, not speaking. 2) The person summoned is marked with को (ko): उसे बुलाओ (use bulaao, 'call him/her'), not से (se). 3) For inviting someone to an event (a wedding, a party), बुलाना (bulaanaa) is the everyday choice; आमंत्रित करना (aamantrit karnaa) sounds formal/written, like on a printed card.
