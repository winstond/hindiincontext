शाबाश (shaabaash)

interjection An interjection of praise or encouragement, used to say 'well done' or 'bravo' to someone -- often to children, students, or anyone who has just done something good.
Sense 1 : well done, bravo (praise/encouragement)
interjection
Synonyms: वाह (vaah, expresses admiration more than praise for an act), बहुत खूब (bahut khoob)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian parent, teacher, or coach reaches for the instant a child does something right -- a good exam score, a completed chore, a goal scored in gully cricket. It carries a slightly senior-to-junior warmth, so it feels a bit odd said to a boss or elder, where वाह (vaah) or बहुत बढ़िया (bahut badhiyaa) fits better.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat शाबाश (shaabaash) as an adjective that needs to agree in gender or number, but it is invariant -- it never changes form regardless of who is being praised. 2) It is used to praise an action just completed or in progress, not as a general compliment on someone's character or looks -- for that, learners should reach for अच्छा (achchhaa) or सुंदर (sundar) instead.
Sample Sentences:
शाबाश! तुमने बहुत अच्छा किया। Well done! You did very well.
(shaabaash! tumne bahut achchhaa kiyaa।)
