हानि पहुँचाना (haani pahunchaanaa)

verb A compound/causative expression meaning 'to cause harm/damage'; built from हानि (haani, 'harm, loss') plus पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa, the causative of पहुँचना (pahunchnaa, 'to reach'), so literally 'to make harm reach [someone/something]'.
Sense 1 : to cause harm or damage
verb, transitive Takes को on the entity harmed: किसी को हानि पहुँचाना (kisii ko haani pahunchaanaa, 'to harm someone').
Synonyms: नुक़सान पहुँचाना (nuqsaan pahunchaanaa), नुक़सान करना (nuqsaan karnaa), क्षति पहुँचाना (kshati pahunchaanaa, formal/literary), चोट पहुँचाना (chot pahunchaanaa, leans toward physical or emotional hurt)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: हानि (haani) itself has a slightly more formal, written flavor than the everyday नुक़सान (nuqsaan) -- you'll meet हानि पहुँचाना (haani pahunchaanaa) in news reports, official warnings, and school essays (like 'तंबाकू स्वास्थ्य को हानि पहुँचाता है' (tambaakoo svaasthy ko haani pahunchaataa hai, 'tobacco harms health')) more than in casual chat, where नुक़सान पहुँचाना (nuqsaan pahunchaanaa) or सिर्फ़ नुक़सान करना (nuqsaan karnaa) feels more natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say हानि करना (haani karnaa) by analogy with नुक़सान करना (nuqsaan karnaa); हानि (haani) idiomatically pairs with पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa) far more often than करना (karnaa) in this fixed expression. 2) The person or thing harmed takes को (ko), not से (se) or पर (par), which learners sometimes reach for by analogy with English 'harm to.'
Sample Sentences:
तंबाकू स्वास्थ्य को हानि पहुँचाता है। Tobacco harms health.
(tambaakoo svaasthy ko haani pahunchaataa hai।)
