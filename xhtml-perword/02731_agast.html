अगस्त (agast)

noun The month of August; a straightforward loanword with a single sense.
Sense 1 : the month of August
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: 15 अगस्त (15 agast) is India's Independence Day, so अगस्त (agast) carries strong patriotic association -- flags, speeches, and TV specials all cluster around this date, making it one of the most emotionally loaded month names on the calendar.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes add an article or treat it like a common noun needing गender agreement on adjectives describing events -- month names in Hindi behave like proper nouns and take no article, just as in English. 2) Date phrasing follows the pattern "[date] अगस्त" (15 agast, 'August 15th'), with the day number coming first, not "अगस्त 15" as in English.
