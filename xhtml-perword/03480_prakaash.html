प्रकाश (prakaash)

noun Primarily means 'light' or 'illumination' in both literal and figurative senses; also very common as a male given name.
Sense 1 : light, illumination
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रोशनी (roshnii), उजाला (ujaalaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In daily bazaar or home talk, most people reach for रोशनी (roshnii) -- 'रोशनी करो' (roshnii karo, 'turn on the light') -- while प्रकाश (prakaash) shows up more in textbooks, science, or slightly elevated speech, like describing sunlight or the light of knowledge. It also carries a spiritual color, as in 'ज्ञान का प्रकाश' (gyaan kaa prakaash, 'the light of knowledge'), often heard in devotional or motivational contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to रोशनी (roshnii) in speech and reserve प्रकाश (prakaash) for writing or science contexts (like light in physics, or 'प्रकाश वर्ष' (prakaash varsh, 'light year')) -- both are correct, but प्रकाश (prakaash) leans slightly more formal/technical in everyday conversation.

Sense 2 : male given name
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: प्रकाश (prakaash) is an extremely common North Indian male first name, often paired as a suffix in names like ओम प्रकाश (om prakaash) or सूरज प्रकाश (sooraj prakaash), both meaning something like 'the light of' the preceding word.
