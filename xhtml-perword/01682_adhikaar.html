अधिकार (adhikaar)

noun Mainly a noun meaning 'right' (as in legal/moral entitlement) or 'authority/control over something'; a related but distinct sense means 'possession' in a more formal, almost legalistic sense.
Sense 1 : right, entitlement
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हक़ (haq)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word constantly in civic contexts -- मौलिक अधिकार (maulik adhikaar, 'fundamental rights') is a phrase every Indian schoolchild learns for the Constitution, so the word carries a slightly official, textbook flavor even in everyday use.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हक़ (haq) in casual speech and अधिकार (adhikaar) in formal or written contexts (constitution, law, rules) -- both are correct, but अधिकार (adhikaar) leans more formal/official. 2) The verb used with this sense is usually होना (honaa) with a dative subject: मुझे यह अधिकार है (mujhe yah adhikaar hai, 'I have this right') -- learners sometimes try to force a transitive construction with रखना (rakhnaa) instead, which sounds stiff here.
Sample Sentences:
शिक्षा हर बच्चे का अधिकार है। Education is every child's right.
(shikshaa har bachche kaa adhikaar hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : authority, control, power over something
noun, masculine Used with पर (par) to mark the thing being controlled: किसी पर अधिकार (kisii par adhikaar, 'authority/control over someone').
Synonyms: इख़्तियार (ikhtiyaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in phrases like अपने आप पर अधिकार रखना (apne aap par adhikaar rakhnaa, 'to have self-control'), where अधिकार (adhikaar) extends from external authority to inner mastery -- a nice example of how Hindi reuses a 'power over' word for emotional self-command.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes mark the object with का (kaa) instead of पर (par) -- उसका अधिकार करना (uskaa adhikaar karnaa) sounds wrong; you need उस पर अधिकार होना/रखना (us par adhikaar honaa, 'to have authority over him/her').
Sample Sentences:
उसे अपने आप पर अधिकार रखना सीखना चाहिए। He should learn to have self-control.
(use apne aap par adhikaar rakhnaa siikhnaa chaahie।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
