उदारता (udaartaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning generosity or magnanimity of spirit, formed from the adjective उदार (udaar, generous) plus the abstract-noun suffix -ता (taa).
Sense 1 : generosity, magnanimity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दानशीलता (daanshiiltaa, specifically about giving/charity), बड़प्पन (badappan, more about magnanimity/graciousness than material giving), खुले दिल से देना (khule dil se denaa, phrase, 'to give open-heartedly')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: उदारता (udaartaa) carries a slightly formal, almost essay-like weight -- it is the word you would use praising a leader, a donor, or a character trait in writing, more than casually complimenting a friend who bought you chai. In everyday warmth, people are more likely to praise the specific act (जैसे उसने खाना खिलाया, jaise usne khaanaa khilaayaa, 'like the fact that he fed people') than to reach for this abstract noun, which belongs more to formal speech, editorials, and school essays on values.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for यह बहुत अच्छा है (yah bahut achchhaa hai) style phrasing instead of using this noun directly with दिखाना (dikhaanaa) or करना (karnaa), as in उदारता दिखाना (udaartaa dikhaanaa, 'to show generosity') -- the noun-plus-verb pattern is how native speakers actually build the idea into a sentence. 2) Do not confuse with उधार (udhaar, 'loan/credit') -- the words look and sound similar but are unrelated in meaning.

मुख्यमंत्री ने बाढ़ पीड़ितों के प्रति अपनी उदारता दिखाई। The chief minister showed generosity towards the flood victims.
(mukhymantrii ne baadh piiditon ke prati apnii udaartaa dikhaaii।)
