राज (raaj)

noun Two related but distinct senses: the everyday idea of a secret (raaz variant spelling with nuqta), and the more literary/political sense of rule or reign, as in ब्रिटिश राज (british raaj).
Sense 1 : secret
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रहस्य (rahasy), भेद (bhed)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear राज़ की बात (raaz kii baat, 'a secret matter') constantly in everyday gossip and drama -- it's the phrase Hindi speakers reach for when leaning in to share something juicy. दिल का राज़ (dil kaa raaz, 'a secret of the heart') is a favorite in songs and soap operas for hidden feelings.
Common Mistakes: 1) Also spelled राज़ (raaz) with a nuqta under the ज; both spellings are common in print and speech, though राज़ (raaz) is more standard for the 'secret' sense specifically. 2) Learners sometimes confuse this with राज (raaj) meaning 'rule/reign' -- context (verbs like खोलना (kholnaa, 'to reveal') or रखना (rakhnaa, 'to keep')) makes the intended sense clear.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : rule, reign, dominion
noun, masculine Commonly used in fixed phrases like किसका राज है (kiskaa raaj hai, 'whose rule is it') and historical references such as मुग़ल राज (mughal raaj, 'Mughal rule') or ब्रिटिश राज (british raaj, 'British rule/the Raj').
Synonyms: शासन (shaasan), हुकूमत (hukoomat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same राज that gives English the historical term 'the Raj,' referring to British colonial rule in India. Indians still use it generically -- मेरा राज है (meraa raaj hai, 'it's my domain/I'm in charge here') can be said half-jokingly about a household or a small turf, not just empires.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is unrelated in meaning to the 'secret' sense even though spelled identically without the nuqta -- do not assume a wordplay connection in ordinary usage. 2) English speakers already know this word via 'the British Raj' -- in Hindi it's simply राज (raaj), not a special historical-only term, and can describe anyone's rule or dominance, even informally, as in उसका राज चलता है (uskaa raaj chaltaa hai, 'his rule prevails/he calls the shots').
