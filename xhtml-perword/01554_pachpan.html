पचपन (pachpan)

numeral The number fifty-five (pachpan), used for counting and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number 55
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are each their own word rather than a clean compound, so पचपन (pachpan) is best learned by rote alongside its neighbors चौवन (chauvan, 54) and छप्पन (chhappan, 56) rather than derived on the fly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like other Hindi numerals in the 50s, पचपन (pachpan) does not derive predictably from पचास (pachaas, fifty) plus पाँच (paanch, five) the way English '55' does -- it must simply be memorized as its own word, as is true across the Hindi teens and decades.
