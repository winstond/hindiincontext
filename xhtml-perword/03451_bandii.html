बंदी (bandii)

noun Two distinct senses live under this spelling: a noun meaning 'prisoner/captive,' and a related noun meaning 'imprisonment/captivity' -- plus a separate feminine noun meaning 'closure/shutdown' (as in a strike or lockdown), which is a different word by origin though it looks identical in Devanagari.
Sense 1 : prisoner, captive
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: कैदी (kaidii), क़ैदी (qaidii, variant spelling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word often in news reports about युद्धबंदी (yuddhbandii, 'prisoner of war') or राजनीतिक बंदी (raajniitik bandii, 'political prisoner') -- it carries a slightly more official or weighty tone than the everyday क़ैदी (qaidii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for क़ैदी (qaidii) by default since it's more common in casual speech; बंदी (bandii) is used interchangeably but leans slightly more formal or is favored in news/legal contexts, e.g. referring to prisoners of war or political prisoners.

(senaa ne kaii bandiyon ko rihaa kar diyaa।)

Sense 2 : imprisonment, captivity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कारावास (kaaraavaas, formal), क़ैद (qaid)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This abstract sense shares its root with the 'prisoner' sense above -- think of बंदी (bandii) as both the state of being bound and the person who is bound, much like English 'captive' can (informally) shade toward 'captivity' in old-fashioned usage.
Sample Sentences:
राजा ने उसे कई वर्षों तक बंदी में रखा। The king kept him in captivity for many years.
(raajaa ne use kaii varshon tak bandii men rakhaa।)

Sense 3 : shutdown, closure (of shops/business, as in a strike)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: हड़ताल (hadtaal, strike, related but not identical), बंद (band, noun form, more common)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll see this in headlines like कारखाना बंदी (kaarkhaanaa bandii, 'factory shutdown') -- it's the noun-with-ी feminine form paired with the everyday बंद (band) that people actually shout about in the street.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse this with बंद (band) the more common masculine noun for a general strike/shutdown (as in 'भारत बंद' bhaarat band) -- बंदी (bandii) in this sense appears more in formal or written contexts, such as referring to the closure of a factory or business.
Sample Sentences:
हड़ताल की वजह से कारखाना बंदी हो गई। Due to the strike, there was a factory shutdown.
(hadtaal kii vajah se kaarkhaanaa bandii ho gaii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अखबार में मिल बंदी की खबर छपी है। The news of the mill shutdown has been printed in the newspaper.
