रुलाना (rulaanaa)

verb The causative of रोना (ronaa, 'to cry'); रुलाना (rulaanaa) means 'to make (someone) cry' -- either literally or by moving them emotionally.
Sense 1 : to make someone cry (literally, through pain or distress)
verb, transitive Causative of रोना (ronaa); the person made to cry is marked with को (ko).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Parents often use this word half-jokingly about naughty kids: बच्चे ने माँ को रुला दिया (bachche ne maan ko rulaa diyaa, 'the child made mother cry') -- said about a child's mischief as much as real tears. It pairs naturally with दे देना (de denaa) as a compound verb, रुला देना (rulaa denaa), to stress the suddenness of the effect.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use रोना (ronaa) with a causee where रुलाना (rulaanaa) is needed -- e.g. to say 'he made the child cry' you need the causative रुलाया (rulaayaa), not just रोया (royaa) which only describes the child's own crying. 2) The person who cries is marked with को (ko), not से (se): बच्चे को रुलाया (bachche ko rulaayaa), not बच्चे से।
Sample Sentences:
उसकी डांट ने बच्चे को रुला दिया। His scolding made the child cry.
(uskii daant ne bachche ko rulaa diyaa।)

Sense 2 : to move someone to tears (emotionally, through a story, song, film, etc.)
verb, transitive Common with inanimate or abstract subjects like a film, song, or story: फ़िल्म ने सबको रुला दिया (film ne sabko rulaa diyaa, 'the film made everyone cry').
Synonyms: भावुक कर देना (bhaavuk kar denaa, 'to make emotional')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you hear in reviews and everyday chatter about tear-jerker films and songs: इस गाने ने मुझे रुला दिया (is gaane ne mujhe rulaa diyaa, 'this song made me cry') is a very natural, almost affectionate thing to say about a favorite sad song.
