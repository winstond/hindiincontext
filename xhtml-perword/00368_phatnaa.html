फटना (phatnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to tear/rip' or 'to burst/crack' -- used for cloth, paper, skin, clouds, and even loudly for things like bombs or storms; also lives inside a common idiom for extreme fear. Its transitive counterpart 'to tear something' is the causative फाड़ना (phaadnaa), a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to tear, rip (of cloth, paper, skin)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चिरना (chirnaa, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly about clothes and shoes -- कमीज़ फट गई (kamiiz phat gaii, 'the shirt tore') is the classic complaint of a kid coming home after playing outside. The shirt is treated as the one doing the action (intransitive), not as something acted upon, which is a very Hindi way of framing everyday wear and tear as just happening.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use this transitively with an object, e.g. wanting to say 'I tore the shirt' with फटना (phatnaa) -- but फटना (phatnaa) only describes the shirt tearing on its own; the agentive version is फाड़ना (phaadnaa), as in मैंने कमीज़ फाड़ी (mainne kamiiz phaadii, 'I tore the shirt').

Sense 4 : to be extremely frightened (idiomatic, 'heart bursts')
verb, intransitive Occurs almost exclusively in the fixed idiom दिल फटना / फटने लगना (dil phatnaa, 'heart to burst [from fear]'), often as दिल फट गया (dil phat gayaa, 'my heart nearly burst').
Synonyms: डर जाना (dar jaanaa, plain equivalent without the vivid imagery)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: जान फटना (jaan phatnaa, 'life/spirit bursting') and दिल फटना (dil phatnaa) both paint fear as something physically rupturing inside you, the same body-as-agent logic behind दिल टूटना (dil tootnaa, 'heart breaking' for heartbreak) -- Hindi loves letting the heart do the emoting while the person just watches it happen.
Common Mistakes: 1) This only works with दिल (dil) or जान (jaan) as the grammatical subject, not with a person directly -- you can't say मैं फट गया (main phat gayaa) to mean 'I got scared.'
Sample Sentences:
उसे देखकर मेरी जान फट गई। I was terrified when I saw him.
(use dekhkar merii jaan phat gaii।)
