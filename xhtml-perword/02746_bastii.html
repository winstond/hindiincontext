बस्ती (bastii)

noun Mainly a noun meaning a settlement or inhabited area, most often used for a poor or informal neighborhood/slum; related to the verb बसना (basnaa, 'to settle/inhabit').
Sense 1 : settlement, neighborhood (often a poor or informal one)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मुहल्ला (muhallaa), झोपड़पट्टी (jhopadpattii, slum specifically), कॉलोनी (kolonii, more neutral/formal colony)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi, बस्ती (bastii) is the word you hear on the news or from an auto driver pointing out a jhuggi cluster along the railway tracks -- it carries a strong association with informal, low-income housing, not just any residential area. It shares its root with बसना (basnaa, 'to settle') and आबादी (aabaadii, 'population'), both painting the idea of people making a place their home.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat बस्ती (bastii) as a neutral synonym for 'town' or 'city area', but in everyday Delhi-NCR usage it usually implies a poorer or unplanned settlement, unlike कॉलोनी (kolonii) or इलाका (ilaakaa) which are more neutral. 2) Do not confuse with बस्ती (bastii) the city name (a place in Uttar Pradesh) -- context makes clear which is meant, but learners sometimes assume it is always a common noun.
