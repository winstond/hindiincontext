धुलना (dhulnaa)

verb This is the intransitive/passive-flavored partner of धोना (dhonaa, 'to wash') -- धुलना (dhulnaa) means 'to get washed' or 'to be washed', describing something undergoing the washing rather than someone doing the washing.
Sense 1 : to get washed, to be washed (clothes, dishes, etc.)
verb, intransitive The doer of the washing, if mentioned, takes से (se): कपड़े साबुन से धुलते हैं (kapde saabun se dhulte hain, 'the clothes get washed with soap').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi loves this kind of intransitive partner verb -- खुलना (khulnaa)/खोलना (kholnaa), टूटना (tootnaa)/तोड़ना (todnaa) -- where the thing itself is framed as undergoing the change, not being acted upon by a stated agent. You will hear this constantly around laundry: कपड़े धुल गए (kapde dhul gae, 'the clothes got washed') is what a mother announces when the wash is done, with no need to say who washed them.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for the passive construction of धोना (dhonaa) (धोया जाता है, dhoyaa jaataa hai) when धुलना (dhulnaa) already does this job more naturally and simply. 2) Do not confuse with धोना (dhonaa) itself -- धुलना (dhulnaa) cannot take a direct object of its own; the cloth or dish is the subject, not the object.
Sample Sentences:

(kapde dhul gae hain।)

Sense 2 : to be worn away, washed out, faded (of color, ink, writing)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मिटना (mitnaa, of writing/marks fading), फीका पड़ना (phiikaa padnaa, of color fading)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a natural extension of the washing sense: just as water washes fabric, rain or repeated washing 'washes away' ink, color, or writing -- स्याही धुल गई (syaahii dhul gaii, 'the ink washed away/faded') is a common thing to say about a rain-smudged letter or a faded sign.
Sample Sentences:
