भूमिका (bhoomikaa)

noun Two senses in everyday use: an actor's or a person's 'role' in a situation, and a written 'preface/introduction' to a book. Both come from the same root idea of 'ground/foundation' being laid before the main action.
Sense 1 : role, part played (in a situation, drama, or plan)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रोल (rol, English loanword, common in casual speech), किरदार (kirdaar, more about the character itself than the function)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in news and office contexts: सरकार की भूमिका (sarkaar kii bhoomikaa, 'the government's role'), अहम भूमिका (aham bhoomikaa, 'important role'). It's the go-to word when talking about someone's contribution or function within a larger event, not just an actor's part.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for रोल (rol) exclusively; भूमिका (bhoomikaa) is the word you'll see in writing, news, and slightly more formal speech, so it's worth knowing both. 2) The common construction is भूमिका निभाना (bhoomikaa nibhaanaa, 'to play a role') -- निभाना (nibhaanaa) here means 'to carry out/fulfill,' not 'to play' in the game sense, so learners sometimes wrongly reach for खेलना (khelnaa).
Sample Sentences:
इस योजना में उसकी भूमिका बहुत अहम थी। His role in this plan was very important.
(is yojnaa men uskii bhoomikaa bahut aham thii।)

Sense 2 : preface, introduction (to a book or speech)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रस्तावना (prastaavnaa), प्राक्कथन (praakkthan, literary, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word printed at the start of a serious book, often written by someone other than the author to introduce the work. It's less used in casual conversation and shows up mainly in written or literary contexts.
Sample Sentences:
इस किताब की भूमिका बहुत रोचक है। The preface of this book is very interesting.
(is kitaab kii bhoomikaa bahut rochak hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
लेखक ने अपनी किताब की भूमिका खुद लिखी। The author wrote the preface of his own book himself.
(lekhak ne apnii kitaab kii bhoomikaa khud likhii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
इस उपन्यास की भूमिका एक प्रसिद्ध आलोचक द्वारा लिखी गई है। The preface of this novel was written by a famous critic.
(is upanyaas kii bhoomikaa ek prasiddh aalochak dvaaraa likhii gaii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
कृपया भूमिका पढ़ने के बाद ही पहला अध्याय शुरू करें। Please start the first chapter only after reading the preface.

Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
भूमिका में लेखक ने अपने विचारों का सार प्रस्तुत किया है। In the preface, the author has presented a summary of his thoughts.

Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगर भूमिका इतनी लंबी न होती, तो पाठक इसे भी पढ़ लेते। If the preface were not so long, readers would have read it too.
(agar bhoomikaa itnii lambii n hotii, to paathak ise bhii padh lete।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active

(kaash koii is kitaab kii bhoomikaa thodii chhotii likhtaa।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active

Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Passive

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive

(bhoomikaa padhte samay mujhe lekhak kii soch kaa andaazaa ho gayaa।)
