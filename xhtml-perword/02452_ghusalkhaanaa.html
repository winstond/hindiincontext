ग़ुसलख़ाना (ghusalkhaanaa)

noun A Persian-Urdu-origin word for 'bathroom,' built from ग़ुसल (ghusal, 'bathing/ablution') plus ख़ाना (khaanaa, 'room/house,' as in डाकख़ाना (daakkhaanaa, 'post office')). One straightforward sense: a room for bathing.
Sense 1 : bathroom (room for bathing)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नहाने का कमरा (nahaane kaa kamraa, everyday descriptive phrase), स्नानघर (snaanghar, formal/Sanskritic), बाथरूम (baathroom, very common in casual speech)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word carries a distinctly Persian-Urdu flavor and shows up more in older households, Urdu literature, or formal/poetic registers than in casual everyday chatter, where बाथरूम (baathroom) has largely taken over in urban Delhi-NCR speech. It is a nice example of the -ख़ाना (khaanaa) suffix pattern, seen also in किताबख़ाना (kitaabkhaanaa, 'library/bookstore') and मुर्ग़ीख़ाना (murghiikhaanaa, 'chicken coop').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with शौचालय (shauchaalay) or टॉयलेट (toylet), which refer to the toilet specifically; ग़ुसलख़ाना (ghusalkhaanaa) technically means the bathing area, though in everyday speech the two spaces are often combined and people use the words loosely. 2) Do not drop the nuqta sounds when speaking -- ग़ (gh) and ख़ (kh) are distinct from ग (g) and ख (kh), though many speakers today pronounce them without the nuqta anyway.
