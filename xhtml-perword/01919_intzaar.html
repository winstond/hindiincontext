इंतज़ार (intzaar)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'wait/waiting', almost always paired with the verb करना (karnaa) 'to do' -- इंतज़ार करना (intzaar karnaa) 'to wait'.
Sense 1 : waiting, expectation
noun, masculine Almost always used with करना (karnaa) as इंतज़ार करना (intzaar karnaa, 'to wait') where the thing waited for takes का (kaa)/की (kii)/के (ke): उसका इंतज़ार (uskaa intzaar, 'wait for him/her').
Synonyms: प्रतीक्षा (pratiikshaa, formal), राह देखना (raah dekhnaa, verb phrase meaning 'to watch the path/await')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: इंतज़ार (intzaar) carries a slightly more emotional or yearning color than the plainer प्रतीक्षा (pratiikshaa), which shows up more in official or written contexts like railway announcements. You'll hear इंतज़ार constantly in songs and daily speech alike -- तेरा इंतज़ार (teraa intzaar, 'waiting for you') is a phrase every Hindi film soundtrack seems to reach for at some point.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say 'मैं इंतज़ार हूँ' by analogy with English 'I am waiting' -- Hindi needs the light verb: मैं इंतज़ार कर रहा हूँ (main intzaar kar rahaa hoon, 'I am waiting'). 2) The thing being awaited is marked with का/की/के, not को: उसकी बस का इंतज़ार (uskii bas kaa intzaar, 'waiting for her bus'), not उसकी बस को इंतज़ार.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे तुम्हारा इंतज़ार है। I am waiting for you.
(mujhe tumhaaraa intzaar hai।)

थोड़ा इंतज़ार कर लो, वह अभी आती है। Wait a little, she'll be here right now.

बरसों का इंतज़ार आज खत्म हुआ। Years of waiting ended today.
