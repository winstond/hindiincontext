निश्चय करना (nishchay karnaa)

verb A slightly formal verb phrase meaning 'to decide' or 'to resolve' -- built from the noun निश्चय (nishchay, 'certainty/decision') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'). Close in meaning to फ़ैसला करना (faislaa karnaa) and तय करना (tay karnaa), but leans more literary/deliberate.
Sense 1 : to decide, to resolve (to do something)
verb, transitive Takes a following infinitive verb marked with the oblique -ने (ne) ending to express 'decided to do X', e.g. जाने का निश्चय करना (jaane kaa nishchay karnaa, 'to decide to go') or जाने का निश्चय किया (jaane kaa nishchay kiyaa, 'decided to go'), often via a का (kaa)-linked construction rather than a bare infinitive.
Synonyms: तय करना (tay karnaa), फ़ैसला करना (faislaa karnaa), ठान लेना (thaan lenaa, more forceful/determined), इरादा करना (iraadaa karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a certain gravity -- it is the kind of verb you would use for a life decision (marriage, career, a vow) rather than deciding what to eat for lunch, where तय करना (tay karnaa) would feel far more natural. You will often meet it in motivational or moral contexts, like निश्चय करो और आगे बढ़ो (nishchay karo aur aage badho, 'resolve firmly and move forward').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तय करना (tay karnaa) or फ़ैसला करना (faislaa karnaa) in speech since निश्चय करना (nishchay karnaa) sounds a touch bookish and formal in casual conversation -- it is more common in writing, speeches, or deliberate, weighty decisions than in everyday chatter about small choices. 2) The object of decision is usually linked with का/की/के (kaa) plus a verbal noun (e.g. करने का निश्चय (karne kaa nishchay)) rather than directly following निश्चय (nishchay).
