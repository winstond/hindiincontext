प्रभावित (prabhaavit)

adjective An adjective/participle meaning 'influenced' or 'affected', built on the noun प्रभाव (prabhaav, 'influence/effect'). It regularly pairs with होना (honaa) to mean 'to be affected/impressed' and with करना (karnaa) to mean 'to affect/impress'.
Sense 1 : affected, influenced (by something)
adjective Used with से (se) to mark the source of influence: किसी चीज़ से प्रभावित होना (kisii chiiz se prabhaavit honaa, 'to be affected by something').
Synonyms: असरग्रस्त (asragrast, rare), प्रभावग्रस्त (prabhaavagrast, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word sits comfortably in both everyday and formal registers -- you will hear it in a casual chat about a movie ("मैं बहुत प्रभावित हुआ" (main bahut prabhaavit huaa, 'I was very impressed')) as well as in news reports about floods or policy. It is one of the most common ways to express being 'impressed' by a person, unlike the more literal खुश (khush, 'happy/pleased'), which doesn't carry the same sense of being moved or influenced.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try को (ko) for the source of influence instead of से (se); it is X से प्रभावित (X se prabhaavit), not X को प्रभावित (as a static state). 2) प्रभावित करना (prabhaavit karnaa) is the transitive 'to affect/impress' counterpart -- learners sometimes use प्रभावित होना (prabhaavit honaa) when they actually mean the causative 'to influence someone', mixing up who is doing the affecting.

Sense 2 : impressed (by a person, positively)
adjective
Synonyms: मुतास्सिर (mutaassir, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is really the same underlying sense as 'affected/influenced' narrowed to a positive, admiring context -- when a Delhi parent says अपने बेटे के काम से बहुत प्रभावित हैं (apne bete ke kaam se bahut prabhaavit hain, 'are very impressed by their son's work'), the warmth comes entirely from context, not from a different word.
