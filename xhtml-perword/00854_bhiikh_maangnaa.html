भीख माँगना (bhiikh maangnaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to beg' (for alms/money) -- भीख (bhiikh) is the noun 'alms/charity' and माँगना (maangnaa) is 'to ask for'; together they form the fixed everyday verb phrase for begging, used both literally and figuratively.
Sense 1 : to beg (literally, for alms/money on the street)
verb, intransitive भीख (bhiikh) takes से (se) for the person begged from: उससे भीख माँगना (usse bhiikh maangnaa, 'to beg from him/her').
Synonyms: भीख माँगना (bhiikh maangnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase is tied to a very visible everyday scene in Indian cities -- people begging at traffic signals, temple gates, or train stations -- so भीख माँगना (bhiikh maangnaa) carries real social weight, not just dictionary neutrality. It is also the base for the common noun भिखारी (bhikhaarii, 'beggar'), which a learner will hear constantly alongside this verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say माँगना (maangnaa) alone to mean 'beg', but without भीख (bhiikh) it just means 'to ask for/request' something ordinary -- the two words together are what specifically signal begging for alms. 2) The person from whom one begs takes से (se), not को (ko): भीख किसी से माँगी जाती है (bhiikh kisii se maangii jaatii hai, 'alms are begged from someone'), not किसी को (kisii ko).

Sense 2 : to beg/plead abjectly for something (figurative, e.g. mercy, forgiveness, favor)
verb, intransitive Used with an abstract object like माफ़ी (maafii, 'forgiveness') or जान की भीख (jaan kii bhiikh, 'begging for one's life'), extending the literal act of begging to emotional pleading.
Synonyms: गिड़गिड़ाना (gidgidaanaa, to plead/grovel), मिन्नत करना (minnat karnaa, to entreat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in intense, high-stakes moments -- someone begging for their life or for forgiveness in a heated family scene or a film climax -- where it conveys utter desperation, stripping away all pride, much like the literal act of begging on the street.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may not realize this figurative sense is very common in dramatic or emotional speech (films, arguments) and is not limited to literal poverty -- जान की भीख माँगना (jaan kii bhiikh maangnaa, 'to beg for one's life') is a set phrase worth knowing as a unit.
