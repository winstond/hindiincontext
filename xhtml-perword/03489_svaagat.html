स्वागत (svaagat)

noun A single core meaning: 'welcome' -- used as a noun and in the fixed greeting phrase स्वागत है (svaagat hai).
Sense 1 : welcome, reception
noun, masculine Most commonly used with करना (karnaa, 'to do/make') -- स्वागत करना (svaagat karnaa, 'to welcome') -- treating welcome as an act you perform for someone, who is marked with का (kaa) or a possessive.
Synonyms: आदर-सत्कार (aadar-satkaar, formal), अभिनंदन (abhinandan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see स्वागत (svaagat) painted on banners and gates at weddings, offices, and airports -- often as आपका स्वागत है (aapkaa svaagat hai, 'you are welcome/we welcome you'), which is the standard formal greeting seen far more in writing and public speech than in casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say स्वागत करता हूँ आपको (svaagat kartaa hoon aapko) when the more natural pattern is आपका स्वागत करता हूँ (aapkaa svaagat kartaa hoon, 'I welcome you') -- the welcomed person is marked with का (kaa/'s), not a direct object postposition. 2) स्वागत है (svaagat hai) alone (without करना) is a very common standalone greeting meaning 'you are welcome (here)' -- learners sometimes over-add करना when the है (hai) form is what's idiomatic at a doorway or entrance.
Sample Sentences:
आपका स्वागत है। You are welcome (here).
(aapkaa svaagat hai।)
