तलना (talnaa)

verb A single-sense kitchen verb: to fry or deep-fry something in oil or ghee.
Sense 1 : to fry, deep-fry (food) in oil/ghee
verb, transitive
Synonyms: भूनना (bhoonnaa, roasting/dry-frying, not deep oil-frying)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi household about breakfast and you'll hear this verb constantly: पूरियाँ तलना (pooriyaan talnaa, 'to fry pooris') or समोसे तलना (samose talnaa, 'to fry samosas') are everyday kitchen phrases, especially around festivals when the whole house smells of hot oil.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse with भूनना (bhoonnaa) -- तलना (talnaa) specifically means frying in a good amount of oil or ghee (like पूरी (poorii) or पकौड़े (pakaude)), while भूनना (bhoonnaa) is roasting or dry-frying with little to no oil, as with मसाले (masaale) or भुना हुआ चना (bhunaa huaa chanaa).
