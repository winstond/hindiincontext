अभ्यास (abhyaas)

noun A common noun meaning 'practice' -- covering both the act of practicing/rehearsing a skill and the concept of habit or familiarity gained through repetition.
Sense 1 : practice, rehearsal (of a skill)
noun, masculine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) as अभ्यास करना (abhyaas karnaa, 'to practice') -- the noun itself does not carry the verbal action.
Synonyms: प्रैक्टिस (praiktis, English loanword, very common in casual speech), रियाज़ (riyaaz, used specifically for practicing music or singing)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in school contexts -- अभ्यास पुस्तिका (abhyaas pustikaa, 'practice workbook') is a staple item every Indian schoolchild has carried. It also carries a slightly more disciplined, effortful connotation than casual 'practice' in English -- think of a student drilling math problems or a yoga practitioner repeating asanas, not just idly rehearsing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to the English loan प्रैक्टिस (praiktis) in speech, which is fine, but अभ्यास (abhyaas) is the word you will meet in schoolbooks, yoga contexts, and more formal or written registers. 2) For music/singing specifically, native speakers usually reach for रियाज़ (riyaaz) rather than अभ्यास (abhyaas), even though both are technically valid.

Sense 2 : habit, familiarity, being accustomed to something
noun, masculine Often appears in the construction X का अभ्यास होना (X kaa abhyaas honaa, 'to be accustomed to X') or अभ्यस्त होना (abhyast honaa, the related adjective form 'accustomed').
Synonyms: आदत (aadat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense connects directly to the 'practice' sense above -- in Hindi, the idea is that repeated practice (अभ्यास) is precisely what produces familiarity or being-used-to-it (also अभ्यास). The same word carries you from 'drilling a skill' to 'now it feels natural,' which is a tidy little piece of native-speaker logic worth noticing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for आदत (aadat) in every case, but अभ्यास (abhyaas) in this sense leans toward being accustomed through repeated exposure or training (like getting used to cold weather or a new routine) rather than a fixed personal quirk -- आदत (aadat) is closer to that everyday sense of 'habit.'
