आसानी से (aasaanii se)

adverb An adverbial phrase meaning 'easily,' built from the feminine noun आसानी (aasaanii, 'ease') plus the instrumental postposition से (se). One core sense.
Sense 1 : easily, without difficulty
adverb
Synonyms: सरलता से (saraltaa se), बिना किसी परेशानी के (binaa kisii pareshaanii ke, meaning scope: emphasizes absence of trouble)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase is everywhere in practical advice and instructions -- recipes, directions, tech support calls -- basically anytime a Delhi shopkeeper or friend is reassuring you that something is doable: यह आसानी से हो जाएगा (yah aasaanii se ho jaaegaa, 'this will get done easily'). It pairs naturally with the compound verb हो जाना (ho jaanaa), which itself carries a sense of things falling into place on their own.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the से (se) and just say आसानी (aasaanii) as if it were the adverb itself; without से it reads as a bare noun ('ease'), not 'easily.' 2) Word order: आसानी से (aasaanii se) typically sits right before the verb it modifies, e.g. वह आसानी से समझ गया (vah aasaanii se samajh gayaa, 'he understood easily'), not at the very start of the sentence the way English 'easily' can float.
Sample Sentences:
यह काम आसानी से हो जाएगा। This work will get done easily.
(yah kaam aasaanii se ho jaaegaa।)
