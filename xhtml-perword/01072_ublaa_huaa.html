उबला हुआ (ublaa huaa)

adjective This is the perfect participle of उबलना (ubalnaa, 'to boil,' intransitive), used as an adjective meaning 'boiled' -- as in उबला हुआ अंडा (ublaa huaa andaa, 'boiled egg').
Sense 1 : boiled (of food, water, etc.)
adjective Formed from the perfective participle of उबलना (ubalnaa) plus हुआ (huaa); agrees in gender/number with the noun it modifies, e.g. उबली हुई सब्ज़ी (ublii huii sabzii, 'boiled vegetable'), उबले हुए आलू (uble hue aaloo, 'boiled potatoes').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around Indian kitchens and street food stalls -- उबले हुए अंडे (uble hue ande, 'boiled eggs') sold by hawkers, or a mother telling you उबला हुआ पानी पियो (ublaa huaa paanii piyo, 'drink boiled water') when you are new to a place, since boiling water is the classic home remedy for making it safe to drink.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop हुआ (huaa) and just say उबला (ublaa) alone before a noun, which sounds incomplete in most everyday contexts -- the full participle उबला हुआ (ublaa huaa) is the natural adjectival form. 2) Because उबलना (ubalnaa) is intransitive ('to boil' as in 'the water boils'), this participle describes the food/liquid itself being boiled, not something actively boiling something else -- for 'to boil something' (transitive), Hindi uses उबालना (ubaalnaa), giving a different participle उबाला हुआ (ubaalaa huaa) with the same meaning in practice but a different verb root underneath.
