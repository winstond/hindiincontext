चल बसना (chal basnaa)

verb A gentle, euphemistic compound verb meaning 'to pass away, die.' Built from चलना (chalnaa, 'to move/go') plus बसना (basnaa, 'to settle/dwell'), together suggesting the soul 'moving on to settle elsewhere' rather than a blunt reference to death.
Sense 1 : to pass away, die (euphemistic)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मर जाना (mar jaanaa, blunter), गुज़र जाना (guzar jaanaa), स्वर्गवासी होना (svargvaasii honaa, formal), दुनिया से जाना (duniyaa se jaanaa), दम तोड़ना (dam todnaa, more dramatic/vivid)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the polite, softened way to announce a death in Hindi, much like English 'passed away' versus the flatter 'died.' You will hear it often in condolence conversations: दादाजी चल बसे (daadaajii chal base, 'Grandfather has passed away') -- gentle, respectful, and never used casually or jokingly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as literally 'to go and settle,' missing that it is a fixed euphemism exclusively used for death, not for moving house or migrating. 2) It is used only for people (and sometimes cherished animals), not for objects or abstract things ending -- unlike ख़त्म होना (khatm honaa) or बंद होना (band honaa) for things coming to an end.
Sample Sentences:
पिछले हफ्ते दादाजी चल बसे। Grandfather passed away last week.
(pichhle haphte daadaajii chal base।)
