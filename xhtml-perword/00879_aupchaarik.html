औपचारिक (aupchaarik)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'formal' or 'official', used to describe tone, procedure, or occasions as opposed to casual/personal ones.
Sense 1 : formal, official
adjective
Synonyms: रस्मी (rasmii, often about ritual/social formality), सरकारी (sarkaarii, official/governmental)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in office and bureaucratic contexts -- an औपचारिक निमंत्रण (aupchaarik nimantran, 'formal invitation') or औपचारिक बैठक (aupchaarik baithak, 'formal meeting') signals a certain distance and protocol, in contrast to घरेलू (ghareloo, 'informal/domestic') warmth. Its opposite, अनौपचारिक (anaupchaarik, 'informal'), is just as common and worth learning alongside it -- the अ- (a) prefix negates the base word, a pattern you'll see across many Hindi adjectives.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for औपचारिक (aupchaarik) to mean 'polite,' but it describes the formality of a setting, procedure, or relationship (औपचारिक बातचीत aupchaarik baatchiit, 'formal conversation'), not good manners -- for that, विनम्र (vinamr) is the word. 2) It is also used to describe something done just for show or as a formality, e.g. औपचारिकता निभाना (aupchaariktaa nibhaanaa, 'to go through the formality') -- learners often miss this mildly dismissive shade, where 'formal' in English wouldn't carry that same sense of empty ritual.
Sample Sentences:
यह एक औपचारिक निमंत्रण है, इसलिए साड़ी पहननी पड़ेगी। This is a formal invitation, so I'll have to wear a sari.
(yah ek aupchaarik nimantran hai, islie saadii pahannii padegii।)
