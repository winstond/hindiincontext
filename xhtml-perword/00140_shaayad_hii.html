शायद ही (shaayad hii)

adverb A fixed two-word combination meaning 'hardly ever' or 'rarely if ever' -- built from शायद (shaayad, 'perhaps') plus the emphatic particle ही (hii), which together push the sense of possibility down toward near-impossibility.
Sense 1 : hardly ever, rarely
adverb Typically pairs with a following negative verb (होगा/होता है (hogaa/hotaa hai)) to express 'it will hardly happen' -- the negative sense comes from the construction as a whole, not from an explicit नहीं (nahiin).
Synonyms: मुश्किल से (mushkil se, with difficulty/rarely), बहुत कम (bahut kam, very rarely)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this a lot in complaints and gentle exaggeration -- वह शायद ही कभी फ़ोन करता है (vah shaayad hii kabhii fon kartaa hai, 'he hardly ever calls') is the classic tone of a mother or friend expressing mild reproach. Note the family link to शायद (shaayad) alone, which stays a neutral 'maybe' or 'perhaps' without the ही (hii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often add नहीं (nahiin) after the verb expecting a double negative like English 'hardly...not', but शायद ही (shaayad hii) already carries the negative force on its own -- शायद ही कभी आता है (shaayad hii kabhii aataa hai, 'he hardly ever comes') needs no extra negative. 2) Confusing this with plain शायद (shaayad) 'maybe' -- adding ही (hii) flips the tone from open possibility to near-certainty that something won't happen.
