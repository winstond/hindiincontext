प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni)

noun A literary/formal word for 'echo' -- both the literal acoustic phenomenon and the figurative 'echo' of an idea or event. Everyday speech usually reaches for गूँज (goonj) instead.
Sense 1 : echo (sound reflection)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गूँज (goonj)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word is built from प्रति (prati, 'against/back') plus ध्वनि (dhvani, 'sound'), so it literally means 'sound thrown back' -- a nice transparent Sanskrit compound once you know the pieces. You will meet it more in a school essay on पहाड़ (pahaad, 'mountain') acoustics than in a friend's story about shouting into a valley.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech about a shout bouncing off a wall; native speakers would say गूँज (goonj) there and save प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni) for writing or a more deliberate, descriptive register.

(pahaadon men chillaane se pratidhvni sunaaii detii hai।)

Sense 2 : echo, reverberation (figurative -- of an idea, event, or feeling)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गूँज (goonj, figurative use also common), असर (asar, impact/effect, broader meaning)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: News writing and political commentary love this figurative sense -- a leader's speech is said to leave a प्रतिध्वनि (pratidhvni) in people's minds, much like English 'the speech echoed through the nation.' It signals a slightly elevated, thoughtful register rather than casual chat.

(unke bhaashan kii pratidhvni aaj bhii logon ke man men sunaaii detii hai।)
