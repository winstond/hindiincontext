सन (san)

noun Two distinct words share this spelling: a year-marker used in dates (as in 'in the year...'), and the plant/fiber name for hemp/jute (sunn hemp). A third, colloquial use appears in the filler phrase सन से (san se) describing a whooshing sound, but the main senses learners need are the date-marker and the fiber.
Sense 1 : year (used before a numeral to mark a calendar year, especially in dates)
noun, masculine Used almost exclusively immediately before a numeral to date an event: सन 1947 (san 1947, 'the year 1947'). It does not stand alone as a general word for 'year' the way साल (saal) does.
Synonyms: वर्ष (varsh, formal), साल (saal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet सन (san) constantly in history textbooks and news dates: सन 1857 (san 1857), सन 2020 (san 2020). It comes from the Persian/Arabic tradition of era-dating and pairs naturally with ईस्वी (iisvii, 'of the Christian era'), as in सन 1947 ईस्वी (san 1947 iisvii, 'the year 1947 CE').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use सन (san) as a general, freestanding word for 'year' (as in 'this year' or 'last year') -- for that, use साल (saal) or वर्ष (varsh) instead; सन (san) is reserved for naming a specific calendar year before a number.

(bhaarat san 1947 men aazaad huaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : sunn hemp / jute-like fiber plant
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पटसन (patsan)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: सन (san) refers to the sunn hemp plant traditionally grown for its strong fiber, used to make ropes, sacks, and coarse cloth in rural India -- you would hear this word in a village or farming context, not in the city.
