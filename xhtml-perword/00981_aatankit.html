आतंकित (aatankit)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'terrified' or 'panic-stricken' -- describes a person gripped by fear or terror, often in a somewhat formal or journalistic register.
Sense 1 : terrified, panic-stricken
adjective Almost always paired with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa): आतंकित होना (aatankit honaa, 'to become terrified') for the state, and आतंकित करना (aatankit karnaa, 'to terrorize/frighten') for the causative action done to someone else.
Synonyms: भयभीत (bhaybhiit), डरा हुआ (daraa huaa), सहमा हुआ (sahmaa huaa, huddled with fright), भयाक्रांत (bhayaakraant, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Hindi news reports -- 'गाँव के लोग आतंकित हैं' (gaanv ke log aatankit hain, 'the villagers are terrified') is the kind of line you'd hear describing fear caused by crime, violence, or natural disaster. It shares its root with आतंक (aatank, 'terror') and आतंकवाद (aatankvaad, 'terrorism'), so once you know this word, those two become easy to remember too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for डरा हुआ (daraa huaa) in every context, but आतंकित (aatankit) is stronger and more formal -- it suggests being terrorized or gripped by dread (e.g. by violence, a threat, or a mob), not everyday nervousness like before an exam. 2) Remember the subject of fear takes आतंकित होना (aatankit honaa) while the one causing fear takes आतंकित करना (aatankit karnaa) -- learners sometimes flip the construction.

(baadh kii khabar sunkar gaanv ke log aatankit hain।)
