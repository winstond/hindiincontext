मर्मस्पर्शी (marmasparshii)

adjective A literary/formal adjective describing something that touches the heart or soul -- 'poignant, moving, touching.' Built from मर्म (marm, 'core/vital point') plus स्पर्शी (sparshii, 'touching').
Sense 1 : deeply moving, touching (emotionally)
adjective
Synonyms: हृदयस्पर्शी (hridyasparshii, literary), भावुक (bhaavuk), दिल को छू लेने वाला (dil ko chhoo lene vaalaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word most often in book reviews, obituary tributes, or descriptions of a moving film or speech -- the kind of formal register you'd read in a newspaper's culture page rather than hear at the dinner table. It pairs naturally with nouns like कहानी (kahaanii, 'story'), दृश्य (drishy, 'scene'), or भाषण (bhaashan, 'speech').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech where a simpler phrase like दिल को छू गया (dil ko chhoo gayaa, 'it touched the heart') would sound more natural -- मर्मस्पर्शी (marmasparshii) belongs to written or formal spoken registers, such as describing a film, story, or speech. 2) It describes something evoking pathos or emotional depth, not simply something 'nice' or 'sweet' -- it is not a casual compliment word.
Sample Sentences:
यह एक मर्मस्पर्शी कहानी है। This is a poignant story.
(yah ek marmasparshii kahaanii hai।)
