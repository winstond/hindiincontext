सिनेमा (sinemaa)

noun A loanword from English 'cinema', used both for the movie theater building and for the medium/art form of film. One sense, two closely related uses.
Sense 1 : movie theater; the place where films are shown
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सिनेमाघर (sinemaaghar), थियेटर (thiyetar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech, सिनेमा जाना (sinemaa jaanaa, 'to go to the movies') is a classic weekend plan among friends and families -- you will also hear the more modern फिल्म देखने जाना (philm dekhne jaanaa, 'to go to watch a film') as an alternative that emphasizes the act of watching rather than the venue.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say सिनेमा में जाना (sinemaa men jaanaa, 'to go into the cinema') when they mean going to watch a movie -- native speakers usually just say सिनेमा जाना (sinemaa jaanaa, 'to go to the cinema') without में (men), treating सिनेमा (sinemaa) almost like a destination adverb rather than a location needing a postposition.

(aaj shaam ham sinemaa jaa rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : the art/medium of film, movies in general
noun, masculine
Synonyms: फ़िल्म (film, often used interchangeably for 'movies' in general)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used the way English speakers use 'cinema' abstractly, as in हिंदी सिनेमा (hindii sinemaa, 'Hindi cinema') to talk about Bollywood as an industry and tradition, rather than any single film or theater.
Sample Sentences:
