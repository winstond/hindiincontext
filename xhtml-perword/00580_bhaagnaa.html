भागना (bhaagnaa)

verb The everyday verb for 'to run' or 'to flee' -- movement away from a place, whether athletic running or escaping/fleeing a situation. One core sense, used both literally and figuratively.
Sense 1 : to run
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: दौड़ना (daudnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a kid late for school being yelled at: भागो, भागो! (bhaago, bhaago, 'run, run!') -- this verb is what a mother shouts when there's urgency, not the calm word for a morning jog.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for दौड़ना (daudnaa) in every 'run' context, but भागना (bhaagnaa) carries a stronger sense of urgency or fleeing -- a jogger दौड़ता है (daudtaa hai) for exercise, while someone भागता है (bhaagtaa hai) from danger or in a hurry.
Sample Sentences:
स्कूल के लिए देर हो रही है, भागो! It's getting late for school, run!
(skool ke lie der ho rahii hai, bhaago!)

Sense 2 : to flee, run away, escape
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: फ़रार होना (faraar honaa, formal/legal register), भाग जाना (bhaag jaanaa, compound form with completive sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word behind the classic Bollywood elopement plot -- लड़की घर से भाग गई (ladkii ghar se bhaag gaii, 'the girl ran away from home') is a stock dramatic line, since भागना (bhaagnaa) is the go-to verb for eloping or fleeing family/society's expectations.
Common Mistakes: 1) भाग जाना (bhaag jaanaa) is extremely common in this sense and often sounds more natural than the bare verb alone, since the compound emphasizes the completed act of escaping.
Sample Sentences:

(chor pulis ko dekhkar bhaag gayaa।)

Sense 3 : to avoid, shy away from (responsibility, work, a person)
verb, intransitive Used with से (se) marking the thing avoided: काम से भागना (kaam se bhaagnaa, 'to shirk work').
Synonyms: कतराना (katraanaa, to shy away, dodge), टालना (taalnaa, transitive: to postpone/avoid a task)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Said of someone dodging responsibility: वह ज़िम्मेदारी से भागता है (vah zimmedaarii se bhaagtaa hai, 'he runs from responsibility') -- a common scolding line from elders about a lazy or irresponsible person.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense always takes से (se) on the avoided thing -- लोग मेहनत से भागते हैं (log mehnat se bhaagte hain, 'people shy away from hard work') -- learners sometimes drop the postposition since English 'avoid' takes a direct object.
