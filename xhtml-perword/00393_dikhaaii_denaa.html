दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to be visible, to come into view' -- built from दिखाई (dikhaaii, the noun 'visibility/sight') plus देना (denaa, 'to give'), and used with a को (ko)-marked experiencer, like दिखना (dikhnaa) but slightly more descriptive/formal in feel.
Sense 1 : to be visible, to come into sight
verb, intransitive Takes a को (ko)-marked experiencer as the logical subject, e.g. मुझे पहाड़ दिखाई दिया (mujhe pahaad dikhaaii diyaa, 'the mountain was visible to me' / 'I saw the mountain'); दिखाई (dikhaaii) itself stays invariant while देना (denaa) agrees with the visible thing.
Synonyms: दिखना (dikhnaa), नज़र आना (nazar aanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase frames seeing as something that happens *to* you rather than something you actively do -- much like नींद आना (niind aanaa) frames sleep as arriving on its own. It sits alongside दिखना (dikhnaa) as a near-synonym; दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa) is slightly more common in writing and careful speech, while दिखना (dikhnaa) feels a touch more casual in daily conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use ने (ne) with the experiencer, but this is a को (ko)-subject construction -- say मुझे दिखाई दिया (mujhe dikhaaii diyaa), never मैंने दिखाई दिया (mainne dikhaaii diyaa). 2) The agreement is with the thing seen, not the experiencer: वह लड़की दिखाई दी (vah ladkii dikhaaii dii, 'that girl was visible') takes feminine दी (dii) because लड़की (ladkii) is feminine, regardless of who is looking.

(yahaan se taajmahal dikhaaii detaa hai।)
