संख्या (sankhyaa)

noun A formal/technical word for 'number' or 'numeral' -- used for counting quantities, statistics, and mathematical figures rather than as the everyday word for 'a few' or 'how many'.
Sense 1 : number, quantity, numeral
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अंक (ank, digit/numeral), गिनती (gintii, counting/tally), तादाद (taadaad)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see संख्या (sankhyaa) everywhere in Indian news broadcasts and government notices -- 'मृतकों की संख्या' (mritkon kii sankhyaa, 'the number of the deceased') is a stock phrase in reporting. In daily bazaar or home talk, though, people just say कितने (kitne) or गिनती (gintii) instead of reaching for this more formal, almost bureaucratic word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for संख्या (sankhyaa) when they mean 'how many' in casual speech, but everyday conversation prefers कितने (kitne) or गिनती (gintii) -- संख्या (sankhyaa) sounds more like a report or form than a chat. 2) It is feminine, so agreeing adjectives and verbs must be feminine even though the word doesn't end in आ (aa) or ई (ii) -- e.g. संख्या बढ़ रही है (sankhyaa badh rahii hai, 'the number is increasing').

मृतकों की संख्या अभी तक स्पष्ट नहीं है। The number of the deceased is not yet clear.
(mritkon kii sankhyaa abhii tak spasht nahiin hai।)
