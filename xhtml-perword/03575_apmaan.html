अपमान (apmaan)

noun A single-sense noun meaning insult or disrespect -- the loss of one's honor or dignity in front of others.
Sense 1 : insult, disrespect, dishonor
noun, masculine Commonly used with करना (karnaa, 'to insult someone') and होना (honaa, 'to be insulted, to feel insulted'); the person insulted is marked with का (kaa) or is the subject of an experiencer construction, e.g. उसका अपमान हुआ (uskaa apmaan huaa, 'he was insulted').
Synonyms: बेइज़्ज़ती (beizztii), तौहीन (tauhiin, formal), निरादर (niraadar, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a culture where इज़्ज़त (izzat, 'honor/respect') carries huge social weight -- within families, at work, in public -- अपमान (apmaan) is a serious word, often invoked when someone feels publicly humiliated or slighted in front of others, not just mildly offended. You'll hear it in family arguments, workplace grievances, and news reports about public figures alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to say 'X insulted me' with a direct object construction; native speakers usually phrase it as मेरा अपमान किया (meraa apmaan kiyaa, 'made my insult' i.e. 'insulted me'), treating अपमान (apmaan) as the noun object of करना (karnaa) rather than using a single verb. 2) Don't confuse with माफ़ी (maafii, 'apology') or शर्म (sharm, 'shame') -- अपमान (apmaan) specifically centers on public loss of respect/honor, inflicted by someone, not just an internal feeling.
