संचालक (sanchaalak)

noun A formal, Sanskrit-derived word for someone who runs, directs, or operates something -- from a company/organization's director to a TV/radio show's host or anchor. Related to the verb संचालन करना (sanchaalan karnaa, 'to conduct/operate').
Sense 1 : director, manager, operator (of an organization/institution)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: निर्देशक (nirdeshak), प्रबंधक (prabandhak, manager), प्रमुख (pramukh, head/chief)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on nameplates and letterheads: school principals' offices, hospital administration boards, and NGO trust documents often list the संचालक (sanchaalak) as the top authority figure. It has a formal, official register -- everyday speech about 'who's in charge' more often uses मालिक (maalik) or बॉस (bos).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this interchangeably with प्रबंधक (prabandhak), but संचालक (sanchaalak) carries a stronger sense of 'the one who steers/runs the whole operation,' often used for founders, principals of institutions, or heads of trusts, rather than a mid-level manager.

Sense 2 : host, anchor, presenter (of a TV/radio show or event)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मेज़बान (mezbaan, for event host), प्रस्तुतकर्ता (prastutkartaa, presenter)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in credits or announcements for a game show or news program: 'इस कार्यक्रम के संचालक हैं...' (is kaaryakram ke sanchaalak hain, 'the host of this program is...'). It has a slightly more formal, scripted feel than एंकर (enkar), which has become common as a loanword for TV news anchors specifically.

Sense 3 : operator, driver (of a machine or vehicle, technical/formal)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चालक (chaalak, driver, more common for vehicles)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense is fairly low-frequency in daily conversation; it shows up in technical manuals, government notices, or job titles at factories and power plants rather than casual talk about who's driving.
Common Mistakes: 1) For everyday 'driver' (car, bus, rickshaw), learners should reach for चालक (chaalak) or ड्राइवर (draaivar) instead -- संचालक (sanchaalak) in this sense appears mostly in technical or official contexts, like 'plant operator' or 'machine operator' in a factory report.
