किसान (kisaan)

noun A single, straightforward sense: a person who farms the land. No hidden complexity here -- the richness is in seeing it used across everyday and civic contexts.
Sense 1 : farmer
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कृषक (krishak, formal), खेतिहर (khetihar, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: किसान (kisaan) is a word loaded with civic and political weight in India, not just an agricultural label -- you hear it constantly in news about किसान आंदोलन (kisaan aandolan, 'farmers' movement'), government schemes, and मंडी (mandii, 'market') prices. In everyday village or small-town talk, it carries real respect, evoking the person who feeds the nation -- अन्नदाता (anndaataa, 'giver of grain') is a poetic near-synonym you will meet in this same emotional register.
