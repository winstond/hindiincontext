मुन्नी (munnii)

noun An affectionate term for a little girl; also used as a common pet name/nickname for a female child within a family.
Sense 1 : little girl, girl child (affectionate)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बच्ची (bachchii), गुड़िया (gudiyaa, affectionate, 'doll')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every family seems to have a मुन्नी (munnii) at home, it is one of the most common pet names for a young daughter, alongside the male equivalent मुन्ना (munnaa). You will also hear it used generically, like calling out to any little girl in the neighborhood, similar to how English speakers might say 'sweetie' or 'little one.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as a formal/neutral word for 'girl'; it is inherently affectionate and used mainly by family members or in casual, warm address, not in neutral descriptions of a stranger's child.

Sense 2 : term of endearment for a woman (colloquial, often romantic/flirtatious)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जान (jaan, term of endearment)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense got a huge pop-culture boost from the Bollywood song 'Munni Badnaam Hui' (munnii badnaam huii), where मुन्नी (munnii) is used as a playful, teasing nickname for a grown woman rather than a literal child. It's the kind of word you'd hear a friend tease another friend with, not something to use in formal settings.
Common Mistakes: 1) This usage is casual and often flirtatious or playful, using it toward someone you don't know well can come across as overly familiar or cheeky rather than simply polite.
