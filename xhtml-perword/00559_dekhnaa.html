देखना (dekhnaa)

verb The basic verb 'to see/look/watch,' which also extends to 'to check/find out,' 'to consider/regard,' and shows up as a light verb after a verb stem to mean 'try doing something.' Notes: heavily used as an auxiliary in the -कर देखना (-kar dekhnaa) construction covered in the second sense.
Sense 1 : to see, look at, watch
verb, transitive
Synonyms: निहारना (nihaarnaa, literary), ताकना (taaknaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the first verbs every learner picks up, and it stretches further than English 'see': ज़रा देखो (zaraa dekho, 'just look') is a very common way to draw someone's attention, said constantly by parents, shopkeepers, and friends alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सुनना (sunnaa) or समझना (samajhnaa) when देखना (dekhnaa) is actually what a native speaker would use, e.g. फ़िल्म देखना (film dekhnaa, 'to watch a movie')- देखना covers both 'watch' and 'see' contexts English splits apart. 2) With things you check by looking, like a watch or the time, Hindi still uses देखना (dekhnaa) -- घड़ी देखना (ghadii dekhnaa, 'to check the time') -- where a learner might instead reach for 'check.'
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा देखो, बाहर बारिश हो रही है। Just look, it's raining outside.
(zaraa dekho, baahar baarish ho rahii hai।)

Sense 2 : to try, to see if (as a light verb after a verb stem)
verb, intransitive Formed as verb stem + कर (kar) + देखना (dekhnaa), e.g. खाकर देखो (khaakar dekho, 'try eating it'). This -कर देखना (-kar dekhnaa) pattern is how Hindi expresses 'try X-ing' and is distinct from the plain infinitive + light-verb compounds covered elsewhere.
Synonyms: कोशिश करना (koshish karnaa, different construction, not a direct swap)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A shopkeeper offering you a sample will often say चखकर देखो (chakhkar dekho, 'taste and see') -- this construction is everywhere in markets and kitchens, encouraging someone to test something out for themselves rather than just take your word for it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the कर (kar) and just say the stem plus देखना (dekhnaa), which sounds off -- the कर is required to signal 'do this and then see.' 2) This is not the verb to use for 'try on clothes' -- that is पहनकर देखना (pahankar dekhnaa) specifically, or often just पहनना (pahannaa) alone in context, so learners should not assume देखना (dekhnaa) alone covers trying on.
Sample Sentences:
एक बार चखकर देखो, बहुत अच्छा बना है। Taste it once and see, it has turned out very good.
(ek baar chakhkar dekho, bahut achchhaa banaa hai।)

Sense 3 : to consider, regard, treat (someone/something as)
verb, transitive Often appears in the pattern X को Y की तरह/नज़र से देखना (X ko Y kii tarah dekhnaa, 'to see/regard X as Y').
Synonyms: मानना (maannaa, to consider/believe), समझना (samajhnaa, to consider/take as)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Parents often say अपने बच्चों को एक जैसा देखना चाहिए (apne bachchon ko ek jaisaa dekhnaa chaahie, 'one should regard one's children equally') -- देखना (dekhnaa) here shifts from physical sight to a stance or attitude, much like English 'I see her as a friend.'

Sense 4 : to check, find out, look into
verb, transitive
Synonyms: जांचना (jaanchnaa, to inspect/verify), पता करना (pataa karnaa, to find out)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In offices and homes alike, ज़रा देखो तो (zaraa dekho to, 'just take a look, will you') is a very common polite request to look into or verify something, from a bill to a bag's contents.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes overuse चेक करना (chek karnaa) borrowed from English when देखना (dekhnaa) is the more natural native choice, e.g. ज़रा देख लो (zaraa dekh lo, 'just check it') for checking an email or a document.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा देखो तो, गैस बंद है या नहीं। Just check, will you, whether the gas is off or not.
