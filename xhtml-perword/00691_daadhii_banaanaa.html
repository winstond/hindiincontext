दाढ़ी बनाना (daadhii banaanaa)

verb A fixed verb phrase meaning 'to shave (the beard)'; literally 'to make/fashion the beard' -- दाढ़ी (daadhii) means 'beard' and बनाना (banaanaa) means 'to make.' Used both for shaving one's own beard and for someone shaving another's.
Sense 1 : to shave (one's beard/face)
verb, transitive Used with an oblique possessive or the reflexive अपनी (apnii) when shaving oneself: अपनी दाढ़ी बनाना (apnii daadhii banaanaa, 'to shave one's own beard'). When someone else does the shaving (e.g., a barber), the person shaved is often the object or marked with की (kii): नाई ने उसकी दाढ़ी बनाई (naaii ne uskii daadhii banaaii, 'the barber shaved him').
Synonyms: शेव करना (shev karnaa, colloquial, from English 'shave'), हजामत बनाना (hajaamat banaanaa, traditional/barber-shop term)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Going to the नाई (naaii, 'barber') for दाढ़ी बनाना (daadhii banaanaa) is still a common ritual in Delhi-NCR neighborhoods, especially before weddings or festivals, where a clean shave is considered a mark of being 'ready' or presentable. The phrase captures a broader Hindi habit of using बनाना (banaanaa, 'to make') for grooming actions, similar to बाल बनाना (baal banaanaa, 'to do one's hair').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say मैं दाढ़ी बनाया (main daadhii banaayaa) forgetting ने-marking; since बनाना (banaanaa) is transitive, the past requires ने: मैंने दाढ़ी बनाई (mainne daadhii banaaii). 2) Note दाढ़ी (daadhii) is feminine, so the verb agrees with it, not with the (often unstated) subject: मैंने दाढ़ी बनाई (mainne daadhii banaaii), not बनाया (banaayaa). 3) Some learners default to शेव करना (shev karnaa) exclusively; दाढ़ी बनाना (daadhii banaanaa) is the more traditional and equally common phrase, especially with older speakers.
