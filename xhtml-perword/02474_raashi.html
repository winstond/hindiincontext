राशि (raashi)

noun Primarily a noun meaning 'amount/sum' (especially of money), with an extended astrological sense of 'zodiac sign' -- the same word Hindi speakers use when discussing horoscopes.
Sense 1 : sum, amount (usually of money)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रकम (rakam), मात्रा (maatraa, quantity in general, less money-specific)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly on bank slips, utility bills, and government forms -- कुल राशि (kul raashi), जमा राशि (jamaa raashi), and बकाया राशि (bakaayaa raashi) are everyday bureaucratic phrases in India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मात्रा (maatraa) when talking about money, but राशि (raashi) is the natural word in banking, bills, and forms -- e.g. कुल राशि (kul raashi, 'total amount') on a receipt. 2) राशि (raashi) is feminine, so watch agreement: राशि जमा हुई (raashi jamaa huii, 'the amount was deposited'), not the masculine form.

Sense 2 : zodiac sign
noun, feminine Used in the fixed phrase राशिफल (raashiphal, 'horoscope/zodiac forecast') and in questions like आपकी राशि क्या है? (aapkii raashi kyaa hai?, 'What is your zodiac sign?').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Asking someone's राशि (raashi) is common small talk in many Indian households, especially around matchmaking, where families check compatibility between two राशियाँ (raashiyaan) before a wedding is arranged.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse राशि (raashi) 'zodiac sign' with कुंडली (kundlii, 'birth chart') -- राशि is just the sign itself (like Leo or Scorpio), not the full astrological chart.
