जुरमाना (jurmaanaa)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning a monetary penalty imposed for breaking a rule or law. Also commonly spelled जुर्माना (jurmaanaa) with a nukta-marked र्; both forms are heard constantly in speech.
Sense 1 : fine, monetary penalty
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दंड (dand, formal/legal), शुल्क (shulk, meaning scope: fee/charge, not always punitive)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear from a traffic cop (चालान chaalaan, the ticket itself, often travels alongside जुरमाना jurmaanaa, the amount you owe), or from a librarian tallying up a late fee. Delhi-NCR conversation almost always prefers जुरमाना (jurmaanaa) or जुर्माना (jurmaanaa) over the stiffer-sounding दंड (dand), which shows up more in written law or formal announcements.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for दंड (dand) as a direct synonym, but दंड (dand) leans formal/legal or even physical punishment in some contexts, while जुरमाना (jurmaanaa) is the everyday word for a cash fine (traffic ticket, library fine, late fee). 2) The common verb pairing is जुरमाना लगना (jurmaanaa lagnaa, 'a fine to be imposed,' intransitive, with the person taking को) or जुरमाना लगाना (jurmaanaa lagaanaa, 'to impose a fine,' transitive) or जुरमाना भरना/देना (jurmaanaa bharnaa/jurmaanaa denaa, 'to pay a fine') -- mixing up लगना (lagnaa) and भरना/देना (bharnaa/denaa) is a frequent error since English just uses 'pay' for both directions.

(traiphik pulis ne use laal battii paar karne par jurmaanaa kiyaa।)
