अंधेरा (andheraa)

noun/adjective Mainly means 'darkness' as a noun, and 'dark' as an adjective describing something (a room, a night, a path) -- the two senses share the same root idea and the same form.
Sense 1 : darkness
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तम (tam, literary), काला (kaalaa, meaning scope: refers to 'black' not 'darkness' itself)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Hindi, darkness is often treated almost like weather -- it 'happens' or 'comes' (अंधेरा हो गया, andheraa ho gayaa, 'it got dark') rather than being a state you simply are in, which mirrors how लगना (lagnaa) and other involuntary-experience verbs work in the language. You'll hear this word constantly in the context of बिजली जाना (bijlii jaanaa, 'power going out') -- when the lights go, someone will say अंधेरा हो गया (andheraa ho gayaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to say 'it is dark' with है (hai) alone, but native speakers usually use the dative-subject frame अंधेरा है (andheraa hai, 'there is darkness') or अंधेरा हो गया (andheraa ho gayaa, 'it has become dark') rather than an adjective + copula construction like English 'it is dark.' 2) अंधेरा (andheraa) as a noun is masculine, so verbs and adjectives agreeing with it take masculine forms, e.g. घना अंधेरा (ghanaa andheraa, 'thick darkness'), not feminine.

बिजली चली गई और कमरे में अंधेरा हो गया। The power went out and darkness fell in the room.
(bijlii chalii gaii aur kamre men andheraa ho gayaa।)

Sense 2 : dark (describing a place, room, night, etc.)
adjective
Synonyms: काला (kaalaa, meaning scope: 'black,' used for color rather than absence of light)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: As an adjective this word inflects normally, so you will meet अंधेरा कमरा (andheraa kamraa, 'dark room'), अंधेरी रात (andherii raat, 'dark night'), and अंधेरे रास्ते (andhere raaste, 'dark paths') -- the same word simply agreeing with the noun it describes.

Sense 3 : figurative darkness (ignorance, hopelessness, despair)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अज्ञान (agyaan, meaning scope: specifically 'ignorance', literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just like in English, अंधेरा (andheraa) extends metaphorically to mean confusion, ignorance, or despair -- ज़िंदगी में अंधेरा (zindgii men andheraa, 'darkness in life') is a common way to talk about hard times, and you'll hear it often in songs, speeches, and heartfelt conversation about struggle and hope.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी ज़िंदगी में बहुत अंधेरा था। There was a lot of darkness in his life.
(uskii zindgii men bahut andheraa thaa।)
