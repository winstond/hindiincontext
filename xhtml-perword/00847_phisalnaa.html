फिसलना (phisalnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning 'to slip/slide,' used both literally (feet, objects on a wet surface) and figuratively (a slip of the tongue, a moral lapse).
Sense 1 : to slip, to lose one's footing
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: लुढ़कना (ludhaknaa, to roll/topple, not identical but related), गिरना (girnaa, to fall, broader sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi winter brings the same complaint: सड़क पर कुहासा है, गाड़ी फिसल सकती है (sadak par kuhaasaa hai, gaadii phisal saktii hai, 'there's fog on the road, the car could skid') -- फिसलना (phisalnaa) is the go-to verb for anything from a banana peel mishap to a car skidding on a foggy flyover.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for गिरना (girnaa) when they mean the loss-of-grip moment itself; फिसलना (phisalnaa) is specifically the slide/skid, which may or may not end in a fall. 2) The surface or cause is usually marked with पर (par) or से (se), as in बर्फ़ पर फिसलना (barf par phisalnaa, 'to slip on ice') -- dropping this makes the sentence feel incomplete.
Sample Sentences:
साबुन की वजह से बाथरूम में फर्श फिसलता है। Because of the soap, the bathroom floor gets slippery.
(saabun kii vajah se baathroom men pharsh phisaltaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to slip out (of words), to make an unintended remark
verb, intransitive Common frame: मुँह से बात फिसल जाना (munh se baat phisal jaanaa, 'for a remark to slip out of one's mouth') -- the compound with जाना (jaanaa) marks the suddenness/unintentionality.
Synonyms: निकल जाना (nikal jaanaa, of words escaping unintentionally)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this when someone regrets a blurted-out secret: मेरे मुँह से बात फिसल गई (mere munh se baat phisal gaii, 'the words just slipped out of my mouth') -- it politely frames the slip as an accident rather than a deliberate act, which softens the confession.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense almost always appears with से (se) marking the mouth/tongue as the source -- मुँह से (munh se) -- rather than describing a person as the grammatical agent in a simple active sentence.

Sense 3 : to lapse morally, to lose one's footing in judgment/character
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बहक जाना (bahak jaanaa, to be led astray, slightly stronger connotation)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Elders often use फिसलना (phisalnaa) gently rather than accusingly when talking about a young person's misstep -- जवानी में इंसान फिसल जाता है (javaanii men insaan phisal jaataa hai, 'a person slips up in their youth') -- treating the lapse as a fall rather than a fixed flaw, which leaves room for recovery.
