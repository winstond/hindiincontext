समझ (samajh)

noun Primarily a feminine noun meaning 'understanding' or 'sense', and also the bare stem used as the base for the common verb समझना (samajhnaa, 'to understand'); this entry covers the noun sense and the closely related verb sense built directly on this stem.
Sense 1 : understanding, comprehension, sense
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बुद्धि (buddhi, formal), सूझ-बूझ (soojh-boojh, practical wisdom), अक्ल (akl)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: The idiom समझ में आना (samajh men aanaa, 'to come into one's understanding') is the everyday way Hindi speakers say 'I understand' or 'it makes sense' -- notice that understanding is framed as something that arrives into you, much like sleep in नींद आना (niind aanaa), rather than something you actively do. You'll hear parents scold kids with इतनी भी समझ नहीं है? (itnii bhii samajh nahiin hai?, 'don't you even have that much sense?') across Delhi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बुद्धि (buddhi) when समझ (samajh) is more natural in everyday speech; बुद्धि (buddhi) sounds more formal/academic, like 'intellect', while समझ (samajh) is what you'd use for ordinary practical understanding. 2) In phrases like मेरी समझ में नहीं आता (merii samajh men nahiin aataa, 'it doesn't come into my understanding' i.e. 'I don't get it'), the verb is आना (aanaa) not समझना (samajhnaa) -- learners try to force the verb समझना (samajhnaa) into this frame and get the construction wrong.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे तुम्हारी बात समझ में आ गई। I understood what you said.
(mujhe tumhaarii baat samajh men aa gaii।)

Sense 2 : opinion, way of thinking, judgment
noun, feminine Used in the construction ...की समझ से (kii samajh se, 'in ...'s view/opinion'), e.g. मेरी समझ से (merii samajh se, 'in my view').
Synonyms: राय (raay), नज़रिया (nazriyaa, perspective)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: मेरी समझ से (merii samajh se, 'in my view') is a softer, more conversational way to preface an opinion than मेरे विचार से (mere vichaar se, 'in my opinion'), which leans a touch more formal.
Common Mistakes: 1) मेरी समझ से (merii samajh se) means 'in my opinion/as I understand it', not 'in my understanding' in the comprehension sense -- learners sometimes confuse this with the core 'understanding' sense above when it is really closer to a personal viewpoint or judgment call.
