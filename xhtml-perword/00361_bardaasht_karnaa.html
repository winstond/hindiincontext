बरदाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to tolerate, bear, put up with' something unpleasant -- physical pain, bad behavior, or difficult situations. Also spelled बर्दाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa) with the nuqta-free/nuqta variation on the second consonant; both forms are common.
Sense 1 : to tolerate, endure, put up with
verb, transitive Takes a direct object marking the thing endured: बरदाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa) + noun/nahin + can, e.g. दर्द बरदाश्त करना (dard bardaasht karnaa, 'to bear pain'). Very frequently used in the negative with नहीं (nahiin) to express a limit being reached.
Synonyms: सहना (sahnaa), सहन करना (sahan karnaa), झेलना (jhelnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word Delhi aunties and uncles reach for constantly -- 'ये गर्मी बरदाश्त नहीं होती' (ye garmii bardaasht nahiin hotii, 'I just can't bear this heat') is a monsoon-season staple. It carries a slightly stronger, more visceral sense than सहना (sahnaa) -- less 'quiet endurance' and more 'gritting your teeth against something that's really testing you.' The word comes from Persian/Urdu, giving it a slightly more urban, everyday-conversational flavor than its Sanskritic cousin सहन करना (sahan karnaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सहना (sahnaa) alone when बरदाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa) is what actually gets used in spoken Hindi for everyday complaints -- 'I can't stand this noise/heat/person' is almost always बरदाश्त नहीं होना (bardaasht nahiin honaa) or बरदाश्त नहीं कर सकता (bardaasht nahiin kar saktaa), not सहना (sahnaa). 2) The related impersonal/involuntary construction मुझसे बरदाश्त नहीं होता (mujhse bardaasht nahiin hotaa, 'I just can't bear it') uses से (se)-marked subject with an intransitive/impersonal होना (honaa) pairing, distinct from the transitive करना (karnaa) version -- learners often try to force करना (karnaa) into every sentence when the से...होना (se...honaa) pattern is what a native speaker would actually say.
Sample Sentences:
मुझसे ये बदतमीज़ी बरदाश्त नहीं होती। I just can't tolerate this rudeness.
(mujhse ye badatmiizii bardaasht nahiin hotii।)
