संयुक्ताक्षर (sanyuktaakshar)

noun A grammatical/linguistic term meaning 'conjunct consonant' or 'ligature' -- the joined-together consonant clusters you see in Devanagari script, like क्ष (ksh) or ज्ञ (gy).
Sense 1 : conjunct consonant, consonant cluster/ligature in script
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जोड़ाक्षर (jodaakshar, rare)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet this word mainly in a Hindi classroom or language-textbook context, when a teacher explains why क् (k) plus ष (sh) fuse into the single glyph क्ष (ksh). It is built from संयुक्त (sanyukt, 'joined/united') plus अक्षर (akshar, 'letter/syllable'), so knowing those two pieces makes the compound transparent rather than something to memorize by rote.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with संयुक्त अक्षर (sanyukt akshar) written as two separate words -- संयुक्ताक्षर (sanyuktaakshar) is the standard single compound noun used in grammar contexts. 2) This is a technical/classroom term -- in casual speech people are far more likely to just point at the letter and say 'यह जुड़ा हुआ है' (yah judaa huaa hai, 'this is joined') than to use this word.
Sample Sentences:
क्ष एक संयुक्ताक्षर है। Ksh is a conjunct letter.
(ksh ek sanyuktaakshar hai।)
