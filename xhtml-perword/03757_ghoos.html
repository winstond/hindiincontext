घूस (ghoos)

noun A single, everyday noun meaning 'bribe' -- the money or favor given to get something done through illegitimate means, especially with officials.
Sense 1 : bribe
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रिश्वत (rishvat), भ्रष्टाचार (bhrashtaachaar, corruption, broader term), उगाही (ugaahii, extortion money, not a direct synonym but often heard alongside it)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: घूस (ghoos) is the word you will actually hear on the street or in a taxi when someone complains about a clerk or traffic constable -- रिश्वत (rishvat) feels a notch more formal, the word a newspaper or an FIR would use. The common expression घूस खानी पड़ी (ghoos khaanii padii, 'had to pay/give a bribe') uses खाना (khaanaa) from the bribe-taker's point of view, and Hindi speakers extend this 'eating' metaphor to any illicit gain, not just food.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for रिश्वत (rishvat) since it is the word taught in textbooks, but घूस (ghoos) is the far more common word in spoken, everyday Hindi -- especially in complaints about officials, police, or paperwork. 2) घूस (ghoos) pairs naturally with the verb खाना (khaanaa) rather than लेना (lenaa) -- घूस खाना (ghoos khaanaa, 'to take/eat a bribe') is the idiomatic collocation a learner should internalize, on the model of पैसा खाना (paisaa khaanaa, 'to embezzle money').
