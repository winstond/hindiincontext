रात का खाना (raat kaa khaanaa)

noun A fixed noun phrase meaning 'dinner/supper' -- literally 'night's food.' Built from रात (raat, night) + का (kaa, possessive marker) + खाना (khaanaa, food/meal).
Sense 1 : dinner, the evening meal
noun, masculine The का (kaa) agrees with खाना (khaanaa) as masculine singular; it does not change even though रात (raat) is feminine, since agreement follows the noun being possessed, not रात (raat) itself.
Synonyms: डिनर (dinar, English loanword, common in urban speech), रात्रि भोजन (raatri bhojan, formal/literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi-NCR households, रात का खाना (raat kaa khaanaa) is typically eaten later than in the West, often between 8 and 10 pm, and is usually lighter than lunch. You will also hear डिनर (dinar) used casually in urban, English-mixed speech, especially among younger speakers or in restaurant contexts, while रात का खाना (raat kaa khaanaa) remains the natural, homely term used within families.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes wrongly change का (kaa) to की (kii) by mistakenly agreeing it with रात (raat) (feminine) instead of खाना (khaanaa) (masculine) -- the possessive marker always agrees with the following noun. 2) Confusing this with रात को खाना (raat ko khaanaa, 'to eat at night' -- a verb phrase) rather than the noun phrase 'dinner.'
