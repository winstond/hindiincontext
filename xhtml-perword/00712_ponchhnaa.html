पोंछना (ponchhnaa)

verb A single core sense: to wipe or mop something clean with a cloth or similar.
Sense 1 : to wipe, mop, clean by wiping
verb, transitive
Synonyms: साफ़ करना (saaf karnaa, broader, not wipe-specific)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a kitchen-and-household staple verb -- you will hear it constantly in phrases like हाथ पोंछना (haath ponchhnaa, 'to wipe one's hands') or आँसू पोंछना (aansoo ponchhnaa, 'to wipe away tears'), the latter carrying real emotional weight in Hindi films and everyday tenderness, like a mother wiping a child's tears. The compound झाड़ू-पोंछा (jhaadoo-ponchhaa) pairs it with sweeping as the standard term for daily house-cleaning in most Indian homes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for साफ़ करना (saaf karnaa) whenever they mean 'clean,' but पोंछना (ponchhnaa) specifically implies the physical action of wiping with a cloth, hand, or mop -- you would not use it for, say, cleaning a house in general (झाड़ू-पोंछा करना (jhaadoo-ponchhaa karnaa, to sweep and mop) is the set phrase for household cleaning). 2) The object being wiped takes ने-marking in the perfective as expected for a transitive verb, but learners often forget the surface being wiped away (like water or dirt) can also appear as the object, e.g. पानी पोंछना (paanii ponchhnaa, 'to wipe up water') versus मेज़ पोंछना (mez ponchhnaa, 'to wipe the table') -- both are grammatical, just different objects for the same verb.
Sample Sentences:
माँ ने बच्चे के आँसू पोंछे। The mother wiped the child's tears.
(maan ne bachche ke aansoo ponchhe।)
