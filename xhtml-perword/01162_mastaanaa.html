मस्ताना (mastaanaa)

adjective/verb Two related strands: an adjective describing a carefree, intoxicated-with-joy quality, and a rarer intransitive verb meaning to be lost in that same euphoric abandon. Both trace to मस्त (mast, carefree/high-spirited).
Sense 1 : carefree, intoxicatingly joyful, swaying with abandon
adjective
Synonyms: मस्त (mast), बेपरवाह (beparvaah), झूमता (jhoomtaa, describes swaying motion)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This is very much a Bollywood-lyric word -- it shows up in song titles and refrains describing a mood of blissful, swaying abandon (a mast (mast) mood taken up a notch). You will hear it far more in songs and poetry than in daily bazaar chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat this as a plain synonym of खुश (khush, happy), but मस्ताना (mastaanaa) carries a specific flavor of swaying, unselfconscious abandon -- think of someone dancing with eyes closed, not just smiling. 2) It leans poetic/song-register; in ordinary conversation मस्त (mast) is the word people actually reach for.

Sense 2 : to become lost in joyful abandon, to revel
verb, intransitive Used almost always in continuous or participial forms describing a mood overtaking someone, e.g. मस्ताते हुए (mastaate hue, while reveling/swaying joyfully).
Synonyms: झूमना (jhoomnaa), मस्त होना (mast honaa)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This verb form is rare outside of poetic or lyrical description -- you are more likely to meet it as मस्ताता (mastaataa) or मस्ताते (mastaate) describing wind, youth, or a crowd swaying with joy than as a verb someone would use about their own day.
