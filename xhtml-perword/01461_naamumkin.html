नामुमकिन (naamumkin)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'impossible' -- the negative prefix ना (naa) attached to मुमकिन (mumkin, 'possible'), an Urdu loanword pair very common in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : impossible, not feasible
adjective
Synonyms: असंभव (asambhav), नामुमकिन-सा (naamumkin-saa, colloquial intensifier form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a certain dramatic flair -- it shows up constantly in Bollywood dialogue and everyday exclamations like यह नामुमकिन है! (yah naamumkin hai!, 'this is impossible!'). Note the family resemblance to मुमकिन (mumkin, 'possible') and नामुमकिन's more Sanskritic cousin असंभव (asambhav) -- both pairs are common, but नामुमकिन/मुमकिन feel a touch more colloquial and heartfelt.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for असंभव (asambhav) by default since it looks 'more standard,' but नामुमकिन (naamumkin) is actually the more common choice in spoken Hindi, especially in emotional or dramatic statements. 2) As an invariant adjective, it does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- नामुमकिन बात (naamumkin baat, 'impossible thing') and नामुमकिन काम (naamumkin kaam, 'impossible task') both keep the same ending.
Sample Sentences:
यह नामुमकिन है! This is impossible!
(yah naamumkin hai!)
