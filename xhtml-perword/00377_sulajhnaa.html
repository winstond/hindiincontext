सुलझना (sulajhnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning to become untangled or to get resolved -- used both literally (thread, hair, knots) and figuratively (problems, confusion, mysteries). Its transitive counterpart सुलझाना (suljhaanaa, 'to untangle/resolve') is a separate causative lemma.
Sense 1 : to get resolved, be sorted out (of a problem, confusion, mystery)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: हल होना (hal honaa), सुधरना (sudharnaa, partial overlap: 'to improve')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi loves to talk about problems as if they were physical threads: a confusing situation is उलझा हुआ (uljhaa huaa, 'tangled') and a solved one is सुलझा हुआ (suljhaa huaa, 'untangled'). You will hear लोग मसला सुलझने का इंतज़ार कर रहे हैं (log maslaa sulajhne kaa intzaar kar rahe hain, 'people are waiting for the matter to get resolved') often in news contexts about disputes or negotiations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हल करना (hal karnaa) constructions when they actually want the intransitive सुलझना (sulajhnaa) -- 'the problem got resolved' is मसला सुलझ गया (maslaa sulajh gayaa, 'the problem got resolved'), not something the problem does to itself with an agent marked by ने (ne). 2) The completive उलझना/सुलझना (sulajhnaa) pair is easy to mix up in meaning: उलझना (ulajhnaa) means to get tangled/confused, सुलझना (sulajhnaa) is its resolution -- they are opposites, not synonyms.
Sample Sentences:
यह मसला अभी तक नहीं सुलझा है। This issue hasn't been resolved yet.
(yah maslaa abhii tak nahiin suljhaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to become untangled (literal: thread, hair, knots)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: खुलना (khulnaa, overlap: 'to open/come loose')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this literally about hair or thread -- बाल उलझ गए हैं, कंघी से सुलझा लो (baal ulajh gae hain, kanghii se suljhaa lo, 'the hair has gotten tangled, untangle it with a comb') -- and this everyday domestic image is exactly what gets carried over into the figurative 'problem-solving' sense.
Sample Sentences:
बाल उलझ गए हैं, कंघी से सुलझा लो। The hair has gotten tangled, untangle it with a comb.
(baal ulajh gae hain, kanghii se suljhaa lo।)
