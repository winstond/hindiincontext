ढक्कन (dhakkan)

noun A concrete household noun: the lid or cap of a container, extended informally to mean a foolish or dim-witted person.
Sense 1 : lid, cap, cover
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ढकना (dhaknaa, variant form), आवरण (aavran, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian kitchen has a drawer or shelf full of mismatched ढक्कन (dhakkan) -- lids for pressure cookers, steel dabbas (boxes), and water bottles -- and finding the right one is a small daily ritual. You will hear this word constantly around the stove: गैस बंद करो, ढक्कन लगा दो (gais band karo, dhakkan lagaa do, 'turn off the gas, put the lid on').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse ढक्कन (dhakkan) with ढकना (dhaknaa) the verb 'to cover' -- ढक्कन (dhakkan) is only the physical object (the lid itself), never used as a verb.
Sample Sentences:

(patiile kaa dhakkan kahaan hai?)

Sense 2 : fool, dimwit (informal, mildly insulting)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बेवकूफ़ (bevkoof), उल्लू (ulloo, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is playground and family-banter language -- an elder brother might call his sibling ढक्कन (dhakkan) for doing something silly, the way English speakers say 'dumbo' or 'blockhead.' It softens the insult with humor rather than real anger.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is casual and mildly mocking, not neutral -- it is used among friends or in scolding, not in polite or formal address, so learners should avoid it with strangers or elders.
