खिलाना (khilaanaa)

verb Mainly means to feed someone (make them eat) khilaanaa, the causative of खाना (khaanaa, 'to eat'). It also has an extended sense of making something/someone 'bloom' or 'flourish,' and a distinct idiomatic sense tied to games/sports, letting or making someone play.
Sense 1 : to feed someone; to make/help someone eat
verb, transitive Causative of खाना (khaanaa); the person fed is the direct object, and the food is often marked with से (se) or added simply as a second object, e.g. उसे खाना खिलाना (use khaanaa khilaanaa, 'to feed him food').
Synonyms: भोजन कराना (bhojan karaanaa, more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Feeding someone -- खिलाना (khilaanaa) -- is loaded with affection in Hindi households; a mother or grandmother insisting रोटी खिलाओ (rotii khilaao, 'go feed him roti') is a small, constant act of care, and hosts often press guests to eat more as a form of hospitality.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use खाना (khaanaa) when they mean to feed someone else -- माँ बच्चे को खाना खिलाती है (maan bachche ko khaanaa khilaatii hai, 'the mother feeds the child') needs the causative खिलाना (khilaanaa), not खाना (khaanaa). 2) The person being fed takes को (ko), which can feel backwards to English speakers used to 'feed the child' with no preposition.

Sense 2 : to let/make someone play; to play a game with someone
verb, transitive Causative of खेलना (khelnaa, 'to play'), distinct in meaning from the 'feed' sense despite the identical spelling; the person who plays is the object, e.g. बच्चों को खिलाना (bachchon ko khilaanaa, 'to let the children play') or कोच खिलाड़ी को खिलाता है (koch khilaadii ko khilaataa hai, 'the coach fields/plays the player').
Synonyms: खेलने देना (khelne denaa, descriptive phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in cricket chatter -- selectors and captains खिलाना (khilaanaa) a player, meaning they give him a place in the playing eleven, a very live topic during India's cricket season.
Common Mistakes: 1) In sports contexts (cricket especially), खिलाना (khilaanaa) means 'to field/select a player to play,' as in टीम में खिलाना (tiim men khilaanaa, 'to include in the playing team') -- learners may not expect this specialized sense from the everyday 'feed' meaning.

Sense 3 : to make bloom or flourish (figurative)
verb, transitive Causative of खिलना (khilnaa, 'to bloom'); used both literally for flowers and figuratively for faces, moods, or fortunes, e.g. चेहरा खिला देना (chehraa khilaa denaa, 'to make a face light up').
Synonyms: खुशहाल बनाना (khushahaal banaanaa, figurative paraphrase)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Poets and songwriters love this image -- बहार खिलाना (bahaar khilaanaa, 'to make spring bloom') -- where khilaanaa turns an inner state, like joy, into something blossoming outward, much like calling a happy face 'khilaa hua' (khilaa huaa).
