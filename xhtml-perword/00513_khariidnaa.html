ख़रीदना (khariidnaa)

verb A single-sense everyday verb meaning 'to buy, to purchase.' Also spelled खरीदना (khariidnaa) without the nuqta, especially in casual writing.
Sense 1 : to buy, to purchase
verb, transitive
Synonyms: क्रय करना (kray karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday bazaar talk, ख़रीदना (khariidnaa) pairs constantly with बेचना (bechnaa, 'to sell') as the two poles of any market transaction -- you'll hear both in the same breath when someone describes a shopping trip or a business deal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget that being transitive, this verb takes ने (ne) with its subject in the perfective: मैंने साड़ी ख़रीदी (mainne saadii khariidii, 'I bought a sari'), not मैं ने ख़रीदा treated as intransitive. 2) The thing bought is often marked with से (se) for the seller/source (e.g. दुकानदार से (dukaandaar se, 'from the shopkeeper')), while the price uses में (men) or को (ko) depending on context -- learners often default to के लिए (ke lie) for price, which sounds off.
