शामिल (shaamil)

adjective/verb An adjective meaning 'included/involved/participating,' almost always used with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) to mean 'to be included/take part' or 'to include.'
Sense 1 : included, part of, involved in
adjective Typically used with होना (honaa) and the postposition में (men): X, Y में शामिल है (X, Y men shaamil hai, 'X is included in Y').
Synonyms: सम्मिलित (sammilit, formal), मौजूद (maujood, present/among)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a everyday, high-frequency word you'll hear constantly in office and school contexts -- meetings, guest lists, exam syllabuses. Someone checking a wedding invite list might say, 'क्या उसका नाम शामिल है? (kyaa uskaa naam shaamil hai, 'Is his/her name included?')' It has a mild formal-neutral tone, comfortable in both spoken and written Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the में (men) that marks what the thing is included *in* -- शामिल (shaamil) almost never stands alone without it. 2) Don't confuse with सामिल (saamil), a common misspelling without the visarga-like nuqta pattern; शामिल (shaamil) with श़ (sh) is the standard form.

Sense 2 : to participate, join in, take part
verb, intransitive Used as शामिल होना (shaamil honaa) with में (men): वह मीटिंग में शामिल हुआ (vah miiting men shaamil huaa, 'He took part in the meeting'). This is a light-verb compound built on the adjective sense of शामिल (shaamil).
Synonyms: भाग लेना (bhaag lenaa, to participate), हिस्सा लेना (hissaa lenaa, to take part)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Common in invitations and event contexts: आप शादी में शामिल होंगे? (aap shaadii men shaamil honge, 'Will you attend/join the wedding?'). This pairs naturally with the adjective sense above -- होना (honaa) turns the state 'included' into the action 'to join.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say शामिल करना (shaamil karnaa) when they mean 'to join/participate' -- but करना (karnaa) here shifts the meaning to 'to include (someone/something)', not 'to participate'. Keep होना (honaa) for 'joining' and करना (karnaa) for 'including'.

Sense 3 : to include, incorporate, add in
verb, transitive Used as शामिल करना (shaamil karnaa) with में (men): उसने मेरा नाम लिस्ट में शामिल किया (usne meraa naam list men shaamil kiyaa, 'He included my name in the list').
Synonyms: सम्मिलित करना (sammilit karnaa, formal), जोड़ना (jodnaa, to add)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the transitive counterpart to शामिल होना (shaamil honaa) -- one person or thing 'includes' another. You'll see this on forms and official notices: सभी दस्तावेज़ शामिल करें (sabhii dastaavez shaamil karen, 'include all documents').
