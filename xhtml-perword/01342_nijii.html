निजी (nijii)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'private' or 'personal' -- used for private property, personal matters, and private (as opposed to government/public) institutions.
Sense 1 : private, personal
adjective
Synonyms: व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat), निजी स्वामित्व वाला (nijii svaamitv vaalaa, private-property specific), खुद का (khud kaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech you'll hear the English loanword प्राइवेट (praaivet) just as often as निजी (nijii) -- 'private school,' 'private job' -- but निजी (nijii) is what shows up on government forms, in news headlines about निजी क्षेत्र (nijii kshetr, 'private sector'), and in slightly more formal or written contexts. It also carries a 'personal' sense close to व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat), as in निजी जीवन (nijii jiivan, 'personal life'), a phrase often used when someone wants to signal a topic is off-limits.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for अपना (apnaa) when they mean निजी (nijii) -- अपना (apnaa) is the possessive 'one's own' tied to a subject, while निजी (nijii) is a standalone descriptive adjective meaning 'private' and doesn't agree with any subject. 2) निजी (nijii) is commonly paired with school, hospital, company, etc. to mean 'private sector' (निजी स्कूल nijii skool, 'private school') -- learners often default to प्राइवेट (praaivet) here, which is also fine and extremely common in speech, but निजी (nijii) is the word you'll see in writing, forms, and news.
