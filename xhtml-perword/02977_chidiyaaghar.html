चिड़ियाघर (chidiyaaghar)

noun A single-sense everyday compound noun meaning 'zoo,' formed from चिड़िया (chidiyaa, 'bird') plus घर (ghar, 'house') -- literally 'bird-house,' though it refers to the whole zoo, not just a bird enclosure.
Sense 1 : zoo
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जंतुआलय (jantuaalay, formal), प्राणि उद्यान (praani udyaan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian city of any size has a चिड़ियाघर (chidiyaaghar), and a school trip there is a childhood rite of passage -- much like a class trip to the zoo anywhere else. The Delhi चिड़ियाघर (chidiyaaghar) near Purana Qila is a well-known weekend outing for families in the NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume from the literal parts (bird + house) that चिड़ियाघर (chidiyaaghar) means an aviary or birdhouse specifically -- in everyday use it means the whole zoo, with all animals, not just birds.
