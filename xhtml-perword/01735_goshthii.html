गोष्ठी (goshthii)

noun A single-sense formal noun meaning a gathering or seminar for discussion, typically academic or literary. Inline Hindi throughout uses placeholder romanization.
Sense 1 : a formal gathering for discussion, talk, or seminar
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सम्मेलन (sammelan, larger-scale conference), संगोष्ठी (sangoshthii, near-synonym, often used interchangeably), विचार-विमर्श (vichaar-vimarsh, focuses on the discussion itself rather than the event)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on posters and invitations for a कवि गोष्ठी (kavi goshthii, 'poets' gathering') -- a common fixture of Delhi's literary and cultural life where poets take turns reciting their work before an audience. It carries a certain intellectual, old-world charm, evoking university halls, Doordarshan-era cultural programs, and Hindi literary societies rather than a casual meetup.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use गोष्ठी (goshthii) for any casual hangout or party, but it specifically implies a structured discussion or talk, usually on a literary, academic, or intellectual topic -- not a social get-together with friends. 2) Confused with संगोष्ठी (sangoshthii); in practice both are used for seminars/conferences, with संगोष्ठी (sangoshthii) slightly more common in official/academic notices.
