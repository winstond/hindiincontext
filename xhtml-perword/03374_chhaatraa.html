छात्रा (chhaatraa)

noun A straightforward single-sense word: the feminine form of छात्र (chhaatr), meaning a female student.
Sense 1 : female student
noun, feminine
Synonyms: विद्यार्थिनी (vidyaarthinii, formal), लड़की (ladkii, meaning scope: girl in general, not specifically 'student')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word everywhere on Indian school and college signage -- छात्र-छात्राएँ (chhaatr-chhaatraaen, 'students, male and female') is the standard paired phrase used in formal announcements, notices, and speeches to address a mixed student body respectfully. It has a slightly more formal, administrative flavor than just calling someone a लड़की (ladkii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to छात्र (chhaatr) for all students regardless of gender; when referring specifically to a female student or a group of only female students, छात्रा (chhaatraa) / छात्राएँ (chhaatraaen) is the natural choice. 2) Don't confuse this with शिक्षिका (shikshikaa, 'female teacher') -- छात्रा is the one being taught, not teaching.
