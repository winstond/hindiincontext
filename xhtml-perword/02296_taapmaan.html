तापमान (taapmaan)

noun A single-sense technical/everyday noun meaning 'temperature', used for weather, body heat, cooking, and science alike.
Sense 1 : temperature
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly on the evening news and in casual weather chat -- आज का तापमान क्या है? (aaj kaa taapmaan kyaa hai, 'what is today's temperature') is a very standard opener for small talk about the weather in Delhi's extreme summers and winters. It is also the word doctors and nurses use for a patient's body temperature, distinct from the more colloquial बुखार (bukhaar) which implies illness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तापमान (taapmaan) with बुखार (bukhaar, 'fever'); तापमान is the neutral measurement word, used even for weather or an oven, while बुखार specifically means an elevated body temperature/illness. 2) The verb usually paired with it is बढ़ना/गिरना (badhnaa/girnaa, 'to rise/fall') rather than होना, e.g. तापमान बढ़ गया है (taapmaan badh gayaa hai, 'the temperature has risen') -- learners often default to है constructions alone and miss this natural verb pairing.
