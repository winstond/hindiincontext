ऊन (oon)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'wool', the fiber/material -- referring to raw wool as well as wool yarn used for knitting.
Sense 1 : wool (the fiber/yarn)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Every winter in Delhi-NCR you'll see women knitting स्वेटर (svetar, 'sweaters') with ऊन (oon) bought loose by weight from a shop, wound into balls at home -- it's a very hands-on, generational image, mothers and grandmothers knitting through the cold months. The related adjective ऊनी (oonii) is what you'll see on clothing labels and in shop signs advertising ऊनी कपड़े (oonii kapde, 'woolen clothes').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse ऊन (oon, 'wool') with ऊनी (oonii), the adjective 'woolen' -- ऊन का स्वेटर (oon kaa svetar, 'a sweater made of wool') uses the noun with का (kaa), while ऊनी स्वेटर (oonii svetar, 'a woolen sweater') uses the adjective directly, no का (kaa) needed. 2) ऊन (oon) itself does not change form to mean 'a ball of wool' or 'a strand of wool' -- learners often add unnecessary classifiers where Hindi speakers just say ऊन (oon) and rely on context.
