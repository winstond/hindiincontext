लेना (lenaa)

verb लेना (lenaa) is Hindi's basic verb for 'to take,' spanning physical taking/receiving, buying in the market, taking on responsibility, consuming food or medicine, conducting an exam or interview, and -- grammaticalized as a vector verb after another verb stem -- marking an action done to completion for oneself. It is the natural partner of देना (denaa, 'to give').
Sense 1 : to take, receive, accept (something offered or handed over)
verb, transitive The person or place something is taken from is marked with से (se): मैंने उससे किताब ली (mainne usse kitaab lii, 'I took the book from him').
Synonyms: ग्रहण करना (grahan karnaa, formal), स्वीकार करना (sviikaar karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This same sense covers respectful gestures like आशीर्वाद लेना (aashiirvaad lenaa, 'to take/receive blessings') when touching an elder's feet. The fixed idiom जन्म लेना (janm lenaa, 'to be born,' literally 'to take birth') also belongs here as a frozen expression rather than a separate meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse लेना (the receiver's verb) with देना (denaa, the giver's verb) -- 'give me the book' is किताब दो (kitaab do), not किताब लो (kitaab lo). 2) The source takes से (se), not के पास (ke paas), even though के पास marks possession elsewhere.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : completive/self-benefit vector verb (V-stem + लेना)
verb, transitive Attaches to the stem of a main verb (V + लेना) to mark an action as completed, often with the sense that the result benefits or stays with the subject: खा लिया (khaa liyaa, 'ate it up'), कर लेता है (kar letaa hai, 'gets it done [for himself]'), सीख लूँगा (siikh loongaa, 'I'll learn it [on my own]').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: लेना and देना form Hindi's classic self-benefit vs. other-benefit pair of vector verbs: कर लेना (kar lenaa) suggests doing something for your own sake, while कर देना (kar denaa) suggests doing it as a favor for someone else -- a distinction English simply doesn't mark grammatically.
Common Mistakes: 1) The main verb must appear as a bare stem, not an infinitive: खा लिया (khaa liyaa) is correct, खाना लिया (khaanaa liyaa) is not. 2) Because लेना itself is transitive, ने-marking and object agreement in the perfective follow the whole compound as a unit, e.g. उसने किताब पढ़ ली (usne kitaab padh lii, 'she read the book [through]').

verb, transitive
Synonyms: खाना (khaanaa, for food specifically), पीना (piinaa, for drinks specifically)

Sense 6 : to conduct, administer (an exam, interview, or class)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: आयोजित करना (aayojit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Teachers परीक्षा लेते हैं (pariikshaa lete hain, 'administer the exam') while students परीक्षा देते हैं (pariikshaa dete hain, 'sit the exam') -- the same लेना/देना logic that runs through so much of this verb's range shows up again here.
Common Mistakes: 1) The subject of लेना here is the examiner or instructor, not the student: परीक्षा लेना (pariikshaa lenaa) means 'to administer an exam,' while परीक्षा देना (pariikshaa denaa) means 'to sit/take an exam' as a student -- learners frequently mix these two up.
Sample Sentences:
आज मैडम हमारी हिंदी की परीक्षा लेंगी। Today the teacher will administer our Hindi exam.
