उत्तर (uttar)

noun Two everyday senses live in this one word: उत्तर (uttar) as 'answer/reply' (the word you'll meet constantly in school and exam contexts) and उत्तर (uttar) as the cardinal direction 'north.' They are unrelated in daily use but share the same spelling and gender.
Sense 1 : answer, reply
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जवाब (javaab), प्रत्युत्तर (pratyuttar, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Indian schoolkid and they'll tell you: उत्तर (uttar) is the word stamped all over exam papers -- 'सही उत्तर चुनिए (sahii uttar chunie, choose the correct answer)' is a phrase every student has stared at. In spoken life, though, जवाब (javaab) usually takes over.
Common Mistakes: 1) उत्तर देना (uttar denaa) and जवाब देना (javaab denaa) both mean 'to give an answer,' but उत्तर (uttar) leans more formal/written -- it's the word used on exam papers and in textbooks -- while जवाब (javaab) is what people actually say in casual conversation. Using उत्तर (uttar) in a casual chat isn't wrong, but it can sound a touch stiff.

Sense 2 : north (the cardinal direction)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same उत्तर (uttar) you see in the state name उत्तर प्रदेश (uttar pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, literally 'northern province') and उत्तराखंड (uttraakhand, Uttarakhand). Direction words like this show up often on road signs and weather reports, so it's worth knowing even though it's less central to daily chit-chat than the 'answer' sense.
