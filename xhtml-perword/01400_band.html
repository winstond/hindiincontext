बंद (band)

adjective/noun Mainly an adjective meaning 'closed/shut' and, extended from that, 'stopped/off', plus a related noun sense meaning 'closure/strike' and an adverbial-ish sense used with verbs like करना (karnaa) and होना (honaa).
Sense 1 : closed, shut
adjective
Synonyms: बंद (band), मुंदा (mundaa, less common, mainly for eyes/eyelids)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in daily life: shopkeepers announce दुकान बंद है (dukaan band hai, 'the shop is closed') at lunch break, and doors, windows, taps, and even phones (फ़ोन बंद है, fon band hai, 'the phone is off/unreachable') all take बंद (band) rather than a separate word for each kind of 'off'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use बंद (band) as a verb by itself; it is an adjective and needs होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) to actually express 'to close' -- दुकान बंद है (dukaan band hai, 'the shop is closed') vs. दुकान बंद हुई (dukaan band huii, 'the shop closed'). 2) For 'closed' referring to eyes, आँखें बंद करना (aankhen band karnaa) is the natural phrase, not a literal translation of 'shut your eyes.'
Sample Sentences:
दुकान बंद है, दोपहर में फिर आना। The shop is closed, come again in the afternoon.
(dukaan band hai, dopahar men phir aanaa।)

Sense 2 : turned off, not functioning, stopped (of machines, processes, services)
adjective
Synonyms: बंद (band), खराब (kharaab, 'out of order', for machines specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll use for everything from a dead phone battery to a stalled car to a canceled train service -- Hindi doesn't reach for separate vocabulary the way English does for 'off,' 'broken,' 'stalled,' and 'out of service.'
Common Mistakes: 1) For electronics, बंद (band) means 'switched off/not working,' while ऑफ़ (of) is also common colloquially -- both are fine, but बंद (band) is the native, everyday choice. 2) Don't confuse this with बंद (band) meaning a strike/shutdown (the noun sense) -- टीवी बंद है (tiivii band hai, 'the TV is off') vs. आज शहर में बंद है (aaj shahar men band hai, 'there's a shutdown/strike in the city today').

Sense 3 : a general strike, forced shutdown of businesses/transport as protest
noun, masculine Commonly heard as भारत बंद (bhaarat band, 'India shutdown') in news contexts, referring to a nationwide strike called by political parties or unions.
Synonyms: हड़ताल (hadtaal, more specifically a labor/worker strike)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बंद (band) as a 'shutdown protest' is a very Indian civic institution -- political parties or unions call a बंद (band) and shops voluntarily (or under pressure) pull down their shutters; you'll see the word plastered across news headlines like भारत बंद (bhaarat band) during major protests.
Common Mistakes: 1) This बंद (band) is a noun naming the event itself, distinct from the adjective sense -- आज बंद है (aaj band hai) can mean either 'it's closed today' or 'there's a strike today' depending on context, which can genuinely confuse learners.
