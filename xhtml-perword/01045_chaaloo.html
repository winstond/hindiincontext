चालू (chaaloo)

adjective Covers two related strands: a literal/functional sense ('running, operational, current') used for machines, systems, and ongoing schedules, and a figurative sense describing a person as 'shrewd, cunning, or sly' -- often with a disapproving edge.
Sense 1 : operational, running, switched on
adjective
Synonyms: जारी (jaarii), सक्रिय (sakriy)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly about gadgets and vehicles: TV चालू है (tiivii chaaloo hai, 'the TV is on'), गाड़ी चालू करो (gaadii chaaloo karo, 'start the car'). It is the everyday opposite of बंद (band, 'off/closed'), and Delhi shopkeepers and mechanics use this pair constantly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for चालू (chaaloo) to mean 'current' as in 'current month/year' -- that sense exists too (see the 'ongoing/present' sense) but is distinct from a machine being switched on. 2) चालू (chaaloo) describes the state of being on/running, not the act of turning something on -- for that, use चालू करना (chaaloo karnaa, 'to start/turn on') as a phrase, not चालू alone as a verb.

Sense 2 : current, ongoing, in progress (of a period or process)
adjective
Synonyms: मौजूदा (maujoodaa), वर्तमान (vartmaan, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This shows up in official and semi-formal language: चालू साल (chaaloo saal, 'the current year'), चालू महीना (chaaloo mahiinaa, 'the current month'). It is common on forms, notices, and in bank or government contexts, sitting a notch more formal than its machine-related cousin above.

Sense 3 : cunning, shrewd, sly (of a person)
adjective
Synonyms: शातिर (shaatir), चालबाज़ (chaalbaaz), घाघ (ghaagh, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you use for the shopkeeper who always finds a way to overcharge you, or the colleague who takes credit for others' work -- बड़ा चालू आदमी है (badaa chaaloo aadmii hai, 'he's a real sly one'). It shares its root with चलना (chalnaa, 'to move/run'), so the underlying image is of someone always 'working an angle,' always in motion finding tricks.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is usually mildly to strongly negative -- calling someone चालू (chaaloo) is rarely a compliment, unlike English 'clever' or 'smart' which can be neutral or admiring. 2) Don't confuse this with तेज़ (tez, 'sharp/quick-witted'), which can be praise; चालू (chaaloo) implies the person manipulates situations or people for personal gain.

बड़ा चालू आदमी है, हर किसी को उल्लू बना देता है। He's a real sly one, he makes a fool of everybody.
(badaa chaaloo aadmii hai, har kisii ko ulloo banaa detaa hai।)
