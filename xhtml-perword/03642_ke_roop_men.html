के रूप में (ke roop men)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'as' or 'in the capacity/form of' -- used to cast one thing in the role, form, or identity of another.
Sense 1 : as, in the capacity/role of
postposition Attaches to the oblique form of the preceding noun, e.g. एक अध्यापक के रूप में (ek adhyaapak ke roop men, 'as a teacher'). The noun before it takes no case marker of its own beyond triggering the oblique -- रूप (roop, 'form/shape') is itself the noun being put in the oblique with में (men, 'in').
Synonyms: की हैसियत से (kii haisiyat se, in the capacity of), की तरह (kii tarah, like, more about resemblance than role)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this constantly on resumes, in news reports, and in formal introductions: भारत के प्रधानमंत्री के रूप में (bhaarat ke pradhaanmantrii ke roop men, 'in the capacity of Prime Minister of India'). It has a slightly formal, written-register flavor compared to casual speech, though it is common enough in spoken Hindi too, especially when people describe their job or role.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for की तरह (kii tarah) when they mean के रूप में (ke roop men) -- की तरह (kii tarah) means 'like/similar to' (resemblance), while के रूप में (ke roop men) means 'in the role/identity of' (actual capacity). 'He works as a doctor' needs के रूप में (ke roop men), not की तरह (kii tarah), since he actually is one. 2) The words रूप (roop) and में (men) must both be present -- dropping में (men) and just saying के रूप (ke roop) is incomplete and sounds broken.

वह एक शिक्षक के रूप में काम करती है। She works as a teacher.
(vah ek shikshak ke roop men kaam kartii hai।)
