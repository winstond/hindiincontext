ख़राबी (kharaabii)

noun A noun meaning fault, defect, or malfunction -- the state of something being ख़राब (kharaab, 'bad/broken'). One core sense, used both for mechanical/physical faults and for more abstract trouble or discord.
Sense 1 : fault, defect, malfunction (of a thing)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गड़बड़ (gadbad), दोष (dosh, formal), नुक़्स (nuqs)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly with mechanics, electricians, and plumbers in Delhi: गाड़ी में कोई ख़राबी है (gaadii men koii kharaabii hai, 'there's some fault in the car'). It is the go-to word for anything from a wonky fan to a glitchy phone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say यह ख़राब है (yah kharaab hai, 'this is bad/broken') when they mean the object itself is faulty, but to name the specific fault inside it, ख़राबी use is needed: इसमें ख़राबी है (ismen kharaabii hai, 'there's a fault in it') -- note it takes में (men), not है alone describing the object. 2) ख़राबी is a noun naming the fault itself; ख़राब (kharaab) is the adjective describing the thing -- learners sometimes use them interchangeably by mistake.
Sample Sentences:
गाड़ी में कोई ख़राबी है। There's some fault in the car.
(gaadii men koii kharaabii hai।)

Sense 2 : trouble, disorder, discord (in a situation or relationship)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गड़बड़ी (gadabdii), बिगाड़ (bigaad)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word stretched to cover a relationship or a plan going sour, e.g. रिश्ते में ख़राबी आ गई (rishte men kharaabii aa gaii, 'trouble crept into the relationship') -- treating discord as something that 'comes' into a bond, much like a fault creeping into a machine.
