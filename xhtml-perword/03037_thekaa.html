ठेका (thekaa)

noun Has two main everyday senses: a business/legal 'contract' (for work, supply, or a lease), and, very commonly in Delhi-NCR speech, a government-licensed 'liquor shop.' Also has a niche musical sense (the rhythmic cycle in tabla accompaniment).
Sense 1 : a contract (for work, supply, or lease)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अनुबंध (anubandh, formal), करार (karaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around Indian construction sites and municipal work: 'sadak ka theka' (road contract), 'safai ka theka' (cleaning contract). The person who holds it is a ठेकेदार (thekedaar, contractor), a very visible figure in local infrastructure and small-business life across North India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use करार (karaar) and ठेका (thekaa) as if fully interchangeable -- ठेका (thekaa) leans toward a contract for a job or supply of goods/services (e.g. construction, cleaning, catering), while करार (karaar) is used for broader agreements, including political or diplomatic ones. 2) The common verb pairing is ठेका लेना (thekaa lenaa, to take on a contract) or ठेका देना (thekaa denaa, to award a contract) -- not 'karnaa' as a beginner might guess.

Sense 2 : government-licensed liquor shop
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शराब की दुकान (sharaab kii dukaan, descriptive phrase), वाइन शॉप (vaain shop, colloquial, English loan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense comes from the fact that liquor sale in India runs on government-issued contracts/licenses, so the shop itself came to be called by the name of the license, 'theka.' It's an extremely common everyday term -- you'll hear 'theke se le aa' (theke se le aa) shouted between friends heading out for the evening.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is purely colloquial and specific to North Indian speech -- do not expect it in formal writing or news reports, where शराब की दुकान (sharaab kii dukaan) or मद्य की दुकान (mady kii dukaan) would be used instead. 2) Context (a shop vs. a business deal) is the only way to distinguish this from the 'contract' sense, since the word itself is identical.

Sense 3 : fixed rhythmic cycle in tabla/percussion accompaniment
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is a specialist term from Hindustani classical music: the ठेका (thekaa) is the basic, named rhythmic pattern a tabla player uses to mark a given ताल (taal, rhythmic cycle), such as तीनताल (tiintaal). Most conversational learners will not need this sense, but it's worth knowing if you ever discuss Indian classical music.
Sample Sentences:
तबले पर तीनताल का ठेका बजाओ। Play the theka of teentaal on the tabla.
