गुंजाइश (gunjaaish)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'room' or 'scope' for something to happen -- physical space, or more often figurative space like room for improvement, negotiation, or doubt.
Sense 1 : scope, room, leeway (for something to happen or be done)
noun, feminine Almost always used with a following genitive or एक postposition phrase: गुंजाइश होना (gunjaaish honaa) 'there to be room/scope', or X में गुंजाइश (X men gunjaaish) 'scope within X'. Frequently paired with कोई नहीं (koii nahiin) to mean 'no room at all', as in कोई गुंजाइश नहीं (koii gunjaaish nahiin, 'no room/chance at all').
Synonyms: जगह (jagah, physical sense only), मौका (maukaa, opportunity sense), संभावना (sambhaavnaa, possibility sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in office, family, and political talk in Delhi -- 'sudhaar ki gunjaaish hai' (room for improvement) is something every boss says gently instead of being blunt. It carries a diplomatic, softening flavor: saying kuch gunjaaish nahi hai (kuchh gunjaaish nahiin hai, 'there's no room at all') shuts a door firmly but politely, which is very much in keeping with how directness is often cushioned in Hindi conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for जगह (jagah) when they mean figurative 'room for X' (like room for improvement or negotiation) -- गुंजाइश (gunjaaish) is the natural word there, while जगह (jagah) sounds oddly literal. 2) It pairs with है (hai) / नहीं है (nahiin hai) via the dative-subject-like frame [किसी के लिए/में] गुंजाइश है (gunjaaish hai) -- learners sometimes try to make it the direct object of a verb like 'have,' producing an unnatural sentence.

इस काम में सुधार की गुंजाइश है। There's room for improvement in this work.
(is kaam men sudhaar kii gunjaaish hai।)
