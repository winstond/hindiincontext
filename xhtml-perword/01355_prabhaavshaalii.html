प्रभावशाली (prabhaavshaalii)

adjective A single adjective meaning 'influential' or 'impressive' -- describing someone or something with notable power, presence, or effect.
Sense 1 : influential, powerful (of a person or position)
adjective
Synonyms: ताकतवर (taakatvar), रसूखदार (rasookhdaar, for social/political clout), शक्तिशाली (shaktishaalii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday talk about politics, business, or family hierarchy, प्रभावशाली (prabhaavshaalii) is the go-to word for someone whose word carries weight -- a प्रभावशाली नेता (prabhaavshaalii netaa, 'influential leader') or प्रभावशाली परिवार (prabhaavshaalii parivaar, 'influential family') gets things done through connections and standing, not just formal power.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this where प्रभावित (prabhaavit, 'affected/impressed') is meant -- प्रभावशाली (prabhaavshaalii) describes the one who has influence, while प्रभावित (prabhaavit) describes the one who receives it. 2) Do not confuse with प्रभावी (prabhaavii), which leans toward 'effective' (as in a policy or method working well) rather than 'influential' as a trait of a person.

Sense 2 : impressive, striking (of a speech, personality, or performance)
adjective
Synonyms: दमदार (damdaar, colloquial), असरदार (asardaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up a lot in reviews and compliments -- a प्रभावशाली भाषण (prabhaavshaalii bhaashan, 'impressive speech') or प्रभावशाली व्यक्तित्व (prabhaavshaalii vyaktitv, 'impressive/commanding personality') praises the impact someone makes on an audience, distinct from sense 1's focus on real-world clout.
