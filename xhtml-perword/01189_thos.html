ठोस (thos)

adjective Primarily an adjective meaning 'solid' -- covering both the physical state of matter and, figuratively, something firm, concrete, or well-grounded (as in an argument or plan).
Sense 1 : solid, firm, concrete (figurative: argument, evidence, plan)
adjective
Synonyms: पुख्ता (pukhtaa), मज़बूत (mazboot)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on the news: नेता ने कोई ठोस जवाब नहीं दिया (netaa ne koii thos javaab nahiin diyaa, 'the leader gave no concrete answer') is a classic line of political commentary -- ठोस (thos) here signals substance versus empty talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to मज़बूत (mazboot) for anything 'strong,' but when talking about an argument, proof, or plan being solid rather than flimsy, ठोस (thos) is the natural native choice -- ठोस सबूत (thos saboot, 'solid evidence') and ठोस योजना (thos yojnaa, 'concrete plan') are set collocations you will hear constantly in news and office contexts.

Sense 2 : solid (physical state of matter)
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word your school science teacher used for 'solid' in ठोस, द्रव और गैस (thos, drav aur gais, 'solid, liquid, and gas') -- the basic states-of-matter trio every Hindi-medium student memorizes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect ठोस (thos) like कड़ा (kadaa) -- it stays invariant no matter the gender or number of the noun it describes, e.g. ठोस चट्टान (thos chattaan, 'solid rock') and ठोस बर्फ़ (thos barf, 'solid ice') both keep the same form.
