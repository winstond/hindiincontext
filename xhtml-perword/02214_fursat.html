फ़ुरसत (fursat)

noun A single-sense noun meaning free time or leisure -- the spare time you have once your work and obligations are out of the way.
Sense 1 : free time, leisure, spare time
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फुर्सत (phursat, variant form without nuqta), अवकाश (avkaash, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a lovely wistfulness in everyday Delhi speech -- कहाँ फ़ुरसत है! (kahaan fursat hai!, 'as if I have the time!') is a classic exasperated reply to an invitation, gently implying life is too busy. Note the nuqta under फ़ (a Persian-origin sound); the spelling without it, फुर्सत (phursat), is just as commonly seen and heard.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मेरे पास फ़ुरसत है (mere paas fursat hai) which is fine, but the more idiomatic negative is मुझे फ़ुरसत नहीं है (mujhe fursat nahiin hai, 'I don't have the time') -- both possessive frames (पास and को) are heard, but को-marked constructions are extremely common with this word and learners tend to overuse पास out of habit. 2) फ़ुरसत से (fursat se) is a fixed phrase meaning 'at leisure, in a relaxed/unhurried way' (e.g. फ़ुरसत से बात करेंगे (fursat se baat karenge, 'we'll talk at leisure')) -- learners sometimes mistranslate it word-for-word instead of recognizing it as a set adverbial expression.
