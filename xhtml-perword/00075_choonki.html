चूँकि (choonki)

conjunction A single-sense conjunction meaning 'since' or 'because', used to introduce a reason clause, typically paired with the correlative अतः (atah) or इसलिए (islie) in the following clause.
Sense 1 : since, because (reason clause)
conjunction Introduces the reason clause and is typically followed later by अतः (atah) or इसलिए (islie) introducing the result clause, forming a correlative pair -- 'चूँकि...अतः/इसलिए' (choonki...atah/islie, 'since...therefore').
Synonyms: क्योंकि (kyonki), जबकि (jabki, one-word variant, less common in this sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet चूँकि (choonki) far more in newspapers, official notices, and formal speeches than in casual chai-time chat -- in daily conversation people almost always reach for क्योंकि (kyonki) instead. Think of चूँकi as the 'since' of a formal report, while क्योंकि (kyonki) is the 'because' of everyday talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use चूँकि (choonki) as a simple drop-in for क्योंकि (kyonki), but चूँकि (choonki) is more formal/written and normally opens the sentence, whereas क्योंकि (kyonki) usually follows the main clause it explains. 2) Forgetting the nuqta and writing चुंकि (chunki) is a common spelling slip; the correct nasalized vowel is चूँकि (choonki).
Sample Sentences:
चूँकि मौसम खराब था, इसलिए उड़ान रद्द कर दी गई। Since the weather was bad, the flight was cancelled.
(choonki mausam kharaab thaa, islie udaan radd kar dii gaii।)
