गंदा (gandaa)

adjective Mainly means 'dirty' in a literal, physical sense, extending naturally to 'filthy/obscene' in speech and behavior, and figuratively to 'bad/messy' situations.
Sense 1 : physically dirty, unclean
adjective
Synonyms: मैला (mailaa), अशुद्ध (ashuddh, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian mother uses on a daily basis: गंदे हाथ (gande haath, 'dirty hands'), गंदे कपड़े (gande kapde, 'dirty clothes'). It is one of the first adjectives kids learn because it comes attached to a scolding.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for अशुद्ध (ashuddh) in casual speech about a dirty room or dirty hands -- native speakers just say गंदा (gandaa); अशुद्ध (ashuddh) sounds bookish here.
Sample Sentences:
अपने गंदे हाथ धो लो, फिर खाना खाना। Wash your dirty hands, then eat your food.
(apne gande haath dho lo, phir khaanaa khaanaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
ये कपड़े कितने गंदे हैं, इन्हें धोना पड़ेगा। These clothes are so dirty, they'll need to be washed.
(ye kapde kitne gande hain, inhen dhonaa padegaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : morally filthy, obscene, vulgar
adjective
Synonyms: अश्लील (ashliil, formal/explicit content), भद्दा (bhaddaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: गंदी सोच (gandii soch, 'dirty/perverted thinking') and गंदी बात (gandii baat, 'dirty talk') are common ways to call out someone's crude comment or joke without using a harsher word.
Common Mistakes: 1) गंदी बात (gandii baat) and गंदा मज़ाक (gandaa mazaak) refer to something crude or off-color, not literal dirt -- learners sometimes miss this figurative jump and take it too literally.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी सोच बहुत गंदी है। His thinking is very dirty (perverted).
(uskii soch bahut gandii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अपने दोस्तों के सामने गंदी बातें मत करो। Don't make dirty (obscene) remarks in front of your friends.
(apne doston ke saamne gandii baaten mat karo।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
वह हर बात में गंदा मतलब निकाल लेता है। He extracts a dirty (lewd) meaning from everything.
(vah har baat men gandaa matlab nikaal letaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उस फिल्म में कुछ गंदे जोक्स थे जो मुझे पसंद नहीं आए। That film had some dirty jokes which I didn't like.

Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर वह गंदी बात कहेगा तो मैं वहाँ से चली जाऊँगी। If he says something dirty (vulgar), I'll walk away from there.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
क्या तुम्हें अपनी गंदी सोच पर शर्म नहीं आती? Aren't you ashamed of your dirty (perverted) thinking?
(kyaa tumhen apnii gandii soch par sharm nahiin aatii?)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगर उसकी सोच इतनी गंदी न होती, तो कोई उससे बात करना ही न छोड़ता। If his thinking weren't so dirty, no one would have stopped talking to him.

Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
उसने मुझसे इतनी गंदी बात कही कि मुझे गुस्सा आ गया। He said such a dirty (vulgar) thing to me that I got angry.
(usne mujhse itnii gandii baat kahii ki mujhe gussaa aa gayaa।)

(samaaj men gandii baaton ko badhaavaa nahiin denaa chaahie।)

Sense 3 : bad, messy, unpleasant (of a situation, habit, or feeling)
adjective
Synonyms: बुरा (buraa), बदतर (badtar, worse, comparative use only)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of गंदा (gandaa) as carrying a little extra emotional charge over बुरा (buraa) -- गंदी आदत (gandii aadat) sounds more disgusted than just बुरी आदत (burii aadat), closer to 'nasty habit' than plain 'bad habit'.
Common Mistakes: 1) गंदी आदत (gandii aadat, 'bad/nasty habit') and गंदी हालत (gandii haalat, 'messy state/condition') use गंदा (gandaa) far beyond physical dirt -- learners often default to बुरा (buraa) here instead, but गंदा (gandaa) is what a native speaker reaches for to add extra disgust or disapproval.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी यह आदत बहुत गंदी है। This habit of his is really nasty.
(uskii yah aadat bahut gandii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
आज का दिन बहुत गंदा रहा। Today was a really bad day.
(aaj kaa din bahut gandaa rahaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
मुझे बहुत गंदा लग रहा है, जैसे सब कुछ बिगड़ गया हो। I feel really nasty, like everything has fallen apart.
(mujhe bahut gandaa lag rahaa hai, jaise sab kuchh bigad gayaa ho।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
काश उसका मूड इतना गंदा न होता। I wish his mood weren't so foul.

पूरा प्रोजेक्ट इतना गंदा हो गया कि किसी को समझ ही नहीं आया कि क्या करें। The whole project got so messed up that no one could figure out what to do.
