विमर्श (vimarsh)

noun A somewhat formal/intellectual noun meaning 'discussion' or 'deliberation' in everyday-ish contexts, and by extension 'discourse' in an academic/media sense (as in feminist discourse, political discourse).
Sense 1 : discussion, deliberation, consultation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चर्चा (charchaa), बहस (bahas, more heated/debate-like), विचार-विमर्श (vichaar-vimarsh, compound form emphasizing mutual deliberation)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in news broadcasts and official meetings -- 'सरकार ने विमर्श किया' (sarkaar ne vimarsh kiyaa, 'the government held discussions') has that stately, bureaucratic ring. Its cousin विचार-विमर्श (vichaar-vimarsh) is extremely common in everyday formal speech, e.g. families 'doing विचार-विमर्श' before a big decision like a wedding or a property purchase.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चर्चा (charchaa) in casual talk and विमर्श (vimarsh) in formal or written contexts; using विमर्श (vimarsh) for a casual chat with friends can sound stiff or bookish. 2) Often paired with करना (karnaa) as विमर्श करना (vimarsh karnaa, 'to hold a discussion') -- learners sometimes use it without करना (karnaa) expecting it to function as a verb on its own, but it is strictly a noun.

(is vishay par vimarsh zaroorii hai।)

Sense 2 : discourse (academic/intellectual sense)
noun, masculine Used especially in academic, media, and literary-critical registers, as in नारीवादी विमर्श (naariivaadii vimarsh, 'feminist discourse') or दलित विमर्श (dalit vimarsh, 'Dalit discourse').
Synonyms: विचारधारा (vichaardhaaraa, closer to 'ideology', not a perfect synonym)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense is common in Hindi academic and journalistic writing, especially in literature and social-sciences circles, where English 'discourse' (as in Foucauldian discourse analysis) gets translated as विमर्श (vimarsh). You will see it in phrases like स्त्री विमर्श (strii vimarsh, 'women's discourse') in essays and college syllabi.
