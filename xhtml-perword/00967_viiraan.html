वीरान (viiraan)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'desolate' or 'deserted' -- used for empty streets, ruined places, and lonely landscapes, and often extended to describe a lonely or barren emotional state.
Sense 1 : desolate, deserted, uninhabited
adjective
Synonyms: सुनसान (sunsaan), उजाड़ (ujaad), बियाबान (biyaabaan, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear वीरान (viiraan) used for a village that has emptied out as people move to cities, or an old haveli standing alone with no one living in it -- it always carries a faint sadness, not just the fact of emptiness. It also extends easily to feelings, as in वीरान ज़िंदगी (viiraan zindgii, 'a desolate life'), where the outer bleakness mirrors an inner one -- a favorite move in Hindi film lyrics and shayari.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for खाली (khaalii, 'empty') when they mean वीरान (viiraan) -- खाली (khaalii) just means unoccupied or vacant (an empty room, an empty glass), while वीरान (viiraan) carries a mood of abandonment or bleakness, like a ghost town or a street with no one on it late at night.
