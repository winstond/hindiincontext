धंधा (dhandhaa)

noun Mainly means 'business, trade, occupation' in a general everyday sense; also carries a colloquial/pejorative sense of a shady or disreputable racket, and by extension can specifically mean 'prostitution' in certain fixed usages.
Sense 1 : business, trade, occupation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: काम (kaam), व्यापार (vyaapaar), कारोबार (kaarobaar), पेशा (peshaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask a shopkeeper or auto driver in Delhi how things are going and you'll often hear धंधा कैसा चल रहा है? (dhandhaa kaisaa chal rahaa hai, 'how's business going?') -- it's the natural, down-to-earth word for one's daily trade or hustle, much more common in speech than the bookish व्यवसाय (vyavsaay).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for व्यापार (vyaapaar) or कारोबार (kaarobaar) as the 'default' business word and forget that धंधा (dhandhaa) is the everyday, slightly informal term people actually use when asking what someone does for a living, e.g. क्या धंधा है? (kyaa dhandhaa hai, 'what's your line of work?'). 2) Because of its other slang sense (see the separate 'shady dealings' sense), using धंधा (dhandhaa) in a very formal or written context (a report, a resume) can sound off; व्यापार (vyaapaar) or व्यवसाय (vyavsaay) fits better there.
Sample Sentences:

(aajkal dhandhaa kaisaa chal rahaa hai?)

Sense 2 : shady dealing, racket, dubious business
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गोरखधंधा (gorakhdhandhaa, colloquial, 'shady/complicated racket'), फ़रेब (fareb, meaning scope: deceit specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word people reach for when grumbling about corruption or a scam -- सब धंधा है (sab dhandhaa hai, 'it's all just a racket') is a common cynical aside about politics, bureaucracy, or business deals that feel rigged.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense depends heavily on tone and context -- यह क्या धंधा है? (yah kyaa dhandhaa hai, 'what kind of racket is this?') is an accusation, not a neutral question about someone's job, so learners should listen for the disapproving tone rather than expect a separate word.

Sense 3 : prostitution (euphemistic/colloquial)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: देह व्यापार (deh vyaapaar, formal euphemism)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This euphemistic use shows up in news reports and conversations about red-light districts like Delhi's GB Road, where धंधा (dhandhaa) and धंधेवाली (dhandhevaalii) are the common, if delicate, ways of referring to sex work without naming it outright.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is entirely context-dependent -- धंधा (dhandhaa) alone does not mean this in ordinary conversation about jobs; it only carries this meaning in specific contexts (e.g. discussing red-light areas) where speakers use it as a euphemism, often as धंधा करना (dhandhaa karnaa) or धंधे वाली (dhandhe vaalii).
