आध्यात्मिकता (aadhyaatmiktaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'spirituality' -- the quality or state of being spiritual, as opposed to material or worldly concerns. Related to the adjective आध्यात्मिक (aadhyaatmik, 'spiritual').
Sense 1 : spirituality
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अध्यात्म (adhyaatm, closely related, often used interchangeably)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a fairly formal, bookish word -- you will hear it in discussions about yoga, meditation, or self-growth, or read it in a magazine article, but it rarely comes up in casual chai-time chatter the way धर्म (dharm) or पूजा (poojaa) might. Many urban Indians today make a deliberate distinction between आध्यात्मिकता (aadhyaatmiktaa) and organized religious practice, often saying things like 'मैं धार्मिक नहीं हूँ, लेकिन आध्यात्मिक हूँ' (main dhaarmik nahiin hoon, lekin aadhyaatmik hoon, 'I am not religious, but I am spiritual').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this word when धर्म (dharm, 'religion') is meant; आध्यात्मिकता (aadhyaatmiktaa) refers to inner spiritual life or practice, not organized religion, and Hindi speakers are careful to keep the two apart in conversation. 2) This is an abstract feminine noun and is rarely if ever used in plural form -- treat it like 'spirituality' in English, not a countable noun.
