खाता (khaataa)

noun/verb Primarily a noun meaning 'account' (bank account, ledger account), commonly seen in banking and business contexts; also occurs as the masculine singular habitual/imperfective form of the verb खाना (khaanaa, 'to eat') in constructions like वह खाता है (vah khaataa hai, 'he eats').
Sense 1 : account (bank/financial)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अकाउंट (akaaunt, common in spoken banking contexts)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian household conversation about money eventually mentions a बैंक खाता (baink khaataa, 'bank account') -- especially since schemes like जन धन खाता (jan dhan khaataa) became a household phrase for financial inclusion. You'll also hear it in the ledger sense at small shops, where the shopkeeper keeps a खाता (khaataa) of customers' credit.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse खाता (khaataa) with खाना (khaanaa, 'food' or 'to eat') because of the similar spelling and shared root -- context (banking vs. eating) is the quickest way to tell them apart. 2) The common banking phrase is खाता खोलना (khaataa kholnaa, 'to open an account'), not बनाना (banaanaa) -- learners often reach for the wrong verb here.

Sense 2 : ledger, register (record-keeping book)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बहीखाता (bahiikhaataa, compound form used in bookkeeping), रजिस्टर (rajistar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the older, more traditional sense -- the physical खाता (khaataa) or ledger book that shopkeepers and accountants used (and many still use) to track debts and dues, often alongside the compound बही-खाता (bahii-khaataa).

Sense 3 : eats (habitual/imperfective, masculine singular form of खाना)
verb, transitive This is the masculine singular imperfective participle of खाना (khaanaa, 'to eat'), used with है/था etc. as in वह रोज़ फल खाता है (vah roz phal khaataa hai, 'he eats fruit daily'). Full conjugation is covered in the morphology table for खाना (khaanaa).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is simply the verb खाना (khaanaa) wearing its masculine singular habitual form -- it's worth cross-referencing the main entry for खाना (khaanaa) to see the fuller picture of how this verb behaves, including its idiomatic extensions like गाली खाना (gaalii khaanaa, 'to get scolded').
Common Mistakes: 1) This form only appears with masculine singular subjects (मैं/तू/यह/वह) -- learners sometimes use it with feminine or plural subjects where खाती (khaatii) or खाते (khaate) is required.
