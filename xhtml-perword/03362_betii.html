बेटी (betii)

noun A single, straightforward sense: 'daughter'. Related family-noun to बेटा (betaa, 'son').
Sense 1 : daughter
noun, feminine
Synonyms: पुत्री (putrii, formal), लड़की (ladkii, meaning shifts to 'girl' generally)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: बेटी (betii) carries real emotional weight in Hindi -- phrases like बेटी बचाओ (betii bachaao, 'save the daughter') anchor public campaigns against gender bias, and you'll often hear elders say बेटी घर की इज़्ज़त होती है (betii ghar kii izzat hotii hai, 'a daughter is the honor of the household') reflecting (for better or worse) how central this word is to family identity and social attitudes. It pairs naturally with बेटा (betaa, 'son') in the common phrase बेटा-बेटी (betaa-betii, 'sons and daughters/children') used when talking about kids in general.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for लड़की (ladkii) when they mean 'daughter' in a family context -- लड़की (ladkii) just means 'girl' and doesn't carry the relational meaning; बेटी (betii) is the word tied to parentage. 2) पुत्री (putrii) is heard in formal or ceremonial speech (wedding cards, official forms) -- बेटी (betii) is what people actually say at home.
