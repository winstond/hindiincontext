पीढ़ी (piidhii)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'generation' -- either a step in a family lineage or a broader age-cohort of society.
Sense 1 : generation (of a family lineage)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वंश (vansh, lineage/dynasty, narrower sense), नस्ल (nasl, breed/lineage)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in family conversations: तीन पीढ़ियाँ एक साथ रहती हैं (tiin piidhiyaan ek saath rahtii hain, 'three generations live together') is a very normal sentence in a Delhi household, reflecting the joint-family setup many learners will encounter. The phrase पीढ़ी दर पीढ़ी (piidhii dar piidhii, 'generation after generation') is a common way to talk about traditions or family businesses passed down.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पीढ़ी (piidhii) with पीढ़ा (piidhaa), an unrelated word meaning 'wooden stool' -- the nasal-less feminine ending and the retroflex ढ़ sound are close but the meanings are worlds apart. 2) पीढ़ी दर पीढ़ी (piidhii dar piidhii, 'generation after generation') is a set phrase learners often try to reconstruct with से (se) or तक (tak) instead of the fixed दर (dar).
Sample Sentences:
हमारे घर में तीन पीढ़ियाँ एक साथ रहती हैं। Three generations live together in our house.
(hamaare ghar men tiin piidhiyaan ek saath rahtii hain।)

Sense 2 : generation (age-cohort / era of people)
noun, feminine Used with a qualifying adjective or noun to name a societal cohort, e.g. नई पीढ़ी (naii piidhii, 'the new/young generation'), पुरानी पीढ़ी (puraanii piidhii, 'the older generation').
Synonyms: युग (yug, era, broader/less about people specifically), दौर (daur, era/period)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you will hear in social commentary: नई पीढ़ी को यह समझ नहीं आता (naii piidhii ko yah samajh nahiin aataa, 'the new generation doesn't understand this') is the classic complaint of any पुरानी पीढ़ी (puraanii piidhii, 'older generation') anywhere, and Delhi elders use it just as freely as anyone else, often at family dinners comparing today's youth to their own.
