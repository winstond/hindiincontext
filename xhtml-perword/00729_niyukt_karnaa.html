नियुक्त करना (niyukt karnaa)

verb A formal, workplace/institutional verb meaning 'to appoint' -- built from the adjective नियुक्त (niyukt, 'appointed') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'). One core sense; the register is consistently formal/official.
Sense 1 : to appoint (to a post, position, or role)
verb, transitive Takes the pattern X ko Y (pad/post) par niyukt karnaa (X ko Y par niyukt karnaa) -- the person appointed is marked with को, and the position with पर.
Synonyms: नियुक्ति देना (niyukti denaa, noun-verb phrase variant), बहाल करना (bahaal karnaa, often used for reinstatement/appointment in government contexts), रखना (rakhnaa, informal, 'to hire/keep on')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb you will see in newspapers, job letters, and government notices -- राष्ट्रपति ने नए राज्यपाल को नियुक्त किया (raashtrapti ne nae raajypaal ko niyukt kiyaa, 'the President appointed the new governor'). In everyday speech about hiring someone informally (a maid, a driver, a tutor), Hindi speakers reach for the much more casual रखना (rakhnaa) instead, reserving नियुक्त करना (niyukt karnaa) for formal, official, or written contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say नियुक्त होना (niyukt honaa) when they mean 'to be appointed' (passive sense) but mistakenly use नियुक्त करना (niyukt karnaa) with a को-marked subject instead -- e.g. wrongly saying राम को नियुक्त करना (raam ko niyukt karnaa) when the intended meaning is 'Ram was appointed,' which actually requires राम नियुक्त हुआ (raam niyukt huaa) or राम को नियुक्त किया गया (raam ko niyukt kiyaa gayaa). 2) The position/role is marked with पर (par), not को -- साफ़ अध्यक्ष के पद पर नियुक्त किया (adhyaksh ke pad par niyukt kiyaa, 'appointed to the post of chairman'), not अध्यक्ष के पद को.
