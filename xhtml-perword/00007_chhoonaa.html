छूना (chhoonaa)

verb The core meaning is physical contact -- 'to touch' -- which extends naturally into emotional ('to move someone') and aspirational ('to reach/attain') senses, much like English 'touch' does.
Sense 1 : to touch (physical contact)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: स्पर्श करना (sparsh karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पैर छूना (pair chhoonaa, 'to touch the feet') is a common gesture of respect toward elders, teachers, and parents -- if you visit a Hindi-speaking family and see someone bend down to touch a grandparent's feet, this is the verb behind it.
Common Mistakes: 1) The object of physical touching is usually को-marked when it's a specific person or thing: मैंने उसे छुआ (mainne use chhuaa, 'I touched him/her'), not मैंने उसको छूता। 2) स्पर्श करना (sparsh karnaa) is the bookish/formal equivalent -- in everyday speech छूना (chhoonaa) is what people actually say.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : to move emotionally, to touch (feelings)
verb, transitive Often appears as दिल को छूना (dil ko chhoonaa, 'to touch the heart') or मन को छूना (man ko chhoonaa, 'to touch the mind/heart'), paralleling English's own heart-touch metaphor.
Synonyms: प्रभावित करना (prabhaavit karnaa, to affect/influence)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense rides on the same body-as-container imagery as दिल (dil, 'heart') and मन (man, 'mind/heart') -- emotions in Hindi are often things that touch or enter you rather than things you simply 'feel,' which is a useful reframe for English speakers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for प्रभावित करना (prabhaavit karnaa) by default for 'move/affect,' but for warm, personal emotional impact दिल को छूना (dil ko chhoonaa, 'to touch the heart') is the far more natural, idiomatic choice.

Sense 3 : to reach, to attain (a height, a level)
verb, transitive Common in the fixed phrase आसमान छूना (aasmaan chhoonaa, 'to touch the sky'), used for achieving great heights of success.
Synonyms: पहुँचना (pahunchnaa, to arrive/reach)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in motivational speech and film dialogue alike -- आसमान छूना (aasmaan chhoonaa, 'to touch the sky') is the standard way to talk about someone's ambitions or achievements soaring.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse this figurative 'reach' with पहुँचना (pahunchnaa) -- छूना (chhoonaa) here specifically carries the sense of just barely reaching or grazing a peak/limit, often used boastfully or aspirationally, e.g. सफलता की ऊँचाइयों को छूना (saphaltaa kii oonchaaiyon ko chhoonaa, 'to touch the heights of success').
