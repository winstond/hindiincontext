या (yaa)

conjunction The everyday word for 'or', linking alternatives; also appears doubled as या...या (yaa...yaa) for 'either...or'.
Sense 1 : or (linking alternatives)
conjunction
Synonyms: अथवा (athvaa, formal), वा (vaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear या (yaa) constantly in everyday choices -- चाय या कॉफी (chaay yaa kophii, 'tea or coffee') is the classic example any host might ask a guest. Doubled up as या...या (yaa...yaa), it becomes 'either...or', as in या यह या वह (yaa yah yaa vah, 'either this or that').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for और (aur) when they mean 'or' -- और (aur) means 'and', not 'or'; keep the two firmly separate since both are extremely frequent. 2) या (yaa) can link words, phrases, or full clauses without any change in form, unlike English where structure sometimes shifts.

Sense 2 : or rather, or maybe (softening/reconsidering)
conjunction Used at the start or middle of a sentence to introduce a self-correction or a second thought, rather than joining two listed alternatives.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In casual speech, या (yaa) often pops up mid-thought when someone reconsiders what they just said -- like चलो चाय पीते हैं, या फिर कॉफी ही सही (chalo chaay piite hain, yaa phir kophii hii sahii, 'let's have tea, or actually coffee is fine too'). It carries the flavor of thinking out loud rather than presenting a clean either-or choice.
