पत्रकार (patrkaar)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'journalist/reporter'; built from पत्र (patr, 'letter/document') + कार (kaar, 'doer/maker'), the same agentive suffix seen in words like कलाकार (kalaakaar, 'artist').
Sense 1 : journalist, reporter
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संवाददाता (sanvaaddaataa, 'correspondent', formal), रिपोर्टर (riportar, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पत्रकार (patrkaar) is the everyday word you will hear on the news itself, in phrases like हमारे पत्रकार ने बताया (hamaare patrkaar ne bataayaa, 'our journalist reported') -- it carries a note of professional respectability, unlike the more casual रिपोर्टर (riportar), which feels borrowed and slightly more informal. The suffix -कार (kaar) is productive and worth learning as a pattern: कलाकार (kalaakaar, 'artist'), साहित्यकार (saahitykaar, 'litterateur'), रचनाकार (rachnaakaar, 'creator/author') all follow the same 'one who does/makes X' logic.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume पत्रकार (patrkaar) is grammatically feminine because it ends in -आ (aa) like many feminine nouns; it is actually masculine by default, and a female journalist is still often called पत्रकार (patrkaar) or specified as महिला पत्रकार (mahilaa patrkaar, 'woman journalist').
