झोला (jholaa)

noun A cloth or jute bag with handles, carried over the shoulder or by hand -- the everyday tote of Indian markets and campuses. One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : cloth/jute shoulder bag
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बैग (baig), थैला (thailaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The झोला (jholaa) is practically an icon of simple living in India -- students sling one over the shoulder to carry books, and it has long been associated with activists, teachers, and the 'jhola-chhaap' (झोला-छाप, jholaa-chhaap) stereotype of a modest, unpretentious person, sometimes used affectionately or mockingly for grassroots doctors or intellectuals who carry one everywhere. Environmentally-conscious shoppers have also revived it as a cloth alternative to plastic bags at the local sabzi mandi (sabzii mandii).
Common Mistakes: 1) झोला (jholaa) specifically suggests a soft, often jute or cloth bag with a simple strap -- not a stiff briefcase or a plastic carry bag, for which बैग (baig) or प्लास्टिक की थैली (plaastik kii thailii) is more natural. 2) थैला (thailaa) overlaps closely but leans slightly more toward a simple sack-like bag (for vegetables, groceries), while झोला (jholaa) often carries a shoulder strap and a sense of everyday, no-frills utility.
