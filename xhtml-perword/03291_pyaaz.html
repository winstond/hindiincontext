प्याज़ (pyaaz)

noun A single everyday sense: the vegetable onion, used constantly in Hindi kitchen and market talk. Gender varies by speaker (see the gender field), which does not split the sense.
Sense 1 : onion
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Onion prices are practically a national mood-ring in India -- प्याज़ के दाम (pyaaz ke daam, 'onion prices') shooting up is recurring news and dinner-table complaint, so this word carries a bit of everyday economic drama beyond just the vegetable itself. You'll hear it constantly at the सब्ज़ी वाला (sabzii vaalaa, 'vegetable vendor')'s cart.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to fix the gender as one or the other, but प्याज़ (pyaaz) genuinely varies between speakers -- you will hear both प्याज़ अच्छा है (pyaaz achchhaa hai, 'the onion is good') and प्याज़ अच्छी है (pyaaz achchhii hai) in Delhi-NCR, sometimes from the same person on different days. 2) The nuqta on ज़ is often dropped in casual writing (प्याज), but the pronunciation with the 'z' sound stays standard.
