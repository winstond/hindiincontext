झोंपड़ी (jhompdii)

noun A single-sense word: a small hut or shanty, typically made of mud, straw, bamboo, or other basic materials.
Sense 1 : hut, shanty
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कुटिया (kutiyaa, evokes a simple, often saintly or rustic dwelling), झुग्गी (jhuggii, slum shanty)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: झोंपड़ी (jhompdii) conjures up a village scene -- a small mud-and-thatch hut with a courtyard, often shown in old Hindi films as the humble home of a poor farmer, in contrast to the zamindar's haveli (havelii). The phrase झोंपड़ी में रहना (jhompdii men rahnaa, 'to live in a hut') carries a strong connotation of poverty and simplicity, sometimes used with pride ('simple living') and sometimes with pity.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use झोंपड़ी (jhompdii) interchangeably with घर (ghar, house), but झोंपड़ी (jhompdii) specifically implies a small, poor, or makeshift dwelling -- using it for an ordinary house sounds odd or even insulting. 2) झुग्गी (jhuggii) is closer to 'slum shack' in an urban context, while झोंपड़ी (jhompdii) is more associated with villages -- swapping them can shift the mental picture for a native listener.
