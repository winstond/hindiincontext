कभी नहीं (kabhii nahiin)

adverb A fixed two-word phrase meaning 'never', combining कभी (kabhii, 'ever/sometime') with the negator नहीं (nahiin); functions as a single adverbial unit in the sentence.
Sense 1 : never (at no time)
adverb Placed like any time adverb, typically before the verb; कभी (kabhii) alone means 'ever/sometime' and only gets the 'never' sense when paired with नहीं (nahiin).
Synonyms: कदापि नहीं (kadaapi nahiin, literary), किसी भी वक़्त नहीं (kisii bhii vaqt nahiin, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase pairs naturally with the perfective past to make firm, sometimes dramatic denials, as in मैंने ऐसा कभी नहीं कहा (mainne aisaa kabhii nahiin kahaa, 'I never said such a thing') -- a classic line in any Indian household argument. It's the direct answer to a कभी (kabhii) question: कभी गए हो? -- कभी नहीं! (kabhii gae ho?, 'have you ever been?' -- kabhii nahiin, 'never!').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop नहीं (nahiin) and use कभी (kabhii) alone hoping it means 'never' -- by itself it means 'ever' or 'sometime,' and needs the negator to flip to 'never.' 2) For emphatic 'never ever,' speakers often say कभी भी नहीं (kabhii bhii nahiin, 'never at all'), inserting भी (bhii) between the two words -- learners unfamiliar with this pattern may find the extra भी (bhii) confusing.
