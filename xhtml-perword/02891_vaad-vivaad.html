वाद-विवाद (vaad-vivaad)

noun A compound noun meaning 'debate' or 'argument/dispute' -- used both for formal, structured debate (as in school competitions) and for a heated back-and-forth disagreement between people.
Sense 1 : formal debate
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बहस (bahas)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Indian schools and colleges take वाद-विवाद प्रतियोगिता (vaad-vivaad pratiyogitaa, 'debate competition') seriously -- it's a staple of annual functions, and doing well in one is a genuine point of family pride, much like elocution or essay contests.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बहस (bahas) in every context, but वाद-विवाद (vaad-vivaad) has a slightly more formal, structured flavor -- it is the word you'll see on a school notice for a 'वाद-विवाद प्रतियोगिता' (vaad-vivaad pratiyogitaa, 'debate competition'), whereas बहस (bahas) covers both formal and casual disagreement.

Sense 2 : argument, dispute
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बहस (bahas), झगड़ा (jhagdaa, sharper, implies a quarrel), तर्क-वितर्क (tark-vitark, more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The word itself is built from वाद (vaad, 'assertion/thesis') and विवाद (vivaad, 'counter-assertion/dispute') -- so it literally carries the shape of a back-and-forth, which is why it fits both a civil debate and a heated squabble equally well, depending on tone and context.
