संक्षिप्त (sankshipt)

adjective A formal, Sanskrit-derived adjective meaning 'brief, abbreviated, condensed' -- used for shortened texts, summaries, and forms rather than for describing a person's speaking style.
Sense 1 : brief, condensed, abbreviated
adjective
Synonyms: छोटा (chhotaa), संक्षेप (sankshep, adverbial use, 'in brief'), सारांशित (saaraanshit, rare, 'summarized')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in formal and written Hindi -- news headlines, office circulars, form instructions like संक्षिप्त विवरण दें (sankshipt vivran den, 'give a brief description') -- but almost never in casual chatter, where छोटा (chhotaa) or सीधा-सीधा (siidhaa-siidhaa) would be used instead. Its adverbial cousin संक्षेप में (sankshep men, 'in brief/in short') is extremely common as a way to begin summarizing something.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छोटा (chhotaa) in all 'short' contexts, but संक्षिप्त (sankshipt) specifically implies something that has been condensed or trimmed down from a fuller version -- a summary, an abridged edition, a shortened form -- not just small in size. 2) It describes the text/document/version itself, not a person's manner of speaking; for a person who talks little, use कम बोलनेवाला (kam bolnevaalaa) or संक्षेप में बोलनेवाला (sankshep men bolnevaalaa) instead of calling the person संक्षिप्त (sankshipt).
Sample Sentences:
कृपया अपना संक्षिप्त विवरण दें। Please give a brief description of yourself.
(kripyaa apnaa sankshipt vivran den।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
यह एक संक्षिप्त रिपोर्ट है, पूरी जानकारी बाद में दी जाएगी। This is a brief report; complete information will be given later.
(yah ek sankshipt riport hai, poorii jaankaarii baad men dii jaaegii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
संक्षेप में, कंपनी को इस तिमाही में भारी नुकसान हुआ है। In brief, the company has suffered heavy losses this quarter.
(sankshep men, kampnii ko is timaahii men bhaarii nuksaan huaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
क्या आप इस मामले का संक्षिप्त सारांश तैयार कर सकते हैं? Can you prepare a brief summary of this matter?
(kyaa aap is maamle kaa sankshipt saaraansh taiyaar kar sakte hain?)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
पत्रकार ने घटना का संक्षिप्त विवरण अखबार में लिखा। The journalist wrote a brief account of the incident in the newspaper.
(patrkaar ne ghatnaa kaa sankshipt vivran akhbaar men likhaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर समय कम होता, तो हम संक्षिप्त संस्करण ही पढ़ते। If there had been less time, we would have read only the abbreviated version.
(agar samay kam hotaa, to ham sankshipt sanskran hii padhte।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
प्रबंधन चाहता है कि सभी रिपोर्ट संक्षिप्त और स्पष्ट हों। Management wants all reports to be brief and clear.
(prabandhan chaahtaa hai ki sabhii riport sankshipt aur spasht hon।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
इस किताब का संक्षिप्त संस्करण स्कूलों में पढ़ाया जाता है। An abbreviated edition of this book is taught in schools.
(is kitaab kaa sankshipt sanskran skoolon men padhaayaa jaataa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
मंत्री ने संक्षिप्त बयान देकर पत्रकारों के सवालों को टाल दिया। The minister deflected reporters' questions by giving a brief statement.
(mantrii ne sankshipt bayaan dekar patrkaaron ke savaalon ko taal diyaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
फॉर्म में संक्षिप्त उत्तर लिखने के निर्देश दिए गए हैं। Instructions have been given in the form to write brief answers.
(phorm men sankshipt uttar likhne ke nirdesh die gae hain।)
