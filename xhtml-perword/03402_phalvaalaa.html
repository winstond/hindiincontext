फलवाला (phalvaalaa)

noun A compound noun meaning 'fruit-seller/fruit-vendor', built from फल (phal, 'fruit') plus the agentive suffix -वाला (vaalaa) that turns a noun into 'the one who deals in X'.
Sense 1 : fruit seller/vendor
noun, masculine
Synonyms: फल बेचनेवाला (phal bechnevaalaa, descriptive phrase, less common than the compound)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi neighborhood has its own फलवाला (phalvaalaa), often pushing a wooden cart (ठेला, thelaa) piled with seasonal fruit and calling out prices as he moves down the street. The -वाला (vaalaa) suffix is one of Hindi's most productive patterns -- सब्ज़ीवाला (sabziivaalaa, 'vegetable seller'), दूधवाला (doodhvaalaa, 'milkman'), and रिक्शावाला (rikshaavaalaa, 'rickshaw driver') all follow the same template, so learning फलवाला (phalvaalaa) well unlocks a whole family of everyday words.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write it as two words, फल वाला (phal vaalaa); it is more commonly written and thought of as one compound word, फलवाला (phalvaalaa), though both are seen. 2) The feminine form फलवाली (phalvaalii) is used for a woman selling fruit -- don't default to the masculine form when referring to a female vendor.
