धड़ (dhad)

noun Primarily means the torso/trunk of the body, with a figurative extension to the main body or bulk of a structure or object.
Sense 1 : torso, trunk of the body
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शरीर (shariir, whole body, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word in news reports about accidents or crime -- सिर और धड़ अलग मिले (sir aur dhad alag mile, 'the head and torso were found separated') is a grim but common headline phrase, so the word carries a slightly clinical or news-register feel rather than everyday warmth.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse धड़ (dhad) with शरीर (shariir) -- धड़ (dhad) refers specifically to the trunk (excluding head and limbs), while शरीर (shariir) means the whole body.
Sample Sentences:
पुलिस को नदी में एक धड़ मिला। The police found a torso in the river.
(pulis ko nadii men ek dhad milaa।)

Sense 2 : main body or bulk of a structure
noun, masculine Used for the central mass of a non-human object, such as a building, vehicle, or tree, by extension from the body sense.
Synonyms: मुख्य भाग (mukhy bhaag, main part)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You might hear an engineer or builder refer to इमारत का धड़ (imaarat kaa dhad, 'the main body of the building') -- the word maps the human-body image onto structures, much like English 'body' of a car or plane.
