के नज़दीक (ke nazdiik)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'near/close to' -- built from नज़दीक (nazdiik, 'near') plus the connector के (ke) that links it to the preceding noun. Covers both physical closeness and figurative/relational closeness.
Sense 1 : physically near, close to (a place)
postposition Governs the oblique case of the preceding noun, like other compound postpositions ending in के (ke): घर के नज़दीक (ghar ke nazdiik, 'near the house').
Synonyms: के पास (ke paas), के करीब (ke kariib)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common on signboards and in directions -- ऑटो वाला (oto vaalaa) or a shopkeeper will casually say मेट्रो के नज़दीक है (metro ke nazdiik hai, 'it's near the metro') to help you get your bearings in Delhi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop के (ke) and just say नज़दीक (nazdiik) alone with a noun, which sounds incomplete; the connector है (hai)... needs के (ke) before नज़दीक (nazdiik) when following a noun, e.g. स्टेशन के नज़दीक (steshan ke nazdiik, 'near the station'), not स्टेशन नज़दीक (steshan nazdiik).
Sample Sentences:
मेरा घर मेट्रो स्टेशन के नज़दीक है। My house is near the metro station.
(meraa ghar metro steshan ke nazdiik hai।)

Sense 2 : close to (in relationship, feeling, or time)
postposition Used for emotional or relational closeness, e.g. दिल के नज़दीक (dil ke nazdiik, 'close to the heart'), or approaching a point in time, e.g. शाम के नज़दीक (shaam ke nazdiik, 'close to evening').
Synonyms: के करीब (ke kariib)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi speakers extend spatial nearness to feelings quite naturally -- वह मेरे दिल के नज़दीक है (vah mere dil ke nazdiik hai, 'he/she is close to my heart') is a common warm expression, paralleling how पास (paas) also does double duty for physical and emotional closeness.
Sample Sentences:
वह मेरे दिल के नज़दीक है। He is close to my heart.
