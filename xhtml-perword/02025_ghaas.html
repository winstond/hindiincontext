घास (ghaas)

noun A simple, everyday noun meaning 'grass' or 'hay/fodder'; it also appears in a couple of common fixed idioms about being ignored or not worth much.
Sense 1 : grass
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR, घास (ghaas) is the word you'd use for the lawn in a park (जैसे लोधी गार्डन की घास, jaise lodhii gaardan kii ghaas, 'like the grass of Lodhi Garden') or for the patchy grass parks outside a housing colony where kids play cricket in the evening.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat घास (ghaas) as countable like English 'a grass, grasses'; it is normally used as a mass noun, so quantity is expressed with words like घास का तिनका (ghaas kaa tinkaa, 'a blade of grass') rather than a plural.

Sense 2 : fodder, hay (for animals)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: चारा (chaaraa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Anyone who has seen a buffalo or cow being fed at a dairy (तबेला, tabelaa) in an urban village has heard घास (ghaas) used this way -- it is the everyday word for what livestock eat, distinct from चारा (chaaraa) which leans more toward cut/prepared feed.

Sense 3 : something worthless, not worth attention (idiomatic)
noun, feminine Lives almost entirely inside the fixed expression घास न डालना (ghaas n daalnaa, 'to not give someone the time of day') and related phrasing like घास डालना (ghaas daalnaa) in the negative -- rarely used as a free-standing noun with this meaning.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture feeding grass to cattle -- कोई घास नहीं डालता (koii ghaas nahiin daaltaa, 'no one throws grass [its way]') captures the image of an animal being deliberately ignored and left unfed, which is exactly the emotional register of this idiom when used about a person: 'nobody gives him any attention.'
Common Mistakes: 1) घास डालना (ghaas daalnaa) meaning 'to pay attention to someone' is almost always used in the negative (घास नहीं डालना, ghaas nahiin daalnaa) -- learners sometimes try to use it in the positive to mean 'I like him,' which sounds odd; positive attention is expressed differently.
Sample Sentences:
उसे कोई घास नहीं डालता। Nobody pays him any attention.
(use koii ghaas nahiin daaltaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
