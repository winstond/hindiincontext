छात्रवृत्ति (chhaatrvritti)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'scholarship' (the financial award for students), formed from छात्र (chhaatr, 'student') + वृत्ति (vritti, 'livelihood/income').
Sense 1 : scholarship (financial award for students)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: स्कॉलरशिप (skolarship, common in casual speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday conversation among students and parents, especially around exam and admission season, स्कॉलरशिप (skolarship) is actually heard more often than छात्रवृत्ति (chhaatrvritti), which feels a bit more official or textbook-ish -- you'll see छात्रवृत्ति on government forms and school notices, while spoken Hindi leans English. Government scholarship schemes (like मेधावी छात्रवृत्ति, medhaavii chhaatrvritti, 'merit scholarship') keep the Hindi term alive in formal contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use यह (yah) with a verb form that doesn't agree in gender -- छात्रवृत्ति (chhaatrvritti) is feminine, so verbs and adjectives referring to it take feminine agreement, e.g. छात्रवृत्ति मिली (chhaatrvritti milii, 'the scholarship was received'), not मिला (milaa). 2) The verb commonly paired with this word is मिलना (milnaa, 'to be received/obtained') rather than पाना (paanaa) or लेना (lenaa) -- students say मुझे छात्रवृत्ति मिली (mujhe chhaatrvritti milii, 'I got the scholarship'), using the दाता-प्राप्तकर्ता (giver-receiver) framing with को (ko) marking the recipient.
