चिड़िया (chidiyaa)

noun Mainly means 'bird' (a small bird, often a sparrow-type), grammatically feminine no matter the bird's actual sex; it also has a colloquial extension meaning an easy target or dupe, chiefly heard in con-artist/underworld slang.
Sense 1 : bird (especially a small bird, e.g. sparrow)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: पंछी (panchhii), पक्षी (pakshii, formal/written), परिंदा (parindaa, common in poetry and film songs)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: चिड़िया (chidiyaa) is the word every Hindi-speaking kid learns in the same breath as कव्वा (kavvaa, crow) and तोता (totaa, parrot) -- it is the generic 'little bird' of nursery rhymes and school essays, not a scientific term. You will also hear चिड़िया रानी (chidiyaa raanii) as a term of endearment for a little girl, likening her to a sweet, flitting bird.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पक्षी (pakshii) in ordinary speech; it sounds bookish there -- चिड़िया (chidiyaa) is what people actually say when pointing at a sparrow on the balcony. 2) The word stays grammatically feminine even when talking about a male bird, so adjectives and verbs agree with the feminine form regardless of the bird's real sex.

Sense 2 : an easy target, dupe, or mark (slang)
noun, feminine Used as the object of verbs like फँसाना (phansaanaa, to trap) or फँसना (phansnaa, to get trapped) -- e.g. चिड़िया फँसाना (chidiyaa phansaanaa, 'to trap a mark') is a set phrase in con-artist slang.
Synonyms: शिकार (shikaar, prey/victim)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This usage comes straight out of the imagery of trapping a bird: in Hindi crime dramas and street slang, the con man's victim is the चिड़िया (chidiyaa) waiting to be netted, and the swindle itself is often called फँसाना (phansaanaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense only makes sense in context involving scams, gambling, or cons -- using it about a random unlucky person outside that frame will just sound confusing rather than clever. 2) Don't confuse this with the literal bird sense; nothing about feathers or flight survives into the slang meaning, only the idea of something small and easily caught.
