भालू (bhaaloo)

noun A common noun meaning 'bear' (the animal), used both literally and, occasionally, as an affectionate or teasing nickname for a big, hairy, or clumsy person.
Sense 1 : bear (the animal)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Sloth bears (bhaaloo) were historically associated with madaari (bear-handler) street performances in India, a practice now banned; older folks may still reference 'भालू का नाच' (bhaaloo kaa naach, 'the bear's dance') when talking about old-time street entertainment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget भालू (bhaaloo) is masculine even when referring to a female bear -- gender agreement stays masculine unless a specific feminine form like भालूनी (bhaaloonii) is used, which is rare in everyday speech.

Sense 2 : big, hairy, or clumsy person (affectionate/teasing)
noun, masculine Used informally as a term of endearment or teasing, often toward a physically large, hairy, or lovably clumsy man.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Calling someone भालू (bhaaloo) affectionately -- like calling a big cuddly guy a 'teddy bear' in English -- is common banter among friends or between couples in casual Hindi speech, especially in urban settings.
