सफ़ेद (safed)

adjective The everyday word for the color white; one core sense, occasionally extended figuratively (as in white hair signaling age, or a 'blank/pale' face).
Sense 1 : white (color)
adjective
Synonyms: श्वेत (shvet, literary), धवल (dhaval, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: White clothing carries strong cultural weight in North India: politicians favor सफ़ेद कुर्ता-पाजामा (safed kurtaa-paajaamaa, 'white kurta-pajama') as a mark of simplicity, while it is also the color of mourning at funerals, unlike in the West where black plays that role. Calling someone's hair सफ़ेद (safed) is a common, gentle way to note they've aged, similar to saying 'gone gray' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often write सफेद (saphed) without the nuqta under फ; both spellings are seen, but सफ़ेद (safed) with the nuqta is the standard form taught in schools and used in print. 2) As an invariant adjective, it does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- सफ़ेद कमीज़ (safed kamiiz, 'white shirt') and सफ़ेद कुर्ता (safed kurtaa, 'white kurta') both use the same form.
