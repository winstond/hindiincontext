अफ़सर (afsar)

noun A single-sense loanword meaning 'officer, official' -- someone in a position of administrative or governmental authority.
Sense 1 : officer, official
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अधिकारी (adhikaarii), साहब (saahab, used as a form of address rather than a plain synonym)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: अफ़सर (afsar) comes from Persian/Arabic via Urdu and carries a distinct whiff of bureaucratic power in Indian life -- the word conjures the classic sarkaari daftar (sarkaarii daftar, 'government office') scene, where an अफ़सर (afsar) sits behind a desk piled with files while people wait outside. It is often paired with बड़ा (badaa, 'big/senior') to mean a high-ranking official, and with सरकारी (sarkaarii, 'government') to specify a public-sector officer as opposed to a private-company manager.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume अफ़सर (afsar) only means a police officer, but it covers any government or administrative official -- a बड़ा अफ़सर (badaa afsar, 'a big/senior officer') might work in a tax office, a bank, or a ministry. 2) The nuqta in अफ़सर (afsar) is often dropped in casual writing as अफसर (aphsar); both are understood, but the dotted form is standard.
