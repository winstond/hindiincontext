जब (jab)

conjunction A relative adverb meaning 'when', almost always paired with तब (tab, 'then') or used alone to mean 'whenever'; it is the backbone of Hindi's जब...तब correlative time construction.
Sense 1 : when (relative time connector)
conjunction Works as the relative half of a correlative pair: जब (relative clause) ... तब/तो (main clause), though तब/तो is often dropped in casual speech.
Synonyms: जिस समय (jis samay, 'the time at which')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of जब (jab) and कब (kab) as a matched pair the way English has 'when' (statement) versus 'when?' (question) -- except Hindi actually uses two different words, which is a common trap for learners used to English's single 'when' doing both jobs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that जब (jab) needs a correlative तब (tab) or तो (to) in the main clause for formal or written style, even though spoken Hindi frequently drops it; dropping it is natural in conversation but can feel incomplete in writing. 2) जब (jab) is not used for yes/no questions about time ("when did you come?" uses कब (kab), not जब (jab)) -- जब is only for linking clauses, never for asking a direct question.

Sense 2 : whenever (indefinite/habitual time)
conjunction Often reinforced with भी (bhii) as जब भी (jab bhii, 'whenever') to stress the indefinite, repeated nature of the time reference.
Synonyms: जब भी (jab bhii, 'whenever, emphatic')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear जब भी मौका मिले (jab bhii maukaa mile, 'whenever I/one gets the chance') constantly in everyday chatter -- it's a handy frame for talking about habits and recurring opportunities.
