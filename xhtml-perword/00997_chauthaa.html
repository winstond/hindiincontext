चौथा (chauthaa)

adjective/noun The ordinal number 'fourth'; it also has an inherited-property/rent sense from Maratha-era history that a learner may encounter in historical or literary contexts.
Sense 1 : fourth (ordinal number)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday ranking talk -- चौथी कक्षा (chauthii kakshaa, 'fourth grade'), चौथा नंबर (chauthaa nambar, 'fourth position') -- much like 'fourth' in English school and sports contexts. It follows the same family pattern as अगला (aglaa) and other variable adjectives ending in -आ (aa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget this word inflects like a regular -आ (aa) adjective: चौथा (chauthaa) becomes चौथी (chauthii) before a feminine noun, e.g. चौथी मंज़िल (chauthii manzil, 'fourth floor'), and चौथे (chauthe) in the oblique/plural, e.g. चौथे नंबर पर (chauthe nambar par, 'in fourth place'). 2) Do not confuse with चार (chaar), the cardinal number 'four' -- चौथा (chauthaa) is only used for ranking/ordering, never for counting quantity.
Sample Sentences:
मेरा बेटा चौथी कक्षा में पढ़ता है। My son studies in fourth grade.
(meraa betaa chauthii kakshaa men padhtaa hai।)

Sense 2 : a quarter; one-fourth share
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चौथाई (chauthaaii, more common for the fraction 'one-fourth')
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Historically, चौथ (chauth) or चौथा (chauthaa) referred to the one-fourth levy the Marathas collected from territories as a form of tribute -- you may meet this in history lessons or period dramas, but it is not something you would use in casual conversation today.
Common Mistakes: 1) For everyday fractions ('a quarter of the mango', 'one-fourth of the money'), native speakers usually reach for चौथाई (chauthaaii) rather than चौथा (chauthaa) as a noun -- this noun sense of चौथा (chauthaa) survives mainly in the historical tax term below.
Sample Sentences:
मराठों ने कई राज्यों से चौथा वसूल किया। The Marathas collected a quarter-levy from many kingdoms.
