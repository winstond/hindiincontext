दिन (din)

noun Basic word for 'day' -- covers both the 24-hour calendar day and the daylight portion as opposed to night. It also appears in several everyday idiomatic phrases about time and circumstance.
Sense 1 : day (calendar unit / 24 hours)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दिवस (divas, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: दिन (din) is one of the first words learners meet, often in greetings like शुभ दिन (shubh din, 'have a good day') or in asking किस दिन? (kis din, 'on which day?'). Note that it is masculine, so आज का दिन (aaj kaa din, 'today's day') takes का (kaa), not की (kii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use को (ko) with दिन (din) the way they would with a clock time, but for 'on that day' the natural phrase is उस दिन (us din, 'on that day') with no postposition needed -- दिन (din) itself functions adverbially here.
Sample Sentences:

(aaj kaun saa din hai?)

Sense 2 : daytime (as opposed to night)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday speech दिन (din) and रात (raat) are constantly paired, as in दिन-रात (din-raat, 'day and night, constantly') -- a common way to describe someone working nonstop, like a mother saying her son studies दिन-रात (din-raat) before exams.

Sense 3 : period, era, time in one's life (often plural in sense)
noun, masculine Used in phrases like अच्छे दिन (achchhe din, 'good days/times') or बुरे दिन (bure din, 'hard times') to talk about a phase of life or fortune rather than a specific date.
Synonyms: ज़माना (zamaanaa, broader scope, era/times), वक़्त (vaqt, broader scope, time in general)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: अच्छे दिन (achchhe din, 'good days') became a famous political slogan in India, but in ordinary conversation you will hear grandparents reminisce about उनके दिन (unke din, 'their days/era') with real nostalgia -- it frames a whole stretch of life, not just 24 hours.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate 'these days' literally as ये दिन (ye din) when आजकल (aajkal, 'these days') is the natural adverb Hindi speakers reach for instead.
Sample Sentences:
वो हमारे कॉलेज के दिन थे। Those were our college days.
(vo hamaare kolej ke din the।)
