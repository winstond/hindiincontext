सुधरना (sudharnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to improve, to reform, to get better' -- used for people's character/behavior, situations, health, or conditions. Its transitive partner, meaning 'to fix/reform something,' is सुधारना (sudhaarnaa), a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to improve, get better (situation, condition, health)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बेहतर होना (behtar honaa), ठीक होना (thiik honaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb every worried parent or teacher uses about a child who needs to shape up: वह अब सुधर गया है (vah ab sudhar gayaa hai, 'he's reformed now') is a classic line in family dramas. It pairs constantly with जाना (jaanaa) as a compound -- सुधर जाना (sudhar jaanaa) -- to add a sense of completed change, 'to turn over a new leaf.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सुधारना (sudhaarnaa) when they mean सुधरना (sudharnaa) -- if the subject itself is getting better (not fixing something else), use सुधरना (sudharnaa): हालात सुधर गए (haalaat sudhar gae, 'the situation improved'), not हालात ने सुधारा। 2) With ने-marking: सुधरना (sudharnaa) is intransitive, so the subject never takes ने (ne) even in the perfective -- वह सुधर गया (vah sudhar gayaa, 'he reformed'), never उसने सुधरा।
Sample Sentences:
मौसम में सुधार हो रहा है, अब हालात सुधर रहे हैं। The weather is improving, conditions are getting better now.
(mausam men sudhaar ho rahaa hai, ab haalaat sudhar rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : to reform, mend one's ways (of character/behavior)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: रास्ते पर आना (raaste par aanaa, idiom)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Used of a person's character rather than external circumstances, this sense carries a moral tone -- a reformed rogue, a disciplined student. Elders often say अब भी सुधर जाओ (ab bhii sudhar jaao, 'reform yourself even now') as a mix of warning and hope, which captures how central this verb is to Hindi scoldings.
Sample Sentences:
अब भी सुधर जाओ, वरना पछताओगे। Reform yourself even now, or you'll regret it.
(ab bhii sudhar jaao, varnaa pachhtaaoge।)
