आहिस्ता (aahistaa)

adverb A single-sense adverb meaning 'slowly' or 'gently' -- often doubled as आहिस्ता आहिस्ता (aahistaa aahistaa) for extra emphasis, much like 'slowly, slowly' in English.
Sense 1 : slowly, gently, softly
adverb
Synonyms: धीरे (dhiire), धीरे-धीरे (dhiire-dhiire, reduplicated form), धीमे (dhiime)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word has a soft, almost tender feel to it -- you will hear a mother telling a child आहिस्ता चलो (aahistaa chalo, 'walk carefully/slowly') or a rickshaw passenger asking the driver to ease up. It carries a slightly gentler, more affectionate tone than the more neutral धीरे (dhiire), and shows up often in Urdu-influenced Hindi film dialogue and songs about tenderness or caution.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for धीरे (dhiire) and miss that आहिस्ता (aahistaa) is just as common in speech, especially when asking someone to slow down or lower their voice, e.g. आहिस्ता बोलो (aahistaa bolo, 'speak softly'). 2) The doubled form आहिस्ता आहिस्ता (aahistaa aahistaa) is not just for emphasis of speed -- it is also used for gradual processes, like a slow, steady change, similar to धीरे-धीरे (dhiire-dhiire).
Sample Sentences:
आहिस्ता चलो, यहाँ फिसलन है। Walk slowly, it's slippery here.
(aahistaa chalo, yahaan phislan hai।)
