फ़ोन करना (fon karnaa)

verb A common compound expression meaning 'to make a phone call'; built from the loanword फ़ोन (fon, 'phone') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do').
Sense 1 : to phone/call someone
verb, transitive The person being called is marked with को (ko): उसे फ़ोन करना (use fon karnaa, 'to call him/her').
Synonyms: कॉल करना (kol karnaa, colloquial), फ़ोन लगाना (fon lagaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday, spoken way to say 'call someone' in Delhi-NCR -- फ़ोन कर देना (fon kar denaa, 'just give a call') is a very common request among friends and family. Note ज़्यादातर (zyaadaatar) young people also freely use कॉल करना (kol karnaa) interchangeably, especially with mobile phones, but फ़ोन करना (fon karnaa) remains the more broadly natural choice across generations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say फ़ोन करना (fon karnaa) with से (se) on the person called, by analogy with 'call from' in English -- but the recipient of the call takes को (ko), not से (se): उसे फ़ोन करो (use fon karo, 'call him'), not उससे फ़ोन करो. 2) फ़ोन (fon) itself is invariant here -- learners occasionally try to pluralize or inflect it as if it were a native noun being modified, but in this fixed expression it stays bare before करना (karnaa).
