कापी (kaapii)

noun A borrowed word meaning a notebook/copybook used for school or office writing; also carries a secondary photocopy/duplicate sense in some contexts, closely tied to the English word 'copy' it comes from.
Sense 1 : notebook, exercise book
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नोटबुक (notbuk)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian schoolkid knows the ritual of covering a new कापी (kaapii) with brown paper at the start of the school year and writing their name and class on the front page. Stationery shops near any school sell these by the dozen before term starts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse कापी (kaapii) with किताब (kitaab, 'book') -- कापी (kaapii) is specifically the blank/lined notebook students write in, not a printed book with content to read.

Sense 2 : copy, duplicate (of a document)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नकल (nakal), प्रतिलिपि (pratilipi, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this at any फोटोकॉपी (photokopii) shop near a government office, where clerks casually ask for 'दो कापी (do kaapii)' of a form -- Hindi and English usage blend seamlessly here.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense कापी (kaapii) usually refers to a photocopy or duplicate of a document, not a copy of a book or film -- for those, native speakers often just switch to English 'copy' or use प्रति (prati).
