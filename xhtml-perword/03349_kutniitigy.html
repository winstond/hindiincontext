कुटनीतिज्ञ (kutniitigy)

noun A formal, single-sense word meaning 'diplomat' -- someone skilled in the tactful handling of relations, whether in official statecraft or, by extension, in everyday dealings with people.
Sense 1 : diplomat, a person skilled in tactful negotiation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: राजनयिक (raajanyik, formal, used specifically for official diplomatic posts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word most in news broadcasts and political discussion, talking about ambassadors and foreign-ministry officials, but Delhi households also use it affectionately or sarcastically -- 'बड़े कुटनीतिज्ञ बने हो' (bade kutniitigy bane ho, 'aren't you being quite the diplomat') when someone dodges a tricky family question with practiced tact.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this word only for professional diplomats, but native speakers also apply it loosely to anyone who handles delicate situations or people tactfully, e.g. a smooth-talking relative or coworker -- similar to calling someone 'a real diplomat' in English. 2) It is spelled with ट, not ट्ट or त, and the nasalized ज्ञ (gy) ending is often mispronounced by learners as 'gya' when it is closer to 'gy-a' with a soft nasal.
