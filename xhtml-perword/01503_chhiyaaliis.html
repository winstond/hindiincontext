छियालीस (chhiyaaliis)

numeral The cardinal number 46 (forty-six).
Sense 1 : the number forty-six (46)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers in the 40s, छियालीस (chhiyaaliis) sits in a stretch of the number line that even native speakers sometimes fumble when rattling off phone numbers or prices quickly -- it is common to hear people double-check by repeating digits individually, e.g. "चार छह" (chaar chhah, 'four six'), especially over the phone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 1 to 99 are each irregular and must be memorized individually rather than built compositionally like English "forty" + "six" -- छियालीस (chhiyaaliis) does not visibly decompose into छह (chhah, six) plus चालीस (chaaliis, forty), so learners should not try to derive it on the fly.

(merii mausii kii umr chhiyaaliis saal hai।)
