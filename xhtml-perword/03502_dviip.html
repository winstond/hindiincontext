द्वीप (dviip)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'island'; a Sanskrit-derived word common in geography, news, and place names.
Sense 1 : island
noun, masculine
Synonyms: टापू (taapoo, everyday, esp. small/informal island)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet द्वीप (dviip) constantly in the names of India's island territories -- अंडमान द्वीप (andmaan dviip) and लक्षद्वीप (lakshadviip, 'the hundred-thousand islands') -- so it is a word learners absorb early even if टापू (taapoo) feels more natural in casual chat about a beach holiday.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for टापू (taapoo) in casual speech and द्वीप (dviip) in writing or formal contexts (news, geography); both are correct but द्वीप (dviip) leans slightly more bookish/formal. 2) Note the compound अंडमान और निकोबार द्वीप समूह (andmaan aur nikobaar dviip samooh, 'Andaman and Nicobar Islands') where द्वीप (dviip) combines with समूह (samooh, group) for 'archipelago' -- learners sometimes try to pluralize द्वीप (dviip) alone instead.
