सैंतीस (saintiis)

numeral The number thirty-seven; a single-sense cardinal numeral with no complications.
Sense 1 : the number 37
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 1 to 100 are each a distinct word rather than a predictable compound, a leftover of how the number system evolved historically -- native speakers just memorize them as children the way English speakers memorize irregular verbs. There is no special cultural weight to सैंतीस (saintiis) itself; it behaves exactly like any other number in the teens-to-nineties range.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सैंतीस (saintiis, '37') with सैंतालीस (saintaaliis, '47') or सत्रह (satrah, '17') due to similar sound patterns -- Hindi numerals 11-99 are not built from a predictable formula the way English ones are, so each has to be learned as its own word. 2) Like most Hindi numerals, it stays the same before both masculine and feminine nouns (सैंतीस लड़के saintiis ladke, 'thirty-seven boys' and सैंतीस लड़कियाँ saintiis ladkiyaan, 'thirty-seven girls'), which can feel odd if you are expecting adjective-like agreement.
