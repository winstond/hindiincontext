कितना (kitnaa)

adjective/pronoun/adverb An interrogative/exclamatory word meaning 'how much/how many' as a question, and 'how (much)!' as an exclamation of degree; agrees in gender and number with the noun it modifies or stands for.
Sense 1 : how much / how many (interrogative)
adjective, masculine/feminine Agrees in gender/number with the noun: कितना पानी (kitnaa paanii, how much water), कितनी किताबें (kitnii kitaaben, how many books).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the go-to word for asking price, quantity, distance, or age -- यह कितने का है (yah kitne kaa hai, 'how much is this for') is one of the most common phrases you will hear in any Indian market or shop.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use कितना (kitnaa) for both countable and uncountable nouns without adjusting gender/number agreement -- remember it changes form: कितना, कितनी, कितने (kitnaa, kitnii, kitne). 2) For asking someone's age, the natural frame is आपकी उम्र कितनी है (aapkii umr kitnii hai, 'what is your age'), not a literal 'how old' construction.

Sense 2 : how much (standing alone, referring to an unspecified quantity)
pronoun, masculine/feminine Used without an accompanying noun when the quantity referred to is understood from context, e.g. इसमें से कितना बचा है (ismen se kitnaa bachaa hai, 'how much of this is left').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This pronoun use overlaps closely with its adjective sense; think of it as कितना (kitnaa) 'standing in' for a noun already mentioned or implied in the conversation.

Sense 3 : how (much), to what degree -- in exclamations
adverb Functions as an intensifier in exclamatory sentences, typically with an adjective: कितना अच्छा है (kitnaa achchhaa hai, 'how good it is!').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the Hindi equivalent of starting a sentence with 'How...!' in English -- कितना प्यारा बच्चा है (kitnaa pyaaraa bachchaa hai, 'what a lovely child!') is exactly the kind of warm exclamation you will hear from aunties admiring a baby.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget the exclamatory tone requires no question mark or interrogative word order -- कितना अच्छा है! (kitnaa achchhaa hai!, 'how nice!') is a statement of emotion, not a question, even though कितना (kitnaa) looks interrogative.
