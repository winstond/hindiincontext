बचना (bachnaa)

verb The core meaning is 'to be saved / escape / survive', with an everyday extended sense of 'to be left over' (money, food, time) and a common idiomatic sense of 'to avoid/steer clear of' something, taking से (se).
Sense 1 : to be saved, escape (danger, harm, death)
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the danger or thing escaped: दुर्घटना से बचना (durghatnaa se bachnaa, 'to escape/survive an accident').
Synonyms: सुरक्षित रहना (surakshit rahnaa), बच जाना (bach jaanaa, with completive nuance)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you hear after any brush with disaster -- a car accident, a fall, an illness -- as in बच गया (bach gayaa, 'he survived/escaped'). Its causative बचाना (bachaanaa, 'to save someone') is a closely related lemma worth studying alongside it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छूटना (chhootnaa) here, but छूटना (chhootnaa) is about being let go/missing something (like a bus), not surviving danger -- बचना (bachnaa) is the word for coming out of harm alive. 2) The thing one is saved *from* is marked with से (se), not से बचाना or को.
Sample Sentences:
एक्सीडेंट में वह बुरी तरह ज़ख़्मी हुआ, लेकिन बच गया। He was badly injured in the accident, but he survived.
(eksiident men vah burii tarah zakhmii huaa, lekin bach gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to be left over, remain (money, food, time)
verb, intransitive The thing left over is the grammatical subject: पैसे बच गए (paise bach gae, 'money was left over'); often paired with a possessor via के पास (ke paas).
Synonyms: शेष रहना (shesh rahnaa, formal), रह जाना (rah jaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around money and food -- महीने के आखिर में कुछ नहीं बचता (mahiine ke aakhir men kuchh nahiin bachtaa, 'nothing is left by the end of the month') is a very relatable household complaint in India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to mark the person with a subject case, but the person who has leftover money/time is expressed with के पास (ke paas) or को (ko), while बचना (bachnaa) agrees with the thing remaining, not the person.
Sample Sentences:

(mahiine ke aakhir men kuchh nahiin bachtaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : to avoid, keep away from (a person, situation, habit)
verb, intransitive The thing avoided is marked with से (se): झगड़े से बचना (jhagde se bachnaa, 'to avoid a quarrel').
Synonyms: परहेज़ करना (parahez karnaa, especially for food/habits), दूर रहना (door rahnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Elders love using this in advice: बुरी संगत से बचो (burii sangat se bacho, 'stay away from bad company') is the kind of line every Hindi-speaking household has heard at least once.
Common Mistakes: 1) The object of avoidance always takes से (se), never को (ko) -- a common slip for learners transferring English 'avoid something'.
Sample Sentences:
