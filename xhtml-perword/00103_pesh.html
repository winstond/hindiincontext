पेश (pesh)

verb पेश (pesh) is a bound word that almost always shows up glued to a light verb -- most commonly पेश करना (pesh karnaa, 'to present/put forward') and पेश आना (pesh aanaa, 'to behave/treat someone in a certain way'); it does not stand alone as a free noun or adjective.
Sense 1 : to present, submit, put forward
verb, transitive Occurs only as part of the compound पेश करना (pesh karnaa); पेश (pesh) itself is an invariant participle-like form borrowed from Persian and does not inflect.
Synonyms: प्रस्तुत करना (prastut karnaa, formal), दिखाना (dikhaanaa, to show/display)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear पेश करना (pesh karnaa) constantly in courtrooms, classrooms, and news broadcasts -- 'सबूत पेश करना' (saboot pesh karnaa, 'to present evidence') is a staple of Bollywood courtroom dramas, and TV anchors say 'अब पेश है...' (ab pesh hai, 'now presenting...') before a news segment or performance.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use पेश (pesh) alone as if it were a standalone adjective or noun meaning 'presented' -- it almost never appears without करना (karnaa) or आना (aanaa). 2) पेश करना (pesh karnaa) is transitive and takes ने (ne) in the perfective: उसने रिपोर्ट पेश की (usne riport pesh kii, 'he/she presented the report').
Sample Sentences:
वकील ने अदालत में सबूत पेश किया। The lawyer presented evidence in court.
(vakiil ne adaalat men saboot pesh kiyaa।)

Sense 2 : to behave or treat (someone) in a certain way
verb, intransitive Used in the fixed frame X से ऐसा/वैसा पेश आना (pesh aanaa) -- 'to behave a certain way toward X'; the manner word (अच्छा, बुरा, ऐसा, etc.) comes before पेश आना (pesh aanaa).
Synonyms: व्यवहार करना (vyavahaar karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a slightly formal, almost old-fashioned courtesy to it -- you might hear an elder say किसी से अच्छा पेश आना चाहिए (kisii se achchhaa pesh aanaa chaahie, 'one should treat others well') as a piece of moral advice.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with पेश करना (pesh karnaa) because both use पेश (pesh) -- but पेश आना (pesh aanaa) is intransitive and describes conduct toward a person, marked with से (se), not को (ko): वह मुझसे अच्छा पेश आया (vah mujhse achchhaa pesh aayaa, 'he treated me well').
Sample Sentences:
हमें सबसे अच्छा पेश आना चाहिए। We should treat everyone well.
(hamen sabse achchhaa pesh aanaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
