वास्तव (vaastav)

adverb/adjective This word is rarely used on its own; it lives almost entirely in the fixed adverbial phrase वास्तव में (vaastav men, 'in reality'), and as an adjective meaning 'real/actual'. The related word you'll hear far more often in casual speech is असल में (asal men).
Sense 1 : in reality, actually, in fact
adverb Almost always appears as the fixed phrase वास्तव में (vaastav men) -- वास्तव (vaastav) alone does not function as this adverb; the में (men) is essential to the meaning.
Synonyms: असल में (asal men), वस्तुतः (vastutah, literary), दरअसल (daraasal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: वास्तव में (vaastav men) is the phrase a news anchor or teacher reaches for to correct a misconception -- 'वास्तव में, ऐसा नहीं है' (vaastav men, aisaa nahiin hai, 'in reality, it isn't like that'). In everyday chat among friends, दरअसल (daraasal) or असल में (asal men) feels much more natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Dropping में (men) and using वास्तव (vaastav) alone to mean 'actually' -- this doesn't work; you need the full phrase वास्तव में (vaastav men, 'in reality'). 2) Learners sometimes overuse this phrase where a native speaker would just say असल में (asal men) or दरअसल (daraasal) in conversation -- वास्तव में (vaastav men) reads slightly more formal, like starting a sentence with 'in actual fact' rather than 'actually.'

Sense 2 : real, actual, true
adjective
Synonyms: असली (aslii), सच्चा (sachchaa), यथार्थ (yathaarth, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see वास्तव (vaastav) far more in newspapers, essays, and formal speeches than in kitchen-table conversation, where असल (asal) and सच (sach) do the same work more casually.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for वास्तव (vaastav) as a standalone adjective before नाम (naam) or बात (baat) type nouns, but native speakers overwhelmingly prefer असली (aslii) in casual speech for 'real' -- वास्तव (vaastav) as a bare adjective sounds a notch more formal or written. 2) Don't confuse this with वास्तविक (vaastvik), which is the more common adjective form actually used before nouns (वास्तविक कारण (vaastvik kaaran, 'the real reason')) -- वास्तव (vaastav) itself is mostly reserved for the में (men) phrase.
