धड़कना (dhadaknaa)

verb A single-sense intransitive verb meaning 'to beat/throb/pound' -- almost always about the heart, but also usable for a fearful, pounding sensation more generally.
Sense 1 : to beat, throb, pound (of the heart)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: फड़कना (phadaknaa, for a twitching pulse or muscle, narrower)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi treats the heart, not the person, as the one doing the beating -- दिल धड़क रहा है (dil dhadak rahaa hai) literally 'the heart is beating,' which fits the wider pattern of body parts acting as their own agents (compare पेट दर्द करना (pet dard karnaa)). It is a favorite verb of Bollywood lyrics for describing nervous excitement or the flutter of new love, so you will hear it constantly in romantic songs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use धड़कना (dhadaknaa) with a human subject the way English says 'I am pounding with fear' -- Hindi instead keeps दिल (dil) or दिल की धड़कन (dil kii dhadkan) as the subject: दिल धड़क रहा है (dil dhadak rahaa hai, 'the heart is pounding'), not मैं धड़क रहा हूँ (main dhadak rahaa hoon). 2) The related noun धड़कन (dhadkan, 'heartbeat/pulse') is often needed alongside the verb in natural speech, e.g. धड़कनें तेज़ हो गईं (dhadaknen tez ho gaiin, 'the heartbeats quickened') as an alternative to using धड़कना (dhadaknaa) itself.
