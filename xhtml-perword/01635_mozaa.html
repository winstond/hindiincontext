मोज़ा (mozaa)

noun A simple everyday noun meaning 'sock'; usually met in its plural form मोज़े (moze) since socks come in pairs.
Sense 1 : sock
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मोज़ा (mozaa) is a Persian-derived loanword that came into Hindi long ago and now feels completely native -- no one thinks of it as foreign. You will hear it constantly in the context of school uniforms, since matching मोज़े (moze) are a standard part of the dress code Indian parents fuss over every morning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use the singular मोज़ा (mozaa) when talking about a pair being worn, but Hindi speakers almost always say मोज़े (moze) since you wear two at once -- singular मोज़ा (mozaa) refers to just one sock. 2) Some learners write or say मोजा (mojaa) without the nuqta on ज़; the nuqta pronunciation (mozaa, 'zaa' sound) is standard in Delhi-NCR speech, though the plain ज is heard too in casual speech.
