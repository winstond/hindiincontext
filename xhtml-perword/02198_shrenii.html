श्रेणी (shrenii)

noun A somewhat formal/bookish word covering 'category, class' (of things) and 'rank/grade' (of people or quality); also used in mathematics for 'series'.
Sense 1 : category, class, group
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वर्ग (varg), प्रकार (prakaar, type/kind)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly on government forms and exam notices -- income category, job category, reservation category -- so it carries a slightly bureaucratic, official flavor rather than a casual, conversational one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for किस्म (kism) or प्रकार (prakaar) in casual speech; श्रेणी (shrenii) sounds more formal/administrative and fits official, academic, or written contexts better than everyday chat.

Sense 2 : rank, grade, class (of quality or standing)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दर्जा (darjaa), स्तर (star, level)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word stamped on old Indian Railways tickets -- प्रथम श्रेणी (pratham shrenii) and द्वितीय श्रेणी (dvitiiy shrenii) for first and second class -- and it still shows up in exam results and job postings as a mark of standing or grade.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense श्रेणी (shrenii) often appears with an ordinal, e.g. प्रथम श्रेणी (pratham shrenii, 'first class') or द्वितीय श्रेणी (dvitiiy shrenii, 'second class') -- learners sometimes use कक्षा (kakshaa) here instead, which is reserved for school grades/classrooms, not general rank.

Sense 3 : series (mathematics)
noun, feminine Specialized use in mathematics for a sequence/series, e.g. गणितीय श्रेणी (ganitiiy shrenii, 'arithmetic/mathematical series').
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the term you would meet in a school maths textbook chapter on progressions -- worth knowing if you ever help a child with homework, but not something that comes up in daily conversation.
