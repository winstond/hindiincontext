हटाना (hataanaa)

verb The causative partner of हटना (hatnaa, 'to move away/aside'): हटाना (hataanaa) means 'to move something aside, remove, or take away.' It carries one core sense with figurative extensions (removing a person from a post, clearing an obstacle).
Sense 1 : to move something aside, remove, take away
verb, transitive
Synonyms: हटा देना (hataa denaa, with completive nuance), निकालना (nikaalnaa, take out/expel), दूर करना (door karnaa, slightly more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in daily traffic and household life: a rickshaw-wallah shouting हटो, हटो! (hato, hato!, 'move, move!') is telling people to get out of the way (हटना (hatnaa)), while a mother saying अपना बैग हटाओ (apnaa baig hataao, 'move your bag') is asking someone to actively remove it (हटाना (hataanaa)) -- the causative pairing is very much alive in ordinary speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse हटाना (hataanaa) with निकालना (nikaalnaa): हटाना (hataanaa) is about shifting something out of the way (a chair, an obstacle, a hand), while निकालना (nikaalnaa) implies taking something out from inside something else or expelling it. 2) Because हटाना (hataanaa) is the transitive causative of हटना (hatnaa, intransitive, 'to move aside'), learners sometimes use हटना (hatnaa) with an object by mistake -- remember only हटाना (hataanaa) takes an object marked with ने (ne) in the past tense.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया अपना बैग यहाँ से हटाओ। Please move your bag away from here.
(kripyaa apnaa baig yahaan se hataao।)

Sense 2 : to remove someone from a position or role
verb, transitive Used with a person as object plus a source phrase like पद से (pad se, 'from the post') or नौकरी से (naukrii se, 'from the job') to specify what they are being removed from.
Synonyms: बर्खास्त करना (barkhaast karnaa, formal, official dismissal), निकाल देना (nikaal denaa, colloquial, 'to kick out')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in news headlines about politics: मंत्री को पद से हटा दिया गया (mantrii ko pad se hataa diyaa gayaa, 'the minister was removed from his post') -- the passive-feeling हटा दिया जाना (hataa diyaa jaanaa) construction is your first hint of the passive voice you're still getting comfortable with.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for निकालना (nikaalnaa) here since both can mean 'fire/remove,' but हटाना (hataanaa) leans more formal/administrative (removing from a post or office) while निकालना (nikaalnaa) is the everyday word for firing someone from a job.
