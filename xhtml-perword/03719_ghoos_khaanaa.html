घूस खाना (ghoos khaanaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to take a bribe' -- literally 'to eat a bribe,' using the eating metaphor Hindi favors for illicit gain.
Sense 1 : to take a bribe
verb, transitive
Synonyms: रिश्वत लेना (rishvat lenaa), घूस लेना (ghoos lenaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The image of 'eating' a bribe is deeply embedded in Hindi -- a corrupt official is often described as एक नंबर का घूसखोर (ek nambar kaa ghooskhor, 'a first-class bribe-taker'), where घूसखोर (ghooskhor) literally means 'bribe-eater.' This eating metaphor for illicit gain shows up elsewhere too, like कमीशन खाना (kamiishan khaanaa, 'to skim a commission'), reflecting a broader Hindi pattern where खाना (khaanaa) extends beyond food to anything consumed for private benefit at others' expense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say घूस लेना (ghoos lenaa, 'to take a bribe') instead, which is also correct and arguably more common in formal/written contexts -- घूस खाना (ghoos khaanaa) is the more colloquial, spoken-register version, evoking the corrupt official 'feeding' off bribes. 2) The object घूस (ghoos) is not marked with ने (ne) despite खाना (khaanaa) being transitive with a direct object -- this follows normal ने-marking rules, but learners sometimes hesitate here because the idiom feels fixed.
Sample Sentences:
वह हर काम के लिए घूस खाता है। He takes a bribe for every single task.
(vah har kaam ke lie ghoos khaataa hai।)
