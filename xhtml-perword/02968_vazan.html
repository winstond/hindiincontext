वज़न (vazan)

noun A loanword from Persian/Arabic meaning 'weight' -- covers both literal physical weight and figurative senses like importance or metrical weight in poetry.
Sense 1 : physical weight, heaviness
noun, masculine
Synonyms: भार (bhaar, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi aunty about her health and she'll likely mention her वज़न (vazan) -- checking and discussing weight, whether at the sabzi mandi's weighing scale or after a doctor's visit, is a constant thread in everyday conversation here.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use भार (bhaar) reflexively as the go-to word for 'weight', but वज़न (vazan) is the far more common everyday choice, especially in the market or at the doctor's -- मेरा वज़न बढ़ गया है (meraa vazan badh gayaa hai, 'my weight has gone up') sounds natural, while भार (bhaar) here sounds bookish. 2) With verbs like बढ़ना (badhnaa) or घटना (ghatnaa), वज़न (vazan) itself is the subject, not marked with ने (ne), since these are intransitive.
Sample Sentences:
मेरा वज़न साठ किलो है। My weight is sixty kilos.
(meraa vazan saath kilo hai।)

Sense 2 : importance, influence, weight (figurative)
noun, masculine Commonly used in the phrase वज़न रखना (vazan rakhnaa, 'to carry weight/importance').
Synonyms: अहमियत (ahmiyat), रसूख़ (rasookh, influence/clout)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense shows up constantly in discussions of social standing -- उसके परिवार का इलाके में बहुत वज़न है (uske parivaar kaa ilaake men bahut vazan hai, 'his family carries a lot of weight in the neighborhood') captures how influence and reputation are talked about in very physical, weighty terms in Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) उसकी बात में वज़न है (uskii baat men vazan hai, 'his words carry weight') is a very natural construction -- learners sometimes reach for भार (bhaar) or महत्व (mahatv) instead, which sound stiffer in casual speech.
Sample Sentences:
उसके परिवार का इलाके में बहुत वज़न है। His family carries a lot of weight in the neighborhood.
(uske parivaar kaa ilaake men bahut vazan hai।)

Sense 3 : poetic meter, metrical weight
noun, masculine Used in discussions of shayari and ghazal composition, referring to whether lines match a required syllabic/metrical pattern.
Synonyms: बहर (bahar, specific to Urdu poetic meter)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You'll hear this sense at any mehfil or poetry gathering -- when someone says इस शेर में वज़न नहीं है (is sher men vazan nahiin hai, 'this couplet doesn't have the right meter'), they're critiquing the technical craft of the verse, a nod to how seriously Urdu-Hindi poetic tradition treats metrical precision.
