उनहत्तर (unahattar)

numeral The numeral for sixty-nine (69) in counting and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number sixty-nine (69)
numeral
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 1 to 100 are famously irregular, and उनहत्तर (unahattar) is a good example -- most native speakers today will confirm a number like this by writing or texting the digits '69' rather than risk a slip, so don't feel bad if you do the same at first. It follows the common pattern of 'one less than seventy' numerals (like उनसठ, unsath, 'fifty-nine') sharing the ऊन- prefix, which can help with recognition even though it doesn't help with production.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like other Hindi numerals in this range, उनहत्तर (unahattar) must simply be memorized as its own word -- it is not built predictably from ऊन (oon, 'minus one') plus सत्तर (sattar, 'seventy') the way an English speaker might expect from a compositional number system. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with the visually/phonetically similar उन्नीस (unniis, 'nineteen') or सत्तर (sattar, 'seventy') -- double-check which decade is meant.
