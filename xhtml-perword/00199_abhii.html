अभी (abhii)

adverb A core time-adverb meaning 'now, right now' or 'just now/a moment ago,' depending on context; also combines with other words to mean 'yet, still' in negative sentences.
Sense 1 : right now, at this very moment
adverb
Synonyms: इस वक़्त (is vaqt), फ़िलहाल (filahaal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear shouted from a kitchen or a shop counter: अभी आया (abhii aayaa, 'coming right now') is the classic (if often not-so-immediate) response when someone's called for. Its flexibility -- covering 'now,' 'just now,' and 'in a moment' -- means Hindi speakers rely on tone and context rather than separate words the way English does.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अभी (abhii) when they mean 'nowadays' -- for that ongoing, habitual sense आजकल (aajkal) is the natural choice, not अभी (abhii). 2) अभी (abhii) alone with a future-tense verb reads as 'right now, immediately,' e.g. अभी आता हूँ (abhii aataa hoon, 'I'm coming right now') -- learners sometimes add extra words to convey urgency that अभी (abhii) already carries.
Sample Sentences:
अभी आया! Coming right now!
(abhii aayaa!)

Sense 2 : just now, a moment ago
adverb Used with the past tense to mark an action completed only moments before speaking, e.g. वह अभी गया (vah abhii gayaa, 'he just left').
Synonyms: थोड़ी देर पहले (thodii der pahle)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: The same word that means 'right now' also means 'just now' -- the tense of the verb is what tells you which side of the present moment अभी (abhii) is pointing to.

Sense 3 : yet, still (in negative constructions)
adverb Pairs with नहीं (nahiin) as अभी नहीं (abhii nahiin, 'not yet') to mean an expected action has not happened up to now.
Synonyms: अब तक (ab tak)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: अभी नहीं (abhii nahiin) is a soft, very common way to defer something -- a parent telling a child अभी नहीं, बाद में (abhii nahiin, baad men, 'not now, later') is a familiar household sound.
Common Mistakes: 1) अभी नहीं (abhii nahiin) means 'not yet' or 'not right now' depending on context -- learners sometimes confuse this with a flat refusal, when it usually just means 'later, not now.'
Sample Sentences:

(abhii nahiin, baad men।)
