भारी तादाद में (bhaarii taadaad men)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'in large numbers' or 'in huge quantity' -- used to describe crowds, quantities of goods, or masses of people/things.
Sense 1 : in large numbers, in huge quantity
adverb Modifies a whole clause, typically describing how many people showed up or how much of something was present/collected -- pairs naturally with verbs like आना (aanaa), पहुँचना (pahunchnaa), मिलना (milnaa).
Synonyms: बड़ी संख्या में (badii sankhyaa men), बहुत बड़ी तादाद में (bahut badii taadaad men, emphatic variant), प्रचुर मात्रा में (prachur maatraa men, formal, used more for goods/quantity than people)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Indian news reports -- 'लोग भारी तादाद में पहुँचे' (log bhaarii taadaad men pahunche, 'people arrived in large numbers') is a standard line for describing turnout at rallies, fairs, or protests. तादाद (taadaad) itself is an Urdu-origin word for 'number/count,' very much at home in everyday Hindi journalism and speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try भारी संख्या में (bhaarii sankhyaa men) instead -- both भारी (bhaarii) and बड़ी (badii) pair with संख्या (sankhyaa, number), but तादाद (taadaad) almost always pairs with भारी (bhaarii) or बड़ी (badii), not with other size adjectives. 2) This phrase describes quantity/crowd size, not physical weight -- learners who know भारी (bhaarii) as 'heavy' sometimes misread the phrase literally.
Sample Sentences:
चुनावी रैली में लोग भारी तादाद में पहुँचे। People arrived in large numbers at the election rally.
(chunaavii railii men log bhaarii taadaad men pahunche।)
