घटिया (ghatiyaa)

adjective One core sense: an invariant adjective meaning 'low-quality, shoddy, inferior' -- used for both objects and, by extension, people's behavior or character.
Sense 1 : of poor quality, cheaply made
adjective
Synonyms: सस्ता (sastaa, can mean 'cheap' with the same negative shade), बेकार (bekaar, useless), निम्न स्तर का (nimn star kaa, formal, 'low-grade')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in markets and homes across Delhi-NCR -- a shopkeeper's rival product is घटिया माल (ghatiyaa maal, 'shoddy goods'), and a mother might call a friend's advice घटिया (ghatiyaa) with real disdain. It is a blunt, opinionated word; using it about someone's work or taste can sting, so save it for genuine complaints rather than mild criticism.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सस्ता (sastaa) to mean 'cheap' in the neutral price sense, but घटिया (ghatiyaa) always carries a negative judgment about quality, not just a low price -- a सस्ता (sastaa) phone might be a great deal, but a घटिया (ghatiyaa) phone is simply bad. 2) As an invariant adjective, it does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- learners sometimes wrongly try to inflect it like कड़ा (kadaa).

Sense 2 : morally low, mean-spirited (of a person or their behavior)
adjective
Synonyms: नीच (niich, stronger, more insulting), कमीना (kamiinaa, vulgar/slang, much harsher), बुरा (buraa, milder, general 'bad')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the extended, figurative use: a घटिया आदमी (ghatiyaa aadmii, 'a low-down person') or घटिया हरकत (ghatiyaa harkat, 'a shabby/mean act') describes someone's character or conduct, not an object's build quality. It is the same underlying idea of 'low-grade' simply projected onto ethics rather than manufacturing.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is much more cutting than the 'poor quality' sense -- calling a person घटिया (ghatiyaa) (rather than their work or an object) is a real insult about character, not a mild critique, so learners should use it carefully in conversation.

(vo itnaa ghatiyaa aadmii hai ki doston ko bhii dhokhaa de detaa hai।)
