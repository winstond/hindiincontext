लादना (laadnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to load or pile goods/burden onto something (a vehicle, animal, or person), with a common figurative extension to imposing a burden, task, or blame on someone.
Sense 1 : to load (physical goods onto a vehicle/animal)
verb, transitive Takes पर (par) for the surface/vehicle receiving the load: सामान गाड़ी पर लादना (saamaan gaadii par laadnaa, 'to load luggage onto the vehicle').
Synonyms: भरना (bharnaa, to fill/load), चढ़ाना (chadhaanaa, to load up onto)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the classic image of a truck or a bullock cart piled high with sacks or furniture -- लादना (laadnaa) is the verb for exactly that stacking-on action, and it carries a sense of the load being heavy or excessive, teetering rather than neatly packed.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for भरना (bharnaa) here, but भरना (bharnaa) is about filling a container to capacity, while लादना (laadnaa) specifically means piling a load onto a carrying surface (a cart, animal, roof, truck) -- the sense is of weight being stacked on top, not space being filled.
Sample Sentences:
मजदूर ट्रक पर बोरियाँ लाद रहे हैं। The laborers are loading sacks onto the truck.
(majdoor trak par boriyaan laad rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : to burden someone with (work, responsibility, blame)
verb, transitive Takes पर (par) for the person receiving the burden: काम मुझ पर लाद दिया (kaam mujh par laad diyaa, 'dumped the work on me'). Very frequently occurs as the compound verb लाद देना (laad denaa) to stress the suddenness or unfairness of the imposition.
Synonyms: थोपना (thopnaa, to impose/force upon), डालना (daalnaa, to put/dump onto)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in office or family complaints: सारा काम मुझ पर लाद दिया (saaraa kaam mujh par laad diyaa, 'they dumped all the work on me') -- the physical image of loading goods onto a cart carries straight over into loading responsibility onto a person, which is a nice window into how Hindi often extends concrete verbs to emotional or social weight.
Common Mistakes: 1) थोपना (thopnaa) and लादना (laadnaa) overlap here, but लादना (laadnaa) keeps a vivid physical-weight flavor -- it is used when the speaker wants to emphasize how heavy or unwelcome the burden feels, almost like a sack being thrown onto someone's back, whereas थोपना (thopnaa) is more neutral about imposing an idea, rule, or decision.
