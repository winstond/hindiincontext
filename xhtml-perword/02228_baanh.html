बाँह (baanh)

noun A body-part noun meaning 'arm', used especially in warm, embracing, or protective contexts; distinct from the more clinical/general हाथ (haath, 'hand/arm').
Sense 1 : arm (the limb, especially upper arm/embrace context)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भुजा (bhujaa, literary), हाथ (haath, broader term covering hand and arm)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बाँह (baanh) carries a tender, embodied warmth that हाथ (haath) doesn't quite have -- think of a mother pulling a child into her बाँहें (baanhen, 'arms') or song lyrics about being held in someone's बाँहों में (baanhon men, 'in the arms of'). It shows up constantly in Bollywood song lyrics and poetry precisely for this affectionate, protective register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हाथ (haath) in every context involving the arm, but बाँह (baanh) is preferred in phrases about embracing, holding, or the sleeve area of the body, e.g. बाँहों में भरना (baanhon men bharnaa, 'to take in one's arms'). 2) Note the nasalized vowel in spelling and pronunciation -- it is बाँह (baanh), not बांह or बाह; both बाँह and बांह are seen in print, but the chandrabindu/anusvara version is standard.
