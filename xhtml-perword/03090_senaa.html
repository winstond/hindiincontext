सेना (senaa)

noun/verb Primarily a noun meaning 'army,' but also a rarer verb meaning 'to sew' -- the two are unrelated in origin and meaning.
Sense 1 : army, military force
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फौज (phauj), सैन्य (sainy, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see सेना (senaa) constantly in news headlines -- भारतीय सेना (bhaartiiy senaa, 'Indian Army') is the standard term, and it also combines into compounds like वायु सेना (vaayu senaa, 'Air Force') and जल सेना (jal senaa, 'Navy'), literally 'air army' and 'water army.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सेना (senaa, 'army') with सैनिक (sainik, 'soldier') -- सेना is the collective force, सैनिक is an individual member of it.
Sample Sentences:
भारतीय सेना देश की सीमाओं की रक्षा करती है। The Indian Army protects the country's borders.
(bhaartiiy senaa desh kii siimaaon kii rakshaa kartii hai।)

Sense 2 : to sew, to stitch
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सिलना (silnaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This verb sense is far less common than सिलना (silnaa) for 'to sew' in everyday Delhi-NCR speech; you are more likely to hear सेना (senaa) used in this sense in older or more literary/regional contexts.
Sample Sentences:
बुढ़िया सुई-धागे से कपड़ा सेती थी। The old woman used to sew cloth with a needle and thread.
