अड़तीस (adtiis)

numeral The cardinal number thirty-eight (adtiis).
Sense 1 : the number 38
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are famously irregular, each one really a fused, historically contracted word rather than a predictable combination of tens and units -- so native speakers themselves often pause to double check unfamiliar ones like अड़तीस (adtiis) when reading phone numbers or prices aloud. It is worth simply drilling these numerals as vocabulary items, the way you would drill irregular verbs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mix up अड़तीस (adtiis, '38') with अट्ठाईस (atthaaiis, '28') or अड़सठ (adsath, '68') since the Hindi numerals 21-99 do not follow a predictable tens-plus-units pattern like English and must be learned as individual words. 2) As with all Hindi numerals above one, it precedes the noun it counts and the noun stays in its base (or oblique, if the sentence calls for it) form: अड़तीस किताबें (adtiis kitaaben, 'thirty-eight books'), not a plural marker on the numeral itself.
