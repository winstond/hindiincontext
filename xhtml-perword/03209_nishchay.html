निश्चय (nishchay)

noun/adverb Primarily a noun meaning 'decision' or 'resolve'; also works as an adverb meaning 'certainly, definitely.' Related to निश्चित (nishchit, 'certain, fixed'), which shares the same root.
Sense 1 : decision, resolve, determination
noun, masculine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) in the phrase निश्चय करना (nishchay karnaa, 'to make a decision/resolve').
Synonyms: फ़ैसला (faislaa), निर्णय (nirnay), इरादा (iraadaa, intention/will), संकल्प (sankalp, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in slightly earnest, self-improvement contexts, like a New Year's resolution: मैंने निश्चय किया है (mainne nishchay kiyaa hai, 'I have resolved...'). It has a touch of moral seriousness that फ़ैसला (faislaa) lacks, which tends to be used for everyday practical choices.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for फ़ैसला (faislaa) by default; निश्चय (nishchay) carries a slightly more formal, deliberate tone, as in resolving to do something after inner reflection, rather than settling an external matter or dispute. 2) It pairs almost always with करना (karnaa), not लेना (lenaa), unlike some near-synonyms.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने निश्चय किया है कि अब रोज़ सुबह योग करूँगा। I have resolved that from now on I will do yoga every morning.
(mainne nishchay kiyaa hai ki ab roz subah yog karoongaa।)

Sense 2 : certainly, definitely, surely
adverb Functions as a sentence-level intensifier of certainty, often placed near the verb it modifies: वह निश्चय आएगा (vah nishchay aaegaa, 'he will certainly come').
Synonyms: ज़रूर (zaroor), अवश्य (avashy, formal/literary), निश्चित रूप से (nishchit roop se)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense leans toward formal or written registers -- a teacher or elder might use it in a serious pronouncement, but friends chatting over chai would almost always say ज़रूर (zaroor) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) This adverbial use is less common in casual speech than ज़रूर (zaroor); learners who overuse निश्चय (nishchay) here can sound slightly bookish or old-fashioned.
Sample Sentences:
