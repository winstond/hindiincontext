ईसा (iisaa)

noun This word refers to Jesus Christ. It also appears in the fixed phrase ईसा पूर्व (iisaa poorv) meaning 'BC' -- a very common everyday use even for learners who never discuss religion.
Sense 1 : Jesus Christ
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ईसा मसीह (iisaa masiih, full form), यीशु (yiishu)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday Hindi, ईसा (iisaa) is the standard name for Jesus, often expanded to ईसा मसीह (iisaa masiih) -- मसीह (masiih) meaning 'Messiah,' borrowed via Persian/Arabic. You will hear this in news reports about Christmas (krismas) and in interfaith contexts, since Delhi-NCR has a visible Christian community and Christmas is a public holiday in India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse ईसा (iisaa) with the calendar term ईसवी (iisvii, 'of the Christian era'), which is related but used only in dates, not to refer to the person himself.
Sample Sentences:
ईसा मसीह का जन्म बेथलेहम में हुआ था। Jesus Christ was born in Bethlehem.
(iisaa masiih kaa janm bethleham men huaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : used in the calendar term for 'BC' (before Christ)
noun, masculine Appears only inside the fixed expression ईसा पूर्व (iisaa poorv, 'BC') and its counterpart ईसा के बाद / ईसवी सन (iisvii san, 'AD'); not used productively outside this dating frame.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this constantly in history textbooks and museum plaques in Delhi, since Indian history is taught using ईसा पूर्व (iisaa poorv) and ईसवी सन (iisvii san) rather than English 'BC/AD' abbreviations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use ईसा (iisaa) alone to mean 'BC' -- it must be paired with पूर्व (poorv, 'before') to form ईसा पूर्व (iisaa poorv, 'BC'); dropping पूर्व (poorv) changes the meaning back to just 'Jesus.'
Sample Sentences:
मौर्य साम्राज्य की स्थापना 322 ईसा पूर्व में हुई थी। The Mauryan Empire was established in 322 BC.
(maury saamraajy kii sthaapnaa 322 iisaa poorv men huii thii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive

(yah mandir paanchviin shataabdii iisaa poorv kaa bataayaa jaataa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
