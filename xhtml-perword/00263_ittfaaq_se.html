इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'by chance, coincidentally.' Built from the noun इत्तफ़ाक़ (ittfaaq, 'coincidence,' from Arabic-Persian) plus the postposition से (se, 'by/from').
Sense 1 : by coincidence, as it happened
adverb Functions as a sentence-initial or clause-initial adverbial, framing the rest of the sentence as an unplanned coincidence.
Synonyms: अचानक (achaanak, focuses on suddenness rather than coincidence), संयोग से (sanyog se)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a slightly storytelling flavor, the kind of thing you say when recounting how you happened to run into an old friend at the market: इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se), वो वहीं मिल गया (vo vahiin mil gayaa, 'he happened to be there too'). It sits comfortably in both casual chat and slightly more polished storytelling, so you will hear it from a rickshaw driver and a news anchor alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with अचानक (achaanak), which means 'suddenly' -- इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se) is about an unplanned coincidence, not speed or abruptness. 2) It is often placed at the very start of the sentence, unlike many adverbs that sit closer to the verb -- putting it mid-sentence can sound awkward.
Sample Sentences:
इत्तफ़ाक़ से, आज मेरी उससे बरसों बाद मुलाक़ात हो गई। Coincidentally, I ran into him today after years.
(ittfaaq se, aaj merii usse barson baad mulaaqaat ho gaii।)
