कामना (kaamnaa)

noun/verb A formal/literary word for a wish or desire, and the verb 'to wish' built from it. Related to but more elevated than everyday चाहना (chaahnaa).
Sense 1 : wish, desire, aspiration
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इच्छा (ichchhaa), चाह (chaah), आकांक्षा (aakaankshaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word hiding inside शुभकामनाएं (shubhkaamnaaen), the standard 'best wishes' you write on greeting cards, birthday messages, and festival texts -- so even though कामना (kaamnaa) itself sounds a touch formal in conversation, every Hindi speaker uses it constantly in that compound. Think of it as the word for a heartfelt or hoped-for wish, one notch more solemn than the everyday चाह (chaah).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कामना (kaamnaa) in casual speech where चाह (chaah) or इच्छा (ichchhaa) would sound more natural; कामना (kaamnaa) tends to appear in greeting cards, speeches, and set phrases like शुभकामनाएं (shubhkaamnaaen, 'best wishes') rather than in everyday chat about wanting something. 2) The verb form कामना करना (kaamnaa karnaa) means 'to wish for' and takes की (kii) before the wished-for thing (उसकी सफलता की कामना करना, uskii saphaltaa kii kaamnaa karnaa, 'to wish for his success'), not a direct object.
Sample Sentences:
मेरी कामना है कि तुम हमेशा खुश रहो। My wish is that you always stay happy.
(merii kaamnaa hai ki tum hameshaa khush raho।)

Sense 2 : to wish, to desire (for someone/something)
verb, transitive Used as कामना करना (kaamnaa karnaa); the thing wished for is marked with की (kii), e.g. उसके स्वास्थ्य की कामना करना (uske svaasthy kii kaamnaa karnaa, 'to wish for his health').
Synonyms: चाहना (chaahnaa), इच्छा करना (ichchhaa karnaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this in slightly formal or heartfelt contexts -- a priest's blessing, a formal toast, a letter -- where someone says वे कामना करते हैं कि... (ve kaamnaa karte hain ki..., 'they wish that...'), giving the sentence a warmer, more solemn weight than a plain चाहते हैं (chaahte hain) would.
Common Mistakes: 1) This verb almost always appears wrapped as कामना करना (kaamnaa karnaa) -- learners sometimes try to conjugate कामना (kaamnaa) alone as if it were a simple verb like चाहना (chaahnaa), but native speakers keep करना (karnaa) as the operating verb and inflect that instead.
