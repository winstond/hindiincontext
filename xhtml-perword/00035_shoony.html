शून्य (shoony)

numeral/noun/adjective शून्य (shoony) covers three linked ideas: the numeral zero, the abstract noun for void/emptiness, and an adjective meaning empty or blank -- all built on the same core image of 'nothingness'.
Sense 1 : the number zero
numeral
Synonyms: जीरो (jiiro, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Indian mathematicians (Aryabhata among them) are widely credited with developing the concept of zero as a number, so शून्य (shoony) carries a bit of national pride beyond its plain math use -- it's often mentioned in the same breath as India's gift to world mathematics.
Common Mistakes: 1) In casual speech, especially with phone numbers or scores, most Delhi speakers just say जीरो (jiiro); शून्य (shoony) is the word you'll hear in school, in written numerals, and in formal contexts like weather reports (तापमान शून्य डिग्री है (taapmaan shoony digrii hai, the temperature is zero degrees)).

Sense 2 : void, emptiness, nothingness
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खालीपन (khaaliipan, emptiness felt inside), रिक्तता (rikttaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense reaches beyond math into feeling -- लोग कहते हैं मन में शून्य छा गया (man men shoony chhaa gayaa, a void spread through the heart/mind) when grief or loss leaves someone hollow. It also echoes the Buddhist idea of शून्यता (shoonytaa), emptiness as a spiritual state rather than mere absence.

उसकी मृत्यु के बाद मन में एक शून्य छा गया। After her death, a void spread through my heart.
(uskii mrityu ke baad man men ek shoony chhaa gayaa।)

पिता के जाने से घर में जो शून्य आया है, वह कभी नहीं भरेगा। The void that has come into the house since father's passing will never be filled.
(pitaa ke jaane se ghar men jo shoony aayaa hai, vah kabhii nahiin bharegaa।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
बौद्ध दर्शन में शून्य को केवल अभाव नहीं, एक गहरी अवस्था माना जाता है। In Buddhist philosophy, the void is considered not mere absence but a profound state.

Sense 3 : empty, blank, null
adjective
Synonyms: खाली (khaalii), रिक्त (rikt, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This adjective sense is the bridge between the numeral and the abstract noun -- 'zero' and 'blank/void' are really the same underlying idea of शून्य (shoony) applied to a quantity versus a state of mind.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for शून्य (shoony) where खाली (khaalii) is what's actually needed -- शून्य describes an abstract or emotional blankness (उसकी आँखें शून्य थीं (uskii aankhen shoony thiin, his eyes were blank/vacant)), while an everyday empty glass or room is गिलास खाली है (gilaas khaalii hai, the glass is empty), not शून्य.
Sample Sentences:
उसका मन आज शून्य लग रहा है। Her mind feels blank today.
(uskaa man aaj shoony lag rahaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उसकी आँखें शून्य थीं, जैसे कुछ महसूस ही न हो रहा हो। Her eyes were blank, as if she felt nothing at all.
(uskii aankhen shoony thiin, jaise kuchh mahsoos hii n ho rahaa ho।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
