बढ़ना (badhnaa)

verb The core meaning is 'to increase, grow, advance' -- covering physical growth, numeric/quantity increase, and forward movement, all intransitive. A causative partner, बढ़ाना (badhaanaa, 'to increase something'), exists as its own lemma.
Sense 1 : to increase, grow (in quantity, degree, or size)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बढ़ोतरी होना (badhotrii honaa, phrase), उभरना (ubharnaa, rare in this sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in everyday talk about prices, weight, population, and traffic -- मेहंगाई बढ़ गई है (mehangaaii badh gaii hai, 'inflation has gone up') is a near-daily complaint in any Delhi household or market conversation. Paired with जाना (jaanaa) as in बढ़ जाना (badh jaanaa), it signals a completed, often noticeable jump in degree.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) when they mean बढ़ना (badhnaa) -- use बढ़ना (badhnaa) when the subject itself is growing ('कीमत बढ़ रही है' (kiimat badh rahii hai, 'the price is increasing')), and बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) only when someone/something else causes the increase. 2) Being intransitive, it never takes ने (ne) in the perfective, even though the meaning feels active -- 'कीमत बढ़ी' (kiimat badhii, 'the price increased'), not 'कीमत ने बढ़ी'.
Sample Sentences:
इस महीने पेट्रोल के दाम फिर बढ़ गए हैं। Petrol prices have gone up again this month.
(is mahiine petrol ke daam phir badh gae hain।)

Sense 2 : to move forward, advance (physically)
verb, intransitive Very frequently used with आगे (aage) to emphasize forward direction, as in आगे बढ़ो (aage badho, 'move ahead').
Synonyms: आगे बढ़ना (aage badhnaa, common fuller phrase), अग्रसर होना (agrsar honaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear आगे बढ़ो (aage badho) shouted by conductors on crowded Delhi buses and metros, telling passengers to move further in -- a very physical, everyday use of this sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with चलना (chalnaa, 'to walk/move') -- बढ़ना (badhnaa) carries a sense of progress or advancing past a point, not just generic movement.
Sample Sentences:
बस में आगे बढ़ो, पीछे जगह नहीं है। Move forward in the bus, there's no room at the back.
(bas men aage badho, piichhe jagah nahiin hai।)

Sense 3 : to progress, advance (in life, career, development)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: तरक्की करना (tarakkii karnaa, common phrase), उन्नति करना (unnti karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Parents and teachers use this figurative sense a lot -- जीवन में बढ़ना (jiivan men badhnaa, 'to advance in life') echoes the same forward-motion image as the literal sense, showing how Hindi treats progress and physical advancing as the same underlying idea.
Sample Sentences:
