झगड़ा (jhagdaa)

noun A common noun for a quarrel or fight (verbal or physical); also used in the compound verb झगड़ा करना (jhagdaa karnaa, 'to quarrel'). One core meaning, no unrelated senses to split.
Sense 1 : quarrel, fight, dispute
noun, masculine Almost always used with करना (karnaa) as a light verb -- झगड़ा करना (jhagdaa karnaa, 'to quarrel') -- or with होना (honaa) -- झगड़ा होना (jhagdaa honaa, 'a quarrel to happen/break out') -- the two frames carry slightly different emphasis (who is doing it vs. it just occurring).
Synonyms: लड़ाई (ladaaii), तकरार (takraar), विवाद (vivaad, formal), बहस (bahas, more about verbal argument/debate)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly in Delhi households and markets -- padosiyon se jhagdaa (neighbor disputes over parking, water, noise) is a classic genre of desi small talk. The phrase घर का झगड़ा (ghar kaa jhagdaa, 'a family/domestic dispute') is a set phrase every household knows, often said with a knowing shrug, since such quarrels are seen as routine and eventually patched up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैंने झगड़ा (mainne jhagdaa) alone, forgetting that झगड़ा usually needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to make a full verb phrase -- the noun by itself doesn't mean 'to fight.' 2) झगड़ा होना (jhagdaa honaa) takes से (se) for the other party -- राम से झगड़ा हुआ (raam se jhagdaa huaa, 'had a quarrel with Ram') -- not के साथ (ke saath), which sounds oddly formal here. 3) Confusing झगड़ा (jhagdaa) with बहस (bahas): झगड़ा implies real hostility or a fight, while बहस is more a heated discussion or argument that can stay civil.
Sample Sentences:
कल रात पड़ोसियों के बीच पार्किंग को लेकर बहुत झगड़ा हुआ। Last night there was a big quarrel between the neighbors over parking.
(kal raat padosiyon ke biich paarking ko lekar bahut jhagdaa huaa।)
