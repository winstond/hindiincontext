हिंसात्मक (hinsaatmak)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'violent' or 'characterized by violence' -- used for actions, protests, behavior, or content, rather than for describing a person's temperament directly.
Sense 1 : violent, marked by violence
adjective
Synonyms: उग्र (ugr, leans toward 'aggressive/fierce' rather than physically violent), आक्रामक (aakraamak, 'aggressive', broader than physical violence)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in news broadcasts and newspaper headlines about protests, riots, or films -- हिंसात्मक फिल्म (hinsaatmak philm, 'violent film') or हिंसात्मक झड़प (hinsaatmak jhadap, 'violent clash') are everyday collocations. It shares its root with हिंसा (hinsaa, 'violence') and हिंसक (hinsak, 'violent/predatory'), so recognizing the हिंस- family helps you guess related words on sight.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use हिंसात्मक (hinsaatmak) to describe a person's temper or personality, but it more naturally describes actions, protests, content, or behavior (हिंसात्मक प्रदर्शन (hinsaatmak pradarshan, 'violent protest')) -- for a violent-tempered person, हिंसक (hinsak) is often preferred. 2) Do not confuse with हिंसक (hinsak), which can describe both violent behavior and predatory/carnivorous animals -- हिंसात्मक (hinsaatmak) is not used for animals.
Sample Sentences:
कल शहर में एक हिंसात्मक झड़प हुई। Yesterday there was a violent clash in the city.
(kal shahar men ek hinsaatmak jhadap huii।)
