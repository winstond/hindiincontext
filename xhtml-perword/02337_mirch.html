मिर्च (mirch)

noun Means 'chili pepper' or 'chili powder' as a food item, and also carries a common figurative sense of sharp/spicy sarcasm or hurt feelings, as in the idiom मिर्च लगना (mirch lagnaa).
Sense 1 : chili pepper (the vegetable/fruit, fresh or dried)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: लाल मिर्च (laal mirch, red chili), हरी मिर्च (harii mirch, green chili)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a Delhi kitchen you'll hear मिर्च (mirch) used loosely for both the fresh green pod tossed into a सब्ज़ी (sabzii, vegetable dish) and the red powder in the मसाला (masaalaa) box -- context, not the word itself, tells you which one. Green mirch tends to be sharper and used raw or tempered in oil, while red mirch powder gives color and background heat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often assume मिर्च (mirch) only means the powder used in cooking, but it equally refers to the whole fresh pod you'd buy at a सब्ज़ी वाला (sabzii vaalaa, vegetable seller)'s cart. 2) The compound मिर्च-मसाला (mirch-masaalaa) doesn't mean 'chili and spices' literally in speech; it's often used loosely for 'spicy/exaggerated details' in a story.

Sense 2 : figurative: something that stings, offends, or provokes a sharp reaction
noun, feminine Almost always seen in the fixed idiom मिर्च लगना (mirch lagnaa, 'to feel stung/offended') with a dative-marked experiencer, e.g. उसे मिर्च लगी (use mirch lagii, 'that stung him/her') -- structurally parallel to other को-subject feeling verbs like लगना on its own.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the classic line for jealousy or a bruised ego: कहीं मिर्च तो नहीं लगी? (kahiin mirch to nahiin lagii?, 'did that sting you, by any chance?') is a teasing jab Delhi friends throw at each other when one person seems annoyed by another's good news.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners translate मिर्च लगना (mirch lagnaa) too literally as 'chili touched me' instead of recognizing it as an idiom for feeling offended or hurt, often at a taunt or someone else's success. 2) This sense is essentially locked to the verb लगना (lagnaa) -- you won't productively combine मिर्च (mirch) with other verbs to get this meaning.
