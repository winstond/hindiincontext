सितारा (sitaaraa)

noun Primarily 'star' (the celestial body, and its figurative extensions to fate and to celebrities); it also carries a decorative sense of a sequin or star-shaped spangle used on clothing.
Sense 1 : star (celestial body)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तारा (taaraa), नक्षत्र (nakshatr, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi film songs love this word for its rhythm and Urdu-tinged flavor; you will hear सितारों (sitaaron, 'stars,' oblique plural) in countless lyrics about night skies and destiny.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to तारा (taaraa) for 'star' since it is introduced earlier; सितारा (sitaaraa) is equally common in everyday speech and slightly favored in poetic or filmi contexts, so both are worth knowing side by side.

Sense 2 : star performer, celebrity
noun, masculine
Synonyms: स्टार (staar, English loanword, common in film contexts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as English calls a top actor a 'star,' Hindi extends सितारा (sitaaraa) the same way -- फ़िल्मी सितारा (filmii sitaaraa, 'film star') is a routine phrase in entertainment news and gossip magazines.

Sense 3 : fortune, destiny (as governed by the stars)
noun, masculine Common in the idiom सितारे चमकना (sitaare chamaknaa, 'for one's stars to shine,' meaning to have good fortune) and its opposite सितारे गर्दिश में होना (sitaare gardish men honaa, 'for one's stars to be in decline').
Synonyms: भाग्य (bhaagy), किस्मत (kismat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This ties directly back to astrology's deep hold on everyday Indian life -- people genuinely say उसके सितारे चमक रहे हैं (uske sitaare chamak rahe hain, 'his stars are shining') the way an English speaker might say 'his luck has turned.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense usually appears in the plural सितारे (sitaare) even when talking about one person's fortune, unlike English 'star' (singular) in 'his star is rising.'
Sample Sentences:

(aajkal uske sitaare chamak rahe hain।)

Sense 4 : sequin, star-shaped spangle (on clothing)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चमकी (chamkii)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Walk into any market selling wedding or festival outfits and you will hear सितारों वाला (sitaaron vaalaa, 'the one with sequins') used casually to describe embellished fabric -- a nice everyday echo of the celestial sense.
