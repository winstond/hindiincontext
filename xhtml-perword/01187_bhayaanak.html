भयानक (bhayaanak)

adjective One core sense: an adjective meaning 'frightening/terrible,' used both literally (scary) and as a casual intensifier (terrible, awful, 'crazy bad').
Sense 1 : frightening, dreadful
adjective
Synonyms: डरावना (daraavnaa), खौफनाक (khauphnaak), भयावह (bhayaavah, slightly more literary), वीभत्स (viibhats, gruesome, stronger)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly on the news describing accidents, storms, and disasters -- भयानक हादसा (bhayaanak haadsaa, 'terrible accident') is a stock phrase in Hindi news reports. It shares the root भय (bhay, 'fear') with भयभीत (bhaybhiit, 'fearful/frightened') and निर्भय (nirbhay, 'fearless'), so learning this word gives you a foothold on that whole family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for डरावना (daraavnaa) for anything scary and miss that भयानक (bhayaanak) tends to describe something on a larger or more serious scale -- a भयानक हादसा (bhayaanak haadsaa, 'a terrible accident') or भयानक तूफ़ान (bhayaanak toofaan, 'a dreadful storm') -- rather than a spooky face or a horror-movie jump scare, which leans more toward डरावना (daraavnaa).

Sense 2 : terrible, awful (intensifier for scale/degree, not fear)
adjective Used the way English speakers use 'terrible' or 'insane' to intensify -- often about heat, traffic, noise, or a mess, with no actual fear involved.
Synonyms: बहुत बुरा (bahut buraa, phrase, not a single word), ज़बरदस्त (zabardast, can go positive or negative depending on context)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday, chatty use you will hear far more than the 'horror movie' sense -- friends complaining about दिल्ली की भयानक गर्मी (dillii kii bhayaanak garmii, 'Delhi's terrible heat') in June, or भयानक जाम (bhayaanak jaam, 'terrible jam') on the way to work. It functions much like 'crazy' or 'insane' does in casual English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Because this sense has drifted away from literal fear, learners sometimes think it always means something scary happened -- but भयानक गर्मी (bhayaanak garmii, 'terrible/scorching heat') or भयानक ट्रैफ़िक (bhayaanak traifik, 'awful traffic') just mean 'to an extreme, unpleasant degree,' with no danger implied.
