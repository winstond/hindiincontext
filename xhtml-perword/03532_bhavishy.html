भविष्य (bhavishy)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'the future' -- the time yet to come. Contrasts with भूत (bhoot, 'past') and वर्तमान (vartmaan, 'present').
Sense 1 : the future
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आगे का समय (aage kaa samay, descriptive phrase, not a single word)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear भविष्य (bhavishy) in serious or reflective contexts -- planning a career, talking about a child's भविष्य (bhavishy, 'future'), or in astrology, where भविष्य बताना (bhavishy bataanaa, 'to tell the future') is what a ज्योतिषी (jyotishii, 'astrologer') claims to do. It sits naturally in the trio भूत, वर्तमान, भविष्य (bhoot, vartmaan, bhavishy, 'past, present, future') that every Hindi-medium schoolchild learns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse भविष्य (bhavishy, 'the future' as a concept/time period) with the future tense of a verb -- there is no direct grammatical link; the future tense is built with verb endings like -गा (gaa), not this word. 2) भविष्य (bhavishy) is used with में (men) to mean 'in the future' -- भविष्य में (bhavishy men, 'in the future') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) instead.
