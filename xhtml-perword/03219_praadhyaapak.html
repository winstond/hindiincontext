प्राध्यापक (praadhyaapak)

noun A formal word for 'professor/lecturer' at a college or university level -- one clear sense, used mainly in official, academic, and written contexts.
Sense 1 : college/university professor or lecturer
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अध्यापक (adhyaapak, more general, any teacher), प्रोफ़ेसर (profesar, common in casual speech), शिक्षक (shikshak, general term for teacher)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up on college appointment letters, university websites, and in newspaper reports about academic staff -- it has that formal, official ring to it. In daily conversation, especially among students in Delhi-NCR, प्रोफ़ेसर (profesar) is far more common; प्राध्यापक (praadhyaapak) is the word you write, not usually the word you say.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this word for a school teacher; प्राध्यापक (praadhyaapak) is reserved for college/university-level teaching staff, not school teachers, who are called अध्यापक (adhyaapak) or शिक्षक (shikshak). 2) In everyday spoken Hindi, most people just say प्रोफ़ेसर (profesar) rather than प्राध्यापक (praadhyaapak), which sounds more like something you'd read on a college notice board or in a newspaper than hear casually among friends.
