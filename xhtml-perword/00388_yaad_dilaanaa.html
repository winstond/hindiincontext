याद दिलाना (yaad dilaanaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to remind' -- literally 'to cause memory' -- built from याद (yaad, memory/remembrance) plus the causative दिलाना (dilaanaa, to cause to give), the causative of देना (denaa, to give).
Sense 1 : to remind (someone of something)
verb, transitive Takes the frame X ko Y kii yaad dilaanaa (X ko Y kii yaad dilaanaa) -- the person reminded is marked with को (ko), and the thing they are reminded of is marked with की (kii) before याद (yaad).
Synonyms: स्मरण कराना (smaran karaanaa, literary/formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of Hindi's classic 'memory as an external visitor' expressions, much like याद आना (yaad aanaa, for a memory to come to mind) -- you don't just 'remember' something, it 'comes' to you, and someone else can 'cause it to be given' to you. A mother might say मुझे याद दिलाना कि दूध लाना है (mujhe yaad dilaanaa ki doodh laanaa hai, 'remind me that I need to bring milk') when heading out the door -- a very everyday household phrase in Delhi homes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the की (kii) linking याद (yaad) to the thing being remembered, saying just X को Y याद दिलाना (X ko Y yaad dilaanaa) instead of X को Y की याद दिलाना (X ko Y kii yaad dilaanaa). 2) The person doing the reminding is the subject and takes ने (ne) in the past tense since दिलाना (dilaanaa) is transitive, while the person reminded stays marked with को (ko) rather than ने (ne) -- learners sometimes mistakenly put ने on the person being reminded.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे याद दिलाना कि दूध लाना है। Remind me that I need to bring milk.
(mujhe yaad dilaanaa ki doodh laanaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
वह हर हफ्ते मुझे बिल भरने की याद दिलाता है। He reminds me every week to pay the bill.
(vah har haphte mujhe bil bharne kii yaad dilaataa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उसने मुझे मेरी माँ के जन्मदिन की याद दिलाई। She reminded me of my mother's birthday.
(usne mujhe merii maan ke janmdin kii yaad dilaaii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
कृपया मुझे कल की मीटिंग की याद दिला दें। Please remind me about tomorrow's meeting.
(kripyaa mujhe kal kii miiting kii yaad dilaa den।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
मैं तुम्हें कल फिर याद दिलाऊँगा। I will remind you again tomorrow.
(main tumhen kal phir yaad dilaaoongaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
काश तुमने मुझे समय पर याद दिलाया होता, वरना मैं भूल ही जाता। I wish you had reminded me in time, otherwise I would have forgotten.
(kaash tumne mujhe samay par yaad dilaayaa hotaa, varnaa main bhool hii jaataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
जब भी वह मुझे फोन करता है, कुछ न कुछ याद दिला ही देता है। Whenever he calls me, he always ends up reminding me of something or other.
(jab bhii vah mujhe phon kartaa hai, kuchh n kuchh yaad dilaa hii detaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
यह पत्र उसे उसके बचपन की याद दिलाता रहा। This letter kept reminding him of his childhood.
(yah patr use uske bachpan kii yaad dilaataa rahaa।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर वह मुझे बार-बार याद न दिलाए, तो मैं ज़रूर भूल जाऊँ। If he doesn't keep reminding me, I would surely forget.
(agar vah mujhe baar-baar yaad n dilaae, to main zaroor bhool jaaoon।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
मुझे उसकी हर बात याद दिलाई जाती है, फिर भी मैं ध्यान नहीं देता। I am reminded of everything he says, yet I don't pay attention.
(mujhe uskii har baat yaad dilaaii jaatii hai, phir bhii main dhyaan nahiin detaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
गुरुजी ने शिष्यों को उनके कर्तव्य की याद दिलाई। The teacher reminded the disciples of their duty.
(gurujii ne shishyon ko unke kartavy kii yaad dilaaii।)
