संस्था (sansthaa)

noun A formal noun meaning 'institution' or 'organization' -- used for anything from an NGO to a school to a corporate body.
Sense 1 : institution, organization, established body
noun, feminine
Synonyms: संगठन (sangthan), संस्थान (sansthaan, often used for research/educational institutes)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see on the nameplate of countless NGOs, charitable trusts, and social organizations across India -- ...सेवा संस्था (sevaa sansthaa, 'service organization') is an extremely common naming pattern. It carries a slightly formal, official register, the kind of word you would use on a form or a signboard rather than in casual chat about 'that place I work at.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse संस्था (sansthaa) with संस्थान (sansthaan) -- both translate as 'institution' in English, but संस्था (sansthaa) leans toward a social/voluntary organization (an NGO, a trust, a society), while संस्थान (sansthaan) leans toward a formal institute (research, educational, or governmental). 2) Learners sometimes treat this as a general 'company' word, but for a for-profit business, कंपनी (kampnii) or फर्म (pharm) is more natural.
