सो (so)

verb/conjunction Mainly the stem/imperative-adjacent form connected to सोना (sonaa, 'to sleep') seen in commands and set phrases; also a literary/archaic conjunction meaning 'so, therefore' still met in proverbs and old-style narration.
Sense 1 : sleep (imperative/stem use)
verb, intransitive This bare form appears as an intimate/familiar imperative ('sleep!') and as the verb stem before endings like -ता, -ए, -एगा in the conjugation of सोना (sonaa); it is not a separate lemma in meaning, just the stem surfacing on its own in casual commands.
Synonyms: सोना (sonaa), निद्रा लेना (nidraa lenaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A mother telling a child सो जा (so jaa, 'go to sleep') is one of the most common bedtime lines in Hindi households; the plain सो (so) by itself feels a touch abrupt, so it is almost always softened with जाओ (jaao) or an endearment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat सो (so) as a standalone polite request, but bare stem-imperatives like this are used only with people you are close to (children, siblings) -- सोइए (soie) or सो जाओ (so jaao) are the everyday polite/neutral options.

सो जा, बेटा, बहुत रात हो गई है। Go to sleep, son, it's very late already.
(so jaa, betaa, bahut raat ho gaii hai।)

Sense 2 : so, therefore, hence
conjunction Functions as a discourse connector at the start of a clause, linking a preceding reason or situation to its consequence, in the style of older narrative or proverbial Hindi.
Synonyms: इसलिए (islie), तो (to), अतः (atah, literary)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This सो (so) turns up in proverbs and older written Hindi -- for instance in lines following the pattern 'X हुआ, सो Y हुआ' (X huaa, so Y huaa, 'X happened, so Y happened') -- but in everyday spoken Delhi Hindi it has been almost entirely replaced by इसलिए (islie) or तो (to).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners meeting सो (so) in an old story or proverb sometimes read it as the verb stem for 'sleep' out of habit; here it is unrelated in meaning and works exactly like इसलिए (islie) or तो (to).

(vah bahut thakaa thaa, so vah jaldii so gayaa।)
