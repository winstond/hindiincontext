अंदाज़ा (andaazaa)

noun A single core sense: a rough estimate or guess. Common in everyday speech for guessing quantities, prices, distances, or someone's feelings/intentions.
Sense 1 : estimate, guess, rough idea
noun, masculine Very commonly used with होना (honaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa): अंदाज़ा होना (andaazaa honaa, 'to have an idea/inkling') takes a को-marked subject, while अंदाज़ा लगाना (andaazaa lagaanaa, 'to guess/estimate') is a regular transitive construction.
Synonyms: अनुमान (anumaan), क़यास (qayaas, less common), अटकल (atkal, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear मुझे अंदाज़ा नहीं था (mujhe andaazaa nahiin thaa, 'I had no idea') constantly in Delhi conversation, often with a rueful or surprised tone -- it is the go-to phrase for admitting you misjudged a situation, a person, or a price at the market. अंदाज़ा लगाओ (andaazaa lagaao, 'guess!') is also a common playful invitation before revealing something, much like English 'guess what.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use सोचना (sochnaa, 'to think') where अंदाज़ा लगाना (andaazaa lagaanaa) is more natural for guessing a quantity, distance, or outcome. 2) The dative-subject phrase मुझे अंदाज़ा नहीं था (mujhe andaazaa nahiin thaa, 'I had no idea') is a fixed way to express not realizing something -- learners sometimes wrongly try to make अंदाज़ा the subject with ने (ne) instead of using को (ko).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे अंदाज़ा नहीं था कि तुम इतनी जल्दी आ जाओगे। I had no idea you'd come so soon.
(mujhe andaazaa nahiin thaa ki tum itnii jaldii aa jaaoge।)
