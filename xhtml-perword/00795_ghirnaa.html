घिरना (ghirnaa)

verb This verb means 'to be surrounded' or 'to be encircled' -- used for clouds gathering, people being hemmed in by a crowd or trouble, or getting caught in a difficult situation. It is intransitive and pairs naturally with से (se) marking whatever is doing the surrounding.
Sense 1 : to be surrounded, encircled (by people, crowd, water, etc.)
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the surrounding agent: भीड़ से घिरना (bhiid se ghirnaa, to be surrounded by a crowd).
Synonyms: घेरे में आना (ghere men aanaa), लिपटना (lipatnaa, less common for this sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a scooter rider suddenly हॉर्न बजाती भीड़ से घिर जाना (horn bajaatii bhiid se ghir jaanaa, 'to get surrounded by a honking crowd') at a Delhi traffic jam -- this is the everyday feel of the word. It is the passive-flavored partner of घेरना (ghernaa) ('to surround'), and Hindi often prefers this kind of intransitive verb over a true passive construction.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for घेरना (ghernaa) here, but that is the transitive 'to surround' -- घिरना (ghirnaa) is what happens *to* the person or thing being surrounded, not what the crowd does. 2) The agent takes से (se), not को (ko) or ने (ne) -- there is no ने-marking since this verb is intransitive.

(merii skootii achaanak bhiid se ghir gaii।)

Sense 2 : to become overcast, to gather (clouds)
verb, intransitive Common subject is बादल (baadal, clouds) or आसमान (aasmaan, sky): आसमान में बादल घिर आए (aasmaan men baadal ghir aae, 'clouds gathered in the sky').
Synonyms: छाना (chhaanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around monsoon talk -- बादल घिरना (baadal ghirnaa) is the standard way to say clouds are gathering before rain, often paired with the compound verb घिर आना (ghir aanaa) to add a sense of clouds 'rolling in.'

Sense 3 : to be caught up in, entangled in (trouble, worry, debt, etc.) -- figurative
verb, intransitive Used with में (men) for the trouble one is caught in: मुसीबत में घिरना (musiibat men ghirnaa, 'to be caught up in trouble').
Synonyms: फँसना (phansnaa), उलझना (ulajhnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same mental image as sense 1 -- being physically hemmed in -- extended to abstract situations, much like English 'surrounded by problems.' You will often hear मुसीबतों से घिरा हुआ (musiibton se ghiraa huaa, 'beset by troubles') in everyday complaints or in news reports about people in crisis.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is easy to miss -- learners sometimes only learn the literal 'surrounded by people' meaning and are surprised to see घिरना (ghirnaa) used for abstract things like कर्ज़ (karz, debt) or समस्याएँ (samasyaaen, problems).
