शेष (shesh)

adjective/noun शेष (shesh) means 'remaining, left over' -- it works as an adjective describing what's left, and as a noun referring to 'the remainder' or 'the rest' of something.
Sense 1 : remaining, left over
adjective
Synonyms: बाकी (baakii), बचा हुआ (bachaa huaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see शेष (shesh) a lot on Indian Railways announcements and exam instructions -- 'शेष प्रश्नों के उत्तर दें (shesh prashnon ke uttar den, 'answer the remaining questions')' has that formal, official flavor. In daily bazaar or home chatter, बाकी (baakii) is what people actually say.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बाकी (baakii) by default since it's the everyday word; शेष (shesh) is not wrong but sits a notch higher in register, common in written notices, exam papers, and formal speech rather than casual chat. 2) As an adjective it typically precedes the noun it modifies, e.g. शेष काम (shesh kaam, 'remaining work'), and is treated as invariant -- it does not change form for gender or number.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया शेष प्रश्नों के उत्तर दें। Please answer the remaining questions.
(kripyaa shesh prashnon ke uttar den।)

Sense 2 : the remainder, the rest
noun, masculine Used as a noun especially in math ('the remainder of a division') and in formal writing to mean 'the rest' of a group or amount, e.g. शेष सब ठीक है (shesh sab thiik hai, 'the rest is all fine').
Synonyms: बाकी (baakii), शेष भाग (shesh bhaag, 'remaining part')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In school math class in India, शेष (shesh) is the standard term for the remainder in a division problem -- भागफल और शेष (bhaagphal aur shesh, 'quotient and remainder') is a phrase every Hindi-medium student has written dozens of times.

बारह को पाँच से भाग दो तो शेष दो बचता है। When you divide twelve by five, the remainder left over is two.

मैंने शेष निकाला और उसे बोर्ड पर लिख दिया। I calculated the remainder and wrote it on the board.
