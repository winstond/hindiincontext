सरकारी (sarkaarii)

adjective An adjective meaning 'governmental, government-run, official,' derived from सरकार (sarkaar, 'government'). Almost always used to describe institutions, jobs, or documents rather than people directly.
Sense 1 : government-run, official, state-owned
adjective
Synonyms: राजकीय (raajkiiy, formal), शासकीय (shaaskiiy, formal, rare in speech)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In India, सरकारी नौकरी (sarkaarii naukrii, 'government job') carries huge cultural weight -- it means job security, a pension, and social respectability, which is why families push hard for it over private-sector work. You will also hear सरकारी काम (sarkaarii kaam, 'government business/bureaucratic work') used with a sigh, evoking slow-moving red tape and long queues at a सरकारी दफ़्तर (sarkaarii daftar, 'government office').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect सरकारी (sarkaarii) like a variable adjective (expecting a masculine form ending in -आ). It never changes -- सरकारी स्कूल (sarkaarii skool, 'government school'), सरकारी नौकरी (sarkaarii naukrii, 'government job'), सरकारी दफ़्तर (sarkaarii daftar, 'government office') all keep the same ending regardless of the noun's gender or number. 2) सरकारी नौकरी (sarkaarii naukrii) is the single most common phrase this word appears in -- learners often reach for 'government job' via a literal translation instead of this fixed collocation.

मेरे पापा चाहते हैं कि मैं सरकारी नौकरी करूँ। My dad wants me to get a government job.
(mere paapaa chaahte hain ki main sarkaarii naukrii karoon।)

Sense 2 : official, formal (of documents, procedures)
adjective
Synonyms: औपचारिक (aupchaarik, formal/procedural sense), आधिकारिक (aadhikaarik)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Beyond literally 'belonging to the government,' सरकारी (sarkaarii) extends to anything that feels bureaucratic or by-the-book -- सरकारी भाषा (sarkaarii bhaashaa, 'officialese/bureaucratic language') is a common gentle jab at stiff, jargon-heavy writing, much like calling something 'red tape' in English.
Sample Sentences:
यह सरकारी भाषा किसी को समझ नहीं आती। Nobody understands this bureaucratic language.
(yah sarkaarii bhaashaa kisii ko samajh nahiin aatii।)
