कष्ट (kasht)

noun A formal, somewhat literary word for pain, suffering, or trouble/inconvenience, most often met in the set phrase कष्ट होना (kasht honaa) and the polite request frame कष्ट करना (kasht karnaa).
Sense 1 : pain, suffering, distress
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दुख (dukh), पीड़ा (piidaa, formal/literary), तकलीफ़ (takliif)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'd hear in a doctor's office, a formal letter, or a grandparent's speech, rather than casual chat -- for everyday aches and troubles, तकलीफ़ (takliif) is far more common. Its weight makes it common in devotional and moral contexts too, like discussions of life's सुख-दुख (sukh-dukh, 'joys and sorrows') alongside कष्ट.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for दर्द (dard) for physical pain, but कष्ट (kasht) leans toward mental/emotional suffering or hardship, though it can cover physical discomfort too in a more formal register. 2) It commonly appears with होना (honaa) in dative-subject constructions, e.g. मुझे कष्ट है (mujhe kasht hai, 'I am in distress'), not with karnaa for this sense.

(budhaape men unhen bahut kasht sahnaa padaa।)

Sense 2 : trouble, inconvenience, bother (caused to someone)
noun, masculine Frequently used in the polite fixed frame कष्ट करना (kasht karnaa) meaning 'to take the trouble (to do something)', often as a formal request or apology.
Synonyms: परेशानी (pareshaanii), तकलीफ़ (takliif), ज़हमत (zahmat, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense in formal invitations and announcements, like आपसे निवेदन है कि कष्ट करके आएं (aapse nivedan hai ki kasht karke aaen, 'you are requested to kindly take the trouble to come') -- the vocabulary of official Hindi, weddings cards, and polite institutional language rather than street conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Beginners may not recognize कष्ट करना (kasht karnaa, 'please take the trouble to...') as a set polite request pattern, similar to English 'would you mind...'; it sounds stiffly formal if overused in casual speech. 2) कष्ट के लिए क्षमा करें (kasht ke lie kshamaa karen, 'forgive me for the trouble') is a common formal apology learners should recognize as a fixed phrase rather than build word-by-word.
Sample Sentences:
आपसे निवेदन है कि कष्ट करके समय पर आएं। You are requested to kindly take the trouble to arrive on time.
(aapse nivedan hai ki kasht karke samay par aaen।)
