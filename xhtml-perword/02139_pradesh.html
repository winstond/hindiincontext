प्रदेश (pradesh)

noun A political/geographic term meaning 'state' or 'province' within a country, and by extension any bounded region or territory. Learners meet it constantly in state names like उत्तर प्रदेश (uttar pradesh, 'Uttar Pradesh') and मध्य प्रदेश (madhy pradesh, 'Madhya Pradesh').
Sense 1 : state/province (administrative unit)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: राज्य (raajy)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word baked into half of India's state names -- उत्तर प्रदेश (uttar pradesh, 'northern province'), मध्य प्रदेश (madhy pradesh, 'middle province'), हिमाचल प्रदेश (himaachal pradesh, 'snow-mountain province'). Once you notice the pattern, you start seeing प्रदेश (pradesh) everywhere on Indian maps and license plates.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use देश (desh, 'country') and प्रदेश (pradesh, 'state/province') interchangeably because they look and sound similar -- but देश (desh) is the whole nation, while प्रदेश (pradesh) is a sub-unit within it. 2) In casual speech राज्य (raajy) and प्रदेश (pradesh) are often used interchangeably for 'state', but प्रदेश (pradesh) is the word that actually appears in most Indian state names.

Sense 2 : region, territory, area
noun, masculine Used for a geographic or conceptual area without necessarily implying formal administrative status, e.g. पहाड़ी प्रदेश (pahaadii pradesh, 'hilly region').
Synonyms: क्षेत्र (kshetr), इलाका (ilaakaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this sense in news reports and geography lessons -- मरुस्थलीय प्रदेश (marusthliiy pradesh, 'desert region') or पर्वतीय प्रदेश (parvtiiy pradesh, 'mountainous region') -- describing a landscape type rather than a political boundary.
