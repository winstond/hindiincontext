बतलाना (batlaanaa)

verb A common variant of बताना (bataanaa, 'to tell'); both mean the same thing and are used interchangeably in speech.
Sense 1 : to tell, to inform
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बताना (bataanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear both बताना (bataanaa) and बतलाना (batlaanaa) constantly in everyday Hindi -- older generations and certain regional speech patterns (especially in Bihar, UP, and parts of the Hindi belt) lean slightly more towards बतलाना (batlaanaa), but there is no hard rule; a Delhi household will use both without a second thought.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes think बतलाना (batlaanaa) and बताना (bataanaa) differ in meaning or nuance -- they don't; pick whichever comes more naturally, though बताना (bataanaa) is somewhat more common overall. 2) Some learners assume बतलाना (batlaanaa) is somehow more formal or more casual than बताना (bataanaa); in reality both sit at the same register and are chosen freely by the same speaker even in the same conversation.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा मुझे रास्ता बतला दो। Please tell me the way.
(zaraa mujhe raastaa batlaa do।)
