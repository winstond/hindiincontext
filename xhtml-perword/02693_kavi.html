कवि (kavi)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'poet' -- straightforward and used across registers from schoolbook praise to serious literary discussion.
Sense 1 : poet
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शायर (shaayar, mainly for Urdu-style/ghazal poets), कवयित्री (kavyitrii, feminine form, not a synonym but often confused)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR you will hear कवि (kavi) most in the context of a कवि सम्मेलन (kavi sammelan, 'poets' gathering'), a beloved public event where several poets take turns reciting -- often mixing humor, patriotism, and pathos -- to a live, vocal audience. Bollywood and school textbooks alike use कवि (kavi) as a title of respect, much like 'poet' is used in English for figures like Tulsidas or Kabir.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use कवि (kavi) for a female poet; the distinct feminine form कवयित्री (kavyitrii) exists and is preferred in careful speech and writing, though कवि (kavi) is also heard used generically. 2) कवि (kavi) and शायर (shaayar) are not fully interchangeable: शायर (shaayar) specifically evokes Urdu poetic tradition (ghazals, shers), while कवि (kavi) is the general Hindi word for poet, often associated with Hindi verse traditions.
