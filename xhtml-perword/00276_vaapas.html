वापस (vaapas)

adverb/adjective One core meaning, 'back/return', used mainly as an adverb (वापस आना (vaapas aanaa, 'to come back'), वापस जाना (vaapas jaanaa, 'to go back')) and occasionally as an invariant adjective before a noun.
Sense 1 : back, in return, again (to a previous place/state)
adverb Pairs with motion and transfer verbs (आना (aanaa), जाना (jaanaa), देना (denaa), लेना (lenaa), करना (karnaa)) to add the sense of 'back/again'; it sits right before the verb it modifies.
Synonyms: लौट कर (laut kar, phrase variant), पुनः (punah, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear constantly at shops and homes: पैसे वापस दो (paise vaapas do, 'give the money back') or सामान वापस कर दो (saamaan vaapas kar do, 'return the item'). It is also the everyday partner of लौटना (lautnaa), which means the same 'return' idea but as a full verb rather than an adverb riding on another verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often add फिर से (phir se) alongside वापस (vaapas) thinking both are needed for 'again' -- but वापस (vaapas) already carries the 'back' sense, so वापस करना (vaapas karnaa) alone means 'to give back', not just 'to do again'. 2) वापस (vaapas) implies returning to a prior state or place, while फिर से (phir se) or दोबारा (dobaaraa) mean plain repetition -- वापस पढ़ना (vaapas padhnaa) sounds odd to a native ear, since reading doesn't 'return' anywhere; दोबारा पढ़ना (dobaaraa padhnaa) is what's meant.

Sense 2 : return (used attributively, as in 'return ticket')
adjective Used invariantly before a noun in set phrases like वापस टिकट (vaapas tikat, 'return ticket') or वापसी टिकट (vaapsii tikat, the more common noun-derived form); the adjectival use of वापस (vaapas) itself is less common than the adverbial one.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In practice, most people reach for the noun वापसी (vaapsii, 'return, homecoming') in compounds like वापसी टिकट (vaapsii tikat) rather than using वापस (vaapas) directly as an adjective -- worth knowing both exist, but वापसी (vaapsii) is your safer everyday choice here.
