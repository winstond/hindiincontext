पूँछ (poonchh)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'tail' -- the body part on an animal. Not to be confused with the verb पूछना (poochhnaa, 'to ask'), which is spelled and pronounced differently (no chandrabindu nasalization) but often confused by learners in writing.
Sense 1 : tail (of an animal)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every kid in Delhi who has grown up around street dogs or has seen a bandar (bandar, 'monkey') swinging by its पूँछ (poonchh) knows this word early. It also shows up in common idiomatic phrases like पूँछ हिलाना (poonchh hilaanaa, 'to wag the tail'), often used mockingly for a person who flatters or fawns over someone, similar to the English image of a 'tail-wagging' sycophant.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse पूँछ (poonchh, 'tail') with पूछ (poochh) / पूछना (poochhnaa, 'to ask') -- the nasalization (chandrabindu) on पूँछ is what marks 'tail' and must not be dropped in writing, even though it is easy to miss when typing fast. 2) Do not confuse with पीछे (piichhe, 'behind') just because both relate to something being at the back -- पूँछ is a concrete body part, not a spatial postposition.
