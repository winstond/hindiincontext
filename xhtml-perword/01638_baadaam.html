बादाम (baadaam)

noun A single everyday sense: the almond, both as the nut itself and the tree/wood, used identically to English 'almond' in most contexts.
Sense 1 : almond (the nut)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian homes, बादाम (baadaam) is not just a snack food but a mini health ritual -- soaked overnight in water, peeled in the morning, and handed to kids by mothers and grandmothers who swear it makes you 'तेज़' (sharp/quick-witted). You will also meet it inside common phrases like बादाम वाला दूध (baadaam vaalaa doodh, 'almond milk') and in Mughlai-influenced sweets and Kashmiri/Punjabi kahwa, reflecting its long history as a prized dry fruit (mेवा, meva) brought via Central Asian and Persian trade routes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often guess it is feminine because many food-item nouns in Hindi are feminine, but बादाम (baadaam) is masculine -- मुझे बादाम पसंद हैं (mujhe baadaam pasand hain, 'I like almonds'), not feminine agreement. 2) In the plural (talking about several almonds), the word itself does not change form, which surprises learners expecting a visible plural marker.

Sense 2 : almond-shaped, almond color/tone (used attributively, especially of eyes)
noun, masculine Used in the compound बादाम जैसी आँखें (baadaam jaisii aankhen, 'eyes like almonds') or shortened to बादामी आँखें (baadaamii aankhen) -- the plain noun बादाम (baadaam) itself is not used as a direct adjective without जैसा (jaisaa) or the derived form बादामी (baadaamii).
Synonyms: बादामी (baadaamii, adjectival form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Describing eyes as बादाम जैसी आँखें (baadaam jaisii aankhen) is a classic compliment in Hindi film lyrics and poetry, putting almond-shaped eyes in the same beauty-vocabulary league as English's own 'almond eyes' -- a rare case where the two languages reach for the exact same image.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use बादाम (baadaam) directly before a noun as an adjective (e.g. for 'almond eyes'), but Hindi actually reaches for the derived adjective बादामी (baadaamii) or the simile बादाम जैसी (baadaam jaisii) instead of the bare noun.
