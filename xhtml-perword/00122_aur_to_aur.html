और तो और (aur to aur)

conjunction A fixed emphatic phrase meaning 'and what's more' or 'on top of that' -- used to introduce a surprising or escalating example that tops what was just said.
Sense 1 : on top of that, and what's more (introduces an escalating/surprising example)
conjunction Sits at the start of the clause it introduces, after an initial statement has set up a comparison; the clause that follows names something even more extreme than what preceded it.
Synonyms: यहाँ तक कि (yahaan tak ki), ऊपर से (oopar se, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in animated Delhi conversation when someone is building up a complaint or a piece of gossip: 'उसने झूठ बोला, और तो और, मेरे सामने ही बोला' (usne jhooth bolaa, aur to aur, mere saamne hii bolaa, 'He lied, and what's more, he lied right in front of me'). The repetition of और (aur) around तो (to) gives it a punchy, rising-intonation feel when spoken -- it's made for drama.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate it word-for-word as 'and then and' since और (aur) means 'and' and तो (to) is 'then/so' -- but the whole phrase is frozen and only makes sense as a unit meaning 'what's more.' 2) It needs a preceding statement to escalate from; used alone at the start of a conversation it sounds incomplete. 3) It is not interchangeable with plain और (aur, 'and') -- dropping तो (to) removes the emphatic 'topping' feel entirely.
