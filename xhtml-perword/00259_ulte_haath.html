उलटे हाथ (ulte haath)

adverb A fixed directional phrase meaning 'on the left side' or 'to the left' -- literally 'reverse/wrong hand.' Built from उलटा (ultaa, reversed/wrong) plus हाथ (haath, hand), used the way English uses 'on your left.'
Sense 1 : to/on the left side (giving directions)
adverb Functions as a locational/directional adverb, typically after मुड़ना (mudnaa, to turn) or with पर (par) -- e.g. उलटे हाथ पर (ulte haath par, 'on the left side').
Synonyms: बायें हाथ (baayen haath), बायीं तरफ़ (baayiin taraf)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the everyday way Delhi shopkeepers, auto drivers, and relatives giving directions say 'left' -- you'll hear उलटे हाथ मुड़ना (ulte haath mudnaa, 'to turn left') constantly when asking for directions on the street. It pairs naturally with its opposite सीधे हाथ (siidhe haath, 'on the right'), and both are far more colloquial-sounding than the more formal बायाँ (baayaan)/दायाँ (daayaan) pair taught in classrooms.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume उलटा (ultaa) always means 'upside down' or 'wrong' and miss that in this fixed phrase it simply signals 'left,' contrasting with सीधे हाथ (siidhe haath, 'on the right'). 2) The phrase is invariant in this directional sense -- it doesn't shift to उलटा हाथ (ultaa haath) or उलटी हाथ (ultii haath) here, unlike ordinary adjective agreement learners expect.
Sample Sentences:
बस स्टॉप से उलटे हाथ मुड़ जाना। Turn left from the bus stop.
(bas stop se ulte haath mud jaanaa।)

मंदिर उलटे हाथ पड़ता है। The temple is on the left.
(mandir ulte haath padtaa hai।)

अगले चौराहे पर उलटे हाथ मुड़ना। Turn left at the next intersection.
(agle chauraahe par ulte haath mudnaa।)

मैं उलटे हाथ मुड़ गया और रास्ता भटक गया। I turned left and lost my way.
(main ulte haath mud gayaa aur raastaa bhatak gayaa।)

अगर तुम उलटे हाथ मुड़ते तो दुकान मिल जाती। If you had turned left, you would have found the shop.
(agar tum ulte haath mudte to dukaan mil jaatii।)

क्या यहाँ से उलटे हाथ जाना है या सीधे हाथ? Do I go left from here or right?
(kyaa yahaan se ulte haath jaanaa hai yaa siidhe haath?)

(oto vaalaa bolaa, ulte haath mud jaao, aage baink milegaa।)

(shaayad vo ulte haath mud gaii ho।)

(galii ke muhaane se ulte haath ek puraanii havelii hai।)
