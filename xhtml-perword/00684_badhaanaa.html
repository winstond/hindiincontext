बढ़ाना (badhaanaa)

verb The causative of बढ़ना (badhnaa, 'to increase/advance'), meaning 'to increase, extend, or move something forward'; senses split between physical increase/extension and moving something along or forward.
Sense 1 : to increase (something)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बढ़ोतरी करना (badhotrii karnaa, noun+verb phrase, used for quantities/rates)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic बढ़ना/बढ़ाना (badhnaa/badhaanaa) intransitive-transitive pair that Hindi loves to build: something 'increases' on its own, or you 'make it increase.' You'll hear it constantly in market haggling and news headlines about prices, speed, and salaries.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use बढ़ना (badhnaa) when they mean the causative -- e.g. saying कीमत बढ़ी (kiimat badhii, 'the price increased') when they actually want to say someone raised the price, which needs बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) with an agent: दुकानदार ने कीमत बढ़ाई (dukaandaar ne kiimat badhaaii, 'the shopkeeper raised the price'). 2) With ने-marking: this verb takes ने (ne) in the perfective since it is transitive, unlike its intransitive counterpart बढ़ना (badhnaa).

Sense 2 : to extend, lengthen, or stretch out (something)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: लंबा करना (lambaa karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in offices and schools all the time: बॉस ने छुट्टी बढ़ा दी (bos ne chhuttii badhaa dii, 'the boss extended the leave') -- the compound with दी (dii) adds a sense of a completed favor or decisive action.
Common Mistakes: 1) Used for extending time, deadlines, or physical length -- छुट्टी बढ़ाना (chhuttii badhaanaa, 'to extend the leave/holiday') -- learners sometimes reach for लंबा करना (lambaa karnaa) here when बढ़ाना (badhaanaa) is the more natural native choice.

Sense 3 : to move forward, advance, extend (a hand, foot, etc.)
verb, transitive Common in fixed phrases with body parts, e.g. हाथ बढ़ाना (haath badhaanaa, 'to extend a hand', literally and in the sense of offering help) and कदम बढ़ाना (kadam badhaanaa, 'to step forward, make progress').
Synonyms: आगे करना (aage karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मदद के लिए हाथ बढ़ाना (madad ke lie haath badhaanaa, 'to extend a hand for help') is a phrase you'll hear in speeches, ads, and everyday appeals for support -- it carries the same warmth as the English idiom 'to lend a hand.'
Common Mistakes: 1) हाथ बढ़ाना (haath badhaanaa) is idiomatic for 'to offer help' or 'to reach out,' not just the literal stretching of an arm -- learners sometimes miss this figurative layer. 2) कदम बढ़ाना (kadam badhaanaa) is used metaphorically for making progress in life or a project, not just literal walking.
Sample Sentences:
उसने मदद के लिए हाथ बढ़ाया। He extended a hand for help.
(usne madad ke lie haath badhaayaa।)
