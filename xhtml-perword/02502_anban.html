अनबन (anban)

noun A feminine noun meaning a falling-out or minor rift between people -- a strained relationship marked by tension or estrangement, not necessarily a loud fight.
Sense 1 : discord, rift, falling-out (between people)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: झगड़ा (jhagdaa, more about active quarreling), मनमुटाव (manmutaav, close in meaning, emotional distance), तनाव (tanaav, general tension, broader than interpersonal rifts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear when a family member explains, with a slight sigh, why two relatives aren't on speaking terms right now -- भाइयों में अनबन है (bhaaiyon men anban hai, 'there's tension between the brothers'). It carries a sense of a relationship gone slightly sour rather than open warfare, which makes it a very handy, deniable way to describe household or office friction without escalating it into a scandal.
Common Mistakes: 1) अनबन (anban) describes a state of strained relations, not a specific shouting match -- for an actual fight, झगड़ा (jhagdaa) is the better word. 2) It is almost always used with होना (honaa) or हो जाना (ho jaanaa), as in दोनों में अनबन हो गई (donon men anban ho gaii, 'there was a falling-out between the two'), rather than with करना (karnaa).
