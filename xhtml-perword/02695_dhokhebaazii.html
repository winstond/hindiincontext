धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning deceitfulness or the act of cheating/betraying someone; built from धोखा (dhokhaa, deceit/trick) plus the agentive suffix -बाज़ (baaz) and the abstract-noun ending -ई (ii).
Sense 1 : deceitfulness, cheating, treachery
noun, feminine
Synonyms: छल (chhal), फ़रेब (fareb), धोखा (dhokhaa, covers the single act more than the habitual trait), बेईमानी (beiimaanii, emphasizes dishonesty broadly), दगाबाज़ी (dagaabaazii, variant form with similar structure)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The -बाज़ (baaz) suffix is very productive in Hindi-Urdu, turning nouns into 'one who does X' (like नौटंकीबाज़ nautankiibaaz, 'a drama-monger'), and -ई (ii) then abstracts that into the behavior itself, so धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii) literally builds up as 'the business of being a deceiver.' It's a common word in everyday complaints about being cheated in business, relationships, or friendships, and carries a fairly strong moral charge -- calling someone's actions धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii) is a real accusation, not a mild criticism.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use धोखा (dhokhaa) and धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii) interchangeably, but धोखा (dhokhaa) usually refers to a single instance of being deceived, while धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii) refers to the ongoing trait or pattern of behavior, closer to 'deceitfulness' as a character flaw. 2) The related agentive noun धोखेबाज़ (dhokhebaaz) means 'a cheater/deceiver' (a person), and learners often confuse this with धोखेबाज़ी (dhokhebaazii), which is the abstract quality or act itself, not the person.
