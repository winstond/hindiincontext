भरती (bhartii)

noun A variant spelling of भर्ती (bhartii); covers 'recruitment/enlistment' and 'admission' (to hospital, school, etc.), both from the same root idea of being 'filled into' a role or institution.
Sense 1 : recruitment, enlistment
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नियुक्ति (niyukti, formal), भर्ती (bhartii, dominant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly on notice boards outside police stations and army recruitment offices: सेना भरती (senaa bhartii, 'army recruitment') or पुलिस भरती (pulis bhartii, 'police recruitment'), especially in North Indian small towns where such drives are major local events.
Common Mistakes: 1) भरती (bhartii) is usually paired with होना (honaa) 'to get recruited' (intransitive, e.g. फ़ौज में भरती होना (fauj men bhartii honaa, 'to enlist in the army')) or करना (karnaa) 'to recruit someone' (transitive) -- learners often use only one and miss that both frames exist depending on who is doing the enlisting.

Sense 2 : admission (to hospital, school, institution)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दाख़िला (daakhilaa), प्रवेश (pravesh, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: अस्पताल में भरती होना (asptaal men bhartii honaa, 'to be admitted to hospital') is the everyday phrase Delhi families use on the phone when someone is unwell -- it carries a note of real concern, unlike the more neutral दाख़िला (daakhilaa) used for school admissions.
Common Mistakes: 1) For hospitals specifically, भरती होना (bhartii honaa) means 'to be admitted' (as a patient) -- learners sometimes reach for दाख़िला (daakhilaa) here, which sounds odd for hospitals though it is fine for schools.
