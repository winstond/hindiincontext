चुपके से (chupke se)

adverb An adverbial phrase meaning 'quietly, secretly, on the sly' -- describes an action done without others noticing. One core sense.
Sense 1 : secretly, stealthily, without being noticed
adverb Formed from चुपके (chupke) + से (se); functions as a fixed adverbial phrase modifying the verb, usually placed right before it.
Synonyms: छिपकर (chhipkar), गुपचुप (gupchup, colloquial), खुफिया तौर पर (khuphiyaa taur par, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase every Hindi-speaking kid has heard from a parent noticing something was taken without permission: 'तुमने चुपके से मेरा फोन लिया?' (tumne chupke se meraa phon liyaa?, 'Did you take my phone secretly/without asking?'). It carries a mild scolding or mischievous flavor -- sneaking a snack, tiptoeing out, or hiding something from family -- and is a staple of everyday household speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the से (se) and just say चुपके (chupke) alone -- this also occurs in casual speech but चुपके से (chupke se) is the fuller, more standard adverbial form. 2) Don't confuse with धीरे से (dhiire se, 'slowly/gently') -- चुपके से (chupke se) is about secrecy and avoiding detection, not about speed or softness of movement.
