बुहारना (buhaarnaa)

verb A concrete, everyday verb meaning 'to sweep' (a floor, courtyard, etc.) -- one sense, no idiomatic extensions worth splitting out.
Sense 1 : to sweep
verb, transitive
Synonyms: झाड़ना (jhaadnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word for the daily chore of sweeping the ghar (ghar) or the aangan (aangan, courtyard) with a jhaadu (jhaadoo) -- something almost every household does first thing in the morning. In many homes there's a strong cultural association between sweeping the floor at dawn and welcoming Lakshmi (lakshmii) or simply starting the day fresh, so you'll hear this word a lot in domestic, everyday conversation rather than in writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with झाड़ना (jhaadnaa), which covers a broader range of 'brushing/shaking off dust' (from clothes, a table, etc.) as well as sweeping floors; बुहारना (buhaarnaa) is used specifically for sweeping a floor or open ground clean, usually with a broom (jhaadoo).
