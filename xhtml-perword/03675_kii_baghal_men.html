की बग़ल में (kii baghal men)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'beside, next to, alongside' -- literally 'in the flank/side of.' One sense only.
Sense 1 : beside, next to
postposition Takes a preceding noun in the oblique case marked with की/के (agreeing with बग़ल, which is feminine), e.g. मेज़ की बग़ल में (mez kii baghal men, 'beside the table').
Synonyms: के पास (ke paas, broader: near, by), के बराबर में (ke baraabar men), के साथ (ke saath, broader: with, alongside)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बग़ल (baghal) literally means 'armpit' or 'flank/side' of the body, so की बग़ल में (kii baghal men) carries a bodily, tucked-in-close image -- much like English 'under one's arm' extends to 'right beside.' You will hear it a lot in everyday directions in Delhi, like दुकान की बग़ल में (dukaan kii baghal men, 'right beside the shop'), often interchangeably with के बराबर में (ke baraabar men) in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to के पास (ke paas) for 'near' without realizing की बग़ल में (kii baghal men) is more specific -- it means directly beside/flanking something, not just generally close to it. 2) The connector is always की (kii), never के (ke) or के बग़ल में (ke baghal men), because बग़ल (baghal) is feminine -- learners sometimes mismatch the connector's gender to the noun before it instead of to बग़ल (baghal) itself.
