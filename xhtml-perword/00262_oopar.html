ऊपर (oopar)

adverb/postposition/adjective Mainly a spatial adverb/postposition meaning 'up, above, on top', with an extended sense meaning 'more, in addition' and a figurative sense about being above/beyond something (e.g. authority or comprehension).
Sense 1 : physical location above or on top
adverb
Synonyms: उपर (upar, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday talk about houses, ऊपर (oopar) is how people say 'upstairs' -- ऊपर वाला कमरा (oopar vaalaa kamraa, 'the room upstairs') is common in describing multi-story family homes typical in Delhi-NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use ऊपर (oopar) alone where a postposition sense is needed -- compare ऊपर जाना (oopar jaanaa, 'to go up/upstairs') with मेज़ के ऊपर (mez ke oopar, 'on top of the table'), where के (ke) is required before ऊपर (oopar) to link it to a noun.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : on top of, above (governing a noun via के)
postposition Used after a noun in the oblique with के (ke) as in मेज़ के ऊपर (mez ke oopar, 'on top of the table'), forming a compound postposition के ऊपर (ke oopar).
Synonyms: पर (par, broader meaning 'on')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of के ऊपर (ke oopar) as more specific than पर (par) -- पर (par) just means 'on', while के ऊपर (ke oopar) stresses being on top or above something, like किताब मेज़ के ऊपर है (kitaab mez ke oopar hai, 'the book is on top of the table').

Sense 3 : in addition, on top of that
adverb Often appears as ऊपर से (oopar se) to mean 'on top of that, in addition' when adding another fact, often a negative one.
Synonyms: इसके अलावा (iske alaavaa), साथ ही (saath hii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear ऊपर से (oopar se) a lot in complaints -- it's the Hindi equivalent of piling on another grievance, much like English 'and to top it off.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes miss that ऊपर से (oopar se) in this sense has nothing to do with physical direction -- it is a connector meaning 'and what's more,' as in बारिश हो रही है, ऊपर से ठंड भी है (baarish ho rahii hai, oopar se thand bhii hai, 'it's raining, and on top of that it's cold too').

Sense 4 : superficial, external, surface-level (figurative)
adjective Used invariantly before a noun, as in ऊपर की बात (oopar kii baat, 'a surface-level remark') or ऊपरी तौर पर (ooprii taur par, 'superficially') -- note the related invariant adjective ऊपरी (ooprii) is more common in this figurative role than ऊपर (oopar) itself.
Synonyms: सतही (satahii)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense connects to the spatial meaning metaphorically: something 'on the surface' (ऊपर (oopar)) is not deep, so it comes to mean 'not genuine' -- ऊपरी दिखावा (ooprii dikhaavaa, 'superficial show') is a common phrase about insincerity.
Common Mistakes: 1) For the figurative 'superficial' sense, native speakers usually reach for the derived form ऊपरी (ooprii) rather than ऊपर (oopar) directly -- learners should recognize both but lean on ऊपरी (ooprii) for this meaning.

उसकी मुस्कान सिर्फ़ ऊपरी थी, दिल से नहीं। Her smile was only superficial, not from the heart.
(uskii muskaan sirf ooprii thii, dil se nahiin।)
