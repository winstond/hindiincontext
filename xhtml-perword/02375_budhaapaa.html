बुढ़ापा (budhaapaa)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'old age' -- the life-stage itself, used both literally and in reflective or emotional contexts about aging.
Sense 1 : old age (the stage of life)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: वृद्धावस्था (vriddhaavasthaa, formal), बुढ़ौती (budhautii, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will often hear worried parents or grandparents say things like बुढ़ापे का सहारा (budhaape kaa sahaaraa, 'support of old age') when talking about children looking after them later in life -- it reflects a very real cultural expectation in Indian families that grown children care for aging parents. The word carries a gentle, sometimes wistful emotional weight beyond its plain dictionary meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बूढ़ा (boodhaa, 'old man/old') when they mean the abstract state; बुढ़ापा (budhaapaa) is the noun for the life-stage itself, not a person. 2) It is often paired with आना (aanaa) -- बुढ़ापा आना (budhaapaa aanaa, 'old age to arrive') -- treating old age as something that comes upon you, similar to नींद आना (niind aanaa); learners sometimes instead try to construct it with होना (honaa) alone, which sounds less natural in this common frame.

(budhaape men sehat kaa dhyaan rakhnaa bahut zaroorii hai।)
