छियासठ (chhiyaasath)

numeral The cardinal number 66 (sixty-six). A single-sense numeral used for counting and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number 66 (sixty-six)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numerals above twenty, छियासठ (chhiyaasath) is one of those irregular forms that native speakers just absorbed as children through counting rhymes, prices, and phone numbers -- there is no shortcut except repetition. You will meet it most often quoting ages, exam scores, or amounts of money.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are not built compositionally the way English ones are, so छियासठ (chhiyaasath) simply has to be memorized as its own word rather than assembled from छह (chhah, 'six') and साठ (saath, 'sixty') on the fly. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with similar-sounding neighbors like छप्पन (chhappan, 'fifty-six') or सड़सठ (sadsath, 'sixty-seven') -- the middle numerals in each decade block share a rhythm that takes practice to tell apart by ear.
