अहमियत (ahmiyat)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'importance' or 'significance' -- a common, everyday word for talking about how much something matters.
Sense 1 : importance, significance
noun, feminine Commonly used with देना (denaa, 'to give') and होना (honaa, 'to be') -- अहमियत देना (ahmiyat denaa, 'to give importance to') and किसी की अहमियत होना (kisii kii ahmiyat honaa, 'for something/someone to have importance').
Synonyms: महत्व (mahatv), महत्ता (mahattaa, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes from Persian/Urdu and is completely at home in everyday Hindi -- you will hear it just as often on the street or in a Bollywood dialogue as महत्व (mahatv), especially when someone is talking about relationships: रिश्तों में अहमियत (rishton men ahmiyat, 'importance in relationships') is a very common phrase, often in the context of someone feeling valued or undervalued by family or a partner.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for महत्व (mahatv) by default since it is taught first in textbooks, but अहमियत (ahmiyat) is at least as common in everyday spoken Hindi and sounds completely natural in casual conversation, not just formal writing. 2) The thing or person that has importance is usually marked with की/का/के (kii/kaa/ke) rather than treated as a direct object: उसकी अहमियत (uskii ahmiyat, 'his/her importance'), not a direct 'importance of him' phrasing carried over from English.
