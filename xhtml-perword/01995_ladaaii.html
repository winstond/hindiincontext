लड़ाई (ladaaii)

noun Covers physical/armed conflict (fight, war, battle) and verbal conflict (quarrel, fight between people); both senses share the root लड़ना (ladnaa, 'to fight').
Sense 1 : quarrel, fight (between people)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: झगड़ा (jhagdaa), तकरार (takraar, somewhat formal), बहस (bahas, meaning scope: verbal argument only, no physical element)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear a mother use about her kids: बच्चों की लड़ाई (bachchon kii ladaaii, 'the kids' fight'). It is completely ordinary and comes up constantly in family and neighborhood contexts, from siblings fighting over the remote to two neighbors arguing over parking.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बहस (bahas) when they mean a heated argument, but लड़ाई (ladaaii) covers both verbal and physical conflict, so it is the safer general-purpose word. 2) The common verb pairing is लड़ाई होना (ladaaii honaa, 'a fight to happen/break out') or लड़ाई करना (ladaaii karnaa, 'to pick/have a fight'), not लड़ाई लड़ना (ladaaii ladnaa) which sounds odd for everyday squabbles (that phrasing is reserved for larger battles, see the war sense).
Sample Sentences:
बच्चों की लड़ाई रोज़ की बात है। The kids' fighting is a daily occurrence.
(bachchon kii ladaaii roz kii baat hai।)

Sense 2 : war, battle, armed conflict
noun, feminine
Synonyms: युद्ध (yuddh, formal), जंग (jang), संग्राम (sangraam, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: लड़ाई (ladaaii) is the everyday word for war that you will hear on the news or in casual talk about history (like 1965 or 1971 की लड़ाई (ladaaii)), while युद्ध (yuddh) feels more like the textbook or formal-speech term for the same idea.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense, the natural collocation is लड़ाई लड़ना (ladaaii ladnaa, 'to fight a war/battle'), unlike the quarrel sense where लड़ाई करना (ladaaii karnaa) is preferred; mixing these up is a common learner slip.

Sense 3 : struggle, fight for a cause
noun, feminine Used metaphorically for a prolonged effort toward a goal, as in हक़ की लड़ाई (haq kii ladaaii, 'the fight for rights').
Synonyms: संघर्ष (sangharsh), जंग (jang, figurative use for a cause)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in the context of social and political movements -- अधिकारों की लड़ाई (adhikaaron kii ladaaii, 'the fight for rights') or गरीबी के खिलाफ़ लड़ाई (gariibii ke khilaaf ladaaii, 'the fight against poverty'). संघर्ष (sangharsh) is its close cousin and often used interchangeably in this figurative sense.
