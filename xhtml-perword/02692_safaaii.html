सफ़ाई (safaaii)

noun Covers the literal sense of cleanliness/cleaning as well as the extended sense of 'clearing oneself' -- giving an explanation or defense. Both senses share the same root idea of 'making clean/clear.'
Sense 1 : cleanliness, cleaning
noun, feminine
Synonyms: स्वच्छता (svachchhtaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR, सफ़ाई (safaaii) is a very everyday word -- you'll hear it from the सफ़ाई वाला (safaaii vaalaa, 'cleaning person/garbage collector') at your door, in society WhatsApp groups organizing सफ़ाई अभियान (safaaii abhiyaan, 'cleanliness drive'), and famously as part of the national सफ़ाई अभियान (safaaii abhiyaan) campaigns. It carries a slightly moral weight too -- cleanliness as a value, not just a chore.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say घर की सफ़ाई करना (ghar kii safaaii karnaa, 'to clean the house') but forget सफ़ाई (safaaii) itself is the noun -- the verb needed alongside it is करना (karnaa), not होना (honaa), when someone is actively doing the cleaning; सफ़ाई होना (safaaii honaa) is used when describing the state/event of cleaning happening, e.g. by a service.

मैं हर इतवार को घर की सफ़ाई करता हूँ। I clean the house every Sunday.
(main har itvaar ko ghar kii safaaii kartaa hoon।)

Sense 2 : explanation, justification, defense (of oneself)
noun, feminine Common in the fixed expression सफ़ाई देना (safaaii denaa, 'to give an explanation/defend oneself').
Synonyms: स्पष्टीकरण (spashtiikran, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'd use when someone's caught red-handed or under suspicion and has to explain themselves -- a kid explaining why they're late, or a politician facing questions. उसने अपनी सफ़ाई दी (usne apnii safaaii dii, 'he gave his defense/explanation') has a slightly defensive, on-the-back-foot flavor that English 'explanation' doesn't always carry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate 'explanation' with सफ़ाई (safaaii) in neutral contexts (like explaining a math problem), but this sense is specifically about defending or justifying oneself after being blamed or suspected -- for a neutral explanation, समझाना (samjhaanaa) or स्पष्टीकरण (spashtiikran) fits better.
