चलाना (chalaanaa)

verb The causative of चलना (chalnaa, 'to move/walk'). Core senses: to drive/operate a vehicle or machine, to run/manage something (a business, a household), and to wield/use a tool or weapon.
Sense 1 : to drive or operate (a vehicle, machine)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic causative pairing: चलना (chalnaa) 'to move' becomes चलाना (chalaanaa) 'to make move/drive' -- the agent causes the vehicle to do what it would otherwise do on its own. You'll hear गाड़ी चलाना (gaadii chalaanaa) for driving a car, स्कूटर चलाना (skootar chalaanaa) for riding a scooter, and even कंप्यूटर चलाना (kampyootar chalaanaa) for operating a computer.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use चलना (chalnaa) when they mean चलाना (chalaanaa) -- गाड़ी चलती है (gaadii chaltii hai, 'the car moves/runs') describes the vehicle moving on its own, while गाड़ी चलाना (gaadii chalaanaa, 'to drive the car') requires an agent doing the driving. 2) For vehicles you 'know how to drive,' Hindi says गाड़ी चलाना आता है (gaadii chalaanaa aataa hai) using आना (aanaa), not सकना (saknaa) -- सकना is about momentary ability, आना is about a learned skill.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe gaadii chalaanii nahiin aatii।)

Sense 2 : to run or manage (a business, household, organization)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: संचालित करना (sanchaalit karnaa, formal), चलाना (chalaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every shopkeeper who दुकान चलाता है (dukaan chalaataa hai, 'runs a shop') and every mother who घर चलाती है (ghar chalaatii hai, 'runs the household') is using this same extended sense -- keeping something moving forward through your own effort and management, just like keeping a vehicle in motion.

Sense 3 : to wield or use (a tool, weapon, or limb)
verb, transitive Common objects include चाकू (chaakoo, 'knife'), तलवार (talvaar, 'sword'), कलम (kalam, 'pen'), and हाथ (haath, 'hand') in idiomatic phrases like हाथ चलाना (haath chalaanaa, 'to raise a hand/strike').
Synonyms: इस्तेमाल करना (istemaal karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense extends the 'making something move' idea to tools and body parts under your control -- कलम चलाना (kalam chalaanaa, 'to wield the pen') is a common way to talk about writing as a craft, almost like saying someone 'has a way with words.'
Common Mistakes: 1) हाथ चलाना (haath chalaanaa) idiomatically means 'to hit/strike someone,' not literally 'to move one's hand' -- learners sometimes miss the aggressive connotation.
Sample Sentences:
