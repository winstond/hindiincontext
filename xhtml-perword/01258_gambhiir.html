गंभीर (gambhiir)

adjective A borrowed-from-Sanskrit adjective meaning 'serious' or 'grave' -- covers a person's temperament, the gravity of a situation, and the severity of a medical condition.
Sense 1 : serious, grave (of a situation, matter, or tone)
adjective
Synonyms: गम्भीर (gambhiir, spelling variant with anusvaar written as म्), गहन (gahan), संगीन (sangiin, formal, used for serious crimes/matters)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on news channels: गंभीर स्थिति (gambhiir sthiti, 'serious situation'), गंभीर मामला (gambhiir maamlaa, 'serious matter'). It carries a certain gravitas -- a बात (baat) can be गंभीर without being sad, just weighty and deserving attention.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सीरियस (siiriyas) since it is common in casual speech, but गंभीर (gambhiir) is the word you want in writing or formal speech, and for weighty topics like गंभीर मुद्दा (gambhiir muddaa, 'serious issue'). 2) Do not confuse with दुखी (dukhii, 'sad') -- गंभीर describes weightiness or seriousness of tone, not sadness itself.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : serious, critical (of a person's medical condition)
adjective
Synonyms: नाज़ुक (naazuk, often used for 'critical' health condition), संगीन (sangiin)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will hear on the news after an accident: हालत गंभीर बनी हुई है (haalat gambhiir banii huii hai, 'the condition remains critical'). It is the standard register for hospital updates, distinct from casual health talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) In hospital contexts, मरीज़ की हालत गंभीर है (mariiz kii haalat gambhiir hai, 'the patient's condition is serious') is the standard formula -- learners sometimes try to describe the person as गंभीर instead of describing their हालत (haalat, 'condition'), which sounds off.

Sense 3 : serious, solemn, grave (of a person's temperament or demeanor)
adjective
Synonyms: शांत (shaant, calm, overlaps but emphasizes composure over seriousness), संयमी (sanymii, literary, self-controlled)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A गंभीर (gambhiir) व्यक्ति (vyakti, 'person') is someone who doesn't joke around much -- steady, composed, maybe a little stern. It's often said approvingly of elders or teachers who carry themselves with quiet dignity, distinct from being boring or humorless.
