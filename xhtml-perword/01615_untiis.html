उनतीस (untiis)

numeral The number 29 (twenty-nine), used both as a counting numeral and an adjective before nouns.
Sense 1 : the number twenty-nine (29)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numerals, उनतीस (untiis) just has to be drilled as a standalone word rather than reasoned out from its parts -- Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 are famous among learners for not following a clean, predictable pattern the way English tens-and-units do. It shows up naturally in ages, dates, prices, and counts, so it is worth learning solidly rather than trying to derive it each time.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 1 to 99 are largely irregular and must be learned individually rather than built compositionally like English "twenty-nine"; उनतीस (untiis) does not obviously derive from उन्नीस (unniis, nineteen) or तीस (tiis, thirty) in a way a learner can guess, so it is best memorized as its own item. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with उन्नीस (unniis, nineteen) or उनतालीस (untaaliis, thirty-nine) because of the similar-sounding uu-n prefix pattern found across the Hindi numerals ending near a multiple of ten.
