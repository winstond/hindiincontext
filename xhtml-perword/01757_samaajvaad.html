समाजवाद (samaajvaad)

noun A single-sense political/economic noun meaning 'socialism.' Built from समाज (samaaj, society) plus the suffix -वाद (vaad, -ism, doctrine), a productive pattern seen in words like राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad, nationalism) and पूंजीवाद (poonjiivaad, capitalism).
Sense 1 : socialism (the political/economic ideology)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries real weight in Indian political history -- the preamble to the Indian Constitution famously calls India a 'sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic,' and समाजवाद (samaajvaad) was inserted there during the Emergency era (1976). Politicians across the spectrum still invoke it, so you will hear it often in news debates and election speeches rather than casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse समाजवाद (samaajvaad) with साम्यवाद (saamyvaad, communism) -- the two ideologies are related but distinct in Indian political usage, and news/political speech keeps them separate. 2) As an abstract mass noun it is not pluralized or used with a counter the way concrete nouns are.
