अथवा (athvaa)

conjunction A formal word for 'or', used mainly in writing and careful speech rather than everyday conversation; compare the everyday या (yaa).
Sense 1 : or
conjunction
Synonyms: या (yaa), किंवा (kinvaa, rare)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will see अथवा (athvaa) constantly on government forms, exam papers, and official notices -- 'name अथवा (athvaa) nickname' type constructions -- but almost never hear it in a casual chat over chai, where या (yaa) rules.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अथवा (athvaa) in casual conversation where या (yaa) is what native speakers actually say; अथवा (athvaa) belongs to formal writing, notices, legal or official language, and careful spoken registers (speeches, news).
