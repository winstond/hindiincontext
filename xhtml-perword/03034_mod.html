मोड़ (mod)

noun/verb Primarily a noun meaning a turn, bend, or curve (as in a road or a life story); also used as the imperative/stem of the verb मोड़ना (modnaa, 'to turn/bend/fold').
Sense 1 : a turn or bend (in a road, path, or river)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: घुमाव (ghumaav), मुड़ान (mudaan, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of giving directions in an Indian city: अगले मोड़ पर दायें मुड़ना (agle mod par daayen mudnaa, 'turn right at the next bend') is the kind of phrase you will hear from an auto driver or a shopkeeper pointing you down a gali. It is the standard word for a street corner or bend, more concrete than चौराहा (chauraahaa), which means a proper crossing/intersection.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use घुमाव (ghumaav) and मोड़ (mod) interchangeably, but मोड़ (mod) is the more natural word for a specific point where a road bends -- as in सड़क का मोड़ (sadak kaa mod, 'the bend in the road') -- while घुमाव (ghumaav) leans toward describing the curving shape itself.

Sense 2 : a turning point, twist, or new direction (in life, a story, events)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बदलाव (badlaav, change), उलटफेर (ulatpher, upheaval/reversal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the metaphorical extension of the road-bend image: just as a physical road turns, a life or a story can also take a मोड़ (mod). You will hear this often in film reviews and dramatic narration -- कहानी में एक नया मोड़ आया (kahaanii men ek nayaa mod aayaa, 'a new twist came into the story') -- and in reflective conversation about one's own life, ज़िंदगी ने एक मोड़ लिया (zindgii ne ek mod liyaa, 'life took a turn').

Sense 3 : turn/bend/fold (something) -- imperative and verb stem
verb, transitive This is the direct (intimate/familiar) imperative form of मोड़ना (modnaa), used to command someone to turn or bend something -- e.g. गाड़ी मोड़! (gaadii mod!, 'Turn the car!'). Full conjugation of मोड़ना (modnaa) belongs to that lemma's own entry; this sense exists because मोड़ (mod) as a bare form is common enough in speech to merit its own listing.
Synonyms: घुमाना (ghumaanaa, to rotate/turn), मोड़ लेना (mod lenaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this shouted from the back seat of an auto or car -- यहाँ से मोड़! (yahaan se mod!, 'Turn here!') -- it is direct, urgent, everyday street language rather than something you would say to an elder or stranger.
Common Mistakes: 1) This bare imperative मोड़! (mod!) is quite blunt and used only with people you'd address as तुम (tum) or तू (too) -- with someone you'd address respectfully, learners should reach for मोड़िए (modie) or मोड़ना (modnaa) plus a modal, not this bare stem.
Sample Sentences:
यहाँ से मोड़! Turn here!
(yahaan se mod!)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

(gaadii ko baaen modo।)
