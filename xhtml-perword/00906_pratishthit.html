प्रतिष्ठित (pratishthit)

adjective An adjective meaning 'established, respected, prestigious' -- used for institutions, people, and awards that carry social standing.
Sense 1 : respected, prestigious, of high standing
adjective
Synonyms: प्रसिद्ध (prasiddh), मशहूर (mashahoor), नामी (naamii, colloquial), सम्मानित (sammaanit, emphasizes being honored rather than famous)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly in newspapers and formal introductions: प्रतिष्ठित विश्वविद्यालय (pratishthit vishvvidyaalay, 'a prestigious university'), प्रतिष्ठित कंपनी (pratishthit kampnii, 'a reputed company'), or introducing a guest as हमारे प्रतिष्ठित अतिथि (hamaare pratishthit atithi, 'our esteemed guest'). It comes from the root प्रतिष्ठा (pratishthaa, 'prestige, standing'), so think of it as 'having प्रतिष्ठा (pratishthaa)' -- a useful link if you already know that noun.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मशहूर (mashahoor) or प्रसिद्ध (prasiddh) in every context, but प्रतिष्ठित (pratishthit) specifically carries a sense of institutional respectability and standing -- it fits a university, a company, or a family's reputation better than a pop song or a movie star, where मशहूर (mashahoor) is more natural. 2) It is rarely used for something merely 'popular' or 'well-known' in a casual sense (a popular street food, for instance) -- reserve it for contexts where dignity or social standing is at stake.
