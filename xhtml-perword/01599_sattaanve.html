सत्तानवे (sattaanve)

numeral The cardinal number 97 (English: 'ninety-seven').
Sense 1 : the number 97
numeral
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Hindi numbers from 11 to 99 are each their own little fossil, not built from clean tens-plus-units like English, so सत्तानवे (sattaanve) just has to be absorbed as a whole word. In practice, for anything above the twenties, most Delhi speakers reach for the digit form written in Devanagari or English (97) far more often than spelling it out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from सत्तर (sattar, seventy) plus सात (saat, seven), but like most Hindi numbers 11-99, सत्तानवे (sattaanve) is a fused, irregular form that must simply be memorized rather than derived. 2) It is easy to confuse with सत्तावन (sattaavan, fifty-seven) or सत्तासी (sattaasii, eighty-seven) since all three share the सत्ता- prefix; listen carefully for the final syllable to distinguish them.
