मसाला (masaalaa)

noun/adjective Primarily the everyday kitchen word for a spice or spice-blend (masaalaa), with a lively figurative extension to 'juicy/sensational content' -- gossip, drama, or scandal that spices up a story.
Sense 1 : spice / spice blend
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मिर्च-मसाला (mirch-masaalaa, often used for 'spices' collectively)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian kitchen has its own मसाला डब्बा (masaalaa dabbaa, 'spice box'), a round tin with little compartments -- ask any Delhi household and they'll show you theirs with pride. Blends like गरम मसाला (garam masaalaa) or चाट मसाला (chaat masaalaa) are practically household brand names by now.
Common Mistakes: 1) मसाला (masaalaa) usually refers to a mixed blend of spices (like गरम मसाला (garam masaalaa)) rather than a single spice; for one specific spice, speakers often just name it (जीरा (jiiraa), हल्दी (haldii)) rather than calling it मसाला (masaalaa) alone.

Sense 2 : spicy/sensational content, drama, masala (figurative)
noun, masculine Commonly used in phrases like मसाला फिल्म (masaalaa philm, 'a masala film' -- a film packed with drama, action, romance, and songs) or खबर में मसाला (khabar men masaalaa, 'spice in the news').
Synonyms: तड़का (tadkaa, informal, used for 'extra flavor/twist')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word behind the famous Bollywood genre label 'masala film (masaalaa film)' -- a film that mixes romance, comedy, action, and songs into one entertaining package, just like a good मसाला (masaalaa) mixes many flavors into one dish.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes miss that this figurative sense has entered English-language Indian journalism directly as 'masala' -- so it is not purely a translation exercise, it is a borrowed concept.

Sense 3 : spiced, masala-flavored (describing a dish or snack)
adjective Used attributively before a noun, as in मसाला डोसा (masaalaa dosaa, 'masala dosa') or मसाला चाय (masaalaa chaay, 'spiced tea') -- functions like an invariant compound-modifier.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मसाला चाय (masaalaa chaay) and मसाला डोसा (masaalaa dosaa) are two of the most-ordered items at any Delhi dhaba or south Indian restaurant -- knowing this word unlocks half the menu.
