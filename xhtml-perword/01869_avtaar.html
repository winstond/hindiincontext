अवतार (avtaar)

noun Primarily a religious/mythological term for a divine descent or incarnation, but also used loosely and figuratively for a person's 'form' or 'guise' in everyday and modern (even internet/gaming) contexts.
Sense 1 : divine incarnation (e.g. of Vishnu)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रूप (roop, form/incarnation, meaning scope), अंश (ansh, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a core concept in Hindu theology: Vishnu is said to take अवतार (avtaar) -- descend into the world in a form like Ram or Krishna -- whenever cosmic balance (dharm) is threatened. You will hear this word constantly around festivals like Ram Navami or Janmashtami, and in casual conversation about mythology or TV serials retelling these stories.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use अवतार (avtaar) as a generic word for 'god,' but it specifically means a *descent* or *incarnation of* a deity, not the deity in the abstract. 2) It is almost always used with a possessive or genitive link to the deity it belongs to, e.g. विष्णु का अवतार (vishnu kaa avtaar, 'Vishnu's incarnation'), not standing alone as a title.

(bhagvaan vishnu ne kaii avtaar lie hain।)

Sense 2 : figurative form, guise, persona (of a person or thing)
noun, masculine Used to describe someone appearing in a strikingly different role, look, or version of themselves, often with a tone of exaggeration or admiration.
Synonyms: रूप (roop, form), स्वरूप (svaroop, somewhat formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in everyday Hindi media: a film critic might say an actor appeared in a 'नए अवतार में' (nae avtaar men, 'in a new avatar') after a surprising role change, or a friend might joke that you're in your 'सुबह के अवतार' (subah ke avtaar, 'morning avatar') when you're grumpy before coffee. It borrows the weight of the religious sense to add a bit of drama to ordinary description.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative use always needs context (a role, a change, a comparison) -- used alone, अवतार (avtaar) defaults to the religious sense, so learners should pair it with a describing phrase like नए अवतार में (nae avtaar men, 'in a new avatar/form').
Sample Sentences:
फिल्म में हीरो एक नए अवतार में नज़र आया। In the film, the hero appeared in a new avatar.
(philm men hiiro ek nae avtaar men nazar aayaa।)

Sense 3 : digital/online avatar (profile icon or character)
noun, masculine A direct borrowing of the English tech term 'avatar,' reinforced by the fact that English itself borrowed this word from Sanskrit/Hindi originally.
Synonyms: प्रोफ़ाइल तस्वीर (profaail tasviir, profile picture, meaning scope)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense has come full circle: English borrowed अवतार (avtaar) from Sanskrit centuries ago, and now that English tech usage ('choose your avatar') has been re-borrowed back into casual Hindi, especially among younger, urban, internet-savvy speakers in Delhi-NCR.
