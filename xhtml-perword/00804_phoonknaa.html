फूँकना (phoonknaa)

verb Basic meaning is 'to blow' (air from the mouth, as on a fire or a musical instrument), with extended senses covering smoking and, colloquially, squandering money.
Sense 1 : to blow (air from the mouth)
verb, transitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture someone crouched over a chulha (choolhaa) blowing on the coals through a hollow pipe (फूँकनी, phoonknii) to get the fire going -- this is the classic mental image for फूँकना (phoonknaa). It is also the verb for blowing into a conch shell or bugle, so आग फूँकना (aag phoonknaa, 'to blow on the fire') and शंख फूँकना (shankh phoonknaa, 'to blow a conch') sit naturally side by side.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with बहना (bahnaa, 'to flow, blow' as wind does) -- फूँकना (phoonknaa) is specifically air blown from the mouth or lungs, like blowing on embers or into a pipe, whereas wind blowing outside uses चलना (chalnaa) (हवा चल रही है, havaa chal rahii hai, 'the wind is blowing').

Sense 2 : to smoke (a cigarette, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: पीना (piinaa, the everyday neutral verb for smoking)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense rides on the image of exhaling smoke like blown breath -- a mother scolding her son might say सिगरेट फूँकता रहता है (sigret phoonktaa rahtaa hai, 'he just keeps puffing away at cigarettes'), with a disapproving tone that पीना (piinaa) alone would not carry.
Common Mistakes: 1) The standard neutral verb for smoking a cigarette is पीना (piinaa) (सिगरेट पीना, sigret piinaa), not फूँकना (phoonknaa) -- using फूँकना (phoonknaa) for smoking has a mildly dismissive or disapproving edge, as if commenting on someone's bad habit, so learners should not swap it in as a plain synonym for पीना (piinaa).

Sense 3 : to squander, blow (money)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उड़ाना (udaanaa, very common near-synonym for squandering money), बर्बाद करना (barbaad karnaa, more general 'to waste/ruin')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The image is money going up in smoke, literally 'blown away' like breath -- a friend might tease you with पूरी सैलरी फूँक दी क्या? (poorii sailrii phoonk dii kyaa?, 'did you blow your whole salary or what?') after a big shopping spree, and the compound verb with देना (denaa) (फूँक देना, phoonk denaa) is very common here, underlining that it's all gone at once.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is colloquial and almost always used about money -- पूरी तनख्वाह फूँक दी (poorii tanakhvaah phoonk dii, 'blew the whole salary') -- so learners should not extend it to other resources like time, for which उड़ाना (udaanaa) or बर्बाद करना (barbaad karnaa) are used instead.
