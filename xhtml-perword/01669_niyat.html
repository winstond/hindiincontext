नियत (niyat)

adjective/noun Mainly used as an adjective meaning 'fixed/decided/appointed' (as in a fixed time or amount); also has a rarer noun use pointing toward 'intention.' Not to be confused with नियति (niyti, destiny/fate), a related-looking but distinct word.
Sense 1 : fixed, appointed, predetermined
adjective
Synonyms: निश्चित (nishchit), तय (tay), मुकर्रर (mukarrar, formal/legal-tinged)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet नियत (niyat) most often on official notices or in slightly formal speech -- a court notice giving a नियत तारीख़ (niyat taariikh, 'fixed/appointed date'), or a school circular naming a नियत समय (niyat samay, 'fixed time') for an event. In everyday conversation, तय (tay) does the same job with far less starch.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse नियत (niyat) with नियति (niyti, 'fate/destiny') because they look and sound alike -- नियत (niyat) is about something being set or scheduled, not about cosmic fate. 2) It tends to appear with formal or bureaucratic nouns like समय (niyat samay, 'fixed/appointed time') or तारीख़ (niyat taariikh, 'fixed date') rather than in casual chit-chat, where तय (tay) is far more common.
Sample Sentences:
अदालत ने सुनवाई के लिए नियत तारीख़ बता दी है। The court has announced the fixed date for the hearing.
(adaalat ne sunvaaii ke lie niyat taariikh bataa dii hai।)

Sense 2 : intention, resolve (formal/literary)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इरादा (iraadaa), नीयत (niiyat, the more common spelling/word for this sense)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This sense is really the territory of its cousin नीयत (niiyat) -- as in the common saying नीयत साफ़ होनी चाहिए (niiyat saaf honii chaahie, 'one's intentions should be clean/honest'). नियत (niyat) itself is rarely used this way in modern speech; it is included here mainly so you recognize the link if you meet it in older or more literary text.
Common Mistakes: 1) This noun sense overlaps heavily with नीयत (niiyat, 'intention, motive'), which is the far more common spelling and word choice in everyday speech; a learner is more likely to actually hear and need नीयत (niiyat) than this noun use of नियत (niyat).
