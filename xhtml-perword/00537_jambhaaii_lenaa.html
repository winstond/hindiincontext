जँभाई लेना (jambhaaii lenaa)

verb A fixed verb-noun combination meaning 'to yawn' -- जँभाई (jambhaaii) is the noun 'yawn' and लेना (lenaa) 'to take' supplies the action; the two words are almost never used apart in this sense.
Sense 1 : to yawn
verb, transitive Functions as a light-verb pairing: जँभाई (jambhaaii) is the object noun and लेना (lenaa) carries the conjugation, so the whole phrase inflects like an ordinary transitive verb with लेना (lenaa) at its head.
Synonyms: उबासी लेना (ubaasii lenaa, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this most in cozy, informal moments -- a mother telling a sleepy child to go to bed because वह जँभाई ले रहा है (vah jambhaaii le rahaa hai, 'he is yawning'), or someone stifling a yawn in a boring meeting. उबासी लेना (ubaasii lenaa) is the common everyday alternative and is heard just as often in Delhi speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to say just जँभाई (jambhaaii) as if it were a verb on its own -- you always need लेना (lenaa) (or आना (aanaa) in the related sense) to complete the action. 2) Because जँभाई (jambhaaii) is grammatically the object, the verb लेना (lenaa) takes ने-marking on the subject in the perfective, e.g. उसने जँभाई ली (usne jambhaaii lii, 'he/she yawned') -- easy to forget since 'yawn' feels intransitive in English.
Sample Sentences:
वह बार-बार जँभाई ले रहा है, उसे नींद आ रही है। He keeps yawning again and again, he's feeling sleepy.
(vah baar-baar jambhaaii le rahaa hai, use niind aa rahii hai।)
