बाई तरफ़ (baaii taraf)

adverb A fixed directional phrase meaning 'on/to the left side' (baaii taraf); used constantly for giving directions in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : on the left side, to the left
adverb बाई (baaii) is the oblique feminine form of बायाँ (baayaan, 'left') agreeing with तरफ़ (taraf, 'side/direction'), which is feminine; the phrase as a whole functions as a fixed adverbial of direction.
Synonyms: बाईं ओर (baaiin or, slightly more formal), बाएँ (baaen, one-word variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this everywhere from auto-rickshaw drivers and shopkeepers giving directions: बाई तरफ़ मुड़ जाना (baaii taraf mud jaanaa, 'turn to the left'). Its mirror partner is दाई तरफ़ (daaii taraf, 'to the right') -- learning them as a pair makes both stick faster.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say बाया तरफ़ (baayaa taraf) using the masculine form of बायाँ (baayaan) instead of the required feminine oblique बाई (baaii), since तरफ़ (taraf) is feminine. 2) The phrase is used with मुड़ना (mudnaa, 'to turn') or है (hai) but not usually with a verb like जाना (jaanaa) alone -- you'd say बाई तरफ़ जाओ (baaii taraf jaao, 'go to the left') rather than treating it as a destination noun phrase with को (ko).
Sample Sentences:
बाई तरफ़ मुड़ जाना। Turn to the left.
(baaii taraf mud jaanaa।)

आगे चौराहे से बाई तरफ़ मुड़ जाना। Turn left from the intersection ahead.
(aage chauraahe se baaii taraf mud jaanaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
मेरा घर बाई तरफ़ है। My house is on the left.
(meraa ghar baaii taraf hai।)

(vah hameshaa baaii taraf chaltaa hai।)

(gaadii baaii taraf mud rahii thii jab achaanak brek lage।)

(dukaan vaale ne baaii taraf ishaaraa kiyaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
क्या आप कृपया बाई तरफ़ रुक सकते हैं? Could you please stop on the left?
(kyaa aap kripyaa baaii taraf ruk sakte hain?)

(agar vah baaii taraf mudtaa, to siidhaa steshan pahunch jaataa।)

बोर्ड पर लिखा था कि बाई तरफ़ से जाएँ। The board said to go from the left side.
(bord par likhaa thaa ki baaii taraf se jaaen।)

(oto vaale bhaaii, baaii taraf lagaa do।)
