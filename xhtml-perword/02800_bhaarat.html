भारत (bhaarat)

noun The proper name for India, used in formal, official, and patriotic contexts; the everyday word for the country in casual speech is often इंडिया (indiyaa) or the same word भारत (bhaarat) itself, depending on register.
Sense 1 : India (the country)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: इंडिया (indiyaa, English loanword, everyday/casual), हिंदुस्तान (hindustaan, warm/patriotic tone)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: भारत (bhaarat) is the name you will hear during cricket matches (भारत जीत गया, bhaarat jiit gayaa, 'India won'), in the national anthem's spirit, and on official documents -- it carries a slightly more rooted, emotional charge than the casual इंडिया (indiyaa), which feels more neutral or global. The name traces back to the ancient king Bharata (bharat) of Indian epic tradition, so it carries a deep civilizational resonance that 'India' does not carry in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume भारत (bhaarat) is only formal/official and इंडिया (indiyaa) is the 'real' spoken word -- in fact भारत (bhaarat) is used constantly in speech too, especially in news, sports commentary, and patriotic contexts like भारत माता की जय (bhaarat maataa kii jay, 'Victory to Mother India'). 2) Do not confuse with भारतीय (bhaartiiy), which is the adjective/noun meaning 'Indian' (the person or the adjective), not the country itself.
