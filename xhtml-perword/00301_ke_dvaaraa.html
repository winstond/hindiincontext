के द्वारा (ke dvaaraa)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'by' or 'through' -- marks the agent or means/instrument of an action, most common in formal, written, and passive constructions. Inline related to द्वारा (dvaaraa), its shorter form used the same way.
Sense 1 : by (agent of an action, especially passive)
postposition Follows the oblique form of the noun it governs, e.g. महात्मा गांधी के द्वारा (mahaatmaa gaandhii ke dvaaraa, 'by Mahatma Gandhi'). Very frequent in passive-voice sentences (X के द्वारा किया गया, 'done by X') and in formal writing like notices, news, and official documents.
Synonyms: द्वारा (dvaaraa, shorter variant, more common)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see के द्वारा (ke dvaaraa) constantly on government forms, courier slips, and formal letters -- for instance 'भेजने वाले के द्वारा' (bhejne vaale ke dvaaraa, 'by the sender') -- because it has that official, written-register flavor English speakers might associate with 'via' or 'per' in bureaucratic English. In spoken Hindi among friends and family, से (se) or plain द्वारा (dvaaraa) often takes over this job more casually.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use से (se) where के द्वारा (ke dvaaraa) is more natural for expressing 'by [a person]' in passive or formal contexts -- से leans toward instrument/means ('with, using') while के द्वारा leans toward the doer or formal channel. 2) In everyday speech, native speakers usually just say द्वारा (dvaaraa) alone rather than the fuller के द्वारा, which can sound slightly bookish or bureaucratic in casual conversation.

(yah patr pradhaanmantrii ke dvaaraa likhaa gayaa hai।)

Sense 2 : through, by means of (channel or medium)
postposition Used to mark a medium or channel through which something happens, e.g. समाचार पत्र के द्वारा (samaachaar patr ke dvaaraa, 'through the newspaper').
Synonyms: से (se), के ज़रिये (ke zariye)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense shows up often in news reports and announcements -- 'रेडियो के द्वारा सूचना दी गई' (rediyo ke dvaaraa soochnaa dii gaii, 'information was given through the radio') -- carrying that same formal, official register as the agent sense above, since both come from the same root idea of द्वारा (dvaaraa) as a 'gateway' or 'means.'

यह सूचना रेडियो के द्वारा दी गई। This information was given through the radio.
(yah soochnaa rediyo ke dvaaraa dii gaii।)
