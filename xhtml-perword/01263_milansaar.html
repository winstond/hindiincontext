मिलनसार (milansaar)

adjective A single-sense adjective describing a person who is warm and easy to get along with -- 'sociable, friendly, affable.'
Sense 1 : sociable, friendly, easy to get along with
adjective
Synonyms: मेलजोल वाला (meljol vaalaa), खुशमिज़ाज (khushmizaaj), मित्रवत (mitrvat, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word literally builds on मिलना (milnaa, 'to meet') plus the agentive-ish suffix सार (saar), so its core image is someone who is naturally good 'at meeting people' -- a very apt way to praise the uncle at a wedding who chats easily with everyone from the caterer to the chief guest. It is a warm, everyday compliment, often paired with हंसमुख (hansmukh, 'cheerful-faced') when describing someone's overall pleasant nature.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this for describing a place or event as 'friendly' -- it applies only to people's personalities, not atmospheres; for a warm gathering, use कुछ और, not मिलनसार (milansaar). 2) It describes a general, stable trait of character, not a one-off friendly act -- for 'he was nice to me yesterday,' Hindi speakers reach for अच्छा व्यवहार किया (achchhaa vyavahaar kiyaa, 'behaved nicely') rather than मिलनसार (milansaar).
