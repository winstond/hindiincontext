छीलना (chhiilnaa)

verb A concrete kitchen/craft verb meaning to peel or pare off an outer layer -- vegetables, fruit, skin, wood, pencils -- with a knife or similar tool. One core sense, used very literally.
Sense 1 : to peel, pare, scrape off the outer skin/layer
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उतारना (utaarnaa, 'skin/layer removed', broader use)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a mother in the kitchen with a thaali of आलू (aaloo) and a छुरी (chhurii, knife), छील-छील (chhiil-chhiil) कर सब्ज़ी बनाना -- peeling vegetables is one of the most everyday domestic images this word evokes. It also extends naturally to pencils (पेंसिल छीलना, pensil chhiilnaa, 'to sharpen a pencil' in casual speech) and to skinning fruit like आम (aam) or संतरा (santraa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for खुरचना (khurachnaa) or उतारना (utaarnaa) when छीलना (chhiilnaa) is the natural word specifically for peeling skins -- potatoes, apples, cucumbers -- with a knife or peeler. 2) The object taking ने-marking in the perfective (उसने आलू छीले, usne aaloo chhiile, 'she peeled the potatoes') is standard transitive behavior, not an exception -- just flagging it since the peeled object agrees with the verb.
