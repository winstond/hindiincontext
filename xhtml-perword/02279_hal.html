हल (hal)

noun Two unrelated words share this spelling: हल (hal) meaning 'solution' (to a problem) and हल (hal) meaning 'plow' (the farming tool).
Sense 1 : solution, answer (to a problem)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: समाधान (samaadhaan), उपाय (upaay, can also mean 'remedy/measure')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly around exams and homework -- सवाल का हल (savaal kaa hal, 'the answer to the question') is classic classroom language in India. Paired with करना (karnaa) it becomes the everyday verb for 'to solve' any problem, mathematical or otherwise.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse हल (hal, solution) with हल करना (hal karnaa, to solve) -- हल alone is the noun, and करना (karnaa) must be added to make it a verb phrase meaning 'to solve.' 2) Do not confuse with हल (hal, plow), an unrelated homonym with a completely different meaning.

Sense 2 : plow (farming implement)
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: हल (hal) is the traditional ox-drawn plow still seen in rural India, and it carries strong symbolic weight in farmer iconography and in words like हलवाहा (halvaahaa, 'ploughman'). It is far less common in everyday urban conversation than the 'solution' sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a separate word from हल (hal, solution) despite identical spelling -- context (farming vs. problem-solving) is the only way to tell them apart, since they are never confused by native speakers but can trip up learners relying on rote vocabulary lists.
