गुज़रना (guzarnaa)

verb An intransitive verb built around passage -- of time, of a person through a place, or of a life event -- with a related figurative sense of 'passing away' (dying) and a construction for 'getting by / making do with.' Also spelled गुजरना (gujarnaa) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : to pass (time)
verb, intransitive Subject is typically time-related (साल (saal), वक़्त (vaqt), दिन (din)); often paired with पर (par) marking whom the time passed for, e.g. मुझ पर (mujh par, on me).
Synonyms: बीतना (biitnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in reflective speech -- वो दिन कैसे गुज़रे (vo din kaise guzre, how those days passed) is the kind of line an elder says while looking back on hard times. It pairs naturally with पर (par) to say what someone went through, not just that time moved.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for होना (honaa) to say time 'passed,' but गुज़रना (guzarnaa) or बीतना (biitnaa) is the natural native choice for elapsing time. 2) When describing a hard experience one lived through, native speakers say वक़्त गुज़रना (vaqt guzarnaa) rather than a literal 'happen to me' construction.
Sample Sentences:
वो दिन कैसे गुज़रे, मुझे याद भी नहीं करना। How those days passed, I don't even want to remember.
(vo din kaise guzre, mujhe yaad bhii nahiin karnaa।)

Sense 2 : to pass through / by (a place)
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the route or place: बाज़ार से गुज़रना (baazaar se guzarnaa, to pass through the market).
Synonyms: निकलना (nikalnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very physical and concrete -- you'll hear this from auto drivers and people giving directions: यहाँ से गुज़रो (yahaan se guzro, pass through here). It sits alongside निकलना (nikalnaa) but गुज़रना (guzarnaa) has a slightly more deliberate, unhurried feel, like moving through rather than darting out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try को (ko) for the place passed through; गुज़रना (guzarnaa) wants से (se) here, not को (ko).

Sense 3 : to get by, manage, make do
verb, intransitive Common in the frame X के बिना गुज़रना (X ke binaa guzarnaa, to get by without X) or X पर गुज़रना (X par guzarnaa, to get by on X).
Synonyms: काम चलाना (kaam chalaanaa, compound verb phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in everyday complaints about money -- इतने में गुज़ारा नहीं होता (itne men guzaaraa nahiin hotaa, one can't get by on this much) -- which leans on the related noun गुज़ारा (guzaaraa, livelihood/subsistence), a very common word in its own right.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is easy to miss because it looks identical in form to the 'pass through' sense -- context (an abstract resource like पैसे (paise) or समय (samay) rather than a physical place) is the only signal.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 4 : to pass away, die (euphemistic)
verb, intransitive Almost always appears as the compound गुज़र जाना (guzar jaanaa) rather than the bare verb in this sense.
Synonyms: गुज़र जाना (guzar jaanaa, compound verb form), मरना (marnaa), चल बसना (chal basnaa, euphemistic)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the polite, softened way to say someone died -- much like English 'passed away.' You will hear वो गुज़र गए (vo guzar gae, he/she passed away) at condolence visits, where blunt मर गए (mar gae) would feel harsh.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners who only know the 'pass by' sense may miss this euphemistic reading entirely when it comes up in condolence contexts; listen for गुज़र गए (guzar gae) used respectfully about a person who has died.

(unke pitaajii pichhle mahiine guzar gae।)
