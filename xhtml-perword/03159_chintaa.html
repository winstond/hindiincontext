चिंता (chintaa)

noun A common feminine noun meaning worry, anxiety, or concern; also sometimes spelled with a nuqta-less variant चिन्ता (chintaa).
Sense 1 : worry, anxiety
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फ़िक्र (fikr), परेशानी (pareshaanii), घबराहट (ghabraahat, more physical unease)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: चिंता मत करो (chintaa mat karo, 'don't worry') is one of the most-heard reassurances in daily Hindi, said by everyone from mothers to shopkeepers. Notice the dative-subject frame: मुझे चिंता है (mujhe chintaa hai, 'I have worry / I am worried') treats worry as something that exists 'to me' rather than something 'I do,' which mirrors how Hindi often frames emotional and physical states.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make चिंता (chintaa) the subject of a verb like सोचना (sochnaa, 'to think'); instead Hindi typically pairs it with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) -- चिंता होना (chintaa honaa, 'to feel worry [happen to someone]') uses a को (ko)-marked experiencer, while चिंता करना (chintaa karnaa) means 'to worry [about something]' and takes की (kii) before the object of worry. 2) Do not confuse चिंता (chintaa) with चिता (chitaa), which means 'funeral pyre' -- a single vowel-length slip changes the word entirely.
