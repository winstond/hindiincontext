लिपटाना (liptaanaa)

verb A causative-feeling transitive verb meaning to wrap something around something else, or to make one thing embrace/cling to another; closely related to the intransitive लिपटना (lipatnaa, 'to cling/wrap oneself around'), which is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to wrap, coil, or wind something around something else
verb, transitive Takes an object plus a से (se) or पर (par) phrase for what is wrapped around what, e.g. कपड़े को दुपट्टे से लिपटाना (kapde ko dupatte se liptaanaa, 'to wrap the cloth with a scarf').
Synonyms: लपेटना (lapetnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of wrapping a baby snugly in a shawl -- बच्चे को शॉल में लिपटाना (bachche ko shol men liptaanaa, 'to wrap the baby in a shawl') -- this word carries a tender, careful sense of enclosing something, unlike the more mechanical लपेटना (lapetnaa) used for parcels or wires.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with लिपटना (lipatnaa) -- लिपटाना (liptaanaa) is the transitive 'to wrap something (around something)', while लिपटना (lipatnaa) is intransitive 'to cling/be wrapped around'. 2) The near-synonym लपेटना (lapetnaa) is far more common in everyday speech for wrapping objects like cloth or paper; लिपटाना (liptaanaa) leans slightly more toward wrapping around a person or body.

Sense 2 : to make someone embrace/cling to someone (causative of clinging)
verb, transitive Used when one person is made to embrace or press against another, e.g. माँ ने बच्चे को खुद से लिपटाया (maan ne bachche ko khud se liptaayaa, 'the mother pressed the child close to herself').
Synonyms: चिपटाना (chiptaanaa, similar sense of pressing close)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense shows up often in emotional or dramatic contexts -- a mother pulling a frightened child close, or two people embracing tightly in a moment of relief or grief -- it has a slightly more intense, physical flavor than the everyday गले लगाना (gale lagaanaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for गले लगाना (gale lagaanaa) for 'to hug' -- that is the standard everyday verb for hugging; लिपटाना (liptaanaa) is used more for a tight, clinging embrace or for pulling someone close, and appears less often in casual conversation.
