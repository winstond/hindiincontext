शोला (sholaa)

noun A masculine noun meaning 'flame' or 'blaze' -- mostly used in poetic, dramatic, or figurative speech rather than everyday small talk about fire.
Sense 1 : flame, blaze (literal fire)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: लपट (lapat), ज्वाला (jvaalaa, literary), आग (aag, everyday word for fire itself)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet शोला (sholaa) far more often in film lyrics, poetry, and dramatic narration than in kitchen conversation -- it carries a charge of intensity that plain आग (aag) doesn't.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शोला (sholaa) when they just mean 'fire' in casual speech -- for that, आग (aag) is the everyday word; शोला (sholaa) specifically evokes a leaping flame or blaze, and shows up more in vivid description than in requests like 'light the stove.'
Sample Sentences:
जंगल में आग लगी और चारों तरफ शोले उठने लगे। A fire broke out in the forest and flames began rising all around.
(jangal men aag lagii aur chaaron taraph shole uthne lage।)

(dekho, choolhe se sholaa nikal rahaa hai, zaraa gais kam karo।)

Sense 2 : figurative: burning passion, fury, or intense emotion
noun, masculine Used in phrases like दिल में शोला भड़कना (dil men sholaa bhadaknaa, 'a fire to blaze in one's heart') to describe anger, love, or vengeance flaring up.
Synonyms: आग (aag, used similarly in idioms of anger), जोश (josh, passion/zeal, broader emotional sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi loves describing intense feeling as fire -- शोला (sholaa) here works like English 'blaze' or 'flame' of passion, often heard in dramatic dialogue or song when someone's anger or love is described as uncontainable.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is almost always paired with a verb of flaring or burning -- भड़कना (bhadaknaa) or उठना (uthnaa) -- learners sometimes drop शोला (sholaa) into a sentence alone expecting it to carry the emotional meaning by itself.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी आँखों में गुस्से का शोला भड़क उठा। A blaze of anger flared up in his eyes.
(uskii aankhon men gusse kaa sholaa bhadak uthaa।)
