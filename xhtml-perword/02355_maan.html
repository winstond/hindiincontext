माँ (maan)

noun The everyday word for 'mother', used both as a term of address and as a common noun. One core meaning; the entry below also covers its extended use in phrases like मातृभूमि-style compounds and endearing address forms.
Sense 1 : mother
noun, feminine
Synonyms: माता (maataa, formal), मम्मी (mammii, colloquial), अम्मा (ammaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: माँ (maan) carries real warmth in Hindi -- it's the word a grown adult still uses, not a childish term that gets replaced by something more 'formal' later in life, unlike some registers of English 'mommy' vs. 'mother'. In many households you'll hear a mix of माँ (maan) and मम्मी (mammii) depending on the family's linguistic background, with मम्मी (mammii) feeling a touch more urban/English-influenced.
Common Mistakes: 1) माँ (maan) is used directly as a term of address without any vocative marker -- you simply call out माँ! (maan) the way you'd say 'Mom!' in English, no अरे (are) needed. 2) Learners sometimes default to मेरी माँ (merii maan) even when talking *to* her; in direct address Hindi speakers usually just say माँ (maan) alone, dropping the possessive, much as English speakers say 'Mom' rather than 'my mom' when addressing her.
