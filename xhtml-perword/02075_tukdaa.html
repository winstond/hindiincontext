टुकड़ा (tukdaa)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'piece' or 'bit' of something, with a common extended sense referring dismissively to a small plot of land or a scrap/fragment in an abstract sense.
Sense 1 : a piece or bit of something
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हिस्सा (hissaa), खंड (khand, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in the kitchen -- ब्रेड का टुकड़ा (bred kaa tukdaa, 'a piece of bread'), टुकड़े करना (tukde karnaa, 'to cut into pieces') -- it's the default word Delhi cooks reach for when chopping vegetables, cake, or paper alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that टुकड़ा (tukdaa) takes का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) to link it to what it's a piece of, e.g. रोटी का टुकड़ा (rotii kaa tukdaa, 'a piece of bread') -- not रोटी टुकड़ा.

Sense 2 : a small plot of land
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्लॉट (plot)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday property talk around Delhi-NCR, ज़मीन का टुकड़ा (zamiin kaa tukdaa, 'a piece of land') is the normal way to describe a small parcel, especially in casual conversation about buying or inheriting land.

Sense 3 : a fragment or scrap, used figuratively
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अंश (ansh, literary)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the same word stretched metaphorically -- ज़िंदगी का टुकड़ा (zindgii kaa tukdaa) or यादों का टुकड़ा (yaadon kaa tukdaa) treat memories or life itself as something that can be broken into pieces, a very natural extension for Hindi speakers.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense (e.g. ज़िंदगी का एक टुकड़ा (zindgii kaa ek tukdaa, 'a fragment/slice of life')) is less common in casual speech than the literal 'piece' sense, so learners may overuse it where a native speaker would just say हिस्सा (hissaa).
Sample Sentences:
ये तस्वीर मेरी ज़िंदगी का एक टुकड़ा है। This photo is a fragment of my life.
(ye tasviir merii zindgii kaa ek tukdaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
