लड़कपन (ladakpan)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'boyhood/childishness' -- built from लड़का (ladkaa, 'boy') plus the abstract-noun suffix -पन (pan).
Sense 1 : boyhood / boyish immaturity
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बचपना (bachapnaa, childish behavior), नादानी (naadaanii, naivete/foolishness), बचपन (bachpan, meaning-scope: refers to childhood generally, not boyish immaturity specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear elders say something like यह लड़कपन छोड़ो (yah ladakpan chhodo, 'drop this childish behavior') to a young man who is being impulsive or silly, gently urging him toward maturity. It carries a mix of affection and mild reproach -- unlike बचपन (bachpan), which is nostalgic and neutral, लड़कपन (ladakpan) is often a light scolding.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with बचपन (bachpan) -- बचपन (bachpan) is the neutral word for 'childhood' as a life-stage, while लड़कपन (ladakpan) leans toward 'boyish/immature behavior,' often used about someone (even an adult) acting childishly. 2) Despite deriving from लड़का (ladkaa, 'boy'), it is grammatically masculine as an abstract noun and is not used to mean 'the state of literally being a boy' in a formal biological sense -- it is about temperament and conduct.

यह लड़कपन छोड़ो और थोड़ी जिम्मेदारी लो। Drop this childish behavior and take on some responsibility.
(yah ladakpan chhodo aur thodii jimmedaarii lo।)
