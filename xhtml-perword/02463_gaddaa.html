गद्दा (gaddaa)

noun A concrete noun for a mattress or thick cushioned pad. It also has a colloquial extended sense for a soft, thick layer of something (like snow or fat), used more casually.
Sense 1 : mattress
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बिस्तर (bistar, covers whole bedding set, not just the mattress), तोशक (toshak, thinner traditional cotton mattress)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian homes you will hear गद्दा (gaddaa) used for the thick mattress laid over a wooden frame (तख्त, takht) or directly on the floor, especially for guests during festivals when extra गद्दे (gadde) get pulled out of storage.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use बिस्तर (bistar) when they specifically mean the mattress itself; बिस्तर (bistar) more properly covers the whole bedding setup (mattress, sheet, pillow), while गद्दा (gaddaa) is just the thick cushioned pad you sleep on.

Sense 2 : thick cushioned pad or layer (extended/figurative use)
noun, masculine Used for any thick, soft, mattress-like layer, such as a padded seat cushion or a thick layer of snow or fat, by extension from the primary sense.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this extended use when someone describes a thick layer of snow as बर्फ़ का गद्दा (barf kaa gaddaa, 'a mattress of snow') or jokes about someone's गद्दा (gaddaa) of extra weight around the belly -- it borrows the image of the soft, thick mattress to describe any soft bulk.
