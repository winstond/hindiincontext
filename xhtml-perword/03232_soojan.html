सूजन (soojan)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning swelling/inflammation of a body part; related to the verb सूजना (soojnaa, to swell).
Sense 1 : swelling, inflammation
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वरम (varam, rare, medical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear at home or at the doctor's clinic in Delhi when someone points to a swollen ankle or a puffy eye and says पैर में सूजन आ गई है (pair men soojan aa gaii hai, 'swelling has come in the foot') -- note how सूजन (soojan) is treated as something that 'arrives' (आना, aanaa), just like दर्द (dard) or बुखार (bukhaar), reflecting the common Hindi pattern of framing bodily afflictions as visitors rather than states you are in.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use the verb सूजना (soojnaa) where the noun is needed, or vice versa -- e.g. साफ़ कहना है कि पैर में सूजन है (pair men soojan hai, 'there is swelling in the foot') uses the noun with होना (honaa), not the verb. 2) The body part that is swollen is marked with में (men) ('in the foot/hand') rather than का (kaa), which learners sometimes default to by analogy with possession.
