उचित (uchit)

adjective A formal/semi-formal adjective meaning 'proper, appropriate, right' -- used to judge whether an action, amount, or decision fits the situation.
Sense 1 : proper, appropriate, fitting
adjective Often used with होना (honaa) in the pattern 'X उचित है' ('X is appropriate'), or with लगना (lagnaa) to mean 'X seems appropriate (to someone)'.
Synonyms: ठीक (thiik), सही (sahii), मुनासिब (munaasib), वाजिब (vaajib, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear उचित (uchit) a lot in official notices, school lectures, and parental advice -- it has a slightly lecturing, 'this is the right thing to do' flavor. In daily bazaar chat about prices or food, ठीक (thiik) or सही (sahii) feels more natural; उचित (uchit) shows up more when someone is being formally reasonable or moralistic, like a teacher or a government form.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ठीक (thiik) in every context, but उचित (uchit) carries a more formal, judgment-based tone -- it is common in advice, official writing, and moral reasoning ('क्या यह उचित है?' (kyaa yah uchit hai?, 'is this appropriate?')) rather than casual chat about whether food tastes fine. 2) उचित (uchit) is invariant -- learners sometimes try to give it gender/number agreement as if it were a variable adjective like अच्छा (achchhaa).
Sample Sentences:
यह फैसला बिल्कुल उचित है। This decision is completely appropriate.
(yah phaislaa bilkul uchit hai।)
