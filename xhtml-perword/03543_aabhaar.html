आभार (aabhaar)

noun A formal, single-sense noun meaning 'gratitude, thanks' -- the word you reach for in writing and speeches rather than casual conversation.
Sense 1 : gratitude, thanks
noun, masculine Commonly used in the fixed phrase आभार व्यक्त करना (aabhaar vyakt karnaa, 'to express gratitude') or आभारी होना (aabhaarii honaa, 'to be grateful'); आभार itself is rarely said alone as a casual 'thanks.'
Synonyms: धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad), शुक्रिया (shukriyaa), कृतज्ञता (kritagytaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word at the end of a wedding invitation, a school function, or a formal speech, often in the line सभी का आभार (sabhii kaa aabhaar, 'thanks to everyone'), read out by an emcee. Its adjective cousin आभारी (aabhaarii, 'grateful') is the more commonly spoken form, as in मैं आपका आभारी हूँ (main aapkaa aabhaarii hoon, 'I am grateful to you').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use आभार (aabhaar) where शुक्रिया (shukriyaa) or धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) would sound more natural in everyday speech -- आभार (aabhaar) belongs to writing, speeches, and formal announcements, not casual chit-chat. 2) It is not used as a direct one-word exclamation like 'thanks!' the way शुक्रिया (shukriyaa) is; it needs a verb like व्यक्त करना (vyakt karnaa) or the adjective आभारी (aabhaarii) to feel natural in a sentence.
Sample Sentences:
कार्यक्रम के अंत में मुख्य अतिथि ने सभी का आभार व्यक्त किया। At the end of the program, the chief guest expressed gratitude to everyone.
(kaaryakram ke ant men mukhy atithi ne sabhii kaa aabhaar vyakt kiyaa।)
