चोरी (chorii)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'theft/stealing,' used both literally (a stolen object/act of stealing) and inside a very common idiom for sneaking around or doing something secretly. Related to चोर (chor, 'thief').
Sense 1 : theft, stealing (the act or crime)
noun, feminine Functions as the object of करना (karnaa) in the standard verb phrase चोरी करना (chorii karnaa, 'to steal'), where चोरी stays uninflected and करना carries the tense/agreement.
Synonyms: चोरी करना (chorii karnaa, the verb phrase 'to steal'), डकैती (dakaitii, dacoity/armed robbery, not a true synonym but nearby in meaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi kid has heard a parent say किसने चोरी की? (kisne chorii kii?, 'who stole it?') over a missing sweet or a few rupees from a purse -- it is the go-to word for household-scale theft as much as for a legal crime reported in the news.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to conjugate चोरी (chorii) itself as if it were the verb; remember it is the noun and करना (karnaa) does the conjugating in चोरी करना (chorii karnaa). 2) चोरी (chorii) names the act/crime; the person is चोर (chor), not चोरी.

Sense 2 : a stolen item (the thing that was stolen)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: माल (maal, colloquial, 'stolen goods/loot')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used when the focus shifts from the act to the object itself, as in yah चोरी का सामान है (yah chorii kaa saamaan hai, 'this is stolen goods') -- the phrase चोरी का (chorii kaa) acts almost like an adjective meaning 'stolen,' modifying whatever noun follows.

Sense 3 : doing something secretly, on the sly (figurative, as in चोरी-चोरी or चोरी से)
noun, feminine Common as the adverbial phrase चोरी से (chorii se, 'secretly, on the sly') and in the doubled form चोरी-चोरी (chorii-chorii), both describing an action done without others' knowledge, not necessarily theft.
Synonyms: छुप-छुप कर (chhup-chhup kar, on the sly), गुपचुप (gupchup, quietly/secretly)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative sense powers the classic scolding chori-chori chupke-chupke (chorii-chorii chhupke-chhupke, 'secretly, on the sly') -- also a beloved Bollywood film title -- capturing that very Indian family drama of doing something behind someone's back, whether it's eating a snack before dinner or meeting someone the family disapproves of.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners can miss that चोरी से (chorii se) often has nothing to do with stealing -- मैंने चोरी से देखा (mainne chorii se dekhaa, 'I peeked secretly') is about sneaking a look, not taking anything.
Sample Sentences:

(usne chorii se meraa phon uthaakar dekh liyaa।)
