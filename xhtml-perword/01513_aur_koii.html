और कोई (aur koii)

pronoun A pronoun phrase meaning 'anyone/anything else, someone/something else' -- combines और (aur, 'more/else') with कोई (koii, 'someone/any'). Used to ask about or refer to an additional or alternative person/thing.
Sense 1 : someone else, anyone else (referring to a person)
pronoun Word order with कोई (koii) is flexible -- और कोई (aur koii) and कोई और (koii aur) are both heard, though कोई और (koii aur) is slightly more common in casual speech.
Synonyms: कोई और (koii aur, word order variant, equally common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in shops and offices: और कोई चाहिए? (aur koii chaahie, 'do you need anything/anyone else?') is the standard follow-up after handing over what was asked for -- it's the Hindi equivalent of a shopkeeper's 'anything else?'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often assume only one word order is correct, but both और कोई (aur koii) and कोई और (koii aur) are natural -- swapping them doesn't change the meaning. 2) Don't confuse this with कोई और नहीं (koii aur nahiin, 'no one else'), which needs the negative नहीं (nahiin) added explicitly.

Sense 2 : anything else, something else (referring to a thing)
pronoun When the referent is clearly inanimate, कुछ और (kuchh aur) is often preferred over और कोई (aur koii) -- कोई (koii) leans toward animate/person reference by default, so context or an explicit noun usually clarifies which is meant.
Synonyms: कोई और (koii aur, word order variant, equally common), कुछ और (kuchh aur, used specifically for things, not people)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The line between the 'person' and 'thing' senses isn't rigid -- और कोई किताब है? (aur koii kitaab hai, 'is there any other book?') shows कोई (koii) comfortably modifying a noun to mean 'other/another' when a specific item is named.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to और कोई (aur koii) for objects when कुछ और (kuchh aur) is the more natural native choice -- e.g. 'anything else to eat?' is more idiomatically कुछ और खाना है? (kuchh aur khaanaa hai, 'is there anything else to eat?') rather than और कोई खाना है? (aur koii khaanaa hai).
