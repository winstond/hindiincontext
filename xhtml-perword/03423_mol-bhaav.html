मोल-भाव (mol-bhaav)

noun A single-sense noun meaning bargaining or haggling over price -- the back-and-forth negotiation you do at a market stall.
Sense 1 : bargaining, haggling over price
noun, masculine Almost always paired with the verb करना (karnaa) -- मोल-भाव करना (mol-bhaav karnaa, 'to haggle') -- since the noun itself doesn't act, it's the thing you 'do'.
Synonyms: मोलतोल (moltol, one-word variant), भाव-ताव (bhaav-taav)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Bargaining is practically a social ritual in Delhi's markets like Sarojini Nagar or Lajpat Nagar -- shopkeepers expect you to push back on the first price, and walking away without any मोल-भाव (mol-bhaav) can even seem a little naive. Elders often say aap ko mol-bhaav karna aana chahiye as basic life-skill advice, half in jest, half seriously.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use मोल-भाव (mol-bhaav) as a verb directly; it needs करना (karnaa) attached, e.g. मोल-भाव किया (mol-bhaav kiyaa, 'bargained') not a bare conjugated form. 2) Don't confuse with भाव (bhaav) alone, which just means 'price/rate' or 'sentiment' -- मोल-भाव (mol-bhaav) specifically means the *act* of negotiating that price.
