उपस्थिति (upasthiti)

noun A formal noun meaning 'presence' or 'attendance' -- the physical fact of being at a place, or the act of being counted as present (as in a class or meeting).
Sense 1 : attendance (at school, work, a meeting)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: हाज़िरी (haazirii, everyday word used in schools/offices)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on the attendance register (उपस्थिति पंजिका, upasthiti panjikaa) in Indian schools and government offices -- it is the official, printed-form version of हाज़िरी (haazirii), which is what a teacher actually calls out loud in the classroom.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for हाज़िरी (haazirii) in casual talk about school/office attendance; उपस्थिति (upasthiti) is the word you will see written on forms, notices, and registers -- it sounds a notch more formal in speech. 2) Common set phrase: उपस्थिति दर्ज करना (upasthiti darj karnaa, 'to record attendance') -- दर्ज (darj) is the verb of choice here, not लिखना (likhnaa).

Sense 2 : presence (of a person or thing at a place)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मौजूदगी (maujoodgii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear at the start of a formal event -- 'माननीय अतिथियों की उपस्थिति में' (maanniiy atithiyon kii upasthiti men, 'in the presence of the honored guests') -- the kind of line an emcee reads out before a school function or political gathering.
Common Mistakes: 1) उपस्थिति (upasthiti) and मौजूदगी (maujoodgii) are close to interchangeable for 'presence,' but उपस्थिति (upasthiti) leans slightly more formal/written, so it turns up more often in speeches, reports, and invitations (e.g. मुख्यमंत्री की उपस्थिति में, mukhymantrii kii upasthiti men, 'in the presence of the chief minister').
Sample Sentences:
माननीय अतिथियों की उपस्थिति में कार्यक्रम शुरू हुआ। The program began in the presence of the honored guests.
(maanniiy atithiyon kii upasthiti men kaaryakram shuroo huaa।)
