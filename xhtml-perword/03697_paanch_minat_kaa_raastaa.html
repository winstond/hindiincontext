पाँच मिनट का रास्ता (paanch minat kaa raastaa)

phrase A common way to describe distance in terms of time rather than space -- literally 'a five-minute path/way,' meaning something is a five-minute walk (or short trip) away. Inline Hindi: पाँच मिनट का रास्ता (paanch minat kaa raastaa).
Sense 1 : a short distance measured in time (about five minutes away)
phrase The template है x का रास्ता (x kaa raastaa) is fully productive -- you can swap in any duration or distance: दस मिनट का रास्ता (das minat kaa raastaa, 'a ten-minute way'), दो किलोमीटर का रास्ता (do kilomiitar kaa raastaa, 'a two-kilometer stretch'). पाँच मिनट का रास्ता (paanch minat kaa raastaa) is simply the most common fixed instance learners hear.
Synonyms: दो कदम की दूरी (do kadam kii doorii, 'just a couple of steps away'), पास ही है (paas hii hai, 'it's right nearby')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi-NCR speech, people describe how far something is by how long it takes to get there rather than in meters or kilometers -- ask for directions to a shop and you're far more likely to hear यहाँ से पाँच मिनट का रास्ता है (yahaan se paanch minat kaa raastaa hai, 'it's a five-minute walk from here') than an actual distance. It pairs naturally with है (hai): 'X, Y से पाँच मिनट का रास्ता है.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate too literally as 'a road of five minutes,' missing that रास्ता (raastaa) here means 'the distance/journey,' not a physical road. 2) The का (kaa) agrees with रास्ता (raastaa) (masculine) regardless of what precedes it -- it does not change to की (kii) or के (ke) based on 'पाँच मिनट.'
Sample Sentences:
यहाँ से बाज़ार पाँच मिनट का रास्ता है। The market is a five-minute walk from here.
(yahaan se baazaar paanch minat kaa raastaa hai।)
