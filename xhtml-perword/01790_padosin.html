पड़ोसिन (padosin)

noun Means 'female neighbor' -- the feminine counterpart of पड़ोसी (padosii).
Sense 1 : female neighbor
noun, feminine
Synonyms: पड़ोसन (padosan, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi-NCR life, पड़ोसिन (padosin) often evokes the classic image of women neighbors chatting over the balcony or exchanging sugar and gossip -- a very domestic, colony-life word tied to mohalla (mohallaa) culture. It's used affectionately or sometimes teasingly, especially in the context of household dramas and TV serials featuring nosy or friendly neighbor characters.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to पड़ोसी (padosii) for all genders, but when specifically referring to a woman, native speakers usually say पड़ोसिन (padosin) or पड़ोसन (padosan). 2) Do not confuse with बहन (bahan, sister) despite the -in ending pattern -- पड़ोसिन (padosin) is purely about neighborly relation, not kinship.
