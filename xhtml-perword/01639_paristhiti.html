परिस्थिति (paristhiti)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'situation' or 'circumstance' -- the overall set of conditions surrounding a person or event.
Sense 1 : situation, circumstance(s)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: हालत (haalat), स्थिति (sthiti), हालात (haalaat, usually plural, circumstances)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in news broadcasts and serious conversation -- politicians love saying हालात ठीक नहीं हैं (haalaat thiik nahiin hain, 'the circumstances are not good') or परिस्थिति के अनुसार (paristhiti ke anusaar, 'according to the circumstances') when they want to sound thoughtful without committing to specifics. It has a slightly more formal, considered flavor than हालत (haalat), which feels more everyday and personal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हालत (haalat) or स्थिति (sthiti) interchangeably with परिस्थिति (paristhiti); in practice परिस्थिति (paristhiti) leans toward the broader external conditions shaping a situation (economic, social, personal), while हालत (haalat) often points to someone's or something's immediate physical/emotional condition. 2) It is grammatically feminine, which surprises learners who expect a masculine ending pattern from words with this shape -- adjectives and verbs agreeing with it must take feminine forms.
Sample Sentences:
देश की परिस्थिति बहुत नाज़ुक है। The country's situation is very delicate.
(desh kii paristhiti bahut naazuk hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
हमें परिस्थिति के अनुसार फैसला लेना चाहिए। We should make the decision according to the circumstances.
(hamen paristhiti ke anusaar phaislaa lenaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(agar paristhiti aisii hii rahii, to haalaat aur bigad jaaenge।)

(mantrii ne kahaa ki sarkaar paristhiti par baariikii se nazar rakh rahii hai।)

(agar paristhiti sachmuch itnii gambhiir hotii, to ab tak karfyoo lag gayaa hotaa।)

(kripyaa vartmaan paristhiti kaa pooraa aaklan kiijie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
परिस्थिति को देखते हुए यह निर्णय लिया गया। This decision was taken in view of the circumstances.
(paristhiti ko dekhte hue yah nirnay liyaa gayaa।)

(kaash paristhiti kuchh aur hotii, to ham yah projekt abhii shuroo kar dete।)
