मोती (motii)

noun मोती (motii) is a single-sense noun meaning 'pearl', occasionally used figuratively for something precious or lustrous.
Sense 1 : pearl
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Pearls show up constantly in Hindi imagery and names -- मोतीलाल (motiilaal) is a common given name, and मोती जैसे दाँत (motii jaise daant, 'teeth like pearls') is a classic beauty description in older film songs and poetry. You will also hear मोतियों जैसे आँसू (motiyon jaise aansoo, 'tears like pearls') for something precious falling drop by drop, so the word carries a gentle, treasured feel beyond its literal sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat मोती (motii) as if it were an adjective-ending word that changes like कड़ा (kadaa); it does not follow that pattern -- it is a plain masculine noun and its plural/oblique forms will be covered in the morphology table. 2) The word is masculine even though it ends in -ी, which surprises learners used to -ी endings marking feminine nouns -- compare मोती (motii, pearl) with रोटी (rotii, bread, feminine).
