जाति (jaati)

noun जाति (jaati) covers the social sense of 'caste'/'community' most learners encounter first, plus a broader classificatory sense of 'kind, species, type' used in biology, grammar, and general speech.
Sense 1 : caste; social community defined by birth
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बिरादरी (biraadrii, community/kinship group), वर्ण (varn, varna, more classical/ritual framing)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a heavy, real word in Indian social life -- it shows up on government forms, in matrimonial ads, and in political discourse around reservation (आरक्षण, aarakshan) policy. Native speakers navigate it carefully; a foreigner asking casually about someone's जाति (jaati) can land as intrusive, so it is good to know the weight this word carries even though textbooks often gloss it flatly as 'caste'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use जाति (jaati) interchangeably with धर्म (dharm, religion) -- they are separate categories on Indian forms and in conversation, so mixing them up confuses native listeners. 2) In casual speech जाति (jaati) can also mean someone's broader community or sub-group, not just the formal caste category studied in sociology, so context matters when someone asks जाति क्या है (jaati kyaa hai, 'what is [your] caste').

Sense 2 : kind, type, species, category
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्रकार (prakaar), किस्म (kism), नस्ल (nasl, breed/species, often for animals)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same root sense as the social meaning -- both come from the idea of 'birth-group' or 'that which one is born into/classified as' -- but in scientific and everyday classificatory talk (animal जाति (jaati), शब्द की जाति (shabd kii jaati, 'part of speech/word class')) it is entirely neutral, like English 'type' or 'species.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners familiar only with the caste meaning are often surprised to see जाति (jaati) in a biology or botany context, e.g. पौधों की जाति (paudhon kii jaati, 'a species of plants') -- here it simply means 'species/kind,' with no social connotation at all.
