धीमा (dhiimaa)

adjective A variable adjective meaning 'slow' (of speed, pace, sound, or flame), with an extended sense describing something 'faint' or 'low' in intensity, such as light or sound.
Sense 1 : slow (in speed or pace)
adjective
Synonyms: मंद (mand, literary), सुस्त (sust, sluggish/lazy connotation)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on Delhi roads: धीमी गाड़ी चलाओ (dhiimii gaadii chalaao, 'drive slowly') is standard advice given to new drivers, and school zones often have signs reading धीमी गति से चलें (dhiimii gati se chalen, 'proceed at slow speed'). Note the feminine agreement in गाड़ी (gaadii) and गति (gati) -- both are feminine nouns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सुस्त (sust) to mean 'slow,' but सुस्त (sust) usually implies laziness or sluggishness in a person, while धीमा (dhiimaa) is the neutral word for slow speed, motion, or pace -- use धीमा (dhiimaa) for a slow car, slow walk, or slow train.

(baarish men gaadii dhiimii chalaanii chaahie।)

Sense 2 : faint, low, dim (of sound, light, or flame)
adjective
Synonyms: मंद (mand, literary), हल्का (halkaa, light/mild, partial overlap)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in everyday household requests: आवाज़ धीमी करो (aavaaz dhiimii karo, 'turn the volume down/lower your voice') is something every parent says at least once a day. It also applies to a stove flame -- गैस धीमी करो (gais dhiimii karo, 'lower the gas flame') is a classic kitchen instruction while simmering dal or curry.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense धीमा (dhiimaa) describes intensity, not speed -- धीमी आवाज़ (dhiimii aavaaz) means 'a quiet/low voice,' not 'a slow voice,' which can confuse learners used to the pace sense.
