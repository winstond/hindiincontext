गोद (god)

noun Primarily a noun meaning 'lap' (the body-part sense, as in holding a child), with an extended sense in the fixed idiom for adoption. Both senses are covered below.
Sense 1 : lap (of the body)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: गोद में बैठना (god men baithnaa, 'to sit in someone's lap') is an everyday image in Hindi households -- a grandmother calling a toddler with गोद में आ जाओ (god men aa jaao, 'come sit in my lap') captures the warmth this word carries. It also underlies the tenderer diminutive गोदी (godii), which you will hear constantly around small children.
Common Mistakes: 1) गोद (god) is the physical lap formed when sitting -- learners sometimes confuse it with गोदी (godii), a common affectionate variant used especially with babies, e.g. गोदी में लेना (godii men lenaa, 'to pick up [a baby] in one's arms'). 2) The postposition used is usually में (men) ('in the lap'), not पर (par) -- Hindi treats the lap as a space you sit *inside*, not *on top of*.

(bachchaa apnii maan kii god men so rahaa hai।)

Sense 2 : adoption (as part of the fixed expression गोद लेना)
noun, feminine This sense exists almost entirely inside the compound verb गोद लेना (god lenaa, 'to adopt') and its passive counterpart गोद जाना/गोद दिया जाना for 'to be given up for adoption.' The image is metaphorical: to 'take someone into one's lap' is to make them your own child.
Synonyms: अंगीकार (angiikaar, literary/formal, rarely used for people)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: गोद लेना (god lenaa) is the everyday way to talk about adoption in Hindi -- you'll see it in news headlines, legal contexts, and family conversations alike. The metaphor (lap = the place a child belongs) is worth noticing: it's the same body-as-relationship logic behind गोद में बैठना (god men baithnaa) in the other sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) गोद लेना (god lenaa) takes ने (ne) marking on the subject like any transitive perfective verb (e.g. उसने बच्चा गोद लिया (usne bachchaa god liyaa, 'she adopted a child')) -- learners sometimes forget गोद (god) itself is not the object being marked, बच्चा (bachchaa) is. 2) Do not translate word-for-word as 'take in lap' in normal conversation -- गोद लेना (god lenaa) is the standard, neutral verb for legal/formal adoption, not a poetic flourish.
