कोहनी (kohnii)

noun A concrete body-part noun meaning 'elbow' -- has just this one everyday sense.
Sense 1 : elbow (body part)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word in everyday physical-contact situations -- someone jostling you on a crowded DTC bus or metro is likely to say संभल कर (sambhal kar, 'carefully') while nudging past with their कोहनी (kohnii). It is also the word used at the doctor's when describing a fall or an ache: कोहनी में दर्द है (kohnii men dard hai, 'there's pain in the elbow').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse कोहनी (kohnii) with घुटना (ghutnaa, 'knee') since both are joint words learned around the same time -- keep the pairing straight: कोहनी is the arm joint, घुटना is the leg joint. 2) Actions done 'with the elbow' use कोहनी से (kohnii se, 'with the elbow'), not पर (par) or को (ko) -- e.g. कोहनी से मारना (kohnii se maarnaa, 'to hit with the elbow').

मेरी कोहनी में बहुत दर्द हो रहा है। My elbow is hurting a lot.
(merii kohnii men bahut dard ho rahaa hai।)
