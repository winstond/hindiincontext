सगा (sagaa)

adjective/noun Primarily an adjective meaning 'blood-related' (as opposed to relations by marriage or affection alone), most often heard in the fixed phrase सगा भाई (sagaa bhaaii, 'real/full brother'); also used as a noun for a close blood relative.
Sense 1 : blood-related, related by blood (not by marriage or informal affection)
adjective Almost always modifies a kinship noun directly, e.g. सगा भाई (sagaa bhaaii, 'full brother'), सगी बहन (sagii bahan, 'full sister'), सगा चाचा (sagaa chaachaa, 'father's real younger brother') -- used to distinguish a true blood tie from a cousin-brother, step-relation, or someone called भाई (bhaaii) out of affection alone.
Synonyms: अपना (apnaa, broader sense of 'own'), खून का रिश्ता (khoon kaa rishtaa, phrase, 'blood relation')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Hindi-speaking families, भाई (bhaaii) and बहन (bahan) get used very loosely -- for cousins, close friends, even neighborhood kids -- so सगा (sagaa) is the word that snaps things back into precision when it matters, like inheritance talk or introducing family to outsiders. You'll also hear it in the bitter idiom सगे भी पराए हो गए (sage bhii paraae ho gae, 'even blood relatives became like strangers'), used when family ties sour.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often skip सगा (sagaa) entirely since English 'brother' doesn't distinguish blood from cousin-brother, but in Hindi family talk this word is doing real work -- मेरा भाई (meraa bhaaii) alone can leave someone wondering if you mean a cousin, while मेरा सगा भाई (meraa sagaa bhaaii) removes all doubt. 2) Don't use सगा (sagaa) for relations by marriage (like a sister-in-law) -- it specifically marks a shared-blood tie, not a shared-family one.

Sense 2 : a close blood relative
noun, masculine Used on its own (without a following kinship noun) to mean 'a blood relative' in general, often in contrastive statements about who truly stood by someone.
Synonyms: रिश्तेदार (rishtedaar, broader, includes non-blood relations), खून का रिश्ता (khoon kaa rishtaa, phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun use shows up in reflective or emotional speech -- न कोई सगा, न कोई पराया (n koii sagaa, n koii paraayaa, 'no one close, no one a stranger') is a common way to describe feeling utterly alone, showing how सगा (sagaa) sits at the emotional center of what 'family' means in Hindi.
