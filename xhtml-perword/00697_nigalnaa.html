निगलना (nigalnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning 'to swallow,' with a common figurative extension to swallowing up money, words, or pride.
Sense 1 : to swallow (food, liquid, a pill)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this used very concretely: a mother telling a child दवाई निगल लो (davaaii nigal lo, 'swallow the medicine') or someone struggling to get a big pill down. The compound-verb form निगल लेना (nigal lenaa) with लेना (lenaa) is extremely common and adds a sense of 'getting it down completely.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with पीना (piinaa, 'to drink') when talking about liquids or medicine taken with water -- निगलना (nigalnaa) specifically means the physical act of gulping something down (like a pill or a lump of food), not drinking as an activity.
Sample Sentences:
दवाई निगल लो, वरना ठीक नहीं होगी। Swallow the medicine, or you won't get better.
(davaaii nigal lo, varnaa thiik nahiin hogii।)

Sense 2 : to swallow up, embezzle, or absorb (money, resources, someone's share)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: हड़प लेना (hadap lenaa, colloquial, specifically for embezzling money)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in conversations about corruption or family property disputes: उसने सारा पैसा निगल लिया (usne saaraa paisaa nigal liyaa, 'he swallowed up all the money'). It paints money or land as something that vanishes into someone's greedy grasp, much like food disappearing down a throat.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to swallow (words, a retort, one's pride/anger, to hold back and not say something)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: दबा लेना (dabaa lenaa, for suppressing feelings)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just like in English, Hindi speakers 'swallow' their words or anger to keep the peace: अपना गुस्सा निगल लिया (apnaa gussaa nigal liyaa, 'swallowed his own anger'). It's a nice example of a physical verb doing double duty for emotional restraint, mirroring the English idiom closely enough that it feels intuitive.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपना गुस्सा निगल लिया। He swallowed his anger.
(usne apnaa gussaa nigal liyaa।)
