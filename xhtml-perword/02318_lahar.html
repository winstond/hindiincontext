लहर (lahar)

noun Basic meaning is a physical wave (water, sound), which extends naturally to a figurative 'wave' or surge of feeling, trend, or activity.
Sense 1 : wave (of water, sound, etc.)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: तरंग (tarang, literary), मौज (mauj)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of नदी में लहरें (nadii men lahren, 'waves in the river') or समुद्र की लहरें (samudr kii lahren, 'ocean waves') -- this is the concrete, everyday sense a learner will meet first, especially in descriptions of rivers, seas, and monsoon weather reports.
Sample Sentences:
समुद्र में बड़ी-बड़ी लहरें उठ रही थीं। Big waves were rising in the ocean.
(samudr men badii-badii lahren uth rahii thiin।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
नदी में लहरें बहुत तेज़ हैं, नाव मत ले जाना। The waves in the river are very strong, don't take the boat out.
(nadii men lahren bahut tez hain, naav mat le jaanaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
तूफ़ान के कारण कल समुद्र की लहरें ऊँची उठेंगी। Because of the storm, the ocean waves will rise high tomorrow.
(toofaan ke kaaran kal samudr kii lahren oonchii uthengii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
अगर हवा तेज़ न होती तो लहरें इतनी ऊँची न होतीं। If the wind hadn't been strong, the waves wouldn't have been so high.

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

कवि ने नदी की लहरों का बहुत सुंदर वर्णन किया है। The poet has beautifully described the waves of the river.

(jahaaz lahron men is tarah doobaa diyaa gayaa jaise koii khilaunaa ho।)

Sense 2 : wave/surge (of emotion, feeling, sensation)
noun, feminine Common in phrases like खुशी की लहर (khushii kii lahar, 'a wave of happiness') or दर्द की लहर (dard kii lahar, 'a wave of pain'), where an emotion or sensation is described as physically washing over a person.
Synonyms: उमंग (umang, surge of enthusiasm specifically), जोश (josh)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi loves this water-based metaphor for feelings -- just as English says 'a wave of nostalgia,' Hindi speakers say दिल में एक लहर उठी (dil men ek lahar uthii, 'a wave rose in the heart'), treating the heart almost like a small sea.
Sample Sentences:
उसकी बात सुनकर मेरे दिल में खुशी की एक लहर उठी। Hearing what he said, a wave of happiness rose in my heart.
(uskii baat sunkar mere dil men khushii kii ek lahar uthii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
जब भी वह गाना बजता है, मन में उदासी की लहर दौड़ जाती है। Whenever that song plays, a wave of sadness runs through my mind.
(jab bhii vah gaanaa bajtaa hai, man men udaasii kii lahar daud jaatii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(manch par tumhen dekhkar shrotaaon men utsaah kii lahar daud jaaegii।)

Sense 3 : wave/trend (of a social, political, or cultural phenomenon)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रुझान (rujhaan, trend, broader usage), फैशन (phaishan, craze/fashion specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Indian news and political commentary -- चुनावी लहर (chunaavii lahar, 'election wave') or किसी लहर में बहना (kisii lahar men bahnaa, 'to be swept along in some wave/trend') describes how public opinion or a craze sweeps across the country like a tide.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for ट्रेंड (trend) by default; लहर (lahar) is the more natural native choice when describing a spreading wave of popularity or sentiment, as in मोदी लहर (modii lahar, 'the Modi wave') from Indian election coverage.
Sample Sentences:
इस बार चुनाव में मोदी लहर साफ़ दिखाई दे रही है। This time in the election, the Modi wave is clearly visible.
(is baar chunaav men modii lahar saaf dikhaaii de rahii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
पूरा देश उस समय राष्ट्रवाद की लहर में बह गया था। The whole country got swept along in the wave of nationalism at that time.
(pooraa desh us samay raashtrvaad kii lahar men bah gayaa thaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
टिक-टॉक की लहर में बहकर बच्चे घंटों वीडियो बनाते रहते हैं। Swept along in the TikTok craze, kids keep making videos for hours.
(tik-tok kii lahar men bahkar bachche ghanton viidiyo banaate rahte hain।)
