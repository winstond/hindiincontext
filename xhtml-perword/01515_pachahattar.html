पचहत्तर (pachahattar)

numeral The number seventy-five (pachahattar); a single numeral sense with no other common usage.
Sense 1 : the number 75
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers between 21 and 99, पचहत्तर (pachahattar) is not built predictably from 'five' (paanch) and 'seventy' (sattar) the way English compounds its teens and tens -- it is its own memorized word, which is why native speakers just say numbers fluently without mentally decomposing them, while learners often pause to do the math.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पचहत्तर (pachahattar, 75) with पैंसठ (painsath, 65) or सत्तर (sattar, 70) since Hindi numerals from 60-99 do not follow a transparent additive pattern the way English 'sixty-five' or 'seventy' do -- each one really has to be learned as its own word.
