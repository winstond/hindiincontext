मालगाड़ी (maalgaadii)

noun A compound of माल (maal, 'goods/cargo') and गाड़ी (gaadii, 'vehicle/train'); a single, concrete sense meaning 'goods train/freight train'.
Sense 1 : goods train, freight train
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Anyone who has waited at a level crossing (railway phaatak, phaatak) in a small town knows the long, slow rumble of a मालगाड़ी (maalgaadii) -- often dozens of wagons carrying coal, grain, or cement, moving at a pace that tests every commuter's patience. It is a everyday, high-frequency word for anyone talking about Indian Railways, alongside यात्री गाड़ी (yaatrii gaadii, 'passenger train').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume गाड़ी (gaadii) alone can mean 'freight train' -- on its own it just means 'train' or 'vehicle/car'; माल (maal) is what specifies 'goods/cargo'. 2) Do not confuse with सामान (saamaan, 'luggage/belongings') -- माल (maal) here has the commercial/cargo sense, not personal baggage.
