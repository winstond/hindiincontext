सहेली (sahelii)

noun A close female friend -- used only by/about women, referring to another woman or girl friend. One core sense.
Sense 1 : female friend
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दोस्त (dost, gender-neutral, used by anyone), मित्र (mitr, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries a warm, almost old-fashioned charm -- think of two schoolgirls sharing secrets on a scooty ride home, or a mother asking her daughter सहेलियाँ (saheliyaan, 'friends [f.]') आ रही हैं क्या (aa rahii hain kyaa, 'are your friends coming?'). It is distinctly gendered in a way English 'friend' is not, reflecting how Hindi often marks social relationships by the speaker's and referent's gender.
Common Mistakes: 1) सहेली (sahelii) is used specifically by a girl/woman about another girl/woman -- a man would not call his male friend सहेली (sahelii); use दोस्त (dost) or यार (yaar) instead. 2) In English 'girlfriend' can imply a romantic partner, but सहेली (sahelii) is strictly platonic -- for a romantic partner Hindi uses गर्लफ्रेंड (garlaphrend) or प्रेमिका (premikaa).
