झूलना (jhoolnaa)

verb One core physical sense -- to swing or sway -- with a natural figurative extension to being emotionally suspended or torn between options. Related noun: झूला (jhoolaa, 'swing').
Sense 1 : to swing, sway (on a swing, in the wind, etc.)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: लहराना (lahraanaa, to sway/wave), हिलना (hilnaa, to move/shake)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Swings ( झूला jhoolaa ) are a beloved fixture of Indian summers and monsoon festivals -- think of Krishna and Radha depicted swinging in miniature paintings, or the झूला (jhoolaa) hung from a mango tree during Teej and Sawan, when women sing and swing together. The verb carries that same festive, leisurely image.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for हिलना (hilnaa) for any swaying motion, but झूलना (jhoolnaa) specifically evokes a pendulum-like back-and-forth arc -- a swing, a hammock, a rope bridge, hair, or a pendant -- rather than a general shake or wobble. 2) This verb is intransitive; the person doing the swinging is the subject, not marked with ने (ne) even in the perfective (बच्चा झूला (bachchaa jhoolaa, 'the child swung')).

Sense 2 : to be suspended between, torn between (emotionally or mentally)
verb, intransitive Common in the frame X और Y के बीच झूलना (X aur Y ke biich jhoolnaa) -- 'to swing/be suspended between X and Y' -- for indecision or mixed emotional states, e.g. आशा और निराशा के बीच झूलना (aashaa aur niraashaa ke biich jhoolnaa, 'to swing between hope and despair').
Synonyms: डगमगाना (dagamgaanaa, to waver/totter), भटकना (bhataknaa, to wander, looser fit)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a lovely example of Hindi turning a physical image into an emotional one: instead of saying someone 'is torn' between two feelings, Hindi pictures them literally swinging back and forth like on a झूला (jhoolaa) -- a very visual, almost cinematic way to describe mental limbo.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense almost always needs the बीच (biich, 'between') frame with two contrasting nouns; used alone without that frame, झूलना (jhoolnaa) reverts to its literal swinging meaning and sounds odd for indecision.
