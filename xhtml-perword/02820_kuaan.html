कुआँ (kuaan)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'well' (the water source), with an extended idiomatic use in a well-known fatalistic proverb about being caught between two bad options.
Sense 1 : well (water source)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: इनारा (inaaraa, rare/rural variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Before piped water became common, the गाँव का कुआँ (gaanv kaa kuaan, 'the village well') was the social hub of a neighborhood, much like a village square -- women gathering water there was a daily ritual full of gossip and news-sharing. Older Hindi literature and films often use the कुआँ (kuaan) as a backdrop for exactly this kind of scene.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget the nasalized vowel and write/say कुआ (kuaa) -- the chandrabindu-like nasal ending (आँ) is part of the standard spelling and pronunciation. 2) Do not confuse with कुँआरा (kunaaraa, 'unmarried'), which looks visually similar but is unrelated in meaning.
Sample Sentences:

(gaanv ke baahar ek puraanaa kuaan hai।)

Sense 2 : no-win situation (idiomatic, from 'well vs. ditch')
noun, masculine Occurs almost exclusively inside the fixed proverb न इधर कुआँ न उधर खाई (n idhar kuaan n udhar khaaii, 'a well on this side, a ditch on that side') or the shorter इधर कुआँ उधर खाई (idhar kuaan udhar khaaii), describing being trapped between two equally bad choices.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same physical well as the literal sense, just pressed into service as one half of a vivid image: step wrong and you fall into the कुआँ (kuaan), step the other way and you fall into the खाई (khaaii) -- either way, disaster. It's the Hindi equivalent of being caught 'between the devil and the deep blue sea.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense barely appears outside the fixed proverb frame -- learners shouldn't try to use कुआँ (kuaan) alone to mean 'dilemma'; it needs the खाई (khaaii, 'ditch') half of the pairing to carry the idiomatic meaning.

एक तरफ कुआँ है, दूसरी तरफ खाई -- मैं क्या करूँ? On one side is the well, on the other the ditch -- what should I do?
(ek taraph kuaan hai, doosrii taraph khaaii -- main kyaa karoon?)
