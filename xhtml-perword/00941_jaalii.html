जाली (jaalii)

adjective/noun Two main threads: a noun meaning a net/mesh/lattice screen (jaalii as in a window screen or grille), and an adjective meaning fake/counterfeit/forged. The adjective sense is far more commonly encountered in everyday speech and news.
Sense 1 : fake, counterfeit, forged
adjective
Synonyms: नकली (naklii), बनावटी (banaavtii, slightly more 'artificial/put-on' in feel)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear on the evening news whenever there's a story about counterfeit currency, fake degrees, or forged property papers -- जाली डिग्री (jaalii digrii, 'fake degree') and जाली नोट (jaalii not, 'counterfeit note') are everyday headline phrases in India. It carries a slightly more official/legal flavor than नकली (naklii), which leans more toward everyday fake goods (जाली सामान (jaalii saamaan) also happens, but नकली सामान (naklii saamaan) is more natural for market-stall knockoffs).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for नकली (naklii) as an automatic substitute in every context, but जाली (jaalii) is the strong default specifically for forged documents, currency, and signatures -- जाली नोट (jaalii not, 'fake/counterfeit note') and जाली हस्ताक्षर (jaalii hastaakshar, 'forged signature') are the collocations you'll hear constantly in news and police contexts. 2) Do not confuse this जाली with the noun जाली (jaalii) meaning a mesh screen -- they are unrelated in daily use despite being spelled identically.

Sense 2 : net, mesh, lattice screen (e.g. window screen, grille)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जालीदार खिड़की (jaaliidaar khidkii, specifically 'lattice window', narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the beautifully carved stone lattice screens at places like Fatehpur Sikri or Humayun's Tomb -- those are जाली (jaalii) work, and the word is still used today for the wire mesh on a window (खिड़की पर जाली लगी है, khidkii par jaalii lagii hai, 'there's a screen on the window') that keeps mosquitoes out in Delhi summers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes mix this up with जाल (jaal, 'net/web/trap' as in a fishing net or spider's web) -- जाली (jaalii) is more the fine woven or perforated screen/lattice you see on windows, doors, or in mosque/tomb architecture (like the famous stone जालियाँ (jaaliyaan) at Mughal monuments), while जाल (jaal) is the broader/coarser net or trap.
