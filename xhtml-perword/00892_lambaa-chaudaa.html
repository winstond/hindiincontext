लंबा-चौड़ा (lambaa-chaudaa)

adjective A compound adjective built from लंबा (lambaa, 'long/tall') and चौड़ा (chaudaa, 'wide'); it has a literal sense describing physical size and a very common figurative sense meaning 'extensive, elaborate, big' (as in a long list, a big speech, or a huge plan).
Sense 1 : extensive, elaborate, exaggeratedly big (figurative)
adjective Frequently modifies abstract nouns like बातें (baaten, 'talk'), वादे (vaade, 'promises'), or योजना (yojnaa, 'plan') to suggest something is grander in words than in substance.
Synonyms: बड़ा-चढ़ा (badaa-chadhaa, less common variant), भारी-भरकम (bhaarii-bharkam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase behind that very Indian eye-roll at someone making लंबे-चौड़े वादे (lambe-chaude vaade, 'big promises']) before an election or a wedding -- big talk that everyone quietly doubts will be fully honored.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often miss the mildly skeptical undertone: लंबी-चौड़ी बातें (lambii-chaudii baaten, 'big talk') usually implies exaggeration or empty promises, not neutral 'long conversation'. 2) It is not used for physical length of time (a long meeting is not लंबा-चौड़ा (lambaa-chaudaa)) -- reserve it for scope/scale of claims, plans, or lists.

Sense 2 : physically large in area/extent (literal)
adjective
Synonyms: बड़ा (badaa), विशाल (vishaal, somewhat more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used the way an English speaker might say 'a sprawling place' -- think of a real estate agent describing a house with लंबा-चौड़ा आँगन (lambaa-chaudaa aangan, 'a big, spacious courtyard'). It paints a picture of generous, stretched-out space rather than just 'big'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as a fixed invariant compound, but both parts agree with the noun's gender/number like ordinary adjectives, e.g. लंबी-चौड़ी सड़क (lambii-chaudii sadak, 'a broad, sprawling road']) for a feminine noun.
