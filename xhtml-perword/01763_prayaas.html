प्रयास (prayaas)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'effort' or 'attempt' -- a slightly formal, Sanskritic word you will meet constantly in news, speeches, and school essays, though it is common enough in everyday careful speech too.
Sense 1 : effort, attempt
noun, masculine Commonly paired with the verb करना (karnaa) -- प्रयास करना (prayaas karnaa, 'to make an effort') -- functioning much like कोशिश करना (koshish karnaa) but with a more formal register.
Synonyms: कोशिश (koshish), मेहनत (mehnat, focuses on hard work rather than a single attempt), चेष्टा (cheshtaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear प्रयास (prayaas) constantly on the evening news -- सरकार का प्रयास है (sarkaar kaa prayaas hai, 'the government's effort is...') -- it has that slightly official, earnest ring, the vocabulary of speeches and editorials rather than casual chat over chai. In daily conversation among friends, कोशिश (koshish) is far more natural; reach for प्रयास (prayaas) when you want to sound a touch more formal or deliberate.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to कोशिश (koshish) in every context; प्रयास (prayaas) is the word you will hear in news reports, government announcements, and formal writing, so recognizing it there matters even if you speak with कोशिश (koshish). 2) It takes करना (karnaa), not कोई और verb -- साय़ास किया (prayaas kiyaa, 'made an effort') is the natural collocation, not प्रयास हुआ (prayaas huaa) for a personal attempt.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार का प्रयास है कि सभी को शिक्षा मिले। The government's effort is that everyone should get education.
(sarkaar kaa prayaas hai ki sabhii ko shikshaa mile।)
