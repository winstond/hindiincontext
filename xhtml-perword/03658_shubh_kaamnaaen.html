शुभ कामनाएँ (shubh kaamnaaen)

phrase A fixed formal greeting meaning 'best wishes' -- used mainly in written messages, cards, and formal speeches rather than everyday casual talk.
Sense 1 : an expression offering good wishes to someone
phrase Functions as a standalone formulaic phrase, often placed at the end of a card, letter, or speech rather than inside a sentence structure.
Synonyms: बधाई (badhaaii, used for congratulations rather than general well-wishing), मंगलकामनाएँ (mangalkaamnaaen, formal, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this phrase printed on greeting cards, at the bottom of formal emails, and in speeches at weddings or farewells -- it carries a slightly more formal, written-register warmth than a spoken phrase like अच्छा हो (achchhaa ho, 'may it go well'). Politicians and dignitaries often close remarks with जनता को शुभ कामनाएँ (jantaa ko shubh kaamnaaen, 'best wishes to the public'), so it also has a slightly ceremonial flavor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use शुभ कामनाएँ (shubh kaamnaaen) where बधाई (badhaaii) is meant -- शुभ कामनाएँ (shubh kaamnaaen) is for wishing someone well going forward (a new job, a journey, a birthday to come), while बधाई (badhaaii) congratulates them on something already achieved (passing an exam, a wedding, a promotion). 2) It is almost always used in the plural कामनाएँ (kaamnaaen) -- the singular कामना (kaamnaa) sounds oddly formal or literary standing alone in a greeting.
Sample Sentences:
आपको जन्मदिन की शुभ कामनाएँ! Best wishes to you on your birthday!
(aapko janmdin kii shubh kaamnaaen!)
