लापरवाह (laaparvaah)

adjective A single-sense adjective describing a person, action, or attitude as careless or negligent. Related to the noun लापरवाही (laaparvaahii, 'carelessness').
Sense 1 : careless, negligent, heedless
adjective
Synonyms: बेपरवाह (beparvaah), असावधान (asaavdhaan, formal), उदासीन (udaasiin, indifferent -- slightly different shade)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Indian civic and parenting speech -- a driver is called लापरवाह (laaparvaah) after an accident, a student is scolded for being लापरवाह (laaparvaah) with homework, and news reports love the phrase लापरवाही से मौत (laaparvaahii se maut, 'death due to negligence'). It's built from ला- (a Persian-origin negative prefix, 'without') plus परवाह (parvaah, 'care/concern'), so literally it means 'without care' -- once you see परवाह (parvaah) in isolation (as in मुझे कोई परवाह नहीं (mujhe koii parvaah nahiin, 'I don't care at all')), the compound becomes easy to remember.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बेपरवाह (beparvaah) and लापरवाह (laaparvaah) as if they were pure synonyms; लापरवाह (laaparvaah) is the far more common everyday choice, while बेपरवाह (beparvaah) leans slightly toward 'unconcerned/carefree' rather than 'sloppy.' 2) The abstract noun is लापरवाही (laaparvaahii) ('carelessness'), not a repetition of the adjective -- learners sometimes try to use लापरवाह (laaparvaah) itself as the noun, e.g. wrongly saying उसकी लापरवाह (uskii laaparvaah) instead of उसकी लापरवाही (uskii laaparvaahii, 'his carelessness').
