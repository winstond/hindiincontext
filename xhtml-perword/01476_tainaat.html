तैनात (tainaat)

adjective An adjective meaning 'posted, stationed, deployed' -- used mainly for people (police, soldiers, officials, guards) assigned to a duty or location. Almost always paired with the verb होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa).
Sense 1 : posted, stationed, deployed (of a person on duty)
adjective Functions as a predicate adjective with होना (honaa) ('to be posted') or as the complement of करना (karnaa) ('to post/deploy someone'), forming a transitive frame: X ने Y को वहाँ तैनात किया (X ne Y ko vahaan tainaat kiyaa, 'X posted Y there').
Synonyms: नियुक्त (niyukt, formal), स्थापित (sthaapit, more about objects/institutions than people)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in news reports: पुलिस तैनात कर दी गई है (pulis tainaat kar dii gaii hai, 'police have been deployed') is a stock phrase during festivals, protests, or VIP visits. It carries an official, on-duty flavor -- a guard तैनात (tainaat) at a gate feels different from one just 'standing' (खड़ा, khadaa) there.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use तैनात (tainaat) as a standalone verb; it is an adjective/participle-like word and needs होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) to function in a sentence. 2) It is reserved for people assigned to duty, not for placing objects -- for furniture or equipment, रखना (rakhnaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa) is used instead.

गेट पर एक सुरक्षा गार्ड तैनात है। A security guard is posted at the gate.
(get par ek surakshaa gaard tainaat hai।)
