चेहरा (chehraa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning 'face', used both literally and figuratively (expression, appearance, or a person's 'true self/character').
Sense 1 : face (the physical body part)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मुख (mukh, literary), मुखड़ा (mukhdaa, affectionate/informal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of चेहरा (chehraa) as the face you'd describe or photograph -- its shape, its glow, its expression -- while मुँह (munh) is the face as a functional opening. A mother might say तेरा चेहरा उतरा हुआ है (teraa chehraa utraa huaa hai, 'your face looks pale/dejected') when she reads worry or tiredness in your expression.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मुँह (munh) when they mean चेहरा (chehraa) -- मुँह (munh) is 'mouth' or the face as the part that eats/speaks, while चेहरा (chehraa) is the face as appearance or visage; 'wash your face' is मुँह धोना (munh dhonaa), but 'a beautiful face' is सुंदर चेहरा (sundar chehraa), not सुंदर मुँह (sundar munh).

Sense 2 : true face, real character or identity (figurative)
noun, masculine Common in the fixed idiom चेहरा बेनकाब होना (chehraa benkaab honaa, 'to be unmasked, exposed') and असली चेहरा सामने आना (aslii chehraa saamne aanaa, 'one's true face/character to come out').
Synonyms: असली रूप (aslii roop), नकाब (nakaab, related idiom word for 'mask')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the चेहरा (chehraa) of gossip and political commentary: लोगों का असली चेहरा सामने आ गया (logon kaa aslii chehraa saamne aa gayaa, 'people's true colors came out') is a very common thing to hear after someone's hidden motives are revealed.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is almost always paired with असली (aslii, 'real') or नकली (naklii, 'fake') -- saying just चेहरा (chehraa) alone rarely conveys 'true character' to a listener without that qualifier or clear context.

Sense 3 : appearance, image, face (of a place, institution, or thing)
noun, masculine Used metonymically for the outward look or public image of a city, organization, or era, as in शहर का चेहरा बदल गया (shahar kaa chehraa badal gayaa, 'the city's face has changed').
Synonyms: स्वरूप (svaroop, formal), छवि (chhavi, 'image/reputation', related but distinct)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in news and civic talk a lot -- दिल्ली का चेहरा बदल रहा है (dillii kaa chehraa badal rahaa hai, 'Delhi's face is changing') is a standard way to talk about a city's development or transformation, echoing how English speakers say a place is 'getting a facelift.'

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
