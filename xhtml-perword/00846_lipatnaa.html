लिपटना (lipatnaa)

verb One core meaning: to wrap, cling, or coil around something -- used for a person embracing someone, a garment wrapping the body, or something coiled like a snake or vine.
Sense 1 : to cling to, embrace, wrap oneself around (someone/something)
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the person or thing being clung to: माँ से लिपटना (maan se lipatnaa, 'to cling to mother').
Synonyms: चिपटना (chipatnaa), गले लगना (gale lagnaa, to embrace, esp. of people), लपेटना (lapetnaa, transitive counterpart -- to wrap something around)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word for the kind of clinging hug where the whole body wraps in -- a child grabbing a parent's leg at the school gate, or two people embracing tightly in a moment of relief or grief. It carries more physical intensity than गले लगना (gale lagnaa), which is closer to a standard hug or greeting.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for गले लगना (gale lagnaa) for every hug, but लिपटना (lipatnaa) suggests a tighter, fuller-body cling or wrap, like a child clinging to a parent's leg or a scared person clutching someone. 2) The person or thing being clung to takes से (se), not को (ko) -- वह उससे लिपट गई (vah usse lipat gaii, 'she clung to him'), not उसे लिपट गई.

Sense 2 : to wrap around, coil around (of a garment, vine, snake, smoke, etc.)
verb, intransitive Common with inanimate or natural subjects: साड़ी बदन से लिपट गई (saadii badan se lipat gaii, 'the sari clung to the body'); साँप डाल से लिपटा था (saamp daal se liptaa thaa, 'the snake was coiled around the branch').
Synonyms: लपेटना (lapetnaa, transitive -- to wrap something around), चिपटना (chipatnaa, to stick/cling to a surface)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in descriptions of a wet सारी (saadii) clinging to the body after rain, a bougainvillea vine wrapped around a gate, or fog wrapped around hills -- Hindi likes this verb for anything that molds itself tightly around a shape.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners familiar only with the 'hug' sense may miss that लिपटना (lipatnaa) is equally at home describing cloth, smoke, or a creeper wrapping around something -- it is not restricted to people.
