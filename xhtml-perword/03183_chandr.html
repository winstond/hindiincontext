चंद्र (chandr)

noun A literary/formal word for 'moon,' used mainly in compounds, names, and elevated writing rather than everyday speech; the common conversational word is चाँद (chaand).
Sense 1 : moon
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चाँद (chaand, everyday word), शशि (shashi, literary), सोम (som, literary), इंदु (indu, literary)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet चंद्र (chandr) constantly as a building block -- चंद्रयान (chandryaan, 'moon vehicle,' India's lunar mission name), चंद्रमा (chandrmaa, the fuller poetic word for moon), or in personal names like चंद्रशेखर (chandrshekhar). Think of it as the Sanskrit-derived 'moon-root' Hindi reaches for in science, mythology, and naming, while चाँद (chaand) stays the word for daily talk and Bollywood songs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not use चंद्र (chandr) in normal conversation to say 'the moon is beautiful' or similar -- चाँद (chaand) is what people actually say; चंद्र (chandr) shows up mainly inside compound words like चंद्रमा (chandrmaa), चंद्रग्रहण (chandragrahan, 'lunar eclipse'), or in names.
