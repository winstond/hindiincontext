निन्यानवे (ninyaanve)

numeral The number ninety-nine (99). A single-sense numeral with no other common meanings.
Sense 1 : the number 99
numeral Like all Hindi numbers 20-99, this has its own unique form rather than being built predictably from नौ (nau, nine) and नब्बे (nabbe, ninety); it simply has to be learned as a whole word.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this number constantly in prices and exam marks -- the classic 'charm pricing' trick of ninety-nine rupees ($99$) instead of a round hundred is just as common in Indian shops as anywhere else, so निन्यानवे रुपये (ninyaanve rupye, ninety-nine rupees) is a phrase worth having ready.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse it with नब्बे (nabbe, ninety) or निन्यानबे-style variant spellings because the middle syllables sound similar when spoken quickly -- listen for the nasalized 'nyaan' sound that marks निन्यानवे (ninyaanve) specifically as 99, not 90.

(is kamiiz kii kiimat ninyaanve rupye hai।)
