महानगर (mahaangar)

noun A compound noun meaning 'metropolis, big city' -- built from महा (mahaa, 'great') plus नगर (nagar, 'town/city'). One core sense, used both literally and with a hint of the sociocultural weight a big city carries.
Sense 1 : metropolis, large city
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बड़ा शहर (badaa shahar), शहर (shahar, covers 'city' more broadly, not just large ones)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in news and civic contexts -- traffic reports, pollution updates, crime stories -- almost always about Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, or Chennai. It carries a faint editorial flavor, evoking the fast pace, anonymity, and pressures of city life (महानगर की भागदौड़ mahaangar kii bhaagdaud, 'the hustle-bustle of the metropolis'), so it appears far more in writing and broadcast Hindi than in casual chat, where लोग बस 'शहर' ही बोलते हैं (log bas 'shahar' hii bolte hain, 'people just say city')।
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use it as a casual synonym for शहर (shahar) 'city' -- महानगर (mahaangar) is reserved for genuinely large, often capital-tier cities (दिल्ली dillii, मुंबई mumbaii) and sounds odd applied to a small town. 2) It is grammatically countable and behaves like an ordinary masculine noun, but learners sometimes leave it unmarked for oblique case before postpositions, e.g. forgetting महानगर में (mahaangar men) needs the oblique form when pluralized: महानगरों में (mahaanagron men).
Sample Sentences:
दिल्ली भारत के सबसे बड़े महानगरों में से एक है। Delhi is one of the largest metropolises in India.
(dillii bhaarat ke sabse bade mahaanagron men se ek hai।)
