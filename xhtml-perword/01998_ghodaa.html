घोड़ा (ghodaa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning 'horse'; it also carries a couple of well-known extended/idiomatic uses (chess piece, bolt/latch mechanism) built on the same image.
Sense 1 : horse (the animal)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अश्व (ashv, literary), घोड़ी (ghodii, female horse -- not interchangeable, see notes)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture the tourist-trap horse carts (tanga taangaa) still seen in some old-city areas, or the decorated mare a groom rides in a baraat baaraat -- घोड़ा (ghodaa) is the everyday word for all of this, not a formal or bookish one.
Common Mistakes: 1) घोड़ी (ghodii) is not simply 'a female version usable anywhere' -- it specifically means 'mare' and is also the word used for the ceremonial mare a groom rides in a wedding procession, so swapping the two changes the picture in a listener's head. 2) Learners sometimes default to अश्व (ashv) from textbook Sanskrit-heavy Hindi; in everyday Delhi speech घोड़ा (ghodaa) is what people actually say.
Sample Sentences:
उसके पास एक सफ़ेद घोड़ा है। He has a white horse.
(uske paas ek safed ghodaa hai।)

Sense 2 : knight (chess piece)
noun, masculine Used specifically within the vocabulary of chess (shatranj shatranj); understood immediately by anyone who plays.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as English calls this piece a 'knight' rather than 'horse,' Hindi keeps the animal name घोड़ा (ghodaa) -- a nice small window into how the game's imagery carried over more literally into Hindi.
Sample Sentences:
शतरंज में घोड़ा ढाई घर चलता है। In chess, the knight moves two and a half squares.

Sense 3 : hammer/cocking mechanism of a gun; bolt of a lock
noun, masculine A mechanical extension of the animal sense, based on a similar shape or trigger-like action; heard mostly in specific fixed phrases like घोड़ा दबाना (ghodaa dabaanaa, 'to pull the trigger').
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is one of those words where the physical resemblance did the work of coining a new sense -- much like how English 'hammer' for a gun part isn't really about tools either.
Sample Sentences:

(usne bandook kaa ghodaa chadhaayaa।)
