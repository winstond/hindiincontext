दबना (dabnaa)

verb The intransitive partner of दबाना (dabaanaa, 'to press'): the core sense is 'to get pressed/crushed,' extending to being suppressed or intimidated by someone else's pressure.
Sense 1 : to be pressed, get crushed
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: कुचलना (kuchalnaa, transitive, contrast), पिचकना (pichaknaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a foot getting stepped on in a crowded Metro coach: पैर दब गया (pair dab gayaa, 'my foot got crushed') -- the foot is the grammatical subject, and no one is blamed, which is very typical of how Hindi handles this kind of physical mishap.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners reach for दबाना (dabaanaa) when the subject is the thing undergoing the pressing rather than the one applying it -- e.g. a foot getting crushed under a bag takes दबना (dabnaa), not दबाना (dabaanaa).
Sample Sentences:
भीड़ में मेरा पैर दब गया। My foot got crushed in the crowd.
(bhiid men meraa pair dab gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to be suppressed, intimidated, cowed
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: झुकना (jhuknaa, close in meaning), डरना (darnaa, narrower, fear-specific)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb behind the common complaint किसी के दबाव में दबना (kisii ke dabaav men dabnaa, 'to buckle under someone's pressure') -- useful for describing office politics, family hierarchy, or anyone who caves rather than argues back.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is often used with से (se) marking the person or thing one is intimidated by, e.g. वह अपने बॉस से दब जाता है (vah apne bos se dab jaataa hai, 'he gets cowed by his boss') -- learners sometimes reach for the wrong postposition here, expecting को (ko).
Sample Sentences:
वह हमेशा अपने बॉस के सामने दब जाता है। He always backs down in front of his boss.
(vah hameshaa apne bos ke saamne dab jaataa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : to be suppressed or hushed up (of a matter, scandal, sound)
verb, intransitive Common with abstract subjects like बात (baat, 'matter'), मामला (maamlaa, 'case'), or आवाज़ (aavaaz, 'voice/sound').
Synonyms: छिपना (chhipnaa, close but broader, 'to be hidden')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in news chatter about scandals: मामला दब गया (maamlaa dab gayaa, 'the case got hushed up') -- a very Indian bureaucratic image of a file quietly buried under paperwork rather than resolved.
Sample Sentences:
भ्रष्टाचार का मामला दब गया। The corruption case got hushed up.
