इकत्तीस (ikattiis)

numeral The number thirty-one (ikattiis), used for counting and dates.
Sense 1 : the number 31
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers from 21 to 99, इकत्तीस (ikattiis) is a fused, memorized word rather than a predictable combination -- native speakers just know it, the way English speakers know 'eleven' isn't 'ten-one.' It shows up most often in dates (like the 31st of a month) and ages.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse it with इकतीस (iktiis) spelling variants or mix up the similar-sounding teens/twenties series; the safest fix is to just memorize इकत्तीस (ikattiis) as its own unit rather than trying to derive it from इक (ik, one) plus तीस (tiis, thirty) on the fly, since Hindi numbers 21-99 are not built compositionally the way English ones are.
