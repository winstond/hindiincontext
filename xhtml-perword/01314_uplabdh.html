उपलब्ध (uplabdh)

adjective A single core meaning: 'available.' Common in formal, official, and written registers -- announcements, forms, product descriptions -- and increasingly in spoken Hindi too.
Sense 1 : available, obtainable
adjective Typically used with होना (honaa) and a dative-marked person: 'X के पास/को उपलब्ध है' (X ke paas/ko uplabdh hai, 'X has X available').
Synonyms: मौजूद (maujood), उपलब्ध (uplabdh)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly on signs, forms, and customer-service scripts -- 'यह सेवा अभी उपलब्ध नहीं है' (yah sevaa abhii uplabdh nahiin hai, 'this service is not available right now') is classic call-center or app Hindi. In casual speech, people often just say मिल जाएगा (mil jaaegaa, 'you'll get it/find it') instead, so उपलब्ध (uplabdh) has a slightly more official, written flavor even though it's very high-frequency in that register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use यह उपलब्ध करता है (yah uplabdh kartaa hai) to mean 'this provides,' but उपलब्ध (uplabdh) itself is not a verb of providing -- for that you need the causative-flavored phrase उपलब्ध कराना (uplabdh karaanaa, 'to make available, to provide'). 2) It pairs with होना (honaa), not the more casual मिलना (milnaa) in formal writing, though मिलना (milnaa) is common in speech for the same idea ('to be gotten/found').
Sample Sentences:
यह सेवा अभी उपलब्ध नहीं है। This service is not available right now.
(yah sevaa abhii uplabdh nahiin hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
क्या यह किताब लाइब्रेरी में उपलब्ध है? Is this book available in the library?
(kyaa yah kitaab laaibrerii men uplabdh hai?)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
टिकट अगले हफ्ते से उपलब्ध होंगे। Tickets will be available from next week.
(tikat agle haphte se uplabdh honge।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
उस समय शहर में डॉक्टर उपलब्ध नहीं थे। At that time, doctors were not available in the city.
(us samay shahar men doktar uplabdh nahiin the।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर सारा डेटा उपलब्ध होता, तो रिपोर्ट पहले ही बन जाती। If all the data had been available, the report would have been ready already.
(agar saaraa detaa uplabdh hotaa, to riport pahle hii ban jaatii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
कृपया सुनिश्चित करें कि सभी दस्तावेज़ उपलब्ध हों। Please make sure that all the documents are available.
(kripyaa sunishchit karen ki sabhii dastaavez uplabdh hon।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Active
यह ऐप अभी सिर्फ एंड्रॉइड पर उपलब्ध है, आईफोन पर जल्द आएगा। This app is currently available only on Android; it'll come to iPhone soon.
(yah aip abhii sirph endroid par uplabdh hai, aaiiphon par jald aaegaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
सारी जानकारी वेबसाइट पर उपलब्ध कराई गई है। All the information has been made available on the website.
(saarii jaankaarii vebsaait par uplabdh karaaii gaii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
सरकार ने बाढ़ पीड़ितों के लिए राहत सामग्री उपलब्ध कराई है। The government has made relief supplies available for the flood victims.
(sarkaar ne baadh piiditon ke lie raahat saamagrii uplabdh karaaii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
काश यह दवा हर मेडिकल स्टोर पर उपलब्ध होती। If only this medicine were available at every medical store.
(kaash yah davaa har medikal stor par uplabdh hotii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
मौजूदा संसाधनों में जो भी उपलब्ध हो, उसी से काम चलाओ। Make do with whatever is available among the current resources.
(maujoodaa sansaadhnon men jo bhii uplabdh ho, usii se kaam chalaao।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
