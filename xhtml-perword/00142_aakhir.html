आख़िर (aakhir)

adverb/particle/noun Covers 'finally/in the end' as an adverb, 'after all' as a discourse particle used to make a point emphatically, and the underlying noun sense 'end.' Also spelled आखिर (aakhir) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : finally, eventually, in the end
adverb
Synonyms: अंततः (anttah, formal), आख़िरकार (aakhirkaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you use when someone shows up late to a wedding and the whole family sighs आख़िर आ ही गए! (aakhir aa hii gae, 'you finally made it!'). It carries a mix of relief and mild exasperation that English 'finally' captures well.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अंततः (anttah) in speech, but आख़िर (aakhir) or आख़िरकार (aakhirkaar) is what people actually say in conversation. 2) It sits comfortably at the start of a sentence -- आख़िर वह आ गया (aakhir vah aa gayaa, 'he finally came') -- unlike English 'finally,' which feels more natural mid-sentence.
Sample Sentences:

(aakhir vah pariikshaa men paas ho gayaa।)

Sense 2 : after all (rhetorical, pressing a point)
particle Typically opens or interrupts a sentence to justify or challenge a claim, often followed by a question: आख़िर तुम क्यों नहीं गए? (aakhir tum kyon nahiin gae, 'why didn't you go, after all?').
Synonyms: भला (bhalaa, adds a similar rhetorical push)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot from an annoyed parent or teacher: आख़िर तुम समझते क्यों नहीं? (aakhir tum samajhte kyon nahiin, 'why don't you understand, after all?') -- it signals mounting frustration while still asking a real question.
Common Mistakes: 1) This use is not about time at all -- it is about pressing a rhetorical point, so translating every आख़िर (aakhir) as 'finally' misses the sense when it appears with a question mark or scolding tone. 2) It often pairs with तो (to) for extra emphasis: आख़िर है तो (aakhir hai to) 'after all, he is...'

Sense 3 : end, conclusion
noun, masculine Common in the fixed phrase आख़िर में (aakhir men, 'in the end, finally') and आख़िर तक (aakhir tak, 'until the end').
Synonyms: अंत (ant), समाप्ति (samaapti, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: आख़िर में (aakhir men) is the phrase a storyteller uses to wrap things up, much like 'in the end' or 'finally' closing off a tale -- it pairs the noun sense with a postposition to do adverbial work.

Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
