भला (bhalaa)

adverb/adjective/interjection Covers a range of uses: as an adjective meaning 'good/kind/decent', as an adverb/particle in rhetorical or ironic questions ('really? indeed?'), and as an interjection expressing mild surprise or dismissal. All senses share a root feeling of 'goodness' or 'well-being', even where the surface meaning has drifted into irony.
Sense 1 : rhetorical marker in questions implying skepticism or irony ('indeed?', 'pray tell')
adverb Appears at the start of a rhetorical question, e.g. भला यह कैसे हो सकता है? (bhalaa yah kaise ho saktaa hai, 'How on earth could this be so?') -- it signals the speaker already doubts the answer.
Synonyms: आखिर (aakhir, after all)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the भला (bhalaa) you hear in exasperated or teasing questions -- a mother might say भला तुम यह क्यों करोगे? (bhalaa tum yah kyon karoge, 'Now why on earth would you do this?') -- it adds a note of disbelief or gentle challenge.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes miss that भला (bhalaa) here has nothing to do with 'goodness' -- it is purely a rhetorical flavoring word, similar to English 'pray tell' or 'really now', and translating it literally as 'good' produces nonsense. 2) It is almost always sentence-initial in this use; placing it mid-sentence sounds off.

Sense 2 : good, kind, decent, well-meaning
adjective
Synonyms: अच्छा (achchhaa), नेक (nek, formal), भला-सा (bhalaa-saa, somewhat colloquial variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear elders describe someone as भला आदमी (bhalaa aadmii, 'a decent man') or भली औरत (bhalii aurat, 'a good-hearted woman') -- it is the word reached for when praising someone's character rather than their skill or looks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat भला (bhalaa) as a simple synonym of अच्छा (achchhaa), but भला (bhalaa) leans toward moral goodness or kind-heartedness (a 'good person' in character), while अच्छा (achchhaa) covers general quality too (a good movie, a good day). 2) In set phrases like भला आदमी (bhalaa aadmii, 'a decent man'), भला (bhalaa) cannot be freely swapped with अच्छा (achchhaa) without slightly shifting the flavor from 'nice/agreeable' to 'upright/decent'.
Sample Sentences:
वह बहुत भला आदमी है। He is a very decent man.
(vah bahut bhalaa aadmii hai।)

Sense 3 : expression of mild surprise, dismissal, or ironic acknowledgment
interjection
Synonyms: अरे (are, mild surprise, broader use)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Said alone with a certain lift in the voice, भला! (bhalaa) can mean anything from 'Oh, is that so!' to a sarcastic 'Sure, sure.' Context and tone of voice carry most of the meaning here.
Common Mistakes: 1) As a standalone interjection, भला! (bhalaa) is used to react to something someone said, often with a touch of sarcasm or amused disbelief ('Oh really!'). Learners may confuse this dismissive tone with genuine approval, since the adjective sense means 'good'.
