भव्य (bhavy)

adjective A single-sense adjective describing something grand, magnificent, or splendid in scale and presentation -- think buildings, weddings, and celebrations rather than small pretty things.
Sense 1 : grand, magnificent, splendid
adjective
Synonyms: शानदार (shaandaar), विशाल (vishaal, emphasizes size more than splendor), आलीशान (aaliishaan, especially for buildings/mansions)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear भव्य (bhavy) constantly around Indian weddings and festivals -- a भव्य समारोह (bhavy samaaroh, 'a grand ceremony') or भव्य स्वागत (bhavy svaagat, 'a grand welcome') is the standard way to describe the kind of over-the-top decor, lighting, and scale that big Indian celebrations are famous for. News anchors also love this word for describing newly inaugurated temples, stadiums, or government buildings.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for भव्य (bhavy) to mean simply 'beautiful,' but it specifically carries a sense of grandeur or scale -- a small pretty flower would not be called भव्य (bhavy), while a huge, ornately lit temple or a lavish wedding hall would. 2) It is mostly used for buildings, events, ceremonies, and celebrations, not for people's physical appearance -- calling a person भव्य (bhavy) sounds odd; use सुंदर (sundar) or handsome-type words instead.
