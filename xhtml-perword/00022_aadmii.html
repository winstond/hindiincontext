आदमी (aadmii)

noun आदमी (aadmii) is the everyday word for 'man,' extends to a generic 'person' regardless of gender, and in casual family speech can even mean 'husband.'
Sense 1 : man (adult male human)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मर्द (mard), पुरुष (purush, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: आदमी (aadmii) traces back to the Arabic name Adam, so it literally carries the sense of 'son of Adam' -- a neat parallel to how many languages borrow the first man's name to mean 'man' in general.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use आदमी (aadmii) interchangeably with इंसान (insaan) for 'human being,' but आदमी leans toward 'male person' while इंसान covers humanity in general, including women.

Sense 2 : person, human being (generic, gender-neutral use)

Sense 3 : husband (colloquial)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पति (pati), शौहर (shauhar, regional)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In many North Indian households a wife might refer to her husband simply as अपना आदमी (apnaa aadmii, 'my man') -- a warm, indirect way of saying 'husband' common in family or neighborhood chatter, rather than using his name.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is strictly informal -- using आदमी (aadmii) for 'husband' in formal writing or polite introductions sounds off; पति (pati) is the neutral, formal choice.
