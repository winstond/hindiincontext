पोतना (potnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning to smear, coat, or plaster a surface with a thick or wet substance -- whitewash on a wall, mud on a floor, paint on wood. It also has a common figurative extension for smearing something (like blame or a mess) messily onto a situation.
Sense 1 : to smear, coat, plaster (a surface with a substance)
verb, transitive Takes the surface as object with the substance marked by से (se): दीवार पर चूना पोतना (diivaar par choonaa potnaa, 'to whitewash the wall with lime').
Synonyms: लगाना (lagaanaa), लीपना (liipnaa, often paired with this word for mud/cow-dung floors)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word brings up village and old-Delhi-mohalla imagery -- women whitewashing outer walls with चूना (choonaa, lime) before Diwali, or plastering courtyard floors with mud and cow-dung (गोबर से लीपना-पोतना (gobar se liipnaa-potnaa, 'to plaster with cow-dung')). लीपना-पोतना (liipnaa-potnaa) is a set phrase for this whole ritual of home upkeep.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लगाना (lagaanaa) here, but पोतना (potnaa) specifically implies a thick, messy, or wet coating spread by hand or brush -- whitewash, mud, tar, paint -- not just any application. 2) The substance being smeared is marked with से (se) while the surface is the direct object: दीवार को रंग से पोतना (diivaar ko rang se potnaa, 'to smear the wall with paint'), which can feel backwards to English speakers who think of 'applying paint to the wall.'

Sense 2 : to smear on messily/carelessly (of makeup, paint, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: थोपना (thopnaa, to slap on/impose, slightly different but overlapping in careless application)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll hear this when someone teases another for overdoing makeup or face cream: चेहरे पर पाउडर पोत लिया (chehre par paaudar pot liyaa, 'she's slapped powder all over her face') -- affectionately mocking, the way a mother might rib a daughter getting ready for a wedding.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense carries a mildly negative or comic tone -- excessive or clumsy application, as with makeup or cream -- so using it for a neat, deliberate application (like applying medicine carefully) can sound off; लगाना (lagaanaa) is safer there.
Sample Sentences:
