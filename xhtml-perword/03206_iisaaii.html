ईसाई (iisaaii)

noun/adjective Means 'Christian', used both as an adjective (Christian, e.g. describing a community or festival) and as a noun (a Christian person). Related to ईसा (iisaa), the Hindi/Urdu name for Jesus.
Sense 1 : a Christian (person)
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In India, religious identity words like ईसाई (iisaaii), हिंदू (hindoo), मुस्लिम (muslim), and सिख (sikh) come up naturally in everyday conversation, for instance when talking about neighborhoods, festivals, or classmates, without the sensitivity such labeling might carry in some English-speaking contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes capitalize it mentally like English 'Christian' and expect a special formal register, but ईसाई (iisaaii) is a completely ordinary, neutral word used in everyday speech, just like हिंदू (hindoo) or मुस्लिम (muslim).

Sense 2 : Christian (relating to Christianity)
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this adjective in phrases like ईसाई धर्म (iisaaii dharm, 'the Christian religion') or ईसाई त्योहार (iisaaii tyohaar, 'Christian festival'), especially around Christmas (बड़ा दिन, badaa din) when schools and shops in Delhi-NCR often mention ईसाई समुदाय (iisaaii samudaay, 'the Christian community').
