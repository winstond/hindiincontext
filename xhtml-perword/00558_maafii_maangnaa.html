माफ़ी माँगना (maafii maangnaa)

verb A fixed verb-phrase built from माफ़ी (maafii, 'forgiveness/pardon') plus माँगना (maangnaa, 'to ask for'), meaning 'to apologize' or 'to beg pardon.' Only one core sense.
Sense 1 : to apologize, to ask for forgiveness
verb, transitive The person being apologized to is marked with से (se): माँ से माफ़ी माँगी (maan se maafii maangii, 'apologized to mother'). Since माँगना (maangnaa) is transitive, the past tense takes ने (ne) on the subject, with माफ़ी (maafii) as the object controlling agreement.
Synonyms: क्षमा माँगना (kshamaa maangnaa, formal), सॉरी बोलना (sorii bolnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Saying माफ़ी माँगना (maafii maangnaa) out loud, especially to elders, carries real social weight in Hindi-speaking households -- it is treated as a formal act of restoring peace, not a casual aside like a quick 'sorry.' In everyday banter among friends, especially younger folks, सॉरी बोलना (sorii bolnaa) or just सॉरी (sorii) often takes over as the lighter, more casual option.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the person apologized to with को (ko) instead of से (se) -- the correct frame is X से माफ़ी माँगना (X se maafii maangnaa), not X को। 2) माफ़ी (maafii) is feminine, so the verb agrees with it in the perfective: उसने माफ़ी माँगी (usne maafii maangii, 'he/she apologized'), not माँगा (maangaa).
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपनी गलती के लिए माफ़ी माँगी। He apologized for his mistake.
(usne apnii galtii ke lie maafii maangii।)
