सचाई (sachaaii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'truth' or 'honesty' -- the quality of being true, or the true facts of a matter. Closely related to सच (sach, 'truth/true') and सच्चाई (sachchaaii, a very common variant spelling).
Sense 1 : truth, honesty, truthfulness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सच्चाई (sachchaaii, variant form), सत्य (saty, formal), ईमानदारी (iimaandaarii, honesty as a trait)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Both सचाई (sachaaii) and सच्चाई (sachchaaii) come from सच (sach, 'true'), and Delhi speakers use them interchangeably in everyday speech -- you'll hear सचाई यह है कि... (sachaaii yah hai ki..., 'the truth is that...') just as often as the double-च version. It carries a slightly more weighty, reflective tone than plain सच (sach), often used when someone is about to reveal an uncomfortable or deeper truth.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to सच्चाई (sachchaaii) since it is the more frequently written form; सचाई (sachaaii) is not wrong or archaic, just the leaner variant with one fewer च. 2) Don't confuse this abstract noun with सच (sach) used as an adjective/adverb meaning 'true' or 'truly' in phrases like सच बोलो (sach bolo, 'speak the truth') -- सचाई (sachaaii) itself is not used that way, it names the quality or the facts themselves.
