पहुँचना (pahunchnaa)

verb The everyday verb for arriving or reaching a place, and by extension reaching a point, level, or person's hands; also used figuratively for something 'getting through' to someone emotionally. Inline Hindi in this note: पहुँचना (pahunchnaa).
Sense 1 : to arrive, reach a place
verb, intransitive The destination is usually marked with तक (tak) or की ओर (kii or), or simply stands as a plain location; the person doing the arriving is the subject and takes no ने (ne) since this is an intransitive verb.
Synonyms: आना (aanaa, covers 'coming' more than 'reaching')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If you call someone in Delhi to ask when they will get to your place, the answer almost always uses this verb: बस पहुँचने वाला हूँ (bas pahunchne vaalaa hoon, 'I am just about to arrive') is a classic (and often optimistic) thing to say while still getting ready.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for आना (aanaa) when they mean the specific sense of 'reaching a destination after travel' -- पहुँचना (pahunchnaa) is the natural choice for that, especially with time or place phrases like समय पर (samay par, on time). 2) It is very common to hear the compound पहुँच जाना (pahunch jaanaa) instead of the bare verb, adding a sense of completion -- both are correct but the compound is what you will hear most in casual speech.

(main das minat men pahunch jaaoongaa।)

Sense 2 : to reach (a level, amount, stage, or age)
verb, intransitive Used with abstract destinations like किनारे तक (kinaare tak, to the edge) or a numeric/age point, e.g. साठ साल की उम्र तक पहुँचना (saath saal kii umr tak pahunchnaa, 'to reach sixty years of age').
Synonyms: पहुँच जाना (pahunch jaanaa, compound form emphasizing completion)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the natural verb for talking about progress -- a project reaching completion, a price reaching a certain figure, or a person reaching a certain age -- extending the physical 'arriving' sense onto a scale or timeline, the way Hindi often maps space onto abstract progress.

Sense 3 : to reach someone (a message, object, or effect reaching a person)
verb, intransitive Common frame is X को Y पहुँचना (X ko Y pahunchnaa) -- the recipient is marked with को (ko) while the thing that arrives (a letter, news, help, or even hurt/nuकसान (nuksaan)) is the grammatical subject.
Synonyms: मिलना (milnaa, focuses on the receiving end rather than the journey)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense covers both literal delivery (a parcel reaching you) and emotional impact -- Hindi speakers say a hurtful remark or a kindness 'reaches' (पहुँचना (pahunchnaa)) someone, much like English 'that really got to me,' treating feelings as things that travel and arrive.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try को (ko) on the wrong noun -- remember it goes on the *recipient*, not the thing that is arriving: खबर उसे पहुँची (khabar use pahunchii, 'the news reached him'), not the reverse.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
