जुआ (juaa)

noun Mainly means 'gambling' as a noun; also has an older, less common sense of a plough's 'yoke.' The gambling sense is what learners will meet in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : gambling, betting
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सट्टा (sattaa, betting/wagering specifically), बाज़ी (baazii, a bet or wager)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: जुआ खेलना (juaa khelnaa) is the standard verb phrase you'll hear -- gambling carries a fair bit of social stigma in most Hindi-speaking households, often mentioned alongside शराब (sharaab, 'liquor') as a vice that 'ruins a household,' घर बरबाद करना (ghar barbaad karnaa). Diwali is a notable cultural exception, when card games (ताश (taash)) for small stakes are traditionally tolerated as a festive custom in many North Indian families.
Common Mistakes: 1) जुआ (juaa) itself is the activity of gambling, not a single bet -- for 'placing a bet' Hindi speakers say बाज़ी लगाना (baazii lagaanaa) or शर्त लगाना (shart lagaanaa), and for 'to gamble' the set phrase is जुआ खेलना (juaa khelnaa), literally 'to play gambling.' 2) Don't confuse it with जुआं/जुऑं, forms of जूँ (joon, 'lice') -- unrelated word, easy to mix up when reading quickly without matras.
Sample Sentences:
वह हर रोज़ जुआ खेलता है। He gambles every day.
(vah har roz juaa kheltaa hai।)

Sense 2 : yoke (of a plough or cart)
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the older, agricultural sense -- the wooden crosspiece harnessing a pair of bullocks to a plough or cart, बैलों का जुआ (bailon kaa juaa, 'the bullocks' yoke'). A learner in Delhi-NCR is far more likely to encounter the gambling sense day to day; this one shows up mostly in rural or literary contexts.
Sample Sentences:
किसान ने बैलों के कंधे पर जुआ रखा। The farmer placed the yoke on the bullocks' shoulders.
(kisaan ne bailon ke kandhe par juaa rakhaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
बैलों का जुआ लकड़ी का बना होता है। The bullocks' yoke is made of wood.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
