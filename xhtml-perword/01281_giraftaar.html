गिरफ़्तार (giraftaar)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'arrested,' almost always used with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa).
Sense 1 : arrested, taken into custody
adjective Functions almost exclusively as a predicate adjective with होना (honaa) 'to be arrested' or करना (karnaa) 'to arrest' -- it does not combine freely with other verbs the way ordinary adjectives might.
Synonyms: बंदी (bandii, noun-like, less common as predicate), पकड़ा गया (pakdaa gayaa, colloquial, passive phrasing)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a Persian-origin loanword you will hear constantly on Hindi news channels -- पुलिस ने गिरफ़्तार कर लिया (pulis ne giraftaar kar liyaa, 'the police arrested [him/her]') is a staple crime-report phrase. Note the compound verb कर लिया (kar liyaa) often replaces plain किया (kiyaa) in these reports to suggest the action was completed and finalized.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use गिरफ़्तार (giraftaar) as a standalone verb; it needs होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) -- गिरफ़्तार होना (giraftaar honaa, 'to get arrested') and गिरफ़्तार करना (giraftaar karnaa, 'to arrest') are the actual verb phrases. 2) The person being arrested takes को (ko) when करना (karnaa) is used: पुलिस ने उसे गिरफ़्तार किया (pulis ne use giraftaar kiyaa, 'the police arrested him') -- learners sometimes forget the object marking here since गिरफ़्तार (giraftaar) itself feels like the main action word.
Sample Sentences:
पुलिस ने चोर को गिरफ़्तार कर लिया। The police arrested the thief.
(pulis ne chor ko giraftaar kar liyaa।)
