किस तरफ़ (kis taraf)

adverb A question phrase meaning 'which direction/way' or 'towards which side' -- combines किस (kis, oblique of कौन) with तरफ़ (taraf, 'side/direction').
Sense 1 : which direction, which way
adverb Functions as a directional question modifying the verb, asking about the path or direction of movement or orientation, e.g. 'which way did he go' or 'which side is it on.'
Synonyms: किस दिशा में (kis dishaa men, more formal), किधर (kidhar, one-word variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly from auto-rickshaw and taxi drivers in Delhi asking किस तरफ़ जाना है (kis taraf jaanaa hai, 'which direction do you need to go')-- it is the everyday, casual way to ask for a heading, much more common in speech than किस दिशा में (kis dishaa men), which sounds a bit more bookish or formal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with किस तरह (kis tarah, 'in what manner'), which asks about method, not direction -- तरफ़ (taraf) is about physical or figurative direction, तरह (tarah) is about manner. 2) तरफ़ (taraf) is often used with की (kii) when it follows a noun ('घर की तरफ़' (ghar kii taraf, 'towards the house')), but as a standalone question phrase किस तरफ़ (kis taraf) does not take an extra की (kii).
