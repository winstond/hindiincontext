व्यावहारिक (vyaavahaarik)

adjective An adjective meaning 'practical' -- covers both 'pragmatic, down-to-earth' (a person or approach) and 'realistic, workable' (a plan or solution); related to व्यवहार (vyavahaar, 'behavior/practice/dealing'), from which it derives.
Sense 1 : practical, pragmatic (of a person or mindset)
adjective
Synonyms: यथार्थवादी (yathaarthvaadii, realistic), समझदार (samajhdaar, sensible)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in career and life-advice conversations: व्यावहारिक सोच रखो (vyaavahaarik soch rakho, 'keep a practical mindset') is classic advice from an uncle or parent steering a young person away from idealism.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with व्यवहारिक (vyavahaarik), a common misspelling missing the long ा; the correct form always has the extra आ after व्य: व्यावहारिक (vyaavahaarik). 2) Do not confuse with व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat, 'personal') despite superficial similarity in sound.

Sense 2 : practical, realistic, workable (of a plan, solution, or approach)
adjective
Synonyms: कार्यसाधक (kaarysaadhak, rare), उपयोगी (upyogii, useful)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Common in office and planning contexts: व्यावहारिक हल (vyaavahaarik hal, 'a practical solution') is the kind of phrase that shows up in meetings when someone is steering the group away from a purely theoretical idea.
