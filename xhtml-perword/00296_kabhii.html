कभी (kabhii)

adverb One core word covering 'ever/at some time' and 'sometimes,' with a strong idiomatic life in the negated and doubled patterns कभी नहीं (kabhii nahiin) and कभी-कभी (kabhii-kabhii).
Sense 1 : ever, at some (unspecified) time
adverb
Synonyms: किसी समय (kisii samay, at some point), कभी न कभी (kabhii n kabhii, sooner or later)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word that turns a plain past-tense question into 'have you EVER...?' -- native speakers drop it in constantly when asking about life experience, travel, or trying something once.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हमेशा (hameshaa) or कोई (koii) when they mean 'ever' -- कभी (kabhii) is the word for an unspecified point in time, not for 'always' or 'any.' 2) In questions like 'have you ever been to Delhi?', learners forget कभी (kabhii) entirely and just use the perfect tense, which sounds flat without it: क्या तुम कभी दिल्ली गए हो? (kyaa tum kabhii dillii gae ho?, 'have you ever been to Delhi?')

Sense 2 : sometimes, occasionally
adverb The doubled form कभी-कभी (kabhii-kabhii) is far more common in this sense than the bare single कभी (kabhii); a lone कभी usually leans toward the 'ever' sense unless context makes 'sometimes' clear.
Synonyms: कभी-कभी (kabhii-kabhii, doubled/more common form), कभी कभार (kabhii kabhaar, every now and then)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: The reduplication कभी-कभी (kabhii-kabhii) is a classic Hindi rhythm -- doubling a time word to soften it into 'now and then' rather than a fixed schedule.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners overuse the single कभी (kabhii) for 'sometimes' when native speakers default to the doubled कभी-कभी (kabhii-kabhii) -- e.g. कभी-कभी मैं चाय नहीं पीता (kabhii-kabhii main chaay nahiin piitaa, 'sometimes I don't drink tea').

Sense 3 : never (in the fixed negative frame)
adverb This sense exists only inside the frame कभी...नहीं (kabhii...nahiin); कभी (kabhii) itself never means 'never' in isolation -- the negation must be spoken or written alongside it.
Synonyms: कभी नहीं (kabhii nahiin, fixed frame), कभी भी नहीं (kabhii bhii nahiin, emphatic variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मैं कभी नहीं जाऊँगा (main kabhii nahiin jaaoongaa, 'I will never go') is the classic dramatic declaration you hear in arguments and vows alike -- the pairing of कभी (kabhii) with नहीं (nahiin) is what carries the force, much like English stresses 'never' itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the नहीं (nahiin) thinking कभी (kabhii) alone can carry 'never' -- it cannot; without the negator it reverts to 'ever/sometimes.' 2) For extra emphasis native speakers insert भी (bhii): कभी भी नहीं (kabhii bhii nahiin, 'never, ever') -- learners often forget this intensifier exists.
