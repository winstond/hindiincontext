ऊबड़-खाबड़ (oobad-khaabad)

adjective A single-sense descriptive adjective for rough, uneven, bumpy surfaces or terrain -- roads, ground, paths.
Sense 1 : rough, uneven (of terrain, roads, surfaces)
adjective
Synonyms: असमान (asmaan, focuses on unevenness generally, less physical texture), खुरदरा (khuradraa, more about a rough/coarse texture than terrain shape)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic Hindi echo-word (जोड़ी शब्द / jodii shabd) -- the second element खाबड़ (khaabad) doesn't mean anything on its own, it just rhymes with and reinforces ऊबड़ (oobad) for emphasis. You'll hear it constantly describing village roads, potholed streets, or rocky hiking trails -- ऊबड़-खाबड़ रास्ता (oobad-khaabad raastaa, 'bumpy path') is an extremely common phrase in everyday complaints about Indian roads.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use this for a rough personality or rough voice -- it is reserved for physical terrain/surfaces (roads, fields, paths), not people, sounds, or abstract things. 2) It's an echo/reduplicated word (ऊबड़ (oobad) + खाबड़ (khaabad)) and is always used as this fixed pair -- there's no standalone ऊबड़ (oobad) in common use.

(yah raastaa bahut oobad-khaabad hai।)
