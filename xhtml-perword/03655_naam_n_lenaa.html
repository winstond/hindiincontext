नाम न लेना (naam n lenaa)

verb A compound-verb idiom built on नाम (naam, 'name') and लेना (lenaa, 'to take'), negated with न (n); it means to refuse to even mention or utter someone's/something's name, usually out of anger, disgust, or estrangement.
Sense 1 : to refuse to mention someone's name (out of anger, hurt, or estrangement)
verb, transitive Usually appears with a का (kaa)-marked possessor before नाम: किसी का नाम न लेना (kisii kaa naam n lenaa, 'to not take someone's name').
Synonyms: ज़िक्र न करना (zikr n karnaa), नाम तक न लेना (naam tak n lenaa, emphatic variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase captures a very Hindi-speaking way of expressing a rift: rather than saying 'I'm angry at him,' a mother might declare आज के बाद उसका नाम मत लेना (aaj ke baad uskaa naam mat lenaa, 'don't you dare say his name after today') to a child who's been hanging out with a bad influence. The silence around the name itself becomes the punishment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes take this literally as just 'not saying a name,' missing that it signals a deliberate emotional refusal -- often after a falling-out or betrayal. 2) The object of the withheld name takes का/की/के, not को: उसका नाम न लो (uskaa naam n lo, 'don't say his name'), not उसे नाम न लो.
Sample Sentences:
आज के बाद उसका नाम मत लेना। Don't you dare say his name after today.
(aaj ke baad uskaa naam mat lenaa।)
