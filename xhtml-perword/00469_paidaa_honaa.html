पैदा होना (paidaa honaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to be born' -- literally 'to become produced/manifest.' पैदा (paidaa) is an invariant Persian-origin adjective that never appears alone in modern speech except in this and a few related fixed frames; होना (honaa) carries all the conjugation.
Sense 1 : to be born
verb, intransitive पैदा (paidaa) stays fixed; only होना (honaa) conjugates and agrees with the subject, e.g. मैं पैदा हुआ (main paidaa huaa, 'I was born' - male speaker), वह पैदा हुई (vah paidaa huii, 'she was born'). The place or time is usually marked with में (men) and the birth-giver with के घर (ke ghar) or से (se), not से alone.
Synonyms: जन्म लेना (janm lenaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will hear constantly when people talk about their hometown or birth year: मेरा जन्म (meraa janm) sounds like a form filled out for officialdom, while मैं दिल्ली में पैदा हुआ (main dillii men paidaa huaa, 'I was born in Delhi') is what actually comes out in conversation over chai. Note how होना (honaa) here works much like passive English 'to be born' -- the subject doesn't act, the event simply happens to them, which fits the broader Hindi pattern of framing life events (नींद आना (niind aanaa), भूख लगना (bhookh lagnaa)) as things that arrive or occur rather than things one actively does.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to conjugate पैदा (paidaa) itself as if it were the main verb -- it never changes form; all tense/agreement happens on होना (honaa). 2) To say 'born to' a certain family, native speakers say किसी के घर पैदा होना (kisii ke ghar paidaa honaa, 'to be born into someone's home/family'), not a direct translation with 'to.' 3) Learners sometimes reach for जन्म होना (janm honaa) or जन्म लेना (janm lenaa) interchangeably with पैदा होना (paidaa honaa) -- all are correct, but पैदा होना (paidaa honaa) is the most everyday, spoken choice, while जन्म लेना (janm lenaa) feels a shade more formal or written.

Sense 2 : to arise, come into existence (of situations, feelings, problems)
verb, intransitive Used with abstract subjects like समस्या (samasyaa, 'problem'), शक (shak, 'doubt'), or फ़र्क़ (farq, 'difference') to say a situation or feeling has come about, e.g. दोनों में झगड़ा पैदा हो गया (donon men jhagdaa paidaa ho gayaa, 'a quarrel arose between the two').
Synonyms: उत्पन्न होना (utpann honaa, formal), उभरना (ubharnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This extended, abstract sense shows how पैदा (paidaa) carries its literal 'produced' meaning into the world of feelings and problems -- a doubt or a rift is talked about as something 'produced,' almost as if emotions have a birth of their own, echoing the same conceptual frame as the literal sense above.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to होना (honaa) alone (शक हुआ) where native speakers prefer the fuller पैदा हुआ (paidaa huaa) for emphasis that something newly came into being rather than simply existed.
