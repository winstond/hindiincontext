अब (ab)

adverb A core time adverb meaning 'now' -- covers the literal present moment and the very common 'from now on' / transitional sense that shows up constantly in spoken Hindi.
Sense 1 : now, at this moment
adverb
Synonyms: इस वक़्त (is vaqt, this moment), फ़िलहाल (filahaal, for now)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear अब क्या करें (ab kyaa karen, 'what to do now') and अब बस (ab bas, 'enough now') constantly in daily complaints and resignation -- अब (ab) often carries a faint sigh of 'well, given how things stand now...' rather than being a neutral time-stamp.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अभी (abhii) when अब (ab) is meant: अभी (abhii) leans toward 'right this second/just now' with urgency or immediacy, while अब (ab) marks a broader shift in time -- 'now' as opposed to 'before.' 2) अब (ab) is frequently paired with तो (to) for emphasis -- अब तो (ab to, 'well, now...') -- which can trip up learners expecting a literal translation of 'तो.'

Sense 2 : from now on, henceforth (marking a change of state)
adverb Often implicitly contrasts with a पहले (pahle, 'before') that may not even be stated -- अब मैं नहीं जाता (ab main nahiin jaataa, 'now I don't go [anymore]') implies a past habit that has changed.
Synonyms: इसके बाद (iske baad, after this), आगे से (aage se, from now on)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the अब (ab) of personal turning points -- अब सब ठीक है (ab sab thiik hai, 'now everything's fine') after a rough patch, or a parent saying अब तुम बड़े हो गए हो (ab tum bade ho gae ho, 'now you've grown up') -- it frames the present as a break from an implied past.

(pahle merii usse baat nahiin hotii thii, par ab ham achchhe dost hain।)
