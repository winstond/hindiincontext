फैलाना (phailaanaa)

verb The causative counterpart of फैलना (phailnaa, 'to spread/expand' intransitively) -- फैलाना (phailaanaa) means to spread, extend, or stretch out something, and by extension, to spread news, rumors, or disease. All senses share this core 'cause to spread' meaning.
Sense 1 : to spread out / stretch out (something physical)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बिछाना (bichhaanaa, for laying out flat things like a mat or sheet), पसारना (pasaarnaa, for limbs/hands)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this in very physical, everyday contexts -- a mother telling a child हाथ फैलाओ (haath phailaao, 'spread out your hands') to receive something, or someone spreading out clothes to dry, कपड़े फैलाना (kapde phailaanaa, 'to spread out clothes'), a common sight on Delhi rooftops and balconies.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with बिछाना (bichhaanaa): फैलाना (phailaanaa) is broader and works for hands, arms, legs, wings, or any material being stretched out, while बिछाना (bichhaanaa) is specifically for laying something flat like a bedsheet or mat.

Sense 2 : to spread (news, rumors, ideas, disease)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: प्रसारित करना (prasaarit karnaa, formal, used for broadcasting/media), फैलना (phailnaa, intransitive counterpart -- what spreads on its own)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense is everywhere in Hindi news and daily conversation -- अफ़वाह फैलाना (afvaah phailaanaa, 'to spread rumors'), बीमारी फैलाना (biimaarii phailaanaa, 'to spread disease'), and जागरूकता फैलाना (jaagrooktaa phailaanaa, 'to spread awareness') are all extremely common collocations, the last one especially in public health and social campaigns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for फैलना (phailnaa) when they actually need the causative: अफ़वाह फैल गई (afvaah phail gaii, 'the rumor spread') describes the rumor spreading on its own, while उसने अफ़वाह फैलाई (usne afvaah phailaaii, 'he spread the rumor') requires the causative फैलाना (phailaanaa) because there's an agent doing the spreading.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया अफ़वाह मत फैलाओ। Please don't spread rumors.
(kripyaa afvaah mat phailaao।)

Sense 3 : to cause a mess or disorder (figurative extension)
verb, transitive Often used in the frame सामान फैलाना (saamaan phailaanaa, 'to scatter things around') or गंदगी फैलाना (gandgii phailaanaa, 'to spread filth/mess').
Synonyms: बिखेरना (bikhernaa, to scatter/strew)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A very domestic, scolding-mother phrase: कमरे में सामान मत फैलाओ (kamre men saamaan mat phailaao, 'don't scatter your things around the room') is something almost every Indian kid has heard growing up.
