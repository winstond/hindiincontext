झूला (jhoolaa)

noun Primarily a noun meaning a swing or cradle-hammock; it also gives rise to a common verb sense (to swing/rock) used in the intransitive frame. Related to the verb झूलना (jhoolnaa).
Sense 1 : swing (the play/garden apparatus)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every park and many verandahs in Delhi-NCR have a झूला (jhoolaa) -- kids fight over the neighborhood one, and grandparents nap on a slow-swinging one strung up on the porch. It's also the word for the ceremonial swing used for baby Krishna idols during festivals like Janmashtami, so the word carries a soft, nostalgic, homely feel.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse झूला (jhoolaa) with झूलना (jhoolnaa), the verb 'to swing' -- झूला (jhoolaa) is the noun for the object itself, not the action.

Sense 2 : cradle / hammock for an infant
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पालना (paalnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In many North Indian homes a baby's झूला (jhoolaa) is a cloth hammock tied between two hooks rather than a Western-style crib -- mothers and grandmothers gently push it while singing lullabies, so the word evokes that rocking, soothing image as much as the object.
