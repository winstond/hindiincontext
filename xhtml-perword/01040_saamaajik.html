सामाजिक (saamaajik)

adjective An adjective built from समाज (samaaj, 'society') meaning 'social' -- relating to society, community, or social life. It has one core meaning with a couple of common collocational uses.
Sense 1 : social, relating to society
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see सामाजिक (saamaajik) constantly in newspaper headlines and NGO-speak -- सामाजिक कार्यकर्ता (saamaajik kaarykartaa, 'social worker'), सामाजिक न्याय (saamaajik nyaay, 'social justice'), सामाजिक बुराई (saamaajik buraaii, 'social evil'). It has a slightly formal, civic-minded flavor rather than a casual chit-chat one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सामाजिक (saamaajik) with सामान्य (saamaany, 'general/common') because of the similar sound -- they are unrelated in meaning. 2) सामाजिक (saamaajik) describes society-level matters (सामाजिक समस्या (saamaajik samasyaa, 'social problem')); for a person's friendly, outgoing nature, native speakers usually say मिलनसार (milansaar) rather than सामाजिक (saamaajik), unlike English 'social' which can describe a person directly.

Sense 2 : social (as in social media, social networking)
adjective Almost always seen in the fixed collocation सोशल मीडिया (soshal miidiyaa) using the English loanword, but सामाजिक मीडिया (saamaajik miidiyaa) also appears in more formal or purist Hindi writing.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Government notices and Doordarshan-style formal Hindi prefer सामाजिक मीडिया (saamaajik miidiyaa) over the everyday सोशल मीडिया (soshal miidiyaa) that most people actually say out loud.
