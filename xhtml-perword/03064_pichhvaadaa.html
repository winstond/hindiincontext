पिछवाड़ा (pichhvaadaa)

noun A homely, everyday word with two related senses: the physical 'backyard/rear area' of a house or building, and the informal, slightly cheeky body-part sense meaning 'backside/buttocks.'
Sense 1 : backyard, rear area of a house or property
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पीछे का हिस्सा (piichhe kaa hissaa, descriptive phrase), पिछला हिस्सा (pichhlaa hissaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In small-town and older Delhi-NCR homes, the पिछवाड़ा (pichhvaadaa) is where the real household life happens -- clothes dry on lines, spare firewood or coolers are stashed, and kids sneak off to play cricket. It's the practical, workaday opposite of the formal front porch or drawing room.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पिछवाड़ा (pichhvaadaa) with बगीचा (bagiichaa, 'garden'); पिछवाड़ा (pichhvaadaa) simply names the rear space of a property, whether it's a garden, a storage area, or just bare ground -- it doesn't imply cultivation.

Sense 2 : backside, buttocks (informal/humorous)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चूतड़ (chootad, cruder/more direct), पीछा (piichhaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word Hindi speakers reach for in the same breath as a mock threat or a scolding joke -- 'पिछवाड़े पर लात पड़ेगी' (pichhvaade par laat padegii, 'you'll get a kick in the backside') is the kind of line an elder brother or a strict uncle says half-seriously. It stays light and comic, never clinical or crude.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is casual and often joking in tone -- it's fine among friends or family but not something you'd use in a formal or polite context, where डिब्बा (dibbaa) or a clinical term would be avoided too, and one would simply not refer to the body part directly.
