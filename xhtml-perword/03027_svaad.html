स्वाद (svaad)

noun Mainly a noun meaning 'taste/flavor' in both the literal (food) and figurative (enjoyment, relish for life or an experience) sense; also gives rise to a common adjective-like compound use meaning 'tasty.' The two are related but kept as separate senses below.
Sense 1 : taste, flavor (of food or drink)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ज़ायका (zaaykaa, colloquial), रस (ras, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: स्वाद (svaad) is a word every Delhi cook and food-loving uncle uses constantly -- 'is mein कुछ स्वाद नहीं है' (is men kuchh svaad nahiin hai, 'there's no flavor in this') is a classic kitchen complaint. It's also one of the six classical 'rasa'-adjacent taste categories (sweet, sour, salty, bitter, pungent, astringent) that Ayurvedic and traditional food talk still references.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say खाना अच्छा स्वाद है (khaanaa achchhaa svaad hai) trying to say 'the food tastes good' -- the natural construction uses लगना (lagnaa): खाने का स्वाद अच्छा लगता है (khaane kaa svaad achchhaa lagtaa hai, 'the food's taste feels/seems good') or simply खाना स्वादिष्ट है (khaanaa svaadisht hai, 'the food is tasty').
Sample Sentences:
इस दाल में कुछ स्वाद नहीं है। There's no flavor in this daal.
(is daal men kuchh svaad nahiin hai।)

Sense 2 : enjoyment, relish, pleasure (figurative extension of taste)
noun, masculine Common in the frame स्वाद लेना (svaad lenaa, 'to take relish/enjoyment in something'), often with से (se) marking the thing enjoyed, e.g. काम से स्वाद लेना (kaam se svaad lenaa, 'to relish one's work').
Synonyms: मज़ा (mazaa), आनंद (aanand, somewhat more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear जिंदगी का स्वाद लेना (jindgii kaa svaad lenaa, 'to savor life') in songs and heartfelt speeches -- the same word for a food's flavor extends naturally to the flavor of an experience, which is a very Hindi way of thinking about pleasure as something almost edible.
Common Mistakes: 1) स्वाद लेना (svaad lenaa) can describe savoring life, a book, or an experience, not just food -- learners often restrict it to eating and miss this broader, more literary-tinged figurative use.
Sample Sentences:
वह जिंदगी का स्वाद लेना जानता है। He knows how to savor life.
(vah jindgii kaa svaad lenaa jaantaa hai।)
