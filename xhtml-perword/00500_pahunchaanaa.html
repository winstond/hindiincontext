पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa)

verb The causative of पहुँचना (pahunchnaa, 'to arrive/reach') -- means 'to deliver, cause to reach' something/someone to a destination, whether physically (a parcel, a person) or figuratively (harm, benefit, a message).
Sense 1 : to deliver / drop off (a person or thing at a place)
verb, transitive Takes को on the destination and often को on a human recipient too: सामान घर पहुँचाना (saamaan ghar pahunchaanaa, 'to deliver the goods home'); दोस्त को स्टेशन पहुँचाना (dost ko steshan pahunchaanaa, 'to drop a friend at the station').
Synonyms: छोड़ना (chhodnaa, 'to drop off', in this sense), डिलीवर करना (diliivar karnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common in everyday requests like मुझे स्टेशन पहुँचा दो (mujhe steshan pahunchaa do, 'drop me at the station'), often with the compound verb pairing with दे/जा (पहुँचा देना (pahunchaa denaa)) to add a sense of completion or favor -- a classic example of how causatives and compound verbs stack in spoken Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छोड़ना (chhodnaa) for 'drop off' but पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa) more specifically stresses that the thing/person actually arrived safely at the destination, not just that you left them somewhere. 2) The destination takes a bare oblique or पर/को depending on the noun -- स्टेशन पहुँचाना (steshan pahunchaanaa) vs घर पर पहुँचाना (ghar par pahunchaanaa) -- both are heard, so don't over-fix one to the other.
Sample Sentences:
क्या तुम मुझे स्टेशन पहुँचा दोगे? Will you drop me at the station?
(kyaa tum mujhe steshan pahunchaa doge?)

Sense 2 : to cause harm/benefit to reach someone (figurative)
verb, transitive Common in fixed collocations: नुकसान पहुँचाना (nuksaan pahunchaanaa, 'to cause harm'), चोट पहुँचाना (chot pahunchaanaa, 'to hurt/injure'), फ़ायदा पहुँचाना (faaydaa pahunchaanaa, 'to benefit'). The recipient of the effect is marked with को.
Synonyms: करना (karnaa, in fixed collocations like नुकसान करना), देना (denaa, partial overlap)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This figurative extension is everywhere in news and formal speech -- सरकार को नुकसान पहुँचाना (sarkaar ko nuksaan pahunchaanaa, 'to damage the government['s interests]') -- because पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa) keeps the image of something concrete (harm, benefit, a blow) physically arriving at its target, even when the 'thing' is abstract.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use करना (karnaa) alone where Hindi prefers the पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa) collocation, e.g. नुकसान पहुँचाना (nuksaan pahunchaanaa) sounds far more natural than नुकसान करना (nuksaan karnaa) to someone specific -- the causative frames the harm as something that 'reaches' the victim.
Sample Sentences:
इस फैसले से किसानों को बहुत नुकसान पहुँचा। This decision caused great harm to the farmers.

Sense 3 : to convey / pass on (a message, information)
verb, transitive Used with सन्देश/खबर/बात as object and को for the recipient: मेरी बात उस तक पहुँचाना (merii baat us tak pahunchaanaa, 'convey my words to him'). Often paired with तक rather than को when stressing the message travelling all the way to someone.
Synonyms: बताना (bataanaa, partial overlap, simpler), सूचित करना (soochit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Notice how यह बात उन तक पहुँचा दो (yah baat un tak pahunchaa do, 'get this word to them') treats a message like a physical parcel being delivered -- a nice window into how Hindi often extends motion verbs to abstract communication.
