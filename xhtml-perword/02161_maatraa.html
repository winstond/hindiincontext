मात्रा (maatraa)

noun Mainly a measurement/quantity word in everyday speech, but also the specific grammar term for a vowel sign attached to a consonant in Devanagari (maatraa).
Sense 1 : amount, quantity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मिक़दार (miqdaar), परिमाण (parimaan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in kitchens and doctor's advice: नमक की मात्रा (namak kii maatraa, 'the amount of salt') or दवा की मात्रा (davaa kii maatraa, 'the dosage of medicine'). It's the go-to word whenever Hindi needs to talk about 'how much' of something rather than 'how many.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for संख्या (sankhyaa) here, but संख्या (sankhyaa) is for countable numbers/counts, while मात्रा (maatraa) is for measurable amounts (of salt, medicine, time, etc.) -- pick based on whether you're counting or measuring.

(is nuskhe men namak kii maatraa thodii zyaadaa hai।)

Sense 2 : vowel sign/diacritic in Devanagari script
noun, feminine Refers specifically to the dependent vowel signs attached to consonants (like the ि in कि (ki) or ा in का (kaa)), as distinct from the independent vowel letters (स्वर, svar) themselves.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Every Hindi-medium schoolkid grows up hearing teachers say मात्रा की गलती मत करो (maatraa kii galtii mat karo, 'don't make a vowel-sign mistake') -- it's the single most common category of spelling error called out in Hindi classrooms, since a wrong or missing मात्रा (maatraa) can completely change a word's meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a script/spelling term, not a sound term -- getting a मात्रा (maatraa) wrong in writing (e.g. writing सिनेमा (sinemaa) as सीनेमा, siinemaa) is called a मात्रा की गलती (maatraa kii galtii, 'diacritic mistake'), a very common phrase in school correction.
Sample Sentences:
इस शब्द में मात्रा की गलती है। There is a vowel-diacritic mistake in this word.
(is shabd men maatraa kii galtii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
