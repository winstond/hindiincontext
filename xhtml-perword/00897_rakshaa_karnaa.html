रक्षा करना (rakshaa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the noun रक्षा (rakshaa, 'protection, defense') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means 'to protect, to defend, to save from harm.'
Sense 1 : to protect, defend, safeguard
verb, transitive Takes the person or thing being protected with की (kii): माँ बच्चे की रक्षा करती है (maan bachche kii rakshaa kartii hai, 'the mother protects the child').
Synonyms: बचाना (bachaanaa), सुरक्षा करना (surakshaa karnaa), हिफ़ाज़त करना (hifaazat karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a formal, almost solemn weight -- you'll hear it in contexts like देश की रक्षा करना (desh kii rakshaa karnaa, 'to protect the country'), or addressed to a deity asking for protection, as in भगवान हमारी रक्षा करें (bhagvaan hamaarii rakshaa karen, 'may God protect us'). It is less used for everyday small acts of saving someone (for that, बचाना (bachaanaa) is far more natural), and more for duty-bound or larger-scale protection -- soldiers, guardians, laws, and gods.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try को (ko) for the protected party, following the pattern of simple transitive verbs, but this compound requires की (kii) linking रक्षा (rakshaa) to its object -- रक्षा करना (rakshaa karnaa) behaves grammatically like 'to do the protection of' someone. 2) Do not confuse with बचाना (bachaanaa), which is more about rescuing from an immediate danger; रक्षा करना (rakshaa karnaa) has a broader, often ongoing or protective-duty sense, as in a guard, deity, or law 'protecting' something.
