अभिनेत्री (abhinetrii)

noun A single, straightforward sense: a female actress/performer. Related to अभिनेता (abhinetaa), the male counterpart.
Sense 1 : female actor, actress
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नायिका (naayikaa, often the lead/heroine specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly in Bollywood gossip magazines and entertainment news -- headlines love pairing अभिनेता (abhinetaa) and अभिनेत्री (abhinetrii) when announcing a new film's cast. It's a fully Sanskritic, neutral, everyday word -- no informality or stigma attached, unlike some English loanwords that get used more casually in spoken Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use अभिनेता (abhinetaa) for women out of habit -- अभिनेत्री (abhinetrii) is the expected form when referring to a woman, especially in writing and news media. 2) Don't confuse with नायिका (naayikaa), which specifically means the heroine/lead female character of a film or story, not just any actress -- a supporting-role performer is अभिनेत्री (abhinetrii) but not necessarily नायिका (naayikaa).
