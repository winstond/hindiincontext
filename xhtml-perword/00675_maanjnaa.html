माँजना (maanjnaa)

verb A physical verb meaning 'to scrub/scour' (utensils, floors), with a common figurative extension to polishing skill or refining oneself through practice.
Sense 1 : to scrub or scour (utensils, vessels, floors) clean
verb, transitive
Synonyms: रगड़ना (ragadnaa, to rub/scrub), साफ़ करना (saaf karnaa, broader 'to clean')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: बर्तन माँजना (bartan maanjnaa, 'to scrub the dishes') is a classic household chore phrase in Delhi-NCR homes -- often assigned to whoever's turn it is, or spoken of as the domestic help's (kaam vaalii) daily task. You'll hear mothers say it as a light threat too: 'बर्तन माँजने पड़ेंगे' (bartan maanjne padenge, 'you'll have to scrub the dishes') if a kid misbehaves.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for साफ़ करना (saaf karnaa) for all cleaning tasks, but माँजना (maanjnaa) specifically implies vigorous scrubbing with a scouring agent (ash, soap, a scrubber) -- it would sound odd applied to, say, wiping a table with a cloth. 2) The object is typically the vessel itself, e.g. बर्तन माँजना (bartan maanjnaa, 'to scrub the utensils'), not the dirt or grease being removed.

Sense 2 : to hone, polish, or refine (a skill, talent, or oneself) through repeated practice
verb, transitive Commonly used reflexively or with abstract nouns like हुनर (hunar, skill) or ख़ुद (khud, oneself) as the object -- e.g. ख़ुद को माँजना (khud ko maanjnaa, 'to polish/refine oneself').
Synonyms: निखारना (nikhaarnaa, to refine/enhance), तराशना (taraashnaa, to carve/sculpt, often for talent or personality)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in the context of sports or arts training -- a coach might say a player's game has been 'माँजा गया है' (maanjaa gayaa hai, 'has been honed') through years of practice. It carries the same 'scrub until it shines' image as the literal sense, just applied to ability rather than a pot.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is easy to miss if you only know the 'scrub dishes' meaning -- context (talent, skill, personality as the object) signals the shift to 'hone/refine.' 2) Do not confuse with तराशना (taraashnaa), which suggests shaping from a raw state; माँजना (maanjnaa) suggests polishing something already formed to make it shine or sharpen further.
