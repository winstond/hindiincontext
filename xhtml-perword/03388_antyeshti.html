अंत्येष्टि (antyeshti)

noun A formal, Sanskrit-derived word for a funeral or the last rites/cremation ceremony; single sense.
Sense 1 : funeral / last rites, cremation ceremony
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अंतिम संस्कार (antim sanskaar), दाह संस्कार (daah sanskaar, specifically cremation), क्रिया कर्म (kriyaa karm, colloquial)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet अंत्येष्टि (antyeshti) mostly in newspapers, obituaries, or formal announcements -- for instance a report might say नेता की अंत्येष्टि आज होगी (netaa kii antyeshti aaj hogii, 'the leader's funeral will take place today'). In daily conversation among family and friends, people almost always say अंतिम संस्कार (antim sanskaar) instead; reaching for अंत्येष्टि (antyeshti) in casual speech can come across as oddly bookish, the way 'obsequies' would sound in English next to 'funeral.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अंतिम संस्कार (antim sanskaar) instead, which is the far more common everyday term; अंत्येष्टि (antyeshti) sounds noticeably more formal or written, like something you'd see in a news report rather than say to a grieving neighbor. 2) This word refers to the rite/ceremony itself, not the corpse or the mourning period, so it doesn't substitute for words like शव (shav, corpse) or शोक (shok, mourning/grief).
