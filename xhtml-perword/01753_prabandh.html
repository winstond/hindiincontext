प्रबंध (prabandh)

noun A formal noun meaning arrangement or management; also extends to the sense of an administrative system and, in older/literary contexts, a written composition. Related to प्रबंधन (prabandhan, management, the more common word for the abstract discipline).
Sense 1 : arrangement, organizing of something (e.g. for an event)
noun, masculine Often paired with करना (karnaa) -- प्रबंध करना (prabandh karnaa, to make arrangements) -- as a light-verb construction.
Synonyms: इंतज़ाम (intzaam), व्यवस्था (vyavasthaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word on wedding cards and event banners -- खाने-पीने का प्रबंध (khaane-piine kaa prabandh, arrangements for food and drink) is a classic phrase for describing hospitality logistics at a shaadi (shaadii) or function.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for इंतज़ाम (intzaam) by default since it's more common in casual speech; प्रबंध (prabandh) is not wrong but sounds a touch more formal/written, so it's more at home on an invitation card or office notice than in a quick phone call. 2) The phrase is प्रबंध करना (prabandh karnaa), not प्रबंध देना (prabandh denaa) or प्रबंध बनाना (prabandh banaanaa).
Sample Sentences:
शादी में खाने-पीने का प्रबंध बहुत अच्छा था। The arrangements for food and drink at the wedding were very good.
(shaadii men khaane-piine kaa prabandh bahut achchhaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : management, administration (of an institution, business, or system)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रबंधन (prabandhan), संचालन (sanchaalan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll most often meet this sense in the compound title प्रबंध निदेशक (prabandh nideshak, Managing Director), a very common designation on Indian business cards and company letterheads.
Common Mistakes: 1) In modern usage, प्रबंधन (prabandhan) has largely taken over as the standard word for 'management' as an abstract discipline (e.g. in college course names); प्रबंध (prabandh) in this sense now sounds a bit more institutional/older, often surviving in fixed titles like प्रबंध निदेशक (prabandh nideshak, managing director).

Sense 3 : a literary composition or treatise, especially a long narrative poem
noun, masculine
Synonyms: रचना (rachnaa, broader term), काव्य (kaavy, poetic composition)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This literary sense shows up in the term प्रबंध-काव्य (prabandh-kaavy, narrative/epic poem), a term from Hindi literary criticism for long narrative poems like those of Tulsidas (tulsiidaas); a learner focused on conversation will meet this far less often than the other two senses.
