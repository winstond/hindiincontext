सभी (sabhii)

pronoun An emphatic form of सब (sab, 'all'), meaning 'all, every one, everybody/everything' -- used as both a pronoun and an adjective-like quantifier with no separate senses beyond this single core meaning.
Sense 1 : all, everyone, everything (emphatic)
pronoun
Synonyms: सब (sab), तमाम (tamaam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear सभी (sabhii) constantly in announcements, speeches, and signage -- सभी छात्र ध्यान दें (sabhii chhaatr dhyaan den, 'all students pay attention') -- because it sounds a touch more formal and inclusive than सब (sab), which is more of the everyday, conversational choice. Think of सभी (sabhii) as the word a teacher, official, or host uses to address a whole group with a bit of extra warmth or formality, like 'one and all.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat सभी (sabhii) and सब (sab) as needing different grammar, but they behave the same way -- सभी (sabhii) just adds emphasis, like 'each and every' versus plain 'all'. 2) Before a noun, सभी (sabhii) does not change form for gender or number, so it is easy to mistakenly add an ending like सभी*ओं* -- the plurality is carried by the noun and its verb agreement, not by सभी (sabhii) itself.
Sample Sentences:
सभी छात्र कृपया ध्यान दें। All students please pay attention.
(sabhii chhaatr kripyaa dhyaan den।)
