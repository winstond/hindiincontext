रोशन (roshan)

adjective An adjective meaning 'lit up, bright, illuminated,' used both literally (a lit lamp or room) and figuratively (a bright face, a name made famous). Related to the noun रोशनी (roshnii, 'light, brightness').
Sense 1 : lit up, illuminated (physically bright)
adjective
Synonyms: प्रकाशित (prakaashit, formal), जगमगाता (jagamgaataa, sparkling/glittering)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around Diwali: घर रोशन है (ghar roshan hai, 'the house is lit up') describing homes strung with diyas and lights. It pairs naturally with करना (karnaa) in the causative sense of 'to light up' -- घर को रोशन करना (ghar ko roshan karnaa, 'to light up the house').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse रोशन (roshan) with रोशनी (roshnii) -- रोशन (roshan) is the adjective describing something as lit up, while रोशनी (roshnii) is the noun 'light' itself, as in the light source or glow.

Sense 2 : bright, radiant (of a face, eyes, future)
adjective
Synonyms: चमकता (chamaktaa, glowing), उज्ज्वल (ujjval, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A mother telling her child तेरा भविष्य रोशन हो (teraa bhavishy roshan ho, 'may your future be bright') is a very common blessing in Hindi households -- रोशन (roshan) here carries the same warm hopefulness as 'bright' does in English for a person's prospects or expression.

Sense 3 : famous, illustrious (of a name)
adjective Common in the fixed phrase नाम रोशन करना (naam roshan karnaa, 'to bring honor/fame to one's name'), where रोशन (roshan) figuratively extends 'lit up' to 'made bright/famous.'
Synonyms: प्रसिद्ध (prasiddh), मशहूर (mashahoor)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: अपने माँ-बाप का नाम रोशन करना (apne maan-baap kaa naam roshan karnaa, 'to make one's parents' name shine/proud') is a phrase parents use often, tied to the cultural weight placed on children bringing honor to the family name.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
