असफल (asphal)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'unsuccessful/failed'; formed by adding the negative prefix अ (a) to सफल (saphal, 'successful').
Sense 1 : unsuccessful, failed
adjective
Synonyms: नाकाम (naakaam), नाकामयाब (naakaamyaab)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around exam season -- परीक्षा में असफल होना (pariikshaa men asphal honaa, 'to fail in an exam') is a stock phrase every Indian student dreads. Its opposite सफल (saphal) is just as common, so learning the pair together is efficient.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to negate सफल (saphal) with नहीं (nahiin) instead of using the prefixed form असफल (asphal) -- both सफल नहीं (saphal nahiin, 'not successful') and असफल (asphal) are heard, but असफल (asphal) is the more natural single-word adjective in writing and formal speech. 2) With होना (honaa) the person or thing that fails takes no special postposition -- वह असफल हुआ (vah asphal huaa, 'he failed'), unlike English 'failed at' which might tempt learners to add a postposition.
Sample Sentences:
वह परीक्षा में असफल हो गया। He failed in the exam.
(vah pariikshaa men asphal ho gayaa।)
