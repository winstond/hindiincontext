पेशाब करना (peshaab karnaa)

verb A common everyday compound meaning 'to urinate,' built from the noun पेशाब (peshaab, urine) plus करना (karnaa, to do).
Sense 1 : to urinate
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: सू सू करना (soo soo karnaa, childish/informal), मूतना (mootnaa, coarse/vulgar), टट्टी-पेशाब जाना (tattii-peshaab jaanaa, to go relieve oneself, euphemistic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday, socially acceptable term you would use with a doctor, a child, or in polite conversation, sitting in the same comfortable middle register as 'urinate' or 'pee' in English -- neither clinical nor crude. With small children, families almost always switch to the cuter सू सू (soo soo) instead, much like 'pee-pee' in English nurseries.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for the blunt verb मूतना (mootnaa) thinking it is neutral, when in fact पेशाब करना (peshaab karnaa) is the polite, standard way to say this in mixed or formal company. 2) To ask permission or express need politely, Hindi speakers often prefer an indirect phrase like मुझे बाथरूम जाना है (mujhe baathroom jaanaa hai, 'I need to go to the bathroom') over stating पेशाब करना (peshaab karnaa) directly, especially with strangers or elders.
