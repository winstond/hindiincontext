भावना (bhaavnaa)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'feeling, emotion' or 'sentiment' -- often used in the plural भावनाएँ (bhaavnaaen) when talking about people's collective sentiments. Related to the adjective भावुक (bhaavuk, emotional/sentimental) and the verb भाना (bhaanaa, to seem pleasing).
Sense 1 : feeling, emotion (inner experience)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अहसास (ahsaas), जज़्बात (jazbaat, plural, often used for deeper/romantic feelings)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear भावनाएँ (bhaavnaaen) constantly in the context of 'hurting someone's feelings' -- लोगों की भावनाओं को ठेस पहुँचाना (logon kii bhaavnaaon ko thes pahunchaanaa, 'to hurt people's feelings/sentiments') is a stock phrase in news and public discourse, especially around religious or communal sensitivities.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अहसास (ahsaas) in every context, but भावना (bhaavnaa) is the more common choice when talking about internal emotional states in a slightly more reflective or literary register -- for raw physical/gut sensations, अहसास (ahsaas) fits better. 2) The plural भावनाएँ (bhaavnaaen) is very frequently used even when English would say 'feeling' in the singular, e.g. talking about someone's 'feelings' generally.

Sense 2 : sentiment, collective feeling/opinion (especially religious or communal)
noun, feminine Frequently appears as धार्मिक भावनाएँ (dhaarmik bhaavnaaen, 'religious sentiments') or जनभावना (janbhaavnaa, 'public sentiment') in formal, media, or political contexts.
Synonyms: जज़्बात (jazbaat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is the backbone of a very common phrase in Indian public life: भावनाओं को आहत करना (bhaavnaaon ko aahat karnaa, 'to hurt sentiments'), used constantly in news reports about controversies, protests, or court cases. It signals a more formal, collective register than the personal 'feeling' sense above.

Sense 3 : intention, idea, notion (as in 'the sense/spirit in which something is meant')
noun, feminine Common in the fixed phrase इस भावना से (is bhaavnaa se, 'with this intent/spirit') or यह भावना (yah bhaavnaa, 'this notion/idea'), used to describe the spirit behind an action or statement.
Synonyms: विचार (vichaar), मंशा (manshaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is a more formal, writerly use -- you'll meet it in speeches, essays, or official statements ('the spirit behind this scheme is...') rather than casual chat, where विचार (vichaar) or मंशा (manshaa) might feel more natural.
