सप्ताह (saptaah)

noun A single, straightforward sense: 'week' as a unit of time. Formal/neutral register compared to its everyday synonym हफ़्ता (haftaa).
Sense 1 : week (period of seven days)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हफ़्ता (haftaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear सप्ताह (saptaah) constantly on the news and in official contexts -- सप्ताहांत (saptaahaant, 'weekend') is the formal counterpart to the more casual वीकेंड (viikend). In daily bazaar chat, though, most Delhi-NCR folks reach for हफ़्ता (haftaa) -- 'दो हफ़्ते बाद (do hafte baad, 'after two weeks')' feels far more natural at the sabzi mandi than the Sanskritic सप्ताह (saptaah).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to हफ़्ता (haftaa) in speech and सप्ताह (saptaah) in writing/news -- both mean the same thing, but mixing them up isn't wrong, just a register mismatch worth noticing. 2) Days of the week combine with वार (vaar) or बार (baar) endings (सोमवार (somvaar, 'Monday')), not with सप्ताह (saptaah) itself -- learners sometimes try to build day names from this word.

हर सप्ताह यहाँ एक नई कार्यशाला होती है। Every week there is a new workshop held here.
(har saptaah yahaan ek naii kaaryshaalaa hotii hai।)
