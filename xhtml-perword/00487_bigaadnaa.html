बिगाड़ना (bigaadnaa)

verb The causative partner of बिगड़ना (bigadnaa, 'to become spoiled/go wrong'): this word means to spoil, ruin, or mess something up, and extends to spoiling a relationship or mood.
Sense 1 : to spoil, ruin, damage (a thing)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: खराब करना (kharaab karnaa), नष्ट करना (nasht karnaa, stronger, 'destroy'), चौपट करना (chaupat karnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear मत बिगाड़ो (mat bigaado, 'don't ruin it') from an exasperated cook when someone adds the wrong masala at the wrong time, or from a parent when a kid fiddles with something delicate. It pairs naturally with काम (kaam, 'work/task'), खाना (khaanaa, 'food'), and मूड (mood, 'mood') as objects.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खराब करना (kharaab karnaa) in every context, but बिगाड़ना (bigaadnaa) is the more natural choice when something that was working or fine gets messed up through carelessness or interference, e.g. काम बिगाड़ना (kaam bigaadnaa, 'to mess up a task/plan'). 2) As a transitive verb it takes ने (ne) marking on the subject in the perfective, unlike its intransitive partner बिगड़ना (bigadnaa) -- learners sometimes drop ने (ne) here by analogy with the intransitive form.

(pliiz mat bigaado, main ise badii mushkil se banaa rahaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : to spoil (a person), indulge to the point of bad behavior
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सिर पर चढ़ाना (sir par chadhaanaa, idiomatic, 'to let someone get overindulgent')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic line grandparents get teased with: दादी ने बच्चे को बिगाड़ दिया है (daadii ne bachche ko bigaad diyaa hai, 'grandma has spoiled the child') -- said half-fondly, half-critically, often about excess sweets or too much leniency.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to खराब करना (kharaab karnaa) for 'spoiling a child,' but बिगाड़ना (bigaadnaa) is the idiomatic verb Hindi speakers use for this exact meaning, e.g. बच्चे को बिगाड़ना (bachche ko bigaadnaa, 'to spoil the child') -- note the child is marked with को (ko) as the affected object.

Sense 3 : to spoil, sour (a relationship, mood, or situation)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: खराब करना (kharaab karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: रिश्ता बिगाड़ना (rishtaa bigaadnaa, 'to ruin a relationship') and बात बिगाड़ना (baat bigaadnaa, 'to ruin/complicate a matter') are everyday phrases you'll hear in family disputes or office gossip -- the sense of 'ruining something that was previously fine' carries over cleanly from the physical-object sense.
