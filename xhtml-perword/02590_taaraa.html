तारा (taaraa)

noun Mainly a noun meaning 'star', both the celestial object and the figurative 'star/celebrity' sense; also used as a common given name.
Sense 1 : star (celestial body)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सितारा (sitaaraa), नक्षत्र (nakshatr, more technical/astronomical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Kids in India learn the nursery rhyme line टिमटिमाते तारे (timtimaate taare, 'twinkling stars') early on, so तारा (taaraa) carries a warm, childhood-song association alongside its plain astronomical meaning. सितारा (sitaaraa) is an equally common everyday synonym, especially in Bollywood contexts (see the 'celebrity' sense below).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तारा (taaraa) with तार (taar, 'wire/telegram') because of the similar spelling -- the final आ (aa) matters.

Sense 2 : star, celebrity (figurative)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सितारा (sitaaraa, most common in this sense), स्टार (staar, English loanword, very common in film contexts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday speech about film and cricket, सितारा (sitaaraa) or the English loan स्टार (staar) is actually heard more often than तारा (taaraa) for 'celebrity' -- तारा (taaraa) in this figurative sense leans slightly more literary/formal, as in फ़िल्मी तारा (filmii taaraa, 'film star') in written contexts.

Sense 3 : given name (proper noun)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: तारा (taaraa) is a widely used personal name for girls across North India, drawing directly on the 'star' meaning -- much like 'Stella' or 'Estelle' in English carry the same root idea.
