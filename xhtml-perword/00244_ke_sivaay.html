के सिवाय (ke sivaay)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'except for' or 'besides' (ke sivaay); it follows the oblique noun/pronoun it governs, like other compound postpositions built on के.
Sense 1 : except, apart from (exclusion)
postposition Governs the oblique case of the preceding noun/pronoun, e.g. मेरे सिवाय (mere sivaay, 'except for me'), and is often reinforced with कोई नहीं (koii nahiin) or कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin) elsewhere in the sentence to stress totality of exclusion.
Synonyms: के अलावा (ke alaavaa), के अतिरिक्त (ke atirikt, formal), बिना (binaa, not equivalent, means 'without')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear के सिवाय (ke sivaay) constantly in complaints and declarations of exclusivity -- तुम्हारे सिवाय कोई नहीं (tumhaare sivaay koii nahiin, 'no one except you') is the classic emotional register, whether in a filmi dialogue or a mother's mild guilt-trip about attention. के अलावा (ke alaavaa) is the more neutral, everyday sibling of this phrase and can often substitute for it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget to put the preceding noun/pronoun in the oblique case, saying राम के सिवाय (raam ke sivaay) correctly but stumbling with pronouns like मैं (main) which must become मेरे (mere) before सिवाय (sivaay). 2) Confusing के सिवाय (ke sivaay) with बिना (binaa) -- सिवाय (sivaay) means 'except' (excluding one thing from a group), not 'without'.
