कूड़ेदान (koodedaan)

noun A straightforward compound noun meaning 'dustbin' or 'trash can' -- कूड़ा (koodaa, 'garbage') plus दान (daan, a container-forming suffix seen in words like पानदान (paandaan)). One core sense.
Sense 1 : dustbin, trash can, waste bin
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कचरा पेटी (kachraa petii), डस्टबिन (dastbin, common in urban speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on municipal bins in parks and markets across Delhi-NCR, often stenciled next to civic slogans about cleanliness (a legacy of the Swachh Bharat (svachchh bhaarat) campaign). In everyday speech at home, many people just say कूड़ा (koodaa) and point, or say कूड़ेदान में डालो (koodedaan men daalo, 'put it in the dustbin') to kids -- a very common household instruction.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say कूड़ा (koodaa) when they mean the container itself -- कूड़ा (koodaa) is the garbage/trash, कूड़ेदान (koodedaan) is the bin you put it in. 2) The compound is fixed -- avoid inventing variants like कूड़ा-दान (koodaa-daan) with a space or hyphen in casual writing; it is written solid.
