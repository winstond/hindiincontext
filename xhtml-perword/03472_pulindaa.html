पुलिंदा (pulindaa)

noun A bundle or packet of things tied or wrapped together, most often papers or documents; also used colloquially to describe a bulky, unwieldy collection of things.
Sense 1 : bundle, packet (of papers, documents, letters, etc.)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गठ्ठर (gaththar), बंडल (bandal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of an old lawyer's or clerk's desk piled with कागज़ों का पुलिंदा (kaagzon kaa pulindaa, 'a bundle of papers') tied up with string -- this word carries a slightly old-fashioned, bureaucratic-office flavor, evoking court files and government paperwork more than a neat modern folder.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use पुलिंदा (pulindaa) for any random pile, but it specifically implies things tied or wrapped together into one unit -- a loose heap of unrelated objects is better called ढेर (dher).

(mez par kaagzon kaa ek pulindaa rakhaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : a large, unwieldy mass or collection (figurative)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ढेर (dher), जंजाल (janjaal, figurative, complicated mess)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Used figuratively, समस्याओं का पुलिंदा (samasyaaon kaa pulindaa, 'a bundle of problems') is a common way to describe someone or something loaded down with issues -- the imagery of a heavy, tied-up bundle maps neatly onto the English idea of being 'weighed down' by troubles.
Sample Sentences:
यह लड़का तो समस्याओं का पुलिंदा है। This boy is a bundle of problems.
(yah ladkaa to samasyaaon kaa pulindaa hai।)
