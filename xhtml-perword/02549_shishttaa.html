शिष्टता (shishttaa)

noun A formal noun meaning politeness or courteous behavior, drawn from शिष्ट (shisht, 'cultured/well-behaved') plus the abstract-noun suffix ता (taa).
Sense 1 : politeness, courtesy, refined manners
noun, feminine
Synonyms: विनम्रता (vinamrtaa), सभ्यता (sabhytaa, broader: civility/decency), भलमनसाहत (bhalmansaahat, colloquial)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the kind of word you meet in etiquette columns, school essays on 'good manners,' or a grandmother's lecture on how to behave with guests, rather than in daily chit-chat -- in conversation people usually just say विनम्रता से बोलो (vinamrtaa se bolo, 'speak politely') or तमीज़ से बात करो (tamiiz se baat karo, 'talk with manners'). It pairs naturally with शिष्ट (shisht), the adjective describing a well-mannered, cultured person.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शिष्टता (shishttaa) in casual speech where विनम्रता (vinamrtaa) or just सीधे शब्दों में बात करना (siidhe shabdon men baat karnaa, 'to speak plainly/nicely') would sound more natural -- शिष्टता (shishttaa) leans formal/written. 2) It describes a quality or manner of behavior, not a single polite act; for a specific polite gesture, speakers usually say कुछ शिष्टता से कहना (kuchh shishttaa se kahnaa, 'to say something politely') rather than using the noun alone as an event.
