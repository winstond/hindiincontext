वैसे (vaise)

adverb/adjective A flexible word with two main lives: as an adverb/discourse-marker meaning 'in that way' or 'by the way, actually,' and as an adjective form (oblique/plural of वैसा (vaisaa)) meaning 'that kind of, such.' The discourse-marker use is by far the most common in everyday conversation.
Sense 1 : by the way / actually / otherwise (discourse marker)
adverb Typically opens or interjects into a sentence to pivot the topic, soften a statement, or add an aside; does not need a following clause to feel complete.
Synonyms: वास्तव में (vaastav men, more formal, 'actually'), अन्यथा (anythaa, literary, 'otherwise')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of those small words that makes your Hindi sound native the moment you start using it naturally -- Dilliwaale drop it into conversation constantly, almost like a verbal comma, to ease from one thought into the next. Notice how it often sits right at the start of a sentence, much like English 'anyway' or 'by the way.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often skip this word entirely and reach for 'by the way' constructions borrowed from English, but वैसे (vaise) is how native speakers actually pivot topics in casual talk. 2) It can also soften a mild contradiction or objection ('otherwise, actually...'), a shade learners tend to miss when they only learn it as 'by the way.'

वैसे, तुमने खाना खाया या नहीं? By the way, did you eat or not?
(vaise, tumne khaanaa khaayaa yaa nahiin?)

Sense 2 : in that way / like that (manner)
adverb Used to refer back to a manner already established in context, often pairing with ऐसे (aise, 'like this') in contrastive statements.
Synonyms: उस तरह (us tarah), उस तरीके से (us tariike se)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear ऐसे... वैसे (aise... vaise, 'like this... like that') used together when someone is comparing two ways of doing something, a very natural pairing in spoken Hindi.
Sample Sentences:
अगर तुम वैसे बात करोगे, तो कोई नहीं सुनेगा। If you talk like that, no one will listen.
(agar tum vaise baat karoge, to koii nahiin sunegaa।)

Sense 3 : that kind of, such (oblique/plural form of वैसा)
adjective This is the oblique or masculine plural inflection of वैसा (vaisaa); it appears before oblique-case nouns or plural masculine nouns, e.g. वैसे लोग (vaise log, 'such people').
Synonyms: उस तरह का (us tarah kaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is really just वैसा (vaisaa) wearing its oblique-case hat, so if you have learned the ऐसा/वैसा/कैसा (aisaa/vaisaa/kaisaa) family of correlative adjectives, this form should feel familiar rather than new.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this adjectival use with the much more frequent discourse-marker sense; context (a following noun) is the giveaway that it means 'that kind of' rather than 'by the way.'
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe vaise kapde pasand nahiin hain।)
