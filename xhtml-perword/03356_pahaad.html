पहाड़ (pahaad)

noun Primarily means 'mountain' or 'hill,' and extends figuratively to describe something felt as huge, daunting, or endless -- most famously in the idiom for a long, hard-to-pass stretch of time.
Sense 1 : mountain, hill
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पर्वत (parvat, formal), गिरि (giri, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: For most Delhi-NCR folks, पहाड़ (pahaad) instantly evokes summer trips to escape the heat -- Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital -- so पहाड़ जाना (pahaad jaanaa, 'to go to the mountains') often just means 'going on a hill vacation,' not literally climbing anything.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use पहाड़ (pahaad) for any elevated land, but in casual speech it also covers hill stations and hilly regions generally, e.g. पहाड़ जाना (pahaad jaanaa, 'to go to the hills') for a vacation, not just a single mountain peak.
Sample Sentences:
गर्मियों में हम हर साल पहाड़ जाते हैं। Every summer we go to the mountains.
(garmiyon men ham har saal pahaad jaate hain।)

Sense 2 : something huge, daunting, or seemingly endless (figurative)
noun, masculine Used predicatively in the fixed idiom X pahaad jaisaa honaa or in the classic phrase रात पहाड़ जैसी लगना (raat pahaad jaisii lagnaa, 'for the night to feel like a mountain,' i.e. endless).
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The classic line रात पहाड़ जैसी कट रही है (raat pahaad jaisii kat rahii hai, 'the night is passing like a mountain') is how someone describes a long, sleepless, or anxious night -- a very Hindi way of making an abstract feeling (endless time) physical and heavy, the way English might say 'this night is dragging on forever.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense almost always appears with लगना (lagnaa) or होना (honaa) rather than standing alone -- learners sometimes try to use पहाड़ (pahaad) as a standalone adjective for 'huge,' which sounds off; it needs the comparison frame (जैसा jaisaa) or the fixed idiom.
Sample Sentences:
बीमारी में रात पहाड़ जैसी कट रही थी। During the illness, the night was passing like a mountain.
(biimaarii men raat pahaad jaisii kat rahii thii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
उसके बिना हर रात पहाड़ जैसी कटती है। Without her, every night drags on like a mountain.
