घटना (ghatnaa)

noun/verb Two related but distinct senses: as a verb, 'to happen/occur' or 'to decrease/reduce'; as a noun, 'an event/incident'. The noun and the 'happen' verb sense share a root idea -- something that occurs.
Sense 1 : event, incident
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वाकया (vaakyaa), हादसा (haadsaa, often implies mishap/accident), वाक्या (vaakyaa, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on the news -- ताज़ा घटना (taazaa ghatnaa, 'breaking incident') or यह घटना कब हुई (yah ghatnaa kab huii, 'when did this event happen') -- it's the default word reporters and police use for 'incident.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with दुर्घटना (durghatnaa), which specifically means 'accident' -- घटना (ghatnaa) itself is neutral and can refer to any event, good or bad.
Sample Sentences:
कल रात एक अजीब घटना हुई। A strange incident happened last night.
(kal raat ek ajiib ghatnaa huii।)

Sense 2 : to happen, occur
verb, intransitive Used almost always in third person, describing events rather than actions of people; the subject is typically the event itself (e.g. दुर्घटना घटी, durghatnaa ghatii, 'the accident happened').
Synonyms: होना (honaa), वाकया होना (vaakyaa honaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense feels a notch more literary or journalistic than होना (honaa) -- you'd read it in a newspaper report more than hear it in casual chat, but it's still well within everyday comprehension.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to होना (honaa) for 'happen' and forget घटना (ghatnaa) exists as a more formal/written alternative, especially common in news reports and narration.
Sample Sentences:
कल रात यहाँ एक अजीब घटना घटी। Last night a strange incident happened here.
(kal raat yahaan ek ajiib ghatnaa ghatii।)

Sense 3 : to decrease, reduce (intransitive)
verb, intransitive Intransitive; describes a quantity or degree going down on its own (price, weight, speed, etc.). Its transitive causative counterpart is घटाना (ghataanaa, 'to reduce/subtract'), a separate lemma.
Synonyms: कम होना (kam honaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in everyday contexts like बुखार घट गया (bukhaar ghat gayaa, 'the fever went down') or दाम घट गए (daam ghat gae, 'the prices dropped') -- very practical, market-and-health kind of vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for घटाना (ghataanaa) when they actually mean the thing decreased by itself -- if there's no one doing the reducing, घटना (ghatnaa) is correct, not its causative.
Sample Sentences:
बुखार घट गया है। The fever has gone down.
(bukhaar ghat gayaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
