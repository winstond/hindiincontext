समाज (samaaj)

noun A single core sense: 'society' as a social collective; also extends naturally to 'community' in some contexts.
Sense 1 : society, the collective social order
noun, masculine
Synonyms: समुदाय (samudaay, community, narrower scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: समाज (samaaj) carries a moral weight in everyday Hindi that 'society' doesn't quite have in English -- when someone says समाज क्या कहेगा (samaaj kyaa kahegaa, 'what will society say'), they're invoking collective judgment, especially around marriage, reputation, and family honor. You'll hear it constantly in conversations about social change: समाज बदल रहा है (samaaj badal rahaa hai, 'society is changing'), often said with a mix of hope and resignation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use समाज (samaaj) where परिवार (parivaar, family) or समुदाय (samudaay, community) is meant -- समाज (samaaj) is broad, referring to society at large or a whole community, not a small group of relatives or friends. 2) Phrases like समाज में (samaaj men, 'in society') and समाज के लिए (samaaj ke lie, 'for society') are common collocations; learners often default to लोगों में (logon men, 'among people') where a native speaker would reach for समाज (samaaj men).

समाज क्या कहेगा? What will society say?
(samaaj kyaa kahegaa?)
