सांस्कृतिक (saanskritik)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'cultural,' related to संस्कृति (sanskriti, 'culture') from which it is derived.
Sense 1 : cultural
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see सांस्कृतिक (saanskritik) constantly on banners and invitation cards in Delhi -- सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम (saanskritik kaaryakram, 'cultural program') is the standard phrase for any school function, festival stage show, or embassy event featuring dance, music, or drama. It pairs naturally with words like विरासत (viraasat, 'heritage') and धरोहर (dharohar, 'heritage/legacy') in formal or civic contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write or say संस्कृतिक (sanskritik) by mistake, dropping the ा; the correct adjective form is सांस्कृतिक (saanskritik) with the long आ. 2) This word describes things related to culture (events, heritage, exchange) -- it is not used for personal politeness or manners, which is सुसंस्कृत (susanskrit) or शिष्ट (shisht).
