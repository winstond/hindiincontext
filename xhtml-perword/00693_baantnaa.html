बाँटना (baantnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to divide' or 'to distribute/share' -- the two meanings are closely related (dividing something often results in sharing it out among people).
Sense 1 : to distribute, share out
verb, transitive
Synonyms: वितरित करना (vitrit karnaa, formal), बख़्शना (bakhshnaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of Diwali or a birthday at school: मिठाई बाँटना (mithaaii baantnaa, to distribute sweets) is a near-ritual phrase, said whenever good news is shared -- a promotion, an exam result, an engagement. The verb carries a warm, communal feel that 'distribute' in English lacks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शेयर करना (sheyar karnaa) under English influence; बाँटना (baantnaa) is the natural native verb for sharing food, sweets, or belongings among people and is what a Hindi speaker actually says. 2) The people receiving the share are marked with को (ko), e.g. बच्चों को मिठाई बाँटी (bachchon ko mithaaii baantii, distributed sweets to the children) -- the thing given stays unmarked as the direct object.
Sample Sentences:

(divaalii par ham sab milkar mithaaii baantte hain।)

Sense 2 : to divide (into parts)
verb, transitive Used for splitting a whole into portions, whether physical (a cake), abstract (work, time), or mathematical (numbers).
Synonyms: विभाजित करना (vibhaajit karnaa, formal), भाग करना (bhaag karnaa, mainly arithmetic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: काम बाँटना (kaam baantnaa, to divide up work) is a common phrase in households and offices alike -- Hindi speakers naturally think of shared tasks as something you 'divide out' among people, echoing the distributive sense of this same verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) In arithmetic contexts (division of numbers), भाग देना (bhaag denaa) or भाग करना (bhaag karnaa) is often preferred over बाँटना (baantnaa) in school settings, though बाँटना (baantnaa) is still heard colloquially, e.g. दस को दो से बाँटो (das ko do se baanto, divide ten by two).
