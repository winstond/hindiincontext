वैसा (vaisaa)

adjective/pronoun A demonstrative/correlative adjective and pronoun meaning 'that kind of, like that' -- pairs with जैसा (jaisaa, 'like which/as') in the common jैसा...वैसा (jaisaa...vaisaa) correlative frame. Inflects like an adjective (वैसा/वैसे/वैसी, vaisaa/vaise/vaisii).
Sense 1 : of that kind, like that
adjective Often appears as the answering half of the correlative pair जैसा...वैसा (jaisaa...vaisaa, 'as...so/like that'), e.g. describing manner or quality by comparison to something already referenced.
Synonyms: उस तरह का (us tarah kaa), ऐसा (aisaa, similar but points to something nearer/just-mentioned)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of वैसा (vaisaa) as the 'that-kind' partner to ऐसा (aisaa, 'this-kind'), following the same near/far logic as यह (yah) versus वह (vah). You'll hear जैसा देश वैसा भेष (jaisaa desh vaisaa bhesh, 'as the country, so the custom' -- i.e., 'when in Rome...') as a classic use of this correlative pattern in everyday wisdom.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse वैसा (vaisaa) with ऐसा (aisaa): ऐसा (aisaa) points to something close/just shown ('this kind'), while वैसा (vaisaa) points to something more distant or previously mentioned ('that kind'), mirroring the यह (yah)/वह (vah) distinction. 2) In the correlative frame, dropping वैसा (vaisaa) and leaving जैसा (jaisaa) alone sounds incomplete to native ears even though English 'as you like' doesn't need a second clause.

मुझे वैसा कोट चाहिए जो उसने पहना था। I want a coat like that, the one he was wearing.
(mujhe vaisaa kot chaahie jo usne pahnaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : that kind of thing/one (standing alone)
pronoun Used without a following noun when the referent is understood from context, e.g. मुझे वैसा चाहिए (mujhe vaisaa chaahie, 'I want one like that').
Synonyms: उस तरह का (us tarah kaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the adjective sense, just standing in for the noun it would otherwise modify -- very natural when pointing at something in a shop or comparing two options.
