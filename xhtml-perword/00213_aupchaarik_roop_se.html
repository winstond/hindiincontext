औपचारिक रूप से (aupchaarik roop se)

adverb A compact adverbial phrase meaning 'formally, officially' -- built from the adjective औपचारिक (aupchaarik, 'formal/ceremonial') plus रूप से (roop se, 'in the manner of'), a very productive pattern for turning adjectives into adverbs.
Sense 1 : in a formal or official manner
adverb Follows the productive [adjective] + रूप से (roop se) pattern used to build adverbs from adjectives; learners can apply the same frame to other adjectives (e.g. सामान्य रूप से (saamaany roop se, 'generally')).
Synonyms: रस्मी तौर पर (rasmii taur par), विधिवत (vidhivat, somewhat literary/formal register)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase belongs to written/spoken formal registers -- office memos, news reports, inauguration speeches -- rather than casual chat; in daily conversation people are far more likely to just say किसी चीज़ को रस्मी तौर पर करना (kisii chiiz ko rasmii taur par karnaa, 'to do something formally') or describe the event itself as formal. Think of it as the register you'd hear a news anchor use: 'उन्होंने औपचारिक रूप से इस्तीफ़ा दे दिया' (unhonne aupchaarik roop se istiifaa de diyaa, 'he formally resigned').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop से (se) and just say औपचारिक रूप (aupchaarik roop), which reads as a noun phrase ('a formal form/format') rather than the adverb -- से (se) is what makes it adverbial. 2) It is easy to overuse this in speech where a Hindi speaker would just say रस्मी तौर पर (rasmii taur par) or simply describe the event as formal with an adjective instead of reaching for the adverb.
Sample Sentences:
उन्होंने औपचारिक रूप से इस्तीफ़ा दे दिया। He formally resigned.
(unhonne aupchaarik roop se istiifaa de diyaa।)
