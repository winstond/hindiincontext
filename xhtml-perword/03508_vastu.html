वस्तु (vastu)

noun A formal/written word for a physical 'thing' or 'object'; also extends to 'item of goods' in commerce and 'subject matter' in more literary or academic contexts.
Sense 1 : a physical thing, object, item
noun, feminine
Synonyms: चीज़ (chiiz), सामान (saamaan, often plural/collective 'goods, belongings')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see वस्तु (vastu) on signboards like उपभोक्ता वस्तुएँ (upbhoktaa vastuen, 'consumer goods') or in a shop's rate list, but if you point at something on a table and ask a friend what it is, you would say यह चीज़ क्या है (yah chiiz kyaa hai, 'what is this thing') and not use वस्तु (vastu) at all.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners overuse वस्तु (vastu) where चीज़ (chiiz) would sound natural in speech -- वस्तु (vastu) belongs to formal writing, labels, notices, and school textbooks, while चीज़ (chiiz) is what people actually say at home or in the market.
Sample Sentences:
यह दुकान केवल उपभोक्ता वस्तुएँ बेचती है। This shop sells only consumer goods.
(yah dukaan keval upbhoktaa vastuen bechtii hai।)

Sense 2 : subject matter, topic, or substance (of a discussion, story, etc.)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: विषय (vishay)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This sense turns up in literary criticism and school essays -- a teacher discussing a poem's वस्तु (vastu) means its 'content' or 'theme,' as distinct from its शैली (shailii, 'style'). It is a bookish use, not something you would reach for in daily conversation.
Sample Sentences:
इस कहानी की वस्तु बहुत गहरी है। The subject matter of this story is very profound.
(is kahaanii kii vastu bahut gahrii hai।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
आलोचक ने कविता की वस्तु और शैली दोनों पर विचार किया। The critic considered both the subject matter and the style of the poem.
(aalochak ne kavitaa kii vastu aur shailii donon par vichaar kiyaa।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
उपन्यास की वस्तु समाज के यथार्थ से ली गई है। The novel's subject matter is drawn from social reality.
(upanyaas kii vastu samaaj ke yathaarth se lii gaii hai।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
निबंध लिखते समय पहले वस्तु का चुनाव करना चाहिए। While writing an essay, one should first choose the subject matter.

Register: Literary -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
