मकान (makaan)

noun A concrete, physical building or house -- the structure itself, as opposed to the warmer, more emotionally loaded घर (ghar) which means 'home'.
Sense 1 : house, building (as physical structure/property)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: घर (ghar, broader, means 'home' too), भवन (bhavan, formal), इमारत (imaarat, usually larger/multi-storey building)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word everywhere in Delhi-NCR on hand-painted signs: मकान किराए के लिए (makaan kiraae ke lie, 'house for rent') pasted on gates and walls. It is the word of landlords, brokers, and property papers -- brick-and-mortar talk -- while घर (ghar) is the word of family and belonging, even if both point to the same building.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use मकान (makaan) and घर (ghar) interchangeably, but मकान (makaan) refers to the physical property or building -- you'd use it when talking about buying, renting, or building a house -- while घर (ghar) is used for 'home' in the emotional/family sense, like मैं घर जा रहा हूँ (main ghar jaa rahaa hoon, 'I am going home'), not मैं मकान जा रहा हूँ. 2) In real estate contexts (rent, sale, construction) मकान (makaan) is the natural word; using घर (ghar) there sounds a bit imprecise, though not wrong.
