भिन्न (bhinn)

adjective/noun A formal/written-register word meaning 'different, distinct'; it also has a specific mathematical sense meaning 'fraction'. Closely related to the everyday word अलग (alag).
Sense 1 : different, distinct
adjective Often used with से (se) to mark the point of comparison, e.g. 'X से भिन्न (X se bhinn)' -- 'different from X'.
Synonyms: अलग (alag), विभिन्न (vibhinn, used before plural nouns to mean 'various'), जुदा (judaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet भिन्न (bhinn) far more in a school textbook, a newspaper editorial, or a formal speech than in kitchen or market talk, where अलग (alag) rules. Reaching for भिन्न (bhinn) in conversation can come across a bit like using 'distinct' instead of 'different' in English -- correct, but noticeably formal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to अलग (alag) for 'different' in speech; भिन्न (bhinn) is not wrong but sounds bookish or academic in casual conversation, more at home in an essay or a textbook than in bazaar chat. 2) The comparison marker is से (se), not से अलग-style को (ko) or की तरह (kii tarah) -- learners sometimes reach for the wrong postposition when constructing the comparison.

Sense 2 : fraction (mathematics)
noun, masculine The standard classroom term for a mathematical fraction, e.g. 'सामान्य भिन्न (saamaany bhinn, common fraction)'.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Any Hindi-medium student meets this word in the maths chapter on fractions -- it is the everyday classroom term, not an obscure technical one, even though it looks identical to the adjective for 'different'.
