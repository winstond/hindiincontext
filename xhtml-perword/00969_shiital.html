शीतल (shiital)

adjective A literary/formal adjective meaning 'cool, cold, soothing'; also survives as a common given name. Its everyday counterpart is ठंडा (thandaa).
Sense 1 : cool, cold (physically or in temperament)
adjective
Synonyms: ठंडा (thandaa), सर्द (sard, literary)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet शीतल (shiital) most often on product labels and signboards -- शीतल पेय (shiital pey) is the standard formal term for 'soft drink' or 'cold beverage', the kind of phrase you see printed on a menu rather than said aloud. It also carries a gentle, soothing connotation beyond mere temperature, as in a calm breeze or a peaceful mind.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शीतल (shiital) as a everyday word for 'cold', but in spoken Delhi-NCR Hindi ठंडा (thandaa) is what people actually say for weather, water, or food -- शीतल (shiital) sounds bookish or poetic in those same contexts. 2) It shows up often in fixed phrases like शीतल जल (shiital jal, 'cool water') or शीतल पेय (shiital pey, 'cold drink/soft drink'), where it is the expected formal register, not a mistake to avoid there.
Sample Sentences:
गर्मी में शीतल पेय बहुत पसंद किया जाता है। In summer, cold beverages are very much liked.
(garmii men shiital pey bahut pasand kiyaa jaataa hai।)
