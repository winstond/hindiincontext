युग (yug)

noun A single core sense: 'era' or 'age' in the sense of a long span of time, used both literally (historical/cosmic eras) and figuratively (an era defined by a person, event, or condition).
Sense 1 : era, age, epoch
noun, masculine
Synonyms: काल (kaal), दौर (daur), ज़माना (zamaanaa, colloquial), अवधि (avdhi, meaning scope: period/duration, less about a defined age)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Hindu cosmology, युग (yug) refers to the four great cosmic ages -- सतयुग (satyug), द्वापरयुग (dvaaparyug), त्रेतायुग (tretaayug), and कलियुग (kaliyug, the current 'age of darkness/decline') -- so the word carries a weighty, almost mythic resonance even in modern use. You'll hear it in phrases like नया युग (nayaa yug, 'a new era') for anything from a technological shift to a political change, and casually as युगों-युगों तक (yugon-yugon tak, 'for ages and ages') to mean 'forever.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for समय (samay) or ज़माना (zamaanaa) when युग (yug) is meant -- युग (yug) implies a whole historical or cosmic age (like 'the digital era'), not just 'time' or 'these days' in a casual sense. 2) In compounds like युगों (yugon, 'ages,' oblique plural) it's used hyperbolically in everyday speech to mean 'a very long time,' e.g. युगों बाद (yugon baad, 'after ages') -- learners sometimes render this too literally as 'after eras.'
