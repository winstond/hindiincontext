इंतज़ाम करना (intzaam karnaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to arrange, to make arrangements'; built from the noun इंतज़ाम (intzaam, 'arrangement') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'). One core sense covers everyday planning and organizing.
Sense 1 : to arrange, to organize, to make arrangements for
verb, transitive Takes का (kaa) before the thing being arranged: शादी का इंतज़ाम करना (shaadii kaa intzaam karnaa, 'to arrange the wedding').
Synonyms: व्यवस्था करना (vyavasthaa karnaa, slightly more formal), बंदोबस्त करना (bandobast karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday go-to phrase for logistics of any kind -- a wedding, a party, travel, even fixing a problem quietly ('मैं कुछ इंतज़ाम कर दूँगा' main kuchh intzaam kar doongaa, 'I'll sort something out'). You will hear it constantly around family events in Delhi-NCR, where relatives are always busy doing 'इंतज़ाम' for the next function.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say इंतज़ाम बनाना (intzaam banaanaa) by analogy with 'make arrangements' -- Hindi always pairs इंतज़ाम with करना (karnaa), never बनाना (banaanaa). 2) The noun इंतज़ाम (intzaam) needs का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) to link to what's being arranged, not the plain noun stuck directly in front: सामान इंतज़ाम करना (saamaan intzaam karnaa) is wrong, it should be सामान का इंतज़ाम करना (saamaan kaa intzaam karnaa).
Sample Sentences:
शादी के लिए सारा इंतज़ाम हो गया है। All the arrangements for the wedding are done.
(shaadii ke lie saaraa intzaam ho gayaa hai।)
