जान बची तो लाखों पाए (jaan bachii to laakhon paae)

phrase A fixed idiom of relief expressed after narrowly escaping danger or trouble -- literally 'if life was saved, then [it's as if] I gained millions.'
Sense 1 : expression of relief at a narrow escape
phrase Used as a standalone exclamation after describing or surviving a dangerous, stressful, or narrowly-averted bad situation -- it comments on the whole preceding event rather than attaching grammatically to it.
Synonyms: बाल बाल बचे (baal baal bache, escaped by a hair's breadth), शुक्र है बच गए (shukr hai bach gae, thank goodness we survived)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the kind of line you hear from a relative recounting a near-miss auto accident or a narrow escape from a scolding boss, said with a hand on the chest and a sigh. It captures a very Hindi-speaking sensibility: survival itself, however plain, is treated as the real jackpot -- more valuable than any money (लाखों, laakhon, 'millions') you could name.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to translate it literally word-for-word ('life was saved so got millions'), but it should be treated as a single fixed unit meaning 'what a relief to have escaped that' -- the 'millions' is figurative, not a literal amount gained. 2) It is used after the fact, once the danger has passed, not as a warning about ongoing danger.

(baal-baal bachaa eksiident se, jaan bachii to laakhon paae।)
