बढ़िया (badhiyaa)

adjective/adverb/interjection A everyday word of praise -- covers the adjective 'good/great/fine quality' and the matching adverb/exclamatory use meaning 'well done' or 'nicely.'
Sense 1 : good, fine, excellent (quality)
adjective
Synonyms: अच्छा (achchhaa), उम्दा (umdaa), शानदार (shaandaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly in markets and kitchens in Delhi -- a shopkeeper says 'बढ़िया माल है' (badhiyaa maal hai, 'it's good stuff') to sell you fruit, sweets, or fabric. It carries a warm, approving, everyday tone rather than a formal or literary one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to inflect it like a regular -आ adjective (बढ़िया/बढ़िये/बढ़िया), but it stays बढ़िया (badhiyaa) in all genders and numbers -- 'बढ़िया लड़की' (badhiyaa ladkii, a fine girl), not 'बढ़िये लड़की'. 2) It sounds slightly informal for very high-stakes formal writing, where शानदार (shaandaar) or उत्कृष्ट (utkrisht) fit better.

Sense 2 : well, nicely (manner)
adverb Used after a verb to describe how something was done, e.g. 'बढ़िया बना है' (badhiyaa banaa hai, 'it's turned out well').
Synonyms: अच्छी तरह (achchhii tarah)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this after tasting food: 'बढ़िया बना है खाना' (badhiyaa banaa hai khaanaa, 'the food has turned out great') -- a very natural compliment to a cook in any Delhi home.

Sense 3 : well done!, great!, nice!
interjection Used alone as an exclamation of approval, independent of any noun it might otherwise modify.
Synonyms: वाह (vaah), शाबाश (shaabaash)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Said on its own -- 'बढ़िया!' (badhiyaa) -- it is a quick, casual thumbs-up you will hear among friends, colleagues, or even from a teacher praising a student's answer.
