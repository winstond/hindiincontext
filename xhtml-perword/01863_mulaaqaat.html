मुलाक़ात (mulaaqaat)

noun A single core meaning: a meeting or encounter with a person. Mostly used with होना (honaa) 'to happen/occur' or करना (karnaa) 'to make/have', as in mulaaqaat honaa and mulaaqaat karnaa.
Sense 1 : meeting, encounter with a person
noun, feminine Typically pairs with से (se) to mark the other person: kisii se mulaaqaat 'a meeting with someone'. Common verb pairings are होना (honaa) for a meeting that happens/occurs, and करना (karnaa) for deliberately arranging one.
Synonyms: भेंट (bhent, formal), मिलना (milnaa, used as a verbal noun rather than a noun proper)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मुलाक़ात (mulaaqaat) comes from Arabic/Persian via Urdu and carries a slightly warmer, more personal flavor than the plainer भेंट (bhent), which feels more formal or official (used for things like a state visit). You'll hear aapse mulaaqaat karke achchhaa lagaa ('nice to have met you') as a common polite closing line after a first meeting.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to मिलना (milnaa) in every context, but मुलाक़ात (mulaaqaat) is the noun used when talking *about* a meeting as an event (e.g. scheduling it, remembering it), whereas मिलना (milnaa) is the verb for the act of meeting itself. 2) The person met is marked with से (se), not को (ko) -- learners sometimes wrongly use को (ko) by analogy with other verbs.
