छियासी (chhiyaasii)

numeral The number 86 (eighty-six), used both as a counting numeral and to mark a year or quantity.
Sense 1 : the number eighty-six (86)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, छियासी (chhiyaasii) is its own word rather than a predictable compound, which is why native speakers just memorize the 1-100 list in school rather than deriving it -- you'll often hear people rattle off table-like sequences ("चौरासी, पिच्चासी, छियासी...", chauraasii, pichchaasii, chhiyaasii, '84, 85, 86') the way kids recite times tables.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build this compositionally from छह (chhah, six) and अस्सी (assii, eighty) the way English says 'eighty-six' -- Hindi numerals from 21-99 are mostly memorized as single fused words, and छियासी (chhiyaasii) does not sound like a simple combination of its parts, so it is best learned as a standalone item.

(mere daadaa jii kii umr chhiyaasii saal hai।)
