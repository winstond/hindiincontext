तानाशाही (taanaashaahii)

noun A noun meaning 'dictatorship' or, extended figuratively, any bossy/authoritarian style of behavior; related to तानाशाह (taanaashaah, 'dictator, tyrant').
Sense 1 : dictatorship (political system)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: निरंकुशता (nirankushtaa, formal), अधिनायकवाद (adhinaayakvaad, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in news debates and political commentary, usually paired with a country or era: सैनिक तानाशाही (sainik taanaashaahii, 'military dictatorship') or तानाशाही शासन (taanaashaahii shaasan, 'dictatorial rule'). It carries a clearly negative charge, contrasted with लोकतंत्र (loktantr, 'democracy').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तानाशाही (taanaashaahii) with तानाशाह (taanaashaah) -- the latter is the person ('dictator'), while तानाशाही (taanaashaahii) is the system or the abstract quality of dictatorial rule.

(us desh men sainik taanaashaahii kaii saal tak chalii।)

Sense 2 : bossiness, high-handed/authoritarian behavior (figurative)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मनमानी (manmaanii, 'willfulness, having one's own way')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday complaints this word jumps from politics into the home or office: a strict boss or an overbearing parent can be accused of चलाना तानाशाही (taanaashaahii chalaanaa, 'to run things like a dictator'). It is a favorite word for venting about a controlling relative or a domineering colleague, said half-jokingly among friends but meant seriously in family arguments.
Sample Sentences:
