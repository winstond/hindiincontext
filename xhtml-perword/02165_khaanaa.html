ख़ाना (khaanaa)

noun/verb This word has two distinct lives: as a noun it means 'box/compartment/cell' (as in a grid, chart, or drawer), and it is also the common alternate spelling of खाना (khaanaa, 'to eat/food') when written with the nuqta under ख. Most learners will meet the eating-related senses far more often; the nuqta form is frequently dropped in casual writing.
Sense 1 : box, compartment, cell (in a grid or structure)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बक्सा (baksaa, box, less abstract), डिब्बा (dibbaa, box/container)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word on government forms and school worksheets -- 'इस ख़ाने में अपना नाम लिखें' (is khaane men apnaa naam likhen, 'write your name in this box') is a classic instruction. It is also used for the little compartments in a steel almirah or tiffin box, a very Indian household image.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with खाना (khaanaa, 'food/to eat') since the words are near-homophones and often written identically without the nuqta -- context (a form, chart, or piece of furniture vs. a meal) is the only reliable clue in casual text.
Sample Sentences:
इस ख़ाने में अपना नाम लिखें। Write your name in this box.
(is khaane men apnaa naam likhen।)

Sense 2 : to eat (variant spelling of खाना)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: खाना (khaanaa, standard spelling without nuqta), भोजन करना (bhojan karnaa, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Whether or not the nuqta appears usually just reflects a writer's habit or keyboard, not a meaning difference -- see खाना (khaanaa) for the full verb entry and its idioms (गाली खाना gaalii khaanaa, मार खाना maar khaanaa, etc.).
Common Mistakes: 1) This is simply खाना (khaanaa) written with a nuqta under ख -- the nuqta is historically not standard for this word (unlike loanwords such as ख़ास khaas), so most native writing drops it; do not treat the two spellings as different words.
Sample Sentences:
वह हर रोज़ बाहर का खाना ख़ाता है। He eats outside food every day.
(vah har roz baahar kaa khaanaa khaataa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
मैंने आज सुबह कुछ नहीं ख़ाया। I didn't eat anything this morning.
(mainne aaj subah kuchh nahiin khaayaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
तुम इतनी जल्दी में क्यों ख़ा रहे हो? Why are you eating in such a hurry?
(tum itnii jaldii men kyon khaa rahe ho?)

Sense 3 : food, a meal (variant spelling of खाना)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खाना (khaanaa, standard spelling without nuqta), भोजन (bhojan, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Again just an alternate rendering of खाना (khaanaa, 'food') -- the underlying word, usage, and cultural weight (the daily 'खाना खा लिया?' khaanaa khaa liyaa?, 'have you eaten?' greeting-check between family members) are identical.
