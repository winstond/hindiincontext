बर्फ़ (barf)

noun One core meaning: frozen water, extended naturally to snow and to ice cream/kulfi-style treats in casual speech.
Sense 1 : ice
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi summers you will hear this word constantly at the thelaa (cart) selling बर्फ़ का गोला (barf kaa golaa, 'ice ball,' a shaved-ice treat) -- keep an ear out for कितने की बर्फ़? (kitne kii barf, 'how much for the ice?') at neighborhood shops that still sell block ice for coolers.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a mass noun, so it is not pluralized the way countable English nouns are -- you talk about a piece or amount of ice with a measure word rather than saying 'ices.' 2) Learners sometimes confuse बर्फ़ (barf) with बर्फ़ी (barfii), the milk-based sweet -- they are related in origin but very different things on your plate.

Sense 2 : snow
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: हिम (him, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: For most Delhi-NCR speakers snow is something you go see in the hills -- शिमला में बर्फ़ पड़ी है (shimlaa men barf padii hai, 'it has snowed in Shimla') is a classic winter-vacation line, since snowfall itself is a novelty on the plains.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi does not sharply lexically separate 'ice' from 'snow' the way English does -- बर्फ़ (barf) covers both, and you rely on context (or बर्फ़ गिरना (barf girnaa, 'snow to fall')) rather than a dedicated word for snow.

Sense 3 : figurative: coldness, frozen stillness (emotional/relational)
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as in English, बर्फ़ (barf) extends to describe a cold, unresponsive mood or relationship -- रिश्तों में बर्फ़ जम गई (rishton men barf jam gaii, 'ice has set into the relationship') is a natural way to describe a couple or friends who have gone silent and distant.
Sample Sentences:
हमारे रिश्तों में बर्फ़ जम गई है। Ice has set into our relationship.
(hamaare rishton men barf jam gaii hai।)
