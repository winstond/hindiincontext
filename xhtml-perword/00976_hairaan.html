हैरान (hairaan)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'surprised, astonished, bewildered' -- covers everything from mild surprise to being flustered or perplexed.
Sense 1 : surprised, astonished, bewildered
adjective Almost always used with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) -- हैरान होना (hairaan honaa) 'to be/become surprised' (intransitive experience) versus हैरान करना (hairaan karnaa) 'to surprise/trouble someone' (causing the state in someone else).
Synonyms: आश्चर्यचकित (aashcharyachkit, formal), अचंभित (achambhit, literary), चकित (chakit, literary), हक्का-बक्का (hakkaa-bakkaa, colloquial, 'utterly dumbfounded')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear हैरान (hairaan) constantly paired with परेशान (pareshaan) in the set phrase हैरान-परेशान (hairaan-pareshaan), used almost like a single word meaning 'frazzled and bewildered' -- someone juggling a broken AC, a demanding boss, and a stuck auto-rickshaw in Delhi traffic is the very picture of हैरान-परेशान (hairaan-pareshaan).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चौंकना (chaunknaa) when they mean हैरान (hairaan): चौंकना (chaunknaa) is the sudden physical startle-reaction (like jumping at a loud noise), while हैरान होना (hairaan honaa) is the more lingering mental state of being puzzled or astonished -- the two overlap but aren't interchangeable. 2) हैरान (hairaan) can drift toward meaning 'troubled, harassed, worn out' in everyday complaints (मैं हैरान हूँ (main hairaan hoon, 'I'm at my wits' end') said about a nagging problem, not just genuine surprise) -- learners who only know 'surprised' miss this shade.
