छियानवे (chhiyaanve)

numeral The numeral for 96 (chhiyaanve). Single-sense word: used in counting, ages, quantities, and years.
Sense 1 : the number 96
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like all Hindi numerals from 21 to 99, छियानवे (chhiyaanve) has to be learned as its own item rather than derived from a rule, since Hindi counting does not simply combine 'ninety' and 'six' the way English does. It shows up naturally in ages ('वह छियानवे साल का है', vah chhiyaanve saal kaa hai, 'he is ninety-six years old'), exam scores, and years (जैसे 1996, unniis sau chhiyaanve, 'nineteen ninety-six').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse छियानवे (chhiyaanve, 96) with छियासी (chhiyaasii, 86) or छानबे-sounding neighbors like नब्बे (nabbe, 90) because the teen-based Hindi number system does not build predictably from tens plus units the way English does -- each number from 21 to 99 is essentially its own word to memorize.
