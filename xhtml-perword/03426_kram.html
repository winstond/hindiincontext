क्रम (kram)

noun A somewhat formal/bookish noun meaning 'order' or 'sequence'; mainly seen in written or careful spoken Hindi and in a handful of set phrases like क्रम से (kram se) and क्रमशः (kramshah).
Sense 1 : sequence, order (of items, events, numbers)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तरतीब (tartiib), सिलसिला (silsilaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet क्रम (kram) constantly on exam papers and forms -- प्रश्न क्रम (prashn kram, 'question number/sequence') or क्रम संख्या (kram sankhyaa, 'serial number') -- so it is worth knowing well even though it rarely comes up in casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तरतीब (tartiib) or सिलसिला (silsilaa) in speech and save क्रम (kram) for writing or formal contexts like exam instructions, official lists, or news reports -- using it in casual chat about, say, whose turn it is can sound stiff. 2) The set phrase क्रम से (kram se, 'in order/turn by turn') is very common and worth learning as a chunk rather than building from scratch.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया संख्याओं को सही क्रम में लिखिए। Please write the numbers in the correct order.
(kripyaa sankhyaaon ko sahii kram men likhie।)

Sense 2 : turn (in a line, queue, or process)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बारी (baarii)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Think of a government office or a school assembly where names are called प्रश्न क्रम (prashn kram) or क्रमानुसार (kramaanusaar, 'according to sequence') -- this is the flavor of context where क्रम (kram) as 'turn' feels natural, versus the market queue where everyone just says अपनी बारी (apnii baarii).
Common Mistakes: 1) In everyday speech about queues or waiting for one's turn, बारी (baarii) is far more natural than क्रम (kram); क्रम (kram) in this sense leans formal, showing up in phrases like क्रम से आना (kram se aanaa, 'to come in turn/in order').
Sample Sentences:
कृपया क्रमानुसार अपना नाम बताएँ। Please state your name in turn/order.
(kripyaa kramaanusaar apnaa naam bataaen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
छात्रों को क्रमानुसार बुलाया जाएगा। The students will be called in turn.
(chhaatron ko kramaanusaar bulaayaa jaaegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive
प्रश्न क्रम के अनुसार उत्तर दीजिए। Please answer according to the question sequence/turn.

Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
क्लर्क ने क्रमानुसार सभी फाइलें जाँची। The clerk checked all the files in order/turn.
(klark ne kramaanusaar sabhii phaailen jaanchii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर क्रमानुसार बुलाया जाता, तो कोई झगड़ा नहीं होता। If people had been called in turn, there would have been no quarrel.
