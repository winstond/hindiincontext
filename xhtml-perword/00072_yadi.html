यदि (yadi)

conjunction A formal/literary word for 'if', used to introduce a conditional clause; the everyday spoken equivalent is अगर (agar).
Sense 1 : if (conditional)
conjunction Often paired with तो (to) in the following clause to mark the result: यदि...तो... (yadi...to..., 'if...then...').
Synonyms: अगर (agar), चूंकि (choonki, not a true synonym, means 'since')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear यदि (yadi) in news broadcasts, official notices, textbooks, and formal speeches -- it has a slightly bookish, elevated flavor compared to its everyday twin अगर (agar). Think of it like the difference between 'should you require assistance' and 'if you need help' in English: same meaning, different register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use यदि (yadi) in casual speech where अगर (agar) would sound far more natural; यदि (yadi) is common in writing, speeches, and formal announcements but can feel stiff in everyday conversation. 2) Forgetting to add तो (to) in the second clause, which is optional but very frequently used to sharpen the if-then link, especially with यदि (yadi) in formal registers.
Sample Sentences:
यदि आप सहायता चाहते हैं, तो कृपया कार्यालय से संपर्क करें। If you require assistance, please contact the office.
(yadi aap sahaaytaa chaahte hain, to kripyaa kaaryaalay se sampark karen।)
