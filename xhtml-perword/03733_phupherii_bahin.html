फुफेरी बहिन (phupherii bahin)

noun A kinship term meaning 'paternal aunt's daughter' -- a specific type of cousin, viewed as an older or same-age female relative. Single sense.
Sense 1 : female cousin (daughter of father's sister)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फुफेरी बहन (phupherii bahan, common spelling variant), फुफी की बेटी (phuphii kii betii, descriptive, less common as a fixed term)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi (like many South Asian languages) has a rich, precise kinship vocabulary where every cousin, aunt, and uncle gets a term that encodes exactly which side of the family and which sibling they descend from -- फुफेरी बहिन (phupherii bahin) tells you at a glance she's your father's sister's daughter, something English's flat 'cousin' completely erases. You'll hear the parallel male form फुफेरा भाई (phupheraa bhaaii, paternal aunt's son) in the same breath, and related terms like मौसेरी बहिन (mauserii bahin, mother's sister's daughter) and ममेरी बहिन (mamerii bahin, mother's brother's daughter) follow the same logic from different branches of the family tree.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use the generic English word 'cousin' and reach for cज़िन (kazin) or a vague बहन (bahan), missing that Hindi kinship terms specify exactly which relative is meant -- फुफेरी बहिन (phupherii bahin) is specifically the daughter of one's फूफी (phoophii, father's sister), not just any female cousin. 2) The spelling बहिन (bahin) vs बहन (bahan) can confuse learners; both are used, with बहन (bahan) being more common in casual writing today.
