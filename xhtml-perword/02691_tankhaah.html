तनख़ाह (tankhaah)

noun A everyday word for 'salary' or 'wages' -- the money you get regularly from a job. One core sense; also commonly spelled तनख्वाह (tanakhvaah).
Sense 1 : salary, monthly pay
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वेतन (vetan, formal), पगार (pagaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: तनख़ाह (tankhaah) comes from Persian and carries a slight Mughal-administrative echo -- it's the word a salaried employee (a नौकरी (naukrii) holder) uses, distinct from money earned by a shopkeeper or farmer. Asking तनख़ाह कब मिलेगी (tankhaah kab milegii, 'when will the salary arrive') is a very common end-of-month complaint in Indian offices. You'll also frequently see it spelled तनख्वाह (tanakhvaah) -- both spellings are completely standard and used interchangeably in speech and writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for वेतन (vetan) by default because it's the 'dictionary' word, but तनख़ाह (tankhaah) is what people actually say at home and at work about their own pay -- वेतन (vetan) sounds more like something you'd read on an official form or hear in a news report. 2) The verb pairing is मिलना (milnaa) -- तनख़ाह मिलती है (tankhaah miltii hai, 'the salary is received') -- not पाना (paanaa) or कमाना (kamaanaa), which describe earning in general rather than the act of getting paid on payday.
