निभाना (nibhaanaa)

verb One core verb meaning 'to fulfill/carry out (a duty, role, relationship)' -- appears constantly with words like रिश्ता (rishtaa), वादा (vaadaa), and फ़र्ज़ (farz).
Sense 1 : to fulfill, carry out, discharge (a duty, role, relationship, promise)
verb, transitive Commonly takes an abstract noun as object: रिश्ता निभाना (rishtaa nibhaanaa, 'to maintain/honor a relationship'), वादा निभाना (vaadaa nibhaanaa, 'to keep a promise'), फ़र्ज़ निभाना (farz nibhaanaa, 'to do one's duty').
Synonyms: पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa), अदा करना (adaa karnaa, formal, often with फ़र्ज़ or ज़िम्मेदारी)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries real emotional weight in Hindi family life -- निभाना (nibhaanaa) is what you do with a रिश्ता (rishtaa) day after day, not just something you declare once. You'll hear it in serious conversations about duty and loyalty: बेटी का फ़र्ज़ निभाना (betii kaa farz nibhaanaa, 'to fulfill a daughter's duty') or पति-पत्नी का रिश्ता निभाना (pati-patnii kaa rishtaa nibhaanaa, 'to sustain a husband-wife relationship'). It has a quiet dignity to it -- less dramatic than 'sacrifice,' more about steady follow-through.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa) in every context, but निभाना (nibhaanaa) carries a sense of sustained commitment or endurance over time -- especially with relationships and roles -- that पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa) doesn't capture as naturally. 2) With रिश्ता (rishtaa), निभाना (nibhaanaa) implies actively maintaining the bond through effort, not just having it exist -- closer to 'to keep up/honor' than a flat 'to complete.'

वह हर रिश्ता बड़ी ईमानदारी से निभाती है। She fulfills every relationship with great honesty.
(vah har rishtaa badii iimaandaarii se nibhaatii hai।)

Sense 2 : to play (a role/character, in film, drama, or life)
verb, transitive Used with किरदार (kirdaar) or भूमिका (bhoomikaa) as object: किरदार निभाना (kirdaar nibhaanaa, 'to play a character').
Synonyms: अदा करना (adaa karnaa, used specifically with भूमिका or किरदार)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in film and TV talk in India -- अभिनेता ने बहुत अच्छा किरदार निभाया (abhinetaa ne bahut achchhaa kirdaar nibhaayaa, 'the actor played the character very well'). It's the standard verb entertainment journalists and fans reach for when praising a performance, distinct from अभिनय करना (abhinay karnaa) which is more about the act of acting itself.
