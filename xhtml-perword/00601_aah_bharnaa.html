आह भरना (aah bharnaa)

verb A fixed compound expression meaning 'to sigh' or 'to heave a sigh' -- built from आह (aah, 'ah/sigh', an interjection of pain or longing) plus भरना (bharnaa, 'to fill'), literally 'to fill a sigh.'
Sense 1 : to sigh (from sorrow, longing, or weariness)
verb, transitive The subject is marked with ने (ne) in the perfective, since भरना (bharnaa) behaves as a transitive verb here (आह (aah) is its object).
Synonyms: आह भरना (aah bharnaa), ठंडी आह भरना (thandii aah bharnaa, common intensified variant), हाय भरना (haay bharnaa, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a slightly literary, emotional flavor -- you'll hear it in Bollywood dialogue, ghazals, and storytelling more than in casual daily chit-chat, where a learner might instead just say कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin, 'nothing') with a heavy tone. It's the classic verb for the sigh of a disappointed lover, a tired parent, or someone recalling old times.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to translate 'sigh' with a literal verb search and miss this fixed noun+verb pairing -- आह (aah) almost never occurs alone as a verb's object with any other verb in this sense; भरना (bharnaa) is the fixed partner. 2) Because आह (aah) is also a standalone interjection ('ah! oh!'), learners sometimes think the phrase is just an exclamation followed by a separate verb, rather than one fused expression. 3) The intensifier ठंडी (thandii, 'cold') is very frequently added -- ठंडी आह भरना (thandii aah bharnaa) -- and learners are often unaware this is the more natural, commonly heard form.

(vah gahrii aah bhartii hai aur khidkii se baahar dekhtii hai।)
