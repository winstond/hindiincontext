ख़ुशी (khushii)

noun A core everyday noun meaning happiness or joy; also used in fixed expressions for 'good news' and for giving willing consent. Sometimes seen spelled without the nuqta as खुशी (khushii).
Sense 1 : happiness, joy
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आनंद (aanand), हर्ष (harsh, literary), प्रसन्नता (prasanntaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Notice how Hindi treats happiness as something you 'have' (मुझे ख़ुशी है, mujhe khushii hai) rather than something you simply 'are' -- this dative-subject framing shows up again and again with emotions and sensations in Hindi, so getting comfortable with it here pays off elsewhere too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मैं ख़ुशी हूँ (main khushii hoon) to mean 'I am happy', copying English adjective-with-'be' structure -- but ख़ुशी (khushii) is a noun, so the natural sentence is मुझे ख़ुशी है (mujhe khushii hai, 'I have happiness / I am happy') or ख़ुश हूँ (khush hoon) using the actual adjective ख़ुश (khush).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे बहुत ख़ुशी है कि तुम आ गए। I am very happy that you came.
(mujhe bahut khushii hai ki tum aa gae।)

Sense 2 : good news, glad tidings
noun, feminine Common in the set phrase ख़ुशी की बात (khushii kii baat, 'a matter of joy/good news') and in ख़ुशी सुनाना (khushii sunaanaa, 'to share good news').
Synonyms: ख़ुशख़बरी (khushkhabrii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: When someone in Delhi says मेरे पास एक ख़ुशी की खबर है (mere paas ek khushii kii khabar hai, 'I have a piece of happy news'), they are usually about to announce something like an engagement or a new job -- this sense often overlaps with celebratory family news.

Sense 3 : willingness, free consent (as in 'of one's own accord')
noun, feminine Chiefly used in the fixed phrase अपनी ख़ुशी से (apnii khushii se, 'of one's own free will').
Synonyms: मर्ज़ी (marzii), इच्छा (ichchhaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear अपनी ख़ुशी से (apnii khushii se) in both everyday bargaining ('I'm giving this discount of my own will') and in more serious contexts like consent forms or marriage discussions, where it signals 'nobody forced me'.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is almost always locked into the phrase अपनी ख़ुशी से (apnii khushii se) -- learners sometimes try to use ख़ुशी (khushii) alone to mean 'willingness' outside this frame, which sounds odd; मर्ज़ी (marzii) is the more flexible word for that.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने यह काम अपनी ख़ुशी से किया है। I did this work of my own free will.
(mainne yah kaam apnii khushii se kiyaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
