निपुणता (nipuntaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning skill, expertise, or proficiency -- the quality of being निपुण (nipun, 'skilled/expert').
Sense 1 : skill, expertise, proficiency
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कुशलता (kushaltaa), दक्षता (dakshtaa, slightly more formal/technical), माहिरी (maahirii, colloquial), महारत (mahaarat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a bookish, formal-register word -- you'll meet it in resumes, school reports, and formal speeches praising someone's ability, rather than in casual chai-shop chatter. In daily conversation, people are far more likely to just say वह बहुत माहिर है (vah bahut maahir hai, 'he is very skilled') or काम बहुत अच्छे से करता है (kaam bahut achchhe se kartaa hai, 'he does the work very well') than to reach for निपुणता (nipuntaa) itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for the adjective निपुण (nipun, 'skilled') when they actually need the noun form -- e.g. to say 'his skill impressed me' you need निपुणता (nipuntaa), not निपुण (nipun). 2) This word is typically used with में (men) to specify the domain of skill, as in किसी काम में निपुणता (kisii kaam men nipuntaa, 'skill in some work') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) instead.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपने काम में असाधारण निपुणता दिखाई। He showed extraordinary skill in his work.
(usne apne kaam men asaadhaaran nipuntaa dikhaaii।)
