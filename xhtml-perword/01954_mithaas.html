मिठास (mithaas)

noun One core sense: the noun 'sweetness,' used both literally (of taste) and figuratively (of speech, voice, or nature).
Sense 1 : sweetness (of taste)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मधुरता (madhurtaa, literary), शीरीनी (shiiriinii, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word in the kitchen a lot: आम में बहुत मिठास है (aam men bahut mithaas hai, 'the mango has a lot of sweetness') is the kind of thing said while picking fruit at the market, comparing one mango's मिठास (mithaas) against another's.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मीठापन (miithaapan) instead -- both exist, but मिठास (mithaas) is by far the more common everyday word for describing how sweet something tastes.

Sense 2 : sweetness (of speech, voice, or manner)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मधुरता (madhurtaa, literary), नरमी (narmii, softness -- close but not identical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the figurative heart of the word: उसकी बातों में मिठास है (uskii baaton men mithaas hai, 'there is sweetness in her words') praises someone's gentle, pleasant way of speaking -- a quality Delhi families genuinely prize, especially in how elders expect the young to address them.

(uskii baaton men itnii mithaas hai ki log ghanton use sunte rah jaate hain।)
