तमीज़ (tamiiz)

noun Mainly means good manners/etiquette/proper sense of behavior; also extends to the ability to distinguish or discern between things. Often heard in scolding phrases about how someone talks or behaves.
Sense 1 : manners, etiquette, proper social behavior
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सलीका (saliikaa), शऊर (shaoor, colloquial), अदब (adab, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian parent reaches for when a child talks back or behaves rudely in front of guests -- बड़ों से तमीज़ से बात करो (badon se tamiiz se baat karo, 'speak respectfully to elders') is a classic household line. It carries real social weight because 'manners' in Hindi-speaking households are tightly bound to respect for hierarchy (elders, teachers, guests), not just table etiquette.
Common Mistakes: 1) तमीज़ (tamiiz) is almost always used with a negative or scolding tone in everyday speech -- तुम्हें कोई तमीज़ नहीं है (tumhen koii tamiiz nahiin hai, 'you have no manners') is far more common than a positive statement praising someone's manners. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with 'intelligence' or 'knowledge' -- it specifically refers to proper conduct/etiquette, not general smartness.
Sample Sentences:
बड़ों से तमीज़ से बात करो। Speak respectfully to elders.
(badon se tamiiz se baat karo।)

Sense 2 : ability to discern, sense of judgment/distinction
noun, feminine Often appears in the pattern X की तमीज़ (X kii tamiiz) meaning 'the ability to tell X apart' -- e.g. सही-गलत की तमीज़ (sahii-galat kii tamiiz, 'the ability to tell right from wrong').
Synonyms: समझ (samajh), परख (parakh)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in slightly philosophical or moralizing contexts -- an elder might say उसे अभी सही-गलत की तमीज़ नहीं है (use abhii sahii-galat kii tamiiz nahiin hai, 'he doesn't yet have the sense to tell right from wrong') about someone considered immature, linking discernment directly to age and upbringing.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense overlaps closely with the 'manners' sense, and learners often miss that तमीज़ (tamiiz) here means judgment/discernment about right and wrong or good and bad, not politeness itself -- अच्छे-बुरे की तमीज़ (achchhe-bure kii tamiiz, 'the sense to distinguish good from bad') is about moral judgment, not table manners.
Sample Sentences:
उसे अभी सही-गलत की तमीज़ नहीं है। He doesn't yet have the sense to tell right from wrong.
(use abhii sahii-galat kii tamiiz nahiin hai।)
