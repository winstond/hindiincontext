इच्छा (ichchhaa)

noun A single core noun meaning 'wish, desire' -- straightforward and mostly one sense in everyday use, with a common idiomatic pairing in the phrase इच्छा होना (ichchhaa honaa).
Sense 1 : wish, desire, will
noun, feminine Most naturally used with होना (honaa) in a dative-subject frame -- 'X को Y की इच्छा है' (X ko Y kii ichchhaa hai, 'X has a desire for Y') -- placing the desire on the person rather than treating them as the grammatical subject, much like पसंद (pasand) or ज़रूरत (zaroorat).
Synonyms: चाह (chaah), ख़्वाहिश (khvaahish), मर्ज़ी (marzii, often 'preference/will' rather than a passing wish), कामना (kaamnaa, slightly formal/literary tone)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: इच्छा (ichchhaa) has a slightly more reflective, inward flavor than चाहत (chaahat) or मर्ज़ी (marzii) -- it is the word you will hear in contexts of personal wishes, even spiritual or philosophical ones (as in the phrase ईश्वर की इच्छा (iishvar kii ichchhaa, 'God's will')), rather than casual everyday cravings, for which लोग अक्सर मन (man) करना (man karnaa) का सहारा लेते हैं ('people often lean on the phrase मन करना instead').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चाहना (chaahnaa) as a verb when a native speaker would use the noun इच्छा (ichchhaa) with होना (honaa) instead, e.g. मेरी इच्छा है कि... (merii ichchhaa hai ki..., 'my wish is that...') rather than forcing a verb construction. 2) Do not mark the person wanting something with ने (ne) -- इच्छा (ichchhaa) takes a को-marked (ko) possessor, not an ergative subject.
Sample Sentences:
मेरी इच्छा है कि मैं विदेश में पढ़ूँ। My wish is that I study abroad.
(merii ichchhaa hai ki main videsh men padhoon।)
