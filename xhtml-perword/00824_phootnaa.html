फूटना (phootnaa)

verb A basic intransitive verb meaning 'to break/burst/crack', extended into several vivid figurative senses -- water or a spring bursting forth, a sound erupting, or emotion breaking loose.
Sense 1 : to break, crack, shatter (of a brittle or hollow object)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: टूटना (tootnaa), दरकना (daraknaa, more like 'to crack/split without full separation')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a glass बर्तन (bartan, 'vessel') slipping and फूट (phoot) जाना (jaanaa) -- shattering into pieces on the kitchen floor; the verb carries that sense of sudden, often noisy, splitting apart.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for टूटना (tootnaa) for everything that breaks, but फूटना (phootnaa) is specifically used for things that shatter or crack open from internal pressure or a blow, like glass, an egg, or a coconut -- नारियल फूटना (naariyal phootnaa, 'the coconut breaks/splits open') at a temple, for instance, sounds natural, while टूटना (tootnaa) there would sound odd.

Sense 2 : to burst forth, gush out (of liquid, especially water)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बहना (bahnaa, more general 'to flow'), उमड़ना (umadnaa, to well up/surge)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this with पानी (paanii, 'water') or चश्मा (chashmaa, 'spring') -- ज़मीन से पानी फूट निकला (zamiin se paanii phoot niklaa, 'water gushed out from the ground'). It paints water as breaking through a surface with force, much like the literal 'crack open' sense but applied to liquid finding a way out.

Sense 3 : to erupt or break out suddenly (of sound, emotion, or a large-scale event)
verb, intransitive Used with abstract nouns like हँसी (hansii, 'laughter'), रुलाई (rulaaii, 'crying fit'), दंगा (dangaa, 'riot'), or महामारी (mahaamaarii, 'epidemic') as the subject: हँसी फूट पड़ी (hansii phoot padii, 'laughter burst out').
Synonyms: फैलना (phailnaa, to spread, used for riots/epidemics), भड़कना (bhadaknaa, to flare up)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind news headlines like दंगा फूट पड़ा (dangaa phoot padaa, 'a riot broke out') or महामारी फूट पड़ी (mahaamaarii phoot padii, 'an epidemic broke out') -- Hindi frames sudden collective events as something that 'cracks open' and spills out, much like the emotional sense of a person's tears or laughter breaking loose.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense very often pairs with the compound verb पड़ना (padnaa) to add suddenness -- फूट पड़ना (phoot padnaa) -- so learners should get used to seeing फूटना (phootnaa) rarely standing alone in this figurative sense in natural speech.
