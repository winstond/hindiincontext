मूल्य (mooly)

noun A somewhat formal/literary word covering both the concrete sense of 'price, cost' and the abstract sense of 'value, worth' -- the everyday spoken word for price is कीमत (kiimat).
Sense 1 : price, cost (of goods, services)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कीमत (kiimat), दाम (daam)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see मूल्य (mooly) printed on packaged goods as अधिकतम खुदरा मूल्य (adhiktam khudraa mooly, 'Maximum Retail Price', abbreviated MRP) -- a phrase every Indian shopper recognizes from product labels, even though they would never say मूल्य (mooly) out loud while actually shopping.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use मूल्य (mooly) in casual bargaining talk at a market, where कीमत (kiimat) or दाम (daam) sound far more natural -- मूल्य (mooly) is common in written price tags, invoices, and formal contexts (e.g. मूल्य सूची (mooly soochii, 'price list')).

Sense 2 : value, worth (abstract, moral, or intrinsic)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: महत्व (mahatv, importance, broader meaning), महत्ता (mahattaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind मूल्यों (moolyon, 'values') as in नैतिक मूल्य (naitik mooly, 'moral values') or पारिवारिक मूल्य (paarivaarik mooly, 'family values') -- a staple phrase in Indian editorials and school moral-science lessons discussing how society and upbringing shape character.
