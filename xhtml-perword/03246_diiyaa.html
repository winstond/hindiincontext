दीया (diiyaa)

noun A small oil lamp, traditionally made of clay, used in worship and festive lighting (most famously at दिवाली (divaalii)). One core sense, with a closely related figurative use in idioms about hope and life.
Sense 1 : small clay oil lamp
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दीपक (diipak, literary), दीप (diip, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every household in Delhi-NCR fills rows of these little clay lamps along windowsills and doorways at दिवाली (divaalii) -- the act of lighting them, called दीया जलाना (diiyaa jalaanaa, 'to light a lamp'), is itself a small ritual of welcoming light and prosperity. You'll also see them floating in rivers during आरती (aartii) or set out during other पूजा (poojaa) occasions.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse दीया (diiyaa) with बल्ब (balb) or बिजली का लैंप (bijlii kaa laimp); दीया (diiyaa) specifically means the traditional wick-and-oil lamp, not any electric light. 2) Don't confuse with दिया (diyaa), the perfective form of देना (denaa, 'to give') -- they're spelled almost identically but the vowel length and meaning differ (दीया (diiyaa) has a long ई).

Sense 2 : life/hope, figuratively (as something that can be lit or extinguished)
noun, masculine Occurs almost exclusively inside fixed phrases like दीया बुझना (diiyaa bujhnaa, 'the lamp to go out,' i.e. a life ending) or घर का दीया (ghar kaa diiyaa, 'the lamp of the house,' i.e. an heir/son) rather than as a freestanding literal noun.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Hindi often figures a person's life or a family's lineage as a दीया (diiyaa) that must be kept burning -- so घर का दीया (ghar kaa diiyaa) can mean 'the one who carries on the family line,' and a death is softened as दीया बुझ गया (diiyaa bujh gayaa, 'the lamp went out'). It's the same poetic instinct that gives English 'the flame of life.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense only surfaces in set expressions -- learners shouldn't try to use दीया (diiyaa) alone to mean 'hope' the way they might use उम्मीद (ummiid); it needs the idiom's full frame to carry that meaning.
