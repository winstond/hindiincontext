सँभलना (sambhalnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning to steady oneself or regain balance, control, or composure; also used for things being handled/managed. Closely related to its transitive counterpart सँभालना (sambhaalnaa, 'to take care of, to hold/manage'), which is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to regain physical balance, steady oneself
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: सीधा होना (siidhaa honaa, 'to right oneself')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture someone stepping off a crowded DTC bus or slipping on a wet marble floor -- सँभलना (sambhalnaa) is exactly that instant of catching yourself before you go down. It is often used with जाना (jaanaa) as a completive: सँभल जाना (sambhal jaanaa) to mean 'to steady up.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for गिरना (girnaa) or its negative when they mean 'almost fell but caught myself' -- सँभलना (sambhalnaa) is the word for the recovery itself, often paired with a near-fall: गिरते गिरते सँभल गया (girte girte sambhal gayaa, 'he almost fell but caught himself'). 2) Because it is intransitive, it does not take ने (ne) in the perfective -- सँभला (sambhlaa), not सँभलाया-type forms.

(bas se utarte vaqt vah girne lagaa par phir sambhal gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to regain emotional or mental composure
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: शांत होना (shaant honaa, 'to calm down')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word a friend uses when consoling someone after bad news: थोड़ा सँभल जाओ (thodaa sambhal jaao, 'get a hold of yourself a little'). It treats emotional stability the same way as physical balance -- something you can lose and then recover, not something you simply 'are.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use ठीक होना (thiik honaa) for 'get better emotionally,' but सँभलना (sambhalnaa) specifically implies recovering from a shock, grief, or a moment of being overwhelmed -- there was a wobble first.
Sample Sentences:
थोड़ा सँभल जाओ, सब ठीक हो जाएगा। Get a hold of yourself a little, everything will be fine.
(thodaa sambhal jaao, sab thiik ho jaaegaa।)

Sense 3 : for a situation, finances, or circumstances to stabilize or come under control
verb, intransitive The subject in this sense is typically an abstract noun -- हालात (haalaat, 'circumstances'), बाज़ार (baazaar, 'market'), गाड़ी (gaadii, 'vehicle,' figuratively 'affairs') -- rather than a person.
Synonyms: स्थिर होना (sthir honaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in news reports about the economy or after a family crisis: अब हालात सँभल गए हैं (ab haalaat sambhal gae hain, 'things have stabilized now'). It extends the physical 'catching balance' image to any situation that was teetering and has now righted itself.
