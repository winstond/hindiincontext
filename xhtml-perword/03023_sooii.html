सूई (sooii)

noun Mainly means 'needle' (sewing needle, syringe/injection needle, or a clock/gauge hand) -- one core object-sense with a couple of common extensions.
Sense 1 : sewing needle
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सुई (suii, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a mother threading a सूई (sooii) to fix a torn shirt button -- this is the everyday domestic image the word calls up before any other. It pairs naturally with धागा (dhaagaa, 'thread') in the common phrase सूई-धागा (sooii-dhaagaa, 'needle and thread').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सूई (sooii) with सुई (suii) as if they were different words -- they are the same word, just two accepted spellings, with सूई (sooii) being more common in Delhi-NCR speech.

(maan sooii men dhaagaa daal rahii hai।)

Sense 2 : injection needle / injection
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इंजेक्शन (injekshan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any kid in India and सूई (sooii) at the doctor's is what they dread most -- parents often bribe with candy right after. This sense is a natural metaphorical extension of the sewing-needle sense: both are thin, sharp, piercing objects.
Common Mistakes: 1) In casual clinic talk, सूई लगना (sooii lagnaa) or सूई लगवाना (sooii lagvaanaa) means 'to get an injection' -- learners sometimes try to say 'take an injection' with लेना (lenaa), which sounds off; लगना (lagnaa)/लगवाना (lagvaanaa) is the natural verb pairing here.
Sample Sentences:
डॉक्टर ने मुझे सूई लगाई। The doctor gave me an injection.
(doktar ne mujhe sooii lagaaii।)

Sense 3 : hand of a clock/watch or gauge needle
noun, feminine
Synonyms: काँटा (kaantaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Older folks pointing to the घड़ी की सूई (ghadii kii sooii, 'the clock's hand') to teach kids to tell time is a common household scene -- the thin, pointing shape is again what links this back to the original needle image.
Sample Sentences:
घड़ी की सूई बारह पर पहुँच गई है। The clock's hand has reached twelve.
(ghadii kii sooii baarah par pahunch gaii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
बच्चे को घड़ी की सूई देखकर समय बताना सिखाओ। Teach the child to tell time by looking at the clock's hand.
(bachche ko ghadii kii sooii dekhkar samay bataanaa sikhaao।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
पेट्रोल की सूई खाली की तरफ जा रही है। The petrol gauge needle is moving toward empty.
