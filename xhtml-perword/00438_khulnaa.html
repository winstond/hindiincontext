खुलना (khulnaa)

verb The basic intransitive verb 'to open/to come undone'; its causative counterpart खोलना (kholnaa, 'to open something') is the transitive partner learners should pair it with. Also extends to figurative senses like a mystery or secret coming to light, and a business or event 'opening.'
Sense 1 : to open (of a door, window, lid, etc.)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic example Hindi teachers use to introduce the transitive/intransitive verb pairs: खुलना (khulnaa) 'to open (on its own)' versus खोलना (kholnaa) 'to open (something).' Once you have this pair solid, other pairs like टूटना (tootnaa)/तोड़ना (todnaa) fall into place the same way.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खोलना (kholnaa) when खुलना (khulnaa) is needed -- दरवाज़ा खुलता है (darvaazaa khultaa hai, 'the door opens') describes the door opening by itself, while दरवाज़ा खोलता है (darvaazaa kholtaa hai, 'he opens the door') needs an agent doing the opening. 2) Since खुलना (khulnaa) is intransitive, it never takes ने (ne) in the past tense -- दरवाज़ा खुला (darvaazaa khulaa, 'the door opened'), not a ने-marked form.
Sample Sentences:
दरवाज़ा खुद ही खुल गया। The door opened by itself.
(darvaazaa khud hii khul gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to come undone, loosen, untie (of knots, laces, buttons, clothing)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: ढीला होना (dhiilaa honaa, to become loose)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear mothers say जूते के फीते खुल गए हैं, बाँध लो (joote ke phiite khul gae hain, baandh lo, 'your shoelaces have come undone, tie them') constantly at the school gate -- a very everyday use of this sense.

Sense 3 : to open for business/operation (shop, school, event, account)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: शुरू होना (shuroo honaa, to start)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Shopkeepers in most Indian markets will tell you their दुकान सुबह दस बजे खुलती है (dukaan subah das baje khultii hai, 'the shop opens at 10am') -- this sense is also used for bank accounts (खाता खुलना, khaataa khulnaa, 'an account gets opened') and even new schools or branches opening.
Common Mistakes: 1) दुकान खुलती है (dukaan khultii hai, 'the shop opens') refers to the shop's regular opening time or its coming into existence/operation, distinct from someone physically unlocking it, which would still often use खोलना (kholnaa) for the specific act.

Sense 4 : to be revealed, come to light (a secret, mystery, truth)
verb, intransitive Very commonly seen in the fixed collocation राज़ खुलना (raaz khulnaa, 'for a secret to come out').
Synonyms: प्रकट होना (prakat honaa, formal), राज़ खुलना (raaz khulnaa, fixed collocation for secrets)

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
