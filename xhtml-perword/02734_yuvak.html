युवक (yuvak)

noun A single-sense noun meaning a young man; commonly seen in news reports and formal contexts.
Sense 1 : young man
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नौजवान (naujvaan), जवान (javaan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in Hindi newspapers and TV news -- 'अज्ञात युवक' (agyaat yuvak, 'an unidentified young man') is a stock phrase in crime reporting. In daily conversation, though, people almost always reach for लड़का (ladkaa) instead, so think of युवक (yuvak) as the 'official register' word for the same idea.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this word in casual conversation about a friend or acquaintance; in everyday speech, लड़का (ladkaa) is far more natural for 'boy/young man,' while युवक (yuvak) has a slightly formal, report-like feel, common in newspapers ('एक युवक ने...' (ek yuvak ne, 'a young man...')) and official statements.

पुलिस एक अज्ञात युवक की तलाश कर रही है। The police are searching for an unidentified young man.
(pulis ek agyaat yuvak kii talaash kar rahii hai।)
