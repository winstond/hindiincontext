बेईमानी (beiimaanii)

noun A noun meaning dishonesty or cheating, built from बे- (be, a negating prefix) plus ईमान (iimaan, 'conscience/integrity') plus the abstract-noun suffix -ई; its adjectival relative बेईमान (beiimaan, 'dishonest') is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : dishonesty, cheating, fraud
noun, feminine
Synonyms: धोखा (dhokhaa), फ़रेब (fareb, literary), छल (chhal, literary), धोखाधड़ी (dhokhaadhdii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot around exams, business deals, and card games in Delhi -- कार्ड में बेईमानी मत करो (kaard men beiimaanii mat karo, 'don't cheat at cards') is a classic scolding among friends and family. It carries a moral weight beyond simple trickery: calling something बेईमानी (beiimaanii) implies a violation of fairness or trust, not just cleverness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for धोखा (dhokhaa) as an automatic synonym, but बेईमानी (beiimaanii) leans specifically toward a betrayal of trust or fairness (cheating in a deal, a game, an exam), while धोखा (dhokhaa) covers deception more broadly, including simply being fooled or misled without any unfairness implied. 2) The common verb frame is बेईमानी करना (beiimaanii karnaa, 'to cheat/act dishonestly') -- learners sometimes try to use बेईमानी (beiimaanii) as if it were itself a verb.

(pariikshaa men beiimaanii karnaa galat baat hai।)
