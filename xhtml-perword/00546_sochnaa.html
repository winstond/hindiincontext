सोचना (sochnaa)

verb A core everyday verb meaning 'to think' -- covering thinking-about, considering/deciding, and worrying/mulling over. One central sense with a few natural shades.
Sense 1 : to think, to consider (a thought or opinion)
verb, transitive Commonly takes a complement clause with कि (ki) -- 'मैं सोचता हूँ कि...' (main sochtaa hoon ki..., 'I think that...').
Synonyms: विचार करना (vichaar karnaa, formal), समझना (samajhnaa, overlaps in 'to think/believe' sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सोचना (sochnaa) is the verb Hindi speakers reach for constantly in casual musing -- सोच रहा हूँ (soch rahaa hoon, 'I'm thinking...') is the classic stalling phrase when someone asks you a question you haven't decided on yet, much like English 'let me think.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say मुझे सोचना है (mujhe sochnaa hai) modeled on दातिव-subject verbs, but सोचना (sochnaa) takes a normal subject: मैं सोचता हूँ (main sochtaa hoon), not the को-marked pattern. 2) In past perfective, यह transitive verb takes ने (ne): मैंने सोचा (mainne sochaa, 'I thought'), which trips up learners still building instinct for ने-marking on 'mental' verbs that feel intransitive in English.
Sample Sentences:
मैं सोचता हूँ कि यह सही फैसला है। I think this is the right decision.
(main sochtaa hoon ki yah sahii phaislaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to think over, decide, plan to (do something)
verb, transitive Frequently paired with an infinitive: सोचना (sochnaa) + oblique infinitive, e.g. जाने का सोचना (jaane kaa sochnaa, 'to think of/about going'), expressing a tentative plan rather than a firm decision.
Synonyms: इरादा करना (iraadaa karnaa, to intend), तय करना (tay karnaa, to decide/settle on)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सोचकर देखते हैं (sochkar dekhte hain, 'let's think it over and see') is a very Delhi way of politely deferring a decision -- neither a yes nor a no, just a graceful pause.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use सोचना (sochnaa) where चाहना (chaahnaa, 'to want') would be more natural -- सोचना (sochnaa) implies mulling over a plan, not a firm desire.

Sense 3 : to worry, to fret (mulling anxiously)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: चिंता करना (chintaa karnaa, to worry -- more explicit), फ़िक्र करना (fikr karnaa, to worry/fret)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A mother telling her child मत सोचो (mat socho, 'don't think/worry [about it]') is comfort-speak heard in almost every Hindi household -- it leans on this worry-flavored shade of the verb, not the neutral 'think' sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) सोचना (sochnaa) alone is milder than चिंता करना (chintaa karnaa) -- calling it 'worrying' can overstate the anxiety; context (tone, repetition, बहुत (bahut)) signals whether it means neutral thinking or genuine worry, e.g. बहुत सोचती रहती है (bahut sochtii rahtii hai, 'she keeps worrying a lot').
