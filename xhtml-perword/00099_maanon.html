मानों (maanon)

conjunction A conjunction/particle meaning 'as if' or 'as though', used to introduce an imagined or hypothetical comparison; related to मानना (maannaa, 'to believe/assume') but frozen into this single connective use.
Sense 1 : as if, as though
conjunction Introduces a clause that draws an imagined or unreal comparison to the main clause, often paired with जैसे (jaise) for extra emphasis: जैसे मानों (jaise maanon, 'as if, as though').
Synonyms: जैसे (jaise), गोया (goyaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in storytelling and description, especially when someone is painting a vivid picture: वह ऐसे रो रहा था मानों उसका सब कुछ लुट गया हो (vah aise ro rahaa thaa maanon uskaa sab kuchh lut gayaa ho, 'he was crying as if he had lost everything'). It has a slightly more literary or heightened flavor than plain जैसे (jaise), so it shows up often in songs, poetry, and expressive speech rather than flat, businesslike sentences.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for जैसे (jaise) and forget मानों (maanon) exists as a near-synonym; the two are frequently doubled up as जैसे मानों (jaise maanon) for dramatic effect rather than used alone. 2) Because it looks like a verb form (from मानना, maannaa), learners sometimes try to conjugate or inflect it -- it is frozen and invariant, never changing form regardless of subject or tense.
