बचाओ (bachaao)

interjection/verb The imperative form of बचाना (bachaanaa, 'to save/rescue'); also stands alone as the urgent cry for help, 'Save (me)!' It is far more often heard as a shouted plea than used as a calm command.
Sense 1 : help! (cry for rescue)
interjection
Synonyms: बचाओ बचाओ (bachaao bachaao, repeated for urgency)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you hear shouted in Hindi films and on the street when someone is drowning, being robbed, or caught in a fire -- often doubled as बचाओ बचाओ (bachaao bachaao) for urgency. It is one of the first 'survival Hindi' words many learners pick up from media even before formal study.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मदद करो (madad karo, 'help me/do help') when in real danger, but बचाओ (bachaao) is the instinctive cry Hindi speakers actually shout -- मदद (madad) sounds calmer and more like asking for assistance with a task.

Sense 2 : save, rescue (imperative)
verb, transitive This is the intimate/familiar (तुम) imperative of बचाना (bachaanaa); the polite form addressed to a group or a respected person is बचाओ (bachaao) also for आप in casual command, though आप-forms typically add -इए as in बचाइए (bachaaie) for full politeness.
Synonyms: बचा लो (bachaa lo, softer/compound form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As a command (rather than a cry), बचाओ (bachaao) shows up in contexts like बचाओ अपने पैसे (bachaao apne paise, 'save your money') -- a parent or advertisement urging thrift. It shares its root with the noun बचत (bachat, 'savings'), a handy link for building vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बचाओ (bachaao) in every polite context, but with strangers or elders बचाइए (bachaaie) is the safer, more respectful choice. 2) बचाओ (bachaao) takes an object with को (ko) when specifying who is being saved, e.g. उसे बचाओ (use bachaao, 'save him'), which learners sometimes drop entirely.
Sample Sentences:
अपने पैसे बचाओ, बेटा, आगे काम आएंगे। Save your money, son, it will be useful later.
(apne paise bachaao, betaa, aage kaam aaenge।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
बिजली बचाओ, बिल कम आएगा। Save electricity, the bill will come lower.
(bijlii bachaao, bil kam aaegaa।)

पानी बचाओ, यह हर बूँद कीमती है। Save water, every drop is precious.
(paanii bachaao, yah har boond kiimtii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
कृपया अपना समय बचाओ और सीधे मुद्दे पर आओ। Please save your time and get straight to the point.
