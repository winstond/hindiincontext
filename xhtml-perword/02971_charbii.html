चरबी (charbii)

noun Refers to fat or grease, mostly the physical, tangible kind -- animal fat, body fat, or the fat you see in meat and on skin. Also spelled चर्बी (charbii).
Sense 1 : fat (bodily or animal, as a physical substance)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वसा (vasaa, formal/scientific), मेद (med, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word most often at the sabzi mandi (sabzii mandii) or meat shop when someone asks the qassab (kasaaii) to trim the चरबी (charbii) off the meat, or from a doctor or elder commenting on someone's पेट की चरबी (pet kii charbii, belly fat) with the blunt directness common in Hindi health talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मोटापा (motaapaa) when they mean चरबी (charbii) -- मोटापा (motaapaa) is the abstract condition of being overweight, while चरबी (charbii) is the physical substance itself, the layer of fat you can see or point to on a body or a piece of meat.

Sense 2 : grease/lubricant fat (industrial or culinary use)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: घी (ghii, meaning scope: clarified butter specifically), तेल (tel, meaning scope: oil specifically)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In older mechanical contexts, चरबी (charbii) could refer to the animal-fat grease used to lubricate machine parts -- a sense with real historical weight in colonial India, since rumors about greased cartridges using cow and pig चरबी (charbii) were a spark for the 1857 uprising. In the kitchen, it can also mean rendered animal fat used for cooking, as opposed to plant oils.
