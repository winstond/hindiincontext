ज्यों ही x त्यों ही (jyon hii x tyon hii)

pattern A correlative pattern meaning 'as soon as x, then y' -- it links two clauses so that the second event follows immediately on the heels of the first. Closely related to the more common जैसे ही (jaise hii) ... वैसे ही (vaise hii) pair.
Sense 1 : as soon as x, (then) y -- immediate sequential correlation
pattern ज्यों (jyon) and त्यों (tyon) are an older correlative pair (like जो (jo) / सो (so)) reinforced here with ही (hii) for emphasis on immediacy; the two clauses are placed side by side with the ज्यों-clause first.
Synonyms: जैसे ही x वैसे ही y (jaise hii x vaise hii y, more common everyday variant)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet ज्यों ही (jyon hii) ... त्यों ही (tyon hii) mostly in written Hindi -- stories, essays, formal narration -- where it lends a slightly old-fashioned, literary flavor, the way 'no sooner had x than y' feels more bookish than 'as soon as' in English. In everyday Delhi conversation, people almost always reach for जैसे ही (jaise hii) ... वैसे ही (vaise hii) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop one of the two ही (hii) particles, but both are needed to carry the 'as soon as / at that very moment' sense -- without them it just reads as a plain sequence, not an immediate one. 2) This pattern is noticeably more literary/written than जैसे ही (jaise hii) ... वैसे ही (vaise hii), so learners should not reach for it as their default in casual speech.
Sample Sentences:
ज्यों ही उसने दरवाजा खोला, त्यों ही ठंडी हवा कमरे में आ गई। As soon as he opened the door, cold air came into the room.
(jyon hii usne darvaajaa kholaa, tyon hii thandii havaa kamre men aa gaii।)
