बदतमीज़ (badatmiiz)

adjective A single-sense adjective (also used as a noun-of-quality label for a person) meaning rude, ill-mannered, or impertinent -- describing someone who lacks basic tameez (tamiiz, sense of propriety).
Sense 1 : rude, insolent, ill-mannered
adjective
Synonyms: गुस्ताख़ (gustaakh, literary), अशिष्ट (ashisht, formal), उद्दंड (uddand, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic word an angry parent or elder uses in Delhi households when a child talks back or interrupts rudely -- it carries real sting, often paired with बदतमीज़ी (badatmiizii, 'rudeness', the noun form) in phrases like बदतमीज़ी मत करो (badatmiizii mat karo, 'don't be so rude'). The prefix बद- (bad-, 'bad-') is a common Persian-derived negative prefix you'll see recycled in words like बदनाम (badnaam, 'notorious') and बदसूरत (badsoorat, 'ugly').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes soften this into a mild word like 'naughty', but बदतमीज़ (badatmiiz) is a real scolding word -- it implies genuine disrespect, especially toward elders, not just childish mischief (for that, शरारती (sharaartii) is the better fit). 2) It is commonly used as a direct address or exclamation -- बदतमीज़! (badatmiiz!, 'You rude thing!') -- without needing a full sentence around it.
Sample Sentences:
तुम बहुत बदतमीज़ हो! You are so rude!
(tum bahut badatmiiz ho!)
