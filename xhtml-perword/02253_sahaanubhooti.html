सहानुभूति (sahaanubhooti)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'sympathy' or 'empathy' -- the feeling of sharing or understanding another person's pain or situation.
Sense 1 : sympathy, empathy, compassion for another's feelings/situation
noun, feminine Commonly used with the verb होना (honaa) in the pattern X को Y से सहानुभूति है (X ko Y se sahaanubhooti hai, 'X sympathizes with Y') -- the person feeling sympathy takes को (ko) and the person/cause receiving it takes से (se).
Synonyms: हमदर्दी (hamdardii), संवेदना (sanvednaa, often used for condolence/sympathy on loss), दया (dayaa, closer to pity/mercy)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a Sanskritic, slightly formal-leaning word -- you will hear it in news reports, speeches, and written condolence messages more than in casual chat, where हमदर्दी (hamdardii) feels warmer and more everyday. A common line you might hear after someone shares bad news is मुझे आपसे पूरी सहानुभूति है (mujhe aapse poorii sahaanubhooti hai, 'I have complete sympathy for you').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say सहानुभूति करना (sahaanubhooti karnaa) by analogy with other noun+करना (karnaa) constructions, but the natural verb here is होना (honaa) or जताना/दिखाना (jataanaa/dikhaanaa, 'to express/show'), as in सहानुभूति जताना (sahaanubhooti jataanaa). 2) Mixing up the postpositions: it is X को Y से सहानुभूति (X ko Y se sahaanubhooti), not X की Y पर सहानुभूति (X kii Y par sahaanubhooti).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे आपसे पूरी सहानुभूति है। I have complete sympathy for you.
(mujhe aapse poorii sahaanubhooti hai।)
