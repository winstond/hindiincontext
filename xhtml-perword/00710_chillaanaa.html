चिल्लाना (chillaanaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to shout, yell, scream' -- covers both raising one's voice in anger/urgency and simply calling out loudly.
Sense 1 : to shout, yell, scream
verb, intransitive Often used with पर (par) to mark the person being shouted at, e.g. किसी पर चिल्लाना (kisii par chillaanaa, to yell at someone).
Synonyms: चीखना (chiikhnaa, sharper/more panicked, as in fear or pain), शोर मचाना (shor machaanaa, to make noise/commotion), गरजना (garajnaa, literary, to roar/thunder)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian parent, vendor, and traffic-stressed uncle reaches for -- चिल्लाओ मत! (chillaao mat, 'don't shout!') is a classic household line. It sits a notch below चीखना (chiikhnaa), which leans toward a scream of pain or fear, while चिल्लाना (chillaanaa) is just plain loud talking, often out of frustration or to be heard over noise.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the target of shouting with को (ko) by analogy with 'telling someone,' but Hindi uses पर (par): माँ बच्चे पर चिल्लाई (maan bachche par chillaaii, 'the mother yelled at the child'), not बच्चे को (bachche ko). 2) चिल्लाना (chillaanaa) is intransitive despite feeling like it should take ने (ne) in the past because the action feels 'done to' someone -- it does not take ने (ne): वह चिल्लाया (vah chillaayaa, 'he shouted'), not उसने चिल्लाया (usne chillaayaa).
