आज्ञा (aagyaa)

noun A formal, somewhat literary word for 'permission' or 'order/command', carrying a tone of respect or hierarchy that everyday इजाज़त (ijaazat) does not.
Sense 1 : permission (formal)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इजाज़त (ijaazat), अनुमति (anumti, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear आज्ञा (aagyaa) in a respectful household when someone asks a parent or elder, आज्ञा दीजिए (aagyaa diijie, 'please give your permission/leave'), especially when taking leave to go somewhere -- it carries a note of deference that इजाज़त (ijaazat) lacks in everyday shop or friend-to-friend talk. It also appears often in devotional and mythological contexts, where a disciple or subject seeks the आज्ञा (aagyaa) of a guru or king.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आज्ञा (aagyaa) in casual requests where इजाज़त (ijaazat) or permission (parmishan) would sound far more natural; आज्ञा (aagyaa) suits formal, respectful, or old-fashioned contexts -- addressing elders, teachers, or in devotional/literary speech. 2) The common verb pairing is आज्ञा देना (aagyaa denaa, to grant permission) or आज्ञा लेना (aagyaa lenaa, to take/ask permission), not a direct verb form of आज्ञा itself.
Sample Sentences:
गुरुजी, मुझे जाने की आज्ञा दीजिए। Respected teacher, please give me permission to leave.
(gurujii, mujhe jaane kii aagyaa diijie।)

Sense 2 : order, command
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आदेश (aadesh), हुक्म (hukm)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in phrases like आज्ञा का पालन करना (aagyaa kaa paalan karnaa, 'to obey an order'), often in stories of kings, gurus, or gods commanding their subjects or disciples -- it frames obedience as a duty owed to legitimate authority, distinct from the more neutral आदेश (aadesh) used in bureaucratic or official settings.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense आज्ञा (aagyaa) is markedly more formal and hierarchical than हुक्म (hukm), which can sound almost military or blunt; आज्ञा (aagyaa) is the word used for a superior's or deity's command, not a boss barking at an employee.
Sample Sentences:
