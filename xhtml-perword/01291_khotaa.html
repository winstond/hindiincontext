खोटा (khotaa)

adjective An adjective meaning 'counterfeit, base, or flawed' -- used literally of debased coin/metal and figuratively of a person's character or intentions.
Sense 1 : false-hearted, dishonest, of bad character
adjective
Synonyms: बेईमान (beiimaan), दुष्ट (dusht, stronger, 'wicked'), कपटी (kaptii, 'deceitful')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Applied to people, खोटा (khotaa) extends the 'counterfeit coin' image to character: a खोटा इंसान (khotaa insaan, 'a false/crooked person') is someone whose outer appearance doesn't match their real, untrustworthy inside -- much like a coin that looks genuine but isn't. You'll also hear खोटी नीयत (khotii niiyat, 'bad/dishonest intention') used when someone's real motives are suspect.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not confuse with खट्टा (khattaa, 'sour') -- the spellings and sounds are close and learners sometimes mix them up in speech.
Sample Sentences:
वह बहुत खोटा इंसान है, उससे बचकर रहो। He is a very crooked person, stay away from him.
(vah bahut khotaa insaan hai, usse bachkar raho।)

Sense 2 : counterfeit, debased (of coin or metal)
adjective
Synonyms: नकली (naklii), जाली (jaalii, especially for forged documents/currency), मिलावटी (milaavtii, for adulterated substances)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The classic phrase is खोटा सिक्का (khotaa sikkaa, 'a counterfeit coin') -- and from there Hindi built a whole proverb, खोटा सिक्का कभी न कभी खनक ही जाता है (khotaa sikkaa kabhii n kabhii khanak hii jaataa hai, 'a counterfeit coin eventually rings itself out'), meaning a fraud always gets exposed in the end. This literal sense is the root image behind the figurative 'false-hearted person' sense below.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नकली (naklii) in every 'fake' context, but खोटा (khotaa) carries an older, more literal flavor tied to debased coin/metal and now survives mainly in the idiom खोटा सिक्का (khotaa sikkaa, 'counterfeit coin') and its figurative extension -- it is not the default word for a fake product.
Sample Sentences:
