निबटना (nibatnaa)

verb A variant spelling of निपटना (nipatnaa) meaning 'to get finished/settled' or 'to deal with, sort out'; both spellings are heard in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : to get finished, be settled/concluded
verb, intransitive Used intransitively for a task, job, or matter reaching completion, often with से (se) marking the task: काम से निबटना (kaam se nibatnaa, 'to finish with the work').
Synonyms: निपटना (nipatnaa, variant form), खत्म होना (khatm honaa), समाप्त होना (samaapt honaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around household chores and errands -- a mother might say घर का काम निबटा लूँ (ghar kaa kaam nibtaa loon, 'let me get the housework done') before guests arrive. It carries a sense of clearing your plate, getting one thing out of the way so you're free for the next.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this only for physical objects being 'used up', but it's most natural for tasks, chores, and pending matters getting wrapped up. 2) The से (se) after the task (जल्दी से काम से निबट लो (jaldii se kaam se nibat lo, 'finish up the work quickly')) is easy to miss since English just says 'finish the work.'
Sample Sentences:
चलो, घर का काम निबटा लूँ, फिर मेहमान आ जाएँगे। Come on, let me get the housework done, then the guests will arrive.
(chalo, ghar kaa kaam nibtaa loon, phir mehmaan aa jaaenge।)

Sense 2 : to deal with, settle, sort out (a person or matter)
verb, intransitive Often appears as the compound verb निबट लेना (nibat lenaa) when the subject settles a matter for their own benefit, e.g. उससे निबट लेना (usse nibat lenaa, 'to sort things out with him/deal with him').
Synonyms: निपटना (nipatnaa, variant form), निपटाना (niptaanaa, causative/transitive form), सुलझाना (suljhaanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in slightly tense contexts -- sorting out a dispute, a difficult relative, or an annoying colleague. पहले उससे निबट लूँ (pahle usse nibat loon) can mean anything from 'let me deal with him first' to something more pointed, so tone and context matter.
Common Mistakes: 1) When used about a person (उससे निबटना है (usse nibatnaa hai, 'need to deal with him')), it can sound confrontational or dismissive depending on tone, closer to 'settle scores with' than a neutral 'talk to'; learners should be careful with context.
Sample Sentences:
पहले उससे निबट लूँ, फिर तुम्हारी बात सुनता हूँ। Let me sort him out first, then I'll listen to what you have to say.
(pahle usse nibat loon, phir tumhaarii baat suntaa hoon।)
