साड़ी (saadii)

noun A single-sense noun: the traditional draped garment worn by women across the subcontinent.
Sense 1 : the sari garment
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear साड़ी (saadii) constantly around weddings and festivals -- from the ritual of a mother gifting her daughter a साड़ी (saadii) at her शादी (shaadii, wedding), to the everyday act of pहनना (pahannaa, 'to wear') it for office, temple, or a family gathering. Note the verb pairing: you always say साड़ी पहनना (saadii pahannaa, 'to wear a sari'), never a verb like 'put on' translated literally.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to pluralize with a bare -याँ ending without adjusting the stem; the plural is साड़ियाँ (saadiyaan), not साड़ीयाँ. 2) साड़ी (saadii) is always feminine -- adjectives and verbs agreeing with it must take feminine forms, e.g. नई साड़ी (naii saadii, 'new sari'), not नया।
