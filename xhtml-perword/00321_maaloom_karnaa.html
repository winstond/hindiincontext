मालूम करना (maaloom karnaa)

verb A compound built from the adjective/participle मालूम (maaloom, 'known') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'), used to mean 'to find out' or 'to inquire' -- distinct from मालूम होना (maaloom honaa, 'to seem/be known'), which is the passive-feeling counterpart with होना (honaa).
Sense 1 : to find out, to ascertain
verb, transitive Commonly takes a clause or का (kaa) phrase as its object: यह मालूम करना है कि... (yah maaloom karnaa hai ki..., 'to find out whether...').
Synonyms: पता करना (pataa karnaa), जानना (jaannaa, meaning scope: 'to know', not 'to inquire')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you use when you tell someone to go check on something -- जाकर मालूम करो (jaakar maaloom karo, 'go and find out') is a classic parental or bossy instruction in Delhi households, whether it is about a train's platform number or whether the milkman has come.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with मालूम होना (maaloom honaa); मालूम करना (maaloom karnaa) is something you actively do (an inquiry), while मालूम होना (maaloom honaa) describes something becoming known to you, with a dative subject. 2) The object of inquiry is usually marked with का (kaa) or introduced by कि (ki), not directly case-marked like a simple transitive object.
Sample Sentences:
जाकर मालूम करो कि गाड़ी किस प्लेटफ़ॉर्म पर आएगी। Go and find out which platform the train will arrive on.
(jaakar maaloom karo ki gaadii kis pletform par aaegii।)

Sense 2 : to inquire (about someone/something), to ask after
verb, transitive Used with के बारे में (ke baare men) or की ख़बर (kii khabar) to mean checking on someone's well-being or status: उसकी ख़बर मालूम करो (uskii khabar maaloom karo, 'find out news of him/her').
Synonyms: पूछना (poochhnaa, meaning scope: general 'to ask', broader than inquiring after news)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This overlaps closely with the first sense (finding out information) but leans specifically toward inquiring after a person's welfare or news, the kind of thing a mother says about a relative who has been out of touch: उसका हाल मालूम करो (uskaa haal maaloom karo, 'find out how he/she is doing').
