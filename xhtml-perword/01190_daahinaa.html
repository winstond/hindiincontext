दाहिना (daahinaa)

adjective Means 'right' as in the right-hand side or direction, the opposite of बायाँ (baayaan, 'left'). One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : right (side/hand/direction)
adjective
Synonyms: दायाँ (daayaan, more common everyday variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday direction-giving -- to an auto driver, on the street -- दाहिने (daahine) and बाएँ (baaen) are the words you will actually hear shouted out, often paired with मुड़ना (mudnaa, 'to turn'). दाहिना (daahinaa) and दायाँ (daayaan) are near-total synonyms in Delhi-NCR speech, so pick whichever comes to mind rather than worrying about which is 'more correct'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to दायाँ (daayaan) since it is heard more often in casual speech; दाहिना (daahinaa) is equally correct and common, especially with हाथ (haath, 'hand') and तरफ़ (taraf, 'side') -- both are natural, not a formal/informal split. 2) Since this is a variable adjective, learners sometimes leave it unagreed, saying दाहिना हाथ (daahinaa haath) correctly but then wrongly carrying the same -आ ending into feminine contexts like दाहिना तरफ़ (daahinaa taraf) instead of दाहिनी तरफ़ (daahinii taraf)).
Sample Sentences:
दाहिने मुड़ जाओ। Turn right.
(daahine mud jaao।)

अगले चौराहे से दाहिने मुड़िएगा। Please turn right at the next intersection.
(agle chauraahe se daahine mudiegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
मेरा दाहिना हाथ अभी भी दर्द कर रहा है। My right hand still hurts.
(meraa daahinaa haath abhii bhii dard kar rahaa hai।)

(kyaa hamen yahaan se daahine mudnaa chaahie?)

उसने दाहिने हाथ से गिलास उठाया। He picked up the glass with his right hand.
(usne daahine haath se gilaas uthaayaa।)

पुल के बाद दाहिनी ओर एक पेट्रोल पंप मिलेगा। After the bridge, you'll find a petrol pump on the right side.
