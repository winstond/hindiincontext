बटोरना (batornaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to gather up' or 'to collect' (things scattered around, or figuratively, attention/praise/support).
Sense 1 : to garner, accumulate (praise, attention, support -- figurative)
verb, transitive Commonly pairs with तालियाँ (taaliyaan, applause), वाहवाही (vaahvaahii, acclaim), or समर्थन (samarthan, support) as its object.
Synonyms: हासिल करना (haasil karnaa), जीतना (jiitnaa, for praise/support specifically), कमाना (kamaanaa, for reputation/praise)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this a lot in election season or after a cricket match -- a politician 'बटोरता है' (batortaa hai) votes or sympathy, a player 'बटोरता है' (batortaa hai) applause. It carries a slight connotation of actively working to pull attention or support toward oneself, sometimes with a hint of opportunism.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is actually the more common one in news and everyday talk about politicians, performers, or celebrities -- e.g. वाहवाही बटोरना (vaahvaahii batornaa, 'to garner praise') -- so don't assume बटोरना (batornaa) is only about physical objects.

(philm ko riliiz hote hii jabardast taariifen batornii shuroo ho gaiin।)

Sense 2 : to gather, collect (physical objects)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: इकट्ठा करना (ikatthaa karnaa), जमा करना (jamaa karnaa), जुटाना (jutaanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture someone sweeping up scattered marigold petals after a puja, or a kid gathering fallen mangoes in the garden -- बटोरना (batornaa) has that physical, hands-on feel of scooping up things that are spread out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for इकट्ठा करना (ikatthaa karnaa) by default; बटोरना (batornaa) is a fine single-verb alternative and sounds a bit more vivid, especially for scattered small items like पत्ते (patte, leaves) or बिखरा हुआ सामान (bikhraa huaa saamaan, scattered belongings).
Sample Sentences:
माली सूखे पत्तों को बटोर रहा है। The gardener is gathering up the dry leaves.
