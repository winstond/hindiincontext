हिन्द (hind)

noun A literary/formal name for India, mostly encountered in compounds and set phrases rather than everyday speech; contrast with the everyday word भारत (bhaarat) and the country-name हिंदुस्तान (hindustaan).
Sense 1 : India (literary/formal name)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: भारत (bhaarat), हिंदुस्तान (hindustaan), इंडिया (indiyaa, colloquial)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This word is the root behind names like हिंदुस्तान (hindustaan) and हिंदी (hindii) itself, tracing back through Persian to the old name for the Indus region. You'll meet it mainly in patriotic and ceremonial contexts, most famously in the sign-off जय हिन्द (jay hind).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use हिन्द (hind) as a everyday standalone word for 'India' the way they'd use भारत (bhaarat); in real speech and writing it appears almost exclusively inside fixed compounds and slogans (जय हिन्द (jay hind, 'victory to India'), हिन्द महासागर (hind mahaasaagar, 'Indian Ocean')) rather than as a freestanding noun in ordinary sentences.
Sample Sentences:
जय हिन्द! Victory to India!
(jay hind!)
