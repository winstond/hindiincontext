झेंपना (jhempnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning to feel bashful, embarrassed, or sheepish, usually with a shy smile rather than distress -- the awkward, blushing kind of embarrassment.
Sense 1 : to feel shy/embarrassed (bashfully)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: शरमाना (sharmaanaa), सकपकाना (sakapkaanaa, to be flustered), शर्माना (sharmaanaa, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb for that specific half-smile, looking-away moment -- like when someone praises your cooking in front of guests and you don't know where to put your hands. It's warmer and more endearing than 'embarrassed' in English, closer to being caught pleasantly off guard.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शरमाना (sharmaanaa) for every kind of shyness, but झेंपना (jhempnaa) specifically describes the awkward, caught-off-guard embarrassment that comes after being complimented, teased, or caught doing something -- not general modesty or coyness. 2) It's intransitive and describes an internal reaction; there's no direct object -- you don't 'झेंपना' someone, you just झेंपना (feel that flush of awkwardness) yourself.
