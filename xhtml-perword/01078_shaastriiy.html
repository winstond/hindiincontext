शास्त्रीय (shaastriiy)

adjective An adjective meaning 'classical' -- used mainly for classical music and dance, and by extension for anything scholarly/systematic in a formal, traditional-discipline sense. Related to शास्त्र (shaastr, scripture/treatise/scientific text).
Sense 1 : classical (music, dance, art)
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see on posters for a शास्त्रीय संगीत (shaastriiy sangiit) concert -- Hindustani or Carnatic classical music -- and for classical dance forms like कथक (kathak) or भरतनाट्यम (bharatnaatyam). It carries real prestige: calling something शास्त्रीय signals it follows a disciplined, guru-taught tradition codified in ancient शास्त्र (shaastr) texts, as opposed to लोक (lok, 'folk') or फ़िल्मी (filmii, 'film') styles.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use शास्त्रीय (shaastriiy) for anything old or traditional; it specifically marks the formal, codified classical tradition (like शास्त्रीय संगीत (shaastriiy sangiit, 'classical music') or शास्त्रीय नृत्य (shaastriiy nrity, 'classical dance')), not just anything old-fashioned -- for that, प्राचीन (praachiin) or पुराना (puraanaa) fit better.

Sense 2 : scholarly, scientific, systematic (in a formal/theoretical sense)
adjective
Synonyms: वैज्ञानिक (vaigyaanik, scientific), पारंपरिक (paaramprik, traditional)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In academic or technical writing, शास्त्रीय (shaastriiy) can describe a systematic, theory-grounded approach -- for instance शास्त्रीय अर्थशास्त्र (shaastriiy arthshaastr, 'classical economics'). This sense is less common in everyday speech than the music/dance sense and shows up mostly in educated or written registers.
