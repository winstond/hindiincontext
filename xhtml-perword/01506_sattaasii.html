सत्तासी (sattaasii)

numeral The numeral meaning 'eighty-seven' (87), used for counting and as a written/spoken number.
Sense 1 : the number 87
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers between 21 and 99, सत्तासी (sattaasii) is an irregular fused form rather than a predictable compound, which is why Hindi speakers themselves often just memorize the 1-100 chart in school rather than derive numbers on the fly. You will meet it most in prices, ages, and phone/address digits rather than in any idiom.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from सत् (sat) + अस्सी (assii, eighty) the way English builds 'eighty-seven'; Hindi numerals from 21-99 are mostly memorized as single fused words rather than assembled, so सत्तासी (sattaasii) has to be learned as its own item, not derived on the fly. 2) It is easily confused with सत्तर (sattar, seventy) and सैंतासी (saintaasii, eighty-seven's neighbor thirty-seven) due to similar sound patterns -- double-check the number when listening quickly.
