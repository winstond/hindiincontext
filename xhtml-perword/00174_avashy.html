अवश्य (avashy)

adverb A formal-leaning adverb meaning 'certainly, definitely, without fail' -- used to add emphasis and firmness to a statement or promise.
Sense 1 : certainly, definitely, surely
adverb
Synonyms: ज़रूर (zaroor), निश्चित रूप से (nishchit roop se, formal), बेशक (beshak)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see अवश्य (avashy) a lot on signboards, in official announcements, or in slightly formal promises like मैं अवश्य आऊँगा (main avashy aaoongaa, 'I will certainly come') -- it gives a sentence a touch of seriousness or old-school politeness. In relaxed daily conversation among friends, ज़रूर (zaroor) is what actually comes out of people's mouths.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat अवश्य (avashy) and ज़रूर (zaroor) as fully interchangeable in casual speech, but अवश्य (avashy) leans noticeably more formal or written -- in everyday chatter, ज़रूर (zaroor) is the far more natural choice. 2) It is an adverb and does not change form, so learners should not try to inflect it like an adjective.
