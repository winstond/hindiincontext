घटाना (ghataanaa)

verb Primarily a math/quantity verb meaning 'to subtract' or 'to reduce, decrease' something; it is the causative partner of घटना (ghatnaa, 'to decrease/happen').
Sense 1 : to reduce, decrease, lessen (an amount, quantity, speed, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: कम करना (kam karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in bazaar haggling -- daam ghataao (daam ghataao, 'lower the price') is a classic line said to a shopkeeper. It pairs neatly with बढ़ाना (badhaanaa, 'to increase'), its opposite causative.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use घटना (ghatnaa) here by mistake -- that is the intransitive partner meaning 'to decrease (on its own)', while घटाना (ghataanaa) needs an agent doing the reducing: दाम घटाओ (daam ghataao, 'reduce the price') vs दाम घटे (daam ghate, 'the price decreased').

(bhaaii, thodaa daam ghataao, bahut zyaadaa maang rahe ho।)

Sense 2 : to subtract (in arithmetic)
verb, transitive Used with से (se) marking what is subtracted from: दस से पाँच घटाना (das se paanch ghataanaa, 'to subtract five from ten').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian schoolkid learns in maths class alongside जोड़ना (jodnaa, 'to add') -- घटाओ (ghataao) is literally printed as the operation label 'subtraction' in textbooks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Word order can trip learners up: Hindi says X से Y घटाना (X se Y ghataanaa) -- 'from X, subtract Y' -- which is the reverse order of how English often phrases it ('subtract Y from X'), but here the से-marked quantity comes first.
Sample Sentences:
