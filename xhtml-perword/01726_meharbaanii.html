मेहरबानी (meharbaanii)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'kindness' or 'favor,' most commonly encountered in the fixed polite request मेहरबानी करके (meharbaanii karke, 'please, by doing a kindness').
Sense 1 : kindness, favor, graciousness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कृपा (kripaa, formal), दया (dayaa), अनुग्रह (anugrah, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear मेहरबानी करके (meharbaanii karke) used the way English speakers say 'please' or 'kindly' when asking a stranger for something, like getting a shopkeeper's attention or asking someone to move aside on a crowded bus. It carries a slightly more formal, old-world courtesy than the plain कृपया (kripyaa), and older Delhi speakers often reach for it instinctively.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this only in the fixed phrase मेहरबानी करके (meharbaanii karke) and forget it also stands alone as an ordinary noun, as in उसकी मेहरबानी है (uskii meharbaanii hai, 'it is his kindness/favor'). 2) Do not confuse with मेहरबान (meharbaan), the adjective 'kind, gracious' -- मेहरबानी (meharbaanii) is the noun form.
Sample Sentences:
मेहरबानी करके यह दरवाज़ा बंद कर दीजिए। Kindly close this door.
(meharbaanii karke yah darvaazaa band kar diijie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
मेहरबानी करके थोड़ा साइड हो जाइए, मुझे उतरना है। Please move aside a little, I need to get off.
(meharbaanii karke thodaa saaid ho jaaie, mujhe utarnaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
उसने बड़ी मेहरबानी से मेरी मदद की। He helped me with great kindness.
(usne badii meharbaanii se merii madad kii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
आपकी मेहरबानी है कि आपने इतनी दूर आकर मुझसे मुलाकात की। It's your kindness that you came so far to meet me.
(aapkii meharbaanii hai ki aapne itnii door aakar mujhse mulaakaat kii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगर आपकी मेहरबानी हो तो मुझे थोड़ा पानी दे दीजिए। If you would be so kind, please give me a little water.
(agar aapkii meharbaanii ho to mujhe thodaa paanii de diijie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
बुज़ुर्ग हमेशा दुकानदार से मेहरबानी करके बात करने को कहते थे। Elders would always ask to speak to the shopkeeper 'kindly'.
(buzurg hameshaa dukaandaar se meharbaanii karke baat karne ko kahte the।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
यह सब आपकी मेहरबानी से ही संभव हुआ है। All this has become possible only because of your kindness.
(yah sab aapkii meharbaanii se hii sambhav huaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
काश आप पर भी कोई ऐसी मेहरबानी करता जैसी आपने दूसरों पर की। If only someone had shown you the same kindness you showed others.
(kaash aap par bhii koii aisii meharbaanii kartaa jaisii aapne doosron par kii।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
मेहरबानी करके अपना नाम और पता यहाँ लिख दें। Kindly write your name and address here.
(meharbaanii karke apnaa naam aur pataa yahaan likh den।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
