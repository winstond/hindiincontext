भूलना (bhoolnaa)

verb One core meaning -- 'to forget' -- but watch how it swings between simple forgetting and the more final sense of 'to leave behind/lose touch with' something or someone; also shows up heavily as a compound verb with जाना (jaanaa).
Sense 1 : to forget (a fact, name, task, etc.)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: विस्मृत करना (vismrit karnaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: As a simple transitive verb this is a bit bookish on its own; in real conversation you will hear भूल जाना (bhool jaanaa) constantly -- Delhi speakers reach for it the moment they walk out without their phone or miss an appointment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say भूलना (bhoolnaa) alone where native speakers default to the compound भूल जाना (bhool jaanaa) to mark that the forgetting is complete/final -- both are grammatical, but भूल जाना (bhool jaanaa) is far more common in everyday speech. 2) The thing forgotten takes a direct object without को (ko) in most cases: मैं उसका नाम भूल गया (main uskaa naam bhool gayaa, 'I forgot his name'), not मैं उसके नाम को भूल गया।
Sample Sentences:
मैं अक्सर लोगों के नाम भूल जाता हूँ। I often forget people's names.
(main aksar logon ke naam bhool jaataa hoon।)

Sense 2 : to forget/leave behind (an object, by accident)
verb, transitive Takes a locative phrase for where the object was left: मैं छाता घर पर भूल आया (main chhaataa ghar par bhool aayaa, 'I left my umbrella at home').
Synonyms: छोड़ आना (chhod aanaa, close in meaning but implies intentional leaving)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind every Indian household's running joke about someone always भूल आना (bhool aanaa)-ing their keys or phone somewhere -- it is used constantly for absent-minded moments.
Common Mistakes: 1) English speakers say 'I forgot my umbrella' with no location; Hindi usually wants the place named -- घर पर (ghar par), टैक्सी में (taiksii men), etc. -- since भूलना (bhoolnaa) here really means 'left behind', not just 'lost track of'.

Sense 3 : to forget/let go of (an emotional bond, grudge, past relationship)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: भुला देना (bhulaa denaa, causative-flavored, 'to put out of one's mind')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the भूलना (bhoolnaa) of Bollywood breakup songs and heartfelt advice -- उसे भूल जाओ (use bhool jaao, 'forget him/her') is the classic line a friend says over chai when consoling someone after a breakup. It carries real emotional weight, more like 'move on' than mere memory failure.
Sample Sentences:
उसे भूल जाओ, अब वो तुम्हारी ज़िंदगी में नहीं है। Forget him, he's not in your life anymore.
(use bhool jaao, ab vo tumhaarii zindgii men nahiin hai।)
