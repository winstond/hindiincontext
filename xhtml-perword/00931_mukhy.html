मुख्य (mukhy)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'main, chief, principal' -- used constantly in news, formal speech, and everyday descriptions of what matters most.
Sense 1 : main, chief, principal
adjective
Synonyms: प्रमुख (pramukh), असली (aslii, meaning scope: 'real/main' overlap only), खास (khaas, meaning scope: 'special' overlap only)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on the news: मुख्यमंत्री (mukhymantrii, 'chief minister') and मुख्य सचिव (mukhy sachiv, 'chief secretary') are everyday political titles built directly on this word, so मुख्य (mukhy) itself starts to feel like the Hindi word for 'chief' in an official, headline sense as much as a casual one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to inflect मुख्य (mukhy) like a variable adjective (मुख्या, मुख्ये), but it stays invariant regardless of the noun's gender or number -- मुख्य बात (mukhy baat, 'main point'), मुख्य कारण (mukhy kaaran, 'main reason'), मुख्य लोग (mukhy log, 'main people') all keep the same form. 2) मुख्य (mukhy) describes importance/centrality, not simply 'first' -- for that, learners should reach for पहला (pahlaa) instead.
