इनसानियत (insaaniyat)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'humanity' in the moral sense -- the quality of being humane, kind, and compassionate. Also spelled इंसानियत (insaaniyat) with a nasalized vowel; both spellings are common.
Sense 1 : humaneness, compassion, humanity as a moral quality
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मानवता (maanavtaa), इंसानियत (insaaniyat, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in moral appeals -- a shopkeeper scolding someone for cheating a poor customer might say 'थोड़ी तो इनसानियत रखो' (thodii to insaaniyat rakho, 'have at least a little humanity'). It carries real emotional weight in everyday speech, often invoked when someone's behavior is seen as cruel or selfish, cutting across religious and social lines as a shared appeal to basic decency.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with इंसान (insaan, 'human being') -- इनसानियत (insaaniyat) is the abstract quality, not the person. 2) It refers to moral decency, not to 'mankind' as a species (that sense leans on मानवता (maanavtaa) or मानव जाति (maanav jaati)), so a sentence like 'इनसानियत ख़तरे में है' (insaaniyat khatre men hai, 'humanity is in danger') is usually about people losing their compassion, not about the species dying out.
