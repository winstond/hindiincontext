सूरज (sooraj)

noun A common everyday word for 'sun' -- one straightforward sense, used constantly in daily speech, weather talk, and poetry alike.
Sense 1 : the sun
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सूर्य (soory, formal), रवि (ravi, literary), भानु (bhaanu, literary), दिनकर (dinkar, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सूरज (sooraj) is the word you will hear from a rickshaw driver complaining about the heat or a grandmother timing her day by it, while सूर्य (soory) shows up in prayers, newspaper headlines, and school textbooks -- same sun, different register. Note also सूरजमुखी (soorajmukhii, 'sunflower', literally 'sun-facing'), a nice compound that shows सूरज (sooraj) still doing everyday work in modern vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for सूर्य (soory) in casual speech out of textbook habit -- सूरज (sooraj) is what people actually say at home, on the street, and in weather chat. 2) Common set phrases use निकलना (nikalnaa, to come out) and डूबना (doobnaa, to set) with सूरज (sooraj) -- e.g. सूरज निकल आया (sooraj nikal aayaa, 'the sun has come out') -- rather than a verb like 'rise' translated literally.
Sample Sentences:
सूरज निकल आया है। The sun has come out.
(sooraj nikal aayaa hai।)
