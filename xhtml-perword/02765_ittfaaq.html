इत्तफ़ाक़ (ittfaaq)

noun A noun meaning 'coincidence' or 'chance occurrence', most often heard in the fixed phrase इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se, 'by coincidence'); it also carries a secondary, less common sense of 'agreement/concord' between people.
Sense 1 : coincidence, chance happening
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संयोग (sanyog)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se, 'by coincidence') constantly in everyday storytelling -- someone bumping into an old friend at a market, or two people wearing the same color kurta to a wedding. It's a Persian-origin word, very much at home in ordinary Hindi-Urdu speech across Delhi, not a fancy or literary choice.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use this only as an adjective ('coincidental'), but इत्तफ़ाक़ (ittfaaq) is a noun -- to describe something as coincidental, you say यह इत्तफ़ाक़ है (yah ittfaaq hai, 'this is a coincidence') or use the adverbial phrase इत्तफ़ाक़ से (ittfaaq se, 'by coincidence'). 2) Don't confuse with एतफ़ाक़/इत्तफ़ाक़ात (ittfaaqaat) the plural, used for 'events/happenings' in a more literary register.
Sample Sentences:
इत्तफ़ाक़ से मेरी मुलाक़ात उससे बाज़ार में हो गई। By coincidence, I ran into him at the market.
(ittfaaq se merii mulaaqaat usse baazaar men ho gaii।)

Sense 2 : agreement, concord (between people)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सहमति (sahamti), मेल (mel)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense is much rarer in daily conversation than the 'coincidence' meaning, and shows up mostly in slightly formal or reflective speech -- for instance, talking about two families finally reaching इत्तफ़ाक़ (ittfaaq) on a marriage proposal.
