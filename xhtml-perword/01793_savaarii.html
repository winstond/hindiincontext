सवारी (savaarii)

noun Covers the ride/journey itself, a passenger who takes it, and by extension a mount or vehicle one rides -- all senses branch from the idea of 'riding.'
Sense 1 : ride, journey (by vehicle)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: यात्रा (yaatraa, journey in general, less about the vehicle itself)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear conductors and rickshaw-wallahs shout सवारी (savaarii) to mean 'passenger' or 'ride available' -- this everyday transport-world usage is where the word really lives for most Delhi-NCR speakers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for यात्रा (yaatraa) for any trip, but सवारी (savaarii) specifically evokes the act of riding in or on a vehicle (bus, rickshaw, horse) rather than the journey as a whole concept -- a flight to Delhi is more naturally यात्रा (yaatraa), while a short auto ride is सवारी (savaarii).
Sample Sentences:
यह ऑटो की सवारी बहुत महंगी पड़ी। This auto ride turned out to be very expensive.
(yah oto kii savaarii bahut mahangii padii।)

Sense 2 : passenger, rider (person being carried)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: यात्री (yaatrii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A rickshaw-puller calling out सवारी चाहिए? (savaarii chaahie?, 'need a ride?') is treating the passenger and the ride as one and the same word -- a nice example of Hindi collapsing the act and the person into a single term.
Common Mistakes: 1) यात्री (yaatrii) is the more neutral, general word for 'passenger' (train, plane); सवारी (savaarii) in this sense is used more in the context of local, short-distance transport like rickshaws, tongas, and shared autos, where the driver is literally waiting to pick up a 'ride' -- i.e. a person to carry.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : mount, vehicle one rides (horse, elephant, chariot, etc.)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: वाहन (vaahan, general vehicle, not necessarily something you sit astride)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in mythology and festival contexts -- each god has a सवारी (savaarii), like Shiva's bull Nandi or Durga's lion, and processions during festivals like Dussehra often feature a deity's सवारी (savaarii) being carried through the streets.

(ganesh jii kii savaarii mooshak hai।)

शिव जी की सवारी नंदी बैल है। Shiva ji's mount is the bull Nandi.
