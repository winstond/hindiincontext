फ़ालतू (faaltoo)

adjective/adverb An everyday adjective for 'extra, spare, useless' that also works as an adverb/interjection of dismissal in casual speech; both senses share the same core idea of something being surplus or not worth bothering with.
Sense 1 : extra, spare, surplus
adjective
Synonyms: अतिरिक्त (atirikt, formal), ज़ायद (zaayad, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask a shopkeeper if he has a फ़ालतू (faaltoo) button or bag lying around and he'll know exactly what you mean -- it's the word for 'got a spare one?' in daily transactions, far more common in speech than अतिरिक्त (atirikt).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अतिरिक्त (atirikt) by default because it is the 'textbook' word for extra, but फ़ालतू (faaltoo) is what people actually say at home or in the market for a spare chair, key, or bit of cloth. 2) This sense is neutral, not negative -- फ़ालतू सामान (faaltoo saamaan, 'spare/extra stuff') just means things you have on hand beyond what's needed, unlike the 'useless' sense below.

Sense 2 : useless, worthless, pointless
adjective
Synonyms: बेकार (bekaar), फिज़ूल (phizool)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in Delhi banter -- फ़ालतू बात मत कर (faaltoo baat mat kar, 'don't talk nonsense') is a classic mild scolding among friends, somewhere between 'don't be silly' and 'cut the crap.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense carries real disdain -- calling a person's बात (baat, 'talk/matter') or काम (kaam, 'work') फ़ालतू (faaltoo) is dismissive and can sting, so learners should be careful using it about people rather than things.
Sample Sentences:
ये सब फ़ालतू बातें हैं, इन पर ध्यान मत दो। These are all useless things, don't pay attention to them.
(ye sab faaltoo baaten hain, in par dhyaan mat do।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
फ़ालतू बात मत कर, काम की बात कर। Don't talk nonsense, say something useful.

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : needlessly, for no good reason
adverb Modifies a verb to say the action itself was pointless, e.g. फ़ालतू परेशान हुआ (faaltoo pareshaan huaa, 'got worried for nothing').
Synonyms: बेवजह (bevjah), बेकार में (bekaar men)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear when someone complains they wasted time or money for nothing -- फ़ालतू पैसे खर्च किए (faaltoo paise kharch kie, 'spent money for nothing') is a very common everyday grumble.
