तिरसठ (tirsath)

numeral The number sixty-three (tirsath). A single-sense cardinal numeral with no other common meanings.
Sense 1 : the number 63
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 1 to 100 are famously irregular, each one essentially its own word rather than a predictable compound, which is why even fluent speakers often just reach for the digits when precision matters (writing 63 rather than spelling out तिरसठ (tirsath)). Most learners get comfortable with the round numbers (दस, बीस, तीस) first and pick up in-between numbers like this one gradually through exposure to prices, ages, and phone numbers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like all Hindi numerals from 21-99, तिरसठ (tirsath) does not build predictably from its parts -- it must simply be memorized as a whole word rather than assembled from तीन (tiin, three) and साठ (saath, sixty). 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with similar-sounding numbers in the sixties like तिरेसठ or उंसठ; the safest approach is to drill the 60s as a block rather than rely on the sound alone.
