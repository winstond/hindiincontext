सूई लगाना (sooii lagaanaa)

verb A compound expression built from सूई (sooii, 'needle') plus लगाना (lagaanaa, 'to apply/administer'); it means 'to give an injection.' A common everyday phrase used at the doctor's or when talking about vaccinations.
Sense 1 : to give an injection
verb, transitive The person receiving the injection is usually marked with को: उसको सूई लगाना (usko sooii lagaanaa, 'to give him/her an injection').
Synonyms: इंजेक्शन लगाना (injekshan lagaanaa), टीका लगाना (tiikaa lagaanaa, often specifically for vaccination)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Kids across India grow up dreading the line सूई लगेगी (sooii lagegii, 'you're going to get an injection') at the doctor's clinic -- it is often used almost as a mild threat to make a child behave. In casual speech many people just say सूई (sooii) alone to mean 'injection,' skipping लगाना (lagaanaa) if the verb is understood from context.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say सूई देना (sooii denaa) by analogy with 'giving' in English, but लगाना (lagaanaa) is the natural verb here, not देना (denaa). 2) The subject who administers the injection (usually a doctor or nurse) takes ने (ne) marking in the past tense, since लगाना (lagaanaa) is transitive: डॉक्टर ने सूई लगाई (doktar ne sooii lagaaii, 'the doctor gave the injection').

नर्स ने मरीज़ को सूई लगाई। The nurse gave the patient an injection.
(nars ne mariiz ko sooii lagaaii।)
