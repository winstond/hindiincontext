मांस-पेशी (maans-peshii)

noun A compound noun meaning 'muscle' -- literally 'flesh-sinew' -- built from मांस (maans, 'flesh/meat') plus पेशी (peshii, 'sinew/muscle fiber'). It has a single straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : muscle
noun, feminine
Synonyms: पेशी (peshii, often used alone with the same meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word most often at the doctor's or in a fitness/yoga context -- a physiotherapist might say मांस-पेशियों में खिंचाव है (maans-peshiyon men khinchaav hai, 'there is a strain in the muscles') -- rather than in casual chat, where पेशी (peshii) alone feels more natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to pluralize or inflect the two halves separately; treat मांस-पेशी (maans-peshii) as a single unit that inflects only at the end, like मांस-पेशियों (maans-peshiyon, 'muscles', oblique/plural). 2) This is an anatomical/clinical register word -- in everyday speech about soreness or exercise, people usually just say पेशी (peshii) or even बदन (badan, 'body') rather than the full compound.
