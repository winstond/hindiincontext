पहाड़ी (pahaadii)

noun/adjective Mainly a noun for a small hill or hillock, and an adjective meaning 'of the hills/mountains'; also used as a noun for hill-region people/language. All senses share the root पहाड़ (pahaad, mountain).
Sense 1 : a hill, hillock (smaller than a mountain)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: टीला (tiilaa, mound, smaller/sandier)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Around Delhi-NCR, you will hear पहाड़ी (pahaadii) used for the Aravalli outcrops and ridges dotted around the region, like the ridge area in Delhi itself -- nothing snow-capped, just a rocky rise.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use पहाड़ी (pahaadii) and पहाड़ (pahaad) interchangeably, but पहाड़ (pahaad) is the big mountain/mountain range, while पहाड़ी (pahaadii) is specifically the smaller hill -- size matters here, not just formality.

Sense 2 : of or from the hills/mountains
adjective
Synonyms: पर्वतीय (parvtiiy, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in food and culture talk -- पहाड़ी खाना (pahaadii khaanaa, 'hill food') for Uttarakhand/Himachal-style dishes, or पहाड़ी इलाका (pahaadii ilaakaa, 'hilly area') for terrain. Sense 3 : a person from the hill regions (Himachal, Uttarakhand, etc.)
noun, masculine/feminine Used as a noun referring to people, distinct from the adjectival use describing things.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Delhi, migrants from Uttarakhand and Himachal are sometimes informally called पहाड़ी (pahaadii) as a shorthand -- usually affectionate among friends, but it can flatten a lot of distinct regional identities into one label, so tone and relationship matter.
Common Mistakes: 1) This usage can sound casual or even mildly reductive depending on tone, so learners should listen to context before using it to describe someone directly to their face.
