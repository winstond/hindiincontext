लेटना (letnaa)

verb A single-sense everyday verb meaning to lie down (rest in a horizontal position). Related to लेट (let, the participle/adjective form used with होना (honaa) to describe the resulting state).
Sense 1 : to lie down
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: पड़ना (padnaa, overlaps only in some contexts), आराम करना (aaraam karnaa, to rest, broader meaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb your mother or grandmother uses when she tells you to go lie down and rest -- ज़रा लेट जाओ (zaraa let jaao, 'just go lie down for a bit') is a classic bit of household care-talk, often said when someone seems tired or unwell. It frequently pairs with जाना (jaanaa) as a compound verb -- लेट जाना (let jaanaa) -- to emphasize the completed action of settling into a lying position.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse लेटना (letnaa) with सोना (sonaa) -- लेटना (letnaa) just means to lie down/recline, not necessarily to sleep; you can lie down wide awake. 2) The resulting state 'lying down' is expressed with लेटा होना (letaa honaa, to be in a lying-down state), not with the simple past of लेटना (letnaa) alone -- so 'he is lying on the bed' is वह बिस्तर पर लेटा है (vah bistar par letaa hai, 'he is lying on the bed'), not a continuous form.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा लेट जाओ, तुम बहुत थके लग रहे हो। Just go lie down for a bit, you look very tired.
(zaraa let jaao, tum bahut thake lag rahe ho।)
