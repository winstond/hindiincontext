शानदार (shaandaar)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'splendid, magnificent, excellent' -- a high-energy word of praise used for everything from a meal to a movie to a cricket shot.
Sense 1 : splendid, magnificent, excellent
adjective
Synonyms: बढ़िया (badhiyaa), उम्दा (umdaa), अद्भुत (adbhut, leans toward 'amazing/astonishing'), लाजवाब (laajvaab, 'unmatched', slightly more emphatic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: शानदार (shaandaar) comes from शान (shaan, 'grandeur, style, panache'), so the word literally carries a sense of 'having shaan' -- style and impressiveness rolled together. You will hear it constantly on Indian cricket commentary for a great shot, from aunties praising a wedding's decor, or from a friend describing a great meal -- it is a go-to word for enthusiastic, everyday praise, not a stiff or bookish one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अच्छा (achchhaa) when शानदार (shaandaar) is meant -- अच्छा (achchhaa) is plain 'good,' while शानदार (shaandaar) is a step up, closer to 'excellent/splendid,' so using it for something merely okay sounds overblown. 2) It is an invariant adjective -- it does not change form before masculine or feminine, singular or plural nouns, unlike कड़ा (kadaa)-type adjectives; learners sometimes try to inflect it.
