पढ़ाना (padhaanaa)

verb The causative of पढ़ना (padhnaa, 'to read/study') -- it means 'to teach' or 'to cause someone to study/read'. One core sense, used constantly for tutoring, teaching in school, and coaching.
Sense 1 : to teach
verb, transitive Takes two objects in practice: the person taught (often को-marked) and the subject taught (e.g. बच्चों को हिंदी पढ़ाना (bachchon ko hindii padhaanaa, 'to teach the children Hindi')).
Synonyms: सिखाना (sikhaanaa, focuses on skills rather than academic subjects), शिक्षा देना (shikshaa denaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In India, being asked क्या करते हैं आप? (kyaa karte hain aap?, 'What do you do?') and answering मैं पढ़ाता हूँ (main padhaataa hoon, 'I teach') is a common and respected reply -- teaching, whether in a school or as private ट्यूशन (tyooshan, 'tuition/coaching'), carries real social status. Note the family resemblance to पढ़ना (padhnaa, 'to read/study') and पढ़ाई (padhaaii, 'studies') -- all three share the same root.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse पढ़ाना (padhaanaa) with सिखाना (sikhaanaa): पढ़ाना (padhaanaa) is used for teaching academic subjects or formal lessons (गणित पढ़ाना (ganit padhaanaa, 'to teach math')), while सिखाना (sikhaanaa) is for teaching a skill or how to do something (साइकिल चलाना सिखाना (saaikil chalaanaa sikhaanaa, 'to teach cycling')). 2) Because it's grammatically the causative of पढ़ना (padhnaa, 'to read'), learners sometimes translate it literally as 'to make read' -- in everyday speech it simply means 'to teach', full stop.
