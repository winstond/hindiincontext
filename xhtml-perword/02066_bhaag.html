भाग (bhaag)

noun/verb Two main threads: a noun meaning 'part/portion/share' (also 'fate' in a fixed idiom), and the imperative form of the verb भागना (bhaagnaa, 'to run/flee') -- 'run!' or 'go away!'
Sense 1 : part, portion, share
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हिस्सा (hissaa), अंश (ansh, formal), टुकड़ा (tukdaa, a physical piece rather than an abstract share)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see भाग (bhaag) constantly in school textbooks and official documents -- chapters are called भाग एक (bhaag ek, 'part one'), भाग दो (bhaag do, 'part two'), and so on. हिस्सा (hissaa) is the word you would actually reach for in casual conversation about splitting a bill or dividing property.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for टुकड़ा (tukdaa) when they mean an abstract share or division (like a part of a book or a share in a business) -- भाग (bhaag) and हिस्सा (hissaa) are the natural choices there, while टुकड़ा (tukdaa) suggests a physically broken-off piece.
Sample Sentences:

(yah kitaab tiin bhaagon men bantii hai।)

Sense 2 : run! flee! (imperative of भागना)
verb, intransitive This is the intimate/familiar singular imperative (तू (too) form) of भागना (bhaagnaa); the everyday commands you will actually hear are भागो (bhaago) (तुम, tum) or भागिए (bhaagie) (formal).
Synonyms: दौड़ (daud, run, in the sense of speed rather than escape), खिसक (khisak, slang, 'scram')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this shouted in Bollywood chase scenes and street cricket alike -- भाग! भाग! (bhaag! bhaag!, 'run! run!') is the classic cry when danger (or the ball) is coming. It shares its root with the noun sense above: to flee is, in a sense, to take your 'portion' and go.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat भाग (bhaag) as a neutral or polite way to say 'run,' not realizing the bare तू-form imperative can sound rude or overly intimate if used with someone who isn't a close friend, child, or in an urgent/dramatic context (like shouting during danger).
Sample Sentences:

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : fate, destiny (as one's allotted portion in life)
noun, masculine Mostly survives in the compound भाग्य (bhaagy) rather than standing alone; बड़े भाग्य से (bade bhaagy se, 'by great fortune') and similar set phrases are more common than भाग (bhaag) alone in this sense.
Synonyms: भाग्य (bhaagy), किस्मत (kismat), नसीब (nasiib)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This sense grew out of the same root idea as 'part' -- your fate is literally the 'portion' life has dealt you. In everyday speech people almost always say भाग्य (bhaagy) or किस्मत (kismat) rather than the bare भाग (bhaag) for this meaning, so treat this sense as background knowledge for recognizing भाग्य (bhaagy), not as an active word choice.
