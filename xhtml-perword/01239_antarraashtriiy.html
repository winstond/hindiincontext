अंतरराष्ट्रीय (antarraashtriiy)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'international'; built from अंतर (antar, 'between') plus राष्ट्रीय (raashtriiy, 'national'), so it literally reads as 'between-national'.
Sense 1 : international
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in the news and on TV -- अंतरराष्ट्रीय हवाई अड्डा (antarraashtriiy havaaii addaa, 'international airport') and अंतरराष्ट्रीय स्तर पर (antarraashtriiy star par, 'at the international level') are staples of Hindi journalism. It pairs naturally with राष्ट्रीय (raashtriiy, 'national') and स्थानीय (sthaaniiy, 'local') as the three levels Indians commonly use to scale any issue, from cricket to politics.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes double the र by mistake, writing अंतरराष्ट्रिय (antarraashtriy) instead of the correct अंतरराष्ट्रीय (antarraashtriiy) -- note the long ई at the end, matching राष्ट्रीय (raashtriiy). 2) As an invariant adjective, it never changes form before masculine, feminine, or plural nouns -- अंतरराष्ट्रीय कानून (antarraashtriiy kaanoon, 'international law') and अंतरराष्ट्रीय सीमा (antarraashtriiy siimaa, 'international border') use the identical word.
