प्रधान मंत्री (pradhaan mantrii)

noun A compound noun meaning 'prime minister' -- literally 'chief minister' (प्रधान (pradhaan, 'chief/principal') + मंत्री (mantrii, 'minister')). One core sense, used in political/news contexts.
Sense 1 : prime minister
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पी.एम. (pii.em., colloquial abbreviation)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly on the news -- प्रधान मंत्री जी (pradhaan mantrii jii, 'Mr./Madam Prime Minister') is the respectful form of address, with जी (jii) tacked on as Hindi does for anyone senior. Compare मुख्य मंत्री (mukhy mantrii), 'chief minister,' the head of a state government -- the two titles are easy to confuse for learners since both use मंत्री (mantrii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write it as one word (प्रधानमंत्री (pradhaanmantrii)) or two (प्रधान मंत्री (pradhaan mantrii)) -- both are seen in print, so don't worry too much about which one you use. 2) The gender is masculine grammatically regardless of who holds the office; to refer to a female prime minister, the word itself does not change, only agreeing verbs/adjectives shift as needed (e.g., referring to her with feminine agreement elsewhere in the sentence).
