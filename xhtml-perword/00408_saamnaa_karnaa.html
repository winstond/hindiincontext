सामना करना (saamnaa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the noun सामना (saamnaa, 'confrontation, facing') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means to face or confront something, usually a difficulty, danger, or opponent.
Sense 1 : to face/confront (a difficulty, situation, or person)
verb, transitive Takes the thing or person faced as an object marked with का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) agreeing with सामना (saamnaa), e.g. समस्या का सामना करना (samasyaa kaa saamnaa karnaa, 'to face the problem'), not से (se).
Synonyms: मुकाबला करना (mukaablaa karnaa), जूझना (joojhnaa, to struggle with)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in news Hindi -- देश को कई चुनौतियों का सामना करना पड़ा (desh ko kaii chunautiyon kaa saamnaa karnaa padaa, 'the country had to face many challenges') is a classic headline pattern, often paired with पड़ना (padnaa) to add a sense of compulsion or hardship.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to mark the faced object with से (se) by analogy with 'facing' or 'confronting from,' but सामना (saamnaa) takes का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke), e.g. चुनौती का सामना करना (chunautii kaa saamnaa karnaa, 'to face the challenge'), not चुनौती से (chunautii se). 2) Since सामना (saamnaa) is masculine, करना (karnaa) is the form used regardless of the gender of the faced object -- learners sometimes wrongly try to inflect करना (karnaa) to agree with the object itself instead of with सामना (saamnaa).

(hamen har mushkil kaa saamnaa karnaa chaahie।)
