जड़ना (jadnaa)

verb Means to set or embed something (like jewels or studs) into a surface; also used figuratively for landing a slap or blow forcefully. Related to the adjective/noun जड़ (jad, 'root').
Sense 1 : to embed, inlay, stud (jewels, gems, etc. into a setting)
verb, transitive Takes the material embedded as object and the surface as a में (men)-marked or पर (par)-marked location: हीरे अंगूठी में जड़े (hiire angoothii men jade, 'the diamonds were set into the ring').
Synonyms: टाँकना (taanknaa, for stitching/fixing on), बैठाना (baithaanaa, to fit/seat into place)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you would hear at a jeweler's shop in Karol Bagh or Chandni Chowk describing how a stone is set into gold: सोने में हीरा जड़ना (sone men hiiraa jadnaa, 'to set a diamond in gold'). It carries a sense of permanence and craftsmanship, unlike a casual attachment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लगाना (lagaanaa) for 'to attach' in all contexts, but जड़ना (jadnaa) specifically implies embedding something into a recessed setting, like a jeweler fitting a stone -- it is not used for simply sticking or attaching flat objects.
Sample Sentences:

(sunaar sone men hiiraa jadtaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to land a forceful blow or slap (figurative)
verb, transitive Common in the fixed collocation एक थप्पड़ जड़ना (ek thappad jadnaa, 'to land a slap') and similar phrases with चूमा (choomaa, 'kiss') or मुक्का (mukkaa, 'punch').
Synonyms: जमाना (jamaanaa, colloquial), रसीद करना (rasiid karnaa, colloquial, slang)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense you will hear in everyday storytelling or Bollywood dialogue -- उसने एक थप्पड़ जड़ दिया (usne ek thappad jad diyaa, 'he/she landed a slap') has a punchy, dramatic flavor that मारना (maarnaa) alone lacks. It plays on the same idea as the jewel sense: the blow is 'set' hard into its target.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense only works with a small set of nouns like थप्पड़ (thappad, 'slap') or मुक्का (mukkaa, 'punch') -- it cannot be used generically for 'to hit' the way मारना (maarnaa) can.
