बेहोश (behosh)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'unconscious' or 'senseless' -- literally 'without होश (hosh, senses/consciousness)', using the Persian negative prefix बे- (be-).
Sense 1 : unconscious, fainted, senseless
adjective
Synonyms: अचेत (achet, formal), मूर्छित (moorchhit, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in everyday drama, from soap operas to real life: someone gets bad news and बेहोश हो गया/गई (behosh ho gayaa/gaii, 'fainted') is the go-to phrase, often followed by holding an onion (प्याज़) to the person's nose as a folk revival trick. The word पैरों तले ज़मीन खिसकना (pairon tale zamiin khisaknaa) describes a related feeling of shock, but बेहोश (behosh) itself is reserved for actual physical unconsciousness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use बेहोश (behosh) with होना (honaa) alone to mean 'to faint,' but the common idiom for the *event* of fainting is बेहोश हो जाना (behosh ho jaanaa, 'to become unconscious') -- plain होना (honaa) describes the resulting *state*, not the sudden change into it. 2) Don't confuse with परेशान (pareshaan, 'worried/distressed') -- बेहोश (behosh) is strictly about loss of consciousness, not emotional upset, even though English 'senseless with worry' might tempt a false link.
