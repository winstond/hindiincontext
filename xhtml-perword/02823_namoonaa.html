नमूना (namoonaa)

noun नमूना (namoonaa) primarily means 'sample' or 'specimen', and extends figuratively to describe a quirky or eccentric person/thing.
Sense 1 : sample, specimen
noun, masculine
Synonyms: उदाहरण (udaahran, example), प्रतिरूप (pratiroop, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in markets -- a shopkeeper offering फ्री नमूना (phrii namoonaa, 'free sample') of a sweet or spice, or a tailor showing you कपड़े का नमूना (kapde kaa namoonaa, 'a cloth sample') before you commit to a purchase.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for उदाहरण (udaahran) when they mean a physical sample to inspect (like a fabric swatch or a free product sample) -- नमूना (namoonaa) is the natural word there, while उदाहरण (udaahran) fits an illustrative example in an explanation.

Sense 2 : an odd or eccentric character (figurative)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अजीब चीज़ (ajiib chiiz, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Said with a laugh, not real insult -- यह तो बड़ा नमूना है (yah to badaa namoonaa hai, 'this guy is a real character') is how you'd describe the friend who always turns up with some strange story or habit. It's affectionate teasing, the kind of line an uncle might use about a mischievous nephew at a family gathering.
Sample Sentences:
