तथा (tathaa)

conjunction A formal written/spoken connector meaning 'and' or 'as well as', used mainly to link nouns or noun phrases in more elevated or official registers than everyday और (aur).
Sense 1 : and (formal connector)
conjunction
Synonyms: और (aur), एवं (evan, formal/written)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet तथा (tathaa) constantly in news broadcasts, official notices, invitation cards, and school textbooks -- it signals a slightly formal or written register. In casual chai-shop conversation, Hindi speakers reach for और (aur) instead, so save तथा (tathaa) for writing, speeches, or formal lists.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use तथा (tathaa) in casual speech where और (aur) is expected; तथा (tathaa) sounds stiff or bookish in everyday conversation. 2) तथा (tathaa) is used to join nouns/phrases, not typically to join two full independent clauses the way 'and then' might in English.
