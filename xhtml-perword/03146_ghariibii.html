ग़रीबी (ghariibii)

noun A single, straightforward sense: the noun for 'poverty,' the abstract-quality form of ग़रीब (ghariib, 'poor').
Sense 1 : poverty
noun, feminine
Synonyms: निर्धनता (nirdhantaa, formal), दरिद्रता (daridrtaa, literary), तंगी (tangii, financial hardship/tightness rather than poverty as a state)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear ग़रीबी (ghariibii) constantly in Indian political speeches and news, especially in the phrase ग़रीबी हटाओ (ghariibii hataao, 'remove poverty'), a slogan that has echoed through Indian politics for decades. It also appears in the common saying ग़रीबी में आटा गीला (ghariibii men aataa giilaa, 'in poverty, the flour gets wet'), used when misfortunes pile on someone already struggling.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the nuqta and write गरीबी (gariibii); both spellings are heard and understood, but ग़रीबी (ghariibii) with the nuqta is the more careful written form since the word comes from Persian/Urdu ग़रीब (ghariib). 2) ग़रीबी (ghariibii) names the state or condition ('poverty'), while ग़रीब (ghariib) is the adjective/noun for a poor person -- learners sometimes try to use ग़रीबी (ghariibii) where they actually mean 'a poor person.'
