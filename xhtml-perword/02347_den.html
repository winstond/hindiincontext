देन (den)

noun A feminine noun meaning a 'gift' or 'contribution' -- something bestowed or given, often used figuratively for a legacy or debt owed to someone's influence. Related to but distinct from देना (denaa, 'to give').
Sense 1 : contribution, gift, legacy
noun, feminine Almost always used with की (kii) to say 'X is the gift/contribution of Y', e.g. 'यह प्रकृति की देन है' (yah prakriti kii den hai, 'this is a gift of nature').
Synonyms: उपहार (upahaar), योगदान (yogdaan, contribution), वरदान (vardaan, boon)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll often hear this word in slightly reflective or philosophical statements -- 'यह समय की देन है' (yah samay kii den hai, 'this is a gift of time/circumstance') is a classic way to express that something is the product of larger forces, not any one person's doing. It carries a gentle, almost literary warmth, common in essays, speeches, and thoughtful conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse देन (den) with दैन (not a word) or with देना (denaa) itself -- देन (den) is a noun and never conjugates like a verb. 2) It is used almost exclusively in the abstract, figurative sense (a gift of nature, time, culture, etc.) rather than for a literal, physical gift handed over -- for that, उपहार (upahaar) or तोहफ़ा (tohfaa) is more natural.
Sample Sentences:
यह मंदिर हमारे पूर्वजों की देन है। This temple is a legacy of our ancestors.
(yah mandir hamaare poorvjon kii den hai।)
