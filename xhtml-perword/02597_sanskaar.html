संस्कार (sanskaar)

noun Covers a cluster of related ideas: inner values/upbringing, a life-cycle religious rite, and a more technical sense of a purifying or refining process. The everyday and cultural senses are the ones learners meet most.
Sense 1 : good values, upbringing, moral/cultural conditioning
noun, masculine
Synonyms: परवरिश (paravrish, focuses on upbringing itself), तालीम (taaliim, formal/literary, focuses on training or education), शिष्टाचार (shishtaachaar, focuses on manners specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of those words every Indian parent uses: अच्छे संस्कार डालना (achchhe sanskaar daalnaa, 'to instill good values') is a phrase you'll hear constantly when families talk about raising children well. It's less about book knowledge and more about how someone was raised -- whether they touch elders' feet, speak respectfully, share food, and so on.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often translate this flatly as "culture" or "tradition," but in everyday speech it is closer to "good values instilled by upbringing" -- it carries a moral weight that "culture" doesn't. 2) It's almost always used positively; saying someone has "bad संस्कार (sanskaar)" sounds odd -- Hindi speakers usually say उसमें संस्कार नहीं हैं (usmen sanskaar nahiin hain, 'he has no values/upbringing') rather than negating the quality of the संस्कार (sanskaar) itself.
Sample Sentences:
उसके माता-पिता ने उसे बहुत अच्छे संस्कार दिए हैं। His parents have given him very good values.
(uske maataa-pitaa ne use bahut achchhe sanskaar die hain।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(har maan-baap chaahte hain ki bachchon men achchhe sanskaar daale jaaen।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
बड़ों के पैर छूना उसके संस्कारों का हिस्सा है। Touching elders' feet is part of his upbringing.
(badon ke pair chhoonaa uske sanskaaron kaa hissaa hai।)

Sense 2 : Hindu life-cycle religious rite or sacrament
noun, masculine Often used in the plural सोलह संस्कार (solah sanskaar, 'the sixteen sacraments') to refer to the traditional Hindu life-cycle rites from birth to death, such as नामकरण (naamakran, naming ceremony) or विवाह-संस्कार (vivaah-sanskaar, marriage rite).
Synonyms: क्रिया (kriyaa, broader, not specific to life-cycle rites), विधि (vidhi, focuses on the ritual procedure)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in words like मुंडन संस्कार (mundan sanskaar, the first head-shaving ceremony for a baby) or विवाह संस्कार (vivaah sanskaar, the marriage rite) -- each major life event traditionally has its own संस्कार (sanskaar). It links back to the first sense: these rites are seen as the very process that plants अच्छे संस्कार (achchhe sanskaar) in a person.
Sample Sentences:
हिंदू धर्म में सोलह संस्कार माने जाते हैं। In Hinduism, sixteen sacraments are recognized.
(hindoo dharm men solah sanskaar maane jaate hain।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
बच्चे का मुंडन संस्कार अगले महीने होगा। The child's head-shaving ceremony will take place next month.
(bachche kaa mundan sanskaar agle mahiine hogaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
उनका विवाह संस्कार पंडित जी ने वेदमंत्रों के साथ करवाया। Their marriage rite was conducted by the priest with Vedic hymns.
(unkaa vivaah sanskaar pandit jii ne vedmantron ke saath karvaayaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अगर परिवार में पैसे होते तो सारे संस्कार धूमधाम से मनाए जाते। If the family had had money, all the rites would have been celebrated with pomp.
(agar parivaar men paise hote to saare sanskaar dhoomdhaam se manaae jaate।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Passive
यह संस्कार बिना पुरोहित के पूरा नहीं हो सकता। This rite cannot be completed without a priest.
(yah sanskaar binaa purohit ke pooraa nahiin ho saktaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अंत्येष्टि संस्कार के बाद पूरा परिवार शांति से बैठा था। After the funeral rite, the whole family sat in silence.
(antyeshti sanskaar ke baad pooraa parivaar shaanti se baithaa thaa।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
पंडित जी, कृपया उपनयन संस्कार का मुहूर्त बता दीजिए। Panditji, please tell us the auspicious time for the sacred-thread ceremony.
(pandit jii, kripyaa upanyan sanskaar kaa muhoort bataa diijie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
गांव में हर संस्कार सामूहिक रूप से मनाया जाता है। In the village, every sacrament is celebrated collectively.
(gaanv men har sanskaar saamoohik roop se manaayaa jaataa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive

(janm se mrityu tak har sanskaar jiivan ko ek dishaa detaa hai।)

Sense 3 : purification, refinement, or processing (technical/chemical or metallurgical)
noun, masculine Used in technical registers, e.g. metallurgy or Ayurveda, for a process that purifies or refines a raw substance.
Synonyms: शोधन (shodhan, more common in scientific/technical contexts), परिष्करण (parishkran, literary/technical, focuses on polishing or perfecting)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll come across this sense in Ayurvedic contexts, where a raw mineral or herb undergoes संस्कार (sanskaar) before it's considered fit for medicinal use -- the same root idea of 'refining something raw into something better' echoes the moral sense of the word.
Sample Sentences:
आयुर्वेद में धातुओं का संस्कार करना ज़रूरी होता है। In Ayurveda, it is necessary to purify metals.
(aayurved men dhaatuon kaa sanskaar karnaa zaroorii hotaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
वैद्य ने पारे का संस्कार करके उसे औषधि के लिए तैयार किया। The physician purified the mercury and prepared it for medicine.
(vaidy ne paare kaa sanskaar karke use aushdhi ke lie taiyaar kiyaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
बिना संस्कार के कच्ची धातु शरीर के लिए हानिकारक मानी जाती है। Without processing, the raw metal is considered harmful for the body.
(binaa sanskaar ke kachchii dhaatu shariir ke lie haanikaarak maanii jaatii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
इस जड़ी का संस्कार कई चरणों में होता है। The purification of this herb happens in several stages.

(guru ne shishy ko samjhaayaa ki sanskaar kie binaa dhaatu kaa sevan n karen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(yadi sanskaar sahii dhang se kiyaa jaae, to ras aushdhi prabhaavshaalii bantii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Passive

(pahle shodhan, phir maaran -- yahii dhaatu-sanskaar kii paaramprik prakriyaa hai।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
इस धातु का संस्कार होते ही उसका रंग बदल गया। As soon as this metal was refined, its color changed.
