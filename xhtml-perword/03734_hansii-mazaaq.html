हँसी-मज़ाक़ (hansii-mazaaq)

noun A compound noun meaning light-hearted joking, banter, or fun between people -- used to describe the general activity of teasing and laughing together, not any single joke.
Sense 1 : playful joking, banter, fun between people
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मज़ाक़ (mazaaq, covers just the joking part), मस्ती (mastii, leans more toward general fun/horsing around), ठिठोली (thitholii, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic Hindi twin-word (dvand) pairing हँसी (hansii, 'laughter') with मज़ाक़ (mazaaq, 'joke') to name a whole mood rather than a single act -- much like family gatherings or tea-stall conversations that drift into हँसी-मज़ाक़ (hansii-mazaaq) once the serious talk is done. It often shows up in the caution हँसी-मज़ाक़ की बात नहीं है (hansii-mazaaq kii baat nahiin hai, 'this is not a laughing matter'), signaling that things have turned serious.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use just मज़ाक़ (mazaaq) where हँसी-मज़ाक़ (hansii-mazaaq) is more natural for describing a whole atmosphere of light banter rather than one specific joke. 2) The word is normally used with करना (karnaa) -- हँसी-मज़ाक़ करना (hansii-mazaaq karnaa, 'to joke around') -- rather than treated as a countable thing you 'tell' or 'make' the way English says 'a joke'.
