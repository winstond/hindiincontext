पलटना (palatnaa)

verb Core meaning is 'to turn/flip/reverse' -- covers turning back while walking, flipping an object (like a page or a roti), and reversing a stance or decision; also has a transitive counterpart meaning 'to turn something over' or 'to flip through.'
Sense 1 : to turn back, return (retrace one's path)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: लौटना (lautnaa), वापस आना (vaapas aanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in everyday travel talk -- someone says वो आधे रास्ते से पलट गया (vo aadhe raaste se palat gayaa, 'he turned back halfway') when a trip got cut short, like a rickshaw driver who changes his mind about a fare.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लौटना (lautnaa) for 'come back' and forget पलटना (palatnaa) is the natural choice when describing the physical act of turning around mid-way, e.g. रास्ते से पलट आया (raaste se palat aayaa, 'turned back from the way')
Sample Sentences:
वो आधे रास्ते से पलट गया। He turned back halfway.
(vo aadhe raaste se palat gayaa।)

Sense 4 : to go back on, reverse (a decision, promise, or stance)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मुकरना (mukarnaa, to renege/go back on a promise)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in political commentary: नेता अपने वादे से पलट गया (netaa apne vaade se palat gayaa, 'the leader backtracked on his promise') is a stock phrase in news debates about broken promises.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is often used with से (se) -- अपनी बात से पलट गया (apnii baat se palat gayaa, 'he went back on his word') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) here instead.
Sample Sentences:
