दूर करना (door karnaa)

verb A compound expression built from the adjective/adverb दूर (door, 'far/distant') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'), meaning 'to remove, eliminate, or resolve' something -- a doubt, problem, pain, distance, or misunderstanding. Functions as a single transitive verbal unit.
Sense 1 : to remove or eliminate (a problem, doubt, difficulty)
verb, transitive Takes the thing being removed as its direct object, e.g. शंका दूर करना (shankaa door karnaa, 'to remove a doubt'). The object noun stays unmarked (not oblique) unless independently definite, following normal transitive rules.
Synonyms: मिटाना (mitaanaa), खत्म करना (khatm karnaa), हल करना (hal karnaa, for problems/doubts specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the go-to phrase for 'sorting something out' in everyday Hindi -- a mother might say अपनी गलतफहमी दूर करो (apnii galatphahmii door karo, 'clear up your misunderstanding') to squabbling kids, or a doctor might say दर्द दूर हो जाएगा (dard door ho jaaegaa, 'the pain will go away') using the intransitive counterpart दूर होना (door honaa). The two forms mirror each other the way English 'clear up' works both transitively and intransitively.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use हटाना (hataanaa) where दूर करना (door karnaa) is idiomatically expected, especially for abstract things like confusion or doubt; हटाना (hataanaa) suits physical objects better, while दूर करना (door karnaa) is the natural choice for feelings, problems, and misunderstandings. 2) The ने-construction applies normally here since this sense is fully transitive, but learners sometimes forget करना (karnaa) still needs ने (ne) marking on the subject in past perfective even though दूर (door) itself is invariant.

Sense 2 : to remove physical distance, to send/push away
verb, transitive Used with a physical or relational distance as the object, e.g. फासला दूर करना (phaaslaa door karnaa, 'to close/remove the distance').
Synonyms: अलग करना (alag karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Often used metaphorically for relationships -- रिश्तों की दूरियां दूर करना (rishton kii dooriyaan door karnaa, 'to bridge the distances in relationships') is a common phrase in advice columns and family dramas, extending the physical sense of दूर (door) to emotional closeness.
