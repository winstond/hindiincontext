भेजना (bhejnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to send' -- an object, a person, a message -- covering both physical and figurative dispatch.
Sense 1 : to send
verb, transitive
Synonyms: रवाना करना (ravaanaa karnaa, for people/vehicles departing), प्रेषित करना (preshit karnaa, formal/written)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: भेजना (bhejnaa) is one of the first 'communication' verbs learners need, since it covers everything from भेजना पैसे (paise bhejnaa, sending money home) to WhatsApp पर मैसेज भेजना (maisej bhejnaa, sending a message) -- the same verb comfortably spans an envelope and an app.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a transitive verb and takes ने (ne) marking on the subject in the perfective past, e.g. उसने चिट्ठी भेजी (usne chitthii bhejii, 'she sent the letter') -- learners sometimes forget this since 'send' feels like a one-way action rather than something 'done to' an object. 2) The person or place something is sent *to* is marked with को (ko), e.g. मैंने उसे पैसे भेजे (usne paise bheje, 'I sent him money') -- learners sometimes reach for पास (paas) or के लिए (ke lie) instead.
Sample Sentences:
मैं तुम्हें कल पैसे भेजूँगा। I will send you money tomorrow.
(main tumhen kal paise bhejoongaa।)
