की तुलना में (kii tulnaa men)

postposition A postpositional phrase meaning 'in comparison to' or 'compared with', built from तुलना (tulnaa, 'comparison') plus the postpositions की (kii) and में (men). Used to set up comparisons between two nouns/pronouns.
Sense 1 : compared to, in comparison with
postposition Attaches to the oblique/genitive form of the noun being compared: X की तुलना में (X kii tulnaa men) means 'compared to X'; the second half of the comparison typically follows with ज़्यादा (zyaadaa), कम (kam), or a comparative adjective.
Synonyms: के मुकाबले (ke mukaable), के सामने (ke saamne, contextual/figurative use)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will reach for whenever a Delhi shopkeeper, teacher, or news anchor sets up a comparison -- 'पिछले साल की तुलना में' (pichhle saal kii tulnaa men, 'compared to last year') is extremely common in everyday talk about prices, weather, and results. It pairs naturally with ज़्यादा (zyaadaa) or कम (kam) later in the sentence to complete the comparison.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that the noun before this phrase takes की/के agreement matching its own gender/number, not a fixed की (kii) -- e.g. लड़कों की तुलना में (ladkon kii tulnaa men, 'compared to the boys') still uses की because तुलना (tulnaa) is feminine, regardless of the compared noun's gender. 2) This phrase compares two things being placed side by side; it is not used for simple superlatives (avoid using it where सबसे (sabse) 'the most' is meant).
