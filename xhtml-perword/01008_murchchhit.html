मुर्च्छित (murchchhit)

adjective A formal/literary adjective meaning 'fainted' or 'unconscious' -- describes someone who has lost consciousness, typically from shock, illness, or injury.
Sense 1 : unconscious, fainted
adjective Almost always paired with होना (honaa) or पड़ना (padnaa) to describe the state or the act of falling into it, e.g. मुर्च्छित हो जाना (murchchhit ho jaanaa, 'to fall unconscious').
Synonyms: बेहोश (behosh), अचेत (achet, literary)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet this word most often in novels, newspaper reports, or mythological stories -- picture a heroine 'मुर्च्छित हो गई' (murchchhit ho gaii, 'fell unconscious') in an old Hindi serial's dramatic scene. In daily life among friends and family, बेहोश (behosh) is what actually comes out of people's mouths.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in everyday speech, but बेहोश (behosh) is what people actually say in conversation -- मुर्च्छित (murchchhit) belongs to written narration, medical/formal description, or old-fashioned storytelling, so using it in casual talk can sound stilted or bookish.
Sample Sentences:
गर्मी से चक्कर आने पर वह मुर्च्छित हो गई। Feeling dizzy from the heat, she fell unconscious.
(garmii se chakkar aane par vah murchchhit ho gaii।)
