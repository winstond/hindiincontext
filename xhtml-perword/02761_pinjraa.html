पिंजरा (pinjraa)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'cage,' with a common figurative extension for anything that confines or traps -- physically or emotionally.
Sense 1 : cage (for birds/animals)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कैद (kaid, confinement, broader sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Almost every Indian household with a pet bird -- a मैना (mainaa, myna) or तोता (totaa, parrot) -- keeps it in a पिंजरा (pinjraa) hung on a hook near a window or veranda; it is a very ordinary domestic object, not an exotic one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पिंजरा (pinjraa) with जेल (jel) -- पिंजरा (pinjraa) is specifically a small enclosure for birds or animals, not a human prison.

Sense 2 : figurative trap or confinement (loss of freedom)
noun, masculine Used with verbs like तोड़ना (todnaa, 'to break') to describe escaping social, emotional, or domestic confinement -- सोने का पिंजरा (sone kaa pinjraa, 'a golden cage') is a set idiomatic image for a comfortable but unfree life.
Synonyms: जाल (jaal, net/trap, broader sense), बंधन (bandhan, bond/tie, more emotional/abstract)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: सोने का पिंजरा (sone kaa pinjraa, 'a golden cage') is a stock phrase in Hindi songs and serials for a woman who has material comfort -- a nice house, jewelry -- but no personal freedom; the bird-in-a-cage image maps very directly onto ideas of marriage, family duty, or a gilded but restrictive job.
