दही (dahii)

noun A single, everyday sense: the dairy product yogurt/curd (dahii), a staple in North Indian kitchens.
Sense 1 : yogurt, curd
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: दही (dahii) is everywhere in a North Indian kitchen: a bowl of it appears alongside almost any meal, it goes into रायता (raaytaa), लस्सी (lassii), and countless marinades, and there's an old belief that eating dahi before an exam or a journey brings good luck ('दही (dahii) खाकर जाओ', 'go after eating some yogurt'). It is grammatically masculine, so you'll hear 'दही अच्छा है (dahii achchhaa hai, the yogurt is good)', not feminine agreement.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make दही (dahii) agree like a regular masculine noun in oblique/plural contexts, but it is mostly used as an uncountable mass noun, so plural forms are rarely needed in everyday speech. 2) Do not confuse with छाछ (chhaachh, buttermilk), which is a thin, salty drink made from dahi, not the same thing.
