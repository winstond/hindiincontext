चुप (chup)

adjective/interjection Mainly an adjective/adverb meaning 'quiet, silent' -- used constantly with होना (honaa) and रहना (rahnaa) to mean 'be quiet' or 'stay quiet,' and it also appears as an interjection meaning 'quiet!' or 'hush!'
Sense 1 : silent, quiet (not speaking)
adjective This is an invariant adjective -- it does not change form for gender or number. It is almost always paired with होना (honaa) or रहना (rahnaa) rather than standing alone before a noun.
Synonyms: खामोश (khaamosh), शांत (shaant, can also mean 'calm' more broadly), मौन (maun, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear चुप हो जाओ (chup ho jaao, 'be quiet/hush up') and चुप रहो (chup raho, 'stay quiet') everywhere from classrooms to living rooms -- it's the go-to phrase for telling someone, gently or sharply depending on tone, to stop talking.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect चुप (chup) like कड़ा (kadaa) -- it never changes form, regardless of the gender or number of the subject. 2) चुप (chup) describes not speaking, not being calm or peaceful in general -- for that, शांत (shaant) is the better word.

Sense 2 : hush!, quiet!, be silent!
interjection
Synonyms: खामोश (khaamosh, sharper/more formal command), शश (shash, a hushing sound, not a full word)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As a single sharp word, चुप (chup) works exactly like English 'quiet!' or 'hush!' -- often doubled for emphasis as चुप चुप (chup chup) or intensified with एकदम (ekdam, 'absolutely') as in एकदम चुप (ekdam chup, 'dead silent').
Sample Sentences:
चुप! Quiet!
(chup!)

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
