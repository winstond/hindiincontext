पहलू (pahloo)

noun Mainly means 'aspect/facet' of an issue or topic; also used more concretely for a 'side' or 'flank' (including the side of the body) in some fixed expressions.
Sense 1 : aspect, facet (of an issue, topic, or situation)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पक्ष (paksh), बिंदु (bindu, one facet/point, narrower), दृष्टिकोण (drishtikon, viewpoint, related but distinct)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly in Indian news debates and panel discussions -- 'iस मुद्दे का एक और पहलू है' (is mudde kaa ek aur pahloo hai, 'there's another facet to this issue') is classic anchor-speak. It signals a thoughtful, multi-sided way of looking at a problem, which is valued highly in argumentative Hindi discourse.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पक्ष (paksh) and पहलू (pahloo) interchangeably, but पहलू (pahloo) leans toward 'a facet of something complex' (e.g. किसी मसले का एक पहलू (kisii masle kaa ek pahloo, 'one facet of an issue')), while पक्ष (paksh) more often means 'side' in a debate or a party to something. 2) It is usually used with numerals or quantifiers -- एक पहलू (ek pahloo), दूसरा पहलू (doosraa pahloo), कई पहलू (kaii pahloo) -- rather than alone.

(is mudde kaa ek aur pahloo hai jis par hamen dhyaan denaa chaahie।)

Sense 2 : side, flank (physical side, including of the body)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तरफ़ (taraf), बगल (bagal, side of the body, narrower)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this concrete sense mostly in older or more literary contexts, describing someone lying करवट (karvat, 'turning side') or resting on one पहलू (pahloo). The abstract 'facet' sense (see other entry) is the one that dominates modern conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) This physical sense is far less common in everyday speech than the 'aspect' sense; most learners will meet पहलू (pahloo) meaning 'facet' long before they meet it meaning 'side of the body,' so don't over-apply the literal sense.
