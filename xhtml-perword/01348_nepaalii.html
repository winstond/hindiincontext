नेपाली (nepaalii)

adjective/noun Refers to Nepal (nepaal): as an adjective it describes something as Nepali/Nepalese, and as a noun it names either a person from Nepal or the Nepali language.
Sense 1 : of or relating to Nepal
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Delhi given the large Nepali-origin community working in the city -- नेपाली खाना (nepaalii khaanaa, 'Nepali food') and नेपाली मोमो (nepaalii momo, 'Nepali momos') are everyday phrases, especially around markets and street-food stalls.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect this like a regular -आ (aa) adjective (e.g. making a feminine form नेपाली* with a different ending); it is actually invariant and stays नेपाली (nepaalii) regardless of the gender or number of the noun it describes -- unlike कड़ा (kadaa), which does change.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे नेपाली खाना बहुत पसंद है। I like Nepali food a lot.
(mujhe nepaalii khaanaa bahut pasand hai।)

Sense 2 : a person from Nepal
noun, masculine/feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: As with other nationality nouns like भारतीय (bhaartiiy) or अमरीकी (amriikii), the same word covers both the adjective and the person -- मेरा दोस्त नेपाली है (meraa dost nepaalii hai, 'my friend is Nepali') uses it exactly as English would.

Sense 3 : the Nepali language
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Nepali is written in the same Devanagari script as Hindi and shares a lot of vocabulary, so Hindi speakers often say they can understand quite a bit of नेपाली (nepaalii) even without formally learning it.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे नेपाली नहीं आती। I don't know Nepali.
(mujhe nepaalii nahiin aatii।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
नेपाली देवनागरी लिपि में लिखी जाती है। Nepali is written in the Devanagari script.
(nepaalii devnaagrii lipi men likhii jaatii hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
हिंदी बोलने वाले नेपाली का बहुत कुछ समझ लेते हैं, क्योंकि दोनों भाषाओं में शब्द मिलते-जुलते हैं। Hindi speakers understand quite a lot of Nepali, because the words in the two languages are similar.
(hindii bolne vaale nepaalii kaa bahut kuchh samajh lete hain, kyonki donon bhaashaaon men shabd milte-julte hain।)
