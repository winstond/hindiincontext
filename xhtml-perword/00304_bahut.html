बहुत (bahut)

adverb/adjective One of the first intensifiers learners meet: as an adverb it means 'very/a lot', and as an adjective/quantifier it means 'much/many'. Same word, same spelling, both senses in constant use.
Sense 1 : very, extremely (intensifier before adjectives/adverbs)
adverb Placed directly before the adjective or adverb it intensifies: बहुत अच्छा (bahut achchhaa, 'very good').
Synonyms: अति (ati, literary), काफ़ी (kaafii, means 'quite/fairly', weaker than bahut), ज़्यादा (zyaadaa, more like 'too/excessively' in comparative contexts)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every learner overuses first, and rightly so -- बहुत अच्छा (bahut achchhaa, 'very good') and बहुत बढ़िया (bahut badhiyaa, 'very nice') are everyday currency in Delhi speech, said with a little head-tilt of approval.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ज़्यादा (zyaadaa) when they mean plain 'very' -- ज़्यादा (zyaadaa) leans toward 'too much/excessive', while बहुत (bahut) is the neutral intensifier, as in बहुत अच्छा (bahut achchhaa, 'very good') versus ज़्यादा अच्छा नहीं (zyaadaa achchhaa nahiin, 'not too good'). 2) As an invariant adverb, बहुत (bahut) never changes form here, unlike the adjective sense which does.

Sense 2 : much, a lot of, many
adjective Functions as a quantifier before a noun: बहुत पानी (bahut paanii, 'a lot of water'), बहुत लोग (bahut log, 'many people'). Agrees with the noun in gender/number like a regular adjective (बहुत सारी बातें (bahut saarii baaten, 'many things')).
Synonyms: अधिक (adhik, formal), ज़्यादा (zyaadaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear बहुत सारा (bahut saaraa, 'a whole lot of') constantly in kitchens and markets in Delhi-NCR -- बहुत सारी सब्ज़ी (bahut saarii sabzii, 'lots of vegetables') is how a vendor or mother describes abundance, softer and more colloquial than plain बहुत (bahut) alone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes leave बहुत (bahut) uninflected even with a feminine or plural noun where agreement is expected, saying बहुत लड़की (bahut ladkii) instead of बहुत सी लड़कियाँ (bahut sii ladkiyaan, 'many girls'); the compound बहुत सा/सी/से (bahut saa) is often the more natural quantifier phrase before a noun.
