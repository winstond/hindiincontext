झेंप (jhemp)

noun A feeling-word for the awkward, sheepish embarrassment you feel when caught out or self-conscious -- more 'shy fluster' than 'shame'. Mainly used as a noun; the related verb झेंपना (jhempnaa) means 'to feel awkward/sheepish'.
Sense 1 : awkward embarrassment, sheepishness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: शर्म (sharm, broader term for shame/shyness), संकोच (sankoch, hesitation/reticence), खिसियानापन (khisiyaanaapan, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture someone who's just been caught in a small lie or has tripped over their own words in front of friends -- that sheepish half-smile and looking away is झेंप (jhemp). It's a very physical, visible embarrassment, often accompanied by a nervous laugh, and Hindi speakers love the phrase झेंप मिटाना (jhemp mitaanaa) for the little cover-up gesture (a joke, a scratch of the head) people do to hide it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for शर्म (sharm) for every kind of embarrassment, but झेंप (jhemp) specifically names the flustered, caught-out feeling -- like being teased or fumbling in front of others -- not general modesty or shyness around elders. 2) It very commonly pairs with the verb मिटाना (mitaanaa) or छिपाना (chhipaanaa) in the sense of 'to hide/cover one's embarrassment', as in झेंप मिटाना (jhemp mitaanaa, 'to cover up one's embarrassment').
