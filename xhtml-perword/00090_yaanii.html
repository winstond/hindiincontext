यानी (yaanii)

conjunction A single-sense discourse connector meaning 'that is (to say)' or 'meaning' -- used to restate, clarify, or explain what was just said.
Sense 1 : that is to say; in other words
conjunction
Synonyms: मतलब (matlab), अर्थात (arthaat, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear यानी (yaanii) constantly in explanations -- someone gives a technical term or a tricky phrase, then pauses and says यानी (yaanii, 'that is to say') before rephrasing it in simpler words. It is the verbal equivalent of a comma followed by 'i.e.' in English, and Delhi speakers use it very naturally to bridge formal and casual registers in the same sentence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for 'मतलब' (matlab) instead, which is fine in casual speech, but यानी (yaanii) sits comfortably in both spoken and slightly more careful/explanatory speech, so it is worth actively practicing rather than defaulting only to मतलब (matlab). 2) यानी (yaanii) introduces a restatement or clarification of what was just said -- it does not mean 'therefore' or 'so' (that sense belongs to इसलिए islie), a mix-up some learners make since both can loosely translate as 'so' in casual English.

(vah meraa mauseraa bhaaii hai, yaanii merii mausii kaa betaa।)
