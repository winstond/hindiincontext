जासूस (jaasoos)

noun A single, straightforward sense: a spy or detective. Common in news, spy novels, and children's detective stories alike.
Sense 1 : spy, detective
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गुप्तचर (guptchar, formal), भेदिया (bhediyaa, informal), खुफ़िया एजेंट (khufiyaa ejent, intelligence agent)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: जासूसी उपन्यास (jaasoosii upanyaas, 'detective novels') are a beloved genre in Hindi popular fiction, and kids grow up on जासूस (jaasoos) characters the way English readers grow up on detective heroes. In everyday family banter, calling someone घर का जासूस (ghar kaa jaasoos, 'the household spy') is an affectionate jab at a nosy relative who always finds out everyone's secrets.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume जासूस (jaasoos) only means a professional government spy, but it is used just as often for a private detective or even a nosy person in casual speech, similar to calling someone a 'detective' jokingly in English. 2) The related abstract noun जासूसी (jaasoosii) means 'spying/espionage' or 'detective work' -- learners often try to use जासूस (jaasoos) itself as if it were the activity rather than the person.
