बसाना (basaanaa)

verb The causative of बसना (basnaa, 'to settle/dwell'): बसाना (basaanaa) means 'to settle someone/something, to found a place, to make a home for' -- it also has an extended figurative sense of building a life or world around someone in the heart.
Sense 1 : to settle (people), colonize, populate
verb, transitive
Synonyms: आबाद करना (aabaad karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in the context of history and geography lessons -- मुग़लों ने यह शहर बसाया (mughlon ne yah shahar basaayaa, 'the Mughals founded/settled this city') is a classic textbook-style sentence. It's the go-to verb for talking about founding towns, resettling refugees, or colonizing new land.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with बसना (basnaa), the intransitive 'to settle down' -- बसाना (basaanaa) always needs someone doing the settling, e.g. सरकार ने शरणार्थियों को बसाया (sarkaar ne sharnaarthiyon ko basaayaa, 'the government settled the refugees'). 2) It is not used for a person settling themselves somewhere -- for that, use बसना (basnaa) instead.
Sample Sentences:
मुग़लों ने यह शहर बसाया था। The Mughals had settled this city.
(mughlon ne yah shahar basaayaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : to found, establish (a city, home, household)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: स्थापित करना (sthaapit karnaa, formal), बनाना (banaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: घर बसाना (ghar basaanaa) is a phrase loaded with emotional weight in Hindi -- it's what elders wish for a newly married couple, meaning far more than bricks and mortar; it's about building a life together. You'll hear it in wedding blessings and also in film dialogues about starting over.
Common Mistakes: 1) घर बसाना (ghar basaanaa) is a set phrase meaning 'to start a family / set up a household,' often after marriage -- it doesn't literally mean 'to build a house' (that's घर बनाना, ghar banaanaa).
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to make a place for someone in one's heart/life (figurative)
verb, transitive Commonly seen in the phrase दिल में बसाना (dil men basaanaa, 'to enshrine in one's heart') or आँखों में बसाना (aankhon men basaanaa).
Synonyms: समाना (samaanaa, intransitive counterpart)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a favorite of Hindi film lyrics and shayari -- मैंने तुम्हें दिल में बसा लिया (mainne tumhen dil men basaa liyaa, 'I have enshrined you in my heart') captures how Hindi treats love as making someone a resident of your inner self, not just a feeling.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is almost always paired with दिल में (dil men) or आँखों में (aankhon men) -- dropping the location phrase makes it sound like the literal 'settle a population' sense instead.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने तुम्हें दिल में बसा लिया है। I have enshrined you in my heart.
(mainne tumhen dil men basaa liyaa hai।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
