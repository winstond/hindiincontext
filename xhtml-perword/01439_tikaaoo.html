टिकाऊ (tikaaoo)

adjective An adjective meaning 'durable, long-lasting', used for objects and materials that hold up over time; related to the verb टिकना (tiknaa, to last/stay put).
Sense 1 : durable, long-lasting
adjective
Synonyms: मजबूत (majboot, focuses on sturdiness rather than lifespan), स्थायी (sthaayii, more formal, leans toward 'permanent')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in Indian markets when haggling over pots, shoes, or furniture -- a shopkeeper selling steel utensils will proudly say ये बहुत टिकाऊ है (ye bahut tikaaoo hai, 'this is very durable'), because in a value-conscious market, durability often matters more to buyers than looks or brand.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use मजबूत (majboot) when they mean टिकाऊ (tikaaoo) -- मजबूत (majboot) is about strength/sturdiness (a strong rope), while टिकाऊ (tikaaoo) is about lasting a long time in use (a phone that still works after five years); a thing can be मजबूत (majboot) but not टिकाऊ (tikaaoo) if it wears out fast despite being tough. 2) Learners often forget this adjective is invariant and try to inflect it like कड़ा (kadaa), producing wrong forms such as टिकाई or टिकाऊं.
