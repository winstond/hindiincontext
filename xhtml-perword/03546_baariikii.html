बारीकी (baariikii)

noun A noun meaning fineness or minute detail -- used both literally (fine craftsmanship, precision) and in the common set phrase बारीकी से (baariikii se) meaning 'in detail, meticulously.'
Sense 1 : minuteness, subtlety, fine detail
noun, feminine Very commonly used in the plural, बारीकियाँ (baariikiyaan), to mean 'the finer points/nuances' of a subject or skill.
Synonyms: सूक्ष्मता (sookshmtaa, formal), नज़ाकत (nazaakat, delicacy of work/craft), गहराई (gahraaii, depth, meaning scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear बारीकी से देखो (baariikii se dekho, 'look closely/carefully') from a tailor checking a stitch, a teacher checking your handwriting, or an uncle inspecting a mango at the market -- it's the go-to phrase for close, detail-oriented scrutiny in everyday Delhi-NCR speech. The plural बारीकियाँ (baariikiyaan) is especially common when discussing mastering a craft or language, as in किसी भाषा की बारीकियाँ सीखना (kisii bhaashaa kii baariikiyaan siikhnaa, 'to learn the nuances of a language').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for विवरण (vivran, 'details/description') when they actually mean the finer, harder-to-see nuances -- बारीकी (baariikii) implies subtlety or skill, not just a list of facts. 2) The adverbial phrase बारीकी से (baariikii se, 'closely, meticulously') is used constantly with verbs like देखना (dekhnaa) and समझना (samajhnaa) -- learners sometimes say गहराई से (gahraaii se) instead, which shifts the sense toward 'depth' rather than 'close attention to detail.'
