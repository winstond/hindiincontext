बार (baar)

noun Two main lives: as a noun meaning 'time/occasion' (used constantly with numerals and words like हर, इस, अगली), and as a borrowed noun meaning 'bar' (the drinking establishment).
Sense 1 : occasion, instance, time (as in 'one time, two times')
noun, feminine Combines with numerals (एक बार (ek baar, 'once'), दो बार (do baar, 'twice')) and with इस/हर/अगली etc. to mean 'this time,' 'every time,' 'next time.'
Synonyms: दफ़ा (dafaa), मर्तबा (martbaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in everyday scolding and encouragement alike: कितनी बार कहा है (kitnii baar kahaa hai, 'how many times have I told you')! is a classic parental line, and अगली बार (aglii baar, 'next time') is the standard way to promise or console.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say एक टाइम (ek taaim) borrowing English 'time' directly -- बार (baar) is the natural native choice for counting occasions. 2) हर बार (har baar, 'every time') and हर वक़्त (har vaqt, 'all the time') get confused -- the former counts discrete occasions, the latter means continuously.
Sample Sentences:
कितनी बार कहा है तुमसे कि जूते बाहर उतारो! How many times have I told you to take your shoes off outside!
(kitnii baar kahaa hai tumse ki joote baahar utaaro!)

Sense 2 : a bar (place serving alcohol)
noun, masculine Direct English loanword; refers only to the drinking establishment, not to a physical bar/rod of metal.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is simply the English 'bar' borrowed as-is, common in urban contexts -- बार में मिलते हैं (baar men milte hain, 'let's meet at the bar') is a typical line among friends in cities like Delhi or Gurgaon.
