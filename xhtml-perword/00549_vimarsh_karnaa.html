विमर्श करना (vimarsh karnaa)

verb A formal compound verb meaning 'to discuss' or 'to deliberate' -- built from the noun विमर्श (vimarsh, discussion/deliberation) plus करना (karnaa, to do). One core sense, but registered as distinctly formal/written.
Sense 1 : to discuss, deliberate (formally)
verb, intransitive Takes the topic with पर (par) or के बारे में (ke baare men): 'X पर विमर्श करना' (X par vimarsh karnaa).
Synonyms: चर्चा करना (charchaa karnaa), बातचीत करना (baatchiit karnaa, everyday), विचार-विमर्श करना (vichaar-vimarsh karnaa, common paired form), बहस करना (bahas karnaa, meaning scope: leans toward debate/argument rather than calm discussion)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the kind of verb you hear on the evening news -- 'नेताओं ने मुद्दे पर विमर्श किया' (netaaon ne mudde par vimarsh kiyaa, 'the leaders deliberated on the issue') -- rather than at the dinner table, where चर्चा करना (charchaa karnaa) or सिर्फ बात करना (sirph baat karnaa) would take over. Think of विमर्श करना (vimarsh karnaa) as reserved for panels, parliament, and press releases.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this when everyday चर्चा करना (charchaa karnaa) or बात करना (baat karnaa) would sound far more natural -- विमर्श करना (vimarsh karnaa) belongs in written Hindi, news reports, official meetings, or academic contexts, not casual chat. 2) The noun विमर्श (vimarsh) is frequently doubled as विचार-विमर्श (vichaar-vimarsh, 'thought-discussion'), and learners may not realize this paired form is actually more common than विमर्श alone.
Sample Sentences:
समिति ने नई नीति पर विमर्श किया। The committee deliberated on the new policy.
(samiti ne naii niiti par vimarsh kiyaa।)
