सज़ा (sazaa)

noun One core meaning: punishment or penalty, most often heard in legal, disciplinary, or parental contexts. Also spelled सजा (sajaa) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : punishment, penalty
noun, feminine
Synonyms: दंड (dand, formal), जुर्माना (jurmaanaa, specifically a fine)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear सज़ा (sazaa) everywhere from courtroom dramas (सज़ा-ए-मौत (sazaa-e-maut, 'death penalty')) to a mother scolding a child with सज़ा मिलेगी (sazaa milegii, 'you'll get punished'). It carries a slightly dramatic, formal weight compared to casual scolding words, so it fits both a judge's verdict and a parent's half-joking threat.
Common Mistakes: 1) सज़ा (sazaa) pairs with देना (denaa, to give) and मिलना (milnaa, to receive), as in सज़ा देना (sazaa denaa, 'to punish') and सज़ा मिलना (sazaa milnaa, 'to be punished/receive punishment') -- learners often reach for a passive construction where Hindi just uses मिलना (milnaa). 2) The person being punished takes को (ko): उसे सज़ा दी (use sazaa dii, 'punished him'), not से (se) or पर (par).
Sample Sentences:
अदालत ने उसे दस साल की सज़ा दी। The court gave him a ten-year sentence.
(adaalat ne use das saal kii sazaa dii।)
