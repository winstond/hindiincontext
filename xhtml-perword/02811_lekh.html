लेख (lekh)

noun Mainly a writing-related noun: an 'article' or 'essay' in a newspaper/magazine, extending to a more formal sense of a written document or piece of writing.
Sense 1 : article, essay (written piece)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: निबंध (nibandh, formal, esp. school essays), आलेख (aalekh, formal/journalistic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly on the front page of any Hindi newspaper like दैनिक भास्कर (dainik bhaaskar) -- हिंदी लेख (hindii lekh, 'a Hindi article') or संपादकीय लेख (sampaadkiiy lekh, 'editorial article') are everyday phrases for students and news readers alike.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse लेख (lekh) with लेखक (lekhak, 'writer/author') because of the shared root -- लेख (lekh) is the piece of writing itself, not the person who writes it.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने अखबार में एक दिलचस्प लेख पढ़ा। I read an interesting article in the newspaper.
(mainne akhbaar men ek dilchasp lekh padhaa।)

Sense 2 : written document, deed, record
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दस्तावेज़ (dastaavez), प्रलेख (pralekh, formal/legal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in more formal or legal contexts -- for example, दानलेख (daanlekh, 'deed of gift') or शिलालेख (shilaalekh, 'stone inscription') -- both built from the same root लेख (lekh) meaning something formally 'written down' or 'inscribed.'
Sample Sentences:
यह भूमि दान का लेख ताम्रपत्र पर खोदा गया था। This deed of land donation was engraved on a copper plate.
(yah bhoomi daan kaa lekh taamrpatr par khodaa gayaa thaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive
मंदिर की दीवार पर एक प्राचीन शिलालेख है। There is an ancient stone inscription on the temple wall.
(mandir kii diivaar par ek praachiin shilaalekh hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
वकील ने दानलेख की मूल प्रति सुरक्षित रखी है। The lawyer has kept the original copy of the deed of gift safe.
(vakiil ne daanlekh kii mool prati surakshit rakhii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
कृपया इस भूमि-लेख पर हस्ताक्षर कर दें। Please sign this land document.
(kripyaa is bhoomi-lekh par hastaakshar kar den।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
पुरातत्वविद उस शिलालेख को पढ़ने की कोशिश कर रहे थे। The archaeologists were trying to read that stone inscription.

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(yadi lekh nasht ho jaataa, to bhoomi kaa svaamitv siddh karnaa kathin hotaa।)
