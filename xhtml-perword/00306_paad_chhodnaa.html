पाद छोड़ना (paad chhodnaa)

verb A blunt, physical-comedy verb phrase meaning 'to fart' -- built from पाद (paad, 'fart', the noun) plus छोड़ना (chhodnaa, 'to release/let go'), literally 'to release a fart.' Casual/crude register; see CulturalNote for tone.
Sense 1 : to fart, to pass gas
verb, transitive The object पाद (paad) is built into the phrase itself, so छोड़ना (chhodnaa) here behaves like a light verb completing a fixed noun+verb pair rather than taking a separate free object.
Synonyms: पादना (paadnaa, one-word variant), गैस छोड़ना (gais chhodnaa, milder/euphemistic)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the kind of phrase that shows up in Hindi comedy sketches and college banter, not in textbooks -- it belongs to the same earthy, body-function humor that Hindi shares with most languages. Among friends it gets a laugh; used carelessly in front of family it can genuinely embarrass, so context and company matter a lot here.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as neutral/clinical, but it is a crude, joking phrase -- fine among close friends or in slapstick contexts, but not something you'd say in front of elders or in polite company. 2) The one-word verb पादना (paadnaa) is just as common and even blunter; पाद छोड़ना (paad chhodnaa) feels very slightly more 'described' or narrated, like in a joke or story, rather than a direct blurted admission.
Sample Sentences:
वो क्लास में पाद छोड़ बैठा था, सब हँसने लगे। He farted in class, and everyone started laughing.
(vo klaas men paad chhod baithaa thaa, sab hansne lage।)
