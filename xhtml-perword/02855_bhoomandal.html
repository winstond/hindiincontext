भूमंडल (bhoomandal)

noun A formal, literary compound noun meaning 'the globe/earth as a sphere' -- built from भू (bhoo, earth) + मंडल (mandal, sphere/circle/disc). Single sense; a bookish word rather than everyday vocabulary.
Sense 1 : the globe; the earth as a sphere
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पृथ्वी (prithvii), विश्व (vishv, broader scope: 'world' rather than the physical sphere), दुनिया (duniyaa, everyday word)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet भूमंडल (bhoomandal) mostly in compounds like भूमंडलीकरण (bhoomandliikran, 'globalization') in news and academic Hindi, rather than in a chai-time conversation. If you are trying to build spoken vocabulary, दुनिया (duniyaa) is the word you actually want; keep भूमंडल (bhoomandal) in your passive recognition pile for reading newspapers or textbooks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word to mean 'the world' in casual conversation, but it is a bookish, almost geography-textbook term -- everyday speech uses दुनिया (duniyaa) or दुनिया भर (duniyaa bhar, 'the whole world') instead. 2) It is not used the way English uses 'globe' for a physical desk object (a spinning model of the earth); that object is called ग्लोब (glob), a direct English loanword.
