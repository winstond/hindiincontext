बदला (badlaa)

noun/verb Two related but distinct senses: as a noun it means 'revenge/retaliation,' and as a verb form it is the past participle of बदलना (badalnaa, 'to change'). The noun sense is far more common as a standalone word in speech.
Sense 1 : revenge, retaliation
noun, masculine Almost always appears with लेना (lenaa) as a light verb: बदला लेना (badlaa lenaa, 'to take revenge'). Can also take चुकाना (chukaanaa) in the sense of 'to settle a score.'
Synonyms: प्रतिशोध (pratishodh, formal), बदला लेना (badlaa lenaa, set phrase, 'to take revenge')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: बदला (badlaa) carries a strong emotional and moral weight in Hindi -- it shows up constantly in Bollywood plots built around family honor and revenge ('बदले की कहानी' badle kii kahaanii, 'a story of revenge'). Note the oblique form बदले (badle) also becomes a common postposition-like phrase, बदले में (badle men, 'in exchange/return'), which is a related but separate use worth knowing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to translate 'to revenge someone' with बदला (badlaa) directly as a verb; it is a noun and needs लेना (lenaa) -- बदला लेना (badlaa lenaa, 'to take revenge'), never बदला करना (badlaa karnaa). 2) The object of revenge is marked with से (se): उससे बदला लेना (usse badlaa lenaa, 'to take revenge on him'), not को (ko), which trips up learners used to को-marking on people.

Sense 2 : changed (past participle of to change)
verb, intransitive This is the masculine singular perfective form of बदलना (badalnaa, 'to change'), used in past tense constructions: मौसम बदला (mausam badlaa, 'the weather changed'). Full conjugation and agreement patterns are covered under बदलना (badalnaa) in the morphology table.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This form is simply the past tense of the very common verb बदलना (badalnaa) agreeing with a masculine singular subject; it is not a separate word to learn but worth recognizing so you don't confuse it with the noun बदला (badlaa, 'revenge') when you hear it in a sentence.
