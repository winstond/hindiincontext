पसली (paslii)

noun A concrete anatomical noun meaning 'rib' -- the bone in the chest wall. It has a single, straightforward everyday sense.
Sense 1 : rib (bone of the ribcage)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will most often hear पसली (paslii) come up when someone describes a fall or an injury -- पसली टूट गई (paslii toot gaii, 'the rib got broken') is a very common way to report a rib fracture, using the intransitive टूटना (tootnaa) since the rib itself is the one undergoing the breaking, not an agent doing the breaking.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पसली (paslii) with छाती (chhaatii, 'chest') -- पसली (paslii) refers specifically to an individual rib bone, not the chest as a whole. 2) In counting or describing pain, Hindi speakers often say पसली में दर्द (paslii men dard, 'pain in the rib') using में (men) rather than any other postposition, so pair it with में (men) when talking about rib pain or injury.

गिरने से उसकी एक पसली टूट गई। He broke one of his ribs from the fall.
(girne se uskii ek paslii toot gaii।)
