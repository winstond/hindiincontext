आगरा (aagraa)

noun A proper noun: the city of Agra in Uttar Pradesh, most famous as the home of the Taj Mahal.
Sense 1 : the city of Agra
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Say आगरा (aagraa) to most people in India and the very next word in their head is ताज महल (taaj mahal, 'Taj Mahal') -- the city and the monument are almost inseparable in everyday conversation. It's also a common day-trip destination from Delhi, so you'll hear people casually say आगरा जा रहे हैं (aagraa jaa rahe hain, 'we're going to Agra') the way New Yorkers might say they're going upstate.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget place names like आगरा (aagraa) still take oblique case before postpositions, e.g. आगरा में (aagraa men, 'in Agra') -- no change to the noun itself since it ends in a consonant-like आ that doesn't inflect, but the postposition still attaches normally.
