उपन्यासकार (upanyaaskaar)

noun A compound noun meaning 'novelist' -- built from उपन्यास (upanyaas, 'novel') plus the agentive suffix -कार (kaar, 'maker/doer'). Single sense, straightforward and stable in meaning.
Sense 1 : novelist
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The -कार (kaar) suffix is a handy building block in Hindi -- you'll meet it again in words like पत्रकार (patrkaar, 'journalist') and कलाकार (kalaakaar, 'artist'). Once you know उपन्यास (upanyaas) for 'novel', this word is easy to reconstruct and remember rather than needing to be memorized separately.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to make this agree like an adjective or add extra gender marking -- it stays the same form for male and female novelists alike (though it grammatically behaves as masculine for agreement purposes); the sex of the person is understood from context, not from the word's form. 2) Don't confuse with कहानीकार (kahaaniikaar, 'story writer/short-story writer') -- उपन्यासकार (upanyaaskaar) is specifically for novel-length fiction.

(premchand ek mahaan upanyaaskaar the।)
