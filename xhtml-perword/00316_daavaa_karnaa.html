दावा करना (daavaa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the noun दावा (daavaa, 'claim') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means 'to claim' or 'to assert,' ranging from ordinary factual claims to formal legal claims.
Sense 1 : to claim, assert (a statement)
verb, transitive Takes the thing claimed either as a कि (ki, 'that') clause or as a noun object marked with का (kaa) before दावा: X का दावा करना (X kaa daavaa karnaa).
Synonyms: दावा जताना (daavaa jataanaa), कहना (kahnaa, weaker: just 'to say')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in news Hindi -- नेता ने दावा किया कि... (netaa ne daavaa kiyaa ki, 'the leader claimed that...') is a stock opening for reported claims in headlines and political speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say दावा बोलना (daavaa bolnaa) by analogy with 'to say a claim' -- the fixed collocation is always दावा करना (daavaa karnaa), never बोलना (bolnaa). 2) When the claim itself is a full sentence, it must be introduced with कि (ki); dropping कि (ki) and just running the clauses together sounds foreign.

(netaa ne daavaa kiyaa ki sarkaar ne apnaa vaadaa pooraa kiyaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to lay claim to, assert a right or entitlement to something
verb, transitive The thing being claimed takes पर (par) or का (kaa): ज़मीन पर दावा करना (zamiin par daavaa karnaa, 'to lay claim to the land').
Synonyms: हक़ जताना (haq jataanaa, 'to assert a right'), मालिकाना हक़ जताना (maalikaanaa haq jataanaa, 'to claim ownership')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up heavily in property and inheritance disputes -- a very common real-life context in Indian families is भाइयों ने ज़मीन पर दावा किया (bhaaiyon ne zamiin par daavaa kiyaa, 'the brothers laid claim to the land'), reflecting how often land disputes among siblings end up being discussed in exactly this phrase.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use को (ko) instead of पर (par) for the object being claimed -- स्वामित्व को दावा (svaamitv ko daavaa) is wrong; it should be स्वामित्व पर दावा (svaamitv par daavaa).

Sense 3 : to file a legal claim, sue
verb, transitive In this legal register, दावा (daavaa) itself often functions as the noun for 'lawsuit' or 'claim petition' (as in दावा दायर करना (daavaa daayar karnaa, 'to file a suit')), with करना (karnaa) as the lighter, more everyday verb choice alongside दायर करना (daayar karnaa).
Synonyms: मुकदमा करना (mukadmaa karnaa, 'to file a lawsuit'), केस करना (kes karnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In courtroom or insurance contexts you'll often hear दावा (daavaa) used almost like the English word 'claim' itself, e.g. an insurance दावा (daavaa) for a car accident -- worth knowing since this legal/financial sense is common in daily bureaucratic life in India.
