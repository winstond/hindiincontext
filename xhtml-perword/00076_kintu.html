किंतु (kintu)

conjunction A formal/literary word for 'but', used mainly in writing and careful speech; the everyday equivalent is लेकिन (lekin).
Sense 1 : but, however
conjunction
Synonyms: लेकिन (lekin), पर (par), मगर (magar), परंतु (parantu, formal/literary variant)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet किंतु (kintu) constantly in newspapers, textbooks, and formal speeches, but almost never at the dinner table -- there, पर (par) and लेकिन (lekin) do all the work. It is also often paired with इसके अलावा-type formal constructions and with यद्यपि (yadypi) to give writing a more classical, deliberate tone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use किंतु (kintu) in casual conversation where लेकिन (lekin) or पर (par) would sound much more natural -- किंतु (kintu) belongs to formal writing, essays, and speeches, not everyday chit-chat. 2) It is frequently paired with यद्यपि (yadypi, 'although') in formal sentences, similar to how 'although...yet' pairs in English; this correlative pairing is a written-register habit, not something you will hear in casual talk.
Sample Sentences:
उसने बहुत प्रयास किया, किंतु सफलता नहीं मिली। He made a great effort, but he did not achieve success.
(usne bahut prayaas kiyaa, kintu saphaltaa nahiin milii।)
