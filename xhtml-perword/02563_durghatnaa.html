दुर्घटना (durghatnaa)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'accident' or 'mishap', typically a road, rail, or industrial accident -- the standard word you'd hear on the news or from a police report.
Sense 1 : accident, mishap
noun, feminine
Synonyms: हादसा (haadsaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly on Hindi news tickers and in police FIRs -- सड़क दुर्घटना (sadak durghatnaa, 'road accident') is an especially common compound given how often it's reported. हादसा (haadsaa) is the word you're more likely to hear in everyday spoken conversation, while दुर्घटना (durghatnaa) has a slightly more formal, official-report feel, though both are widely understood and used.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for इत्तफ़ाक़ (ittfaaq) thinking 'accident' -- that word actually means 'coincidence,' not a mishap; दुर्घटना (durghatnaa) is the word for a crash or disaster. 2) The common verb pairing is दुर्घटना होना (durghatnaa honaa, 'an accident happens/occurs') or दुर्घटना घटना (durghatnaa ghatnaa, 'an accident occurs') -- not दुर्घटना करना (durghatnaa karnaa), since you don't 'do' an accident, it 'happens' to you.

कल शाम एक सड़क दुर्घटना में तीन लोग घायल हो गए। Yesterday evening, three people were injured in a road accident.
(kal shaam ek sadak durghatnaa men tiin log ghaayal ho gae।)
