बनाना (banaanaa)

verb The main transitive verb 'to make/build/prepare/form,' with extended senses covering cooking, constructing relationships or things, and figurative uses like 'to fool someone.' Related to the intransitive बनना (bannaa, 'to be made/become').
Sense 1 : to make, create, build
verb, transitive
Synonyms: तैयार करना (taiyaar karnaa, 'to prepare')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the most useful verbs in the language -- you will use it for everything from making chai to building a career. Note the neat pair बनना (bannaa) / बनाना (banaanaa) mirrors the English intransitive/transitive pair 'to become' / 'to make.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use बनना (bannaa) when they mean the transitive act of making something -- remember बनाना (banaanaa) needs an agent doing the making, while बनना (bannaa) describes the thing coming into being on its own. 2) In the perfective, this verb takes ने (ne) marking on the subject since it is transitive: उसने घर बनाया (usne ghar banaayaa, 'he built a house').

Sense 2 : to cook, prepare food
verb, transitive
Synonyms: पकाना (pakaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi mother क्या बना है? (kyaa banaa hai, 'what's been made?') at lunchtime and she means what's cooking. Hindi treats a finished dish the same way it treats a finished house -- both are 'made,' बनाया (banaayaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) For food, बनाना (banaanaa) is far more common in everyday speech than पकाना (pakaanaa), which can sound slightly more formal or technical (e.g. describing the cooking process itself rather than 'making' a dish).

Sense 3 : to form or establish a relationship, plan, or opinion
verb, transitive Common collocations: दोस्ती बनाना (dostii banaanaa, 'to make friendship'), योजना बनाना (yojnaa banaanaa, 'to make a plan'), राय बनाना (raay banaanaa, 'to form an opinion').
Synonyms: स्थापित करना (sthaapit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This extended, abstract use of बनाना (banaanaa) shows how flexible the verb is -- Hindi speakers 'make' friendships, plans, and opinions just as naturally as they make food or furniture.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने कॉलेज में कई अच्छे दोस्त बनाए। I made several good friends in college.

Sense 4 : to fool, deceive, or tease someone
verb, transitive Usually appears as the compound बेवकूफ़ बनाना (bevkoof banaanaa) or with an object plus बनाना (banaanaa) alone in context, e.g. तुम मुझे बना रहे हो? (tum mujhe banaa rahe ho, 'are you fooling me?').
Synonyms: बेवकूफ़ बनाना (bevkoof banaanaa, 'to make a fool of'), मूर्ख बनाना (moorkh banaanaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a favorite in banter among friends -- मुझे मत बनाओ! (mujhe mat banaao, 'don't fool me!') is something you will hear constantly in casual Delhi conversations when someone suspects they're being teased or misled.
Common Mistakes: 1) Used alone with a person as object, बनाना (banaanaa) can mean 'to fool/tease' rather than 'to make' -- context (a person, not an object, being 'made') signals this figurative sense.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe mat banaao!)
