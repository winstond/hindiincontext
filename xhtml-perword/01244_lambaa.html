लंबा (lambaa)

adjective Mainly a physical-dimension adjective covering both 'tall' and 'long', extending into time-duration ('lengthy') and a few fixed idiomatic phrases.
Sense 1 : tall (of people, standing objects)
adjective
Synonyms: ऊँचा (oonchaa, used for height of things/buildings more than people)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi aunty sizing up a prospective match and लंबा (lambaa) is one of the first words out of her mouth -- height is a big deal in matrimonial talk, and 'लंबा-चौड़ा (lambaa-chaudaa)' (tall and broad) is practically a stock phrase for describing a well-built man.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ऊँचा (oonchaa) to describe a person's height, but लंबा (lambaa) is the natural choice for people; ऊँचा (oonchaa) fits buildings, walls, mountains. 2) Remember लंबा (lambaa) agrees with the noun it describes -- वह लंबा है (vah lambaa hai, 'he is tall') vs वह लंबी है (vah lambii hai, 'she is tall').
Sample Sentences:

(vah bahut lambaa hai।)

Sense 2 : long (of objects, distance, roads, hair, etc.)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Same word, same root idea as the 'tall' sense -- Hindi doesn't split 'tall' and 'long' the way English does, so लंबा (lambaa) covers a tall man, a long road, and long hair alike. Think of it as measuring extent along the dominant axis, whatever that happens to be.

Sense 3 : long, lengthy (of time, duration, processes)
adjective
Synonyms: दीर्घ (diirgh, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear लंबा इंतज़ार (lambaa intzaar, 'a long wait') and लंबा सफ़र (lambaa safar, 'a long journey') constantly -- in a country of long train journeys and long queues, this sense of लंबा (lambaa) gets a lot of daily use.
Common Mistakes: 1) लंबा समय (lambaa samay, 'a long time') is the natural collocation -- learners sometimes overuse बहुत देर (bahut der) instead, which is fine but has a slightly different feel of 'a long while' in the moment rather than an extended period.

आज बहुत लंबा दिन था। Today was a very long day.
(aaj bahut lambaa din thaa।)

Sense 4 : extended, prolonged, exaggerated (used idiomatically, e.g. लंबी-चौड़ी बातें, लंबा चौड़ा दावा)
adjective Common in the fixed phrase लंबी-चौड़ी बातें करना (lambii-chaudii baaten karnaa, 'to talk big / make tall claims').
Synonyms: बड़ी-बड़ी (badii-badii, used similarly for boastful talk)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the Hindi equivalent of English 'tall talk' or 'tall claims' -- a nice coincidental overlap in the 'tall = exaggerated' metaphor across both languages, though in Hindi it's almost always paired with चौड़ा (chaudaa, 'wide') for emphasis.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense only really surfaces in set phrases like लंबी-चौड़ी बातें (lambii-chaudii baaten) or लंबा-चौड़ा दावा (lambaa-chaudaa daavaa) -- learners shouldn't extend लंबा (lambaa) freely to mean 'exaggerated' outside these collocations.
Sample Sentences:
वह हमेशा लंबी-चौड़ी बातें करता है। He always talks big talk.
(vah hameshaa lambii-chaudii baaten kartaa hai।)
