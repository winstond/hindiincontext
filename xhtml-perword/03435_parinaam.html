परिणाम (parinaam)

noun A common formal-register noun meaning 'result, outcome, consequence' -- often heard around exam results and cause-effect discussions. One core sense, with a well-known idiomatic use for 'as a result of.'
Sense 1 : result, outcome
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नतीजा (natiijaa), फल (phal, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word every Indian student dreads and celebrates each year: परिणाम (parinaam) is the official term for exam results, so board and university result day is often called परिणाम दिवस (parinaam divas, 'results day') in school announcements. In everyday spoken Hindi, नतीजा (natiijaa) is the more casual go-to, while परिणाम feels a notch more formal or written -- the kind of word you see on a notice board rather than hear shouted across a room.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अंत (ant, 'end') when they mean 'result' -- अंत is about finality/conclusion, not the outcome or consequence of an action. 2) In the fixed phrase के परिणामस्वरूप (ke parinaamasvroop, 'as a result of'), परिणाम gets glued to स्वरूप (svaroop) -- learners sometimes try to build this compound themselves with other words, but it's a fixed formal connector, not a productive pattern.
