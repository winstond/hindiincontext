खुजली (khujlii)

noun Primarily a noun meaning 'itch/itching' or 'scabies/skin rash', with a common figurative extension for someone who can't sit still or can't resist meddling/talking.
Sense 1 : itch, itching sensation
noun, feminine
Synonyms: खुजलाहट (khujlaahat)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday word you'd use at the chemist's counter or with a doctor -- मुझे खुजली हो रही है (mujhe khujlii ho rahii hai, 'I have an itch') is the natural way to describe it, framing the itch as something that happens to you rather than something you do.
Common Mistakes: 1) The verb paired with this noun for 'to itch' is होना (honaa) or आना (aanaa) with a को (ko)-marked person, e.g. मुझे खुजली हो रही है (mujhe khujlii ho rahii hai, 'I'm itching') -- learners sometimes try to make खुजली (khujlii) itself the verb, but it stays a noun; the action of scratching is a separate verb, खुजाना (khujaanaa).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे खुजली हो रही है। I have an itch.
(mujhe khujlii ho rahii hai।)

Sense 2 : scabies, skin rash/infection causing itching
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday speech खुजली (khujlii) covers both a passing itch and an actual skin condition like scabies -- context (and whether it's spreading or persistent) tells you which one someone means, much as 'a rash' does in English.

उसे हाथों पर खुजली हो गई है, डॉक्टर को दिखाना पड़ेगा। He's developed a rash on his hands, we'll have to show it to a doctor.
(use haathon par khujlii ho gaii hai, doktar ko dikhaanaa padegaa।)

Sense 3 : restlessness or compulsive urge to meddle, provoke, or act out (figurative)
noun, feminine Common in the fixed phrase खुजली होना (khujlii honaa) or खुजली उठना (khujlii uthnaa) applied to a person's behavior rather than their skin, e.g. उसे बोलने की खुजली है (use bolne kii khujlii hai, 'he can't resist running his mouth').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Delhi aunties and friends alike use this scoldingly -- कुछ लोगों को झगड़ा करने की खुजली होती है (kuchh logon ko jhagdaa karne kii khujlii hotii hai, 'some people just have an itch for picking fights') -- it's affectionately (or not so affectionately) accusing someone of not being able to leave well enough alone, much like English 'itching to...' but usually with a more negative, meddlesome edge.
Sample Sentences:

(kuchh logon ko jhagdaa karne kii khujlii hotii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
