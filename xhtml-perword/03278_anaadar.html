अनादर (anaadar)

noun A formal noun meaning 'disrespect' or 'disregard' -- the negation (अ-) of आदर (aadar, 'respect'). One sense only.
Sense 1 : disrespect, disregard
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अपमान (apmaan, insult, stronger), बेइज़्ज़ती (beizztii, colloquial), निरादर (niraadar, one-word variant, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word belongs to a more formal, almost written register -- in everyday speech about a rude relative or a disrespected elder, most Delhi households would reach for बेइज़्ज़ती (beizztii) or अपमान (apmaan) instead. You will meet अनादर (anaadar) more often in moral or civic contexts -- a lecture on respecting elders, a newspaper editorial -- than in a kitchen argument.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अपमान (apmaan) when they mean अनादर (anaadar) -- अपमान leans toward a sharper, deliberate insult or humiliation, while अनादर is the quieter, more formal idea of failing to show due respect or regard. 2) As a formal noun, अनादर (anaadar) pairs with करना (karnaa) ('to disrespect someone') or होना (honaa) ('disrespect occurs'), not with a direct verb of its own.

गुरुजनों का अनादर नहीं करना चाहिए। One should not disrespect one's teachers and elders.
(gurujnon kaa anaadar nahiin karnaa chaahie।)
