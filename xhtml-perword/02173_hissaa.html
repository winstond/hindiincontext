हिस्सा (hissaa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning 'part, share, portion' -- covers both a physical/abstract portion of something and a person's share or role in an event.
Sense 1 : part, portion, section
noun, masculine
Synonyms: भाग (bhaag), अंश (ansh, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of siblings dividing up a plate of mithai (mithaaii) or a family splitting ancestral property -- हिस्सा (hissaa) is the word that comes up in both the kitchen and the courtroom. It pairs naturally with the verb बँटना (bantnaa, 'to be divided') and with बाँटना (baantnaa, 'to divide').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for भाग (bhaag) by default since it's the 'dictionary' word, but हिस्सा (hissaa) is what people actually say in daily speech about dividing food, property, or a group into portions.
Sample Sentences:
यह किताब कई हिस्सों में बँटी है। This book is divided into several parts.
(yah kitaab kaii hisson men bantii hai।)

Sense 2 : share, one's role or involvement in something
noun, masculine Common in the fixed phrase हिस्सा लेना (hissaa lenaa, 'to take part') and हिस्सा बनना (hissaa bannaa, 'to become a part of').
Synonyms: भाग (bhaag)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you hear when someone says a wedding celebration or a protest was कोई इसका हिस्सा बना (koii iskaa hissaa banaa, 'someone became a part of it') -- हिस्सा here is about belonging and involvement, not just a physical slice of something.
Common Mistakes: 1) हिस्सा लेना (hissaa lenaa, 'to participate') is the natural way to say 'take part' in an event -- learners sometimes translate 'participate' with a borrowed verb instead of reaching for this very common collocation.
Sample Sentences:
मैं भी इस मुहिम का हिस्सा बनना चाहता हूँ। I want to become a part of this campaign too.
(main bhii is muhim kaa hissaa bannaa chaahtaa hoon।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
