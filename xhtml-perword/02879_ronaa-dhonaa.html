रोना-धोना (ronaa-dhonaa)

noun A reduplicated compound noun built from the verb रोना (ronaa, 'to cry') plus its rhyming echo धोना (dhonaa); it refers to the whole business of crying and wailing, often with a mildly dismissive or weary undertone.
Sense 1 : crying and wailing (as a general activity or fuss)
noun, masculine Functions as a masculine noun, commonly paired with करना (karnaa) as रोना-धोना करना (ronaa-dhonaa karnaa, 'to cry and carry on') or seen ending in a fuss/scene.
Synonyms: रुदन (rudan, literary), विलाप (vilaap, literary), आँसू बहाना (aansoo bahaanaa, phrase: 'to shed tears')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a classic Hindi echo-word (जोड़ी शब्द jodii shabd pattern) where a rhyming syllable is tacked onto a real word purely for rhythm, much like 'hustle-bustle' in English -- धोना (dhonaa) itself contributes no separate meaning of washing here. You will often hear a mother or grandmother say उसका रोना-धोना बंद नहीं होता (uskaa ronaa-dhonaa band nahiin hotaa, 'his/her crying just won't stop'), with a tone that mixes sympathy and mild annoyance.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this as just a poetic doubling of रोना (ronaa) with no added meaning, but in real speech it carries a slightly impatient or exasperated tone -- someone describing another's रोना-धोना (ronaa-dhonaa) is often subtly criticizing the fuss, not just reporting tears. 2) It is used only as a noun in this fixed form; you cannot conjugate धोना (dhonaa) separately here as if it meant 'washing' -- that literal meaning is absent, it exists only to rhyme with रोना (ronaa).
Sample Sentences:
उसका रोना-धोना बंद नहीं होता। His crying just won't stop.
(uskaa ronaa-dhonaa band nahiin hotaa।)
