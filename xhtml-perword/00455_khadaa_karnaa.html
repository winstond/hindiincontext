खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa)

verb The causative pairing of खड़ा होना (khadaa honaa, 'to stand up') -- it means 'to make stand/erect' literally, and extends to 'to set up, establish, create' figuratively. The senses below split literal placement from figurative creation.
Sense 1 : to make someone/something stand; to park (a vehicle)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: रखना (rakhnaa, for parking a vehicle)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask a Delhi auto-wallah or a security guard where to park and you'll hear गाड़ी यहाँ खड़ी कर दो (gaadii yahaan khadii kar do, 'park the car here') constantly -- खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa) is the default verb for parking, not पार्क करना (paark karnaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खड़ा होना (khadaa honaa) when they actually need the causative -- if *you* are making someone or something else stand (a child, a ladder, a car), you need खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa), not the intransitive form. 2) For parking, गाड़ी खड़ी करना (gaadii khadii karnaa, 'to park the car') is the everyday phrase; गाड़ी पार्क करना (gaadii paark karnaa) also works but sounds slightly more borrowed/English.

Sense 2 : to set up, establish, create (a business, structure, situation)
verb, transitive Frequently takes abstract or institutional objects: कंपनी खड़ी करना (kampnii khadii karnaa, 'to set up a company'), मुसीबत खड़ी करना (musiibat khadii karnaa, 'to create trouble').
Synonyms: स्थापित करना (sthaapit karnaa, formal), बनाना (banaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb you'll hear in business talk -- अपना बिज़नेस खड़ा करना (apnaa biznes khadaa karnaa, 'to build up one's own business') -- carrying the image of erecting a structure from nothing, much like the literal sense of raising something to stand.
Common Mistakes: 1) With abstract objects like मुसीबत (musiibat, 'trouble') or सवाल (savaal, 'question'), learners often default to बनाना (banaanaa) -- but खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa) is idiomatic here and carries a sense of something suddenly rising up in front of you, which बनाना (banaanaa) doesn't quite capture.
