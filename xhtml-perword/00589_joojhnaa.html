जूझना (joojhnaa)

verb One core meaning: to struggle or grapple with something difficult -- a problem, a hardship, an opponent. It is almost always used figuratively about difficulties rather than literal physical combat.
Sense 1 : to struggle, grapple, contend (with a difficulty, problem, or hardship)
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the thing being struggled with: समस्या से जूझना (samasyaa se joojhnaa, 'to struggle with a problem').
Synonyms: संघर्ष करना (sangharsh karnaa), लड़ना (ladnaa, can also mean literal fighting), मुक़ाबला करना (muqaablaa karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in news Hindi -- किसान क़र्ज़ से जूझ रहे हैं (kisaan qarz se joojh rahe hain, 'farmers are struggling with debt') is the kind of headline register this word lives in. It carries a sense of prolonged effort against odds, so it suits describing someone battling illness, debt, or a tough exam season better than a one-off scuffle.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for लड़ना (ladnaa) here, but जूझना (joojhnaa) specifically evokes an ongoing, often losing or exhausting struggle against circumstances (illness, poverty, a crisis) rather than a discrete fight against a person. 2) The struggled-with thing takes से (se), not को (ko) or से-less direct object -- बीमारी से जूझना (biimaarii se joojhnaa, 'to struggle with an illness'), not बीमारी को जूझना.

किसान क़र्ज़ से जूझ रहे हैं। Farmers are struggling with debt.
(kisaan qarz se joojh rahe hain।)
