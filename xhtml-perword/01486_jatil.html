जटिल (jatil)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'complex, complicated, intricate' -- used for problems, situations, systems, or issues rather than physical objects.
Sense 1 : complex, complicated
adjective
Synonyms: पेचीदा (pechiidaa), उलझा हुआ (uljhaa huaa, 'tangled up'), मुश्किल (mushkil, covers 'difficult' more broadly than 'complex')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear जटिल (jatil) constantly in news Hindi and office speech -- जटिल मुद्दा (jatil muddaa, 'a complex issue'), जटिल प्रक्रिया (jatil prakriyaa, 'a complicated process') -- it has a slightly formal, analytical flavor compared to the everyday उलझा हुआ (uljhaa huaa), which paints a more physical picture of things being knotted together.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मुश्किल (mushkil) or कठिन (kathin) when जटिल (jatil) is meant -- मुश्किल/कठिन describe something hard to do, while जटिल describes something intricate or tangled in structure (a jटिल समस्या (jatil samasyaa, 'a complex problem') has many interlocking parts, it isn't just hard). 2) जटिल is not used for physical difficulty (a heavy box, a steep climb) -- reserve it for problems, situations, systems, relationships, and processes.

(yah muddaa bahut jatil hai।)
