तकनीकी (takniikii)

adjective/noun Means 'technical' -- used as an adjective describing something related to technique/technology, and less commonly as a feminine noun meaning 'technicality' or 'technical matter'.
Sense 1 : technical (relating to technique, machinery, or specialized skill)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in news and official contexts: तकनीकी खराबी (takniikii kharaabii, 'technical fault') is the standard phrase Indian announcers use when a flight or train is delayed, and तकनीकी शिक्षा (takniikii shikshaa, 'technical education') is the common term for engineering/vocational training.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect this like a regular -ई (ी)-ending adjective, but it stays the same before masculine and feminine nouns alike: तकनीकी समस्या (takniikii samasyaa, 'technical problem') and तकनीकी क्षेत्र (takniikii kshetr, 'technical field') both use the same form.
Sample Sentences:
उड़ान तकनीकी खराबी के कारण रद्द कर दी गई। The flight was cancelled due to a technical fault.
(udaan takniikii kharaabii ke kaaran radd kar dii gaii।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive

Sense 2 : technicality, technical detail
noun, feminine Used mainly in formal, legal, or bureaucratic speech to refer to a fine technical point, often in the phrase तकनीकी में जाना (takniikii men jaanaa, 'to get into technicalities').
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This noun use is far less common than the adjective and shows up mostly in formal or written registers -- a lawyer or official might say इस तकनीकी को छोड़ो (is takniikii ko chhodo, 'leave this technicality aside') to wave away a minor procedural point.
Sample Sentences:
इस तकनीकी को छोड़ो, असली मुद्दे पर आते हैं। Leave this technicality aside, let's get to the real issue.
(is takniikii ko chhodo, aslii mudde par aate hain।)

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
