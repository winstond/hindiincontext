शिथिल (shithil)

adjective/noun A formal/literary adjective meaning 'loose, slack' both physically and figuratively (of grip, discipline, rules, or a body/limb losing tension); a related abstract-noun sense means 'laxity, slackening.'
Sense 1 : loose, slack, lax (physical or figurative)
adjective
Synonyms: ढीला (dhiilaa), लचर (lachar, of arguments/rules), कमज़ोर (kamzor, weak)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word most often in a slightly bookish register -- a news anchor saying अनुशासन शिथिल हो गया है (anushaasan shithil ho gayaa hai, 'discipline has become lax') rather than a friend complaining about a loose shoelace. It also shows up describing a body going limp with fatigue or old age, e.g. शिथिल शरीर (shithil shariir, 'a frail/slack body'), which carries a gentler, almost literary sympathy compared to the blunter कमज़ोर (kamzor).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ढीला (dhiilaa) in casual speech instead; शिथिल (shithil) is the word you'll meet in written or formal contexts -- news reports, official language, or descriptions of discipline/rules becoming lax, rather than in everyday chatter about a loose shirt button. 2) It is commonly paired with पड़ना (padnaa) or होना (honaa) rather than used as a plain adjective before every noun -- शिथिल पड़ गया (shithil pad gayaa, 'went slack/limp') is a very natural collocation to learn as a chunk.

Sense 2 : laxity, slackening, loosening (abstract state)
noun, feminine In practice, the abstract noun is almost always the derived form शिथिलता (shithiltaa) rather than शिथिल (shithil) used bare as a noun; this sense appears mainly in compound/formal phrasing.
Synonyms: ढिलाई (dhilaaii), शिथिलता (shithiltaa, the more common abstract-noun form)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: If you're reading government or academic Hindi, you'll far more often see शिथिलता (shithiltaa) -- as in नियमों में शिथिलता (niymon men shithiltaa, 'a relaxation in the rules') -- than the bare adjective used nominally; think of शिथिल (shithil) as the root that this more common abstract noun is built from.
