भाग्य (bhaagy)

noun A single core idea, fate/fortune, expressed as a noun; the everyday word for 'luck' or 'destiny' in both casual talk and reflective/philosophical speech.
Sense 1 : fate, destiny, fortune (as an abstract force controlling one's life)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: किस्मत (kismat), नसीब (nasiib), तक़दीर (taqdiir)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: भाग्य (bhaagy) carries a slightly more classical, almost philosophical weight than किस्मत (kismat) -- you'll hear it in phrases about life's larger design, in astrology talk, or when someone shrugs and says यह तो भाग्य की बात है (yah to bhaagy kii baat hai, 'this is a matter of fate'). It shares its root with भगवान (bhagvaan) and भाग (bhaag, 'share/portion'), reflecting the idea that fate is the 'portion' allotted to you.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for किस्मत (kismat) since it's more common in spoken Hindi; भाग्य (bhaagy) is just as usable but leans slightly more reflective/written, so don't hesitate to use it in speech too. 2) भाग्य (bhaagy) is grammatically masculine and singular even when talking about someone's whole life's fortune -- it doesn't pluralize the way English 'fortunes' might.

यह तो भाग्य की बात है। This is simply a matter of fate.
(yah to bhaagy kii baat hai।)

Sense 2 : good luck, good fortune (a stroke of luck, often in exclamatory use)
noun, masculine Often appears in set phrases praising someone's luck, e.g. मेरा भाग्य अच्छा है (meraa bhaagy achchhaa hai, 'my luck is good'), rather than as a standalone word for 'good luck' the way सौभाग्य (saubhaagy) can be used alone.
Synonyms: सौभाग्य (saubhaagy), खुशकिस्मती (khushkismtii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense overlaps closely with the main 'fate' sense above -- it's really the same word narrowed by context (usually with अच्छा (achchhaa) or बुरा (buraa)) rather than a separate word, so think of it as fate's 'positive' shading rather than a distinct vocabulary item.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say भाग्य (bhaagy) alone expecting it to mean 'good luck' the way सौभाग्य (saubhaagy) does; भाग्य (bhaagy) is neutral and needs अच्छा (achchhaa, 'good') or similar to specify it's good fortune, since on its own it just means 'fate' (which could be good or bad).
Sample Sentences:
यह मेरा भाग्य है कि मुझे ऐसे अच्छे दोस्त मिले। It's my good fortune that I got such good friends.
(yah meraa bhaagy hai ki mujhe aise achchhe dost mile।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
