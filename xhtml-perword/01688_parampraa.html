परंपरा (parampraa)

noun A single core meaning: 'tradition, custom, convention' -- something handed down over generations. Also spelled with the anusvara written as a full nasal, परम्परा (parampraa).
Sense 1 : tradition, custom, established practice
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रिवाज (rivaaj), रीति (riiti, formal), प्रथा (prathaa, formal), रस्म (rasm, often ritual/ceremony-specific)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around festivals and weddings -- लोग कहते हैं यह हमारी परंपरा है (yah hamaarii parampraa hai, 'this is our tradition') to justify everything from a specific ritual to a family recipe. It carries a gentle authority: invoking परंपरा (parampraa) in a conversation often ends debate, the way 'it's tradition' does in English, but with a bit more weight in a culture that prizes continuity with elders and ancestors.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for रिवाज (rivaaj) or प्रथा (prathaa) interchangeably with परंपरा (parampraa); परंपरा (parampraa) leans toward a longstanding, inherited practice passed across generations, while रिवाज (rivaaj) can be a more local or recent custom. 2) It is feminine, so adjectives and past-tense verb agreement must match: पुरानी परंपरा (puraanii parampraa, 'old tradition'), not पुराना।
