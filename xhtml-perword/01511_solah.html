सोलह (solah)

numeral The number sixteen (16), used both for counting and as an ordinary numeral adjective before nouns.
Sense 1 : the number sixteen
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सोलह (solah) shows up in the well-known phrase सोलहवाँ साल (solahvaan saal, 'the sixteenth year') to mean the cusp of youth/adulthood, much like 'sweet sixteen' in English -- Hindi film songs love this image of a girl turning सोलह (solah) as a metaphor for blooming youth.
Common Mistakes: 1) सोलह (solah) is invariant regardless of the gender or number of the noun it counts, unlike एक (ek) which changes -- learners sometimes hunt for a feminine form that does not exist. 2) Don't confuse with सोला (solaa, a type of pith/cork material) or साल (saal, year) -- similar sounds but unrelated meanings.
