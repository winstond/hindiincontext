उदाहरण (udaahran)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'example' or 'illustration' -- the word you reach for whenever you want to say 'for example' or ask someone to explain with an instance.
Sense 1 : example, instance, illustration
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मिसाल (misaal), नमूना (namoonaa, sample/specimen sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear उदाहरण के लिए (udaahran ke lie, 'for example') constantly in classrooms, news broadcasts, and even casual explanations -- it is the go-to phrase teachers use when illustrating a grammar point or a math problem, much like 'for instance' in English. मिसाल (misaal) is a very close everyday synonym, often preferred in spoken Hindi and in idiomatic phrases like मिसाल के तौर पर (misaal ke taur par, 'by way of example'), while उदाहरण (udaahran) leans slightly more textbook/formal in feel.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say केलिए (ke lie) with उदाहरण (udaahran) when they mean 'for example,' but the fixed phrase is उदाहरण के लिए (udaahran ke lie, 'for example') or उदाहरण के तौर पर (udaahran ke taur par, 'as an example') -- dropping के तौर पर/के लिए and just saying उदाहरण alone at the start of a sentence sounds incomplete to native ears. 2) उदाहरण (udaahran) is a Sanskrit-origin masculine noun and does not change form in the plural without context cues (े endings), unlike many native masculine nouns -- learners sometimes wrongly add ों expecting regular oblique plural behavior in all contexts.

उदाहरण के लिए, यह शब्द दो तरह से इस्तेमाल होता है। For example, this word is used in two ways.
(udaahran ke lie, yah shabd do tarah se istemaal hotaa hai।)
