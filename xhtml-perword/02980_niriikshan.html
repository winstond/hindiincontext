निरीक्षण (niriikshan)

noun A formal noun meaning 'inspection' or 'supervision' -- one core sense, used mostly in official, institutional, or written contexts rather than casual chat.
Sense 1 : inspection, examination (formal/official checking)
noun, masculine Commonly appears with करना (karnaa) as निरीक्षण करना (niriikshan karnaa, 'to inspect') or with होना (honaa) for the passive-feeling 'an inspection takes place.'
Synonyms: जांच (jaanch), मुआयना (muaaynaa), पड़ताल (padtaal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on government notices and in news reports -- स्कूलों का निरीक्षण (skoolon kaa niriikshan, 'inspection of schools') or फैक्ट्री का निरीक्षण (phaiktrii kaa niriikshan, 'factory inspection') -- it has a bureaucratic, official flavor rather than a casual one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for जांच (jaanch) in everyday speech (like checking a bill or a fact) but निरीक्षण (niriikshan) is reserved for more formal, official, or systematic inspections -- a factory, a school, a construction site -- not a quick personal check. 2) It is a noun, not a verb; you must pair it with करना (karnaa) to actually 'inspect' something -- there is no verb *निरीक्षणना.

अधिकारियों ने स्कूल का निरीक्षण किया। The officials inspected the school.
(adhikaariyon ne skool kaa niriikshan kiyaa।)

Sense 2 : supervision, oversight
noun, masculine
Synonyms: देखरेख (dekhrekh), निगरानी (nigraanii)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In offices and schools you might hear a senior person say यह काम मेरे निरीक्षण में हुआ (yah kaam mere niriikshan men huaa, 'this work was done under my supervision') -- it carries a sense of formal responsibility rather than casual watching over someone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Confused with निगरानी (nigraanii), which leans toward ongoing watchfulness (like guarding or monitoring), while निरीक्षण (niriikshan) implies a formal, often one-time or periodic review or audit.
