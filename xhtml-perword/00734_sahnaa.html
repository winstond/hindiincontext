सहना (sahnaa)

verb A single core meaning: to endure, bear, or tolerate pain, hardship, or unpleasant treatment.
Sense 1 : to endure/bear (pain, hardship, insult)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बर्दाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa), झेलना (jhelnaa), सहन करना (sahan karnaa, more formal/written)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सहना (sahnaa) carries a quiet, almost stoic weight in Hindi -- it's the verb behind the cultural ideal of सहनशीलता (sahanshiiltaa, 'tolerance/forbearance'), often praised as a virtue, especially in how mothers, wives, or elders are depicted as silently enduring hardship. You'll hear it in phrases like दर्द सहना (dard sahnaa, 'to bear pain') or ज़ुल्म सहना (zulm sahnaa, 'to endure cruelty/oppression'), the latter carrying real emotional and even political charge in conversation and cinema.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बर्दाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa) by default; सहना (sahnaa) is the plainer, more native verb and fits everyday speech about pain, hardship, or ill-treatment just as well, often better. 2) सहना (sahnaa) is transitive but frequently appears without an explicit object when the endured thing is understood from context, e.g. वह चुपचाप सहती रही (vah chupchaap sahtii rahii, 'she kept quietly enduring [it]') -- learners sometimes force in a dummy object where none is needed.
