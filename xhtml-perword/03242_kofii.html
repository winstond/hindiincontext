कॉफ़ी (kofii)

noun A loanword from English 'coffee', used exactly as in English for the drink and the bean/powder it's made from.
Sense 1 : coffee (the beverage)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a Delhi household or office, कॉफ़ी (kofii) usually means instant coffee -- Nescafe is practically a generic term the way 'coffee' itself is. Chai still dominates daily life, so offering coffee ('कॉफ़ी पिएँगे?' (kofii pienge?, 'will you have coffee?')) can feel like a slightly more modern or cafe-culture gesture compared to the everyday chai offer.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write it without the nuqta as कॉफी (kophii) -- both spellings are seen, but कॉफ़ी (kofii) with the nuqta under the फ is the more careful, standard rendering of the English 'f' sound. 2) The verb used with कॉफ़ी (kofii) is usually पीना (piinaa, 'to drink'), not खाना (khaanaa, 'to eat') -- learners occasionally reach for the wrong verb by analogy with food words.
