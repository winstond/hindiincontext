बासठ (baasath)

numeral The cardinal number 62 (English 'sixty-two').
Sense 1 : the number sixty-two
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Numbers in this range show up constantly in everyday contexts like ages, prices, phone numbers, and cricket scores, so native speakers rattle them off without pausing to build them piece by piece -- it is worth drilling बासठ (baasath) as its own memorized chunk rather than translating digit by digit.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from साठ (saath, sixty) plus दो (do, two), but Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 are mostly memorized as single fused words rather than constructed on the fly -- बासठ (baasath) does not visibly contain साठ (saath), unlike English 'sixty-two.' 2) It behaves like an adjective/quantifier before a noun (बासठ किताबें baasath kitaaben, 'sixty-two books') and does not itself inflect for gender or case.
Sample Sentences:

(mere daadaajii kii umr baasath saal hai।)
