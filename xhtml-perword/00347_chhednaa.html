छेड़ना (chhednaa)

verb A transitive verb with a warm, everyday sense of teasing or bantering with someone, plus related senses of provoking/starting a fight, bringing up a topic, and touching/plucking an instrument.
Sense 1 : to tease, to playfully bother someone
verb, transitive
Synonyms: तंग करना (tang karnaa), चुहलबाज़ी करना (chuhalbaazii karnaa, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word for the gentle, affectionate needling that runs through Indian family and friend life -- cousins teasing a newly married couple, an older sister teasing a younger one about a crush. It carries none of the harshness English 'tease' can sometimes have; it is usually a sign of closeness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तंग करना (tang karnaa) when they mean playful, affectionate teasing -- छेड़ना (chhednaa) is the natural choice for lighthearted banter between friends, siblings, or a couple, while तंग करना (tang karnaa) leans more toward genuinely annoying someone. 2) The person being teased is marked with को (ko), e.g. उसे छेड़ना (use chhednaa), not से (se).

Sense 2 : to harass or make unwanted advances (especially street harassment of women)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: छींटाकशी करना (chhiintaakshii karnaa), परेशान करना (pareshaan karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word behind the now-dated but still-heard term 'eve-teasing,' used in Indian English and news media for the harassment of women in public spaces. It is a distinct, heavier register from the playful family sense above, so listen for context -- a mother warning her daughter about boys on the street versus a cousin ribbing her about a crush.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is serious and negative -- unlike the playful-teasing sense, लड़की को छेड़ना (ladkii ko chhednaa) refers to street harassment, not friendly banter; the context and tone (news reports, complaints, warnings) make the difference clear, not the grammar.

Sense 4 : to bring up or start (a topic, subject, or issue)
verb, transitive Takes an abstract noun like बात (baat) or मुद्दा (muddaa) as its object, e.g. बात छेड़ना (baat chhednaa).
Synonyms: उठाना (uthaanaa), शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बात छेड़ना (baat chhednaa) is the phrase for opening up a sensitive or charged topic in conversation -- politics, an old family dispute, a touchy personal matter. It carries a slight sense that the topic might stir things up once raised, echoing the 'provoke' sense above.

Sense 5 : to pluck or strum (a musical instrument's strings)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बजाना (bajaanaa, broader term for playing an instrument)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Used specifically for stringed instruments, as in सितार के तार छेड़ना (sitaar ke taar chhednaa, 'to pluck the sitar's strings') -- a poetic, almost tactile way of describing the first touch that brings an instrument to life, distinct from the more general बजाना (bajaanaa).
