निपुण (nipun)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'skilled' or 'expert' at something -- a bookish, somewhat formal word for praising competence.
Sense 1 : skilled, expert, proficient
adjective Used with में (men) to name the field of skill, e.g. 'expert in X'.
Synonyms: कुशल (kushal), माहिर (maahir), दक्ष (daksh, formal), प्रवीण (praviin, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word in school report cards and formal write-ups ('वह गणित में निपुण है', vah ganit men nipun hai, 'he is skilled in mathematics') more than in casual conversation, where माहिर (maahir) or कुशल (kushal) carry the same idea with a lighter touch.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in casual speech where कुशल (kushal) or माहिर (maahir) would sound more natural; निपुण (nipun) leans toward written or formal praise -- a resume, a speech, a teacher's remark about a student -- rather than everyday chat about, say, a friend's cooking.
Sample Sentences:
वह गणित में निपुण है। He is skilled in mathematics.
(vah ganit men nipun hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
