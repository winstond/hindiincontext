आकाश (aakaash)

noun A single core meaning: the sky or heavens, used in everyday speech as well as in more elevated or poetic registers.
Sense 1 : sky, heavens
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आसमान (aasmaan), गगन (gagan, literary), नभ (nabh, literary), आस्मान (aasmaan, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In daily conversation, most Delhi-NCR speakers reach for आसमान (aasmaan) -- 'आसमान में बादल हैं' (aasmaan men baadal hain, 'there are clouds in the sky') -- while आकाश (aakaash) shows up more in written Hindi, children's rhymes, and set phrases like आकाशगंगा (aakaashgangaa, 'Milky Way', literally 'sky-river'). Hearing आकाश (aakaash) used as a boy's name is also extremely common, much like 'Sky' or 'Skye' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to आसमान (aasmaan) in speech and are surprised to meet आकाश (aakaash) instead in formal writing, weather reports, or school textbooks -- both mean the same thing, but आकाश (aakaash) leans slightly more bookish/formal even though it is not literary-only. 2) आकाशवाणी (aakaashvaanii) is the name of India's state radio broadcaster (literally 'voice from the sky') -- learners sometimes mistake it for a poetic phrase rather than recognizing it as a proper noun/institution.
