माँ-बाप (maan-baap)

noun A compound noun meaning 'parents' -- literally 'mother-father' (maan + baap). One straightforward sense, always used in the plural.
Sense 1 : parents (mother and father together)
noun, masculine/feminine Always used as a plural, agreeing with plural verbs even though it names two people jointly rather than a countable set.
Synonyms: माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa, formal), अभिभावक (abhibhaavak, guardians, broader/legal term), पेरेंट्स (perents, colloquial, English loan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the warm, everyday word you'll hear inside the family -- a mother scolding a child might say तेरे माँ-बाप ने क्या सिखाया है (tere maan-baap ne kyaa sikhaayaa hai, 'what have your parents taught you'). माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa) is what shows up on a school form or in a news report; माँ-बाप (maan-baap) is what shows up at the dinner table.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat it as singular because it looks like one compound word; it must take plural agreement: माँ-बाप आए हैं (maan-baap aae hain, 'the parents have come'), not आया है (aayaa hai). 2) माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa) is the more formal/written equivalent; माँ-बाप (maan-baap) is what you'd actually say at home or among friends.
