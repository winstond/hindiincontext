फाटक (phaatak)

noun A gate or barrier -- most often a large or heavy one like a railway crossing gate, a level-crossing barrier, or a big house/compound gate. One sense, one everyday meaning.
Sense 1 : gate, barrier (especially a large gate or railway crossing gate)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दरवाज़ा (darvaazaa, usually smaller/door-like), गेट (get, colloquial, English loan), द्वार (dvaar, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Anyone who has driven around Delhi-NCR knows the dread of a रेलवे फाटक (relve phaatak, 'railway crossing gate') coming down just as you're in a hurry -- it's a very concrete, everyday word tied to traffic jams near train tracks. It also shows up for the big gates of old havelis, schools, and government compounds, giving it a slightly more solid, official feel than the more generic दरवाज़ा (darvaazaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for दरवाज़ा (darvaazaa) for everything, but फाटक (phaatak) is specifically used for bigger structures -- a railway crossing barrier, the iron gate of a bungalow or school compound, a level-crossing -- not the door of a room or cupboard. 2) रेलवे फाटक (relve phaatak, 'railway gate/crossing') is a fixed phrase for the barrier at a level crossing; do not substitute गेट (get) here, as फाटक (phaatak) is the standard term in traffic and civic contexts.
