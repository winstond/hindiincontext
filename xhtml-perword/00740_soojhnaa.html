सूझना (soojhnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning for an idea, thought, or solution to 'occur to' someone -- almost always used with a को-marked experiencer, since the idea is the grammatical subject and the person is on the receiving end.
Sense 1 : for an idea/thought to occur to someone
verb, intransitive Takes a को-marked experiencer while the idea/thought itself is the grammatical subject: मुझे एक तरकीब सूझी (mujhe ek tarkiib soojhii, 'a trick occurred to me'). This is the same dative-subject frame as लगना (lagnaa).
Synonyms: ध्यान में आना (dhyaan men aanaa), दिमाग़ में आना (dimaagh men aanaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi treats thinking-of-something as an event that happens *to* you rather than something you actively do -- much like नींद आना (niind aanaa) frames sleep as arriving on its own. जब कुछ सूझ न रहा हो (jab kuchh soojh n rahaa ho, 'when nothing is coming to mind'), a Delhi friend might just shrug and say कुछ सूझ नहीं रहा, यार (kuchh soojh nahiin rahaa, yaar, 'nothing's occurring to me, man'), capturing that blank, stuck feeling perfectly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make the person the subject, saying something like मैं सूझा (main soojhaa) -- but सूझना (soojhnaa) is one of Hindi's involuntary-experience verbs, so the idea does the 'occurring' and the person only receives it via को (ko). 2) It's commonly used in the negative to mean drawing a blank -- कुछ नहीं सूझ रहा (kuchh nahiin soojh rahaa, 'nothing is coming to me') -- which learners may not think to reach for instinctively.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे एक अच्छा आइडिया सूझा। A good idea occurred to me.
(mujhe ek achchhaa aaidiyaa soojhaa।)
