आदरणीय (aadarniiy)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'respected' or 'honorable', used mainly as a polite address or descriptor for someone deserving respect -- typically an elder, teacher, or senior figure.
Sense 1 : respected, honorable (as a descriptor or form of address)
adjective Commonly placed before a name or title at the start of a letter or speech, e.g. आदरणीय प्रधानाचार्य जी (aadarniiy pradhaanaachaary jii, 'respected principal').
Synonyms: माननीय (maanniiy, formal, used for officials/dignitaries), पूज्य (poojy, literary, stronger reverence), सम्मानित (sammaanit)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see opening formal letters, invitations, and speeches in India -- आदरणीय महोदय (aadarniiy mahoday, 'respected sir') is a classic letter-writing opener taught in school. It carries a slightly old-fashioned, ceremonial politeness, much like starting an English letter with 'Dear Sir/Madam' but a notch more reverential.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use it in casual speech about friends or peers -- it sounds odd and overly formal there; reserve it for elders, teachers, officials, or ceremonial/written contexts. 2) It is almost always followed by जी (jii) or a title when addressing someone directly, e.g. आदरणीय सर (aadarniiy sar), rather than standing alone.
