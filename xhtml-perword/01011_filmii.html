फ़िल्मी (filmii)

adjective An adjective derived from फ़िल्म (film, 'film'), used both literally ('related to films') and figuratively (dramatic, exaggerated, unrealistic in a movie-like way).
Sense 1 : exaggerated, dramatic, unrealistic (like something out of a movie)
adjective
Synonyms: नाटकीय (naatkiiy, dramatic), बनावटी (banaavtii, artificial/exaggerated)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word Delhi friends use when someone's love confession, fight, or dramatic exit feels straight out of a Bollywood scene -- फ़िल्मी अंदाज़ (filmii andaaz, 'filmy style') is a very common phrase for over-the-top behavior. It captures how deeply Hindi cinema has shaped everyday Indian expression, to the point that 'behaving like a movie' is its own recognized category of behavior.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often assume फ़िल्मी (filmii) is neutral like 'cinematic' in English, but it usually carries a mild critical or mocking edge, implying something is exaggerated or fake, e.g. यह सब फ़िल्मी बातें हैं (yah sab filmii baaten hain, 'this is all just movie-style talk/nonsense'). 2) It's invariant in form despite ending in ी -- it does not change for gender or number like a typical -ई adjective might be assumed to.

Sense 2 : related to or characteristic of the film industry/cinema
adjective
Synonyms: सिनेमाई (sinemaaii, film industry-related)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used straightforwardly for anything connected to the movie world, as in फ़िल्मी दुनिया (filmii duniyaa, 'the world of films') or फ़िल्मी गाने (filmii gaane, 'film songs') -- the neutral, descriptive use of the word, distinct from its more common judgmental cousin above.
