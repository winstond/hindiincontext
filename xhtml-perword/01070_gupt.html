गुप्त (gupt)

adjective An adjective meaning 'hidden, secret, concealed'; also lives on as a common surname (Gupta) and appears inside several compound words about secrecy and privacy.
Sense 1 : hidden, secret, confidential
adjective
Synonyms: छिपा (chhipaa), गोपनीय (gopniiy, formal/official contexts), राज़ (raaz, used more as a noun 'secret' but overlaps in meaning), पोशीदा (poshiidaa, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see गुप्त (gupt) stamped on old government files right next to गोपनीय (gopniiy), and it's also the second half of the famous surname Gupta (gupt), as in the Gupta Empire (गुप्त वंश (gupt vansh)) -- a nice little historical anchor for remembering the word's core sense of 'hidden' or 'protected.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for राज़ (raaz) when they mean 'a secret' as a noun, and गुप्त (gupt) when they need the adjective 'secret/hidden' -- the two aren't interchangeable in a sentence like 'this is a secret' (यह एक राज़ है (yah ek raaz hai, 'this is a secret')) versus 'secret information' (गुप्त जानकारी (gupt jaankaarii, 'secret information')). 2) In official or bureaucratic Hindi, गोपनीय (gopniiy) is the word stamped on files ('confidential'), while गुप्त (gupt) is the everyday word for anything hidden -- learners sometimes use them as if fully interchangeable, but a native speaker reserves गोपनीय (gopniiy) for formal/official secrecy.
