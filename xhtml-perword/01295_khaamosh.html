ख़ामोश (khaamosh)

adjective An adjective meaning 'silent' or 'quiet' -- used for people staying quiet as well as for a hushed atmosphere or scene. Also spelled without the nuqta as खामोश (khaamosh).
Sense 1 : silent, not speaking
adjective
Synonyms: शांत (shaant), मौन (maun, formal/literary), चुप (chup)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word a judge or a stern teacher uses -- ख़ामोश! (khaamosh) as a bare command feels weightier and more theatrical than चुप रहो (chup raho). It also appears in Bollywood dialogue and courtroom drama far more than in casual chit-chat, where चुप (chup) still dominates.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for चुप (chup) by default since it is taught earlier; ख़ामोश (khaamosh) is just as common in speech but carries a slightly more dramatic or formal edge, so it shows up a lot in commands like ख़ामोश हो जाओ (khaamosh ho jaao, 'be quiet!') and courtroom or classroom settings. 2) It describes a state, not an action -- to tell someone to become quiet you need हो जाना (ho jaanaa) or रहना (rahnaa), not ख़ामोश alone as a verb.
Sample Sentences:
ख़ामोश! Silence!
(khaamosh!)

Sense 2 : hushed, still (of a place, night, atmosphere)
adjective
Synonyms: सुनसान (sunsaan, deserted/empty rather than merely quiet), शांत (shaant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used poetically for a ख़ामोश रात (khaamosh raat, 'a silent night') or ख़ामोश गलियाँ (khaamosh galiyaan, 'quiet lanes') -- it paints stillness the way English 'hushed' does, giving a scene an almost held-breath quality rather than just describing low noise levels.
Sample Sentences:

(raat khaamosh thii।)

(khaamosh galiyon men sirf hamaare qadmon kii aavaaz sunaaii de rahii thii।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
