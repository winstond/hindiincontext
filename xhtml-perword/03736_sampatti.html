संपत्ति (sampatti)

noun A formal-register noun meaning 'property, assets, wealth' -- covers both physical/real property and total financial worth; also spelled सम्पत्ति (sampatti) with the anusvaar written as a full nasal consonant.
Sense 1 : property, real estate, assets
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जायदाद (jaaydaad), मिल्कियत (milkiyat, less common), असबाब (asbaab, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see on legal documents, in news reports about disputes, and on government forms -- it carries a formal, almost bureaucratic weight, the kind of word a lawyer or a news anchor uses rather than a friend chatting about their new scooter. Related idea: जायदाद (jaaydaad) is the more everyday word for the same concept, especially in conversations about inheritance within a family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for यह मेरी संपत्ति है (yah merii sampatti hai, 'this is my property') in casual speech about small personal belongings; native speakers reserve संपत्ति (sampatti) for real estate, land, or substantial holdings, and would say चीज़ (chiiz) or सामान (saamaan) for everyday items. 2) It is grammatically feminine, so possessives and adjectives must agree accordingly -- मेरी संपत्ति (merii sampatti), not मेरा संपत्ति.

इस ज़मीन के टुकड़े पर संपत्ति विवाद अदालत में चल रहा है। The property dispute over this piece of land is going on in court.
(is zamiin ke tukde par sampatti vivaad adaalat men chal rahaa hai।)

Sense 2 : wealth, total assets/worth
noun, feminine
Synonyms: धन (dhan), दौलत (daulat), माल (maal, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this sense in the context of a company's balance sheet (कुल संपत्ति, kul sampatti, 'total assets') or when describing someone as wealthy in a formal way -- उसके पास बहुत संपत्ति है (uske paas bahut sampatti hai, 'he has a lot of wealth'). It sits a notch above दौलत (daulat) in formality, the way 'assets' sits above 'riches' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use संपत्ति (sampatti) interchangeably with पैसा (paisaa, 'money'), but संपत्ति (sampatti) refers to accumulated worth or holdings, not cash in hand or income.
