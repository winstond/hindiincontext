अंत (ant)

noun A common noun meaning 'end' or 'conclusion' -- of a story, a period of time, a life, or a process. One main sense, though it shows up in a few fixed phrases worth knowing.
Sense 1 : end, conclusion, finish (of a story, event, period, or life)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: समाप्ति (samaapti, formal), खत्म (khatm, colloquial, used mainly in verb phrases like खत्म होना (khatm honaa)), समापन (samaapan, formal, used for formal events/ceremonies)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear अंत में (ant men, 'in the end') constantly in spoken Hindi, whether someone is wrapping up a long story or a grandmother is concluding a piece of advice. It has a slightly more reflective, storytelling weight than the blunter खत्म (khatm), which is more about something simply stopping or running out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खत्म (khatm) as a direct synonym in every context, but अंत (ant) is the noun used in more narrative or formal contexts (कहानी का अंत (kahaanii kaa ant, 'the end of the story')), while खत्म (khatm) tends to appear as part of the verb phrase खत्म होना (khatm honaa, 'to get finished'). 2) अंत में (ant men) is a very common fixed phrase meaning 'in the end, finally' -- learners sometimes construct this awkwardly instead of using the set phrase.

Sense 2 : death, demise (euphemistic/literary use)
noun, masculine Appears in phrases like अंत होना (ant honaa, 'to meet one's end') or उसका अंत हुआ (uskaa ant huaa, 'his end came') as a softer or more literary way to refer to someone's death.
Synonyms: मृत्यु (mrityu, formal), मौत (maut)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense leans literary and somewhat formal or dramatic -- you'd meet it in a novel, a news report, or a solemn speech more than in casual chat, where मौत (maut) or गुज़र जाना (guzar jaanaa) would be the everyday choice.
