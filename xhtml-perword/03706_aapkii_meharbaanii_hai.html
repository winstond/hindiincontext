आपकी मेहरबानी है (aapkii meharbaanii hai)

interjection A polite, slightly formal phrase of thanks meaning 'it is your kindness' -- used to acknowledge a favor or courteous gesture from someone you address respectfully with आप (aap).
Sense 1 : polite thanks for a favor or kindness shown
interjection Built from मेहरबानी (meharbaanii, 'kindness/favor') plus the आपकी (aapkii) possessive agreeing with the feminine noun; functions as a fixed formulaic response rather than a sentence one builds freshly each time.
Synonyms: बड़ी मेहरबानी (badii meharbaanii), आपका शुक्रिया (aapkaa shukriyaa), आपकी कृपा है (aapkii kripaa hai, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in slightly formal or old-fashioned contexts -- a shopkeeper thanking a regular customer, a subordinate thanking a boss, or in the courtly speech of older Delhi households where gratitude is expressed with a touch of humility. It has a faint echo of old Mughal-court courtesy, positioning the other person as having generously extended themselves on your behalf, so it can feel a notch more deferential than a plain शुक्रिया (shukriyaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes swap in तुम्हारी (tumhaarii) or तेरी (terii) to sound more casual, but this phrase is inherently tied to the respectful आप (aap) register -- with तुम or तू it would sound odd or sarcastic. 2) It is a response to a favor or courtesy, not a general-purpose 'thank you' for small everyday things like handing over change -- शुक्रिया (shukriyaa) or धन्यवाद (dhanyvaad) cover that ground instead.
