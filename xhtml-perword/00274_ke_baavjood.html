के बावजूद (ke baavjood)

postposition A postposition meaning 'despite/in spite of', governing the oblique case of the preceding noun/pronoun like other compound postpositions (ke baare men, ke lie).
Sense 1 : despite, in spite of
postposition Takes a noun or pronoun in the oblique case before it, e.g. उसके बावजूद (uske baavjood, 'despite that'); can also govern a clause nominalized with का/की, e.g. उसके बीमार होने के बावजूद (uske biimaar hone ke baavjood, 'despite him being sick').
Synonyms: बावजूद इसके (baavjood iske, variant word order), फिर भी (phir bhii, 'even then' -- not a direct substitute, follows the clause rather than the noun)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a Persian-origin word (बावजूद) that has become完全 naturalized in everyday Hindi -- you will hear it constantly in news reports and daily complaints alike, as in बारिश के बावजूद हम गए (baarish ke baavjood ham gae, 'despite the rain, we went'). It pairs well with फिर भी (phir bhii) later in the sentence for emphasis, e.g. मेहनत के बावजूद, वह फेल हो गया (mehnat ke baavjood, vah phel ho gayaa, 'despite the effort, he failed').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the oblique marker के (ke) before बावजूद (baavjood) and just say मेहनत बावजूद (mehnat baavjood) instead of the correct मेहनत के बावजूद (mehnat ke baavjood). 2) Confusing यद्यपि/हालांकि...फिर भी (haalaanki...phir bhii) with के बावजूद (ke baavjood) -- the former is a clause-level conjunction pair, the latter directly attaches to a noun phrase.
Sample Sentences:
बारिश के बावजूद हम पिकनिक पर गए। Despite the rain, we went on a picnic.
(baarish ke baavjood ham piknik par gae।)
