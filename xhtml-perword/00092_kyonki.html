क्योंकि (kyonki)

conjunction A single-sense word: the everyday conjunction meaning 'because', used to introduce a reason clause.
Sense 1 : because
conjunction Introduces a subordinate clause that gives the reason for the main clause; the main clause usually comes first, e.g. main clause + क्योंकि (kyonki) + reason clause.
Synonyms: चूँकि (choonki, literary, precedes the reason clause and pairs with इसलिए (islie))
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is simply the everyday word for 'because' that you will hear constantly in explanations, excuses, and arguments alike -- from a child explaining to a parent why homework isn't done, to a friend explaining why they are late. It is one of the first conjunctions learners should make fully active, since giving reasons comes up in almost every conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to open a sentence with क्योंकि (kyonki) the way English can start with 'because', but the natural Hindi order puts the result first: मैं नहीं आया क्योंकि बारिश हो रही थी (main nahiin aayaa kyonki baarish ho rahii thii, 'I didn't come because it was raining') rather than leading with the reason. 2) Do not pair क्योंकि (kyonki) with इसलिए (islie) in the same sentence -- unlike चूँकि (choonki), which forms a correlative pair with इसलिए (islie), क्योंकि (kyonki) stands alone.
