विशेष (vishesh)

adjective/adverb A common formal-register adjective meaning 'special/particular', with an adverb-like usage meaning 'especially' and a noun-ish use in fixed phrases meaning 'particular thing/detail'.
Sense 1 : special, particular, distinctive
adjective
Synonyms: खास (khaas), विशिष्ट (vishisht, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see विशेष (vishesh) constantly on Indian TV news and official signage -- विशेष रिपोर्ट (vishesh riport, 'special report'), विशेष अतिथि (vishesh atithi, 'special guest') -- it has that announcer/press-release flavor, whereas खास (khaas) is what you'd say to a friend.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खास (khaas) in casual speech and विशेष (vishesh) in writing or formal announcements -- both mean 'special', but विशेष (vishesh) sounds noticeably more formal/bookish if used in ordinary conversation, like saying 'particular' instead of 'special' in English.

(aaj kii vishesh riport men ham ek bade ghotaale kaa khulaasaa karenge।)

Sense 2 : especially, particularly
adverb Used before a noun or clause to single it out, often paired with तौर पर (taur par) or रूप से (roop se) as विशेष तौर पर (vishesh taur par) / विशेष रूप से (vishesh roop se).
Synonyms: खासकर (khaaskar), विशेष रूप से (vishesh roop se, fuller phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This adverbial use rides on the same root idea as the adjective sense -- something is being marked out from the ordinary -- so a teacher would call it the same 'flavor' of विशेष (vishesh), just doing adverb duty.
