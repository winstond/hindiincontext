छिहत्तर (chhihattar)

numeral The cardinal number 76 (chhihattar). A single-sense numeral with no other common usage.
Sense 1 : the number 76
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers between 21 and 99, छिहत्तर (chhihattar) is a fused, irregular form rather than a compound you can build from छह (chhah, 'six') and सत्तर (sattar, 'seventy') -- native speakers just know it as a whole word, the way English speakers know 'eleven' isn't 'ten-one.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse छिहत्तर (chhihattar, '76') with similar-sounding numbers in the 70s like सत्तर (sattar, '70') or छियासी (chhiyaasii, '86') -- Hindi numerals 1-99 are each memorized individually rather than built predictably from tens and units, so there is no shortcut but repetition.
