हवाई जहाज़ (havaaii jahaaz)

noun A compound noun meaning 'airplane' -- literally 'aerial ship/vessel.' Single, straightforward sense; often shortened in casual speech to just जहाज़ (jahaaz).
Sense 1 : airplane
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जहाज़ (jahaaz, commonly shortened form), विमान (vimaan, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: हवाई (havaaii) comes from हवा (havaa, 'air/wind') and जहाज़ (jahaaz) originally means 'ship' or 'vessel' -- so हवाई जहाज़ (havaaii jahaaz) literally paints the plane as a 'ship of the air,' a lovely image that mirrors how English once called early aircraft 'airships.' In casual Delhi conversation you will hear people just say जहाज़ (jahaaz) when talking about flights, saving the full compound for more careful or written speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop हवाई (havaaii) and say just जहाज़ (jahaaz) thinking it is incomplete -- in everyday speech this shortened form is completely normal and native speakers use it constantly, especially when context is clear. 2) हवाई (havaaii) alone is an adjective meaning 'aerial' or 'of the air' (as in हवाई अड्डा (havaaii addaa, 'airport')) -- it cannot stand alone to mean 'airplane.'
