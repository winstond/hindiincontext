मामला (maamlaa)

noun A common noun meaning 'matter, affair, issue' -- ranging from everyday situations to legal cases; a related idiomatic sense captures romantic entanglements.
Sense 1 : matter, affair, issue
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बात (baat), मसला (maslaa), मुद्दा (muddaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in Delhi -- from a shopkeeper saying पैसों का मामला है (paison kaa maamlaa hai, 'it's a matter of money') to news anchors discussing a पुलिस मामला (pulis maamlaa, 'police matter'). It has a slightly serious, weighty flavor compared to the more casual बात (baat).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बात (baat) in every context, but मामला (maamlaa) suggests something with more weight or complexity -- a situation with layers, not just a remark or topic. 2) It frequently appears with है (hai) in the pattern 'X का मामला है (X kaa maamlaa hai, it's a matter of X)' -- learners sometimes drop the का (kaa) and say it awkwardly.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : legal case
noun, masculine
Synonyms: केस (kes, colloquial), मुकदमा (mukadmaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In courtroom or news contexts, मामला (maamlaa) is the standard formal word for a legal case -- you'll see headlines like भ्रष्टाचार का मामला (bhrashtaachaar kaa maamlaa, 'corruption case'). English केस (kes) has crept into casual speech as a synonym, but मामला (maamlaa) stays the go-to word in written and official Hindi.

Sense 3 : romantic affair or entanglement (idiomatic)
noun, masculine Almost always used with चलना (chalnaa) as in 'मामला चल रहा है (maamlaa chal rahaa hai, something's going on between them)', or with होना (honaa) to mean a romantic connection exists.
Synonyms: चक्कर (chakkar, colloquial), इश्क़ (ishq, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is classic Hindi gossip-speak -- friends teasing each other with क्या मामला है? (kyaa maamlaa hai?, 'what's going on [between you two]?') when they suspect a budding romance. It's playful and colloquial, the kind of line you'd hear among college friends in a Delhi hostel, not in formal writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense only works in context -- used alone, मामला (maamlaa) just means 'matter'; it's the surrounding gossip-toned phrasing (often with चलना (chalnaa)) that signals the romantic reading.

अरे, तेरा और सोनम का क्या मामला है? Hey, what's going on between you and Sonam?
