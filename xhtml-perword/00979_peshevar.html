पेशेवर (peshevar)

adjective/noun A borrowed-feel word (from Persian पेशा, 'occupation') meaning 'professional' -- it works both as an adjective describing something done in a skilled, businesslike way and as a noun for a person who does a job for a living, especially as opposed to an amateur.
Sense 1 : professional (in manner or quality)
adjective
Synonyms: व्यावसायिक (vyaavsaayik)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this a lot in office and media contexts -- पेशेवर अंदाज़ (peshevar andaaz, 'a professional style/manner') is a common compliment for someone who is competent and businesslike rather than sloppy or overly personal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for व्यावसायिक (vyaavsaayik) for everything 'professional', but पेशेवर (peshevar) is the word people actually use in speech about someone's skill or conduct on the job, e.g. पेशेवर रवैया (peshevar ravaiyaa, 'a professional attitude').

Sense 2 : a professional (person)
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: विशेषज्ञ (visheshagy, expert, meaning scope narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up often in sports and business talk, contrasting a पेशेवर (peshevar) with a शौकिया (shaukiyaa, 'amateur/hobbyist') -- the same root पेशा (peshaa, 'profession, trade') underlies both the adjective and noun senses of this word.
Common Mistakes: 1) पेशेवर (peshevar) as a noun leans toward 'someone who does this for a living/skillfully' (often contrasted with an amateur), not simply 'an expert' -- for pure expertise, विशेषज्ञ (visheshagy) fits better.
