आगे (aage)

adverb Core spatial/temporal adverb meaning 'ahead, forward, in front' -- extends naturally into time ('in future') and sequence ('further on, next'). One word, one core image (being out front), applied across space, time, and discourse.
Sense 1 : ahead, in front (spatial)
adverb
Synonyms: सामने (saamne, in front, facing), पहले (pahle, further ahead in a line/queue)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a queue at a Delhi metro counter or a line of traffic at a signal -- आगे (aage) is simply 'further up that line.' It is one of the first direction words learners pick up alongside पीछे (piichhe, behind).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse आगे (aage) with सामने (saamne) -- आगे (aage) is about being further along a path or line (ahead of), while सामने (saamne) is about facing something directly (in front of, opposite). 2) आगे (aage) often pairs with बढ़ना (badhnaa, to move forward) in the common phrase आगे बढ़ो (aage badho, 'move ahead/go forward') -- learners sometimes reach for चलो (chalo) alone when the direction 'forward' needs आगे (aage) too.
Sample Sentences:
तुम आगे चलो, मैं पीछे आता हूँ। You walk ahead, I'll come behind.
(tum aage chalo, main piichhe aataa hoon।)

Sense 2 : in the future, from now on
adverb
Synonyms: भविष्य में (bhavishy men, formal/literary phrase), आने वाले समय में (aane vaale samay men, formal, 'in the coming time')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the same 'ahead' pushed onto the timeline: just as you walk आगे (aage) down a road, time also moves आगे (aage). You will hear parents say आगे देखेंगे (aage dekhenge, 'we'll see, going forward') when deferring a decision -- a very common hedge in Hindi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners overuse भविष्य में (bhavishy men) where a native speaker would just say आगे (aage) -- in casual speech आगे (aage) is the natural, unmarked way to say 'from here on' or 'going forward.'

Sense 3 : further, next, subsequently (in a sequence or narrative)
adverb Common in narrating a story or giving multi-step instructions: आगे क्या हुआ? (aage kyaa huaa?, 'what happened next?').
Synonyms: इसके बाद (iske baad, 'after this', slightly more explicit connector)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the storyteller's आगे (aage) -- the word that keeps a listener leaning in, as in आगे सुनो (aage suno, 'listen further/what happened next'), a phrase you'll hear from grandparents mid-story or from someone recounting office gossip.
Sample Sentences:

(phir aage kyaa huaa, zaraa bataao।)
