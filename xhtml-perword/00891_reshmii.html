रेशमी (reshmii)

adjective Means 'silky' or 'made of silk' -- derived from रेशम (resham, 'silk'). Has a literal sense (silk fabric) and a common figurative sense (silky-smooth, e.g. hair, voice, skin).
Sense 1 : made of silk
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around weddings and festivals: रेशमी साड़ी (reshmii saadii, 'silk sari') or रेशमी कुर्ता (reshmii kurtaa, 'silk kurta') are classic gift and shopping-trip vocabulary in Delhi's markets like Chandni Chowk.

Sense 2 : silky-smooth, soft like silk (figurative)
adjective
Synonyms: मुलायम (mulaayam, soft, not silk-specific), मखमली (makhamlii, velvety)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi loves stretching fabric words onto the body and voice -- रेशमी बाल (reshmii baal, 'silky hair') or रेशमी आवाज़ (reshmii aavaaz, 'silky voice') are common in songs and ads, much like English 'silky' itself.
