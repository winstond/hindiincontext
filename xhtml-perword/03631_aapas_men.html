आपस में (aapas men)

adverb A fixed reciprocal phrase meaning 'among themselves/each other' -- it always frames an action as happening mutually between the people involved, and is almost always followed by a verb describing shared or reciprocal activity.
Sense 1 : mutually, among themselves, with each other
adverb Functions as an adverbial modifying the verb, marking the action as reciprocal among the subjects; commonly paired with verbs like बात करना (baat karnaa), लड़ना (ladnaa), मिलना (milnaa), and बाँटना (baantnaa).
Synonyms: एक दूसरे से (ek doosre se, more explicit reciprocal phrase)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in family and community contexts -- भाई-बहन आपस में झगड़ते हैं (bhaaii-bahan aapas men jhagadte hain, 'siblings fight among themselves') is the kind of line every Indian household has said at some point. It carries a warm, communal flavor, evoking a closed group (family, friends, neighbors) doing something together or to one another, rather than a formal or distant 'each other.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to attach a case-marking postposition directly after आपस (aapas) alone, but the word almost never appears without में (men) -- the two are learned together as one chunk. 2) Some learners substitute a personal pronoun like हम (ham) or वे (ve) where आपस में (aapas men) is needed to express the 'each other' relationship; the plain pronoun does not carry the reciprocal sense on its own.
Sample Sentences:
भाई-बहन आपस में झगड़ते हैं। Siblings fight among themselves.
(bhaaii-bahan aapas men jhagadte hain।)
