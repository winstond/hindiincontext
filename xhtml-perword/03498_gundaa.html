गुंडा (gundaa)

noun/adjective A common everyday word for a thug, goon, or ruffian; also used loosely as an adjective/descriptor for someone acting like a bully.
Sense 1 : thug, goon, hooligan
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बदमाश (badmaash), लफंगा (laphangaa, colloquial), मवाली (mavaalii, colloquial), दबंग (dabang, more like 'strongman/dominant type')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: गुंडा (gundaa) is a staple word in Hindi film dialogue and news reports about local crime -- politicians accusing rivals of running 'गुंडा राज' (gundaa raaj, 'rule of thugs') is a very common phrase in Indian political speech. It carries real social weight, evoking neighborhood toughs who intimidate shopkeepers or bully people in the street, not just cartoonish villains.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use गुंडा (gundaa) as a mild insult like 'naughty,' but it actually implies real menace or criminal thuggery -- calling a mischievous child गुंडा (gundaa) is a joking exaggeration, not literal. 2) The feminine form गुंडी (gundii) exists but is rarely used; speakers usually keep गुंडा (gundaa) even for female troublemakers, or use a phrase instead.

Sense 2 : thuggish, hooligan-like
adjective Used attributively before a noun, e.g. गुंडा तत्व (gundaa tatv, 'hooligan elements'), often in news register.
Synonyms: बदमाश (badmaash)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear this in the compound गुंडा तत्व (gundaa tatv), a stock phrase in Hindi news broadcasts referring to disruptive troublemaking elements at a protest or public event.
