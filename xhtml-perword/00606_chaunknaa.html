चौंकना (chaunknaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to be startled, to jolt/flinch in surprise' -- describes the involuntary physical reaction of being startled, not a calm or deliberate emotional state.
Sense 1 : to be startled, to jump/flinch in surprise
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चकित होना (chakit honaa, more literary), हड़बड़ाना (hadabdaanaa, flustered/jumpy reaction)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture someone deep in thought over chai when a scooter horn blares outside -- वह चौंक गया (vah chaunk gayaa, 'he was startled') is exactly that jolt. It very often pairs with जाना (jaanaa) as चौंक जाना (chaunk jaanaa) to sharpen the sense of suddenness, a pattern you'll meet constantly with this verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आश्चर्य होना (aashchary honaa) or हैरान होना (hairaan honaa) for all kinds of 'surprise,' but चौंकना (chaunknaa) specifically carries the sense of a sudden bodily jolt or flinch, like when a loud noise or unexpected sight makes you jump -- it's not used for calm intellectual surprise like learning surprising news. 2) It is intransitive and takes से (se) for the stimulus, as in आवाज़ से चौंकना (aavaaz se chaunknaa, 'to be startled by a sound') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) here by analogy with other reaction verbs.
Sample Sentences:
अचानक आवाज़ सुनकर वह चौंक गया। He was startled on suddenly hearing the sound.
(achaanak aavaaz sunkar vah chaunk gayaa।)
