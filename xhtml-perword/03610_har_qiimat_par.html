हर क़ीमत पर (har qiimat par)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'at any cost, no matter what' -- literally 'at every price.' Built from हर (har, 'every'), क़ीमत (qiimat, 'price'), and पर (par, 'on/at').
Sense 1 : at any cost, no matter what
adverb Functions as a sentence-level adverbial, typically placed right before the verb it modifies to stress determination or urgency: यह काम हर क़ीमत पर पूरा करना है (yah kaam har qiimat par pooraa karnaa hai, 'this work must be finished at any cost').
Synonyms: किसी भी क़ीमत पर (kisii bhii qiimat par, slightly more emphatic variant), हर हाल में (har haal men, 'in any case/circumstance')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the kind of phrase you hear in determined pep-talks -- a coach, a parent, or a politician saying हमें जीतना है, हर क़ीमत पर (hamen jiitnaa hai, har qiimat par, 'we must win, at any cost'). It carries a slightly dramatic, resolute flavor, so it fits high-stakes or emotionally charged speech more than casual chit-chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes translate 'price' too literally and use it only for money-related contexts -- but this phrase is almost always figurative, about effort, risk, or sacrifice, not actual currency. 2) क़ीमत (qiimat) has a nuqta under the क; dropping it to कीमत (kiimat) is common in casual writing and still understood, but the nuqta spelling is considered more correct.
Sample Sentences:
हमें यह मुक़ाबला हर क़ीमत पर जीतना है। We have to win this match at any cost.
(hamen yah muqaablaa har qiimat par jiitnaa hai।)
