कुछ हद तक (kuchh had tak)

adverb A set adverbial phrase meaning 'to some extent' -- it hedges a statement, softening a claim without connecting it to anything before it.
Sense 1 : to a limited or partial degree
adverb Can open a sentence on its own to qualify the whole clause that follows, e.g. कुछ हद तक (kuchh had tak) यह सही है (yah sahii hai, 'to some extent this is correct') -- it does not need a preceding clause to link back to, unlike a true conjunction.
Synonyms: थोड़ा बहुत (thodaa bahut, somewhat), किसी हद तक (kisii had tak, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a handy hedging phrase for diplomatic answers -- when a Delhi shopkeeper or a colleague doesn't want to fully agree or disagree, कुछ हद तक (kuchh had tak) sahi hai is a very common, polite middle-ground response. It pairs naturally with पूरी तरह (poorii tarah, 'completely') as its opposite end of the scale.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat हद (had) here as needing its own case-marking logic, but the whole phrase behaves as a frozen unit -- हद (had) is not separately declined or replaced with other nouns in this expression. 2) Do not confuse with हद से ज़्यादा (had se zyaadaa, 'beyond limit, excessively'), which uses the same root word हद (had) but means the opposite -- too much, not somewhat.
Sample Sentences:
आप सही कह रहे हैं, लेकिन कुछ हद तक ही। You're right, but only to some extent.
(aap sahii kah rahe hain, lekin kuchh had tak hii।)
