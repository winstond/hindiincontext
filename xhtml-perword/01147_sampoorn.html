संपूर्ण (sampoorn)

adjective A single-sense formal adjective meaning 'complete, entire, whole.' Often heard in written and spoken formal registers -- news, official announcements, religious contexts.
Sense 1 : complete, whole, entire
adjective
Synonyms: पूरा (pooraa), पूर्ण (poorn, formal), समग्र (samagr, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot on the news -- संपूर्ण भारत (sampoorn bhaarat, 'the whole of India') or संपूर्ण जानकारी (sampoorn jaankaarii, 'complete information') -- and in temple or devotional contexts describing something whole and unblemished. In daily kitchen-and-market Hindi, though, पूरा (pooraa) is what people actually reach for; संपूर्ण (sampoorn) is its more official, slightly Sanskritized cousin.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this in casual speech where पूरा (pooraa) would sound far more natural; संपूर्ण (sampoorn) belongs to formal writing, announcements, and set phrases like संपूर्ण जानकारी (sampoorn jaankaarii, 'complete information') rather than everyday chat about finishing dinner or homework.
