मज़दूर (mazdoor)

noun A single-sense word meaning a manual laborer or worker, someone who earns a living through physical work.
Sense 1 : manual laborer, worker
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कामगार (kaamgaar), श्रमिक (shramik, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly near any construction site in Delhi-NCR, where मज़दूर (mazdoor) gather each morning at a मज़दूर चौक (mazdoor chauk, 'laborers' corner') hoping to get picked up for a day's work. It carries real social weight in political and labor-rights speech too, showing up in phrases like मज़दूर संघ (mazdoor sangh, 'labor union') and मज़दूर दिवस (mazdoor divas, 'Labor Day').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use कर्मचारी (karmchaarii) as a synonym, but that word points more to a salaried employee or office staff, not someone doing physical labor -- मज़दूर (mazdoor) specifically evokes manual work like construction or farm labor.
