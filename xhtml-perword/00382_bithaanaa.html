बिठाना (bithaanaa)

verb The causative of बैठना (baithnaa, 'to sit') -- means 'to seat/make sit,' and extends into 'to install/appoint' and (in compound-verb use) 'to settle/adjust something into place.' Often heard as बैठाना (baithaanaa), a variant spelling with the same meaning.
Sense 1 : to seat someone, make someone sit
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बैठाना (baithaanaa, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a host at the door saying आइए, बैठिए (aaie, baithie, 'come, sit') -- बिठाना (bithaanaa) is what the host actively does, physically guiding a guest to a seat of honor, which matters in Indian hospitality where where you seat someone signals respect.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use बैठना (baithnaa, 'to sit') when they mean to seat someone else -- बिठाना (bithaanaa) is the causative form needed when one person makes another sit. 2) The person being seated takes को (ko): बच्चे को कुर्सी पर बिठाया (bachche ko kursii par bithaayaa, 'made the child sit on the chair').

Sense 2 : to install, appoint, set someone in a position
verb, transitive
Synonyms: नियुक्त करना (niyukt karnaa, formal), स्थापित करना (sthaapit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used for putting someone into a position of power or responsibility, as in गद्दी पर बिठाना (gaddii par bithaanaa, 'to put on the throne/seat of power') -- a phrase you'll meet in stories about kings and successions, and also loosely in office politics gossip about who got 'placed' where.
Sample Sentences:
राजा ने अपने बेटे को गद्दी पर बिठाया। The king put his son on the throne.

Sense 3 : to adjust, fit, or settle something into place
verb, transitive Common in compound-verb constructions like जमाकर बिठाना (jamaakar bithaanaa) or as the second element after a main verb, giving the sense of carefully adjusting or settling something so it fits well.
Synonyms: जमाना (jamaanaa), फिट करना (phit karnaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this when someone describes arranging furniture, fitting a part into a machine, or even settling an account or plan just right -- the underlying image is always the same seat-related one: making something sit properly where it belongs.
Sample Sentences:
पहले टेबल को दीवार के पास बिठाओ, फिर बाकी सामान रखेंगे। First settle the table into place near the wall, then we'll put the rest of the things.
