धुन (dhun)

noun Covers two related ideas: a musical tune or melody, and a fixed obsession or single-minded zeal to do something (often in the phrase धुन में, dhun men).
Sense 1 : tune, melody
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सुर (sur), राग (raag, musical mode/melody, more technical)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around music: कोई धुन गुनगुनाना (koii dhun gungunaanaa, 'to hum some tune') is a very ordinary thing to say about someone lost in a song under their breath, on a scooter or in the kitchen.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use राग (raag) when they just mean a simple hummable tune -- धुन (dhun) is the everyday word for 'melody' or 'the tune of a song,' while राग (raag) points to a classical musical framework.

(vah rasoii men koii dhun gungunaa rahii thii।)

Sense 2 : obsession, single-minded zeal
noun, feminine Very commonly used in the postpositional phrase धुन में (dhun men, 'in the grip of an obsession/absorbed in doing something'), often with होना (honaa) or सवार होना (savaar honaa) -- 'X की धुन सवार होना' (X kii dhun savaar honaa, 'to be possessed/driven by an obsession with X').
Synonyms: जुनून (junoon), सनक (sanak, leans toward an eccentric or irrational fixation), ज़िद (zid, stubborn insistence, narrower)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense captures a very Hindi way of describing someone absorbed in their own world -- अपनी धुन का पक्का (apnii dhun kaa pakkaa, roughly 'firmly set on his own track') is a classic compliment-cum-complaint for a stubbornly focused person, the eccentric uncle who paints all day or the neighbor who jogs rain or shine.
Common Mistakes: 1) धुन (dhun) in this sense is almost always followed by में (men) when describing someone acting under its influence, e.g. अपनी धुन में (apnii dhun men, 'lost in his own world/obsession') -- dropping में changes the flavor of the sentence. 2) Don't confuse with जुनून (junoon), which can carry a more intense, even violent edge; धुन (dhun) more often describes a quirky, personal, almost admirable fixation.
