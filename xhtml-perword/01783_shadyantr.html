षड्यंत्र (shadyantr)

noun A single-sense formal/literary word meaning 'conspiracy' or 'plot' -- the kind of word you meet in news reports and political speech rather than casual kitchen-table talk.
Sense 1 : conspiracy, plot
noun, masculine
Synonyms: साज़िश (saazish), कुचक्र (kuchakr, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Hindi news channels and political debates -- 'सरकार के खिलाफ षड्यंत्र' (sarkaar ke khilaaph shadyantr, 'conspiracy against the government') is a stock headline phrase. Its weight and Sanskritic form make it feel almost courtroom-serious, so you will rarely hear it tossed around about petty household drama the way English speakers might loosely say 'conspiracy.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word in everyday gossip about a friend's scheme, but native speakers save षड्यंत्र (shadyantr) for serious matters -- political plots, criminal conspiracies, big betrayals -- and use साज़िश (saazish) for the smaller, everyday version of the idea. 2) The verb pairing is रचना (rachnaa) or करना (karnaa) -- षड्यंत्र रचना (shadyantr rachnaa, 'to hatch a conspiracy') is the set phrase learners should absorb rather than building their own verb combination.
