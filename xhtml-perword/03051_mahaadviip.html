महाद्वीप (mahaadviip)

noun A single-sense geography word meaning 'continent'; built from महा (mahaa, 'great') plus द्वीप (dviip, 'island/landmass').
Sense 1 : continent
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a textbook/geography-class word -- the kind you first meet in a school भूगोल (bhoogol, 'geography') lesson listing एशिया (eshiyaa, 'Asia'), अफ्रीका (aphriikaa, 'Africa'), and so on. It is common in writing and formal speech but rarely comes up in casual conversation unless you are discussing travel, maps, or world affairs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with द्वीप (dviip, 'island') since महाद्वीप (mahaadviip) is literally built on that root -- remember महा (mahaa) 'great' flips the meaning from a small landmass to a whole continent.
Sample Sentences:
एशिया दुनिया का सबसे बड़ा महाद्वीप है। Asia is the largest continent in the world.
(eshiyaa duniyaa kaa sabse badaa mahaadviip hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
