शिक्षा (shikshaa)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'education' -- covering both the abstract idea of schooling/instruction and, more formally, a 'lesson' drawn from experience.
Sense 1 : education, schooling, instruction
noun, feminine
Synonyms: तालीम (taaliim, everyday, especially in spoken/Urdu-influenced Hindi), शिक्षण (shikshan, formal, refers more to the act of teaching)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: शिक्षा (shikshaa) is the word you will see everywhere in Indian civic life -- on ministry signboards (शिक्षा मंत्रालय, shikshaa mantraalay, 'Ministry of Education'), in NGO slogans, and in every parent's conversation about their children's future. It carries a slightly formal, almost reverent weight, similar to how English speakers treat 'education' in policy contexts rather than casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse शिक्षा (shikshaa) with शिक्षक (shikshak, 'teacher') because of the shared root -- शिक्षा is the education itself, not the person imparting it. 2) When talking about a field of study or level of education, शिक्षा often pairs with प्राप्त करना (praapt karnaa, 'to obtain') or दिलाना (dilaanaa, 'to have someone given'), not सीखना (siikhnaa), which is reserved for the act of learning a specific skill.

Sense 2 : a lesson, moral teaching (drawn from an experience or story)
noun, feminine Common in the fixed phrase शिक्षा लेना (shikshaa lenaa, 'to take a lesson from something') and शिक्षा देना (shikshaa denaa, 'to teach someone a lesson'), often used about stories, fables, or hard experiences rather than formal schooling.
Synonyms: सीख (siikh, more everyday and concrete, often used for the moral of a story), नसीहत (nasiihat, carries a tone of advice or admonition)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the sense you meet at the end of a Panchatantra (panchtantr) fable or a grandmother's bedtime story, where the closing line is often something like इस कहानी से हमें यह शिक्षा मिलती है (is kahaanii se hamen yah shikshaa miltii hai, 'this story teaches us this lesson'). It is the same word as 'education' above, but here it points to a single moral takeaway rather than years of schooling.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to सीखना (siikhnaa, 'to learn') when they mean 'to draw a lesson,' but the natural collocation here is शिक्षा लेना (shikshaa lenaa), not शिक्षा सीखना (shikshaa siikhnaa), which sounds odd.

(is kahaanii se hamen yah shikshaa miltii hai ki sachchaaii kii hameshaa jiit hotii hai।)
