आकर्षण (aakarshan)

noun A noun meaning 'attraction' -- covers both the physical/scientific pull (magnetism, gravity) and the emotional/personal pull of being drawn to someone or something.
Sense 1 : attraction (personal, emotional, or aesthetic pull toward someone/something)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खिंचाव (khinchaav), मोह (moh, leans toward infatuation/attachment)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi speakers often frame attraction as something that happens *toward* a direction -- किसी की तरफ़ आकर्षण (kisii kii taraf aakarshan, 'attraction toward someone') -- echoing the word's root sense of being pulled or drawn, much like a magnet.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use आकर्षण (aakarshan) where प्यार (pyaar, love) is meant -- आकर्षण (aakarshan) is the pull or draw someone feels, not the deeper emotion of love itself, so कोई किसी की तरफ़ आकर्षित होता है (koii kisii kii taraf aakarshit hotaa hai, 'someone feels drawn to someone') is a lighter claim than प्यार करना (pyaar karnaa). 2) The verb form is आकर्षित होना (aakarshit honaa, 'to be attracted'), not a direct verb from आकर्षण (aakarshan) itself -- learners sometimes try to verb the noun directly.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे उसकी तरफ़ एक अजीब सा आकर्षण महसूस होता है। I feel a strange kind of attraction toward her.
(mujhe uskii taraf ek ajiib saa aakarshan mahsoos hotaa hai।)

Sense 2 : attraction (a tourist or crowd-drawing feature; a draw)
noun, masculine Common in phrases like मुख्य आकर्षण (mukhy aakarshan, 'main attraction') describing a highlight of an event, place, or show.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this sense on tourism posters and event flyers across Delhi -- मेले का मुख्य आकर्षण (mele kaa mukhy aakarshan, 'the fair's main attraction') is a very common phrase at fairs (मेला, melaa) and festivals.
Sample Sentences:
ताजमहल आगरा का मुख्य आकर्षण है। The Taj Mahal is Agra's main attraction.

Sense 3 : attraction (physical force, e.g. gravitational or magnetic)
noun, masculine Appears in science contexts like गुरुत्वाकर्षण (gurutvaakarshan, 'gravitational attraction'), where आकर्षण (aakarshan) is the base word combined with गुरुत्व (gurutv, 'gravity/heaviness').
Synonyms: खिंचाव (khinchaav)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the sense taught in school science classes -- चुंबकीय आकर्षण (chumbkiiy aakarshan, 'magnetic attraction') is a standard textbook phrase, so learners with a science background may recognize this sense first.

(prithvii kaa gurutvaakarshan har chiiz ko apnii or khiinchtaa hai।)
