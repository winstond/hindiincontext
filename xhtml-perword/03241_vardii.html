वरदी (vardii)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'uniform' -- the clothing worn by police, soldiers, and other officials.
Sense 1 : uniform (official dress)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: यूनिफ़ॉर्म (yooniform, common everyday alternative)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday speech, वरदी (vardii) carries a strong association with authority and respect -- a policeman is often referred to by his वरदी (vardii) rather than his rank, as in वरदी वाला (vardii vaalaa, 'the man in uniform'). Putting on the वरदी (vardii) is culturally loaded with a sense of duty and pride, often invoked in patriotic or dramatic film dialogue about soldiers and police.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse वरदी (vardii) with सूट (soot) or ड्रेस (dres) -- वरदी (vardii) specifically implies an official/uniformed role (police, army, guard, pilot, etc.), not just any matching outfit. 2) The spelling is often mixed up with वर्दी (vardii) -- both are used, but वर्दी (vardii) is more common in print; they mean exactly the same thing.
