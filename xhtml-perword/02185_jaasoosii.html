जासूसी (jaasoosii)

noun/adjective Primarily a noun meaning 'spying/espionage/detective work,' also used as an adjective meaning 'related to spying/detective' (जासूसी उपन्यास (jaasoosii upanyaas, 'detective novel')). Related to जासूस (jaasoos, 'spy/detective').
Sense 1 : spying, espionage, detective work
noun, feminine Commonly paired with करना (karnaa) -- जासूसी करना (jaasoosii karnaa, 'to spy/to do detective work') is the standard verb collocation.
Synonyms: जासूसी काम (jaasoosii kaam, descriptive phrase), गुप्तचरी (guptachrii, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi kids grow up on जासूसी उपन्यास (jaasoosii upanyaas, 'detective novels') -- the pulpy detective-fiction genre (think Hindi versions of Sherlock Holmes-style stories) was hugely popular for decades and jासूसी (jaasoosii) still carries that slightly thrilling, mystery-novel flavor even in ordinary conversation about nosy neighbors or office gossip.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat जासूसी (jaasoosii) itself as a verb; it is a noun and needs करना (karnaa) to become an action -- जासूसी करना (jaasoosii karnaa), not a bare conjugated जासूसी (jaasoosii). 2) जासूसी (jaasoosii) takes की (kii) when specifying whose spying it is or on whom, e.g. उसकी जासूसी (uskii jaasoosii, 'spying on him/her'), which can feel backwards to English speakers who'd expect 'on.'

Sense 2 : spy-related, detective (attributive)
adjective Used attributively before a noun, e.g. जासूसी कहानी (jaasoosii kahaanii, 'detective story'), जासूसी फिल्म (jaasoosii philm, 'spy film').
Synonyms: गुप्तचर (guptchar, formal, used attributively)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the noun sense used to modify another noun -- Hindi often does this rather than deriving a separate adjective, so जासूसी (jaasoosii) shifts smoothly between 'spying' (the activity) and 'spy-' (the descriptor) depending on position in the sentence.
