माँगना (maangnaa)

verb A single-sense transitive verb meaning 'to ask for, request, demand.' It also appears as the base of common compound-verb and idiomatic constructions, noted below.
Sense 1 : to ask for, request, demand
verb, transitive Takes the thing requested as direct object and the person asked from marked with से (se): उससे पैसे माँगना (usse paise maangnaa, 'to ask him/her for money').
Synonyms: याचना करना (yaachnaa karnaa, formal/literary), दरख्वास्त करना (darakhvaast karnaa, formal, used for formal requests/petitions)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi life this verb covers everything from a child asking for pocket money (जेब खर्च माँगना, jeb kharch maangnaa, 'to ask for pocket money') to a beggar at a traffic signal asking for change, so tone and context matter a lot -- the same verb spans polite requests and outright begging. It also shows up in the common idiom माफ़ी माँगना (maafii maangnaa, 'to ask for forgiveness, apologize'), which every learner should know early.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the person being asked with को (ko) instead of से (se) -- माँगना (maangnaa) takes से (se) for the source of the request, e.g. माँ से पैसे माँगे (maan se paise maange, 'asked mother for money'), not माँ को। 2) Do not confuse with मागना/मंगाना (mangaanaa) -- मंगाना (mangaanaa) is the causative, meaning 'to have something sent for / order,' a distinct lemma. 3) माँगना (maangnaa) is a regular transitive verb, so ने (ne) marking on the subject in the perfective is expected and not an exception -- learners sometimes forget it precisely because the verb feels 'soft' or requestive in meaning.
