बाल-बच्चे (baal-bachche)

noun A fixed plural compound meaning 'wife and children' or 'family/dependents' collectively -- always used in the plural, never referring to a single child.
Sense 1 : one's wife and children; family/dependents
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: परिवार (parivaar, broader: whole family incl. parents), बीवी-बच्चे (biivii-bachche, colloquial, emphasizes wife specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a distinctly 'family man' flavor -- you'll hear it when someone talks about the burden or joy of responsibility, as in अब तो बाल-बच्चों की जिम्मेदारी है (ab to baal-bachchon kii jimmedaarii hai, 'now I have the responsibility of a wife and kids'). It's warm and slightly old-fashioned, the kind of phrase an uncle at a family gathering might use about himself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use बाल-बच्चे (baal-bachche) to mean just 'children' -- it specifically evokes the whole domestic unit a person is responsible for, typically including a spouse. 2) It is grammatically always plural; there is no meaningful singular form to fall back on.

अब तो बाल-बच्चों की जिम्मेदारी है। Now I have the responsibility of a wife and kids.
(ab to baal-bachchon kii jimmedaarii hai।)
