नब्बे (nabbe)

numeral The number ninety (90); a straightforward cardinal numeral with one core meaning.
Sense 1 : the cardinal number ninety (90)
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear नब्बे (nabbe) constantly in everyday contexts like ages ('वह नब्बे साल के हैं', vah nabbe saal ke hain, 'he is ninety years old'), prices, exam percentages (नब्बे प्रतिशत, nabbe pratishat, 'ninety percent'), and phone numbers. Hindi speakers in Delhi-NCR often use नब्बे के दशक (nabbe ke dashak) to nostalgically refer to 'the nineties' as a cultural decade, much like English speakers do.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse नब्बे (nabbe, 'ninety') with नौ (nau, 'nine') or उन्नीस (unniis, 'nineteen') since Hindi numerals from 1-100 are not built as transparently as English ones and each decade has to be memorized somewhat separately. 2) Like other Hindi numbers, नब्बे (nabbe) does not change form for gender or case when counting nouns, so learners should not try to inflect it the way they might inflect an adjective.
