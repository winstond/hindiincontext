दावे के साथ कहना (daave ke saath kahnaa)

verb A compound verb phrase meaning 'to say something with confidence/certainty' -- literally 'to say with a claim.' Built from दावा (daavaa, 'claim') in its oblique form दावे (daave) plus the postposition के साथ (ke saath, 'with') and कहना (kahnaa, 'to say').
Sense 1 : to state something confidently, to assert with certainty
verb, transitive Takes a following clause (often with कि, ki) as the content of what is being asserted, e.g. दावे के साथ कहना कि... (daave ke saath kahnaa ki, 'to assert with confidence that...').
Synonyms: यकीन से कहना (yakiin se kahnaa), विश्वास से कहना (vishvaas se kahnaa), पूरे भरोसे से कहना (poore bharose se kahnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase is common in everyday arguments and debates when someone wants to underline that they are not guessing -- you'll hear it in news discussions and casual chats alike, as in मैं दावे के साथ कह सकता हूँ (main daave ke saath kah saktaa hoon, 'I can say this with confidence'). It pairs naturally with सकना (saknaa) to express confident ability to assert something.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop के (ke) and say दावा साथ कहना (daavaa saath kahnaa), which is wrong -- दावा (daavaa) must appear in its oblique form दावे (daave) before के साथ (ke saath). 2) This phrase describes the *manner* of speaking (confidently), not a formal legal claim -- learners familiar with दावा (daavaa) as 'lawsuit/claim' sometimes overthink the register, but in this phrase it just means firm personal conviction.
Sample Sentences:
मैं दावे के साथ कह सकता हूँ कि यह काम समय पर पूरा होगा। I can say with confidence that this work will be finished on time.
(main daave ke saath kah saktaa hoon ki yah kaam samay par pooraa hogaa।)
