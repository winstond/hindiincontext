आधार (aadhaar)

noun A noun with a literal sense of 'base/foundation' and a common extended sense of 'basis, grounds' for an argument, claim, or decision; also the name of India's biometric ID system.
Sense 1 : basis, grounds, foundation (abstract)
noun, masculine Very common in the phrase इस आधार पर (is aadhaar par, 'on this basis') and के आधार पर (ke aadhaar par, 'on the basis of'), which functions almost like a compound postposition.
Synonyms: बुनियाद (buniyaad), नींव (niinv, more physical/literal), आश्रय (aashray, support, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear के आधार पर (ke aadhaar par) constantly in news reports and official language -- 'रिपोर्ट के आधार पर' (riport ke aadhaar par, 'on the basis of the report') is classic bureaucratic/journalistic Hindi. It has a slightly more formal, written-register feel than बुनियाद (buniyaad), though both are widely understood.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the postposition पर (par) after आधार (aadhaar) when meaning 'on the basis of' -- you need के आधार पर (ke aadhaar par), not just आधार से (aadhaar se) or आधार में (aadhaar men). 2) Confusing आधार (aadhaar) with कारण (kaaran, 'reason/cause') -- आधार (aadhaar) is the grounds or foundation for a claim or decision, while कारण (kaaran) is the cause of an event.

Sense 2 : physical base, foundation, support
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नींव (niinv), बुनियाद (buniyaad), पाया (paayaa, base/pedestal of a structure, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the literal, physical sense -- the आधार (aadhaar) of a pillar or statue, for instance. In everyday speech, this concrete sense is used less often than the abstract 'basis' sense above, but it is the root image the abstract sense grows from.

Sense 3 : Aadhaar (India's biometric identity card/number)
noun, masculine Used as a proper noun in this sense; often heard as आधार कार्ड (aadhaar kaard) or just आधार (aadhaar) in casual speech about paperwork and identity verification.
Synonyms: आधार कार्ड (aadhaar kaard, full form, very common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is now one of the most-heard uses of the word in daily life across India -- banks, SIM cards, school admissions, everything asks for your आधार (aadhaar). The name was chosen precisely because it plays on the 'foundation/basis' sense: it is meant to be the base document of your identity.
