और कुछ (aur kuchh)

pronoun An indefinite pronoun phrase meaning 'something else, anything more'; also used as a stand-alone reply asking or offering 'anything more?'.
Sense 1 : something/anything additional
pronoun Functions as an indefinite pronoun standing in for an unnamed additional thing; often paired with a negative to mean 'nothing else' (और कुछ नहीं (aur kuchh nahiin, 'nothing else')).
Synonyms: कुछ और (kuchh aur, word-order variant, more common in speech)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in shops and restaurants: a shopkeeper finishing your order asks और कुछ? (aur kuchh?, 'anything else?') -- a short, everyday exchange every learner should have ready.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often fix the word order as और कुछ (aur kuchh) when native speakers just as often flip it to कुछ और (kuchh aur) -- both are fine, but कुछ और (kuchh aur) is the more frequent order in casual speech. 2) Learners forget that negating the phrase (और कुछ नहीं (aur kuchh nahiin)) gives 'nothing else,' not 'not something else,' which can trip up literal translation.
