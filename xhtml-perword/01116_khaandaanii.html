ख़ानदानी (khaandaanii)

adjective An adjective built from ख़ानदान (khaandaan, 'family/lineage') plus the -ई suffix; covers both 'belonging to a family, ancestral' and the extended sense of 'well-bred, of good/noble stock'.
Sense 1 : belonging to one's family, ancestral, hereditary
adjective
Synonyms: पैतृक (paitrik, formal), वंशानुगत (vanshaanugat, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in phrases like ख़ानदानी मकान (khaandaanii makaan, 'ancestral house') or ख़ानदानी धंधा (khaandaanii dhandhaa, 'family trade/business') -- it's the natural word Delhi families use when talking about a shop, recipe, or house that's been passed down for generations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this where पैतृक (paitrik) is expected in formal/legal contexts (e.g. property documents); ख़ानदानी (khaandaanii) is the everyday spoken equivalent.

Sense 2 : well-bred, of good/noble lineage, classy by birth
adjective
Synonyms: कुलीन (kuliin, literary), शरीफ़ (shariif, different emphasis: decent/respectable rather than lineage)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Often used approvingly of manners or taste -- ख़ानदानी तहज़ीब (khaandaanii tahziib, 'refined, family-bred etiquette') is a classic compliment in Delhi's old-city Urdu-inflected speech, evoking nawaabi (navaabii) gentility.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense carries a value judgment about breeding/status, not just origin -- calling a person ख़ानदानी (khaandaanii) implies their good manners or class come from their family background, similar to saying someone has 'good breeding' in English.
