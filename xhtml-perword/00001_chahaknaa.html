चहकना (chahaknaa)

verb One core meaning -- to chirp or twitter, used literally for birds and figuratively for a person speaking in a bright, cheerful, animated way.
Sense 1 : to chirp, to twitter (birds)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चहचहाना (chahchahaanaa, birds twittering together)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word for the cheerful racket of sparrows and mynahs on a Delhi balcony at sunrise -- चिड़िया चहक रही है (chidiyaa chahak rahii hai, 'the bird is chirping') is the classic morning-scene sentence in Hindi textbooks and songs alike.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : to speak or laugh in a bright, cheerful, lively way (of a person)
verb, intransitive Used almost always of someone in an upbeat, playful mood -- a happy child, a cheerful bride, a lively friend -- carrying the same bird-like brightness as the literal sense.
Synonyms: खिलखिलाना (khilkhilaanaa, leans more toward bright laughter than speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this about a child home from school or a girl excited about a new outfit -- वो सुबह से चहक रही है (vo subah se chahak rahii hai, 'she has been chirping cheerfully since morning') -- the image is literally a bird-like brightness in someone's voice and mood.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not reach for चहकना (chahaknaa) for ordinary excited talking or gossip -- that is बकबक करना (bakbak karnaa) or बतियाना (batiyaanaa); चहकना (chahaknaa) specifically carries a note of lightness and joy, not just volume or speed.
