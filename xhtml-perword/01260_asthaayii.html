अस्थायी (asthaayii)

adjective A Sanskritic adjective meaning 'temporary, not permanent'; formed by adding the negative prefix अ (a) to स्थायी (sthaayii, 'permanent, stable').
Sense 1 : temporary, not permanent
adjective
Synonyms: कामचलाऊ (kaamachlaaoo, makeshift), क्षणिक (kshanik, momentary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in job and government contexts -- अस्थायी नौकरी (asthaayii naukrii, 'temporary job') versus स्थायी नौकरी (sthaayii naukrii, 'permanent job') is a huge deal in Indian employment talk, since 'permanent' government jobs come with job security that everyone's parents want for them. You'll also hear it for makeshift arrangements, like an अस्थायी पुल (asthaayii pul, 'temporary bridge') put up during monsoon floods.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with अस्थिर (asthir, 'unstable, shaky') because of the similar-sounding start -- अस्थायी (asthaayii) is about duration (temporary vs permanent), while अस्थिर (asthir) is about steadiness (stable vs wobbly/volatile). 2) As an invariant adjective, it does not change form before masculine or feminine nouns -- अस्थायी नौकरी (asthaayii naukrii, 'temporary job') and अस्थायी घर (asthaayii ghar, 'temporary house') both use the same form.
