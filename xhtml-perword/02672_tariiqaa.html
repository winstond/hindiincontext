तरीक़ा (tariiqaa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning 'way' or 'method' -- the manner in which something is done.
Sense 1 : way, method, manner of doing something
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तरीका (tariikaa, variant form without nuqta), उपाय (upaay), ढंग (dhang), विधि (vidhi, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word constantly in practical, problem-solving talk -- कोई तरीका बताओ (koii tariikaa bataao, 'tell me some way/method') is what you say to a friend, a shopkeeper, or a mechanic when you want a solution, not a lecture. It has a nuqta on the ज़ (z) sound from its Persian/Urdu origin, though in fast casual speech many speakers pronounce it without the nuqta, and both तरीका (tariikaa) and तरीक़ा (tariiqaa) are accepted as the same word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often skip the से (se) marker when describing 'the way of doing' something, saying किस तरीका (kis tariikaa) instead of the correct किस तरीके से (kis tariike se, 'in what way'), since तरीका (tariikaa) must go into its oblique form तरीके (tariike) before से (se). 2) इस तरह (is tarah) and इस तरीके से (is tariike se) are near-synonyms in casual speech, but learners sometimes treat तरह (tarah) and तरीका (tariikaa) as fully interchangeable in every construction, when तरीका (tariikaa) leans more toward a concrete method or technique while तरह (tarah) leans toward manner or type in general.
Sample Sentences:
यार, कोई तरीका बताओ, ये लॉक नहीं खुल रहा। Dude, tell me some way, this lock isn't opening.
(yaar, koii tariikaa bataao, ye lok nahiin khul rahaa।)
