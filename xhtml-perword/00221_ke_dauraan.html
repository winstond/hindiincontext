के दौरान (ke dauraan)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'during'; it always follows a noun in the oblique case and marks the time-span within which something happens.
Sense 1 : during, in the course of
postposition Governs the oblique case of the preceding noun, just like other compound postpositions (e.g. के लिए (ke lie), के बारे में (ke baare men)): यात्रा के दौरान (yaatraa ke dauraan, 'during the journey').
Synonyms: के बीच (ke biich, meaning scope: 'in the midst of', not fully interchangeable), के समय (ke samay, 'at the time of')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in news reports and formal announcements -- बारिश के दौरान (baarish ke dauraan, 'during the rain'), बैठक के दौरान (baithak ke dauraan, 'during the meeting') -- it has a slightly more formal, reportorial flavor than casual speech, where people often just say जब (jab, 'when') plus a clause instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use के दौरान (ke dauraan) with a clause the way English uses 'while'; it only takes a noun phrase, not a full verb clause -- for 'while I was traveling' Hindi instead uses a construction like यात्रा करते हुए (yaatraa karte hue, 'while traveling'), not के दौरान (ke dauraan) plus a clause. 2) Some learners drop दौरान (dauraan) and just say के बीच (ke biich), which shifts the meaning toward 'amid/between' rather than a clean time-span 'during.'
Sample Sentences:
बारिश के दौरान सड़कों पर पानी भर जाता है। During the rain, the roads get filled with water.
(baarish ke dauraan sadkon par paanii bhar jaataa hai।)
