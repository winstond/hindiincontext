इक्यानवे (ikyaanve)

numeral The number ninety-one (ikyaanve), used for counting and in numeral expressions.
Sense 1 : the number 91
numeral
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, इक्यानवे (ikyaanve) is one of those forms that even native speakers learn as a fixed chunk in school rather than by deriving it from नब्बे (nabbe, ninety) and एक (ek, one). You will meet it mostly in ages, prices, exam scores, and phone numbers rather than in everyday conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 1 to 99 are each their own irregular word rather than a predictable compound of tens and units, so इक्यानवे (ikyaanve) must simply be memorized -- it does not decompose neatly the way English 'ninety-one' does.

(mere daadaa jii ikyaanve saal ke hain।)
