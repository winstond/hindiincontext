तारीफ़ (taariif)

noun A single core meaning: praise or compliment, most often used with the verb करना (karnaa) 'to do/give praise.'
Sense 1 : praise, compliment
noun, feminine Almost always paired with करना (karnaa) 'to praise' as तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa, 'to praise'); the person praised is marked with की (kii).
Synonyms: प्रशंसा (prashansaa), सराहना (saraahnaa), स्तुति (stuti, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: तारीफ़ (taariif) is a lovely word to know because Indians give and receive compliments with a certain ritual flourish -- someone might say वाह, क्या तारीफ़ करूँ! (vaah, kyaa taariif karoon, 'wow, what praise can I even give!') when something is truly excellent. It's a Persian-origin word, very much at home in everyday Hindi-Urdu speech across Delhi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say तारीफ़ बोलना (taariif bolnaa) by analogy with 'saying a compliment' -- the natural verb is always करना (karnaa), as in उसकी तारीफ़ करना (uskii taariif karnaa, 'to praise him/her'), not बोलना (bolnaa). 2) The object of praise takes की (kii), not को (ko): मेरी तारीफ़ की (merii taariif kii, 'praised me'), not मुझे तारीफ़ की.
Sample Sentences:
सबने उसके काम की तारीफ़ की। Everyone praised his work.
(sabne uske kaam kii taariif kii।)
