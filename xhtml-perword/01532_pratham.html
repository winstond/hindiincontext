प्रथम (pratham)

adjective A formal, literary word for 'first', used mainly in ordinal numbering, titles, and set phrases rather than everyday counting.
Sense 1 : first (ordinal)
adjective
Synonyms: पहला (pahlaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see प्रथम (pratham) on report cards, medal podiums, and government notices -- प्रथम स्थान (pratham sthaan, 'first place') is the standard phrase for a winner's rank. In casual conversation about daily life, though, a Delhi household will almost always say पहला (pahlaa) instead -- 'first' in the sense of first cup of tea or first day of the week.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for प्रथम (pratham) in everyday speech where पहला (pahlaa) is the natural choice -- प्रथम (pratham) belongs to formal writing, exam results, rankings, and official titles like प्रथम श्रेणी (pratham shrenii, 'first class'). 2) Unlike पहला (pahlaa), which inflects for gender and number (पहला/पहली/पहले, pahlaa/pahlii/pahle), प्रथम (pratham) stays the same form regardless of the noun it describes.
