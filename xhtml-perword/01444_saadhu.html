साधु (saadhu)

noun/adjective Primarily a noun for a Hindu holy man or ascetic; it also works as an adjective meaning 'virtuous' or 'good' (as in the classic exclamation साधु साधु! saadhu saadhu).
Sense 1 : holy man, ascetic, sadhu
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संत (sant), महात्मा (mahaatmaa), बाबा (baabaa, colloquial), सन्यासी (sanyaasii, stresses renunciation specifically)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see साधु (saadhu) figures at temples, on riverbanks, and especially at the Kumbh Mela (kumbh melaa), often recognizable by ash-smeared skin, matted hair, and saffron cloth. The word carries real respect in Hindu culture, but Delhi speakers also use it loosely and half-jokingly for anyone who seems detached from worldly concerns, as in 'वो तो साधु आदमी है' (vo to saadhu aadmii hai, 'he's such a saintly/unworldly guy').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use साधु (saadhu) and संत (sant) as if perfectly interchangeable; साधु (saadhu) leans toward the wandering, ochre-robed ascetic image, while संत (sant) can also describe a revered poet-saint (like कबीर kabiir) who was not necessarily a renunciate wanderer. 2) बाबा (baabaa) is the everyday spoken word people actually use when pointing one out on the street; साधु (saadhu) is what you'd write or say more formally.

Sense 2 : virtuous, good, proper (esp. in the fixed exclamation)
adjective Almost always encountered today in the doubled exclamation साधु साधु (saadhu saadhu), an approving 'well done, well done' uttered after a wise or virtuous act or saying -- this use is closer to a fixed interjection than a productive adjective in modern speech.
Synonyms: अच्छा (achchhaa), उत्तम (uttam, formal/literary), भला (bhalaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: साधु साधु (saadhu saadhu) is the classic response you hear in religious storytelling or discourse (कथा kathaa) when a guru or wise figure says something profound -- the listeners murmur it back as a mark of approval, much like 'amen' or 'well said' in English religious contexts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners rarely try to inflect or use this adjective sense productively (e.g., साधु काम saadhu kaam, 'a virtuous deed') since it survives mainly in the fixed exclamation साधु साधु (saadhu saadhu) rather than everyday descriptive use -- reaching for अच्छा (achchhaa) or भला (bhalaa) instead is more natural in ordinary sentences.
