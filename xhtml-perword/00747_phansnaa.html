फँसना (phansnaa)

verb Basic sense is physical: to get caught, trapped, or stuck (in a net, in mud, in a door). From there it extends naturally to being caught in a difficult situation, and to getting entangled emotionally or in a scam/trap.
Sense 1 : to get caught in a difficult or troublesome situation
verb, intransitive Commonly used with में (men) marking the situation: झगड़े में फँसना (jhagde men phansnaa, 'to get caught up in a quarrel').
Synonyms: उलझना (ulajhnaa), मुसीबत में पड़ना (musiibat men padnaa, phrase meaning 'to fall into trouble')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Delhi's traffic jams are practically the textbook example every local reaches for: ट्रैफिक में फँस गया (traiphik men phans gayaa) is something you will hear constantly as an excuse for being late.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is often paired with जाना (jaanaa) as a compound -- फँस जाना (phans jaanaa) -- to emphasize the suddenness of getting trapped, e.g. मैं ट्रैफिक में फँस गया (main traiphik men phans gayaa, 'I got stuck in traffic'). Learners who only know the plain verb miss how natural this compound sounds in speech.
Sample Sentences:
मैं ट्रैफिक में फँस गया, इसलिए देर हो गई। I got stuck in traffic, that's why I'm late.
(main traiphik men phans gayaa, islie der ho gaii।)

Sense 2 : to get physically caught or stuck
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: अटकना (ataknaa), उलझना (ulajhnaa, often used for threads/hair getting tangled)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a kite string catching on a tree branch, or a scooter's wheel getting stuck in mud after monsoon rain -- these everyday Delhi scenes are exactly where फँसना (phansnaa) lives.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पकड़ना (pakadnaa) which means 'to catch (something)' -- फँसना (phansnaa) is intransitive and describes the thing itself getting caught, not an agent catching it. 2) The object/location is marked with में (men): दुपट्टा दरवाज़े में फँस गया (dupattaa darvaaze men phans gayaa, 'the scarf got caught in the door').
Sample Sentences:
मेरी पतंग पेड़ की डाल में फँस गई। My kite got caught in the tree branch.
(merii patang ped kii daal men phans gaii।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : to be duped, scammed, or emotionally entangled
verb, intransitive Frequently used reflexively about emotional entanglement, as in किसी के प्यार में फँसना (kisii ke pyaar men phansnaa, 'to fall into someone's love/get caught up in love with someone') -- carrying a slight connotation of being trapped rather than freely choosing.
Synonyms: ठगा जाना (thagaa jaanaa, passive construction, 'to be cheated'), जाल में आना (jaal men aanaa, 'to fall into a trap')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in warnings about online fraud -- ऑनलाइन ठगी में फँस गया (onlaain thagii men phans gayaa, 'got caught in an online scam') -- and also in filmi dialogue about love as a trap, where फँसना (phansnaa) casts romance as something you fall into rather than pursue.
Common Mistakes: 1) In the context of scams, फँसना (phansnaa) describes the victim's state ('I got scammed') while फँसाना (phansaanaa), the causative, describes the scammer's action ('he scammed me') -- learners often confuse which one takes the subject role.
Sample Sentences:
वह ऑनलाइन ठगी में फँस गया। He got caught in an online scam.
(vah onlaain thagii men phans gayaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
