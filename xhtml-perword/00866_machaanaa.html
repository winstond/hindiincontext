मचाना (machaanaa)

verb A transitive verb used almost exclusively with a small set of noise/commotion nouns (शोर (shor), हंगामा (hangaamaa), धूम (dhoom), तबाही (tabaahii)) as their object, meaning 'to kick up, create (a ruckus/fuss/noise)'. It has one core sense that extends across several stock collocations rather than several unrelated meanings.
Sense 1 : to create/kick up (noise, commotion, a fuss)
verb, transitive Almost always appears as [noun] + मचाना (machaanaa) -- शोर मचाना (shor machaanaa, 'to make noise'), हंगामा मचाना (hangaamaa machaanaa, 'to create an uproar'), धूम मचाना (dhoom machaanaa, 'to cause a sensation'). The verb rarely stands alone without one of these collocational objects.
Synonyms: करना (karnaa, generic 'to do/make', needs the same object nouns), फैलाना (phailaanaa, to spread, used for chaos/panic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb behind a whole family of everyday complaints and compliments: a strict parent might scold बच्चे शोर मचा रहे हैं (bachche shor machaa rahe hain, 'the kids are making a racket'), while a hit song or film is said to have धूम मचा दी (dhoom machaa dii, 'set the town abuzz'). Notice the intransitive partner मचना (machnaa) ('to break out, arise') -- शोर मचना (shor machnaa) describes noise arising on its own, while शोर मचाना (shor machaanaa) says someone actively caused it; keeping this transitive/intransitive pair straight is useful.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use मचाना (machaanaa) as a general-purpose 'to make', but it is not interchangeable with बनाना (banaanaa) or करना (karnaa) outside its fixed noise/commotion collocations -- you can't say 'खाना मचाना' for 'to make food'. 2) The subject of मचाना (machaanaa) is the one causing the commotion, so it takes ने (ne) in the past perfective like a normal transitive verb -- बच्चों ने शोर मचाया (bachchon ne shor machaayaa, 'the children made noise') -- learners sometimes drop ने (ne) by treating the sentence as if the noise were 'happening' rather than being caused.
Sample Sentences:
बच्चे कमरे में बहुत शोर मचा रहे हैं। The kids are making a huge racket in the room.
(bachche kamre men bahut shor machaa rahe hain।)
