दाख़िल (daakhil)

adjective/noun An Urdu-origin loanword meaning 'entered' or 'admitted' -- almost always found paired with the verb होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) in fixed expressions about entering a place or being enrolled/admitted somewhere.
Sense 1 : entered, admitted (into a place, institution, or record)
adjective Functions as an invariant predicate adjective, almost always followed by होना (honaa, 'to become/be') or करना (karnaa, 'to make/cause') to form the actual verb phrase -- दाख़िल होना (daakhil honaa, 'to enter/get admitted') and दाख़िल करना (daakhil karnaa, 'to admit/enroll someone').
Synonyms: प्रविष्ट (pravisht, literary), भर्ती (bhartii, used for hospital/school admission)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around school and college admission season -- दाख़िले शुरू हो गए हैं (daakhile shuroo ho gae hain, 'admissions have started') is a common notice-board and newspaper phrase in Delhi-NCR every spring. It also shows up in everyday contexts like किसी के घर में दाख़िल होना (kisii ke ghar men daakhil honaa, 'to enter someone's house'), so it is not purely bureaucratic vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to conjugate दाख़िल (daakhil) itself like a verb; it never changes form -- only होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) after it carries the tense/agreement. 2) दाख़िल होना (daakhil honaa) is intransitive (the person enters) while दाख़िल करना (daakhil karnaa) is transitive (someone admits/enrolls another person or thing) -- mixing these up is common. 3) The place entered is marked with में (men), not को: स्कूल में दाख़िल हुआ (skool men daakhil huaa, 'got admitted into the school').
Sample Sentences:
मेरा बेटा इस साल पहली क्लास में दाख़िल हुआ। My son got admitted to first class this year.
(meraa betaa is saal pahlii klaas men daakhil huaa।)

Sense 2 : admission, entry (as a formal/institutional event)
noun, masculine This nominal use overlaps heavily with the related noun दाख़िला (daakhilaa); दाख़िल (daakhil) itself is rarely used as a standalone noun in modern speech, which prefers दाख़िला (daakhilaa) for 'admission' as a thing/event.
Synonyms: दाख़िला (daakhilaa, the more common noun form), प्रवेश (pravesh)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Because दाख़िला (daakhilaa) has taken over the noun job so completely, this sense is really just the bridge explaining why the two words feel related -- most learners will end up using दाख़िला (daakhilaa) far more often than this one as a noun.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use दाख़िल (daakhil) where native speakers would say दाख़िला (daakhilaa) -- for 'I got my admission done,' natural speech is दाख़िला करा लिया (daakhilaa karaa liyaa), not दाख़िल करा लिया (daakhil karaa liyaa).

स्कूल में दाख़िल की प्रक्रिया अगले हफ़्ते शुरू होगी। The admission process for the school will start next week.
