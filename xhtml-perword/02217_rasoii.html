रसोई (rasoii)

noun A kitchen, the physical room in a home where food is cooked; also extends naturally to mean the cooking/cuisine itself. One core sense with a common figurative extension.
Sense 1 : kitchen (the room)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: रसोईघर (rasoiighar), किचन (kichan, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian homes, रसोई (rasoii) is traditionally considered a semi-sacred space -- many families still remove their shoes before entering, and older generations may have rules about who can cook there and when. You will often hear रसोई में जाना (rasoii men jaanaa, 'to go into the kitchen') used as shorthand for going to cook or prepare food, not just physically entering the room.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use रसोई (rasoii) and रसोईघर (rasoiighar) as if they were totally interchangeable in every context -- रसोईघर (rasoiighar) more explicitly emphasizes the room/structure, while रसोई (rasoii) is the everyday word used for both the room and the activity of cooking that happens in it.
Sample Sentences:
माँ रसोई में खाना बना रही हैं। Mom is cooking food in the kitchen.
(maan rasoii men khaanaa banaa rahii hain।)

Sense 2 : cooking, cuisine (the activity/style of food preparation)
noun, feminine Used when talking about the practice or style of cooking rather than the room, e.g. साउथ इंडियन रसोई (saauth indiyan rasoii, 'South Indian cuisine/cooking').
Synonyms: खाना बनाना (khaanaa banaanaa, verb phrase, cooking as an activity), पकवान (pakvaan, dishes/cuisine as items)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: When someone says रसोई का काम (rasoii kaa kaam, 'kitchen work'), they mean all the cooking-related labor -- chopping, cooking, cleaning up -- not just standing at the stove. This reflects how the word extends smoothly from the room itself to the whole domain of domestic cooking.
