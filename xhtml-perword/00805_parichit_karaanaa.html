परिचित कराना (parichit karaanaa)

verb A causative-flavored compound built from परिचित (parichit, 'acquainted') plus कराना (karaanaa, the causative of करना), meaning 'to introduce' (make someone acquainted with someone/something).
Sense 1 : to introduce (people to each other, or someone to a topic/place)
verb, transitive Takes से (se) for the person/thing being introduced: X को Y से परिचित कराना (X ko Y se parichit karaanaa, 'to introduce X to Y').
Synonyms: मिलवाना (milvaanaa, more common in casual speech for introducing people)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in slightly more formal settings -- a teacher introducing a new topic to students, an anchor introducing a guest, or a book's preface introducing an author to readers. For casually introducing your friend to another friend at a chai stall, मिलवाना (milvaanaa) feels more natural and warm.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मिलवाना (milvaanaa) in casual social introductions ('meet my friend') and save परिचित कराना (parichit karaanaa) for more formal or written contexts, like introducing a topic, guest speaker, or new concept. 2) The person being introduced is marked with को (ko) and what/whom they're being introduced to is marked with से (se) -- mixing these up is a common error. 3) Because यह करना (karnaa) part is a causative, learners sometimes forget the causative agreement rules that कराना (karaanaa) verbs follow with ने (ne).
Sample Sentences:
आज मैं आपको एक नए विषय से परिचित कराऊँगा। Today I will introduce you to a new topic.
(aaj main aapko ek nae vishay se parichit karaaoongaa।)
