अक्तूबर (aktoobar)

noun The month of October (aktoobar) in the Gregorian calendar used alongside the traditional Hindu calendar in everyday Indian life.
Sense 1 : October (the calendar month)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this spelled both अक्तूबर (aktoobar) and ऑक्टोबर (oktobar) -- the former is the more Hindi-ized rendering taught in schools and used in print, while the latter closely mirrors the English pronunciation and shows up often in spoken city Hindi. Both are completely normal and understood everywhere in Delhi-NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect it like a native noun for the oblique case (e.g. before postpositions), but month names like अक्तूबर (aktoobar) stay invariant: अक्तूबर में (aktoobar men, 'in October'), not a changed form.
