तबलची (tabalchii)

noun One sense: a person who plays the tabla (tablaa), the paired hand-drums central to Hindustani music. The ending -ची (chii) is a Persian-derived agentive suffix meaning 'one who does/handles' -- you will see it recycled on other instrument or trade names.
Sense 1 : tabla player
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तबला वादक (tablaa vaadak, more formal/written)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear तबलची (tabalchii) used warmly and a little informally, often about the accompanist sitting beside a singer or sitar player at a concert -- the word carries a sense of craft and long apprenticeship, since tabla playing is traditionally learned through years with a guru (guru) in a gharana (gharaanaa) tradition. The everyday alternative तबला बजानेवाला (tablaa bajaanevaalaa, 'one who plays the tabla') uses the far more common and productive -वाला (vaalaa) construction, which most learners should lean on for new formations rather than trying to coin their own -ची (chii) words.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume -ची (chii) is a general 'person' suffix they can attach freely to any noun; in practice it survives only on a handful of older trade/instrument words (like तबलची (tabalchii) or बाजीगर-type words), so it is not productive the way -वाला (vaalaa) is. 2) तबलची (tabalchii) refers specifically to the player, not the drum itself -- do not confuse with तबला (tablaa).
