संपर्क करना (sampark karnaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to get in touch' or 'to contact someone', built from the noun संपर्क (sampark, 'contact') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do').
Sense 1 : to contact, to get in touch with
verb, transitive Takes से (se) for the person contacted, e.g. मुझसे संपर्क करें (mujhse sampark karen, 'contact me') -- not को (ko), which is the common learner error.
Synonyms: मिलना (milnaa, to meet/reach out to), बात करना (baat karnaa, to talk to), जुड़ना (judnaa, to connect)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you will see on every Indian company website, government form, and business card: 'अधिक जानकारी के लिए संपर्क करें' (adhik jaankaarii ke lie sampark karen, 'contact us for more information'). It has a slightly formal, official flavor compared to just calling someone up or saying मिलना (milnaa) or बात करना (baat karnaa) in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mark the contacted person with को (ko) by analogy with other verbs, but this word requires से (se): कृपया मुझसे संपर्क करें (kripyaa mujhse sampark karen, 'please contact me'), not मुझको (mujhko). 2) As a transitive compound verb in the perfective, it takes ने-marking on the subject: उसने संपर्क किया (usne sampark kiyaa, 'he contacted'), which learners sometimes forget since संपर्क (sampark) feels like an abstract noun rather than a concrete object.
Sample Sentences:
अधिक जानकारी के लिए हमसे संपर्क करें। Contact us for more information.
(adhik jaankaarii ke lie hamse sampark karen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
कृपया इस नंबर पर संपर्क करें। Please contact us on this number.
(kripyaa is nambar par sampark karen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
मैंने कल ही उस कंपनी से संपर्क किया था। I had contacted that company just yesterday.
(mainne kal hii us kampnii se sampark kiyaa thaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
वह हर हफ्ते अपने ग्राहकों से संपर्क करता है। He contacts his clients every week.
(vah har haphte apne graahkon se sampark kartaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगर कोई समस्या हो तो तुरंत संपर्क करना। If there's any problem, get in touch immediately.
(agar koii samasyaa ho to turant sampark karnaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
अधिकारी अभी उस विभाग से संपर्क कर रहे हैं। The officials are contacting that department right now.
(adhikaarii abhii us vibhaag se sampark kar rahe hain।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
क्या तुमने अपने वकील से संपर्क किया? Did you contact your lawyer?
(kyaa tumne apne vakiil se sampark kiyaa?)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
उम्मीदवार से एक हफ्ते के अंदर संपर्क किया जाएगा। The candidate will be contacted within a week.
(ummiidvaar se ek haphte ke andar sampark kiyaa jaaegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive
मुझे लगता है कि तुम्हें सीधे प्रधानाचार्य से संपर्क करना चाहिए। I think you should contact the principal directly.
(mujhe lagtaa hai ki tumhen siidhe pradhaanaachaary se sampark karnaa chaahie।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
काश मैंने पहले ही दूतावास से संपर्क किया होता। If only I had contacted the embassy earlier.
(kaash mainne pahle hii dootaavaas se sampark kiyaa hotaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Perfective -- Voice: Active
यदि आपको सहायता चाहिए, तो हेल्पलाइन पर संपर्क करें। If you need help, contact the helpline.
(yadi aapko sahaaytaa chaahie, to helplaain par sampark karen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
