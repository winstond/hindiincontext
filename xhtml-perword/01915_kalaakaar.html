कलाकार (kalaakaar)

noun A single core sense: someone who practices an art -- an artist or performer, used broadly across visual, musical, and performing arts, and often extended figuratively to someone skilled or crafty in a non-artistic sense.
Sense 1 : artist, performer
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: कलाविद (kalaavid, literary), शिल्पकार (shilpkaar, more for craftsmen/sculptors), अभिनेता (abhinetaa, specifically an actor)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech, कलाकार (kalaakaar) is the go-to word when praising someone's performance at a wedding sangeet, a school function, or a TV show -- 'क्या कलाकार है!' (kyaa kalaakaar hai, 'what an artist he is') is common applause-talk. It pairs naturally with कला (kalaa, 'art'), the root noun from which it's formed.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume कलाकार (kalaakaar) means only 'painter,' but it covers any practitioner of an art -- musicians, dancers, actors, singers are all कलाकार (kalaakaar). 2) It's used for both amateurs and professionals; don't reserve it only for the famous.

Sense 2 : cunning or crafty person (figurative)
noun, masculine/feminine Used teasingly or with mild sarcasm to call out someone's clever maneuvering or excuse-making, not genuine artistry.
Synonyms: चालबाज़ (chaalbaaz, more clearly negative), उस्ताद (ustaad, can be admiring or teasing)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the classic Delhi jibe when a friend dodges a bill or spins a smooth excuse for being late -- 'क्या कलाकार निकला!' (kyaa kalaakaar niklaa, 'what a smooth operator he turned out to be'). It's the same word as the literal 'artist' sense, just turned ironic -- the skill has shifted from canvas to conning people.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative use can sound sarcastic or accusatory depending on tone -- 'बड़े कलाकार हो!' (bade kalaakaar ho, 'you're quite the operator!') is often said with a smile but can sting if said sharply, so learners should gauge context carefully.
