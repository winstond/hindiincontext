विचार (vichaar)

noun A thought or idea (noun); also carries a formal sense of consideration/deliberation, and shows up in the common phrase विचार करना (vichaar karnaa) meaning 'to think over, to consider.'
Sense 1 : an idea or thought
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ख़याल (khayaal), सोच (soch)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear विचार (vichaar) a lot on the news or in a debate-style conversation -- अपना विचार व्यक्त करना (apnaa vichaar vyakt karnaa, 'to express one's view') is a staple phrase in Indian civic and school life, right from essay-writing class onward.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सोच (soch) in every context, but विचार (vichaar) sounds a touch more formal or considered -- closer to 'a thought/idea' than 'thinking' as an activity. 2) मेरा विचार है (meraa vichaar hai, 'my thought/opinion is') is a common way to introduce an opinion in slightly formal speech or writing; learners sometimes default to मुझे लगता है (mujhe lagtaa hai) even when the more deliberate phrasing would fit better.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे एक अच्छा विचार आया है। I've had a good idea.
(mujhe ek achchhaa vichaar aayaa hai।)

Sense 2 : consideration, deliberation (as in the verb phrase to think over)
noun, masculine Almost always seen with करना (karnaa) in the compound विचार करना (vichaar karnaa), 'to think over/consider', or with के अधीन (ke adhiin) / में in bureaucratic phrasing like विचाराधीन (vichaaraadhiin, 'under consideration').
Synonyms: मनन (manan, literary), चिंतन (chintan, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense is the backbone of official and formal Hindi: सरकार इस मामले पर विचार कर रही है (sarkaar is maamle par vichaar kar rahii hai, 'the government is considering this matter') is classic news-anchor language, distinct from the everyday 'idea' sense above.
Common Mistakes: 1) विचार करना (vichaar karnaa) takes पर (par) or के बारे में (ke baare men) for the thing being considered -- e.g. इस पर विचार करें (is par vichaar karen, 'please consider this') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) here by analogy with other verbs.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार इस मामले पर विचार कर रही है। The government is considering this matter.
(sarkaar is maamle par vichaar kar rahii hai।)
