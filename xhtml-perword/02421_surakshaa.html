सुरक्षा (surakshaa)

noun A single core meaning -- safety, security, or protection -- used across everyday, institutional, and formal contexts. Feminine noun throughout.
Sense 1 : safety, security, protection
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बचाव (bachaav, protection/defense), हिफ़ाज़त (hifaazat, everyday, protective care), संरक्षण (sanrakshan, formal, conservation/patronage sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll see सुरक्षा (surakshaa) everywhere in Delhi-NCR public life -- airport signs say सुरक्षा जांच (surakshaa jaanch, 'security check'), housing societies talk about सुरक्षा गार्ड (surakshaa gaard), and news broadcasts open with राष्ट्रीय सुरक्षा (raashtriiy surakshaa, 'national security'). It sits at a slightly more formal register than the warmer, more personal हिफ़ाज़त (hifaazat), which you'd more likely hear from a mother worrying about her child.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say सुरक्षा करना (surakshaa karnaa) when they mean 'to protect,' but the natural verb pairing is सुरक्षा प्रदान करना (surakshaa pradaan karnaa, 'to provide security') or बचाना (bachaanaa, 'to save/protect') for the action itself -- सुरक्षा (surakshaa) itself is the state or condition, not the act. 2) In the sense of 'guard/security staff,' सुरक्षा (surakshaa) is used as a collective noun (सुरक्षा आ गई (surakshaa aa gaii, 'security has arrived')) rather than referring to one guard -- for a single person, use सुरक्षा गार्ड (surakshaa gaard).
