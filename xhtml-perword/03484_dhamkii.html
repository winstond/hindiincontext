धमकी (dhamkii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'threat' -- used with verbs like देना (denaa, to give) and मिलना (milnaa, to receive) to talk about threatening someone or being threatened.
Sense 1 : threat, intimidation
noun, feminine Almost always appears with देना (denaa, to give) for 'to threaten (someone)' -- धमकी देना (dhamkii denaa) -- or with मिलना (milnaa) / मिलना (milnaa) for 'to receive a threat.' The person threatened is marked with को (ko): उसे धमकी दी (use dhamkii dii, 'threatened him/her').
Synonyms: चेतावनी (chetaavnii, warning, broader than धमकी), डर (dar, fear, not a direct synonym but used loosely)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in news reports and political drama -- जान से मारने की धमकी (jaan se maarne kii dhamkii, 'a death threat') is a very common phrase in Hindi media. It is closely related to the verb धमकाना (dhamkaanaa, to threaten/intimidate someone), which a learner will often meet around the same time.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say धमकाया (dhamkaayaa, scared/threatened -- from धमकाना) when they mean the noun धमकी (dhamkii); keep the verb धमकाना (dhamkaanaa) and the noun धमकी (dhamkii) distinct in your head, they are related but not interchangeable. 2) The verb paired with धमकी is देना (denaa, to give), not करना (karnaa) -- 'धमकी करना' (dhamkii karnaa) is not natural, say धमकी देना (dhamkii denaa).
Sample Sentences:
पुलिस को जान से मारने की धमकी मिली है। The police have received a death threat.
(pulis ko jaan se maarne kii dhamkii milii hai।)
