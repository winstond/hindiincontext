हमला (hamlaa)

noun हमला (hamlaa) is a masculine noun meaning 'attack' or 'assault' -- physical, military, or verbal/figurative. It pairs almost always with the verb करना (karnaa) or बोलना (bolnaa).
Sense 1 : physical or military attack
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आक्रमण (aakrman, formal), चढ़ाई (chadhaaii, military campaign/invasion)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear हमला (hamlaa) constantly in Hindi news broadcasts -- आतंकी हमला (aatankii hamlaa, 'terrorist attack') is a near-daily headline phrase, so this word is worth learning early just for following the news.
Common Mistakes: 1) The standard verb pairing is हमला करना (hamlaa karnaa) 'to attack' or हमला बोलना (hamlaa bolnaa, 'to launch an attack') -- learners sometimes reach for आक्रमण करना (aakrman karnaa) instead, which is correct but sounds noticeably more formal/written, like 'to launch an offensive' versus just 'to attack.' 2) The target of the attack takes पर (par): हमला करना (hamlaa karnaa) is used with पर (par), e.g. शहर पर हमला (shahar par hamlaa, 'attack on the city'), not पर (par) replaced by को (ko).
Sample Sentences:

(kal raat shahar par hamlaa huaa।)

Sense 2 : verbal or figurative attack, criticism
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आलोचना (aalochnaa, criticism, narrower -- focuses on critique rather than aggression)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Indian political reporting loves this word: नेता का बयानी हमला (netaa kaa bayaanii hamlaa, 'the leader's verbal attack') or विपक्ष का हमला (vipaksh kaa hamlaa, 'the opposition's attack') show up constantly when politicians criticize each other sharply in speeches.
Common Mistakes: 1) In political and media contexts हमला (hamlaa) often means a sharp verbal attack rather than physical violence, as in किसी पर तीखा हमला (kisii par tiikhaa hamlaa, 'a sharp attack on someone') -- learners who only know the literal sense may miss this figurative use in news commentary and debates.
Sample Sentences:
विपक्ष के नेता ने सरकार पर तीखा हमला बोला। The opposition leader launched a sharp verbal attack on the government.
(vipaksh ke netaa ne sarkaar par tiikhaa hamlaa bolaa।)
