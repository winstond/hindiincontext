आराम-कुरसी (aaraam-kursii)

noun A compound noun meaning 'easy chair, armchair, recliner' -- literally 'comfort-chair,' combining आराम (aaraam, 'rest/comfort') with कुरसी (kursii, 'chair'). One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : easy chair, armchair (a chair designed for relaxing/reclining)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: आराम-कुर्सी (aaraam-kursii, variant spelling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture the classic scene on a verandah in Delhi or in a small-town courtyard: an elderly grandfather (दादा जी, daadaa jii) leaning back in his आराम-कुरसी (aaraam-kursii) with the day's newspaper, maybe a cane-and-wood recliner with a long, sloped armrest for the legs. It carries a strong association with leisurely retirement and unhurried afternoons, distinct from a dining chair or office chair.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the hyphen and write it as two separate words; both spellings with and without the hyphen are seen, but it functions as a single compound noun rather than 'a chair for rest' phrased freely. 2) Don't confuse with सोफ़ा (sofaa, 'sofa') -- an आराम-कुरसी (aaraam-kursii) is specifically a single reclining chair, often with a footrest, not a multi-seat couch.
