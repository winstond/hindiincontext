अर्थशास्त्र (arthshaastr)

noun A compound of अर्थ (arth, 'wealth/meaning') and शास्त्र (shaastr, 'science/discipline'), this word has one main sense: the academic subject of economics.
Sense 1 : economics (the academic discipline)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: The अर्थ (arth) here carries its older sense of 'wealth' or 'material gain' (one of the four classical purusharthas alongside धर्म, काम, and मोक्ष), so अर्थशास्त्र (arthshaastr) literally means 'the science of wealth.' Any Indian student who has gone through school will recognize this as a serious, textbook-heavy subject, often mentioned alongside राजनीति शास्त्र (raajniiti shaastr, 'political science') as a college major.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume अर्थशास्त्र (arthshaastr) refers to money or finance generally, but it specifically names the academic subject/discipline, like 'economics' as a field of study, not 'finances' or 'budget'. 2) The word is grammatically singular and does not pluralize the way English 'economics' can feel plural; treat it as a normal masculine noun.
