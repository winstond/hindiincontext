उनतालीस (untaaliis)

numeral The cardinal number 39 (thirty-nine) in Hindi's traditional counting system.
Sense 1 : the number thirty-nine
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like many Hindi numbers ending in nine, उनतालीस (untaaliis) historically means 'one less than forty,' echoing an old pattern (compare उनतीस, untiis, 'one less than thirty'). You will meet it mostly in practical contexts like ages, prices, or bus and train numbers rather than in conversation for its own sake.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numbers 1-99 are each an individually memorized word rather than built compositionally from tens and units, so उनतालीस (untaaliis) must simply be learned as its own item, not derived from तीस (tiis, thirty) plus नौ (nau, nine). 2) It is easy to confuse with the visually and phonetically similar numbers around it, such as उनतीस (untiis, twenty-nine) and उनचास (unchaas, forty-nine), all of which use the same 'one-less-than' pattern for the following decade.
