सूना (soonaa)

adjective An adjective describing emptiness or desolation, used for places, houses, or life situations that feel abandoned, quiet, or lacking someone/something.
Sense 1 : empty, deserted, desolate
adjective
Synonyms: खाली (khaalii), वीरान (viiraan), उजाड़ (ujaad)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word is often used for a house or a life that feels empty after someone has left or passed away -- घर सूना लगता है (ghar soonaa lagtaa hai, 'the house feels empty/desolate') is something you might hear from a mother whose children have moved away for work or studies, a very common reality in Delhi-NCR families. It pairs naturally with लगना (lagnaa) to describe this felt emptiness as something that happens to you, not a fact you simply state.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse सूना (soonaa, 'empty/desolate') with सुना (sunaa, 'heard', past participle of सुनना (sunnaa)) -- the spellings differ only in vowel length but the meanings are unrelated. 2) सूना (soonaa) carries an emotional weight of loneliness or loss that खाली (khaalii) (simply 'empty', like an empty glass) does not -- a सूना घर (soonaa ghar, 'a desolate/empty-feeling house') suggests someone is missing, not just that the rooms are unoccupied.

माँ के जाने के बाद घर सूना लगता है। After mother left, the house feels empty.
(maan ke jaane ke baad ghar soonaa lagtaa hai।)
