तक (tak)

postposition A postposition covering both 'until/up to' (time or place limits) and 'even' (emphatic inclusion). The two uses share one word but feel quite different in the sentence.
Sense 1 : up to / until (limit of time, place, or extent)
postposition Marks the noun it follows as a limit; the noun before तक (tak) takes the oblique case where applicable.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear से ... तक (se ... tak) constantly for giving ranges -- सुबह से शाम तक (subah se shaam tak, 'from morning to evening'), दिल्ली से मुंबई तक (dillii se mumbaii tak, 'from Delhi to Mumbai'). Think of तक (tak) as marking the far edge of whatever से (se) started.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for से (se) alone to mean 'until,' but से (se) marks the starting point, not the limit -- you need the pair से ... तक (se ... tak, 'from ... to/until') to express a full range. 2) With time expressions, तक (tak) can mean either 'by' (deadline) or 'until' (duration) depending on context -- शाम तक (shaam tak, 'by evening') versus रात तक (raat tak, 'until night') both use the same word, and learners often try to force a single translation.
Sample Sentences:
मैं सुबह से शाम तक दफ़्तर में रहता हूँ। I stay at the office from morning until evening.
(main subah se shaam tak daftar men rahtaa hoon।)

Sense 2 : even (emphatic inclusion)
postposition Attaches directly after the emphasized noun/pronoun to signal that even this extreme or unlikely case is included.
Synonyms: भी (bhii, less emphatic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the तक (tak) you hear in complaints and exasperated storytelling -- उसने फ़ोन तक नहीं किया (usne fon tak nahiin kiyaa, 'he didn't even call'). The word leans into disbelief, so it shows up a lot in gossip and venting among friends.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to भी (bhii) for 'even' and miss that तक (tak) carries a stronger, more surprised tone -- उसने मुझे देखा तक नहीं (usne mujhe dekhaa tak nahiin, 'he didn't even look at me') hits harder than using भी (bhii) there.
Sample Sentences:
