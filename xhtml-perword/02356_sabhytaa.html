सभ्यता (sabhytaa)

noun A noun meaning 'civilization' -- either the abstract concept/process of being civilized, or a specific historical civilization (सिन्धु घाटी सभ्यता (sindhu ghaatii sabhytaa, 'the Indus Valley Civilization')). Related to सभ्य (sabhy, 'civilized, polite').
Sense 1 : civilization (a historical/cultural entity)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll see in school textbooks and museum plaques -- सिन्धु घाटी सभ्यता (sindhu ghaatii sabhytaa, 'Indus Valley Civilization') is probably the single most common phrase students encounter it in. It carries a slightly academic, historical weight, so it shows up more in writing and formal speech than in casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for संस्कृति (sanskriti) instead -- सभ्यता (sabhytaa) refers to a whole civilization (a people, era, or society viewed historically, like मिस्र की सभ्यता (misr kii sabhytaa, 'Egyptian civilization')), while संस्कृति (sanskriti) refers to culture -- customs, arts, values -- which can belong to a single family or region without being a 'civilization.'

(sindhu ghaatii sabhytaa vishv kii sabse puraanii sabhytaaon men se ek hai।)

Sense 2 : civilization, being civilized (abstract quality/process)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: तहज़ीब (tahziib)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear elders use this in scolding-mode: यह सभ्यता नहीं है (yah sabhytaa nahiin hai, 'this is not civilized behavior') when someone behaves rudely -- it frames good manners as a marker of being 'civilized' rather than just polite, which is a bigger, more moralized claim than English 'manners' usually carries.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this abstract sense सभ्यता (sabhytaa) overlaps with तहज़ीब (tahziib), which leans more toward personal manners and etiquette (especially in Urdu-influenced speech), while सभ्यता (sabhytaa) keeps a broader, almost sociological flavor even when talking about an individual's behavior.
Sample Sentences:
यह सभ्यता नहीं है कि कोई बड़ों के सामने चिल्लाए। It's not civilized behavior to shout in front of elders.
(yah sabhytaa nahiin hai ki koii badon ke saamne chillaae।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
थोड़ी सभ्यता सीखो, ऐसे मुँह पर दरवाज़ा नहीं बंद करते। Learn some manners, you don't slam the door in someone's face like that.
(thodii sabhytaa siikho, aise munh par darvaazaa nahiin band karte।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
