यात्री (yaatrii)

noun A single core meaning: a person who travels -- 'traveler' or 'passenger', depending on context (tourist on a journey vs. commuter on a bus/train).
Sense 1 : traveler, passenger
noun, masculine/feminine
Synonyms: मुसाफ़िर (musaafir), सैलानी (sailaanii, tourist/wanderer sense), पर्यटक (parytak, tourist specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly at Indian railway stations: announcements begin with कृपया ध्यान दें, यात्रीगण (kripyaa dhyaan den, yaatriigan, 'Attention please, passengers') -- यात्री (yaatrii) is the standard, respectful word for anyone in transit, from a daily commuter to a pilgrim on a तीर्थयात्रा (tiirthyaatraa, 'pilgrimage'), which shares its root यात्रा (yaatraa, 'journey').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पर्यटक (parytak) by default, but यात्री (yaatrii) is the broader, more everyday word -- it covers a bus or train passenger just as easily as a pilgrim or tourist, whereas पर्यटक (parytak) is specifically a sightseeing tourist. 2) In formal announcements you will hear यात्रीगण (yaatriigan, 'passengers' as a collective address) -- learners sometimes try to pluralize यात्री (yaatrii) itself with -गण (gan) incorrectly attached to other nouns by analogy; this suffix is specific to a small set of formal collective address terms.
