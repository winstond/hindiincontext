बौछाड़ (bauchhaad)

noun Primarily a burst or spray of water/rain, extended figuratively to a sudden barrage of words, questions, or bullets.
Sense 1 : a sudden burst or spray of rain/water
noun, feminine
Synonyms: छींटा (chhiintaa), फुहार (phuhaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the sharp, wind-driven spray that hits your face when a monsoon gust sweeps rain sideways through a doorway or window -- that sudden gust-and-spray combo is exactly what बौछाड़ (bauchhaad) captures, more dramatic than the steady patter of बारिश (baarish).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with बारिश (baarish, 'rain' in general) -- बौछाड़ (bauchhaad) is a sudden, forceful gust or spray, not steady rainfall.
Sample Sentences:

(havaa ke saath baarish kii ek bauchhaad khidkii se andar aa gaii।)

Sense 2 : a sudden barrage (of words, bullets, questions)
noun, feminine Common in phrases like सवालों की बौछाड़ (savaalon kii bauchhaad, 'a barrage of questions') or गोलियों की बौछाड़ (goliyon kii bauchhaad, 'a hail of bullets'), following the pattern [noun]-की बौछाड़.
Synonyms: बरसात (barsaat, figurative use), झड़ी (jhadii, figurative use)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: News reports love this word for dramatic effect -- गोलियों की बौछाड़ (goliyon kii bauchhaad, 'a hail of bullets') or सवालों की बौछाड़ (savaalon kii bauchhaad, 'a barrage of questions') both borrow the imagery of rain hitting you all at once, relentless and hard to escape.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense always needs the की (kii) construction linking it to what is being showered -- बौछाड़ (bauchhaad) alone doesn't convey 'barrage of' without that noun-plus-की phrase.
