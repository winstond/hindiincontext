किफ़ायत (kifaayat)

noun A noun meaning thrift or economy in the sense of careful, non-wasteful use of money or resources; also appears in the common adjectival phrase किफ़ायत से (kifaayat se) meaning 'economically/sparingly.'
Sense 1 : thrift, economy, frugality
noun, feminine Most often used with से (se) as an adverbial phrase, किफ़ायत से (kifaayat se, 'economically, sparingly'), describing how something is done rather than as a bare object.
Synonyms: बचत (bachat), कंजूसी (kanjoosii, negative connotation, stinginess), संकोच (sankoch, rare in this sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word in everyday household contexts -- a mother telling her kids to use ghee किफ़ायत से (kifaayat se, 'sparingly') or a shopkeeper praising a customer's किफ़ायत (kifaayat) with money. It carries a respectable, almost virtuous tone, unlike the mildly negative कंजूसी (kanjoosii).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse किफ़ायत (kifaayat) with कंजूसी (kanjoosii); किफ़ायत (kifaayat) has a positive, sensible-saving connotation, while कंजूसी (kanjoosii) implies stinginess or being miserly. 2) The common phrase is किफ़ायत करना (kifaayat karnaa, 'to economize, to be thrifty'), not किफ़ायत होना (kifaayat honaa).
