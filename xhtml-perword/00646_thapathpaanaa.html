थपथपाना (thapathpaanaa)

verb A tactile, sound-imitative verb meaning to pat or tap gently and repeatedly -- on a back, shoulder, cheek, or drum-like surface. One core sense, with an easy extension to comforting or encouraging gestures.
Sense 1 : to pat, tap gently (with hand, repeatedly)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: थपकी देना (thapkii denaa, said especially of patting a child to sleep), सहलाना (sahlaanaa, more of a stroking motion than a pat)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word is onomatopoeic -- थप-थप (thap-thap) mimics the soft repeated sound of patting, much like Hindi's other reduplicated sound-words (खटखटाना (khatakhtaanaa, 'to knock'), टटोलना (tatolnaa, 'to feel around')). Patting someone's back (पीठ थपथपाना (piith thapathpaanaa)) is a very common gesture of encouragement or praise in India, roughly like an English pat on the back, and is used just as often figuratively as literally.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for थपथपाना (thapathpaanaa) for any touch, but it specifically implies a rhythmic, repeated patting motion, not a single touch or a stroke -- for the latter, सहलाना (sahlaanaa) or छूना (chhoonaa) fit better. 2) The object of the patting takes को (ko), as with most transitive verbs acting on a person: उसकी पीठ थपथपाई (uskii piith thapathpaaii, 'patted his/her back') is more natural than making 'back' the subject of focus -- Hindi tends to name the body part directly rather than saying 'patted him on the back.'

Sense 2 : to praise, encourage, commend (figurative extension of patting the back)
verb, transitive Almost always occurs as the fixed idiom पीठ थपथपाना (piith thapathpaanaa, 'to pat [someone's] back') -- the figurative sense rarely stands on the bare verb alone without पीठ (piith).
Synonyms: पीठ थपथपाना (piith thapathpaanaa, fixed idiom, 'to pat on the back'), सराहना (saraahnaa, more general word for 'to praise', not tied to the gesture)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as in English, 'to pat someone on the back' in Hindi doubles as literal gesture and as a stand-in for verbal praise or encouragement -- a teacher, coach, or boss 'patting the back' (पीठ थपथपाना (piith thapathpaanaa)) of a student or employee is a everyday image of approval in Hindi as much as in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may drop पीठ (piith) and just say थपथपाना (thapathpaanaa) expecting the praise meaning to carry through, but without पीठ (piith) the sentence reads as literal patting, not commendation.
