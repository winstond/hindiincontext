हीरा (hiiraa)

noun Primarily 'diamond,' the precious stone; also used figuratively for a person of exceptional worth or a real gem of a person.
Sense 1 : diamond (the gemstone)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Diamonds carry heavy symbolic weight in Indian culture, from wedding jewelry to Bollywood song lyrics -- a mother might proudly call her son मेरा हीरा (meraa hiiraa, 'my diamond') without any connection to actual gemstones, moving straight into the figurative sense below.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse हीरा (hiiraa) with हीरो (hiiro, 'hero,' the English loanword) because of the similar sound -- they are unrelated words with different meanings.

Sense 2 : a person of exceptional worth or quality (figurative)
noun, masculine Used as a warm, affectionate label for a person, often with a possessive: मेरा हीरा (meraa hiiraa, 'my gem/star').
Synonyms: रत्न (ratn, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in families and workplaces -- a proud parent, teacher, or boss calling someone हीरा (hiiraa) is one of the most common ways Hindi speakers express 'you're a real gem' or 'you're the best,' paralleling English usage of 'gem' or 'star' for a person rather than an object.
