भुनभुनाना (bhunbhunaanaa)

verb An onomatopoeic verb meaning to mutter or grumble under one's breath -- the sound of low, indistinct, irritated speech.
Sense 1 : to mutter, grumble, mumble complaints under one's breath
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बड़बड़ाना (badabdaanaa), बुनबुनाना (bunbunaanaa, variant form), कुड़कुड़ाना (kudkudaanaa, grumble with irritation)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is one of many Hindi echo-words built from a repeated sound-root (like बड़बड़ाना (badabdaanaa) and कुड़कुड़ाना (kudkudaanaa)) that imitate the texture of speech rather than naming an action directly. You'll hear it used affectionately about a grandmother muttering to herself in the kitchen, or about someone grumbling under their breath after being scolded -- it carries a mild, often fond, sense of harmless irritation rather than real anger.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बोलना (bolnaa) or शिकायत करना (shikaayat karnaa) when they mean this word, but भुनभुनाना (bhunbhunaanaa) specifically evokes the low, half-audible sound of grumbling, not just the act of complaining or speaking. 2) It is almost always used to describe someone else's muttering (often critically or with amusement) rather than one's own speech in the moment.
