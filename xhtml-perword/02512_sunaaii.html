सुनाई (sunaaii)

noun Almost always met in the fixed pair सुनाई देना (sunaaii denaa) 'to be audible', mirroring दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa) for sight; the noun सुनाई (sunaaii) barely occurs outside this construction.
Sense 1 : audibility, hearing (only in the compound सुनाई देना)
noun, feminine Occurs almost exclusively as the noun component of the compound सुनाई देना (sunaaii denaa, 'to be audible/heard'), a dative-subject construction: the person who hears takes को (ko), and the thing heard is the grammatical subject, e.g. structurally parallel to मुझे आवाज़ सुनाई देती है (mujhe aavaaz sunaaii detii hai, 'I can hear the sound').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word is the exact aural counterpart of दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa) for vision -- Hindi treats both hearing and seeing as things that 'arrive' to a passive experiencer rather than actions the experiencer performs, which is why both take dative-subject (को) constructions. Once a learner has दिखाई देना down, सुनाई देना (sunaaii denaa) follows the identical pattern and is easy to pick up as a pair.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use सुनाई (sunaaii) as a free-standing noun meaning 'hearing' (as in 'his hearing is weak'); it does not work that way -- that sense is carried by सुनने की शक्ति (sunne kii shakti) or श्रवण शक्ति (shravan shakti), not सुनाई alone. 2) The verb agrees with देना (denaa) in the compound, not with the person listening; learners sometimes wrongly try to mark the hearer as the subject instead of using को (ko).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे बाहर से कोई आवाज़ सुनाई दे रही है। I can hear some sound from outside.
(mujhe baahar se koii aavaaz sunaaii de rahii hai।)
