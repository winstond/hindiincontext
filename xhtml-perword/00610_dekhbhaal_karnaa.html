देखभाल करना (dekhbhaal karnaa)

verb A compound formed from देखभाल (dekhbhaal, 'looking-after, care') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means 'to take care of, look after' -- used for children, elders, patients, pets, plants, or property.
Sense 1 : to take care of, look after
verb, transitive The person or thing being cared for is marked with की (kii) as in किसी की देखभाल करना (kisii kii dekhbhaal karnaa, 'to take care of someone') -- देखभाल is treated as a feminine noun object of करना, so it agrees like a normal noun + करना construction.
Synonyms: ख़्याल रखना (khyaal rakhnaa), संभालना (sambhaalnaa, also 'to manage/handle'), देखना (dekhnaa, meaning scope: only in contexts like 'बच्चों को देखना')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase carries a warm, dutiful connotation in Hindi households -- बच्चों की देखभाल (bachchon kii dekhbhaal, 'care of the children') or बुज़ुर्गों की देखभाल (buzurgon kii dekhbhaal, 'care of the elderly') are common phrases you will hear discussing family responsibilities, especially around joint-family living arrangements common in North India. It is also the standard phrase used on medicine labels and health pamphlets, e.g. त्वचा की देखभाल (tvachaa kii dekhbhaal, 'skin care').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say किसी को देखभाल करना (kisii ko dekhbhaal karnaa) by analogy with English 'take care of him', but the correct marking is किसी की देखभाल करना (kisii kii dekhbhaal karnaa) -- देखभाल is a possessed noun, not a direct object taking को. 2) Do not confuse this with the simple verb देखना (dekhnaa, 'to see/watch'); देखभाल करना specifically implies ongoing responsibility and nurturing, not just looking at something. 3) In the past tense, ने (ne) attaches to the subject as usual (e.g. उसने देखभाल की (usne dekhbhaal kii, 'she took care')), since करना is transitive here -- learners sometimes forget the feminine agreement with देखभाल.
