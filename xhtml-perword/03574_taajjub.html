ताज्जुब (taajjub)

noun A noun meaning 'surprise' or 'astonishment', most often used in the fixed phrase ताज्जुब होना (taajjub honaa) -- 'to feel surprised' -- with a को-marked experiencer.
Sense 1 : surprise, astonishment
noun, masculine Almost always appears with होना (honaa) in a dative-subject frame: मुझे ताज्जुब है (mujhe taajjub hai, 'I am surprised') -- the person who feels the surprise is marked with को/मुझे/उसे etc., not the subject of an active verb.
Synonyms: आश्चर्य (aashchary), हैरानी (hairaanii), अचरज (achraj, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday Delhi speech: मुझे बड़ा ताज्जुब हुआ (mujhe badaa taajjub huaa, 'I was quite surprised') is a very natural way to react to unexpected news. It pairs closely with आश्चर्य (aashchary), which feels slightly more formal/written, while ताज्जुब (taajjub) is the word you actually reach for in conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to make the experiencer the grammatical subject with a transitive verb (e.g. attempting 'मैं ताज्जुब करता हूँ' (main taajjub kartaa hoon) for 'I am surprised') -- the natural pattern is the को+होना construction: मुझे ताज्जुब हुआ (mujhe taajjub huaa, 'I was surprised'). 2) Confusing ताज्जुब (taajjub) with ताज (taaj, 'crown') due to similar-looking spelling.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे बड़ा ताज्जुब हुआ जब उसने अचानक नौकरी छोड़ दी। I was quite surprised when he suddenly quit his job.
(mujhe badaa taajjub huaa jab usne achaanak naukrii chhod dii।)
