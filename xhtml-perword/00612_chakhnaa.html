चखना (chakhnaa)

verb A simple transitive verb meaning to taste or sample food, usually with the tongue and in a small quantity.
Sense 1 : to taste (food)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: स्वाद लेना (svaad lenaa, to take the taste of)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a Delhi kitchen, the cook almost always चखती है (chakhtii hai, 'tastes [it]') the daal or sabzi with a spoon before serving -- it's an everyday, almost reflexive action tied to getting the namak-mirch (salt-spice balance) right. You will also hear it at food stalls: दुकानदार अक्सर कहता है, 'चख कर देखो' (chakh kar dekho, 'taste and see'), inviting customers to sample pickles, chutneys, or sweets before buying.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse चखना (chakhnaa) with खाना (khaanaa, to eat) -- चखना (chakhnaa) implies a small sampling bite or sip to check flavor, not a full meal. 2) It is commonly used with 'a little' -- ज़रा चखना (zaraa chakhnaa, 'just taste a little') is very natural, whereas the same phrasing with खाना (khaanaa) sounds odd since eating implies a proper quantity.

(maan daal chakhtii hai aur namak dekhtii hai।)
