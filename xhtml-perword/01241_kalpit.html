कल्पित (kalpit)

adjective An adjective meaning 'imagined, fictional, or invented' -- describes something constructed by the mind rather than real. Related to कल्पना (kalpnaa, 'imagination'), the noun from which it derives.
Sense 1 : imaginary, fictional (not real)
adjective
Synonyms: काल्पनिक (kaalpnik), कपोल-कल्पित (kapol-kalpit, stronger, 'utterly made-up'), झूठा (jhoothaa, meaning scope: only for 'false' not 'fictional')
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will see this word on the disclaimer screen of Hindi films and shows: यह कहानी कल्पित है (yah kahaanii kalpit hai, 'this story is fictional') or पात्र और घटनाएँ कल्पित हैं (paatr aur ghatnaaen kalpit hain, 'the characters and events are fictional'). Outside such formal or written contexts, काल्पनिक (kaalpnik) is the word people actually reach for in conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for झूठा (jhoothaa) to mean 'made up,' but झूठा (jhoothaa) implies a deliberate lie, while कल्पित (kalpit) implies something imagined or invented, not necessarily to deceive. 2) कल्पित (kalpit) is more literary/formal than काल्पनिक (kaalpnik), which is the word you will hear far more often in everyday speech for 'fictional' (e.g., describing a movie or story).
Sample Sentences:
यह कहानी पूरी तरह से कल्पित है। This story is entirely fictional.
(yah kahaanii poorii tarah se kalpit hai।)

(film kii shuruaat men likhaa thaa ki yah kathaa kalpit hai।)

(nirmaataaon ko spasht karnaa chaahie ki kathaa kalpit hai taaki darshak bhramit n hon।)
