इकसठ (iksath)

numeral A cardinal number: 'sixty-one' (iksath).
Sense 1 : the number sixty-one
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, इकसठ (iksath) is just something you drill and memorize -- there isn't a clean logical shortcut the way there is in English. It shows up in everyday contexts like ages, prices, and phone numbers, so it's worth having cold rather than calculating on the fly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Hindi numerals from 1 to 99 are mostly irregular and must be memorized individually rather than built compositionally like English 'sixty' + 'one'; इकसठ (iksath) does not visibly break down into साठ (saath, sixty) plus एक (ek, one) the way learners expect. 2) Easy to confuse with similar-sounding neighbors like इकतालीस (iktaaliis, forty-one) or बासठ (baasath, sixty-two) due to the shared इक- prefix pattern seen across several numbers ending in one or two.
