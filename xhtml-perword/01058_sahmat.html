सहमत (sahmat)

adjective An invariant adjective meaning 'in agreement, agreed', almost always used with होना (honaa) 'to be/become agreed' or करना (karnaa) 'to make agree, get someone to agree'.
Sense 1 : in agreement, agreed
adjective Used predicatively with से (se) marking the person or thing one agrees with: किसी से सहमत होना (kisii se sahmat honaa, 'to agree with someone'), and पर/के लिए (par/ke lie) marking the point of agreement.
Synonyms: राज़ी (raazii), सम्मत (sammat, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In meetings, family discussions, or news reports, आप इस बात से सहमत हैं? (aap is baat se sahmat hain?, 'do you agree with this?') is the standard polite way to check consensus -- it has a slightly more formal, deliberative flavor than the more casual राज़ी (raazii), which leans toward personal willingness or consent rather than intellectual agreement.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say सहमत को (sahmat ko) instead of the correct सहमत से (sahmat se) when naming the person agreed with -- सहमत (sahmat) takes से (se), not को (ko). 2) सहमत (sahmat) itself never changes form for gender or number since it is invariant; only the following होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa) inflects.
