ज़रूर (zaroor)

adverb A single-sense adverb meaning 'certainly, definitely, for sure' -- one of the most common words in spoken Hindi for giving assurance or emphasis.
Sense 1 : certainly, definitely, for sure
adverb
Synonyms: अवश्य (avashy, slightly more formal), निश्चित रूप से (nishchit roop se, more formal, used in writing/speeches), बेशक (beshak)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you reach for when you want to reassure someone warmly -- if you ask a Delhi shopkeeper or a friend for a favor, the answer is very often just ज़रूर! (zaroor, 'Of course!') on its own, no verb needed. It carries a friendly, obliging tone, almost like saying 'absolutely, no problem' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often place it awkwardly at the very start of the sentence as a translation of English 'Surely, ...'; in natural Hindi it usually sits right before the verb, e.g. मैं ज़रूर आऊँगा (main zaroor aaoongaa, 'I will definitely come') rather than at the front. 2) It is sometimes confused with ज़रूरी (zaroorii, 'necessary'), which is an adjective, not an adverb -- ज़रूर never means 'necessary' by itself.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रूर! Of course!
(zaroor!)
