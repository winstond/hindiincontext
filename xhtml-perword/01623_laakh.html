लाख (laakh)

numeral/adjective/noun Primarily a number word meaning 'hundred thousand' (100,000), the standard unit for large numbers in the Indian counting system; also used loosely to mean 'countless/tons of' something, and as a separate noun means 'lac' (the resinous substance).
Sense 1 : hundred thousand (100,000)
numeral Used as the base unit above हज़ार (hazaar, thousand) in the Indian numbering system: दस लाख (das laakh, ten lakh = one million).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Money, salaries, and property prices in India are very commonly quoted in लाख (laakh) -- you will hear things like 'यह घर पचास लाख का है' (yah ghar pachaas laakh kaa hai, 'this house costs fifty lakh') constantly in everyday conversation, on the news, and in real estate ads. It pairs with करोड़ (karod, ten million) to form the backbone of how Indians talk about large sums.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners coming from the Western numbering system often miscalculate: एक लाख (ek laakh) is 100,000, not 10,000 or 1,000,000 -- it sits between हज़ार (hazaar, thousand) and करोड़ (karod, ten million). 2) In writing and speech, big numbers are grouped differently than in English (e.g. 1,00,000 not 100,000), which affects how numbers are read aloud in लाख (laakh) and करोड़ (karod).

Sense 2 : countless, a huge number of
adjective Used before a plural noun in an exaggerated, non-literal sense: लाख बार (laakh baar, 'a thousand times / countless times') or लाख कोशिशें (laakh koshishen, 'countless attempts').
Synonyms: अनगिनत (anginat, countless), असंख्य (asankhy, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the number लाख (laakh) stretched into everyday hyperbole, much like English 'a million times' -- a mother saying 'मैंने तुझे लाख बार कहा है' (mainne tujhe laakh baar kahaa hai, 'I've told you a thousand times') is a classic scolding line heard in homes across Delhi-NCR.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a figurative exaggeration, not an actual count -- लाख बार समझाया (laakh baar samjhaayaa, 'explained it a thousand times') does not mean literally 100,000 times, and learners sometimes take it too literally or hesitate to use it hyperbolically the way native speakers do freely.

Sense 3 : lac (the resin)
noun, masculine
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This लाख (laakh) is the sticky resin traditionally used to make bangles (लाख की चूड़ियाँ, laakh kii choodiyaan, 'lac bangles') and sealing wax -- it is a completely unrelated word from the number, just a homonym, so don't try to connect the meanings.
