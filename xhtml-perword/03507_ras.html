रस (ras)

noun Covers the literal sense of 'juice/liquid essence' and the extended senses of 'flavor/taste' and 'aesthetic delight, emotional essence' -- the last being a key concept in Indian poetics and art.
Sense 1 : juice, liquid extract
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जूस (joos, loanword, common for fruit drinks specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of the roadside गन्ने का रस (ganne kaa ras) cart, where sugarcane is crushed fresh in front of you -- रस (ras) is the word for that sweet extracted liquid, whether from fruit, cane, or even meat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for जूस (joos) exclusively; रस (ras) is the native word and appears in compounds like अंगूर का रस (angoor kaa ras, 'grape juice') and गन्ने का रस (ganne kaa ras, 'sugarcane juice') even in everyday speech.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : taste, flavor (of food)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: स्वाद (svaad)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear someone say खाने में रस आ गया (khaane men ras aa gayaa, 'the food became truly flavorful/delightful') -- this usage already starts blending into the next sense, aesthetic pleasure, since good taste and good feeling are linked in Hindi thought.
Common Mistakes: 1) स्वाद (svaad) is the everyday word for 'taste'; रस (ras) in this sense leans slightly more literary/appreciative, often used when praising how flavorful or satisfying a dish is rather than in plain questions like 'how does it taste?'

Sense 3 : aesthetic delight, emotional essence (as in poetics/art)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आनंद (aanand, broader, 'joy/bliss' rather than the technical aesthetic sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the रस (ras) of classical Indian aesthetics (नवरस, navras, 'the nine rasas') -- the idea that a poem, dance, or film scene is crafted to make you taste a specific emotion, like शृंगार रस (shringaar ras, the rasa of romantic love) or वीर रस (viir ras, the rasa of heroism). In daily speech, कहानी में रस नहीं है (kahaanii men ras nahiin hai, 'there's no relish/interest in the story') is a natural, non-academic use of this same idea.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is abstract and shows up in set phrases like रस लेना (ras lenaa, 'to relish, take delight in') and बे-रस (be-ras, 'dull, flavorless' -- of a story, speech, or person); learners sometimes miss that रस (ras) here means the emotional 'flavor' an experience gives you, not a physical substance.
Sample Sentences:
भारतीय काव्यशास्त्र में नौ रस माने गए हैं। In Indian poetics, nine rasas are recognized.
(bhaartiiy kaavyshaastr men nau ras maane gae hain।)
