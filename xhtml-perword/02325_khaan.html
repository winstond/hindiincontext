खान (khaan)

noun Two distinct words share this spelling: a feminine noun meaning 'mine' (as in a coal or resource mine, also used figuratively for 'a treasure trove of'), and a masculine noun/surname 'Khan', a common Muslim surname/title of Central Asian origin.
Sense 1 : mine (literal, e.g. coal mine)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of coal-rich belts like Jharkhand or Chhattisgarh when this word comes up in the news -- कोयले की खान (koyle kii khaan, 'coal mine') is the phrase you will hear most often in reports about mining accidents or labor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with खाना (khaanaa, 'food/to eat') because of the similar spelling and sound -- खान (khaan) has a short final vowel while खाना (khaanaa) ends in a long आ.
Sample Sentences:

(jhaarkhand men koyle kii khaan hai।)

Sense 2 : treasure trove, rich source (figurative)
noun, feminine Used in the fixed pattern [noun] + की खान (kii khaan) -- 'a mine of [quality/knowledge]', e.g. गुणों की खान (gunon kii khaan, 'a treasure trove of virtues').
Synonyms: भंडार (bhandaar, storehouse), खजाना (khajaanaa, treasure)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the literal 'mine' -- Hindi extends the mining image the same way English does with 'a mine of information', so ज्ञान की खान (gyaan kii khaan, 'a mine of knowledge') will feel intuitive once you know the literal sense.

Sense 3 : Khan (surname/title)
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Khan is one of the most recognizable surnames in India, historically a title carried by Pathan and Central Asian nobility, and today the name of some of Bollywood's biggest stars (Shah Rukh Khan, Aamir Khan, Salman Khan) -- hearing खान (khaan) on its own in conversation, without की खान (kii khaan) following, almost always signals this sense.

(shaahrukh khaan boliivud ke sabse bade sitaaron men se ek hain।)
