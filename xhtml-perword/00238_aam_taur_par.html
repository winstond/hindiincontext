आम तौर पर (aam taur par)

adverb A common fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'generally, usually' -- used to talk about what normally happens rather than one specific occasion.
Sense 1 : generally, usually
adverb
Synonyms: सामान्यतः (saamaanytah, formal), प्रायः (praayah, formal), अक्सर (aksar, often, meaning scope: emphasizes frequency more than the general/typical case)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: तौर (taur) itself means 'manner, way' (from Persian/Urdu), so आम तौर पर (aam taur par) literally means something like 'in the common manner' -- a nice example of how many Hindi adverbial phrases are built from a noun plus पर (par), the way एक तरह से (ek tarah se, 'in a way') or किसी तरह (kisii tarah, 'somehow') are. You'll hear it constantly in news reports, weather talk, and everyday explanations of habits: लोग आम तौर पर ऐसा नहीं करते (log aam taur par aisaa nahiin karte, 'people generally don't do this').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with अक्सर (aksar) -- आम तौर पर (aam taur par) describes what is typically/normally true as a rule, while अक्सर (aksar) is closer to 'frequently, many times'; the two overlap but aren't perfect substitutes. 2) The phrase is invariant -- learners sometimes try to inflect तौर (taur) or पर (par) separately, but the whole thing functions as one fixed adverbial unit.

आम तौर पर लोग रविवार को छुट्टी मनाते हैं। Generally, people take a holiday on Sunday.
(aam taur par log ravivaar ko chhuttii manaate hain।)
