वसूल (vasool)

adjective/noun Two related strands: recovering/collecting money that is owed (a debt, a fee, a fine), and the colloquial adjective/idiom sense of getting your money's worth. Both come from the same root idea of 'what was due, extracted.'
Sense 1 : worth it, value for money
adjective Used predicatively, most often in the fixed colloquial phrase पैसा वसूल (paisaa vasool, 'money's worth') -- e.g. of a movie, meal, or purchase that delivered full value.
Synonyms: सार्थक (saarthak, meaning scope: worthwhile in a broader sense, less about money)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: पैसा वसूल (paisaa vasool, 'worth every rupee') is classic Bollywood-review language -- critics and audiences alike use it for a masala film that delivers action, comedy, and drama in full paisa-vasool measure. It captures a very Indian consumer instinct: did I get my money's worth?
Common Mistakes: 1) This is an invariant adjective in practice -- it does not inflect like कड़ा (kadaa) would; you'll always see it as पैसा वसूल (paisaa vasool) regardless of the gender/number of what's being described. 2) Learners sometimes try to use it outside this fixed phrase (e.g. as a general adjective for 'satisfying'), which sounds odd -- it's really glued to पैसा (paisaa).

Sense 2 : recovery/collection (of dues, money owed)
noun, masculine Almost always paired with the verb करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) -- वसूल करना (vasool karnaa, 'to collect/recover') and वसूल होना (vasool honaa, 'to be recovered').
Synonyms: आदायगी (aadaaygii, formal), वापसी (vaapsii, meaning scope: return, not always of money owed)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around landlords, shopkeepers, and tax officers -- किराया वसूल करना (kiraayaa vasool karnaa, 'to collect the rent') is a classic phrase for a landlord chasing a tenant. It has a slightly businesslike, sometimes even mildly threatening edge, as in वसूली (vasoolii), the related noun for forceful collection/extortion, which you might hear in news reports about goons collecting 'protection money.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use वसूल (vasool) loosely for any 'collection' (like collecting stamps) -- it specifically means collecting something that is *owed*, like rent, a debt, a fine, or a fee. 2) पैसे वसूल करना (paise vasool karnaa) implies the money was due to you, unlike सामान्य 'लेना' (lenaa, 'to take') which carries no such implication.
