कोसना (kosnaa)

verb A single core meaning: to curse or berate someone verbally, usually out of frustration or misfortune, blaming them (aloud or in one's mind) for one's troubles.
Sense 1 : to curse, to berate, to heap curses on
verb, transitive
Synonyms: गाली देना (gaalii denaa, more direct abuse), बुरा-भला कहना (buraa-bhalaa kahnaa, scold/rebuke)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word for the muttered curses of a frustrated mother, a stuck-in-traffic uncle, or someone cursing their own luck -- अपनी किस्मत को कोसना (apnii kismat ko kosnaa, 'to curse one's own fate') is an extremely common phrase in everyday complaining. It carries more of a 'blaming and grumbling at' flavor than the violent abuse of गाली देना (gaalii denaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for गाली देना (gaalii denaa) as a direct swap, but कोसना (kosnaa) is milder and usually about blaming someone for one's misfortune (e.g. अपने भाग्य को कोसना (apne bhaagy ko kosnaa, 'to curse one's fate')) rather than hurling obscenities. 2) The person or thing being cursed is marked with को (ko), not से (se) -- वह मुझे कोसती है (vah mujhe kostii hai, 'she curses at me'), not मुझसे।
Sample Sentences:
वह अपनी किस्मत को कोसती रहती है। She keeps cursing her own fate.
(vah apnii kismat ko kostii rahtii hai।)
