गाय (gaay)

noun A simple, single-sense word: the female cow. No hidden complexity here, but plenty of cultural weight, which you'll see in the note below.
Sense 1 : cow
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गौ (gau, literary/devotional), गौमाता (gaumaataa, reverential term)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: The cow holds a special place in Hindu culture as a sacred and maternal figure, often called गौमाता (gaumaataa, 'cow mother'), and you will see cows wandering freely on streets even in busy Delhi neighborhoods, generally left undisturbed out of respect. This reverence also shows up in everyday speech and idiom, so don't be surprised to hear गाय (gaay) invoked in comparisons about gentleness or innocence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume गाय (gaay) is grammatically neutral like English 'cow', but it is strictly feminine, which matters for adjective and verb agreement -- 'a black cow' is काली गाय (kaalii gaay) not काला गाय (kaalaa gaay).
