मूल्यवान (moolyvaan)

adjective A somewhat formal adjective meaning 'valuable' or 'precious', used for both objects (money-value) and abstract things like time or advice (worth/significance).
Sense 1 : valuable, precious (having high monetary or material worth)
adjective
Synonyms: कीमती (kiimtii), बहुमूल्य (bahumooly, formal, higher-value emphasis), अनमोल (anmol, priceless, cannot be bought)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word more on the page -- news reports about stolen मूल्यवान (moolyvaan) वस्तुएँ (vastuen, 'valuable items'), or museum plaques -- than in casual bazaar chat, where लोग (log, 'people') just say कीमती (kiimtii) चीज़ (chiiz, 'expensive/valuable thing').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कीमती (kiimtii) in speech and मूल्यवान (moolyvaan) in writing without realizing the two overlap heavily -- मूल्यवान (moolyvaan) just leans a shade more formal/written, not a different meaning. 2) Do not confuse with बेकार (bekaar, 'useless') as a false opposite pairing -- the natural antonym pair in speech is often सस्ता (sastaa, 'cheap') for cost, or बेकार (bekaar) for worth.

Sense 2 : valuable, worthwhile (of abstract things: time, advice, experience, contribution)
adjective Commonly pairs with abstract nouns like समय (samay, 'time'), सुझाव (sujhaav, 'suggestion'), and अनुभव (anubhav, 'experience') to praise their worth rather than a market price.
Synonyms: महत्वपूर्ण (mahatvpoorn, 'important', broader sense of significance), बहुमूल्य (bahumooly, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the polite, formal way to thank someone for their time or input -- आपका मूल्यवान समय (aapkaa moolyvaan samay, 'your valuable time') is a stock phrase in speeches, emails, and event invitations across Delhi-NCR offices and institutions, much like English 'your valuable time' in formal correspondence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to महत्वपूर्ण (mahatvpoorn) for every 'important/valuable' context; मूल्यवान (moolyvaan) specifically frames something as worth treasuring or not wasting, e.g. आपका समय मूल्यवान है (aapkaa samay moolyvaan hai, 'your time is valuable'), which sounds slightly more appreciative/polite than महत्वपूर्ण (mahatvpoorn) would in the same slot.
