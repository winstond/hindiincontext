फँसाना (phansaanaa)

verb The causative of फँसना (phansnaa, 'to get trapped/stuck'); फँसाना (phansaanaa) means 'to trap, ensnare' someone else, physically or figuratively (into trouble, blame, or a scheme).
Sense 1 : to trap, entangle (into trouble or a scheme)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: जाल में फंसाना (jaal men phansaanaa, 'to trap in a net/web'), उलझाना (uljhaanaa, to entangle), झांसे में लेना (jhaanse men lenaa, to dupe/deceive)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes up constantly in Delhi conversations about office politics, family disputes, or shady deals -- किसी को फँसा देना (kisii ko phansaa denaa, 'to get someone framed/trapped') is the classic complaint when someone feels wrongly blamed. It pairs naturally with फँसना (phansnaa), which describes the same event from the victim's side ('I got trapped') rather than the agent's.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use it only for physical entrapment, missing its very common figurative use for getting someone into legal, financial, or social trouble, e.g. केस में फँसाना (kes men phansaanaa, 'to frame someone in a court case'). 2) Since this is the causative of फँसना (phansnaa), the person doing the trapping takes ने (ne) in the perfective, while the one trapped is the object -- मत्रुक: उसने मुझे फँसाया (usne mujhe phansaayaa, 'he framed/trapped me').
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपने दोस्त को झूठे केस में फँसा दिया। He got his friend framed in a false case.
(usne apne dost ko jhoothe kes men phansaa diyaa।)

Sense 2 : to entice, lure (especially romantically or financially)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: लुभाना (lubhaanaa, to entice/allure), बहकाना (bahkaanaa, to lead astray)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in gossip about relationships or scams -- किसी को पैसों के लालच में फँसाना (kisii ko paison ke laalach men phansaanaa, 'to lure someone with the temptation of money') is a common frame for describing con artists or manipulative suitors.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is often used disapprovingly about romantic manipulation, e.g. लड़की को प्यार के जाल में फँसाना (ladkii ko pyaar ke jaal men phansaanaa, 'to lure a girl into a trap of love') -- learners may miss the judgmental tone it usually carries.

(usne bholii ladkii ko jhoothe pyaar men phansaa liyaa।)
