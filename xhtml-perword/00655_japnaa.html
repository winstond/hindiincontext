जपना (japnaa)

verb A verb meaning to chant or repeat something quietly, especially a name, mantra, or prayer, over and over; it also has a common figurative extension for going on and on about the same thing.
Sense 1 : to chant or repeat (a mantra, name, prayer) silently or in a low murmur
verb, transitive
Synonyms: रटना (ratnaa, to repeat mechanically, often used for rote memorization), गुनगुनाना (gungunaanaa, to hum/murmur, softer nuance)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word lives close to the practice of जाप (jaap), the repeated chanting of a deity's name or a mantra, often done with a माला (maalaa, prayer beads) while sitting quietly early in the morning. You will hear elders describe grandparents who माला फेरते हुए राम-नाम जपते थे (maalaa pherte hue raam-naam japte the, 'used to chant Ram's name while turning prayer beads') -- it evokes a specific devotional image of quiet, private repetition rather than public recitation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पढ़ना (padhnaa) or बोलना (bolnaa) to say 'chant,' but जपना (japnaa) specifically implies quiet, repetitive, meditative recitation, not reading aloud once. 2) The object is usually a name or mantra -- राम नाम जपना (raam naam japnaa, to chant Ram's name) -- rather than a sentence or story, which would not fit this verb.

दादी हर सुबह माला फेरते हुए राम-नाम जपती थीं। Grandmother used to chant Ram's name every morning while turning her prayer beads.
(daadii har subah maalaa pherte hue raam-naam japtii thiin।)

Sense 2 : to go on repeating the same thing (a complaint, idea, or word) over and over, figuratively
verb, transitive This figurative sense usually appears with an abstract object like बात (baat, a matter/thing said) or a fixed phrase, rather than a mantra or name.
Synonyms: रटना (ratnaa, to repeat mechanically), रट लगाना (rat lagaanaa, to keep harping on, compound-verb idiom)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is a natural metaphorical stretch from the devotional sense above: just as one chants a mantra tirelessly, a person can 'chant' the same complaint or excuse tirelessly, often with a note of mild exasperation from the speaker.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may not realize जपना (japnaa) can be used mildly critically about a person who won't stop repeating the same complaint or excuse, e.g. वही बात जपे जा रहा है (vahii baat jape jaa rahaa hai, 'he keeps harping on the same thing') -- this is an extension from the devotional sense, not a separate unrelated word.
Sample Sentences:
वह दिन-रात यही शिकायत जपता रहता है। He keeps harping on the same complaint day and night.
(vah din-raat yahii shikaayat japtaa rahtaa hai।)
