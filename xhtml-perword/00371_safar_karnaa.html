सफ़र करना (safar karnaa)

verb A compound expression combining सफ़र (safar, 'journey/travel', a Persian-origin loanword) with करना (karnaa, 'to do'), meaning 'to travel' or 'to make a journey'.
Sense 1 : to travel, to make a journey
verb, intransitive Takes से (se) for the mode of travel (बस से bas se, 'by bus') and तक (tak) or को (ko) for the destination.
Synonyms: यात्रा करना (yaatraa karnaa, slightly more formal), घूमना (ghoomnaa, meaning shifts toward 'to roam/wander' rather than a purposeful journey)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सफ़र (safar) carries a slightly romantic, reflective flavor in Hindi -- it shows up constantly in film lyrics and shayari about life's journey (ज़िंदगी का सफ़र zindgii kaa safar, 'life's journey'), so सफ़र करना (safar karnaa) can feel a touch more evocative than the plain, bureaucratic यात्रा करना (yaatraa karnaa), which you'd see on a travel agency's website or a train ticket.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say यात्रा करना (yaatraa karnaa) exclusively and avoid सफ़र करना (safar karnaa), but the latter is actually the more common, everyday choice in spoken Hindi -- यात्रा (yaatraa) leans slightly more formal or written. 2) सफ़र (safar) itself is a noun and can be used alone with होना (honaa) (सफ़र अच्छा था safar achchhaa thaa, 'the journey was good') without करना (karnaa) at all -- learners over-attach करना (karnaa) even when just describing the trip rather than the act of traveling.
