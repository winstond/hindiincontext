जुलूस (juloos)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning a procession or march, whether festive (a wedding or religious procession) or political (a protest march).
Sense 1 : procession, march
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शोभायात्रा (shobhaayaatraa, formal, used for ceremonial/religious processions), रैली (railii, specifically a political rally/march)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear जुलूस (juloos) constantly around festivals like दुर्गा पूजा (durgaa poojaa) or मुहर्रम (muharram), where idols or tazias are carried through the streets with music, as well as in news reports about विरोध जुलूस (virodh juloos, protest march). The verb commonly paired with it is निकालना (nikaalnaa) -- 'julus nikalna' literally means to 'take out' a procession, reflecting how Hindi frames a procession as something a community brings out into public space.
Common Mistakes: 1) जुलूस (juloos) is used for both festive processions (a wedding baraat, a religious procession) and protest marches -- context, not the word itself, tells you which. 2) Learners sometimes reach for परेड (pared) when they mean a formal military parade; जुलूस (juloos) is the general word for any crowd moving together through the streets.
