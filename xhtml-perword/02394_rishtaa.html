रिश्ता (rishtaa)

noun Mainly means 'relationship/relation' between people, and by extension a 'marriage proposal/match' -- a very common word in the context of arranged marriage discussions.
Sense 1 : relationship, bond (between people)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संबंध (sambandh), नाता (naataa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Family is often described through रिश्ते (rishte) -- you'll hear खून का रिश्ता (khoon kaa rishtaa, 'blood relation') to stress that a bond runs deeper than mere friendship. This sense is the emotional root from which the 'marriage proposal' sense below extends.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use रिश्ता (rishtaa) for casual friendships or professional ties where संबंध (sambandh) or ताल्लुक़ (taalluq) sounds more natural; रिश्ता (rishtaa) leans toward family, kinship, or emotionally significant bonds. 2) The phrase रिश्ता रखना (rishtaa rakhnaa, 'to maintain a relationship') and रिश्ता तोड़ना (rishtaa todnaa, 'to break off a relationship') are set collocations worth learning as chunks.
Sample Sentences:
हमारा रिश्ता बहुत गहरा है। Our relationship is very deep.
(hamaaraa rishtaa bahut gahraa hai।)

Sense 2 : marriage proposal, match
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शादी का प्रस्ताव (shaadii kaa prastaav, multi-word)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In the context of arranged marriage, families say रिश्ता देखना (rishtaa dekhnaa, 'to consider/vet a match') or रिश्ता तय होना (rishtaa tay honaa, 'for a match to be finalized'). This extended sense treats a prospective marriage as itself a 'relationship' being proposed -- a natural outgrowth of the first sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) रिश्ता आना (rishtaa aanaa, 'a proposal to arrive') and रिश्ता भेजना (rishtaa bhejnaa, 'to send a proposal') are the standard verb pairings; learners sometimes reach for शादी (shaadii) alone when they specifically mean the proposal stage, before the wedding itself.
Sample Sentences:
उसके लिए एक अच्छा रिश्ता आया है। A good marriage proposal has come for her.
(uske lie ek achchhaa rishtaa aayaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

(ham kal sharmaa jii ke bete ke lie rishtaa dekhne jaa rahe hain।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
