घनिष्ठ (ghanishth)

adjective An adjective meaning 'close' or 'intimate,' used almost exclusively to describe close relationships or friendships, e.g. घनिष्ठ मित्र (ghanishth mitr, 'close friend').
Sense 1 : close, intimate (of a relationship or friendship)
adjective
Synonyms: गहरा (gahraa), अभिन्न (abhinn, formal/literary), निकट (nikat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a somewhat formal, bookish word -- you will hear it in speeches, writing, or careful spoken Hindi more than in casual chat, where लोग गहरे दोस्त (gahre dost, 'close friends') is more common. It pairs almost like a fixed phrase with मित्र (mitr) or संबंध (sambandh, 'relationship').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use घनिष्ठ (ghanishth) for physical closeness (like two objects being near each other) -- it is reserved for emotional/relational closeness, mainly friendship or acquaintance. For physical nearness, use नज़दीक (nazdiik) or पास (paas) instead.
Sample Sentences:

(ve donon ghanishth mitr hain।)
