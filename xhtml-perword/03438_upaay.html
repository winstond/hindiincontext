उपाय (upaay)

noun A single core meaning: a solution, remedy, or way of doing something to fix a problem or achieve a goal.
Sense 1 : solution, remedy, means to solve a problem
noun, masculine
Synonyms: हल (hal), इलाज (ilaaj, especially for a problem framed like an illness), तरीका (tariikaa, more general, 'method' or 'way')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in everyday problem-talk: कोई उपाय सोचो (koii upaay socho, 'think of some solution') is the kind of thing a worried mother, a stressed office colleague, or a friend in a jam will say. It carries a slightly practical, hands-on flavor -- more 'workaround' or 'fix' than an abstract 'solution,' which is why it pairs so naturally with everyday troubles like traffic, money, or health.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तरीका (tariikaa) for 'way/method' in general, but उपाय (upaay) specifically implies a fix for a problem or difficulty -- it's not used for a neutral 'method of cooking' or 'way of speaking.' 2) Common frame: कोई उपाय नहीं है (koii upaay nahiin hai, 'there's no way out/no solution') -- learners sometimes misuse हल (hal) here, which leans more toward a solved 'answer' (as to a math problem) than a practical course of action. 3) Frequently paired with करना (karnaa) -- उपाय करना (upaay karnaa, 'to take measures/find a remedy') -- learners sometimes use बनाना (banaanaa) by analogy with 'make a solution,' which is not idiomatic here.

इस समस्या का कोई उपाय सोचो। Think of some solution to this problem.
(is samasyaa kaa koii upaay socho।)
