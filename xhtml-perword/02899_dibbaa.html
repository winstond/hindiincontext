डिब्बा (dibbaa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning a box, container, or tin -- and by extension, casually, a train compartment or a battered old vehicle. All senses share the core image of a boxy container.
Sense 1 : box, tin, container
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बक्सा (baksaa), पिटारा (pitaaraa, for a decorated or ornate box), कंटेनर (kantenar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of the steel डिब्बे (dibbe) stacked in every Indian kitchen for storing spices, lentils, and biscuits -- this word is deeply tied to household life. A tiffin box is often called खाने का डिब्बा (khaane kaa dibbaa, 'the food box') or just डिब्बा (dibbaa) in context.
Common Mistakes: 1) डिब्बा (dibbaa) is used for a wide range of sizes, from a small tiffin tin to a large cardboard carton; learners sometimes reach for बक्सा (baksaa) by default, but डिब्बा (dibbaa) is actually the more common everyday word for small kitchen and packaging containers.

Sense 2 : train/bus compartment
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बोगी (bogii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Delhi commuter which डिब्बा (dibbaa) to board and they'll instantly picture the general, ladies', or AC compartment on a local train -- railway announcements and platform chatter keep this sense extremely alive.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this sense डिब्बा (dibbaa) and बोगी (bogii) overlap heavily; डिब्बा (dibbaa) is the more colloquial, everyday choice while बोगी (bogii) sounds slightly more official, but both are understood everywhere.

Sense 3 : old, rickety vehicle (colloquial/derogatory)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खटारा (khataaraa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Said with a laugh about a wheezing old car or scooter -- 'यह गाड़ी तो डिब्बा है' (yah gaadii to dibbaa hai, 'this car is a total clunker') -- the word paints the vehicle as nothing more than a boxy shell that barely runs.
Sample Sentences:
यह गाड़ी तो डिब्बा है। This car is a total clunker.
(yah gaadii to dibbaa hai।)
