और भी (aur bhii)

adverb A two-word intensifier meaning 'even more' -- combines और (aur, 'more/and') with भी (bhii, 'also/even') to push a comparison further. One core sense, used before adjectives, adverbs, or nouns.
Sense 1 : even more, still more (intensifying a comparison)
adverb Placed immediately before the adjective, adverb, or noun it intensifies: और भी अच्छा (aur bhii achchhaa, 'even better'). Often pairs with a following comparative structure using से (se).
Synonyms: कहीं ज़्यादा (kahiin zyaadaa, used before adjectives for emphasis), बहुत ज़्यादा (bahut zyaadaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in bargaining at a market -- इससे और भी सस्ता दो (isse aur bhii sastaa do, 'give it even cheaper than this') -- or in comparisons among siblings, dishes, or cities, where Hindi speakers love stacking intensifiers for dramatic effect.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say भी और (bhii aur) reversing the word order -- the fixed order is always और भी (aur bhii), never the reverse. 2) Confusing this with plain और (aur) alone -- और अच्छा (aur achchhaa) already means 'more/better,' while और भी अच्छा (aur bhii achchhaa) pushes it further, closer to 'even better still.'
