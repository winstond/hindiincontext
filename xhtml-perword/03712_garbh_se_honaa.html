गर्भ से होना (garbh se honaa)

verb A common colloquial way to say someone is pregnant, literally 'to be from/with womb.'
Sense 1 : to be pregnant
verb, intransitive Used with a subject marked by से (se) fixed in this construction; होना (honaa) inflects for tense/aspect as usual, but गर्भ से (garbh se) stays invariant.
Synonyms: गर्भवती होना (garbhavtii honaa), पेट से होना (pet se honaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a softer, everyday way to say someone is expecting, often used within families or among neighbors rather than in a clinical setting -- similar in feel to English 'she's expecting' versus the more direct 'she's pregnant.' It sits alongside गर्भवती होना (garbhavtii honaa), which is more the standard/formal term you'd hear from a doctor or read in writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try को (ko) with the subject, but this construction uses a plain subject plus से (se) fixed before होना (honaa) -- e.g. वह गर्भ से है (vah garbh se hai, 'she is pregnant'), not वह को गर्भ से है. 2) Do not translate से (se) literally as 'from' in English -- the whole phrase गर्भ से होना (garbh se honaa) is a fixed idiom meaning 'to be pregnant,' not 'to come from a womb.'
Sample Sentences:
सुना है भाभी गर्भ से हैं। I heard sister-in-law is expecting.
(sunaa hai bhaabhii garbh se hain।)
