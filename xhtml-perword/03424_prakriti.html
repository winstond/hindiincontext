प्रकृति (prakriti)

noun Mainly means 'nature' (the natural world), with a related but distinct sense of 'nature/temperament' (a person's inherent disposition).
Sense 1 : nature, the natural world
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कुदरत (kudrat)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see on nature documentaries, school essays, and environmental slogans in India -- प्रकृति की रक्षा (prakriti kii rakshaa, 'protection of nature') is a common phrase in civic campaigns. कुदरत (kudrat) is a common poetic/everyday alternative, especially in songs and casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse प्रकृति (prakriti) with स्वभाव (svabhaav); when talking about forests, mountains, rivers and the environment, प्रकृति (prakriti) is the natural choice, not स्वभाव (svabhaav), which is reserved for a person's temperament.

Sense 2 : nature, temperament, innate disposition (of a person)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: स्वभाव (svabhaav), मिज़ाज (mizaaj)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in reflective or philosophical contexts -- मानव प्रकृति (maanav prakriti, 'human nature') is a stock phrase in essays and discussions about ethics or behavior, echoing the same root sense as 'nature' in the environmental meaning above.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense overlaps heavily with स्वभाव (svabhaav); native speakers reach for स्वभाव (svabhaav) more often in casual talk about someone's personality, while प्रकृति (prakriti) here can sound a touch more formal or philosophical, e.g. in the phrase मानव प्रकृति (maanav prakriti, 'human nature').
