दबाना (dabaanaa)

verb A transitive verb built on the intransitive दबना (dabnaa, 'to be pressed/suppressed'); covers physical pressing, suppressing/controlling something, and pressuring or dominating a person.
Sense 1 : to press (physically)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: दबाव डालना (dabaav daalnaa, to apply pressure)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very physical and concrete -- a mother telling a child पैर दबाओ (pair dabaao, 'press/massage my feet') is a classic household scene, and the same verb covers pressing a doorbell (घंटी दबाना ghantii dabaanaa) or a button.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for दबाना (dabaanaa) when they mean 'to push' (धकेलना dhakelnaa) -- दबाना (dabaanaa) is pressing downward or inward, not shoving something along.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा मेरे पैर दबा दो, बहुत दर्द हो रहा है। Please press my feet a bit, they hurt a lot.
(zaraa mere pair dabaa do, bahut dard ho rahaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : to suppress, hold back, contain
verb, transitive Takes abstract objects like गुस्सा (gussaa, anger), आवाज़ (aavaaz, voice), or दर्द (dard, pain) to mean holding something in rather than letting it show.
Synonyms: रोकना (roknaa, to stop/hold back), दमन करना (daman karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: अपना गुस्सा दबाना (apnaa gussaa dabaanaa, 'to suppress one's own anger') is a very common phrase in Hindi advice about self-control, especially in family or workplace contexts where showing emotion openly is discouraged.
Sample Sentences:
वह अपना गुस्सा दबाता है और कुछ नहीं कहता। He suppresses his anger and says nothing.

Sense 3 : to pressure, dominate, or suppress a person/group
verb, transitive Used with human or social objects to describe one party overpowering or intimidating another, often in political, social, or interpersonal contexts.
Synonyms: सताना (sataanaa, to torment), दबाव में लेना (dabaav men lenaa, to put under pressure)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb behind common social commentary like ताकतवर कमज़ोर को दबाते हैं (taakatvar kamzor ko dabaate hain, 'the powerful oppress the weak') -- it frames power as literally 'pressing down' on someone, echoing the physical sense of the word.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense can sound stronger than intended -- कमज़ोर को दबाना (kamzor ko dabaanaa, 'to oppress the weak') implies real domination, not mild persuasion, so learners should not use it loosely for ordinary convincing.
Sample Sentences:
ताकतवर हमेशा कमज़ोर को दबाते हैं। The powerful always oppress the weak.
(taakatvar hameshaa kamzor ko dabaate hain।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
