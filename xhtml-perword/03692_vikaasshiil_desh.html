विकासशील देश (vikaasshiil desh)

noun A compound noun phrase meaning 'developing country' or 'developing nation,' combining विकासशील (vikaasshiil, 'developing') with देश (desh, 'country'); has one straightforward sense used in news, economics, and civics contexts.
Sense 1 : developing country
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विकासमान देश (vikaasmaan desh, rare), उभरता हुआ देश (ubhartaa huaa desh, 'emerging country')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is bookish, textbook-and-newspaper vocabulary -- you will meet it in civics class, economics articles, and government speeches about भारत (bhaarat, 'India') as a विकासशील देश (vikaasshiil desh). It pairs naturally with its opposite, विकसित देश (viksit desh, 'developed country'), and knowing both together is the easiest way to keep them straight.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say विकसित देश (viksit desh) by mistake when they mean 'developing country' -- but विकसित (viksit) means 'developed' (already advanced), the opposite of विकासशील (vikaasshiil) 'developing' (still advancing). Mixing these up completely reverses the meaning. 2) The word order is fixed as adjective-then-noun, just like English 'developing country' -- learners should not try to reorder it as देश विकासशील (desh vikaasshiil).
Sample Sentences:
भारत एक विकासशील देश है। India is a developing country.
(bhaarat ek vikaasshiil desh hai।)
