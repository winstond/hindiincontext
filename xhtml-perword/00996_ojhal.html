ओझल (ojhal)

adjective An invariant adjective meaning 'out of sight, vanished from view' -- used almost exclusively with होना (honaa) or the causative frame with करना/कर देना (karnaa/kar denaa).
Sense 1 : out of sight, vanished from view
adjective Almost always appears in the fixed frame X ओझल होना (X ojhal honaa, 'X to disappear from view') -- something moves out of sight, often gradually, like a person walking away or the sun setting behind clouds.
Synonyms: गायब (gaayab), लुप्त (lupt, literary), अदृश्य (adrishy, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture standing on a railway platform watching the train pull away -- ट्रेन आँखों से ओझल हो गई (tren aankhon se ojhal ho gaii, 'the train disappeared from sight') captures that specific fading-from-view feeling. It pairs naturally with आँखों से (aankhon se, 'from the eyes') to anchor whose viewpoint is losing sight of the thing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse ओझल (ojhal) with गायब (gaayab) -- गायब (gaayab) suggests a thing has vanished/gone missing altogether (often unexpectedly), while ओझल (ojhal) specifically describes something passing out of the range of sight, often while the speaker watches it happen (like a train leaving the platform). 2) It does not mean 'lost' in the sense of misplaced belongings -- use खो जाना (kho jaanaa) for that.
