पूर्वज (poorvaj)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'ancestor, forefather' -- used mainly in formal, historical, or reverential contexts rather than everyday chit-chat.
Sense 1 : ancestor, forefather
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पूर्वज (poorvaj) (invariant across singular/plural in form, though gender/number shows on verb agreement), बुज़ुर्ग (buzurg, usually living elders, not ancestors), पितर (pitar, literary/ritual, especially in the context of ancestor worship)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries a certain formal or reverential weight -- you will hear it in speeches, textbooks, or when someone talks about family heritage and tradition, as in हमारे पूर्वज (hamaare poorvaj, 'our ancestors'). It pairs naturally with the idea of पितृ-पक्ष (pitri-paksh), the period in the Hindu calendar dedicated to honoring one's ancestors, though पूर्वज (poorvaj) itself is a general, everyday-formal word, not a ritual term.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use बुज़ुर्ग (buzurg) to mean 'ancestor,' but बुज़ुर्ग (buzurg) usually refers to living or recently-living elders (grandparents, senior relatives), while पूर्वज (poorvaj) specifically means those from past generations, often long deceased. 2) The plural is used very frequently -- हमारे पूर्वज (hamaare poorvaj, 'our ancestors') is far more common in real speech than the singular, since people rarely talk about just one ancestor.
Sample Sentences:
हमारे पूर्वज इस गाँव में सदियों पहले बसे थे। Our ancestors settled in this village centuries ago.
(hamaare poorvaj is gaanv men sadiyon pahle base the।)
