मिट्टी का तेल (mittii kaa tel)

noun A compound noun meaning 'kerosene' -- literally 'earth's oil,' from मिट्टी (mittii, 'earth/soil/clay') plus तेल (tel, 'oil'), joined by का (kaa).
Sense 1 : kerosene
noun, masculine
Synonyms: किरासन तेल (kiraasan tel, regional variant), केरोसिन (kerosin, loanword, less common)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Before piped gas and electricity were common in Indian homes, मिट्टी का तेल (mittii kaa tel) was the fuel for the तेल का दीया (tel kaa diiyaa, 'oil lamp') and the स्टोव (stov, 'stove') in many households, and government-subsidized kerosene rations (राशन, raashan) are still a memory for older generations. You will still hear shopkeepers in older Delhi neighborhoods refer to it this way when selling it by the liter for lamps or camping stoves.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to translate word-for-word as 'mud oil' or 'clay oil,' missing that मिट्टी का तेल (mittii kaa tel) is simply the standard Hindi word for kerosene, with no earthy connotation intended. 2) The का (kaa) here is fixed and does not change with the gender of तेल (tel) since तेल (tel) is masculine -- learners familiar with का/की/के agreement should note the whole phrase behaves as one lexical unit.
