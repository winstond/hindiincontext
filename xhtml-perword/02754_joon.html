जून (joon)

noun This word has one meaning: the month of June, borrowed from the English calendar.
Sense 1 : the month of June
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: जून (joon) marks peak Delhi-NCR heat, right before the monsoon breaks in July -- you will hear people say जून की गर्मी (joon kii garmii, 'June's heat') with real feeling, since this is when the loo (hot dry wind) is at its worst.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like all Hindi month names, जून (joon) does not inflect for the oblique case before postpositions in casual speech the way most masculine nouns do -- learners often try to write जून (joon) as if it needed an oblique form (e.g. before में, men) and end up over-modifying it; it simply stays जून (joon) in जून में (joon men, 'in June').
