माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa)

noun A compound noun meaning 'parents' -- literally 'mother-father' -- built from माता (maataa, mother) and पिता (pitaa, father). Single sense, always used in this fixed pair form.
Sense 1 : parents
noun, masculine Grammatically plural, agreeing with plural verb forms even though it is a fixed dvandva (pair) compound rather than a true plural noun.
Synonyms: अभिभावक (abhibhaavak, guardians/parents, formal), मां-बाप (maan-baap, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa) is the standard, respectful, everyday way to say 'parents' in Hindi -- more neutral and slightly more formal-sounding than मां-बाप (maan-baap), which is common in casual speech and carries a warmer, more colloquial flavor. Respect for माता-पिता (maataa-pitaa) is a deeply embedded value in Indian family life, often invoked in schools, festivals, and everyday moral conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to split and inflect माता (maataa) and पिता (pitaa) separately, but the compound is treated as a single unit taking plural agreement as a whole, e.g. मेरे माता-पिता आए (mere maataa-pitaa aae, 'my parents came'). 2) Because it looks singular in form (no plural marker), learners forget it needs plural verb and adjective agreement, not singular.
