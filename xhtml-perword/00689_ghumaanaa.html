घुमाना (ghumaanaa)

verb The causative of घूमना (ghoomnaa, 'to roam/turn'): covers turning or rotating something, and taking someone/something around/for a stroll.
Sense 1 : to turn, rotate, twist (something)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: मोड़ना (modnaa, to bend/turn), फिराना (phiraanaa, to spin/rotate)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a rickshaw driver saying हैंडल घुमाओ (haindal ghumaao, 'turn the handlebar') -- घुमाना (ghumaanaa) is your go-to verb for any circular or rotating motion, from a ceiling fan to a doorknob.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मोड़ना (modnaa) in every 'turn' context, but घुमाना (ghumaanaa) suits full rotation or spinning (like a key, wheel, or fan), while मोड़ना (modnaa) is better for bending or changing direction (like turning a corner). 2) With body parts, गर्दन घुमाना (gardan ghumaanaa, 'to turn one's head/neck') is the natural idiom -- learners sometimes use मोड़ना (modnaa) here, which sounds off.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा हैंडल घुमाओ, गाड़ी मोड़नी है। Turn the handlebar a bit, I need to turn the vehicle.
(zaraa haindal ghumaao, gaadii modnii hai।)

Sense 2 : to take someone around, show someone around (sightseeing, a stroll)
verb, transitive This sense takes a person as its object plus a place, e.g. उसने मुझे बाज़ार घुमाया (usne mujhe baazaar ghumaayaa, 'he took me around the market').
Synonyms: टहलाना (tahlaanaa, to take for a short walk), सैर कराना (sair karaanaa, to take on an outing)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic host's promise in India: हम आपको पूरी दिल्ली घुमाएँगे (ham aapko poorii dillii ghumaaenge, 'we'll show you all around Delhi') -- said to guests with genuine pride, since hosting and touring guests around is a point of honor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say ले जाना (le jaanaa) when they mean a leisurely tour, but घुमाना (ghumaanaa) specifically implies wandering around for pleasure, not just transporting someone from A to B.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to swindle, deceive, run someone around (figurative)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बहलाना (bahlaanaa, to distract/fob off), चकमा देना (chakmaa denaa, to trick)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly about government offices: दफ्तर वाले बस घुमा रहे हैं (daphtar vaale bas ghumaa rahe hain, 'the office people are just stringing me along') -- a very Delhi complaint about being sent from desk to desk without getting anything done.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense (as in किसी को घुमाना (kisii ko ghumaanaa), 'to string someone along') is common in colloquial complaints about bureaucracy or bad service -- learners often miss this idiom and take घुमाना (ghumaanaa) too literally in such contexts.
Sample Sentences:
