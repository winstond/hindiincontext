फुफेरा भाई (phupheraa bhaaii)

noun A kinship term meaning 'paternal aunt's son' -- a specific type of cousin, distinguished from other cousin-types in Hindi's kinship system.
Sense 1 : cousin (son of one's father's sister)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: फुफेरा भाई (phupheraa bhaaii), फुफेरे भाई (phuphere bhaaii, oblique/plural form used before another noun or postposition)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi kinship terms are famously precise: every kind of cousin, uncle, and aunt gets its own word depending on which side of the family and which sibling they descend from. फुफेरा (phupheraa) derives from फूफा (phoophaa), your father's sister's husband (your paternal aunt's husband), showing how these cousin-terms are built directly off the relevant uncle/aunt term. In everyday joint-family life in North India, फुफेरे भाई-बहन (phuphere bhaaii-bahan, 'cousins from the paternal aunt's side') often grow up as close as siblings, especially around festivals like राखी (raakhii) and शादी (shaadii) season when the whole extended family gathers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to English 'cousin' and miss that Hindi has no single word for it -- you must specify which type; फुफेरा भाई (phupheraa bhaaii) is specifically the son of your पापा (paapaa)'s sister, not your माँ (maan)'s sister (that would be मौसेरा भाई (mauseraa bhaaii)) or your पापा's brother's son (चचेरा भाई (chacheraa bhaaii)) or your माँ's brother's son (ममेरा भाई (mameraa bhaaii)). 2) The first part फुफेरा (phupheraa) agrees in gender/number with भाई (bhaaii) -- the female counterpart is फुफेरी बहन (phupherii bahan), not फुफेरा बहन.
