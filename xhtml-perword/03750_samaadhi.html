समाधि (samaadhi)

noun Primarily a term from yoga and meditation for a deep, absorbed spiritual state, but also very commonly used for the memorial/tomb built at the site where a revered person (like a leader or saint) was cremated or buried.
Sense 1 : memorial/tomb of a revered person
noun, feminine
Synonyms: मक़बरा (maqabraa, typically for Muslim tombs), स्मारक (smaarak, general memorial, broader meaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If you visit Delhi, you will see this word on signposts everywhere: राजघाट (raajghaat) is Mahatma Gandhi's समाधि (samaadhi), and nearby are the समाधियाँ (samaadhiyaan) of other leaders like Nehru (शांति वन, shaanti van) and Indira Gandhi. Calling these स्थल (sthal) 'graves' would feel off in Hindi -- समाधि (samaadhi) carries a note of reverence and national memory that a plain grave word lacks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners may confuse समाधि (samaadhi) with क़ब्र (qabr, 'grave') -- समाधि (samaadhi) specifically implies a dignified, often visited memorial site for someone considered great, not an ordinary grave.

Sense 2 : deep meditative/spiritual absorption
noun, feminine
Synonyms: ध्यान (dhyaan, broader term for meditation), तल्लीनता (talliintaa, absorption)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same समाधि (samaadhi) you meet in yoga philosophy as the eighth and final limb of Patanjali's ashtanga yoga -- the state beyond dhyana (dhyaan) where the meditator and the object of meditation dissolve into one. In everyday speech, you will hear it used a bit loosely too, as in किसी की समाधि टूट गई (kisii kii samaadhi toot gaii, 'someone's trance broke'), often half-jokingly about someone lost in deep thought.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use समाधि (samaadhi) interchangeably with ध्यान (dhyaan), but ध्यान (dhyaan) is the practice/act of meditating, while समाधि (samaadhi) is the deep absorbed state that (ideally) results from it -- they are not simply synonyms. 2) The common verb pairing is समाधि लगाना (samaadhi lagaanaa, 'to enter/attain a trance') for the one meditating, or समाधि लगना (samaadhi lagnaa) for the state occurring -- learners often reach for a more literal verb like करना (karnaa) instead.
