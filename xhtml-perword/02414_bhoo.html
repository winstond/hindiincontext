भू (bhoo)

noun A literary/classical word for 'earth, land, ground'; in everyday speech learners will meet it mostly inside compound words like भूकंप (bhookamp, 'earthquake') or भूमि (bhoomi, 'land') rather than standing alone.
Sense 1 : earth, land, ground
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भूमि (bhoomi), धरती (dhartii), ज़मीन (zamiin)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This is a classical Sanskrit-root word that survives in Hindi mainly as a building block: think of it as the 'earth-' prefix in English words like 'geo-'. You will see and hear it constantly inside compounds, but a Delhi speaker chatting about land or ground will reach for ज़मीन (zamiin) or धरती (dhartii) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use भू (bhoo) as a standalone noun in conversation the way they would use ज़मीन (zamiin) or धरती (dhartii); in practice it almost always appears bound to another word as the first part of a compound, such as भूकंप (bhookamp, 'earthquake') or भूगोल (bhoogol, 'geography'), rather than alone in a sentence.
