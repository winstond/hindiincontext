गाली (gaalii)

noun A feminine noun meaning a curse word, swear word, or verbal abuse; it appears constantly in the fixed phrase गाली देना (gaalii denaa), 'to swear at/abuse someone.'
Sense 1 : swear word, abusive/obscene word
noun, feminine Almost always used with देना (denaa) as its verb: गाली देना (gaalii denaa, 'to hurl abuse') or गाली सुनना (gaalii sunnaa, 'to receive/hear abuse').
Synonyms: अपशब्द (apshabd, formal), गंदी बात (gandii baat, colloquial, euphemistic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In Delhi especially, गाली-गलौज (gaalii-galauj) is a whole social register of its own -- friends among close (usually male) circles trade गालियाँ (gaaliyaan) almost affectionately, while the same words directed at a stranger or elder are a serious insult. Context and relationship decide everything here, more than the word itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try 'बोलना (bolnaa)' with गाली (gaalii), but native speakers overwhelmingly pair it with देना (denaa) -- गाली बोलना (gaalii bolnaa) sounds off. 2) गाली is singular in form but frequently used in the plural, गालियाँ (gaaliyaan), when someone is on a real cursing streak -- उसने गालियाँ दी (usne gaaliyaan dii, 'he swore repeatedly') is more natural than the singular in many contexts.
