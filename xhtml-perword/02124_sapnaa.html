सपना (sapnaa)

noun/verb Mainly a noun meaning 'dream' (both the sleep kind and the aspiration kind); it also has a much rarer intransitive verb use meaning 'to appear in a dream, to dream'.
Sense 1 : dream (during sleep)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: स्वप्न (svapn, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday speech सपना (sapnaa) almost always shows up inside the phrase सपना देखना (sapnaa dekhnaa) -- literally 'to see a dream' -- which is the standard way to talk about dreaming, whether at night or about your future. The literary word स्वप्न (svapn) means the same thing but belongs more to poetry, songs, and philosophical writing than to kitchen-table conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) The verb 'to dream' is expressed with सपना देखना (sapnaa dekhnaa, 'to see a dream'), not a single verb -- learners often try to say सपना करना (sapnaa karnaa) by analogy with English, which is wrong. 2) The dreamer is the subject who 'sees' the dream, so the sentence takes ने (ne) marking in the past: मैंने सपना देखा (mainne sapnaa dekhaa, 'I had a dream'), not मुझे (mujhe)-marked as one might expect from other involuntary-experience verbs.

Sense 2 : dream, aspiration (figurative)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आकांक्षा (aakaankshaa, formal), ख़्वाब (khvaab)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the सपना (sapnaa) of Bollywood songs and motivational talk -- 'बड़े सपने देखो' (bade sapne dekho, 'dream big') is a phrase every Hindi-speaking parent or teacher has said at some point. It sits right alongside ख़्वाब (khvaab), a near-synonym borrowed from Urdu poetic tradition that carries a slightly more romantic or wistful color, often heard in ghazals and film lyrics.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for इच्छा (ichchhaa, 'wish/desire') where सपना (sapnaa) is more natural for a long-term life goal like 'my dream is to become a doctor' -- सपना (sapnaa) carries a stronger sense of an imagined future self, not just a want.

Sense 3 : to appear in a dream
verb, intransitive Used impersonally with a को (ko)-marked experiencer: किसी को सपनाना (kisii ko sapnaanaa, 'to appear in someone's dream'), the reverse construction of सपना देखना (sapnaa dekhnaa).
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This verb form सपनाना (sapnaanaa) is rare in ordinary conversation -- native speakers overwhelmingly prefer the noun-plus-verb phrase सपने में आना (sapne men aanaa, 'to come in a dream') or सपना देखना (sapnaa dekhnaa) instead. It survives mainly in poetry and song lyrics.
