थूकना (thooknaa)

verb A concrete physical verb meaning 'to spit,' used both literally and in a common figurative idiom about going back on one's word.
Sense 1 : to spit (saliva)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Spitting in public -- especially of paan or tobacco juice -- is a common enough sight in India that थूकना (thooknaa) comes up more in everyday warnings and civic signage (थूकना मना है, thooknaa manaa hai, 'spitting is prohibited') than you might expect for such a basic verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget this is intransitive when there's no object mentioned -- मैं थूका (main thookaa, 'I spat') takes no ने, unlike थूकना's transitive use with an object such as थूक थूकना (thook thooknaa, 'to spit saliva') which does take ने in the perfective. 2) Don't confuse with छींकना (chhiinknaa, 'to sneeze') -- both are bodily-expulsion verbs but mean very different things.

Sense 2 : to spit (something) out, expel from the mouth
verb, transitive Takes a direct object -- what is being spat out -- and behaves as an ordinary transitive verb with ने-marking in the perfective.
Synonyms: उगलना (ugalnaa, to spit/cough up)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this with चबाया हुआ पान थूकना (chabaayaa huaa paan thooknaa, 'to spit out chewed paan') or बीज थूकना (biij thooknaa, 'to spit out a seed') -- the object being expelled is what distinguishes this from the plain intransitive sense of just spitting.

Sense 3 : figurative: to go back on one's word, retract a promise (as in थूक कर चाटना)
verb, transitive Lives almost entirely in the fixed idiom थूक कर चाटना (thook kar chaatnaa, 'to spit and then lick it up') -- describing someone who breaks a promise and then shamelessly returns to it.
Synonyms: मुकरना (mukarnaa, to renege)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: थूक कर चाटना (thook kar chaatnaa, 'to spit and lick it up') is a vivid, slightly earthy insult used for someone who shamelessly reverses a firm public stance -- the image of licking up your own spit captures the disgust better than any English equivalent.
Common Mistakes: 1) This meaning doesn't stand alone as a normal transitive sentence -- it's really only recognized inside the idiom थूक कर चाटना (thook kar chaatnaa); using थूकना (thooknaa) alone won't convey 'go back on one's word' to a listener.
