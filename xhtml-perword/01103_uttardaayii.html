उत्तरदायी (uttardaayii)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'responsible/accountable,' used mainly in written, official, or careful spoken registers rather than casual conversation. Related to उत्तरदायित्व (uttardaayitv, 'responsibility'), the noun form of the same idea.
Sense 1 : responsible, accountable (for something)
adjective Takes के लिए (ke lie) to mark what one is responsible for, or का (kaa) before a following noun of accountability, e.g. इस गलती का उत्तरदायी (is galtii kaa uttardaayii, 'responsible for this mistake').
Synonyms: जिम्मेदार (jimmedaar), ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedaar, variant form), दोषी (doshii, guilty, narrower)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in Hindi news broadcasts and government language -- 'सरकार जवाबदेह और उत्तरदायी होनी चाहिए' (sarkaar javaabdeh aur uttardaayii honii chaahie, 'the government should be answerable and responsible') is the kind of sentence that shows up in editorials. In daily speech at home or with friends, जिम्मेदार (jimmedaar) is what people actually reach for.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to जिम्मेदार (jimmedaar) in all contexts, which is correct, but should recognize उत्तरदायी (uttardaayii) as its more formal twin, common in news reports, official statements, and essays rather than everyday chat. 2) Some learners try को (ko) to mark the cause instead of के लिए (ke lie) -- के लिए (ke lie) is the standard choice here.
