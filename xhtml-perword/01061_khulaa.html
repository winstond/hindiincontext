खुला (khulaa)

verb/adjective This is the masculine singular perfective form of the verb खुलना (khulnaa, 'to open/become open'), used both as a finite past-tense verb and as a common adjective meaning 'open.'
Sense 1 : to open, become open, come undone (past tense)
verb, intransitive Masculine singular perfective form of खुलना (khulnaa); agrees with a masculine singular subject: दरवाज़ा खुला (darvaazaa khulaa, 'the door opened').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi carefully distinguishes things that open on their own (खुलना, khulnaa) from things someone deliberately opens (खोलना, kholnaa) -- a distinction English collapses into one verb 'open.' This intransitive/transitive pair pattern (खुलना/खोलना) recurs across many Hindi verbs, so getting comfortable with this one pays off elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use ने (ne) with खुला (khulaa) by analogy with transitive verbs, but खुलना (khulnaa) is intransitive and never takes ने (ne) -- the subject itself is treated as undergoing the action, not performing it on something else. 2) खुला (khulaa) means 'opened by itself/became open,' while 'X opened Y' requires the separate transitive verb खोलना (kholnaa), e.g. उसने दरवाज़ा खोला (usne darvaazaa kholaa, 'he opened the door').
Sample Sentences:

(darvaazaa apne aap khulaa।)

Sense 2 : open, unlocked, uncovered
adjective Inflects like a regular -आ adjective: खुला/खुले/खुली (khulaa/khule/khulii) agreeing with the noun it modifies, e.g. खुली खिड़की (khulii khidkii, 'open window').
Synonyms: अनावृत (anaavrit, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear खुला (khulaa) everywhere in daily life -- खुली हवा (khulii havaa, 'open/fresh air'), खुला मैदान (khulaa maidaan, 'open field'), and even खुला पैसा (khulaa paisaa, 'loose change/small denomination money'), since खुला (khulaa) can extend metaphorically to money that is 'broken down' into smaller units rather than one large note.

Sense 3 : free, uninhibited, frank (of a person, manner, or relationship)
adjective
Synonyms: स्पष्टवादी (spashtvaadii, of a person's speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: खुला (khulaa) describing a person or their nature -- as in खुले विचारों वाला (khule vichaaron vaalaa, 'open-minded') -- carries approval in modern urban Hindi, praising someone as broad-minded and unrestrained by orthodoxy, much like 'open-minded' in English.
