चुस्ती (chustii)

noun Mainly means physical briskness/agility or alertness, with an extended sense of smartness/sharpness in manner; also has a tailoring-related sense of 'tightness/snugness' of clothing.
Sense 1 : briskness, agility, alertness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: फुर्ती (phurtii), स्फूर्ति (sphoorti, literary), तेज़ी (tezii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear चुस्ती-फुर्ती (chustii-phurtii) constantly in health and fitness contexts -- from a grandmother praising a grandchild's energy to an ad for a tonic promising to keep you 'chusti-phurti' well into old age. It carries a warm, approving tone, like complimenting someone's get-up-and-go.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तेज़ी (tezii) when they mean speed of movement, but चुस्ती (chustii) is broader -- it covers alert, lively readiness of body and mind, not just fast motion. 2) The set phrase चुस्ती-फुर्ती (chustii-phurtii) is very common in everyday speech to describe someone's overall physical liveliness; learners tend to use चुस्ती (chustii) alone where a native speaker would pair it.

Sense 2 : sharpness, smartness (of manner or mind)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: होशियारी (hoshiyaarii), फुर्तीलापन (phurtiilaapan, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Applied to a person's manner, चुस्ती (chustii) suggests someone who is on-the-ball -- quick to respond, quick to notice, quick to act -- the kind of employee a boss in Delhi's office culture would praise as 'बहुत चुस्त है' (bahut chust hai, 'is very sharp/on top of things').

Sense 3 : tightness, snugness (of clothing/fit)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कसावट (kasaavat)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: A tailor in a Delhi market might use चुस्ती (chustii) to describe how snugly a kurta or blouse fits -- this sense shares its root with the adjective चुस्त (chust, 'tight-fitting'), just as the agility sense shares the same root idea of something held taut and ready, not loose or slack.
