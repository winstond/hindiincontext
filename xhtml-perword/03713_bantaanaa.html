बँटाना (bantaanaa)

verb The regular causative of बँटना (bantnaa, 'to get divided/distributed'), meaning 'to have (something) divided or distributed' by someone else. Distinct from बाँटना (baantnaa, 'to divide/distribute' directly) and from बटाना (bataanaa, the unrelated causative of बटना, 'to have weighed').
Sense 1 : to have (something) divided or distributed
verb, transitive Causative of बँटना (bantnaa); the underlying doer of the actual dividing is marked with से (se), e.g. 'नौकर से' (naukar se, 'by/through the servant'), while the subject is the one who causes or arranges the division.
Synonyms: बँटवाना (bantvaanaa, variant form)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This verb shows up when talking about property, land, or inherited wealth being split up through someone's arrangement -- for instance a family elder getting the ancestral land बँटाना (bantaanaa) among the brothers rather than dividing it personally. It is a lower-frequency word, so learners are far more likely to encounter बाँटना (baantnaa) in daily speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with बाँटना (baantnaa), which means to directly divide or distribute something oneself; बँटाना (bantaanaa) instead means to get it divided by someone else, or to cause a division to happen. 2) It is easy to misread or mistype as बटाना (bataanaa), a completely different causative (of बटना, related to twisting/weighing), so the nasalization (chandrabindu) on बँ is important to notice.
