पैंसठ (painsath)

numeral The number sixty-five (painsath), used for counting and quantities like any other numeral.
Sense 1 : the number 65
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Numbers like पैंसठ (painsath) come up constantly in everyday haggling at the market, quoting ages, or reading prices, so it's worth drilling the sixties (साठ (saath), इकसठ (iksath), बासठ (baasath), ...) as a set rather than trying to derive them logically, since Hindi's number system from 21 to 99 doesn't compound the way English does.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पैंसठ (painsath, 65) with similar-sounding numbers in the sixties like साठ (saath, 60) or पचपन (pachpan, 55) -- Hindi numerals 21-99 are irregular and don't follow a predictable tens-plus-units pattern like English, so each one has to be learned individually. 2) The nasalization (chandrabindu) in पैंसठ (painsath) is easy to drop in writing or speech; leaving it out can make it look like a typo rather than a different number.
