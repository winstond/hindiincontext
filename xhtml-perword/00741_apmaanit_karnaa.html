अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa)

verb A compound expression meaning 'to insult' or 'to humiliate' -- literally 'to do/make insulted', built from the adjective अपमानित (apmaanit, 'insulted, disgraced') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'). One core sense, used for deliberate acts of insulting someone.
Sense 1 : to insult, to humiliate someone
verb, transitive Takes the person insulted as a direct object, often marked with को: उसको अपमानित करना (usko apmaanit karnaa, 'to insult him/her'). In the past tense this behaves as an ordinary transitive verb and takes ने on the subject, since करना (karnaa) is the finite verb carrying the action.
Synonyms: बेइज़्ज़त करना (beizzat karnaa), ज़लील करना (zaliil karnaa, stronger, more cutting), बदनाम करना (badnaam karnaa, to defame/malign rather than directly insult), अपमान करना (apmaan karnaa, one-word-noun variant using का instead of adjective form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Hindi social life, insult and honor (इज़्ज़त, izzat) are taken very seriously, so अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa) is a fairly strong, formal-register phrase -- you'll hear it in serious conversations, news reports, or arguments about someone's dignity being violated, rather than in casual teasing among friends, where बेइज़्ज़त करना (beizzat karnaa) or just चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa, 'to tease') might be more natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes say अपमान करना (apmaan karnaa) and अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa) as if fully interchangeable in every context -- अपमान करना (apmaan karnaa) treats 'insult' as a noun-object ('to do an insult'), while अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa) treats it as a result-state ('to make insulted'); both are common and largely overlap in meaning, but native speakers often reach for अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa) when describing the effect on the person, and अपमान करना (apmaanit karnaa) when describing the act itself. 2) Do not confuse with अपमानित होना (apmaanit honaa, 'to be/feel insulted'), the passive/experiential counterpart -- होना (honaa) marks the person as the one undergoing insult, not carrying it out.
