चौवन (chauvan)

numeral The cardinal number 54 (fifty-four).
Sense 1 : the number fifty-four
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers between 21 and 99, चौवन (chauvan) is just something you memorize as its own word rather than calculate -- native speakers do not mentally decompose it into 'four' plus 'fifty' any more than English speakers parse 'eleven' letter by letter. You will meet it most often in ages, prices, and phone-related contexts (like the last digits of a number) rather than in abstract math talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build this compositionally from चार (chaar, 'four') and पचास (pachaas, 'fifty') the way English does; Hindi numerals from 21-99 are mostly memorized as single fused words rather than derived on the fly, and चौवन (chauvan) is one of these irregular fusions rather than a predictable combination.
