महसूस (mahsoos)

adjective Almost always seen in the compound महसूस करना (mahsoos karnaa, 'to feel') and महसूस होना (mahsoos honaa, 'to be felt'); the word itself is a borrowed adjective/participle meaning 'felt, perceived' and rarely stands alone.
Sense 1 : felt, perceived (as in 'to feel/experience something')
adjective Functions as the fixed complement in the compounds महसूस करना (mahsoos karnaa, 'to feel/sense') and महसूस होना (mahsoos honaa, 'to be felt/seem'); महसूस itself does not inflect.
Synonyms: अनुभूत (anubhoot, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: महसूस (mahsoos) comes from Arabic/Persian via Urdu, and is one of the most common Urdu-origin words in everyday Hindi -- you will hear it constantly in Delhi speech, songs, and news alike, with no sense of it being a 'foreign' word. Notice the parallel with लगना (lagnaa): both put the feeler in the को (ko) slot, reflecting the Hindi habit of framing emotions and sensations as things that happen *to* you rather than things you actively do.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use महसूस (mahsoos) as a standalone verb; it always needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to function as a verb -- 'मुझे दर्द महसूस' (mujhe dard mahsoos) is incomplete, you need मुझे दर्द महसूस हुआ (mujhe dard mahsoos huaa, 'I felt pain'). 2) The subject of feeling is marked with को (ko), not से or ने -- मुझे ऐसा महसूस होता है (mujhe aisaa mahsoos hotaa hai, 'it feels this way to me'), following the same dative-subject pattern as लगना (lagnaa).

मुझे बहुत अकेलापन महसूस हो रहा है। I am feeling very lonely.
(mujhe bahut akelaapan mahsoos ho rahaa hai।)
