झुग्गी (jhuggii)

noun A single everyday sense: a makeshift shack or slum dwelling, most often heard in the plural/collective phrase झुग्गी-झोपड़ी (jhuggii-jhopdii).
Sense 1 : makeshift shack, slum dwelling
noun, feminine
Synonyms: झोपड़ी (jhopdii), झोंपड़ी (jhompdii, variant form), कच्चा मकान (kachchaa makaan, improvised phrase, less specific)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Delhi-NCR, झुग्गी (jhuggii) is the everyday word for the informal settlements you see along railway lines, under flyovers, or on the edges of colonies -- it carries real social and political weight, showing up constantly in news reports about झुग्गी बस्ती (jhuggii bastii, 'slum settlement') demolitions and resettlement. It is descriptive rather than a slur, but it is not a word you'd use casually about someone's home to their face.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat झुग्गी (jhuggii) as a neutral synonym for घर (ghar, 'house') -- it specifically implies a poor, informally built dwelling, often on unauthorized or government land, so using it for an ordinary home sounds odd or even insulting. 2) The common compound झुग्गी-झोपड़ी (jhuggii-jhopdii) is usually used as a collective/plural concept (referring to a slum cluster or slum-dwellers as a class), not as a simple plural of one hut.
