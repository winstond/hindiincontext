मेला लगना (melaa lagnaa)

verb A fixed collocation meaning 'for a fair/market/gathering to be held or set up' -- मेला (melaa, fair) pairs with लगना (lagnaa) rather than होना (honaa) because the fair is conceived as something that 'gets set up' at a place, the same logic seen in बाज़ार लगना (baazaar lagnaa, for a market to be set up).
Sense 1 : for a fair or festive gathering to be held/set up
verb, intransitive मेला (melaa) is the grammatical subject and agrees with लगना (lagnaa); the place takes पर (par) or में (men), e.g. दिल्ली में मेला लगता है (dillii men melaa lagtaa hai, a fair is held in Delhi).
Synonyms: मेला होना (melaa honaa, less idiomatic, less commonly used this way)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you'll hear about village and neighborhood मेले (mele) that spring up around festivals like दशहरा (dashahraa) or पूर्णिमा (poornimaa) -- ask any Delhi-NCR local about the historic Dussehra मेला (melaa) grounds and they'll immediately picture stalls, Ferris wheels, and jalebi vendors 'लगना' (lagnaa) overnight. The same लगना (lagnaa) pattern shows up in बाज़ार लगना (baazaar lagnaa, a market being set up) and भीड़ लगना (bhiid lagnaa, a crowd gathering), so learning this one collocation unlocks a whole family of 'gathering/event happens' expressions.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मेला होना (melaa honaa) by analogy with English 'there is a fair,' but native speakers overwhelmingly say मेला लगना (melaa lagnaa) -- लगना (lagnaa) captures the sense of stalls and crowds physically 'setting up' at a spot. 2) The time or occasion is often marked with पर (par), as in दीवाली पर मेला लगता है (diivaalii par melaa lagtaa hai, a fair is held on Diwali), which learners sometimes replace incorrectly with को (ko).
Sample Sentences:
हर साल दशहरे पर यहाँ मेला लगता है। Every year a fair is held here on Dussehra.
(har saal dashahre par yahaan melaa lagtaa hai।)

(abhii maidaan men melaa lag rahaa hai, chalo dekhne chalte hain।)

क्या इस साल भी दशहरे का मेला लगेगा? Will the Dussehra fair be held this year as well?
