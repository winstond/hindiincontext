बिंदु (bindu)

noun A noun meaning 'point' or 'dot' -- used both literally (a mark or geometric point) and abstractly (a point in a discussion, an item on a list, a specific moment or aspect).
Sense 1 : a point, item, or aspect in a discussion/argument
noun, masculine Very common in formal speech and writing when listing items: पहला बिंदु (pahlaa bindu, 'the first point'), अगला बिंदु (aglaa bindu, 'the next point').
Synonyms: मुद्दा (muddaa, issue/topic), विषय (vishay, topic, broader)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Indian news debates and office meetings -- अगले बिंदु पर आते हैं (agle bindu par aate hain, 'let's move to the next point') is a stock phrase for chairing a discussion.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for मुद्दा (muddaa) here, which works but carries more weight (a 'matter' or 'issue') -- बिंदु (bindu) is the neutral word for a listed point or item in a structured discussion, common in meetings, essays, and news reports.
Sample Sentences:

(agle bindu par aate hain।)

Sense 2 : a dot or point (mark, geometric point)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चिह्न (chihn, mark, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the little dot above certain letters in Devanagari, or the bindi (bindii) worn on the forehead -- both share this root, since बिंदी (bindii) is literally a small बिंदु (bindu).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with टिपका (tipkaa) or धब्बा (dhabbaa), which refer to a smudge or stain rather than a precise mark -- बिंदु (bindu) is neutral and often technical/geometric.
Sample Sentences:
इस रेखा पर एक बिंदु बनाओ। Draw a point on this line.
(is rekhaa par ek bindu banaao।)

Sense 3 : a specific moment, stage, or juncture (abstract point in time/situation)
noun, masculine Appears in fixed expressions like इस बिंदु पर (is bindu par, 'at this point/juncture').
Synonyms: पड़ाव (padaav, stage/halt, more concrete), चरण (charan, phase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: इस बिंदु पर (is bindu par, 'at this juncture') is the kind of phrase you will hear in a formal speech or a serious conversation, roughly parallel to English 'at this point in time' -- it carries a slightly weighty, deliberate tone rather than casual chat.
Sample Sentences:
इस बिंदु पर हमें बहुत सावधानी से आगे बढ़ना है। At this juncture, we need to proceed very carefully.
