क़िस्त (qist)

noun A financial/administrative term for a partial payment made as part of a series -- an 'installment.' Also commonly seen spelled without the nuqta as किस्त (kist).
Sense 1 : installment (of a payment or loan)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in everyday Indian financial life -- EMI ads on TV literally say आसान क़िस्तों में (aasaan qiston men, 'in easy installments'), and shopkeepers selling appliances or scooters on credit will ask कितनी क़िस्तें चाहिए? (kitnii qisten chaahie?, 'how many installments do you want?'). You will also hear it in family conversations about school fees or loan repayments, so it is a genuinely high-utility word for daily life in India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say पैसा (paisaa) generically for 'payment' when क़िस्त (qist) is specifically one scheduled part of a larger sum -- e.g. मकान की क़िस्त (makaan kii qist, 'the installment for the house') implies a recurring payment plan, not a one-off amount. 2) The verb that pairs with it is usually भरना (bharnaa) or देना (denaa) -- 'to pay an installment' is क़िस्त भरना (qist bharnaa), not the more literal-sounding pay-word learners might reach for.
Sample Sentences:
यह टीवी आसान क़िस्तों में मिल जाएगा। This TV will be available in easy installments.
(yah tiivii aasaan qiston men mil jaaegaa।)
