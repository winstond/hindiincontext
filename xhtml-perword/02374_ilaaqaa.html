इलाक़ा (ilaaqaa)

noun A single, everyday sense: a geographic or administrative 'area, region, locality.' Also spelled इलाका (ilaakaa) without the nuqta, especially in casual writing.
Sense 1 : area, region, locality, territory
noun, masculine
Synonyms: क्षेत्र (kshetr), जगह (jagah, less formal, more general 'place'), प्रदेश (pradesh, can also mean 'state/province')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in everyday Delhi conversation -- asking someone 'aap kis ilaake mein rehte hain?' (which area do you live in?) is a completely normal way to place someone socially and geographically, much like asking about a neighborhood in English. It also shows up in a slightly tougher, turf-flavored sense, as in 'yeh hamara ilaaka hai' (this is our territory), often heard in the context of local dominance, gang turf, or a shopkeeper's patch.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse इलाक़ा (ilaaqaa) with शहर (shahar, 'city') -- इलाक़ा (ilaakaa) is more like a neighborhood, district, or zone within or around a city, not the city itself. 2) The nuqta on क़ (q) is often dropped in casual writing (इलाका (ilaakaa)) -- both are acceptable and pronounced the same in most Delhi-NCR speech.
