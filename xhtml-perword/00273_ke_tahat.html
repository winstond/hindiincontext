के तहत (ke tahat)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'under' or 'in accordance with' -- used for rules, laws, schemes, and agreements rather than physical position. Built from तहत (tahat, 'under, beneath'), a Persian-derived noun that survives almost only in this fixed frame.
Sense 1 : under (a law, rule, scheme, or agreement)
postposition Always follows a noun in the oblique-marked genitive frame: [noun] + के तहत (ke tahat). Common with words like कानून (kaanoon, 'law'), योजना (yojnaa, 'scheme'), नियम (niyam, 'rule'), and समझौता (samjhautaa, 'agreement').
Synonyms: के अनुसार (ke anusaar), के अंतर्गत (ke antargat, more formal/bureaucratic)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this constantly in news broadcasts and government notices: इस योजना के तहत (is yojnaa ke tahat, 'under this scheme') is a classic opening for describing a government program on the evening news. It has a slightly official, bureaucratic flavor -- in casual speech, people often just say के हिसाब से (ke hisaab se) or के अनुसार (ke anusaar) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use के तहत (ke tahat) for physical location ('under the table'), but that sense belongs to के नीचे (ke niiche) -- के तहत (ke tahat) is reserved for rules, laws, and official frameworks, never physical space. 2) The के (ke) is fixed and does not change with the gender/number of the following noun, since तहत (tahat) itself is invariant here.
