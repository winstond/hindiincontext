खाँसना (khaansnaa)

verb A simple, everyday verb meaning 'to cough'. It has one core sense, used both literally (a physical cough) and sometimes as a small conversational sound-effect verb.
Sense 1 : to cough
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: खाँसी आना (khaansii aanaa, phrase, lit. 'a cough comes')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this verb a lot in winter in Delhi when the smog sets in -- someone politely excusing a coughing fit might say माफ़ कीजिए, खाँस रहा हूँ (maaf kiijie, khaans rahaa hoon, 'sorry, I'm coughing'). Related noun खाँसी (khaansii, 'a cough') is the word you will actually use most, as in मुझे खाँसी है (mujhe khaansii hai, 'I have a cough') -- the two words are cousins worth learning together.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खाँसी होना (khaansii honaa) or खाँसी आना (khaansii aanaa) to describe having a cough as a condition, and then are unsure how खाँसना (khaansnaa) itself fits in -- खाँसना (khaansnaa) is the actual act of coughing (the sound/action in the moment), while खाँसी (khaansii) is the noun for the ailment or fit of coughing. 2) Because it is intransitive, it is easy to wrongly add ने (ne) in the past tense -- वह खाँसा (vah khaansaa, 'he coughed'), not उसने खाँसा।

सर्दियों में दिल्ली का धुआँ इतना बढ़ जाता है कि लोग सड़क पर खाँसने लगते हैं। In winter, Delhi's smog increases so much that people start coughing on the street.
