हँसना (hansnaa)

verb A single core sense: to laugh or smile. Also appears in a few common idioms about mocking or making fun of someone, noted below.
Sense 1 : to laugh, to smile
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मुस्कुराना (muskuraanaa, to smile), खिलखिलाना (khilkhilaanaa, to giggle/laugh brightly)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: हँसना (hansnaa) covers both a hearty laugh and a simple smile depending on context and tone, so a sentence like वह हँस रही थी (vah hans rahii thii, 'she was laughing/smiling') can mean either, unlike English which forces you to pick one. In everyday speech you'll also hear it in the common expression हँसते हँसते (hanste hanste, 'while laughing'), used to describe doing something amid laughter.
Common Mistakes: 1) हँसना (hansnaa) is intransitive and does not take ने (ne) in the perfective -- learners sometimes wrongly add it by analogy with other past-tense verbs: वह हँसा (vah hansaa, 'he laughed'), not वह ने हँसा। 2) To laugh AT someone uses पर (par): वह मुझ पर हँसा (vah mujh par hansaa, 'he laughed at me') -- learners often reach for पे (pe, colloquial variant of पर) or गुस्से से translate literally with 'at' using को, which is wrong here.

बच्चे पार्क में खेलते हुए हँस रहे हैं। The children are laughing while playing in the park.
(bachche paark men khelte hue hans rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : to mock, make fun of (someone)
verb, intransitive This sense is built on the same verb but requires the पर (par) frame -- किसी पर हँसना (kisii par hansnaa) -- to shift the meaning from plain laughter to mockery.
Synonyms: मज़ाक उड़ाना (mazaak udaanaa, to make fun of), उपहास करना (upahaas karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is really the same verb as plain 'laughing', just given a target with पर (par) -- Hindi doesn't need a separate word for 'mock' the way English sometimes reaches for one; the postposition alone does the work of turning innocent laughter into ridicule.
Sample Sentences:
सब लोग उसकी गलती पर हँसने लगे। Everyone started laughing at his mistake.
(sab log uskii galtii par hansne lage।)

(mujh par mat hanso, yah koii mazaak nahiin hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
बच्चे हमेशा उसके पहनावे पर हँसते हैं। The kids always laugh at his clothes.
(bachche hameshaa uske pahnaave par hanste hain।)
