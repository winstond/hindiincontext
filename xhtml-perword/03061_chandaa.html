चंदा (chandaa)

noun Two distinct senses: (1) a collected contribution or donation (as for a community event), and (2) an affectionate, poetic word for the moon, often used with children. Also spelled चांदा (chaandaa) in some regions, though चंदा (chandaa) is dominant in Delhi-NCR.
Sense 1 : collection/donation of money
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दान (daan), अनुदान (anudaan, formal, often for larger/institutional grants), योगदान (yogdaan, contribution in a broader sense, not always money)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear when neighborhood boys knock on your door before a festival asking for चंदा (chandaa) for the pandal or the loudspeaker system -- it has a slightly informal, community-organizing flavor that दान (daan) lacks. It is also the everyday word for a magazine or newspaper subscription fee in some contexts, though that use is less common now.
Common Mistakes: 1) चंदा (chandaa) specifically implies money collected from a group of people for a shared cause (a wedding gift pool, a Durga Puja pandal, a local cricket tournament) -- it is not used for a single person's personal charity to a beggar or temple, where दान (daan) is preferred. 2) The common verb pairing is चंदा इकट्ठा करना (chandaa ikatthaa karnaa, 'to collect contributions') or चंदा देना (chandaa denaa, 'to give a contribution') -- learners sometimes reach for दान देना (daan denaa) here, which sounds oddly formal/religious for a casual neighborhood collection.

Sense 2 : the moon (affectionate/poetic)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चाँद (chaand), चंद्रमा (chandrmaa, literary/formal), चंद्र (chandr, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the moon of lullabies -- चंदा मामा (chandaa maamaa, 'Uncle Moon') is how generations of Hindi-speaking children have been taught to address the moon, treating it as a beloved family elder who might come down and play. It carries none of the collection-money sense above; the two are unrelated homonyms that happen to share a form.
Common Mistakes: 1) चंदा (chandaa) is the diminutive, endearing form -- it is what you say to a baby or in a lullaby, not what you'd use in a weather report or a scientific sentence about the moon's orbit, where चाँद (chaand) or चंद्रमा (chandrmaa) fits better.
