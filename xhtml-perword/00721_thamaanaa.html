थमाना (thamaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to hand something over to someone, put it into their hands -- often with a sense of thrusting or pressing it on them rather than a neutral handover.
Sense 1 : to hand over, place into someone's hands
verb, transitive Takes an indirect object marked with को (ko) for the recipient: किसी को कुछ थमाना (kisii ko kuchh thamaanaa, 'to hand something to someone').
Synonyms: देना (denaa), सौंपना (saumpnaa, slightly more formal, 'to entrust/hand over')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a rickshaw-wallah thrusting change into your hand before you can even count it, or a boss offloading extra work onto a junior with काम थमा दिया (kaam thamaa diyaa, 'dumped the work on [him]') -- that physical, slightly forceful quality is the heart of this verb. It pairs naturally with the perfective compound-verb construction थमा देना (thamaa denaa) to add a sense of completion or suddenness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for देना (denaa) in every handing-over context, but थमाना (thamaanaa) specifically suggests physically placing something into someone's hands, often abruptly or without much ceremony -- like shoving a bag, a bill, or a piece of paper into someone's grip. 2) It frequently carries a mildly negative or dismissive shade, as in dumping an unwanted task or responsibility onto someone, which देना (denaa) does not automatically imply.
Sample Sentences:
उसने मुझे चाय का कप थमा दिया। She handed me a cup of tea.
(usne mujhe chaay kaa kap thamaa diyaa।)
