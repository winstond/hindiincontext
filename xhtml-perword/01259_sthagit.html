स्थगित (sthagit)

adjective A formal/administrative word meaning 'postponed' or 'adjourned', almost always paired with the verb करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa).
Sense 1 : postponed, adjourned, deferred
adjective Almost always used with करना (karnaa) ('to postpone something') or होना (honaa) ('to be postponed'), forming a fixed pattern rather than standing alone as a simple descriptive adjective.
Synonyms: टाला हुआ (taalaa huaa), मुल्तवी (multvii, formal, often used in legal/court contexts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word constantly in news broadcasts and official announcements -- a train is स्थगित (sthagit), a parliamentary session is स्थगित (sthagit), a cricket match is स्थगित (sthagit) due to rain. It has a stiff, official flavor, much like English 'adjourned' versus the everyday 'put off'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners try to use स्थगित (sthagit) as a standalone verb; it needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) attached to actually express the action of postponing or being postponed. 2) This word is bureaucratic/formal in tone -- in casual speech, लोग टालना (taalnaa) or स्थगित करना (sthagit karnaa) directly, but for everyday plans (like a coffee meetup), टालना (taalnaa) alone sounds far more natural than स्थगित करना (sthagit karnaa).

आज की बैठक स्थगित कर दी गई है। Today's meeting has been postponed.
(aaj kii baithak sthagit kar dii gaii hai।)
