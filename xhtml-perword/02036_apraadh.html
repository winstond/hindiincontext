अपराध (apraadh)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'crime' or 'offense' -- the legal/moral wrongdoing, not the person who commits it (that would be अपराधी (apraadhii)).
Sense 1 : crime, offense, wrongdoing
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जुर्म (jurm), गुनाह (gunaah), पाप (paap, moral/religious wrongdoing rather than legal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear अपराध (apraadh) constantly on Hindi news channels -- अपराध की खबरें (apraadh kii khabren, 'crime news') is practically a genre of its own on Delhi TV. It sits at the formal/legal end of the spectrum, while जुर्म (jurm) feels a touch more everyday and is common in police-drama dialogue.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use अपराध (apraadh) for a minor social slip like being late or forgetting something -- Hindi speakers reserve it for genuinely criminal or serious moral wrongdoing; a small lapse is better called गलती (galtii). 2) The verb pairing is करना (karnaa) -- अपराध करना (apraadh karnaa, 'to commit a crime') -- not बनाना (banaanaa) or कोई अन्य क्रिया।
Sample Sentences:
दिल्ली में पिछले कुछ सालों में अपराध बहुत बढ़ गया है। Crime has increased a lot in Delhi in the last few years.
(dillii men pichhle kuchh saalon men apraadh bahut badh gayaa hai।)
