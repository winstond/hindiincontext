सेहत (sehat)

noun A single-sense everyday word meaning physical health/well-being, from Arabic-Persian via Urdu; very common in daily conversation about wellness and greetings.
Sense 1 : physical health, well-being
noun, feminine
Synonyms: स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy, formal), तंदुरुस्ती (tandurustii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear सेहत (sehat) constantly in everyday well-wishing -- अपनी सेहत का ख़याल रखना (apnii sehat kaa khayaal rakhnaa, 'take care of your health') is a standard thing to say when someone is leaving or signing off a call, especially from elders. It also shows up in gym/akhara culture as सेहत बनाना (sehat banaanaa), meaning to build up one's body through exercise and good food, reflecting an older Indian fitness tradition distinct from the modern gym vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy) by default because it looks like the 'textbook' word, but सेहत (sehat) is what people actually say at home and in casual conversation -- e.g. asking after someone's सेहत (sehat) rather than their स्वास्थ्य (svaasthy). 2) सेहत (sehat) pairs with बनाना (banaanaa) in the idiom सेहत बनाना (sehat banaanaa, 'to build up one's health/physique'), which learners may not expect -- it's not just 'to make health.'
