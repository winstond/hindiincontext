बौद्धिक (bauddhik)

adjective/noun An adjective meaning 'intellectual' (relating to the mind/reasoning); it also works as a noun for 'an intellectual, a thinking person.'
Sense 1 : relating to the intellect or reasoning
adjective
Synonyms: मानसिक (maansik, mental, broader scope), विचारशील (vichaarshiil, thoughtful)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot in the context of बौद्धिक बहस (bauddhik bahas, 'intellectual debate') or बौद्धिक संपदा (bauddhik sampdaa, 'intellectual property') -- it has a slightly formal, newspaper-and-seminar-room flavor rather than being everyday chai-time vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use बौद्धिक (bauddhik) where मानसिक (maansik) is meant -- बौद्धिक (bauddhik) points specifically to reasoning/ideas (जैसे बौद्धिक बहस (bauddhik bahas, 'intellectual debate')), while मानसिक (maansik) covers the mind more broadly, including emotional/psychological states (जैसे मानसिक तनाव (maansik tanaav, 'mental stress')).

Sense 2 : an intellectual, a person of learning/ideas
noun, masculine
Synonyms: विद्वान (vidvaan, scholar, meaning scope), चिंतक (chintak, thinker)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Indian public discourse, बौद्धिक (bauddhik) as a noun often carries a mild edge -- it can describe genuine scholars, but in casual conversation it is sometimes used with a hint of sarcasm for someone seen as overly theoretical or detached from real life, similar to calling someone 'an intellectual' in a knowing tone in English.
