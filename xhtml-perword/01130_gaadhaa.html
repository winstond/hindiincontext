गाढ़ा (gaadhaa)

adjective An adjective meaning 'thick' or 'dense' (of liquids, textures, or darkness of color) that also extends figuratively to describe deep, close relationships like friendship.
Sense 1 : thick, dense (of liquids or textures)
adjective
Synonyms: गझिन (gajhin, rare), भारी (bhaarii, meaning scope: heavy/dense texture)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of your Nani stirring dal or kheer on the stove and saying it needs to become और गाढ़ा (aur gaadhaa, 'more thick') before it's ready -- this word lives in the kitchen as much as anywhere else.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मोटा (motaa) to mean 'thick,' but मोटा (motaa) describes thickness/fatness of solid objects (a thick book, a fat person), while गाढ़ा (gaadhaa) is for the consistency of liquids or mixtures, like milk, gravy, or paint.

(yah daal abhii bahut gaadhii hai, thodaa paanii milaa do।)

Sense 2 : deep, dark (of color)
adjective
Synonyms: गहरा (gahraa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used for a saturated, intense shade -- गाढ़ा लाल (gaadhaa laal, 'deep red') or गाढ़ा हरा (gaadhaa haraa, 'deep green') -- much like गहरा (gahraa), with which it overlaps closely in this sense.

उसने गाढ़े लाल रंग की साड़ी पहनी थी। She was wearing a sari of deep red color.

Sense 3 : close, intimate (of a bond or friendship)
adjective Most commonly heard in the fixed phrase गाढ़ी दोस्ती (gaadhii dostii, 'a deep/close friendship'), agreeing in gender with दोस्ती (dostii).
Synonyms: गहरा (gahraa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as a liquid thickens, a friendship 'thickens' too in Hindi -- गाढ़ी दोस्ती (gaadhii dostii) paints closeness as something with body and substance, not just duration.
Sample Sentences:
हमारी बहुत गाढ़ी दोस्ती है। We have a very close friendship.
(hamaarii bahut gaadhii dostii hai।)
