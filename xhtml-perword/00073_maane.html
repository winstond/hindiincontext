माने (maane)

particle/noun Colloquial word with two related but distinct everyday uses: (1) as a noun, the plural/oblique form of माना (maanaa, 'meaning'), used when asking or giving the meaning of something; (2) as a filler/discourse particle meaning roughly 'I mean' or 'that is to say', very common in casual spoken Hindi.
Sense 1 : discourse filler: 'I mean', 'that is'
particle Used mid-sentence to rephrase, clarify, or buy a moment to think, much like English 'I mean' -- it does not need to attach grammatically to what follows.
Synonyms: मतलब (matlab), यानी (yaanii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very Delhi/UP-flavored casual speech: someone fumbling for the right word will often drop in माने... माने... (maane... maane..., 'I mean... I mean...') while thinking. It sits alongside मतलब (matlab) as one of the two default fillers of spoken Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for मतलब (matlab) as filler and don't realize माने (maane) does the same job in casual speech -- they're largely interchangeable here. 2) Because माने (maane) looks like a verb form (from मानना (maannaa)) some learners try to conjugate it or treat it as part of the sentence's grammar; as a filler it is frozen and invariant.

Sense 2 : meaning(s); sense
noun, masculine Functions as the oblique/plural form of माना (maanaa) in set questions like इसके माने क्या हैं (iske maane kyaa hain, 'what does this mean'); in this pattern माने (maane) is treated as plural even for a single meaning.
Synonyms: मतलब (matlab), अर्थ (arth, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word family as मानना (maannaa, 'to consider/believe') -- 'meaning' here is literally 'what something is taken/understood to be'. Asking इसके माने क्या हैं (iske maane kyaa hain, 'what does this mean') is a very everyday, slightly informal way to ask for a definition, more casual than अर्थ (arth).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use the singular form माना (maanaa) in questions where native speakers default to माने (maane), e.g. इसका माना क्या है (iskaa maanaa kyaa hai) sounds off compared to इसके माने क्या हैं (iske maane kyaa hain).
