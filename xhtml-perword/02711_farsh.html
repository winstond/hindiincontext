फ़र्श (farsh)

noun A noun meaning the floor or flooring of a room/building, also used for a paved surface outdoors.
Sense 1 : floor (of a room or building)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ज़मीन (zamiin, can also mean ground/land generally)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian homes, फ़र्श (farsh) is the word you will hear used for the cool marble or tile floor that people sit, sleep, or spread a chatai (chataaii) on during summer nights or festive gatherings -- floor-sitting is still very much a part of everyday Delhi-NCR home life, whether for eating, praying, or chatting.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse फ़र्श (farsh) with छत (chhat), which is the roof/ceiling -- फ़र्श (farsh) is always the ground-level surface you walk on inside a room. 2) The nuqta under फ़ (f) is often dropped in casual writing (फर्श), which is acceptable in informal contexts but sounds better pronounced with the nuqta in careful speech.

Sense 2 : paved/laid surface (outdoor pavement, flooring material)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: फ़र्श (farsh) also extends to outdoor paved areas -- a courtyard or a market lane that has been tiled or cemented over is described as होने (farsh honaa) or having फ़र्श (farsh) laid down, distinguishing it from bare, unpaved ground.
