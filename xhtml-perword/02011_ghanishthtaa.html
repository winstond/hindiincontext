घनिष्ठता (ghanishthtaa)

noun A formal noun meaning 'closeness' or 'intimacy' in relationships -- used for deep personal bonds like close friendships, not physical intimacy.
Sense 1 : closeness, intimacy (in relationships)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नज़दीकी (nazdiikii), निकटता (nikattaa), अपनापन (apnaapan, emotional warmth/belonging), गहरा रिश्ता (gahraa rishtaa, deep relationship, phrase not single word)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word shows up a lot in writing -- essays, formal speeches, news articles about diplomatic or social ties -- more than in everyday chai-time conversation. If you are describing two friends who are thick as thieves in casual speech, करीबी दोस्ती (kariibii dostii, close friendship) or गहरी दोस्ती (gahrii dostii, deep friendship) will sound far more natural than घनिष्ठता (ghanishthtaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for this word to translate 'intimacy' in a romantic/physical sense, but घनिष्ठता (ghanishthtaa) refers to emotional or social closeness -- deep friendship, family bonds, close working relationships -- not physical intimacy. 2) It is a fairly formal/written-register word; in casual speech, people are far more likely to just say कोई बहुत करीबी दोस्त है (koii bahut kariibii dost hai, someone is a very close friend) using करीबी (kariibii) rather than reaching for this noun.

(donon deshon ke biich ghanishthtaa badh rahii hai।)
