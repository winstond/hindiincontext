संकट (sankat)

noun A single core meaning: a crisis, danger, or difficulty -- used for everything from a national emergency to a personal tight spot.
Sense 1 : crisis, danger, difficulty
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मुसीबत (musiibat), विपत्ति (vipatti, formal), आपदा (aapdaa, large-scale calamity), खतरा (khatraa, danger specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in the news -- आर्थिक संकट (aarthik sankat, 'economic crisis'), राजनीतिक संकट (raajniitik sankat, 'political crisis') -- but it works just as naturally in daily speech for something as small as being stuck without cash before payday. It also appears in the devotional hymn संकट मोचन (sankat mochan, 'remover of troubles'), a title for Hanuman, which is why the word carries a faint echo of divine rescue even in secular use.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खतरा (khatraa) when they mean संकट (sankat) -- खतरा (khatraa) is the danger or risk itself, while संकट (sankat) is the wider crisis or predicament that the danger has caused. 2) संकट (sankat) commonly takes में (men) to describe being 'in' a crisis, as in संकट में होना (sankat men honaa, 'to be in crisis') -- learners sometimes try को (ko) here by analogy with other constructions.
Sample Sentences:
देश इस समय गंभीर आर्थिक संकट से गुजर रहा है। The country is currently going through a severe economic crisis.
(desh is samay gambhiir aarthik sankat se gujar rahaa hai।)
