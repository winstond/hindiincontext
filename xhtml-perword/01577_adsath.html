अड़सठ (adsath)

numeral The cardinal number sixty-eight (adsath).
Sense 1 : the number sixty-eight
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numbers from 19 to 99 each evolved their own fused form rather than following a clean 'tens plus units' pattern like English, so अड़सठ (adsath) for 68 just has to be learned as its own word -- this is exactly why Hindi-medium schoolkids traditionally chant the full 1-100 number table (pahaadaa) by rote rather than deriving it on the fly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like all Hindi numbers 19-99, अड़सठ (adsath) does not follow a predictable pattern built from अड़ (a would-be 'eight' prefix) and साठ (saath, sixty) -- learners try to derive it logically instead of just memorizing it, and end up confusing it with अड़सठ's neighbors अड़सठ, सड़सठ (sadsath, sixty-seven), and उनहत्तर (unahattar, sixty-nine). 2) It is invariant -- it never changes form for gender, number, or case of the noun it counts.
