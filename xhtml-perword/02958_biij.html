बीज (biij)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'seed,' with a natural figurative extension to 'seed/root cause' of something abstract like an idea, conflict, or feeling.
Sense 1 : seed (of a plant)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दाना (daanaa, also means grain/kernel)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Farmers across the Delhi-NCR belt and the broader Hindi heartland talk about बीज (biij) constantly, especially at sowing season -- बीज बोना (biij bonaa, 'to sow seed') is a phrase you will hear on the news around monsoon time when discussing crop policy.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बीज (biij) with दाना (daanaa); बीज (biij) is specifically the reproductive seed you sow, while दाना (daanaa) is more general for a grain or granule (like a rice grain or sugar grain).

Sense 2 : seed/root cause of something abstract (idea, conflict, feeling)
noun, masculine Common in the fixed phrase बीज बोना (biij bonaa, 'to plant the seed [of an idea/feeling]') used metaphorically, and बीज पड़ना (biij padnaa, 'for the seed [of something] to be laid').
Synonyms: जड़ (jad, 'root', used similarly for root cause), मूल (mool, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Just as English speakers say 'plant a seed of doubt,' Hindi speakers say झगड़े का बीज (jhagde kaa biij, 'the seed of the quarrel') or शक का बीज (shak kaa biij, 'the seed of suspicion') -- the imagery of something small germinating into a bigger consequence carries over very naturally between the two languages.
