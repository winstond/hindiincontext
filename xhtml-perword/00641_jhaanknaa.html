झाँकना (jhaanknaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to peep, peer, or glance in' -- the everyday word for stealing a look through a window, door, curtain, or gap.
Sense 1 : to peep, peer in, glance furtively
verb, intransitive Commonly takes में (men) for the opening peeped into (खिड़की में झाँकना (khidkii men jhaanknaa, 'to peep into the window')) or से (se) for the gap/aperture used to peep through.
Synonyms: देखना (dekhnaa, much broader, plain 'to look'), नज़र डालना (nazar daalnaa, a quick glance, less about hidden/restricted viewing)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a kid standing on tiptoe to peep over a wall, or a neighbor peering through a slightly open door to see who's knocking -- झाँकना (jhaanknaa) is the word for exactly that half-curious, half-cautious glance. It carries a mild connotation of nosiness or stealth, so it is often used gently teasingly, as when an elder catches a child sneaking a look into the kitchen before dinner is ready.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for देखना (dekhnaa) when झाँकना (jhaanknaa) is meant -- झाँकना (jhaanknaa) specifically implies a partial, cautious, or curious/furtive look, often through a gap, from outside to inside (or vice versa), not a general act of looking. 2) The postposition after झाँकना (jhaanknaa) is often में (men) even where English would say 'through' -- खिड़की में झाँकना (khidkii men jhaanknaa) rather than खिड़की से (khidkii se) in many everyday uses, though से (se) is also fine when emphasizing the aperture itself.
