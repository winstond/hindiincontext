काना (kaanaa)

adjective/noun An adjective describing someone blind in one eye; it also has a rougher colloquial edge as an insult, so tone and context matter.
Sense 1 : one-eyed, blind in one eye
adjective
Synonyms: एक-आँख वाला (ek-aankh vaalaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Physical-description words like काना (kaanaa) sit close to the line between plain description and insult in Hindi, much like calling someone 'one-eyed' bluntly in English -- native speakers often soften it with a more roundabout phrase like एक आँख से नहीं दिखता (ek aankh se nahiin dikhtaa, 'can't see from one eye') in polite conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) काना (kaanaa) is often used bluntly as a description of a person's physical condition, which can sound rude in polite company; अंधा (andhaa) 'blind' is a related but distinct word for total blindness in both eyes, not to be confused with this one. 2) Learners sometimes use this word casually in front of the person being described, not realizing it can land as an insult rather than a neutral description.

(us gaanv men ek kaanaa aadmii rahtaa thaa।)

Sense 2 : a one-eyed person (used as an insult or blunt label)
noun, masculine Functions as a noun when used to address or label a person directly, e.g. as a taunt.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In everyday speech, काना (kaanaa) used as a noun toward someone is a taunt, similar in force to name-calling based on a physical trait in English -- it shows up in playground insults and heated arguments rather than neutral description.
Common Mistakes: 1) Using this as a direct noun of address (calling someone काना (kaanaa) to their face) is genuinely insulting in Hindi, more so than the adjective use describing a third person's condition -- learners should be cautious about ever using it this way.
