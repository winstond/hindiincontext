गहरा (gahraa)

adjective गहरा (gahraa) starts from physical depth (गहरा कुआँ (gahraa kuaan, 'deep well')) and stretches into color intensity (गहरा रंग (gahraa rang, 'deep/dark color')) and figurative intensity like deep sleep, deep sorrow, or close bonds.
Sense 1 : deep (physical depth)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the base, literal sense: a गहरा कुआँ (gahraa kuaan, 'deep well') or गहरा गड्ढा (gahraa gaddhaa, 'deep pit') describes actual vertical depth, the same physical image Hindi then borrows for the figurative senses below.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : dark, deep (of color)
adjective
Synonyms: गाढ़ा (gaadhaa, also means 'thick' for liquids)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Walk into any sari or clothing shop in Delhi and you will hear this constantly: गहरा लाल (gahraa laal, 'deep red'), गहरा हरा (gahraa haraa, 'deep green'). It always precedes a color word, describing intensity rather than actual depth.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for काला (kaalaa, 'black') when they mean a deep shade of another color; गहरा नीला (gahraa niilaa, 'deep blue') is not black, just an intense blue.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe gahraa laal rang bahut pasand hai।)

Sense 3 : deep, profound, intense (feelings, sleep, closeness)
adjective
Synonyms: गहन (gahan, literary, more for thoughts or issues than feelings)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind गहरी नींद (gahrii niind, 'deep sleep'), गहरा दुख (gahraa dukh, 'deep sorrow'), and गहरी दोस्ती (gahrii dostii, 'deep friendship') -- Hindi reuses the physical image of depth for emotional intensity and closeness, much like English does with 'deep' feelings.
Common Mistakes: 1) For a deep or gruff voice, Hindi more often reaches for भारी आवाज़ (bhaarii aavaaz, 'heavy voice') rather than गहरी आवाज़ (gahrii aavaaz, 'deep voice') -- the latter is understood but sounds slightly bookish.
Sample Sentences:
उसे बहुत गहरी नींद आती है। He falls into a very deep sleep.
(use bahut gahrii niind aatii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
