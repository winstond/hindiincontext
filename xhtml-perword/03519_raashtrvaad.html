राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'nationalism'; built from राष्ट्र (raashtr, 'nation') plus the abstract-noun suffix -वाद (vaad, '-ism'), a pattern shared with words like समाजवाद (samaajvaad, 'socialism').
Sense 1 : nationalism (the political ideology or sentiment)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: देशभक्ति (deshbhakti, patriotism -- narrower, feeling rather than ideology)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Indian public discourse राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad) is a live, often contested word, heard constantly in news debates about who counts as a 'true nationalist' versus a 'true patriot' (deshbhakti); the distinction between the two is itself a common talking point on Indian TV panels. Note the -वाद (vaad) suffix pattern: the same building block gives you समाजवाद (samaajvaad, socialism), साम्राज्यवाद (saamraajyvaad, imperialism), and गांधीवाद (gaandhiivaad, Gandhism), so learning this one word unlocks a whole family of '-ism' nouns.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad) interchangeably with देशभक्ति (deshbhakti), but the two are not the same: देशभक्ति (deshbhakti) is love for one's country (a feeling, generally positive), while राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad) names the political ideology itself, which in news and academic contexts can carry a critical or contested tone (as in 'rising nationalism' discourse). 2) As an abstract -वाद (vaad) noun it is grammatically masculine and does not take a plural in ordinary usage, even when discussing 'nationalisms' of different countries.
