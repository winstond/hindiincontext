जंगली (janglii)

adjective/noun Primarily an adjective meaning 'wild' (of animals, plants, or terrain), with a strong figurative extension to describe an uncultured or rude person; also used as a noun for such a person.
Sense 1 : wild, feral, growing/living in nature (not domesticated or cultivated)
adjective
Synonyms: वन्य (vany, formal), जंगल का (jangal kaa, descriptive phrase, not a single word)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word for the wild cat, wild boar, or wild flowers you'd see in a jungle safari (जंगल सफारी, jangal saphaarii) -- it directly derives from जंगल (jangal, 'forest/jungle'), so the mental image is always 'of the jungle, untouched by people.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for जंगली (janglii) to mean 'natural' in a positive sense (like organic food), but it just means undomesticated/uncultivated -- for 'wild' berries or animals this is correct, but praising something as 'natural' or 'pure' needs a different word like प्राकृतिक (praakritik).

Sense 2 : uncivilized, boorish, rude in manners (figurative, of a person or behavior)
adjective
Synonyms: गँवार (ganvaar, colloquial), असभ्य (asabhy, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A parent scolding a child for eating messily or shouting in public might snap जंगली हो क्या? (janglii ho kyaa?, 'are you an animal or what?') -- it's a very common, very cutting piece of Delhi-household scolding, comparing bad manners to an untamed creature.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a genuinely harsh insult, not a mild tease -- learners who know only the 'wild animal' sense sometimes underestimate how rude जंगली (janglii) sounds when aimed at a person's manners or upbringing.

Sense 3 : an uncivilized or ill-mannered person (used as a noun of address or description)
noun, masculine/feminine Used as a noun by dropping any following noun it would otherwise modify, e.g. calling someone सीधा जंगली है (siidhaa janglii hai, 'he's just a total savage').
Synonyms: गँवार (ganvaar, colloquial), बर्बर (barbar, formal, closer to 'barbarian')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun use overlaps completely with the adjective sense above -- Hindi adjectives ending in ई (ii) very often double as nouns for 'one who is X,' so जंगली (janglii) the noun and जंगली (janglii) the adjective are really the same word wearing two grammatical hats.
