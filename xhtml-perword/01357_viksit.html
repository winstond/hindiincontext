विकसित (viksit)

adjective A single core sense: 'developed' -- used for countries/economies/regions and for the growth or blossoming of things like flowers, skills, or ideas.
Sense 1 : developed (economically, socially, or in growth/maturity)
adjective
Synonyms: उन्नत (unnat), विकासशील (vikaasshiil, means 'developing', not synonymous but often contrasted), प्रगत (pragat, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a classic word from the language of Indian news and civics class -- you'll hear विकसित भारत (viksit bhaarat, 'developed India') constantly in government speeches and slogans about India's growth ambitions, especially around Independence Day addresses. It pairs naturally with देश (desh, 'country'), राष्ट्र (raashtr, 'nation'), and अर्थव्यवस्था (arthavyvasthaa, 'economy') in this register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse विकसित (viksit, 'developed') with विकासशील (vikaasshiil, 'developing') -- the two describe opposite stages and are frequently paired in news contexts like विकसित देश (viksit desh, 'developed country') versus विकासशील देश (vikaasshiil desh, 'developing country'). 2) As an invariant-feeling but actually variable adjective, learners sometimes forget to inflect it to agree with a feminine or plural noun, e.g. विकसित अर्थव्यवस्था (viksit arthavyvasthaa, 'developed economy') keeps the -इत ending unchanged since it doesn't follow the -आ pattern, which can cause hesitation about whether to alter it at all.

Sense 2 : developed/grown (of a flower, plant, faculty, or trait)
adjective Used for the organic growth or blossoming of living things and abstract qualities, e.g. a flower, a talent, or a personality trait, distinct from the socio-economic sense.
Synonyms: खिला हुआ (khilaa huaa, used specifically for flowers), विकासित (vikaasit, rare variant, less standard)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this sense in slightly more literary or descriptive contexts, like विकसित पुष्प (viksit pushp, 'a blossomed flower') in poetry, or when a teacher talks about a student's विकसित व्यक्तित्व (viksit vyaktitv, 'developed personality'). It shares the same root idea as the first sense -- growth or unfolding -- just applied to living or abstract things rather than nations.
