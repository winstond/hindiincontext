स्वस्थ (svasth)

adjective An adjective meaning 'healthy' -- used for physical health and, less often, for a settled/composed state of mind.
Sense 1 : healthy, in good physical health
adjective
Synonyms: तंदुरुस्त (tandurust), निरोग (nirog, literary), ठीक (thiik, everyday/informal way to say 'fine, well')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see स्वस्थ (svasth) a lot on government health posters and in doctor's-office language -- स्वस्थ रहें (svasth rahen, 'stay healthy') is a common public-health slogan. In casual conversation, though, people more often just ask तुम ठीक हो? (tum thiik ho, 'are you okay/well?') -- स्वस्थ (svasth) carries a slightly more formal, considered weight, like 'in good health' versus 'doing fine.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बीमार (biimaar, 'sick') as a simple opposite, but the everyday negative check-in is usually ठीक हूँ (thiik hoon, 'I'm fine') rather than स्वस्थ हूँ (svasth hoon) -- स्वस्थ (svasth) itself sounds a notch more formal/considered than casual chit-chat. 2) Don't confuse with स्वच्छ (svachchh, 'clean') -- similar-looking word, different meaning entirely.

Sense 2 : sound, settled, composed (of mind or condition)
adjective Applied to मन (man, 'mind') or मस्तिष्क (mastishk, 'mind/brain') to mean mentally sound or composed, rather than physically healthy.
Synonyms: शांत (shaant, calm -- overlaps only in the mental-composure sense), संतुलित (santulit, balanced)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This extended sense shows up in phrases like स्वस्थ मन (svasth man, 'a sound mind') or स्वस्थ समाज (svasth samaaj, 'a healthy society'), echoing the English idea of 'healthy' applied beyond the body -- a healthy debate, a healthy mind.
