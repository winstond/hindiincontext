सूती (sootii)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'made of cotton'; derived from सूत (soot, thread/yarn).
Sense 1 : cotton (made of)
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सूती कपड़े (sootii kapde, 'cotton clothes') are the default recommendation every summer in Delhi -- shopkeepers and mothers alike will tell you to switch to सूती (sootii) once the heat sets in, since it breathes better than synthetic fabric. You'll see it constantly on clothing tags and in markets like Sarojini Nagar, right alongside रेशमी (reshmii, 'silk') and ऊनी (oonii, 'woolen') as the three basic fabric-type adjectives every shopper learns early.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse सूती (sootii) with सूत (soot) itself, the noun for thread/yarn -- सूती (sootii) is only the adjective describing cloth or clothing made from it, never the thread itself. 2) It describes material, not color or pattern, so it is common to see it paired with another adjective, as in सूती और छपी हुई साड़ी (sootii aur chhapii huii saadii, 'a cotton and printed sari').
