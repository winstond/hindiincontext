के चारों ओर (ke chaaron or)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'around, on all sides of' -- built from चारों (chaaron, 'all four') plus ओर (or, 'direction/side'), governed by के (ke).
Sense 1 : around, surrounding
postposition Takes the oblique/genitive के before it, attaching to the noun being surrounded: शहर के चारों ओर (shahar ke chaaron or, 'around the city').
Synonyms: के आस-पास (ke aas-paas, nearby/around, more casual), के इर्द-गिर्द (ke ird-gird)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a chowk (chauk) or a park in Delhi -- if you say पार्क के चारों ओर पेड़ हैं (paark ke chaaron or ped hain, 'there are trees all around the park'), you're invoking that full-circle, 360-degree sense baked into चारों (chaaron). It's a common phrase in descriptions of scenery, crowds gathering, or armies surrounding a fort in old stories.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop चारों (chaaron) and just say के ओर (ke or), which instead means 'toward' or 'in the direction of' -- the opposite spatial idea of surrounding something. 2) चारों (chaaron) literally references 'four' (as in four directions), but it is fixed in this phrase and never changes for number or gender, so learners should not try to inflect it.
