टँगना (tangnaa)

verb The intransitive verb 'to hang, be suspended' -- used for things like clothes, pictures, or bags that are hung up on a hook, wall, or line. Closely related to the transitive verb टाँगना (taangnaa, 'to hang something').
Sense 1 : to be hung, to hang (of an object)
verb, intransitive Takes पर (par) for the surface/support the thing hangs from or on, e.g. कील पर (kiil par, 'on the nail').
Synonyms: लटकना (lataknaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a Delhi household: coats टँगे रहते हैं (tange rahte hain, 'stay hung up') on hooks behind the door, and calendars or framed photos टँगे होते हैं (tange hote hain, 'are hanging') on the wall. The word carries a sense of something placed and staying put, which is why it pairs so naturally with everyday fixtures like hooks, nails, and racks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लटकना (lataknaa) by default in every 'hanging' context; टँगना (tangnaa) specifically suggests something deliberately hung up and secured (a coat on a hook, a picture on a wall), whereas लटकना (lataknaa) is more general and covers things dangling loosely too. 2) As an intransitive verb describing a state, it does not take ने (ne) in the perfective -- कमीज़ अलमारी में टँगी थी (kamiiz almaarii men tangii thii, 'the shirt was hanging in the cupboard'), not a ने-marked construction.
