अंक (ank)

noun Covers three connected everyday senses: a numeral/digit, a mark or score (as in exams), and an issue/edition of a magazine or journal.
Sense 1 : mark, score (academic)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नंबर (nambar), ग्रेड (gred)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian school kid knows the anxiety of अंक (ank) season -- report cards, board exam results, and the family cross-examination that follows. You will see अंक (ank) printed on the मार्कशीट (maarkshiit, 'mark-sheet') itself, while नंबर (nambar) is what your mother actually asks you at the dinner table: 'कितने नंबर आए?' (kitne nambar aae?, 'how many marks did you get?').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नंबर (nambar) exclusively and miss that अंक (ank) is the more formal/written word used on report cards and official mark-sheets, while नंबर (nambar) is what people actually say out loud when discussing scores casually. 2) अंक (ank) in this sense is usually plural in meaning ('marks') even when used with a singular-looking construction like कुल अंक (kul ank, 'total marks').

(is baar mere ganit men achchhe ank aae hain।)

Sense 2 : numeral, digit, number (mathematical)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संख्या (sankhyaa, a full number/quantity, vs. अंक for a single digit)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word your school math teacher uses for 'digit' -- इस संख्या में तीन अंक हैं (is sankhyaa men tiin ank hain, 'this number has three digits') is a classic classroom sentence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse अंक (ank) with संख्या (sankhyaa): अंक (ank) is a single digit (0-9), while संख्या (sankhyaa) is a whole number that may be made of several अंक (ank) -- e.g. 25 is one संख्या (sankhyaa) made of two अंक (ank).
Sample Sentences:

(is sankhyaa men tiin ank hain।)

Sense 3 : issue, edition (of a magazine or journal)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रति (prati, copy, not quite the same as an issue), संस्करण (sanskran, edition, broader term also used for book editions)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will see this on the cover of Hindi magazines and journals: विशेषांक (visheshaank, 'special issue') is a compound built right from अंक (ank) plus विशेष (vishesh, 'special') -- a word worth recognizing if you ever browse a Hindi newsstand or library.
