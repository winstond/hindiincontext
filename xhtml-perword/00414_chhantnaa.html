छँटना (chhantnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning to be sorted/sifted out or thinned out, and by extension to clear up or disperse (as clouds, fog, or crowds); it is the intransitive partner of the transitive छाँटना (chhaantnaa, 'to sort/select/prune').
Sense 1 : to thin out, disperse, clear up (crowd, clouds, fog, workforce)
verb, intransitive Frequently used with जाना (jaanaa) as a compound verb -- बादल छँट गए (baadal chhant gae, 'the clouds cleared') -- to mark the completed, resultant state.
Synonyms: छँट जाना (chhant jaanaa, common compound form), साफ़ होना (saaf honaa, for weather), कम होना (kam honaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in weather chat and traffic reports around Delhi: कोहरा छँट गया है (kohraa chhant gayaa hai, 'the fog has cleared') is practically a winter-morning ritual phrase on the news. The same word slides naturally into the corporate world for layoffs -- छंटनी (chhantnii) (retrenchment) is the noun built straight from this verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) When talking about layoffs or downsizing (staff being 'thinned out'), learners sometimes default to निकालना (nikaalnaa) or हटाना (hataanaa), which imply someone actively removing people; छँटना (chhantnaa) instead frames the workforce itself as thinning, useful when the agent is unclear or de-emphasized (compare English 'jobs were cut' vs 'the workforce thinned out').

Sense 2 : to be sorted out / selected / weeded out
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: चुनना (chunnaa, intransitive use is rare, mostly transitive), अलग होना (alag honaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Think of grain being sifted in a village kitchen: the husk is 'छाँटा' (chhaantaa) by the person winnowing, but the good grain 'छँटता है' (chhanttaa hai) -- it separates out on its own, from the grain's point of view. This agentless framing is very Hindi: the process is described from the side of what happens, not who does it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छाँटना (chhaantnaa) here because it looks so similar -- but छाँटना (chhaantnaa) is the transitive act of *doing* the sorting, while छँटना (chhantnaa) describes the item itself *undergoing* the sorting, with no agent expressed. 2) Note the chandrabindu (nasalization) on छँटना (chhantnaa) versus its absence on छाँटना (chhaantnaa) -- easy to miss when typing or reading quickly.
