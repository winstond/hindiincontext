ब्याह (byaah)

noun/verb A homey, everyday word for 'marriage/wedding' -- basically an alternative to शादी (shaadii) with a slightly more rustic, folk flavor. Also functions as a verb root meaning 'to marry off.'
Sense 1 : wedding, marriage
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शादी (shaadii), विवाह (vivaah, slightly more formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: ब्याह (byaah) has a warm, folksy ring to it -- you will hear it a lot in Hindi film songs, in the Haryana-UP countryside, and from grandparents, whereas शादी (shaadii) is the more neutral, everyday word you would use in Delhi conversation. Think of it like 'wedding' vs. a more old-fashioned, homespun way of saying the same thing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to शादी (shaadii) in all contexts and never encounter ब्याह (byaah) in speech, then are surprised to hear it in villages, folk songs, or from older relatives -- it is not a rare or literary word, just more common in rural/folk registers than in urban Delhi small talk. 2) The phrase ब्याह करना (byaah karnaa) means 'to get married / to marry someone off,' not 'to do a wedding' in the event-planning sense.

Sense 2 : to marry off, to give in marriage
verb, transitive Used especially in the causative-flavored sense of parents/family marrying off a child, e.g. बेटी का ब्याह करना (betii kaa byaah karnaa, 'to marry off one's daughter').
Synonyms: शादी करना (shaadii karnaa), विवाह करना (vivaah karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear ब्याहना (byaahnaa)/ब्याह करना (byaah karnaa) most often in the context of parents marrying off their children, especially daughters -- बेटी ब्याहना (betii byaahnaa) carries a strong cultural weight in North Indian family life, tied to ideas of duty and responsibility that go beyond the neutral English 'to marry off.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat this only as a noun; it is also a common verb, often paired with करना (karnaa) as ब्याह करना (byaah karnaa), or heard in the passive-ish ब्याहा जाना (byaahaa jaanaa, 'to be married off'), especially of daughters.
