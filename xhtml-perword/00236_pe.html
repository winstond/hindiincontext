पे (pe)

postposition A short, casual spoken variant of the postposition पर (par, 'on/at/upon'); very common in everyday speech and texting.
Sense 1 : on, at, upon (locative)
postposition
Synonyms: पर (par)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear पे (pe) constantly in Delhi-NCR chatter -- 'मेज़ पे रखो' (mez pe rakho, 'put it on the table') -- while the same sentence written formally would use पर (par). Think of पे (pe) as the relaxed, spoken-only cousin of पर (par).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often think पे (pe) is a separate word with its own rules, but it simply substitutes for पर (par) in casual speech -- same case-marking behavior applies. 2) पे (pe) is rarely used in formal writing or news Hindi; पर (par) is preferred there, so switching to पे (pe) in a written application or essay can feel too casual.

Sense 2 : about, concerning (topic marker)
postposition Marks the topic or subject of discussion, e.g. 'इस बात पे बहस' (is baat pe bahas, 'argument about this matter').
Synonyms: पर (par)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the same पे/पर (pe/par) doing extra duty -- once you've internalized the locative sense, the 'about/concerning' use follows the identical pattern, just with an abstract topic instead of a physical location.
