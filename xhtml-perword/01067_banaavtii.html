बनावटी (banaavtii)

adjective An adjective meaning 'artificial, fake, contrived' -- used for both physical objects (fake materials) and human behavior (put-on, insincere). Related to बनाना (banaanaa, 'to make') and बनावट (banaavat, 'make, structure, appearance').
Sense 1 : fake, put-on, insincere (of behavior, emotion, manner)
adjective
Synonyms: दिखावटी (dikhaavtii), झूठा (jhoothaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word Delhi aunties reach for when someone's politeness feels performed rather than genuine -- 'उसकी हंसी बनावटी लगती है' (uskii hansii banaavtii lagtii hai, 'her laugh seems fake') is a classic bit of social commentary, often said behind someone's back with a knowing look.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is about manner and affect -- a बनावटी मुस्कान (banaavtii muskaan, 'a fake smile') or बनावटी अंदाज़ (banaavtii andaaz, 'a put-on/affected style') -- not about lying per se; for accusing someone of outright lying, झूठा (jhoothaa) or धोखेबाज़ (dhokhebaaz) fit better.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : fake, artificial (of objects/materials)
adjective
Synonyms: नकली (naklii), कृत्रिम (kritrim, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word applied to plastic flowers, synthetic fabric, or artificial sweeteners in a very matter-of-fact way -- no moral judgment, just describing the material as not natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for नकली (naklii) and बनावटी (banaavtii) interchangeably for objects, which mostly works, but बनावटी leans slightly more toward 'artificially made/synthetic' (जैसे बनावटी फूल (banaavtii phool, 'artificial flowers')) while नकली often carries a stronger sense of 'counterfeit/deceptive' (जैसे नकली नोट (naklii not, 'fake currency note')).
