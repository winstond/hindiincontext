धार्मिक (dhaarmik)

adjective An adjective meaning 'religious' -- used both for religious matters/practices and for describing a person as pious or devout. Related to धर्म (dharm, 'religion, duty, righteousness').
Sense 1 : relating to religion
adjective
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up constantly in the news and in everyday talk about festivals, sites, and customs -- धार्मिक स्थल (dhaarmik sthal, 'religious site') and धार्मिक कार्यक्रम (dhaarmik kaaryakram, 'religious program/event') are extremely common phrases you will hear announced at temples, on TV, or in family conversations around festival time.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use धार्मिक (dhaarmik) to mean 'a religious person' as a noun on its own without a noun following it; it works better attached to a noun like धार्मिक व्यक्ति (dhaarmik vyakti, 'a religious person') or used predicatively, e.g. वह धार्मिक है (vah dhaarmik hai, 'he is religious').

Sense 2 : pious, devout (describing a person)
adjective
Synonyms: पवित्र (pavitr, meaning scope: 'pure/sacred', not identical), भक्त (bhakt, meaning scope: 'devotee', noun-leaning)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday family talk, calling someone धार्मिक (dhaarmik) usually means they pray regularly, observe fasts, or visit temples/mosques/gurudwaras often -- it is a warm, respectful description, often said of grandparents: मेरी दादी बहुत धार्मिक हैं (merii daadii bahut dhaarmik hain, 'my grandmother is very devout').
