तैंतालीस (taintaaliis)

numeral The cardinal number 43 (forty-three). A single-sense numeral used to count and to label positions in a sequence.
Sense 1 : the number 43
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most two-digit Hindi numbers, तैंतालीस (taintaaliis) is one of nearly a hundred distinct number-words with no predictable pattern, which is why Hindi speakers who grew up in India often just recite the whole 1-100 list from memory rather than compute numbers the way English speakers do. You will meet it most often in ages, prices, roll numbers, and addresses.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this number compositionally from तीन (tiin, 'three') and चालीस (chaaliis, 'forty') the way English says 'forty-three' -- Hindi numerals 21-99 are memorized as single unpredictable words, and तैंतालीस (taintaaliis) must simply be learned as its own item rather than derived. 2) The nasalized ऐं is sometimes misspelled or mispronounced as a plain ऐ; the nasal sound is essential to the word.
