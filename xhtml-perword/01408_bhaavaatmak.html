भावात्मक (bhaavaatmak)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'emotional' in the sense of pertaining to feelings/sentiment -- distinct from भावुक (bhaavuk), which describes a person's temperament as sentimental or easily moved.
Sense 1 : emotional; relating to feelings or sentiment
adjective
Synonyms: संवेगात्मक (sanvegaatmak, formal/technical), मनोभावी (manobhaavii, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet this word a lot in Indian school civics and NCERT-style textbooks in the stock phrase भावात्मक एकता (bhaavaatmak ektaa, 'emotional integration'), a term used to talk about national unity across India's diverse states and languages. That textbook association is a good anchor: भावात्मक (bhaavaatmak) belongs to the register of essays and analysis, not the register of describing your chatty, soft-hearted cousin -- for that, reach for भावुक (bhaavuk).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this where भावुक (bhaavuk) is meant -- भावात्मक (bhaavaatmak) describes a quality, statement, appeal, or bond as being 'of the nature of feeling' (e.g. भावात्मक लगाव (bhaavaatmak lagaav, 'emotional attachment')), not a person's personality; to call a person sentimental/easily-moved, use भावुक (bhaavuk) instead. 2) This word skews toward written, essay-style, or psychological/analytical registers (e.g. discussing भावात्मक एकता (bhaavaatmak ektaa, 'emotional unity/integration'), a phrase common in civics textbooks); in casual speech, लोग जल्दी इमोशनल हो जाते हैं (log jaldii imoshnal ho jaate hain, 'people get emotional quickly') with the English loan is far more natural than reaching for भावात्मक (bhaavaatmak).
