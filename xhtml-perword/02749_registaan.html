रेगिस्तान (registaan)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'desert' -- both the literal geographic sense and its figurative extension to a barren, empty place or situation.
Sense 1 : desert (arid, sandy region)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मरुस्थल (marusthal, formal/literary), थार (thaar, the Thar Desert specifically, not a generic synonym)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: For most Delhi-NCR speakers, रेगिस्तान (registaan) instantly evokes Rajasthan -- camel safaris, sand dunes, and the Thar. It is a word learned early because of school geography lessons and family trips west of Delhi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse रेगिस्तान (registaan) with सहरा (sahraa), a less common but valid synonym borrowed from Persian/Urdu; both mean 'desert' but रेगिस्तान (registaan) is the everyday word used in Delhi-NCR speech. 2) The retroflex ग in रेगिस्तान (registaan) is sometimes mispronounced by learners transferring English 'g' sounds -- it is a simple hard 'g' as in 'go', not a soft one.

Sense 2 : figurative: a barren, lifeless, or emotionally empty place/situation
noun, masculine Used metaphorically for a life, relationship, or place felt to be devoid of warmth, life, or hope, e.g. 'ज़िंदगी एक रेगिस्तान बन गई है' (zindgii ek registaan ban gaii hai, 'life has become a wasteland').
Synonyms: वीराना (viiraanaa, emphasizes desolation/emptiness), सन्नाटा (sannaataa, emphasizes silence rather than barrenness)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi film lyrics and shayari love this image -- a heart or a life 'turning into a रेगिस्तान (registaan)' after loss or heartbreak, playing on the desert's total absence of water and greenery as a stand-in for absence of love or hope.
