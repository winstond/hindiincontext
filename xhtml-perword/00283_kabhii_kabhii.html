कभी कभी (kabhii kabhii)

adverb A doubled form of कभी (kabhii, 'sometimes/ever') meaning 'sometimes, now and then, occasionally' -- the reduplication softens and spreads out the frequency it describes.
Sense 1 : occasionally, now and then
adverb
Synonyms: कभी-कभार (kabhii-kabhaar, slightly more casual), कुछ समय बाद (kuchh samay baad, not exact synonym, avoid using), इधर-उधर (idhar-udhar, rare, different flavor)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of Hindi's many doubled words (like धीरे धीरे (dhiire dhiire, 'slowly slowly') or थोड़ा थोड़ा (thodaa thodaa, 'little by little')), where repetition itself carries grammatical meaning -- here turning a point-in-time word into a spread-out, recurring one. You will hear it constantly in casual chat, like when someone shrugs and says कभी कभी बारिश हो जाती है (kabhii kabhii baarish ho jaatii hai, 'it rains sometimes') about unpredictable Delhi weather.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use plain कभी (kabhii) when they mean 'sometimes,' but unreduplicated कभी (kabhii) more often means 'ever' or 'at some point,' especially in questions and negatives -- the doubling in कभी कभी (kabhii kabhii) is what locks in the 'occasionally' reading. 2) It describes a pattern spread over time, not a single instance -- for one occasion, use कभी (kabhii) alone, not the doubled form.
Sample Sentences:
कभी कभी बारिश हो जाती है। It rains sometimes.
(kabhii kabhii baarish ho jaatii hai।)
