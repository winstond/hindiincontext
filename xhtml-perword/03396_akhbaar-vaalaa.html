अख़बार-वाला (akhbaar-vaalaa)

noun A compound noun meaning 'newspaper man/vendor' -- the person who delivers or sells newspapers. Built from अख़बार (akhbaar, newspaper) plus the agentive suffix वाला (vaalaa).
Sense 1 : newspaper vendor/deliveryman
noun, masculine
Synonyms: समाचार-पत्र वाला (samaachaar-patr vaalaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In most Indian neighborhoods, the अख़बार-वाला (akhbaar-vaalaa) is a familiar daily figure who tosses the paper onto the doorstep every morning, often on a bicycle, and is known well enough by name or face to be paid a monthly bill rather than per issue. The वाला (vaalaa) suffix here works exactly like in दूधवाला (doodhvaalaa, milkman) or सब्ज़ीवाला (sabziivaalaa, vegetable seller) -- a hugely productive pattern for naming 'the person associated with X' that learners should internalize as a whole family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write it as two unhyphenated words (akhbaar vaalaa); both the hyphenated and spaced forms are seen in practice, but treat वाला (vaalaa) as attaching to the noun it modifies. 2) Don't confuse with अख़बार (akhbaar) alone, which is just the newspaper itself, not the person.
