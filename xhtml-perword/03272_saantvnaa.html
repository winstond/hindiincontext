सांत्वना (saantvnaa)

noun A somewhat formal noun meaning 'consolation' or 'comfort' -- the kind of solace offered in words, not physical comfort. Also spelled सान्त्वना (saantvnaa) with the conjunct nasal; both are used.
Sense 1 : consolation, solace offered to someone in grief or distress
noun, feminine
Synonyms: तसल्ली (tasallii), दिलासा (dilaasaa), ढारस (dhaaras, less common)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word a lot on the news after accidents or disasters -- 'मुख्यमंत्री ने शोक संतप्त परिवार को सांत्वना दी' (mukhymantrii ne shok santapt parivaar ko saantvnaa dii, 'the chief minister consoled the grieving family') is a very standard sentence template. In daily life among friends, though, तसल्ली (tasallii) or दिलासा (dilaasaa) feel warmer and more natural.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सांत्वना (saantvnaa) in casual speech where a native speaker would say तसल्ली (tasallii) or दिलासा (dilaasaa) -- सांत्वना (saantvnaa) leans a bit formal/written, common in news reports and condolence contexts (e.g. 'सरकार ने पीड़ितों को सांत्वना दी' (sarkaar ne piiditon ko saantvnaa dii, 'the government gave consolation to the victims')). 2) The standard verb pairing is सांत्वना देना (saantvnaa denaa, 'to console/give consolation') -- learners sometimes try to verb-ify सांत्वना (saantvnaa) directly instead of using this देना (denaa) construction.
Sample Sentences:
मुख्यमंत्री ने शोक संतप्त परिवार को सांत्वना दी। The chief minister consoled the grieving family.
(mukhymantrii ne shok santapt parivaar ko saantvnaa dii।)

Sense 2 : a consolation prize or token compensation (extended/idiomatic use)
noun, feminine Occurs almost exclusively in the fixed phrase सांत्वना पुरस्कार (saantvnaa puraskaar, 'consolation prize').
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the phrase you'd hear at a school function or a TV game show when someone doesn't win but still gets something small -- सांत्वना पुरस्कार (saantvnaa puraskaar) is the direct equivalent of the English 'consolation prize,' and the pairing is fixed enough that सांत्वना (saantvnaa) alone rarely carries this sense outside of it.
Sample Sentences:
उसे सांत्वना पुरस्कार मिला। He got a consolation prize.
(use saantvnaa puraskaar milaa।)
