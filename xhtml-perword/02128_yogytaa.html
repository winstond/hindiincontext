योग्यता (yogytaa)

noun A formal noun meaning 'qualification, eligibility, or capability' -- the abstract quality of being fit or suited for something, often used for jobs, exams, and admissions.
Sense 1 : qualification, eligibility (formal criteria met)
noun, feminine Common in the phrase योग्यता रखना (yogytaa rakhnaa, 'to possess the qualification') and in job/exam contexts as न्यूनतम योग्यता (nyoontam yogytaa, 'minimum qualification').
Synonyms: पात्रता (paatrtaa), अर्हता (arhtaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see this word everywhere on Indian government job notices and university admission forms, usually under a column heading like शैक्षणिक योग्यता (shaikshnik yogytaa, 'educational qualification'). It carries a slightly bureaucratic, official flavor, much like 'eligibility criteria' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for 'डिग्री (digrii)' when they mean a specific degree, but योग्यता (yogytaa) is broader -- it covers overall eligibility (education, experience, skills combined), not just a paper credential. 2) In formal notices and forms, योग्यता (yogytaa) is the standard word for 'qualification' -- क्वालिफिकेशन (kvaaliphikeshan) is heard colloquially but sounds less official in writing.

Sense 2 : ability, capability, merit (innate capacity)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: क्षमता (kshamtaa), काबिलियत (kaabiliyat, colloquial), प्रतिभा (pratibhaa, talent, meaning scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in everyday encouragement too, like तुम में यह काम करने की योग्यता है (tum men yah kaam karne kii yogytaa hai, 'you have it in you to do this work') -- praising someone's inherent capability rather than a paper credential.
Common Mistakes: 1) योग्यता (yogytaa) leans toward 'merit' or 'competence earned/possessed,' while क्षमता (kshamtaa) leans toward raw 'capacity' -- native speakers often use them interchangeably in casual speech, but in formal contexts (like merit-based admissions, योग्यता के आधार पर (yogytaa ke aadhaar par, 'on the basis of merit')), योग्यता (yogytaa) is the natural choice.
