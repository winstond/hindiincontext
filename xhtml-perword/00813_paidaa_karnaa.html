पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built on the borrowed element पैदा (paidaa, 'produced/born') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); it means 'to produce, create, or generate' in a broad sense covering everything from farming to feelings to problems.
Sense 1 : to create, cause, generate (an abstract effect, problem, feeling)
verb, transitive Takes an abstract noun as object -- मुश्किल (mushkil, 'difficulty'), शक (shak, 'doubt'), जागरूकता (jaagrooktaa, 'awareness') -- and frames the subject as the source that brings the abstract thing into being.
Synonyms: उत्पन्न करना (utpann karnaa, formal), उपजाना (upjaanaa, for feelings/situations), खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa, to set up/cause, informal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you'll hear in news and everyday complaints alike: समस्या पैदा करना (samasyaa paidaa karnaa, 'to create a problem'), भ्रम पैदा करना (bhram paidaa karnaa, 'to create confusion'). It has a slightly formal, reportage-like flavor compared to casual verbs like बनाना (banaanaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) English speakers often default to बनाना (banaanaa) for 'create a problem,' but पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) is the idiomatic choice for causing an abstract effect -- मुसीबत पैदा करना (musiibat paidaa karnaa, 'to create trouble') sounds natural, मुसीबत बनाना (musiibat banaanaa) does not.

Sense 2 : to produce, grow (crops, goods)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उपजाना (upjaanaa, for crops), उगाना (ugaanaa, to grow/cultivate), बनाना (banaanaa, to make/manufacture)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday talk about agriculture -- so central to Indian life -- पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) is the default verb: you'll hear it constantly in news about फ़सल (fasal, 'crop') production and in mandi (grain market) talk about how much a field yielded.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for उगाना (ugaanaa) or उपजाना (upjaanaa) for farming contexts and forget पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) works just as naturally there -- किसान गेहूँ पैदा करते हैं (kisaan gehoon paidaa karte hain, 'farmers grow wheat') is completely standard.

Sense 3 : to give birth to, bring into being (a child, life)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: जन्म देना (janm denaa, more common for childbirth)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet the related intransitive पैदा होना (paidaa honaa, 'to be born') far more often in daily conversation -- लोग कहाँ पैदा हुए (log kahaan paidaa hue, 'where were people born') -- than this transitive causative form, which leans slightly formal or literary when applied to human birth.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense exists but is far less common in everyday speech than जन्म देना (janm denaa); learners should recognize पैदा करना (paidaa karnaa) in this meaning (often passive, as in पैदा होना (paidaa honaa), 'to be born') more than actively produce it themselves.
