कहानी (kahaanii)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'story' or 'tale', used both literally (fiction, narrated events) and figuratively (the 'story' behind a situation).
Sense 1 : story, tale, narrative
noun, feminine
Synonyms: किस्सा (kissaa), दास्तान (daastaan, literary), गाथा (gaathaa, literary), वृत्तांत (vrittaant, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian childhood runs on दादी की कहानियाँ (daadii kii kahaaniyaan, 'grandmother's stories') told before bed, so कहानी (kahaanii) carries a warm, nostalgic pull beyond its plain dictionary meaning. It is also the natural word for a Bollywood plot -- फ़िल्म की कहानी (film kii kahaanii, 'the film's story') -- and for the exasperated question ये क्या कहानी है? (ye kyaa kahaanii hai?, 'what's going on here?'), where it shifts from 'tale' to 'situation/drama'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for इतिहास (itihaas) when they mean 'story' -- but इतिहास (itihaas) means 'history', not a narrated tale; कहानी (kahaanii) is the everyday word for a fictional or personal story. 2) In casual speech, कहानी (kahaanii) is often used figuratively to mean 'the whole situation' or 'the real explanation', as in पूरी कहानी बताओ (poorii kahaanii bataao, 'tell me the whole story') -- this extended sense trips up learners who only expect it in a storytelling context.
