के साथ (ke saath)

postposition A compound postposition built from साथ (saath, 'company') plus के (ke); the bare noun साथ (saath) itself is a separate, closely related lemma meaning 'company/togetherness.'
Sense 1 : with, along with, in the company of
postposition
Synonyms: सहित (sahit, formal), समेत (samet, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: साथ (saath) alone is a noun meaning 'company' or 'companionship' -- तेरा साथ (teraa saath, 'your company') is a common line in songs and heartfelt talk -- and के साथ (ke saath) is just that noun pressed into service as a postposition via के (ke). In everyday speech you will also hear the doubled form साथ साथ (saath saath, 'together, side by side'), used to emphasize simultaneity, as in हम साथ साथ चलते हैं (ham saath saath chalte hain, 'we walk along together').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for के पास (ke paas) when they mean के साथ (ke saath) -- के पास (ke paas) means 'near/in one's possession,' not 'accompanying.' 2) के साथ (ke saath) also marks the person on the receiving end of a treatment or action, e.g. उसके साथ अच्छा व्यवहार करो (uske saath achchhaa vyavahaar karo, 'treat him well') -- English drops 'with' here ('treat him'), so learners under-use साथ (saath) in this pattern and reach for को (ko) instead. 3) After a pronoun, साथ (saath) still needs के in its oblique-possessive form rather than being dropped -- मेरे साथ (mere saath, 'with me'), not मैं साथ (main saath).
Sample Sentences:
मैं अपने दोस्त के साथ बाज़ार गया। I went to the market with my friend.
(main apne dost ke saath baazaar gayaa।)
