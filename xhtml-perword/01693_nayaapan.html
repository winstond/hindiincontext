नयापन (nayaapan)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'newness' or 'novelty' -- built from नया (nayaa, 'new') plus the abstract-noun suffix -पन (pan).
Sense 1 : newness, novelty
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नवीनता (naviintaa, formal), नयापन (nayaapan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This -पन (pan) suffix is a handy pattern -- attach it to many adjectives to get the matching abstract noun: अपनापन (apnaapan, 'sense of belonging') from अपना (apnaa), बचपन (bachpan, 'childhood') from बच्चा (bachchaa). Once you notice नयापन (nayaapan), you start seeing the pattern everywhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use नया (nayaa) where the abstract noun is needed -- e.g. to say 'the newness of this idea has worn off', you need नयापन (nayaapan), not just नया (nayaa), which only works as an adjective modifying a noun. 2) The word is often paired with the verb खोना (khonaa, 'to lose') or ख़त्म होना (khatm honaa, 'to end') to talk about novelty fading -- नयापन खो जाना (nayaapan kho jaanaa, 'for the newness to be lost').
