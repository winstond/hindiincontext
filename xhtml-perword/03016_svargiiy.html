स्वर्गीय (svargiiy)

adjective An adjective meaning 'late' or 'deceased' (literally 'heavenly'), used respectfully before the name of someone who has died. It relates to स्वर्ग (svarg, heaven).
Sense 1 : late, deceased (honorific)
adjective
Synonyms: मरहूम (marahoom, used mainly for Muslims), दिवंगत (divangat, formal/written)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see स्वर्गीय (svargiiy) on wedding invitations, obituaries, and formal introductions, placed right before the name of a deceased parent or grandparent as a mark of respect, literally casting them as having gone to स्वर्ग (svarg, heaven). It carries a gentle, honoring tone rather than a clinical one, so it feels warmer than साधारण words for 'dead.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use स्वर्गीय (svargiiy) as a general word for 'dead' -- it is specifically an honorific placed before a deceased person's name or title (e.g. स्वर्गीय श्री शर्मा (svargiiy shrii sharmaa, 'the late Mr. Sharma')), not a description of a corpse or of death in general (मरा हुआ (maraa huaa) or मृत (mrit) cover that). 2) It is not used for animals or objects, only for people being spoken of with respect.
