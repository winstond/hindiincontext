नक़्शा (naqshaa)

noun A borrowed word (from Persian/Arabic) meaning primarily 'map' or 'plan/blueprint,' with a common extended sense of 'layout, design, or outline' of something. Also spelled नक्शा (nakshaa) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : map
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मानचित्र (maanchitr, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If you ask an auto driver or a shopkeeper for directions in Delhi, they might sketch a quick नक़्शा (naqshaa) on a piece of paper -- the word covers both a printed map and a hand-drawn one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मानचित्र (maanchitr) by default because it's the textbook word, but नक़्शा (naqshaa) is what people actually say for a map in daily conversation -- for directions, atlases, or a city map.

क्या तुम्हारे पास दिल्ली का नक़्शा है? Do you have a map of Delhi?

Sense 2 : plan, blueprint, layout
noun, masculine Used especially for the floor plan of a house or building, or the architectural layout of a construction project.
Synonyms: योजना (yojnaa), डिज़ाइन (dizaain, informal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: When a family builds a house, the first thing they get made is the नक़्शा (naqshaa) -- the architect's blueprint that also has to be approved by the local municipal office (नगर निगम, nagar nigam) before construction can start.

Sense 3 : figure, build, or facial features (of a person)
noun, masculine Used descriptively for a person's overall build or facial structure, e.g. नक़्शा-नुमा तुम्हारी माँ जैसा है (naqshaa-numaa tumhaarii maan jaisaa hai, 'your build/features are just like your mother's').
Synonyms: बनावट (banaavat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Relatives love to comment on a baby's नक़्शा (naqshaa), tracing whose nose or whose eyes the child has inherited -- it's a favorite topic at family gatherings.
Sample Sentences:
बच्चे का नक़्शा बिल्कुल अपने पापा जैसा है। The baby's features are exactly like his father's.

Sense 4 : mess, disorderly state (idiomatic, often with बिगाड़ना)
noun, masculine Common in the idiom नक़्शा बिगाड़ना (naqshaa bigaadnaa, 'to ruin the look/plan of something') and नक़्शा बिगड़ना (naqshaa bigadnaa, 'to become disfigured/ruined,' intransitive).
Synonyms: हालत (haalat)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in scoldings: अपने कमरे का नक़्शा बिगाड़ दिया! (apne kamre kaa naqshaa bigaad diyaa, 'you've wrecked your room!') -- the room's 'layout' has been thrown into disorder.
Common Mistakes: 1) This idiomatic sense only surfaces in fixed phrases like नक़्शा बिगाड़ना (naqshaa bigaadnaa) -- don't try to use नक़्शा (naqshaa) alone to mean 'mess.'
Sample Sentences:
तुमने अपने कमरे का नक़्शा बिगाड़ दिया! You've wrecked your room!
