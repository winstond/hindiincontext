परंतु (parantu)

conjunction A formal-register word meaning 'but/however', used interchangeably with लेकिन (lekin) though slightly more literary in feel.
Sense 1 : but, however
conjunction
Synonyms: लेकिन (lekin), मगर (magar), किंतु (kintu, slightly more literary), पर (par, colloquial, shorter)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet परंतु (parantu) constantly in written Hindi -- news articles, essays, textbooks, formal speeches -- but in everyday chatting among friends, लेकिन (lekin) or मगर (magar) is far more natural. Think of it like choosing 'however' over 'but' in English: correct in any context, but it signals a slightly more formal register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often assume परंतु (parantu) is interchangeable with पर (par) in every context, but पर (par) is much more casual and used constantly in speech, while परंतु (parantu) leans toward writing, speeches, and careful/formal talk. 2) Some learners overuse परंतु (parantu) in casual conversation where a native speaker would simply say लेकिन (lekin), making the sentence sound stiff or bookish.
