रखना (rakhnaa)

verb A core transitive verb meaning 'to put/place' that fans out into several everyday uses: literal placement, keeping something in one's possession, fixed idioms of abstract 'keeping' (ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa)), a grammatical auxiliary marking a lasting result (लिख रखना (likh rakhnaa)), hiring domestic help, and observing a religious fast.
Sense 1 : to put/place (an object somewhere)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the plain, physical sense you'll hear constantly around the house: सामान वहीं रख दो (saamaan vahiin rakh do, 'just put the stuff right there') is something every Indian mother says while cleaning up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Location goes with पर (par) or में (men), not को: किताब मेज़ पर रखो (kitaab mez par rakho, 'put the book on the table'), never किताब मेज़ को रखो.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : to keep/maintain (an abstract quality, attitude, or relationship)
verb, transitive Combines with abstract nouns to form fixed collocations: ध्यान रखना (dhyaan rakhnaa, to take care/pay attention), ख़्याल रखना (khyaal rakhnaa, to look after), भरोसा रखना (bharosaa rakhnaa, to trust), उम्मीद रखना (ummiid rakhnaa, to hope), संबंध/रिश्ता रखना (rishtaa rakhnaa, to maintain a relationship).

Sense 3 : auxiliary marking a lasting, deliberate result (verb stem + रखना)
verb, transitive Attached to a verb stem (खोल रखना (khol rakhnaa), बना रखना (banaa rakhnaa)), रखना (rakhnaa) marks that an action was done on purpose and its result is still holding -- e.g. दरवाज़ा खोल रखा है (darvaazaa khol rakhaa hai, 'the door has been kept open') implies someone deliberately left it open and it's still open now.
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a very Hindi way of talking about preparedness -- मैंने चाय बना रखी है (mainne chaay banaa rakhii hai, 'I've got tea ready') is exactly what a host says when guests are expected any minute.
Common Mistakes: 1) Don't confuse this with the plain perfect: मैंने खाना बनाया है (mainne khaanaa banaayaa hai, 'I have cooked') just reports the event, while मैंने खाना बना रखा है (mainne khaanaa banaa rakhaa hai, 'I've kept food made/ready') stresses that it's sitting ready right now.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने चाय बना रखी है, कोई भी आ जाए तो चलेगा। I've got tea made, so it's fine whoever comes.
(mainne chaay banaa rakhii hai, koii bhii aa jaae to chalegaa।)
