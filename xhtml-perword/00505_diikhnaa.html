दीखना (diikhnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to be visible, to appear to the eye' -- close in meaning to the far more common दिखना (dikhnaa) and दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa).
Sense 1 : to be visible, to appear (to the eye)
verb, intransitive Takes a को-marked experiencer, like दिखना (dikhnaa): पहाड़ दीखता है (pahaad diikhtaa hai, 'the mountain is visible') but मुझे पहाड़ दीखता है (mujhe pahaad diikhtaa hai, 'the mountain is visible to me / I can see the mountain').
Synonyms: दिखना (dikhnaa), दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa, more common construction), नज़र आना (nazar aanaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This verb has a slightly older or more literary flavor next to its everyday cousin दिखना (dikhnaa) -- you will meet it in writing, songs, or careful speech, but a Delhi household will almost always say दिखना (dikhnaa) or दिखाई देना (dikhaaii denaa) instead when talking about ordinary things like seeing someone across the street.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often don't realize दीखना (diikhnaa) and दिखना (dikhnaa) are near-twins; in everyday Delhi-NCR speech दिखना (dikhnaa) is heard far more often, so लगभग हमेशा दिखना कहा जाता है (lagbhag hameshaa dikhnaa kahaa jaataa hai, 'दिखना is used almost always') -- reach for दिखना (dikhnaa) first and treat दीखना (diikhnaa) as a passive-recognition word.
