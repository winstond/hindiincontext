विश्वास (vishvaas)

noun A core noun meaning 'trust, faith, belief/confidence', with a related verb-phrase usage and a common extended sense of religious faith.
Sense 1 : trust, faith (in a person or relationship)
noun, masculine Commonly used with पर (par) or पे (pe) marking the person/thing trusted: किसी पर विश्वास करना (kisii par vishvaas karnaa, 'to trust someone').
Synonyms: भरोसा (bharosaa), यकीन (yakiin)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech, भरोसा (bharosaa) and विश्वास (vishvaas) overlap heavily, but विश्वास (vishvaas) often carries a slightly more considered, less purely emotional flavor -- you'll hear it in contexts like trusting someone's word or character, while भरोसा (bharosaa) leans toward everyday reliability ('I trust you'll come on time').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use को (ko) instead of पर (par) for the trusted person -- कहो, 'मुझे उस पर विश्वास है' (mujhe us par vishvaas hai, 'I trust him'), not 'मुझे उसे विश्वास है'. 2) विश्वास (vishvaas) pairs naturally with करना (karnaa) (to place trust) or होना (honaa) (to have trust, dative-subject construction), and learners sometimes force it with रखना (rakhnaa) when a simpler verb would sound more natural.
Sample Sentences:

(mujhe tum par pooraa vishvaas hai।)

Sense 2 : belief, conviction (in an idea, fact, or oneself)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: यकीन (yakiin), आत्मविश्वास (aatmvishvaas, self-confidence, related compound)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in everyday encouragement -- a parent or friend might say 'अपने ऊपर विश्वास रखो' (apne oopar vishvaas rakho, 'have faith in yourself'), a very common phrase of reassurance in Hindi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) आत्मविश्वास (aatmvishvaas) 'self-confidence' is a distinct compound word, not just विश्वास (vishvaas) used reflexively -- learners sometimes say 'खुद पर विश्वास' (khud par vishvaas, 'confidence in oneself') where both are fine, but conflating the compound with the plain noun in writing can look off.

Sense 3 : religious or spiritual faith
noun, masculine
Synonyms: श्रद्धा (shraddhaa, reverential faith, devotional contexts), आस्था (aasthaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In religious contexts you'll often hear विश्वास (vishvaas) and आस्था (aasthaa) used almost interchangeably for faith in God or a tradition, e.g. भगवान में विश्वास (bhagvaan men vishvaas, 'faith in God') -- note the में (men) marking here, distinct from the पर (par) used for trusting a person.
