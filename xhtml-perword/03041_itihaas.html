इतिहास (itihaas)

noun A single-sense word meaning 'history' -- both the academic subject and the past record of a person, place, or thing.
Sense 1 : history
noun, masculine
Synonyms: तवारीख़ (tavaariikh, less common)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian schoolkid knows इतिहास (itihaas) as one of the core subjects alongside भूगोल (bhoogol, geography) and नागरिक शास्त्र (naagrik shaastr, civics) -- so the word carries a mild classroom association even in casual talk, similar to how English speakers might jokingly groan about 'history class.' It's built from इति (iti, 'thus, so') plus हास (haas, related to 'account'), reflecting the classical Sanskrit sense of itihaas as 'thus indeed it was' -- a recorded/handed-down account.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use इतिहास (itihaas) for a personal 'story' or 'account of events' -- for that, कहानी (kahaanii) or किस्सा (kissaa) is the natural word; इतिहास (itihaas) is reserved for history in the broader, more formal sense (a country's history, a subject in school, the history of an illness). 2) The phrase इतिहास रचना (itihaas rachnaa, 'to make history') is a set collocation -- learners often reach for बनाना (banaanaa) instead of रचना (rachnaa), which sounds off in this fixed expression.
