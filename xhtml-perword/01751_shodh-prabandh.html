शोध-प्रबंध (shodh-prabandh)

noun A compound noun meaning 'thesis' or 'dissertation' -- शोध (shodh, research) plus प्रबंध (prabandh, treatise/composition). Single sense, used mainly in academic contexts.
Sense 1 : thesis, dissertation
noun, masculine
Synonyms: थीसिस (thiisis, common in spoken academic use), शोध-पत्र (shodh-patr, more like a research paper than a full thesis)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is very much a university-register word -- you will hear it from professors and see it on notice boards about PhD submissions (piiechdii), but everyday conversation about 'writing my thesis' often just switches to English 'thesis' with Hindi grammar wrapped around it, like थीसिस लिखना (thiisis likhnaa, to write a thesis). Knowing शोध-प्रबंध (shodh-prabandh) marks you as comfortable with formal, Sanskritized academic Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop the hyphen or write it as one word -- शोधप्रबंध (shodhaprbandh) -- both forms are seen, but the hyphenated form is more standard in formal writing. 2) Do not confuse with शोध (shodh) alone, which just means 'research' in general and is used far more broadly (e.g. शोध करना (shodh karnaa, to do research)), while शोध-प्रबंध (shodh-prabandh) refers specifically to the written document submitted for a degree.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपना शोध-प्रबंध पिछले महीने जमा किया। She submitted her thesis last month.
(usne apnaa shodh-prabandh pichhle mahiine jamaa kiyaa।)
