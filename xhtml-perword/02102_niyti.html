नियति (niyti)

noun A single-sense literary/philosophical noun meaning 'fate' or 'destiny' -- the impersonal force believed to determine the course of one's life.
Sense 1 : fate, destiny
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भाग्य (bhaagy), किस्मत (kismat), तक़दीर (taqdiir), प्रारब्ध (praarabdh, literary)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: नियति (niyti) carries a heavier, almost philosophical weight than किस्मत (kismat) -- it's the word you'd meet in a poem, a serious novel, or a speech about the inevitability of one's path, closer to the English 'destiny' than the more colloquial 'luck.' It shares a root with नियम (niyam, 'rule'), both pointing to the idea of something fixed or ordained.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नियति (niyti) in casual speech where किस्मत (kismat) or भाग्य (bhaagy) would sound far more natural -- नियति (niyti) belongs to reflective, written, or philosophical registers, not everyday chit-chat about luck. 2) Unlike किस्मत (kismat), which is commonly used in fixed phrases like किस्मत अच्छी है (kismat achchhii hai, 'the luck is good'), नियति (niyti) is rarely modified with everyday adjectives like अच्छी (achchhii) or बुरी (burii) -- it tends to appear as an abstract subject or object, as in नियति को कौन टाल सकता है (niyti ko kaun taal saktaa hai, 'who can avert destiny').
