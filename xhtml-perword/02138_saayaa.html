साया (saayaa)

noun Primarily 'shadow' or 'shade' cast by a body or object, extending naturally into figurative senses of protection/influence and, in a separate everyday sense, a woman's underskirt (petticoat).
Sense 1 : protective presence, influence (figurative)
noun, masculine Common in the fixed phrase साया होना (saayaa honaa) or सर पर साया होना (sar par saayaa honaa), meaning to have someone's protective presence/guardianship over you.
Synonyms: छत्रछाया (chhatrchhaayaa, formal), संरक्षण (sanrakshan, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Hindi families, losing a parent is often described as बाप का साया उठ जाना (baap kaa saayaa uth jaanaa, 'the father's shadow lifting away') -- a gentle, deeply felt way of saying the protective figure is gone, without stating death bluntly.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is about a person being a guardian/protector, not literally standing nearby -- a father's साया (saayaa) over his children means his protection and guidance, even from a distance.

(pitaa kaa saayaa sir par ho to dar kis baat kaa।)

Sense 2 : shadow, shade
noun, masculine
Synonyms: छाया (chhaayaa), परछाई (parchhaaii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Saayaa (saayaa) shows up constantly in Bollywood lyrics and shayari, almost always about a person's protective or lingering presence rather than the literal shadow you'd see at noon -- 'तेरा साया' (a phrase you'll hear often) means something like 'your shadow/presence stays with me,' evoking closeness and guardianship.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for छाया (chhaayaa) by default since it is the more 'textbook' word, but साया (saayaa) is what you will actually hear in speech, especially in phrases about someone's presence or protection. 2) परछाई (parchhaaii) is the more common word specifically for the visible shadow cast on the ground; साया (saayaa) is slightly more literary/poetic in that concrete physical sense but very much alive in figurative use.
Sample Sentences:
दोपहर में पेड़ का साया बहुत ठंडा लगता है। In the afternoon, the shade of the tree feels very cool.
(dopahar men ped kaa saayaa bahut thandaa lagtaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
दीवार पर उसका साया साफ़ दिख रहा था। His shadow was clearly visible on the wall.
(diivaar par uskaa saayaa saaf dikh rahaa thaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
धूप तेज़ थी, इसलिए हम पेड़ के साये में बैठ गए। The sun was strong, so we sat in the shade of the tree.
(dhoop tez thii, islie ham ped ke saaye men baith gae।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : petticoat, underskirt
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पेटीकोट (petiikot)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Ask any woman shopping for a साड़ी (saadii) and साया (saayaa) will come up naturally as the underskirt worn beneath it -- a purely practical, everyday clothing term with no poetic baggage attached.
Common Mistakes: 1) Context is everything here -- साया (saayaa) meaning 'petticoat' shows up in conversations about clothing (worn under a साड़ी (saadii)), completely unrelated to the shadow/protection senses, so don't force a figurative reading when a tailor or shopkeeper is just talking about garments.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे साड़ी के साथ मैचिंग साया भी चाहिए। I need a matching petticoat with the saree too.
(mujhe saadii ke saath maiching saayaa bhii chaahie।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
यह साया थोड़ा ढीला है, कमर पर टाइट करना पड़ेगा। This petticoat is a bit loose, I'll have to tighten it at the waist.
(yah saayaa thodaa dhiilaa hai, kamar par taait karnaa padegaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
