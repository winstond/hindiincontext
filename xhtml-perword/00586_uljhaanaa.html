उलझाना (uljhaanaa)

verb The causative/transitive verb 'to entangle, tangle up' -- used both literally for physical things like thread or hair, and figuratively for confusing or embroiling someone in a problem. Related to the intransitive उलझना (ulajhnaa, 'to get tangled/entangled').
Sense 1 : to confuse, embroil, entangle (a person, in a problem or situation)
verb, transitive Commonly takes the pattern X ko Y mein उलझाना (X ko Y men uljhaanaa) -- 'to entangle X in Y.'
Synonyms: फँसाना (phansaanaa), भ्रमित करना (bhramit karnaa, more formal/bookish)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you will hear far more often than the literal one -- Hindi speakers love saying बातों में उलझाना (baaton men uljhaanaa, 'to entangle someone in talk') for the classic move of distracting or stalling someone with chatter, whether it is a salesman keeping you busy or a kid stalling before homework.
Common Mistakes: 1) The person being confused/entangled is marked with को (ko), and the problem or situation they're entangled *in* takes में (men) -- learners sometimes drop one or both markers. 2) उलझाना (uljhaanaa) implies deliberately or effectively tangling someone up in a mess, distinct from just भ्रमित करना (bhramit karnaa) which is more purely about mental confusion without the 'stuck in a mess' feel.
Sample Sentences:
वह हमेशा मुझे बातों में उलझा देता है। He always entangles me in talk to distract me.
(vah hameshaa mujhe baaton men uljhaa detaa hai।)

Sense 2 : to entangle, tangle up (physically)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: गुंथाना (gunthaanaa, rare)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a kid's kite string in a knot after a windy afternoon on a Delhi rooftop -- that everyday image is exactly what उलझाना (uljhaanaa) captures literally, before it stretches into the more common figurative sense.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for उलझना (ulajhnaa) when they mean the causative -- if someone or something *causes* the tangling (e.g. the wind tangling your hair), you need उलझाना (uljhaanaa), not the intransitive form.
Sample Sentences:
बच्चे ने पतंग की डोर उलझा दी। The child tangled up the kite string.
(bachche ne patang kii dor uljhaa dii।)
