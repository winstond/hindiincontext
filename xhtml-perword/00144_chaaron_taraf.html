चारों तरफ़ (chaaron taraf)

adverb A fixed spatial expression meaning 'all around, on all sides' -- literally 'in all four directions.' Built from चार (chaar, 'four') plus the emphatic-plural ों and तरफ़ (taraf, 'direction, side').
Sense 1 : all around, on every side
adverb Functions as an adverbial phrase of place, typically placed before the verb or the thing being described as surrounding something: चारों तरफ़ पानी था (chaaron taraf paanii thaa, 'there was water all around').
Synonyms: हर तरफ़ (har taraf), चारों ओर (chaaron or), इधर-उधर (idhar-udhar, not exact, more 'here and there')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in descriptions of scenes -- a crowded market, a flooded street, a garden in bloom -- चारों तरफ़ भीड़ थी (chaaron taraf bhiid thii, 'there was a crowd all around'). It pairs naturally with चारों ओर (chaaron or), which is essentially interchangeable and slightly more literary-leaning, though both are common in speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat तरफ़ (taraf) as needing a postposition of its own here, but चारों तरफ़ (chaaron taraf) already functions as a complete adverbial and needs no extra से (se) or में (men) attached to mean 'around.' 2) Confusing this fixed phrase with चार तरफ़ (chaar taraf) (without ों), which sounds incomplete/unnatural to native ears -- the ों plural-oblique marker is fixed in this expression.
