मूँछ (moonchh)

noun A body-part noun meaning 'mustache'; almost always seen in the plural मूँछें (moonchhen) since a mustache has two sides, and it anchors a whole family of idioms about male pride and honor.
Sense 1 : mustache (facial hair)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In North India a mustache is tied up with masculine pride and honor -- you'll hear men say उसने मेरी मूँछ का बाल उखाड़ लिया (usne merii moonchh kaa baal ukhaad liyaa, 'he plucked a hair from my mustache') to mean someone insulted or defied them, and मूँछ ऊँची होना (moonchh oonchii honaa) or मूँछ नीची होना (moonchh niichii honaa) map directly to one's honor rising or falling. A hearty, twirled mustache (twirling gesture: मूँछों पर ताव देना (moonchhon par taav denaa)) is a classic swagger move in Haryanvi and Punjabi culture, often shown in films to signal a proud, confident man.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use the singular मूँछ (moonchh) where a native speaker would default to the plural मूँछें (moonchhen) to describe someone's mustache, since both sides are usually referenced together. 2) Growing a mustache is usually expressed with रखना (rakhnaa) -- मूँछ रखना (moonchh rakhnaa, 'to keep/grow a mustache') -- not with a verb like 'grow' translated literally.
