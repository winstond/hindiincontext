हराना (haraanaa)

verb The causative-transitive verb meaning 'to defeat/beat' someone in a game, contest, or fight; built from हारना (haarnaa, 'to lose').
Sense 1 : to defeat, beat (in a game, contest, or war)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: पराजित करना (paraajit karnaa, formal), मात देना (maat denaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the causative of हारना (haarnaa, 'to lose') -- literally 'to cause someone to lose.' You'll hear it constantly around cricket season: भारत ने पाकिस्तान को हरा दिया (bhaarat ne paakistaan ko haraa diyaa, 'India defeated Pakistan'), often with the compound verb हरा देना (haraa denaa) to stress the completeness of the win.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse हराना (haraanaa, 'to defeat') with हरना (harnaa, a literary verb meaning 'to take away, remove'), which is much rarer and not the causative partner here. 2) The person defeated takes को (ko): मैंने उसे हराया (mainne use haraayaa, 'I defeated him'), not a direct object without को in most contexts.
Sample Sentences:
भारत ने पाकिस्तान को हरा दिया। India defeated Pakistan.
(bhaarat ne paakistaan ko haraa diyaa।)
