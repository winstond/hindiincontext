तानाशाह (taanaashaah)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'dictator,' used both literally in political contexts and figuratively for a domineering, controlling person.
Sense 1 : dictator (political ruler with absolute power)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: निरंकुश शासक (nirankush shaasak, descriptive phrase, not a single word), सर्वेसर्वा (sarvesarvaa, one who has total control, slightly literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Hindi news and political commentary, applied to historical figures and also thrown around in domestic arguments -- a father who won't let anyone speak at dinner might jokingly get called घर का तानाशाह (ghar kaa taanaashaah, 'the household dictator'). The compound literally breaks down as ताना (taanaa, roughly 'command/threat') plus शाह (shaah, 'king/ruler'), giving the sense of someone who rules by decree alone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use तानाशाह (taanaashaah) loosely for any strict authority figure like a strict teacher or boss; native speakers reserve it for someone genuinely absolute and unaccountable in their control, using it for a merely strict person mostly in exaggeration or humor. 2) The related abstract noun तानाशाही (taanaashaahii, 'dictatorship') is often confused with तानाशाह (taanaashaah) itself -- तानाशाह (taanaashaah) is the person, तानाशाही (taanaashaahii) is the system or behavior.
