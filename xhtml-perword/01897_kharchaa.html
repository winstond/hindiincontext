ख़र्चा (kharchaa)

noun A common everyday noun meaning 'expense/expenditure' -- money spent on something, often household or running costs. Closely related to the verb ख़र्च करना (kharch karnaa, 'to spend').
Sense 1 : expense, expenditure, cost
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ख़र्च (kharch, common variant, often used interchangeably), व्यय (vyay, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in domestic conversation -- घर का ख़र्चा (ghar kaa kharchaa, 'household expenses'), बच्चों का ख़र्चा (bachchon kaa kharchaa, 'children's expenses') -- it is the word of the monthly household budget, the kind of thing a mother or father tallies up at the end of the month over chai. It carries a slightly more homely, grounded feel than the more clipped ख़र्च (kharch), somewhat like 'the household costs' versus the more clinical 'expenditure.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use ख़र्च (kharch) and ख़र्चा (kharchaa) as if they were completely different words -- in daily speech they largely overlap, though ख़र्चा (kharchaa) has a slightly more colloquial, 'everyday household cost' feel, while ख़र्च (kharch) is used more broadly, including in formal and written contexts. 2) The verb pairing is ख़र्चा होना (kharchaa honaa, 'to be incurred as an expense') or ख़र्चा करना (kharchaa karnaa, 'to incur/spend an expense') -- learners sometimes default only to करना (karnaa) and miss होना (honaa), which is common when talking about costs that 'happen' to you, like घर का ख़र्चा हो गया (ghar kaa kharchaa ho gayaa, 'the household expense came up/happened').
Sample Sentences:
इस महीने घर का ख़र्चा बहुत बढ़ गया है। The household expenses have gone up a lot this month.
(is mahiine ghar kaa kharchaa bahut badh gayaa hai।)
