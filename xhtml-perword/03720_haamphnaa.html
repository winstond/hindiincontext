हाँफना (haamphnaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to pant, to gasp for breath' -- typically from exertion, running, or exhaustion.
Sense 1 : to pant, to gasp for breath
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: हांफना (haamphnaa, variant spelling without chandrabindu), सांस फूलना (saans phoolnaa, 'to be out of breath')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this used affectionately about dogs and about people after climbing stairs or running for a bus -- कुत्ta हाँफ रहा है (kuttaa haamph rahaa hai, 'the dog is panting') is a very common everyday image in Delhi, where strays pant heavily in the summer heat. It pairs naturally with थकना (thaknaa, 'to get tired') in describing physical exhaustion.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for सांस लेना (saans lenaa, 'to breathe') when they mean हाँफना (haamphnaa) -- the latter specifically implies labored, heavy breathing from exertion, not ordinary breathing. 2) The nasalization (chandrabindu) is often dropped in casual writing, giving हांफना (haamphnaa) -- both are seen and accepted.
Sample Sentences:
गर्मी में कुत्ता सड़क के किनारे हाँफ रहा है। In the heat, the dog is panting by the side of the road.
(garmii men kuttaa sadak ke kinaare haamph rahaa hai।)
