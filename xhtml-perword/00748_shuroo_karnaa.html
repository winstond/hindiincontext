शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to start/begin' something -- शुरू (shuroo, the borrowed Persian/Urdu noun/adjective 'start') paired with करना (karnaa, 'to do') as the light verb. One core sense, used transitively across everyday and professional contexts.
Sense 1 : to begin, to start (something)
verb, transitive Takes a direct object marking what is started: either a noun (काम शुरू करना, kaam shuroo karnaa, 'to start the work') or another verb's oblique infinitive (करना शुरू करना pattern, e.g. पढ़ना शुरू करना, padhnaa shuroo karnaa, 'to start reading').
Synonyms: आरंभ करना (aarambh karnaa, formal), प्रारंभ करना (praarambh karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the most common करना-compounds in daily Hindi -- you will hear it constantly in offices, kitchens, and classrooms: मीटिंग शुरू करते हैं (miiting shuroo karte hain, 'let's start the meeting'). Its intransitive partner is शुरू होना (shuroo honaa, 'to begin [by itself]'), used when no agent is doing the starting, e.g. फ़िल्म शुरू हो गई (film shuroo ho gaii, 'the film started') -- worth learning as a pair since Hindi leans on this transitive/intransitive split much more than English does.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget that when शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa) takes a verbal object, that verb must be in its oblique infinitive form before शुरू, e.g. खाना शुरू करना (khaanaa shuroo karnaa, 'to start eating'), not the bare root. 2) Since करना (karnaa) is transitive, ने-marking applies to the subject in the perfective: उसने काम शुरू किया (usne kaam shuroo kiyaa, 'he started the work') -- learners sometimes leave off ने because शुरू itself feels like a state rather than an action.
