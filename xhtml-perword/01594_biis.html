बीस (biis)

numeral/adjective The number twenty (biis) -- a numeral used for counting and quantities, with a common idiomatic extension meaning 'superior/one-up' in the fixed phrase बीस पड़ना (biis padnaa).
Sense 1 : the number twenty
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: बीस (biis) shows up constantly in everyday money and age talk -- बीस रुपये (biis rupye, 'twenty rupees'), बीस साल का (biis saal kaa, 'twenty years old'). Like other Hindi numerals, it precedes the noun it counts and the noun typically stays in its base form.

Sense 2 : superior, one better (in comparison)
adjective Occurs almost exclusively in the fixed comparative frame X पर बीस पड़ना (X par biis padnaa, 'to outdo X'), where बीस (biis) functions as a predicate adjective meaning 'ahead/superior' rather than as a literal count.
Synonyms: बेहतर (behtar), बढ़कर (badhkar)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: The idiom इस पर वह बीस पड़ता है (is par vah biis padtaa hai, 'he outdoes this one') likely comes from the old numeral pairing उन्नीस-बीस (unniis-biis, 'nineteen-twenty'), where being 'twenty' (the higher number) came to mean being the better or superior one in a close comparison.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often miss that बीस (biis) here has nothing to do with the number twenty in meaning -- it is a fossilized idiom, not a comparison involving quantity. 2) The person or thing being outdone is marked with पर (par), not से (se) as an English speaker might expect from 'better than'.
