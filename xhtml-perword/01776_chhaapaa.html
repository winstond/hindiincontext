छापा (chhaapaa)

noun Primarily a noun meaning 'raid' or 'police/tax raid,' with a related but distinct noun sense meaning 'print/impression' (as of a stamp or pattern); also seen as an inflected form of the verb छापना (chhaapnaa, 'to print').
Sense 1 : raid, sudden police/official search
noun, masculine
Synonyms: धाड़ (dhaad, rare), दबिश (dabish)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly on the news -- आयकर विभाग का छापा (aaykar vibhaag kaa chhaapaa, 'an income tax department raid') is a staple headline in India, where raids on businesses or politicians' homes for tax evasion or corruption are a recurring media event.
Common Mistakes: 1) Almost always used with मारना (maarnaa) in the fixed expression छापा मारना (chhaapaa maarnaa, 'to conduct a raid') -- learners sometimes try to use करना (karnaa) instead, which sounds off. 2) The raided location takes पर (par): पुलिस ने दुकान पर छापा मारा (pulis ne dukaan par chhaapaa maaraa, 'the police raided the shop').
Sample Sentences:
कल रात पुलिस ने उस होटल में छापा मारा। Last night the police raided that hotel.
(kal raat pulis ne us hotal men chhaapaa maaraa।)

Sense 2 : print, stamped pattern or impression
noun, masculine
Synonyms: छपाई (chhapaaii, the process rather than the pattern itself), डिज़ाइन (dizaain)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this at the fabric shop: छापे वाला कपड़ा (chhaape vaalaa kapdaa, 'printed cloth') describes block-printed or patterned textiles, connecting this sense back to छापना (chhaapnaa, 'to print') -- the raid sense and this one share the same root idea of something being 'stamped' or 'struck.'
