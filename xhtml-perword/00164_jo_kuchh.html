जो कुछ (jo kuchh)

pronoun A correlative pronoun phrase meaning 'whatever' -- combines जो (jo, 'who/which') with कुछ (kuchh, 'something') to form an all-encompassing relative expression, typically paired with a correlative like वह (vah) later in the sentence.
Sense 1 : whatever (relative/correlative)
pronoun Functions as the relative half of a जो...वह correlative pair: जो कुछ (jo kuchh) opens the relative clause and वह (vah) or उसे (use) picks it up in the main clause. The correlative pronoun can often be dropped in casual speech, leaving जो कुछ (jo kuchh) to carry the whole meaning on its own.
Synonyms: जो भी (jo bhii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear जो कुछ (jo kuchh) in sentences like जो कुछ है, ले लो (jo kuchh hai, le lo, 'take whatever there is') -- it has a generous, all-inclusive feel, often used when someone is offering everything they have without holding back, which fits well with the everyday hospitality of 'jo hai, khaa lo' type phrases in Hindi households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use जो कुछ (jo kuchh) alone as if it were a simple question word like 'what,' but it is not interrogative -- it cannot be used to ask a question; it only introduces a relative clause. 2) Beginners sometimes forget to place a correlative (वह (vah), उसे (use), etc.) later in the sentence, which can make the sentence feel incomplete in careful speech, though it is commonly dropped colloquially.
Sample Sentences:
जो कुछ है, ले लो। Take whatever there is.
(jo kuchh hai, le lo।)
