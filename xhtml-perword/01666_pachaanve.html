पचानवे (pachaanve)

numeral The number ninety-five (95) -- a cardinal numeral used for counting and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number ninety-five (95)
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like all two-digit Hindi numbers, पचानवे (pachaanve) has to be learned as its own item rather than derived from 'ninety' + 'five' -- native speakers just have the whole 1-100 sequence memorized from childhood rhymes and school drills (paharaa/counting tables), so building real fluency with numbers like this one really does mean rote practice, the same way Hindi speakers themselves learned it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to build this compositionally from पच (pach) + नवे (nave) the way English builds 'ninety-five' from 'ninety' + 'five' -- but Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 are memorized as whole words, not assembled. 2) It is easy to confuse with पचास (pachaas, 'fifty') or पिचानवे (pichaanve, an alternate pronunciation of the same number) due to similar sound.
