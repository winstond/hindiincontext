बोली (bolii)

noun Covers two main senses: a dialect/way of speaking, and a bid/offer (as at an auction); it is also the feminine past form of the verb बोलना (bolnaa, to speak), which is not treated separately here.
Sense 1 : dialect, way of speaking, speech/tongue
noun, feminine
Synonyms: भाषा (bhaashaa, broader term for 'language'), जुबान (jubaan, colloquial, also means 'tongue')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In the Delhi-NCR belt you will hear people proudly distinguish their घर की बोली (ghar kii bolii, 'the dialect of home') from the हिंदी (hindii) they use formally -- someone from western UP might say their बोली (bolii) is खड़ी बोली (khadii bolii) or ब्रज (braj), even though they write and read in standard Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use बोली (bolii) and भाषा (bhaashaa) interchangeably, but बोली (bolii) usually refers to a regional/spoken dialect (like हरियाणवी (hariyaanvii) or भोजपुरी (bhojpurii)) while भाषा (bhaashaa) refers to a standardized, written language like हिंदी (hindii) or अंग्रेज़ी (angrezii).

Sense 2 : bid, offer (as at an auction)
noun, feminine Commonly used in the phrase बोली लगाना (bolii lagaanaa, to place a bid).
Synonyms: नीलामी (niilaamii, refers to the whole auction process, not just the bid itself)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word at village cattle fairs and property auctions alike -- लोग बोली लगाते हैं (log bolii lagaate hain, 'people place bids'), and the highest bidder wins with सबसे ऊँची बोली (sabse oonchii bolii, 'the highest bid').
