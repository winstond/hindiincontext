टूटना (tootnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to break, to shatter' -- physically, and in several extended senses (a fast breaking, a bond breaking, hopes breaking down). Pairs with the transitive causative तोड़ना (todnaa, 'to break something').
Sense 1 : to break, shatter (physical object)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: टुकड़े होना (tukde honaa, 'to become pieces'), फूटना (phootnaa, used mainly for glass/eggs/things that crack open)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Notice the pairing pattern: टूटना (tootnaa) is what happens to the object, तोड़ना (todnaa) is what an agent does to it -- this same intransitive/transitive shape shows up across many Hindi verb pairs (खुलना (khulnaa)/खोलना (kholnaa), बनना (bannaa)/बनाना (banaanaa)), so getting comfortable with टूटना (tootnaa) here pays off elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for तोड़ना (todnaa) when the subject itself is what breaks -- if you're describing the glass breaking (not someone breaking it), टूटना (tootnaa) is the intransitive partner and takes no ने (ne). 2) कप टूट गया (kap toot gayaa, 'the cup broke') sounds complete and natural because of the compound with जाना (jaanaa); the bare form कप टूटा (kap tootaa) is grammatical but less commonly how this gets said in casual speech.

(kaanch kaa gilaas pharsh par girkar toot gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to break (a fast, a vow, a habit, a record)
verb, intransitive The thing that breaks (उपवास (upvaas), व्रत (vrat), रिकॉर्ड (rikord)) is the grammatical subject, just as with the physical sense.
Synonyms: भंग होना (bhang honaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around festivals: व्रत टूट गया (vrat toot gayaa, 'the fast broke') or उसका उपवास टूट गया (uskaa upvaas toot gayaa, 'his/her fast got broken') -- the same intransitive verb that describes a shattered plate quietly extends to describe a discipline giving way.

Sense 3 : to break down, collapse (relationship, hope, spirit, health)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: बिखरना (bikharnaa, 'to scatter/fall apart', used for emotional collapse), दरकना (daraknaa, literary, 'to crack')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is where टूटना (tootnaa) becomes genuinely poetic in everyday speech -- रिश्ता टूट गया (rishtaa toot gayaa, 'the relationship broke') or उम्मीद टूट गई (ummiid toot gaii, 'hope broke/was shattered') treat abstract things exactly like breakable objects, which is a very natural metaphor in Hindi and worth internalizing rather than translating literally.
Common Mistakes: 1) दिल टूटना (dil tootnaa, 'heart to break') and हौसला टूटना (hauslaa tootnaa, 'courage/morale to break') are set idiomatic combinations -- learners sometimes translate 'my heart is broken' word-for-word with तोड़ा (todaa) instead of the natural intransitive मेरा दिल टूट गया (meraa dil toot gayaa, 'my heart broke').
Sample Sentences:
उसका रिश्ता दो साल पहले टूट गया था। Her relationship broke down two years ago.
(uskaa rishtaa do saal pahle toot gayaa thaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
