रवानी से (ravaanii se)

adverb An adverbial phrase meaning 'fluently, smoothly' -- describing speech, writing, or narration that flows without hesitation. Related to the noun रवानी (ravaanii, 'flow, fluency').
Sense 1 : fluently, smoothly (of speech, writing, reading)
adverb Typically pairs with verbs like बोलना (bolnaa, 'to speak'), पढ़ना (padhnaa, 'to read'), or लिखना (likhnaa, 'to write') -- e.g. रवानी से बोलना (ravaanii se bolnaa, 'to speak fluently').
Synonyms: धाराप्रवाह (dhaaraaprvaah, slightly more formal/written), बिना रुके (binaa ruke, 'without stopping')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: रवानी (ravaanii) comes from the same root as रवाना (ravaanaa, 'departed, sent off') -- both carry the idea of smooth forward motion, so रवानी से (ravaanii se) literally evokes words 'flowing' or 'moving along' effortlessly. You will often hear it as praise: किसी की उर्दू रवानी से चलती है (kisii kii urdoo ravaanii se chaltii hai, 'someone's Urdu flows fluently') is a classic compliment in Delhi's Urdu-Hindi-mixed literary circles.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for धाराप्रवाह (dhaaraaprvaah) as the default word for 'fluent,' but रवानी से (ravaanii se) is the more natural, commonly heard choice in everyday conversation about someone's speech or writing style. 2) The postposition से (se) here is fixed as part of this adverbial phrase -- it does not vary the way से (se) does in its other uses (instrument, source, etc.).
