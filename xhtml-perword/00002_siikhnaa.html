सीखना (siikhnaa)

verb One core meaning: to learn or acquire a skill/knowledge. Closely paired with its causative सिखाना (sikhaanaa, 'to teach') -- same root, opposite direction of the action.
Sense 1 : to learn (a skill, language, or lesson)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सीख लेना (siikh lenaa, compound-verb variant with completive nuance), ज्ञान प्राप्त करना (gyaan praapt karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi treats teaching and learning as a tight causative pair: सिखाना (sikhaanaa) is literally 'to cause to learn,' so a teacher सिखाता है (sikhaataa hai) and a student सीखता है (siikhtaa hai) -- keep the pair in mind together rather than memorizing them separately. You'll also hear सबक सीखना (sabak siikhnaa, 'to learn a lesson') used the same way English uses it figuratively, for a hard-won life lesson rather than a classroom one, e.g. after a bad business deal or a family quarrel.
Common Mistakes: 1) As a transitive verb, सीखना (siikhnaa) takes ने (ne) in the perfective, with the verb agreeing with the object: उसने हिंदी सीखी (usne hindii siikhii, 'she learned Hindi'), not वह हिंदी सीखा (vah hindii siikhaa). 2) Don't confuse सीखना (siikhnaa, 'to learn') with सिखाना (sikhaanaa, 'to teach') -- the short vowel/long vowel and nasalization difference is easy to slip on when writing quickly. 3) The person you learn from is marked with से (se), not को (ko): मैंने अपने दादा से खाना बनाना सीखा (mainne apne daadaa se khaanaa banaanaa siikhaa, 'I learned to cook from my grandfather').
