नीचा (niichaa)

adjective An adjective meaning 'low' or 'short in height', with an important figurative extension into social/moral 'lowness' seen in common idioms like नीचा दिखाना (niichaa dikhaanaa, 'to humiliate/show down').
Sense 1 : low, not high (physical height/level)
adjective
Synonyms: छोटा (chhotaa, scope: short/small, not always about height alone)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around the house: नीचा तकिया (niichaa takiyaa, 'a low pillow') or दरवाज़ा नीचा है (darvaazaa niichaa hai, 'the door is low') when someone almost bangs their head walking through an old haveli doorway.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for छोटा (chhotaa) to mean 'low' in height, but नीचा (niichaa) is the natural word for something being low to the ground or low in level, e.g. a low wall or low table, while छोटा (chhotaa) is about overall size.

Sense 2 : downcast, lowered (of eyes, head -- in fixed idiomatic phrases)
adjective Mainly appears fixed with सिर (sir, 'head') or आँखें (aankhen, 'eyes') and the verb करना/होना, as in सिर नीचा करना (sir niichaa karnaa, 'to lower one's head in shame').
Synonyms: झुका हुआ (jhukaa huaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the emotional heart of the word: नीचा दिखाना (niichaa dikhaanaa, 'to make someone look down, i.e. to humiliate them') and सिर नीचा होना (sir niichaa honaa, 'to feel ashamed, head hung low') both frame shame as a physical lowering, exactly like English 'to be shamefaced', but Hindi builds it right into this one adjective.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is not really a free-standing sense on its own but shows up glued to fixed idioms about shame or humility; using नीचा (niichaa) loosely to mean 'sad' or 'depressed' outside these set phrases will sound off to a native speaker.
Sample Sentences:
उसने मुझे सबके सामने नीचा दिखाया। He humiliated me in front of everyone.
(usne mujhe sabke saamne niichaa dikhaayaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : lowly, inferior, base (social/moral standing)
adjective
Synonyms: तुच्छ (tuchchh, formal), घटिया (ghatiyaa, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense carries real social weight in Hindi -- नीच (niich, a related but distinct word) is the harsher caste-tinged insult, while नीचा (niichaa) used this way is milder, more about someone's conduct or standing feeling 'small' or 'base' than a fixed slur.
Sample Sentences:
उसने ऐसी नीची हरकत की कि सब हैरान रह गए। He did such a base thing that everyone was left stunned.
(usne aisii niichii harkat kii ki sab hairaan rah gae।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(paison ke lie doston ko dhokhaa denaa bahut niichii baat hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
वह अपने आपको नीचा मत समझ, तूने कुछ गलत नहीं किया। Don't think of yourself as lowly, you haven't done anything wrong.
