औसत (ausat)

noun/adjective औसत (ausat) is primarily a noun meaning 'average,' with a closely linked adjective sense meaning 'average, mediocre, so-so.'
Sense 1 : average (the calculated mean)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मध्यमान (madhymaan, technical/statistics term)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around exam season and cricket -- कितने रन का औसत है? (kitne ran kaa ausat hai?, 'what's the batting average?') is a very common construction, since batting averages are a national obsession.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the postposition पड़ना (padnaa) or निकालना (nikaalnaa) pattern used with this noun -- to say 'calculate the average' Hindi typically uses औसत निकालना (ausat nikaalnaa, 'to work out the average'), not a literal 'calculate' verb.
Sample Sentences:
उसका बैटिंग औसत क्या है? What is his batting average?
(uskaa baiting ausat hai?)

Sense 2 : average, ordinary, mediocre
adjective
Synonyms: सामान्य (saamaany), साधारण (saadhaaran), मामूली (maamoolii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Calling someone's work or a film औसत (ausat) is a polite way of saying it was disappointing without being harsh -- a very Delhi way of damning with faint praise.
Common Mistakes: 1) Used to describe quality or performance as 'middling,' e.g. औसत दर्जे का (ausat darje kaa, 'of average/mediocre caliber') -- learners sometimes miss that this phrase carries a mildly negative shade, closer to 'nothing special' than a neutral statistical middle.
Sample Sentences:

(yah film bilkul ausat thii।)
