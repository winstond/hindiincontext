नेता (netaa)

noun नेता (netaa) covers two related senses: a general 'leader' (of any group, movement, or team) and, more specifically, a 'politician' -- the second sense often carries a cynical or sarcastic edge in everyday speech.
Sense 1 : leader (of a group, team, or movement)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नायक (naayak), मुखिया (mukhiyaa, head of a village or small group), अग्रणी (agrnii, leading figure)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the neutral, respectful sense you hear in phrases like टीम का नेता (tiim kaa netaa, 'the team's leader') or आंदोलन का नेता (aandolan kaa netaa, 'the movement's leader'). It is also the root of the famous honorific नेता जी (netaa jii), used above all for Subhas Chandra Bose.
Common Mistakes: 1) The oblique plural is नेताओं (netaaon), not a regular -आ pattern -- learners sometimes try to inflect it like a normal -आ noun.

हर टीम को एक अच्छे नेता की ज़रूरत होती है। Every team needs a good leader.
(har tiim ko ek achchhe netaa kii zaroorat hotii hai।)

Sense 2 : politician
noun, masculine
Synonyms: राजनेता (raajnetaa), राजनीतिज्ञ (raajniitigy, more formal/neutral, sounds like a textbook term)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask almost anyone on a Delhi street what they think of नेता लोग (netaa log) and you will likely get an eye-roll -- this sense of नेता (netaa) is loaded with everyday cynicism about corruption and empty promises, quite unlike the respectful 'leader' sense above.
Common Mistakes: 1) In casual conversation नेता (netaa) almost always means 'politician' rather than 'leader' in general -- context (politics, elections, corruption) usually makes clear which sense is meant. 2) The plural नेता लोग (netaa log, 'politicians as a class') is a very common colloquial way to talk about politicians dismissively, not just 'leaders.'
