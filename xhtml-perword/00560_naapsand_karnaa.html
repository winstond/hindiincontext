नापसंद करना (naapsand karnaa)

verb A compound built from नापसंद (naapsand, 'disliked/disapproved of', itself ना- 'not' + पसंद 'liked') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); means 'to dislike, disapprove of'.
Sense 1 : to dislike, to disapprove of
verb, transitive The disliked thing/person is usually the direct object, not marked with को unless definite: वह चाय नापसंद करता है (vah chaay naapsand kartaa hai, 'he dislikes tea').
Synonyms: नफ़रत करना (nafrat karnaa, stronger, 'to hate'), नामंज़ूर करना (naamanzoor karnaa, 'to reject/disapprove')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of नापसंद (naapsand) as the flipped mirror of पसंद (pasand) -- Hindi builds a lot of vocabulary this way with the ना-/ना- negative prefix (नाराज़ (naaraaz, 'displeased'), नालायक (naalaayak, 'worthless')), so learning this word also teaches you a productive pattern. In everyday Delhi speech, मुझे पसंद नहीं (mujhe pasand nahiin) is far more common than नापसंद करना (naapsand karnaa), which shows up more in slightly formal or written contexts, or when someone wants to sound a touch more decisive about their distaste.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पसंद न करना (pasand n karnaa) or पसंद नहीं करना (pasand nahiin karnaa) instead, which are also correct and arguably more common in casual speech -- नापसंद करना (naapsand karnaa) is a bit more formal/deliberate, like saying 'disapprove of' rather than just 'not like.' 2) As a ने-taking transitive verb in the past, remember perfective agreement: उसने यह फ़िल्म नापसंद की (usne yah film naapsand kii, 'she disliked this film') -- the verb agrees with फ़िल्म, not with उसने.
