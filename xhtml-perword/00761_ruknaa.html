रुकना (ruknaa)

verb A basic intransitive verb meaning 'to stop' or 'to halt' -- covers both a moving thing coming to rest and a person pausing or waiting somewhere.
Sense 1 : to stop moving
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: थमना (thamnaa, of motion/flow slowing to a halt)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly from auto and bus drivers -- रुको! (ruko!, 'stop/wait!') or ज़रा रुकिए (zaraa rukie, 'please wait a moment') -- it's one of the first commands learners pick up on the street. Note the close pair रुकना/रोकना (ruknaa/roknaa): the intransitive-transitive split is very productive in Hindi and worth internalizing early.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for बंद होना (band honaa) for 'stop' -- that works for machines, sounds, or processes switching off, but रुकना (ruknaa) is the natural choice for anything in motion (a car, a person walking, rain) coming to a halt. 2) रुकना (ruknaa) is intransitive; to stop something else you need the transitive रोकना (roknaa), a different verb, not just a ने-marked version of this one.
Sample Sentences:
बस अगले स्टॉप पर रुकेगी। The bus will stop at the next stop.
(bas agle stop par rukegii।)

Sense 2 : to pause, wait, or stay briefly
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: ठहरना (thaharnaa, slightly more formal, also used for lodging/staying overnight)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense overlaps a lot with ठहरना (thaharnaa); at a friend's house you might say मैं यहाँ रुक जाऊँगा (main yahaan ruk jaaoongaa, 'I'll stay/stop over here') for an informal overnight stay, where ठहरना (thaharnaa) would sound a touch more formal, closer to 'lodging.'
Common Mistakes: 1) In the sense of 'wait a moment,' learners often default to इंतज़ार करना (intzaar karnaa) -- that's fine for waiting *for* something or someone, but रुकना (ruknaa) is what you say for a simple pause in place, as in एक मिनट रुको (ek minat ruko, 'wait/hold on a minute').
Sample Sentences:

(kyaa tum ek minat ruk sakte ho?)
