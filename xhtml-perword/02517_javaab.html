जवाब (javaab)

noun Mainly means 'answer/reply' in everyday speech, with an extended sense of 'response/retort/match' (as in 'no match for' or a comeback), and it also appears in a couple of fixed phrases about giving an account of oneself.
Sense 1 : answer, reply
noun, masculine
Synonyms: उत्तर (uttar, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If you call someone and they don't pick up, you'd say उसने जवाब नहीं दिया (usne javaab nahiin diyaa, 'he/she didn't answer') -- जवाब (javaab) covers replies to questions, letters, calls, and messages alike, much more broadly than English 'answer' sometimes feels.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for उत्तर (uttar) by textbook habit, but in spoken Hindi जवाब (javaab) is what people actually say for 'answer' -- in class, on the phone, in an exam. 2) The verb pairing is जवाब देना (javaab denaa, 'to give an answer') for answering and जवाब मिलना (javaab milnaa, 'to get an answer') for receiving one -- learners sometimes try to force जवाब करना (javaab karnaa), which is not idiomatic here.
Sample Sentences:
मैंने उसे फ़ोन किया, पर उसने जवाब नहीं दिया। I called him, but he didn't answer.
(mainne use fon kiyaa, par usne javaab nahiin diyaa।)

Sense 2 : response, retort, match/rival (figurative)
noun, masculine Common in the pattern X का जवाब नहीं (X kaa javaab nahiin, 'there's no match for X') to praise something as unrivaled.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in everyday praise: दिल्ली की चाट का कोई जवाब नहीं (dillii kii chaat kaa koii javaab nahiin, 'Delhi's chaat has no match') -- a very Delhi way to boast about street food. It also shows up in political and film contexts as a 'reply' to a rival's move, like a sharp comeback in an argument.
Common Mistakes: 1) इसका कोई जवाब नहीं (iskaa koii javaab nahiin, 'there's nothing like it') is a fixed compliment structure -- learners sometimes translate it too literally as 'no answer' and miss that it means 'unbeatable, unmatched'.
Sample Sentences:
दिल्ली की चाट का कोई जवाब नहीं है। There's no match for Delhi's chaat.
(dillii kii chaat kaa koii javaab nahiin hai।)
