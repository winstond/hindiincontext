बेचैनी (bechainii)

noun A single noun meaning restlessness or unease, covering both physical discomfort and mental/emotional agitation. Related to the adjective बेचैन (bechain, 'restless, uneasy') with the abstract-noun suffix -ई (ii).
Sense 1 : restlessness, unease (physical or mental)
noun, feminine Commonly appears in the pattern X को बेचैनी होना/महसूस होना (X ko bechainii honaa, 'X feels restless') or बेचैनी में होना (bechainii men honaa, 'to be in a state of restlessness'), following the dative-subject pattern the learner already knows from लगना (lagnaa).
Synonyms: व्याकुलता (vyaakultaa, literary), घबराहट (ghabraahat, nervous agitation), बेकरारी (bekraarii, literary), अशांति (ashaanti, scope: broader, also social/political unrest)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in everyday health talk -- a mother might ask a feverish child, बेचैनी हो रही है क्या? (bechainii ho rahii hai kyaa?, 'are you feeling restless/uneasy?') -- as well as in emotional contexts, like waiting anxiously for exam results or a loved one's phone call. It sits at the intersection of physical fidgetiness and mental disquiet in a way English keeps more separate.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to make बेचैनी (bechainii) the subject of an active verb like a person would be, e.g. saying मैं बेचैनी हूँ (main bechainii hoon) instead of the correct मुझे बेचैनी हो रही है (mujhe bechainii ho rahii hai, 'I am feeling restless') -- the feeler takes को (ko), not मैं (main) as subject of 'to be' the noun. 2) It covers both a racing, anxious mind and physical restlessness (like being unable to sit still or feeling unwell), unlike English words that split these into separate terms.
