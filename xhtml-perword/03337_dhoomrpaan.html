धूम्रपान (dhoomrpaan)

noun A formal, single-sense noun meaning 'smoking' (of tobacco), built from धूम्र (dhoomr, smoke) + पान (paan, drinking/consuming) -- the word learners see on official signage rather than hear casually at home.
Sense 1 : smoking (of tobacco/cigarettes)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सिगरेट पीना (sigret piinaa, everyday verb phrase), स्मोकिंग (smoking, colloquial loanword)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in India without ever really 'hearing' it spoken -- it is the word stenciled on 'No Smoking' signs (धूम्रपान वर्जित है, dhoomrpaan varjit hai, 'Smoking is prohibited') in trains, offices, and hospitals, and on the mandatory health warnings on cigarette packets. In real conversation, people almost always say सिगरेट पीना (sigret piinaa) instead, so think of धूम्रपान (dhoomrpaan) as the 'official register' twin of a very ordinary habit.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for धूम्रपान (dhoomrpaan) in casual speech, but everyday conversation almost always uses the simple verb phrase सिगरेट पीना (sigret piinaa, 'to drink/smoke a cigarette') or बीड़ी पीना (biidii piinaa) instead -- धूम्रपान (dhoomrpaan) sounds like it belongs on a warning label, not in a chat with friends. 2) As a noun, it needs a supporting verb like करना (karnaa) to form an action -- धूम्रपान करना (dhoomrpaan karnaa, 'to smoke') -- it is not itself a verb.
