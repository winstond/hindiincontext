सब्ज़ीमंडी (sabziimandii)

noun A compound noun combining सब्ज़ी (sabzii, 'vegetable') and मंडी (mandii, 'wholesale market'), meaning 'vegetable market' as a general category of place.
Sense 1 : vegetable market
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सब्ज़ी बाज़ार (sabzii baazaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Every Indian town of any size has its सब्ज़ीमंडी (sabziimandii), usually a bustling, crowded area best visited early in the morning when the produce is freshest and prices are best before the midday heat sets in. In several cities (Delhi included) 'Sabzi Mandi' is also the actual name of a neighborhood that grew up around a historic wholesale market -- much like how 'Market Street' can be both an ordinary phrase and a real address elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat सब्ज़ीमंडी (sabziimandii) as always a specific proper place name because several Indian cities have a locality actually called this; as a common noun it just means 'the vegetable market' in general, the way you'd say 'I'm going to the vegetable market' about any town's produce market. 2) Don't confuse मंडी (mandii) here with a regular दुकान (dukaan, 'shop') -- a मंडी (mandii) is specifically a large wholesale market, not a single stall.
