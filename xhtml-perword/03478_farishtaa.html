फ़रिश्ता (farishtaa)

noun A borrowed noun meaning 'angel' -- used both literally (in religious/folk contexts) and figuratively for a kind, selfless person.
Sense 1 : angel (kind, selfless person)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: देवदूत (devdoot)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech, फ़रिश्ता (farishtaa) is most often heard as praise for someone who helped a stranger -- 'वह तो भगवान का फ़रिश्ता बनकर आया (vah to bhagvaan kaa farishtaa bankar aayaa, he came like an angel sent by God).' It carries a slightly Urdu-Persian devotional flavor, common in both Hindu and Muslim contexts, unlike the more Sanskritic देवदूत (devdoot).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for देवदूत (devdoot) as the default word for 'angel,' but फ़रिश्ता (farishtaa) is the word people actually reach for in everyday speech, especially when praising someone's kindness -- 'आप तो फ़रिश्ता हैं (aap to farishtaa hain, you are an angel)' is a very natural compliment.

Sense 2 : angel (religious/celestial being)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: देवदूत (devdoot)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In Islamic tradition (and borrowed into general Hindi religious vocabulary), फ़रिश्ता (farishtaa) refers to the celestial beings who serve God, like Gabriel/Jibrail -- distinct from the more casual 'kind person' usage but rooted in the same image of a messenger of good.
