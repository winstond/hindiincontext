फिर भी (phir bhii)

conjunction A connective meaning 'even so, nevertheless, still' -- used to introduce a statement that goes against what was just said or expected.
Sense 1 : nevertheless, even so, still
conjunction Sits at the start of the second clause to mark it as contrary to what would logically follow from the first: X, फिर भी Y (X, phir bhii Y, 'X, but even so Y').
Synonyms: तो भी (to bhii), लेकिन फिर भी (lekin phir bhii, emphatic variant), बावजूद इसके (baavjood iske, more formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday complaining and storytelling: बारिश हो रही थी, फिर भी वह गया (baarish ho rahii thii, phir bhii vah gayaa, 'It was raining, but he went anyway') is the exact shape of a thousand daily anecdotes. It pairs naturally with बावजूद (baavjood, 'in spite of') constructions -- फिर भी (phir bhii) is the everyday, spoken-register way to express that same 'despite everything' idea.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to translate 'even so' word-for-word using फिर (phir, 'then/again') and भी (bhii, 'also') as if they carry independent meaning here -- but फिर भी (phir bhii) functions as one fixed connective, not a literal combination of the two. 2) It is placed at the start of the clause it introduces, not tacked onto the end like English 'still' sometimes is.
