शतरंज (shatranj)

noun A single-sense word: the board game chess (shatranj), and by extension the physical chessboard/set used to play it.
Sense 1 : chess (the game, and the board/set)
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: शतरंज (shatranj) comes from Persian, itself tracing back to Sanskrit चतुरंग (chaturang, 'four divisions/limbs' -- referring to the four parts of an ancient army: elephants, chariots, cavalry, infantry), which is widely credited as an ancestor of modern chess. You will often hear the phrase शतरंज की बाज़ी (shatranj kii baazii, 'a game/round of chess'), and in political or strategic contexts people say things like शतरंज की बिसात पर चाल चलना (shatranj kii bisaat par chaal chalnaa, 'to make a move on the chessboard of politics/strategy') to describe scheming or maneuvering.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to say 'play chess' with बजाना (bajaanaa, used for instruments), but the correct verb is खेलना (khelnaa) -- शतरंज खेलना (shatranj khelnaa, 'to play chess'). 2) The board itself is also called शतरंज (shatranj) or शतरंज की बिसात (shatranj kii bisaat), not a separate word -- context tells you whether the game or the physical board is meant.
