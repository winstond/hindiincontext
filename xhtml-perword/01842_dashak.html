दशक (dashak)

noun A single, straightforward sense: दशक (dashak) means 'decade,' a period of ten years. It's a common word in news, history, and casual talk about time gone by.
Sense 1 : decade
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in news and history talk: पिछले दशक में (pichhle dashak men, 'in the last decade') or इस दशक के अंत तक (is dashak ke ant tak, 'by the end of this decade'). It's the natural way to talk about spans of ten years, much more common in everyday Hindi than a Sanskrit-sounding word might suggest.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse दशक (dashak, 'decade') with दशकों (dashkon, 'decades', oblique plural) versus दशक (dashak) itself, which is singular. 2) Don't confuse with दशमी (dashmii, 'tenth day of the lunar fortnight') or दशा (dashaa, 'condition/state') -- similar-sounding but unrelated words.
Sample Sentences:
पिछले दशक में भारत की अर्थव्यवस्था तेज़ी से बढ़ी। In the last decade, India's economy grew rapidly.
(pichhle dashak men bhaarat kii arthavyvasthaa tezii se badhii।)
