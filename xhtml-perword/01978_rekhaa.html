रेखा (rekhaa)

noun A noun meaning 'line' -- ranging from a simple drawn line to more figurative or technical uses like a boundary line or a line on the palm/face.
Sense 1 : a drawn or marked line
noun, feminine
Synonyms: लाइन (laain, everyday, from English), लकीर (lakiir)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In a school-math context you will hear सरल रेखा (saral rekhaa, 'straight line') and वृत्त की रेखा (vritt kii rekhaa, 'line of a circle'); रेखा (rekhaa) has a slightly more formal, written-page feel compared to the everyday लकीर (lakiir) or लाइन (laain).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लाइन (laain) by default since it is borrowed from English and feels easier -- but रेखा (rekhaa) is the word you will see in textbooks, geometry class, and more formal or written contexts, while लाइन (laain) dominates casual speech (queues, phone lines, etc.).

(adhyaapak ne blaikbord par ek siidhii rekhaa khiinchii।)

Sense 2 : a boundary or dividing line (often figurative)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सीमा (siimaa, boundary, broader sense), लकीर (lakiir)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense in set phrases like गरीबी की रेखा (gariibii kii rekhaa, 'poverty line') -- a very common phrase in news and civics discussions -- where रेखा (rekhaa) marks a conceptual threshold rather than a physical mark.
Sample Sentences:
देश की एक तिहाई आबादी अभी भी गरीबी की रेखा से नीचे रहती है। A third of the country's population still lives below the poverty line.
(desh kii ek tihaaii aabaadii abhii bhii gariibii kii rekhaa se niiche rahtii hai।)

(donon deshon ke biich kii siimaa rekhaa par aaj bhii tanaav banaa huaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : a line on the palm or face (as in palmistry/astrology), or a faint trace/streak
noun, feminine
Synonyms: निशान (nishaan, mark/trace, broader sense)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in the world of हस्तरेखा (hastrekhaa, 'palm-line', i.e. palmistry) -- a पंडित (pandit) or ज्योतिषी (jyotishii) reading someone's हाथ की रेखाएँ (haath kii rekhaaen, 'lines of the hand') is a familiar image in Indian households, especially around weddings and major life decisions.

(pandit jii ne mere haath kii rekhaaen dekhkar bhavishy bataayaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
इस ज्योतिषी को हस्तरेखा पढ़ने में बड़ी महारत है। This astrologer has great expertise in reading palm lines.
(is jyotishii ko hastrekhaa padhne men badii mahaarat hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
शादी से पहले दादी ने दुल्हन के हाथ की रेखाएँ जरूर दिखवाई थीं। Before the wedding, grandmother had definitely gotten the bride's palm lines examined.
