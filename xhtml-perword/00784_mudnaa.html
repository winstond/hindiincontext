मुड़ना (mudnaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to turn' or 'to bend' -- covers physical turning (a person, vehicle, or road changing direction) and the extended sense of paper/cloth folding or bending. Related to the transitive causative मोड़ना (modnaa, 'to turn something').
Sense 1 : to turn (change direction)
verb, intransitive Commonly used with दिशा (dishaa, 'direction') or with a postpositional phrase like की ओर (kii or, 'towards') or बाएँ/दाएँ (baaen/daaen, 'left/right') to specify the new direction.
Synonyms: घूमना (ghoomnaa, also means 'to roam/rotate')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Give directions in Delhi and you will hear this constantly: सीधे चलिए, फिर बाएँ मुड़िए (siidhe chalie, phir baaen mudie, 'go straight, then turn left'). Autorickshaw drivers and street vendors use it as naturally as English speakers say 'turn here.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for मोड़ना (modnaa) when they mean मुड़ना (mudnaa) -- मोड़ना (modnaa) is transitive ('to turn something,' like a steering wheel or a page), while मुड़ना (mudnaa) is intransitive ('to turn' oneself or 'to take a turn'). 2) With vehicles or roads, गली मुड़ती है (galii mudtii hai, 'the lane turns') sounds natural, but learners often over-specify with an unnecessary object marker, forgetting the verb is intransitive.
Sample Sentences:
सीधे चलिए, फिर बाएँ मुड़िए। Go straight, then turn left.
(siidhe chalie, phir baaen mudie।)

Sense 2 : to bend, fold, curve (of an object)
verb, intransitive Used for pliable or rigid objects that curve or fold under pressure -- a nail, a page corner, a wire -- rather than for a person changing direction.
Synonyms: झुकना (jhuknaa, to bend/stoop, often of a person or a rod)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in everyday domestic contexts -- a mother scolding a child that the corner of a book's page has मुड़ गया है (mud gayaa hai, 'has gotten bent/folded'), or a tailor noting that the wire in a garment has मुड़ गया (mud gayaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners conflate this with झुकना (jhuknaa): मुड़ना (mudnaa) suggests the shape itself has changed (a bent nail, a folded page), while झुकना (jhuknaa) suggests lowering or stooping (a bowed head, a bent back).
Sample Sentences:
किताब के पन्ने का कोना मुड़ गया है। The corner of the book's page has gotten bent.
