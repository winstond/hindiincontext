घड़ी (ghadii)

noun Primarily means a clock or watch, the timekeeping device; also extends to mean a moment or point in time, as in a memorable or fateful occasion.
Sense 1 : clock, watch (timekeeping device)
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In an Indian household you will hear kaii bar ghadii dekho (kaii baar ghadii dekho, 'keep checking the clock') from an elder hurrying someone along, since checking the time is often framed as watching the clock itself rather than 'checking time' the way English does. Wristwatches and wall clocks are both just घड़ी (ghadii) -- context, not vocabulary, tells you which one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for अलार्म (alaarm) or टाइमर (taaimar) when they mean an ordinary wall clock or wristwatch; घड़ी (ghadii) covers both a wristwatch and a wall/table clock without distinguishing them the way English 'watch' vs 'clock' does. 2) Time-telling phrases use घड़ी (ghadii) with में (men) or बजे (baje) constructions (e.g., घड़ी में कितने बजे हैं (ghadii men kitne baje hain, 'what time is it on the clock')) rather than a direct possessive frame an English speaker might expect.
Sample Sentences:
मेरी घड़ी में अभी पाँच बजे हैं। It's five o'clock on my watch right now.
(merii ghadii men abhii paanch baje hain।)

दीवार पर लगी घड़ी रुक गई है। The clock hanging on the wall has stopped.

Sense 2 : moment, point in time (often a significant or fateful one)
noun, feminine Common in set phrases like शुभ घड़ी (shubh ghadii, 'auspicious moment') and नाज़ुक घड़ी (naazuk ghadii, 'delicate/critical moment'), where घड़ी (ghadii) refers to an occasion or juncture rather than a device.
Synonyms: पल (pal), क्षण (kshan, literary), वक़्त (vaqt, meaning scope: general time, not a specific instant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often hear this in emotionally charged contexts -- a wedding priest announcing the शुभ घड़ी (shubh ghadii, 'auspicious moment') for a ritual, or someone reflecting on a नाज़ुक घड़ी (naazuk ghadii, 'critical moment') in a family crisis. It carries a sense of gravity that plain समय (samay) lacks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners familiar only with the 'clock' sense may miss this figurative use entirely and default to वक़्त (vaqt) or समय (samay) in every context; घड़ी (ghadii) specifically suits a weighty, singular moment, not time in general.
Sample Sentences:
आखिरकार वह शुभ घड़ी आ गई। Finally, that auspicious moment arrived.
(aakhirkaar vah shubh ghadii aa gaii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
