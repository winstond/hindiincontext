जुदा (judaa)

adjective An adjective meaning 'separate' or 'apart', most often met in the emotional sense of being separated from a loved one; it also feeds the common verb phrase जुदा होना (judaa honaa, 'to part/separate').
Sense 1 : separated, apart (from someone, emotionally or physically)
adjective
Synonyms: अलग (alag), बिछड़ा (bichhdaa, used of people parted, esp. in poetic/filmi contexts)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word of separation in Hindi film songs and shayari -- lovers become जुदा (judaa) from each other, not अलग (alag), because जुदा (judaa) has that ache of longing built into it. It comes from Persian, and you will hear it constantly in ghazals and romantic dialogue.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for अलग (alag) in every context, but जुदा (judaa) carries a heavier emotional weight -- it is the word of heartbreak and separation from loved ones, not of sorting objects into piles. 2) जुदा (judaa) is invariant -- it does not change form for gender or number, unlike many other Hindi adjectives ending in आ (aa).

Sense 2 : different, distinct (in kind or nature)
adjective
Synonyms: अलग (alag), भिन्न (bhinn, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Here जुदा (judaa) just means 'a different sort' -- as in वह सबसे जुदा है (vah sabse judaa hai, 'he is different from everyone'), praising someone's uniqueness. It sits comfortably next to अलग (alag) in this sense, though it leans slightly more literary/poetic.
