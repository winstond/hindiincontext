खड़ा (khadaa)

adjective An everyday adjective/participle meaning 'standing' or 'upright/stationary,' also used in fixed idioms about setting up, halting, or waiting; closely tied to the verb खड़ा होना (khadaa honaa, 'to stand up').
Sense 1 : standing (posture)
adjective Almost always paired with होना (honaa) or रहना (rahnaa) to describe a person or animal in a standing posture: वह खड़ा है (vah khadaa hai, 'he is standing').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a crowded Delhi bus or metro: कोई सीट नहीं है, हमें खड़ा रहना पड़ेगा (koii siit nahiin hai, hamen khadaa rahnaa padegaa, 'there's no seat, we'll have to remain standing') is a very lived phrase here. खड़ा होना (khadaa honaa) is the natural opposite of बैठना (baithnaa, 'to sit') in daily conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use बैठना (baithnaa) or उठना (uthnaa) constructions here, but 'stand up' as an action is खड़ा होना (khadaa honaa), literally 'to become standing' -- होना (honaa) is doing the work of 'become' here, not just 'be.' 2) Because it agrees like a regular adjective, learners sometimes forget to change it for gender/number, saying खड़ा for a feminine or plural subject when it should be खड़ी or खड़े.

Sense 2 : stationary, parked, halted (of vehicles)
adjective Used for vehicles or machines that are stopped/parked rather than moving: गाड़ी बाहर खड़ी है (gaadii baahar khadii hai, 'the car is parked outside').
Synonyms: रुका हुआ (rukaa huaa, of a vehicle stopped in traffic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In markets and colonies across Delhi-NCR, a guard or neighbor might ask गाड़ी कहाँ खड़ी करूँ? (gaadii kahaan khadii karoon?, 'where should I park the car?') -- this sense extends the 'standing' idea from people to vehicles quite naturally.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for पार्क (paark, a borrowed verb) when खड़ी करना (khadii karnaa, 'to park') is the more natural everyday phrase, e.g. गाड़ी खड़ी करो (gaadii khadii karo, 'park the car').

Sense 3 : set up, established, erected
adjective Used metaphorically for something that has been 'put up' or 'raised,' from tents and structures to figurative situations: मुसीबत खड़ी हो गई (musiibat khadii ho gaii, 'a problem has arisen').
Synonyms: स्थापित (sthaapit, formal, for institutions/monuments)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is where खड़ा (khadaa) becomes almost like English 'to crop up': सवाल खड़ा होना (savaal khadaa honaa, 'a question to arise') or दीवार खड़ी करना (diivaar khadii karnaa, 'to erect a wall') both use the same underlying image of something rising into existence.
Common Mistakes: 1) The idiom मुसीबत खड़ी होना (musiibat khadii honaa, 'trouble to arise') surprises learners because खड़ा here has drifted from physical posture to 'a situation coming into being' -- there's no literal standing involved.
