कथक (kathak)

noun Primarily the name of a classical North Indian dance form; can also refer to a performer/narrator of stories in the older, literary sense.
Sense 1 : Kathak (classical dance form)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: कथक (kathak) is one of the eight classical dance forms of India, closely associated with Lucknow and Jaipur gharanas, and known for its intricate footwork and spins. In Delhi you will hear the word constantly around cultural events at places like Kamani Auditorium or India Habitat Centre.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat कथक (kathak) as if it were the word for a dancer, but in this sense it names the dance style itself; the performer is कथक नर्तक (kathak nartak) or कथक नृत्यांगना (kathak nrityaangnaa) for a woman.

Sense 2 : storyteller/narrator (older, literary sense)
noun, masculine This sense derives from कथा (kathaa, 'story') plus the agentive suffix, literally 'one who tells a story'; it is the etymological root from which the dance form's name also comes, since Kathak performers historically narrated stories through dance.
Synonyms: कथावाचक (kathaavaachak, literary), वक्ता (vaktaa)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This older sense survives mainly in religious or literary contexts, describing someone who narrates katha (kathaa) -- stories from scriptures like the Ramayana or Bhagavad Gita -- to an audience, often in a temple setting.
