पीछे (piichhe)

postposition/adverb Core meaning is spatial/temporal 'behind, after, back' -- it functions as a postposition (with के, ke), a plain adverb, and shows up in a few common idioms about persistence and pursuit.
Sense 1 : behind (place)
postposition Takes के (ke) before it: घर के पीछे (ghar ke piichhe, 'behind the house').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of पीछे (piichhe) as the spatial opposite of आगे (aage, 'ahead, in front') -- the two are almost always taught and used as a pair, e.g. आगे-पीछे (aage-piichhe, 'here and there / around').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop के (ke) and say घर पीछे (ghar piichhe) instead of घर के पीछे (ghar ke piichhe) -- पीछे needs the के link like most compound postpositions. 2) Confusing पीछे (piichhe, 'behind') with बाद (baad, 'after,' time-only) -- पीछे covers space and can extend to time, but बाद is the default for pure time sequencing.
Sample Sentences:

(merii kitaab mez ke piichhe hai।)

Sense 3 : after (time/sequence)
postposition Also takes के (ke): मेरे जाने के पीछे (mere jaane ke piichhe) is less natural than मेरे जाने के बाद (mere jaane ke baad) for plain time sequence -- पीछे in this sense is more common in the fixed idiom क्यों पीछे... (see CulturalNote) and in causal framing than in simple 'after' statements.
Synonyms: बाद (baad)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense overlaps with the 'because of' sense below -- मेरे पीछे (mere piichhe) can mean both 'behind me' and 'because of me / on my account,' and context (usually के कारण-style causal talk) tells you which.
Common Mistakes: 1) Overusing पीछे (piichhe) where बाद (baad) is the natural choice for 'after [event]' -- native speakers reach for बाद (baad) by default for time and reserve पीछे mostly for 'because of / on account of' or physical trailing.

Sense 4 : because of, on account of (causal)
postposition Common in the pattern X के पीछे [reason/story] है (ke piichhe, 'behind X [there is/lies a reason]'), e.g. इस बात के पीछे एक कहानी है (is baat ke piichhe ek kahaanii hai, 'there's a story behind this').
Synonyms: के कारण (ke kaaran), की वजह से (kii vajah se)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the 'behind the scenes' logic transferred straight from English -- जब कोई पूछता है 'इसके पीछे क्या है?' (iske piichhe kyaa hai, 'what's behind this?'), they're asking for the hidden reason or motive, exactly as in English.

Sense 5 : to keep pursuing/nagging someone (in the idiom X के पीछे पड़ना/लगना)
adverb Lives inside compound-verb idioms: किसी के पीछे पड़ना (kisii ke piichhe padnaa, 'to go after/pester someone') and किसी के पीछे लगना (kisii ke piichhe lagnaa, 'to be on someone's case, to chase someone').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Hindi speaker knows the feeling of माँ पीछे पड़ जाना (maan kaa piichhe pad jaanaa, 'mom getting on your case') about homework or marriage -- it's a very domestic, very relatable idiom.
