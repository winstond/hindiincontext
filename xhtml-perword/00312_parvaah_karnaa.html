परवाह करना (parvaah karnaa)

verb A compound built from the noun परवाह (parvaah, 'care/concern') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'), meaning 'to care about' or 'to worry about' something or someone.
Sense 1 : to care about, to be concerned about
verb, transitive The thing or person cared about is marked with की (kii): किसी की परवाह करना (kisii kii parvaah karnaa, 'to care about someone'). Very frequently used in the negative -- परवाह न करना (parvaah n karnaa) or मुझे परवाह नहीं (mujhe parvaah nahiin) -- to express indifference or defiance.
Synonyms: ख्याल रखना (khyaal rakhnaa), फ़िक्र करना (fikr karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear the dative-subject version मुझे परवाह नहीं (mujhe parvaah nahiin, 'I don't care') constantly in Delhi speech -- it's the go-to way to shrug something off, often said with a flick of the hand. The करना (karnaa) version, जैसे उसकी परवाह करो (uskii parvaah karo), sounds more like active advice or a scolding -- 'go on, actually care about this person/thing' -- rather than a statement of feeling.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to mark the object with को (ko) as with many other transitive verbs, but परवाह (parvaah) itself takes की (kii): उसकी परवाह करो (uskii parvaah karo, 'care about him/her'), not उसे परवाह करो. 2) The very common construction मुझे उसकी परवाह नहीं है (mujhe uskii parvaah nahiin hai, 'I don't care about him/her') uses परवाह (parvaah) with होना (honaa), not करना (karnaa) -- both patterns exist and learners mix up which verb goes with which meaning nuance.
