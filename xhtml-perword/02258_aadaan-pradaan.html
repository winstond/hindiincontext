आदान-प्रदान (aadaan-pradaan)

noun A compound noun meaning 'mutual exchange' -- literally 'taking and giving.' It has one core sense used across contexts from trade to ideas to greetings.
Sense 1 : exchange, mutual giving and taking
noun, masculine Commonly used with करना (karnaa) as the verb: विचारों का आदान-प्रदान करना (vichaaron kaa aadaan-pradaan karnaa, 'to exchange ideas'). Takes the thing exchanged marked with का/की/के (kaa/kii/ke) before the noun.
Synonyms: लेन-देन (len-den, everyday, transactional), विनिमय (vinimay, formal, often economic)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in news and diplomatic contexts -- विचारों का आदान-प्रदान (vichaaron kaa aadaan-pradaan, 'exchange of ideas') or सांस्कृतिक आदान-प्रदान (saanskritik aadaan-pradaan, 'cultural exchange') -- it has a slightly formal, considered ring to it, like something you'd say in a speech or write in an article rather than toss off casually over chai.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for लेन-देन (len-den) in all cases, but आदान-प्रदान (aadaan-pradaan) leans toward abstract or formal exchanges (ideas, greetings, culture, information) while लेन-देन (len-den) suits everyday transactional exchanges (money, favors, goods). 2) It is always used as a set compound noun -- learners sometimes try to split or inflect आदान (aadaan) and प्रदान (pradaan) separately, but the hyphenated pair functions as a single invariant noun.

(donon deshon ke biich saanskritik aadaan-pradaan badh rahaa hai।)
