ताई (taaii)

noun Kinship term: the wife of one's father's elder brother (elder paternal aunt-in-law). Related to ताऊ (taaoo, father's elder brother).
Sense 1 : wife of father's elder brother
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most North Indian joint families, ताई (taaii) often holds a position of quiet authority in the household, sometimes taking on a role similar to a second mother, especially in matters of running the home. Kids growing up in Delhi-NCR households will often hear ताई (taaii) used constantly at family gatherings, since precise kinship terms (rather than a blanket 'aunty') are the norm in Hindi even in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use ताई (taaii) as a generic word for 'aunt,' but Hindi kinship terms are precise: ताई (taaii) is specifically the wife of one's ताऊ (taaoo, father's elder brother), distinct from चाची (chaachii, wife of father's younger brother) and मौसी (mausii, mother's sister) or बुआ (buaa, father's sister). 2) Younger siblings' wives are not called ताई (taaii) even if older than the speaker -- the term tracks the husband's birth order relative to the speaker's father, not age.
