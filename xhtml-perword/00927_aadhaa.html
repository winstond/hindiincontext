आधा (aadhaa)

adjective Means 'half' -- used as an adjective (half of something), and idiomatically to mean 'incomplete' or 'partial.'
Sense 1 : half (a quantity)
adjective Agrees in gender/number with the noun it modifies, e.g. आधा गिलास (aadhaa gilaas, 'half a glass') vs आधी रोटी (aadhii rotii, 'half a roti').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in daily transactions -- ordering आधा किलो (aadhaa kilo, 'half a kilo') of vegetables at the sabzi mandi, or asking for आधा कप चाय (aadhaa kap chaay, 'half a cup of tea') when you don't want a full serving. It's one of the first quantity words learners pick up because it's so practical.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often place आधा (aadhaa) after the noun as in English ('glass half'), but Hindi puts it before, like any other adjective: आधा गिलास (aadhaa gilaas) not गिलास आधा (gilaas aadhaa). 2) With time expressions, साढ़े (saadhe) is used for 'half past' (साढ़े तीन (saadhe tiin, 'three thirty')) rather than आधा (aadhaa) -- learners sometimes wrongly try to build clock-time phrases directly with आधा (aadhaa).
Sample Sentences:
मुझे आधा किलो आलू दे दो। Give me half a kilo of potatoes.
(mujhe aadhaa kilo aaloo de do।)

Sense 2 : incomplete, partial
adjective
Synonyms: अधूरा (adhooraa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear आधा काम (aadhaa kaam, 'half-done work') or आधा मन (aadhaa man, 'half-hearted,' literally 'half a mind') used to describe someone not fully committed to a task -- a nice window into how Hindi builds emotional idioms around body/mind-as-container imagery.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this figurative sense आधा (aadhaa) and अधूरा (adhooraa) overlap, but आधा (aadhaa) leans toward 'half-finished' (implying some measurable portion remains), while अधूरा (adhooraa) is the more general word for 'incomplete' -- learners sometimes use them as if fully interchangeable.
Sample Sentences:
उसने आधा काम छोड़ दिया। He left the work half-done.
(usne aadhaa kaam chhod diyaa।)

Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
