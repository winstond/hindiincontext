सूखना (sookhnaa)

verb One core sense: to dry, to become dry -- used for clothes, plants, skin, rivers, and by extension for the body wasting away or feelings drying up. Intransitive; its transitive causative partner is सुखाना (sukhaanaa, 'to dry something').
Sense 1 : to become dry (clothes, weather-exposed objects)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a Delhi household this is the verb for clothes on the छत (chhat, 'terrace/roof') line: कपड़े धूप में सूख रहे हैं (kapde dhoop men sookh rahe hain, 'the clothes are drying in the sun'). Its transitive partner सुखाना (sukhaanaa) is what you do to the clothes (hang them out); सूखना (sookhnaa) is what the clothes themselves do.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners reach for सुखाना (sukhaanaa) when they mean 'the clothes are drying' -- but if the clothes themselves are the subject and no one is actively drying them, use सूखना (sookhnaa): कपड़े सूख रहे हैं (kapde sookh rahe hain, 'the clothes are drying'), not कपड़े सुखा रहे हैं (kapde sukhaa rahe hain), which would mean someone is drying them.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : to dry up (water bodies, plants, moisture)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मुरझाना (murjhaanaa, for plants/flowers)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Used for rivers, wells, and ponds running dry -- तालाब सूख गया (taalaab sookh gayaa, 'the pond dried up') -- as well as for plants: पौधा सूख गया (paudhaa sookh gayaa, 'the plant dried up/withered'). This sense carries a note of loss, especially in stories about drought or neglected gardens.
Sample Sentences:
इस साल गर्मी में तालाब पूरी तरह सूख गया। This summer, the pond dried up completely.
(is saal garmii men taalaab poorii tarah sookh gayaa।)

Sense 3 : to become thin/wasted (body), or for feelings/relationships to wither
verb, intransitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A worried mother might say बेटा सूख गया है (betaa sookh gayaa hai, 'the boy has become thin/wasted') about a child who has lost weight from illness or stress -- the body 'drying up' the way a plant does. The same image extends to emotions: प्यार सूख गया (pyaar sookh gayaa, 'the love dried up') for affection that has faded.
Sample Sentences:
बीमारी की वजह से वह बहुत सूख गया है। He has become very thin because of his illness.
(biimaarii kii vajah se vah bahut sookh gayaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
बेटा सूख गया है, माँ को बहुत चिंता हो रही है। The boy has become thin and wasted, and his mother is very worried.
(betaa sookh gayaa hai, maan ko bahut chintaa ho rahii hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
समय के साथ उनका प्यार सूख गया। Over time, their love dried up.
