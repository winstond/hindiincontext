दृश्य (drishy)

noun/adjective Mainly a noun meaning 'scene, view, sight' (as in a landscape or a film/play scene), with a related adjective sense meaning 'visible.'
Sense 1 : scene, view
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नज़ारा (nazaaraa), मंज़र (manzar), तमाशा (tamaashaa, often for a spectacle or commotion)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear दृश्य (drishy) a lot in the context of films and plays -- हास्य दृश्य (haasy drishy, 'comic scene') or यह दृश्य याद रहेगा (yah drishy yaad rahegaa, 'this scene will be remembered') -- as well as in nature writing, where it describes a scenic view. It has a slightly more formal, literary flavor than everyday words like नज़ारा (nazaaraa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use दृश्य (drishy) for any 'view' interchangeably with नज़ारा (nazaaraa), but दृश्य (drishy) leans more descriptive/visual or literary (a scene one observes or describes), while नज़ारा (nazaaraa) carries more everyday appreciative warmth, as when admiring a sunset.

(pahaadon se sooraj doobne kaa drishy bahut sundar lagtaa hai।)

Sense 2 : visible
adjective Used mostly in formal, scientific, or descriptive registers -- e.g. दृश्य प्रकाश (drishy prakaash, 'visible light').
Synonyms: दिखने वाला (dikhne vaalaa), दृष्टिगोचर (drishtigochar, literary)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This adjective sense shares its root with the noun sense (both come from the idea of 'that which is seen'), and you'll meet it in compound technical terms like दृश्य-अदृश्य (drishy-adrishy, 'visible-invisible') more often than in casual speech.
