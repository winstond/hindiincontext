जाल (jaal)

noun Primarily a physical 'net/web' (fishing net, spider's web) that extends naturally into a figurative 'trap' or 'network' sense; also used for a deceptive scheme.
Sense 1 : net (physical object for catching things, or a web)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: जाला (jaalaa, especially for a spider's web)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a machhuaara (machhuaaraa, fisherman) on the Yamuna casting his jaal (jaal) into the water -- that everyday image is the anchor for this word. From there, Hindi naturally stretches the same word to any woven, tangled structure, including a spider's web.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse जाल (jaal) with जाला (jaalaa) -- जाला (jaalaa) is used specifically for a spider's cobweb, while जाल (jaal) is the more general word covering fishing nets, hair nets, and webs alike.

Sense 2 : trap, snare (figurative entanglement or deception)
noun, masculine Common in the phrase जाल में फँसना (jaal men phansnaa, 'to get caught in a trap') and जाल बिछाना (jaal bichhaanaa, 'to lay a trap').
Synonyms: फंदा (phandaa, more physical/literal snare), चक्कर (chakkar, colloquial for 'mess/tangle')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear in political commentary or crime dramas -- a corrupt neta (netaa, politician) 'laying a jaal (jaal)' for someone, or a person being warned that they have 'fallen into a jaal (jaal).' It carries the same emotional weight as 'trap' or 'web of lies' in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is almost always paired with verbs like फँसना (phansnaa, 'to get entangled') or बिछाना (bichhaanaa, 'to spread/lay') -- using जाल (jaal) alone without one of these collocations can sound incomplete to a native speaker.

Sense 3 : network (interconnected system, e.g. roads, communication, spies)
noun, masculine Used in compounds like सड़कों का जाल (sadkon kaa jaal, 'network of roads') or जासूसी जाल (jaasoosii jaal, 'spy network').
Synonyms: नेटवर्क (netvark, loanword, common in tech/infrastructure contexts), जाल-तंत्र (jaal-tantr, rare, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this in news headlines about infrastructure -- रेलवे का जाल (relve kaa jaal, 'railway network') is a very common collocation describing how Indian Railways spreads across the country like a woven net.
