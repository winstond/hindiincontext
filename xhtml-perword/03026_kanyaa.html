कन्या (kanyaa)

noun A formal/traditional word for a young unmarried girl or daughter, most alive today in the ritual and cultural context of कन्या पूजन (kanyaa poojan) during Navratri, and in the phrase कन्यादान (kanyaadaan) at weddings.
Sense 1 : young girl / maiden (unmarried female, especially pre-adolescent)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: लड़की (ladkii), बच्ची (bachchii), बेटी (betii, daughter sense)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word's main home in daily life is कन्या पूजन (kanyaa poojan), the Navratri ritual where young unmarried girls are invited home, fed, and worshipped as forms of the goddess -- if you hear कन्या (kanyaa) in a Delhi household in autumn, this is almost certainly the context. It also survives in कन्यादान (kanyaadaan), the wedding ritual where the father 'gives away' his daughter.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use कन्या (kanyaa) as a casual everyday word for 'girl,' but in ordinary conversation लड़की (ladkii) is what people actually say; कन्या (kanyaa) sounds formal, ritual, or literary in comparison. 2) It specifically implies an unmarried, typically young/prepubescent girl, not just any female child -- it is not interchangeable with बच्ची (bachchii) for a baby girl.

Sense 2 : Virgo (zodiac sign)
noun, feminine Used in the fixed astrological term कन्या राशि (kanyaa raashi, 'the sign Virgo').
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Anyone who reads a Hindi horoscope column or discusses राशि (raashi, zodiac sign) will meet कन्या (kanyaa) as the name for Virgo, literally 'the maiden,' mirroring the Western zodiac's own imagery.
Sample Sentences:
