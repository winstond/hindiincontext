झूमना (jhoomnaa)

verb One core meaning: to sway, to swing, or to sway with delight/intoxication -- physically or emotionally. Covers everything from trees swaying in the wind to people swaying to music or dancing with joy.
Sense 1 : to sway with joy, ecstasy, or intoxication (people, crowds)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मस्त होना (mast honaa), लहराना (lahraanaa, usually for objects, not people), नाचना (naachnaa, to dance, broader)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Picture a crowd at a dandiya (daandiyaa) night or a qawwali (kavvaalii) session, eyes closed, swaying side to side as the music builds -- that full-body sway of collective joy is exactly what झूमना (jhoomnaa) captures. It is also the verb used for someone swaying on their feet after one too many drinks, so context tells you whether the sway is from bliss or from being tipsy.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नाचना (naachnaa) whenever they mean 'to dance,' but झूमना (jhoomnaa) is specifically the swaying, rhythmic body-motion of being caught up in music or emotion -- you can झूमना without any real dance steps. 2) झूम उठना (jhoom uthnaa) is a very common compound-verb form meaning 'to suddenly break into swaying/rapture' -- learners tend to only learn the simple form and miss this everyday intensified version.

Sense 2 : to sway or billow (trees, branches, clouds, fields of crops)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: लहराना (lahraanaa), हिलना (hilnaa, to move/shake, broader)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb of Hindi film songs about the monsoon: बादल झूम रहे हैं (baadal jhoom rahe hain, 'the clouds are swaying/billowing') paints rain clouds as almost dancing across the sky, echoing how the human-joy sense of this verb bleeds into nature imagery.
Common Mistakes: 1) With natural scenery -- pेड़ (ped, trees), बादल (baadal, clouds), खेत (khet, fields) -- झूमना (jhoomnaa) suggests a slow, graceful, almost joyful swaying rather than violent shaking; for that, हिलना (hilnaa) or काँपना (kaampnaa) fit better.
