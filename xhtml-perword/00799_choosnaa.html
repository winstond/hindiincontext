चूसना (choosnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to suck' -- used literally for drawing liquid or juice out of something with the mouth, and figuratively for draining someone dry (money, resources, energy).
Sense 1 : to suck (liquid, juice, etc. out of something with the mouth)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a kid gnawing and sucking on a mango seed (गुठली, guthlii) after the fruit is gone, or someone chewing on sugarcane (गन्ना, gannaa) at a roadside stall -- चूसना (choosnaa) is the verb for that whole messy, satisfying activity.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse चूसना (choosnaa) with पीना (piinaa, 'to drink'); चूसना (choosnaa) specifically implies extracting liquid through suction with lips/mouth, like sucking a mango seed or sugarcane, not simply drinking from a cup or glass.

Sense 2 : to drain/exploit someone (figurative: money, resources, energy)
verb, transitive Common in the fixed phrase खून चूसना (khoon choosnaa, 'to suck someone's blood') describing exploitative people or institutions.
Synonyms: निचोड़ना (nichodnaa, to squeeze/wring out), शोषण करना (shoshan karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear खून चूसना (khoon choosnaa, 'to suck blood') used for anyone from a greedy landlord to a corrupt official -- the imagery of a leech or mosquito draining someone dry is right there in the verb, no separate word for 'exploit' needed.
