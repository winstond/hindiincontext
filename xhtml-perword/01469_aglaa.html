अगला (aglaa)

adjective A variable adjective meaning 'next' or 'following', used for time, sequence, and position; it also appears as part of a common set expression.
Sense 1 : next (in time or sequence)
adjective
Synonyms: आने वाला (aane vaalaa, upcoming)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear अगला स्टेशन (aglaa steshan, 'next station') on the Delhi Metro announcements constantly -- it is one of the first practical phrases most learners absorb just from riding around the city.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आने वाला (aane vaalaa) for every 'next', but अगला (aglaa) is the everyday default with days, weeks, months, years, and stops -- अगला साल (aglaa saal, 'next year'), अगला स्टेशन (aglaa steshan, 'next station'). 2) Remember it agrees like a normal -आ adjective: अगली बार (aglii baar, 'next time') takes the feminine form because बार (baar) is feminine.
Sample Sentences:
अगला स्टेशन कश्मीरी गेट है। The next station is Kashmiri Gate.
(aglaa steshan kashmiirii get hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
हम अगले हफ्ते जयपुर जा रहे हैं। We are going to Jaipur next week.
(ham agle haphte jaypur jaa rahe hain।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगली बार मुझे पहले से बता देना। Next time, tell me in advance.
(aglii baar mujhe pahle se bataa denaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
पिछले साल हम गोवा गए थे, अगले साल शायद केरल जाएँगे। Last year we went to Goa, next year maybe we'll go to Kerala.
(pichhle saal ham govaa gae the, agle saal shaayad keral jaaenge।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
अगली सुबह उसे सच पता चल गया। The next morning, the truth became known to him.

अगली पीढ़ी को यह जिम्मेदारी सौंपी जाएगी। This responsibility will be handed over to the next generation.

Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
Sense 2 : next, following (in a physical row or order)
adjective
Synonyms: आगे वाला (aage vaalaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used the same way you'd say 'the next one' pointing down a line -- at a bank counter or ticket window you might hear अगला कौन है? (aglaa kaun hai?, 'who's next?').
Sample Sentences:
अगला कौन है? Who's next?
(aglaa kaun hai?)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
अगली सीट पर कोई नहीं बैठा है। No one is sitting in the next seat.
(aglii siit par koii nahiin baithaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
लाइन में अगला व्यक्ति कौन था? Who was the next person in the line?
(laain men aglaa vyakti kaun thaa?)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
कृपया अगली खिड़की पर जाएँ। Please go to the next window.
(kripyaa aglii khidkii par jaaen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
अगली दुकान से थोड़ा दूध ले आओ। Bring some milk from the next shop.
(aglii dukaan se thodaa doodh le aao।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
अगली मेज़ पर बर्तन नहीं रखे गए थे। The dishes had not been placed on the next table.

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Passive
अगर मैं थोड़ा आगे खड़ा होता, तो अगली पंक्ति में होता। If I had stood a bit further ahead, I would have been in the next row.

अगली सीढ़ी पर पैर रखते समय सावधान रहना। Be careful while putting your foot on the next step.

(shaayad aglii kursii khaalii ho।)
