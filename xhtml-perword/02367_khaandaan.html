ख़ानदान (khaandaan)

noun A noun meaning 'family' or 'lineage,' with a stronger sense of extended family, ancestry, and family reputation than the more neutral परिवार (parivaar).
Sense 1 : family / lineage / clan
noun, masculine
Synonyms: परिवार (parivaar), कुनबा (kunbaa, colloquial, extended family/clan), वंश (vansh, lineage/dynasty)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries real weight in everyday Hindi conversation -- people talk about ख़ानदानी इज़्ज़त (khaandaanii izzat, 'family honor') or say कोई ख़ानदान से है (koii khaandaan se hai, 'someone comes from a good family'), tying the word closely to social respectability and reputation, not just blood relations. It comes from Persian, reflecting the deep Persian influence on everyday Hindi-Urdu vocabulary around family and status.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use ख़ानदान (khaandaan) interchangeably with परिवार (parivaar), but ख़ानदान (khaandaan) leans toward the extended family line and its social standing, while परिवार (parivaar) more often refers to the immediate household. 2) The nuqta under the ख़ (kh) softens it to a 'kh' sound closer to Persian/Urdu pronunciation; dropping it (pronouncing it as ख) is common in casual speech and still understood, but the spelling ख़ानदान (khaandaan) is the standard form.
