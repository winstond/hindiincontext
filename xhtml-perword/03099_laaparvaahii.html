लापरवाही (laaparvaahii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'carelessness' or 'negligence' -- built from लापरवा (laaparvaa, 'careless') plus the abstract-noun suffix ी.
Sense 1 : carelessness, negligence
noun, feminine
Synonyms: असावधानी (asaavdhaanii), बेपरवाही (beparvaahii, variant form), अनदेखी (andekhii, meaning scope: overlooking/ignoring something rather than general carelessness)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this word a lot in Delhi traffic complaints and news reports -- लापरवाही से गाड़ी चलाना (laaparvaahii se gaadii chalaanaa, 'to drive negligently') is a stock phrase in accident reports. It also shows up in parental scoldings: थोड़ी सी लापरवाही (thodii sii laaparvaahii, 'a little bit of carelessness') is the classic prelude to a lecture about spilled milk or lost keys.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say लापरवाह (laaparvaah) (the adjective 'careless') when they mean the noun -- लापरवाही (laaparvaahii) is the thing itself ('carelessness'), while लापरवाह (laaparvaah) describes a careless person. 2) It usually pairs with करना (karnaa) as in लापरवाही करना (laaparvaahii karnaa, 'to be careless/act negligently') or बरतना (baratnaa) in more formal contexts, not with होना (honaa) alone when describing an action.
