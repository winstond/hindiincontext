लीद (liid)

noun A single-sense noun meaning the dung of large animals like horses, camels, or donkeys.
Sense 1 : dung (of horses, camels, donkeys)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गोबर (gobar, used for cow/buffalo dung instead)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear लीद (liid) most in rural or market contexts -- near tongas, camel carts, or stables -- rather than in city conversation, since most Delhi-NCR households have no daily contact with horses or camels. It is also used mildly figuratively in some rough insults implying worthlessness, similar to how dung-related words work in English slang, though this use is coarse and best avoided in polite speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for गोबर (gobar) as a general word for 'dung,' but गोबर (gobar) is specifically cow or buffalo dung, while लीद (liid) is reserved for horses, camels, and donkeys -- swapping them sounds off to a native ear.
