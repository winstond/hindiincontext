बृहस्पतिवार (brihasptivaar)

noun The formal/full name for 'Thursday,' literally 'the day of Brihaspati' (guru of the gods in Hindu mythology, associated with the planet Jupiter). In everyday speech, गुरुवार (guruvaar) is far more common.
Sense 1 : Thursday
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गुरुवार (guruvaar), वीरवार (viirvaar, regional/colloquial)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This name ties the day directly to Brihaspati (brihaspti), the guru of the gods and the deity linked to the planet Jupiter, which is why Thursday is also considered an auspicious day for worshipping Vishnu or visiting a guru in some traditions. You'll encounter बृहस्पतिवार (brihasptivaar) mainly in written Hindi, formal calendars, and almanacs (पंचांग panchaang), while spoken Hindi almost always defaults to the shorter गुरुवार (guruvaar).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often assume this is the everyday word for Thursday because it's the one they see in textbooks or calendars; in actual conversation, गुरुवार (guruvaar) is what people say and hear far more often. 2) The length and unfamiliar consonant cluster (बृ) make this word hard to pronounce smoothly at first; native speakers rarely reach for it in casual talk precisely because it feels heavier on the tongue than गुरुवार (guruvaar).
