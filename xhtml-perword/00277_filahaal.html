फ़िलहाल (filahaal)

adverb A single-sense adverb meaning 'for now, at present, currently' -- used to mark the current moment as temporary or provisional. Borrowed from Persian/Arabic via Urdu.
Sense 1 : for the time being, currently
adverb
Synonyms: अभी (abhii), इस समय (is samay), फ़िलवक़्त (filvaqt, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in Delhi offices and shops when someone hedges a commitment: फ़िलहाल तो यही प्लान है (filahaal to yahii plaan hai, 'for now, this is the plan'), with the implication that things could change. It carries a slightly formal, considered tone compared to the more casual अभी (abhii), so it shows up a lot in news reports and official statements too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often treat फ़िलहाल (filahaal) as identical to अभी (abhii), but फ़िलहाल (filahaal) specifically flags the current state as temporary or subject to change later, while अभी (abhii) is neutral about that. 2) It is commonly placed at the start of a sentence or clause, unlike अभी (abhii), which moves around more freely.
