जुलाई (julaaii)

noun The month of July -- a straightforward calendar-month noun with a single meaning.
Sense 1 : the month of July
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: जुलाई (julaaii) is a borrowed word from English, used exactly like all Hindi month names alongside the older Hindu calendar months (like सावन saavan or भादों bhaadon) which are still used for festivals and seasonal references. In Delhi-NCR, जुलाई (julaaii) is squarely monsoon season -- expect people to talk about बारिश (baarish, 'rain') and उमस (umas, 'humidity') whenever this month comes up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Month names like जुलाई (julaaii) are feminine, so pair them with feminine agreement when needed, e.g. जुलाई आ गई (julaaii aa gaii, 'July has arrived') -- learners used to masculine default agreement sometimes say आ गया (aa gayaa) by mistake. 2) To say "in July," use जुलाई में (julaaii men), not जुलाई पर (julaaii par) or जुलाई को (julaaii ko) alone -- को (ko) is reserved for a specific date, as in दो जुलाई को (do julaaii ko, 'on July 2nd').
