पद (pad)

noun पद (pad) covers a cluster of related senses: a position or post (job/rank), a foot/step (mostly in compounds and formal registers), and a verse/stanza (especially in devotional poetry).
Sense 1 : post, position, rank (job/office)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ओहदा (ohdaa), पदवी (padvii, somewhat formal), मुक़ाम (muqaam, rare in this exact sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you will meet constantly in job ads, government notices, and office contexts: पद के लिए आवेदन (pad ke lie aavedan, 'application for the post') is a very common phrase. It is the everyday word for 'post' or 'position' in bureaucratic and professional Hindi, right alongside its more colloquial cousin नौकरी (naukrii, 'job').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पद (pad) with पत्र (patr, 'letter/document') because they look similar in writing -- they are unrelated. 2) पद (pad) refers to the position/office itself, not the person holding it; for the person, use words like अधिकारी (adhikaarii, 'officer') or the specific title.
Sample Sentences:
इस पद के लिए बहुत आवेदन आए हैं। Many applications have come in for this post.
(is pad ke lie bahut aavedan aae hain।)

Sense 2 : verse, stanza (especially devotional poetry/song)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: श्लोक (shlok, usually Sanskrit religious verse), दोहा (dohaa, a specific two-line verse form), छंद (chhand)
Frequency: devotional
Cultural Note: This is the word you meet in the context of bhakti poetry -- collections of Surdas's or Meera's devotional songs are described as पद (pad) or grouped into 'पदावली' (padaavlii, 'a string of verses'). It carries a devotional, musical flavor distinct from the more secular कविता (kavitaa, 'poem').
Sample Sentences:

मीरा के पद आज भी भक्ति भाव से गाए जाते हैं। Meera's verses are still sung with devotional feeling today.

यह पद राधा के विरह का वर्णन करता है। This verse describes Radha's pangs of separation.

Sense 3 : foot, step (formal/literary; mostly in compounds)
noun, masculine This sense survives mainly inside compounds and set phrases (like पदचिह्न (padchihn, 'footprint')) rather than as a standalone word in casual speech.
Synonyms: पैर (pair), कदम (kadam)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense tucked inside compound words rather than standing on its own -- पदचिह्न (padchihn, 'footprint') and अनुपद (anupad) are the kind of places it survives. On its own, it has largely been replaced by पैर (pair) in daily talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) In everyday conversation, learners should reach for पैर (pair) or कदम (kadam) for 'foot/step' -- पद (pad) alone sounds old-fashioned or bookish in this sense.
Sample Sentences:
साधु के पदचिह्न रेत पर स्पष्ट दिखाई दे रहे थे। The sage's footprints were clearly visible on the sand.
(saadhu ke padchihn ret par spasht dikhaaii de rahe the।)
Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Register: Literary -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
