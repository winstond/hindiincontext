फावड़ा (phaavdaa)

noun A concrete noun for a common digging tool; only one everyday sense.
Sense 1 : spade, shovel
noun, masculine
Synonyms: बेलचा (belchaa, smaller/flatter shovel used for scooping)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a village-and-construction-site word: you will hear it on farms, at building sites, and in monsoon-season conversations about digging drains. It also shows up in the common expression फावड़ा चलाना (phaavdaa chalaanaa, 'to wield a spade'), used for someone doing manual digging labor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use फावड़ा (phaavdaa) and बेलचा (belchaa) interchangeably, but फावड़ा (phaavdaa) is the heavier tool for digging into earth, while बेलचा (belchaa) is more for scooping loose material like sand or coal.
