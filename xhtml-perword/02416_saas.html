सास (saas)

noun A single-sense kinship noun: 'mother-in-law' (husband's or wife's mother). Related family terms like ससुर (sasur, father-in-law) pair naturally with it.
Sense 1 : mother-in-law
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: सास (saas) is a loaded word in Indian family life -- the सास-बहू (saas-bahoo, mother-in-law and daughter-in-law) relationship is a whole genre unto itself, endlessly dramatized in Indian TV soaps where domestic tension between the two is the central plot engine. In many joint families the सास is still the household's senior authority, especially over kitchen and childrearing matters, though this is shifting with nuclear family living in urban India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes mix up सास (saas, mother-in-law) with ससुर (sasur, father-in-law) since they're often mentioned together -- सास is specifically the mother-in-law, always feminine in form and agreement. 2) Don't confuse with the near-homophone सांस (saans, breath) -- the nasalization and meaning are completely different, though the spelling difference (chandrabindu vs. no nasal mark in some regional spellings) can trip up readers.
