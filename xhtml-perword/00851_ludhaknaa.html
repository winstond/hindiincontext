लुढ़कना (ludhaknaa)

verb An intransitive verb meaning 'to roll' or 'to topple over' -- used for objects rolling on a surface as well as for a person tumbling or collapsing. It also has a common figurative sense of flopping down/sprawling out from tiredness.
Sense 1 : to roll (of an object)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: गड़गड़ाना (gadagdaanaa, rare, used more for rolling sound), घूमना (ghoomnaa, meaning scope: general turning/rotating, not specifically rolling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a ball, a coin, or a fruit rolling off a table -- सेब मेज़ से लुढ़क गया (seb mez se ludhak gayaa, 'the apple rolled off the table') is the classic mental image for this word. Its causative partner लुढ़काना (ludhkaanaa) is what you'd use if someone actively rolls the object.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with घुमाना (ghumaanaa) or लुढ़काना (ludhkaanaa) -- the causative 'to roll (something)' -- लुढ़कना (ludhaknaa) itself is intransitive and describes the object doing the rolling on its own, e.g. गेंद लुढ़क गई (gend ludhak gaii, 'the ball rolled away').
Sample Sentences:
सेब मेज़ से लुढ़क गया। The apple rolled off the table.
(seb mez se ludhak gayaa।)

Sense 2 : to topple over, collapse (of a person)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: गिरना (girnaa, more general 'to fall'), ढेर हो जाना (dher ho jaanaa, colloquial, 'to collapse in a heap')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word for collapsing onto a bed or sofa after a long day -- थक कर बिस्तर पर लुढ़क गया (thak kar bistar par ludhak gayaa, 'exhausted, he flopped onto the bed') is a very natural, everyday image, almost affectionate in how it describes tiredness taking over the body.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense often appears with पर (par) or में (men) marking where the person ends up, e.g. बिस्तर पर लुढ़क गया (bistar par ludhak gayaa, 'he flopped onto the bed') -- learners sometimes reach for गिरना (girnaa) here, but लुढ़कना (ludhaknaa) carries a softer, more informal 'flop/sprawl' feel rather than a hard fall.
