बहना (bahnaa)

verb The core meaning is 'to flow' -- water, rivers, wind, tears, blood, sweat all बहना (bahnaa). It also extends figuratively to emotions or crowds flowing/streaming.
Sense 1 : to flow (liquid, wind)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: प्रवाहित होना (pravaahit honaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Think of a Delhi summer evening when the loo (loo) is बह रही है (bah rahii hai, 'is blowing') -- this verb covers not just rivers and taps but any moving current, including wind. It pairs naturally with नदी (nadii, river), पसीना (pasiinaa, sweat), and आंसू (aansoo, tears).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for चलना (chalnaa) when describing water or wind movement, but बहना (bahnaa) is the natural verb for something flowing along a course -- rivers, taps, wind, blood -- while चलना (chalnaa) is for things that move/walk/operate. 2) This verb is intransitive, so it never takes ने (ne) in the past tense: पानी बहा (paanii bahaa, 'water flowed'), not पानी ने बहा.

Sense 2 : to stream/flow (tears, blood, emotion)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: आंसू बहना (aansoo bahnaa, 'tears to flow') is the standard way to describe crying in narration or song lyrics -- Hindi frames tears and emotion as things that flow on their own, much like water, rather than something a person actively 'cries out.' This is the same body-as-passive-site logic you see in verbs like दर्द होना (dard honaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) With emotional or bodily fluids like आंसू (aansoo, tears) or खून (khoon, blood), learners may want a more dramatic verb, but बहना (bahnaa) itself is already the natural, common choice -- no need to hunt for something fancier.

Sense 3 : to stream/pour (crowd, traffic, figurative movement)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will hear this at a mela (melaa, fair) or during a big cricket match: लोगों की भीड़ बह रही थी (logon kii bhiid bah rahii thii, 'the crowd of people was streaming/pouring along') -- the image of a river is extended to describe a mass of people moving as one continuous current.
Sample Sentences:
