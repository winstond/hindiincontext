पेचीदा (pechiidaa)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'complicated, complex, tangled' -- used for situations, problems, and language rather than physical objects.
Sense 1 : complicated, intricate, convoluted
adjective
Synonyms: उलझा हुआ (uljhaa huaa), जटिल (jatil, formal), मुश्किल (mushkil, meaning scope: also just 'difficult')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a favorite word in offices and courtrooms across Delhi -- a lawyer will call a case पेचीदा (pechiidaa), a bureaucrat will call the paperwork पेचीदा (pechiidaa), and a friend will call your love life पेचीदा (pechiidaa) if you let them. It carries a slightly Persianized, formal-ish flavor compared to the everyday मुश्किल (mushkil), so it shows up more in serious or exasperated speech about tangled situations than in casual small talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use पेचीदा (pechiidaa) for physically tangled objects like wires or hair -- it is normally reserved for abstract things: cases, situations, questions, relationships, paperwork. For a literally tangled string or hair, उलझा हुआ (uljhaa huaa) is the natural choice. 2) As an invariant adjective it never changes form before masculine or feminine nouns, unlike native -आ adjectives such as अच्छा (achchhaa) -- learners sometimes wrongly write पेचीदी (pechiidii) before a feminine noun.
