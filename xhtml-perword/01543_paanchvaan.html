पाँचवाँ (paanchvaan)

adjective The ordinal number 'fifth' -- an adjective built from पाँच (paanch, 'five').
Sense 1 : fifth (ordinal number)
adjective Agrees in gender/number/case with the noun it modifies, like an ordinary -आ (aa) adjective: पाँचवाँ लड़का (paanchvaan ladkaa, 'the fifth boy'), पाँचवीं लड़की (paanchviin ladkii, 'the fifth girl').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet this constantly with class levels in school -- पाँचवीं कक्षा (paanchviin kakshaa, 'fifth grade') -- and with dates and floors, e.g. पाँचवीं मंज़िल (paanchviin manzil, 'fifth floor'). Ordinals like this one are a core building block once you already know the cardinal numbers.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget the nasalization and write/say पाचवां (paachvaan) instead of पाँचवाँ (paanchvaan); the chandrabindu/anusvara on both syllables is essential. 2) The feminine/oblique form is पाँचवीं (paanchviin), not the expected पाँचवी (paanchvii) pattern seen in some other ordinals -- it keeps a long ी throughout.
