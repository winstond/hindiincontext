दिखाना (dikhaanaa)

verb The transitive causative of दिखना (dikhnaa, 'to be visible'), meaning 'to show'; it also runs the whole spectrum from literal showing to figurative displaying/pretending, and appears in a few common idiomatic frames.
Sense 1 : to show, display (something to someone)
verb, transitive Takes an indirect object marked with को (ko) for the person shown to: उसे किताब दिखाना (use kitaab dikhaanaa, 'to show him/her the book').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the causative partner of दिखना (dikhnaa) -- think of दिखना (dikhnaa) as 'to be seen' and दिखाना (dikhaanaa) as 'to make [someone] see.' You will hear it constantly in markets and clinics alike: ज़रा दिखाओ (zaraa dikhaao, 'just show me') when someone wants a closer look at goods, or डॉक्टर को दिखाना (doktar ko dikhaanaa, 'to show [oneself] to the doctor,' i.e. 'to see a doctor').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes mark the person being shown with से (se) instead of को (ko) -- it is मुझे दिखाओ (mujhe dikhaao, 'show me'), not मुझसे दिखाओ (mujhse dikhaao). 2) दिखाना (dikhaanaa) is often confused with दिखना (dikhnaa) 'to be visible' -- दिखाना (dikhaanaa) needs an agent doing the showing, while दिखना (dikhnaa) just describes something appearing to view on its own.

Sense 2 : to demonstrate, prove, or make evident (an ability, quality, or fact)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: साबित करना (saabit karnaa, 'to prove')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Used for showing off inner qualities, not just objects: हिम्मत दिखाना (himmat dikhaanaa, 'to show courage') or दिलचस्पी दिखाना (dilchaspii dikhaanaa, 'to show interest') are everyday collocations you will hear in encouragement, scolding, and news reports alike.

उसने मुश्किल हालात में बहुत हिम्मत दिखाई। He showed a lot of courage in a difficult situation.

Sense 3 : to pretend, put on a show of (a feeling or state one may not genuinely have)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: ज़ाहिर करना (zaahir karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi loves this overlap between 'showing' a feeling and 'faking' it -- रोना दिखाना (ronaa dikhaanaa) or प्यार दिखाना (pyaar dikhaanaa) can slide from sincere expression into performance depending on who is speaking and how, much like English 'putting on a show of tears.'
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense is easy to miss because the sentence looks identical to sense 2 (demonstrating a real quality) -- गुस्सा दिखाना (gussaa dikhaanaa) can mean 'to genuinely show anger' or 'to put on a show of anger'; context and tone decide which.
Sample Sentences:
वह बीमार होने का दिखावा करके ऑफिस से छुट्टी ले लेता है। He fakes being sick to take leave from the office.
(vah biimaar hone kaa dikhaavaa karke ophis se chhuttii le letaa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
