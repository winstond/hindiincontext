आवश्यकता (aavashyaktaa)

noun A formal-register noun meaning 'need' or 'requirement' -- the more official/written counterpart to the everyday ज़रूरत (zaroorat).
Sense 1 : need, requirement, necessity
noun, feminine Commonly used with होना (honaa) in dative-subject constructions, e.g. 'X को Y की आवश्यकता है' (X ko Y kii aavashyaktaa hai, 'X needs Y'), paralleling the ज़रूरत (zaroorat) construction the learner already knows.
Synonyms: ज़रूरत (zaroorat), जरूरत (jaroorat, variant spelling without nuqta)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word plastered across classified ads: 'आवश्यकता है' (aavashyaktaa hai, 'wanted/required') is the standard header for job postings and 'help wanted' notices in Hindi newspapers, much like 'Wanted' in English classifieds. In spoken Delhi-NCR Hindi, though, people overwhelmingly reach for ज़रूरत (zaroorat) instead -- आवश्यकता (aavashyaktaa) signals a slightly Sanskritized, official register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to ज़रूरत (zaroorat) everywhere; आवश्यकता (aavashyaktaa) is the better choice in written notices, job ads, official announcements, and formal speech -- using it in casual chat about, say, needing milk sounds stiff. 2) The thing needed is marked with की (kii) (genitive agreeing with आवश्यकता (aavashyaktaa), which is feminine), not से (se) or को (ko): 'पैसों की आवश्यकता' (paison kii aavashyaktaa, 'need for money'), not 'पैसों से आवश्यकता' (paison se aavashyaktaa).
Sample Sentences:
इस पद के लिए एक अनुभवी लेखाकार की आवश्यकता है। An experienced accountant is required for this position.
(is pad ke lie ek anubhvii lekhaakaar kii aavashyaktaa hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
