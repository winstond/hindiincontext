तय होना (tay honaa)

verb An intransitive pairing with तय करना (tay karnaa, 'to decide/settle something') -- तय होना (tay honaa) describes something becoming decided or fixed, often without a clear agent, close to a passive sense of 'to be decided/settled/fixed'.
Sense 1 : to be decided, to be settled/fixed (a plan, date, price, matter)
verb, intransitive Takes a से or के बीच phrase for 'between parties' when relevant, e.g. दोनों के बीच तय हुआ (donon ke biich tay huaa, 'it was settled between the two')). The 'decider' if mentioned takes से (se), not ने, since the verb itself is intransitive.
Synonyms: निश्चित होना (nishchit honaa), निर्धारित होना (nirdhaarit honaa, formal), पक्का होना (pakkaa honaa, colloquial)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb you hear constantly around planning logistics in Delhi -- from wedding dates to auto-rickshaw fares: भाड़ा तय हो गया? (bhaadaa tay ho gayaa?, 'has the fare been settled?'). Hindi prefers this agentless 'it became settled' framing over a heavier passive, much like लगना (lagnaa) constructions -- the outcome is foregrounded, not who decided it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for a passive construction with जाना (jaanaa) (like तय किया जाता है) when तय होना (tay honaa) already does this job more naturally and idiomatically. 2) Since तय होना (tay honaa) is intransitive, the person who made the decision is marked with से (se), not ने (ne) -- e.g. मुझसे तय हुआ (mujhse tay huaa, 'it was decided by me'), not मैंने तय हुआ.
