तवा (tavaa)

noun A concrete kitchen noun: the flat or slightly concave griddle used for cooking roti (rotii) and paratha (paraanthaa). One sense, with a common idiom built on it.
Sense 1 : griddle, flat pan
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian kitchen has a तवा (tavaa) sitting right on the stove, usually a well-seasoned black one that never gets fully washed with soap -- just wiped, because that seasoning is what keeps rotis from sticking. You will also hear the phrase तवे पर हाथ सेंकना (tave par haath senknaa, 'to warm one's hands on the griddle'), used figuratively for someone who profits opportunistically from a situation, similar to 'making hay while the sun shines' but with a slightly selfish/exploitative edge.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes call any pan तवा (tavaa), but a deep pan with sides for frying is कड़ाही (kadaahii), not तवा -- तवा is specifically the flat/near-flat disc used for roti and paratha.
