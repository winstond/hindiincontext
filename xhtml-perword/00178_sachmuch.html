सचमुच (sachmuch)

adverb/interjection An adverb meaning 'really, truly, actually' -- used both to assert the truth of a statement and to express genuine surprise. One core sense, but two common flavors of use worth noting in the sentences to come.
Sense 1 : really, truly (asserting genuineness)
adverb
Synonyms: वास्तव में (vaastav men, formal), सच में (sach men), असल में (asal men)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you reach for when someone tells you something surprising and you want to say 'seriously?' or when you want to underline that you really mean something -- सचमुच (sachmuch) मुझे बहुत अच्छा लगा (mujhe bahut achchhaa lagaa, 'I really liked it a lot'). It's built from सच (sach, 'truth') plus मुच (much), an old intensifying suffix, so at its root it just means 'truth-indeed.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for वास्तव में (vaastav men) in every context out of textbook habit -- in casual speech सचमुच (sachmuch) or सच में (sach men) sound far more natural. 2) सचमुच (sachmuch) usually sits right before the word or phrase it emphasizes, not necessarily at the start of the sentence, so its position shifts with what's being stressed.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे सचमुच बहुत अच्छा लगा। I really liked it a lot.
(mujhe sachmuch bahut achchhaa lagaa।)

Sense 2 : really?! (expressing surprise as a standalone exclamation)
interjection Used alone, often with rising intonation, as a one-word reaction rather than modifying a following clause.
Synonyms: सच में (sach men), क्या सच में (kyaa sach men)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Drop this on its own as a quick reaction -- सचमुच? (sachmuch) -- when a friend tells you some juicy bit of news, much like an English 'really?!' or 'no way!' It carries genuine surprise, not sarcasm.
