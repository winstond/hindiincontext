महिला (mahilaa)

noun A respectful, formal-register word for 'woman' -- the word you reach for in official, polite, or public contexts (signage, news, forms), as distinct from the everyday word औरत (aurat).
Sense 1 : woman (respectful/formal register)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: औरत (aurat, everyday), स्त्री (strii, formal/literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see महिला (mahilaa) constantly in compounds and institutional names -- महिला थाना (mahilaa thaanaa, women's police station), महिला डिब्बा (mahilaa dibbaa, the women's compartment on Delhi Metro trains), महिला सशक्तिकरण (mahilaa sashaktikran, women's empowerment). It carries a built-in tone of respect, which is exactly why it dominates official and journalistic Hindi over औरत (aurat).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to औरत (aurat) everywhere; but on forms, signs, news reports, or when speaking politely about someone (e.g. introducing an older or respected woman), महिला (mahilaa) is the expected word -- औरत (aurat) can sound slightly blunt or informal in those same contexts. 2) महिला (mahilaa) is grammatically feminine but does not itself carry any special inflection beyond the regular feminine noun pattern -- don't overthink it as an exception.
