अवधी (avdhii)

noun/adjective Refers to the Awadhi language/dialect and, as an adjective, to things belonging to the Awadh region (of which Lucknow is the historic center) -- most familiar to learners through Awadhi cuisine.
Sense 1 : the Awadhi language
noun, feminine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Awadhi is historically centered on Lucknow and the surrounding Awadh region in eastern Uttar Pradesh; it is one of the major languages behind what is loosely called 'Hindi' in casual speech, alongside Bhojpuri (bhojpurii) and Braj (braj).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume अवधी (avdhii) is just a style of Hindi rather than its own language with a rich literary tradition (Tulsidas wrote the Ramcharitmanas (raamachritmaanas) in Awadhi, not standard Hindi).

(avdhii avadh kshetr kii ek pramukh bhaashaa hai।)

Sense 2 : of or relating to Awadh (region, culture, cuisine)
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Most learners meet this word on a restaurant menu: अवधी बिरयानी (avdhii biryaanii, 'Awadhi biryani') or अवधी खाना (avdhii khaanaa, 'Awadhi food') refers to the refined, Nawabi-era cooking style of Lucknow, distinct from Mughlai or Hyderabadi styles.
