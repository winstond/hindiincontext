आय (aay)

noun A formal noun meaning 'income' -- the money that comes in, used mainly for personal, household, or national earnings rather than everyday casual speech.
Sense 1 : income, earnings
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कमाई (kamaaii, everyday word), आमदनी (aamadnii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see आय (aay) constantly on government forms, bank documents, and news reports about the economy (राष्ट्रीय आय (raashtriiy aay, 'national income') is a staple phrase in economics classes and budget-day headlines), while in the kitchen or living room families are far more likely to talk about कमाई (kamaaii) -- what a son or daughter is bringing home. Think of आय (aay) as the word for a ledger, and कमाई (kamaaii) as the word for a conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for कमाई (kamaaii) in casual talk about a person's earnings and save आय (aay) for formal or written contexts like tax forms, government reports, or news -- using आय (aay) in a casual chat about one's salary can sound stiff. 2) आय (aay) is commonly paired with कर (kar) to form आयकर (aaykar, 'income tax'), a compound learners should recognize as a set unit rather than two separate words.

इस फॉर्म में अपनी वार्षिक आय भरें। Fill in your annual income in this form.
(is phorm men apnii vaarshik aay bharen।)
