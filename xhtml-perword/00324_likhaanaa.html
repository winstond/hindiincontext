लिखाना (likhaanaa)

verb The causative of लिखना (likhnaa, 'to write'): 'to have (something) written' or 'to get someone to write, enroll, register.' Also covers the common extended sense of getting oneself registered/enrolled somewhere.
Sense 1 : to enroll, register, get (oneself/someone) registered
verb, transitive Common in fixed collocations like नाम लिखाना (naam likhaanaa, 'to enroll/register one's name') and शिकायत लिखाना (shikaayat likhaanaa, 'to file/lodge a complaint').
Synonyms: दर्ज कराना (darj karaanaa), भर्ती कराना (bhartii karaanaa, for hospital/institutional admission)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: स्कूल में नाम लिखाना (skool men naam likhaanaa, 'to enroll in school') and थाने में शिकायत लिखाना (thaane men shikaayat likhaanaa, 'to file a police complaint') are everyday civic-life phrases -- the causative captures the reality that an official actually writes the entry while you merely initiate it.
Common Mistakes: 1) शिकायत लिखाना (shikaayat likhaanaa) means 'to file a complaint' (at a police station, with an official), not literally 'to write a complaint' oneself -- the sense is bureaucratic/procedural. 2) नाम लिखाना (naam likhaanaa) for enrolling in a school or race is easily confused with नाम लेना (naam lenaa, 'to take/say a name'); they are unrelated.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपने बेटे का नाम स्कूल में लिखाया। He enrolled his son in school.
(usne apne bete kaa naam skool men likhaayaa।)

Sense 2 : to get/have something written (by someone else)
verb, transitive Causative frame: subject arranges for a second person (marked with से, se) to do the actual writing, e.g. चिट्ठी लिखाना (chitthii likhaanaa, 'to have a letter written').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Very much alive in scenes like the old bazaar scribe writing letters for the illiterate, or a lawyer's clerk drafting an affidavit -- the customer 'लिखाता है' (likhaataa hai) rather than writing it himself. This causative habit of thought -- getting things done through an intermediary -- shows up constantly in Hindi's causative verbs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use लिखना (likhnaa) when they mean they got someone else to write something -- अपने बारे में लिखाया (apne baare men likhaayaa, 'had it written about myself') needs लिखाना, not लिखना, once another person is doing the physical writing. 2) The person who actually writes is marked with से (se), not को (ko): मुंशी से चिट्ठी लिखाई (munshii se chitthii likhaaii, 'had the letter written by the clerk').
