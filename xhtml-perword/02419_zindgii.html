ज़िंदगी (zindgii)

noun A warm, everyday word for 'life' -- the span of living, one's existence, or the way one lives. Single core sense, but very rich in idiom and everyday philosophical talk.
Sense 1 : life (existence, one's lifespan or way of living)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जीवन (jiivan, formal), हयात (hayaat, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word carries real emotional weight in everyday Hindi -- it shows up constantly in film dialogue, song lyrics, and heartfelt conversation, from ज़िंदगी अच्छी है (zindgii achchhii hai, 'life is good') to ज़िंदगी भर (zindgii bhar, 'for the whole of one's life'). It comes from Persian and sits comfortably alongside its Sanskrit-origin cousin जीवन (jiivan) -- the two are near-synonyms, but ज़िंदगी (zindgii) feels warmer and more spoken, while जीवन (jiivan) leans toward the reflective or formal register.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for जीवन (jiivan) in casual speech where a Delhi speaker would naturally say ज़िंदगी (zindgii) -- जीवन (jiivan) tends to sound more formal or written. 2) The nuqta on ज़ is often dropped in casual typing (जिंदगी (jindgii)), which is common and acceptable, not an error.
