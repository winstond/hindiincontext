पाठ (paath)

noun Mainly a school/study word meaning 'lesson' or 'chapter' (from a textbook), and also the act of ritually reciting or reading aloud a sacred text; both senses share the root idea of 'text to be read.'
Sense 1 : lesson, chapter (in a book)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अध्याय (adhyaay, chapter, formal/bookish), भाग (bhaag, part/section)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Hindi textbook in Indian schools is divided into पाठ (paath) -- 'पाठ १' (paath 1, 'Lesson 1'), 'पाठ २' (paath 2, 'Lesson 2'), and so on -- so this is one of the first words a child (or a learner using school-style materials) meets on the very first page.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पाठ (paath) with पाठ्यक्रम (paathyakram, 'syllabus/curriculum') -- पाठ (paath) is just one lesson/chapter within that larger course. 2) With verbs like पढ़ना (padhnaa), the phrase is पाठ पढ़ना (paath padhnaa, 'to read/study a lesson'), not पाठ करना (paath karnaa) in this sense (करना (karnaa) is reserved for the recitation sense).
Sample Sentences:
आज हमें नया पाठ पढ़ना है। Today we have to read a new lesson.
(aaj hamen nayaa paath padhnaa hai।)

Sense 2 : ritual recitation, reading aloud (of scripture/sacred text)
noun, masculine Typically appears in the collocation पाठ करना (paath karnaa, 'to recite/read scripture') or पाठ कराना (paath karaanaa, 'to have [a priest] recite'), often naming the text: रामायण का पाठ (raamaayan kaa paath, 'recitation of the Ramayana').
Synonyms: पारायण (paaraayan, literary, full ritual reading of a scripture), जाप (jaap, repeated chanting, esp. of a mantra/name)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Families often arrange a पाठ (paath) at home -- for example हनुमान चालीसा का पाठ (hanumaan chaaliisaa kaa paath, 'recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa]') -- especially during festivals, illnesses, or new beginnings, sometimes inviting a पंडित (pandit, 'priest') to lead it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not use पढ़ना (padhnaa) here the way you would for a school lesson -- ritual recitation is expressed with पाठ करना (paath karnaa), not पाठ पढ़ना (paath padhnaa). 2) पाठ (paath) in this sense implies a somewhat formal or ritual reading, not casual reading of any text.
