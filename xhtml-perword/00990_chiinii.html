चीनी (chiinii)

noun/adjective Two distinct everyday senses live under this one spelling: the noun for 'sugar' and the adjective/noun meaning 'Chinese.' They are unrelated in origin and meaning, so treat them as separate words that happen to sound alike.
Sense 1 : sugar
noun, feminine
Synonyms: शक्कर (shakkar), शक्र (shakr, rare)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a Delhi-NCR household, offering chai you will almost always be asked कितनी चीनी (kitnii chiinii, 'how much sugar')? -- and answering कम चीनी (kam chiinii, 'less sugar') is such a common health-conscious reply these days that it has become a small social ritual in itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) Do not confuse with चाय (chaay, 'tea') when ordering -- चीनी (chiinii) is the sweetener you add, not a drink. 2) Learners sometimes use बर्फ़ (barf, 'ice/frost') by mistake for sugar because both can look white and granular in casual description -- they are unrelated words.

(chaay men kitnii chiinii daaloon?)

Sense 2 : Chinese (relating to China)
adjective Functions as an invariant adjective describing nationality or origin, e.g. चीनी खाना (chiinii khaanaa, 'Chinese food'), चीनी भाषा (chiinii bhaashaa, 'Chinese language').
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: चीनी खाना (chiinii khaanaa, 'Chinese food') is everywhere in Indian cities as its own hybridized cuisine (chilli chicken, Manchurian, hakka noodles) -- so when someone says चीनी खाना खाते हैं (chiinii khaanaa khaate hain, 'let's eat Chinese food'), they usually mean the Indian-Chinese version, not authentic Chinese cooking.
Common Mistakes: 1) Unlike most nationality words, चीनी (chiinii) does not change form for gender or number -- चीनी लड़की (chiinii ladkii) and चीनी लड़के (chiinii ladke) both keep the same spelling.

Sense 3 : a Chinese person
noun, masculine/feminine Used as a noun referring to a person of Chinese nationality/ethnicity, e.g. वह चीनी है (vah chiinii hai, 'he/she is Chinese').
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun use shares its form with the adjective sense above -- context tells you whether चीनी (chiinii) points at a person or describes a thing.
