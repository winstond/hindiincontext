गाड़ना (gaadnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to dig into the ground and fix something there -- to plant, bury, or drive something (a stake, a flag, a nail) into a surface.
Sense 1 : to bury (something) in the ground
verb, transitive
Synonyms: दफ़नाना (dafnaanaa, used especially for burying the dead), दबाना (dabaanaa, to press/push under)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a gardener burying a sapling's roots, or old stories of misers who गाड़ना (gaadnaa) their gold coins in a clay pot under the courtyard floor -- this verb carries that same earthy, hands-in-the-soil feel.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for दफ़नाना (dafnaanaa) for every kind of burying, but गाड़ना (gaadnaa) is the more general word for pushing something into earth (a pot, treasure, a plant) and is not limited to funerals. 2) Do not confuse with गाना (gaanaa, 'to sing') -- the retroflex ड़ in गाड़ना (gaadnaa) gives it a completely different sound and meaning.
Sample Sentences:
किसान ने खेत में पौधे की जड़ें गाड़ दीं। The farmer buried the sapling's roots in the field.
(kisaan ne khet men paudhe kii jaden gaad diin।)

Sense 2 : to drive/plant something into a surface (stake, flag, nail)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: ठोकना (thoknaa, to hammer in, used more for nails), खड़ा करना (khadaa karnaa, to erect/stand up)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: झंडा गाड़ना (jhandaa gaadnaa) literally means planting a flag, but it is used all the time figuratively for triumph -- someone who dominates a field is said to have 'planted their flag' there, much like claiming victory in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) With flags or stakes, learners often default to लगाना (lagaanaa), but गाड़ना (gaadnaa) is the word that specifically evokes forcing something down into the ground so it stands firm -- झंडा गाड़ना (jhandaa gaadnaa, 'to plant a flag') is the natural collocation.
