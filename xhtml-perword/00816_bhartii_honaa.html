भरती होना (bhartii honaa)

verb A compound verb meaning 'to get admitted/enrolled' (hospital, school, army, job) or 'to be recruited/enlisted'; भरती (bhartii) itself means 'filling up/recruitment' and pairs with होना (honaa) to describe the process from the person's side.
Sense 1 : to be admitted (hospital, school, institution)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: दाखिल होना (daakhil honaa), प्रवेश लेना (pravesh lenaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday phrase you'll hear when someone's grandfather has been hospitalized (अस्पताल में भरती हो गए, asptaal men bhartii ho gae, 'he got admitted to the hospital') or when a child starts school (स्कूल में भरती होना, skool men bhartii honaa). Also spelled भर्ती होना (bhartii honaa) with the added र् -- both spellings are extremely common in Delhi-NCR speech and print, so don't be surprised to see either.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say में भरती होना (men bhartii honaa) with the institution unmarked or wrongly postposed; the standard frame is X में भरती होना (men bhartii honaa, 'to be admitted into X'), e.g. अस्पताल में भरती होना (asptaal men bhartii honaa, 'to be admitted to the hospital'). 2) Because होना (honaa) is intransitive, the subject takes no ने (ne) even though 'admit' feels like an action the person undergoes -- Hindi frames it as something that happens to them, not something they do.

(mere daadaa jii kal raat asptaal men bhartii ho gae।)

Sense 2 : to be recruited/enlisted (job, army, police)
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: नौकरी लगना (naukrii lagnaa, colloquial), भर्ती होना (bhartii honaa, variant spelling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Common in the context of सेना में भरती होना (senaa men bhartii honaa, 'to join/enlist in the army') or पुलिस में भरती होना (pulis men bhartii honaa, 'to be recruited into the police') -- you'll see this phrase on recruitment posters and hear it in news reports about army/police bharti drives, a major event in many small towns and villages.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with भरती करना (bhartii karnaa), which is the transitive counterpart used by the institution doing the recruiting (e.g. सेना ने उसे भरती किया, senaa ne use bhartii kiyaa, 'the army recruited him') -- भरती होना (bhartii honaa) is what happens from the recruit's own side.
