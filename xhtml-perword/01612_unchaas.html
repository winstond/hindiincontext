उनचास (unchaas)

numeral A cardinal number meaning 'forty-nine' (unchaas); used in counting and quantities exactly like any other Hindi numeral.
Sense 1 : the number 49
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Like most Hindi numbers from 21 to 99, उनचास (unchaas) is a fossilized, irregular form rather than a compositional one -- Hindi numerals historically fused a base number with a modifier (here, roughly 'one less than fifty') into a single unpredictable word. Native speakers simply memorize these as a block, the same way English speakers just know 'eleven' isn't 'onety-one'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often mix up उनचास (unchaas, 49) with उनचालीस (unchaaliis, 39) and उनसठ (unsath, 59) since Hindi numerals 41-99 do not follow a predictable additive pattern like English and must simply be learned as individual words. 2) The initial उन- (un) sound (a contraction meaning 'one less than fifty') gives no obvious clue to its value, unlike English 'forty-nine', so learners should not try to derive it logically from parts.
