सूचना (soochnaa)

noun/verb Two related senses: as a noun it means 'information/notice', and as a verb (mainly in formal or written registers) it means 'to inform, to notify'.
Sense 1 : information, notice, announcement
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जानकारी (jaankaarii), खबर (khabar, everyday, esp. news-like updates), समाचार (samaachaar, formal, news bulletins), नोटिस (notis, official/written notice)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see सूचना (soochnaa) stamped on office notice boards, railway announcements, and government forms -- it has a slightly official, bureaucratic flavor compared to the more casual खबर (khabar). Think of it as the word behind India's Right to Information law, सूचना का अधिकार (soochnaa kaa adhikaar, 'Right to Information'), a very commonly referenced piece of civic vocabulary.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खबर (khabar) in every context, but सूचना (soochnaa) is preferred for official, printed, or formally announced information -- like a notice board or government circular -- while खबर (khabar) suits everyday news and gossip. 2) सूचना देना (soochnaa denaa, 'to give notice/inform') is the standard collocation with this noun; learners sometimes try to build a sentence around the noun alone without देना (denaa) or मिलना (milnaa).

Sense 2 : to inform, to notify
verb, transitive Used almost exclusively in formal, written, or bureaucratic Hindi -- notices, letters, official announcements -- rather than casual speech, where बताना (bataanaa) is used instead.
Synonyms: बताना (bataanaa, everyday equivalent), अवगत कराना (avgat karaanaa, formal/bureaucratic), जानकारी देना (jaankaarii denaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You will meet this in formal letters and office emails: कृपया सूचित करें (kripyaa soochit karen, 'please inform/notify') is a stock phrase in Indian bureaucratic and business Hindi.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this verb in casual conversation where a native speaker would simply say बताना (bataanaa); सूचित करना (soochit karnaa) sounds stiff or overly formal in everyday chat. 2) The verb almost always appears as the compound सूचित करना (soochit karnaa, 'to inform') -- सूचना itself is not conjugated directly as a verb in modern usage.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया मुझे समय पर सूचित करें। Please inform me on time.
(kripyaa mujhe samay par soochit karen।)
