गूँगा (goongaa)

adjective/noun Primarily an adjective/noun meaning 'mute, dumb (unable to speak)'; also used figuratively for someone silent or tongue-tied, and lives inside a well-known idiom about a mute person's experience being inexpressible.
Sense 1 : unable to speak, mute
adjective
Synonyms: मूक (mook, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word inflects like an ordinary -आ (aa) adjective, so you will meet गूँगी (goongii) for a woman/girl and गूँगे (goonge) in the plural or oblique. It pairs constantly with बहरा (bahraa, 'deaf') in the fixed phrase गूँगा-बहरा (goongaa-bahraa) describing someone who is both.
Common Mistakes: 1) गूँगा (goongaa) is now considered an insensitive/blunt term in polite or clinical contexts; बोलने में असमर्थ (bolne men asmarth, 'unable to speak') or गूँगा-बहरा (goongaa-bahraa, 'deaf-mute') is often preferred when describing a real disability respectfully, similar to the shift away from 'dumb' in English.

Sense 2 : a mute person
noun, masculine/feminine Functions as the adjective used substantively -- 'the mute one' -- taking the same gender-marked forms (गूँगा/गूँगी/गूँगे, goongaa/goongii/goonge).
Synonyms: मूक (mook, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This noun sense is the heart of the classic saying गूँगे का गुड़ (goonge kaa gud, 'a mute person's jaggery') -- meaning an experience or feeling so personal it cannot be put into words, because the mute person who tastes the sweet jaggery has no way to describe its taste to anyone else. You will hear this idiom used for anything from a private joy to an unspeakable pain.

Sense 3 : silent, unable or unwilling to express oneself (figurative)
adjective Used figuratively for a person struck silent by shock, fear, or helplessness, or for something (like pain or a situation) that has no voice.
Synonyms: खामोश (khaamosh), मौन (maun, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in lines like वह गूँगा बनकर सब सुनता रहा (vah goongaa bankar sab suntaa rahaa, 'he kept listening to everything, having become mute') -- describing someone who swallows their words out of helplessness rather than choice.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for चुप (chup) here, which just means 'quiet/not talking right now' -- गूँगा (goongaa) in this figurative sense implies a deeper, almost helpless inability to speak up, not simple silence.
