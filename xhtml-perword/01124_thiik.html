ठीक (thiik)

adjective/adverb/interjection One of the most versatile words in everyday Hindi: an adjective meaning 'correct/fine/okay', an adverb meaning 'exactly/precisely', and the backbone of the stock interjection ठीक है (thiik hai, 'okay').
Sense 1 : fine, well, all right, correct
adjective
Synonyms: सही (sahii), दुरुस्त (durust), अच्छा (achchhaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: ठीक (thiik) is the everyday word for 'fine' or 'okay' when someone asks about your health, a plan, or whether something works -- ठीक है? (thiik hai?, 'is that okay?') is one of the most common check-in questions in Delhi-NCR speech, tossed in constantly to confirm agreement.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to inflect ठीक (thiik) like कड़ा (kadaa), giving ठीका (thiikaa) or ठीकी (thiikii) -- it never changes form, regardless of the gender or number of the noun it describes. 2) As an answer to 'how are you', ठीक हूँ (thiik hoon, 'I am fine') is the normal reply -- ठीक is not used alone as a full sentence the way 'fine' can be in English.

(kyaa tum thiik ho?)

Sense 2 : exactly, precisely, right (at/on)
adverb Used before a location, time, or number to sharpen precision, e.g. ठीक बारह बजे (thiik baarah baje, 'at exactly twelve o'clock') or ठीक सामने (thiik saamne, 'right in front').
Synonyms: बिल्कुल (bilkul), एकदम (ekdam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: When a Delhi auto-wallah or shopkeeper says ठीक यहीं रोक दो (thiik yahiin rok do, 'stop right here'), that ठीक is doing the job of English 'right' or 'exactly' -- pinning down a location precisely rather than judging it as good or correct.

Sense 3 : okay, all right (as a standalone response)
interjection Functions as a one-word conversational response confirming agreement or acknowledgment, distinct from its use as a descriptive adjective within a sentence.
Synonyms: अच्छा (achchhaa), ठीक है (thiik hai, fuller form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A quick ठीक (thiik) or ठीक ठीक (thiik thiik) dropped into conversation is how people signal 'got it, moving on' -- much like a clipped 'okay' in English, it closes a topic without needing a full sentence.
Sample Sentences:

(thiik, main abhii aataa hoon।)
