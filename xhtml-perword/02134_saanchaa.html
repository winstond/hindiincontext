साँचा (saanchaa)

noun A physical mold or die used to shape material; by extension, a fixed template or pattern that something is made to conform to.
Sense 1 : mold, cast, die (a hollow form used to shape material)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ढाँचा (dhaanchaa, structure/framework sense overlaps)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of the little metal or wooden molds used at home to shape mithai (mithaaii) like besan ke laddoo (besan ke laddoo) or shakkarpara (shakarpaaraa) -- that's a साँचा (saanchaa) in its most everyday sense. Potters, brick-makers, and sweet-shops all rely on this word constantly.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse साँचा (saanchaa) with ढाँचा (dhaanchaa): साँचा is the physical mold you pour or press material into (like for making bricks, sweets, or metal parts), while ढाँचा is more the resulting skeleton/structure or framework of something (a building's ढाँचा, a plan's ढाँचा).

Sense 2 : fixed pattern, template, mould (figurative -- a rigid model that something is expected to fit)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ढर्रा (dharraa, routine/rut sense), खाका (khaakaa, outline/blueprint)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear parents or society described as wanting to 'fit' a person into a साँचा (saanchaa) -- ek hi saanche mein dhalna (ek hii saanche men dhalnaa, 'to be molded into the same fixed pattern'), often used with mild criticism of conformity, as in complaints about rigid expectations placed on children.
Common Mistakes: 1) In this figurative sense साँचे में ढलना (saanche men dhalnaa, 'to be cast/molded into the mold') is a common idiom meaning to conform to a set pattern or expectation -- learners should recognize this as figurative, not literal casting.
