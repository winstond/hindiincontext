तरबूज़ (tarbooz)

noun A common fruit name: watermelon. One straightforward sense; also spelled तरबूज (tarbooj) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : watermelon
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: तरबूज़ (tarbooz) is the fruit of Indian summers -- you will see carts piled high with them on Delhi roadsides from April onward, and street vendors slicing them into red wedges to cool people down in the heat. Families also love to thump a whole तरबूज़ (tarbooz) to check if it sounds hollow enough to be ripe before buying.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तरबूज़ (tarbooz) with खरबूजा (kharboojaa), which is muskmelon/cantaloupe, not watermelon -- the two are different fruits with different Hindi names, not variants of the same word.
