पटाका (pataakaa)

noun Literally a firecracker (pataakaa); the same word is also used figuratively for a strikingly attractive or dazzling person, and colloquially for a piece of shocking or explosive news.
Sense 1 : firecracker
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पटाखा (pataakhaa, variant spelling), आतिशबाज़ी (aatishbaazii, meaning scope: fireworks display overall)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a Diwali (divaalii) word above all -- markets fill with stalls selling पटाके (pataake) in the weeks before the festival, and the loud bursts on the night itself are called पटाके फोड़ना (pataake phodnaa, 'to burst firecrackers'). Note the spelling varies freely between पटाका (pataakaa) and पटाखा (pataakhaa) with no difference in meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often only know पटाका (pataakaa) in its figurative 'attractive person' sense from Bollywood songs and are surprised to learn the literal, more common meaning is simply 'firecracker,' as used around Diwali (divaalii).

Sense 2 : a stunning, dazzling person (usually a woman)
noun, masculine Used almost always as a predicate or exclamation about a person's looks, e.g. calling someone पटाका है (pataakaa hai, 'she's a knockout').
Synonyms: बम (bam, colloquial), हॉट (hot, colloquial, English loan)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative use took off through Hindi film songs and dialogue -- calling someone a पटाका (pataakaa) is a flirtatious, filmi compliment, the same instinct behind English slang like 'firecracker' or 'bombshell' for a striking person.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is casual and a bit cheeky; using it about someone in a formal or professional context would land as inappropriate or disrespectful. 2) It is applied overwhelmingly to describe women's looks in everyday speech, so learners should be cautious about tone when using it about men.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : explosive/sensational news or event
noun, masculine Common in phrases like पटाका फूटना (pataakaa phootnaa, 'for a bombshell to drop/burst') used about sudden news, scandals, or announcements.
Synonyms: धमाका (dhamaakaa)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Just as firecrackers burst suddenly and loudly, this sense borrows that image for news that explodes onto the scene -- a scandal, a surprise announcement, or a shocking twist, often heard in TV news anchors' dramatic phrasing.
