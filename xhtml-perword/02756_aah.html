आह (aah)

interjection/noun Primarily an interjection expressing pain, longing, or a heartfelt sigh; also used as a feminine noun meaning 'sigh' or 'groan', especially in poetic and emotional speech.
Sense 1 : exclamation of pain, sorrow, or longing
interjection
Synonyms: हाय (haay)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear आह (aah) escape someone's lips when they stub a toe, remember an old heartbreak, or watch a sad scene in a film -- it carries a soft, drawn-out quality that fits both physical pain and emotional ache. It pairs naturally with sighing gestures, closed eyes, or a hand pressed to the chest.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use आह (aah) for surprise or delight, but it leans toward pain, sorrow, or wistful longing -- for happy surprise, वाह (vaah) is the word natives reach for instead.

Sense 2 : a sigh, groan, or heartfelt lament
noun, feminine Common in the fixed phrase आह भरना (aah bharnaa, 'to heave a sigh'), which functions almost like a compound verb.
Synonyms: उसाँस (usaans, literary), आहट (aahat, different nuance: 'sound of footsteps/presence')
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: In Hindi film lyrics and Urdu-flavored shayari, an आह (aah) is almost always the sigh of a wounded lover -- it is a staple of romantic and melancholic poetry, evoking someone gazing out a window lost in thought.
Common Mistakes: 1) आह भरना (aah bharnaa) is the natural collocation for 'to sigh' -- learners sometimes try आह करना (aah karnaa), which sounds off to native ears.
