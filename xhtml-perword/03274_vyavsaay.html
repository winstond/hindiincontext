व्यवसाय (vyavsaay)

noun A somewhat formal noun covering the world of business/occupation: it can mean a person's line of work/profession or a business/trade enterprise itself. Related to लेकिन-free everyday words like काम (kaam, work) and धंधा (dhandhaa, colloquial for business/trade).
Sense 1 : occupation, profession
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पेशा (peshaa), काम (kaam)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you will see printed on official Indian forms next to 'occupation' -- job applications, bank forms, hotel registers. In casual conversation, people are much more likely to just ask तुम क्या करते हो (tum kyaa karte ho, 'what do you do') than to use व्यवसाय (vyavsaay) out loud.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for काम (kaam) in all contexts, but व्यवसाय (vyavsaay) is the word used on forms, in interviews, and in formal writing when asking someone's profession -- आपका व्यवसाय क्या है (aapkaa vyavsaay kyaa hai, 'what is your profession'). 2) Do not confuse with व्यवहार (vyavahaar, behavior/dealings) -- the two words look similar but are unrelated in meaning.
Sample Sentences:
कृपया इस फॉर्म में अपना व्यवसाय लिखें। Please write your occupation on this form.
(kripyaa is phorm men apnaa vyavsaay likhen।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
फॉर्म में व्यवसाय वाले कॉलम में क्या लिखा जाए? What should be written in the occupation column on the form?
(phorm men vyavsaay vaale kolam men kyaa likhaa jaae?)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive

Sense 2 : business, trade, commercial enterprise
noun, masculine
Synonyms: धंधा (dhandhaa, colloquial), कारोबार (kaarobaar), व्यापार (vyaapaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in news broadcasts and business talk -- छोटे व्यवसाय (chhote vyavsaay, 'small businesses') is a common phrase in economic reporting. It carries a slightly formal, official flavor compared to धंधा (dhandhaa), which a shopkeeper might use casually about his own trade.
Common Mistakes: 1) व्यापार (vyaapaar) leans toward trade/commerce specifically (buying and selling), while व्यवसाय (vyavsaay) is broader and can cover any organized enterprise or industry -- in practice the two overlap heavily and are often used interchangeably in news and business contexts.
Sample Sentences:
यह कंपनी कपड़ों का व्यवसाय करती है। This company runs a clothing business.

(sarkaar chhote vyavsaayon ko badhaavaa dene ke lie naii yojnaa laaii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
