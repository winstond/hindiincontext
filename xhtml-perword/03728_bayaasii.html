बयासी (bayaasii)

numeral The cardinal number 82 (eighty-two).
Sense 1 : the number 82
numeral
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 21 to 99 each have their own irregular shape rather than following a clean tens-plus-units pattern like English, so बयासी (bayaasii) just has to be learned as a standalone item -- most native speakers rely on memorized number sequences (often from school chanting) rather than any logical build-up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to build this compositionally from अस्सी (assii, 'eighty') plus दो (do, 'two'), but like all Hindi numbers 21-99, बयासी (bayaasii) is a fused, unpredictable form that must simply be memorized rather than derived.
