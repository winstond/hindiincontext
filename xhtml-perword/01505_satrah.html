सत्रह (satrah)

numeral The number seventeen (17), used both for counting and as a numeral adjective before nouns.
Sense 1 : the number seventeen
numeral
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Numbers in Hindi from 1 to 99 are mostly learned as individual memorized words rather than built from a clean formula, and सत्रह (satrah) is one of these -- there is no shortcut from सात (saat, 'seven') plus दस (das, 'ten') the way English builds 'seventeen' from 'seven' and 'teen'. Most learners just drill the teens as a block.
Common Mistakes: 1) सत्रह (satrah) is invariant regardless of the gender or number of the noun it modifies, unlike ordinal or quantity words that some learners expect to change form: सत्रह किताबें (satrah kitaaben, 'seventeen books') and सत्रह लड़के (satrah ladke, 'seventeen boys') both use the same form.
