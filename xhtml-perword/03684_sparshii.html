स्पर्शी (sparshii)

adjective An adjective meaning 'touching' or 'tangent (to)', mostly met in geometry (of a line touching a circle) and in compound/technical words; from the same root as स्पर्श (sparsh, touch).
Sense 1 : tangent, touching (geometry)
adjective Used almost exclusively in math contexts, typically in the fixed phrase स्पर्श रेखा (sparsh rekhaa, tangent line), of which स्पर्शी (sparshii) is the adjectival form describing the line's relation to a circle or curve.
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word will mostly surface in a school math classroom in India, in the phrase स्पर्श रेखा (sparsh rekhaa) for a tangent line -- it is a textbook word rather than something you would use in daily conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use स्पर्शी (sparshii) for everyday physical touching (like touching an object with your hand); that everyday sense is carried by स्पर्श करना (sparsh karnaa) or छूना (chhoonaa), not by this adjective, which is really confined to the geometric 'tangent' meaning in modern usage.

Sense 2 : touching, affecting (emotionally); used as a compound suffix
adjective Mostly appears as the second element of a compound to mean 'pertaining to/touching upon' a topic, e.g. विषय-स्पर्शी (vishay-sparshii, 'touching on the subject'), rather than standing alone in ordinary speech.
Synonyms: संबंधी (sambandhii, related to), भावुक (bhaavuk, emotionally touching -- narrower, feeling-focused)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: You'll meet this more in formal or literary writing -- essays, editorials -- where a compound like हृदय-स्पर्शी (hriday-sparshii, 'heart-touching') is used for something emotionally moving, similar to how English uses 'touching' for a moving story.
