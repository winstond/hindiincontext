संजीदा (sanjiidaa)

adjective A single-sense adjective describing a person's temperament or a moment's mood as serious, grave, or composed.
Sense 1 : serious, grave, composed (of temperament or mood)
adjective
Synonyms: गंभीर (gambhiir), गम्भीर (gambhiir, spelling variant), शांत (shaant, meaning scope: calm rather than serious)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word used to praise someone as steady and level-headed: एक संजीदा इंसान (ek sanjiidaa insaan, 'a serious, level-headed person') is a compliment, suggesting someone you can trust to not act rashly. It carries a slightly Urdu-literary flavor and shows up often in film dialogue and journalism describing someone's demeanor.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use संजीदा (sanjiidaa) to mean 'sad,' but it describes a sober, thoughtful seriousness, not sorrow -- for sadness use उदास (udaas). 2) It describes people and their manner or a mood in the air, not serious problems or serious injuries; for a serious matter or injury, गंभीर (gambhiir) is the natural choice.
