जलाना (jalaanaa)

verb The causative partner of जलना (jalnaa, 'to burn'): जलाना (jalaanaa) means 'to burn/light something' or, figuratively, 'to torment/annoy someone.' Both the literal and figurative senses are common in daily speech.
Sense 1 : to set fire to, burn (something)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: फूँकना (phoonknaa, for burning up completely/destroying), भस्म करना (bhasm karnaa, literary/formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Lighting a दीया (diiyaa, 'oil lamp') is the quintessential use of this verb -- दीया जलाना (diiyaa jalaanaa, 'to light a lamp') is what you hear every evening during आरती (aartii) or on Diwali. Think of जलाना (jalaanaa) as the 'make it burn' action-word, paired conceptually with the intransitive जलना (jalnaa, 'to burn/be lit').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use जलना (jalnaa) when they mean to burn something (transitive) -- जलना (jalnaa) is intransitive ('to burn/be on fire' by itself), while जलाना (jalaanaa) needs an object: दीया जलाओ (diiyaa jalaao, 'light the lamp'). 2) With ने-marking in the past tense, remember जलाना (jalaanaa) is transitive: उसने कागज़ जलाया (usne kaagaz jalaayaa, 'he burned the paper'), not उसने जलाई।
Sample Sentences:
हर शाम माँ मंदिर में दीया जलाती है। Every evening, mother lights a lamp in the temple.
(har shaam maan mandir men diiyaa jalaatii hai।)

Sense 2 : to torment, annoy, make someone jealous or irritated
verb, transitive
Synonyms: तंग करना (tang karnaa, to harass/pester), चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa, to tease/provoke), खिझाना (khijhaanaa, less common)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense rides on the same 'burning' image as emotional jealousy -- if जलना (jalnaa) is 'to burn (with envy)', जलाना (jalaanaa) is 'to make someone burn.' You will hear this constantly in domestic banter, like a sister-in-law teasing another: वह जलाने के लिए ऐसा कहती है (vah jalaane ke lie aisaa kahtii hai, 'she says that just to needle her').
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense overlaps with जलना (jalnaa) meaning 'to burn with envy' -- जलाना (jalaanaa) is what you do *to* someone to make them feel that way: वह मुझे जलाता है (vah mujhe jalaataa hai, 'he winds me up/needles me'). 2) Learners may reach for चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) for lighter teasing and miss that जलाना (jalaanaa) carries a sharper edge of provoking jealousy or real irritation, not just playful teasing.
