शायरी (shaayrii)

noun A single-sense noun: the art or body of Urdu-Hindi poetry, especially the romantic/lyrical verse tradition associated with mushairas and ghazals.
Sense 1 : poetry (especially Urdu-style lyrical/romantic verse)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कविता (kavitaa, broader term, covers all poetry), काव्य (kaavy, literary), शेर-ओ-शायरी (sher-o-shaayrii, set phrase, poetry in general)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: शायरी (shaayrii) carries a distinctly romantic, often melancholic flavor in everyday Hindi -- think of a friend sighing over a WhatsApp forward of couplets about love and loss, or someone saying वो शायरी करता है (vo shaayrii kartaa hai, 'he writes/recites poetry') to mean he's the sentimental, poetic type. It is closely tied to the word शायर (shaayar, 'poet'), the person who composes it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use कविता (kavitaa) and शायरी (shaayrii) interchangeably, but शायरी (shaayrii) specifically evokes the Urdu tradition -- ghazals, shers, and the culture of mushairas -- while कविता (kavitaa) is the general, register-neutral word for poetry taught in school. 2) शायरी (shaayrii) is used both for the art form itself ('he does शायरी (shaayrii)') and for a specific piece or couplet someone recites, so context decides whether it means 'poetry' as a craft or 'a poem/couplet' being quoted.
