पुकारना (pukaarnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to call out to' someone, usually to get their attention or summon them, and by extension 'to call someone by (a name/title)'.
Sense 1 : to call out to (someone), to summon by voice
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बुलाना (bulaanaa), आवाज़ देना (aavaaz denaa, phrase: 'to give a voice/call')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a mother standing at the door shouting her child's name into the gali (galii, lane) at dusk -- that is the classic पुकारना (pukaarnaa) scene. It carries a slight sense of distance and volume that बुलाना (bulaanaa) does not, since बुलाना (bulaanaa) is the more neutral, everyday 'to call/invite'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बुलाना (bulaanaa) in every 'call' context, but पुकारना (pukaarnaa) specifically evokes raising one's voice across a distance -- calling from a doorway, across a field, or after someone walking away -- rather than simply inviting or summoning someone to come. 2) The person being called takes को (ko): उसने मुझे पुकारा (usne mujhe pukaaraa, 'he called out to me'), not से (se).
Sample Sentences:
माँ छत से उसका नाम पुकार रही थी। Mother was calling out his name from the roof.
(maan chhat se uskaa naam pukaar rahii thii।)

Sense 2 : to call/address someone by a particular name or title
verb, transitive Takes the frame X ko Y (se/kar ke) pukaarnaa -- 'to call X (by) Y', e.g. sab use netaajii kah kar pukaarte hain (sab use netaajii kahkar pukaarte hain, 'everyone calls him netaji').
Synonyms: कहना (kahnaa, overlaps loosely), संबोधित करना (sambodhit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb behind nicknames and pet names in a Hindi household -- a grandmother might say ham use pyaar se 'guddu' kah kar pukaarte hain (ham use pyaar se 'guddoo' kahkar pukaarte hain, 'we affectionately call him Guddu'), tying the word to the warmth of family nicknames rather than formal naming.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense often needs the helper कहकर (kahkar) or से (se) before पुकारना (pukaarnaa) when introducing the name/title, which learners frequently drop: सब उसे शेरू कहकर पुकारते हैं (sab use sheroo kahkar pukaarte hain, 'everyone calls him Sheru'), not just सब उसे शेरू पुकारते हैं.
Sample Sentences:
हम उसे प्यार से 'गुड्डू' कहकर पुकारते हैं। We affectionately call him 'Guddu'.
(ham use pyaar se 'guddoo' kahkar pukaarte hain।)
