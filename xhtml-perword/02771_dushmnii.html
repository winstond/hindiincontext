दुश्मनी (dushmnii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning enmity or hostility -- the state of being at odds with someone, whether personal, political, or between groups.
Sense 1 : enmity, hostility, animosity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बैर (bair, literary), वैमनस्य (vaimnasy, formal), विरोध (virodh, opposition/conflict, meaning scope), नफरत (naphrat, hatred, meaning scope)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: दुश्मनी (dushmnii) is the abstract-noun form of दुश्मन (dushman, enemy), built with the common -ई (-ii) suffix that turns a person-noun into a state-noun, the same pattern as दोस्त (dost, friend) becoming दोस्ती (dostii, friendship). You will often hear the pair दोस्ती और दुश्मनी (dostii aur dushmnii, 'friendship and enmity') invoked together, especially in conversations about family feuds or old rivalries that outlast anyone's memory of how they started.
Common Mistakes: 1) दुश्मनी (dushmnii) usually pairs with से (se) or की तरफ से (kii taraph se) to mark who the enmity is with, as in किसी से दुश्मनी (kisii se dushmnii, enmity with someone) -- learners sometimes reach for के साथ (ke saath) by analogy with 'friendship with,' which sounds off here. 2) दुश्मनी करना (dushmnii karnaa, to bear/practice enmity) and दुश्मनी निभाना (dushmnii nibhaanaa, to carry an enmity through/see it out) are both common but mean subtly different things -- the first is about harboring hostility, the second about acting on it persistently, almost like a duty.
