इस्तेमाल करना (istemaal karnaa)

verb A common compound verb meaning 'to use'; इस्तेमाल (istemaal) is a noun borrowed from Persian/Urdu, paired here with करना (karnaa, 'to do') to form the action. One core sense, but very high-frequency in daily speech.
Sense 1 : to use, to make use of
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उपयोग करना (upyog karnaa, formal), प्रयोग करना (prayog karnaa, formal), काम में लाना (kaam men laanaa, idiomatic)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: इस्तेमाल करना (istemaal karnaa) is the everyday, spoken-register choice you will hear constantly in markets, offices, and homes across Delhi-NCR -- for instance a shopkeeper asking क्या आप ये इस्तेमाल करते हैं? (kyaa aap ye istemaal karte hain?, 'do you use this?'). Its more Sanskritic cousins उपयोग करना (upyog karnaa) and प्रयोग करना (prayog karnaa) show up more in writing, instructions, or formal announcements, so reaching for इस्तेमाल (istemaal) first will almost always sound natural in conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget this is a transitive compound verb and drop ने (ne) in the past tense: it must be उसने इस्तेमाल किया (usne istemaal kiyaa, 'he/she used'), not उस इस्तेमाल किया. 2) The noun इस्तेमाल (istemaal) stays invariant in the phrase; only करना (karnaa) inflects for tense/agreement, so learners sometimes mistakenly try to change the noun's form. 3) When the object is specific, it can take an extra frame like X का इस्तेमाल करना (kaa istemaal karnaa, 'to use X'), e.g. फोन का इस्तेमाल करना (phon kaa istemaal karnaa, 'to use the phone') -- learners often omit का (kaa) and just say फोन इस्तेमाल करना, which sounds off in careful speech though it is heard colloquially.
