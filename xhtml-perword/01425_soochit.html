सूचित (soochit)

adjective A formal word meaning 'informed/notified'; almost always used as part of the compound verb सूचित करना (soochit karnaa, 'to inform/notify') or its passive सूचित होना (soochit honaa, 'to be informed').
Sense 1 : informed, notified
adjective Functions mainly as the non-verbal part of the compound सूचित करना (soochit karnaa); rarely stands alone as a simple descriptive adjective outside that frame.
Synonyms: अवगत (avgat, formal), जानकार (jaankaar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is classic office and government-notice register in Delhi-NCR -- you will see it constantly on circulars and forms: 'सभी को सूचित किया जाता है कि...' (sabhii ko soochit kiyaa jaataa hai ki..., 'all are hereby informed that...'). In casual chat, people just say बताना (bataanaa, 'to tell') instead; सूचित करना (soochit karnaa) signals a formal, often written or official communication.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use सूचित (soochit) alone as if it were a plain adjective like 'informed' in English (e.g. 'वह सूचित है' vah soochit hai); in practice it almost always appears with करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) as a set verbal phrase, not standing by itself. 2) Do not confuse with सुचित्रित (suchitrit) or सूचना (soochnaa, the noun 'information/notice') -- सूचित is the adjective/participle form, सूचना is what gets communicated.
Sample Sentences:
सभी कर्मचारियों को सूचित किया जाता है कि कार्यालय कल बंद रहेगा। All employees are hereby informed that the office will remain closed tomorrow.
(sabhii karmchaariyon ko soochit kiyaa jaataa hai ki kaaryaalay kal band rahegaa।)

(jab tak koii soochit n kare, baithak kii taariikh vahii rahegii।)

(yadi samay rahte soochit kiyaa gayaa hotaa, to nuksaan tal saktaa thaa।)

(nae niymon ke baare men sabhii nivaasiyon ko soochit kiyaa jaaegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Passive

(vibhaag ne aadesh jaarii karte hue sabhii shaakhaaon ko soochit kiyaa।)
