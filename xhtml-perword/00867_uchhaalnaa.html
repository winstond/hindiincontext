उछालना (uchhaalnaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to toss/throw something up' -- literal (tossing a ball, coin) and figurative (tossing around an idea, or flinging an accusation). Related to the intransitive उछलना (uchhalnaa, 'to jump/bounce'), which describes the subject itself leaping.
Sense 1 : to toss/throw upward
verb, transitive
Synonyms: फेंकना (phenknaa, general throwing)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture a vendor tossing a coin to check it, or kids tossing a ball in a park (gend uchhaalnaa, 'to toss a ball') -- this verb is very physical and visual. It pairs naturally with सिक्का (sikkaa, 'coin') and गेंद (gend, 'ball') in everyday speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners confuse this with उछलना (uchhalnaa): उछालना (uchhaalnaa) is transitive (you toss something), while उछलना (uchhalnaa) is intransitive (something/someone jumps or bounces by itself). 2) The thing tossed takes को (ko) only if definite/specific; otherwise it appears unmarked as the direct object.
Sample Sentences:
बच्चे पार्क में गेंद उछाल रहे हैं। The children are tossing a ball in the park.
(bachche paark men gend uchhaal rahe hain।)

Sense 2 : to bring up/float an idea or rumor
verb, transitive Used with abstract objects like बात (baat, 'matter'), मुद्दा (muddaa, 'issue'), or अफ़वाह (afvaah, 'rumor') to mean floating something into public discussion, often casually or provocatively.
Synonyms: फैलाना (phailaanaa, for rumors specifically), पेश करना (pesh karnaa, for ideas/proposals)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this a lot in news and political talk: नेता ने यह मुद्दा उछाला (netaa ne yah muddaa uchhaalaa, 'the leader raised/floated this issue') -- it has a slight edge of stirring things up, like tossing a stone into calm water.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for फैलाना (phailaanaa) here, but उछालना (uchhaalnaa) carries a nuance of deliberately or provocatively tossing something into the conversation, not just spreading it passively.
