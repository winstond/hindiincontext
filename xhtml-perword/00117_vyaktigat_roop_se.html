व्यक्तिगत रूप से (vyaktigat roop se)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'personally' -- built from व्यक्तिगत (vyaktigat, 'personal') plus रूप से (roop se, 'in the form of/manner of'), a common pattern for turning adjectives into 'in an X manner' adverbs.
Sense 1 : personally, in one's own opinion/capacity
adverb Typically opens or inserts into a sentence to frame a personal opinion or involvement, e.g. व्यक्तिगत रूप से, मुझे लगता है (vyaktigat roop se, mujhe lagtaa hai, 'personally, I feel that...').
Synonyms: मेरी समझ से (merii samajh se, 'in my understanding'), अपनी तरफ़ से (apnii taraf se)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a fairly formal, somewhat writerly phrase -- you will hear it in debates, interviews, and opinion pieces more than in casual chai-time chatter, where लगता है (lagtaa hai, 'it seems to me') or मुझे तो (mujhe to) alone often does the same job more naturally.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop से (se) and just say व्यक्तिगत रूप (vyaktigat roop), which sounds incomplete -- the से (se) is required to complete the 'in the manner of' construction. 2) This phrase is used to voice opinions or personal stakes, not to mean 'in person' (physically) -- for 'meeting in person' Hindi speakers usually say ख़ुद (khud) or आमने-सामने (aamne-saamne) instead.
Sample Sentences:
व्यक्तिगत रूप से, मुझे यह फ़िल्म बहुत पसंद आई। Personally, I liked this film a lot.
(vyaktigat roop se, mujhe yah film bahut pasand aaii।)
