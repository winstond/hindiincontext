सुलझाना (suljhaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning to untangle or resolve something -- used both literally, for physical knots and tangles, and figuratively, for problems, disputes, or confusions. Related to the intransitive उलझना (ulajhnaa, 'to get tangled') and सुलझना (sulajhnaa, 'to become untangled/resolved').
Sense 1 : to resolve, sort out (a problem, dispute, confusion)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: हल करना (hal karnaa), निपटाना (niptaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Hindi speakers love the physical metaphor here: a problem (मसला/समस्या, maslaa/samasyaa) is imagined as a tangled thread or knot, and सुलझाना (suljhaanaa) is the patient, careful act of working it loose -- much gentler in feel than the more clinical हल करना (hal karnaa). You'll hear it constantly in contexts of family disputes, office misunderstandings, or emotional confusion.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for हल करना (hal karnaa) by default for every 'solve' context, but सुलझाना (suljhaanaa) is the natural choice when the problem is described as tangled or confused (a dispute, a misunderstanding, a knotty situation) rather than a math or logic puzzle. 2) The subject that resolves the issue takes ने (ne) in the perfective, since this is a straightforward transitive verb -- मैंने मसला सुलझाया (mainne maslaa suljhaayaa, 'I resolved the issue').
Sample Sentences:
हमें यह मसला मिलकर सुलझाना चाहिए। We should sort out this issue together.
(hamen yah maslaa milkar suljhaanaa chaahie।)

Sense 2 : to untangle, disentangle (something physically knotted)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: खोलना (kholnaa, meaning scope: also 'to open/undo')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the literal root sense -- think of untangling a ball of wool (उलझा हुआ धागा, uljhaa huaa dhaagaa, 'tangled thread') or messy hair. It sits right beside the figurative sense above; native speakers move fluidly between the physical and the metaphorical without feeling like they've switched words.
