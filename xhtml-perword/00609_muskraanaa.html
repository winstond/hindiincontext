मुस्कराना (muskraanaa)

verb A simple, everyday verb meaning 'to smile.' Also commonly spelled मुस्कुराना (muskuraanaa) with the extra उ; both forms are used constantly in speech and writing.
Sense 1 : to smile
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: मुस्कुराना (muskuraanaa, variant form), हँसना (hansnaa, to laugh/smile, broader), मुस्कान देना (muskaan denaa, to give a smile)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is a warm, gentle word -- the kind of smile you'd give a baby or share with an old friend across a room, not a boisterous laugh. You'll often hear it paired with धीरे से (dhiire se, 'softly') or हल्के से (halke se, 'lightly') to describe a subtle, closed-mouth smile, which is a very common image in Hindi film dialogue and poetry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse this with हँसना (hansnaa): मुस्कराना (muskraanaa) is specifically a quiet smile, while हँसना (hansnaa) covers laughing out loud as well as smiling in some contexts -- pick मुस्कराना (muskraanaa) when you mean a gentle, silent smile. 2) The subject of a smile is never ने-marked even though the verb looks like it could take an object -- it behaves as a plain intransitive verb: मैं मुस्कराया (main muskraayaa, 'I smiled'), not मैंने मुस्कराया (mainne muskraayaa).

(vah dhiire se muskraaii।)
