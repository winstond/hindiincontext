संभावना (sambhaavnaa)

noun A noun meaning 'possibility' or 'likelihood' -- covers both the abstract sense of something being possible and the sense of potential/promise in a person or situation.
Sense 1 : possibility, likelihood (of something happening)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: इमकान (imkaan, formal), गुंजाइश (gunjaaish, leans toward 'room/scope for')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in weather and news reports -- बारिश की संभावना (baarish kii sambhaavnaa, 'possibility of rain') is practically a fixed phrase on the evening news. It's a slightly formal, Sanskritic-feeling word, so in casual chat people often reach for the simpler chance (chaans, a common English borrowing) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say संभावना है कि (sambhaavanaa hai ki) when they mean something is likely -- the कि (ki) clause is essential and cannot be dropped the way it sometimes can in English ('there's a chance...'). 2) Don't confuse with संभव (sambhav), the adjective 'possible' -- संभावना (sambhaavnaa) is the noun 'possibility,' so 'यह संभव है' (yah sambhav hai, 'this is possible') versus 'इसकी संभावना है' (iskii sambhaavnaa hai, 'there is a possibility of this').
Sample Sentences:
आज शाम बारिश की संभावना है। There is a possibility of rain this evening.
(aaj shaam baarish kii sambhaavnaa hai।)

Sense 2 : potential, promise (in a person or talent)
noun, feminine Common in the phrase किसी में संभावना होना (kisii men sambhaavnaa honaa, 'to have potential in someone'), often used by teachers, coaches, or talent scouts describing a young person's promise.
Synonyms: क्षमता (kshamtaa, leans toward 'capacity/ability' rather than future promise)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: A cricket coach spotting a raw young player might say, इस लड़के में बहुत संभावना है (is ladke men bahut sambhaavnaa hai, 'this boy has a lot of potential') -- this sense is closely tied to the first, just shifted from events to people.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for क्षमता (kshamtaa) when they mean untapped future promise -- क्षमता (kshamtaa) is more about present capability, while संभावना (sambhaavnaa) in this sense points to what someone could become.
