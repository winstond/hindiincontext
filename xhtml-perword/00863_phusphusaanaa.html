फुसफुसाना (phusphusaanaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to whisper' -- it is inherently onomatopoeic, echoing the soft 'fus-fus' sound of hushed speech.
Sense 1 : to whisper
verb, intransitive
Synonyms: कान में कहना (kaan men kahnaa, lit. 'to say in the ear'), गुपचुप बात करना (gupchup baat karnaa, to talk secretively)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word is a great example of Hindi's love of sound-mimicking reduplication -- फुस-फुस (phus-phus) is practically the sound of air escaping between lips near an ear. You will hear it used for kids passing secrets in class, or for the slightly gossipy hiss of दो औरतें फुसफुसा रही थीं (do aurten phusphusaa rahii thiin, 'two women were whispering') outside a neighbor's door -- it often carries a faint whiff of gossip or conspiracy, not just quietness.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for धीरे बोलना (dhiire bolnaa) to mean 'whisper', but that just means 'to speak softly/quietly' -- it can describe a lowered voice in normal conversation. फुसफुसाना (phusphusaanaa) specifically evokes the breathy, secretive hiss of a true whisper, often between two people sharing something private. 2) The person being whispered *to* is usually marked with से (se) -- वह मुझसे फुसफुसाया (vah mujhse phusphusaayaa, 'he whispered to me') -- not को (ko), which can feel off here.
