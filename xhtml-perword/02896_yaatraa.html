यात्रा (yaatraa)

noun A single core sense: a journey or trip, ranging from an everyday commute to a pilgrimage. Compounds like तीर्थयात्रा (tiirthyaatraa) build on this same root.
Sense 1 : journey, trip, travel
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सफ़र (safar), सैर (sair, leisurely outing/stroll)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will see यात्रा (yaatraa) everywhere in Indian public life -- on Indian Railways announcements (यात्री कृपया ध्यान दें, yaatrii kripyaa dhyaan den, 'passengers please note'), on political रैलियाँ called यात्रा (like the famous भारत जोड़ो यात्रा), and of course in तीर्थयात्रा (tiirthyaatraa, 'pilgrimage'), since in Hindu tradition visiting a holy place is itself thought of as a sacred journey, not just a destination. This gives the word a certain weight that a casual 'trip' in English doesn't quite carry.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सफ़र (safar) as a direct swap in every context, but यात्रा (yaatraa) is the word that shows up on train tickets, travel agency signs, and formal announcements -- it carries a slightly more official or momentous feel, fitting for a pilgrimage or a long-distance trip, not just a short hop across town. 2) The common verb pairing is यात्रा करना (yaatraa karnaa, 'to travel/take a journey') -- learners sometimes try to verb यात्रा directly instead of using करना (karnaa) as the light verb.

(hamaarii yaatraa kal subah shuroo hogii।)
