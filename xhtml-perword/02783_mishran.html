मिश्रण (mishran)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'mixture' or 'blend' -- used both for physical mixtures (like a spice or snack mix) and more abstract combinations.
Sense 1 : mixture, blend
noun, masculine
Synonyms: मेल (mel), मिश्रित पदार्थ (mishrit padaarth, more technical/formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will often see मिश्रण (mishran) on snack packets -- नमकीन मिश्रण (namkiin mishran, 'savory mixture') is a common label for the mixed nuts-and-sev snack found in Delhi shops. It is also the standard word used in recipes and chemistry classes alike, making it a nicely versatile, everyday-to-technical word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse मिश्रण (mishran, 'mixture') with मिलावट (milaavat, 'adulteration'); the latter carries a negative sense of impurity or fraud, while मिश्रण (mishran) is neutral. 2) In everyday snack contexts, मिश्रण (mishran) is often used loosely for a savory namkeen mix, distinct from its more scientific/technical use in chemistry or cooking instructions.
