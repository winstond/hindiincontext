तबदीली (tabdiilii)

noun This is a variant spelling of the more common तब्दीली (tabdiilii), meaning 'change' or 'alteration'; both come from the same Persian-derived root as बदलना (badalnaa, 'to change').
Sense 1 : change, alteration
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बदलाव (badlaav), परिवर्तन (parivartan, formal), तब्दीली (tabdiilii, variant form)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly in Delhi weather chat and office small talk -- मौसम में तबदीली (mausam men tabdiilii, 'a change in the weather') or जगह की तबदीली (jagah kii tabdiilii, 'a change of place/posting'). Government and news Hindi tends to prefer the spelling तब्दीली (tabdiilii), so don't be surprised to see both forms used interchangeably in print and speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to परिवर्तन (parivartan) for every 'change', but तबदीली (tabdiilii) and its more common cousin तब्दीली (tabdiilii) are the everyday spoken choice for practical changes (job, address, weather, plans), while परिवर्तन (parivartan) leans toward formal or abstract change (social, political). 2) The word takes में (men) or की (kii) to specify what changed, e.g. मौसम में तबदीली (mausam men tabdiilii, 'a change in the weather') -- learners sometimes drop the postposition.
