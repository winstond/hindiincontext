संपादित (sampaadit)

adjective This is the passive/perfective participial adjective meaning 'edited' or 'compiled', derived from the verb संपादन करना (sampaadan karnaa, 'to edit') -- it is almost always used before or after a noun to describe an edited/compiled work, not as a standalone action verb.
Sense 1 : edited, compiled (of a book, journal, text)
adjective Typically pairs with a following करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) to form a full verb phrase: संपादित करना (sampaadit karnaa, 'to edit') and संपादित होना (sampaadit honaa, 'to be edited').
Synonyms: संकलित (sanklit, compiled), संशोधित (sanshodhit, revised/corrected)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly on the title pages of Hindi books and magazines, right next to the editor's name -- संपादक: name, संपादित पुस्तक (sampaadit pustak, 'edited book'). It has a bookish, print-media feel, so it belongs to written and formal spoken Hindi rather than casual chat.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use संपादित (sampaadit) alone as a finite verb -- it needs करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa) attached to actually function as 'edited' in a sentence, e.g. यह किताब संपादित की गई (yah kitaab sampaadit kii gaii, 'this book was edited'). 2) Confusing संपादित (sampaadit) with संपादक (sampaadak, 'editor' -- the person, not the action).
Sample Sentences:
यह पुस्तक डॉ. शर्मा द्वारा संपादित है। This book is edited by Dr. Sharma.
(yah pustak do. sharmaa dvaaraa sampaadit hai।)
