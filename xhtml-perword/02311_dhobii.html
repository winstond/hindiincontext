धोबी (dhobii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning washerman -- traditionally the person who washes and irons clothes for a living, often collecting them door to door.
Sense 1 : washerman (person who washes/irons clothes for a living)
noun, masculine
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In most Indian neighborhoods, the धोबी (dhobii) is a familiar figure who does rounds collecting and delivering ironed clothes, often piled impossibly high on a bicycle or handcart -- calling someone's shirt collar or crease 'sharp' is sometimes jokingly credited to a good धोबी (dhobii). Historically धोबी (dhobii) names a caste/community associated with washing clothes, so the word carries a social dimension beyond just 'job title,' something worth being aware of even though daily usage is mostly functional and neutral.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes treat धोबी (dhobii) as a caste name only, but in everyday speech it is used simply for 'the washerman/laundry guy,' the person you hand your clothes to, regardless of how formally caste is intended. 2) The feminine counterpart धोबिन (dhobin) (washerwoman) is a distinct word, not just an inflected form of धोबी (dhobii) -- do not expect the -ई (-ii) ending to simply switch to -इन (-in) the way some other nouns behave.
