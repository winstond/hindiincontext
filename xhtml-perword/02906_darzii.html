दरज़ी (darzii)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'tailor.'
Sense 1 : tailor
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सिलाई करने वाला (silaaii karne vaalaa, descriptive phrase, not a single-word synonym)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every neighborhood market in Delhi-NCR has its own दरज़ी (darzii), often a small shop with a sewing machine humming in the doorway, where you get blouses, kurtas, and suits stitched or altered. Taking fabric to the दरज़ी (darzii) for a wedding outfit, and the ritual of getting measurements taken and then going back two or three times for fitting, is a very familiar experience for most North Indian households.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write or say दर्जी (darjii) without the nuqta -- this is a common and widely accepted variant spelling, not an error, but दरज़ी (darzii) with the nuqta is closer to the Persian-derived pronunciation. 2) The word is masculine regardless of the tailor's actual gender in casual speech; a female tailor is still often called दरज़ी (darzii), though some speakers add महिला (mahilaa, woman) before it to specify.
