छलाँग (chhalaang)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'jump/leap,' almost always paired with the verb मारना (maarnaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa); also used figuratively for a sudden big change or advance.
Sense 1 : a physical jump or leap
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कूद (kood)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear kids on a playground shout छलाँग लगाओ! (chhalaang lagaao!, 'take a jump!') -- the word has a springy, athletic feel, distinct from the gentler, more general कूद (kood).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use छलाँग (chhalaang) as a verb by itself; it is a noun and needs a light verb like मारना (maarnaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa) to mean 'to jump' -- छलाँग मारना (chhalaang maarnaa, 'to take a jump'), not a conjugated form of छलाँग (chhalaang) itself.

(chhalaang lagaao!)

Sense 2 : a sudden big change, jump, or advance (figurative)
noun, feminine Used with मारना (maarnaa) or लगाना (lagaanaa) just like the literal sense, to describe abstract things like careers, prices, or technology advancing suddenly -- तरक्की में छलाँग (tarakkii men chhalaang, 'a leap in progress').
Synonyms: उछाल (uchhaal, often for prices/markets)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: News anchors love this word for describing markets or scientific progress: शेयर बाज़ार ने छलाँग लगाई (sheyar baazaar ne chhalaang lagaaii, 'the stock market took a leap') -- it carries the same energetic, upward feel as the physical jump, just applied metaphorically.
