हालाँकि (haalaanki)

conjunction A single-sense conjunction meaning 'although / even though', used to introduce a concessive clause.
Sense 1 : although, even though
conjunction Introduces a concessive clause, often paired later in the sentence with लेकिन (lekin) or फिर भी (phir bhii) to state the contrast, similar to 'although...but' patterns some learners overuse in English.
Synonyms: यद्यपि (yadypi, literary), भले ही (bhale hii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear हालाँकि (haalaanki) a lot in news broadcasts and considered, careful speech -- someone explaining a nuanced opinion ('हालाँकि (haalaanki) यह सच है, फिर भी...' haalaanki yah sach hai, phir bhii, 'although this is true, still...') sounds thoughtful and balanced. In casual chat among friends, लेकिन (lekin) alone often does the same job more simply.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often feel compelled to add लेकिन (lekin) or फिर भी (phir bhii) after the हालाँकि (haalaanki) clause, mirroring English 'although...but' -- in Hindi this is actually the natural and expected pattern, not a redundancy to avoid, so don't over-correct it away. 2) It is used only to open the concessive clause itself, not as a standalone answer meaning 'however' at the start of a new, unrelated sentence.
Sample Sentences:
हालाँकि बारिश हो रही थी, फिर भी वे मैच देखने गए। Although it was raining, they still went to watch the match.
(haalaanki baarish ho rahii thii, phir bhii ve maich dekhne gae।)
