सामान्य (saamaany)

adjective/adverb A common adjective meaning 'normal, ordinary, general' -- used across everyday, administrative, and academic registers. Also has a fixed-phrase use meaning 'in general.'
Sense 1 : normal, ordinary, usual
adjective
Synonyms: आम (aam), साधारण (saadhaaran), मामूली (maamoolii)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear सामान्य (saamaany) constantly in news broadcasts -- 'जनजीवन सामान्य हो गया' (janjiivan saamaany ho gayaa, 'normal life has resumed') is a stock phrase after any disruption like a strike, flood, or curfew. It carries a slightly more formal, official flavor than आम (aam) or साधारण (saadhaaran).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for साधारण (saadhaaran) in every context, but सामान्य (saamaany) is the word of choice in official, medical, and weather contexts -- 'सामान्य तापमान' (saamaany taapmaan, 'normal temperature'), 'स्थिति सामान्य है' (sthiti saamaany hai, 'the situation is normal'). 2) Do not confuse with असामान्य (asaamaany, 'abnormal') -- the अ- prefix simply negates it.

Sense 2 : general, common (as opposed to specific/special)
adjective Common in fixed institutional phrases like सामान्य ज्ञान (saamaany gyaan, 'general knowledge') and सामान्य सभा (saamaany sabhaa, 'general assembly').
Synonyms: सार्वजनिक (saarvajnik, overlaps for 'public/general'), आम (aam)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In India this sense is unavoidable on any government form: सामान्य वर्ग (saamaany varg) refers to the 'general category' of applicants, as distinct from reserved categories -- a term loaded with real social and political weight, not just a grammar note.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use विशेष (vishesh, 'special') and सामान्य (saamaany) without noticing they form a natural opposite pair, similar to English 'general vs. special' -- recognizing this pairing helps read forms, notices, and exam categories (सामान्य वर्ग (saamaany varg, 'general category') vs. विशेष वर्ग (vishesh varg, 'special category')).

Sense 3 : generally, usually
adverb Used in the fixed phrase सामान्य तौर पर (saamaany taur par, 'generally speaking') to modify a whole clause.
Synonyms: आमतौर पर (aamtaur par), सामान्यतः (saamaanytah, formal variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear सामान्य तौर पर (saamaany taur par) often in TV interviews and panel discussions, where speakers use it to hedge a broad claim before giving a specific exception -- a very Hindi rhetorical habit of qualifying generalizations.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to आमतौर पर (aamtaur par) for 'generally' and forget सामान्य तौर पर (saamaany taur par) is an equally natural, slightly more formal alternative heard in speeches and writing.
