प्रशंसा करना (prashansaa karnaa)

verb A compound expression combining the noun प्रशंसा (prashansaa, 'praise') with the light verb करना (karnaa, 'to do'), meaning 'to praise' or 'to compliment' -- a single sense with a real object relationship.
Sense 1 : to praise, to compliment
verb, transitive The person or thing being praised is marked with की (kii) as in किसी की प्रशंसा करना (kisii kii prashansaa karnaa, 'to praise someone') -- प्रशंसा (prashansaa) itself takes की (kii) the way most feminine nouns in this N + की + करना (N + kii + karnaa) frame do.
Synonyms: सराहना (saraahnaa), तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech, तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa) is what you will actually hear at the dinner table when someone compliments the cooking, while प्रशंसा करना (prashansaa karnaa) shows up more in speeches, school essays, and slightly more formal praise -- like a teacher praising a student's hard work in a report. Both are completely natural, but if you want to sound a little more polished or 'proper,' प्रशंसा (prashansaa) is your word.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say प्रशंसा करना किसी को (prashansaa karnaa kisii ko) by analogy with English 'to praise someone,' but the correct frame marks the person with की (kii), not को (ko): किसी की प्रशंसा करना (kisii kii prashansaa karnaa). 2) तारीफ़ करना (taariif karnaa) is the more everyday, spoken choice; प्रशंसा करना (prashansaa karnaa) leans a shade more formal or written, though both are widely used and understood.

(adhyaapikaa ne mere kaam kii prashansaa kii।)
