संसदीय (sansdiiy)

adjective A single-sense adjective meaning 'parliamentary,' related to संसद (sansad, 'parliament'). Common in news and civic contexts.
Sense 1 : parliamentary, relating to parliament
adjective
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this word constantly in Indian news broadcasts and newspapers -- संसदीय सत्र (sansdiiy satr, 'parliamentary session') is a staple headline phrase, especially around budget time or when debates get heated in Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha coverage. It has a formal, civic-affairs register rather than a casual conversational one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to inflect it like a variable adjective (संसदीया (sansdiiyaa), संसदीये (sansdiiye)) -- it stays संसदीय (sansdiiy) regardless of the noun's gender or number. 2) Don't confuse with संसद (sansad) itself, which is the noun 'parliament'; संसदीय (sansdiiy) is only the adjective form used to describe things related to it, like संसदीय क्षेत्र (sansdiiy kshetr, 'parliamentary constituency') or संसदीय बहस (sansdiiy bahas, 'parliamentary debate').

(sansdiiy satr agle somvaar se shuroo hogaa।)
