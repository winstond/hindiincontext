अपनाना (apnaanaa)

verb A transitive verb built on the root अपना (apnaa, 'own'), meaning to make something one's own -- to adopt, embrace, or take up (a habit, custom, technology, or even a person into one's fold).
Sense 1 : to adopt (a practice, method, technology, habit)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: ग्रहण करना (grahan karnaa, formal), स्वीकार करना (sviikaar karnaa, to accept/adopt)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in public-service messaging -- सावधानी अपनाएँ (saavdhaanii apnaaen, 'adopt caution/be careful') on health posters, or नई तकनीक अपनाना (naii takniik apnaanaa, 'adopting new technology') in news about farmers or businesses. It carries a sense of consciously making something part of your own life, not just trying it once.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for स्वीकार करना (sviikaar karnaa) or ग्रहण करना (grahan karnaa) when अपनाना (apnaanaa) is the natural, everyday choice for adopting a habit, method, or lifestyle -- the latter two sound noticeably more formal or bookish in casual speech. 2) यह तरीका अपनाओ (yah tariikaa apnaao, 'adopt this method') is a very common everyday frame; learners sometimes overtranslate 'adopt' with grahan karnaa even in casual advice-giving contexts where apnaanaa is what a native speaker would actually say.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार ने लोगों से अपील की है कि वे सावधानी अपनाएँ। The government has appealed to people to adopt caution.
(sarkaar ne logon se apiil kii hai ki ve saavdhaanii apnaaen।)

Sense 2 : to embrace/accept as one's own (a person, culture, identity, cause)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: अंगीकार करना (angiikaar karnaa, literary), गले लगाना (gale lagaanaa, figurative, 'to embrace')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This sense shows up in emotionally weighted contexts -- adopting an orphaned child, a new country accepting you, or a community welcoming an outsider: उन्होंने उसे अपनी बेटी की तरह अपनाया (unhonne use apnii betii kii tarah apnaayaa, 'they took her in as their own daughter'). The root अपना (apnaa, 'own') makes the emotional logic transparent -- you are literally making someone or something 'your own.'
Sample Sentences:
उन्होंने अनाथ बच्चे को अपनी बेटी की तरह अपनाया। They took in the orphaned child as their own daughter.
(unhonne anaath bachche ko apnii betii kii tarah apnaayaa।)
