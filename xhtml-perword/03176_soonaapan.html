सूनापन (soonaapan)

noun A noun meaning emptiness or desolation, especially the emotional kind felt when a place or life feels hollow after someone or something has left. Related to सूना (soonaa, 'empty/deserted'), of which this is the abstract noun form.
Sense 1 : emptiness, desolation (emotional or of a place)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: खालीपन (khaaliipan), वीरानी (viiraanii, more about a deserted place), उजाड़ (ujaad, desolation of a place)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word carries the particular ache of a house or a heart going quiet after someone leaves -- a mother describing the house after her children move out for college might say the house has fallen into सूनापन (soonaapan). It pairs naturally with feelings of loss (a person's absence, a festival season ending) rather than just physical bareness, which is the territory of its root adjective सूना (soonaa).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for खाली (khaalii, 'empty') as an adjective when they actually want this noun for the *feeling* of emptiness -- सूनापन (soonaapan) is the abstract state, not a description of a container or space being unfilled. 2) It is used almost as often for a feeling inside a person (loneliness after a loss) as for a literally empty place, which surprises learners who first meet सूना (soonaa) only in physical descriptions.

(bachchon ke jaane ke baad ghar men ek ajiib soonaapan chhaa gayaa।)
