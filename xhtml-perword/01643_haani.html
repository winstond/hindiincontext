हानि (haani)

noun A formal/written-register word for 'loss, damage, harm' -- covers both financial loss and physical/abstract harm. There is also a fixed idiom pairing it with लाभ (laabh, 'profit').
Sense 1 : loss, damage (financial or material)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नुकसान (nuksaan), घाटा (ghaataa, especially financial/business loss), क्षति (kshati, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in the fixed pairing लाभ-हानि (laabh-haani, 'profit and loss') -- a phrase you will see on the cover of school accountancy notebooks and hear in business news. In everyday bazaar talk, though, people almost always say नुकसान (nuksaan) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नुकसान (nuksaan) by default since it is more common in speech; हानि (haani) is not wrong but sounds more formal or written, like something from a news report or textbook rather than casual conversation. 2) Used with होना (honaa) ('to occur') or उठाना (uthaanaa) ('to bear/suffer'), not करना (karnaa) when describing loss happening to someone -- you say व्यापार में हानि हुई (vyaapaar men haani huii, 'there was a loss in the business'), not that someone 'did' the loss.
Sample Sentences:
इस सौदे में कंपनी को भारी हानि हुई। The company suffered a heavy loss in this deal.
(is saude men kampnii ko bhaarii haani huii।)

Sense 2 : harm, injury (to health, reputation, etc.)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: नुकसान (nuksaan), क्षति (kshati, literary), नुक्सान (nuksaan, variant spelling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this sense often in public-health slogans and warning labels -- धूम्रपान स्वास्थ्य के लिए हानिकारक है (dhoomrpaan svaasthy ke lie haanikaarak hai, 'smoking is harmful to health') uses the related adjective हानिकारक (haanikaarak), built directly from this noun.
Common Mistakes: 1) In sentences about health or well-being, हानि (haani) pairs naturally with पहुँचाना (pahunchaanaa, 'to cause harm to') -- e.g. सेहत को हानि पहुँचाना (sehat ko haani pahunchaanaa, 'to harm one's health') -- the person or thing affected takes को (ko), not से (se).
Sample Sentences:
धूम्रपान से स्वास्थ्य को बहुत हानि पहुँचती है। Smoking causes great harm to health.
(dhoomrpaan se svaasthy ko bahut haani pahunchtii hai।)
