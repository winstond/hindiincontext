मेज़बानी (mezbaanii)

noun A feminine noun meaning 'hosting' or 'hospitality' -- the act or role of being a host. Related to मेज़बान (mezbaan, 'host'), a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : the act of hosting; hospitality shown to guests
noun, feminine
Synonyms: अतिथि-सत्कार (atithi-satkaar, formal), आदर-सत्कार (aadar-satkaar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this word constantly around weddings, festivals, and cricket -- India 'की मेज़बानी करता है' (kii mezbaanii kartaa hai, 'hosts [an event]') a World Cup, a family 'मेज़बानी करता है' (mezbaanii kartaa hai) relatives who drop in unannounced. It carries a real sense of duty and pride: in most Indian households, being a good मेज़बान (mezbaan) is a point of family honor, not just logistics.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse मेज़बानी (mezbaanii) with मेहमान-नवाज़ी (mehmaan-navaazii) -- both mean roughly 'hospitality,' but मेज़बानी (mezbaanii) leans toward the formal act or role of hosting (often paired with करना, karnaa), while मेहमान-नवाज़ी (mehmaan-navaazii) leans toward the warmth and generosity shown to guests. 2) The verb used with it is usually करना (karnaa) ('to do the hosting') rather than देना (denaa).

(bhaarat ne 2023 men kriket varld kap kii mezbaanii kii।)
