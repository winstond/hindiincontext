अरब (arab)

noun/numeral/adjective Two distinct senses: the proper noun for 'Arabia'/'Arab' (and its adjective use), and an unrelated number word meaning 'billion' -- these two are homonyms, not related meanings.
Sense 1 : Arabia / an Arab person
noun, masculine
Synonyms: अरब देश (arab desh, 'Arab country/countries', used for the region)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll meet अरब (arab) most often in the compound अरब सागर (arab saagar, 'Arabian Sea'), the sea bordering Mumbai and Gujarat, and in phrases about अरब देश (arab desh, 'Arab countries') in news contexts (Gulf migration, oil, etc.), which are a live topic given how many Indians work in the Gulf.
Common Mistakes: 1) As a person-noun ('an Arab'), अरब (arab) is used for people from the Arab world generally; for the language, learners should reach for अरबी (arbii) instead, not अरब (arab).

Sense 2 : billion (10^9)
numeral Used in the Indian numbering system alongside लाख (laakh, hundred thousand) and करोड़ (karod, ten million); अरब (arab) is the next unit up from करोड़ (karod).
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll rarely need अरब (arab) in daily bazaar chat, but it shows up constantly in news about population, budgets, and economics -- India's population and GDP figures are routinely quoted in करोड़ (karod) and अरब (arab) rather than 'million' and 'billion,' so news-watching learners benefit from getting comfortable with this scale.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners coming from the Western system often map अरब (arab) to 'billion' correctly in value but stumble on the grouping: numbers this large are written and spoken in the 2-2-3 digit grouping (as in 1,00,00,00,000) rather than the Western 3-3-3 grouping, so saying or writing the number aloud takes practice. 2) Don't confuse अरब (arab, billion) with the homonymous अरब (arab, Arabia) -- context (numbers vs. geography) always disambiguates.

Sense 3 : Arab, Arabian
adjective Functions as an invariant modifier before a noun, e.g. अरब सागर (arab saagar, 'Arabian Sea'); in most other adjectival uses अरबी (arbii) is preferred.
Synonyms: अरबी (arbii, more common as the adjective form)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This adjectival use is narrow -- mostly locked into fixed names like अरब सागर (arab saagar); for describing something as 'Arab-style' in free conversation, learners will hear अरबी (arbii) far more often.
