निंदा करना (nindaa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the noun निंदा (nindaa, 'censure/slander') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do'); means to criticize, condemn, or speak ill of someone. Single-sense word.
Sense 1 : to criticize, condemn, denounce
verb, transitive Takes the person or thing being criticized with की (kii): किसी की निंदा करना (kisii kii nindaa karnaa, 'to criticize/speak ill of someone').
Synonyms: बुराई करना (buraaii karnaa), आलोचना करना (aalochnaa karnaa, more formal/measured criticism), बदनाम करना (badnaam karnaa, to defame/malign)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the verb you hear in news reports and political statements -- सरकार ने इस घटना की निंदा की (sarkaar ne is ghatnaa kii nindaa kii, 'the government condemned this incident') is classic formal register. In everyday gossip-ier contexts, people are more likely to say पीठ पीछे बुराई करना (piith piichhe buraaii karnaa, 'to badmouth someone behind their back') than निंदा करना (nindaa karnaa), which carries a slightly more formal, moralizing weight.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for बुरा कहना (buraa kahnaa) or गाली देना (gaalii denaa) when they mean निंदा करना (nindaa karnaa) -- but निंदा करना (nindaa karnaa) is about condemning someone's actions or character (often behind their back or in public discourse), not hurling abuse directly at them; गाली देना (gaalii denaa) is cruder, direct insult. 2) The object takes की (kii), not को (ko): किसी की निंदा करना (kisii kii nindaa karnaa) is correct, not किसी को निंदा करना.
Sample Sentences:
सरकार ने इस घटना की कड़ी निंदा की। The government strongly condemned this incident.
(sarkaar ne is ghatnaa kii kadii nindaa kii।)
