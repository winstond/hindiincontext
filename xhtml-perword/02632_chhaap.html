छाप (chhaap)

noun Covers the physical/visual sense of an imprint, mark, or stamp, and the figurative sense of a lasting impression someone or something leaves. Closely related to the verb छापना (chhaapnaa, 'to print/stamp'), from which this noun derives.
Sense 1 : lasting impression, influence, imprint on someone's mind or character
noun, feminine Common in the phrase छाप छोड़ना (chhaap chhodnaa, 'to leave an impression') and छाप पड़ना (chhaap padnaa, 'an impression to form/fall on someone').
Synonyms: प्रभाव (prabhaav, broader word for 'influence')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in reviews and obituaries: 'unhonne is kshetra mein apni chhaap chhodi' -- 'they left their mark in this field.' It's the standard way Hindi speakers talk about someone's legacy or influence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for प्रभाव (prabhaav) here, which works too, but छाप (chhaap) specifically evokes something being 'stamped' or 'imprinted' onto a person -- it has a more vivid, physical flavor even in this abstract sense. 2) The natural verb pairing is छोड़ना (chhodnaa, 'to leave') -- 'usne mujh par gahri chhaap chhodi' (he left a deep impression on me), not the more literal-sounding 'banaana'.
Sample Sentences:
उन्होंने इस क्षेत्र में अपनी छाप छोड़ी है। They have left their mark in this field.
(unhonne is kshetr men apnii chhaap chhodii hai।)

Sense 2 : impression, imprint, mark (physical)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: निशान (nishaan), मुहर (muhar, specifically a stamp/seal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of a rubber stamp officer pressing मुहर (muhar) onto a document -- the resulting mark left behind is the छाप (chhaap). It's also used for a footprint or paw-print pressed into mud or dust.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : print or printing (as in newspaper/book printing), by extension
noun, feminine
Synonyms: छपाई (chhapaaii, more common for 'the act of printing')
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This sense is less common in everyday conversation than the impression-related senses above; छपाई (chhapaaii) is what you'll actually hear when people talk about printing a book or newspaper.
Sample Sentences:
