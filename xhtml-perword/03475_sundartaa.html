सुंदरता (sundartaa)

noun A single-sense abstract noun meaning 'beauty' or 'prettiness' -- the quality named from the adjective सुंदर (sundar, 'beautiful').
Sense 1 : beauty, prettiness (the quality of being beautiful)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: सुंदरी (sundrii, means 'a beautiful woman', not the abstract quality -- narrower scope), खूबसूरती (khoobsoortii), हुस्न (husn, literary/poetic), रूप (roop, often 'physical beauty/form' specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in descriptions of nature and art -- प्रकृति की सुंदरता (prakriti kii sundartaa, 'the beauty of nature') is a stock phrase in essays, songs, and tourist brochures alike. It sits comfortably in both spoken and written registers, unlike its more literary cousin हुस्न (husn), which you will mostly meet in Urdu-flavored poetry and old film songs.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for सुंदर (sundar) with a possessive to mean 'her beauty' (e.g. literally 'her beautiful') instead of using the noun: उसकी सुंदरता (uskii sundartaa, 'her beauty') is the natural phrase. 2) खूबसूरती (khoobsoortii) is the more common everyday word in casual Delhi speech; सुंदरता (sundartaa) leans slightly more formal or written, which can make spoken use feel a touch bookish if overused.
Sample Sentences:
इस जगह की सुंदरता देखकर मैं दंग रह गया। I was stunned seeing the beauty of this place.
(is jagah kii sundartaa dekhkar main dang rah gayaa।)
