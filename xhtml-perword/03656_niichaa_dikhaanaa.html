नीचा दिखाना (niichaa dikhaanaa)

verb A compound verb idiom meaning 'to humiliate' or 'to put someone down,' literally showing someone their 'lowness' -- used when someone is deliberately made to feel inferior or defeated, often in front of others.
Sense 1 : to humiliate, to put someone down, to make someone feel inferior
verb, transitive Takes the person humiliated marked with को (ko): किसी को नीचा दिखाना (kisii ko niichaa dikhaanaa, 'to humiliate someone'). Since दिखाना (dikhaanaa) is the causative of देखना (dekhnaa), this phrase literally means 'to cause someone to see [themselves as] low.'
Synonyms: अपमानित करना (apmaanit karnaa), बेइज़्ज़त करना (beizzat karnaa), ज़लील करना (zaliil karnaa, stronger, harsher)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase comes up constantly around family competition, exams, and social status -- a mother might warn her son ज़्यादा शोहरत की चाह में दोस्तों को नीचा मत दिखाओ (zyaadaa shohrat kii chaah men doston ko niichaa mat dikhaao, 'don't put your friends down chasing more fame'). It captures a very Delhi-NCR social anxiety: being seen as 'lower' in front of relatives, neighbors, or colleagues (izzat/status culture) is a real, felt stake in everyday speech, not just a figure of speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use से (se) instead of को (ko) for the person being humiliated -- the target always takes को (ko), not से (se). 2) Because दिखाना (dikhaanaa) is transitive, this compound takes ने (ne) in the past with the subject: उसने मुझे नीचा दिखाया (usne mujhe niichaa dikhaayaa, 'he/she humiliated me') -- learners sometimes forget ने (ne) here since नीचा (niichaa) looks like the 'main' word.

(vah hameshaa doosron ko niichaa dikhaane kii koshish kartaa hai।)
