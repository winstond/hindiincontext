संक्षेप में (sankshep men)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'in short, briefly' -- used to summarize or wrap up a point. Built from संक्षेप (sankshep, 'brevity/summary') plus the postposition में (men, 'in').
Sense 1 : in short, to summarize
adverb Typically used as a sentence-initial discourse marker introducing a summary or conclusion of what was just said.
Synonyms: छोटे में (chhote men, colloquial), मुख्य बात यह है कि (mukhy baat yah hai ki, phrase variant), निष्कर्ष रूप में (nishkarsh roop men, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot when someone is about to wrap up a long story or explanation, much like an English speaker saying 'long story short' or 'bottom line'. It has a slightly more formal, considered feel than a casual 'बस' (bas) at the end of a rant -- you might hear it from a teacher summarizing a lesson or a news anchor closing a segment.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop में (men) and just say संक्षेप (sankshep) on its own, which sounds incomplete -- the phrase needs the postposition to work as an adverb. 2) It is easy to confuse with जल्दी में (jaldii men, 'in a hurry') because both use में, but संक्षेप में (sankshep men) is about brevity of speech, not speed.
Sample Sentences:
संक्षेप में, हमें कल तक यह प्रोजेक्ट पूरा करना है। In short, we have to finish this project by tomorrow.
(sankshep men, hamen kal tak yah projekt pooraa karnaa hai।)
