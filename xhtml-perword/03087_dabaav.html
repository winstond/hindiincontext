दबाव (dabaav)

noun A single core meaning of 'pressure', used both physically (as in tyre or blood pressure) and figuratively (as in mental/social/political pressure).
Sense 1 : pressure (mental, social, political)
noun, masculine Frequently used with डालना (daalnaa) 'to put/exert' as in दबाव डालना (dabaav daalnaa, 'to put pressure on someone') and with में होना (men honaa) as in दबाव में होना (dabaav men honaa, 'to be under pressure').
Synonyms: तनाव (tanaav, overlaps for mental strain), दबाव डालना (dabaav daalnaa, 'to put pressure', the common verb phrase)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear constantly around exam season and family expectations -- माता-पिता का दबाव (maataa-pitaa kaa dabaav, 'parental pressure') is a very real and very discussed part of growing up in India, whether it is about marks, marriage, or career choices. Politicians and news anchors also use it heavily: सरकार पर दबाव (sarkaar par dabaav, 'pressure on the government') is a staple phrase in news broadcasts.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse दबाव (dabaav) with तनाव (tanaav) -- दबाव (dabaav is pressure exerted on you, often from outside (family, work, deadlines), while तनाव (tanaav) is the resulting internal stress/tension. 2) The common verb pairing is दबाव डालना (dabaav daalnaa) 'to exert pressure' (with डालना (daalnaa), not लगाना (lagaanaa) or करना (karnaa)), which learners often get wrong.

Sense 2 : pressure (physical)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: प्रेशर (preshar, common in casual/technical speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word on medical charts and in the doctor's office -- रक्तचाप (raktchaap) is the more technical/compound term for 'blood pressure' but दबाव (dabaav) alone is what most people say colloquially, as in दबाव बढ़ गया है (dabaav badh gayaa hai, 'the pressure has gone up').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for the English loanword प्रेशर (preshar) exclusively; दबाव (dabaav) is the standard word in written and formal Hindi for physical pressure, such as टायर का दबाव (taayar kaa dabaav, 'tire pressure') or रक्त का दबाव (rakt kaa dabaav, 'blood pressure').
