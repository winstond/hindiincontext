यथार्थ (yathaarth)

noun/adjective A somewhat formal/literary word covering 'reality, truth' as a noun and 'real, actual' as an adjective; you will meet it more in essays, news commentary, and thoughtful conversation than in casual chat.
Sense 1 : reality, actuality
noun, masculine
Synonyms: सच्चाई (sachchaaii), वास्तविकता (vaastviktaa), हकीकत (hakiikat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word is a favorite in Hindi literary criticism -- you will hear terms like यथार्थवाद (yathaarthvaad, 'realism') when discussing novels or films that depict life 'as it really is,' contrasted with आदर्शवाद (aadarshvaad, 'idealism'). It carries an intellectual, slightly bookish weight that हकीकत (hakiikat) does not.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for यथार्थ (yathaarth) in everyday talk where वास्तविकता (vaastviktaa) or हकीकत (hakiikat) would sound far more natural; यथार्थ (yathaarth) tends to show up in writing, philosophy, or literary criticism rather than casual speech. 2) It is masculine, so pair it with masculine agreement, e.g. यथार्थ यही है (yathaarth yahii hai, 'this is the reality') -- not feminine agreement, which is a common slip since सच्चाई (sachchaaii) and हकीकत (hakiikat) are both feminine.

Sense 2 : real, actual, true to life
adjective
Synonyms: वास्तविक (vaastvik), असली (aslii)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet this adjective most often in phrases like यथार्थ चित्रण (yathaarth chitran, 'realistic depiction') when someone praises a book or film for showing life honestly, without sugar-coating -- a common compliment in Hindi film and literature reviews.
Common Mistakes: 1) As an adjective it is invariant -- it does not change form for gender or number the way असली (aslii) does not either, but learners sometimes mistakenly try to inflect it like a regular -आ adjective (e.g. treating it like कड़ा kadaa).
