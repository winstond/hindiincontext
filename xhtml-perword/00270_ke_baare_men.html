के बारे में (ke baare men)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'about' or 'regarding' something; used constantly to introduce the topic of speech, thought, or writing.
Sense 1 : about, regarding, concerning
postposition Attaches to the oblique form of the preceding noun/pronoun: उसके बारे में (uske baare men, 'about him/her'), मेरे बारे में (mere baare men, 'about me').
Synonyms: विषय में (vishay men, formal), संबंध में (sambandh men, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Literally, बारे (baare) comes from a Persian-derived word related to 'matter' or 'affair', so के बारे में (ke baare men) reads almost like 'in the matter of' -- a useful mental image for why it takes की/के-style possessive marking on whatever precedes it. You will hear this phrase constantly in everyday questions like आप किसके बारे में बात कर रहे हैं? (aap kiske baare men baat kar rahe hain?, 'who are you talking about?').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget to put the preceding noun or pronoun into its oblique form before के बारे में (ke baare men) -- it is लड़के के बारे में (ladke ke baare men, 'about the boy'), not लड़का के बारे में (ladkaa ke baare men). 2) With pronouns, the possessive-oblique form is used, so it is मेरे बारे में (mere baare men) and उसके बारे में (uske baare men), not मैं के बारे में (main ke baare men) or वह के बारे में (vah ke baare men).
