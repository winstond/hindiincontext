नसलवाद (nasalvaad)

noun A single-sense borrowing-plus-native-suffix noun meaning 'racism, racialism' -- the ideology or system of prejudice based on race, not a single act of prejudice.
Sense 1 : racism (the ideology/system, not a single act)
noun, masculine Formed from नसल (nasal, 'race, breed') plus the abstract-ideology suffix -वाद (-vaad), the same pattern seen in साम्यवाद (saamyvaad, 'communism') and राष्ट्रवाद (raashtrvaad, 'nationalism').
Synonyms: जातिवाद (jaativaad, often used loosely for both caste and race prejudice), रंगभेद (rangbhed, specifically color-based/apartheid-style discrimination)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word shows up mainly in news broadcasts, op-eds, and classroom discussions of global politics (American, South African, or European racism) rather than in everyday household chat, since India's own internal discrimination talk is more often channeled through जातिवाद (jaativaad, casteism) or साम्प्रदायिकता (saamprdaayiktaa, communalism). Recognizing this word helps you follow serious news Hindi even before you're fluent in casual speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for जातिवाद (jaativaad) to mean 'racism,' but जातिवाद (jaativaad) usually points at caste-based prejudice within Indian society, while नसलवाद (nasalvaad) is the word reached for when discussing race in the international or foreign-context sense (e.g. discrimination against Black or Asian people abroad). 2) It is an abstract mass noun, so it is not pluralized or used with a counter the way an English speaker might expect when talking about 'racist incidents.'
