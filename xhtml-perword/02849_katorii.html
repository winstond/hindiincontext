कटोरी (katorii)

noun A small bowl, the kind used for serving individual portions of curry, dal, or chutney alongside a main plate. Diminutive of कटोरा (katoraa), the larger bowl.
Sense 1 : small bowl
noun, feminine
Synonyms: प्याली (pyaalii, used more for tea/liquids), कटोरा (katoraa, larger size)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a typical Indian thali (thaalii), the main plate is surrounded by several small कटोरी (katorii) bowls holding dal, sabzi, raita, and chutney -- so the word is deeply tied to how meals are physically laid out and served in North Indian homes and restaurants.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use कटोरी (katorii) and कटोरा (katoraa) interchangeably, but कटोरी (katorii) is specifically the smaller, individual-portion size -- think of the little steel bowls in a thali (thaalii).
