भेद (bhed)

noun Mainly a noun meaning 'secret' or 'difference/distinction'; also has a verbal-noun-adjacent role in a few fixed phrases meaning 'division, discrimination.' The two core noun senses (secret vs. difference) share a root idea of 'that which separates or is kept apart' but are used quite differently in daily speech.
Sense 1 : secret
noun, masculine
Synonyms: राज़ (raaz), रहस्य (rahasy, slightly more formal/literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the भेद (bhed) of भेदभाव (bhedbhaav, 'discrimination') and गुप्तचर (guptchar, 'spy') stories -- it carries a flavor of something guarded and consequential, the kind of secret a general keeps or a friend swears never to tell. आपसी भेद (aapsii bhed, 'mutual secret/rift') often shows up when two people who were once close now hide something from each other.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for राज़ (raaz) for 'secret' -- भेद (bhed) is equally common, especially in the set phrase भेद खोलना (bhed kholnaa, 'to reveal a secret') and भेद लेना (bhed lenaa, 'to find out a secret'). 2) भेद (bhed) skews slightly more toward secrets that involve strategy, trust, or a hidden inner matter (a person's true nature, a plan) rather than a casual bit of gossip.
Sample Sentences:
यह हमारे बीच का भेद है, किसी और को मत बताना। This is a secret between us, don't tell anyone else.
(yah hamaare biich kaa bhed hai, kisii aur ko mat bataanaa।)

Sense 2 : difference, distinction
noun, masculine Commonly appears as भेद करना (bhed karnaa, 'to distinguish/discriminate') and in the compound भेदभाव (bhedbhaav, 'discrimination, differential treatment').
Synonyms: फ़र्क़ (farq), अंतर (antar)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will most often meet this sense inside जाति-भेद (jaati-bhed, 'caste distinction') or लिंग-भेद (ling-bhed, 'gender distinction') in discussions of social inequality -- the word carries a slight weight of 'a distinction that divides people,' not just a neutral difference in size or color.
Common Mistakes: 1) In everyday chat about differences between two things (sizes, opinions, prices), फ़र्क़ (farq) or अंतर (antar) is far more natural than भेद (bhed) -- भेद (bhed) in this sense leans formal/written or shows up specifically inside भेदभाव (bhedbhaav) type constructions about unequal or biased treatment between people or groups.

समाज में जाति-भेद आज भी गहरा है। Caste distinction is still deep in society today.
(samaaj men jaati-bhed aaj bhii gahraa hai।)
