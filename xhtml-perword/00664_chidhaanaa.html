चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa)

verb A transitive verb meaning 'to tease' or 'to annoy/irritate someone deliberately' -- it is the causative counterpart of चिढ़ना (chidhnaa, 'to get irritated/annoyed').
Sense 1 : to tease (playfully)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सताना (sataanaa, can mean tease or torment more broadly), खिझाना (khijhaanaa, less common variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the classic sibling-and-schoolyard verb -- kids चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) each other over nicknames, crushes, or losing a game, often in a singsong tone. It sits alongside चिढ़ना (chidhnaa) ('to get irritated') as its causative partner: चिढ़ना (chidhnaa) is what happens to the victim, चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) is what the teaser does to cause it.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सताना (sataanaa) for all teasing, but चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) specifically evokes provoking a reaction (annoyance, embarrassment) rather than general troubling or harassing. 2) The person being teased takes को (ko): उसे चिढ़ाना (use chidhaanaa, 'to tease him/her') -- do not mark them with से (se).

(bachche use uske naam ko lekar chidhaate hain।)

Sense 2 : to annoy, provoke, irritate (deliberately)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: तंग करना (tang karnaa), खिझाना (khijhaanaa, less common variant)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Same verb, harder edge: a neighbor deliberately blasting music to चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) the family next door, or a kid needling a sibling until they lose their temper, both use this word -- the line between 'playful tease' and 'deliberately provoke' is drawn by tone and relationship, not by a different verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense carries a sharper edge than the playful-tease sense -- context (tone, relationship, situation) tells the learner whether चिढ़ाना (chidhaanaa) here means harmless fun or genuine provocation meant to upset someone.
