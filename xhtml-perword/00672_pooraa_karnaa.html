पूरा करना (pooraa karnaa)

verb A compound verb built from the adjective पूरा (pooraa, 'full/complete') plus करना (karnaa, 'to do') -- the everyday way to say 'to complete, fulfill, or finish' something. It has one core sense with several natural English translations depending on the object (a task, a promise, a wish, a deadline).
Sense 1 : to complete, finish, fulfill
verb, transitive Takes a direct object marking what is completed: काम पूरा करना (kaam pooraa karnaa, 'to finish the work'), वादा पूरा करना (vaadaa pooraa karnaa, 'to keep a promise'), सपना पूरा करना (sapnaa pooraa karnaa, 'to fulfill a dream').
Synonyms: समाप्त करना (samaapt karnaa, more formal, esp. for finishing a task or period), निभाना (nibhaanaa, for fulfilling a promise or duty), खत्म करना (khatm karnaa, everyday, esp. for finishing work)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the verb every Hindi-speaking parent, boss, or friend uses to talk about getting things done -- काम पूरा करो (kaam pooraa karo, 'finish the work') is a staple household and office phrase. It pairs naturally with abstract nouns too: ज़िम्मेदारी पूरी करना (zimmedaarii poorii karnaa, 'to fulfill a responsibility') and ख्वाहिश पूरी करना (khvaahish poorii karnaa, 'to fulfill a wish') carry real emotional weight in conversation, especially around family duty and aspiration.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget that पूरा (pooraa) agrees in gender/number with the object being completed, since it functions here as an adjective inside the compound: काम पूरा करना (kaam pooraa karnaa) but बात पूरी करना (baat poorii karnaa). 2) Don't confuse this with पूरा होना (pooraa honaa), the intransitive counterpart meaning 'to be completed' or 'to come true' -- सपना पूरा हुआ (sapnaa pooraa huaa, 'the dream came true') describes the dream itself completing, while सपना पूरा करना (sapnaa pooraa karnaa) is someone actively making it happen.
Sample Sentences:

(kaam pooraa karo, phir khelne jaanaa।)
