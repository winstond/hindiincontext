दस्तावेज़ (dastaavez)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'document' -- the formal paper-based kind (legal, official, or administrative), not a general 'piece of writing.'
Sense 1 : document (official/legal paper)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कागज़ात (kaagzaat, papers collectively), पत्र (patr, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear दस्तावेज़ (dastaavez) constantly in Indian bureaucratic life -- at a bank, a registrar's office, or when renting a flat, someone will ask ज़रूरी दस्तावेज़ (zaroorii dastaavez, 'necessary documents') like आधार कार्ड (aadhaar kaard) or पैन कार्ड (pain kaard). It carries the same slightly formal, official weight as English 'document' versus casual 'paper.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use दस्तावेज़ (dastaavez) for any piece of writing (an essay, a letter, a text message) -- it is reserved for formal/official papers like id proofs, deeds, certificates, and contracts. 2) The gender is masculine even though it ends in a consonant sound with a nuqta -- learners sometimes guess feminine by analogy with other ए-sound loanwords.
