इज़हार करना (izahaar karnaa)

verb A compound verb combining the noun इज़हार (izahaar, 'expression/declaration', from Persian-Arabic) with करना (karnaa, 'to do'); mainly used for expressing feelings, especially love, or formally declaring something. Closely related to the standalone noun इज़हार (izahaar).
Sense 1 : to express or declare feelings (especially love)
verb, transitive Commonly takes प्यार का इज़हार करना (pyaar kaa izahaar karnaa, 'to express love') or एहसास का इज़हार करना (ehsaas kaa izahaar karnaa, 'to express feelings') with a का-marked object naming what is being expressed.
Synonyms: प्रकट करना (prakat karnaa), जताना (jataanaa), बयान करना (bayaan karnaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is THE phrase for a romantic confession in Hindi -- 'इज़हार-ए-इश्क़ (izahaar-e-ishq)' or simply 'इज़हार करना (izahaar karnaa)' is what a shy boy finally works up the courage to do. It carries a slightly filmy, dramatic flavor, evoking Bollywood scenes of confessing love, so it feels a notch more emotionally loaded than plain कहना (kahnaa, 'to say').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say प्यार इज़हार करना (pyaar izahaar karnaa) instead of the correct प्यार का इज़हार करना (pyaar kaa izahaar karnaa) -- the thing being expressed needs का (kaa) before इज़हार (izahaar). 2) This phrase is heavily associated with romantic confession; using it for everyday statements of opinion can sound overly dramatic where जताना (jataanaa) or कहना (kahnaa) would be more natural.

Sense 2 : to formally declare or state (an opinion, intention)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: घोषित करना (ghoshit karnaa, formal), व्यक्त करना (vyakt karnaa, formal)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: Outside of romance, this sense shows up in more formal or written contexts -- a politician might do विचार व्यक्त (vichaar vyakt) or इज़हार (izahaar) of an opinion in a speech or interview, giving the phrase a slightly official, deliberate weight compared to casual speech.
