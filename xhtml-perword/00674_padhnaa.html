पढ़ना (padhnaa)

verb A core everyday verb covering both 'to read' and 'to study,' plus an extended sense for reciting/chanting. Closely related to the causative पढ़ाना (padhaanaa, 'to teach'), which is a separate lemma.
Sense 1 : to read
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is one of the first verbs every learner meets, right alongside लिखना (likhnaa, 'to write') -- the two are taught as a pair in every Hindi classroom, much like they are in English.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for देखना (dekhnaa) when they mean 'read,' but देखना (dekhnaa) is only for looking/watching -- पढ़ना (padhnaa) is the verb for reading text. 2) When talking about reading something out loud to someone, the person read to often takes को (ko): माँ बच्चे को कहानी पढ़ती है (maan bachche ko kahaanii padhtii hai, 'the mother reads the story to the child').
Sample Sentences:
मैं हर रोज़ अख़बार पढ़ता हूँ। I read the newspaper every day.
(main har roz akhbaar padhtaa hoon।)

Sense 2 : to study
verb, intransitive Used intransitively and without an object when talking about being enrolled in school/studying generally: वह दिल्ली में पढ़ता है (vah dillii men padhtaa hai, 'he studies in Delhi').
Synonyms: पढ़ाई करना (padhaaii karnaa, compound-verb phrase)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Ask any Indian parent and they'll say पढ़ो! (padho!, 'study!') at least once a day -- this verb is the anchor of the whole 'study hard, get good marks' family conversation that looms large in Indian households.
Common Mistakes: 1) 'What do you study?' or 'What class are you in?' both use पढ़ना (padhnaa) -- तुम क्या पढ़ते हो? (tum kyaa padhte ho?, 'what do you study?') is a very common question to ask a student, more natural than asking about their 'subject' directly. 2) Don't confuse this with सीखना (siikhnaa, 'to learn') -- पढ़ना (padhnaa) is the act of studying/going to school, while सीखना (siikhnaa) is about acquiring a skill or knowledge, often outside formal schooling.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 3 : to recite, chant (prayers, verses)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: जपना (japnaa, for mantras/repeated chanting)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in a devotional context, e.g. नमाज़ पढ़ना (namaaz padhnaa, 'to offer/recite namaz') or मंत्र पढ़ना (mantr padhnaa, 'to recite a mantra') -- here पढ़ना (padhnaa) means reciting from memory or ritual text rather than 'reading' in the everyday sense.
Sample Sentences:
वह रोज़ पाँच बार नमाज़ पढ़ता है। He offers namaz five times a day.
