माथा (maathaa)

noun Means 'forehead' -- both the literal body part and the figurative site of worry, frowning, and fate in common idioms.
Sense 1 : forehead (body part)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ललाट (lalaat, literary), भाल (bhaal, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the spot where a tilak (tilak) or bindi (bindii) is applied, so माथा (maathaa) carries a small ceremonial weight beyond plain anatomy -- think of an elder pressing kumkum (kumkum) onto a child's माथा (maathaa) before an exam or a journey.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for सिर (sir) when they mean specifically the forehead; सिर (sir) is the whole head, माथा (maathaa) is just the front-top part where a tikaa (tilak) or bindi (bindii) goes.
Sample Sentences:
उसके माथे पर पसीने की बूँदें चमक रही थीं। Drops of sweat were glistening on his forehead.
(uske maathe par pasiine kii boonden chamak rahii thiin।)

Sense 2 : to worry/rack one's brains (in idiom माथा खपाना)
noun, masculine Occurs mainly inside the fixed idiom माथा खपाना (maathaa khapaanaa, 'to rack one's brains') and माथापच्ची (maathaapachchii, 'a headache of an effort') -- listed here as the idiomatic sense of the noun rather than as a separate compound verb.
Synonyms: दिमाग़ (dimaagh, used in a parallel idiom दिमाग़ खपाना)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this in everyday complaints like इस काम में बहुत माथा खपाना पड़ा (is kaam men bahut maathaa khapaanaa padaa, 'I had to really rack my brains over this work') -- it paints thinking hard as something that wears down the very forehead itself.
Common Mistakes: 1) माथा खपाना (maathaa khapaanaa) is about mental effort or frustration over a problem, not physical pain in the forehead -- do not confuse it with सिरदर्द (sirdard, 'headache').

(is samasyaa ko suljhaane men mujhe bahut maathaa khapaanaa padaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

(kyon apnaa maathaa khapaa rahe ho, vah khud hii sab samajh jaaegaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : fate/destiny written on the forehead (as in idiom माथे पर लिखा होना)
noun, masculine Appears in the idiom माथे पर लिखा होना (maathe par likhaa honaa, 'to be written on one's forehead/fate') -- the oblique माथे (maathe) form is standard here, covered further in the morphology table.
Synonyms: भाग्य (bhaagy), किस्मत (kismat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi shares with much of North Indian folk belief the image that one's fate is literally inscribed on the माथा (maathaa) by a divine hand -- so जो माथे पर लिखा है वही होगा (jo maathe par likhaa hai vahii hogaa, 'whatever is written on the forehead will happen') is a common fatalistic saying, often heard from grandparents consoling someone after a setback.
Sample Sentences:
