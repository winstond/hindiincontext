व्यक्ति (vyakti)

noun A single core meaning: 'person, individual' -- a neutral, somewhat formal way to refer to a human being, common in writing and careful speech.
Sense 1 : person, individual
noun, masculine
Synonyms: आदमी (aadmii), इंसान (insaan), शख़्स (shakhs, somewhat formal/Urdu-flavored)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will meet व्यक्ति (vyakti) constantly on forms and in news broadcasts -- 'एक व्यक्ति की मृत्यु हो गई' (ek vyakti kii mrityu ho gaii, 'one person died') is classic news-report phrasing. It carries a slightly more formal, almost bureaucratic neutrality compared to the warmer, everyday आदमी (aadmii) or इंसान (insaan), the latter of which also carries a humanistic 'human being' undertone (as in इंसानियत (insaaniyat), 'humanity').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for आदमी (aadmii) in every context, but व्यक्ति (vyakti) is the word you want in official, written, or careful-speech contexts (forms, news, formal descriptions) -- आदमी (aadmii) sounds more casual and everyday. 2) व्यक्ति (vyakti) is grammatically masculine even when referring to a woman -- यह व्यक्ति (yah vyakti, 'this person') stays masculine in agreement regardless of the actual person's gender.
Sample Sentences:
एक व्यक्ति की सड़क दुर्घटना में मृत्यु हो गई। One person died in a road accident.
(ek vyakti kii sadak durghatnaa men mrityu ho gaii।)
