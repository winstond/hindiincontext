पड़ाव (padaav)

noun A noun with a concrete sense (a stopping place / camp / halt on a journey) that extends naturally into a figurative sense (a stage or phase in a longer process, like life or a career).
Sense 1 : a stopping place, halt, or camp during a journey
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ठिकाना (thikaanaa), विश्राम स्थल (vishraam sthal, formal), मुकाम (mukaam)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Think of old trading caravans or pilgrim groups on foot, stopping each evening at a village or riverbank to rest -- that resting point is a पड़ाव (padaav). The word still carries that flavor of a physical, temporary halt on the way to somewhere else.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पड़ाव (padaav) with यात्रा (yaatraa, journey) itself -- पड़ाव (padaav) is specifically the place or point where a journey is paused, not the journey as a whole.

यात्री शाम होते ही एक गाँव में पड़ाव डालते हैं। The travelers make a halt in a village as soon as evening falls.
(yaatrii shaam hote hii ek gaanv men padaav daalte hain।)

Sense 2 : a stage or phase in a longer process, journey of life, or career
noun, masculine Often used with जीवन (jiivan, life), करियर (kariyar, career), or सफर (saphar, journey) to mark a significant phase reached -- जीवन का एक पड़ाव (jiivan kaa ek padaav, 'a stage of life').
Synonyms: चरण (charan), मोड़ (mod, turning point), मुकाम (mukaam)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This figurative use leans on the same image as the literal one: life itself is imagined as a यात्रा (yaatraa, journey), and a पड़ाव (padaav) is a meaningful point where you pause and look back before moving on -- a graduation, a new job, a marriage.
