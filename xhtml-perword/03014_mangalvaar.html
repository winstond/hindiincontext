मंगलवार (mangalvaar)

noun The day name Tuesday, formed from मंगल (mangal, the planet Mars/the god Mars) plus वार (vaar, 'day'). One straightforward sense.
Sense 1 : Tuesday
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: मंगल (mangal) is both the planet Mars and the god associated with it, so मंगलवार (mangalvaar) literally means 'Mars-day' -- the same logic English uses with Tuesday going back to the Norse god Tiw. Many Hindus consider मंगलवार (mangalvaar) auspicious for visiting a Hanuman temple, since Tuesday is traditionally linked to Hanuman (hanumaan) worship in North India.
Common Mistakes: 1) Day names like मंगलवार (mangalvaar) take को (ko) when marking 'on Tuesday' -- मंगलवार को (mangalvaar ko, 'on Tuesday') -- rather than any other postposition a learner might reach for by analogy with English 'on'. 2) Learners sometimes try to make it agree or inflect like an adjective; it stays the same form regardless of what surrounds it, aside from the regular oblique-for-postposition pattern shared by all masculine nouns of this shape.
