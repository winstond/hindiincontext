संबंधित (sambandhit)

adjective A formal adjective meaning 'related' or 'concerned' -- most often met in official, administrative, or written contexts (e.g., 'the concerned department', 'the related matter') rather than casual chat.
Sense 1 : related, connected (to something)
adjective Takes से (se) to mark what something is related to, e.g. इस मुद्दे से संबंधित (is mudde se sambandhit, 'related to this issue').
Synonyms: जुड़ा हुआ (judaa huaa), संबद्ध (sambaddh, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see this word constantly on forms, notices, and news reports -- it belongs to the same formal register as शासकीय (shaaskiiy) or प्रशासनिक (prashaasnik) language. In daily conversation, most Delhi speakers would just say से जुड़ा (se judaa) instead.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use के साथ (ke saath) instead of से (se) to mark the thing something is related to -- संबंधित (sambandhit) pairs with से (se), not के साथ (ke saath). 2) It is easy to overuse this word where a simpler, everyday phrase like से जुड़ा (se judaa) would sound more natural in casual speech.

(yah muddaa shikshaa se sambandhit hai।)

Sense 2 : concerned, the relevant (person/authority/department)
adjective Common in bureaucratic phrases like संबंधित अधिकारी (sambandhit adhikaarii, 'the concerned officer') or संबंधित विभाग (sambandhit vibhaag, 'the relevant department'), where it points to whichever person or office is applicable in context rather than describing a relationship to something specific.
Synonyms: प्रासंगिक (praasangik, formal), विषय से जुड़ा (vishay se judaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the classic 'kindly contact the concerned department' word -- you will meet it on office notices, in government letters, and in news bulletins constantly. It rarely appears in casual talk between friends.
