कमाना (kamaanaa)

verb A transitive verb centered on earning or making money through work, with a common extended sense of earning something intangible like a reputation, name, or credit.
Sense 1 : to earn (money, a living)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: अर्जित करना (arjit karnaa, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly in everyday talk about work and family life: कौन कमाता है? (kaun kamaataa hai, 'who earns [in the family]?') is a very ordinary question in India, since household income is often discussed openly among relatives and neighbors in a way that might feel more private elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say पैसे बनाना (paise banaanaa) as a direct calque of 'make money' -- कमाना (kamaanaa) is the natural verb Hindi speakers use for earning through work. 2) रोज़ी कमाना (rozii kamaanaa, 'to earn a livelihood') and पैसा कमाना (paisaa kamaanaa, 'to earn money') are both extremely common collocations worth learning as chunks.
Sample Sentences:
मेरे पिता जी हर महीने अच्छे पैसे कमाते हैं। My father earns good money every month.
(mere pitaa jii har mahiine achchhe paise kamaate hain।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : to earn (reputation, name, credit -- figurative)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: हासिल करना (haasil karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: बदनामी कमाना (badnaamii kamaanaa, 'to earn a bad name') is a set phrase you will hear in scoldings and gossip alike -- it treats reputation, like money, as something you accumulate through your actions.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense pairs with abstract nouns like नाम (naam, 'name/fame'), बदनामी (badnaamii, 'bad reputation'), or इज़्ज़त (izzat, 'respect') -- learners sometimes reach for बनाना (banaanaa) here, but कमाना (kamaanaa) is idiomatic when the thing gained is the result of one's effort or conduct, good or bad.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपनी बेईमानी से पूरे इलाके में बदनामी कमाई। He earned a bad name throughout the neighborhood through his dishonesty.
(usne apnii beiimaanii se poore ilaake men badnaamii kamaaii।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
अपनी मेहनत और ईमानदारी से उसने बहुत नाम कमाया है। He has earned a great name through his hard work and honesty.
(apnii mehnat aur iimaandaarii se usne bahut naam kamaayaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
ऐसी हरकतों से तुम बस बदनामी कमाओगे, कुछ और नहीं। With such behavior you will only earn a bad name, nothing else.
(aisii harakton se tum bas badnaamii kamaaoge, kuchh aur nahiin।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
अच्छा काम करो, नाम कमाओ, बदनामी नहीं। Do good work, earn a good name, not a bad one.
(achchhaa kaam karo, naam kamaao, badnaamii nahiin।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
अगर वह इतनी बेइज्जती करता रहता, तो और भी बदनामी कमाता। If he had kept insulting people like that, he would have earned even more of a bad name.
(agar vah itnii beijjtii kartaa rahtaa, to aur bhii badnaamii kamaataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Counterfactual -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
समाज सेवा करते-करते उसने लोगों के बीच बड़ा सम्मान कमा लिया। Through his social service, he earned great respect among the people.
(samaaj sevaa karte-karte usne logon ke biich badaa sammaan kamaa liyaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
झूठ बोलने की आदत के कारण उसकी बदनामी कमाई जा रही है। Because of his habit of lying, a bad reputation is being earned for him.
(jhooth bolne kii aadat ke kaaran uskii badnaamii kamaaii jaa rahii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Continuous -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Passive
काश वह ऐसी बदनामी न कमाता। If only he hadn't earned such a bad name.
(kaash vah aisii badnaamii n kamaataa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Aspect: Habitual -- Voice: Active
शायद उसकी ईमानदारी से घर का नाम फिर से कमाया जाए। Perhaps through his honesty the family's good name may be earned back again.
(shaayad uskii iimaandaarii se ghar kaa naam phir se kamaayaa jaae।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Subjunctive -- Voice: Passive
अपनी करनी से इतनी बदनामी मत कमाओ कि घर वालों को भी शर्म आए। Don't earn such a bad name through your actions that even your family feels ashamed.
(apnii karnii se itnii badnaamii mat kamaao ki ghar vaalon ko bhii sharm aae।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
