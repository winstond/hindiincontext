सीधे (siidhe)

adverb The adverbial form of सीधा (siidhaa, 'straight/direct'), used to mean 'straight, directly' or 'straightaway, right away'.
Sense 1 : directly, straight (in direction or path)
adverb
Synonyms: सीधा (siidhaa, variant form used adverbially too), बिना रुके (binaa ruke, without stopping)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: If you ask for directions in Delhi, you'll constantly hear सीधे जाओ (siidhe jaao, 'go straight'), often paired with hand gestures. It's one of the first practical phrases any learner picks up for getting around the city.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to use सीधा (siidhaa) and सीधे (siidhe) interchangeably in all contexts; while both can work adverbially in casual speech, सीधे (siidhe) is the safer, more standard adverbial choice, especially before a verb.

Sense 2 : directly, without intermediary (figurative: skipping steps or people)
adverb Commonly used with से (se) to mark the source being bypassed, as in सीधे बॉस से बात करना (siidhe bos se baat karnaa, 'to speak directly to the boss') -- skipping any middle step or person.
Synonyms: सीधा (siidhaa, variant form used adverbially too)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This sense shows up a lot in office and bureaucratic talk -- सीधे शिकायत करो (siidhe shikaayat karo, 'complain directly') meaning skip the usual chain of people and go straight to the source. It reflects a very practical, no-nonsense way of framing directness in Hindi.

Sense 3 : immediately, straightaway, at once
adverb
Synonyms: फ़ौरन (fauran), तुरंत (turant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: A parent telling a kid सीधे घर आना (siidhe ghar aanaa, 'come straight home') is a very familiar household instruction -- it carries a slight scolding undertone, implying 'don't dawdle or wander off.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for तुरंत (turant) exclusively for 'immediately' and miss that सीधे (siidhe) is equally natural here, especially in constructions like सीधे घर जाओ (siidhe ghar jaao, 'go straight home') where it implies both directness and immediacy at once.
