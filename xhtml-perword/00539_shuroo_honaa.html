शुरू होना (shuroo honaa)

verb The intransitive partner of शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa, 'to start something'): शुरू होना (shuroo honaa) means 'to begin/start' on its own, with the subject as the thing that starts rather than an agent starting it.
Sense 1 : to begin, to start (of an event, process, etc.)
verb, intransitive शुरू (shuroo) is a Persian-origin invariant word paired with होना (honaa); the whole functions as a single intransitive verbal unit, so agreement is with the subject of होना (honaa) alone.
Synonyms: आरंभ होना (aarambh honaa, formal), शुरू हो जाना (shuroo ho jaanaa, emphasizes onset)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around schedules and events in daily life -- क्लास शुरू हो गई (klaas shuroo ho gaii, 'class has started'), बारिश शुरू हो गई (baarish shuroo ho gaii, 'the rain has started') -- often with जाना (jaanaa) added (शुरू हो जाना) to sharpen the sense of a sudden or completed onset, much like English 'started up' versus plain 'started'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use शुरू करना (shuroo karnaa) when the subject is the thing starting rather than an agent starting it -- e.g. 'the movie starts' is फ़िल्म शुरू होती है (film shuroo hotii hai, 'the movie starts'), not फ़िल्म शुरू करती है (film shuroo kartii hai), which wrongly implies the movie is doing the starting to something else. 2) Because होना (honaa) is intransitive, this verb never takes ने (ne) marking on its subject, even in the perfective -- फ़िल्म शुरू हुई (film shuroo huii, 'the movie started'), not फ़िल्म ने शुरू हुई (film ne shuroo huii).
Sample Sentences:

(philm aath baje shuroo hotii hai।)
