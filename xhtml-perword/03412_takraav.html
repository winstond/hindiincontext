टकराव (takraav)

noun A noun meaning 'collision' or 'clash' -- used both literally (vehicles, objects) and figuratively (conflict between people, groups, or ideas). Related to the verb टकराना (takraanaa, 'to collide/clash').
Sense 1 : conflict, clash between people/groups/interests
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संघर्ष (sangharsh), विवाद (vivaad, dispute, more about disagreement than confrontation), झगड़ा (jhagdaa, colloquial, more personal/petty quarrel)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in political and news contexts -- दोनों पार्टियों में टकराव (donon paartiyon men takraav, 'clash between the two parties') or सीधा टकराव (siidhaa takraav, 'direct confrontation'). It carries a sense of head-on opposition, echoing its root verb टकराना (takraanaa, 'to strike against').
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for झगड़ा (jhagdaa) when they mean a larger, more serious conflict -- टकराव (takraav) fits political, ideological, or interest-based clashes (सीमा पर टकराव (siimaa par takraav, 'border clash')) better than a domestic spat.

(donon paartiyon men takraav badhtaa jaa rahaa hai।)

Sense 2 : physical collision
noun, masculine
Synonyms: भिड़ंत (bhidant), दुर्घटना (durghatnaa, accident, broader meaning)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly in traffic news -- गाड़ियों में टकराव (gaadiyon men takraav, 'collision between vehicles') is standard headline language on Delhi radio and TV.
