विद्या (vidyaa)

noun A respected, somewhat formal word for 'knowledge' or 'learning' -- covers both the abstract idea of scholarship and specific fields of learning or skill.
Sense 1 : knowledge, learning (formal/scholarly sense)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: ज्ञान (gyaan), शिक्षा (shikshaa, more about formal schooling)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: विद्या (vidyaa) carries a reverent tone in Hindi -- it's the word behind Saraswati's epithet विद्या की देवी (vidyaa kii devii, 'goddess of learning'), and you'll see it in school names and mottos across India. It feels a notch more formal and 'weighty' than everyday words for study or school.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for ज्ञान (gyaan) as a default translation of 'knowledge'; विद्या (vidyaa) leans more toward learning acquired through study or schooling, while ज्ञान (gyaan) can be broader, including wisdom or insight gained from life or spirituality. 2) विद्या (vidyaa) is grammatically feminine, so verbs and adjectives agreeing with it take feminine forms -- e.g. विद्या अच्छी है (vidyaa achchhii hai, 'learning/education is good'), not the masculine form.

Sense 2 : a field of learning, science, or specialized skill/art (often in compounds)
noun, feminine Common as the second element of compound words naming specific disciplines or skills, e.g. गणित विद्या (ganit vidyaa, 'mathematics'), राजनीति विद्या (raajniiti vidyaa, 'political science').
Synonyms: शास्त्र (shaastr, used similarly in compounds for a discipline)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind words like ज्योतिष विद्या (jyotish vidyaa, 'astrology') or जादू विद्या (jaadoo vidyaa, 'magic/sorcery') -- विद्या (vidyaa) here signals a whole organized body of skill or lore, almost like '-ology' or '-craft' in English.
