के बीच में (ke biich men)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'between' or 'in the middle of', built from बीच (biich, 'middle') framed by के (ke) and में (men).
Sense 1 : between (two things)
postposition Takes two nouns joined by और (aur, 'and') or a plural/set noun in the oblique case before it: 'X और Y के बीच में' (X aur Y ke biich men, 'between X and Y').
Synonyms: के मध्य (ke madhy, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly in directions and family conversations, like चाय की मेज़ के बीच में रख दो (chaay kii mez ke biich men rakh do, 'put it in the middle of the tea table'). It pairs naturally with के आस-पास (ke aas-paas, 'around') and के सामने (ke saamne, 'in front of') as part of a family of location postpositions.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop में (men) and just say के बीच (ke biich) -- both are correct and common, but के बीच में (ke biich men) is the fuller, slightly more colloquial-emphatic form. 2) The noun before के बीच में (ke biich men) must be in the oblique case, e.g. घर के बीच में (ghar ke biich men, 'in the middle of the house'), not the direct form.

(kitaab do kursiyon ke biich men rakhii hai।)

Sense 2 : in the midst of, during (a situation or event)
postposition Used figuratively with events or situations rather than physical objects: बातचीत के बीच में (baatchiit ke biich men, 'in the middle of the conversation').
Synonyms: के दौरान (ke dauraan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase behind the common complaint बीच में मत बोलो (biich men mat bolo, 'don't interrupt') -- a line every Indian kid has heard at the dinner table when cutting into adults' conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for के दौरान (ke dauraan) for every 'during' context, but के बीच में (ke biich men) is preferred when something interrupts or happens partway through an ongoing action, like किसी के बीच में बोलना (kisii ke biich men bolnaa, 'to speak in between/interrupt someone').

(miiting ke biich men uskaa phon aa gayaa।)
