तबादला (tabaadlaa)

noun A single core meaning: an official transfer -- of a job posting, employee, or (less commonly) of property or funds.
Sense 1 : transfer (of a job posting or employee)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: स्थानांतरण (sthaanaantran, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word comes up constantly in the context of Indian government and bank jobs, where transfers to new cities are a routine (and often dreaded) part of a career -- you will hear families discuss उसका तबादला हो गया (uskaa tabaadlaa ho gayaa, 'his transfer came through') the way other families might discuss a job change entirely. It is a Persian-origin word fully naturalized into everyday Hindi-Urdu office speech.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for स्थानांतरण (sthaanaantran) by default because it looks 'dictionary correct,' but तबादला (tabaadlaa) is what people actually say at the office or in casual conversation about a posting change. 2) The verb पाना is often used with it in the passive-ish sense of 'getting transferred' -- तबादला हो जाना (tabaadlaa ho jaanaa, 'to get transferred') is the natural phrasing, not a direct transitive verb built on तबादला itself.
Sample Sentences:
पापा का तबादला दिल्ली से लखनऊ हो गया है। Dad's transfer from Delhi to Lucknow has come through.
(paapaa kaa tabaadlaa dillii se lakhnaoo ho gayaa hai।)
