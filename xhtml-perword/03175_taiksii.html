टैक्सी (taiksii)

noun A borrowed word from English 'taxi', used exactly as in English for the hired car; only one everyday sense.
Sense 1 : taxi, cab
noun, feminine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: टैक्सी (taiksii) sits alongside ऑटो (oto, auto-rickshaw) as the two go-to words for hired transport in Delhi-NCR; these days it also covers app-based rides, so लोग अक्सर कहते हैं 'टैक्सी बुक करो' (taiksii buk karo, 'book a taxi') for calling an Ola or Uber, not just a street-hailed cab.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to make this agree like a native feminine noun ending in ई (ii) with unusual plural forms -- it stays टैक्सियाँ (taiksiyaan) in the plural, which is regular, but the point of confusion is usually the postposition: one takes a taxi with से (se) -- मैं टैक्सी से आया (main taiksii se aayaa, 'I came by taxi') -- not में (men) as English 'in a taxi' might suggest, though में (men) is also heard when emphasizing being inside the vehicle.

(mainne ophis ke lie taiksii buk kii।)
