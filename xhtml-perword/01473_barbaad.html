बरबाद (barbaad)

adjective An adjective meaning 'ruined' or 'wasted' -- used for wrecked lives, plans, money, health, or relationships. Also spelled बर्बाद (barbaad); both forms are common.
Sense 1 : ruined, destroyed, wasted
adjective Almost always used with होना (honaa) 'to become ruined' or करना (karnaa) 'to ruin (something/someone)', e.g. ज़िंदगी बरबाद हो गई (zindgii barbaad ho gaii, 'the life got ruined').
Synonyms: तबाह (tabaah), नष्ट (nasht, more formal), खराब (kharaab, broader meaning, not just ruin)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This word shows up constantly in Bollywood song lyrics and dialogues about heartbreak and lost love -- 'ज़िंदगी बरबाद हो गई' (zindgii barbaad ho gaii, 'my life is ruined') is the classic dramatic complaint. It's also common in everyday talk about wasted money, wasted time, or a ruined exam/interview, so it's not purely melodramatic -- you'll hear parents scold kids with तुमने अपना भविष्य बरबाद कर लिया (tumne apnaa bhavishy barbaad kar liyaa, 'you have ruined your own future') too.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use it for minor damage or a small mistake, but बरबाद (barbaad) carries a strong, often dramatic sense of total ruin -- for small mishaps, खराब (kharaab) is the natural choice instead. 2) Note the pairing verbs: बरबाद होना (barbaad honaa) for something becoming ruined on its own, versus बरबाद करना (barbaad karnaa) for someone actively ruining it.
