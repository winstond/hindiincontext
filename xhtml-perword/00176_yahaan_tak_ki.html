यहाँ तक कि (yahaan tak ki)

conjunction A fixed connective meaning 'even' or 'to the extent that,' used to introduce an extreme or surprising example. Built from यहाँ (yahaan, 'here') + तक (tak, 'up to') + कि (ki, 'that'), literally 'up to here that.'
Sense 1 : even (introducing an extreme example)
conjunction Introduces a clause or phrase that represents the most extreme point on a scale, often to emphasize surprise: 'X, यहाँ तक कि (yahaan tak ki) Y' meaning 'X, even to the extent of Y.'
Synonyms: यहां तक कि (yahaan tak ki, variant spelling without chandrabindu)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this a lot in storytelling and complaining -- someone listing how bad or surprising something got: 'उसने सब कुछ बेच दिया, यहाँ तक कि अपना घर भी' (usne sab kuchh bech diyaa, yahaan tak ki apnaa ghar bhii, 'he sold everything, even his own house'). It has a slightly more formal, narrative feel than just piling on भी (bhii) after every noun.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often confuse this with भी (bhii, 'also/even'); भी attaches directly to a single word to mean 'even that one', while यहाँ तक कि (yahaan tak ki) introduces a whole clause or phrase to show how far a situation extends. 2) It typically comes at the start of the extreme example, not right after the word being emphasized, unlike भी (bhii) which sits right after its target.
