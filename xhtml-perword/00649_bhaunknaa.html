भौंकना (bhaunknaa)

verb A single-sense verb meaning 'to bark' (of a dog), with a common figurative extension used for a person who talks nonsense, shouts rudely, or rants -- both senses are covered below.
Sense 1 : to bark (of a dog)
verb, intransitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian neighborhood has its resident stray dogs, and कुत्ते भौंक रहे हैं (kutte bhaunk rahe hain, 'the dogs are barking') is a stock line in stories about a restless night. The word itself has a slightly harsh, guttural sound in Hindi that mirrors the noise it describes.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use this only for dogs and miss the very common figurative sense below -- in real speech the figurative use is at least as frequent as the literal one. 2) The subject that barks is marked with normal nominative case, not से (se) or को (ko) -- कुत्ता भौंक रहा है (kuttaa bhaunk rahaa hai, 'the dog is barking'), no extra postposition needed.
Sample Sentences:

(raat ko kutte bahut bhaunk rahe the।)

Sense 2 : to rant, talk nonsense or rudely (figurative, about a person)
verb, intransitive Used dismissively about a person's speech, often in the imperative or in rhetorical questions to shut someone down, e.g. भौंकना बंद कर (bhaunknaa band kar, 'stop yapping').
Synonyms: बकबक करना (bakbak karnaa, to yap/chatter), बड़बड़ाना (badabdaanaa, to mutter/grumble)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Comparing a person's talk to a dog's bark is just as insulting in Hindi as in English, maybe more so -- you'll hear it in heated arguments or from an annoyed elder telling someone to shut up: क्या भौंक रहा है? (kyaa bhaunk rahaa hai?, 'what are you yapping about?'). It's a word you'll understand long before you should use it yourself.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a rude, insulting way to describe someone's talking -- do not use it about a person you actually respect, even jokingly, unless you're very sure of the relationship. 2) It's typically used to dismiss what someone says as nonsense or noise, not just to mean 'talk loudly.'
Sample Sentences:
