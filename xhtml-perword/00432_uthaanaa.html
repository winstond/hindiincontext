उठाना (uthaanaa)

verb The causative of उठना (uthnaa, 'to rise/get up'): the core sense is 'to lift, pick up, raise' something, with several natural extensions -- taking on a burden or expense, waking someone, and raising a topic or one's voice.
Sense 1 : to lift, pick up
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उठा लेना (uthaa lenaa, compound-verb variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the everyday physical sense you will hear constantly -- सामान उठाना (saamaan uthaanaa, 'to pick up luggage') at a train station, or a mother telling a kid बच्चे को उठाओ (bachche ko uthaao, 'pick up the child'). Think of उठाना (uthaanaa) as 'make [something] rise' -- the causative logic of उठना (uthnaa) is baked right into the meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often forget this is the transitive partner of उठना (uthnaa) and try to use उठना (uthnaa) when there is a direct object -- e.g. 'pick up the bag' needs उठाना (uthaanaa), not उठना (uthnaa). 2) With the perfective, ने (ne) is required since this is a true transitive verb: उसने बैग उठाया (usne baig uthaayaa, 'he picked up the bag'), not उसने बैग उठाया (vah baig uthaayaa).
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : to bear, take on (burden, expense, responsibility)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: वहन करना (vahan karnaa, formal), भुगतना (bhugatnaa, to bear consequences)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Very common in practical contexts: ज़िम्मेदारी उठाना (zimmedaarii uthaanaa, 'to take on responsibility') or नुकसान उठाना (nuksaan uthaanaa, 'to bear a loss'). The image is the same as lifting a physical weight -- you are metaphorically hoisting a burden onto your own shoulders.
Common Mistakes: 1) खर्च उठाना (kharch uthaanaa) means 'to bear the expense,' which learners sometimes confuse with 'to raise the expense' (i.e. increase it) -- the meaning here is about shouldering a cost, not increasing it.

Sense 3 : to wake someone up
verb, transitive
Synonyms: जगाना (jagaanaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this from parents every morning: मुझे सात बजे उठा देना (mujhe saat baje uthaa denaa, 'wake me up at seven'). It carries the same 'make rise' logic as the physical sense -- getting someone out of bed is, quite literally, making them get up.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes default to जगाना (jagaanaa) only, not realizing उठाना (uthaanaa) is an equally common everyday alternative, especially when talking about physically getting someone out of bed rather than just ending their sleep.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे सुबह सात बजे उठा देना। Wake me up at seven in the morning.
(mujhe subah saat baje uthaa denaa।)

Sense 4 : to raise (a topic, voice, question, issue)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: उछालना (uchhaalnaa, to raise dramatically/provocatively)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense behind common civic and political language, like आवाज़ उठाना (aavaaz uthaanaa, 'to raise one's voice/protest') heard often in news and activism contexts. It extends the physical 'lift' meaning into the abstract: bringing a topic 'up' into the conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) मुद्दा उठाना (muddaa uthaanaa, 'to raise an issue') and आवाज़ उठाना (aavaaz uthaanaa, 'to raise one's voice,' often figuratively meaning 'to protest') are fixed collocations -- learners should learn them as set phrases rather than building them word by word.
Sample Sentences:
