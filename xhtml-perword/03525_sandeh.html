संदेह (sandeh)

noun A formal noun meaning 'doubt' or 'suspicion' -- one core sense, used mainly in slightly elevated or written registers compared to its everyday counterpart शक (shak).
Sense 1 : doubt, suspicion
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शक (shak), शंका (shankaa, literary), संशय (sanshay, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will meet संदेह (sandeh) constantly in news reports, formal writing, and courtroom or police drama dialogue -- 'पुलिस को संदेह है' (pulis ko sandeh hai, 'the police suspect') is a classic news-anchor line. In daily bazaar or family chatter, though, लोग शक (shak) ही बोलते हैं -- so think of संदेह (sandeh) as the word's formal-register sibling.
Common Mistakes: 1) संदेह (sandeh) pairs with the postposition पर (par) or में (men) depending on the frame -- संदेह होना (sandeh honaa) typically takes पर (par) for the person/thing doubted (e.g., 'उस पर संदेह है'), which learners sometimes replace with के बारे में (ke baare men) by over-translating 'about'. 2) In casual speech, native speakers usually reach for शक (shak) rather than संदेह (sandeh); using संदेह (sandeh) in a very casual chat can sound a touch bookish or formal.
Sample Sentences:
पुलिस को हत्यारे की पहचान पर संदेह है। The police have doubts about the murderer's identity.
(pulis ko hatyaare kii pahchaan par sandeh hai।)
