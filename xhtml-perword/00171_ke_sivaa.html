के सिवा (ke sivaa)

postposition A compound postposition meaning 'except, besides' -- used after a noun/pronoun in the oblique case to exclude something. Also spelled के सिवाय (ke sivaay), which is at least as common; both forms are used interchangeably.
Sense 1 : except, apart from
postposition Takes the oblique form of the preceding noun/pronoun, as with other compound postpositions ending in के (ke).
Synonyms: के सिवाय (ke sivaay, variant form), के अलावा (ke alaavaa), के बिना (ke binaa, meaning scope: 'without' rather than 'except')
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this often in slightly exasperated or emphatic statements like इसके सिवा कोई चारा नहीं (iske sivaa koii chaaraa nahiin, 'there is no option except this') -- it has a bit more rhetorical weight than के अलावा (ke alaavaa), which feels more neutral and administrative.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for के अलावा (ke alaavaa) by default since it is taught first; के सिवा (ke sivaa) is equally natural and slightly more emphatic in tone, often pairing with कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin, 'nothing') or कोई नहीं (koii nahiin, 'no one') to mean 'nothing/no one except.' 2) Do not confuse with के बिना (ke binaa, 'without'), which excludes by absence rather than by exception from a set.
