निहायत (nihaayat)

adverb An intensifier meaning 'extremely' or 'utterly', used before adjectives to strengthen them; single sense.
Sense 1 : extremely, utterly
adverb Placed directly before the adjective it intensifies, e.g. निहायत बेवकूफ़ (nihaayat bevkoof, 'utterly foolish').
Synonyms: बहुत (bahut), अत्यंत (atyant), बेहद (behad), निपट (nipat, rare)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This word comes from Persian/Urdu and has a slightly literary, formal flavor compared to everyday बहुत (bahut) or बेहद (behad); you will hear it more from an older, educated speaker or in writing than in casual chai-time chatter, often when someone is complaining about how extremely something is the case.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use निहायत (nihaayat) with verbs, but it only intensifies adjectives (and occasionally adverbs), not actions themselves. 2) It tends to carry a slightly negative or critical shade in modern speech, e.g. निहायत बदतमीज़ (nihaayat badatmiiz, 'utterly rude'), so using it with a strongly positive adjective can sound odd or bookish to native ears.
