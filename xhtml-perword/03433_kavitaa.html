कविता (kavitaa)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'poem' or 'poetry' -- the word a schoolchild uses for a class recitation piece just as much as a literary critic uses it for verse in general.
Sense 1 : poem / poetry
noun, feminine
Synonyms: कविता (kavitaa), पद्य (pady, literary, more technical term for verse), शायरी (shaayrii, usually Urdu-style poetry, especially ghazals)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Hindi-medium schoolkid in Delhi-NCR has stood up in class to do कविता सुनाना (kavitaa sunaanaa), reciting a poem from the Hindi textbook -- the word has a warm, slightly nostalgic schoolroom association for most Indians. It contrasts with शायरी (shaayrii), which evokes mehfils, Urdu couplets, and often love or longing rather than the classroom.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for शायरी (shaayrii) as a direct synonym, but it carries a specific flavor of Urdu-style romantic/ghazal poetry, while कविता (kavitaa) is the neutral, general word used in schools and everyday speech for any poem. 2) कविता लिखना (kavitaa likhnaa, 'to write a poem') and कविता सुनाना (kavitaa sunaanaa, 'to recite a poem') are the two verbs learners need most; कविता पढ़ना (kavitaa padhnaa) covers both 'reading' and, loosely, 'reciting' depending on context.
