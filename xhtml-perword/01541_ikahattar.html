इकहत्तर (ikahattar)

numeral The cardinal number 71 (English: 'seventy-one'). A single-sense number word.
Sense 1 : the number seventy-one
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numbers from 21 to 99 are each their own memorized word rather than a predictable formula, which is why native speakers often just default to English digits for anything beyond basic counting in fast conversation -- don't be surprised to hear 'seventy-one' dropped into an otherwise Hindi sentence about ages or prices.
Common Mistakes: 1) Like all Hindi numbers 21-99, इकहत्तर (ikahattar) must simply be memorized as its own unit rather than built compositionally from 'one' and 'seventy' -- Hindi numerals in this range do not follow a predictable tens-plus-units pattern the way English does. 2) Learners sometimes confuse it with इकसठ (iksath, sixty-one) or इक्यासी (ikyaasii, eighty-one) since all three start with the 'इक' prefix marking '...-one'; the तर (tar) ending here is specific to the seventies.

मेरे दादाजी की उम्र इकहत्तर साल है। My grandfather's age is seventy-one.
(mere daadaajii kii umr ikahattar saal hai।)
