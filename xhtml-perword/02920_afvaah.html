अफ़वाह (afvaah)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'rumor' -- gossip or unverified news circulating among people.
Sense 1 : rumor, hearsay
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गप (gap, colloquial), किंवदंती (kinvdantii, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear अफ़वाह फैल गई (afvaah phail gaii, 'the rumor spread') constantly around elections, cricket matches, and neighborhood gossip -- Hindi treats a rumor as something that spreads on its own, almost like fire or a disease, which fits the cultural sense that rumors take on a life of their own once released.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often pair it with the wrong verb -- अफ़वाह (afvaah) most naturally goes with फैलना (phailnaa, 'to spread') when intransitive or फैलाना (phailaanaa) when someone spreads it, rather than verbs like 'to say' or 'to tell' that English speakers reach for. 2) The set phrase is अफ़वाह है कि (afvaah hai ki, 'the rumor is that...') -- learners sometimes try to build this with a relative clause structure instead of the simple है कि (hai ki) frame.
Sample Sentences:
बाज़ार में अफ़वाह फैल गई कि बैंक बंद हो जाएगा। A rumor spread in the market that the bank would shut down.
(baazaar men afvaah phail gaii ki baink band ho jaaegaa।)
