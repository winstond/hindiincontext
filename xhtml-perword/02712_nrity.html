नृत्य (nrity)

noun A formal/classical word for 'dance' as a noun; it also has a less common verb-phrase use in the compound नृत्य करना (nrity karnaa) meaning 'to dance.' The everyday spoken word for dancing is नाचना (naachnaa).
Sense 1 : dance (as an art form or performance)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नाच (naach, everyday/colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll see in the names of classical dance forms, like भारतनाट्यम (bhaaratnaatyam) or कथक नृत्य (kathak nrity), and on invitations to cultural programs -- it carries a formal, respectful, almost Sanskritic weight. In daily conversation, though, Delhi families are far more likely to say नाचना (naachnaa) or बच्चे नाच रहे हैं (bachche naach rahe hain, 'the kids are dancing') than to reach for नृत्य (nrity).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this word in casual speech where नाच (naach) or the verb नाचना (naachnaa) would sound more natural -- नृत्य (nrity) is the word you'd see on a concert poster or hear in a formal announcement, not the word you'd use to say 'let's dance' at a party. 2) नृत्य (nrity) itself is not a verb; to say 'to dance' using this root, you need the compound नृत्य करना (nrity karnaa, 'to do dance'), not a direct conjugation of नृत्य (nrity).
