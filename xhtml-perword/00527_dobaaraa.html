दोबारा (dobaaraa)

adverb A single-sense adverb meaning 'again, once more, a second time'; used constantly to talk about repeating an action.
Sense 1 : again, once more
adverb
Synonyms: फिर से (phir se), फिर (phir), पुनः (punah, literary)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear दोबारा (dobaaraa) constantly in everyday requests and complaints -- a shopkeeper saying दोबारा आइएगा (dobaaraa aaiegaa, 'do come again') or a frustrated parent saying दोबारा मत करना (dobaaraa mat karnaa, 'don't do that again'). It literally breaks down as दो (do, 'two') plus बार (baar, 'time'), so it carries a built-in sense of 'a second time' that फिर (phir) doesn't always convey as sharply.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for फिर (phir) alone where दोबारा (dobaaraa) sounds more natural when emphasizing 'a second/repeated attempt,' e.g. दोबारा कोशिश करो (dobaaraa koshish karo, 'try again') versus the vaguer फिर करो (phir karo, 'do it again/later'). 2) दोबारा (dobaaraa) is invariant -- learners sometimes try to inflect it like an adjective, but it never changes form.

(kripyaa dobaaraa aaiegaa।)
