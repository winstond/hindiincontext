थाना (thaanaa)

noun Most commonly a masculine noun meaning 'police station' -- an essential everyday civic-life word in India.
Sense 1 : police station
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पुलिस स्टेशन (pulis steshan, common English loanword form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In India, each थाना (thaanaa) has jurisdiction over a specific area, and people often refer to 'their' local थाना (thaanaa) the way Americans might say 'precinct.' You'll hear एफ़आईआर (efaaiiaar, 'FIR') mentioned constantly alongside थाना (thaanaa), since that's where a first information report gets filed to start a police case.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse थाना (thaanaa) with जेल (jel, 'jail') -- a थाना (thaanaa) is the local police post where reports are filed and cases registered, not where people are imprisoned. 2) The common phrase थाने में रिपोर्ट लिखाना (thaane men riport likhaanaa, 'to file a report at the police station') uses में (men), not पर (par) or को (ko), to mark location.
