पृष्ठ (prishth)

noun A formal, Sanskrit-origin word for a 'page' of a book or document; it also has a literary/technical sense meaning 'back' or 'surface,' seen mainly in compound words.
Sense 1 : page (of a book, document, or website)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: पन्ना (pannaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will see पृष्ठ (prishth) printed at the bottom of nearly every Hindi book, textbook, and government form -- it is the standard written register word for 'page,' while पन्ना (pannaa) is what you would actually say if asking someone to turn to a page in conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often default to पन्ना (pannaa) in speech and are surprised to see पृष्ठ (prishth) used instead in books, forms, and formal writing -- both mean 'page,' but पृष्ठ (prishth) is the one printed at the bottom of a book page, not the one people say out loud. 2) In the sense of a webpage, learners may not realize वेब पृष्ठ (veb prishth, 'web page') is the formal equivalent of the commonly heard English loanword 'पेज' (pej).
Sample Sentences:
कृपया पुस्तक का पृष्ठ संख्या दस खोलें। Please open page number ten of the book.
(kripyaa pustak kaa prishth sankhyaa das kholen।)

Sense 2 : back, surface (literary/technical)
noun, masculine Mostly encountered inside compounds like पृष्ठभूमि (prishthbhoomi, 'background') rather than used alone in this sense.
Synonyms: पीठ (piith)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This older sense of 'back/surface' survives mainly inside compound words -- पृष्ठभूमि (prishthbhoomi) literally means 'the ground at the back,' which is why it is the standard word for 'background,' both literally and figuratively (as in someone's family background).
Sample Sentences:
इस घटना की पृष्ठभूमि को समझना ज़रूरी है। It is necessary to understand the background of this event.
(is ghatnaa kii prishthbhoomi ko samajhnaa zaroorii hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
उसका पारिवारिक पृष्ठभूमि बहुत साधारण है। His family background is very ordinary.
(uskaa paarivaarik prishthbhoomi bahut saadhaaran hai।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
पहाड़ पृथ्वी के पृष्ठ पर उभरे हुए भाग हैं। Mountains are raised portions on the surface of the earth.
