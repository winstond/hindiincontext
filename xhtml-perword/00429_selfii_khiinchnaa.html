सेल्फ़ी खींचना (selfii khiinchnaa)

verb A modern compound expression meaning 'to take a selfie', combining the English loanword सेल्फ़ी (selfii) with the verb खींचना (khiinchnaa, 'to pull/draw'), which is the everyday Hindi verb for taking a photo.
Sense 1 : to take a selfie
verb, transitive
Synonyms: सेल्फ़ी लेना (selfii lenaa, equally common variant)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This phrase rides on an older, well-worn pattern: फ़ोटो खींचना (foto khiinchnaa, 'to take a photo') has long used खींचना (khiinchnaa) to evoke 'pulling' an image out of the camera, likely from old camera shutters or film being drawn. सेल्फ़ी खींचना (selfii khiinchnaa) simply slots the English loanword सेल्फ़ी into that native frame, a common pattern in urban Hindi where a new noun rides on an old verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सेल्फ़ी बनाना (selfii banaanaa), which sounds off; खींचना (khiinchnaa) or लेना (lenaa) are the natural choices, mirroring how फ़ोटो खींचना (foto khiinchnaa) works for photos generally. 2) The नुक़्ता on फ़ (selfii) is often dropped in casual writing (सेल्फी) without changing meaning or pronunciation for most speakers.
