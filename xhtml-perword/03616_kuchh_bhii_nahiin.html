कुछ भी नहीं (kuchh bhii nahiin)

pronoun An emphatic negation meaning 'absolutely nothing' or 'nothing at all' -- built from कुछ (kuchh, 'something/anything') plus भी (bhii, 'even/also') plus नहीं (nahiin, 'not'), stacking the emphatic भी (bhii) onto the base indefinite to push it into total negation.
Sense 1 : nothing at all, absolutely nothing
pronoun Functions as a negative-polarity pronoun standing for the object/complement of the verb; the verb itself still carries a separate नहीं if the sentence structure requires it, but often this phrase alone carries the whole negation, e.g. उसने कुछ भी नहीं खाया (usne kuchh bhii nahiin khaayaa, 'he/she ate absolutely nothing').
Synonyms: कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin, less emphatic), ज़रा भी नहीं (zaraa bhii nahiin, 'not even a little')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the go-to phrase when someone wants to underline total emptiness or total refusal -- a parent asking क्या हुआ? (kyaa huaa, 'what happened?') and getting कुछ भी नहीं (kuchh bhii nahiin, 'nothing at all') as a flat, slightly evasive answer is a very familiar exchange in Hindi-speaking households. It pairs with the same भी (bhii) emphatic pattern seen in कोई भी नहीं (koii bhii nahiin, 'no one at all'), so once you know this phrase you can build the parallel one for people.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often drop the भी (bhii) and just say कुछ नहीं (kuchh nahiin), which is fine for plain 'nothing' but loses the emphatic 'nothing at all/whatsoever' force this phrase carries. 2) Word order matters: भी (bhii) sits right after कुछ (kuchh), not after नहीं (nahiin) -- कुछ नहीं भी (kuchh nahiin bhii) is not correct.
