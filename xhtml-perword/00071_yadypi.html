यद्यपि (yadypi)

conjunction A formal, literary conjunction meaning 'although' -- it sets up a contrast that is usually finished off with a correlative word like तथापि (tathaapi) or फिर भी (phir bhii).
Sense 1 : although, even though
conjunction Typically pairs with a correlative like तथापि (tathaapi) or फिर भी (phir bhii) later in the sentence to complete the 'although...still' structure, though the correlative can be dropped in casual writing.
Synonyms: हालाँकि (haalaanki), यद्यपि तो (yadypi to, rare variant)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This word has a distinctly bookish, Sanskritic flavor -- you will meet it in newspaper editorials, school essays, and formal speeches far more than in kitchen or market conversation. Its everyday cousin हालाँकि (haalaanki) does the same job in ordinary speech, so that is the word to reach for when talking, while यद्यपि (yadypi) is worth recognizing for reading comprehension and formal writing.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often use this in spoken conversation where हालाँकि (haalaanki) would sound far more natural -- यद्यपि (yadypi) belongs to formal writing, essays, and speeches, not everyday chat. 2) Forgetting the correlative पर (par), फिर भी (phir bhii), or तथापि (tathaapi) later in the sentence can leave the contrast feeling unfinished, since native writers usually signal 'but still' explicitly.
Sample Sentences:
यद्यपि वर्षा हो रही थी, फिर भी वह यात्रा पर निकल पड़ा। Although it was raining, he still set out on his journey.
(yadypi varshaa ho rahii thii, phir bhii vah yaatraa par nikal padaa।)
