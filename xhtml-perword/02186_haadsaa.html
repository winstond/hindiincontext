हादसा (haadsaa)

noun A single-sense everyday noun meaning 'accident' or 'mishap' -- most often a road accident or sudden tragic event; borrowed from Persian/Urdu and extremely common in spoken and news Hindi.
Sense 1 : accident, mishap, tragic incident
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दुर्घटना (durghatnaa), हादिसा (haadisaa, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear हादसा (haadsaa) constantly on Hindi news channels -- सड़क हादसा (sadak haadsaa, 'road accident') is a stock phrase reporters use almost every day. It carries a slightly more dramatic, human-tragedy flavor than the drier, bureaucratic दुर्घटना (durghatnaa), which you will find more in official reports and school textbooks.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach only for दुर्घटना (durghatnaa) from textbooks, but हादसा (haadsaa) is what people actually say in conversation and on the news for car crashes, fires, and other sudden mishaps. 2) हादसा (haadsaa) can stretch to mean any sudden misfortune (a fall, a fire, even a personal tragedy), not just vehicle collisions, so do not restrict it mentally to 'road accident' the way दुर्घटना (durghatnaa) often gets used in formal writing.
Sample Sentences:
कल रात एक बड़ा सड़क हादसा हुआ। A big road accident happened last night.
(kal raat ek badaa sadak haadsaa huaa।)
