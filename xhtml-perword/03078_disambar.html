दिसंबर (disambar)

noun The month of December in the standard Gregorian calendar as used in everyday Hindi speech.
Sense 1 : the month of December
noun, masculine
Synonyms: दिसम्बर (disambar, variant spelling)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Like all twelve month names in everyday Hindi, दिसंबर (disambar) is simply the English word adapted to Devanagari spelling and pronunciation -- there's no separate traditional Hindi name in common use, since the Gregorian calendar runs daily life, business, and school years across India. The older Vikram Samvat month names (like पौष, paush, which roughly overlaps with December) survive mainly in religious and astrological contexts, not casual conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to decline it like a native Hindi noun, but month names borrowed from English stay invariant across cases -- only the following postposition changes, e.g. दिसंबर में (disambar men, 'in December'), दिसंबर तक (disambar tak, 'until December').
