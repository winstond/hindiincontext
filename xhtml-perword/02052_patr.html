पत्र (patr)

noun Mainly a formal/written word: (1) a letter (written correspondence), and (2) a document/certificate, plus a few compound-based extended uses like newspaper (पत्र patr as short for समाचार पत्र samaachaar patr) and leaf in botanical/literary compounds. The everyday spoken word for 'letter' is चिट्ठी (chitthii); पत्र (patr) is its more formal register-mate.
Sense 1 : letter (written correspondence)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: चिट्ठी (chitthii), खत (khat)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: पत्र (patr) is the word you meet on government forms, school circulars, and news headlines about someone 'writing a letter to the editor' (संपादक के नाम पत्र, sampaadak ke naam patr). In daily chat about writing to a friend or family member, most Delhi speakers still say चिट्ठी (chitthii) or the more modern मेल (mel) / मैसेज (maisej).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach only for चिट्ठी (chitthii) in speech; पत्र (patr) is what you'll see on forms, in office contexts, and in compounds like प्रेम पत्र (prem patr, 'love letter') or निमंत्रण पत्र (nimantran patr, 'invitation letter'), so recognizing it in writing matters even if you say चिट्ठी (chitthii) out loud.

Sense 2 : official document, certificate
noun, masculine Almost always appears as the second half of a compound naming a specific document, e.g. प्रमाण पत्र (pramaan patr, 'certificate'), पहचान पत्र (pahchaan patr, 'ID card'), राशन पत्र (raashan patr / राशन कार्ड, 'ration card').
Synonyms: दस्तावेज़ (dastaavez), प्रमाण पत्र (pramaan patr, certificate)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the पत्र (patr) of bureaucratic life in India: आधार कार्ड (aadhaar kaard), पहचान पत्र (pahchaan patr), जाति प्रमाण पत्र (jaati pramaan patr, 'caste certificate') and dozens of other official पत्र (patr) compounds fill every government office queue. Knowing this sense helps you decode form names even before you can speak fluently.
Common Mistakes: 1) पत्र (patr) alone rarely means 'document' in casual speech; you'll almost always hear it fused into a compound like पहचान पत्र (pahchaan patr) rather than standing on its own the way 'document' does in English.
Sample Sentences:
मुझे पहचान पत्र के लिए आवेदन देना है। I have to submit an application for an identity certificate.
(mujhe pahchaan patr ke lie aavedan denaa hai।)

Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : newspaper, periodical (as part of a compound)
noun, masculine Used chiefly in the compound समाचार पत्र (samaachaar patr) or in journalistic register; अखबार (akhbaar) is the everyday word.
Synonyms: समाचार पत्र (samaachaar patr, full form), अखबार (akhbaar)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this in formal writing and news broadcasts as समाचार पत्र (samaachaar patr), but around the chai stall people just ask for the अखबार (akhbaar).
Sample Sentences:

Sense 4 : leaf (botanical/literary)
noun, masculine Literary/technical register; the everyday word for a leaf is पत्ता (pattaa).
Synonyms: पत्ता (pattaa)
Frequency: literary
Cultural Note: This older, more literary sense survives in classical vocabulary and compounds like पत्रावली (patraavlii, 'foliage' or 'a series of leaves/letters'), but a Delhi speaker pointing at a tree leaf will say पत्ता (pattaa), not पत्र (patr).
