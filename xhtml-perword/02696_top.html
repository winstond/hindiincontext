तोप (top)

adjective/noun Primarily a noun meaning 'cannon,' and by extension used colloquially as an adjective/exclamation for something or someone impressive or excellent.
Sense 1 : excellent, impressive, top-notch (colloquial)
adjective Used predicatively, often as an exclamation about a person or thing: तू तो तोप है (too to top hai, 'you're amazing/a real star').
Synonyms: बढ़िया (badhiyaa), शानदार (shaandaar), कमाल (kamaal, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a classic bit of Delhi slang -- calling someone तोप (top) is like calling them a 'blast' or 'a total star' in English; you'll hear it a lot among friends praising someone's cleverness or guts, especially in casual banter.
Common Mistakes: 1) This is a colloquial, spoken-register usage -- it sounds odd in formal writing or polite formal speech. 2) It is invariant here (does not inflect like a regular -आ adjective) since it functions more like a fixed exclamatory label borrowed from the noun.

Sense 2 : cannon, artillery piece
noun, feminine
Synonyms: गोला-बारूद (golaa-baarood, ammunition/artillery, broader term)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Old forts across Delhi-NCR (Purana Qila, Red Fort) display historic तोपें (topen) from Mughal and colonial times, so the word carries a strong visual, historical association for most Hindi speakers, not just a dictionary meaning.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse तोप (top) with बंदूक (bandook) 'gun/rifle' -- तोप (top) specifically means a large cannon, not a handheld firearm.
