पूरा (pooraa)

adjective/adverb Core meaning is 'complete/whole/full,' extending to 'entire' as a quantifier over a noun and 'fulfilled/achieved' when paired with certain verbs like करना (karnaa) or होना (honaa).
Sense 1 : complete, whole, entire
adjective
Synonyms: सारा (saaraa), समस्त (samast, literary), संपूर्ण (sampoorn, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear पूरा (pooraa) constantly in daily life -- पूरा दिन (pooraa din, 'the whole day'), पूरा परिवार (pooraa parivaar, 'the whole family') -- it is the default word Delhi speakers reach for when English speakers would say 'whole' or 'entire.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for सारा (saaraa) and पूरा (pooraa) interchangeably, which mostly works, but पूरा (pooraa) leans toward 'complete/intact' while सारा (saaraa) leans toward 'all of it' -- e.g. पूरा घर (pooraa ghar, 'the whole house') suggests the entire structure, while सारा दिन (saaraa din, 'the whole day') is more natural for stretches of time.
Sample Sentences:

(mainne pooraa din uskaa intzaar kiyaa।)

Sense 2 : fulfilled, accomplished (of a wish, task, or need)
adjective This sense shows up in fixed pairings like सपना पूरा होना (sapnaa pooraa honaa, 'for a dream to be fulfilled') and इच्छा पूरी करना (ichchhaa poorii karnaa, 'to fulfill a wish') -- पूरा (pooraa) here functions almost like a verbal complement with होना (honaa) or करना (karnaa).
Synonyms: संपन्न (sampann, formal), सम्पूर्ण (sampoorn, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the phrase behind countless Hindi film lines and everyday aspirations -- सपना पूरा होना (sapnaa pooraa honaa) is how people talk about dreams coming true, and मेरी बात पूरी करो (merii baat poorii karo, 'let me finish what I am saying') is a common interruption-stopper in conversation.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use सिद्ध (siddh) or similar bookish words where पूरा होना (pooraa honaa) is what a native speaker would actually say for 'a wish came true' or 'a goal was achieved.' 2) Remember agreement: पूरा (pooraa) changes to पूरी (poorii) with feminine nouns like इच्छा (ichchhaa) or बात (baat) -- this trips up learners focused only on the meaning.
Sample Sentences:
आखिरकार मेरा सपना पूरा हो गया। Finally my dream came true.
(aakhirkaar meraa sapnaa pooraa ho gayaa।)

Sense 3 : full, filled to capacity
adjective
Synonyms: भरा (bharaa), भरपूर (bharpoor)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used for things reaching capacity or a set amount, like गिलास पूरा भर गया (gilaas pooraa bhar gayaa, 'the glass got completely full') -- often paired with भरना (bharnaa) for emphasis on totality rather than just fullness.

Sense 4 : completely, fully, entirely
adverb Functions adverbially before an adjective or verb, e.g. पूरा तैयार (pooraa taiyaar, 'completely ready'), distinct from its use as an attributive adjective modifying a noun.
Synonyms: बिलकुल (bilkul), पूर्णतया (poornatyaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In casual speech you will hear पूरा (pooraa) used loosely as an intensifier, almost like 'totally' in English -- पूरा गड़बड़ हो गया (pooraa gadbad ho gayaa, 'it got totally messed up').
