ख़ास तौर पर (khaas taur par)

adverb A fixed adverbial phrase meaning 'especially, particularly' -- built from ख़ास (khaas, 'special') plus तौर पर (taur par, 'in a manner/way'). One sense only.
Sense 1 : especially, particularly, specifically
adverb Typically placed right before the word or phrase it singles out, e.g. before a noun, time expression, or clause: ख़ास तौर पर सर्दियों में (khaas taur par sardiyon men, 'especially in winter').
Synonyms: विशेष रूप से (vishesh roop se, slightly more formal), ख़ासकर (khaaskar, one-word variant), ख़ास तौर से (khaas taur se, variant form)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly when someone is qualifying a general statement, e.g. a mother saying सब बच्चों को, ख़ास तौर पर छोटे वाले को, (sab bachchon ko, khaas taur par chhote vaale ko, 'to all the kids, especially the little one') -- it is the natural way Hindi speakers narrow focus mid-sentence rather than restructuring the whole thing the way English often does. Note the nuqta in ख़ास (khaas); dropping it (खास) is common in casual typing and still read the same way.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes drop पर (par) and just say ख़ास तौर (khaas taur), which sounds incomplete -- the phrase is fixed as a unit. 2) Confusing this with ख़ासकर (khaaskar) as if one were more correct: both are genuinely interchangeable, so the choice is just about rhythm/register, not correctness.

मुझे मसालेदार खाना पसंद है, ख़ास तौर पर राजमा चावल। I like spicy food, especially rajma chawal.
(mujhe masaaledaar khaanaa pasand hai, khaas taur par raajmaa chaaval।)
