कोना (konaa)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'corner' -- of a room, a shape, or a place -- with a natural extended sense of an out-of-the-way or secluded spot.
Sense 1 : corner (of a room, object, or space)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: कोण (kon, technical/geometric angle)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Indian household has a पूजा का कोना (poojaa kaa konaa, 'prayer corner') tucked somewhere -- a small shelf or nook with idols and a diya. When someone says कमरे के कोने में (kamre ke kone men, 'in the corner of the room'), this is the word they reach for.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for कोण (kon) when they mean a physical corner of a room -- कोण (kon) is the geometric term for 'angle' and sounds odd in everyday domestic contexts, where कोना (konaa) is correct.
Sample Sentences:

(kitaab mez ke kone men rakhii hai।)

Sense 2 : secluded or remote spot; out-of-the-way place
noun, masculine
Synonyms: गोशा (goshaa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Used figuratively for any quiet, tucked-away place -- दुनिया के किसी कोने में (duniyaa ke kisii kone men, 'in some corner of the world') is a common way to talk about a remote, out-of-reach location, physical or emotional.
