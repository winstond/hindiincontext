दूरदर्शन (doordarshan)

noun A compound noun literally meaning 'far-seeing/far-vision', best known as the proper name of India's state-run television broadcaster, but also usable as a generic word for 'television' in a slightly formal or old-fashioned register.
Sense 1 : Doordarshan (India's national public TV broadcaster)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: डी.डी. (dii.dii., common abbreviation)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Before cable and satellite TV arrived in the 1990s, दूरदर्शन (doordarshan) was the only channel most Indian households could watch, so for anyone who grew up before that era the word carries real nostalgia -- Sunday morning cartoons, the evening news reader in a sari, and serials that the whole neighborhood watched together on one shared set. Older Delhiites will often say 'डीडी (diidii)' fondly, the way you might say 'the Beeb' for the BBC.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes assume this is just the everyday word for 'a TV set' -- for that, speakers overwhelmingly say टी.वी. (tii.vii.) or टेलीविज़न (teliivizan); दूरदर्शन (doordarshan) in daily speech almost always points specifically to the government channel, not the appliance. 2) As a proper noun (the broadcaster's name), it does not take a plural or an article the way 'a television' would in English.

Sense 2 : television (the medium/device, formal usage)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: टेलीविज़न (teliivizan), टी.वी. (tii.vii.)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: This generic sense is much rarer in casual conversation than टी.वी. (tii.vii.); you are more likely to meet दूरदर्शन (doordarshan) used this way in formal writing, textbooks, or government notices than in someone's living room.
