ऐसा-वैसा (aisaa-vaisaa)

adjective An echo-pair built from ऐसा (aisaa, 'like this'), used mainly as a dismissive adjective meaning 'this sort of (bad/disreputable)' or, negated, to mean 'nothing out of the ordinary / not that kind of person.'
Sense 1 : of a disreputable or objectionable sort
adjective Almost always used to characterize a person, place, or behavior negatively, e.g. ऐसी-वैसी जगह (aisii-vaisii jagah, 'that kind of (disreputable) place'); rarely used in a neutral or positive sense.
Synonyms: उल्टा-सीधा (ultaa-siidhaa, of dubious/improper sort), फालतू (phaaltoo, colloquial)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear worried parents or neighbors warn ऐसी-वैसी हरकत मत करो (aisii-vaisii harkat mat karo, 'don't do that kind of (disreputable) thing') -- it's a classic tool of Indian moral scolding, gesturing at bad behavior without spelling it out.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use ऐसा-वैसा (aisaa-vaisaa) as a neutral synonym for 'this or that,' but it almost always carries a negative, disapproving shade -- it's not interchangeable with plain ऐसा (aisaa). 2) Because it inflects like ऐसा (aisaa), learners forget to change both halves together: it's ऐसी-वैसी लड़की (aisii-vaisii ladkii), not ऐसा-वैसी लड़की (aisaa-vaisii ladkii).

Sense 2 : ordinary, nothing special (used with negation)
adjective Common in the negated frame कोई ऐसी-वैसी बात नहीं (koii aisii-vaisii baat nahiin, 'it's not just any (ordinary) matter') to assert that someone/something is significant, not to be underestimated.
Synonyms: मामूली (maamoolii, ordinary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is a favorite bit of Bollywood-style bravado: वह ऐसा-वैसा आदमी नहीं है (vah aisaa-vaisaa aadmii nahiin hai, 'he's no ordinary man') puffs someone up as important or dangerous, often right before a dramatic reveal.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often miss that this sense only surfaces under negation -- वह ऐसा-वैसा आदमी नहीं है (vah aisaa-vaisaa aadmii nahiin hai, 'he's not just any (ordinary) man') praises the person; without नहीं (nahiin) the phrase reverts to the negative/disreputable sense.
