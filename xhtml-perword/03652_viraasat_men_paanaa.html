विरासत में पाना (viraasat men paanaa)

verb Literally 'to get in inheritance' -- used both for actually inheriting property and, very commonly, for figuratively inheriting a trait, skill, tradition, or problem from one's family or predecessors.
Sense 1 : to inherit (a trait, skill, tradition, or problem) figuratively
verb, transitive Very commonly used with abstract nouns -- गुण (gun, 'qualities'), प्रतिभा (pratibhaa, 'talent'), आदत (aadat, 'habit'), समस्या (samasyaa, 'problem') -- as the direct object of पाना (paanaa).
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This figurative use is extremely common in interviews and biographical writing -- a singer might say उसने अपनी आवाज़ अपनी माँ से विरासत में पाई (usne apnii aavaaz apnii maan se viraasat men paaii, 'she inherited her voice from her mother'), reflecting a cultural instinct to root personal gifts in family lineage rather than individual effort alone.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners default to सीखना (siikhnaa, 'to learn') when talking about an inherited talent or habit, but विरासत में पाना (viraasat men paanaa) specifically implies it came naturally from one's family or lineage, not through effort. 2) The source (parent, family, ancestors) is marked with से (se), not से का (se kaa) or की तरफ़ से (kii taraf se): माँ से (maan se, 'from mother'), not extra postpositions stacked on.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपनी आवाज़ अपनी माँ से विरासत में पाई। She inherited her voice from her mother.
(usne apnii aavaaz apnii maan se viraasat men paaii।)

Sense 2 : to inherit (property, wealth, or a title)
verb, transitive The thing inherited is the grammatical object and takes ने-marking on the subject in the perfective, since पाना (paanaa) is transitive: उसने घर विरासत में पाया (usne ghar viraasat men paayaa, 'he inherited the house').
Synonyms: उत्तराधिकार में पाना (uttraadhikaar men paanaa, formal)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In everyday Delhi speech this phrase is used just as often for a shop, a business, or a family trade passed down as for cash or land -- उसने अपने पिता से यह दुकान विरासत में पाई (usne apne pitaa se yah dukaan viraasat men paaii, 'he inherited this shop from his father') is a very natural sentence.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try को (ko) with the inherited thing, as if 'inherit' takes a dative object like मिलना (milnaa); here the thing inherited is a plain direct object of पाना (paanaa), not marked with को (ko). 2) विरासत (viraasat) alone means 'inheritance/heritage' as a noun -- learners forget में पाना (men paanaa) is needed to make it a verb phrase.

उसने अपने पिता से यह दुकान विरासत में पाई। He inherited this shop from his father.
(usne apne pitaa se yah dukaan viraasat men paaii।)
