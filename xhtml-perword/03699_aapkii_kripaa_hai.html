आपकी कृपा है (aapkii kripaa hai)

phrase A polite, formal phrase meaning 'it is your kindness/grace', used to graciously deflect thanks or praise back onto the other person.
Sense 1 : polite deflection of thanks/praise
phrase Used as a courteous response when someone thanks you, compliments you, or credits you with something -- it redirects the credit to the listener rather than accepting it outright.
Synonyms: आपकी मेहरबानी है (aapkii meharbaanii hai), आपका बड़प्पन है (aapkaa badappan hai, you are being generous)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This phrase reflects a broader cultural instinct in Hindi to respond to compliments with humility rather than acceptance -- rather than saying 'yes, I did well', you shift the credit to the other person's generosity or blessing. You will hear it often from shopkeepers, hosts, or juniors addressing elders, especially paired with the respectful आप (aap) form.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often try to respond to praise with a direct acceptance like 'thank you' (धन्यवाद dhanyvaad), but a native speaker is more likely to deflect with आपकी कृपा है (aapkii kripaa hai), similar to how English speakers might say 'you're too kind' rather than simply agreeing. 2) This phrase is fairly formal/respectful in tone -- among close friends or peers, people often use something lighter or skip the response altogether.

आप बहुत तारीफ़ कर रहे हैं, यह आपकी कृपा है। You're praising me too much, that's very kind of you.
(aap bahut taariif kar rahe hain, yah aapkii kripaa hai।)
