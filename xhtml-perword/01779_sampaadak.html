संपादक (sampaadak)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'editor,' the person who prepares written material for publication. Related to the verb संपादन करना (sampaadan karnaa, 'to edit').
Sense 1 : editor (of a publication)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: एडिटर (editar, common in casual speech)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly on the masthead of Hindi newspapers as प्रधान संपादक (pradhaan sampaadak, 'editor-in-chief') or मुख्य संपादक (mukhy sampaadak, 'chief editor') -- a title of real social weight in Indian journalism. It's a Sanskrit-derived formal/institutional word, so you'll meet it in print and news media far more than in casual chat about, say, editing a friend's essay.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse संपादक (sampaadak, 'editor') with लेखक (lekhak, 'writer/author') -- the संपादक (sampaadak) compiles, reviews, and finalizes content that others may have written, while the लेखक (lekhak) is the original author. 2) The feminine form संपादिका (sampaadikaa) is used for a female editor in more formal or written contexts; in casual speech many speakers just use संपादक (sampaadak) for both genders, so learners should recognize both forms rather than expect strict consistency.
