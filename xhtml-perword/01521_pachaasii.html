पचासी (pachaasii)

numeral The number eighty-five (85), used for counting and as an ordinal-style label in dates, ages, and quantities.
Sense 1 : the number 85
numeral
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Hindi numerals from 1 to 99 are each their own word rather than following a clean formula like English, so पचासी (pachaasii) is simply learned as a whole -- most Delhi learners drill these in blocks of ten (81-90) rather than trying to reconstruct them from पचास (pachaas) and पाँच (paanch).
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse पचासी (pachaasii, '85') with पचहत्तर (pachahattar, '75') or पचानवे (pachaanve, '95') since all three share the पचा- (pachaa) start; the endings need careful listening practice. 2) Like other Hindi numerals above twenty, पचासी (pachaasii) is a single memorized word rather than a compound built predictably from पचास (pachaas, '50') and पाँच (paanch, '5'), so learners should not try to derive it logically.
