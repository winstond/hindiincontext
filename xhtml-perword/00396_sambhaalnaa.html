सँभालना (sambhaalnaa)

verb A single core meaning of 'to handle, manage, take care of' that extends across physical, emotional, and situational objects -- catching something before it falls, managing a household or job, or steadying oneself/someone emotionally. Also spelled संभालना (sambhaalnaa) without the chandrabindu; both forms are common.
Sense 1 : to manage, take care of, look after (a task, household, business, responsibility)
verb, transitive Commonly used with घर (ghar, 'household'), दुकान (dukaan, 'shop'), काम (kaam, 'work'), and ज़िम्मेदारी (zimmedaarii, 'responsibility') as objects.
Synonyms: देखना (dekhnaa, to look after), निभाना (nibhaanaa, to fulfil/carry out), चलाना (chalaanaa, to run/operate)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the sense you hear constantly in Indian households: a daughter-in-law who 'सँभालती है (sambhaaltii hai)' the ghar (ghar), or an elder son who has to 'सँभालना (sambhaalnaa)' the family business after his father retires. It carries a quiet weight of responsibility, not just task-completion.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use चलाना (chalaanaa) when सँभालना (sambhaalnaa) is meant -- चलाना (chalaanaa) is about operating/running something day-to-day, while सँभालना (sambhaalnaa) carries the extra sense of holding things together, often under pressure or in someone's absence.

Sense 2 : to calm, compose, or steady oneself or someone emotionally
verb, intransitive Frequently reflexive: खुद को सँभालना (khud ko sambhaalnaa, 'to compose oneself') or अपने आप को सँभालना (apne aap ko sambhaalnaa).
Synonyms: संयत करना (sanyat karnaa, formal), शांत करना (shaant karnaa, to calm)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear this at moments of grief or shock -- a friend telling someone to 'अपने आप को सँभालो (apne aap ko sambhaalo)' after bad news. It treats emotional composure the same way as physically catching something before it falls, which is a nice window into how Hindi links the physical and emotional senses of this verb.
Common Mistakes: 1) खुद को सँभालो (khud ko sambhaalo, 'compose yourself') is a common encouragement to someone upset -- learners may default to शांत हो जाओ (shaant ho jaao, 'calm down') instead, which sounds more like a command to stop being loud, whereas सँभालना (sambhaalnaa) is gentler and implies regaining inner balance.
Sample Sentences:
खबर सुनकर वह रो पड़ी, फिर भी उसने अपने आप को सँभाला। Hearing the news she broke into tears, but still she composed herself.
(khabar sunkar vah ro padii, phir bhii usne apne aap ko sambhaalaa।)

Sense 3 : to catch/steady something physical before it falls or slips
verb, transitive
Synonyms: थामना (thaamnaa), पकड़ना (pakadnaa, to grab/hold)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Picture someone lunging to catch a stack of plates sliding off a shelf -- that reflexive save is exactly what सँभालना (sambhaalnaa) captures in this sense. It is the physical root from which the 'manage/take care of' sense (below) extends metaphorically.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for पकड़ना (pakadnaa) here, but सँभालना (sambhaalnaa) specifically implies preventing a fall or restoring balance -- catching a glass as it tips, not just holding something steady in your hand. 2) गिरते हुए बच्चे को सँभाला (girte hue bachche ko sambhaalaa, 'caught the falling child') -- the sense of 'rescue from falling' is central and easy to under-translate as plain 'held.'

उसने गिरते हुए गिलास को झट से सँभाल लिया। He quickly caught the glass as it was falling.
