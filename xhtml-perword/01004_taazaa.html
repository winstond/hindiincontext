ताज़ा (taazaa)

adjective Mainly an adjective meaning 'fresh' (food, air, news), with a common extended sense of 'recent/up-to-date'. Also spelled ताजा (taajaa) without the nuqta.
Sense 1 : fresh (food, produce, flowers)
adjective
Synonyms: नया (nayaa, overlaps for 'newly made')
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: At the sabzi mandi (sabzii mandii, vegetable market) you'll hear vendors shout ताज़ी सब्ज़ी (taazii sabzii, 'fresh vegetables') to pull in customers -- freshness is a major selling point in daily Indian shopping culture, unlike pre-packaged produce culture elsewhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use नया (nayaa) for 'fresh vegetables/bread', but Hindi speakers reach for ताज़ा (taazaa) specifically when talking about food that hasn't spoiled or gone stale, not just 'new'. 2) Don't confuse with ठंडा (thandaa, 'cold') -- ताज़ा (taazaa) is about freshness, not temperature, even though 'fresh and cold' often go together with drinks.
Sample Sentences:
बाज़ार से ताज़ी सब्ज़ियाँ ले आओ। Bring fresh vegetables from the market.
(baazaar se taazii sabziyaan le aao।)

Sense 2 : fresh (air, water, feeling of being refreshed)

Cultural Note: You'll often hear ताज़ी हवा (taazii havaa, 'fresh air') in the context of morning walks in a park -- it's a common phrase tied to health and well-being talk, much like in English.

Sense 3 : recent, up-to-date, latest
adjective Commonly pairs with ख़बर (khabar, 'news') as ताज़ा ख़बर (taazaa khabar, 'latest news'), a phrase you'll hear constantly on TV news channels.
Synonyms: नवीनतम (naviintam, formal), नया (nayaa)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Turn on any Hindi news channel and you'll hear anchors say ताज़ा ख़बरें (taazaa khabren, 'latest news') as a segment title -- it's practically a broadcasting cliche, the Hindi equivalent of 'breaking news'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for नया (nayaa) when they mean 'the latest update on something', but ताज़ा (taazaa) is the natural choice with news, information, and updates -- it carries a sense of 'just happened' rather than just 'new'.
