तशरीफ़ (tashriif)

noun A polite/honorific noun used almost exclusively in fixed courteous expressions about someone's arrival, seating, or presence -- it does not stand alone with an independent everyday meaning outside these set phrases.
Sense 1 : honorific term for a respected person's presence/arrival (used in polite set phrases)
noun, feminine Almost always appears as part of a fixed collocation such as तशरीफ़ लाना (tashriif laanaa, 'to come, honorific'), तशरीफ़ रखना (tashriif rakhnaa, 'to sit down, honorific'), or तशरीफ़ ले जाना (tashriif le jaanaa, 'to leave, honorific'). It is not used as a free-standing noun with its own sentence outside these frames.
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This word comes from the same courteous register as other Persian-origin polite vocabulary in Hindi-Urdu, and you will hear it most from shopkeepers, hosts, and hotel staff welcoming a guest: तशरीफ़ लाइए (tashriif laaie, 'please come in') or तशरीफ़ रखिए (tashriif rakhie, 'please have a seat'). It carries the same warm formality as calling someone आप (aap) rather than तुम (tum), and pairing तशरीफ़ with तू (too) or तुम (tum) would feel jarringly mismatched.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes try to use तशरीफ़ (tashriif) alone as if it simply meant 'you' or 'arrival' -- it only works glued to a verb like लाना (laanaa) or रखना (rakhnaa) in these honorific set phrases. 2) Because it is deferential, it is odd (even comic) to use it about yourself in a plain statement -- e.g. saying मैंने तशरीफ़ रखी (mainne tashriif rakhii) sounds like you are mocking your own importance; it is meant for addressing or describing someone else respectfully.
Sample Sentences:
आइए, तशरीफ़ लाइए। Please, come in.
(aaie, tashriif laaie।)

तशरीफ़ रखिए, चाय अभी आती है। Please have a seat, the tea is coming right away.
(tashriif rakhie, chaay abhii aatii hai।)

(agar aap tashriif laate to hamen bahut khushii hotii।)

(hotal kaa darbaan har mehmaan se 'tashriif laaie' kahkar svaagat kartaa hai।)

(unkii tashriif kaa hamen besabrii se intzaar rahegaa।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Indicative -- Tense: Future -- Voice: Active
दुकानदार ने हाथ जोड़कर कहा, 'साहब, तशरीफ़ लाइए, देखिए क्या पसंद है।' The shopkeeper joined his hands and said, 'Sir, please come in, see what you like.'
(dukaandaar ne haath jodkar kahaa, 'saahab, tashriif laaie, dekhie kyaa pasand hai।')
