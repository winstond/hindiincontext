मीनार (miinaar)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'tower' or 'minaret' -- most often the tall, slender tower of a mosque or a monument like Qutub Minar (qutub miinaar).
Sense 1 : tower, minaret
noun, feminine
Synonyms: बुर्ज (burj)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word in क़ुतुब मीनार (qutub miinaar), Delhi's most famous monument, and in the मीनार (miinaar) of any mosque -- so for a Delhi learner it carries a strong visual, almost skyline association rather than being a generic architectural term.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use मीनार (miinaar) for any tall building, but it is reserved for a slender tower-like structure (a minaret, a monument tower) -- an office tower or apartment block is इमारत (imaarat) or टावर (taavar), not मीनार (miinaar).
