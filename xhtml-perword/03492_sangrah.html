संग्रह (sangrah)

noun A somewhat formal/literary word meaning 'collection' or 'accumulation' -- covers a physical collection (of books, stamps, etc.) and the more abstract act of collecting or gathering something.
Sense 1 : collection (of objects, writings, etc.)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: संकलन (sanklan, often used specifically for literary/writing collections), जमावड़ा (jamaavdaa, informal, more about a gathered pile or crowd of things)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll see this word constantly on book covers and library shelves -- कहानी-संग्रह (kahaanii-sangrah, 'collection of stories') and काव्य-संग्रह (kaavy-sangrah, 'collection of poetry') are standard genre labels in Hindi publishing. It carries a slightly bookish, formal flavor compared to everyday words for 'stuff gathered together.'
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for इकट्ठा (ikatthaa) as a direct synonym, but इकट्ठा (ikatthaa) is mainly an adjective/adverb ('gathered, together'), not a noun for the collection itself -- संग्रह (sangrah) is the noun you want for 'a collection of...'. 2) In compounds like कविता-संग्रह (kavitaa-sangrah, 'poetry collection') or कहानी-संग्रह (kahaanii-sangrah, 'story collection'), संग्रह (sangrah) sits after the noun it collects, unlike English 'collection of poems.'

Sense 2 : act of collecting/gathering, accumulation
noun, masculine Often appears in compound nouns describing the collecting of a resource, e.g. जल-संग्रह (jal-sangrah, 'water collection/storage') or कर-संग्रह (kar-sangrah, 'tax collection').
Synonyms: जमाव (jamaav), इकट्ठा करना (ikatthaa karnaa, verbal phrase rather than a noun, but commonly used to express the same idea)
Frequency: low
Cultural Note: You'll meet this sense in slightly formal or administrative contexts -- news reports about जल-संग्रह (jal-sangrah, water harvesting/storage) schemes, or करों का संग्रह (karon kaa sangrah, 'collection of taxes'). It has a bureaucratic, official ring rather than a warm, everyday one.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense is more abstract than sense 1 -- it refers to the process or resulting mass of gathering something (like data, water, or funds), not a curated set of items like a book collection. Learners sometimes use संग्रह (sangrah) for 'a collection of friends' or similar casual groupings, where जमावड़ा (jamaavdaa) or इकट्ठा होना (ikatthaa honaa) sound far more natural.
