व्यक्तित्व (vyaktitv)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'personality' -- the overall character and presence a person projects.
Sense 1 : personality; individual character
noun, masculine
Synonyms: शख्सियत (shakhsiyat), स्वभाव (svabhaav, leans toward temperament/nature rather than overall presence)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: This is the word you will hear in job interviews, school essays ('मेरा आदर्श' meraa aadarsh type writing), or when Indians describe a public figure's charisma -- 'उसका व्यक्तित्व बहुत प्रभावशाली है' (uskaa vyaktitv bahut prabhaavshaalii hai, 'his personality is very impressive'). It carries a slightly more formal, almost evaluative weight than casual chat about someone's nature, which is where स्वभाव (svabhaav) takes over.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use this where स्वभाव (svabhaav) fits better -- व्यक्तित्व (vyaktitv) is the fuller, more formal 'personality/persona' someone projects (used in interviews, essays, descriptions of leaders), while स्वभाव (svabhaav) is the everyday word for someone's nature or temperament, like 'वह गुस्सैल स्वभाव का है' (vah gussail svabhaav kaa hai, 'he has a short-tempered nature'). 2) It is grammatically masculine despite ending in a soft sound -- do not default to feminine agreement just because it sounds abstract.
