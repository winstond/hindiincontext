खिलौना (khilaunaa)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'toy'; it also has a common figurative sense describing a person used or manipulated by others, as in खिलौना बनना (khilaunaa bannaa, 'to become a plaything/pawn').
Sense 1 : toy
noun, masculine
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Any trip to Delhi's Sadar Bazaar or Lajpat Nagar during Diwali or a birthday season will have vendors calling out खिलौना ले लो (khilaunaa le lo, 'take a toy') -- it is one of the first everyday nouns kids learn because they are constantly asking for one.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes forget this noun is masculine and inflects to खिलौने (khilaune) in the plural and oblique -- e.g. बच्चों के खिलौने (bachchon ke khilaune, 'the children's toys') -- not left invariant like some other nouns ending in -आ.
Sample Sentences:

Sense 2 : a person used/manipulated by others; a pawn or plaything
noun, masculine Most often seen in the frame [X] का खिलौना बनना (kaa khilaunaa bannaa, 'to become someone's plaything/pawn'), describing a person controlled by another's whims.
Synonyms: कठपुतली (kathputlii, 'puppet' -- similar figurative use)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You'll hear this in political commentary and family dramas alike -- a leader accused of being विदेशी ताकतों का खिलौना (videshii taakton kaa khilaunaa, 'a plaything of foreign powers') or a child lamenting भाग्य का खिलौना (bhaagy kaa khilaunaa, 'a toy of fate'). It carries a note of pity or contempt for someone stripped of their own agency.
Common Mistakes: 1) This figurative sense almost always needs the possessive frame (किसी का खिलौना, kisii kaa khilaunaa, 'someone's plaything') to make sense -- used alone, खिलौना (khilaunaa) will just be heard as the literal toy.
