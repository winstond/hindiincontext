पृथ्वी (prithvii)

noun The everyday word for 'earth/Earth,' used both for the planet and, more poetically, for the ground/land we live on.
Sense 1 : the planet Earth
noun, feminine
Synonyms: धरती (dhartii), भूमि (bhoomi, formal), ज़मीन (zamiin, ground/land sense)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In school textbooks and news about space missions, पृथ्वी (prithvii) is the standard, slightly formal term for 'Earth' -- think of it as the word you'd see in a science chapter, whereas धरती (dhartii) is what you'd hear in daily speech or songs with more warmth and feeling.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes reach for ज़मीन (zamiin) when talking about the planet in a science or geography context -- पृथ्वी (prithvii) or धरती (dhartii) is the natural choice there, while ज़मीन (zamiin) is better kept for 'ground' underfoot.

Sense 2 : the earth/land/ground as a nurturing, living entity
noun, feminine
Synonyms: धरती (dhartii), भूमि (bhoomi, formal), वसुंधरा (vasundhraa, literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: पृथ्वी (prithvii) carries a reverential, almost maternal charge in Hindi -- you'll hear it in phrases like पृथ्वी माता (prithvii maataa, 'Mother Earth'), echoing the older idea of the earth as a goddess who sustains all life, much like धरती माता (dhartii maataa) in more colloquial speech.
