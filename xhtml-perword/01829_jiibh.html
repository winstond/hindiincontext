जीभ (jiibh)

noun A concrete noun meaning 'tongue,' the body part; it also shows up in a few common idioms about speech and taste.
Sense 1 : tongue (body part)
noun, feminine
Synonyms: जबान (jabaan)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: जीभ (jiibh) is the everyday, physical word for the organ itself -- when Delhi speakers talk about being outspoken, having a sharp tongue, or slipping on a word, they usually reach for जबान (jabaan) instead, so it is worth learning both side by side.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes write जीभ (jiibh) as masculine because it ends in a consonant sound with no obvious feminine marker -- it is always feminine, so adjectives and verbs agreeing with it take feminine forms, e.g. मेरी जीभ जल गई (merii jiibh jal gaii, 'my tongue got burned').
Sample Sentences:
डॉक्टर ने कहा, "अपनी जीभ बाहर निकालो।" The doctor said, "Stick out your tongue."
(doktar ne kahaa, "apnii jiibh baahar nikaalo।")
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active

Sense 2 : taste, sense of taste (figurative)
noun, feminine Used with verbs like चटपटा लगना (chataptaa lagnaa) or in fixed expressions like जीभ में पानी आना (jiibh men paanii aanaa, 'to start salivating') to talk about craving or reacting to a taste.
Synonyms: स्वाद (svaad)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: You will hear जीभ में पानी आ गया (jiibh men paanii aa gayaa, 'my mouth started watering') the moment good street food -- golgappe, chaat -- comes into view; it is one of the most vivid, sense-driven idioms in everyday Hindi food talk.
Common Mistakes: 1) जीभ में पानी आना (jiibh men paanii aanaa, 'mouth-watering') is the natural Hindi idiom for craving food -- learners often try to translate 'my mouth is watering' literally with मुँह (munh) instead, which sounds off in this context.
Sample Sentences:
गोलगप्पे देखते ही मेरी जीभ में पानी आ गया। The moment I saw the golgappe, my mouth started watering.
(golgappe dekhte hii merii jiibh men paanii aa gayaa।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
इस हलवाई की मिठाई देखकर सबकी जीभ में पानी आ जाता है। Seeing this sweet-shop's mithai, everyone's mouth starts watering.
(is halvaaii kii mithaaii dekhkar sabkii jiibh men paanii aa jaataa hai।)
Register: Casual -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Habitual -- Tense: Present -- Voice: Active
