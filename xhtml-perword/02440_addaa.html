अड्डा (addaa)

noun A colloquial noun with a concrete sense (a hangout spot / stand / stop) and a related figurative sense (a den or haunt, often with a slightly disreputable edge). Inline Hindi as (placeholder).
Sense 1 : hangout spot, stand, or stop (for transport, gathering)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ठिकाना (thikaanaa), स्टैंड (staind, for vehicle stands specifically)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: Every Delhi neighborhood has its चाय (chaay) का अड्डा (addaa) -- the tea stall corner where the same uncles gather every evening to chat and put the world to rights. बस अड्डा (bas addaa) and रिक्शा अड्डा (rikshaa addaa) are the everyday transport senses you'll see on signs everywhere.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for जगह (jagah) when they mean a casual, familiar hangout -- अड्डा (addaa) carries a sense of regular, informal gathering that जगह (jagah) alone doesn't convey. 2) In the transport sense (auto-रिक्शा (rikshaa) अड्डा (addaa), बस अड्डा (addaa)), it means a stand or depot, not a random stopping place.

Sense 2 : den, haunt (often disreputable)
noun, masculine
Synonyms: ठिकाना (thikaanaa), गढ़ (gadh, stronghold, slightly literary)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the same word as the friendly tea-stall sense, just with a shadier crowd -- context and the noun before अड्डा (addaa) (जुआ (juaa), चोर (chor), etc.) tells you which meaning is in play.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense leans negative -- जुए का अड्डा (jue kaa addaa, 'gambling den') or शराब का अड्डा (sharaab kaa addaa, 'drinking den') -- so don't use it casually for a nice cafe or a friend's house unless you intend a wry or disapproving tone.
