बहू (bahoo)

noun Means 'daughter-in-law', with an extended everyday sense of 'young married woman of the household'. A cornerstone word in Hindi family vocabulary.
Sense 1 : daughter-in-law
noun, feminine
Synonyms: पुत्रवधू (putravdhoo, formal)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In a joint-family household, बहू (bahoo) is a major social role, not just a kinship label -- the family's सास (saas, 'mother-in-law') and बहू (bahoo) relationship is a classic theme in Hindi TV serials and everyday conversation, often loaded with expectations about how a new bride adjusts to her husband's family.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse बहू (bahoo) with बहन (bahan, 'sister') because of the similar sound -- the words are unrelated in meaning. 2) बहू (bahoo) refers specifically to one's son's wife (or, loosely, a daughter-in-law of the household), not to a daughter-in-law relationship in general the way English 'in-law' can be used loosely.

Sense 2 : young married woman (of the household/family), bride
noun, feminine Used by extended family, neighbors, or the community to refer to any young married woman belonging to a household, not necessarily the speaker's own son's wife.
Synonyms: दुल्हन (dulhan, bride specifically), नई बहू (naii bahoo, 'new bride', common set phrase)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: Neighbors or relatives might refer to a newly married woman in the colony simply as उनकी बहू (unkii bahoo, 'their daughter-in-law/bride') -- the word extends naturally from the strict kinship term to a broader social label for a young married woman who has joined a family.
