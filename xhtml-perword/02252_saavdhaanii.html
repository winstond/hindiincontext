सावधानी (saavdhaanii)

noun A single-sense noun meaning 'caution, care, alertness' -- the quality of being careful. Related to the adjective सावधान (saavdhaan, 'careful, alert'), from which it is derived with the abstract-noun suffix -ई (ii).
Sense 1 : caution, carefulness, alertness
noun, feminine
Synonyms: एहतियात (ehtiyaat), चौकसी (chauksii, vigilance/watchfulness), होशियारी (hoshiyaarii, leans toward cleverness/alertness rather than pure caution)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear सावधानी से (saavdhaanii se) constantly in everyday warnings -- from a mother telling a child crossing the road, to announcements at railway stations reminding passengers to mind the gap. It carries the same brisk, practical tone as English 'carefully' or 'mind yourself,' without being alarmist.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often say सावधानी करना (saavdhaanii karnaa) when the natural phrasing is सावधानी रखना (saavdhaanii rakhnaa, 'to maintain caution') or सावधानी बरतना (saavdhaanii baratnaa, 'to exercise caution') -- बरतना (baratnaa) is the verb native speakers reach for here and is worth learning alongside this noun. 2) The adverbial form सावधानी से (saavdhaanii se, 'carefully') is extremely common and often gets skipped in favor of the clunkier धीरे धीरे (dhiire dhiire) when 'carefully' rather than 'slowly' is meant.
Sample Sentences:
सड़क पार करते समय सावधानी बरतें। Exercise caution while crossing the road.
(sadak paar karte samay saavdhaanii barten।)
Register: Formal -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
कृपया सावधानी से चलें, फर्श गीला है। Please walk carefully, the floor is wet.
(kripyaa saavdhaanii se chalen, pharsh giilaa hai।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Imperative -- Voice: Active
माँ ने बच्चे से कहा कि सड़क पार करते वक्त सावधानी रखे। The mother told the child to exercise caution while crossing the road.
(maan ne bachche se kahaa ki sadak paar karte vakt saavdhaanii rakhe।)

बिजली के तारों के पास हमेशा सावधानी बरती जाती है। Caution is always exercised near electric wires.

वह हर काम बड़ी सावधानी से करता है। He does every task with great care.

(doktar ne chot kii pattii bahut saavdhaanii se baandhii।)
