ढकेलना (dhakelnaa)

verb A single-sense action verb meaning 'to push, shove' -- physically forcing something or someone forward or aside.
Sense 1 : to push, shove (physically)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: धकेलना (dhakelnaa, variant spelling, more common), धक्का देना (dhakkaa denaa, to give a shove)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You'll hear this constantly around a stalled scooter or a heavy cart in the bazaar -- someone shouts ज़रा ढकेलो! (zaraa dhakelo!, 'give it a push!') to bystanders. Note the near-universal variant धकेलना (dhakelnaa), which many speakers actually use more often; both are completely standard.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners often reach for धक्का देना (dhakkaa denaa) for a single shove and forget that ढकेलना (dhakelnaa) works well for sustained pushing, like pushing a cart or a stuck door, not just one jolt. 2) Confusing this with धकेलना (dhakelnaa) -- both are heard in Delhi-NCR speech, so don't assume one is wrong; they are the same word with a ढ/ध variation.
Sample Sentences:
ज़रा गाड़ी को ढकेलो, यह स्टार्ट नहीं हो रही। Just push the car a bit, it isn't starting.
(zaraa gaadii ko dhakelo, yah staart nahiin ho rahii।)
