पचाना (pachaanaa)

verb The causative of पचना (pachnaa, 'to be digested'): literally 'to digest (something)', and widely extended to figurative senses like 'to embezzle/pocket (money)' and 'to stomach/tolerate (an insult or unfair situation)'.
Sense 1 : to digest (food)
verb, transitive
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: You will hear this constantly around food talk in Delhi: भारी खाना है, पचेगा नहीं (bhaarii khaanaa hai, pachegaa nahiin, 'it's heavy food, it won't digest') uses the intransitive पचना (pachnaa), while खाना ठीक से पचाओ (khaanaa thiik se pachaao, 'digest your food properly') uses this causative -- the pair is a nice small window into how Hindi loves to distinguish 'it happens to X' from 'X makes it happen'.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes use पचना (pachnaa) when they mean the active 'I digested the food' -- but पचना (pachnaa) is intransitive ('the food digests/gets digested'), while पचाना (pachaanaa) is the transitive causative you need for 'I digested it'; the subject who eats and processes the food takes पचाना (pachaanaa), not पचना (pachnaa).
Sample Sentences:
रात को इतना भारी खाना मत खाओ, पचाना मुश्किल हो जाता है। Don't eat such heavy food at night, it becomes hard to digest.
(raat ko itnaa bhaarii khaanaa mat khaao, pachaanaa mushkil ho jaataa hai।)

Sense 2 : to embezzle, misappropriate, pocket (money)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: हड़पना (hadapnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: This is the word you'll hear in everyday gossip about corrupt officials or a shady relative: पैसे पचा गया (paise pachaa gayaa, 'he pocketed the money')-- the image is of someone 'digesting' stolen money so thoroughly that it disappears without a trace, which is exactly the sly, guilt-free tone the word carries.
Sample Sentences:
वह कंपनी के लाखों रुपये पचा गया। He embezzled lakhs of rupees from the company.
(vah kampnii ke laakhon rupye pachaa gayaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active

Sense 3 : to stomach, tolerate, come to terms with (an insult, loss, or unfair situation)
verb, transitive
Synonyms: बर्दाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: English 'digest' can mean 'come to terms with an idea' too, so this extension often feels intuitive to English speakers -- but Hindi speakers reach for it especially when someone swallows a personal insult or humiliation without reacting, e.g. उसने अपमान पचा लिया (usne apmaan pachaa liyaa, 'he swallowed the insult'), often with a note of admiration or pity for their restraint.
Common Mistakes: 1) This sense often appears with an abstract noun like बात (baat) or अपमान (apmaan) as object, e.g. यह बात पचाना मुश्किल है (yah baat pachaanaa mushkil hai, 'it's hard to stomach this') -- learners sometimes reach for बर्दाश्त करना (bardaasht karnaa) by default, but पचाना (pachaanaa) adds a flavor of quietly absorbing and moving on, not just enduring in the moment.
Sample Sentences:
उसने अपमान पचा लिया। He swallowed the insult.
(usne apmaan pachaa liyaa।)
Register: Neutral -- Mood: Indicative -- Aspect: Perfective -- Tense: Past -- Voice: Active
