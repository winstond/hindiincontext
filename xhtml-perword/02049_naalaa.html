नाला (naalaa)

noun Names a drainage channel or stream, mostly the open drains/nullahs you see running through Indian streets and neighborhoods; also used for a natural rivulet or stream.
Sense 1 : open drain, gutter, sewer channel
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नाली (naalii, smaller/narrower channel), गटर (gatar)
Frequency: high
Cultural Note: In most Indian cities and towns, नाला (naalaa) is the word you use for the often-smelly open drains running alongside roads or through low-lying colonies -- it comes up constantly in monsoon complaints about flooding and blocked drains. Municipal workers who clean these are sometimes called नाला सफाई कर्मचारी (naalaa saphaaii karmchaarii, drain-cleaning workers), a phrase you will see on civic notices.
Common Mistakes: 1) Learners sometimes confuse नाला (naalaa) with नाली (naalii) -- नाला (naalaa) is the larger open drain or storm channel running along/under a road, while नाली (naalii) is the small household or street-side gutter; they are not interchangeable in size.

Sense 2 : natural stream or rivulet
noun, masculine
Synonyms: नदी (nadii, larger river, not a close synonym but related), झरना (jharnaa, waterfall/stream, partial overlap)
Frequency: medium
Cultural Note: In hilly or rural areas, नाला (naalaa) can also mean a small natural stream or seasonal rivulet coming down from higher ground, distinct from the urban drain sense -- context (a village or hillside versus a city street) tells you which meaning is meant.
