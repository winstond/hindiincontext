Hindi in Context

Hindi words in real sentences, with audio

Learn Hindi words with real sentences that demonstrate the various usages, meanings, and contexts in which they are typically used.

Each word included covers all meanings/senses of the word and comes with real example sentences across a range of registers, so you're not just memorizing definitions -- you're seeing how words actually behave in context. The goal is a rich, well-rounded core vocabulary, built one word at a time, that lets you understand and form more natural sentences and start moving comfortably into conversational Hindi.

A study order is a list of words in a deliberate sequence, built for a particular kind of learner. Choose the one that fits where you are.

Grammar to Vocabulary in Context

3,702 words

This word list is built for learners who have already worked through the core grammar of Hindi -- verb agreement, tenses, the major sentence patterns, compound and vector verbs, etc. -- but who don't yet feel they have an adequate vocabulary to participate in conversations. If grammar rules still take conscious effort to apply, and you're ready to focus less on structure and more on words, this order is for you.

The ordering of words focuses first on "structural" words like conjunctions, patterns, postpositions etc. but with verbs (especially auxiliary and vector verbs) shown early interleaved throughout the study order. A rich set of nouns, adjectives, etc. rounds it out.
